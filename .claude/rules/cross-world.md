Code here follows [cross-world rules](docs/CROSS_WORLD_RULES.md) XWR1–XWR28 (hubs, carry per crossing, authority per edge, time across worlds, local prices and cross-world trade, travel authorities, converged worlds, what others know of a traveler): working defaults under AD56, adjustable; XWR6, XWR8 and XWR27 stay proposals (the three held limiting lines in the limiting lines review). Every crossing names its carrier and what it carries (XWR5), goods and money cross as conserved stock at the carrier's valuation (XWR9, XWR16), prices are local shapes and never invented stock (XWR17), the world left behind is frozen and the recap says so (XWR12), and a return reaches an instance, not a name (XWR24). Cite the codes you apply in the task record or commit message; `node scripts/rule_routes.mjs --for <path>` lists them.
