# Cheat catalogue

Proposal catalogue, 2026-11-10 (AD32); palette, not spec. Requested by Weibao: "Maybe types of cheats should be kept as a catalogue somehwere, do be drawn upon and implmented eventually? other stuff and more?" and "D5, we should look further into this?" Rules: [CHEAT_RULES](CHEAT_RULES.md) CHT1-CHT20. His standing line applies here too: "The idea that there is no forced weakness should honestly apply to everything", so the limits column is an optional per-card adjective list (CHT12), with no required drawback (default: none; dial: per card).

This page dedupes the roughly 95 cheats spread over the [library](CHEAT_LIBRARY.md#more-cheat-types-2026-10-10) (49 families, 20 gameplay families, AQ routes, 13 idea cards), the [cosmology](CHEAT_COSMOLOGY.md#simple-cheats) (simple cheats), [cheats by world](CHEATS_BY_WORLD.md) (about 90 book and game rows), the [simulator doc](SIMULATORS_AND_CHEAT_IMPLEMENTATION.md) and the [core audit](audits/CHEAT_CORE_AUDIT.md) into types. A type is a shape of advantage; a card (CHT4) is one world's version with terms. Rows marked "new, agent addition" are common web-novel or game tropes the repo does not yet name; they are agent proposals, not accepted direction.

Reading the columns. **Backing** is the CHT5 list: soul, body, item, system, world law, patron, knowledge, concept. **Engine hooks** name the seam a card would use: power registry (ABR16, as `cheat.rehearsed_foe` does), knowledge carriers (KNW), time bank (`native-clock.forward`, Patient Furnace), making (crafting patterns), ties and standing, entry (WER and the CHT16 warning), carry ledger (AD11), gates, seeded streams, harvest. **Status** names built modules in `src/` (`cheat_definitions.js`, `cheat_cards.js`, `cheat_growth.js`, `rehearsed_foe.js`), "partly" where an engine piece exists without a cheat card, and "not built" otherwise.

Implementation orientation, checked against source on 2026-11-25: the quiet base faculty (Continuing insight) has eight facets (True Appraisal, Loaded Dice, Fate Ledger, Earned Habit, Pattern Book, Heirloom Compact, Patient Furnace, Freshness Sense). There is also an optional quest-system card with awakening and a finite first-victory point claim, a ring-spirit card with an unreliable voice, kind skins and a growth track, and one NPC-held power-registry cheat (`cheat.rehearsed_foe`). Grants route through `applyStartGrants` from a start's `grants`. Continuing insight also offers a reversible starting point until it is spent; the [native comparison](gm/dispatch/play-surface-2026-11-14/CHEAT_STARTING_INSIGHT_2026-11-25.md) follows equal earned milestones and an actual fourth appraisal.

The comparisons below name actionable decisions, not additional grants or an execution queue. **N** means a dated native browser receipt supports that bounded behavior; its fixture, bundle and acquisition limits remain in the receipt. **S** means inspected source supplies a narrower behavior or an ordinary seam, without a new browser claim here. **P** means the named cheat decision is proposed, even when an ordinary consumer exists. No status certifies every form of a family. Each example names the holder and place that would own its outcome.

The [hypothetical card-world trial](representations/CARD_WORLD_WORKBENCH_TRIAL_2026-11-25.md) separately proves +1 opening affordability, target-specific ward, a useful collected key and bounded orders in developer Workbench. It is not a native Quiet Faculty, physique or corruption-ward implementation. For standing orders, compare the same action costs and information as manual play, with a player-selected goal, expense and stop. [Worked ward and noncombat examples](CHEAT_LIBRARY.md#idle-execution-story-agency-and-overwhelming-cheats-2026-11-25) describe the design direction; a named order still needs its own consumer.

## How to draw from it

1. Pick a type by what the world and start need, not by rarity. The row's backing (CHT5) says where it goes when the player crosses worlds.
2. Write one card per CHT4: id, family, terms (gift, matured, lease, loan and so on), backing, kind for the skin (CHT6), hooks, and a state block. Put limits on the card as adjectives when the card wants them (CHT12). Declare the base cheat per start (CHT3) before any other cheat.
3. Pick the route (CHT8, AQ1-AQ14) and rarity dials: how common, who knows, how one learns it is real.
4. Choose the cheapest seam first. In order: a per-start grant through `applyStartGrants` (one `grants` entry per start); then a power-registry effect like `cheat.rehearsed_foe`; then a knowledge or time-bank facet like the existing eight modules. The existing growth tier names are presentation; an additional tier effect needs an actual consumer and its own proof.
5. Add the entry line for the cheat's reach (CHT16). Cheats that talk state their reliability (CHT20).
6. Cheats that read or foresee show uncertainty by their grade and follow sealed-secret discovery routes (CHT19). Record the one-line result in the owning doc and add the row's Status here.

Earlier candidate ordering, retained as an agent proposal rather than current execution priority: CTC6 daily intelligence (knowledge and rumor seams exist), CTC24 sign-in (calendar and map), CTC5 future knowledge as a card over the existing knowledge carry, CTC82 patron gift (terms already exist), CTC23 effort pays off (a pacing dial).
