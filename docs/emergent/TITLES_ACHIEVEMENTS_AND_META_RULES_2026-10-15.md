# Titles, achievements and tags acting on rules

2026-10-15. Connected expansion under [E4 tags and rules](E4_TAGS_AND_RULES.md),
following the [Proto23 label/title pass](../research/proto23/TAGS_IDENTITY_AND_COMIC_LOGIC_2026-10-15.md).
[User direction](../USER_VISION.md#titles-achievements-and-tags-on-rules-2026-10-15)
accepts the possibility of actual title powers through worlds or relevant
cheats, achievements as a related avenue, and tags affecting rules themselves.
The mechanics below are proposals, not selected implementation or canon lore.
Current priorities and the one-playable-slice direction remain unchanged.

## C2. Deed, recognition, grant and power

An achievement recognizes an accomplishment; a title can name it or a role;
a grant supplies an effect; a power is a capability under its backing. These
steps can combine or be absent. A world may grant an unnamed power for a
deed. A title may itself be an operative metaphysical object. A cheat may
recognize achievements where the world has no achievement system.

`event or recognition premise -> achievement/title -> issuer/cheat grant ->
capability/rule/resource -> useful consumer`.

Recognition need not certify an objectively performed feat in every premise.
A legend power might grow from belief, bureaucratic magic from registration,
or a thief might appropriate a genuine title-power. Identify what actually
empowers the result. Some titles remain social standing or biography.

| Code | Proposed grant mode | Original example | Distinctive consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | World awards a power for a deed | Restore a refuge through a winter and gain a hearth faculty | The world supplies the grant; the title may merely announce it |
| G2 | Title is actual authority | The recognized Keeper can close a particular boundary | Succession or contested recognition matters if that title's rules say so |
| G3 | Cheat empowers titles | A collector turns a craft epithet into a technique | The cheat supplies something the ordinary title lacks |
| G4 | Private achievement system | An unrecognized feat grants a point, skill, item or law fragment | Public recognition is unnecessary unless this system requires it |
| G5 | A deed changes the world | A founded refuge remains available to later visitors | Reward belongs to the place, service, route or world, not necessarily the holder |
| G6 | Selected expression | Wear an epithet to express its domain | Held history and permanent learning need not switch off; no universal slot count |
| G7 | Evolving title | Care, teaching and protection develop different hearth facets | Development changes verbs or scope, not just rarity and percentages |
| G8 | Bestowal, inheritance or appropriation | A successor receives a mantle | Receiving its power may differ from earning its original deed |

One deed can satisfy multiple independent grant sources when their premises
permit it. Do not accidentally grant the same reward twice through two
listeners. Intentional repeatable achievements, escalating rewards and reward
loops remain possible. Repeating play and replaying one settlement after
reload are different events. No universal anti-farming cap is selected.

## C3. Cheat families and their different loops

| Code | Cheat proposal | Loop | Required meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Title collector | Accumulate, combine, develop or express epithets | Does power derive from acquisition, belief, display, deeds or a combination? |
| H2 | Achievement architect | Discover, choose or define challenges and rewards | Who creates/funds the reward; can defining a challenge itself create power? |
| H3 | Deed distiller | Turn life events into techniques, memories, materials or rule facets | What is copied, retained or consumed; output versus personal mastery |
| H4 | Mantle thief/inheritor | Transfer operative entitlement | Power, reputation, issuer recognition and original history need not all transfer |
| H5 | Rule annotator | Alter a property of an operative rule | Exact target, annotation meaning and changed behavior |
| H6 | Rule collector | Learn, copy, carry or compose encountered rules | Observation becomes ownership only if this power grants that |
| H7 | Legend maker | Grow through belief and retelling | Witnesses and dissemination are causal inputs for this particular premise |
| H8 | Continuity anchor | Retain more powers or rule fragments between lives/worlds | Memories and the base cheat already persist; other retention depends on means and can improve |

Use the library's existing backing/carrier, acquisition, control, growth,
continuity, compatibility, observers and consumer questions. Costs, counters,
slots, patrons, equalization and visible tells are possible properties, not
requirements. A cheat may be free, automatic, absolute or unusually broad.
The current informational status-window stage does not cap what another cheat
or a later stage can do. This expansion does not silently enlarge that build.

## C4. Rules can be entities and targets

Distinguish selecting a rule by its tags, changing its operative properties,
rewriting its definition and changing what an observer calls it. A vocabulary
can make a tag an actual modifier; another can use it only for classification.

| Code | Rule property | Original proposed operation | Actual consequence to define |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Domain | Select combustion rules | Separate physical combustion from a legal ban on arson |
| M2 | Carrier/source | Make an artifact rule soul-carried | Continued operation requires an actual transfer of backing |
| M3 | Scope | Extend hearth protection over a refuge | Covered events really change, not just displayed radius |
| M4 | Trigger | Make a contact curse contagious through another contact route | Specify what transmits, to whom and under which conditions |
| M5 | Target predicate | Make an oath-door recognize the rightful bearer rather than a seal | Physical token and entitlement now produce different outcomes |
| M6 | Duration/cadence | Make an effect enduring, recurring or retroactive | Define affected history and material consequences |
| M7 | Interaction/priority | Give a ward precedence against selected attacks | Resolve actual authority; absolute has its selected operative meaning |
| M8 | Information | Hide, reveal or falsify a detectable rule signature | Observer knowledge changes; physics changes only if also granted |
| M9 | Reward semantics | Change a title-award rule to grant a technique instead of a temporary buff | Decide whether future awards, current holders or both change |
| M10 | Mutability | Seal a rule against one editor or authorize a cheat to revise it | Rule-editing is itself an interaction with a resolved outcome |

This extends to rules that award achievements, interpret evidence, apply
tags or permit rule editing. A power can modify the conditions of its own
growth. E4's technique/intent/concept/law ladder is one possible progression,
not a universal ceiling. Unusual permissions can come from the world or cheat.

Self-reference needs a selected meaning: next event, a defined phase of this
event, or an explicit recursive/fixed-point model. Preserve that meaning.
An unhandled infinite engine loop is not an infinite in-world power. An
intentionally unbounded effect can have a finite truthful representation.
Do not solve implementation limits by inventing universal in-world caps.

Adding a word to a display cannot teach an unsupported interpreter new
behavior. A true rule-writing cheat may supply that behavior in the fiction;
implementation needs an admitted operation and consumer. The implementation
boundary does not forbid the broader design possibility.

## C5. NPCs, institutions and shared accomplishments

| Code | Connection | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | NPCs earn or receive operative titles through eligible routes | A keeper can develop a genuine refuge power independently of the player |
| N2 | Holders have motives and different cheats | An NPC may seek recognition, conceal a grant, refuse an office or pursue a private achievement |
| N3 | Joint deeds have causal contributions and credited recognition | Rescue can grant shared or role-specific rewards; an issuer may credit it wrongly; no universal last-hit policy |
| N4 | Institutions and witnesses have limited knowledge | Certification, rumor and actual capacity can disagree; belief may still empower a legend where supported |
| N5 | Inheritance, copying, teaching and theft target particular things | A mantle need not include memory, trust or mastery; an extraordinary transfer can explicitly include them |
| N6 | Independent growth changes revisits | Preserve deeds, resources and grants; avoid inventing progress solely to match player strength |

Ordinary actors may remain templates. Important holders need durable identity
and the history their consequences depend on. Offscreen growth uses supported
simulation or declared coarse resolution. This is not a claim that all NPCs
already have achievement simulation. Hidden holders must not leak through
title lists, sorting, threat estimates or reward previews.

## C6. Worlds and continuity

| Code | Original world shape | Recognition/power behavior | Travel question |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | Institutional | Offices and deeds confer local standing; a cheat may add actual powers | Can that cheat operate without local support? |
| W2 | System | Eligible beings gain titles, abilities or rewards through world rules | Is the grant permanent or maintained by that world? |
| W3 | Mythic | An epithet becomes mantle or domain | Does it belong to soul, office, place, believers or another backing? |
| W4 | Conceptual | Understanding and practice confer facets and law fragments | Which facets travel and which destination rules consume them? |
| W5 | Comic/literal | A recognized phrase has real force through authored comic logic | Does the authority stay local or travel with an unusual cheat? |
| W6 | Converged/nested | Multiple grant and interpretation systems coexist | Which treaty, barrier, dominant law or personal power resolves this event? |

No universal cancellation, conversion, equalization or home-world superiority
is selected. A local office can coexist with a portable technique and a
soul-backed title. Source-qualified worlds retain their actual rules; these
examples are original possibilities, not new franchise claims.

Separate retained history, entitlement, active capability, required means and
local recognition. Losing the issuer need not erase learned technique; a
maintained grant may end when its backing ends. Permanent grants also remain
possible. Memories and the base cheat retain accepted continuity; retention
of other gains can improve. A remembered former title need not still power
an office, but memory can inform earning or reclaiming it.

## C7. Persistence, interactions and current implementation boundary

For a selected consequential grant, retain the event/recognition premise,
holder, issuer/backing, achievement/title identity, grant identity/revision,
resulting capability or rule revision and continuity. Keep attributed claims
separate where they matter. These are semantic questions, not mandatory new
fields on every flavor word.

For a rule change, retain target identity, before/after revisions, effective
boundary, scope and causal source. Existing products and settled events keep
their historical truth unless the power explicitly changes that history.
Decide whether the new rule governs future grants, existing grants or both.
Removing a title must not accidentally erase independently learned mastery;
keeping the historical record must not accidentally prolong a revoked grant.

Compound events can intentionally chain: deed, title, new power, further
achievement, revised award rule. Settle the sequence once per actual event
while allowing authored repetition. Secret/public goals, manual/automatic
claims and retroactive recognition are world/card choices. UI refreshes never
issue grants. Durations do not imply attendance chores or offline punishment.

Current source: `tag_store.js` is a presence/query/snapshot helper, while
`rule_modules.js` admits the bounded `village.mix@2` grammar with specific
roles, facts and effects. These do not already make rules generic target
entities or implement all meta-effects. Native title records, simulation
title facts, cheat-card state and knowledge retain their owners. The prior
[current-source mapping](../research/proto23/TAGS_IDENTITY_AND_COMIC_LOGIC_2026-10-15.md#f8-the-live-yairpg-seams-are-more-developed-than-the-old-draft)
owns the checked seams and focused-test limits.

If selected for implementation, extend one admitted typed operation and useful
consumer, preserve old readers/resolved revisions, and exercise ordinary
acquisition, changed-positive behavior, an unaffected control, scope,
observer boundaries and changed-state save/reload/export/import on a rebuilt
desktop origin. Definition text alone proves no gameplay behavior.

## C8. Disposition and bounded next possibilities

ACCOUNT FOR NOW: actual title powers and achievement rewards may derive from
worlds or cheats; rules themselves may be targets; reuse existing owners and
preserve actual authority, continuity and observation.

LATER, unselected: one title grants a useful action; one cheat converts an
achievement to a retained technique; an NPC earns a related grant through
supported events; one rule-editing power changes a supported predicate or
scope. Choose one playable slice and finish it before the next. The earlier
repair-and-return idea remains optional, not a new required opening world.

Documentation only. No runtime edit, build, save change, new gameplay proof,
commit or push. No new external source or canon assertion is introduced.

## C9. Evolving and combining titles and achievements

Follow-up requested by Weibao: extend evolution, combination, inheritance and
theft; titles for items, places and worlds; cheats that change achievement
criteria. These are permitted design avenues under the [recorded direction](../USER_VISION.md#titles-achievements-and-tags-on-rules-2026-10-15).
Specific transformations and examples below remain proposals.

Evolution can be a graph, not only a rarity ladder. Preserve an identity's
history while its expression branches, combines, divides, becomes dormant,
is reinterpreted or is reclaimed. Distinguish a title changing from a holder
earning a different title, and an achievement changing its criteria from a
completed achievement gaining another stage.

| Code | Transformation | Original example | Connection to prior ideas |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Deepen | A hearth title gains finer control over warmth and shelter | E8 aspects: improvement can be control or new verbs, not just magnitude |
| E2 | Branch | A sheltering title develops toward household care, sanctuary or boundary defense | Actual practice and chosen means can matter; branches need not exclude one another |
| E3 | Compose without consumption | A craft title and teaching title jointly enable a lesson | A rule reads both grants; no third title or fusion is required |
| E4 | Fuse or synthesize | Care and warding epithets become one refuge mantle | Define whether the inputs remain active, become historical or are actually consumed |
| E5 | Split | A broad office divides into stewardship of the hearth and stewardship of its boundary | E8 shards suggest transferable facets; title fragments need not inherit shard wills or sensing |
| E6 | Reinterpret | A conqueror's title develops toward maintaining peace | Current meaning can change while prior deeds remain in history; forgiveness is not an automatic universal result |
| E7 | Awaken or recover | An inherited title becomes usable after understanding its method | Dormant entitlement, knowledge and operational means are distinct |
| E8 | Combine achievements | Several distinct rescues qualify as a larger accomplishment | Preserve which deeds satisfy which clauses; repeated credit is allowed when the granting rules allow it |

Combining names is not necessarily combining powers. Combining powers need
not combine their social authority. Two words may form an epithet through
literal adjective magic, while another system requires compatible actual
faculties. Do not derive an arbitrary technique merely from a pleasing name.
A sufficiently broad synthesis cheat can supply that missing operation.

Existing superlative adjectives suggest contested titles: best craftsperson,
oldest refuge, strongest blade. Each needs a comparison domain and meaning:
strongest known to an issuer, strongest presently measured, or a genuine
world-certified maximum. A holder can lose a current championship without
losing the historical achievement. Unique, shared and divisible titles are
all possible; a display name alone establishes no global uniqueness.

## C10. Inheritance, theft, copying and succession

Treat transfer as an operation on a specified entitlement, grant, record or
backing. It is not automatically a move of everything associated with a name.

| Code | Operation | What may move | Question that changes the result |
|---|---|---|---|
| TR1 | Inherit | Mantle, artifact-bound faculty, claim to office, title fragment | Is succession determined by a will, kinship, appointment, soul, institution or the title itself? |
| TR2 | Steal | Actual operative grant or its carrier | Does taking the carrier suffice, or must the cheat sever/redirect a backing link? |
| TR3 | Copy | A duplicate technique, title expression or independent grant | Does copying preserve the original, share a power source or create new backing? |
| TR4 | Delegate or lend | Temporary exercise of some authority | Owner, holder, permitted user and beneficiary can differ; expiry and return follow the agreement/power |
| TR5 | Bestow | A new grant created or authorized by an issuer | It need not reduce the issuer's own power unless that premise requires it |
| TR6 | Usurp or contest | Recognition, office, effective control or metaphysical authority | An institution's judgment and the title's operative truth can disagree |
| TR7 | Reclaim or reunite | A lost mantle or separated facets | Identify current holders, prior history and whether reunification changes their powers |

A stolen title can genuinely work if its world or cheat permits that. It
need not carry an automatic curse, legitimacy test or weakness. Conversely,
stealing a badge can be mere impersonation in a different system. Legal
ownership, metaphysical entitlement, public belief and operational capability
are separate questions, any of which may be directly changed by an appropriate
power. Theft need not erase the original deed; memory rewriting or history
theft is a separate, possible scope.

Inheritance is not necessarily death-triggered. Retirement, appointment,
abdication, rebirth, a place choosing a keeper or a world splitting can all
transfer authority. NPCs can initiate, refuse, negotiate or contest transfers
where their agency allows. No automatic NPC hostility or universal succession
contest is required. Claims and consequences need their actual causal routes.

Achievement inheritance can pass a completed record's entitlement, an unlocked
route, a reward or partial progress rather than the ancestor's personal feat.
An achievement thief might steal claimability before a reward is claimed,
redirect its future yield, or take the completed achievement itself under a
system that treats it as transferable property. Retain original performer
and transfer history unless the power explicitly rewrites those facts.
Unclaimed, claimed, repeatable and inherited rewards need distinct outcomes;
receiving an old record must not accidentally reissue a spent one-time reward,
but a cheat can intentionally grant exactly that second claim.

## C11. Items, places, organizations and worlds as title holders

Separate subject, performer, credited achiever, issuer, carrier and beneficiary
when they differ. A person can perform a deed with a sword; an issuer may
recognize the person, sword, group or place. A world achievement need not be
the player's personal achievement. These roles are semantic distinctions,
not mandatory fields on every minor flavor label.

| Code | Holder | Original title idea | Useful consequence and identity question |
|---|---|---|---|
| EN1 | Unique item | A blade remembered for cutting a particular binding | Its accumulated history may grant a specific severing technique; determine whether repair/reforging preserves the same object |
| EN2 | Artifact component or set | A shield's repaired boss carries the old defense mantle | Does authority follow the part, the assembled shield or a transferable grant? |
| EN3 | Place | A refuge earns recognition for sheltering travelers through a disaster | Safe rest, a ward or a route may benefit eligible visitors; ownership does not automatically control its power |
| EN4 | Organization | A guild earns a teaching or rescue distinction | Its members can receive services or shared techniques under actual membership/eligibility rules |
| EN5 | Region | A valley earns a restoration epithet | Local growth, access or environmental processes change within a defined boundary |
| EN6 | World | A realm survives an invasion or restores a broken cycle | The achievement may open an exit, restore a law or change eligible future starts; universal resident bonuses are not implied |
| EN7 | A joint holder | A crew, household or alliance shares a title | Its membership and continuity can change without inventing identical individual deeds |

An item title could be inherent, accumulated, bestowed, forged into it,
believed about it or supplied by its wielder's cheat. The same is true at
larger scales where the premise supports it. An object need not be conscious
to acquire history or receive an operative grant. A sentient artifact may
also have its own goals, deeds and claims.

Titles belonging to a stackable type differ from titles on one unique
instance. Repair, component replacement, disassembly, merging stacks, copying
an artifact and forging a successor need explicit identity decisions before
consequential transfers. Reusing a visible name does not establish identity.
This connects to E4's item-instance decision and the prior material/product
research, not to a new universal string-tag inventory key.

A titled place can move, be abandoned, split or merge. A settlement's people
may migrate while its old site remains. Which retains the mantle can depend
on land, inhabitants, institution, portable hearth or a world law. Similarly,
a pocket world, branch timeline and parent world can have different titles.
Destruction, copying or reunion does not automatically settle all their claims.
Existing multiverse identity/continuity owners remain authoritative.

## C12. Cheats that change what counts as an achievement

Achievement criteria are rules too. Changing eligibility is distinct from
changing an event, accepted evidence, credited actor, reward or historical
record. A cheat can act on any of these if its scope grants it.

| Code | Changed criterion | Original proposed cheat effect | Distinction to retain |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC1 | Event meaning | Count preventing a battle as overcoming its threat | Prevention and victory can both qualify without pretending the battle occurred |
| AC2 | Method | Let delegated work or a noncombat solution satisfy a feat | Completion, personal practice and credited contribution can differ |
| AC3 | Eligible subject | Let an artifact, group or world earn a normally personal achievement | Define the real holder and beneficiaries rather than silently crediting the player |
| AC4 | Measure/threshold | Count quality, difficulty or saved lives instead of raw action totals | The measuring rule changes; source events remain available unless explicitly rewritten |
| AC5 | Time/window | Admit deeds before acquiring the system or accumulate progress across lives | Distinguish retained evidence and memory from omniscient access to an unrecorded past |
| AC6 | Evidence/recognition | Accept a sincere witness, institutional decree or legend as qualifying | Such recognition can be genuinely causal under the selected premise, not merely a fake record |
| AC7 | Attribution | Let the tool, planner, rescuer or appointed successor receive credit | Credit transfer need not transfer mastery; a broader power can explicitly do both |
| AC8 | Composition | Treat several smaller feats as one larger achievement, or split a feat into stages | The same events may legitimately serve multiple conditions under the selected rules |
| AC9 | Repeatability | Make another eligible success grant again or develop the existing reward | Authored repetition is different from accidental duplicate settlement |
| AC10 | Criterion authorship | Define a new challenge and its payoff | Specify whether a system allocates a reward or the cheat itself creates it; no universal finite reward budget |
| AC11 | Rule priority or reach | Make private recognition override a chosen local grant condition | This edits authority only within the granted scope; it does not silently rewrite every institution |
| AC12 | Inversion or absence | Reward a deliberate refusal, a prevented event or a sustained peaceful interval | Absence is a real criterion with a context/window; missing telemetry is not proof it happened |

Nested achievements can reward learning to change achievements. An editor
could change its own growth condition; retain the self-reference semantics
from C4. Challenges can remain authored, be generated from actual opportunities,
or be privately defined by a holder. No mandatory quest board, daily attendance
or endless checklist follows from permitting this power.

Changing a criterion now may affect future evaluations, re-evaluate retained
past evidence, preserve existing grants as grandfathered, revoke maintained
grants or explicitly rewrite history. Choose the consequence. Quietly
re-evaluating every old achievement against the latest data would accidentally
choose for the player. Deliberate retroactive powers remain possible.

## C13. Connected examples, continuity and disposition

Original example A: a refuge earns a winter-survival title; an NPC keeper
inherits its local office; a visitor's cheat credits prevention of exposure as
a rescue achievement. The refuge gains a protection facet, the keeper gains
authority to direct it and the visitor learns a portable method. None needs
to receive every other participant's history or power.

Original example B: a blade's deed-title develops a severing facet; a collector
combines that with a personal binding title to create a technique. Another
holder steals the blade. Whether they receive the severing faculty depends
on its actual carrier, while the collector's independently learned technique
can remain. A more powerful theft could target that technique separately.

For a selected feature, retain stable holder and title/grant identities,
transformation lineage, input/output grants and their disposition, transfer
parties and authority, criteria revisions, credited evidence, and effective
time/scope. A transfer receipt must settle both sides together; a split must
not accidentally duplicate an exclusive mantle, while an explicitly copying
power may deliberately create another one. Preserve declared shared grants.

Names, aliases and history are read views, not unique identities. A broken or
unknown future title ID should not silently acquire a new meaning on import.
Retain old criteria/readers and resolved awards unless a selected transition
explicitly changes them. Saved records must distinguish dormant entitlement,
revoked maintained effects and retained learned capabilities.

ACCOUNT FOR NOW: holders can extend beyond people; evolution and transfer
have explicit meanings; achievement criteria can themselves be power targets.
These extend C2-C7 rather than replacing them. World/cheat premises govern
which possibilities apply; no universal inheritance, theft, fusion cost,
exclusivity or reward cap is selected.

LATER: choose one grant and a meaningful evolution, transfer or criterion edit
with a real consumer, then verify ordinary play and exact persistence through
the existing acceptance gates. A world-scale title does not require simulating
every inhabitant or implementing every other holder type first. Documentation
only; no new runtime, browser or persistence claim.
