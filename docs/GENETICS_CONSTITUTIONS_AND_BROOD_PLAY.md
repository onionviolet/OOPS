# Genetics, constitutions and brood play

Proposal palette, 2026-10-11. User prompt: “Genetics, constitutions and more?
pllaying as a genestealer? more to consider?”

This expands the existing [person layers and genetics](LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#layers-of-a-person)
and [constitution processes](LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#p7-constitutions-change-processes-not-just-totals).
It records possibilities, not accepted rules, a runtime claim or an execution
assignment. No existing secret is disclosed. No universal flaw, slot budget,
dominance model, rarity ladder or power ceiling is introduced.

## G1 Distinct layers, connected effects

| Layer | Question it answers | Possible gameplay |
|---|---|---|
| Genetics | What can this body inherit, express or transmit? | Dormant traits, hybrid offspring, engineered changes, acquired genetic material |
| Constitution | How does the whole body function? | Unusual recovery, energy circulation, digestion, temperature tolerance or cultivation |
| Bloodline | What lineage faculty exists in this world? | Ancestral senses, awakening, inherited authority or a specific supernatural organ |
| Anatomy | What structures actually perform the work? | Extra limbs, venom glands, altered lungs, distributed organs or replacement parts |
| Adaptation | What changes through experience or exposure? | Acclimation, learned regulation, persistent remodeling or temporary expression |
| Identity | Who inhabits and directs the body? | One person, shared host, clone, colony, symbiont or brood connection |

These are authoring distinctions, not six compulsory player panels. A fantasy
bloodline need not be DNA; a constitution can be acquired without heredity.
World-specific mechanisms decide what transfers and how. Simple dominant and
recessive pairs are one model, not a universal account of real genetics.

Examples: inheriting a heat-tolerant structure, learning to regulate it, and
passing it to an heir are separate events. A rebuilt body can retain a soul
faculty while losing a limb-dependent technique. A constitution can improve
many processes without requiring a compensating flaw.

## G2 Two meanings of genestealer

**Warhammer Genestealer:** a setting-specific candidate. Official material
supports multigenerational infiltration, a Patriarch and a psychic Broodmind
that coordinates people while retaining individuality. It also describes
Genestealers as Tyranid shock troops. These bounded sources do not establish
an unrestricted ability to loot arbitrary genes from defeated targets.

- [Official faction introduction](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/WdP1REPb/starting-a-genestealer-cults-army-in-warhammer-40000-everything-you-need-to-know-from-painting-to-lore/)
- [Official Patriarch retrospective](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/f6DpT12Z/40-years-of-warhammer-bow-before-the-genestealer-cult-patriarch/)

A direct route needs a later bounded source pass on life cycle, psychic
limits, host species and the chosen era. Divergence is possible under the
project's existing premise choices, but changed behavior must be labeled.

**Original gene thief:** another candidate, with its own mechanism. Collect
samples through hunting, scavenging, trade or medical access; identify useful
structures; integrate them; develop their expression. Copying a body trait,
an individual's learned skill and a soul-bound power are separate targets.
One route need not grant all three, though a world can support exceptions.

## G3 Different playable seats

| Seat | First useful play | Longer progression |
|---|---|---|
| Lone organism | Find shelter, feed, scout and survive with an unusual body | Grow faculties, establish a foothold or remain solitary |
| Hybrid individual | Work, maintain relationships and navigate divided loyalties | Gain influence, specialize or choose a different allegiance |
| Patriarch / brood founder | Establish a nest and a small connected group | Build a lineage, coordinate cells and reshape institutions |
| Brood perspective | Act through reachable members with distinct jobs | Grow a distributed network, negotiate autonomy and handle succession |
| Original gene thief | Acquire and understand a useful sample | Build an evolving body or genetic archive |

These are proposals, not interchangeable canonical roles. Player perspective
and actual command authority must be explicit. Members keep individual
knowledge, capabilities, histories and motives unless the specific connection
really carries them. A network does not automatically reveal every mind.

## G4 More to consider

| Consideration | Useful questions and consequences |
|---|---|
| Expression versus possession | Is a trait present but dormant? Does development, a trigger or an organ make it usable? |
| Somatic versus inherited change | Does a graft affect only this body, future descendants, clones, or an archived template? |
| Sample versus faculty | Does collecting tissue grant information, a blueprint, an organ or immediate power? |
| Development | Do juvenile and mature forms have different actions? Can growth be redirected? |
| Multiple lineages | Can traits coexist, fuse, stay in separate tissues or produce an exceptional combination? No compulsory conflict. |
| Host and symbiont | Who pays resources, perceives, acts and retains memories? Can the relationship change? |
| Body-shaped everyday life | Can this body wear gear, speak, write, eat local food, enter buildings and use existing tools? |
| Social recognition | Who notices anatomical signs, follows family links or has a real diagnostic means? |
| Network reach | What carries messages and orders? What happens when a member is isolated? |
| Institutional footholds | Do workers, medics, traders and officials provide distinct access rather than a generic influence score? |
| Succession and loss | What survives the founder: individuals, connections, genetic archive, territory or teachings? |
| Crossing worlds | Which body, sample, organ, memory or host physically carries the change? Does it still function there? |
| Hidden knowledge | What the body truly is, what its owner suspects and what an observer can diagnose are distinct. |
| Idle progression | What can members safely do while time advances? Which transformations or crises pause for a choice? |

## G5 Small playable proof candidate

My preferred first proof is an original lone-organism start with one useful
acquired organ and one small network foothold. Give it actual body-specific
actions, a visible change in everyday interaction, and an observer who needs
evidence to recognize it. It should support remaining solitary or developing
the foothold, rather than forcing a colony campaign immediately.

This is a recommendation, not an authorized build. A direct Warhammer start
would instead need its own source-grounded proof and must not inherit the
original gene-looting premise accidentally.

If selected: inspect current body, actor, source and knowledge adapters;
declare retention and save compatibility; build; exercise changed gameplay
from a fresh origin; save, reload and import. Verify body effects, individual
versus network knowledge, and loss/succession separately. Until then this
document establishes design coverage only.

## Deeper expansion, 2026-10-11

Follow-up: “MOre expantions and more, deeper considerations on the micro and
macro level?” The following extends the palette. Examples are original
proposals, not additional Warhammer lore claims. G5 is still only one possible
proof; neither a weak start nor sequential solo-to-colony progression is required.
Random entry can place the player in a mature lineage or an established network
if that snapshot has coherent history and usable actions.

### G6 Micro: a living body with usable differences

| Detail | Choice or interaction | Continuing consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Trait expression | Wake, suppress or redirect a supported faculty | Conceal an adaptation, specialize it or keep it continuously active |
| Tissue location | Put a function in skin, eyes, a limb or a separate organ | Local injury, replacement and equipment affect the actual function |
| Mosaic body | Different parts retain different templates | Preserve a trusted limb while trying an unfamiliar form elsewhere |
| Development stage | Mature naturally, accelerate or keep a useful juvenile form | Stages can offer different actions rather than a strict upgrade ladder |
| Sensory channels | See heat, taste traces, feel vibration or hear another range | The same room offers different evidence and possible errors |
| Motor learning | Learn to use changed fingers, wings, balance or reach | Anatomical capacity and practiced coordination improve separately |
| Diet and processing | Use ordinary food, sunlight, minerals or another supported input | Cooking, farming and trade can become body progression routes |
| Storage and secretion | Store fuel, produce resin, shed plates or secrete a useful material | A body becomes a tool, producer or support specialist |
| Regulation | Choose a resting, concealed, working or combat state | Actual physiology determines transitions; no obligatory stance tax |
| Repair template | Heal toward a birth form, current form or a saved template | Regeneration, medicine and deliberate transformation can cooperate |
| Personal variation | Two members express the same trait differently | Familiarity with a person matters beyond their species label |
| Symbiotic ecology | Carry organisms with their own functions and needs | A partner may supply digestion, sensing or protection without being an organ |
| Equipment interfaces | Adapt gloves, handles, clothes, saddles or instruments | Tailoring, smithing and engineering remain valuable at high power |
| Voice and gesture | Speak, sign, sing or communicate through another channel | A stronger body need not use the same social tools as its old form |
| Appearance and traces | Leave recognizable prints, shed tissue or show visible anatomy | Evidence has a physical route; NPCs do not gain automatic detection |

Choose details that create useful actions, meaningful observations or texture.
Do not turn every tissue into a maintenance bar. A constitution can replace
several mundane needs entirely when its actual mechanism supports that.

Possible constitutions should have distinct operating logic: an energy reservoir
that stores several fuels; a body that grows stronger structures under load;
distributed vital organs; a body rebuilt around an external core; a regenerative
template that permits deliberate edits; or a body whose circulation is also its
spell geometry. These are mechanisms to develop, not six accepted archetypes.

Progression can improve precision, ease of use, scale, persistence, transmission
or versatility. More damage is only one direction. A previously inconvenient
faculty becoming natural enough for everyday work can be a substantial advance.

### G7 Household, clinic and nest: the bridge to macro play

| Place or group | Actual activities | Why it changes wider play |
|---|---|---|
| Household | Raise children, teach movement, share meals, preserve family records | Traits, knowledge, wealth and belonging travel by different routes |
| Clinic | Examine symptoms, offer services, compare outcomes, rehabilitate bodies | Builds knowledge and trust; can reveal patterns only with relevant evidence |
| Workshop | Fit tools and equipment to unfamiliar anatomy | Makes an exceptional body useful to others and supports a new trade |
| Nest / nursery | Provide shelter, incubation, care and accessible materials | A stable place supports development rather than merely adding population |
| Sample archive | Record origin, custody, known properties and preservation method | A collected sample is an asset, not an already expressed power |
| Teaching circle | Teach an ordinary skill using bodies with different means | Shared expertise need not imply identical movements or faculties |
| Brood cell | Coordinate work, scouting, care or defense through actual communication | A role is defined by actions and access, not a generic worker bonus |
| Mobile caravan | Carry members, supplies, records and equipment | Lets a lineage or network migrate without automatic global reach |

Distinguish quantity, capacity and competence. Fifty members do not create a
surgeon, a sequencer or a functioning nursery. Conversely, a sufficiently capable
individual might replace a facility's entire function. Specify the real means.

Care and reproduction need their own setting-specific time and agency policies.
Family formation, recruitment, transformation, conversion and psychic connection
are distinct relations. Keep origin and influence truthful; a coerced relation
does not become loyalty just because an influence meter fills. Support choices
about such relationships where the selected perspective actually has them.

### G8 Macro: populations, institutions and history

| Scale | Potential expansion | Causal detail to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| Lineage | Branch families, mixed inheritances, adoption, apprentices and estranged heirs | Genetic relation, household membership and political allegiance differ |
| Local economy | Body-derived materials, specialized labor and adapted housing | Real users, substitutes, ownership and demand determine useful production |
| Institution | Clinic, guild, temple, research school or covert network | Members have offices, resources, access and motives rather than blanket control |
| Region | Migration, refuges, new settlements and changing occupations | Routes, climate, food and existing inhabitants affect actual opportunities |
| Ecology | Organisms alter habitats, food chains and available materials | Effects follow supported feeding and reproduction, not a universal conquest meter |
| Political order | Recognition, bargains, alliances, faction splits or persecution | Knowledge and interests differ by actor; neither universal hostility nor acceptance |
| Research tradition | Private archive, open teaching, independent discovery or monopoly | Knowledge spreads only through its actual carrier and recipient's means |
| Generational time | A short-lived founder leaves a long-lived community | Leadership, teachings, property, templates and memories have separate successors |
| Civilization | New architecture, medicine, work patterns and transport | Population differences become lived infrastructure, not only military bonuses |
| Other worlds | Diasporas, isolated branches, sample trade and cross-world families | Each crossing carries specific bodies and things under local rules |

A lineage can become culturally influential without dominating population.
A powerful organism can remain solitary. A brood can specialize as a rescue,
craft or exploration network. A biological civilization can grow sideways into
new niches instead of always replacing weaker life. Conquest and predation are
also possibilities when fitting; none is a compulsory endpoint.

Other people continue their own lives. Rival researchers, ordinary families,
local wildlife and institutions do not exist solely to react to the player.
An intervention should change their supported course, not freeze every other
cause until the next player action.

### G9 Brood play, autonomy and feedback across scales

For an original brood route, author what a connection carries: presence,
emotion, a signal, sensory evidence, memories, commands, a skill or direct
control. These channels may differ by relationship and can develop separately.
Do not import this menu into Warhammer canon without source verification.

| Connection question | Playable consequence |
|---|---|
| Who is the player now? | One member, a founder, a shared consciousness or a commander has different actions |
| What happens to an isolated member? | It can continue its assignment, choose independently or lose a particular function |
| What counts as agreement? | Advice, negotiated duties, loyalty and compulsion need distinct interactions |
| Who knows a discovery? | A scout's observation remains local until a supported channel communicates it |
| Can members specialize? | A healer, artisan and explorer retain different practice and equipment |
| Can a cell split or rejoin? | Membership, relationships, shared property and any real connection change explicitly |
| What survives a death? | Surviving people and records differ from copied memories or continued consciousness |
| Can an enemy join or listen? | Access, authentication and interception depend on actual setting means |

Example original causal chains, not hidden world truths:

| Micro cause | Intermediate use | Macro change | Return to the individual |
|---|---|---|---|
| An organ produces a useful resin | An artisan makes reliable seals | A settlement improves water storage | Better local supplies support new body-development options |
| A member senses vibration | It learns to distinguish structural faults | A guild adopts a new inspection practice | Teaching provides income, tools and more exact practice |
| A constitution supports unusual cold tolerance | A caravan reaches an inaccessible pass | A new route changes trade and migration | New materials and contacts open further transformations |
| A lineage expresses unfamiliar hands | A workshop builds suitable instruments | A craft tradition develops different methods | Later children learn skills their founder had to invent |
| A reliable message channel connects cells | Members coordinate a medical response | A region develops a distributed care network | Individuals gain access to expertise without acquiring every skill |

The reverse direction also matters: a city changes its housing and that lets
large bodies live there; an institution acquires a diagnostic means and it can
notice evidence it previously missed; a political boundary separates a family
without erasing its shared ancestry. Several causes can interact without every
outcome becoming a scripted reward chain.

### G10 Starts, exceptions and manageable depth

Possible starting situations: unfamiliar body in an ordinary household; a hybrid
with an established profession; a solitary scavenger with a partial archive;
an heir in an old lineage; a founder of an existing brood; a disconnected member;
or a body specialist who can help others before changing themselves. Starts can
be powerful, vulnerable or socially established. Author actual starting means
and circumstances rather than forcing all of them through the same tutorial.

| Boundary | Question to settle for a selected slice |
|---|---|
| Transformation during work | Does the job continue with new means, adapt its method or pause at a clear point? |
| Knowledge without capacity | Can you teach or commission a procedure you cannot perform personally? |
| Capacity without understanding | Can instincts produce a useful result before you can explain it? |
| Repair versus replacement | Which identity, learned coordination and faculty belong to this tissue? |
| Clone versus heir | What is copied, inherited or taught? Similar bodies do not establish identical selves. |
| Powerful combination | Does it really resolve the problem? Do not add a universal penalty to undo it. |
| No descendants | Can you preserve teachings, an archive, adopted heirs or a nonhereditary legacy? |
| Network interruption | Which queued actions continue locally and which genuinely require contact? |
| Offline time | What advances safely, and which irreversible commitments require an authored policy? |
| Changing body scale | Can existing rooms, tools, actions and navigation still serve the new body? |
| Restoring a save | Do body state, archive custody, membership, knowledge and succession restore coherently? |

For usability, show familiar impressions, accessible actions and investigated
properties. Detailed anatomy belongs in an optional body view when the actor
has the means to know it. A player can enjoy a strange body without knowing its
entire explanation. Automation can handle settled routines; interruptions should
reflect actual meaningful changes rather than incessant upkeep.

Use the [completion standard](WORLD_COMPLETION_STANDARD.md) for selected details,
especially S02, S04–S09 and S11–S14. Choose authored joins with real consequences,
not the Cartesian product of every tissue and every world. The adjacent
[crafting/technology palette](research/CRAFTING_TECH_MICRO_MACRO_2026-10-11.md)
already owns workshop throughput and industrial diffusion; reference it instead
of inventing a second economy for biological products.

Current source inspection shows inherited/trained/acquired capability provenance
in `src/capabilities.js` and small body practice/recognition examples in
`src/body_progression.js`. Those provide candidate integration points; they do
not establish organ simulation, genetic inheritance or brood control. No build
or browser journey was run for this proposal-only expansion.

## Cultivation, wizardry and related traditions, 2026-10-11

User follow-up: “save accordingly, consider the concept of constitutions and
bloodlines in cultivation and wizardry and more?” Saved direction: expand these
concepts across traditions. The specific mechanics below remain proposals.
These are original design possibilities, not claims that every cultivation or
wizard setting works this way. Exact adaptations use their local source ledger.

### G11 Distinguish the source from what it enables

| Concept | Authoring question | Possible distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Constitution / physique | What enduring arrangement makes this person function differently? | Flesh, channels, soul, magical core or their relationship, as the world permits |
| Bloodline | What lineage-associated faculty or transmission exists? | Biological inheritance, soul inheritance, ancestral pact or another explicit mechanism |
| Aptitude / roots / affinity | What is accessible, easy to learn or compatible? | Affinity is not automatically a reserve of energy or a complete technique |
| Cultivation method | What deliberate practice develops power? | The method can reshape a body rather than merely read innate eligibility |
| Learned spell / technique | What procedure does the person understand and execute? | Knowing it does not necessarily grant its required organ, fuel or access |
| Realm / rank | What transformation or recognized stage occurred? | Social title, measurable power and actual transformation need not coincide |
| Ancestral legacy | What did earlier generations leave? | Archives, tools, obligations and instruction differ from inherited faculties |

One ability may span several rows without needing duplicate rewards. A bloodline
can create a special organ; that organ contributes to a constitution; a method
develops its control; a learned technique applies it. Removing the organ changes
execution without automatically erasing knowledge or ancestry.

Bloodline need not mean noble pedigree, genetic purity or one scalar strength.
A newly founded lineage can transmit a new faculty. An ancestral pact might
follow designated succession rather than reproduction if the world supports it.
A constitution need not be congenital, hereditary or biologically localized.
These distinctions supplement G1; the same label can name different mechanisms
in different worlds.

### G12 Cultivation: physique, practice and transformation

| Proposed family | Micro function | Wider consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Reservoir constitution | Store, refine or separate supported energies | Distinct preparation, refining and supply practices |
| Channel constitution | Different routes, circulation or simultaneous operations | A school develops methods for its actual users |
| Body-tempering physique | Grow useful structures through a deliberate regimen | Training grounds, materials, teachers and artisans become meaningful |
| Perceptive physique | Sense a local energy structure or relevant pattern | Investigation, navigation, medicine and teaching gain new approaches |
| Regenerative constitution | Restore or deliberately rebuild a supported template | Recovery and bodily experimentation follow that mechanism |
| Beast / elemental lineage | Specific anatomy, faculty or transformation | Family practices, habitats and relations follow actual functions |
| Soul-associated inheritance | Pass a mark, faculty or connection by a declared route | Body replacement and descendants have distinct retention questions |

Develop discovery, awakening, control, refinement, transformation and transmission
as separate transitions where appropriate. Not every path needs all six or a
common tier ladder. A realm breakthrough can change a constitution; acquiring
a constitution need not award a realm. Dormant power can be present before its
owner knows its name.

Practice can include breathing, movement, meditation, research, craft, service,
combat or environmental exposure when the selected method supports them. Give
the practice an actual consumer: better regulation, a new route, a usable sense
or changed execution. Elixirs and treasures can be shortcuts, necessities or
optional alternatives according to that setting, not a universal recipe.

Strong inherited advantages can be strong. Do not force every unrelated build
to match them at the same task or give every exceptional physique a curse.
Other paths can have different ambitions, access or strengths; any alternate
entry promised by a particular world needs an actual route.

### G13 Wizardry: embodied magic and learned procedure

| Proposed tradition | Constitution / bloodline role | What still needs development |
|---|---|---|
| Scholarly spellcraft | Faculties may support perception, memory or channeling | Language, theory, precision and actual spell procedures |
| Inborn casting | A lineage or altered body may supply an immediate function | Control, new applications and any supported transformations |
| Ritual magic | A body or ancestry may be an interface, witness or key | Tools, participants, preparation and relevant circumstances |
| Pact magic | A relation may authorize or supply a faculty | Terms, communication and the actual carrier of access |
| Runic / alchemical practice | A constitution may help handle or sense materials | Recipes, instruments, workmanship and usable products |
| Familiar / shared-host magic | A partner may supply perception, power or execution | Relationship, coordination and separate ownership of knowledge |

For example, an original mirror-associated constitution could enable a particular
reflection channel; it would not automatically teach every optical spell. An
inherited faculty for hearing enchanted structures could assist instrument-making
and investigation as well as combat. A learned spell could work without either
faculty if its own mechanism permits that.

Ask where a spell resides: memory, learned movement, inscribed object, organ,
external patron or another declared carrier. Ask what bloodline actually
transmits: capacity, a function, affinity, access or perhaps a stored procedure.
Do not presume that an inherited faculty is only a small bonus, or that scholarly
magic always requires hereditary eligibility. Local rules decide.

### G14 Other traditions and combinations

| Tradition | Original consideration |
|---|---|
| Martial arts | Frame, tendons, balance and senses interact with learned methods and acquired body change |
| Psionics | Perception, attention or network connection can reside in body, mind or another substrate |
| Divine practice | A blessing, incarnation and hereditary pact have different holders and succession rules |
| Necromancy | Preserved ancestry, animated tissue, soul identity and acquired undeath need distinct records |
| Artifice | Manufactured organs or cores can create a constitution without biological birth |
| Alchemy | Temporary expression, lasting reconstruction and transmissible change are separate products |
| Monstrous evolution | Anatomy, metamorphosis, lineage and individual learning can progress independently |

Cross-tradition combinations need a concrete meeting point. An inherited furnace
organ could fuel a learned refining procedure. Runic surgery could redirect a
channel. A pact could supply an energy that a constitution genuinely accepts.
Knowledge might transfer while bodily requirements do not. Equivalent names do
not prove compatibility; unfamiliar names do not prove incompatibility.

### G15 Everyday consequences, generations and acceptance

Keep micro and macro joined: a physique changes work and practice; teachers
adapt methods; a household preserves instructions; a school forms around them;
later descendants inherit some faculties but still need their own education.
Inherited powers can also disrupt an established school's assumptions or make
its conventional test give an incomplete reading.

Possible institutional responses include scholarships, tailored instruction,
commissions, research, alliances, indifference or hostility grounded in motives.
Ancestry, demonstrated ability, reputation and legal recognition are separate.
Adopted heirs might inherit a school or artifact but not a birth faculty; a
biological descendant might inherit the faculty without the school's allegiance.
Transmission follows the selected mechanism, not a universal purity hierarchy.

For each selected family, record acquisition, actual mechanism, development,
applications, what observers can establish, interactions, loss/retention and
transmission. Save real state separately from diagnoses and family claims.
UI presents what this observer can know. NPCs can develop these powers through
their own means and history, without automatic player-level scaling.

Existing [cultivation research](RESEARCH_CULTIVATION_PATHS.md#innate-capacity-roots-and-acquired-constitution)
and [source-development proposals](RNG_ENCOUNTERS_AND_SOURCE_DEVELOPMENT.md#bloodlines-and-enemy-power-builds-refinement-2026-10-02)
remain source/context owners. Earlier narrow proof constraints are local
prototype choices, not universal requirements for every physique or bloodline.
No new canon assertion, numerical balance, save schema or runtime support is
established by this addition. A selected implementation still requires its
actual native effects and the rebuild/browser/save acceptance gates in G5.

examples, not sealed discoveries or rules imposed on every setting.
