## 2. Identity across bodies and worlds: OA1/OA2

The common frame is a way to ask who continues, who acts and who remembers.
It need not declare that every world has six metaphysical substances.
Represent only distinctions that affect the chosen life; keep explicit
absence, disputed belief and unknown truth distinguishable.

| Term | Meaning in this frame, by pointer | Default + Dial, reasonably per world |
|---|---|---|
| Person | The continuing individual whose intentions, relationships and history matter. Identity L3/L4; canon NC1/NC10/NC13. | Default play that individual. Dial co-occupants, branches or a newly formed person. Personhood can differ from local legal recognition; a new body alone does not settle continuity. |
| Soul | A world-defined spiritual entity or carrier, where that world has one. Cosmology backing; transformation TF8/TF9. | Default use the world's actual claim. Dial soul continuity, soul fragments, no soul or an unresolved account. A database ID is not evidence of a soul. |
| Mind | Cognition, experience, beliefs and memory ownership. Identity L3/K; canon NC2/NC11, requirements N11 and minds MN1/MN12/MN18. | Default the selected person's declared mind. Dial native nonhuman cognition, human memories, shared or partitioned minds. Animation, common ancestry and copied memories do not alone establish one mind. |
| Body | Actual configuration, senses, functions, reserves, reach and bodily history. Engine E1/E2/E7/E9; transformation TF1-TF12. | Default one supported embodiment. Dial rooted, carried, virtual or several bodies where authored. Configuration change, replacement and control transfer remain separate events. |
| Controller | Who can initiate which actions through which channels. Identity O3-O7/L3; engine spec §4. | Default ordinary interaction binding, without an extra fictional spirit. Dial advisor, pilot, co-control or remote executor. Control is not title, friendship, shared knowledge or a grant of every body's functions. |
| Record | An account or retained artifact about a life, with author, holder, scope and provenance. Identity K; OT9/OT23/OT24; north-star persistence layers. | Default a local record or player-facing diary. Dial soul archive, institution, item memory or profile codex. An account can survive while its subject does not; a surviving account need not be true or character-accessible. |

**UNKNOWN:** a universal answer to “the same person” across every copied,
fused, reborn or restored being. Identity L4 R7 and canon NC10/NC13 leave
that answer source/premise-specific. A meaningful intentionally unresolved
question can stay unresolved. Executable return, learning and ownership
behavior still needs explicit terms, even when the philosophy remains open.

## 3. Cross-world and cross-life continuity

Three transitions ask different questions: changing a form within a life,
traveling to another world, and continuing after a life ends. E13,
TF1-TF12 and [multiverse structures](MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES.md#three-independent-decisions)
own those distinctions. World topology, delivered coverage and persistence
are independent; a contract ending need not erase its destination.

**STATED (Weibao, USER_VISION current direction):** heir-only carryover is
not a design limit. **STATED (accepted rule 5):** what crosses still needs
a carrier. Record the real cost or condition where applicable. The rulebook's
“Now” explanation permits free carry/conversion where a world, hub, cheat
or equalization mode supplies it; do not invent a surcharge merely to make
a generous source harder. The older blanket no-free-carry wording is not
a new universal tax selected by this lane.

| Continuity subject | Default proposal: what remains and where | Dial + Reasonably; existing owner |
|---|---|---|
| Legends and reputation | Witnesses and records preserve a deed in its actual world instance; later lives may encounter that account. | Oral tale, monument, institution, cross-world messenger or name-based power. A new world's audience learns through a channel, not an automatic fame flag. OT22/OT23, SL6-SL8, HL7. |
| Kin and households | Surviving relatives keep their own lives, holdings and memories. A predecessor's promise may leave actual unfinished business. | Descendant, adoptive heir, apprentice, forge sibling, surviving brood or guardian. Kinship does not grant control, guaranteed affection or past-life recognition. NR1-NR12, N42, BD14/BD16; kin NK-I1-NK-I8/NK-E. |
| Bodies and embodied places | A surviving blade, parked vessel, rooted grove or core remains at its actual location with actual condition/title/holder. | Carry the same body, repair it, return to it, inherit an object or acquire another form through a mechanism. Moving an anchor is not exporting its whole territory. E6-E8/E13, TF9-TF12, NX3/NX9, SL15. |
| Knowledge, skills and habits | Learning remains with its real owner/carrier (minds MN1/MN18). Bodily training stays body-qualified; retained theory is not automatic motor control. | Soul memory, taught method, written record, source-backed skill transfer or quiet habit. One sunlight preference can return through a selected source without copying a tree's roots. E9, C13, TX6, PG, SB4/SB11/SB16. |
| Bonds and traveling companions | Each bond names its participants; each traveler has its own body, means, crossing and continuation policy. | Same remembered partner, new relationship, inherited custody, distant household or surviving companion witness. New holder/heir and same soul can elicit different recognition. BD16, PL3, companions I1-I5/BE/BF/CJ. Traveling companions stay with that separate owner. |
| Goods, debts and standing | Objects, reserves and claims remain with actual owners/holders/issuers in actual instances unless transferred. | Estate, body lease, mission settlement, transferable title or local conversion. Refusal creates no invented debt; returning a shell does not erase earned wages or local claims. SL12/SL16, DM6/DM8, HL10/HL13. |
| Body codex | OT9's opt-in player record recalls lives actually played and what those lives could observe. | Character journal, in-world archive, profile collection or soul-carried record. Declare each audience; a profile entry does not prove character knowledge. No compulsory collection gate for creative-open starts. SB15, CV9, Identity K1-K7. |

These are carrier dispositions, not a new persistent store. Reuse the
north star's [four persistence layers](../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#four-layers-of-persistence)
and [carryover CL/layer owner](world-depth-2026-10-13/17_CARRYOVER_AND_RETROFIT.md).
A local legend is world state; a remembered life may be soul state;
an opt-in unlock may be profile state; the human's learning needs no save
field. Their lifetimes need not match. Reports from those owners are dated
evidence, not a fresh runtime audit here.

### Three continuity walks, all original proposals

| Walk | Actual continuity and meaningful later choice |
|---|---|
| Hound → later independent resident, O1 + O8 | The hound's rescue leaves a collar, a witness and an account in the same instance, if that persistence is supported. The resident can visit the memorial or speak with the witness. The resident does not inherit scent mastery or recognize the rescued child without its own knowledge. OT23/OT24, SB8/SB12; no second realm or prestige layer needed. |
| Rooted person → temporary shell → rooted person, O1 + O6 | The root body stays local; a supported shell performs an errand. Shell practice, cargo and root-body growth settle to their actual owners. Return neither rejuvenates the roots nor duplicates the delivered parcel. TF10, E7-E9, CK clock ownership. A traveling gardener remains an independent companion under the companion owner. |
| Contractor → authored mission vessel → return or local continuation | A PL1/N18 proposal declares the anchor's care, active vessel, local household and one return receipt. Retained calibration knowledge differs from local material growth. Choosing to stay can continue the local life where authored; expiry/failure uses the chosen contract. DM8/DM12. No universal canon guarantee or implementation is claimed. |

Same-instance return finds its own changed history under the selected absence
policy. A branch counterpart has a different history. A new shell, copied
record and restored person are different authoring claims, following
[continuity: retained history and defeat](LORE_AND_CONTINUITY.md#transfer-and-retained-history),
NC10/NC13 and N39. Body death, remembered grief, revival, replacement and
prestige should each resolve under their own selected rules.

## 4. Optional long arcs and fit to the life sim

OA3/OA4 name possible themes, not objectives assigned to every player.
Every arc below can stay within one world, stop at an ordinary good life,
or expand later through supported routes. None requires becoming human,
founding an empire, collecting all species or acquiring more prestige layers.

| Arc | Lived thread, local first expression | Default + Dial + Reasonably |
|---|---|---|
| AR1 Understanding many kinds of life | Learn one being's signals, practice one distinctive sense, keep a qualified diary. | Default curiosity within the current life. Dial live other forms, interview neighbors or use perception preview. Understanding can change cooperation without awarding automatic moral superiority or mastery. N11, SB1/SB6, OT9, CV10. |
| AR2 Founding or tending a mixed society | Share one workshop, service, home or rule that lets different bodies participate. | Default ordinary reciprocal work. Dial citizen, caretaker, founder, ruler or mobile diaspora. Growth comes from actual people, access and provision; society is not a hive mind. NM8, NC14, MS13, OA4. |
| AR3 A soul's journey through forms | Under a selected O2 premise, retain one supported memory or habit and decide what it means now. | Default no universal soul journey. Dial deliberate rebirth, recovered fragments or a return to a chosen form. A quiet animal life can be the destination, not an inferior stage. OA3, N15, TF8, SB16. |
| AR4 Keeping a home through generations | A grove, blade, house or guardian remembers successive caretakers and preserves a useful legacy. | Default one shared place and named relationship. Dial institution, family archive or recurring new holder. New generations choose their own ties; neither centuries of waiting nor compulsory grief is needed. O7, NR7, BD14, CK. |
| AR5 Crossing boundaries of belonging | Be a neighbor, worker or witness across bodies, roles and institutions. | Default one earned connection. Dial emissary, refugee, reformer, hunter who changes sides or hub resident. Status changes need actual causes; changing allegiance need not change anatomy. MS1/MS15, SL, BS4/BS5, HL8. |

**STATED (north-star opening):** the proposed quality test is whether a
feature makes one life feel lived. **INFERRED application:** a scent search
that earns useful pay and changes a neighbor's behavior meets that aim more
directly than a species label with only removed buttons. Use M1/M2,
C2/C6/C12, NP11, PB3/PB5 and SB3/SB10. Making MK-C1/MK-C4/MK-C7 routes production and shared homes through actual means. A small habit, practiced craft,
remembered name, home and dependable routine can carry a full nonhuman life.
Combat, evolution, romance and grand arcs remain premise/world dials.

**STATED (current priority):** Harry Potter, D&D and source-covered book
worlds lead canon work; the tier includes LOTM/CoI, Shadow Slave and
Paradise, plus anime/manga worlds led by JJK, Bleach and Fate. Source
coverage orders work within that direction. Fallout, Skyrim and Elden
Ring remain kept but inactive. This frame neither reopens their queues
nor makes a new original multiverse the prerequisite for canon play.

The Village hound/wolf recommendation in C1/C12 and engine Slice 2 is a
bounded foundation proof candidate, not a vote to replace the canon-first
order. N31's one well-made nonhuman seat per active world is also a proposal,
not a requirement to finish a catalog before advancing an existing slice.
World and start admission can be adopted when that world becomes active;
engine spec B8 preserves deliberate exclusions rather than deleting all
form checks indiscriminately.

For the **first milestone**, one supported nonhuman life can deepen the
selected realm using its existing jobs, home, NPCs and the one selected
prestige contract. AR1/AR2/AR4 can produce local outcomes. O8 can explain
choosing another life without shipping travel; a local codex can remember
it without shipping profile power. OA5's scope guard does not demand a
working multiverse, mission-body service, full ecology, ensemble engine,
offline resolver or all O models before that slice is playable.

Presentation planning stays with SU/SS: proposed desktop checks at
**1366×768**, with **1920×1080** where the larger layout is used. The
visible outcome should be current body/seat, useful routine actions,
known partner/support state and qualified history readable together.
Mobile optimization remains deferred. This document makes no UI-fit claim;
future selected behavior needs rebuilt fresh-origin browser evidence and
save/reload/export/literal import where persistence changes.
