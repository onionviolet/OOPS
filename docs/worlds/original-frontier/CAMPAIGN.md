The campaign lets the traveler become useful, explore, protect a chosen activity and develop extraordinary competence without abandoning the familiar village. Its central question is practical: what can you and the people around you actually accomplish here? Four careers and the five existing service arcs remain. Three connected strands, **OF.EXPLORE**, **OF.CONTROL** and **OF.MASTERY**, make field discovery, combat decisions and applied technique meaningful alternatives to managing trade. A good village life remains a worthwhile outcome alongside adventure.

## Preservation and entry

The original opening, rat work, native equipment/skills, jobs, dialogue and geography remain primary. An unjoined character sees no campaign panel, actor, debt, timer, stock reward, world identity discovery or institution enrollment. Short Measure is offered only through an actual Village meeting after the relevant existing access is earned; Town square must already be available. It does not grant a permit on an old save.

Existing accomplishments are inputs when genuinely relevant. A returned town ledger can explain better signs; a built rack can prepare real stock; field membership can introduce a specialist. None substitutes for physically present goods, another person's consent or a delivery receipt. Missing campaign fields mean undiscovered/unjoined, with no manufactured childhood, career rank or past shortage.

The expanded campaign stays in `base.frontier`. Native inventory/XP/reputation retain their owners. The existing optional Field apprenticeship renewal is the sole current prestige. Mature local careers do not require that reset, a new realm, an age ending or a universal career level bar. The character can return to inherited quests at any point.

## Local lives at three stages

Stages describe possible lives, not mandatory age or level gates. One person can pursue several careers; a newcomer with strong inherited/native skills may solve difficult physical work early while still lacking local contacts or agreements.

| Life | Daily and longer choices | Persistent change | What does not become mandatory |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early: an unfamiliar worker | Preserve native opening; observe one real field pattern; choose a learning source or ordinary preparation; choose Short Measure's audit or supplied shortfall; make the actual Village–Town journey when ready | First witnessed contribution, a known field question, ordinary Load tally or field understanding, distinct later account and a finite local wage opportunity | Campaign membership, new power, room visit, perfect attendance or paying to clear a fabricated debt |
| Established: someone people call back | Choose a finite commission, field comparison or protection plan; use a Shack recipe; ask a known producer for terms; practice a specific technique; test observed conditions | A stable specialty, improved work process, one usable field dossier, a consented protection outcome and paid resident partnerships | Doing every job, universal relationship grinding or upgrading every site |
| Mature: an organizer or exceptional specialist | Resolve contested local work, demonstrate a mastered compatible technique or an organized ordinary alternative, train a willing successor and make a useful local return | A completed charter or deliberate closure, a proven local route/protection/technique result, remembered tradeoffs and a useful hand-off that survives departure | A northern/river outing, a second prestige, compulsory household/aging, automatic world control or endlessly escalating daily shortages |

At every stage, a quiet loop exists: choose a known job, prepare at home, do the work, receive an actual wage/output, rest or train, meet a changed resident. More ambitious play adds travel, contested contracts and difficult investigation. No phase requires keeping the browser open for a hidden deadline before the player has joined that phase.

## Four mechanically different careers

These are routes through actions, knowledge, skill milestones and usable equipment. Titles are optional observed recognition after concrete feats, not a second XP currency. Each route has paid or slow ordinary work, a useful idle policy where supported, an active decision and a mature problem that pure repetition cannot answer.

### Dispatch and account work

The dispatcher separates promised quantities, available stock, reserved stock and accepted delivery. In Short Measure, Load tally becomes an actual learned procedure: the player can perform finite-stock sorting in Village and acceptance work in Town square. It grants no global preparation multiplier or blanket access to private purses.

Early work counts a named lot and resolves a discrepancy before anyone promises more than exists. Established work batches real received lots, agrees partial deliveries, chooses priority and pays one actual courier from an actual purse. Mature work negotiates who owns the buffer and who pays for a failed specification, with audits available to people who were not present for dispatch. Its reward is reliable work capacity and portable process knowledge, not secretly doubled goods.

A fast physical courier may beat a slow one but cannot carry nonexistent supplies. Literacy supports checking records; Haggling supports agreed terms; Perception supports inspected condition. Native XP is awarded only by supported content with declared amounts, not by merely viewing a ledger. A proposed multi-leg controller needs actual location/occupancy/travel bindings. One accepted lot holds the dispatcher or receiving site so simultaneous orders cannot spend the same log or wage budget.

### Material craft and prepared stock

The maker chooses between using a real kit/rope now, retaining ingredients for another job or accepting a supplied customer specification. Existing Village gathering, measured packing, Tamsin's braced kits and Shack rack/rope are valid distinct methods when actually learned. They are not reskinned as a new universal recipe.

Early work uses Rough wood logs and the current known recipes. Established work compares ordinary Wood logs, Ash wood logs and sound fittings only after the relevant native gathering/craft access. Mature work produces a standard load frame or moisture-resistant container for a particular route, proving durability with an actual trial and delivering the same owned object. New frame/container items and material tests need new definitions and their shared adapters; they are campaign proposals, not outputs of existing recipes.

Mechanically, this route manages recipes, material choice, facility capacity and item ownership. Useful improvements reduce repeated preparation or enlarge a declared supported batch. A rare material is not automatically better for every task. A craftsman's attention remains separate from the player's home bench, and another maker needs their own tools/materials/pay rather than copied oldCraftsman focus. Cancelled jobs follow their disclosed refund policy; finished customer work is transferred once.

### Field inquiry and route knowledge

The inquirer finds out which claim holds in which place. Existing reading/teaching/deduction, surveyed anchors, town ledger and native Perception/Climbing/Running support the early route. A report is attributed and timestamped; a field observation is a separate record.

Established work compares two genuinely different contexts or tests a sample under two conditions. A supplied report may unlock a question, while firsthand evidence permits a more specific action. Mature required work returns from the Forest-road corridor or nearby cave with a tested local schedule, approach note or handling condition that helps a chosen activity. The optional mountain/river branch can later widen that result; it cannot block the first mature dossier. One room visit does not reveal an atlas.

Mechanically, this route changes information and lawful action options. It spends travel, focus/stamina or sampling goods instead of producing kits by default. Strong inquiry can reject a profitable unsafe order, find an ordinary workaround or reveal a claim's uncertainty. Rat-step helps a compatible personal footing task, not arbitrary cargo mass; remembered Load-bearing Step supports only its registered execution. Broader source/faculty discoveries are separate optional later packets.

### Stewardship, escort and teaching

The steward keeps a small service usable and helps other people become independently capable. Early play uses known ordinary training, consented work and native patrol when unlocked. Lio's agreed guard contest and Mara's rivalry remain their own encounters, not the first escort contract.

Established play makes one actual paid protection or work agreement: who is present, who holds the supplies, when the service ends and what withdrawal means. A noncombat steward may allocate shelter/stock, teach an inspected packing step or arrange alternate transport. Mature play trains a willing successor, negotiates a joint charter or manages an evacuation/supply priority. A successor's competence is their own learned knowledge/body/tool access, not a player-stat clone.

Mechanically, this route reserves participants and uses real stamina, body, owned tools and commitments. A combat expert can protect a specific convoy; native fighting ability alone cannot grant consent, decide a legal claim or restore destroyed stock. General native convoy/escort combat is an integration gap. Early optional service remains possible without combat, and ordinary fainting/recovery does not become death because a charter uses serious language.

## A voluntary shared institution

Working name: **The Open Counter**, a small supply cooperative within the existing field association's social reach. It begins as Mira and Wen agreeing on a plainly counted handover, not a new faction enrollment or global reputation meter. The player can work with it without becoming its leader; the association, town office, millers and native guild retain separate identities.

The first shared project is an accepted stock agreement across Village and Town. Its concrete phases are: name the actual owners and commitments; fund one buffer without taking from unrelated inventories; complete one real delivery/acceptance; hear what the receiver did next; then choose a public or private service arrangement. A learned process can survive loss of a physical copy, but the document/tool/materials themselves have inventory owners.

Proposed established capacity: one Village sorting bench, one Town receiving slot and one open outbound lot. Each is a registered capacity-one site; upgrading capacity must acquire actual tools/work/time and choose a funded resident plan. No institution resource account is added merely to mirror its members. If a future jointly owned depot is selected, its single explicit inventory owner and withdrawal permissions must be implemented first.

Useful project outcomes differ. A public counter makes a small known queue and openly specified partial delivery available. A member service offers larger agreed lots but holds more of participants' stock. A private workshop arrangement prioritizes the player/customer and leaves neighbors to use other services. All can sustain a livelihood; the public route is not automatically the best payout.

Monthly dues, mandatory upkeep, automatic shortages and a second native chest are outside this pack. Voluntary work may consume an agreed buffer; declining the next lot leaves native shops and quests intact. Regional reputation is changed only by an explicitly witnessed public act through the current native owner. Private patience, payment and disappointment use directed memories instead.

## Five connected arcs

### 1. Short Measure: promise, count, delivery

First additive episode, with its authoring selection frozen after the live source review. At Village, Mira presents a three-log Town consignment and one-log local reserve against three owned logs. The player can audit the lot and agree a two-log shipment while keeping the reserve, or supply one owned log to preserve the three-log promise and the reserve. Both are competent outcomes. Donation is a real transfer and its opportunity cost stays visible.

Mira's own finite effort funds dispatch; the actual load moves into Wen's inventory. The traveler reaches already-unlocked Town square using native travel, meets Wen and hears a materially different later reply. Teaching ordinary Load tally enables finite-stock paid sorting in Village and acceptance work in Town; hearing its description alone does not make it executable. No generic preparation bonus, map repair, surprise native reward or duplicated town contract is added.

Refusal creates no debt and makes no promise. Mira chooses from her own means and can make a smaller lawful shipment or seek other funded help according to the module's declared branch. Delay preserves a specific current offer, pending paid job or expired opportunity rather than turning silence into consent. A later report can differ without awarding a missed wage. This episode's exact branches/means/checks are defined in the implementation packet; this campaign does not invent extra shipped outcomes.

### 2. The return lot: making work repeatable

Proposal. Wen has an actual next need, and Mira has the previously retained local reserve. The player chooses a finite paid return lot, assists a producer with an agreed material specification or leaves it to another funded worker. A maker may use the existing rack/rope method; a clerk may agree a smaller acceptable lot; an inquirer may show that the original destination specification was unnecessary. All must deliver the same actual goods they claim to own.

The arc introduces one shared service agreement, not another one-time six-coin supply commission. It offers repeated work only while the named customer has real demand and wages. Finished jobs change that customer's inventory and next priority. A smaller lot can leave time/materials for another pursuit; a large one can improve a declared project faster. Declining closes that customer's offer or lets a legal NPC order take its place, while native work remains available.

Functional unlock: queue one accepted lot under a chosen stop rule, with explicit budget, expected maximum output and cancellation. The existing routine/job engine is a candidate; UI/owner binding and exact participant custody must be specified by the integrator. An indefinite automated money faucet is not the intended loop.

### 3. Whose sound stock: skill, evidence and standards

Proposal. A received lot is unsuitable for one use but acceptable for another. Ordinary counting worked; material judgment now matters. Iven asks permission to examine a specific sample. The player can pay for inspection, test it with learned craft knowledge, consult a willing actual producer or downgrade the lot's declared use without inventing a diagnosis.

A route specialist checks exposure and handling at the actual endpoint. A maker changes preparation, using real materials and a real test. A steward negotiates partial acceptance so the producer is paid for the usable part and the receiver owns the agreed risk. A very powerful traveler can provide hard-to-obtain replacement material, but cannot change the original lot's history or force someone to accept it.

Functional unlocks: a known specification, one actual compatible recipe/container proposal and a named sample record tied to its source owner. These are new content/adapters, not a claim that native items currently track spoilage or per-piece provenance. Refusal leaves an unaccepted lot with its owner; taking another job does not contaminate the player's whole bag. The examiner may independently spend his one allowance, produce a narrower result and offer it later.

### 4. The closed counter: a consequential supply threat

Proposal. Halden offers to buy a producer's next actual lot in exchange for exclusive service. Several lawful small agreements could leave the Open Counter without material or receiving capacity, even though each person has an understandable reason to take the money. The threat is loss of a voluntary affordable service and a remembered broken public commitment if one was actually signed, not an apocalyptic rumor or forced destruction of native shops.

Evidence unfolds through offered terms, witnessed sales and dated reports. Supply pressure is real only after the agreed stock/slots have changed owners. Halden has a finite purse, limited appointments and no automatic success against all producers. Mira/Wen can decline, negotiate or continue a smaller service using stock still lawfully theirs. The player chooses a role instead of becoming mayor on arrival.

| Trained exceptional competence | A high native carrying/handling specialist moves a difficult real lot; compatible mastered field control supports a declared loaded task; an earned movement method traverses a compatible personal route | Can make an otherwise costly service economical or beat a supported closing window. Method/body/means/fuel and cargo applicability still apply; neither Rat-step nor Load tally manufactures material. |
| Greater power, later optional packet | A source with an explicitly authored transport/material function changes a selected operation | Can exceed ordinary output and remove a genuine local bottleneck. Acquiring it, teaching it, fuel/authority and sustained execution need their own delivered packet. No unlearned magic school, forced scaling enemy or universal anti-power ward is invented. |
| Refusal or withdrawal | Accept no charter; finish only already signed obligations, or use its disclosed termination terms | Public cooperative work may close or continue at smaller scale under residents' means. Private/native livelihoods remain usable, and a later restart is a new agreement rather than missed backpay. |

There is no arbitrary duel with Halden: striking a buyer does not cancel another person's contract. A later genuinely coercive or monster attack can have its own authored nonlethal/ordinary-danger encounter, but is not smuggled into this trade arc. Player defeat keeps the selected native recovery semantics. Full violence/death policy requires separate creative and engine decisions.

### 5. A route beyond home: reach and succession

Proposal. After choosing a service arrangement or its deliberate closure, the traveler undertakes a useful Village–Forest road–Town return or a nearby-cave outing, using actual earned access. Native travel makes a central question visible: who keeps an agreed local activity useful while the traveler explores? A funded successor can carry out a limited local plan, while Mira/Wen hold only the stock and permission they were actually granted. Mountain/downstream travel is an optional expansion of this outcome.

A craft specialist returns with an actual local sample and tests its use. An inquirer returns with a narrower approach observation and must distinguish that from old reports. A steward trains someone willing to manage one job; a dispatcher negotiates a return lot that makes the journey worthwhile. A powerful character may accomplish this quickly, but knowledge of the place and consent of the locals still arise through actual encounters. Choosing to close a service returns its unused owned stock and cancels only its future commitments; a useful personal dossier or taught operation can remain.

Functional outcomes: a useful return service, a resident's own learned operation, a publicly remembered charter choice and one durable home improvement or known procedure. This is a full local career outcome without a second reset. Leaving permanently, maintaining a small home counter and building a broader regional service are all valid endings to this pack. Future arcs can use the same people and decisions; no historical rewind, automatic offspring or universal realm ladder is required.
