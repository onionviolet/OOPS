## What complete means

Deliver the current Village/forest/Town campaign with the familiar opening, seven purposeful existing places, four interacting career paths, all five existing proposed arcs, functional exploration/control/mastery, early/established/mature outcomes and ordinary life after a chosen ending. Reuse native items, activities, quests, skills, travel, home, known people and one optional field renewal. Local signatures are evidence that changes legal options, practical control with actual means and preparation that changes a particular result. [WORLD_BRIEF](WORLD_BRIEF.md), [CAMPAIGN](CAMPAIGN.md), [RULES_AND_STARTS](RULES_AND_STARTS.md) and [ABILITIES](ABILITIES.md) own the detail.

World delivery and an individual player's ending differ. Delivery implements the required paths, alternate branches and aftermath. A player follows one coherent path, may decline other offers and reaches a mature result without clearing every native zone, doing every job or taking prestige. Delivery cannot call an unimplemented alternative a terminal refusal to remove it from the checklist. An unsupported adapter stays remaining work.

The present-era campaign ends at a specialist/charter/handoff/departure result. Distant native regions, a broader magic school, imported cosmology, full ecology, generated households, lethal NPC combat, general convoy combat, alternate-life starts and historical rewind are optional. Their absence does not block these IDs. Future additions receive a reviewed requirement revision rather than retroactively making this pack unfinishable.
