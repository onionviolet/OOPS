## Identity, scope and player experience

Become a capable local adventurer, maker, investigator or organizer whose discoveries and methods change real situations, then choose a worthwhile local life or hand work over and leave. Preserve the familiar opening, native progress and the existing `base.frontier` branch. Author metadata is not a player discovery of a parallel world.

Represent the current Village/forest/Town corridor: Village, earned Shack, Infested field, accessible Nearby cave margin, Forest road, Town outskirts and earned Town square. These places have different purposes, not seven copies of a work counter. Eastern mill/storehouse, Town farms, mountain and downstream places remain genuine native content and elective extensions. Their locks, quests and travel are preserved. The finite Frontier pack does not require every player to clear all native zones or unlock the distant river campaign.

Local era is the current saved adventure. There is no fabricated predecessor career, new childhood, second native campaign slot or world reset. Skills can be very strong; access, evidence, ownership and consent still have separate causes. No universal rank, rarity ceiling or level-scaled opposition is added.

## Signature decisions and paths

Three connected signatures make Frontier worth staying in. First, evidence opens specific actions: a reported lead differs from a witnessed condition, and an old observation cannot locate somebody forever. Second, practical control has actual body/tool/stamina/fuel requirements and changes a guard, crossing or loaded response. Third, preparation changes a particular material or route outcome instead of awarding an interchangeable preparation score. Their exact required functions are OF.EXPLORE, OF.CONTROL and OF.MASTERY in the completion owner.

Keep four careers: dispatch/account work, material craft, field inquiry, stewardship/escort/teaching. Inquiry includes exploration and dangerous discoveries. Stewardship includes consented protection and control, with supported noncombat organization alternatives. Every career reaches a mature result without requiring every other career or the optional field renewal. Initial inherited rat combat remains part of vanilla; a later noncombat career is not a promise to remove the original opening.

Keep five arcs: Short Measure; The return lot; Whose sound stock; The closed counter; A route beyond home. The new adventure/control/mastery strands intersect these arcs through discovered preparation, useful samples, protected contact, lawful solutions and a local hand-off. There is one finite final charter/specialist/departure decision. Repeatable life survives its closure.

## Continuity, pacing and outcomes

Use native player travel and current live quotes. Base corridor travel is 240 + 240 + 40 game minutes one way before native modifiers. No short inspection creates a transport shortcut. Group Town objectives into a useful journey; early practice remains possible near home. [RULES_AND_STARTS](RULES_AND_STARTS.md) owns first-pass learning, demand, clocks, automation and branch closure.

The early outcome is one learned useful function and an observed consequence. Established play connects evidence, craft or control with an actual resident/project result. Mature play proves a specialist feat, settles the chosen local threat/charter or declines it lawfully, then stays, hands off one funded operation or leaves. A quiet native gathering/training/job/rest life remains viable. Whole-world delivery must implement the required alternative outcomes; one player need only resolve their chosen path.

Closed-browser advancement remains unsupported. No new loss is charged during unsupported absence. Existing common played-time progression and paid commitments keep their owners; future physical absence needs an explicit reviewed policy. The existing Field apprenticeship renewal remains the only prestige, optional to this campaign's conclusions.
