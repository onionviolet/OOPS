# Lore, continuity and consequences

Status: Develop/Research proposal, 2026-10-03. Owner: lore/continuity GM, reporting to the master. This dossier owns cross-world source boundaries, parallel histories, identity, causality and evidence rules. It adds no runtime, travel, death policy, save migration or gameplay verification.

Read [PROJECT](../../PROJECT.md), [GM role](../gm/LORE_CONTINUITY_GM_PROMPT.md), [campaign framework](CAMPAIGN_FRAMEWORK.md) and [progression/assembly](PROGRESSION_AND_ASSEMBLY.md) first. Local campaigns own people/quests; module catalogues own components/tiers; source ledgers own exact inspected lore. This file supplies constraints and worked consequence patterns without becoming another local content catalogue.

## Authoring recommendation

Use a finished, explicitly bounded work when a stable history matters. It makes the cast, available revelations and known endpoint easier to check. A closed arc in an ongoing universe can serve the same purpose if its dependencies are bounded too. Neither choice makes every setting detail verified or supplies a complete playable campaign.

The useful promise is: **this branch begins from this inspected source snapshot and then follows its own causes**. A source ending establishes what happened in that source history. It does not compel a visitor-altered history to reach that ending. Later publications require a separately reviewed snapshot instead of silently changing an existing instance.

Authoring priority proposal: deepen the five assigned campaigns first; use Naruto and Bleach to test trainable versus body/soul-bound acquisition; keep Invincible as the first comic comparison; reserve Secret Wars for deliberately complex parallel identity; use Watchmen only if bounded investigation is the desired fantasy. This does not select a sixth world or change the first one-realm/one-prestige build milestone.

## Evidence vocabulary

| Mark | Meaning | Permitted claim |
|---|---|---|
| `C` | Inspected primary source or official source-specific explanation | Only the facts and range actually inspected, with locator |
| `A` | Official adaptation corroboration | Evidence about that adaptation; manga compatibility must be checked separately |
| `I` | Inference from bounded evidence | State the reasoning and uncertainty; do not call it a quoted canon rule |
| `O` | Original branch addition or gameplay adaptation | Allowed creative proposal, with its own costs, limits and consequences |
| `U` | Unverified or conflicting claim | Findable question and locator; not a prerequisite advertised as settled canon |

`C` does not mean a whole franchise was audited. A publisher's final-volume page can establish completion without verifying every chapter. An official game manual establishes that game's represented rules, not every metaphysical possibility in its universe. An official merchandise/game announcement is weak corroboration of a named power and cannot establish a complete acquisition rule.

If canon is silent, author a bounded original experiment rather than invent a citation. If canon contradicts the experiment, label a deliberate divergence and identify the changed rule. Avoid disguising a copied franchise power as an ordinary recipe merely because the module template requests eight components.

Keep source paraphrases compact and citations near the claim. Ledgers carry the principal factual summaries; other documents should cite claim IDs instead of repeating pages of synopsis. No source dialogue, manga panels, comic art or game assets are imported by this run.

## Six distinct continuity promises

| Mode | Promise | Consequence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Canon-compatible side story | Original people and local events occupy a source-compatible gap | Preserve declared anchors; do not promise the entire unseen world is unchanged |
| Explicit divergence | Begin from checked anchors, then change one event or local rule | Record the first changed cause and re-evaluate every dependent future |
| Earlier independent snapshot | Author an earlier cast/body/institution/resource state | Starts a separate history; no rewind or repair of the active later instance |
| Parallel alternate world | A distinct history has already developed differently | Declare differences at entry; by default similarity does not transfer memories, debts or rank; a resonance law, cheat or authored link can |
| Post-ending continuation | Start after the selected source endpoint | Prior ending remains that instance's past; subsequent events are original unless separately sourced |
| Controlled overlap | Distinct instances can exchange actual people, objects or information | Requires a passage, custody, support and causality contract; no silent merge |

### Canon-compatible side story

Choose a few invariant anchors and their inspected range, such as a village's leadership during a stated chapter window or an institution's existence. Develop original livelihoods, local disputes and expeditions around them. Canonical characters can be absent, busy, reluctant or represented through a dated report. Their schedules need credible means, not convenient omnipresence.

Compatibility is a positive authoring promise, not a hidden rubber wall. If a capable player can materially alter a protected anchor, either declare the action outside this scenario's represented scope before commitment, or transition to explicit divergence when it succeeds. Do not accept a rescue and secretly kill the rescued person solely to preserve a book's future.

Use small invariant sets. Reproducing every off-screen canon event while allowing consequential intervention is an expensive and often contradictory promise. A local story can remain compatible without claiming a full simulation of an entire manga.

### Explicit divergence

Record `baseline revision`, `divergence cause`, `affected actors/assets`, `invalidated expectations`, `new dependencies` and `discovery channels` as proposed author records. The first change may be a rescued person, exposed fraud, acquired technology or denied resource. Distinguish a new historical event from changing a physical/metaphysical rule.

After the change, retain facts unaffected by it. Saving a courier need not abolish a distant war; preserving a leader can materially change orders, recruitment or diplomacy. Recompute dependent choices from actual goals and means. Avoid both universal butterfly-effect invention and a predetermined return to canon.

An enemy may adapt if it learns what happened and can pay for another method. It need not reproduce its canon maneuver with a substitute victim. Failed resources, exposed secrets, deceased agents and lost authority can terminate its plan permanently.

### Earlier independent snapshot

An earlier era is an independently authored start. Name a source-attested chapter/era anchor where available; mark invented dates and local prehistory `O`. Do not copy later ranks, shops, implants, relationships or buildings into it. Check every canonical participant's age/body, affiliation, acquired powers and location.

The snapshot can develop an authored descendant history, but this is a new instance lineage. It cannot refund a destroyed ring, revive a dead teacher or change a workshop already lost in another instance. No live rollback is the default. A regression or loop cheat or world can grant it at a cost; that runtime project is separate and unsupported today.

Two snapshots of one named character represent historical counterparts unless a selected travel rule moves one actual person between them. Counterparts need distinct instance-qualified identity. Exact ages may remain unknown with an explicit age band; never present an invented integer as verified biography.

### Parallel alternate world

Declare both inherited anchors and meaningful differences: leadership, survival, institutional law, resource distribution or power access. A parallel world can have another Kakashi or Rukia; neither shares the first instance's memories by default. A visitor can recognize a face while being a stranger to the counterpart.

Similarity does not imply destiny. A younger counterpart might reject a remembered mentor, take a different job or never obtain the expected power. Conditions that generated a later rival in the baseline can be averted, while a new grievance may create a rival that never existed there.

### Post-ending continuation

Choose precisely where the source ends, including any included epilogue. Starting beyond that point is an original continuation proposal. Establish surviving actors, unresolved obligations, political arrangements, age/body state and power custody from the actual selected endpoint before writing the next crisis.

Do not import a sequel merely because it is official, or silently remove the source epilogue to obtain a convenient cast. A different endpoint or included sequel is a new baseline. Unknown distant history can stay unknown; it does not authorize manufactured canon.

### Controlled overlap

An overlap needs named origin and destination instances, one actual traveler or item, departure/arrival causes, passage limits, memory/body/source retention, return conditions and time policy. Alternate counterparts may coexist only with explicit identities and a reason. Neither gets the other's lineage, bond, account balance or obligations through a shared display name alone; a resonance law or cheat can link them.

If an original person travels, their absence changes their home responsibilities. A counterpart remains at home only if independently instantiated. A summoned projection, copy, corpse, reincarnation and living original are distinct forms with explicit duration, autonomy, source and custody. Observation alone creates none of them.

## Identity and institution checks

For each canonical appearance, record source identity, actual instance ID, era anchor, age/body state, affiliation/rank, powers already acquired, current goal, means, knowledge and commitments. Add checked locators for every consequential claim; a familiar name is not sufficient evidence.

Use an author's identity comparison before combining eras. An adult mentor and their child-era counterpart cannot share a school timetable, organ or debt. A name inherited by a successor is not automatically the same person. An artificial soul/body requires its own source, creation and persistence decision.

Institutions have independent assets, membership, rules, authority and communication. Teaching access, work permits and formal rank differ from combat output. A captain-level fighter need not hold a captain's office; a capable outsider need not be a recognized village shinobi.

When a ruler dies, decide who can issue orders, what succession rules exist and who controls the material means. A surviving clerk may retain records while losing enforcement. A hidden faction cannot magically inherit everybody's knowledge. Leaving a faction can end service rights while retaining learned methods and owned gear.

Local authority belongs to the institution and instance that grants it. Recognition elsewhere needs evidence, an agreement or coercive means. A copied certificate, remembered membership or alternate counterpart cannot confer the original office.

## Knowledge, news and future memory

Keep three owners distinct: actual state, each observer's belief and author-only source expectations. An occurrence can complete before the player discovers it. A report can arrive late, omit details or lie. An NPC can be mistaken without changing what really happened.

A proposed evidence record needs subject and instance, claim, observer, channel, observation time, reported event time, provenance, confidence and contradictions. This is richer than the current runtime's seen/interpreted/understood record; it is not a new exported API or validated save field.

Foreknowledge is a recollection tied to a source/previous instance and believed date. It supplies a lead, such as where to investigate or whom to warn. It does not grant a current map, verified enemy identity, medical diagnosis, faction license or knowledge of another person's private intent.

Useful states: remembered expectation, current observation, corroborated interpretation, disproved prediction and unresolved conflict. Keep the obsolete prediction with its cause rather than deleting the memory when contradicted. Confidence cannot rise merely because the player waited.

Example: a visitor recalls a future attack on a supply depot. Inspection finds different guards and no original cargo. A warning may fund an evacuation; it need not prove an attack exists. The enemy can cancel its plan because the cargo moved. Later news can confirm the cancellation, while an uninformed village still believes an assault is imminent.

Known endings stay in author notes until evidence reveals them. A player menu cannot show a future betrayal, soul identity or hidden hereditary source simply because the author consulted a final-volume synopsis. Tooltips use the actual observer's available evidence.

## Power and acquisition continuity

The [assembly contract](PROGRESSION_AND_ASSEMBLY.md) separates training, structure, quality, condition, source/body, institution and research. By default canon stages do not turn those axes into a global numbered tier; verse equalization is the dial that does. Local rank and item rarity are poor substitutes for actual function, fuel, reach and support.

| Acquisition | What can be retained | What cannot be inferred |
|---|---|---|
| Trained technique | Learned pattern, practice history and tested applications | Immediate safe execution in an unsupported body or energy system |
| Observed/copied method | Whatever the actual copying faculty can perceive and encode | The target's organ, lineage, stock, soul bond, authority or every hidden step |
| Bodily source | The actual acquired organ/implant/lineage in its actual body | A free duplicate in a new body or counterpart |
| Soul-bound development | The actual soul's supported acquisition and history | A modular release sold like an interchangeable battery |
| Crafted equipment | One owned object and current condition | Automatic operator mastery, spare materials or a second copy |
| Institution | Granted access, duties and recognized standing | Portable jurisdiction or omniscient collective memory |

Learning a Naruto hand-sign pattern can be useful while chakra control, required nature, actual body/source and technique-specific costs still govern execution. The ledgers separate training, eye acquisition, bloodline and exceptional cases. Copying a puppet motion does not craft its mechanisms or poison. General modern scientific ninja tools are outside the original-manga default boundary unless specifically sourced or declared `O`.

Bleach development must keep the actual spiritual actor, weapon relation, learned methods and institutional support explicit. The [Bleach ledger](bleach/SOURCE_LEDGER.md) supports Bankai and creator/research anchors but leaves precise Shikai/Asauchi formulas and exceptions for chapter inspection. Support equipment, repair work and laboratories can be modular without pretending a workshop manufactures another person's release.

An original cross-world test can investigate compatibility. Name the hypothesized mechanism, actual inputs, safe test conditions, observable result and failure. Success creates a tested branch capability with provenance; it does not rewrite the source series. Failure consumes declared costs while retaining useful evidence.

An effective import can overwhelm lower threats when its real capabilities suffice. Do not add an unsupported immunity to preserve difficulty. Equally, strength alone does not supply a seal's target, a compatible catalyst, a consenting witness or legal office. Distinguish physical inability from institutional refusal.

A variant may simply be superior once its actual acquisition/support exists. Earlier methods can retain a concrete use, become obsolete or be replaced; none needs a manufactured niche. Do not add a fuel, strain, fragility or ethical penalty solely to equalize two abilities. The master's current assembly/ability-family policy preserves this latest steering.

## Transfer and retained history

| Domain | Same-body visit proposal | Alternate-body / soul-entry proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Actual anatomy, wounds, implants and condition travel | New authored body; specify each retained/lost function before entry |
| Methods/memory | Retained learning; execution rechecks support | Retained only under declared policy; memory remains instance-qualified belief |
| Soul/personal bond | Do not duplicate or detach it by a menu action | Explicit soul identity, bonding and body interface required |
| Gear/cargo | One actual holder, object identity and condition | Remains with actual holder unless a real transfer mechanism moves it |
| Companions | Actual person, consent by default (coercion, possession or a cheat can bypass it at a cost), availability and passage | A counterpart or summoned form is a different authored identity |
| Obligations | Remain attached to actual issuer/history | Another instance does not inherit enforcement automatically |
| Sites/claims | Remain in their actual instance | Earlier/parallel claims have separate causes and custody |

Same-instance reentry preserves the changed people, projects, ownership and unique claims. A later new instance is not a reload trick to reclaim a unique organ or artifact. If repeated counterpart acquisition is deliberately allowed, author finite local costs and explicit campaign uniqueness/aggregation policy rather than hiding it behind travel.

Draft default freezes absent worlds and browser-closed work; offline catch-up or harsh loss are opt-in modes. A later selected policy can advance funded actor projects on a common clock, with declared limits and discoverable return reports. A story document cannot quietly activate that policy. Current native independent-world and absence-clock adapters remain missing.

No travel preview grants safe escape from every committed danger. An inaccessible gate, dead passage keeper or destroyed return device can prevent return if the selected failure policy supports it. Preserve the cause and give discovery-appropriate evidence; don't silently teleport lost cargo home.

## Consequences have causes

For a material event, define trigger, prerequisites, actual occurrence, assets/actors changed, who detects it, possible responses, immediate costs, downstream dependencies, irreversible losses and later evidence. A timer starts from the initiating occurrence or accepted commitment, not from every later menu visit.

Refusal means the player makes no offered commitment. Other actors may still act and the opportunity may disappear. Default on an accepted obligation is different. Leaving a region does not create a debt merely because an unaccepted mission existed there.

NPCs need goal, current knowledge, finite resources, legal/physical access, method, time and fallback. A rival can study, buy a tool, gather witnesses or seek a sponsor if those means exist. It cannot learn an undisclosed player build from author metadata or conjure an unlimited replacement army.

Track opportunity costs: saving a warehouse can leave insufficient labor to defend a clinic. Supplying one institution may strengthen its rivals' motive to act without guaranteeing a war. A paid service can succeed while the surrounding faction loses. Rewards reflect the accomplished obligation, not a universal happy ending.

### Created and averted rivals

A rival arc begins with a person and a grievance/ambition, not a universal hostility meter. An original apprentice loses a funded hospital place after the player reallocates supplies; they first petition, then train with a sponsor if rejected. Witnessed theft can motivate pursuit; an unobserved theft needs a later investigation before blame.

Averted arc: preserve the apprentice's access and credible livelihood, and the revenge motive may never arise. They can still compete for rank for unrelated reasons. Created arc: an imported method makes an established contractor obsolete; the contractor can adapt, bargain or retaliate using retained resources. Competition need not imply murder.

Progression happens through actual paid projects and acquired supports. Success may make a rival much stronger than the player. Failure, death, lost patronage or a resolved grievance can end that arc. Do not respawn the same rivalry under another name because the catalogue expects recurring content.

### Institutions and permanent losses

A destroyed archive can permanently remove its unreproduced contents, witnesses and legal evidence. Copies already made survive in their actual holders' inventories. Reconstructing part of the record is a new project with uncertainty, not a refund of the original loss.

Death is final by default. A dead teacher stops future instruction unless an actual sourced or original recovery method (a revival cheat, ritual or world law, at a cost) succeeds under its declared constraints. Already learned methods remain with students. Institutional succession may preserve routine work while unique expertise, private trust or a personal bond is gone.

An expelled faction member can retain skills and possessions while losing lawful facility access. A seized workshop changes ownership, supply, scheduling and who receives profits. A reinstatement can create new terms without erasing remembered coercion or earlier damage.

No consequence must be repairable. Some can be partly repaired, compensated or replaced while the old loss persists. Repeated collection, travel, reload or counterpart contact cannot settle one claim twice. Recognition and grief remain specific to the people who know the history.

## Defeat, death and continuity

Native defeat and the existing Mara duel retain their current recovery/nonlethal policies. Severe unfairness authorizes design research, not a silent change to old saves. Local defeat, body death, soul persistence, local objective loss, blocked return, run ending and optional prestige are separate questions.

| Event | Required author decision | Forbidden shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Incapacitation | Who can capture/treat/loot, with what means and cost | Free recovery or universal death inferred from zero HP |
| Body death | Actual body/cargo loss and any sourced recovery | A fresh copy of organs/equipment on reentry |
| Spiritual death/change | Actual soul identity and local metaphysics | Every Bleach visit becoming an extra life |
| Resurrection/reanimation | Specific method, caster, target, materials, costs and resulting form | Turning exceptional canon cases into ordinary renewable crafting |
| Run ending/prestige | Selected losses/retention and explicit entry/commitment disclosure | Every failure becoming the current field-training renewal |

Game respawn mechanics do not prove all local people are immortal. Manga recovery exceptions do not prove a generic transferable resurrection recipe. Ledgers leave exact exceptional methods unresolved until inspected. An earlier counterpart is not a resurrection of the lost person; the player may care about both while their histories remain distinct.
