## Origin is a composition, not one bonus

| Axis | What it specifies |
|---|---|
| World / source snapshot | Local physical/metaphysical rules and included source range |
| Continuity / era / region | Actual history, dated institutions/people and accessible place |
| Entry route | Native birth, reincarnation, existing-body arrival, summoning, projection, body replacement or other authored passage |
| Body / life stage | Anatomy, senses, mobility, age, health and actual support requirements |
| Mind / memory | Language, old-life recollections, learned methods, mental faculties and uncertainty |
| Source / potential | Acquired/inherited bodily or soul source, dormant potential and control; not universal genre aptitude |
| Family / social role | Particular relationships, recognition, support, duties and access |
| Means / custody | Actual resources, belongings, household-owned goods and available helpers |
| Scenario | Immediate situation, possible actions, stakes, discoveries and aftermath |
| Time / loss | Growth/absence/advancement policy, body/life/run retention and severe-risk terms |

Same world, different starts: an ordinary adult can earn a workshop; a school-age child can earn supervised study; a reincarnated infant can preserve an observation and build a caregiver relationship; a rooted spirit can bargain for protection or irrigation. Their first useful actions differ. A world/era/origin combination is validated, not a random unrestricted product of labels.

### Scratch, birth, reincarnation and replacement

| Scenario family | Retention and starting means | Distinct first play and long arc |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh ordinary start | Local body, authored basic language/means; no prior life or earned imported power | Choose labor, training, craft or a relationship; later access grows from actual work |
| Born into a generated family | Specific household, body/potential and current relations; adult family property stays theirs | Learn habits/places, earn trust/access, choose or reject an expected career |
| Reincarnated baby with memories | Selected recollections/methods remain, while new anatomy and sources are explicit | Observe, signal, develop control and communicate; future knowledge supplies uncertain leads |
| Reincarnated baby without memories | New life with its local support/potential and no hidden player-character knowledge | Discover family/world normally; later source awakening or investigation is not assumed |
| Older transmigrated outsider | Same actual body and declared cargo/sources cross intact | Find shelter, language, work and legitimate access; retain supported earned strength |
| Inhabit an existing body | Declare surviving body physiology, consciousness, memories, belongings and consent/conflict | Learn which functions actually work and who recognizes the body; reconcile inherited duties |
| Take a predecessor's social role | Role/appearance/history are authored; trained methods, sources and authority are separately declared | Survive expectations without unearned techniques; admit incapacity, seek teaching, buy services or investigate |
| Reborn as tree/plant | Rooted body, local senses/growth, support resources and any acquired communication source | Secure water/light/protection, communicate through real channels, research mobility or symbiosis |
| Beast/familiar/construct | Specific body/source/controller/custody and communication abilities | Learn cooperative roles or independence; meaningful anatomy and ownership constraints |
| Projected/dream visitor | Actual remote body/connection, returning domains and interruption defined | Investigate within supported senses/means; no automatic physical loot/body-source transfer |

These are original scenario families. A reference world's lore may permit, prohibit or leave a route unresolved. A deliberately different rule is labelled a branch change. No scenario retroactively edits the user's current save or grants all of a canonical protagonist's power.

## No automatic predecessor-power inheritance

Taking a name, body or narrative role is not identical to possessing the person's techniques, source, mastery, fuel, soul bond, equipment or recognized office. Each domain gets an explicit starting rule. If actual retained anatomy supports an innate faculty, say what survives and whether it is dormant, controllable or unsupported. Do not silently remove an operative organ solely because a challenge needs weakness.

Examples of proposed starts:

- A named ninja's role-only counterpart has their local expectations but no earned technique records. A real eye/lineage, if retained, needs its actual body/source/control contract. An authored source-less counterpart is an explicit variant, not a claim about ordinary canon reincarnation.
- A spiritual worker's remembered sword practice can survive while their original soul-bound release does not. Whether this new soul/body can acquire another source is a local question; knowing a name is not purchasing a Bankai.
- A technician's descendant inherits a family story and possible workshop access. The parent's suit remains one owned object, and the parent's engineering mastery is not in the child's mind.
- A child remembers fictional plot events but starts with no magical body/source. Such memories may guide investigation; the local magic rule determines whether execution can ever be acquired.

Known losses/support are reviewed at origin commitment. Player-facing copy discloses known start means and limitations while preserving hidden family motives/potential. A mode advertised as “no inherited powers” must define whether that means no learned techniques, no bodily faculty, no soul source, no gear, or all of them.

## Generated families and fair representation of unfairness

Generate from source/era-compatible household templates, not a rank-only lottery. Proposed fields: household identity, members/ages/bodies, livelihoods/resources, residence/access, language, social ties, obligations, caregiving means, potential source provenance, known/hidden facts and viable activities. Novel original families can exist beside a source cast without pretending to be canon.

Random generation freezes candidate pool/revision, seed, actual result and acquisition cause once selected. Previewing a profile never rerolls a wealthy family; reload retains the same parents and private history. Future new-origin reroll options need a declared cost/restart policy. No automatic favorable outcome is selected.

A poor supportive household, wealthy restrictive household, isolated caretaker, politically contested lineage or debt-bound apprenticeship changes opportunities and duties. Wealth is not affection, rank is not knowledge, and harshness is not a substitute for authored causes. A difficult start can lack a safe win; terms and actual information still matter.

Household stock belongs to its actual owner/holder. Permission to use a tool can create a loan, not a duplicate item. Parents/guardians have their own work/knowledge/means. They can notice unusual speech or demonstrated talents; they do not know the reincarnator's private memories automatically. A hidden family faculty remains unknown until a supported observation or discovery.

## Infant and child play without long forced waits

An infant's visible options can be attention, observation, recall, simple signaling and supported motor practice. Early wins include a recognized signal, remembered route/voice, a small controlled action, useful trust or a discovered hazard. Adult memories do not supply adult muscle, fluent vocal speech or locally verified facts.

A child phase can open language, literacy, independent movement, chores, apprenticeships and supervised experiments as actual functions develop. Anatomy/control, physical age and retained mental memories are distinct. Exact developmental ages/time and cultural expectations are world-local proposals, not medical guidance or a universal schedule.

Avoid mandatory real-time years of growth. A proposed chapter advance can move to a later phase after meaningful choices. Before acceptance it declares uncompleted work, participating people, calendar changes, body/source development, retained evidence, relevant risks and losses. Its resulting state is frozen and validated, with concrete consequences; it cannot silently mark every future canon event as completed.

Care work can become a consenting NPC controller/routine with actual supplies and capacity. A narrative abstraction can cover unrepresented ordinary days, but must label the scope. It does not prove a full minute-by-minute childhood simulation. Current no-browser-closed-advancement remains; a newborn origin does not introduce offline starvation or aging.

## Tree and other bodies change available actions

A proposed rooted spirit perceives nearby contact, light/water or a declared magical sense. It need not have human eyes/hearing. Ordinary movement is unavailable until an actual mobility faculty, movable vessel, consenting carrier or transplant route is acquired. Root reach and place access are functional, not a cosmetic race modifier.

Progress can include stronger roots, protected bark, supported communication, useful fruit/sap, habitat cooperation, a guardian relationship, a planted grove or a mobile seed/avatar under its own policy. Biological outputs need real support and a declared production rule. The grove is a facility; a seed/avatar does not automatically duplicate the original consciousness/source and possessions.

Interactions include signaling a tending person, lending shelter, sensing a nearby trace, negotiating relocation, protecting a nursery or deciding who receives a scarce output. A visitor may misunderstand the signal. A woodcutter's reaction depends on what they can observe/know and their actual aims. No universal recognition that the tree is secretly the player.

Planting/transplant/symbiosis/graft and body loss need actual custody, survival, source retention and compatibility rules. A human spell's gestures might be unavailable while a compatible learned principle remains useful. Returning to a human body preserves only declared methods/sources, not every plant organ by default.

## Overarching history with local calendars

Represent three author concepts: campaign event ordering, each world's local calendar/era, and the traveler's actual body/experience duration. The runtime still has one authoritative scheduler and durable publication path. Any mapping/local time rate is a selected adapter, not a second unmanaged clock.

Proposed timeline record: world-instance/source revision, local calendar anchor, traveler entry/exit/elapsed exposure, actual event causes, NPC body/life ages, known reports, active commitments and absence policy. Parallel worlds may be in different years or mythic eras while their visits are ordered in one campaign. Similar dates do not merge histories.

An origin can begin before, during, after or alongside a source arc. Earlier versions need earlier people, resources and institutions. Growing from baby to adulthood can overlap real local changes when authored; it does not automatically age frozen absent worlds. A source endpoint is author context, not a character's calendar notification.

Absence alternatives remain proposals: freeze the instance; advance funded relevant actors/events under bounded common-clock work; or author a declared chapter snapshot. Review effects before a time jump that would move a commitment. Repeated preview/import cannot grant retroactive years of production, mastery, inherited wealth or invented missed-deadline loss.

### A worked branching life proposal

An original family lives near a repair/clinic route. In infancy, the reincarnator preserves a heard route warning and learns an intelligible signal. At a later authored child phase, the family can spend scarce funds on literacy, supervised craft or travel; the choice changes who teaches and who owns the household tool. During a regional crisis, the teenager can warn an actual witness, support evacuation, sell unverified future knowledge or abstain.

Saving a supplier may preserve a later training relationship; an ignored warning may prove false in this branch; a planted refuge/grove may support people after the player's departure. Each cause updates only the relevant instance and knowledge. A later reincarnation retains selected memory while body-bound mastery/source/gear follow their declared terms. This is an original coherent arc, not an executed lifespan mechanic.

## Source candidate and implementation gate

[Harry Potter candidate](harry-potter/WORLD_BRIEF.md) develops a bounded school/family/herbology/craft origin reference. Its [source ledger](harry-potter/SOURCE_LEDGER.md) separates the seven novels, bounded companion explanations and original starts. It is outside the five completed deep campaign dossiers; another world GM could expand it under a later exact reservation.

Inspected existing service: `chooseSimulationOrigin` in `src/simulation.js` requires an eligible registered origin and a one-time `origin.selected` claim, then applies staged effects. It is not a new-game body/world initializer, random-family generator, per-life reselection or time-jump engine. Current body primitives and authored NPC lifespan policies likewise do not adapt native player menus/combat to infants or plants.

Missing selected delivery: valid new-game/per-life origin initialization, body-specific legal native actions/stats/inventory, deterministic family/household generation, age/communication/care state, chapter-time projection and safe world-calendar conversion, source/soul/role retention, instance-native bindings and migration/import/UI. Reuse current time/people/custody/knowledge/commands rather than another save or reward manager.

Future gate: fresh ordinary and limited-body origins; legal differing first actions; frozen family/name/resources after reload; no predecessor-power entitlement; one meaningful body/learning milestone with actual cost/knowledge; reviewed chapter advance; preserved original native progress/old-save defaults; rejected invalid/stale/duplicate selection and failed-save publication. Browser proof requires rebuilt source and generated saves on a fresh origin. No such checks run in this design pass.
