## LWF2: Strength has several axes

Compare concrete tasks, eligible targets and actual support. A level title is
local information, not a universal contest winner.

| Axis | What changes | Original influence example |
|---|---|---|
| Depth | Which decision layer is reached | Calm a feeling; suggest an act; issue a command; alter a standing priority. |
| Breadth | Number and kind of targets | One known person versus a crowd; humans versus eligible spirits. |
| Precision | How selective and controllable the effect is | Suppress panic while preserving caution; replace only one specified instruction. |
| Penetration | Which named defenses it can affect | A stronger version can penetrate a ward its earlier version cannot. |
| Permanence | How long the imposed state lasts and how it ends | Until interrupted, timed, persistent until cured or irreversible by the selected ordinary remedy. |
| Source dependence | What must remain available | Caster concentration, carried talisman, local pact authority or no continuing source. |
| Cost | Initial, maintained and cumulative payment | Mana to apply; focus to sustain; strain per controlled target. |
| Range | Contact, line of effect and continuing reach | Touch to install; continued nearby control; installed command survives departure. |

Speed, reliability, information quality and detectability can matter too.
Duration is not automatically strength: a brief precise command can achieve more
than a long vague mood bias. A powerful broad effect may remain poorly selective.
Genuinely stronger versions are allowed to dominate every relevant axis on the
same task. Do not invent a mandatory weak-version advantage or cheap counter.
An improved tier should state the new supported outcome and retain that result.

## LWF3: Installation, active state, consequence and knowledge differ

Four questions must be answered independently:

- Was an effect legally installed, and on which actual target?
- Is it currently operating, suspended, expired or removed?
- What actions and lasting changes happened while it operated?
- Who knows or suspects what, from which evidence?

Original example: a five-minute command makes a clerk open a gate. At expiry,
the clerk regains ordinary choice. The gate remains open until someone shuts it;
the departed traveler is not teleported back. The clerk may remember opening it
without knowing why. A later witness can discover the manipulation after expiry.
An enthrallment may instead install an enduring priority, but the name alone
says nothing about command scope, memory, cure or awareness.

Consent, resistance and harmony are local premise outcomes. No universal consent
gate, automatic resistance or mandatory harmonious result is proposed here.
Permanent means no ordinary automatic expiry under the stated rule. It does not
mean undetectable, unopposable, impossible to cure or infinite authority.
A momentary action can have permanent consequences; a permanent faculty can
produce a momentary output. Keep those timescales distinct on the authored card.

## LWF4: Ending and countering have different meanings

| Operation | Specific meaning to author | Does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Resistance | Reduces application chance, depth, duration or control under a named contest. | Blanket immunity or certain recognition. |
| Immunity | The defined target state disallows this defined application. | Protection against every adjacent method or undoing a preexisting effect. |
| Suppression | Stops specified operation while the suppressor's rule holds. | Deletion, cured memory, source destruction or permanent immunity. |
| Cure/removal | Ends the installed state through a supported remedy. | Reversal of every action, wound, agreement or belief it caused. |
| Expiration | Reaches its declared time boundary. | Automatic discovery, retaliation or emotional reset. |
| Sustaining | Maintains support and pays ongoing costs at declared boundaries. | Unlimited payment, cross-world support or free recasting. |
| Severance | Cuts a named channel between source and output. | Destruction of already independent outputs. |
| Transfer | Moves the actual eligible source/effect under declared ownership rules. | Duplication, transferred skill or a new target automatically inheriting its predecessor's allegiance. |

Authored local answers include whether suppressed time continues to elapse,
whether a cure removes one caster's layer or all qualifying layers, whether
source death ends dependent control, and whether reactivation requires a new cast.
No automatic immunity through repeated exposure unless that world's rules say so.

## LWF5: Layering and conflicts need a local policy

Proposal: give each selected effect a composition rule. Compatible layers can
coexist; conflicting layers can reject, contest, suppress or replace according
to the authored world rule. Force tiers may settle a contest when genuinely
sufficient. Keep the winner, displaced layer and reason reproducible.

Original case: calming panic and issuing a travel command can both operate;
calming does not make the command consensual. Two contradictory movement orders
need an order/contest rule. Removing the latest command may reveal an earlier
still-live order if the profile explicitly preserves it; it must not resurrect
an expired command. Memory alteration can coexist with behavior control without
silently hiding an independent physical trace or third-party recollection.

Current sum/max condition aggregation is useful for simple scalar statuses. It
is insufficient evidence of separate overlapping caster instances. Reapplying a
condition currently writes a new expiry and cause while merging intensity
(`src/simulation_effects.js:307-313`), so new attribution/layer requirements
need an explicit compatibility choice before implementation.

## LWF6: Worlds can define different lifecycle rules

These are original illustrative world profiles, not franchise canon claims.

| Profile | Supported rule | Distinct outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Concentration craft | Commands require a live local focus channel. | Severance frees current behavior; completed actions and memories remain. |
| Inscribed craft | A command is installed in a target-carried seal with its own expiry. | Caster departure does not end it; destroying the seal does. |
| Oath jurisdiction | Control applies only to explicitly eligible acts inside an institution's bounds. | Leaving jurisdiction suspends authority; return behavior follows a declared clock policy. |
| Soul imprint | An enduring priority binds to a specifically identified soul. | Body replacement may preserve it; an unrelated heir does not inherit it merely by succession. |
| Bodily chemistry | Temporary altered feeling depends on concentration in this body. | Soul transfer does not automatically carry the chemical state. Learning from the experience may persist. |

Crossing must name what travels: source, learned method, installed effect,
material carrier, link, identity and independently produced consequences.
Destination support, local defenses and conversion rules are checked separately.
Rebirth, replay and heir succession are different boundaries. A soul-bound
proposal cannot bypass the actual identity/carrier contract with its label.
Current transition domains can clear conditions, sources, knowledge and relations
separately (`src/simulation.js:572-579`). They are groundwork, not proof of every
crossing profile. Native/heir pathways need their own actual-owner audit.

## LWF7: Idle clocks and compatibility are part of the mechanic

Proposal: specify the clock for effect expiry and sustaining payment. Played
minutes, local-world elapsed time and wall-clock absence are different choices.
Do not infer offscreen autonomy from a scheduler or punish closing the browser.
A maintained effect stops when its selected support runs out; unused stock and
payment must remain exact. Catch-up processes expiry before post-expiry acts and
settles each paid act once, even across reload. Discovered knowledge may arrive
later than the effect that caused it. Unobserved NPC belief stays private.

A new layered record must declare migration for old scalar conditions, preserve
unrelated state and forbid fabricated historical casters, cures or witnesses.
Historical receipts need their original revision semantics or an explicit
conversion. Unsupported saved content must remain recoverable or reject safely;
a field name alone is not a migration. Private user saves remain untouched.

## LWF8: Minimal selected contract and proposed acceptance

For one authored example, require identity/revision; actual source and target;
eligibility; supported decision layer; initial and sustained cost; clock and
termination; support dependency; conflict rule; aftermath; and observation route.
Reuse existing commands, typed effects and owners where these actually express
the result. Add only the missing adapter for the selected example, not a generic
mind engine or a universal rank translation framework.

All cases below are proposed and NOT RUN:

| Case | Expected observable distinction |
|---|---|
| A timed suggestion and enduring enthrallment receive the same request. | Their distinct scope, refusal rights and termination produce authored different outcomes. |
| Two tiers face the same actual ward and target. | A genuinely superior tier penetrates where the weaker cannot, with exact cost and result. |
| Dependent command and independent seal lose their caster's support. | Only the dependent effect stops automatically; produced consequences stay. |
| A timed effect expires during catch-up, then reload occurs. | No later controlled act, repeated payment or restarted expiry. |
| A cure removes one layer while another has expired. | No expired order returns; current control and retained consequences are reported separately. |
| Target knows the act, but not its cause; investigator later finds a trace. | Suspicion/discovery can change without changing effect lifetime or rewriting prior knowledge. |
| Body transfer, world crossing and heir succession are compared. | Each applies its own identity/carrier/reset rule; none receives free universal persistence. |
| An old scalar-status save is imported after a future layer change. | Declared migration preserves unrelated data and does not invent caster history. |
