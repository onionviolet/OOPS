## WA-P1: Describe the act before choosing the effect

A single action label such as Help, Teach, Hire or Lead hides materially different acts.
Use authored local distinctions, with actual means and recipients, rather than a global intensity slider.

| Action kind | Material change | Social change | Who can know | What persists | Next useful choice |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Repair one cart | One working brace; supplied material spent | Recipient owes nothing beyond declared terms | Participants; road users can see the working cart without knowing who repaired it | Cart condition and receipt | Leave, receive agreed pay, teach the method |
| Cover one shift | A specific duty is performed | One named promise/favor settles | Worker and post officer; player receives the handover | Wage, duty receipt, remaining favor count | Assign another finite job or resume own work |
| Employ for a week | Capacity is reserved under funded terms | Employer obligations and worker exit terms | Signatories; coworkers know observed attendance | Completed work and earned pay survive contract end | Renew, release, alter hours by agreement |
| Join a household | Agreed stock/access and chores become shared | Mutual commitments, privacy and boundaries | Members know their agreement; outsiders know only disclosures | Personal goods, shared goods and commitments retain ownership | Negotiate workload, travel, change arrangement |
| Hold an office | Particular funds/access and duties are delegated | Decisions represent the institution within a remit | Members know appointment; a public notice may reach others | Authorized past decisions and outstanding commitments | Complete term, resign, transfer custody |

Favor, employment, household and office are not successively stronger friendship bonuses.
A friend can refuse employment; an employee can perform reliable work without liking the employer.
A temporary officer can sign a durable supply order if that authority was actually granted.
Term expiry ends new authority, not the already completed authorized act or its consequences.
Someone discovering the signature later learns who signed; it does not mean everyone knew at signing.

## WA-P2: Distinguish private facts, methods and transferable sources

| Transmission | What recipient gets | What they still need | Diffusion and visibility | Durable result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tell a fact | A claim with a speaker and basis | Evidence to verify it; no execution competence implied | Can repeat the claim without proving it | Attributed belief or later corrected belief |
| Demonstrate once | Observed sequence and possibly useful clues | Practice, tools and enough explanation | Witnesses see the act, not necessarily the method | A remembered observation |
| Teach a usable method | Learned procedure within demonstrated limits | Recipient practice and real execution means | Teacher and learner know the lesson; outsiders need a route | Competence and named transmission receipt |
| Supply a written recipe | Custody of an interpretable source | Literacy, equipment, stock and missing practical details | Copies can travel independently if actually made/delivered | Paper plus acquired understanding, separately |
| Train a teacher | Capacity to diagnose another learner's errors | Teaching willingness, time, suitable learner | A new teaching route becomes available locally | A second transmission chain with attribution |
| Publish supported notes | Accessible source for a declared audience | Distribution/access and each reader's acquisition | Audience is neither everyone nor automatically all competent | Available edition; actual readers' records |

Proposal: one authored method has two workable uses and one known limitation.
Teaching it to one collaborator unlocks their local service only after actual practice and means.
Publishing notes makes access possible, without granting every NPC the player's skill level.
If a reader misuses incomplete instructions, the effect follows that misuse and the evidence available.
There is no automatic reputation penalty simply because invention disrupted another occupation.

## WA-P3: Successful methods change local needs through named adopters

Original example: an inexpensive reusable parcel brace reduces damage on one route.
Private use improves your deliveries; teaching a depot worker improves their eligible deliveries.
Giving that worker permission and a readable guide to teach creates a second local route to learning.
Those are separate choices; publication is not an automatic reward at maximum mastery.

| Stage | Actual local change | Independent actor response | Knowledge boundary | Continuing choice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Private use | Your supplied parcels need fewer replacement braces | Receiver notices less damage, without knowing the cause | Inspection proves parcel condition, not method ownership | Explain, sell finished braces, keep practicing |
| One trained adopter | Depot can repair eligible parcels using its own stock | Worker chooses useful assigned work within agreed remit | Worker knows the taught method; dispatcher learns through a report | Train further, supply stock, undertake another task |
| Supported local spread | A second practiced worker can provide the service | Existing repairer can buy stock, teach a finer technique or seek other work | Only actual lesson/report routes identify the inventor | Collaborate, maintain exclusivity where possible, specialize |
| Institutional adoption | One published specification changes one supply contract | Buyer may order fewer finished braces and more raw supplies | Signatories know terms; affected seller receives notice | Negotiate supply/inspection role, leave the market, keep a small shop |

Adaptation includes neutral and beneficial outcomes: reduced workload, safer routes, new customers,
more time for another craft, or an NPC choosing a different supplier for practical reasons.
Do not force scarcity, sabotage, accusations or escalating danger to punish useful success.
Any adverse outcome needs a cause: limited stock, faulty teaching, an actual lost order,
known exclusivity terms, or an independent person's conflicting goal.
Demand changes only in the named local rows; no universal economy or infinite price simulation follows.
Finished goods survive later loss of the lease or office that helped create them.

## WA-P4: An independent person can like you and still choose differently

Represent a relevant local goal, known boundary and current obligation in each proposed scene.
Expose a stated reason when the character hears it; do not expose a hidden motive by default.
Refusal need not damage affection, and permission in one area does not imply permission in another.

| Request | Plausible independent response | Distinct aftermath |
|---|---|---|
| Ask a friend for a dangerous favor | Decline; offer equipment or an introduction | Friendship can persist, while the requested mission remains undone |
| Ask a taught colleague to keep a method private | Agree to bounded secrecy, negotiate terms, or refuse | Only an accepted agreement creates that obligation |
| Request work outside an employment remit | Ask for amended terms or refuse | Existing agreed work remains owed; no free expansion of obedience |
| Ask a household member to manage a shop | Agree to certain hours, reserve their own plans | Shared home does not grant unlimited labor or stock access |
| Use temporary compulsion to obtain a signature | Behavior occurs if the power supports it; agreement legitimacy is a separate local rule | Goods delivered and witnesses' observations can outlast the influence; perceived cause may remain unknown |

The last row joins the sibling influence audit; this lane specifies only downstream work/authority.
Cooperation, trust, legal validity and ability to refuse must not collapse into a single value.
No default universal social punishment is selected. Compelling a signature alone
does not grant affection; a separate affection-changing operation requires its
own declared mechanism.

## WA-P5: Imperfect histories, repair and quiet lives

Original applicant scene: two people seek a workshop post; one is selected for demonstrated competence.
The other can take a paid assistant task if it exists and its terms fit their means.
The loss remains a loss: no automatic superior secret reward, invented consolation office or erased rivalry.
The winner has specific duties and access, not unrestricted use of the entire institution.

Repair scene: a delayed order hurts a customer who actually depended on its delivery.
Tell them, refund an agreed deposit, repair the disruption or negotiate a later delivery.
Each settles a different part of the problem; returning money does not restore the missed occasion.
The customer might resume practical trade without trusting another urgent order.
Unknown misconduct does not create knowledge-based grievance until evidence reaches that person.
An unreceived explanation cannot repair their interpretation, though material restitution can still help.

Quiet-life scene: finish a craft apprenticeship and choose a funded small-shop routine.
Useful payoff is actual reliable goods, sufficient local income, familiar customers and time for a chosen project.
An annual gathering or a student's improvement can supply texture without always creating a quest.
Offer continue, travel or chapter closure; a quiet choice is not a lesser ending needing escalation.
Do not invent family membership, automatic future children or absent-world achievements to fill the epilogue.
Closure records what was actually earned; continuation follows the world's declared time/absence policy.

## WA-P6: Finite orders and standing delegation differ

The existing Iona patrol is a finite supported example, not a general servant simulator.
Proposed standing delegation adds one plain recurring instruction for a mastered local task.
Specify stock holder, budget, pay, permitted decisions, pause reasons, report recipient and termination.

| Instruction | Execution boundary | Exception | End/expiry consequences |
|---|---|---|---|
| Make two known braces | Consume allocated stock; stop after two results | Missing wood pauses remaining work | Completed braces and paid labor remain; unused stock returns by custody terms |
| Keep up to two braces stocked | Repeat only within a funded cap and demand policy | New recipe/order requires review | Canceling ends new starts; current job follows explicit cancel/finish terms |
| Manage ordinary shop sales for a week | Sell declared goods at agreed local prices | Credit request or uncommon item requires approval | Sale receipts endure; cash and unsold goods are handed over |
| Act as post supervisor this term | Assign only named duties within granted authority | Unfunded work or conflicting orders are referred | Authority ends; lawful accepted duties require an explicit transfer/settlement |

An exception pauses the affected work; it need not stop unrelated training or the entire game.
Reports distinguish completed, reported and unresolved; a missing report is not proof of misconduct.
Routine stock/time/pay is material gameplay, not another relationship upkeep meter.
No constant attendance, decay for closing the browser, general scheduler or programming language is needed.
Default future slices can use the existing played-clock/frozen-absence policy unless separately selected.
