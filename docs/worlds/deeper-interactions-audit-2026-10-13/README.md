## Integrated distinctions

**F1: Mechanism, duration and consequence are independent.** Enthrallment is not
necessarily permanent, and temporary compulsion is not necessarily shallow.
An effect can alter feelings, beliefs, attention, remembered experience, chosen
goals, motor control or allegiance. Define which actually changes. Duration
describes how long that operation holds, not how long its caused outcomes last.

**F2: Awareness is not an automatic consequence of effect application or release.**
Keep the actual causal record separate from a target's experience and knowledge.
Noticing an odd feeling, recognizing interference, identifying a source and
proving an actor's responsibility require different evidence. A target might
never find out, misattribute it or learn years later through a supported route.
Witnesses and the affected person can know different things.

**F3: Strength is a comparison on a task, not a universal rung.** Precision,
depth, number of targets, reliability, penetration, range, duration and support
can differ. One source may simply dominate another. Counterplay, detectability,
cost and backlash come from the ability and world rules, not a compulsory
balancing tax. Real supernatural changes need not always wear off or backfire.

**F4: Material results and social interpretation survive differently.** Ending
compulsion need not restore spent money, unsay a warning or erase a witnessed
promise. Removing an emotion modifier does not specify the person's subsequent
opinion. A person's trust, affection, belief and behavior can disagree. Discovery
can cause anger, gratitude, uncertainty, negotiation or no change, depending on
what happened and how they interpret it.

**F5: Wider world change needs the same precision.** Teaching a fact, teaching a
method and transferring a power source are different actions. A favor, one job
and a standing appointment create different commitments. Delegation and diffusion
need declared recipients, resources, time, result ownership and reporting rather
than treating every relationship increase as the same reward.

## Small comparison scene

Original design example, not franchise canon or implemented gameplay: gain entry
past a gatekeeper. Compare effects on the same action rather than damage numbers.

| Method | Actual change | Possible discovery | After it ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| Persuade with a valid reason | Gatekeeper voluntarily makes an exception | They know your argument; no magical inference needed | Remembers the decision and its outcome |
| Temporary emotional charm | You feel appealing or safe to them; orders still need separate authority | May feel ordinary; detection needs a fitting clue or faculty | Artificial warmth ends; entry and their remembered interaction remain |
| One-command compulsion | Makes them open the gate within the specified control envelope | Could be consciously resisted or experienced as their own impulse | Control ends; cannot reverse the already-opened gate or admitted visitor |
| Sustained enthrallment | Maintains a declared attachment, allegiance or decision bias | Profile may be overt, unnoticed or discoverable later | Release policy determines feelings; past deeds and material results are separate |
| Illusory credential | Changes perceived evidence, without directly changing loyalty | Inspection, contradiction or a supported detector can expose it | Belief can persist after the illusion unless corrected |
| Memory alteration | Changes a declared memory, not the guard's entire identity by default | Their record may conflict with a ledger or another witness | Other people's memories and the entry record are unaffected unless separately changed |

These are alternative profiles. Exact control, cost, memory and discovery terms
must be stated for the specific ability before implementation.

## Minimal authoring questions

For a useful effect definition answer: what is changed; which actor/part is
affected; what actions become possible; how it starts and ends; what supports it;
what remains after release; who can know what and through which evidence.
Add stacking, time/crossing and save compatibility rules when that effect needs
them. Do not build a universal architecture merely to store this design palette.
