## EK-F2: A proposed default currently overstates awareness

A target can remember an action without identifying influence; a witness can see an action without recognizing coercion.

## EK-P1: Taxonomy by actual operation

These are original comparison families, not mandatory world categories or a strength ladder.
A local ability named “charm” may implement several rows or none of their usual assumptions.

| Family | State actually changed | Proposed action and output | What does not follow automatically |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary persuasion | A person's interpretation or chosen plan through reasons, incentives or evidence | Explain a route advantage; they voluntarily change tomorrow's itinerary | Affection, loyalty, truth of the argument, or agreement on unrelated requests |
| Ordinary deception | Beliefs through misleading testimony, impersonation, concealment or forged evidence | Supply a forged delivery order; the clerk believes this parcel is authorized | Magical control, guaranteed success, actual office rights, or discovery of the lie |
| Emotional influence | Fear, calm, confidence, anger, attraction or salience | Suppress panic for ten minutes; the worker can attend to a repair | Obedience, changed beliefs, affection toward the caster, or acceptance of danger |
| Favorability charm | An evaluative/relational response toward a defined person or object | Make the caster seem welcoming; a guarded interview becomes possible | Trustworthiness of every claim, unlimited cooperation, or erased prior knowledge |
| Enthrallment | Sustained alignment of decisions, attachment or command acceptance, exactly as the source defines | Establish a bond whose holder may issue continuing orders | Permanence, unconsciousness, total scope, memory loss, or lack of independent reasoning |
| Discrete compulsion | A specified action, omission, response or decision | Force one truthful answer to one defined question | Knowledge of unknown answers, continuing loyalty, or literal interpretation in every source |
| Motor/body control | Bodily movements or control rights | Move a hand to open a latch while the mind remains aware | Consent, belief change, memory access, ownership of possessions, or ability to execute an unknown technique |
| Possession | Occupancy/controller of a body, vessel or other eligible form | Enter a supported host; act through its body under the actual source rule | Reading memories, stealing faculties, suppressing every occupant, or surviving destruction of the vessel |
| Perception/illusion | Sensory input, apparent environment or selection of what is perceived | Hide one doorway visually; witnesses route around the apparent wall | A physically missing doorway, changed written records, or later forgetting that they saw a wall |
| Memory alteration | Access, encoding, recall, deletion or fabricated recollection | Hide recall of one meeting; later evidence can still exist elsewhere | Erasure of all learned skill, personality, third-party records, or objective history |
| Identity/value alteration | Self-concept, goals, priorities or core commitments | Change whom a person regards as their sovereign or what objective they pursue | Actual appointment, legal title, matching public records, or other people's acceptance |
| Supernatural contract | Supported permissions, obligations, penalties or enforced clauses | Bind a voluntarily signed delivery agreement to its declared enforcement | Enthrallment, private mind access, universal jurisdiction, or unrestricted interpretation by the caster |

Control can be beneficial, harmful, consensual, imposed or mixed; those descriptions do not substitute for the operation.
An authorized calming effect and an imposed calming effect may change the same feeling while creating different histories.
A genuinely belief-changing method is allowed to change belief. A genuinely affection-changing method may create felt affection.
Do not deny a declared power's output by insisting every emotion remains untouched. Record its cause separately where supported.

## EK-P2: Levels are a comparison envelope, not one number

| Axis | Independent local answers | Why two abilities can differ |
|---|---|---|
| Depth | Attention, feeling, interpretation, action selection, motor control, identity | A broad distraction can be shallower than a one-person identity rewrite |
| Scope | One impulse, one request, one task, one topic, all supported behavior | A permanent ban on one word may be narrower than five minutes of broad control |
| Duration | Instant resolution, elapsed time, sustained channel, trigger-bound, persistent | Brief application may create a lasting memory or contract |
| Agency retained | Free planning within a goal, bounded discretion, literal movement, shared control | A thrall may solve problems independently; motor control may require every movement |
| Precision | Named action/object/person, semantic category, broad aim | “Move this box” differs from “protect my interests” |
| Reach and delivery | Touch, sight, sound, mark, known identity, network, jurisdiction | Targets and entry routes come from the actual source |
| Target count | One, finite set, a crowd, institution membership | Group breadth does not establish depth on each participant |
| Retention | No aftereffect, retained memory, habit, learned procedure, durable changed priority | Expiry of active control can leave practiced skill without continuing obedience |
| Awareness | Obvious presence, vague wrongness, normal-seeming reasoning, no sensation | Detectability may remain unchanged as duration or scope improves |
| Reliability and strength | Guaranteed eligible effect, uncertain effect, overcoming a supported obstacle | Greater rank need not improve every facet, and no invented counter is required |

Original comparisons: a weaker calming mist can affect twenty people; a stronger single-target method can remove panic completely.
A temporary master-level command can cover more decisions than a permanent apprentice-level prohibition.
An advanced source may improve all these facets and dominate its predecessor. Its acquisition story can matter without a contrived weakness.
“Temporary” answers when influence ends; “enthralled” answers a local control relationship. Neither answers the other.

## EK-P3: Actions must have different committed outputs

| Action chosen | Example committed output | Distinct output to avoid silently granting |
|---|---|---|
| Invite | Offer and independent accept/decline response | Forced attendance |
| Persuade | Supported belief/plan update | Global regard bonus for every topic |
| Deceive | Particular actor holds a particular misleading belief with a basis | World truth rewritten |
| Soothe | Defined fear/panic component suppressed | Willingness to sign or reveal secrets |
| Attract attention | Target notices caster/object | Target likes or obeys caster |
| Charm regard | Declared warm evaluation toward specified subject | Ownership or command authority |
| Suggest | Supported idea becomes salient or plausible | Guaranteed motor execution unless the source supplies it |
| Command | One resolved directive under the actual control rule | Unlimited future orders |
| Establish enthrallment | Persistent relation/control policy with supported command scope | New lawful office or friendship history |
| Pilot/possess | Eligible body's control changes | Owner's inventory/source transfers |
| Obscure perception | Specific signal replaced or hidden | Memory erase or object deletion |
| Edit recall | Named memory access/content changed | All recipients of earlier testimony forget |
| Rewrite priority | Particular future decisions use changed value | Past choices and public records change retroactively |
| Bind agreement | Actual eligible parties receive declared obligation/enforcement | A nonparty acquires the same burden |
| Release/suppress | Named ongoing effect ends or cannot operate | Refunds, healing, property return or restored relationships |

The target's actual means still determine execution. “Bring the key” does not create a key or reveal its location.
If a source grants otherwise missing knowledge or strength, record that as another explicit output.
An NPC using these actions has the same means, commitment and information gates as the player.

## EK-P4: Temporary influence can leave permanent outcomes

Separate four clocks: application time, active influence time, resulting action time, and aftermath time.
Expiry stops only the declared active operation. It does not rewind intervening work.
A compelled clerk can transfer one real parcel; removing compulsion does not teleport it back.
An illusion may last seconds while a traveler spends hours on the wrong road.
A calm worker can finish a repair that stays repaired after calm expires.
A temporary wish to study can yield real learned detail; retained skill is not proof of retained loyalty.
A persistent enthrallment can instead cease before any material action occurs, leaving no stolen goods or completed work.
Evidence of those results and recognition of their cause must remain distinct.

## EK-P5: Interruption, release and overlap need local answers

| Event | Required authored resolution, with no universal default |
|---|---|
| Application interrupted | Whether it never applied, applied partially, or already committed; which startup resources stay spent |
| Caster loses concentration | Whether control ends, pauses, weakens or continues independently |
| Caster dies/leaves | Whether a lasting imprint, bound source or command remains supported |
| Target crosses world/body boundary | Whether the effect follows a mind, body, mark, identity, jurisdiction or none |
| Voluntary release | Which ongoing operations stop; whether the target can know release occurred |
| Suppression | Whether expression stops while ownership/imprint remains, and what restoration resumes |
| Two calming sources | Add, cap, strongest active, independent facets or explicit exclusion |
| Conflicting commands | First/last priority, source precedence, eligible scope, negotiated occupant control or rejected commitment |
| Charm plus memory alteration | Whether affection remains without recall of the meeting; do not collapse the two |
| Possession plus an oath | Which person/body is bound, who can act, and how actual terms resolve |

Current condition sum/max intensity is not enough for competing semantic directives.
A proposed control receipt needs source, target, operation, scope, revision, commitment, termination policy and internal cause.
Observer-facing records are separate. Do not expose that receipt by default in a people panel.
Each selected adapter must reject stale target/body/support and resolve each paid action once across reload/import.

## EK-P6: Concrete playable comparisons, all original proposals

**EK-C1: The repair crew.** A worker refuses a roof repair because of panic, not hostility.
Ordinary reassurance may change their estimate of danger; calming suppresses panic; compulsion makes them undertake the named repair; motor control moves their hands without supplying roofing knowledge.
The same roof can end repaired under several routes, while their experience, remaining fear, knowledge and actual work costs differ.
Do not force identical success: an adequately supported stronger method may complete the task reliably.

**EK-C2: One missing parcel.** Compare one short handover compulsion, a one-day enthrallment with bounded courier discretion, and a forged authorization.
All can move the same actual parcel if eligibility holds. The clerk's knowledge, independent choices and later action differ.
The compulsion expires before arrival; the parcel still arrives. The enthralled courier can choose a practical route if its source preserves discretion.
The forged order creates a false belief rather than a controlled person. Discovery is possible only through supported evidence routes.

**EK-C3: A consenting vessel.** Use the inspected clay-body action as a comparison seed, not a general possession claim.
Compare a proposed shared-control variant with a full motor-control variant on the same seal-making task.
Body stamina and the player's blank stay real; return stops control and does not unmake the seal.
Host recall, mental communication and memory access are individually authored; none follows from the word possession.

**EK-C4: A remembered promise.** A memory editor removes recall of an agreement, while the actual written contract and counterparty remain.
A priority editor instead leaves accurate recall but changes whether the signer wants to honor it.
An enforcement source can require performance in either case if its actual terms support that result.
Compare truthful later explanation, continued imposed change and explicit restoration without inventing automatic regret or discovery.

## EK-O1: Suggested bounded design selection

One two-person parcel case can compare temporary discrete command, sustained enthrallment and ordinary deception against the same target/task.
Freeze what each source actually changes, then compare action, material output, release and actor knowledge.
This is a design candidate, not a replacement for active crafting/Foundation work or paused world lanes.
Open decisions: selected world/source, target agency under each effect, duration/termination and the supported discovery routes.
