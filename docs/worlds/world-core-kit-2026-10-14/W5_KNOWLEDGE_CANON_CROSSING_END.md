### K11.6 / K12.6: knowledge and clock discipline

In each starter table **actor** means the controlled character's memories,
training and current observations; **player** means reader familiarity and the
start card's permitted information; **world** means author truth plus distributed
NPC knowledge. World truth is not universal NPC awareness. Player familiarity
does not populate actor memory unless a selected premise supplies that carrier.
The card displays observable body, means, duties and rumors, not the world column.

Default: confidence is attached to an individual proposition and its provenance:
observed here, local report, remembered source, contradicted or untested. Dial:
more explicit confidence wording; no invented numeric accuracy. Divergence lowers
confidence only in causally affected claims. A wrong date need not invalidate a
map. Failed appraisal means inconclusive within that channel, not “nothing exists.”
All `W5-<world>-S01` IDs below are **proposed unallocated slots**, not claims that a
sealed record already exists. Their eventual truth belongs in the world's sealed
owner. W5 records only an ID and route, with no proposed answer.

Use [canon dispatch](../../gm/dispatch/canon-events-2026-10-14/COMMON.md),
[MoL lessons G1–G6 / K1–K8](../../gm/dispatch/canon-events-2026-10-14/pilot-mol/LESSONS.md)
and [FV lessons F1–F5 / L1–L4](../../gm/dispatch/canon-events-2026-10-14/pilot-fv/LESSONS.md).
Do not copy their graphs. W5 event rows are floor selections, not replacement
event data. Each needs occurrence, actor receipt, belief, response, actual means
and aftermath in its owning graph. Warning receipt does not guarantee rescue;
source prominence does not justify `fixed:true`. Surviving causes continue on
abstention; substitute actors require actual means, not automatic plot repair.
The dispatch's newer v2 proposal and pilot accounts of tested earlier metadata
are different evidence stages. Neither establishes integrated browser behavior.

Clocks below use dates only where qualified, otherwise source-relative windows
or order. A chapter locator is not an elapsed-time unit. Before a playable slice,
resolve its relative windows to a declared local calendar and pacing dial.
“Without you” assumes other prerequisites survive. It does not impose
browser-closed progress, which remains separately owned. Crossings use a proposed
physical retained-body default; rebirth, projection and local loop reset are
separate edges. Source/fuel/authority can go dormant without deleting ownership.
