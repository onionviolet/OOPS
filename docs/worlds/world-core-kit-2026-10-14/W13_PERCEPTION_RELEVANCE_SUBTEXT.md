### KP.W13.1: proposed data flow

Author candidate → validate world/instance/era/place/channel/law/discovery eligibility → choose eligible literal/detail/interpretation variant → score KR2 → project safe text + KP7 → optionally commit a promoted observation through its owning action.

Author records may carry `publicCueId`, content revision, scope refs, channel, observation predicate, interpretation predicate, local fact refs, known participant/item/goal refs, tone tags, public receipt templates, repeat family and optional promotion route. Private situation pointers remain author/executor inputs. They do not enter public ID, label, reason, search text, DOM attributes or preview payloads. This is an authoring proposal, not a field list the current pack compiler accepts.

The supplied snapshot has current actor/body endpoint, controlled body, known skill labels/effective levels, known active faculties and paid sensor receipt if any, active public conditions, language/learned-local facts, received people/object/goal/event refs and local played time. Output contains only public cue ID, selected safe text, safe reason, channel, local time and optional received-evidence link. Author diagnostics may explain rejected gates privately; the player does not receive a list of unseen candidates.

Known costs, time, commitment terms and essential access constraints remain explicit for every eligible build before an action is accepted. A specialist may notice a comparison sooner, but DD2 and similar optional cues do not substitute for the ordinary agreement preview.

Default hard gates: actual observation opportunity, appropriate body or instrument, understood method if interpretation requires it, source permission, selected era/place/time, and permitted revelation grade. A high relevance score cannot buy missing sight, language, licence, fuel, or disclosure authority. Knowing a name does not imply knowing its owner's location now. Supernatural sensors use local masks; a sense can detect presence without identity, cause or intent. B4 machine psionic blindness and JJ exceptional barrier recognition need distinct rules, not one power-level comparison.
