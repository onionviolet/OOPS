## Evidence, floors and scope

**SHOWN(source)** means live source text inspected, never gameplay proof.
**STATED(owner)** means an inspected doc says it, with that doc's proposal,
secondary-source or translation limits retained. **INFERRED** means a new
proposal. No unchecked MEMORY claim supplies a canon fact here.

Floor status describes evidence **before this extension**, in the inspected
owners below. **Met** needs the whole root floor; **partial** has useful pieces
but lacks the whole floor; **missing** lacks a world-specific floor there.
Missing does not mean the franchise lacks that culture or that every file in
the repo was searched. A named person is not a name pool; a church is not a
belief inventory; a report command is not a news network. No row establishes a
rebuilt playable floor. These new proposals do not upgrade their own status.

Every floor cell below is INFERRED. Defaults supply one bounded district's
content; dials alter intensity, audience or region for an in-world reason.
K19 needs a smell, sound and texture **at every represented hub place**, then
actual use in look/log text. Each row's cue trio is an anchor to extend to the
hub, not a claim that one trio completes all its places. K20 needs a stable local
name pool, address forms and two or three original paraphrased idioms. K21 needs
beliefs about death, luck and power, plus a daily ritual. K22 needs illness,
healing and aging rules plus a reachable medicine. K23 needs carriers and
elapsed delivery times. K24 needs distinct petty-crime, debt and rudeness
