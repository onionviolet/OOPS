## Reading the rows

Each world covers KM1-KM18. Supported families get two separate details;
thin families get two explicitly original candidates, not fabricated canon.
STATED means an inspected official page, wiki section or source dossier says
it. Dossier evidence retains its source edition and speaker limits; W7 did
not independently read every novel. INFERRED means the **detail itself** is
proposed, even when its basis is sourced. No unchecked MEMORY row is shipped.
Source IDs resolve in the register below; the suffix in each row is its
section, claim or chapter locator. A source basis does not verify a proposed
habit, price, gesture or line of dialogue.

KM-d levels are proposed authoring bands: N0 newcomer (plain surface), N1
familiar (local labels and routine), N2 regular (deviations from a learned
baseline), N3 practiced observer (specialist interpretation with actual
access). They are not an XP formula. Noticing never grants language, hearing,
sight, proximity or private knowledge. Default: N0-N1 texture; Dial: expose
more local context through repeated ordinary visits, because habits take time
to learn. Every row lists one intended display, not an implemented consumer.

H = hint candidate for [part 15](../world-depth-2026-10-13/15_JOKES_AS_HINTS.md).
T = proposed tell under K6.7. Both promotions are INFERRED even on STATED base
rows. H has no assigned target or payoff here. T records a proposed local baseline,
not a lie detector. Assign it to a particular actor and establish repeat
sightings before it can be read as that actor's tell. All remain ordinary texture until separately authored.
No sealed files were opened and no sealed truth or target was authored.
