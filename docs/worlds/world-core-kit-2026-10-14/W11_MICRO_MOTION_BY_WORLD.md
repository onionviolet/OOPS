Prefer derived or existing records at floor; do not smuggle these models into log text.

Idle column: **Yes** means safe learned routine during supported played-time progression, with means and availability rechecked. **Passive** means an observation/projection when context is actually observed, not a reward job. **Read** means a callback can render passively from an earned record. **No** means deliberate encounter/choice before safe repetition.
