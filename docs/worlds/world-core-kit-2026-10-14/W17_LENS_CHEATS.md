Combine matching world/episode/as-of first; show mismatched or stale bases side by side. Default small explanations, dial wider traces and sampling costs. Exact+exact is not automatically future.

**H2 boundaries:** projection/render is read-only, deterministic and free of RNG draws, writes, XP awards, costs, grants and imported world implementation code. **H3 redaction order:** choose subject/instance/episode → verify supplier and actual channel → apply observer knowledge and allowed discovery route → grade/calibrate → validate composite disclosure → frame/round → emit identical safe content to visual, accessible, log, export-summary and search surfaces. Hidden existence yields no counts, sorting gaps, error labels or suggestive timing. A strong lens may conduct an authored discovery action; it may not skip the discovery action by reading privateTruth. Sandbox does not itself unseal. Do not claim changing UI projection makes the complete raw save export safe; export storage has its own policy/gate.

**H4 minimal storage:** persist acquired cards/items and irreducible observations with timestamp/basis, sampled episode, calibration/earned milestones and supported knowledge retention. Derive current authorized self values live. Historical exact observations are stored if recall needs them, even when the current value can be recomputed. Never replace history with current truth. Current C0 schema is not a guarantee that all future nested state is compatible.

**H5 attention and presentation:** default one selected lens overlays or sits beside the subject's existing view, with a short “why/as of” row and visible range, not one compulsory panel per cheat.
