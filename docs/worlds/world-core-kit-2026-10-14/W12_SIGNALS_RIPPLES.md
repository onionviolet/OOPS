## W12.E: reading key and boundaries

**SHOWN** means directly inspected document text, with a locator. **STATED** means a source claim in an inspected official page, indexed excerpt or local source dossier. Dossier claims retain their narrator, witness, testimony and edition limits; W12 did not reread the novels. **INFERRED** means adaptation proposal. **MEMORY** means unchecked recall, unused as a factual basis here. All tables of game signals, payoffs, abstention, hub ripples, proposed lead times and pacing dials are INFERRED, including rows based on STATED scenes. Source support for a mechanism does not establish that the novel uses our proposed price or patrol cue.

Source tell lead times below are qualitative reading distances, not measured source medians. Exact elapsed time is UNKNOWN unless a source locator explicitly supplies it. A chapter gap is not a number of days. Each section's E1/E2/E3 names local event anchors, not registered event IDs or a replacement canon graph. Their actual prerequisites decide applicability after intervention. Signals cease, change or expire if the cause changes; no substitute villain is spawned to preserve the rhythm.

Rows use `world-S1..S5` for KS1 market, KS2 people, KS3 place, KS4 power, KS5 institution, and `world-Q1/Q2` for two K29 first-hub acts. Q rows name the act as well as what appears later. Lead for S is warning before its linked event; lead for Q is delay after the act. K30 requires proximity, access, a real sense/tool and learned interpretation. Ordinary people can hear attributed reports without acquiring the reporter's faculty. A power sense is not a motive detector.

KQ5 receipt floor for every Q row: cause/act ID, actual actor and affected owner, place/copy, occurrence clock, witness or gossip carrier, received clock, visible change, expiry and any retained memory. Receipt text reveals only the recipient's known cause. Default small local magnitude and fade after one work cycle/week; Dial severity and persistence with actual stock, injury, repair or witness memory. A proposed event does not create accepted duty or loss merely because the player skips it. Abstention means the source baseline remains eligible if its causes survive; it does not guarantee exact canon after unrelated divergence. No new browser-closed harm or hunger death is selected.

Later presentation target: laptop/desktop **1366×768 and wider**, a known lead beside its current route/action and a short received aftermath. Mobile optimization, UI implementation, source authentication and gameplay acceptance are deferred. Ambient calm text cannot bury an agreed deadline.
