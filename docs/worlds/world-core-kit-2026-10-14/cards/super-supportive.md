## 2. Growth

Selection, trade, affixation; levels from magical capability; deliberate authority practice;
wordchains and spells learned from teachers; school rank clubs; knight paths for Artonans. Plateaus:
fixed rank, skill caps, slow affixation. Breaks: chaos disasters, original skill designs.

## 3. Living beside the protagonist

The clock runs through a school year at Celena North on Anesidora (year inferred 2040): a stranding
in a chaos zone, school admission, a mass-casualty flood with emergency teleports, holidays, visits
to an Artonan estate, a healing arc, a communal ritual, a simulated battle (ongoing). Ordinary lives
are rich in the text: roommates, rank clubs, staff, estate households, families negotiating trades.

## 4. Society, economy, crafting

Summons as paid jobs; argold economy with dated prices; Earth families that hoard rare classes;
Artonan society of wizards, knights, votaries, ordinary class, estates and an institution that records
wordchain debt; schools; crafting by Wrights, Shapers, Adjusters and healers.

## 5. Cross-world

An outside-party Contract travels with its holder and compares to spirit contracts and shards; a
bound/free authority ratio is a portable growth meter; recorded debt magic depends on an institution;
consent-gated support skills interact with other worlds' items.
