Day one for an ordinary character: a goblin villager scouting, salvaging and hunting with a wolf
partner; a Free Guild F-rank adventurer renting a bed for a few silver; a merchant on a
repayable deed; an apprentice at a forge, loom or kitchen. None of them start with a unique
skill, a name from a patron, souls or a high rank. A dated contract and an introduction are the
main early resources.

## 2. Growth (K3)

Naming and evolution through a patron; awakening through a soul harvest; skill acquisition by
desire at an occasion, integration and evolution; guild ranks by trial; training under a master
(technique beats a stronger body in the source); contracts with demons and spirits. Plateaus:
naming risk and permanent loss, holding a skill without mastery, rank as a reading of operational
strength. Curve breakers arrive as events (a harvest festival, mass revival, a patron's subsidy).

## 3. Living beside the protagonist (K4, K12)

A relative clock from stated anchors (few absolute dates): Veldora's disappearance; town
founding; the Orc Lord war at Lake Sisu; Falmuth's conquest decision and invasion (about 100
residents killed, then revival by retained souls); the first Walpurgis; the Hinata duel and the
tournament; the dungeon opening; a year of roads and rail; the Empire war (civilians moved
underground); the second Walpurgis and the heavenly army (rural villages exposed by design);
postwar schools over eight years.

Starts (proposal): Tempest resident apprentice (strongest fit), guild visitor in Blumund,
dungeon staff, postwar exchange student, summoned recruit (medium), a second slime (needs a new
origin). No fan survey ran this round.

## 4. Society, economy, crafting (K7, K8, K9)

Tempest institutions; Free Guild ranks F upward with trials; human nations (Blumund, Falmuth,
Dwargon's state-allocated crafts); the Council; prices and wages kept as dated contracts tied to
an era and place. Crafts: smithing (copies handle worse than a master's originals), weaving,
cooking, potions, herb identification. Tournament and dungeon as hospitality industries.

## 5. Cross-world (K15)

Naming and skills engraved on the soul travel with a soul; magicule density sets local strength
the way ambient mana does elsewhere; soul payment collides with worlds that count souls or Od as
life; advisor intelligences need data and are fooled by free will. Full table in the private
round 2 report, section 5.
