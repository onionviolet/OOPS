## One power foundation, several ways to live

**Stated, creator cross-check:** Malcolm Tent describes innate abilities, Shane's inherited Wish, and cultivation increasing the stats underlying abilities. This supports a shared foundation for superhero and cultivation behavior, rather than separate species of rule system. [Author reply to Bryek](https://www.reddit.com/r/ProgressionFantasy/comments/15lhkt1/my_first_book_wish_upon_the_stars_was_just/). The held editions govern exact mechanics; an external explanation does not settle every exception.

**Shown/stated, source:** Impact gives reality weight; statistics, learned Skills, personal execution and soul tolerance matter separately. A high institutional standing, high cultivation rank and winning a particular fight are distinct observations. Early testimony says provisional I is not a full Ascendant rank while the I sheet already prints Impact 1; preserve that tension rather than silently repair it (V1 C2 `text/part0004.html` p8-18; C7 `text/part0009.html` p12-16, coordinator checked).
