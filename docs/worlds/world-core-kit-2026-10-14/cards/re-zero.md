## 1. Power as engine state (K2, K3)

Six separate stocks; the source keeps them apart and so should the engine (AD38):

| Stock | Default | Dial | In-world reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ambient mana | in the air; dense pools cause stupor | site density | world property |
| Gate (body intake organ) | size per person; wears with overuse and potion doping; can lose function | water healing repairs over days; a lost gate closes self-casting | it is a physical organ |
| Od | fixed life energy; spending it shortens life | can be crystallized into a permanent core | it is the life itself |
| Spirit contracts | spirit mage draws air mana through a spirit; individual terms | spirit strength; one spirit can monopolize a contractor | the spirit sets the terms and a soul judges breach |
| Blessings | innate traits, sometimes unknown to the holder | birth, bloodline, species, crafted, succession | world law grants them |
| Witch Factor / Authority | custody differs from use; a Factor selects a bearer | seats conferred; forced holding at body cost | compatibility |

Day one for an ordinary character: chores, a trade, basic script literacy, no magic until a gate
test. The merchant Otto is the source's proof that preparation and bargaining change outcomes.
Proposed start kit: one trade, hidden gate stat, rare hidden blessing roll (rarity is a dial),
no Factor.

## 2. Growth (K3, K19-K34 lived parts)

Practice without rewind gains; magic by gate, affinity and a teacher (decades for a strong
caster); spirit contracts by pledge and maturity; blessings fixed at birth or succession;
Factors by selection or conferral; the Sanctuary Trials as a once-per-night accepted-choice
gate that damages on repeat failure; race forms with permanent loss (a broken horn); rank by
exploit and public recognition. Curve breakers arrive as events: an Archbishop, the whale's
erasing mist, a name eaten.

## 3. Living beside the protagonist (K4, K12)

Timed canon events a non-protagonist life can meet: Royal Selection opening; the mansion and
village curse nights; the village Cult attack; the White Whale fight; the forest Cult camps;
the convoy ambush; the Sanctuary Trials and the Hare; the mansion assassination night; later
arcs per round 1.

Bystander rules: the rewind resets the bystander's world and memory; in the source the
looper is stopped from telling them (the penalty lands on the teller and can kill a listener); a bystander who names it
themselves triggers nothing; foresight and a Witch scent leak as clues.

Starts (proposal): ordinary farmer's child or temporary maid in Mathers territory (best fit);
reincarnated child with decaying foreknowledge; Sanctuary half-blood resident; Cult agent
holding a Gospel; second looper (heavy, needs its own anchor and memory ledger, RZ-S1).

## 4. Society, economy, crafting (K7, K8, K9)

Employers: the Margrave's household, five candidate camps (a duchy, a trading company with
beast-man mercenaries, a widow's house, a slum-born candidate, the half-elf), the capital
Merchants' Guild. Copper, silver and gold coins (nine copper to a silver). Trade in oil, metal
goods, magic crystals and talk mirrors. Crafts: light and anti-magic crystals, barrier
crystals, crystal clocks and heaters. Dragon carriages for travel. Three scripts. Council of
Elders rules during the Selection. Demi-human prejudice is social, with no statute shown.

## 5. Cross-world (K15)

Gate-linked mind attacks reach any intake organ; Od spending is life spending; contract breach
is judged by belief and wording; name and memory eating erases record but not skill; a scent
mark is readable by aura senses; two loop owners keep separate ledgers. Full table in the
private round 2 report, section 5.
