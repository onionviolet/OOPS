## 1. Power as engine state

| Stock | Default | Dial | In-world reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elixir bond | one true Elixir, one power matched to the drinker | rare stable doubles; a second usually makes a Psycho | Elixirs rewrite genes |
| Color and Flux | seven colors plus Black; powers draw from color dimensions | blends; batteries store Flux | powers are links to a source |
| Knockoff | template copy, weaker, risky; tens of thousands of euros | branded lines cost more | no source link |
| Time-stop (protagonist) | about 10 s, cooldown equal to length | armor extends; anti-power zones block | power design |
| Save (protagonist) | one save, new erases old, set by a held stop | reload only on real death | power design |
| Memory carry | the looper alone | consented helmet transfer through an unbroken chain | Genius tech |

Day one for an ordinary person: a job (Dynamis clerk, Colosseum vendor, Rust Town factory hand,
casino staff), no Genome, protection fees, a knockoff as an aspiration priced above a year's guard
wage. The text gives ordinary people little page time.

## 2. Growth

Elixirs are the only power route; true Elixirs are no longer made. Knockoffs are attainable with
Psycho risk. Unpowered growth is skill, tech and employment; hero firm tiers and contracts.

## 3. Living beside the protagonist

Present loops run from May 8 2020 in New Rome. Timed beats: a bar attack, the harbor run, the
orphanage raid, the crime-family party, the Ischia lab assault, the Junkyard bunker, a Dynamis lab
raid, Antarctica, the final battle. A bystander's life resets with each loop; memory carries only by
a consented transfer.

## 4. Society, economy, crafting

Dynamis (corporate state, media, hero brand, knockoff labs), the Augusti crime family, the
Meta-Gang, the Carnival, Monaco's anti-power pocket. Prices and wages stay dated (guard about 3,000 a
month; knockoffs 50k-100k euros). Genius tech and legacy robots as crafting.

## 5. Cross-world

A symmetric-cooldown time-stop is a portable cost template; source-cutting dispels and Black Flux
interact with any mana-like resource; consented memory shipping meets soul-memory worlds; three loop
shapes (Re:Zero, Perfect Run, Mother of Learning) stay separate fields.
