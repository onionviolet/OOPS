## Why this source matters

It is YAIRPG's own shape seen from inside one ordinary person: summoned to a different world per job,
returned home with rewards, with shared transit rules across worlds and each world's own physics on top.

## 1. Entry, stay, return, carryover

- **Entry:** selection near gathered mana, screened by mental state; usually no warning (a headache
  before link summons); compliance forced, limited or full per summons; escape tools cost something.
- **Stay:** ordinary summons need no food or sleep, contracts do; a death ends the summons with a rating,
  and the body is repaired in a home space. Deaths at home are real.
- **Return:** a gauge fills across a series; home is nearly the same time and place; later a chosen
  return location.
- **Three clocks:** Earth away-time (a minute to under an hour), world time (minutes to months; worlds run
  on their own clocks, linked worlds track home more closely), and ordinary days between jobs.
- **Carryover by kind:** System-held growth always carries; coins and gems carry if withdrawn; figurines
  carry, scrolls fail; mana carries only if shielded; minions stay behind; skills learned on a job carry
  once committed in the home space; contract penalties carry.

## 2. Fit to YAIRPG rules

Matches AD10 (one continuing self with a carrier), AD11 (carryover by world and means, every crossing
needs a carrier), AD12 (reciprocal return near the same moment), AD13 (home deaths real) and AD14
(bindings carry). Adds a "summoned" entry route with compliance level and escape tools, a between-worlds
commit room, and a series gauge.

## 3. Power, growth, society

Stats, mana, tier and rank, a summoner class that evolves, figurines as stored summons, summoning points
spent in a market city outside any world. On Earth: a refuge group that houses and taxes the summoned,
government deals, cults that hunt summoned beings, dungeons behind ordinary doors, and mana slowly
integrating into Earth.
