# Scenario worlds and cheats

Design proposal, November 18, 2026. Companion to [Multiverse structures](MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES.md), [Cross-world rules](../CROSS_WORLD_RULES.md), and [Cheat rules](../CHEAT_RULES.md). Fits the existing engine-first build order.

## D1. A scenario is an episode in a world

Put a premise, entry terms and one or more resolution conditions around an episode of ordinary play. The player can enter a living world, a constructed trial, a dream, or a possible future. A short exam, a season stranded abroad, and a years-long second life can share the same outline. The episode can end while the place continues. Entry terms describe the role and body, what comes in, whose clock runs, who can end the visit, and what can travel back. Surface the stated terms and give unknowns a source. A patron can be wrong about its own trial.

Compare three starting designs. Use **a bounded episode in a persistent world** first because OOPS already has a candidate. A **repeatable constructed trial** supports practice, fresh variants, and deliberately repeatable payouts where someone funds them. A **possible life** can play out from a fork, return one lived insight or a future warning, and leave the player deciding which parts of that future to bring about. Its reach and accuracy come from the power. Each belongs on the same palette. The last two exercise different persistence and carry rules.

## D2. Give the player a result they can use

Read what happened from authoritative facts and causal receipts. For each result, answer three things.

| Part | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Local outcome | The lower pier burned. Hank survived because you got him to shelter before the fire reached him. Repair work is available if you come back. |
| What you brought back | You retain practiced rigging and a witness's account. The borrowed winch stayed at the pier. |
| What someone granted | The desk paid for the verified report. It offers rescue work after hearing how you got Hank out. |

Illustrative wording and rewards. Populate them only when corresponding native actions, witnesses, transfers and offers exist. The report can close with an unfinished goal, a partial result, expiry or withdrawal. Extraction can happen before the desk hears about it. A sponsor can pay for the pier and omit the rescue. The player still has their experience, and Hank still has his relationship. A grade belongs to an evaluator with criteria, such as a Paradise clerk or exam board. Let players read the reasons they can know and keep sealed facts behind their existing discovery routes. Put the return account in Reports and make follow-on offers available through their usual surfaces.

Treat ordinary gains as ordinary gameplay. Practiced methods, repairs, spending, introductions and injuries land through their existing owners. On resolution, read the resulting facts and the significant causes. On return, transfer only what the actual crossing and its mechanisms can carry. On settlement, apply any grants or obligations via the institution or power that issues them. A report view reads the receipt. A sponsor can reward a previously unanticipated method when it achieves a recognized result. Give a novel solution credit for what it actually changed.

## D3. Let cheats change a lever in the episode

| Cheat | What it changes | What play it opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extra time | The entrant's available local interval, or the relation between home and local clocks | Train after meeting the minimum, or spend a season building a local life instead of taking the first ride out. |
| Rehearsal | Access to a constructed copy, with memory or insight carried by the power | Test a route, learn where a plan stalls, and decide how much to trust the rehearsal when conditions change. |
| Alternate incarnation | The body, role or viewpoint of entry | Live as a different person with their capabilities and history. A mind-carried method might return ready to use or need retraining in the home body. |
| Extraction or inheritance | What the entrant can bring home and by which carrier | Bring a real tool, take a consenting passenger, or turn a local power into something the home body can use, where the cheat has those means. |
| Appraisal or settlement manipulation | What the player can read about an evaluator, or what a power can actually change in its rules | Find a sponsor with different criteria, show evidence the current one missed, or use a privilege to change the terms. Local history follows what happened. |

These can come from a start, world law, a patron, an earned power or an item. They belong on the power and crossing seams and follow their backing. The quiet base cheat and a visible task system are distinct in OOPS. If a time power plausibly gives a whole extra lifetime, honor that scale with scheduled routines, milestones, and intervention at consequential moments. No weakness is owed. An exclusion from a crossing needs a reason in the power's reach, contract or world law.

## D4. Build from the Goa contract, then vary the mechanism

1. **Enrich one existing settlement.** The [Paradise host](../../src/book_world_paradise_host.js) already handles accept, report or withdraw, extraction, actual return and a desk payout. Its [state validator](../../src/book_world_paradise_state.js) already checks the order. Preserve its current financial terms on the first pass. Prove two legal routes with a materially different local outcome and a result that explains the difference, including a withdrawal or missed main goal with a surviving consequence. Link actions to a return report using the facts and receipts already written by their owners. The [readiness review](world-core-kit-2026-10-14/W1_READINESS.md#reincarnation-paradise) describes the slice as a retained independent alternate branch.
2. **Make a different second consumer.** Use an original short scenario with a time-granting cheat, real practice and no required desk. Try a floodplain ferry town where the same forecast can lead to evacuation, a repaired crossing, or a trade that saves one district at another's expense. The extra interval can make a fourth route physically possible. Compare runs with and without the power from the same starting conditions. Use the [activity rules](../ACTIVITY_ABSTRACTION_RULES.md) to batch repairs or training while preserving supplies, deadlines and interventions.
3. **Extract the common pieces after the second consumer.** Candidate data are a definition revision, episode ID, actual instance ID, return endpoint, relevant clock anchors, phase and resolution evidence. Keep settlement receipts tied to both the specific episode and reward source. Reuse the [world-instance records](../../src/world_instances.js) and [traveler projection](../../src/native_realm_traveler.js) where their native integration supports the transition. The world-instance module is a detached record/planning seam. The [capability-run host](../../src/capability_runs_domain.js) demonstrates a persisted detached engine exercise, which is useful for a rehearsal proof. A live visit needs authoritative traveler state, return and carry. Branches and loops should consult the [rewind machinery](../../src/rewind_kit.js) when they actually move history.

## D5. Account for the seams that change the player's life

- **Clocks.** Start with the existing off-world freeze default and make a different ratio or home advance explicit on the edge. Distinguish time lived, contract deadlines, home elapsed time and the away bank. Account for each tick once. Stepping, idle progression and reload should land the same result for the same inputs. A deadline that comes due during a batch needs a chance to surface or interrupt at the appropriate point.
- **Revisit and replay.** Revisit a persistent episode's world using its existing instance. A fresh trial can reuse the premise under a new instance. Use the actual holder's identity and originating history for cargo, knowledge and relationships. A practice replica of a relic and the original are separate claims. Intentional repeatable rewards belong to the scenario's terms. Repeated load or retry of a single settlement must resolve to one reward and one receipt.
- **Settlement, branching and recovery.** Persist payout and its cause together. A save during extraction must resume that phase, and failure to apply a transfer must leave a coherent location, body and inventory. If a timeline retains a payout through rewind, resolve the grant and its linked costs as a unit according to the power's actual carry rules. Keeping a reward across a branch can be a genuine power. Verify save, reload, export and import at consequential boundaries, plus a rebuilt desktop browser walk through entry, play, withdrawal or completion, and return. Use a separate developer fixture or inspector to reproduce the routes through native commands.
- **Scale and incentives.** Draw from existing actors, jobs and world rules. For generated episodes start with combinations of known pressures, roles and deadlines, then check for feasible entry and outcomes. Keep active timelines at the fidelity their mechanics need. Ask what sponsor grades incentivize and how much of a character's growth players can get through ordinary life. An optional cheat can be extraordinary without making the same optimal grind the local answer to every problem.

## D6. Generate opportunities from people, events and means

Start each opportunity with someone who wants something or a change that creates an opening. Combine a pressure, people with actual capabilities and incentives, a window in which they can act, and something an interested party can offer. Use authored rules and initialized state to make each combination possible. Expose it through a notice, a witness or a discovery. Allow player plans to create openings the generator never offered. The existing [quest board](../../src/quest_generator.js) derives leads from needs and tracks their cause. It is a precedent for discovery. A scenario still needs live actions and a world that reacts to them.

An original premise to try: a ferry route is drowning, and the river recognizes whoever holds the ferry charter. Save the passengers, salvage the charter, sell a crossing to a rival town, or spend your extra interval building a replacement route. If the world allows it, moving the charter to a different vessel can change what the river recognizes. If a cheat can rehearse several futures, the player could find a route the ferry owner never imagined. Getting the charter is a local event. Owning its authority across worlds depends on the binding and who can honor it. A town could instead owe you safe passage or ask you to run the new route. This one premise can produce a livelihood, a political claim, an evacuation, or a later expedition.

## D7. Calculate settlement in layers

| Layer | Decision | Origin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World result | Which people, goods, claims, methods and conditions changed, and which known causes explain the change? | Actual play through the native owners. Record enough to explain consequential outcomes and avoid paying off the same fact twice. |
| Carry | Which changes come through the actual route, body or power? | Existing traveler, body, item, knowledge and power rules. Apply costs and ownership changes with the transfer. |
| Evaluator | What was agreed, what evidence can the issuer verify, and does it change the payout or standing? | Terms and authority of each institution or power. The same outcome can get different answers from the desk and the people affected. |
| Grant | What can the issuer actually provide or authorize, and when? | Reserved funds, real stock, training, access, patron favor or world law. Pay through the existing owner and record the confirmed grant. |

Start with a deterministic, authored settlement for each issuer. The current Goa desk reserves four coins, pays four for its complete survey, one for qualifying partial work, and zero for withdrawal or breach, with a separate extraction and arrival. Preserve that deal in a first proof. A rescue the desk does not pay for can still leave a person alive and change what they offer. Resolve creative methods by whether their results satisfy the terms. A ferry agreement to deliver people to safety can recognize a bridge when the bridge actually gets them there. A different agreement can explicitly buy a working ferry.

Take the **breakdown and explainable rank** from *Reincarnation Paradise*. Its World Source, grade, money, attributes and rank have meaning in that world's own rules. *Terror Infinity* is the contrasting fixture: individual credits, mission rewards, ranked rewards and repair bills can all show up after one team return. Retain each contributor's claim and distinguish shared completion, individual achievements, shared spending and competing claims on a unique thing. Neither fixture requires combat contribution to be the sole rule, and a contract can credit a team member who kept others alive.

Add procedural **offers** once the authored passes work. Derive candidates from the issuer's capacity, what was achieved, and existing relationships or claims. A saved keyed draw can vary offers of equal eligibility where the issuer or world uses chance. Stable inputs include episode, issuer, recipient and grant identity. Show the source and reason. Example: a guild that lacks money can offer berth access or a paid future commission if it actually controls those things. A player who exceeds terms can get an unsolicited invitation from someone who heard about it. Keep guaranteed compensation, cause-backed offers, and intentional chance draws visible in the breakdown. Big rewards can be unique items, titles or new world access where the mechanisms support them.

Acceptance for a later procedural pass: a settlement for the same facts and terms previews identically before and after reload, two contributors can earn different receipts from one shift, and a shared claim or a unique asset has a clear holder. If the evaluator hears new evidence later, use a separate decision or appeal. A successful settlement and its grant commit together. Prove an unusual solution, a missed goal with something real to carry, and an unavailable offer with a legible cause. Test whether players would actually choose different routes once the terms are known.

## D9. Generate situations and resolve actions through ordinary rules

**Unit of authoring: a pressure with something at stake.** Author the bindings and the next few changes the world can make. A pressure definition specifies which existing facts qualify, who is present or can learn about it, what changes with time, what native mechanisms can affect those facts, and how to tell that the pressure ended. Assign one writer to each fact. Use a deadline as a decision point for the actor or environment that owns it. A notice does not start the flood, and dismissing it does not stop the flood.

| Step | First ferry pass | Existing seam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Produce | A rising-river episode threatens a crossing with passengers and limited capacity. The pressure exists even if the lead works elsewhere. | Native local clock and state. [Pressure candidates](../../src/situation_candidates.js) can turn a live, owner-supplied reading into a situation proposal with a stable episode ID. |
| Surface | A warning comes from a visible water mark, a witness or the ferry office. The director can prioritize when the notice reaches the feed. | [Event knowledge and placements](../../src/world_events.js), [pacing director](../../src/story_director.js), [story rules](../STORY_RULES.md). |
| Act | Move people, repair, barter, transfer custody or use an applicable power through their normal commands. Quote what is knowable and recheck current means on commit. | Existing movement, crafting, ties, items and power effects. [Situation state](../../src/causal_situations.js) records episode phases and evidence. |
| React | Recheck the threatened crossing and people's options after relevant changes or the next local deadline. An informed owner can respond to a missing charter through the route they can actually use. | Event-specific bindings over existing owners. Begin with bounded actor responses. Consider broader scheduling once this route needs it. |
| Resolve | Count who arrived, what operates, who holds the charter, and what claims remain. Apply the actual contract to those results. | Scenario report and settlement in D2 and D7. |

**Start with a small palette:** a scheduled event, a pressure threshold, and a person acting on a need or something they learned. A follow-up event may be the same kind triggered by a new fact, such as a guild hearing that the bridge is open. Initially hand-bind one region with these rules. Then generate valid sites, participants and pressures from known local facts. Save causal identity and selected variants that affect play. Refresh quotes, coalesce repeated readings of the same episode and only wake affected rules at meaningful changes or scheduled boundaries. Ambient material can represent off-screen life at a lower fidelity, with promotions to concrete people and goods where play starts to depend on them.

**Design for a few ways to change facts and many ways to combine them.** If an existing ability changes the river, boat or charter within its reach, the event reads the resulting state automatically. If the player wants to abandon the contract, transfer their duties to someone who agrees, or arrive after the deadline, those are ordinary changes with different receipts. A player who destroys a charter might lose that route and leave a vacancy someone can fill through the world's rules. Saving everyone by teleportation succeeds when the power actually gets them to safety. An official can still require evidence, and a contract specifically for a working ferry can remain open. Where a physical or social mechanism is unmodeled, show the gap or offer a closely related action whose result we can compute. An optional future intent parser could propose a plan using supported verbs and preview it without writing world truth. New verbs are added where a held-out case exposes a missing mechanism players would use elsewhere.

**Proof before a bigger generator:** save one fixture at the warning and use native commands for direct success, diversion through a second mechanism, and a surprising sequence such as transferring the charter while a repair is queued. Run an abstention control, an early exit, and a deadline collision. Check actor knowledge independently from what happened, no double delivery of the same person or charter, no recurring payout from reloading, and stable outcomes for equal inputs across normal ticks and batch stepping. Use the [procedural core's emergence work](../PROCEDURAL_CORE.md#emergent-behavior-pc34-pc43-2026-11-12) and existing seeded harness to find new action sequences after the first authored case works. Only promote a sequence to a regression fixture when it exercises a distinct rule interaction. Later content can add templates without copying a complete branch graph.

Source discovery and reader assignments live in the [scenario source shelves and batches](../research/SCENARIO_SOURCE_SHELVES_AND_BATCHES_2026-11-19.md).

## D10. Grade an agreement when the grade changes something

**Proposal, November 20, 2026.** A scenario can feel like a dungeon: enter under known terms, make consequential choices, reach a checkpoint, leave with gains, and decide whether to return. Recommend the D7 breakdown as the default return account, with an evaluator's grade where it determines payment, qualification, access or a later offer. A grade should answer “how did this authority assess this undertaking?” Ordinary learning, survival, relationships and possession already have their own results. A universal S-to-F rank would hide those differences without yet serving a demonstrated consumer. AD38, AD49, AD51 and AD56 remain the direction; this section proposes local defaults and adds no runtime behavior.

### D10.1. Three useful shapes, with actual dungeon comparisons

| Option | When it helps | Cost and proposed use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| O1. Contract verdict with a breakdown | A desk buys an account, a guild certifies a rescue, or an examiner awards a qualification. | Best first pass for Goa. Use complete, qualifying partial, withdrawn or breach plus reasons. Different issuers can value the same expedition differently. |
| O2. Comparable challenge score | A constructed dungeon or trial offers repeat attempts under a declared ruleset, with score buying something the issuer controls. | Keep the definition revision, difficulty, clock and solo/team conditions with the record. Useful for a challenge world; a costly detour to rescue someone may lower the timed score while producing a better local outcome. |
| O3. No final grade, followed by claims and opportunities | A living town has competing needs and nobody can fairly summarize the whole episode. The unusual version is a return hearing where several witnesses or creditors bring incompatible claims. | Best second pass for the ferry. Display outcomes and who owes what. A hearing can change recognized claims or open an appeal; it does not rewrite who survived. It needs observers and agreements rather than an omniscient score. |

**Verified comparison.** Square Enix's *Palace of the Dead* guide separates dungeon levels from outside levels, retained aetherpool strength from resettable progress, progress rewards from appraisable treasure, and solo/party challenge scores above floor 100. Pomanders stay inside. Its weapon exchange consumes some aetherpool strength. This is a useful example of several gains with different exit rules within one dungeon. It does not establish a general grading formula for OOPS. [Official guide, growth, save data, rewards and scores](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/playguide/contentsguide/deepdungeon/).

**Historical comparison.** Blizzard's *Chains of Domination* introduction describes Mythic+ rating using key level, affixes and time relative to the timer, with personal records, seasonal resets and key upgrades. That is a comparable challenge instrument with a particular purpose and ruleset, rather than a judgment of every consequence of the adventure. This is the 2021 design, not a claim about current tuning. [Official introduction](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23686103/learn-about-the-mythic-dungeon-rating-in-chains-of-domination).

**Design inference:** borrow the dungeon's explicit attempt, checkpoint, exit and reward identities where they help. Keep score, actual cargo and ordinary growth distinct. OOPS already has first-clear, repeat and seeded clear-bonus rewards in [the location-clear owner](../../src/main.js); that simpler route suffices for an ordinary combat-site reward. A civilian survey needs the agreement and issuer seams instead. Reject O2 as the default for all lives; retain it for worlds that actually run comparable trials. O3 remains useful even when there is no cash reward.

### D10.2. Assess facts, recognized proof and entitlement separately

| Question | Proposed representation and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| What happened? | Read authoritative owner facts and causal receipts at the relevant resolution boundary: people delivered, route operating, goods held, work performed, deadline reached. A report action does not make its claim true. |
| What can this evaluator know? | Record the evidence received, its source, scope and disputed status. An ordinary clerk may withhold recognition pending a witness; an established world-system power can observe within its actual reach. A forged report could persuade a fallible clerk if deception is modeled, while the underlying world result stays different. |
| What counts under these terms? | Use revisioned criteria and explicit qualifiers: objective state, eligible recipient or team, deadline clock, required proof, and any declared method or collateral condition. A rescue contract can accept a bridge, hired pilot or teleportation when it delivers the people. A contract buying a working ferry can remain unfinished after that rescue. |
| What can be granted? | Read the issuer's reserved currency, stock, teaching capacity, authority or supported power. Separate an assessed entitlement, an unpaid claim and a confirmed transfer. A grade does not create solvency or title to someone else's cargo. |

Prefer a small vector of applicable criteria over a weighted total: **objective / proof / obligations / return / entitlement**. Each criterion can be satisfied, partial, failed, unresolved or inapplicable, with a reason and evidence pointer. These are illustrative fields, not mandatory new save structures. Avoid treating “unknown” as zero or letting many minor points cancel a failed requirement. A local trial may deliberately use `score = sum(weight_i * result_i)` once it has declared units, weights, thresholds and any qualifying gates. Fix those with the attempt's terms; explain the contributions. Neither the checked [knowledge bank S05](../research/KNOWLEDGE_BANK.md#source-cases) nor the dungeon examples supplies a universal conversion from good deeds to attributes.

Creative methods are assessed through the effect they actually cause. Give ally help its own causal receipt and costs; no automatic downgrade for help or a cheat. An examiner can explicitly test unaided work when that is the actual qualification, while an evacuation buyer can pay the team for delivering passengers. Shared success, each participant's agreed share, individual side awards and unique-item custody are separate claims. Do not infer an individual's share from damage, last hit or proximity. A saved person can owe gratitude to several helpers without multiplying a finite cash pool.

Hidden evidence stays hidden through the existing knowledge route. The visible reason may be “payment pending confirmation” without naming a concealed witness or event. A later witness can support a linked appeal or supplemental award; keep the earlier decision and transfer in history. The first Goa implementation need not add deception or a hearing: its current authored fact reads are an explicit limited evaluator. Richer observation is a later consumer, not implied omniscience for every issuer.

### D10.3. Two worked settlements

**Goa, existing branch, existing money.** [The host](../../src/book_world_paradise_host.js) reserves four employer coins on acceptance. Its report records complete when both `book.goa.arcClosed` and `book.goa.lifeDone` hold, otherwise partial unless withdrawn or already in breach. [The actions owner](../../src/book_world_paradise_actions.js) closes the civil account after the relevant local reports and independent work. Extraction takes two local minutes; actual hub arrival and settlement are separate commands. [The state owner](../../src/book_world_paradise_state.js) sets breach when local residence exceeds the committed deadline, including during extraction. The nominal deadline is 90 local minutes from arrival; a report at minute 89 does not guarantee an on-time return.

| Native route | Desk verdict and payment | What the proposed report should distinguish |

Preserve 4/1/0 exactly in this first pass. The host's pre-arrival withdrawal instead immediately restores the reserve and clears the mission; the player can take the contract again while unspent, without a payout. After a settled contract its retained history prevents a fresh funding cycle. Personal reentry keeps the same Goa world and does not replenish this contract. These are current branch semantics, not a universal repeat limit. Do not attach protagonist World Source or attribute awards to this civilian agreement. A known rescue can appear in the report without inventing a new reward or relationship mutation unsupported by its owner.

**Ferry, original proposed fixture.** Six named passengers need to reach the high bank before the river deadline. The town buys delivery, not a particular vessel: reserve six existing coins for the crew, one per distinct named passenger delivered alive by the cutoff. Let `D` be that set and `N` the subset recognized by the town from admissible evidence. Then `earned = |N|`, with `N ⊆ D` in this first fixture; unconfirmed deliveries in `D minus N` remain pending. A later fraud-capable evaluator could mistakenly recognize a false delivery while the actual delivery set stays unchanged. Allocate the confirmed pool by an agreement made by the player and two helpers before accepting. For an illustrative equal-share agreement, six confirmed deliveries pay two coins each. Four pay one each plus a remainder held for their agreed allocation rule, rather than silent rounding or duplicating the remainder. Itemize any separately agreed costs by payer.

From the same warning fixture, ordinary boat runs deliver four before the deadline. An extra personal interval lets the player repair a bridge while the ordinary river clock is paused by that specific power, then all six cross. Both earn the corresponding deliveries regardless of method. A different cheat that merely grants more training days before entry does not pause the flood. Record subjective practice time, river deadline and extraction clock separately; the better method or retained skill comes from real practice, not a score multiplier. This numerical example is original design, not the asserted formula of *48 Hours a Day*; [KB3/S04](../research/KNOWLEDGE_BANK.md#source-cases) establishes the useful distinction between objective completion, continuing life and later return while leaving its general clock formula open.

The repaired crossing remains local. Carry a rope or charter through an actual supported route with its real holder; transporting paper does not establish that another world's river recognizes its authority. The town might offer future berth access if it controls that berth, independently of the six-coin contract. If the town has no reserve, surface a debt or an explicitly agreed alternative instead of calling it paid. If nobody hired the crew, saving six still changes the world and can lead to offers without a formal grade. This exercises O3 and materially differs from Goa's returned survey contract.

### D10.4. Repeat, return and settlement identity

Tie an assessment to the episode, actual world instance, terms revision, evaluator, recipient and qualifying event. Tie each grant to its particular entitlement. A persistent revisit retains delivered people and paid claims. A fresh constructed attempt can fund new rewards where its sponsor's terms and means support them; a practice score need not carry spendable loot. Reentry into an unfinished episode resumes its clock and remaining commitments. Withdrawal ends the agreement according to its terms, while injuries, learning, cargo and changes already made remain with their native owners.

For the proposed settlement path, preview reads the same facts without transfer. Commit rechecks the current eligible state, evidence and capacity, then writes the decision, reserve release or debit, recipient credit or item transfer, and durable grant identity together. Repeating that command returns the existing receipt. A failed commit leaves the obligation or reserve coherent. New evidence gets a new linked adjudication and any additional delta grant, not another copy of the original payout. Exactly-once settlement protects one entitlement; it does not forbid intentionally recurring work or a power that carries a paid reward across a history change. A rewind must carry its linked grant and cost under that power's rules, as D5 already requires.

## D11. A scenario system can exist independently of the player's cheat

**Proposal, November 20, 2026.** Give a scenario an independent judge when an institution, patron or active world law has a reason to assess it. A cross-world system is one kind of judge. It can recruit participants, issue terms, observe conditions and authorize settlement within its actual reach. Other participants can receive its notices and owe its terms. An unpowered entrant can complete the same agreement. The player's cheat can affect how they act, what evidence they can learn or what they can carry. Give it power over a judge only when a particular cheat has an established means to affect that judge or its rules.

| Design | What it observes and can do | First use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Local authority.** A clerk, guild or exam board. | Receives testimony, documents and inspections through its own routes. It can pay from its reserve and grant standing, training or access it controls. | Goa's desk reads the existing survey facts and settles on actual return. The town can use passenger arrivals as evidence for its own agreement. |
| **Cross-world system.** A continuing service or power with admission terms and a defined observation channel. | Can mark entrants or a place, announce assignments, receive scoped facts and issue awards. It can transfer a participant, restore a body or impose a penalty where it has the means and terms to do so. Local people keep their decisions and their own claims. | A later repeatable trial can recognize outcomes in its marked domain. Outside it, evidence comes through whatever witnesses or records the system can actually receive. |
| **Impersonal rule.** A threshold or charter recognizes a particular event. | A seal attests a crossing or permits passage. It can register a result without knowing motives, ranking the person or holding a purse. A separate sponsor can use that proof. | A ferry charter might verify arrivals at its crossing, while a boat or bridge beyond that reach needs other evidence. |

Keep four roles explicit even when one system fills several. The **issuer** states and commits the terms. The **judge** reads admissible evidence against those terms. The **executor** owns the actual transfer, fine, reward or crossing. The **interface** tells each participant what they can know. A voice can relay a verdict from a desk, and the same desk can ask a separate power to extract someone. A reply or a grade records a decision. A crossing or reward takes effect through its actual owner.

*Terror Infinity* provides the strongest contrast between instructions, the run and its account. The opening briefing describes a scope around a named person and a penalty for overheard disclosure. The source cases establish the briefing claims and the visible marker, plus an observed side award and separate charges. The exact triggers still need source checks. *Reincarnation Paradise* demonstrates a return that reports multiple measures and grants. *48 Hours a Day* shows why a visible system can track assignments, achievements and extraction while available time changes what one entrant can do. Its checked windows establish those effects and leave the exact power-to-clock formula open. [S03–S05, S10–S13 and S16](../research/KNOWLEDGE_BANK.md#source-cases).

**First implementation target, if selected.** In Goa, make the desk's existing verdict explain the qualifying work, closure, deadline, actual arrival and 4/1/0 payout. Build that receipt from committed terms and saved evidence. It should appear in Reports and survive save, reload, export and import with the original grant identity. Reuse the existing [Goa settlement](../../src/book_world_paradise_host.js) and [contract state](../../src/book_world_paradise_state.js). Use the [authority witness seam](../../src/authority_witness.js) if a later judge needs evidence from human observers.

**Contrast to prove later.** Two entrants accept the same rescue under a town's terms. One has extra time and can prepare a bridge. The other gets help from a boat crew. If six distinct passengers arrive on time and the town receives equivalent proof, its verdict is the same for both. If the authority can see only four arrivals, the report can show confirmed payment for four and the remaining claim pending. A system with jurisdiction over all six might certify the additional two. It can do so without controlling the entrant's cheat, paying from the town's purse, or declaring what happened outside its reach. Waive the desk entirely for a visit with no sponsor or binding trial.

Keep the first judge specific to its contract. Compare it with a materially different observer before extracting a common evaluator. The first report needs a named authority, known terms, visible reasons and a confirmed transfer. It has no dependency on a global mission queue, a shared morality score or a language model deciding who earned what.

## D12. External actors take their own actions

**Proposal, November 20, 2026.** Use existing actor commands for a person or organization that can act independently. A controller picks an eligible command from the actor's options. A playable role can let the player pick a command from the options visible to that same actor. Both paths should use the same checks, costs and effects. A world law such as a seal can react through its owning rule without a controller or a playable role.

Goa has an example already. Mia spends her own effort producing a duty account, the king can respond to a warning, and Su Xiao has a separate sequence of preparation and attempts. Their controllers have named actions and wake times. The simulation checks their own context and calls the normal command path. The desk contract is a separate owner that currently settles on the entrant's command. Making the desk proactive would need its own supported action and trigger. [Goa actors](../../src/book_world_paradise_actions.js), [simulation controller loop](../../src/simulation.js), [desk settlement](../../src/book_world_paradise_host.js).

| What the external participant needs | Ferry implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Identity, knowledge and actual means | The skipper has a location, boat access, capacity, and a reason to assist. The inspector sees a charter claim only through an observation or a received account. The town has a real reserve and a defined agreement. |
| One reason to wake up | A passenger asks for help, water hits a threshold, the inspector receives testimony, or a settlement comes due. Use scheduled boundaries and known changes. An inactive world follows its established freeze or time rules. |
| A choice with consequences | The skipper can accept, refuse, wait, or run a crossing if conditions allow. A crossing reserves capacity, advances time and records exactly who arrived. The inspector can verify, leave pending, or act within their jurisdiction. |
| Evidence for other roles | Record who acted, the material outcome and who could know. The town can count the skipper's confirmed deliveries. The same receipt can support a later dispute without duplicating the delivery or its payment. |

**Build three levels of participation.**

1. **Autonomous actor first.** Give a selected participant enough rules to act or abstain without the entrant. In the first proof, compare a direct rescue, an ally-led rescue and the entrant walking away. Confirm the controller cannot spend the entrant's supplies, see their hidden knowledge or move passengers twice. Check equivalent ticks and save/reload at the intervention boundary.
2. **A short playable role for a useful second perspective.** A skipper's shift can test passenger selection, timing and reporting from the person who actually moves them. A desk shift can test reserve management and disputed proof. Start from a separate copy of the same episode state, use the actor's own knowledge and rights, and run the same command a controller can choose. Commit one causal outcome in each run. This is a capability run in the spirit of the [lead-life guide](../LEAD_LIFE_GUIDE.md#capability-runs-2026-11-12), with a bounded job and a stopping point.
3. **Full lead lives where the perspective earns one.** Su Xiao's life can eventually qualify the participant side through actual story windows. A sponsor or rival can earn a full campaign when their decisions, responsibilities and progression sustain it. Keep shared verbs available to compatible actors and player lives. Select playable starts by the world's permissions, source evidence and the depth of play they support. Existing lead order and the scope of other seats stay with [the project's cast-life direction](../USER_VISION.md#cast-lives-build-everything-the-leads-life-needs-choose-later-for-other-seats-2026-10-28).

**First add a second perspective that proves something.** Use the skipper if the open question is whether an ally can carry out a rescue. Use the clerk if it is whether an authority can distinguish a credible account from a claim awaiting proof. A rule-driven System can remain an observable authority. A separate playable steward could have interesting work if decisions about scarce funds, competing teams or jurisdiction become part of the game.

## Source lessons

- *48 Hours a Day* makes extra subjective time itself a reward multiplier. In the island arc, the longer stay gives Zhang Heng room to build, cultivate and practice. Accomplishments and skill progression can coexist. The transferable design idea is time to do things a character values apart from the assigned task. [Novel chapter 12](https://www.webnovel.com/ru/book/15722400505743705/42571075451678841), [novel chapter 15](https://www.webnovel.com/zh/book/15722400505743705/42571150596822233).
- *Reincarnation Paradise* shows a return assessment that uses completed missions and World Source, then grants attribute points, currency and rank advancement in the first settlement. Another entry discloses an option to leave after enough required missions or stay for a stronger assessment. Use this shape when a sponsor actually runs the loop. [Chapter 22](https://www.webnovel.com/tr/book/7815685203305503/21217950513910855), [next world's mission terms](https://www.webnovel.com/zh/book/7815685203305503/21356614875826236).
- *Terror Infinity* uses points and ranked rewards, including rewards from side quests. Later, a team pools resources to bring people out. That suggests a hub economy where returns can be spent on obligations and people as well as power. [Early rewards](https://spa-prod.wuxiaworld.com/novel/terror-infinity/ti-vol-1-chapter-4-1), [team extraction](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/terror-infinity/ti-vol-20-chapter-1-02).
- *Into the Breach* gives defeat a continuation by sending help to another timeline. The OOPS analogue is a consequential failure with a specific carry mechanism. [Developer's game description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/590380/Into_The_Breach/).
