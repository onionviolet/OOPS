## Reading the evidence and the dial

**IL1: Identity is not one resource.** Distinguish a soul, a mind with memories, a body, an executive controller, a record-derived representation and a social/legal identity. A second body can share one mind; a copied memory can begin an independent person; a remnant can carry power without carrying the deceased’s whole mind. Every operation below concerns its named carrier. Unknown carriers remain unknown.

**IL2: Evidence strength.** SHOWN means the inspected local dossier reports an event or explicit narration with a scene locator, not that this lane reread the raw book. STATED names the speaker, archived wiki or project authoring contract. INFERRED marks analysis or a proposed design. MEMORY would mean recall outside the local material; none is used to establish a law here. UNKNOWN means the inspected evidence does not decide it. A wiki’s statement remains secondary STATED evidence even when an earlier audit called it CANON. Dossier compilation authentication, incomplete series coverage and conflicting continuities remain qualified. A synopsis is a lead, not a checked mechanic. Every UNKNOWN row cites its evidence boundary rather than pretending to cite an absent law.

**IL3: The round-8 dial governs the offered experience, not source truth.** None excludes another version of the same person in this run; it does not delete independent earlier characters (OS1–OS6), forbid other NPCs having souls, or establish a universal uniqueness theorem. Implied keeps counterparts as lore, memories or demonstrations. Present allows meeting them. Playable proposes controlling/switching/merging a supported self, and is not a claim of runtime support. A strong source mechanism can have a None ordinary start without rewriting the source to make that mechanism impossible. Player preference filters starts/experiences; a deliberately different world law needs an explicit divergent/AU premise. NS3’s “copies are new people” cannot be silently applied to a source’s shared-soul autonomous copies.

Read alongside [intro rounds6–8](../INTRO_FLOW_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md#round-6-start-audit-villain-starts-other-selves-more-links-2026-10-14), [selves and echoes](../SELVES_AND_ECHOES.md) and [identity/control O1–O8](PLAYER_CHARACTER_IDENTITY_AND_CONTROL_2026-10-13.md). ## Minimal engine shape for a WC start

**IL29: A world law is a versioned record; a start binds actual entities to it.** This is a schema proposal, not new runtime/save fields. Reuse existing origin, timeline, body/source, actor and knowledge owners instead of making a second identity store. A minimal law definition can have these fields:

| Field | Minimal content | Why the intro needs it |
|---|---|---|
| `lawRef` | World instance/continuity + era/source cutoff + version | WC4/WC5 distinguish original, divergent, AU and later-source rules. |
| `operations` | Per D/C/Sp/Sw/M/P/R/regress/reincarnate: allowed / conditional / forbidden / unknown; affected carrier; concrete method/source requirements; evidence refs | WC2/WC3 admission can distinguish a vessel, memory transfer and an independent copy. Unknown is not treated as false or as an unlocked method. |
| `continuityRules` | Shared / forked / new / unresolved mind and soul relation; retention through departure/death/return; shared-resource link only when real | Prevents multiplying a reserve, moving knowledge without a channel or conflating revival with a copy. |
| `coexistenceRule` | Scope of uniqueness if any; actor matching test; collision outcome; explicit exception reference | Separates person uniqueness from unique items, divine offices or one active network holder. “Unspecified” is valid; it does not call a collapse handler. |
| `recognitionRules` | Observer qualification, channel, target carrier, evidence gained, error/uncertainty and concealment conditions | Soul/face/habit/system are tests, not public engine truth. Ties to knowledge owner and OM5/OM6. |
| `experienceDial` | Proposed world default; start’s required self content and optional controllable-self operations | None/Implied/Present/Playable concerns this start’s experience. World default alone does not prove admission. |
| `evidenceRefs` | Local path + scene/revision locator + SHOWN/STATED/INFERRED/MEMORY/UNKNOWN + continuity limits | A conditional operation cannot be implemented from an unsupported synopsis or a MEMORY lead without labeling its invented policy. |

Each composed WC entry also needs **`identityBinding`**: person/life IDs; body and current occupants/controllers; source or parent-copy/fork/merge event; former occupant outcome (absent/dead/elsewhere/dormant/shared/fused/unknown); and actual knowledge/control channel. Reference these existing entities instead of duplicating their entire records. Ordinary O1 needs only the actor/body association; extra fields appear when the chosen route makes them consequential. A backup with no independent mind remains a vessel record, not a fabricated NPC. A secret binding points to a sealed ID/discovery route, not a spoiler in the intro.

**IL30: Intro qualification sequence, INFERRED proposal.**

1. Resolve WC4/WC5’s law version and compose origin/entry/advantage layers. Use WC2 admission to check required body, source and operation. A position-swap or copied technique cannot satisfy a soul-swap start.
2. Compare the start’s required/optional self experience with the preference dial. Prefer compatible ordinary starts within the world. A dial preference cannot overwrite an unavoidable source fact; reroll the incompatible start or use an explicitly offered divergent premise.
3. Validate occupant outcome, continuity/resource links and any destination counterpart or uniqueness exception. When an operation depends on UNKNOWN, keep that operation/start unqualified until a source check or explicitly original contract supplies its behavior. This is a route-specific hold, not a hold on every ordinary start in that world.
4. Check WC9: first action and lead under the actual control contract. A ghost/object/advisor can qualify through its supported communication or influence. Show known control/transfer commitments and discoverable cues; withhold unknown author truth.
5. Keep private validation and the visible offer separate. Record actual split/merge/entry provenance in each life history (OM7); disclose a selves view only after the character has evidence (OM6). No browser/persistence acceptance is claimed by this research filter.
