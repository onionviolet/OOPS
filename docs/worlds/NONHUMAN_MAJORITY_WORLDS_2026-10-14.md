# Nonhuman-majority worlds, 2026-10-14

Status: research and **INFERRED proposals**, MAJORITY lane for OT1, OT2 and
OT16. No accepted roster, implementation, balance or browser result. Only this
report and the [checkpoint](../gm/dispatch/nonhuman-starts-2026-10-14/majority/CHECKPOINT.md)
are lane-owned.

A nonhuman-majority world makes the ordinary institutions fit its residents.
The interesting inversion is a human negotiating a scent deed, finding a dry
berth in an aquatic city, or working for a spirit household. Nonhuman natives
also need ordinary careers, neighbors and purchases, rather than spending every
scene proving personhood to humans.

## 2. Eight original world proposals

Each is a bounded district in a world that can grow, not a resettable disposable
trial. Following [MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES](MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES.md), cosmology,
represented coverage and persistence are independent. Direct native starts,
human minority starts and later arrivals are all reachable when supplied with
actual means. Default: named independent residents, local paid/barter work,
body-fitting services, no compulsory human transformation. Dial: peaceful,
political, hierarchical, harsh or AU-friendly variants. Reasonably each chosen
variant supplies a useful routine, communication and recovery route.

World-card additions: MW01's swap test is negotiating through ground/scent
institutions; MW02 is route scale and load; MW03 is fixed residence with mobile
collaboration; MW04 is water-first infrastructure; MW05 is channel-qualified
