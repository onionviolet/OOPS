# Jujutsu Kaisen campaign: an ordinary assignment under an impossible rule

**Status: `P` proposal.** This campaign is a source-bounded authoring plan, not a completed adaptation or accepted fiction. See [SYNTHESIS](SYNTHESIS.md), [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md), [module catalogue](MODULE_CATALOGUE.md), and the shared [campaign framework](../CAMPAIGN_FRAMEWORK.md).

It is secondary, sometimes incomplete and internally layered. The first slice proposes one modern-era main-series continuity, with an explicit chosen cutoff still required before implementation. `P` Keep Simurian / Simuria / Modulo material as a separate era and continuity. `P` Canon, divergence, relationship AU, swapped-role AU, and original echoes are distinct definitions; none silently changes a live instance.

## Campaign promise

`P` A player can be a trained sorcerer, support worker, clan-linked entrant, tool specialist or non-sorcerer witness. Their first job is to learn what they can actually see and do, decide whether to accept risk with incomplete evidence, then live with the consequences. Routine assignments, work, lessons, recovery, relationships and institutional duties matter between major fights. A mission can be refused without acquiring a fictional debt.

## Ordinary loop and harsh encounter

`P` Ordinary loop: accept paid window work, inspect source and entry requirements, train or prepare with a queue, investigate a local report, recover, then file evidence and settle costs. A dangerous job shows the known stakes and unknowns before commitment; no guaranteed victory or safety label. If refused, no mission clock or escrow begins. If accepted then abandoned, only terms actually accepted resolve.

`P` Unequal threat: a special-grade curse can exceed the player's current means. The player may still report, evacuate, support a qualified team, use a real compatible counter, or lose people/access if they commit poorly. The threat is not scaled down to the selected profile. Escalation stays tied to a cause, not GM surprise.

## Three worked careers

### New student with a narrow source

`P` The student has one actual method, limited training and school access. They run errands, attend drills, learn one extension, and choose when to join a mission. A correct report can be more useful than an attempted duel. The student does not receive a domain for hitting an XP threshold.

### Tool and barrier practitioner

`P` A noncombat-leaning worker inspects a damaged talisman case, obtains the correct fitting and learns a supervised curtain procedure. In a field assignment, they can identify the barrier condition and create a safe route for a stronger teammate. Their tools and learned barrier skill remain useful if their innate energy output is low.

### Clan-linked negotiator with a contested claim

`P` A player has a recognized family claim but not immediate custody. They can seek a key, persuade a custodian, gather evidence of a will condition or accept a costly alliance. A succession declaration does not transfer contents automatically, and political recognition may lag behind physical access.

## Five escalating arcs

1. **The missing report.** `P` A window's brief omits a witness statement. Find the witness, accept the mission with stated uncertainty, or refuse. Durable result: report confidence and local access change according to observed action.
2. **The classroom boundary.** `P` A training curtain excludes one eligible group under a stated condition. Learn the rule, test it safely, or ask its author. Durable result: knowledge and instructor trust change; no generalized barrier immunity is awarded.
3. **A tool without a technique.** `P` A team needs a real cursed tool repaired and authenticated. Custody, compatibility, repair and knowledge stay separate. Durable result: same specimen returns with preserved owner and provenance, or loss remains.
4. **The disputed heir.** `P` A conditional succession and a dangerous storehouse put permission, key, stock and political recognition in conflict. Durable result: actors hold distinct claims and knowledge; no omniscient court settles them.
5. **Rules in a colony.** `P` An entrant must learn the effective game-wide rule set, decide whether to spend points on a permissible addition, and account for transport/removal as separate outcomes. A rule addition changes the Culling Game rules; colony barriers and local consequences remain separately scoped. Record when a rule takes effect; do not rewrite prior events.

All local scenes, people and consequences above are invented proposals. They are not franchise canon.

## Era and start variants

Each snapshot states location, date/cutoff, body, current knowledge, existing relationships, access, source ownership, mission status and which major events already occurred.

Relationship AUs and swapped-role AUs may change eligibility and knowledge, but must identify changed truths through sealed slots if the player is meant to discover them. No spoiler truth is repeated here.

## Travel, failure and carry

`P` Travel uses shared visit composition. Declare body, source, memory, learned method, tools, vows, companions, obligations, return conditions and absence clock. A technique bound to a body does not automatically travel with a soul; the source pages contain case-specific exceptions, so the selected continuity must state its rule. A vow crossing worlds has no default guarantee. Carrying a tool does not prove local compatibility.

`P` Separate local defeat, injury, mission failure, capture, body death, visit interruption, life completion and prestige. Set no run-loss or offline-danger mode by implication. End the visit with explicit custody, unresolved work and consequences. Existing prestige terms remain outside this proposal.

## Access routes, gates and start selection

`P` Offer this world through the existing in-world invitation/door pattern or an explicitly selected new-run profile once a real playable slice exists. Keep a route to test starts in a sandbox profile, labelled as sandbox and separated from ordinary unlock receipts. A menu choice is the player's configuration, not an ability of the character.

Previewing never rerolls or commits state. On confirmation, show what body, memory, learned source, tools, relationships, mission state and liabilities will copy, reset or stay behind. Persist the selected start before publishing its first playable state.

`P` Candidate JJK-specific routes are (a) school recruitment/enrollment, (b) a window or instructor assignment, (c) an invitation by a clan or trusted actor, (d) discovery of a sealed place/object, and (e) an explicitly new-run start card. Each route must define what the character is told, what they do not yet know, why the body/source fits, and whether the arrival creates any obligation. A curse-induced summoning or reincarnation start requires a selected continuity rule and declared cost; it is not assumed by a generic “visit world” action.

## AU and parallel-copy access

`P` A canon entry starts from its frozen selected cutoff. A relationship AU, role swap or divergence gets its own continuity/instance ID and explicit changed-cause record. A relationship flag can alter who knows, trusts or recruits the player only when the AU defines the consequence. An AU does not inherit later canon events that its altered cause invalidated. Distinct copies preserve their own actors, unique claims, site states and RNG. Returning to canon means entering the canon copy or a fresh authored snapshot, not rewinding the changed copy.

`P` If an AU changes or randomizes a public franchise truth, runtime-facing material stores it under a sealed slot ID. Before discovery, this pack and UI can identify only the slot and its route, never the altered truth. Public canon twists stay public under the project rule.
