# Jujutsu Kaisen interactions: testimony, cost and authority

**Status: `P` interaction examples.** All people, exchanges and local event details here are original proposals, not canon scenes. Source rules are attributed only in the adjoining evidence note. Actor knowledge is not author truth.

## People, means and voice

| Role | What they can know/do | What they cannot assume |
|---|---|---|
| P Window / dispatcher | Receive reports, classify a request, pass a mission and pay a stated rate | See the site directly or guarantee that grade equals actual danger |
| P Student | Use their learned method and disclose their own observations | Know hidden technique rules, truth of a rumor, or another person's vow |
| P Barrier instructor | Explain or demonstrate the boundary they authored | Know every entrant or control what a separate institution decides |
| P Resident witness | Report sights, sounds, timing and harm they experienced | Perceive curses automatically or identify an unseen attacker |
| P Clan custodian | Hold a key, current stock or assigned access | Override every will, successor or political decision |
| P Curse / adversary | Pursue goals supported by its source and observed information | React to private player state without detection or report |
| P Headquarters reviewer | Issue an institutional order and act on evidence received | Possess omniscient judge knowledge unless the selected method grants it |

## Compact reaction matrix

| Player action | Immediate reaction | Durable consequence |
|---|---|---|
| P Ask how a report was classified | Dispatcher shows the evidence they received | Confidence can rise or remain disputed; no hidden truth is revealed. |
| P Refuse an assignment | Dispatcher offers ordinary alternatives if available | No mission debt or started deadline. Relationship may change only if authored. |
| P Ask to inspect the curtain | Instructor names the test and its known risks | Knowledge record stores the observer and result. |
| P Claim a vow benefit without proof | Other party disputes fulfillment | Obligation remains pending/disputed under its written evidence rule. |
| P Request access to a storehouse | Custodian checks actual permission and possession | Access, recognized claim and remaining inventory can differ. |

## 1. `P jjk.interaction.report`: incomplete dispatch

**Player:** “What did the window actually see?”  
**Dispatcher:** “A resident saw movement at the east stair and heard another voice.”  
**Dispatcher:** “No one confirmed a curse. The barrier report is old.”  
**Player:** “Then I’ll take the witness statement before the entrance.”  
**Dispatcher:** “That delays the grade review.”  
**Dispatcher:** “You can request a senior team or decline.”

`P` The dialogue reveals only the dispatcher’s records. Accepting begins only the stated terms. Refusal creates no debt. Evidence later changes the report confidence, not the event’s author truth by itself.

## 2. `P jjk.interaction.barrier`: safe test

**Player:** “Does this line stop everyone?”  
**Instructor:** “That is not what the written condition says.”  
**Instructor:** “We can test a paper tag and volunteer with the boundary open.”  
**Player:** “Will a wall tell us anything?”  
**Instructor:** “Not if this formula ignores buildings. The report needs a person-sized test.”

`W` Barrier r74038 says barriers recognize cursed-energy-bearing individuals and ignore inanimate objects/buildings; the article also says selective entry can be difficult. `P` This invented instructor does not know how an uninspected second layer behaves. The safe test commits the measured result to that actor’s knowledge only.

## 3. `P jjk.interaction.vow`: consent and terms

**Other party:** “Limit your technique to this doorway, and I will open the sealed route.”  
**Player:** “State the period, the crossing condition and what you give me.”  
**Player:** “Then say what you believe happens if either of us breaks it.”  
**Other party:** “I know what my side loses.”  
**Other party:** “I cannot promise a universal penalty for your side.”  
**Player:** “I’ll decide after the terms are written.”

`W` Binding Vow r78401 distinguishes self-vows and mutual pacts; it does not provide a general enforcement schedule. `P` Never present agreement as informed if the terms are hidden or coercion is unresolved. The player may refuse before a durable commitment.

## 4. `P jjk.interaction.heir`: office and custody

**Claimant:** “The will names me if the acting head is incapacitated.”  
**Custodian:** “The key opens the inner gate. I still control the shelves.”  
**Reviewer:** “The clan’s status is under review. That does not settle who may remove stock today.”

`W` Sorcerer Clan/Zenin Clan r79324 describes conditional succession, initial refusal/acceptance, storehouse access, inventory removal and institutional recognition as separate events. `P` The three proposed speakers have different information and power; the dialogue cannot grant inventory just because an office claim exists.

## 5. `P jjk.interaction.domain`: procedure before punishment

**Defendant:** “Do I have to answer?”  
**Caster:** “The rule I can explain permits a statement, denial, confession or silence.”  
**Observer:** “The judge may hold evidence neither of us has seen.”

`W` Deadly Sentencing r73036 describes a case-specific court domain, no-violence rule, evidence and statements, and differing judge/caster knowledge. `P` This scene is not a universal law court and does not decide any sanction until its authored method and target are known.

## Interaction gate

`P` Conversation actions can request evidence, teach a known rule, negotiate an explicit term, refuse, report, consent, challenge or leave. Keep costs and commitments visible. NPCs act from their own means and knowledge. Hidden changed truths appear only through the assigned sealed discovery route. No dialogue runtime was changed or tested.
