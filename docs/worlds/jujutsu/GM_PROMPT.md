## Assignment and ownership

Author one source-bounded Jujutsu Kaisen world proposal. Keep first implementation scope to one realm and one prestige layer. Finish one playable slice before proposing the next. Desktop/laptop is primary; the first slice’s viewport and visible outcome are in IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET. Mobile optimization is deferred.

Use the source revision cited for each claim. Wiki content is secondary. Keep manga, anime, guidebook, movie/game and later Modulo claims at their stated continuity and medium. Never generalize one battle page’s demonstrated outcome into a universal technique rule. Recheck actors and stated conditions; JJK lane spotcheck identified actor swaps and omitted conditions as recurring errors.

## World-specific rules

- `P` Fuel, output, control, perception and leakage are distinct.
- `P` Body/source, soul, memory, learned procedure, item, vow and institutional status have separate owners.
- `P` A technique source is not the same as an extension, learned barrier, tool effect, imitation or ritual.
- `P` A self-vow, mutual vow and birth-imposed restriction are separate types. Never invent universal mutual sanctions or assume consent.
- `P` Barrier shell, target predicate, inner space, embedded procedure, sure-hit, counter, clash and burnout resolve separately.
- `P` Healing, external treatment, soul repair, anti-curse positive energy and technique reversal are different actions.
- `P` Grades, observed power, hidden power, access, office and custody can disagree.
- `P` Fixed threats keep their authored strength. Do not invent immunity or rescue to preserve a planned result.

## Causality, knowledge and secrets

For every scene, record author truth, what each actor observed or was told, confidence, goals, available means, trigger, action alternatives, immediate results and later consequences. Information may be wrong or incomplete. No actor receives private player-state knowledge without a valid observation or report. Refusal does not start a mission deadline. An accepted commitment follows its actual terms.

Public franchise twists are not sealed. A changed or randomized truth uses a stable sealed slot ID and its discovery route only outside that route. Do not put the hidden truth in public dialogue, indexes, reports or summaries. Pack authors may inspect it for implementation; player-facing context refreshes may not expose it.

## Execution and recovery

No native ability, world binding, save schema or source-owned runtime contract is established by this pack. Before implementation, identify one authoritative owner for each durable effect. Preserve upstream attribution and existing saves. Persistence changes need an explicit compatibility decision. No build, installed state, gameplay or human acceptance may be claimed from this design packet.

## Review questions for each proposed scene

1. Which dated source and continuity support this rule? Is this a fact, inference or proposal?
2. Who is the exact actor, target, speaker and knowledge owner? What conditions did the source state?
3. What is the capability’s actual trigger, fuel, scope, interruption, limit and counter?
4. What does refusal, failure, success and later recovery change in durable state?
5. Is this premise a canon event, a divergence, a relationship AU or original material?
6. Does the scene expose any sealed changed/randomized truth before its discovery route?
