## Comparison 1: find one concealed target

Task: locate a curse or person inside an authored site while keeping the observer’s evidence distinct from author truth.

| Route | Lower | Developed | Mature | Actual limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P Witness trace | Interview one person and mark what they saw | Compare timestamps, route and conflicting testimony | Fund several independent leads and expose a false report | Direct identification needs a fitting faculty; indirect traces can support an inference, with its basis and uncertainty retained. |
| P Energy perception | Sense one supported presence/residual | Distinguish a known signal from environmental noise | Conceal/reveal a signature under a defined counter-test | Energy amount is not identity or intent; physical-only actors can confound the signal. `W` Heavenly Restriction r79234: a fully zero-energy body can perceive curses through heightened physical senses while evading cursed-energy detection. Keep this exceptional sensory route separate from energy sensing and physical barrier interaction. |
| P Barrier survey | Observe one boundary and test a permitted object/actor | Infer target predicate through safe controlled probes | Map layered boundary, entry state and exits | A barrier rule may ignore buildings and track energy-bearing people; case and revision apply. |
| P Innate tracking method | Execute one actual source method in its valid scope | Improve control and evidence retention | Outperform ordinary survey inside its authored mechanism | No global tracking is granted by a technique label. |

`P` Result is evidence with confidence, not automatic arrival or custody. Existing authorities must supply a local knowledge record before implementation.

## Comparison 2: prevent one incoming effect

Task: prevent a fixed attack whose source, target and timing are known. First establish that body/source/tool can interact with it.

| Route | Lower | Developed | Mature | Actual limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P Physical defense | Move behind an actual obstruction or use a compatible tool | Learn timing against one observed path | Exceptional body/weapon beats a weaker strike | Does not contact an intangible target without source support. |
| P Reinforcement | Cover one eligible body region with controlled energy | Maintain flow while moving or acting | Sustain high output against a named threat | Reserve/output/control differ; no universal defense multiplier. |
| P Anti-domain method | Use one source-supported method against sure-hit | Maintain its own trigger/geometry under pressure | Coordinate with a real ally or overlapping domain | It suppresses the effect specified by its source, not every domain action. |
| P Barrier/domain | Close a defined route or install one defined procedure | Use target conditions and entry geometry tactically | Resolve a clash, counter, rescue or escape on its authored terms | Shell, interior, embedded effect and sure-hit are distinct outcomes. |

`P` A nonlethal domain may enforce a procedure rather than damage. Higuruma’s Deadly Sentencing is source-qualified by r73036: violence is suspended, evidence/rules matter, judge and caster knowledge differ, and sanction routing depends on case. Do not generalize this to every domain.

## Comparison 3: restore one lost field function

Task: return one actor to a specified assignment after damage. Diagnose body, tool, technique, soul and obligation separately.

| Route | Restores | Does not automatically restore |
|---|---|---|
| P Rest and ordinary care | Time, ordinary fatigue or a compatible minor injury | Burnout, technique source, soul or death |
| P Positive energy | A supported tissue/body condition with a qualified user | Every recipient, poison, vow-caused scar, soul shape or technique burnout |
| P External treatment | Eligible patient with compatible output and skill | The healer’s own capacity or unsupported diagnosis |
| P Tool repair | Same tool’s damaged physical function with retained custody | Tool owner’s innate technique or a destroyed unique source |
| P Technique recovery | Only a source-described burned-out method after its actual condition ends or treatment applies | Generic restoration from a healing item |
| P Soul/body method | Precisely stated condition if the selected source grants it | Universal identity continuity or resurrection |

These boundaries follow the separate mechanisms described by Reverse Cursed Technique r79210, Cursed Technique r79254 and tool sources. `P` Before spend, show target, provider, source, cost, known limit and unresolved exception.

## Copy, transfer and imitation

`P` A witnessed sequence can create a study record; practice lets a compatible receiver perform a learned method with their own means. An instrument can record a signal without teaching it. A copied/borrowed technique needs its own selected source, eligibility, storage, term and cost. Body transfer, soul transfer, incarnation, vow transfer and tool custody cannot share a single “copy” action.

The source snapshot’s claims about unusual technique storage or body use are individual examples. They do not establish a general transplant rule. See the source ledger’s continuity and contradiction entries.

## Mastery and subprogression

`P` Track control, timing, target selection, range, setup, interruption resilience, resource efficiency and knowledge independently. Do not make all techniques share levels or identical actions. Technique-specific conditions remain hidden from the player until observed or taught; public source twists may be explicit, but any new/randomized mechanic stays a sealed slot ID with its discovery route.
