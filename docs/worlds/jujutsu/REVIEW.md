# Independent Jujutsu Kaisen world-pack review

**Verdict: usable with fixes, as a research and proposal pack.** The eight core engine-law rows are supported by their saved wiki payloads, and the proposed mechanics are marked `P`. This is dated secondary evidence, not primary manga/anime verification, accepted design, or playable implementation. The proposed Culling Game rule scope needs correction before it becomes a game rule; the curtain slice needs its specific source attached.

## Method and scope

Read `AGENTS.md`, `PROJECT.md`'s standing priority, the source deep-dive playbook checklist, both wiki-lane spotchecks, the eight new pack owners, the older `WORLD_BRIEF.md` boundary, the JJK qualifier and full-review notes/checkpoints, and the cited private payloads. For each payload used below, I opened the `read_file` in `docs/research/power-systems/wiki-digests/full-dumps/jujutsu-kaisen_SOURCES.json` and verified its `text` length and SHA-256 against that row's `read_characters` and `read_sha256`. Payload `L` numbers below mean lines in its stored `text.splitlines()`, not wiki page lines. The article title and revision identify the exact manifest row and private file. This was a claim sample, not a reread of all 501 selections or a primary-source audit. SPOTCHECK_1's JJK span-coordinate warning remains relevant; these locators use restored payload lines rather than the affected chunk offsets.

## Fixes in priority order

1. **F1, rule scope:** Clarify `CAMPAIGN.md:63` and the `jjk.rule_game` route that a Culling Game rule addition can affect the game-wide rule set; colony barriers and local consequences are separate. The present word “local” could create the wrong engine authority or persistence scope. The specific source is Culling Game r79139, Rules L199–230.
2. **F2, first-slice source:** Add Curtain r77658 as a direct anchor to the curtain dispatch in `SYNTHESIS.md:104` and `IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md:3–16`, and select which curtain behavior the authored instance uses. Its payload distinguishes exterior appearance, concealment, shell force, selective entry/exit, talismans and overlapping curtains (L14–28, L44–51). The generic Barrier page alone does not settle those first-slice choices. Keep the invented predicate `P`.
3. **F3, medium traceability:** Give the source-ledger index explicit medium/continuity tags or a clear per-claim pointer for anime, film, game and Modulo rows. At present `SOURCE_LEDGER.md:53–56` says the boundaries live in individual notes, but the displayed coverage cells all say `W, retained selected text marked read-complete`. This is a reuse risk, not an observed film/game claim leak. The game-only Kensuke page (r71591, L16) shows why the index alone cannot authorize a main-series character.

## Supported material the pack has not yet turned into a rule

- **F4, curtain observation:** Curtain r77658, L14, L21–28 says outsiders see a frozen view of the scene, while a curtain's black shell is visible to qualified observers, including windows. This could make the first dispatch's witness and player observations more precise; the pack currently describes concealment and a boundary without choosing that observer behavior.
- **F5, zero-energy sensing:** Heavenly Restriction r79234, L24–26 says the fully zero-energy body can perceive curses through heightened physical senses while evading cursed-energy detection. The current witness default in `SYNTHESIS.md:84` is safe, but a future exceptional-body start needs this specific path rather than ordinary cursed-energy perception.

## Other gates

Proposals remain visibly marked `P`; no changed or randomized secret truth appears outside a slot ID/discovery-route instruction in the reviewed text. Public franchise facts are treated separately. I found no raw source prose or quotation over 15 words in the pack: a normalized 16-word overlap scan of all nine JJK Markdown files against the 501 restored selections found zero matches, and the dialogue examples are marked invented. That scan does not prove absence of shorter paraphrase errors.

The pack honestly leaves primary manga/anime/guide verification, the exact main-series cutoff, unexpanded template/nested-list coverage, the open empty-input audit, fan-premise survey, runtime ownership, build, persistence and native browser acceptance open (`SYNTHESIS.md:6–12,108–116`; `SOURCE_LEDGER.md:3–12,36–45`). The older `WORLD_BRIEF.md:28–35` explicitly subordinates its memory-based mechanic tables to the new source-bounded pack; its parked 2026-10-10 slices are not acceptance evidence or a mandate to build them. No gameplay or save behavior was tested in this review.
