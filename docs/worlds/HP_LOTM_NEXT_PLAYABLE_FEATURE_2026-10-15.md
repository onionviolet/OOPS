## Source check and important correction

### HP: a tool response is not a single ownership gate

**HP-C1:** freshly checked [official wand encyclopedia](https://www.harrypotter.com/fact-file/objects/wand)
describes wands as channeling tools and acknowledges wandless magic. Therefore a
wand-required authored method must not become a universal “no wand, no magic” law.

**HP-C2:** [Rowling's wand-length/flexibility writing](https://www.harrypotter.com/writing-by-jk-rowling/wand-lengths-and-flexibility)
treats the wand/owner pairing as a combined set of properties. It does not supply
an affinity formula, hidden personality score or canonical numerical penalty.

**HP-C3:** the [official allegiance feature](https://www.harrypotter.com/features/how-loyal-is-a-wand-to-a-wizard)
describes borrowed/other wands producing differing results. **Correct the possible
reading of BF1:** custody, allegiance and usable performance are different facts;
“not chosen by this wand” is not sufficient for a universal cannot-cast flag.
Damage, unfamiliarity, differing affinity and an actual refusal remain separate
causes. Unknown compatibility is not evidence of zero ability. An item transfer
also cannot automatically transfer allegiance.

These are C-X supplemental/official-reference checks, not a newly accepted source
set or a seven-book audit. Existing H2 admission/source questions remain; none of
these pages defines acquisition of magic by an ordinary nonmagical adult. The
bounded repository search found no HP original-book corpus in the inspected
research paths; no assertion about the user's entire library follows. A source-
qualified start can explicitly have eligibility without inventing a universal
inheritance test. Exact foreign-body eligibility stays an unselected adapter.

## REC1: concrete feature boundary and resolved defaults

**D1, profile:** propose the existing design owner's T2 local adult records
probationer, early Tingen E1 horizon, in an explicitly original branch. Keep actual
clerical rights distinct from field membership and potion permission. No Klein
replacement, memories, fog access, automatic special resistance or earned canon
credit. Local invitation, supervisor, supplier, wages, prices, budgets and scheduling
are authored proposals. This does not silently select a default new-game world.
A retained foreign visitor needs separate compatibility, not an assumed free dose.

**D2, acquisition:** a willing qualified supplier actually owns one correctly
prepared eligible dose and has an explicit authorized allocation. Separately obtain
the supported body/consent and supervised use. Ingestion consumes that dose once
and records the acquisition cause. Reading a recipe, holding a cup or accepting
employment grants no faculty. Begin with a supervised prepared-dose service rather
than a general public potion editor; supplier provenance and finite source materials
still need an actual producer/accounting path, never an item spawned by dialogue.
Specific material quantities remain in the qualified recipe data, not a renamed
arbitrary crafting-quality roll. Unknown unsafe outcomes stay outside this narrowly
admitted operation; do not advertise supervision as universal immunity.

**D3, learning and growth:** initial source ownership does not learn every power.
Actual teacher time and the learner's control exercise learn one bounded method;
performed supported uses refine its control. Knowledge, owned source, mastery,
spirituality/exertion and digestion remain separate. Do not map `faculty.development`
or generic Literacy XP directly to Sequence rank. The first feature completes
acquisition/control/use; **digestion and promotion remain a later feature**, rather
than a fake “digestion complete” button. Do not invent a daily-job digestion wage.

**D4, real consumer and reuse:** permit deliberate, finite examination of an
actually present inspectable material using the learned perception channel. First
use can distinguish a source-qualified ordinary comparator from a supported
supernatural sample at a local workbench. Later apply the same method to another
supported owned/consented sample, with actual preparation/handling consequences.
A narrow positive finding can route material to the qualified preparation tray;
an inconclusive result routes it to expert review. The custody/tray/result change
is an original local application, not a canonical source recipe or proof of safety.
The ability must read the changed actual item/material record, not a fixture label.
No extra quest reward is needed for the ability to be useful. Unknown targets
remain unknown; do not reveal all stock, sealed contents, disease or hidden identities.
A follow-up method such as pendulum can reuse the same acquired source later.

**D5, cost and continued life:** use one local source-specific resource/strain
policy over existing owners, with authored prototype values clearly distinguished
from source measurements. Choose one authoritative reserve representation at
implementation, not a simultaneous resource bar plus disconnected energy ledger.
A bounded supported action spends actual means; deliberate stop and a source-
qualified played recovery interval permit later use. No rendering, repeat click,
absence or save reload refills. Ordinary clerical life and explicit refusal remain
possible; no compulsory dangerous sleep, weekly promotion or maintenance chore.

These D1–D5 are recommended reversible defaults. The user authorized comparison
and saving; this file does not accept every premise, number or implementation.
The earlier local world-design packet deferred initiation outside its ordinary
opening. REC1 is a proposed follow-on, not a retroactive expansion of that opening.
