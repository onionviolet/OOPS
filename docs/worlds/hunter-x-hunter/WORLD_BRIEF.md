## P1. World promise

Live in a world where curiosity grants access but not safety. A license can open doors without making its holder good, powerful or immune. Nen makes personal intent mechanically legible, yet its rules depend on a person's actual ability and conditions. Hunting, study, teaching, preparation, care, trade, investigation and institutional service can all be meaningful lives.

## P3. Canon and proposal boundary

The series has manga, 1999 and 2011 anime, films, games, databooks and a continuing manga publication history. This pack's evidence snapshot is secondary Hunterpedia revisions, largely reflecting manga through chapters 420, with a tracked series digest snapshot dated 2026-10-10. It does not establish a complete current canon cutoff or certify all manga events. See [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md).

Public franchise reveals remain unsealed. Any original, altered or randomized cause, identity, ability truth or timeline outcome is a sealed slot and may appear to the player only through its route. This brief gives no sealed truth. See `SLOT-HXH-01` onward in the ability and interaction owners; only IDs, routes and status are exposed outside the sealed payload (which is not included in this tracked proposal).

## P5. Play and progression promise

An unlicensed applicant should be able to earn travel money, practice useful ordinary skills, ask for help, decline the exam and keep living. Taking the exam creates a real commitment with uncertain and potentially severe outcomes. A license opens opportunities but creates no automatic Nen knowledge. Nen requires a distinct eligible teacher/route, actual learning and practice. High power stays high; a prepared weaker actor may instead win through information, equipment, timing or a different objective.

## P6. Scope

This pack proposes a broad authoring map across the Hunter Exam, Whale Island, Heavens Arena, Yorknew, Greed Island, Meteor City, Chimera Ant contexts and the Succession Contest. That map is not a promise to implement every era or arc in the first milestone. The first slice remains one realm and one prestige layer. No alternate timeline, protagonist romance, randomized canon event, death/rebirth contract or mobile-first UI is accepted here.
