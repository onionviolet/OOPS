Status: proposal r1, 2026-10-14. See [brief](WORLD_BRIEF.md), [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md), and [shared campaign framework](../CAMPAIGN_FRAMEWORK.md). Every row below is proposed gameplay; citations establish source anchors only.

## P1. Opening snapshot and entry choices

| Entry ID | Proposal | Actual initial means | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 `exam_candidate` | Arrive in a port town with a lead to the annual Hunter Exam and a short window to prepare. The specific town, NPCs, costs and dates are original. | Fare, food, ordinary tools, one personal skill and a few local contacts. | Do not copy Gon's wager or give the candidate his fishing feat or family ties. | C2 `licensed_field_assistant` | Start after an independently authored exam pass, as a new license holder choosing a first paid assignment. | License access only where the pack has qualified the privilege; no assumed Nen or concealed Nen-related professional qualification. | P1 avoids making the exam mandatory. | C3 `whale_island_local` | A self-contained local-life start with an eventual option to seek the exam. | Fishing, repair, transport and local relationships. | Region is canon; family, neighbors and events are original. No altered protagonist history is implied. |
| C4 `arena_student` | A later-start candidate who already has a sourced teacher and is knowingly entering high-risk competitive training. | Qualified body, basic instruction and limited funds. | Do not grant Nen awakening just because the player selects the label. Selection is a proposal for the shared start selector, not a local random/save writer. Compatible snapshots are authored individually; no every-axis lottery. Keep player knowledge, actor history, public rumors and author truth distinct.

## P3. Progression careers

| Path | Entry function | Mature function | Cost / weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Field hunter | Track, survey, survive and report using ordinary means | Specialist expeditions and evidence-led contracts | Weather, access, injury, incomplete maps and powerful fixed threats |
| Licensed specialist | Work within demonstrated field expertise | Restricted research, appraisal, care or negotiation | Credential boundaries, evidence quality and local authority |
| Nen student | Learn Ten/Zetsu/Ren/Hatsu under a qualified route | Develop personal ability within its authored predicates | Practice, fatigue, reserve, affinity and condition-specific failure |
| Combatant | Train a supported body, weapon and tactics | Compete against strong opponents or fulfill a bounded tactical role | Injury, reach, opponent knowledge and actual Nen disparity |
| Researcher / exorcist | Read records, test claims and commission specialists | Identify an ability's operating conditions or arrange supported exorcism | No automatic answer; exorcism is rare, costly and may burden its practitioner (*Nen* r421055) |
| Provider / trader | Repair, supply, carry and appraise | Fund multiple expeditions or operate a specialist service | Stock, time, custody, reputation and travel risks |
| Association service | Examination, records, dispatch and field support | Earn office/access by actual service | Institutional trust is not combat strength and does not erase local law |

## P4. Proposed escalating arcs

| Arc | Trigger / actual choice | Possible lasting outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| A1: Ticket before deadline | Accept a paid repair/courier shift and miss the exam window, or spend scarce cash to travel now with less preparation. | Retained wages, missed entry, local trust, or arrival with thinner supplies. An unaccepted deadline creates no debt. |
| A2: A bad report | Compare a broker's map against terrain and a second witness, or act on the first account. | A route opens, a lead is disproved, or cargo/time is lost. The character learns only what evidence supports. |
| A3: License and access | Take a supported field assignment or reject it for ordinary paid work. | Keep card custody and holder privileges distinct; a sold card does not transfer privileges, and a lost/stolen card is not replaced in the wiki account. A license holder may still lack the concealed Nen-related professional qualification required by some jobs. Exact legal treatment remains open, with no automatic legal immunity. |
| A4: Teach or force | Pursue gradual Nen instruction with a willing qualified teacher, or refuse; a dangerous forced method can appear only as an explicitly warned and independently sourced option. | Training, relationship, fatigue or lasting harm under the chosen authored rule. No free reset or forced tutorial. |
| A5: Same obstacle, different means | Combine preparation, a team, a personal Nen ability or a noncombat objective against one fixed threat. | Actual capability can win; clever route can bypass; failure remains possible. Do not scale the obstacle to preserve a plot. |

## P6. Time, danger and continuity

The yearly exam, varied phases, different geography and evolving actors have source anchors, but this pack pins no exact timeline. Proposed era snapshots are separate starting instances unless a bounded causal branch is explicitly authored. A historical start does not roll back another instance. Absence freezes by default only as a design proposal pending engine support; offline catch-up is not assumed. Severe injury, capture, property loss and death may occur in authored high-risk contexts, but their exact policy, recovery and persistence need a separate explicit gate. No permadeath or death-rebirth system is selected here.
