# Independent review: Hunter × Hunter world pack

2026-10-14. **Verdict: usable with fixes** as a secondary-source design proposal. It is not ready to be treated as a canon-verified ruleset or a playable world. The ten engine-rule rows have a direct cached-page basis, but two lose qualifications that would affect ability authoring. One campaign source anchor places a post-exam location in the exam route. The pack otherwise keeps proposed gameplay, other media and sealed slots distinguishable.

## Evidence method and limits

I read the pack, `AGENTS.md`, the source deep-dive checklist and both wiki-lane spot-checks. For every row below I opened the cached wiki text named by the matching `read_file` in `workers/hxh-full/**/READ_LEDGER.jsonl`, rather than relying on `NOTES.jsonl`. Locators are **page title, revision, and zero-based character offset in that cached page's `text` field**; section names help locate the passage. The relevant reader notes are `hxh-full/NOTES.jsonl` (Nen #8, License #2, Association #15, Chimera Ants #18, Greed Island #61), `r2-2026-10-13/shard-a/NOTES.jsonl` (Meteor City #1, Basho #2, Heavens Arena #86, Hunter Exam arc #120, Hunter Exam #270), and `r2-2026-10-13/shard-b/NOTES.jsonl` (Succession Contest #432). These are dated Hunterpedia payloads, not manga pages. The chapter references inside them were not independently checked.

`Supported` means the cited secondary payload supports the bounded statement. `Fix` means a statement needs a qualification or its use as a source anchor is wrong. Original gameplay marked `P` is judged as a proposal, not as a wiki fact.

## Fixes needed, in priority order

1. **F1, vow consequences:** Qualify `SYNTHESIS.md:42`, `SOURCE_LEDGER.md:23` and `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md:24`. The wiki distinguishes unbreakable ability conditions from breakable vows and describes loss of the affected ability plus possible loss of Nen capacity after breach. AV4's selected-risk wording currently makes the latter disappear. Direct manga review should decide how a canon-compatible player vow is authored; until then, show both risks and keep AV4 unqualified for play.
2. **F2, Specialist boundary:** Add the page's all-or-nothing Specialist creation/use boundary to `SYNTHESIS.md:41`, `SOURCE_LEDGER.md:22` and `MODULE_CATALOGUE.md:15`. Continue treating numerical efficiency and individual cases as secondary, but do not offer a partial Specialist proficiency on an ordinary affinity ladder.
3. **F3, exam geography and completion:** Separate Testing Gate from exam-site anchors in `CAMPAIGN.md:21`. `SYNTHESIS.md:120` and `IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md:7` should say whether the two authored phases are a preview of a longer exam or an explicitly exceptional complete exam before any license award. The wiki's phase count varies, with five or six as its reported average; this is a design qualification, not a required fixed phase count.
4. **F4, credential loss and professional status:** Add the wiki-reported no-replacement/no-retake consequence to the license model and failure-policy gap (`SYNTHESIS.md:45,87,128-129`; `SOURCE_LEDGER.md:25`). Clarify for `CAMPAIGN.md:10,36-41` that a card holder and a person who completed the concealed Nen-related professional step can face different job gates. Keep legal privileges secondary until primary review.
5. **F5, source disposition:** Keep the existing `needs_scope_review` rows and seven unread supplemental spans open. Before a rule or character from those rows becomes a runtime requirement, resolve its scope and pin a manga cutoff. The wiki's page revision and cited chapters do not certify a full primary read.

## Supported material the pack missed or underused

- **Exam routing:** *Hunter Exam* r419181 `text[3793:4050]` says passing the third phase grants direct access to the following year's exam. That could matter for a failed applicant's next-year path; it is not a universal retake grant for all failures.
- **Credential irreversibility:** *Hunter License* r405166 `text[940:1250]` says a lost/stolen card is not replaced and a successful applicant cannot retake the exam. This matters more to the proposed custody and save model than a generic sale flag.
- **Nen secrecy has an actor rule:** *Nen* r421055 `text[24997:26450]` reports that licensed Hunters generally may not disclose Nen to the uninformed, with an emergency exception. The pack models observer knowledge but has not given its proposed teaching/disclosure routes this institutional constraint.
- **Ability categories are not a damage formula:** *Nen* r421055 `text[51400:54600]` states the Specialist use/creation boundary separately from the approximate adjacent-type efficiencies. A data model should represent this as a gate, not another percentage.

## Boundary checks

- **Proposal and continuity:** The pack's new starts, abilities, jobs, prices, parallel branches and instances are labelled `P` or original. The ledger separates manga, 1999/2011 anime, film, game and databook evidence. The Testing Gate placement above is the one concrete continuity/location blend found; no film or game rule was promoted into the manga baseline in the checked claims.
- **Sealed secrets:** The four public `SLOT-HXH-*` entries give IDs, discovery routes and status only. I found no changed or randomized truth exposed in the nine pack files. Public franchise facts are handled as unsealed.
- **Source prose:** The nine pack files contain no raw wiki page body or long quotation. An exact 16-word sliding-window comparison against the 1,256 distinct cached payload files referenced by the 1,257 ledger rows found zero matches; manual reading found no quoted passage over 15 words. This is a check of the present pack, not a license to copy prose later.
- **Honest gaps:** `SYNTHESIS.md:126-136` names primary manga, exam/license, Greed Island, cutoff, category conflicts, coverage, runtime ownership and M3 fan-premise gaps. Those are real. F1 through F4 add design-relevant qualifications already present in the cached wiki text; they do not substitute for the primary chapter pass.
