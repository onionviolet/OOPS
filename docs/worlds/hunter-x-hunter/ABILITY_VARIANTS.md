# Hunter × Hunter ability variants proposal

Status: proposal r1, 2026-10-14. All new abilities, parameters and game mechanics are P. Canon character abilities are examples only where their pages are cited; no player receives them by default. Full source status is in [ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md).

## P1. Authoring contract

Every ability record should name its actual owner, source/capacity, effect, valid target, trigger, costs, duration, range, attention, visibility, counters, collateral and persistence. Label each item `P` until direct source review and gameplay qualification. Distinguish:

- inability to activate because a prerequisite is absent;
- an active effect that can be resisted, interrupted or countered;
- voluntary restriction that strengthens a method;
- breach of a vow and its individually authored consequence;
- ordinary aura expenditure, bodily strain and irreversible risk.

This separation follows the candidate distinctions in *Nen* r421055; exact universal implementation is not established by that secondary summary.

## P5. Conflicts / counters checklist

Every selected variant needs a positive use, a valid no-effect condition, a costly or interrupted case and a counter that follows its own predicate. Do not invent universal aura resistance, exact numerical aura units, or a shared all-categories proficiency equation from the wiki summary. Keep source/creator semantics when ability use is borrowed, stolen, inherited or post-mortem where evidence supports it. Mark anything else `OPEN`.
