# Hunter × Hunter interaction proposals

Status: proposal r1, 2026-10-14. These are authored scenarios, not simulated facts. Every person, local detail, cost and outcome invented here is P. Source anchors are in [ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md).

## P2. Readable choice contract

Before a commitment, show known cost, actual owner, required access, expiry, disclosed risk and unknowns. A contract deadline begins only after acceptance. Character-facing text cannot reveal sealed truth or author-only counters. Afterward, report only observed result and evidence. Rumor, witnessed event, correct inference and confirmed fact are different states.

## P3. Failure and recovery cases

| Failure | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Failed exam stage | Keep the precise result and accepted terms; do not award a license or assume a second attempt is allowed. The license page says a successful passer cannot retake. The exam page separately reports following-year access for someone who passes phase three, which may apply to an otherwise unsuccessful applicant; it does not establish a universal retake path for every failure. Confirm both in primary material. |
| Forced aura exposure | Treat as a distinct high-risk, explicit route, never a routine fail-forward tutorial. Model uncontrolled leakage, bodily harm and uncertain recovery only with a qualified source decision. |
| Broken personal vow | Keep the source-reported baseline visible: loss of the affected ability and risk to Nen capacity. Any added consequence is specific to the authored ability. Do not substitute a universal formula or infer a specific death outcome. |
| Lost credential | Preserve actual loss and consequences. The wiki says a lost/stolen card is not replaced and a previous passer cannot retake the exam; confirm this before treating it as an implemented canon rule. |
| Wrong knowledge | Keep the mistaken reading attached to its observer and evidence. Later correction needs another route. |
| Body/custody loss | Keep cargo, actor, source and world instance under their real owners; no narrative recovery without a supported route. |

## P4. Sealed branch guard

Changed or randomized truths must use `SLOT-HXH-01` through `SLOT-HXH-04` or a newly reviewed ID, with their full records stored privately. Public reports and UI show only ID, route and status until discovery. Public manga revelations are not sealed. None of the current rows chooses which private truth is active.
