## Scouting and promotion gate

Use a small representative source set before deepening a candidate: ordinary life/acquisition, one signature interaction, one failure/counter, and the relevant travel or historical boundary. Save edition/chapter/scene locators, supporting fact, inference/invention, unresolved questions and the source range. A publisher synopsis supports its synopsis-level facts; it does not support exact power limits.

Promote a candidate to a local world folder when its distinct decision rule, connected careers/cast, useful return, source boundary and one concrete proof are authored. Promote a campaign to engineering only with named state/effect owners, actual missing adapters, frozen failure/transfer policy and a visible/save acceptance gate. Do not make a universal tier mapping, reset, protagonist identity or inter-world physics from a book title.

A completed source offers a stable cutoff. A serial offers a selected inspected cutoff. Either can support an alternate branch with original people and outcomes. Several references can inspire one original setting if their mechanics are reconciled explicitly; they are not silently merged into a claim of shared canon.
