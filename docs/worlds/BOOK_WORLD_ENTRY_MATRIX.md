# Book and fiction worlds: creation, entry, systems and observation (proposal, 2026-10-10)

Weibao: "Consider deepening each idea and more? Especially consider each
book world and fiction." This applies the north star's creation, entry,
observation, system-level and integration ideas to every world we have
notes for. Built from our research notes on 2026-10-10. Everything is a
proposal. Paths are relative to `docs/`. The "source route" and "creation screen"
columns for franchise worlds (Bleach, Elden Ring, Fallout, Naruto, Skyrim)
come partly from Claude's general knowledge of those works, not our notes.

Linked from [the north star](../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#building-an-infinite-variant-world-kang-style-conquerors-and-cheat-placement-proposal-2026-10-10).
Cheat placements are in [the cheat cosmology](../CHEAT_COSMOLOGY.md).

**Status key:** *Built* means implemented code exists (mostly
implemented-unverified). *Brief* means a world brief or campaign only.
*Research* means notes only. (U) means unverified or machine-translated
source.

**System levels:** S0 none, S1 info, S2 helper, S3 shop, S4 authority, S5
world-wide, S6 multiversal, S7 meta.

## What deepening across all worlds shows

- **Every world already has a natural creation heading.** The source's own
  first scene is the screen: an academy, an awakening, a muster, a
  tutorial, a trigger.
- **Point-buy fits only some worlds natively** (Fallout, Legendary
  Mechanic, system worlds). Elsewhere it needs a cheat or a system to make
  sense, as already proposed.
- **Integration worlds cluster:** A Novel Concept, Omniscient Reader,
  Labyrinth, Solo Leveling. They can share one integration campaign engine
  with different dials.
- **Rewind and loop worlds cluster:** Reverend Insanity, Mother of
  Learning, Regressor's Tale, You Have Died Again, Circle of Inevitability.
  They can share the rewind-and-branch engine, which also powers variant
  worlds.
- **Some worlds hide power from sight by law:** Bleach (spirits), Reverend
  Insanity (tampering), Lord of the Mysteries (masks). These are where the
  observation contest matters most.
- **Genetics is the stat system** in Genetic Ascension and central in
  Naruto and Harry Potter, so those are the best first homes for the
  genetics layer.
