## Instance state and first loop

Prefix proposed clinic.carrierIntact, clinic.route, clinic.deliverySettled, clinic.stockKnown and local site/person/claim IDs with the integrator-selected durable instance namespace.
Aki owns the bench and offered timber; Ren owns clinic stock and treatment capacity; Toma owns his puppet/control source and finite reserve. Actual borrowed carrier goods remain in custody.
Author truth remains actual local state/controller definitions; each observer's known report goes to their existing knowledge record. Unique delivery settlement belongs to one claim across routes; jobs hold frozen inputs/participants/due times. No second plot reward manager or player inventory.

**Route A, ordinary work:** On a generated fixture, buy two timber and one cloth from an actually stocked supplier, pay three proposed local units for Aki's bench/repair agreement, and commit four played minutes/two stamina to fit the carrier. Deliver the real clinic parcel through a three-minute supervised handoff. These O fixture values are not manga economics. Missing timber, bench, recipient or inspection evidence rejects before spend.

**Route B, genuinely qualified source/service:** Use a generated fixture with an actually acquired compatible chakra/body source and taught controlled-placement method, or pay Toma four proposed units for that actual service. Reserve the fitted linkage, relevant operator and three-minute work; spend two real units of that operator's authored local reserve. Control/source eligibility is explicit, not granted by entering the world. Completing one supported carrier placement preserves timber but spends fuel/service, and hands off the same real parcel. This bounded O control application is not a general puppet combat or chakra-physiology proof.

**Shared settlement:** One shared delivery claim grants a remembered contribution and sheltered-rack study once. Ordinary route retains a fitting lesson; sourced-control route retains only the earned applicable practice/service memory. No lineage, chakra source or medical jutsu is a settlement prize.

**Refusal, expiry and accepted failure:** The caused departure offer lasts twelve proposed present-played minutes. Observation reveals remaining time without restart/debt. Refusal or expiry causes the clinic to fund a longer route and postpone one service; later Ren report reveals actual stock/service facts. Accepted default retains spent inputs and declared custody obligations, unlike refusal. A later ordinary repair can reopen service but cannot farm the one delivery reward.

**Useful local export:** A paid ordinary carrier-fitting application at a Frontier workshop, using actual materials and a compatible native item adapter. No village rank or chakra conversion exports.

**Missing local/runtime capabilities:** Native instance/clock/item/currency integration; explicit eligible local body/chakra source and reserve recovery; bounded supported control targeting; later puppet control channels/parts/condition, Gates injuries, source seals, summoning consent and general shinobi combat.


Entry/return preserves actual body, wounds, methods, sources/reserves and named cargo. The first fixture keeps companions home until consent/body/cargo support is implemented.
A compatible Frontier step may brace mundane load at its real three-drive-unit/strain cost after destination binding. It does not create the local supernatural source or grant unsupported targeting.
Fixed threats may overwhelm the visitor, while genuinely sufficient compatible imports work. First proof excludes lethal combat; terminal death, run end, source revival, aging/offline losses and full-life retention remain unselected.
