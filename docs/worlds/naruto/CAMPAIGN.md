## Campaign promise and horizons

Become useful in a world where control, bodily training, acquired secrets and institutions develop along different routes.
Early play repairs a carrier and protects clinic stock through ordinary labor or a genuinely learned control method.
Middle play chooses a specialty: dangerous body training, medical work, puppet engineering, studied techniques or intelligence/logistics.
Mature play can coordinate a field hospital, powerful puppet team or supported specialist mission, with real consequences for people beyond the main cast.
The character can live as an artisan, supplier or clinic worker without taking the source hero's identity or every exam.
Strong fighters remain strong. An institution may still demand actual evidence, loyalty terms or competent medical work.
Witnessed intervention can diverge from expected manga history; later chapters are an author reference, not fate enforced on living actors.
The first code milestone still limits implementation to one realm/one prestige. Campaign coverage does not select these source powers for runtime.

Detailed stage numbers/interfaces belong to [the module owner](MODULE_CATALOGUE.md); this campaign never duplicates their balance values.

O clinic assembly: sterile storage, treatment bench, actual medic and supplied evacuation carriers, all with separate capacity/ownership.
N7 Hiruko shows armor-like puppetry exists; the O rescue shell is a proposed extension, not a copied canon model or high-tech suit.
Exclude Boruto-style scientific tools and unsourced electric networks from the selected original-manga baseline.
Mechanical modules require real tolerances and maintenance. A blocked joint can remove its movement function without erasing other skills.
Control mastery and chassis tier differ. Adding a second puppet can increase operator burden rather than automatically double output.
Payload use consumes its actual stock; no regenerating poison, unlimited weapons or unearned human-puppet organ appears on reload.
Installed parts retain one physical identity. Refit requires supported workshop labor and can change available field actions.
Unverified seal/lineage modules are authoring placeholders until the ledger supplies actual source support; ordinary kit remains buildable as O design.

## Three worked careers

### Resource-poor civilian fitter

1. Take a paid net/carrier repair using ordinary tools, timber, cloth and stamina.
2. Inspect the bridge carrier and learn which joint fails under load; no chakra source is needed for ordinary observation.
3. Fit a brace, pay a trained handler if needed and deliver clinic stock through actual custody.
4. Build a sheltered drying rack and acquire repeatable repair orders rather than a magical body by menu.
5. Teach a paid apprentice and become a border supplier with discovered routes and specific customer memories.

Means: common material, one bench, body stamina, lawful cargo and available transporter.
New functions: fit safe carriers, fulfill real clinic orders, maintain repeat production and negotiate stock access.
Weakness: cannot invoke source techniques or survive a superior shinobi attack merely because craft mastery is high.
Tradeoff: useful livelihood without rare lineage, while repeatedly paying others for dangerous field capability.

### Developed puppet field medic

1. Acquire genuine compatible chakra/control through an authored source/teaching gate; an outsider cannot receive it implicitly.
2. Learn ordinary care and a separately sourced medical method with supervised actual trials.
3. Buy or make a lawful rescue chassis, joints and gripper; assemble under real workshop capacity.
4. Train the combined puppet/medical use with finite reserves, cargo and a willing patient fixture.
5. Retrieve an injured person on a supported route, or leave the puppet to protect clinic stock while paying a bearer.

Means: actual control/body, taught medicine, puppet structure, materials, reserve and usable route/range.
New functions: remote handling, coordinated extraction and suitable field treatment; greater mastery opens genuine simultaneous control.
Weakness: close pressure, control disruption, damaged mechanisms and limited sterile stock; rescue does not guarantee cure.
Tradeoff: support specialization protects others but spends control/cargo capacity that an attack puppet would use differently.

### Mature inter-village recovery compact

1. Gather verified treatment/repair evidence and inspect supplier claims in the branch's current state.
2. Secure actual partners and separately supply Leaf clinic, Sand workshop and border stores.
3. Develop a genuinely capable body/technique specialist or contract strong protection within actual terms.
4. Publish common maintenance/triage standards or retain proprietary village-restricted knowledge.
5. Face a fixed large conflict through paid evacuation, evidence diplomacy, sufficient direct power or narrow contracted retrieval.

Means: several real facilities, people, route access, reserves and institutional permission.
New functions: funded parallel teams, broader missions and sustainable specialist teaching.
Weakness: war can destroy partners or sever routes; one great fighter cannot automatically treat every person or prove a forged report.
Tradeoff: shared standards improve civilian resilience but reveal methods/routes; secrecy can preserve a village's advantage while limiting aid.

## Five escalating arcs

### 1. The river carrier

Trigger: inspect Aki's cracked carrier while a public clinic departure offer is active; offer occurrence precedes observation.
Choice A: ordinary fitting spends timber/cloth, bench time and stamina; accept a supervised cart transfer after real repair.
Choice B: genuinely learned compatible control plus fitted linkage moves the carrier with less ordinary labor, spending actual reserve and operator time.
Alternative: pay Toma's finite service if he has means; owning a puppet without control cannot choose B.
Aftermath: one clinic-stock claim settles, with different spent resources and craft/control/service memories; neither route grants a bloodline.
Abstention: the clinic buys a longer route from actual budget and postpones one service; report reveals the result without new player debt.

### 2. The examination of useful work

Trigger: completed repairs/treatment create an actual offer for the practice annex or specialist apprenticeship.
Choices: body drill assessment, controlled medical trial, puppet handling trial or continued civilian supply career.
Aftermath: routes open different functions/access with paid instruction; they are original local assessments, not a copied compulsory Chunin Exam.
Abstention: teacher uses the slot for another actual trainee. Ordinary orders remain useful without guaranteed later same-price access.
Later consequence: selected competence influences who can accept field contracts and who must hire help.

### 3. The poisoned shipment claim

Trigger: a tested sample conflicts with a supplier's manifest; contamination and intent are separate questions.
Choices: pay comparative testing, disclose actual evidence, quarantine at supply cost, or route around the supplier privately.
Aftermath: patients/stock, supplier access and witness memories differ. A false accusation cannot be justified by author-only truth.
Abstention: Ren follows an actual triage policy and finite stock, while Daigo may buy the contested shipment.
Later consequence: verified cause changes compact membership and which research is lawful, without magically identifying every enemy.

### 4. The shared method

Trigger: real puppet/clinic improvement attracts exclusive village contracts and a cross-border relief offer.
Choices: restricted patronage, public tested method, paid neutral compact or private independent work.
Aftermath: better supplies may bind duties; disclosure improves collaborators and exposes technique information; neutrality requires funded protection.
Abstention: sponsors choose actual rival applicants; shared relief can fail without guaranteed replacement funding.
Later consequence: Daigo's means and access change from funded results, not player-level scaling.

### 5. War reaches the recovery road

Trigger: a separately sourced source-era conflict or clearly O branch conflict reaches the established route.
Choices: sufficient direct combat, funded evacuation, intelligence intervention with genuine evidence, or a narrow retrieval contract and departure.
Aftermath: powerful builds overcome lower threats; legendary enemies retain actual advantages and can destroy a site or participant.
Abstention: partners use finite supplies and known routes; some people and knowledge can be permanently lost.
Later consequence: surviving hospitals/workshops determine mature careers. Saving a worker invalidates downstream expected death scenes.
Exact Gates risk, terminal death, medical recovery and run retention require separately selected failure/body rules; current native fainting persists.
