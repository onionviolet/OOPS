## Entry and connected local life

Fantasy: Build a local life through ordinary craft, careful control, dangerous training or service while missions change the surrounding world.
Signature rule: Technique knowledge, trained body, actual chakra/source and puppet parts are different acquisitions. An ordinary fitter can supply a clinic; building a puppet alone does not make a chakra operator.
Starting situation: Aki's cracked carrier interrupts a clinic shipment. Repair it with actual timber/cloth/stamina or use genuinely learned compatible control with fitted linkage and actual reserve. A separately paid Toma service remains an alternative where the visitor lacks that source.
Ordinary paid loop: Paid net/carrier work funds ordinary fittings and clinic stock. Reserve the actual bench/operator and custody before delivery; completion settles one clinic-stock receipt. Original proof values remain packet fixtures, not manga formulas.
Connected pursuits: Immediate carrier work feeds a sheltered rack/clinic bench; middle specialty apprenticeship changes mission access; long contaminated-shipment, method-distribution and war-relief arcs decide a neutral compact or restricted patron.
Recurring people/anchors: Aki, Ren and Toma are the opening originals; Chika, Yae and Daigo supply courier, archive and rival means. Tsunade/Sakura, Lee/Guy and Kankuro/Sasori are verified bounded source anchors with era-specific status.
Career scope: Taijutsu/body, chakra control/technique, medicine, puppet engineer/operator, ordinary tools/seal research, reconnaissance and civilian supply/institution.
Era/parallel choices: O `part_one_side_story`, source-bounded `part_two_relief` and optional O predecessor history have different teachers/means. Neutral compact and restricted patron are later causal variants.
