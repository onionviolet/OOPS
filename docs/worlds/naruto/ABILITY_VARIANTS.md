## Comparison 1: reproduce the same demonstrated action

Task: observe one eligible demonstrated hand/action sequence, then attempt its supported function on a training target.
O `naruto.variant.deliberate_observation` and O `naruto.variant.taught_reconstruction` compare with a bounded Sharingan copying reference, not an invented universal eye implementation.
N5 locates Kakashi copying Zabuza's signs/jutsu. It does not settle every copying target, exclusion, permanence, eye stage or exact acquisition cost; NU2 owns those questions.

| Use | Deliberate observation | Taught reconstruction | Bounded Sharingan copy case |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | Notice visible segments and retain an incomplete study lead | Teacher explains an ordinary supported method; student practices parts | Actual acquired eye/source supplies the source-attested example, with no new canonical stage declared |
| Developed | Repeated clear evidence and paid tests yield an understood sequence | Tested explanation/body practice supports repeatable execution | Qualified observed method can be reproduced with the user's actual source/body/fuel where the inspected rule permits |
| Mature | Expert reconstructs more difficult visible ordinary actions | Skilled learner refines beyond the taught procedure through actual tests | A source-qualified rapid copier may be simply faster/better than ordinary study; full envelope awaits NU2 |

Observing, recording, understanding, owning a method and executing it are separate outputs. A copying animation should not award all five automatically.
Do not add “cannot copy any lineage technique” or “copies every jutsu” as a global law here. Both require exact scoped original-scene evidence.
A receiver without the required actual support can retain a copied method while failing execution. This is causal support checking, not an invented copying ban.
N11's body/speed example keeps trained physique real: seeing a movement does not supply muscle condition, reach, reaction or injury tolerance.
N2's nature training keeps control/nature qualification separate from a visible gesture; no recipe creates combined lineage merely by learning two natures.
N4 records an exceptional eye provenance/intervention. It is neither a repeatable free organ grant nor a universal permanent afterlife transfer procedure.
Observation costs O attention/time/stamina; reconstruction costs teacher/material/trial means; exact eye/fuel/vision costs remain NU2 rather than invented numbers.
Contact/evidence: freeze demonstrator, witnessed segments, visibility, evidence time and source revision. An unseen transition may remain a fragment.
Interruption of observation leaves actual observed segments; interrupted execution retains paid startup costs and its selected partial effect.
Donor keeps the learned method under ordinary observation/teaching. A source transfer requires a separate exact donor-loss/body contract.
Copied records retain what was seen, explained, inferred and tested. The donor's later development never silently upgrades the receiver's old record.
Suppression of the eye/source stops source-dependent copying; remembered methods and ordinary practice remain unless their own rule says otherwise.

## Copying by victory or sacrifice is a different acquisition

O comparator `conceptual.variant.earned_imprint` in [reference cases](../conceptual/REFERENCE_CASES.md) unlocks an eligible method after a verified defeat; it is not Naruto canon.
Kill-gated copying can require an actual eligible death without transferring the body/source; donor death, reward eligibility and copied output are three separate facts.
Sacrifice extraction can consume an actual owned token/specimen to acquire one declared function. Its stronger output can be real without becoming a universal organ factory.
Source theft can instead move a real faculty and remove donor execution, with compatibility, surgery/binding, history and restoration explicitly authored.
Do not reinterpret N7's human-puppet case as “kill anyone, copy all abilities.” NU3 requires exact retained-technique/living-core evidence.
Do not implement killing through a victory flag or a nonlethal native duel. Current lethal policy remains unselected; research can specify a later severe-profile gate.
The same learned technique can therefore arise from different acquisition routes, while different copied outcomes can share the word “copy.” UI must name the actual result.
All these O comparisons can be retained for future design without importing them into the current Naruto body/eye baseline.

## Comparison 2: deliver the same concentrated impact

Task: break one measured training block with an actual supported impact. The block's strength is fixed; output, control and body condition remain separate.
O modes compare trained bodily strike, a source-qualified chakra-enhanced strike and a sourced controlled-shape technique.
N1/N15 locate Rasengan's rotation/output/control and shape/nature development; N6 locates Sakura's trained control/strength. Exact effects and damage stay source-gated.

| Use | Trained bodily strike | Qualified control-enhanced strike | Qualified controlled-shape technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | Learned alignment uses actual muscle/stance and stamina | Compatible body and taught control apply the selected sourced enhancement | Learning rotation/output/control theory yields practice evidence, not a completed Rasengan |
| Developed | Practiced timing and conditioning produce more actual force | Trained control channels paid reserve into an eligible strike | Individually completed source-qualified method supplies its actual focused output |
| Mature | Exceptional supported body can surpass the same ordinary target | Mature actual control/source can simply deliver greater force than a weaker body | Further supported shape/nature development may change function, not merely rename a bigger damage number |

Theory acquisition and execution qualification are distinct; rotation success does not mark output/control mastered automatically.
A refinement can improve one facet while another remains weak. High reserve cannot compensate for an uncontrolled shape under the authored test.
Training output can be a stable rotation record, calibrated output trial or clean release, each with actual paid practice and retained evidence.
Body readiness controls repeated strain. Source Gates remain separately qualified N11/NU rules, not a free mature-use switch or purchased glove bonus.
Counters use actual reach, startup, body condition and selected defenses. Sufficient supported force really breaks the weaker block.
Interruption before clean release follows the selected method's cost/failure contract; no newly invented self-injury claim is passed off as canon.
Teaching the receiver's hand motions cannot copy the teacher's stored reserve, precise nature qualification, organ or accumulated bodily training.

## Comparison 3: retrieve the same injured person

Task: carry a living patient across the same broken walkway, preserving custody and known treatment needs. Retrieval is not cure.
O `naruto.variant.carry_guard` uses a trained body; O `naruto.variant.puppet_retrieve` uses the campaign's ordinary rescue puppet; qualified medical support treats selected conditions separately.
N7 supports powerful puppetry/craft in bounded source cases; our padded gripper, cradle, reach and payload are O, without a copied Sasori arsenal.

| Use | Carry Guard | Puppet Retrieve | Qualified medical support |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | One trained carrier supports a measured load within ordinary reach | Owned serviced chassis/control supports one practiced retrieval path | Ordinary care/supervised sourced control assesses one eligible treatment |
| Developed | Better movement protects patient and stock over uneven footing | Qualified operator directs gripper/cradle with real range/control and reserve | Learned method and actual supplies treat the selected condition after retrieval |
| Mature | Sufficient body strength/speed handles the crossing directly | Stronger supported puppet/operator can retrieve more safely or remotely and simply exceed the lower carrier | Superior sourced treatment can outperform mundane care on its eligible condition, without restoring every possible loss |

Puppet acquisition is craft/property; learned actuation/control is separate. Copying the operator's gesture creates neither puppet nor compatible chakra.
Remote contact is established by the actual gripper and path; a control-family tag supplies no unlimited range, spirit contact or through-wall reach.
O retrieval costs finite reserve and actual operator attention; replacing a damaged joint costs the retained part/material/labor, not a fresh puppet grant.
Control disruption stops the supported motion. Patient position, damaged mechanisms and spent stock remain; an emergency physical support needs real means.
Medical work needs actual diagnosis/body/stock. N6 proves a learned category, not a generic cure, resurrection or copyable antidote.
A trained body or genuinely superior technique may make the puppet unnecessary on this target. Another route need not receive a forced equal reward.
Two real puppets can cooperate under qualified channels/attention; general simultaneous target resolution remains a missing adapter.
N8's exceptional seal/life cost cannot be reduced to copied ink. Exact source seal methods and storage/containment remain NU4/NU8-gated.

## Mastery within one technique

O facets for the selected method: evidence segmentation, understanding, body alignment, channel control, output stability, efficiency and transition/recovery.
An actor can understand the whole pattern but still fail control; another can reliably perform a rehearsed narrow version without generalizing it.
Practice pays actual reserve/time, tests one facet and records a specific result; repeated identical trivial evidence need not award unlimited new insight.
Branch options can be precise low-waste execution, fast release or coordinated support, with actual demands rather than mandatory equal penalties.
Mature use may improve all three after real development. Branches are techniques/modes when source supports them, not compulsory permanent class exclusions.
A new functional threshold opens an observable action, such as safe supported interrupted recovery, rather than only a label or hidden stat increment.
Catalogue retains structural/body/source stages; this semantic subprogression belongs to each learned technique and its tests.

## Implementation gate and checkpoint

Existing command/power/knowledge/faculty/energy services are groundwork; definition-ID copy does not clone a donor organ, body or exact personal technique specimen.
Missing: chakra physiology/nature checks, eye evidence/retention rules, personal-method provenance, puppet path/contact/channel support and native jutsu combat.
Reject absent body/source/fuel, wrong target, unseen required evidence, occupied operator and stale specimen before cost; unknown facts stay private.
Legal paid failure retains observed segments/trial loss; interruption/reload preserves actual reserve, injury, puppet identity and current patient position.
Repeated copying cannot mint organ/puppet/source or duplicate unique acquisition; suppressed faculties restore without reacquiring ownership.
Failed persistence publishes neither paid practice nor new method; generated save/reload/import and rebuilt browser gates remain unrun.
