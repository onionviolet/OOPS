## Frozen completed-work baseline

- Work: Masashi Kishimoto's original *Naruto* manga, volumes 1-72, VIZ/Shueisha publication boundary. Author snapshot `naruto.original.1-72.primary.r1`.
- Completion `C`: [VIZ's volume 72 page](https://www.viz.com/manga-books/manga/naruto-volume-72/product/3500) supplies the last-volume endpoint; [VIZ's publication announcement](https://www.viz.com/blog/posts/2619) explicitly identifies it as the final volume. [Official vol.72 locator](https://naruto-official.com/en/comics/01_154) identifies chapters 691-700, corroborating the original 1-700 boundary without a full chapter audit.
- Inspected evidence is publisher metadata, official manga-referencing character/training columns and bounded official anime locators. This is not a reading of all 72 volumes.
- Exclude Boruto, anime filler, films, novels, licensed game mechanics and later one-shots by default. The 2023 Minato one-shot portions of otherwise useful columns are outside this baseline.
- Official anime summaries are `A` corroboration; chapter/volume locators in official manga columns support bounded `C` facts. Neither imports every omitted manga detail.
- A visitor is not automatically Naruto, an Uchiha, a jinchuriki, a village shinobi or an owner of a summoning contract. Entry body, actual source and identity must be authored.

Marks: `C` bounded primary verified; `A` official adaptation corroboration; `I` inference; `O` original branch addition/gameplay adaptation; `U` exact evidence unfinished. A canon-compatible label requires the relevant inspected claim, not name recognition.

## Acquisition distinctions for concrete designs

| Route | What is acquired | What must remain separately real |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary chakra/training | Tested control, practiced pattern and local nature applications | Compatible body/source, reserve, teacher and technique-specific constraints |
| Observation/copying | Information the actual faculty can perceive/encode | Eye/faculty, trained body, needed nature, energy and hidden prerequisites |
| Lineage/organ | Actual acquired source in one actual body | Provenance, compatibility, procedure, condition and consequences; no generic organ duplication |
| Clan tradition | Taught method and secrets under an actual relationship | A clan label does not prove every technique is genetically exclusive or openly taught |
| Puppet craft | Real frame, joints, mechanisms and trained control | Actual materials, repair/poison stock and operator capacity; human puppets are an exceptional branch |
| Medical work | Learned diagnosis/control, tested methods and clinical access | Actual patient condition, supplies, skill and limits; not universal resurrection |
| Sealing | Learned target-specific method and actual prepared support | Caster/body, energy, target/source, terms and potentially irreversible costs |
| Institution | Rank, mission/service access and duties | Recognition and orders in that instance; neither universal combat tier nor future memory |

Five basic elements and bloodline combinations require exact original-manga text before publishing an acquisition recipe. N2 supports a trained wind-nature example; it does not prove learning two natures grants every combined lineage. An official park game supplies no canon recipe.
No copied hand signs create a Sharingan, a tailed beast, an antidote or a completed puppet. They can supply a promising study lead. Conversely, don't invent a universal copying ban where a real faculty and supported body would work.
The visitor's imported stamina or magical source is not automatically chakra. A new conversion, altered body or copying faculty is an explicit original experiment with real costs and uncertainty.
Puppet/support projects are a stronger source-bounded assembly foundation than an unsourced powered suit. Later scientific ninja tools, artificial prosthetic systems and Boruto devices stay excluded unless independently verified within a new selected boundary.
Summoning requires a separate contract/entity/consent source question; don't fabricate a creature from learning the visible gesture. N13 verifies teaching, not a complete summoning recipe.
