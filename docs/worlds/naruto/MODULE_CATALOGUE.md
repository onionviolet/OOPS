# Naruto module catalogue: training, tools and puppet craft

Status: Develop/Research proposal r1, 2026-10-03. Sole local granularity owner. Proposed authoring detail only; none of these modules registers an engine power, equipment item or save field.
Read [campaign](CAMPAIGN.md), [entry brief](WORLD_BRIEF.md), [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md) and [shared assembly contract](../PROGRESSION_AND_ASSEMBLY.md). Local plots/institutions and canon adjudication remain with their separate owners.
Boundary: Masashi Kishimoto's completed original Naruto manga, volumes 1-72. Earlier and later eras remain separate snapshots. No Boruto scientific tools, game rarity ladders, anime filler or sequel patch is assumed.
N6: the [official Sakura column](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1743), with manga references including volumes 27-30, supports learned medical practice, chakra control and her developed physical output.
N7: the [official Sasori column](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1641), with volumes 30-31 and 55 references, supports crafted puppets, exceptional multi-puppet control and a body source that cannot be replaced by ordinary construction.
N11: the [official Rock Lee column](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1652) supports a substantial taijutsu route and real injury/recovery costs. His personal development is evidence for a distinct route, not a generic free Gate unlock.
Additional shape/nature evidence, alongside ledger N1/N2: [official Minato development column](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1521) distinguishes shape and nature research in its original-manga discussion. The [Kushina column](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1833), original vol.53 ch.500-504 material only, supports seal-target and life-cost constraints (ledger N8). Its sealing-teaching scene belongs to the excluded 2023 one-shot and provides no verified teaching/acquisition rule here.
Exact chakra-thread limits, elemental techniques, medical procedures, storage seals and clan organs require ledger primary chapter evidence before implementation. Official adaptation/game pages do not override the manga boundary.
P marks original training stages, ordinary tools, puppet designs and numerical budgets. A new puppet is source-compatible proposed craft, never a claim it appears in the manga.

Evidence marks inherit the ledger: C primary fact, A official adaptation corroboration, I inference, O original extension, U unresolved. P in this catalogue is explicitly an O proposal, never an official adaptation mark.
[Ability variants](../ABILITY_AUTHORING.md) permits stage/context differences and genuinely superior methods. The lower-cost roles illustrated here are concrete options, not a universal rule that every weak variant must survive or every mature method must pay a compensating penalty.

## Independent dimensions and actual sources

- Family IDs use `naruto.module.<family>` revision 1; each physical tool/puppet has its own proposed specimen identity.
- Structural stage is method/architecture: one control channel, separately controlled limbs or coordinated multiple puppets. It is not a shinobi rank or damage percentage.
- Grade/rarity records actual material/source category or scarcity; fictional game SS ratings are excluded from this manga design.
- Quality records carving, balance, joint tolerances and medicine batch evidence. Rare wood can still produce a poorly balanced puppet.
- Condition records joint wear, broken strings/linkages where applicable, contamination, injury and spent tool payload independently.
- Mastery records control, taijutsu, learned technique, surgery or puppetry. Buying a puppet or a scroll cannot grant execution practice.
- Source/body records actual chakra-supporting physiology, organ and acquired constituent. Imported outsider chakra compatibility is unknown until an explicit acquisition/test contract establishes it.
- Institution records village career, mission authorization, medical access and teacher permission. Chunin/jonin labels are not universal numerical power or procurement rights.
- Research separates observation, learned theory, source possession and executable technique. Watching an eye technique does not acquire the eye.
- A clan organ is body-bound source, not a generic purchasable attachment. A stolen/transplanted source needs actual compatible body, procedure, acquisition and consequences.
- Source-qualified bloodline combinations, tailed-beast access, Sage methods and exceptional bodily transformations keep their real prerequisites; this catalogue grants none by default.
- No fuel substitution turns a Fallout battery into chakra or a generic soul gem into a tailed beast. A conversion hypothesis starts as a paid unproven experiment.
- Technique methods can remain learned when injury, missing tools or absent source prevents execution. Restoring support does not require buying knowledge again.

### Central operating records

- Control / measured: actual qualified source and one taught output exercise support cheap repeatable precision. Larger reserve alone does not improve control.
- Control / maintained: practice sustained output on a specific task with observed failure. Fine medicine and puppet movement require different training evidence.
- Control / transitions: separately test switching and combination. Keeping a simple low-output method preserves endurance when complex work is unnecessary.
- Taijutsu / foundation: actual body and instruction enable movement/guard without a large tool supply. Injury still changes executable range.
- Taijutsu / specialist: practice retrieval-team timing or close defense, choosing role rather than simply leveling damage.
- Taijutsu / exceptional: earn a specific evidenced advanced method and disclosed body costs. The strongest damaging method is not an automatic idle training routine.
- Shape / known: learn one source-qualified method and pay its actual chakra. Copying appearance records observation, not complete method possession.
- Shape / controlled: adjust the tested envelope only where the method permits. A failed shape trial consumes training time/reserve and preserves evidence.
- Shape / research: qualified expert explores a new variation. An original technique remains P until its mechanics and source compatibility are selected.
- Nature / qualification: inspect actual source and teacher evidence. A good tool builder can remain useful without owning any elemental technique.
- Nature / specialist: learn one defined technique with reach, control and cost. More natures are separate acquisitions/training, not rarity upgrades.
- Nature / combined: N1/N2 supports deliberate shape/nature development, not automatic bloodline creation or every possible element pair.
- Chassis / simple: few joints make a cheap puppet light and field-repairable. It can retrieve cargo while a more complex unit is under repair.
- Chassis / dedicated: capture geometry and replaceable guard enable a new supported role. Large housing costs carry space and maneuver clearance.
- Chassis / paired: two independent units perform different jobs; each keeps identity, payload and wear. One controller needs actual qualified divided attention.
- Puppet control / single: qualified user learns one predictable puppet. Manual strings used at the bench are an ordinary test aid, not field chakra substitution.
- Puppet control / articulated: train independent movement and payload timing. A detachable hand does not automatically execute a learned ninjutsu.
- Puppet control / multiple: deliberately earned control allows coordinated roles. N7's exceptional hundred-puppet example establishes possibility, not a universal cap or ordinary entitlement.
- Payload / hook: cheap mechanical retrieval saves live-worker exposure and carries no poison obligation.
- Payload / capture: physical panel/cord containment changes positioning decisions. It does not automatically seal immortal or reincarnated targets.
- Payload / mission set: actual stored alternatives can be refitted at a safe workshop; replacement consumes fitting time, not a free battle menu.
- Medicine / ordinary: real bandages and observed injury triage stabilize supported cases; this does not perform chakra healing.
- Medicine / qualified: acquired method, body/control and practice permit a defined intervention; knowing anatomy is useful without extraordinary source output.
- Medicine / team: trained assistant and medicine stock expand simultaneous care. One medic is not counted as treating and controlling a puppet at the same time.
- Sealing / study: trace and interpret lawful record while paying teacher/material time; output is knowledge evidence, not an active seal.
- Sealing / execution: one verified record opens its supported function. Unknown target/source compatibility can make it fail despite perfect penmanship.
- Sealing / research: test a new bounded interaction only after actual constituent methods. A lethal source cost cannot be replaced by ordinary paper quality.

## Failure, counter and service

| Family | Proposed discovery/test and failure/counter | Repair/refit/salvage / irreversible edge |
|---|---|---|
| Control | Test steady small motion; reserve-rich novice still overshoots | Practice/rest source-supported body; equipment service does not raise control mastery |
| Taijutsu | Spar with known safety policy; reach/timing or injury defeats entry | Rest/source-qualified treatment; severe advanced bodily cost needs its actual loss contract |
| Shape | Controlled trial exposes incomplete method | Retain learned fragments/evidence; spent reserve/time stays spent |
| Nature | Qualified method tested against observed material/target | Learn correct support; more metal parts cannot create missing source or bloodline |
| Chassis | Sand jams joint; loaded retrieval cracks thin member | Clean/replace member, retain surviving frame; broken cargo and missed rescue may remain lost |
| Puppet control | Divided control lags or actual range/context fails | Practice or use one unit; intact puppet retains value when controller is unavailable |
| Payload | Jammed latch or empty launcher defeats action | Refit real cassette, recover only unused viable payload; fired/destroyed stock not refunded |
| Tools | Blade/line stock runs out or target counters ordinary reach | Resupply/sharpen source-compatible tools; throwing accuracy remains learned |
| Medicine | Wrong diagnosis, exhausted control or unknown toxin defeats treatment | Source-qualified consultation/testing; no generic cure or resurrection from workshop completion |
| Sealing | Unsupported target/source or incomplete record denies effect | Relearn/test under exact method; a sacrificed life/source cannot be repaired with fresh ink |
| Workshop | Sand contamination, absent teacher or denied mission access pauses work | Clean bay/pay alternate lawful access; stolen plans can retain their actual social consequence |

- Prototype control budgets describe the declared method, not a franchise-wide chakra equation or ten-finger cap.
- Repair restores surviving mechanical function. It does not reconstruct another shinobi's chakra nature or a human puppet's acquired source.
- Human puppetry/body replacement is an exceptional source-sensitive branch under N7, not the default recipe for salvageable enemies.
- Poison, forbidden surgery and lethal seals require exact primary evidence and selected consequences. Ordinary craft/medicine routes remain complete without them.
- Known safe carving/tool batches can idle with stock/site limits. Unknown body techniques, organ procedures and first live seal tests stay active commitments.

### Family salvage and irreversible constraints
- Chakra control: learned practice remains with the person; salvaging a puppet does not salvage chakra, and missing body/source needs actual restoration.
- Taijutsu: recover ordinary training gear separately; body injury or advanced method cost is not undone by purchasing higher-quality weights.
- Shape methods: retain learned fragments and paid failure evidence; no physical technique token is harvested from discarded trial material.
- Nature methods: retain actually learned nature research; source/body prerequisites remain, with no bloodline recovered from ordinary elemental residue.
- Puppet chassis: dismantle sound frame and joint pins after custody check; broken wood/pins retain condition and cannot also remain installed.
- Puppet control: control mastery remains personal; another controller needs their own source/training even when inheriting the same maintained puppet.
- Payload: recover only unspent safe usable fittings/stock; fired, destroyed or contaminated payload never becomes a full fresh magazine.
- Tools: sound blade/cord may be serviced; lost tool and consumed supply stay lost unless actually recovered in the continuing instance.
- Medicine: retain unused clean supply and diagnostic evidence; spent treatment is not fresh medicine, and failed patient opportunity need not be recoverable.
- Sealing: preserve intact lawful record for study; target, actual source and irreversible cost are not reconstructed from salvaged paper.
- Workshop: transfer owned spare stock, preserve bench lease and staff autonomy; forbidden/source-bound remains cannot silently enter ordinary crafting inventory.

## Three worked loadouts

### Poor specialist: tool maker and close-guard courier
- Owns ordinary blade, cord, pouch, bandages, light workwear and hand tools; learns taijutsu foundation and ordinary repair, with chakra execution only if actual source qualifies.
- Inputs: lawful wood/metal/cloth, one borrowed bench and paid training. No eye, elemental technique, sealing storage or medical ninjutsu granted.
- Budget P: six carry units, one repaired tool per work interval, finite food/rest and two bandage sets.
- Action unlocked: fix a mission tool, move a small medical parcel and defend a known close approach with practiced ordinary means.
- Decision: carry extra repair parts or spare projectile stock, changing support versus defense capacity.
- Conflict: heavy guard hinders fine carving; leave it in named storage or accept slower work.
- Counterplay: distant superior ninjutsu, injury or seized mission route can overwhelm this specialist; ordinary support remains useful to stronger teammates.
- Replacement: use simple joint pins and rope that local craft can repair; buy training before spending all stock on a beautiful puppet.
- Lasting result: verified tool repair and specific client memory; consumed supplies and missed accepted delivery remain actual consequences.

### Developed field build: retrieval puppeteer
- Owns first Dune puppet, hook/panel alternatives, spare pins and finite sourced tools; has actual chakra support and separately practiced single/articulated control.
- Acquisition: build/service puppet, learn control, test safe reach/load and practice retrieval. Gear ownership never supplies control proficiency.
- Budget P: twenty-four reserve, single-unit movement plus retrieval costs, eight carry units and one workshop refit window before departure.
- Action unlocked: recover a trapped parcel or retrieve a person under defined load while staying outside the immediate mechanical hazard.
- Decision: fit hook for access or panel for protection; neither makes the puppet a universal healer, sensor or seal.
- Conflict: optional launcher removes cargo margin; a dedicated taijutsu partner can defend while the controller handles retrieval.
- Counterplay: joint jamming, controller interruption, source-qualified control counter or fixed superior foe defeats different parts of the chain.
- Replacement: use one simple unit when reserve/control is inadequate; swap actual damaged limb at safe halt, retaining frame identity.
- Lasting result: field-tested method, used puppet and evidence of rescue; damaged stock or failed rescue can permanently alter local relationships.

### Mature project: paired recovery team and teaching workshop
- Owns both Dune units, real payload alternatives, clean repair bay and medicine stocks; employs independently trained medic and carrier under actual contracts.
- Controller earns multi-unit qualification; a separate source-qualified nature/shape specialist or exceptional taijutsu member is an acquired team option, never a default perk.
- Budget P: two staffed work areas, twenty-four controller reserve with qualified divided-control envelope, twelve personal cargo units plus paid carrier, finite medical materials.
- Action unlocked: coordinate defense, cargo recovery and treatment at once through different real actors, surpassing the poor courier's hazard envelope.
- Decision: teach proven simple chassis/control for village reliability or keep mature coordination private while funding additional field stock.
- Conflict: medicinal research and contaminated puppet repair cannot share the clean treatment area; protect capacity or risk losing the next medicine batch.
- Counterplay: stronger canon threat, specific source disruption, poisoned stock or loss of mission authorization can still block success; rank is no immunity.
- Replacement: keep cheap single unit for narrow access; invest in another trained medic or carrier instead of an ever-larger puppet collection.
- Lasting result: qualified personal methods, real maintained gear and institution relationship; staff retain their own sources and leave under their actual terms.

## Worked craft/upgrade chain and imported failure

1. Gather lawful dry wood, buy pins/cloth and inspect actual frame dimensions. Reserve material and carving bench; chakra qualification is checked separately.
2. Research retrieval geometry and taught single-unit control; pay teacher time and keep observed technique separate from executable mastery.
3. Produce frame/joints/hook and ordinary repair covers. Test with manual unloaded support before a source-powered trial.
4. P failed test: sand cover restricts joint travel and hook overshoots cargo. Consume test interval/reserve and bend a cheap pin; preserve that failure evidence.
5. Refit cover/pin and retest the same frame. Buying a new control scroll does not erase the controller's need to practice timing.
6. Upgrade into paired roles only after acquiring second frame, actual qualified divided control and separate medicine support. Never infer a clan organ from the upgrade.
7. Use in a committed rescue/delivery, paying motion, payload and treatment supplies. Missing medic can leave retrieval successful but patient outcome unresolved.
8. Lasting consequence: maintained puppet identities, tested control and witnessed service; spent reserve, bent pin and missed patient opportunity do not reset.
Hybrid P: import Fallout-made joint bearings; pay one specimen, one fit/temperature comparison and one sand trial. Better tolerances can help ordinary motion if qualified.
Failed hybrid P: connect an electrical motor to the puppet and ask it to execute a chakra technique autonomously. Mechanical movement supplies no chakra body, learned technique or judgment; retain motor and paid failed evidence.
Second hybrid P: use Skyrim dry-storage craft for medicine boxes. Test contamination and local material behavior; weatherproof storage cannot replace medical diagnosis or a verified seal.
Unsupported body/organ combinations remain rejected or explicitly unknown, with owned knowledge preserved. No failed test manufactures training for free.
