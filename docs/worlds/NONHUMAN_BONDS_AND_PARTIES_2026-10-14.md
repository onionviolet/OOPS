## 2. Nine bond kinds and the means partners lend

Kind describes a relationship, not anatomy or permanent authority. Kinds can stack under RM4: a mount can be guardian and friend; a wielder can be temporary carrier without owner title. The grants below are **possible offers**, not free functions. Each offer needs provider functions, agreement or an actual command mechanism, location/channel, real inputs, duration and a beneficiary.

| Code / kind | What the nonhuman side can grant | What the counterpart can grant | Default + Dial + Reasonably per world |
|---|---|---|---|
| BKD1 Familiar | Scouting, warning, chosen sensory messages, spell relay or shared faculty **if sourced** | Lend grip through a joint action, carry, speak publicly, provide compatible meals/energy, tend or repair anchor | Default cooperative link. Dial remote control, independent spirit or ritual bond. Reasonably separate perception sharing, advice, casting and motor control; NM4/NM9, BK10. |
| BKD2 Pet | Company, signals, watch, scent search or retrieval from actual training | Shelter, carry, feed, speak for a known request, fitted gear/care; repair artificial pet parts where applicable | Default household care and meaningful local choices. Dial ordinary animal, awakened person or constrained custody. Reasonably no magic channel from the word pet; BF6/BF7, BK1/BK10/BK15. |
| BKD3 Mount | Carry passenger/cargo, locomotion, scent/navigation or joint combat positioning | Saddle/harness fitting, route cues, feed, groom, heal or repair construct mount; speak/handle papers for the pair | Default compatible rider/load. Dial peer rider, trained command, vehicle-like construct or giant carrier. Reasonably passenger has no walking power loan; capacity, access and each contribution remain real. |
| BKD4 Wielder | Own edge/property, guidance, resonance or loaned technique under its rule | Hands and bodily execution, carry in a sheath, public speech, commission repair, supply declared energy | Default item and wielder are separate actors. Dial autonomous blade, compelled bearer or hired holder. Reasonably unique blade is one body/item; wielding does not transfer its mind or the wielder's XP. BK6/NM6. |
| BKD5 Host | A body channel, senses, grip/speech or temporary executive control under specific terms | Guest may lend knowledge/advice, healing or faculty, supply a compatible source, help repair host/vessel | Default negotiated occupancy; record who is host and guest. Dial shared control, remote vessel, hostile possession or replacement premise. Reasonably occupancy does not disclose private memory or grant owner title. BK5/NM12. |
| BKD6 Summoner | Manifestation service, scouting, travel or fighting within actual link/range | Invocation/return route, anchor, compatible energy, communication and maintenance | Default finite service terms. Dial autonomous contracted being, remote puppet, persistent summon or one-time call. Reasonably dismissing a body and killing a person are distinct; identity on re-summoning needs a world rule. |
| BKD7 Symbiont | Senses, organ support, protection, nutrient processing or sourced faculty | Habitat/transport, compatible inputs, signals and care; specialist separation/repair | Default two identifiable participants where two minds exist. Dial mutual dependency, weak coupling, parasite or fusion. Reasonably bodily interdependence does not force equal trust, shared control or universally lethal separation. NM12. |
| BKD8 Owner | The held being/object may grant services through willingness, training or actual coercive mechanism | Custody, shelter, supply, maintenance, access and agent transactions within title/terms | Default keep legal claim, actual holder, controller and regard separate. | BKD9 Guardian | Signals, participation in learning, suitable chores, mutual companionship or protection when capable | Care time, carry, feed, public advocacy, supervision and treatment/repair; legal representation where valid | Default named capable caregiver with their own commitments. Dial family, chosen guardian, institutional care or guardian beast. Reasonably dependence can lessen/change with maturation; guardianship does not make the ward's decisions vanish. BK12/X06. |

**Lending hands is delegated execution:** a speaking sword asks a smith to bind its wrap; the smith uses their grip, skill and materials while the sword supplies its own instruction or power. A shared-control lease can instead let the guest execute through an occupied hand, but that is E8 with explicit bodily practice/permission. Merely being friends should not make the sword's action evaluator report that its metal body has two hands.

**Speaking for you is scoped representation:** record the intended message, intermediary, recipient and delivered wording. A translator may misunderstand; a trusted intermediary may deliberately choose another reply. Private telepathy does not automatically become public speech. Feeding consumes compatible real stock and grants its selected meal/support benefit; repair changes the actual damaged body through a qualified method. N5 should make offers change through lived trust and terms, rather than convert trust into anatomy.

## 3. Bond as state: regard, terms and consequences

Default: use existing directed relationship edges for personal regard, with a proposed versioned typed tie/support record linked by stable IDs. Dial: adapt existing chronicle ties or a shared relationship owner when selected. Reasonably: do not force a store merge before one bounded offer; part 29 RQ1/RQ9/RQ15 owns that decision. The notation below is an authoring contract, not a supported save schema.

| Code | State slice | Reader and change |
|---|---|---|
| BS1 Participants and kinds | Person/life/world-instance IDs, body references only where function-specific; several bond kinds, establishment cause and known identity basis | Recognition follows actual observation; a new body can keep the same person's history without automatically convincing others. NT5/RCL1. |
| BS2 Directed regard | Each participant's trust/familiarity/fear/respect and memories only where used | Provider's regard of recipient can affect an offer. Recipient's trust in provider is not the same edge. Two numbers need not match; RQ8/RM9. |
| BS3 Dependence | Direction, needed support, available reserves/routes, provider capacity and substitutes | “Needs a carrier to leave this room” is a function dependency, not low obedience. A tree may rely on gardener trade while the gardener relies on its shade. |
| BS4 Obedience and partnership | Scope of orders, trained responses, free agreement, compulsion source/expiry and refusals; decision policy | A command succeeds by the selected authority/mechanism. High obedience need not mean affection; cooperative partnership may include refusal. RHE1/RHE6. |
| BS5 Support offers | Provider/recipient, method/functions, consent or control basis, inputs/payer, destination/audience, interval, expiry/withdrawal and completion settlement | Quote and commit recheck means; sustained work rechecks at fitting boundaries. Trust may open carrying, grip assistance or advice without granting permanent use. |
| BS6 Disagreement | Known request, response, reason/evidence, changed policy and still-valid duties | Refuse dangerous route, propose shorter duty, ask for pay, stay home or continue another job. No compulsory betrayal roll or loss of all unrelated support. |
| BS7 Breakup | Active/paused/estranged/ended, cause/time, contact boundary, loan return, control release, remaining care/contract duties | End the kind or offer, retain history. A calm handoff can preserve trust, as BF6 illustrates. Reconciliation is a new cause, not deleting the ending. |
| BS8 Partner loss | Actual life/body outcome, learned notification, survivor's history, canceled/paused support, custody/estate, recovery/continuation route | Injured, departed, dead, dismissed and missing differ. Unknown loss stays unknown to actors. Mourning may be narrative, not a mandatory stat debuff; RHE4/RCL8. |

**N5 death walk, INFERRED:** a blade's wielder dies. Revoke that wielder's grip/carry/speech offers; stop dependent work and clear held guard/control at a declared boundary. Blade remains at its actual location with its actual holder/custody or estate claim, not automatically in the player's pocket. Its own local perception/study may continue if supported. A witnessed loss can produce mourning; a distant blade receives news through a real route. Another person can find, carry or bond with it, keeping old history. No automatic affection, estate title or combat mastery transfers.

Dial: a source may tether a summon to its summoner, preserve an anchor after host death, permit revival, or genuinely end a linked life. Reasonably publish known stakes when selecting that premise; no universal partner-death cascade. If all channels are lost, a coherent continuation can be waiting/dormancy, an authored rescue, a remains/estate chapter or a new LS3 seat. Do not conjure a rescuer or force human transformation to rescue an unsupported design. Persistence and meaningful fallback should be designed before offering the start.

## 5. Mixed parties: roles follow functions (N21)

Default: each task names roles and assigns a real executor with fitting body functions, skill/method, availability and willingness. Dial: cooperative planning, leader commands, autonomous decisions or shared controller. Reasonably a role is an assignment, not a species class: a wolf can speak if it has sourced speech, a human can fly if equipped for it, and a construct can be a medic with actual means.

| Code / domain | Example task decomposition | Costs, control and missing means |
|---|---|---|
| BR1 Combat | Beast tracks/bites; ghost warns through a perceptual/message channel; human uses grip/shield; blade enables its own edge while holder executes a strike | Separate attacker, equipment/body contributor, targetable bodies, hit channels, effort and credit. A ghost needs a sourced material attack to damage a material enemy. One sword/wielder strike is not automatically two attacks. |
| BR2 Travel | Mount moves and carries fitting load; rider navigates/speaks at checkpoint; small scout examines a gap; spirit crosses a ward only under local admission | Movement mode, passenger/cargo fit, route, carrier willingness, habitat and destination admission per participant. BF4's braced contest already demonstrates a guard role with actual bodily support, ownership and withdrawal. Extending that idea means removing hard-coded Lio/player-only action routing under an explicit combat contract, not renaming him wolf and declaring body combat complete. Target selection must handle a wielder carrying a sentient weapon and a host sharing a body without duplicate HP bars or inconsistent death. E11 owns the actual combat consumers.

Loot, wages and experience should name recipients by agreement and contribution. Default own purse and earned practice; Dial shared purse, equal split, contract wages, sourced shared XP or communal household. Reasonably temporary party membership does not transfer personal property, obligations, knowledge or rank. A party can dissolve while two members retain a friendship or bond.

## 6. NPC nonhumans under the same rules (U10)

Default: NPC hound, mount, spirit, blade and guardian use the same relevant body-function, fit, communication, effort, ownership, defeat and support checks as the corresponding player bodies. Dial: lower simulation detail, small authored policy or source-specific powers. Reasonably those powers have declared provenance rather than hidden free hands, fuel or information because the NPC is offscreen.

BF6 proves a bounded NPC dog route uses actual jaws/legs, satiety/stamina, location and willingness; it does not prove every NPC body is generalized. BF7 additionally binds its identity and learned results. Keep those useful constraints while deciding whether its exact four-leg requirement is workshop-content policy or should be generalized to an adequate movement function under E2.

An NPC companion can initiate help, ask to rest, disagree, leave or maintain a prior commitment with a recorded cause. Switching from player seat to NPC policy changes decision source, not the person's identity. An awakened pet may need respect, debt, grievance or partnership axes where content uses them; RM15's reduced set is a simple animal default, not a hard ceiling on nonhuman minds. A commanded puppet without an independent mind does not need invented affection, while an autonomous summon does not lose agency merely because it is summoned.
