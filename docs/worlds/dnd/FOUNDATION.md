# D&D long-term world foundation

2026-10-11, revision 1. Research and design packet, not accepted fiction or implemented gameplay. Local integrator: fresh D&D GM. [Assignment](../../gm/dispatch/dnd-world-2026-10-11/ASSIGNMENT.md), [checkpoint](../../gm/dispatch/dnd-world-2026-10-11/CHECKPOINT.md).

**D1 recommendation:** develop Forgotten Realms as a durable world family, with SRD 5.2.1 as the first proposed rules profile. Start with Phandalin and a short surrounding route. Let ordinary work, magical study and expedition preparation all affect the same local problem. Keep Harry Potter and held novels' near-term prioritization with their separate owners.

**D2 latest authority:** Weibao explicitly allowed downloading sources and raised different rulebooks for different eras. Three official PDFs were downloaded outside git; [source contract and receipts](SOURCE_CONTRACT.md) distinguish acquired from inspected. Multiple editions and eras are supported design directions, not implemented selectors. Other assignment exclusions remain: no gameplay code, shared-owner writes, commits, publishing, library changes, recurring work or messages to other chats.

**D3 composition proposal:** use independent axes: source family `dnd`; setting `forgotten-realms`; continuity branch; setting era; region; rules profile; supplements; origin; initial situation; adaptation policy. These are proposed identifiers, not registered runtime IDs. An ancient era can use revised fifth edition, or an explicitly selected older rules profile. An edition is not an in-world date. Bind every world instance and durable effect to its exact profile revision; do not apply new errata to existing saves silently. See [edition and era contract](SOURCE_CONTRACT.md#edition-and-era-contract).

The fantasy is living as a person with specific means in a large magical society, rather than collecting every famous class ability. Craft, care, negotiation, study, exploration and combat should produce different routes through the same conditions. Spell knowledge, casting capacity, components, actual targets, social permission and consequences stay separate. Learning a rumor about a spell grants no casting faculty.

First entry is an authored branch after Phandalin's rebuilding, using only the public setting anchors in S04. Its exact DR year and canonical event schedule remain unresolved. Do not advertise a faithful adventure-era simulation until the selected setting chapter is inspected. No protagonist role or plot completion is assumed. Existing canon normally continues; any local invention has an explicit branch ledger rather than silently rewriting source history.

The packet contains a [whole-world coverage map](COVERAGE_MAP.md), [first slice with engine gaps](FIRST_SLICE.md), and [source contract](SOURCE_CONTRACT.md). All numbers, new people, starts, job terms and local events below are proposals. No sealed truth is defined or revealed in this public packet; later scenario truth and trigger data need a separate sealed owner before player-facing projections.

**F1 evidence:** official SRD 5.2.1, SRD 5.1, conversion guide, setting overview and Phandalin overview inspected within the bounds recorded in the ledger. Prior D&D wiki digest remains blocked, not evidence. Selected actual engine source was inspected; no engine or browser verification was run.

**R1 remaining gate:** choose the first profile/setting snapshot and verify its origin, local economy and event details against exact source sections. This research packet is decision-ready; playable delivery remains a later, separately assigned implementation and fresh-origin browser/save gate.
