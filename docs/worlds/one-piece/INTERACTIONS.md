## Interaction 1: Medicine delivery

**Place/time:** harbor office, late morning, with storm clouds visible. **Participants:** player, Neri, Toma or Sela if available. **Known state:** one medicine delivery has a real deadline, the crate is waiting under custody, and a damaged tide ledger may provide route evidence.

Neri asks whether the player can deliver the crate. The player can fund Toma's repair and carry it by skiff, pay Sela for a shorter route and witness, or decline. Each route spends a different resource and produces different knowledge. Resolve a crossing as a bounded authored outcome, not a sea simulation; the storm is the deadline. If the player leaves, the delivery window closes and someone else may claim the berth. The characters do not learn the player's undisclosed powers.

## Interaction 2: The doubtful hull

**Place/time:** shipyard slip, before the client commits to a crossing. **Participants:** player and Toma; Neri may file the report. **Known state:** actual cracked fitting and the repair hand's scoped inspection.

The player can publish the defect, arrange the material-intensive repair, or take payment for a temporary patch. The client may refuse the delay. Actual hull state determines the next trip; a receipt cannot certify safety. This fictional setup takes its evidence boundary from Water 7 Arc rev. 2118675, where failed repair attempts and an unfit voyage are distinguished.

## Interaction 3: Berth offer

**Place/time:** convoy dock at departure. **Participants:** player and Rusk, with the person who owns the berth. **Known state:** one passage, one watch, and finite ration shares.

Accepting consumes the berth and creates the stated watch obligation; negotiate only if the issuer agrees; refuse and lose this crossing without penalty debt. If the danger is overwhelming, the crew may fail. Do not guarantee escape or rescue. The player can use a compatible method at its genuine strength, but the characters respond only to what they observe.
