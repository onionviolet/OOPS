## Authority and evidence

Weibao is creative director. This world pack is Develop/Research. All local campaign and game mechanics are proposals until he accepts them; this pack creates no runtime authority. Keep W secondary wiki claims distinct from manga-attributed assertions and from direct primary checks. Never use memory to fill a fact after the frozen snapshot. Preserve visible contradictions and underlying medium labels.

The frozen snapshot does not establish an ending. Do not write an ending, post-snapshot event, character status or reveal as fact. Public franchise twists can appear as public source truth. Any changed, randomized or alternate-history truth is a sealed slot; refer to it by ID, discovery route and status outside the discovery path.

## World-specific decisions

- Keep Devil Fruit source, bearer, ability function and swimming cost distinct from Haki training and reserve.
- Treat ability conditions as exact: actor, trigger, target, range, medium, resource, counter and persistence. P2/P3 from SPOTCHECK_1.md require checking actor attribution and conditions before using lane notes.
- Seastone, ordinary water, Armament, fruit-specific material counters and Haki resistance are not interchangeable global nullifiers.
- Keep ships, crews, food, medicine, information, custody and authority meaningful. A powerful fighter can still lack a viable route or safe hull.
- Give severe enemies their fixed authored strength. Allow a genuinely sufficient imported capability to work without invented balance immunity; do not guarantee escape, victory or recovery.
- Make refusal a real option. An offer creates no debt until accepted. Abstention can still let an opportunity expire or an independent actor succeed/fail.
- Distinguish a physical visit from a historical branch, replacement body, death/rebirth or resurrection. None is implied by a chart or ship.
- Keep ordinary livelihood useful and finish one playable slice before adding more sagas.

## Scene and cast rules

Each recurring person gets a goal, means, limits, availability, actual knowledge and concrete memory. A report reveals its evidence and observer, not author truth. Track cause, occurrence, discovery, response, obligation, cost and aftermath. A character speaks only from what they know in the selected snapshot. Do not imitate long copyrighted dialogue or place source prose in tracked files.

The pack's five opening people and three interaction briefs are invented. Source characters can enter only after era, location, status, current obligations and source version are checked. A profile label or canonical fame is not a substitute.

## Recovery and handoff

On resume, reread current owners and working state before writing. Preserve unrelated dirty state. One writer owns a file area. A handoff links owners rather than duplicating their specs. State whether work is proposal, implemented, rebuilt, browser-exercised, persisted/imported, human-accepted or committed. Never claim the next stage is gameplay-complete based on setup or design text alone.
