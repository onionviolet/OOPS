Status: Develop/Research world-pack proposal, 2026-10-15. This pack turns the dated One Piece Fandom snapshot and existing start/AU palette into implementation-ready proposals. It is not accepted gameplay, a primary canon audit, or a claim that the source story has reached an ending.

**Proposal identity:** family `fan.one_piece`; example continuity `one_piece.chartmaker.branch`; example era `east_blue_opening`; example region `foosha_coast`; entry `harbor_apprentice`; scenario `missing_tide-ledger`. These are authoring labels, not registered IDs or save bindings.

**Core experience proposal:** live in a world where a meal, a repair, a route, a reputation, a promise, and a fight all depend on who actually has the means to act. A Devil Fruit is a lasting, individual gift with a swimming cost; Haki is a distinct trained will-based path; local knowledge and logistics can matter as much as either.

**First playable slice proposal:** one East Blue harbor needs a medicine crate delivered with route evidence before a storm closes the delivery window. Repair the skiff and carry the crate using owned materials and time, hire an available navigator for a shorter witnessed crossing, or refuse and let the window close. The choice changes cost, custody, knowledge and obligation. No canon character is required to appear. A damaged tide ledger may be an optional clue for establishing route evidence, not a second cargo objective; food and a pirate shortcut are deferred proposals.

**Sea-scope boundary:** this slice has no general sea simulation, map traversal, weather model, navigation skill check, or open-water encounter loop. Each crossing is one authored, bounded resolution with its stated cost, time, evidence and outcome. The storm is a deadline state, not simulated weather. A later sea system needs its own proposal and acceptance gate.

## Boundary and direction

One Piece is in the canon-first tier. This pack provides a wide proposal palette and a small first slice; it does not make a long campaign the first milestone. Keep the initial implementation to one realm and one prestige layer. Finish one playable slice before expanding into later sagas.

The source is the frozen Fandom S3 snapshot described in the [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md), not a complete primary manga read. Snapshot claims are secondary evidence; their cited manga, SBS, guidebook, novel, anime, film and game sources stay distinct. The snapshot's latest underlying page dates are in August 2026. Anything later than its frozen pages, including the world-depth note's 2026-10-11 chapter-1195 status, is an explicit gap.

All campaign, module, branch and interaction rows in this folder are `O: proposal`. Page-backed claims are `W: dated wiki assertion`, not promoted canon. `I` denotes inference and `U` unresolved. Specific story twists already public in the franchise are not sealed. Any altered or randomized truth must be a sealed slot in the discovery route; reports may name its ID, route and status only.

## Living and visiting

A new run may enter directly at a compatible start; Frontier is not a required route. A later in-world invitation, chart, ship, carrier or hub can also open travel if that route has a real owner, cost and return condition. Re-entry returns to the same committed instance unless the character selects a separately authored branch. An earlier era is not a rewind of an existing life.

A visit declares body, learned methods, actual sources and remaining fuel, cargo, companions, obligations, return route and absence policy. Imported powers retain ownership; they execute only when their real support and compatibility exist. The first slice needs no Devil Fruit or Haki grant and should remain useful to an ordinary visitor.
