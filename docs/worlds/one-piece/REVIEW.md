# One Piece world-pack independent review

**Verdict: usable with fixes** as a dated, secondary-source design proposal. The nine engine-rule rows in `SYNTHESIS.md` have direct support in at least one frozen private payload each. They are not primary manga authentication or playable acceptance. The first slice needs one consistent specification, and several ability conditions need to be carried into the variants before implementation.

**Method and locator key.** I read `AGENTS.md`, the source deep-dive playbook checklist, both `SPOTCHECK` reports, the pack, and the cited shard summaries. For the checks below I opened the private `read_file` gzip selected by *title and revision* in `docs/research/power-systems/wiki-digests/full-dumps/onepiece_SOURCES.json`. `P: Title rID @offset` means a zero-based character offset in that payload's `text`, not a note or a live wiki page. The quoted chapter numbers below are citations embedded in the wiki payload, not independently read primary chapters. “Supported” means supported by that dated wiki text; “qualified” identifies an inference, mixed source, or omitted condition. I sampled ten further claims, marked S1–S10, from the three requested files.

## Fixes needed, in priority order

1. **F1, settle the first playable slice across owners before handing it to an implementer.** `WORLD_BRIEF.md:22` offers food and a pirate shortcut; `SYNTHESIS.md:100`, `CAMPAIGN.md:57–59`, `INTERACTIONS.md:30–34` and `IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md:7–20` define a medicine crate, two routes and refusal. Pick one cargo and route set, and specify what “no sea system” means when both positive routes cross water. This is an internal design conflict, not a canon dispute.
2. **F2, retain source conditions in the ability variants.** Add Hobi's immediate manual contract window and free-will escape case, Kage's donor coma, and the precise Rumble Ball later timing distinction. The broad engine table is sound, but these conditions change state, recovery and tests. See S3–S5 and shard C `SUMMARY.md:24`.
3. **F3, expose the Nikyu contradiction as unresolved.** The same private page lists illnesses among pushed targets at @3166 and says diseases such as a named case apparently cannot be expelled at @8968. A generic disease-removal implementation would overclaim. Keep the conflict in the ledger/variant and make any future case a primary-source gate. Shard C already flags this at `SUMMARY.md:29`.
4. **F4, keep claim-level medium and primary gates when selecting powered content.** The pack generally separates manga, anime, film, game and version-specific assertions, and marks wiki material W. Some aggregate W2/W8/W13 rows still mix manga, SBS, guidebook, anime or live-action citations. Before a specific power, cast event or historical era becomes content, identify that claim's underlying medium and directly inspect the cited primary scene; `SYNTHESIS.md:106–111` already records most of this gap.

## Further findings and limits

- **Proposals and sealed secrets:** O labels are consistent throughout the pack. The proposed AU/start palette names no changed or randomized truth outside an opaque slot and route. No sealed-secret disclosure found. Public franchise facts are treated as public. `AGENTS.md`'s rule remains the gate when a future branch actually changes a truth.
- **Continuity:** Anime, films, games, novels, attraction rules, manga-associated claims, and the weekly/volume difference are kept separate in the reviewed rows. The pack does not promote a post-snapshot ending or chapter status into source fact. This is a pack-label review, not an exhaustive audit of every wiki citation in 5,096 selections.
- **Source prose:** No long quotation found in the pack's nine Markdown files. A normalized exact 16-word overlap scan against the 17 directly inspected payloads found zero matches; this does not exhaustively compare every uninspected payload. The inspected prose is paraphrased, with no observed source quotation over 15 words.
- **Missed source-supported mechanics:** Hobi's post-transformation contract timing/free-will/loophole state and Kage's donor coma are immediately relevant to persistence tests. The Ope page also distinguishes swapping two targets from moving an object within ROOM without an exchange (P: Ope Ope no Mi r2121122 @3918); the pack's “no generic teleport” boundary is sensible, but a future ROOM implementation must not erase that distinction. Nikyu's disease tension belongs in the source ledger, not only a generic “individual cases” gap.
- **Honest open gaps:** `SYNTHESIS.md:104–112` already names primary manga checks, source-specific conditions, era/cast availability, local sea economics, missing M3 survey, shared state ownership, and completion coverage. These are real design-changing gaps. No build, browser exercise, save/import, or human acceptance was part of this review or the source pack.

## Fixes applied

1. **F1, first playable slice reconciled.** Standardized the proposal on one medicine crate, two routes (repair and carry; hire a navigator), and refusal without debt. The tide ledger is optional route evidence; food cargo and the pirate shortcut are deferred. Defined “no sea system” as no general sea, weather or navigation simulation: each crossing is one bounded authored resolution and the storm is only a deadline. Files touched: `WORLD_BRIEF.md`, `CAMPAIGN.md`, `INTERACTIONS.md`, `IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`.
2. **F2, conditions retained.** Added Hobi's immediate manual contract window, escape with retained free will and loophole qualification; Kage's two-day donor coma; and distinct early three-dose, trained three-minute and later enhanced 30-minute Rumble Ball states. The later aftereffect duration and safe repeat interval remain unknown. Files touched: `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md`, `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`. Confirmed against frozen Hobi r2129333, Kage r2122819 and Rumble Ball r2109438 payloads; shard C summary corroborates.
3. **F3, Nikyu contradiction exposed.** The variant and W7 ledger row now state that the same page lists illnesses among removable targets while saying diseases such as Sapphire Scales apparently cannot be expelled. Generic disease removal or immunity is not specified; a named case requires primary-scene verification. Files touched: `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md`, `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`. Confirmed against Nikyu r2118422 and shard C summary.
4. **F4, claim-level source gates made explicit.** Before powered content selection, require a claim-level record of asserted rule, medium/version and cited primary scene, then inspect that scene. Mixed-medium aggregate rows do not authenticate one another. File touched: `SYNTHESIS.md`.
5. **Additional source-supported distinction added.** ROOM exchange of two targets is kept distinct from moving an object within the field without exchange. Files touched: `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md`, `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`. Confirmed against Ope r2121122 and shard C summary.

**Declined:** none of the review's fixes or its source-supported missed mechanics were declined. No runtime behavior, canon truth, or proposal status was promoted by these document changes. Primary source authentication and playable acceptance remain open.
