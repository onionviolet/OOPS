# One Piece module catalogue (proposal)

Status: Develop/Research. Every module name, track, stage and acquisition route below is an authoring proposal (`O`), not a registered game module or canon-wide progression ladder. Each input law remains tied to its page and revision in [SOURCE_LEDGER](SOURCE_LEDGER.md). Use shared [ability authoring](../ABILITY_AUTHORING.md) and [progression/assembly](../PROGRESSION_AND_ASSEMBLY.md).

## Independent state dimensions

- **Body and source:** ordinary human/body condition, a specific Devil Fruit grant, learned Haki, lineage or another independently sourced capability are distinct. Do not infer mixed heritage.
- **Method and mastery:** a power's function, user's training, reserve, accuracy, range and situational judgment are separate records.
- **Gear and custody:** weapon, ship, chart, Seastone restraint, medicine and crafted housing have actual owners, condition and location. Equipment does not grant the previous owner's power.
- **Standing and authority:** crew position, Marine role, bounty, faction or legal status changes access only through an actual institution or actor.
- **Knowledge and interpretation:** observation, copied record, translation, hypothesis and character belief do not collapse to omniscience.
- **Continuity and medium:** manga-associated, SBS, guidebook, novel, anime, film, game and attraction claims keep their cited medium and scope.

A body can be too tired or restrained to execute a known method while the method remains learned. A useful ordinary career is always available; no generic XP ladder is required.

## Proposed staged development

A module advances only when a source-qualified event or authored practice supplies the relevant evidence. `O` stages for future scenario content:

1. **Usable:** can safely attempt one function and describe the conditions known to the actor.
2. **Practiced:** has repeated it with meaningful control under a specific condition.
3. **Specialized:** has a new supported application, target or method with its own gate/cost.
4. **Exceptional:** named user/source evidence supports this exact advanced use; it is not a universal level reward.

This is a content organization aid, not a universal power rank or multiplier. A weakly placed character can have a useful route/medical/craft role; a powerful imported method can work at full capability when genuinely compatible.

## Special records that resist flattening

- **Seastone:** material identity, contact area/duration/density, holder and target fruit state remain explicit. Haki consequences on fruit users and effects on non-fruit users are not a universal suppression law (Seastone rev. 2124153).
- **Rumble Ball:** product formula, user training, dose count, time window, form control and aftereffect remain one sourced procedure, not an item stat (Rumble Ball rev. 2109438).
- **Ope operations:** field placement, target, medical understanding, stamina and exceptional life-cost procedure remain separate (Ope Ope no Mi rev. 2121122; page-specific costs in character and technique notes).
- **Hobi transformation:** deliberate activation/contact, persistent record/memory effects and unconsciousness/death reversal have separate world consequences (Hobi Hobi no Mi rev. 2129333).
- **Kage shadow work:** donor/corpse, sunlight, shadow custody, salt vulnerability and time-limited boosts remain distinct; defeating the user alone does not settle every stolen-shadow state (Kage Kage no Mi rev. 2122819 and linked notes).
- **Poneglyph work:** stone custody, copy, language and interpretation are different capabilities (Poneglyph rev. 2128060).
