## Visible disagreement and reliability notes

- Devil Fruit r2129490 states a normal one-power rule and documents an exceptional bearer; its mechanism is unexplained. Keep both claims visible. Do not turn the norm into an unbreakable engine validation.
- Logia r2130814 notes that Katakuri's fruit classification differed between the weekly release and volume. Do not flatten version-specific labels into one certainty.
- Haki family pages mix manga, anime, SBS, guidebook and novel evidence; the meaning and thresholds of some advanced interactions are not uniform. Keep citation medium at claim level.
- Seastone r2124153 leaves non-fruit-user effects unknown. Reduced density can permit movement/work while retaining suppression in the wiki account; no binary shackle behavior is assumed.
- Ope, Nikyu and other fruit pages preserve within-page counterexamples/limitations. They do not support a global combat matchup formula.
- C shard's supplemental audits and all shards' append-only correction files must be applied when reading notes. The global [SPOTCHECK_1](../../gm/dispatch/wiki-lanes-2026-10-13/SPOTCHECK_1.md) flags P2 actor/user assignment and P3 dropped conditions as recurring risks. For this synthesis, conditions above were checked against the page payload; a note's omitted condition is not evidence that it is unknown.

## Source boundaries and open questions

Original manga chapters, SBS, guidebooks, novels, anime, films and games have not been comprehensively read directly for this pack. No complete article-by-article primary authentication is claimed. The Fandom live site returned 403 in the older digest; this synthesis uses only the frozen dump. The snapshot itself includes pages last changed through 2026-08-29; manifest freeze on October 10 does not advance the underlying story. All content first asserted after the snapshot is out of scope pending source acquisition and verification.

Before a source-dependent mechanic is implemented, check its cited primary chapter/edition directly, including the actor, trigger, target, range, medium, resource, counter, persistence and aftermath.
