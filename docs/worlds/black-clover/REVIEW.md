# Black Clover world-pack independent review

**Verdict: usable with fixes.** The pack is a source-bounded design proposal, not an accepted or playable world. Most sampled mechanics match the frozen wiki payloads. Fix the spatial counter, the misstated Nacht/Morgen discrepancy, and the exposed divergent outcome before treating this as a source authority for implementation.

Review scope: `SYNTHESIS.md`'s twelve engine-rule rows and ten additional claims sampled across `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md`, `CAMPAIGN.md`, and `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`. I read the reader's `SUMMARY.md`, `NOTE_CORRECTIONS.md`, and relevant `NOTES.jsonl` rows, then reopened the cited private payloads directly. A locator such as **`Mana Method` rev86194, text L30–35** means lines of the `text` field in the compressed `read_file` named by that title and revision in `docs/research/power-systems/wiki-digests/full-dumps/workers/black-clover-full/READ_LEDGER.jsonl`. The snapshot wiki is secondary evidence; manga chapters were not independently read. **Pass** means the source claim is supported at that boundary; **qualified** means the engine shape is a labelled proposal or the citation needs narrower scope; **fix** means the wording misstates evidence or breaks a project rule.

## Fixes, in priority order

1. **Correct the spatial counter before implementation.** Replace “mana affinity plus greater control” with the page's actual loved-by-mana condition and greater control. Give BC-IX-06 the same three explicit branches, with no generic relative-stat shortcut. Evidence: C9.
2. **Seal the divergent proposal.** `CAMPAIGN.md` BC-AU-01 currently states a changed event in a public row. Per `AGENTS.md` and `WORLD_SECRETS_AND_DISCOVERY.md`'s sealed rule, leave only an opaque slot ID, discovery route, and status outside a sealed record. BC-AU-05 already describes the intended handling. No altered truth is repeated here.
3. **Repair the source ledger's contradiction.** Label the Nacht/Morgen disagreement as an age discrepancy, then update `SYNTHESIS.md` gap 4's matching “twins count” wording. Keep the separate Canis × Felis participant conflict unresolved: `Nacht Faust` rev95697, L107 versus `Unite Mode: Canis × Felis` rev95780, L18. Evidence: S10.
4. **Tighten medium and relationship labels.** `SOURCE_LEDGER.md` L65 says the Moonlight pages cite the Side-Story, but `Moonlight Magic` rev95790, L10–12 cites the Quartet Knights game; `Bewitching Moonlight` rev95791, L19–21 uses the game for its basic effect and the Side-Story chapter for simultaneous control. Record those per claim. Keep spirit host bond/transfer distinct from a formal pact, and scope Yami's mixed chapter/film compound examples. Evidence: C8, S2, and these payload lines.

## Supported material the pack missed

- **Distinct recovery tests:** `NOTES.jsonl` rows `Grey` rev95984, `Flower Princess Utopia` rev83647, and `Aquenelle Lore` rev83687 point to three different outcomes. Direct payloads: `Grey` L97, L115 (tissue reassembly; permanent nonmagical transmutation is a separate statement), `Flower Princess Utopia` L18–20 (fatal-wound healing), and `Aquenelle Lore` L18–20 (cannot heal fatal wounds). The broad healing module should not flatten these if expanded.
- **Ki branch and exceptional cost:** `NOTES.jsonl` rows `Zetten` rev95188 and `Tengentsu` rev95134 support a mechanically distinct branch beyond basic Ki reading. Direct payloads: `Zetten` L26–31 ties force to stored power and motion speed, with weaker split strikes; `Tengentsu` L11–15 requires high initial power and permanently spends it. These could materially alter a later Ki progression design, but are outside the first slice.
- **Spirit succession matters:** `Spirit Magic` rev96046, L23–25 says the spirit can leave, choose another host, decline to return after a revival, or pass down a lineage; full use also calls for grimoire spells. `MODULE_CATALOGUE.md` tracks host/transfer in outline, but `SYNTHESIS.md`'s core rule and gaps do not call out these failure and succession cases. They matter before a spirit route is implemented.

## Boundary checks

The proposal/runtime distinction holds across the pack: no file claims accepted gameplay or a completed build. Manga-cited source reports, the anime-only Mana Skin tool, film examples, game effects, and parallel-world proposals are generally separated, subject to fix 4. The AU boundary fails at BC-AU-01 as noted in fix 2. I found no copied source prose run of 16 or more normalized words in the nine pack Markdown files against the 1,102 selected payload spans; inspection found no raw page dump in the pack. Primary manga authentication, post-snapshot changes, the unexpanded wiki portal, unheaded character mechanics, and the unrun fan-premise survey remain honestly open in `SYNTHESIS.md` L89–97. The additional recovery, Ki, and spirit cases above should be added to later design-gap triage, not treated as requirements for the proposed first playable slice.

1. **Spatial counter corrected.** Updated the three branches and removed the generic relative-stat test. Files: `SYNTHESIS.md`, `INTERACTIONS.md`.
2. **Divergent outcome sealed.** BC-AU-01 now exposes only slot ID S1, discovery route BC-DISC-01, and reserved/undiscovered status. Files: `CAMPAIGN.md`.
3. **Age discrepancy corrected.** Replaced the erroneous twins-count conflict with the age discrepancy; retained the separate Canis × Felis participant conflict as unresolved. Files: `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`.
4. **Medium and relationship labels tightened.** Split Moonlight game and Side-Story attribution by claim, clarified spirit bonds versus devil contracts, and separated Yami's chapter and film compound examples. Files: `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`, `INTERACTIONS.md`, `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md`, `MODULE_CATALOGUE.md`.
5. **Supported missed rules added for later design triage.** Recovery methods retain distinct fatal-wound and tissue-reassembly outcomes; Ki coverage distinguishes Zetten and Tengentsu; spirit coverage includes departure, host succession, refusal to return, lineage transfer and grimoire access. These do not expand the first playable slice. Files: `SYNTHESIS.md`, `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md`, `MODULE_CATALOGUE.md`, `INTERACTIONS.md`.

No review fix or supported addition was declined. Payloads were reopened from the cited manifest `read_file` entries; claims remain bounded to the dated secondary wiki snapshot. No runtime, gameplay, build or source-authentication claim is made.
