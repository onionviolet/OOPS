### Proposed regional contexts

- **Hage / Forsaken Realm:** sparse supplies, low-status residents and long travel to central institutions. The social gradient is supported by Clover Kingdom rev93261; local prices and characters are new proposals.
- **Kikka / Common Realm:** trade, exams and public movement; use Kikka rev89445 for the source setting, with all new shops and prices clearly proposed.
- **Royal Capital / Noble Realm:** headquarters, courts, barriers and elite access; Royal Capital rev86147 and Clover Kingdom rev93261. A capital visit does not grant political authority.
- **Heart Kingdom:** abundant ambient mana and rune instruction; Heart Kingdom rev95598. Training still requires the prerequisites in Mana Method rev86194.
- **Dungeon frontier:** changing hazards, limited information and rival teams; Dungeon rev95914. A dungeon is a fixed danger, not scaled to the entrant.

## Connected pursuits

All tracks below are proposals mapping sourced behaviors into YAIRPG play.

1. **Control and craft:** practice a spell safely, repair a grimoire-compatible tool, and learn what inputs or target states make a spell reliable. Spell improvement is individual; do not make every known spell a loot drop.
2. **Mission and standing:** accept a bounded assignment, spend supplies or time, protect a party, and return with an observed result. Mission success may earn stars or rank consideration, but status remains institution-specific (Black Bull rev95897; Wizard King rev93541).
3. **Research and technique:** study mana sensing, Ki, Mana Skin, Mana Zone or runes with qualified instruction. Each is a distinct method with its own prerequisites (Ki rev95847; Mana Skin rev96042; Mana Zone rev94318; Mana Method rev86194).
4. **Civilian livelihood:** courier work, repair, trade or care offers a useful lower-risk route and a reason to know places and people. All job/economy details are proposed.

## Failure and return

**Proposal:** distinguish failed exam, failed job, capture, injury, defeat, abandoned expedition and terminal death. No general resurrection or offline loss policy is inferred from the source. Declining an offer creates no obligation; accepting one commits only its stated terms. Separate remembered source knowledge from what a new character has actually learned. Any cross-world import keeps its actual carrier/source, fuel, body support and targeting fit; a label such as “magic” grants no universal compatibility.
