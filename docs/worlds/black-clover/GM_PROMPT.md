# Black Clover world author role

Status: proposal for future content authors. This is not an implementation dispatch.

## Authority and sources

Follow repository `AGENTS.md`, `PROJECT.md`, [campaign framework](../CAMPAIGN_FRAMEWORK.md), [source deep-dive playbook](../../research/SOURCE_DEEP_DIVE_PLAYBOOK.md), and [lore/timeline audit contract](../../research/LORE_TIMELINE_AUDIT_CONTRACT.md). Weibao is creative director. User vision and current project direction outrank inherited notes and this proposal.

Use [SOURCE_LEDGER](SOURCE_LEDGER.md) as the revision authority. Wiki statements are secondary reports. Cite the page title and revision on each source-backed rule. Label manga-cited claims separately from anime, movie, game, novel, guidebook and inference. Later chapters than the 2023-12-24 snapshot are a gap, not memory fill.

Before writing a mechanic, re-open its selected payload and check actor, speaker, condition, cost, material, counter, duration and exception. Do not carry a prior page's fact into the current claim. An unknown is allowed only after the cited page was searched for it. Preserve contradictions with each source attribution intact.

## Content rules

- Label every authored row as **Proposal** until Weibao accepts it. Canon fact, inference, original fiction and runtime status remain separate.
- Preserve distinct mana, ambient mana, Anti Magic, Ki, grimoire access, institution rank, spirit/contract relation, body, equipment and knowledge.
- A character's identity, lineage, rank or power does not give the player that character's body, spell or source.
- Keep fixed threats fixed. Do not smooth inequality by secretly scaling enemies or inventing guaranteed escape.
- Every offer has issuer, terms, trigger, costs, refusal/expiry and consequence. Refusal creates no accepted obligation.
- A player-visible action uses actor-known facts only. Unknown truths remain sealed under the world secret rule.
- Changed or randomized canon truth gets a reserved opaque sealed slot ID. Public franchise twists may be designed freely but are not printed to the player before their in-world discovery route.
- Never place raw prose or a quote longer than 15 words in tracked files.
- New local people and jobs need actual goals, means, schedule, resources, knowledge and consequences. Do not use famous names as decorative quest givers.

## Review checklist proposal

1. Confirm each claim's revision and attribution.
2. Check every stated gate and counter against the payload.
3. Run timeline and actor-knowledge review on any divergence.
4. Confirm each owner row has one state/effect owner and a concrete observation.
5. Leave runtime, build, browser, persistence and director acceptance OPEN unless separately exercised and recorded.

This role file grants no source, runtime, save, build, publication or commit authorization.
