## Identity and boundary

**Proposal:** family `fan.black_clover`; baseline continuity `black_clover.manga_snapshot_2023_12_24`; first era `grimoire_ceremony`; first region `forsaken_realm_hage`; initial role `new_magic_knight_candidate`. These are authoring labels, not registered runtime IDs.

**Source boundary:** Black Clover Wiki pages from the WikiTeam Internet Archive snapshot dated 2023-12-24, each pinned to its revision in [SOURCE_LEDGER](SOURCE_LEDGER.md). This is secondary evidence, not direct manga authentication. The archive's last chapter coverage cited by the read audit reaches chapter 368. Material after the snapshot is an explicit gap. Anime-only, movie, game, novel, guidebook and manga claims are labeled per claim where the notes distinguish them.

## Fantasy and signature loop

**Proposal:** Live as a mage whose grimoire, attribute, control, training, institution and allies all matter, then decide what kind of Magic Knight or civilian life to build. The recognizable loop is: learn what your magic can do, train a condition or technique, take a mission with real stakes, earn supplies and standing, then choose whether to deepen your own method or rely on a team.

The world should make mana and magic legible while preserving unequal starting talent and dangerous fixed threats. Rank, noble status, mission merit, raw mana, spell access and mastery are separate facts. A poor person can advance through a demonstrated result; that does not erase class prejudice. (Proposal mapping from `Clover Kingdom` rev93261, `Wizard King` rev93541, `Magic Knight` and squad pages; see source ledger.)

## First situation

**Proposal:** At the Hage grimoire ceremony, the candidate receives a grimoire if one selects them, or leaves without one. Their first usable choice is practical: train an available spell with a local mentor, enter the Magic Knight exam circuit, or earn travel money through a village job. The opening records only the character's witnessed outcome and knowledge. It does not assign Asta's or Yuno's exceptional history to the player.

## Progression and access

**Proposal:** A canon start should be one eligible option among direct era/place/situation starts, not a mandatory Frontier trip. Additional starts can open through known access routes, prior play or an explicit sandbox choice. AU copies should use a base pack plus a small declared divergence patch; an altered or randomized truth gets a sealed slot ID and stays out of visible cards until discovered.

Initial authoring can cover Hage and the Clover Kingdom, then add Heart, Diamond and Spade as distinct institutions and resource contexts. A complete franchise roster is not a first-slice requirement. Each start must name body, age, grimoire status, magic attribute, place, supplies, local knowledge and eligible actions.

## Limits and implementation status

All pack content below is proposal. The first milestone remains one realm and one prestige layer. This research does not create a world instance, combat adapter, save migration, offline policy, or mobile layout. First build target, if later authorized, should be desktop/laptop. All mechanics in these files are unimplemented and unverified.
