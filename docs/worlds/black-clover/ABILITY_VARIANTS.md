# Black Clover ability variants proposal

Every row is an authoring proposal grounded in wiki-reported manga mechanics unless a row says otherwise. Exact actor pages and revisions live in [SOURCE_LEDGER](SOURCE_LEDGER.md). No numeric balance is accepted.

## Ability grammar

| ID | Proposal | Source anchors | Conditions and boundaries to preserve |
|---|---|---|---|
| BC-AB-01 | Attribute spell family | Asta rev96080; Yuno rev96105; Noelle rev96082; Magna rev95568 | Learn each spell and its specific form; attribute does not imply every effect in the family. |
| BC-AB-02 | Creation spell | Revchi Salik rev94192; Noelle rev96082 | Shapes objects/entities using the caster's magic. Keep created construct, duration and independent action explicit where the page states them. |
| BC-AB-03 | Compound magic | Heath Graice rev96049; Magna Swing rev95568; Yami Sukehiro rev96062 (chapter and film examples kept separate) | Require the actual contributing casters/effects and timing. One participant does not automatically own the combined output; do not combine Yami's chapter 324 instance with the separate film-only entry. |
| BC-AB-04 | Rune array spell | Rune array rev93898 | Instruction grammar, complexity/setup, medium, caster capacity, fizzle/explosion risk; reuse only for carved array cases. |
| BC-AB-05 | Natural-mana spell | Mana Method rev86194 | Method access, adequate personal magic, stage proficiency, reflex/control, environment and compatible attribute. |
| BC-AB-06 | Mana Zone | Mana Zone rev94318 | Ambient range and control; improves size/count/range and directional casting. Do not treat it as unlimited personal mana. |
| BC-AB-07 | Mana Skin | Mana Skin rev96042 | Protective coating that needs control, especially in wild mana. Anime-only assisting tool stays separate. |
| BC-AB-08 | Ki reading and later branch | Ki rev95847; Zetten rev95188; Tengentsu rev95134; Yami Sukehiro rev96062 | Basic Ki reading remains distinct from later Zetten (stored power and action speed; split attacks weaken) and Tengentsu (high initial magic, then permanent loss of all magic). These are later design coverage, not first-slice gates. |
| BC-AB-09 | Anti Magic weapon | Anti Magic Weapon rev95931; Demon-Slayer Sword rev95749; Demon-Dweller Sword rev95908 | Distinguish nullifying magic, deflecting with a flat, spell absorption/projection, and causal removal. Keep sword-specific behavior, physical weight and condition. |
| BC-AB-10 | Spirit enhancement and succession | Spirit Magic rev96046; Spirit Dive rev92818 | Compatible attribute and host bond must exist; do not model the bond as a formal devil-style contract. Track spirit departure, new-host choice, refusal to return after host revival, lineage succession, and required grimoire spells. Near-total resonance and attack-type requirement apply to Saint Stage. |
| BC-AB-11 | Devil Unite | Unite rev96045; Canis rev95782; Equus rev95783; Felis rev95784; Gallus rev95785 | Devil-Binding Ritual first. Master-servant control differs from equal partnership; equals need synchronized thoughts. Exact mode depends on devil/form, not a generic union perk. |
| BC-AB-12 | Sealing | Secre Swallowtail rev95890 | Include target, container, caster's magic and release condition. Sealing wounds/exhaustion is not ordinary healing or permanent restoration. |

| BC-AB-14 | Curse and Forbidden Magic | Forbidden Magic rev94321; Curse-Breaking Blood Cocoon rev59887 | Source, lasting change, bodily cost and control loss are effect-specific. Do not generalize one curse/blood spell. |

## Proposed Anti Magic weapon distinctions

| Weapon | Proposal for authored function | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Demon-Slayer | Edge cuts magic; flat can deflect spells; extended edge increases reach/coverage. Physical hits on nonmagic targets may be blunt. | Demon-Slayer Sword rev95749 |
| Demon-Dweller | Can project absorbed magic as a flying slash carrying that attribute; page examples involve allies' magic. | Demon-Dweller Sword rev95908 |
| Demon-Destroyer | Causality Break removes lingering magical effects, with stated exceptions/residual conditions; resolve by exact page case. | Demon-Destroyer Sword rev95860 |
| Demon-Slasher | Spares protected targets in its described slash. Its target selection is not a universal safety effect. | Asta rev96080 |

## Proposed training receipts

Record for each ability: source/teacher; body and grimoire; learned grammar; current proficiency; preparation; personal mana; ambient mana; carrier/condition; known targets; observed failure; supporting allies; observer evidence. A new spell can be discovered or taught, but practice changes reliability only after a visible, authored threshold. Keep numeric costs proposed until a playable balance pass.
