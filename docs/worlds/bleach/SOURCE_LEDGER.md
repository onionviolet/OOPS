## What release progression means for the design

Proposed authoring sequence: identify the actual spiritual actor and eligible weapon/source, establish supported personal development, then author the local release's function and constraints. B5 corroborates Shikai before Bankai for the represented ordinary release path. It does not make every soul identical or apply that ladder to Quincy/Arrancar.
Personal release is a development of that actor and weapon relationship. A high-quality blank, protective outfit or workshop cannot replace the missing relationship. Buying another person's sword does not automatically buy their spirit, true name, learned release or authority.
Release mastery, spiritual reserve, artifact quality, body condition and office are independent dimensions. B2's noncaptain Bankai users show why office is not the release itself. A mature release can still develop without being renamed a universal T4 module.
Exact success gates, duration, power ratios, broken-release recovery and exceptions remain source questions. Do not turn a glossary's general wording into a mandatory tenfold stat multiplier, universal timer or prohibition on all exceptional advancement.
Craft supports can improve carrying, protection, observation, safe testing, body interfaces and ordinary repairs when independently sourced or original. A custom training aid is `O`; its capability cannot silently reproduce Urahara's exceptional devices or Nimaiya's craft.
Permanent failure is possible: a destroyed source/bond or lost person can remove functions that routine repair does not restore. Already learned sword/movement practice and separate ordinary equipment remain with the actor if their actual body/soul survives.

| Route | Materially distinct path | Boundaries to preserve |
|---|---|---|

| Ordinary institution/service | Courier work, records, equipment repair, treatment logistics and duty | Means, privacy, faction/instance authority and separate combat strength |
| Quincy candidate | Separately authored lineage/source, training and spiritual-material use | Canon inheritance/method and compatible ambient support need exact scenes |
| Fullbring candidate | Personal eligible source/object relationship and training under B12 | Exact origin, bond, transformation and transfer are unverified; sentimental ownership alone is insufficient |
| Hollow/Arrancar candidate | Separately authored soul/form development and release architecture | Not a routine Soul Reaper upgrade; acquisition/permanence/cost need source inspection |

Soul Society, World of the Living and Hueco Mundo are source cosmological locations, not automatically YAIRPG alternate histories. They can coexist within one Bleach continuity. A parallel Bleach instance has separate versions of that history and those places.
Seeing a spirit is not proof that ordinary material gear can strike it, that a physical visitor can enter every realm, or that their source converts into spiritual pressure. Perception, contact, passage, body interface and retained custody need separate selected rules.
Mixed heritage is not a mandatory reward for progressing. The official episode's Ichigo-specific revelation supplies no universal fusion recipe. An original hybrid experiment must name actual sources, compatibility, uncertainty and lasting bodily/soul consequences.
Artificial souls/bodies and source-specific crafting remain possible research routes, but B2 proves only named existence. It does not provide a generic printable companion, duplicate consciousness or native save field.

### Corrections and unresolved differences

- Zanpakutō rev. 582599 records source exceptions to an absolute one-actor transfer model: Zaraki made a dead Shinigami’s Asauchi his own; certain unique weapons pass to another Shinigami with their abilities; the Ise family weapon is inherited and prevents ordinary Asauchi imprinting. These are distinct exceptions, not a general marketplace/API. Keep the bond model `O` pending BU1/BU2 primary scenes.
- BU1 lead from Zanpakutō rev. 582599: Urahara’s special device forced Zanpakutō-spirit materialization and enabled Ichigo/Urahara to attain Bankai in three days; the account describes a dangerous time bound. Inspect the scene; no general shortcut is selected.
- Quincy rev. 587658 adds BU4 leads: exceptional share-power routes, Hollow poison to Quincy, and ambient-Reishi dependence. Fullbringer rev. 584513 distinguishes manga-attributed parental Hollow-attack origin from a later novel causal layer; it also describes incomplete versus realized stages, a potentially damaging completion burst and a Fullbringer suppression role. These remain secondary leads, not default-manga eligibility/fuel/support rules.

- Earlier local materials sometimes describe all listed Bleach world-depth concepts as ordinary canon. Correction: BL1–BL33 remain proposals, with evidence level varying by row. This pass did not authenticate them against the manga; inspect relevant manga scenes before making them canon-labelled pack content.
- Races rev. 578082 bundles multiple media. Its anime-only Zanpakutō-spirit lifecycle and other-media Bount details must not be generalized into the original-manga continuity. Keep manga, official adaptation, guidebook/novel and game claims on separate evidence rows.
- Zanpakutō rev. 582599 distinguishes ordinary Shikai recovery from broken Bankai: its specific account requires the wielder’s will/Reiatsu over time and an intact hilt; broken Bankai normally cannot return to their former state, with named cases for Komamura, Hitsugaya, Mayuri, Nimaiya and Orihime.
