## Instance state and first loop

Prefix proposed street.blockage, street.detour, manifest.delivered, recordStore.ready and all actual site/person/claim IDs with the integrator-selected durable instance namespace.
Emi owns repair stock/bench; Jun owns the damaged ledger and actual resident testimonies; Nao owns her source, learned bounded survey and finite reserve. A purchased report belongs to the recipient knowledge record with observer provenance, not a new perception faculty.
Author truth remains actual local state/controller definitions; each observer's known report goes to their existing knowledge record. Unique delivery settlement belongs to one claim across routes; jobs hold frozen inputs/participants/due times. No second plot reward manager or player inventory.

**Route A, ordinary work:** On a generated fixture, buy two timber and one cloth from real stock; pay two proposed local units for Emi's workshop access and spend four played minutes/two stamina on a witnessed mundane detour. Carry the owned/borrowed parcel through a three-minute lawful transfer to the available recipient. These O proof values do not claim manga economics or spiritual targeting.

**Route B, genuinely qualified source/service:** Pay Nao five proposed local units, reserve her actual availability, and fund a three-minute bounded O survey with two units from her real eligible spiritual source reserve. Supported observation supplies only an authored route lead from an actual relevant local fact; it cannot read every spirit/source or a hidden future outcome. If the shortened transfer route is actually lawful/available, spend one further played minute on the same parcel handoff. The player receives the report, not spiritual perception or Nao's source. No spirit battle, realm-crossing body conversion or copied Zanpakuto is proved here.

**Shared settlement:** One manifest claim grants a particular memory and dry-record-store study once. Ordinary detour versus qualified report retain different stock/fuel/route facts and observer provenance. Neither route grants Quincy lineage, a Fullbring bond, release or revival.

**Refusal, expiry and accepted failure:** The caused public departure offer lasts twelve proposed present-played minutes, independently of when the player observes it. Refusal/expiry makes the shelter fund a longer service and postpone one delivery. Actual aftermath stays hidden until a witness/report. Accepted default retains consumed inputs and custody/declared terms; refusal creates neither duty nor cosmic punishment.

**Useful local export:** Paid ordinary instrument-housing or archive preservation work at Frontier using real materials/knowledge/native item bindings. The report retains provenance; personal source releases and jurisdiction do not become generic portable recipes.

**Missing local/runtime capabilities:** Native instance/clock/item/currency isolation; exact body/source eligibility and supported observation projection; spiritual perception/contact separate from physical targeting; later soul/body travel and material custody, source-bound release development, qualified reserve, instruments and general spiritual combat.


Entry/return preserves actual body, wounds, methods, sources/reserves and named cargo. The first fixture keeps companions home until consent/body/cargo support is implemented.
A compatible Frontier step may brace mundane load at its real three-drive-unit/strain cost after destination binding. It does not create the local supernatural source or grant unsupported targeting.
Fixed threats may overwhelm the visitor, while genuinely sufficient compatible imports work. First proof excludes lethal combat; terminal death, run end, source revival, aging/offline losses and full-life retention remain unselected.
