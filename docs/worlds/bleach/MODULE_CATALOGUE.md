# Bleach module catalogue: personal sources, taught arts and spiritual service

Status: Develop/Research proposal r1, 2026-10-03. Sole local component/technique owner: progression/granularity GM. Authoring data only, with no new power API, native equipment or save fields.
Read [campaign](CAMPAIGN.md), [entry brief](WORLD_BRIEF.md), [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md) and [shared assembly contract](../PROGRESSION_AND_ASSEMBLY.md). Campaign owns personal arcs/institutions; ledger owns canon rulings; this file owns local function stages/builds.
Boundary: Tite Kubo's completed original manga, volumes 1-74. Anime-only additions, filler, novels, one-shot continuations and game rank/loot systems are excluded unless separately selected later.
B2/B7 A: [official character profiles](https://bleach-anime.com/en/character/) corroborate Nimaiya's Zanpakuto creation, Urahara's supply/research role, Yoruichi's Hoho, Nanao's Kido talent and Squad 12 research. These are bounded anchors, not the entire site's later anime continuity.
B4 A: [official episode 13 summary](https://bleach-anime.com/en/story/?id=13) corroborates actual heritage and Asauchi testing in Ichigo's reforging story. It does not make his exceptional body a default visitor template.
B5/B6 A: [official terminology page](https://bleach-anime.com/keyword/) corroborates personal release development, substantial Bankai support costs and the general distinction between Shikai repair and unrecovered broken Bankai capability.
The glossary mixes later adaptation material. Use only those bounded base-story anchors and require [ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md) manga locators for precise implementation; no anime expansion is silently imported.
P means original training stage, work device, personal technique proposal or budget. A crafted housing can carry tools; ordinary metalwork cannot produce another person's Zanpakuto release.

Evidence marks inherit the ledger: C primary fact, A official adaptation corroboration, I inference, O original extension, U unresolved. P in this catalogue is explicitly an O proposal, never an official adaptation mark.
[Ability variants](../ABILITY_AUTHORING.md) permits stage/context differences and genuinely superior methods. The lower-cost roles illustrated here are concrete options, not a universal rule that every weak variant must survive or every mature method must pay a compensating penalty.

## Independent dimensions and compatibility

- Definition IDs use `bleach.module.<family>` revision 1. Tool identity, body/source identity and learned method are different proposed records.
- Structural stage identifies an actual practiced function, device architecture or source-qualified release state. It is not a faction-wide three-tier ladder.
- Grade/rarity records source/material scarcity where verified. A rare blade, soul or research contact supplies no automatic personal release.
- Quality records ordinary craft fit and calibration for P devices. It does not multiply every spiritual ability or assign every soul a loot color.
- Condition records housing wear, depleted device supply, injury, source damage and blade state separately. A repaired scabbard is not a repaired Bankai.
- Mastery records Zanjutsu, Hakuda, Hoho, Kido, perception and personal release practice independently. Equipment acquisition supplies none of these for free.
- Source/body records actual spiritual body/compatibility and personally acquired power source. A living human, Soul Reaper, Hollow or Quincy is not the same interchangeable body slot.
- Institution records Academy/Squad/Kido Corps/research affiliation and actual duties. Social office can open training or custody while lacking another person's power.
- Research records observation, interpreted method, acquired support and tested execution. Observing a release is not knowing its true name or owning its source.
- Owner and holder differ for supplied devices and training weapons; custody never grants another person's soul-bound power.
- Portability records actual spirit/body, gear and learned methods separately from shop/Squad facilities and lawful access.
- Fullbring, Hollow/Arrancar release, Quincy techniques and Soul Reaper release retain different acquisition/compatibility rules. No default multi-faction hybrid or exceptional protagonist heritage.
- A source loss can prevent execution while retaining learned knowledge. Generic rest or better housing cannot restore a missing soul/body constituent.
- The B6 A repair corroboration describes a general broken-Bankai constraint; exact exceptions/alterations need source-specific evidence and are not universal repair recipes.

### Central operating records

- Body / qualification: an actual test establishes whether this body can perceive/interact with the intended task. Unknown is not zero or automatic eligibility.
- Body / sustained: practice observed supported work and recover actual reserve. A modest qualified courier remains useful before large combat output.
- Body / demanding: source-qualified capacity can support stronger methods. Maximum effort competes with control, duration and bodily stability.
- Release / unreleased: the actual acquired source and weapon support their real baseline. Ordinary sword practice is separately useful and paid.
- Release / Shikai: actual personal acquisition opens the individually defined effect. It cannot be installed into another owner's blade through a hardware swap.
- Release / Bankai: source-qualified personal development changes the operating envelope. B5/B6 A's substantial support cost and damage constraint remain; no universal top tier is imposed.
- Zanjutsu / guard: simple practiced reach serves routine defense without spending a large spiritual reserve.
- Zanjutsu / engagement: learn timing/retreat with the actual arm and body. A longer beautiful blade can worsen close quarters.
- Zanjutsu / integration: earned mastery coordinates a known release's function. Acquiring release alone does not supply tactical control.
- Hakuda / defense: cheap unarmed method needs compatible contact/body support and remains useful when equipment is unavailable.
- Hakuda / restraint: trained positioning opens a distinct capture/escape role rather than another attack multiplier.
- Hakuda / advanced: exact source-qualified combination has its own acquisition and costs. A borrowed device cannot produce it automatically.
- Hoho / movement: repeated footwork/route practice reduces uncertainty on a known path; narrow passages and actual terrain still matter.
- Hoho / reposition: taught high-speed method changes engagement reach. More speed without stop/control training can overshoot the target.
- Hoho / specialist: deliberate individually earned method opens sustained/deceptive movement where source supports it; no automatic cross-franchise speed ranking.
- Kido / complete: one learned procedure, source and practice support one actual effect. A complete slower method can remain more reliable than an unqualified shortcut.
- Kido / complementary: learn another method separately and test interruption/control. A high numerical designation does not substitute for the actual spell's mechanics.
- Kido / specialist: coordinate verified barrier/binding roles with a real trained partner. Personal research remains P until its source/counter envelope is selected.
- Perception / observe: retain witnessed presence and uncertainty; a sensor report does not teach power acquisition.
- Perception / discriminate: compare known observations without revealing private author truth. Similar signatures may remain uncertain.
- Perception / interference: qualified method tests a bounded masking/counter situation. No unconditional ability to locate every absent enemy is added.
- Device / acquired: inspect and fit an actual source-qualified tool; its limited role is useful without personal combat release.
- Device / housed: replaceable protective case/handle supports field service. Fitting material must genuinely interact with that object/body.
- Device / project: multiple tested instruments and staff support separate tasks. Redundant devices are distinct owned specimens, not duplicated values.
- Craft / ordinary: wraps/cases remain useful for compatible ordinary cargo, even when spiritual handling is unavailable.
- Craft / spiritual: acquire appropriate material and trained process; mundane smithing knowledge can help geometry without creating a soul-bound source.
- Craft / service: maintain actual instruments and supplies under separate custody. A mature workshop outperforms one powerful fighter at simultaneous repair work.

| Career | Denied access, duty conflict or absent teacher halts work | Negotiate actual terms or another route; rank advancement does not erase grievances or obligations |

- Distinguish source-qualified spiritual tool, ordinary housing and individual release at every inspection. They have different repair rights.
- Removing a tool fitting returns the same worn part; removing an owner's soul-bound source is not routine workshop salvage.
- Exceptional Quincy/Hollow/Fullbring paths require their own body/source method, costs and compatibility. No universal three-stage release mapping is selected.
- Soul Reaper purification, Quincy destruction and other faction effects cannot be merged into generic damage without source-specific consequences.
- Novel source/body trials and irreversible release risks remain active choices; only learned safe craft batches may idle with stock and capacity limits.

### Family salvage and irreversible constraints
- Spiritual body: retained practice and surviving source are separate; no generic salvage of a damaged soul into a replacement compatible body.
- Personal release: actual source/bond follows ledger evidence; Shikai/Bankai loss cannot be replaced by a newly forged look-alike or repaired scabbard.
- Zanjutsu: learned sword practice remains when gear changes; actual blade source is neither duplicated nor harvested through teaching.
- Hakuda: retain method with its body's actual capacity; ordinary glove scrap supplies no source-bound unarmed art.
- Hoho: learned route/movement control remains; repaired shoes restore fitting, not a lost spiritual source or speed faculty.
- Kido: retain learned complete procedures and test evidence; consumed casting source is not recovered from discarded ritual support.
- Perception: preserve witnessed observations with uncertainty; broken tool/source does not yield another person's faculty as a salvage reward.
- Devices: recover only compatible inspected surviving tool/housing parts; altered source-sensitive pieces retain their actual state and acquisition provenance.
- Craft: reuse verified substrate only if condition allows; ordinary metal that failed spiritual contact remains ordinary metal.
- Recovery/supply: recover unused compatible clean stock; used medicine and dead/incompatible body require their actual rules, not factory output.
- Career/workshop: actual owned instruments/material can leave; institutional offices, other staff's sources and expired access cannot be packed as inventory.

P service budget: one instrument bay and one separate supervised practice area; reserve each worker/task independently.
P spiritual-reserve fixture: eighteen units; bounded observation two, taught barrier trial six, controlled movement three. One of each leaves seven contingency.
This is author accounting for an eventual selected method, not canon universal reiryoku units. Body strain/control and release source remain separate gates.
P carry budget: eight units; actual instrument/case three, supplies two, held weapon two, reference one. Heavy additional gear needs another holder.
Bankai deployment is not hidden inside the station's spare reserve. It requires actual acquired release, its individual envelope and disclosed source-specific risk.

## Three worked loadouts

### Poor specialist: compatible craft courier
- Owns work clothing, compatible wraps/case, plain service tools and actual cargo; learns record keeping/repair and foundational movement appropriate to their qualified body.
- Entry assumes no release, high spiritual capacity, faction rank or protagonist hybrid. Ordinary living-world cargo route remains available where body context supports it.
- Budget P: six carry units, one rented work interval, finite food/rest and one client appointment; spiritual work withheld until source qualification.
- Action unlocked: repair supplies or carry an actual compatible parcel with provenance, supporting a stronger practitioner's project.
- Decision: spend on proper substrate/interface or more decorative metalwork; only the first can open the tested spiritual service if qualification succeeds.
- Conflict: carrying a large weapon displaces reference/cargo; hire another carrier or keep light ordinary tools.
- Counterplay: incompatible environment, actual spiritual threat or denied access blocks unsupported work; craft support remains useful on compatible tasks.
- Replacement: repair liner, preserve cheap case and rent a qualified observer instead of pretending the courier became one.
- Lasting result: tested service and specific client trust; failed material trial/time and lost accepted delivery remain actual consequences.

### Developed field build: taught-art survey practitioner
- Has actual qualified spiritual body, source-qualified unreleased personal weapon where acquired, trained guard/movement and two independently learned Kido/observation methods.
- Owns qualified observation support in serviced P housing and compatible supplies; no personal Shikai or copied release is assumed.
- Budget P: eighteen spiritual reserve, one observation/barrier/movement sequence leaving seven, eight carry units and a separate recovery appointment.
- Action unlocked: survey a bounded spiritual disturbance and protect an ordinary service task within the actual taught method's envelope.
- Decision: reserve recovery/support capacity or spend another field method on a stronger obstacle; remaining means and unknown danger change visibly.
- Conflict: complex observation and Kido casting compete for one actor's attention; a real trained partner permits overlap at paid reservation cost.
- Counterplay: masking, incompatible body/source, stronger pressure or damaged device removes a specific capability without deleting retained training.
- Replacement: use complete slower Kido procedure where qualified shortcut fails, choose observer instead of device, refit actual housing at safe bay.
- Lasting result: trained arts, serviced tool and bounded evidence persist; reserve, consumed supplies and failed local objective do not reset on return.

### Mature project: personal release and staffed spiritual workshop
- Acquires actual source-qualified personal Shikai and separately earns deeper release only if its true prerequisites are met; employs trained observer/crafter and recovery specialist.
- P personal Shikai candidate **Thread of Return**: an individually earned tether to one deliberately touched compatible owned tool, indicating only tether contact/direction within tested range, not global people tracking.
- Candidate requires ledger compatibility and human fiction selection; it is not canon, not a copy of another character and not currently executable. Bankai design remains unselected rather than a generic purchased extension.
- Budget P: workshop two staffed areas, three finite service kits, individual eighteen-unit fixture subject to actual release cost; tether attention competes with casting and breaks at tested range/obstruction.
- Action unlocked: qualified personal release can aid retrieval while mature martial/Kido capacity genuinely defeats lesser fixed threats and staff repair unrelated devices simultaneously.
- Decision: pursue further personal release research, train another qualified observer or spend scarce substrate on a second service instrument.
- Conflict: Bankai damage risk cannot be solved by an extra smith shift. Personal source progress and institution project progress remain different routes.
- Counterplay: severed tether, incompatible object, stronger source-bound threat or workshop access loss can block different tasks; no manufactured universal immunity.
- Replacement: ordinary labeled tool/observer for cheap work; retain learned Kido when source-bound release is unavailable; hire support instead of adding a false soul module.
- Lasting result: actual personal acquisition if selected, retained gear/staff agreements and specific test history; broken source capability follows its verified irreversible rule.

## Worked craft/upgrade chain and failed hybrid tests

1. Acquire source-qualified device/material or choose the compatible ordinary-cargo alternative. Inspect ownership/body interface before paying for spiritual work.
2. Research teacher's actual procedure and source ledger evidence; separately assess perception/body qualification. A copied device drawing grants no personal source.
3. Produce P housing/liner at the appropriate bench with actual material and trained labor. Preserve tool identity and reserve reference specimen.
4. P failed test: thick housing protects the case but obstructs the verified interface. Spend labor/sample interval, record lost function and retain the intact source tool.
5. Refit only housing/liner, return removed part in current condition, retest against reference, then train actual method before field commitment.
6. Use station and taught-art practitioner on a declared survey. Pay observation/casting/supply, distinguish actual world result from each witness's knowledge.
7. Service after use, preserving tool wear and the bounded report. If a source-qualified Bankai were broken in a separate selected engagement, ordinary station repair cannot restore it.
8. Lasting consequence: maintained tool, learned arts and witnessed service claim; consumed material, failed trial and any verified permanent source damage remain.
Hybrid P: import Fallout fabrication knowledge to improve a compatible tool case. Pay substrate comparison, one sample and one interface/load test; geometry can help without making ordinary steel spiritual.
Failed hybrid P: forge a metal replica of another owner's released blade. Form matches, but replica lacks that owner's soul-bound source/name/relationship; spent metal/labor, ordinary replica retained.
Second failed test P: use a Skyrim gem to refill the practitioner's spiritual reserve. No evidenced conversion supplies compatible source; stop after a bounded inert comparison rather than damaging a body through an assumed recipe.
Successful ordinary support and failed source conversion both retain useful evidence. Neither grants faction powers, release training or bodily compatibility for free.
