The nine engine-rule rows have direct support in the archived secondary payloads where they describe Bleach facts, and their engine shapes are marked `O`. ## Fixes in priority order

1. **F1, source ownership:** In the source/ability owners, point BU2 to the exact secondary exceptions: dead-owner Asauchi re-imprinting and inherited special weapons (`Zanpakutō@582599 text:48,84-86`, with manga chapter references on that page). Keep the one-actor/bond model a proposal pending those primary scenes. This affects acquisition, transfer and persistence.
2. **F2, recovery:** Reword the apparent regeneration disagreement in `SYNTHESIS.md:15,28` and `SOURCE_LEDGER.md:113`. The wiki itself restricts ordinary recovery to Shikai with an intact hilt and separately gives broken-Bankai exceptions (`Zanpakutō@582599 text:44-46,157-61`). The official glossary remains separate corroboration; exact primary exceptions stay BU1.
3. **F3, crossing:** Qualify `SYNTHESIS.md:27` as route/body dependent. Soul-form, Konsō, a human Reishi conversion device and Hollow/Garganta access are different assertions, with some wiki citations to anime (`Soul Society@581045 text:37-41`). Do not freeze one universal conversion rule from that row.
4. **F4, path gaps:** Add explicit research leads to BU4: the Quincy page's share-power exception and Hollow poison/environment dependencies (`Quincy@587658 text:41,106-07`), and the Fullbringer page's manga-attributed parental attack account versus later novel causal layer (`Fullbringer@584513 text:24,47`). Keep the novel outside the default original-manga continuity. These may change eligibility and fuel design.
5. **F5, label:** Call the observation/provenance row at `SYNTHESIS.md:30` an `I/O` design rule, so an episode's particular dialogue is not mistaken for an original-manga universal law.

## Further notes-supported leads the pack missed

- The shard-a Zanpakutō note records a specific exceptional Bankai training device and a dangerous time bound (`A20`; `Zanpakutō@582599 text:152`). The pack rightly rejects a universal shortcut, but BU1 should name this scene as a possible exceptional route to inspect.
- The shard-b Fullbringer note records a potentially damaging completion burst, another Fullbringer's suppression role, and a distinction between incomplete growth and realized function (`B728`; `Fullbringer@584513 text:35-39`). These could change the proposed mature career and its support costs. They remain secondary leads until the cited manga scenes are checked.

## Scope and hygiene

The pack's proposed starts, local people, Thread of Return, instruments, engine fields and arc outcomes are visibly marked `O`; I found no unlabelled proposal presented as Bleach canon in the checked claims. Anime-only, film, game, later-novel and parallel-instance content stays separate in the inspected owners. The only changed-truth slot visible here is `BL27`, with an ID, discovery route and status; its truth is not disclosed. A normalized 16-word match scan of all nine pack Markdown files against the 2,193 manifest payloads found **zero** source-text runs, and inspection found no raw source prose. `SPOTCHECK_1.md` reports one 16-word run in an upstream shard-b note; the pack does not reproduce it.

The pack honestly keeps original manga scenes, M3 fan premises, source-era casting, native adapters, persistence and playable browser acceptance open. The four source leads above should be attached to those existing gaps before implementation choices harden. This review changes no pack owner, runtime, save or accepted design.
