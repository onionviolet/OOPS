## Campaign promise and horizons

Build a meaningful life where perceiving a problem, physically reaching it and having the right soul/source to solve it are different achievements.
Early play preserves civilian records and creates a reliable contact between mundane work and a qualified spiritual service.
Middle play chooses a real eligible body/source career or remains a useful artisan, investigator or institutional supporter.
Mature play can develop powerful personal functions, a specialist workshop or a shelter network while major source threats remain genuinely dangerous.
The character can live in the living district, a separately selected soul-resident start or an authorized cross-domain visit.
Those profiles have explicit body/cargo/return rules; they are not menu labels that turn the same native body into a soul.
Neither a powerful sword nor an expensive instrument automatically makes every hidden spirit visible.
A strong supported spiritual power can defeat lower spiritual threats. A mundane attack still needs an actual interaction bridge before it can target them.

O blade practitioner's field ensemble can add ordinary protection/tools; the soul-bound blade remains its own source/relationship.
No generic magic suit is imposed on the Zanpakuto path. Research equipment supports tasks, not a substitute soul-release ladder.

## Three worked careers

### Poor living-district investigator

1. Take ordinary repair/parcels work from Emi and buy food/shelter through local means.
2. Compare a damaged address ledger with actual witnesses and inspect the public street blockage.
3. Build an ordinary marked detour, spending timber/cloth and labor; buy a qualified spiritual report where perception is absent.
4. Preserve the ledger and deliver a shelter manifest once, keeping particular memories and lawful cargo.
5. Establish a reliable relief office that funds others' spiritual work rather than pretending mundane tools can kill a Hollow.

Means: materials, civic permissions, ordinary stamina, actual witnesses and paid qualified contact.
New functions: safe mundane routing, supported report comparison, paid stock coordination and real neighborhood access.
Weakness: cannot see/target an unperceived spiritual threat without an actual bridge and does not acquire lineage through research.
Tradeoff: inexpensive meaningful livelihood, with dependence on finite specialist services during spiritual crises.

### Developed soul-bound blade and support practitioner

1. Select an eligible authored body/source profile with explicit retained/lost domains, or acquire a legitimately sourced local transition.
2. Obtain actual blade/teacher support and learn movement/control, without borrowing the protagonist's exceptional shortcuts.
3. Develop a personal sourced release through its real prerequisites; keep blade relationship separate from garment/tool condition.
4. Learn one suitable support method and test target eligibility, costs and interruption on explicit fixtures.
5. Serve a dangerous local patrol or rescue, using actual power against lower threats and preserving wounds/spent reserves.

Means: actual eligible soul/body, source/relationship, instruction, fuel and local access.
New functions: genuinely spiritual combat, source-specific release and prepared specialist support.
Weakness: superior fixed captain/Arrancar threats, limited reach/fuel and body/source incompatibility; no guaranteed revival.
Tradeoff: strong personal capability entails specific identity/support obligations and cannot be lent like an ordinary hammer.

### Mature cross-domain relief and research director

1. Preserve separate living and spiritual testimony with actual observer provenance.
2. Fund clinic, workshop and travel-office contributions from real people/sites.
3. Develop a tested instrument network using explicit local range/support, with no omniscient global feed.
4. Train/hire sufficiently capable protection and select institutional disclosure versus protected private records.
5. Face a major branch crisis by fighting suitable threats, evacuating selected people, protecting evidence or taking a narrow contract and leaving.

Means: several actual facilities, willing partners, compatible travel, qualified sources and sustained supply.
New functions: supported information exchange, larger relief operations and paid original experiments.
Weakness: domains/crossings can be blocked and staff lost; one instrument cannot resolve every conceptual/source conflict.
Tradeoff: public legitimacy brings scrutiny; private archives protect people but constrain institutional help.

## Five escalating arcs

### 1. The missing street record

Trigger: a real street blockage and damaged address ledger interrupt a shelter delivery; the public offer exists before observation.
Choice A: ordinary paid repair/detour with actual materials and witnesses; retains the specialist's reserve but consumes more physical stock/time.
Choice B: pay Nao's qualified survey, reserve her actual source/fuel/time and use a witnessed supported lead for a shorter lawful transfer.
Both require actual parcel custody and willing recipient; one settlement claim grants record-store study once.
Aftermath: different stock, service memories and observer provenance; buying a report does not grant spiritual perception to the player.
Abstention: shelter purchases a longer route from real funds and postpones service; later report reveals it without player debt or phantom reward.

### 2. Whose report counts?

Trigger: living and qualified spiritual witnesses give apparently conflicting accounts of a disappearance.
Choices: pay supported perception/research, establish mundane timeline through witnesses, or publish uncertainty and restrict dangerous routing.
Aftermath: verified scope changes safe commitments; forcing an unsupported conclusion damages a specific relationship or service access.
Abstention: Jun preserves uncertain records while Ritsu uses actual authority to close the route.
Later consequence: archive evidence affects body/domain travel clearance and research permission.

### 3. The tool and the soul

Trigger: a paid instrument trial appears useful but its support does not match an offered personal blade/Fullbring career.
Choices: develop the tool within actual scope, pursue legitimately eligible personal-source training, or buy a specialist service.
Aftermath: equipment opens a bounded function; personal training develops its distinct source; neither creates the other's prerequisites.
Abstention: Seki seeks another actual sponsor and may lose the sample; no guaranteed later recovery of rare evidence.
Later consequence: different research partners, duties and institutional access lead into regional relief work.

### 4. Relief across the boundary

Trigger: a real compatible travel offer intersects a shelter crisis in another domain.
Choices: funded personnel/cargo crossing, remote support through a sourced interface, private evidence extraction or refusal.
Aftermath: imported bodies/materials follow explicit conversion/retention rules; companions consent, and duties remain with actual people.
Abstention: Fuyu's partners use their own means and some services can close permanently.
Later consequence: surviving staff/testimony determine public office versus protected private network.

### 5. The threat above the patrol

Trigger: a separately sourced major conflict or clearly O divergence reaches the actual service network.
Choices: use genuinely sufficient power, evacuate selected people, defend a limited site, surrender records or complete a narrow contract and withdraw.
Aftermath: advanced sources are powerful; fixed greater threats can still overwhelm a build or destroy an institution.
Abstention: independent people may lose despite player success elsewhere; no guaranteed rescue or return route is implied.
Later consequence: lost body/source, destroyed originals and surviving relationships remain separate domains, with no automatic prestige conversion.
Terminal death, reincarnation/resurrection, source repair and full-run retention require explicit profile selection; source afterlife lore does not implement them.
