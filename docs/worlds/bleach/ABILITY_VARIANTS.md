# Bleach ability variants: personal sources, learned arts and compatible contact

Status: Develop/Research proposal r2, 2026-10-14, with dated secondary-wiki cross-check. No playable release, soul transfer, body conversion or spiritual realm is added.
Inherit [ability contract](../ABILITY_AUTHORING.md), [campaign](CAMPAIGN.md), [module owner](MODULE_CATALOGUE.md), [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md) and [engine packet](../conceptual/ENGINE_PACKET.md).
Baseline: `bleach.original.1-74.primary.r1`; bounded official anime corroboration does not import new TYBW additions, novels, games or later one-shots.
`C/A` follows the ledger; `O` is original adaptation; `U` is an unresolved original-scene rule. Every proposed variant/facet ID here is O.
Catalogue owns actual body/source/tool and local structural stages. Release development, learned arts, mastery, condition and office remain different tracks.

## Comparison 1: locate the same missing service tool

Task: find one actual compatible tool last seen in the district. Location evidence, reach, retrieval and rightful custody are four different outcomes.
O `bleach.variant.trace_read` uses ordinary witnesses/marks; O `bleach.variant.survey_report` uses Nao's qualified instrument/faculty; O Thread of Return remains the catalogue's personal-release candidate.
The candidate is not canon or an accepted Shikai design. Its acquisition/relationship eligibility still needs BU1/BU2 and human fiction selection.

| Use | Trace Read | Qualified Survey Report | Thread of Return candidate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | Interview an actual witness or follow an observed material mark | Qualified observer reports one supported signal with uncertainty | Actual earned source binds one deliberately touched compatible owned tool; indicates contact/direction within tested range |
| Developed | Compare timestamped leads and known routes; detect witnessed contradictions | Trained discrimination separates the selected signal from defined interference | Better learned control interprets motion/direction while maintaining the same real tether/support |
| Mature | Reliable funded reports cover more known routes | Superior qualified faculty/instrument can outperform ordinary trace reading on its supported signal | Mature tested personal function can locate its eligible tethered tool faster than both lower methods, without a forced weak-method niche |

The O tether never provides global people tracking, hidden memories, every tool's position or automatic retrieval. Those would be distinct functions requiring new briefs.
Catalogue currently proposes breakage at its tested range/obstruction. Preserve that local candidate rule; do not infer it for every canonical release.
Acquisition: witnesses supply testimony; equipment/faculty supplies observation support; personal release requires an actual actor/source relationship rather than a bought housing. Exception leads, not universal rules: the secondary page describes Zaraki claiming a dead Shinigami’s Asauchi, inherited special Zanpakutō that pass with abilities, and the Ise hereditary weapon as an exception to imprinting. Keep the one-actor/bond model `O` and inspect those primary scenes under BU1/BU2 before freezing transfer or persistence behavior.
Execution: a report grants actor-specific knowledge; a tether use grants its scoped direction/contact evidence; neither moves the tool or transfers its owner.
O costs are paid witness/service time, instrument support or the selected release's real reserve/control; no universal spiritual-energy ratio is asserted.
Contact needs compatible target/body/material. Seeing a spirit alone does not prove an ordinary metal tracker can bind or strike it.
Lost trace can end ordinary investigation; false witness evidence can mislead; unsupported survey channel produces honest uncertainty rather than an invented exact coordinate.
Ending the tether preserves the tool's actual custody/location and learned source relation. Rebinding must target the same real eligible object, not a fresh copy.
Tool removal/sale can affect eligibility under the selected O rule. Source suppression disables the tether, not unrelated learned footwork or archive evidence.
No source expiry/reload silently grants another soul, source or tool. Identity and bond must survive through their actual owner contract.

## Comparison 2: prevent the same eligible incoming strike

Task: protect one compatible participant from an authored strike with fixed path, timing and strength. Physical and spiritual contact must be established before damage rules.
O semantic modes compare trained blade/body guard, one source-qualified Kidō defense and a separately authored personal-release defense if later selected.
B7 corroborates learned arts; B1/B5 establish personal stages only. No named barrier, chant omission, generic release multiplier or copied soul is verified here.

| Use | Trained guard | Qualified taught defense | Personal-release defense candidate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | Actual compatible body/weapon and stance meet one known strike | Learned complete method, eligible source and fuel supply the sourced defense | No automatic defense from Shikai/Bankai labels; first define the actual personal function |
| Developed | Timing/reach improve against observed paths without universal invulnerability | Trained control and selected target/range support actual protection | Earned source plus practiced function protects its real eligible scope |
| Mature | Exceptional body/weapon can genuinely overpower the lesser strike | A powerful qualified barrier can simply exceed weaker guard on this task | A superior actual release may dominate both lower defenses when its mechanism supports it |

Mastery does not let ordinary steel contact a spiritual target by author fiat. Strong physical force remains useful wherever actual contact is supported.
A real source-provided protection or immunity works against its eligible mechanism. No last-minute special immunity bypass is invented to preserve a puzzle.
Acquisition of a taught art, weapon possession and personal release are separate. Institutional title/captain office does not award a release or guarantee strength.
B2's noncaptain Bankai users are evidence for stage/office separation, not a formula for every Soul Reaper's mature output.
Startup before impact, maintained control, target movement and interruption determine protection. The resolver must choose ordering before the player acts.
Source-specific chant/tradeoff rules remain BU3. Do not assert that silent casting always has one fixed penalty or that the same number means the same function.
O overlapping defenses use declared compatible domains/order. Two effects can cooperate; an unsupported simultaneous solver is not replaced by whichever result helps the plot.
Stopping protection retains spent reserve, wounds already sustained and source ownership. It never rewinds the attacker or observer memories.
NPCs can own superior sources and win. Their knowledge of an imported counter requires an actual observed use, report or sourced detection.

## Comparison 3: restore the same lost field function

Task: restore a selected practitioner's ability to undertake a patrol after damage. First identify whether the fault is equipment, body or personal release.
O `bleach.variant.case_repair`, source-qualified treatment and source-specific release recovery can all restore capability, but they transform different actual state owners.
B6 corroborates damaged Shikai recovery and normally nonreturning destroyed Bankai. The cited wiki limits ordinary Shikai recovery to the wielder’s will/Reiatsu over time with the hilt intact. It separately names broken-Bankai cases: Komamura’s linked healing, Hitsugaya’s water-dependent regeneration, Mayuri’s remodeling, Nimaiya’s reforging and Orihime’s rejection. These remain source-specific leads, not a generic repair API; exact primary exception scenes remain BU1/BU2.

| Use | Ordinary compatible tool repair | Qualified bodily treatment | Source-specific release recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | Inspect damaged case, buy real materials and restore its retained support | Diagnose the actual eligible patient; treatment category is not a universal cure | Identify source state; no restoration from a generic “repair” button |
| Developed | Paid fitting/calibration restores the same tool's tested function | Supported source/method/stock treats its selected condition | Apply only an inspected source recovery rule to the actual bound weapon/actor |
| Mature | Skilled team repairs several retained tools with real stock/labor | Superior sourced treatment can exceed ordinary care on eligible conditions | A genuine exceptional sourced procedure may restore more, but must retain its exact exceptions/costs |

Tool repair preserves specimen, installed support, owner, holder and condition history. It does not manufacture the owner's spirit or learn a release.
Positive HP is not universal restoration of spiritual source, lost limb, soul identity, broken Bankai or dead person. Each requires its own target/transformation.
Repairing an ordinary sheath can fully solve a sheath fault while leaving the blade's actual release fault unchanged.
Damaged Shikai and destroyed Bankai cannot share one generic integrity slider with the same routine repair recipe.
Exceptional restoration must not be prohibited by an invented absolute ban; exact source-qualified exceptions can work once inspected.
Treatment and source repair need actual compatible contact, qualified provider, materials/fuel, available actor and declared interruption policy.
Failed paid diagnosis/trial retains used inputs and supported evidence. A stale/borrowed destructive specimen rejects before spend.
Stopping an ordinary repair leaves the retained part in its current paid-work state; it does not award a fresh item or reset source damage.
Body/soul death, reincarnation and realm passage remain BU6 plus unselected policy. A lore label never adds an extra life to the current game.

## Copy, transfer and imitation are different routes

O observation can copy a visible guard sequence into a study record; taught practice makes the receiver execute it with their own body/source.
An instrument can record supported signals without understanding the method. Replay does not create the teacher's reserve, blade bond or faculties.
Learning one exact sourced Kidō method can retain teaching provenance; reading its name or number is not qualified execution.
A forged blade can reproduce shape and ordinary material handling. It does not acquire its model's soul/name/relationship by visual similarity.
B10's Rukia transfer is a particular source event, not a generic transfer API. B12 Fullbring training and B11 Quincy difference remain distinct eligible paths.
O victory/kill/sacrifice copying from the conceptual dossier is not a Bleach inheritance law. BU4/BU6 must qualify any actual source-specific transfer.
Copying a registered power definition in the current engine supplies no faithful personal-source transfer. Donor ownership, faculty, body and provenance need separate adapters.
Source theft can legitimately disable the donor if its chosen mechanic says so; neither physical blade theft nor title theft automatically steals a soul.

## Mastery and subprogression within a personal function

O facets: recognition/attunement evidence, actual source relation, execution control, target selection, stable reach, reserve efficiency and integration/recovery.
These are testable local dimensions, not new canonical release stages. Bankai is neither the automatic end of mastery nor a uniform numerical multiplier.
A practitioner may have a powerful earned release but poor precision; another may have excellent taught control without that personal source.
Practice can qualify a narrow target transition, then an interrupted restart, then supported multi-action integration, paying actual means at each trial.
Completing one facet does not level every other facet. New functions need demonstrated source support; efficiency alone cannot create a missing bond.
A mature superior function can improve precision and output together after real development; no mandatory efficiency-versus-power penalty is imposed.
Catalogue owns tool/body/source tracks; this file owns semantic facet tests and resulting modes, avoiding a duplicate progression table.

## Implementation gate and checkpoint

Missing adapters: spiritual perception/contact/passage, actual personal weapon/source identity, release state/damage, qualified target rules and general native combat.
Freeze a narrow qualified survey or ordinary compatible repair proof first; exact canonical release/source transfers wait on BU1-BU6.
Preview grants no hidden soul/build information; wrong domain, missing body/fuel, stale owner/source and occupied provider reject without spend.
Legal use changes only its declared target state; observer knowledge, donor retention/loss, repair identity and interruption costs remain inspectable.
Repeated/reloaded copy/repair cannot mint weapon/source or erase permanent damage; failed storage publishes neither cost nor restored function.
Rebuild and exercise execution/save/reload on generated fixtures later. This dossier adds no playable release, extra life, migration or human acceptance.
