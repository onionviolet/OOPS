## Patterns across the books

1. **Continuity comes in separate pieces.** Soul, memory, body, practiced
   skill, power source, items and office persist separately. Inheritance
   is rarely total.
2. **Death is usually final, and revival is a specific, priced
   exception:** a one-use feather, a daily charge, a respawn allowance,
   twelve linked knights, a body that must be intact.
3. **What the dead leave behind is material that moves:** genes,
   shadows, Beyonder characteristics, Gu, Memories, voices, runestones.
   Heritage is often gated by tests, bloodline or conduct.
4. **Recognition is narrow and fallible.** A ghost sees death layers but
   not regression; Heaven's Will flags one class of outsider; diviners
   detect a rewind; ordinary people notice behavior, not souls.
5. **Descendants get the story, debts, curses and seals more often than
   the mastery or memories.** Loop worlds charge for repetition: soul
   scars, orphaned branches, lost relationships.

### Evidence rules (W01-W07)

- **W01** Record body death, surviving soul or remnant, soul confinement,
  identity loss and proven soul destruction separately. A surviving item or
  power is not necessarily the dead person.
- **W02** **Shown** = demonstrated in the work as reported by the reviewed
  source; **stated** = exposition, rule or testimony; **inferred** = R4's
  reading. Wiki retellings stay secondary. Unknown is not impossible.
- **W03** NE2 does not always mean a newborn with memories. Native returns
  may form an adult body, cycle a species with memory loss, or carry only a
  component. Name the actual entry form and retention.
- **W04** NE1 (fresh perspective), NE9 (fresh campaign elsewhere), an actual
  successor and prestige stay separate options. A world need not support
  resurrection to support another run.
- **W05** A native exception needs the specific actor or species, source,
  era and surviving carrier. Wider use is a labeled cheat or AU. Keep
  source-specific counts, cooldowns and kill conditions; add no global
  one-return cap and invent no cost the source lacks.
- **W06** Era bounds below are evidence bounds for an authored start pool,
  not limits on where life can exist. Unrepresented periods need an authored
  premise.
- **W07** Recognition needs a channel, a known signature or records, an
  available observer and a confidence level. Detecting a soul is not
  identifying a prior life. Proposed ladder (W42): presence detected,
  familiar pattern, prior identity corroborated.
