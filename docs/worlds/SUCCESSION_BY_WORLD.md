## Patterns across the books

1. **Continuity comes in separate pieces.** Soul, memory, body, practiced
   skill, power source, items and office persist separately. Inheritance
   is rarely total.
2. **Death is usually final, and revival is a specific, priced
   exception:** a one-use feather, a daily charge, a respawn allowance,
   twelve linked knights, a body that must be intact.
3. **What the dead leave behind is material that moves:** genes,
   shadows, Beyonder characteristics, Gu, Memories, voices, runestones.
   Heritage is often gated by tests, bloodline or conduct.
4. **Recognition is narrow and fallible.** A ghost sees death layers but
   not regression; Heaven's Will flags one class of outsider; diviners
   detect a rewind; ordinary people notice behavior, not souls.
5. **Descendants get the story, debts, curses and seals more often than
   the mastery or memories.** Loop worlds charge for repetition: soul
   scars, orphaned branches, lost relationships.
