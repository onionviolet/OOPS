# Chainsaw Man: Fear Has a Price

Status: deep world pack proposal, 2026-10-13. Source-backed design, not accepted gameplay or a runtime world. [SYNTHESIS](SYNTHESIS.md) is the research and M5 owner; this brief owns the entry promise and checkpoint.

## Authority and boundary

Weibao placed this anime world in the canon-first tier. Proposal follows the world-depth direction: canon play first, with direct starts and later AU overlays as options. Snapshot is the Chainsaw Man Wiki through 2023-11-27, including early Part 2. It is secondary and does not authenticate canon. Part 2 material after the cutoff is a gap. Do not fill it from memory. Public franchise twists may be used; changed or randomized truth is sealed by ID and discovered in play.

## Entry promise (proposal)

Play an ordinary person whose survival depends on what they know, whom they can trust, what they can afford to give up, and whether the danger is something they can actually fight. Fear changes Devils' strength at population scale; a contract is a personal, binding bargain with a specific Devil; public employment, private hunting and school access are different routes into danger.

First proposed opening: a civilian near a Devil incident is offered a paid evacuation task, a Public Safety support role, or a private hunter's lead. No start grants a Devil contract, special lineage, Hybrid body, or knowledge of hidden terms by default. Abstaining remains playable and does not create a supernatural penalty.

## Connected pursuits (proposals)

| Proposal | Loop | Distinct payoff |
|---|---|---|
| P1 Civilian support | Earn wages through delivery, shelter, records and supply work around incidents | Access, contacts and evidence without combat power |
| P2 Hunter career | Choose agency, partner, preparation and bounded contract terms | Qualified access plus ongoing costs, obligations and risk |
| P3 Devil research | Compare witness reports, anatomy, blood effects and public fear | Better forecasts; uncertainty stays visible where sources conflict |
| P4 School route | Attend a club, build trust and investigate local incidents | Peer relationships and different institutional exposure |

All rows are proposals. Exact cast, era, dates, local people and job details await a selected starting snapshot and primary-source review.

## Danger and return

Proposal: threats keep their source-authored strength; no automatic level scaling. A player can lose a job, ally, resource or objective and may face permanent local consequences only under an explicit later failure policy. Death, irreversible loss, offline consequences, revival, and export are unselected. Reasons to return include ongoing employment, relationships, research, contracts and consequences in the same durable instance.
