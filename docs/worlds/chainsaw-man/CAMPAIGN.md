## Institution and place proposals

Proposal: model Public Safety, private hunting, school clubs, civilian neighborhoods, hospitals, shelters and Devil incident sites as separate access contexts. The wiki states that in Japan only Devil Hunters may legally contract with Devils or carry guns; a civilian may still choose illegal access as a consequential proposal. Assignments, reports, benefits and other legal reach come from local evidence and authored roles, not a universal hunter rank. Sources: “Devil Hunter” rev24927; “Contract” rev25006; “Tokyo Special Division 4” rev21887; “Fourth East High School” rev23281. Exact institutional procedure is not established by these summaries.

## Worked journeys (proposals)

### C1: report versus direct pursuit

Start as CS1. A witnessed incident creates a local report. The player can spend time and funds to support a qualified investigator, or pursue a public lead with greater danger and weaker information. Knowledge belongs to the actual observer; a paid report does not grant perception. Consequence: resources, access and certainty differ. The incident's actual source and aftermath remain hidden until a supported discovery route.

### C2: contract offer

Start as eligible human hunter. A specific Devil offers a specific ability for a stated payment. Inspect, ask questions, refuse, or accept only after terms are recorded. Acceptance is binding under wiki summary; no generic menu contract. Proposal: model counterparty, legal parties, exact trigger, price, use/access, persistence, breach, and any explicitly sourced exit. No offer is compulsory.

### C3: containment without combat

Start civilian, support worker or hunter. Secure shelter and a route, gather witnesses, and transfer supplies while a threat occupies the site. Ordinary work is valuable but cannot damage a Devil without a source-supported means. Success settles through custody and witnesses, not an invented combat XP reward.

### C4: Academy investigation

Start CS3 in a school context. Build trust, attend club activity and compare rumors about an incident. School access does not itself grant a contract, weapon, or accurate knowledge. Source presence: “Fourth East High School” rev23281 and “Devil Hunter Club” rev23133. Events beyond the wiki snapshot remain gaps.

## Change, continuity and knowledge

Proposal: store actual event occurrence separately from revelation, belief, and response. A changed branch gets its own continuity/instance. Do not quietly restore canon after player intervention. Foreknowledge can guide investigation but never auto-completes it. Re-entry retains settled people, jobs, claims and costs under shared instance rules; freeze absent-world time unless a world-specific accepted policy says otherwise.
