Direct payload check: “Devil” rev25098 says Primal Fear Devils have never experienced the usual death-and-rebirth cycle and have retained their identities; it does not state what happens if one is killed. War's claim about Falling's present untouchability belongs to her belief, not a class property. A source condition says Devil strength can be boosted by flesh or blood from a living Devil, while a separate flesh-boost bullet is unconditioned; preserve this internal tension. Positive emotion can weaken Devils generally. Fiends cannot contract and Devils cannot contract with one another. “Contract” rev25006 describes binding terms, party and sacrifice rules, plus an apparent peaceful termination example for Fox/Aki; do not turn the generic breach summary into a universal rule that erases this exception. It also says a mistaken Devil identity does not invalidate a deal when both sides meet the terms, and describes remote sacrifice.

Additional directly checked rules: “Contract” rev25006 lists own body parts, senses, lifespan, legal children and people under legal responsibility as sacrifice categories; unrelated people are ineligible, though an incapacitated person may be offered through a proxy. “Devil Hunter” rev24927 states the Japan-only-hunters legal rule for contracts and guns. “Fiend” rev24028 describes Power's return to full Devil state after a blood pact, with uniqueness uncertain. “Hybrid” rev25122 says trigger activation can heal/revive without drinking blood and heart removal can let the Devil reform. “Devil” rev25098 reports that abilities often spend the Devil's own blood and that Aki says these effects end when the Devil dies; it hedges that all fragments or the core may need destruction before reincarnation, and that Horsemen-level Devils faintly remember erased concepts. These remain secondary wiki claims, not primary canon authentication.
