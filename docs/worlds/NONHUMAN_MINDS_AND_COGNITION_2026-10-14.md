## 1. Knowledge modes in depth: N11

A mind profile describes a knower, provenance and accessible memories. A body
profile describes channels and practiced execution. A controller selects actions;
it need not be the owner of every controlled actor's knowledge (C13, TX10).
Default: no mind mode confers narrator omniscience. Dial: declared foreknowledge,
native expertise, memory magic or stronger starts. Reasonably: supplied history
can grant substantial expertise immediately without a mandatory ignorance phase.

| Mode | Knows at entry | Forgets or cannot retrieve | Characteristic misreading | Useful play and dials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human-memory reincarnator | Declared personal history, known languages/scripts, learned theory, memories of human manners. MN-E1 is the comparison, not a free Rimuru skill package. | Old context may be vague by selected premise; bodily skill can be remembered but inexecutible. Default transformation alone does not delete earned memory (TX6). | Expects nods, smiles or coins held in fingers; assumes a familiar-looking human custom has the old meaning. Source foreknowledge may belong to another edition or era. | Use retained knowledge to advise a scribe or classify a recipe while practicing a new channel. Dial complete/partial memory, no canon foreknowledge, remembered fiction with explicit reliability. Staying nonhuman remains a full route (N15). |
| Native animal mind | Local landmarks, familiar individuals, reinforced signals, routines and bodily competence supported by this start. It need not know human words to distinguish several people or solve a route problem. | No human autobiography by default. Unlearned contracts and scripts are unknown, not remembered and suppressed. Forgetting uses real age/interference/premise, not a daily intelligence tax. | Models pointing as an interesting moving hand, or hears a command word inside unrelated speech; those are authored misunderstandings, not species-wide rules. | Choose destination, signal, investigate or decline. Plain readable choices preserve player agency even if the character lacks inner verbal sentences. Dial ordinary animal cognition, awakened reasoning or human-level native sapience. |

## 2. Instinct, reason and source compulsion: N8/TX7

Default: instinct highlights an attractive action; reason lets the player compare
it with a chosen goal. Neither is automatically morally better. A hound can want
to follow a scent while remembering a delivery promise. Present: “Fresh trail.
The parcel is still due.” Offer investigate, continue, or ask a partner to check.
The useful scent action can have a local bonus without taxing every alternative.

| Cause | Small UI/play treatment | Boundary and dial |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physiological or learned pull | One optional cue beside a relevant action; remembered comfort or attention shift. Show its basis if known: scent, season, learned association. | N8: decline remains an ordinary action. Dial strong migration/dormancy/body attraction with useful preparation; no hidden attack at a threshold. |
| Deliberate reasoning | Known evidence and one practical alternative, including asking, testing or waiting. | A reasoned hypothesis can be wrong. Dialogue should not silently reveal an NPC motive because the player chose “think.” |
| Source compulsion | Under a selected premise, disclose the known restricted verb, trigger, duration and available resistance/mitigation/exit. If the cause is unknown, name the observed restriction without exposing its sealed truth. | Canon/AU attribution required. HP duty is not automatically mind control (MN-E5); chosen service stays reachable. Offline processing stops at a new unapproved consequential decision. |
| Controller contest | Show who can currently issue the affected action and which actual channel/lease is contested. | TX7/E8: body urges, enforced source rules and another controller taking over are three different events. Resistance, shared control or hostile takeover need authored terms. |

Reasonably per world: allow habituation, an alternative outlet, medicine, ward,
partner warning or negotiated release where supported. Suppressing an urge need
not reduce all XP or erase the species' useful senses. A source-specific loyalty
rule such as Paradise BC3 is not an engine-wide obedience switch. An unselected
compulsion does not arrive merely because a body evolves. A selected dark premise
can constrain actions substantially, but should preserve purposeful play within
its stated scope and never smuggle in hunger death.

## 3. Emotion and expression without a human face

Default: intention, emitted signal, observer interpretation and response are four
steps. An NPC can see agitation and still mistake its cause. The player knows its
chosen expression, not every involuntary reflex or private observer belief.

| Channel palette | Possible authored expression | Possible local misread | Playable repair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tail, ears, posture | Short stiff wag, relaxed lean, turned head, lowered body | Enthusiasm, aggression or guilt assigned without context | Slow approach, retreat a pace, point toward the cause with another channel |
| Skin/shell color or glow | Excitement, overload, intentional pattern | Human reads warning as celebration; same hue means a different thing in another community | Pair pattern with an agreed symbol; ask a native interpreter |
| Temperature | Blade deliberately warms through a supported faculty | Holder assumes sunlight or an unsafe fault | Repeat after cooling with consent; no warmth cue for an entity that cannot emit or sense it |
| Vibration, scent, sound | Local greeting, warning pulse, familiar scent marker | Human sees damage, stink or a threat rather than courtesy | Demonstrate one signal and its intended action without granting universal translation |
| Silence or stillness | Chosen restraint, attentive listening, grief, privacy | “Gone,” “broken,” “obedient,” or uninterested | Explicit wait/listen signal, agreed lantern mark, later witnessed response |

Color cues include words; sound cues include text; silence is described as an
observed action when witnessed. Plain text retains the same information.
Default expressions are low-noise and contextual (MM7, SB1/SB5/SB9), with no
universal mandatory mood meters. Dial private feelings, involuntary signals,
perfect expression control or culture-specific restraint. Reasonably, trusted
contacts learn recurring signals through actual encounters. An NPC's understanding
of a signal and legal recognition of personhood remain different (SL6, M4).

MN-E7 is a useful check: armor without touch/warmth cannot discover comfort by
feeling a hand's heat. It can value the person's choice to stay nearby, hear a
voice or remember past touch. Emotion survives loss of one sensory route.

## 4. Language, translation, literacy and numbers

Default: distinguish recognizing a signal, understanding words, producing them,
reading a script, writing it, and using its number notation. Skill retention and
current access follow E9/E10, TX6 and progression PM12. No eyes is not a universal
ban on reading; no voice is not a ban on understanding or dialogue.

An illustrative learning chain is useful act → stable shared signal → repeated
word in context → comparison with a second use → wider grammar. This is an
authoring route, not a compulsory grind. A native educated nonhuman or a supported
translation power can start far along it. A queued lesson names tutor/material,
language, opportunity and outcome; overhearing a word does not yield full fluency.

| Distinction | Example and playable route | What remains unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speech versus comprehension | Slime understands a request but signals by movement until an articulation method exists (MN-E1). Partner may voice a selected answer. | Partner only conveys the selected message; the NPC cannot hear inner narration. |
| Translation versus truth | Spell relays a merchant's claim; metaphor or unfamiliar role gets a qualified gloss. Dial exact semantic translation if truly sourced. | Successful translation does not authenticate a lie, explain every custom or make a telepathic channel private. |
| Script versus content | Former human knows letters; a scribe reads an inaccessible page aloud, or a supported tactile/mental adapter supplies the symbols. | Hearing the content teaches that content, not proof the listener decoded the script. |
| Quantity versus numeral notation | Native animal distinguishes a missing bundle in a learned routine; monster trader knows local weights but learns decimal written prices. Dial sophisticated native mathematics. | Approximate “more” need not grant arithmetic; counting tokens does not teach interest, contracts or a currency's value. |
| Telepathy versus shared thought | Aranea exchanges a bounded message or packet with a compatible recipient (MN-E4). A ward may block it. | A mental message is not universal language, intent detection or open access to all memories. |
| Expression failure versus reasoning failure | Damaged created core disrupts speech while remembered identity remains declared (MN-E6 BC5). Offer known fallback signals. | A garbled reply is not automatic loss of intelligence, trust or personhood. |

Reasonably per world: signboards, tokens, dictated ledgers, tutors, touch writing,
mental images and actual translation faculties all keep communication reachable.
Dial no common language, partial understanding, native scripts or perfect sourced
translation. A player may read clear interface labels while the character knows
only a local routine; interface usability need not be an in-world magic power.

## 5. Memory and time perception

Default: remember the event's actual time and provenance; present recollection
through the mind's own cues. Scent can retrieve “the visitor at the mill,” territory
can retrieve “past the warm stone,” and a blade can remember a particular grip
through whatever channel it actually had. A cue alone does not prove the visitor's
current location, mood or identity. Rain, rebuilding and a different carrier can
make a cue ambiguous. Forgetting is a named premise or consequence, not automatic
background decay added to every unusual mind (SB4/SB6/SB7).

| Memory/time palette | Default + Dial | Useful consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scent-indexed recollection | Context association with known encounter; dial persistent supernatural signature or scent archive | Compare with a fresh trace; unknown mixture stays ambiguous. No exact event replay without a source. |
| Territorial map | Route relative to real landmarks and reach; dial precise map or changing root network | Detect that a familiar passage is obstructed; ask a witness rather than silently rewriting the world to match memory. |
| Social or procedural memory | Known person's routine or demonstrated technique; dial maker-installed routines or perfect recall | Recognize an exception, teach an explanation, or replay a supported procedure. Retained theory does not transfer absent muscles. |
| Packet/copy memory | Attributed received account with transfer time, missing parts and uncertainty; dial intact transfer | Separate the recipient's discovery from the donor's experience; MN-E2/E4/N39. |
| Ghost stuck in one day | Selected recurring recollection while world time continues; dial an actual local time loop with its own clock contract | A new receipt or visitor contradicts expectation. Breaking the memory pattern can be a route, not mandatory cure of all ghosthood. |
| Seasonal or accelerated awareness | Narrative rhythm uses long/short spans; dial tree-season summaries or sourced thought acceleration | A season-feeling tree still sees the objective appointment deadline; accelerated thinking creates no extra physical work or duplicate queue payout. |

The recurring-day ghost has an authored policy for new learning: it may retain
anchor-linked changes, retain an external journal while losing recall, or retain
all learning despite an emotional sense of recurrence. Each is reachable; say
which. “Every morning feels familiar” does not silently erase NPC history or paid
work. No plot outcome is made inevitable just to preserve the motif.

Subjective time, body age, experienced learning time, world calendar and offline
elapsed time are distinct. OT3/OT6 and PG7 own scheduling. Default text can say
“one bloom later,” with the calendar date/deadline still accessible. Dial compressed
chapters or absent-world continuation only under the selected clock policy.
Reasonably, let the player use ordinary scheduling tools irrespective of a mind's
poetic time sense. A fast processor still needs inputs, attention, fuel where
applicable and an executable method (MN-E1 TM4).

## 6. Theory of mind, deception, play and curiosity

Default: represent a few specific beliefs that affect an authored encounter,
not a simulated psychological model for every being. A hound predicts a familiar
person will open a door. It can learn that the person did not see the parcel, or
that the stranger does not understand the same signal. Conversely a human can
learn that silence means waiting in this relationship, without knowing the hound's
private reasoning. Intelligence, empathy, vocabulary and obedience are separate.

Human-memory minds can overestimate how much a human observer recognizes them;
native minds can overestimate humans' ability to smell or receive a thought.
A distributed knower can underestimate separate human perspectives; a human can
mistake one member's report for agreement by the whole collective. An AI can
model declared rules well but miss unstated courtesy, while a human mistakes its
clarifying question for defiance. These are authored opportunities, not universal
deficiencies attached to a mode.

**Deception:** distinguish absent evidence, misunderstood signal, deliberate lie,
concealment and feigned incompetence. A sword overhearing talk while people assume
it is inert gains only what reached its channel (SB2). Faking broken speech does
not fool a witness who saw a fluent demonstration unless they have a reason to
discount it. Panda's tactical misdirection (MN-E8) supports a particular capable
created mind; it does not turn every puppet into an expert deceiver. NPCs can lie
to a nonhuman or misread sincere behavior under the same evidence rules.

**Play:** a mock chase, rhythm game, puzzle or repeated harmless surprise can
teach shared expectations and offer pleasure without a combat requirement.
Default both parties can stop; a new partner may mistake a play signal for threat.
Dial species-local games, purely private experimentation or no play routine.
Reasonably, learning “this person is playing” can change the next encounter's
response, not automatically unlock legal rights or a large affection reward.

**Curiosity:** “inspect again” should have an available observation or experiment,
such as checking whether the bell works when the caretaker is absent. Default
reward is an actual revised belief or useful clue. Dial novelty-seeking instinct,
methodical research or uninterested temperament. Repeated inspection without new
input should not farm knowledge XP or manufacture revelations. Quiet refusal and
choosing a familiar routine remain meaningful play.
