# Nonhuman kin and relationships, 2026-10-14

Status: research and **INFERRED proposals**, not accepted decisions, implemented gameplay or browser proof. Lane request, **STATED (Weibao):** “Non human relationships (monster family and more?)”. [COMMON](../gm/dispatch/nonhuman-starts-2026-10-14/COMMON.md) governs this lane; [checkpoint](../gm/dispatch/nonhuman-starts-2026-10-14/kin/CHECKPOINT.md) records completion. Only this report and the kin checkpoint are this lane's write area.

A monster family needs more than a species tag or a bond score. Who produced you, who raised you, who shares your home, who can give orders and who receives your legacy can be different people. A queen can be a progenitor without being your caregiver. Two blades can regard one another as siblings because they remember the same forge. An adopted human can belong to a monster household without gaining its organs or powers.

## 2. Family forms and the roles inside them (NK1-NK18)

These are illustrative original palettes. “Parent” may mean progenitor, caregiver or recognized adoptive parent; keep the basis. “Sibling” may mean shared progenitor, same clutch, shared workshop or chosen kin. “Mate” can name a reproductive partner or a lasting social bond; each needs its actual basis and does not imply the other. An elder may be a teacher rather than a blood relative. No table row asserts real zoological universality. Roles can overlap, and regard is directed. Leaving changes membership or duties through an actual cause; it need not erase affection or ancestry. Returning checks current access, recognition, vacancies and unresolved duties rather than restoring an old snapshot.
