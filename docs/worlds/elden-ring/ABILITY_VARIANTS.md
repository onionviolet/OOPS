# Elden Ring ability variants: arm, spell, recovery and assistance

Status: Develop/Research proposal r1, 2026-10-03. No registered Elden Ring command, realm or executed encounter is claimed.
Read [ability contract](../ABILITY_AUTHORING.md), [campaign](CAMPAIGN.md), [module owner](MODULE_CATALOGUE.md), [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md) and [engine packet](../conceptual/ENGINE_PACKET.md).
Baseline: `eldenring.base.primary.r1`; base premise only, without DLC/Nightreign/later-edition additions. Outsider grace eligibility remains unselected.
`C` is bounded ledger evidence, `O` an original local adaptation, `U` exact unverified source rules. Every variant ID and fixture number here is O.
Weapon reinforcement, Ash/affinity eligibility and physical loadout stages belong to MODULE_CATALOGUE. These comparisons do not create another universal rank ladder.

## Comparison 1: stop the same toll patrol

Task: open one route through a fixed ordinary patrol while protecting the bearer. The patrol has authored reach, cover and output independent of player level.
O semantic modes are `er.variant.reach_guard`, practiced arm handling; `er.variant.ash_burst`, a selected source-qualified weapon skill; `er.variant.catalyst_strike`, a selected source-qualified spell.
E2d/E7 establish eligible Ash/weapon skill use and FP; E2a establishes sorcery catalyst support. Exact named damage/range/effects stay EU1/EU2/EU7-gated.
The modes below are proposed task envelopes, not canon claims that every Ash is a burst or every sorcery follows these rules.

| Use | Reach Guard | Qualified Ash Burst | Qualified Catalyst Strike |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | Fitted arm and learned stance protect one approach within reach; real stamina | Actual eligible arm/Ash and learned use perform one sourced skill with its actual FP | Equipped compatible catalyst, learned spell and usable body supply the selected cast |
| Developed | Trained timing handles known feints and guards the bearer's movement | Actual skill timing/output integrates with guard and recovery | Control and positioning use the chosen range/target rule, retaining casting costs |
| Mature | Strong trained body/arm defeats the fixed lesser patrol directly | Powerful qualified skill can simply exceed ordinary guard on the same eligible target | Sufficient source-qualified spell output can eliminate this patrol without a compensating immunity |

Acquisition: practice learns handling; an Ash is an actual held/refitted source; a spell is a learned method with separate catalyst/body/fuel.
Observation of a thrust grants visible movement evidence O. It does not generate an Ash, whetblade, staff, FP reserve or named spell knowledge automatically.
An observed skill can become a tested study lead. Whether a particular canon spell can be learned that way needs its own source, not a universal copying ban.
E7's Whetstone Knife and grace refit apply to eligible equipment. No on-demand combat swap or free duplicated Ash is inferred.
An arm's built-in/unique skill and an installed eligible Ash have different ownership/compatibility; a family label does not make them interchangeable.
O test tuning can select a demand-four patrol and output-six mature method. Exact franchise scaling and rank equivalence are not inferred from those numbers.
Numerical strength really changes outcome. A weak character can fail; enough supported force can win without solving an extra author-invented ritual.
Commitment freezes actual bearer, patrol, arm/spell revision, resources and selected action. Unknown patrol capabilities remain unknown at preview.
Startup, impact and recovery occur in declared order. A destroyed catalyst or lost range before impact needs the selected interruption rule.
Losing the catalyst removes supported casting, not the remembered spell or unrelated weapon handling. Spent FP remains spent after withdrawal.
Compatible guard plus casting is allowed when hands, load, timing and actual support fit. Simultaneous resolution is a missing general adapter.
Custody/title aftermath follows witnesses and the issuer's actual rule; force does not automatically establish rightful urn ownership.

## Comparison 2: recover the same eligible wounded traveler

Task: restore an eligible living traveler's ordinary wound before another sortie. Disease, poison, soul damage and bodily death are separate target questions.
O `er.variant.field_dress` uses mundane care; `er.variant.healing_cast` adapts one sourced healing incantation; qualified grace/rest is E2c's source-player context.
E4 supports healing as an incantation category. It does not verify a specific seal, self/ally target, amount, cure range or resurrection.

| Use | Field Dress | Qualified Healing Cast | Qualified grace/rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | Suitable dressing/care stabilizes the declared wound with actual stock and time | Eligible caster/target, learned incantation and compatible support provide its selected effect | Supported source-player context restores E2c's represented HP/FP/flasks |
| Developed | Better diagnosis/technique treats more verified ordinary cases | Practiced control improves actual supported efficiency/application where source permits | Plan actual return access and recovery without implying a portable grace |
| Mature | Staffed care treats several eligible people with independently supplied labor | Superior sourced healing can outperform ordinary care in speed and output on the same wound | Valid complete recovery can be strictly better for its eligible user at that site |

No artificial loss of healing power is required to preserve bandages. Price, access and eligibility explain routes without requiring equal strength.
Diagnosis knowledge, treatment method, actual materials, sacred-seal eligibility and FP are separate. Buying a seal does not grant the incantation.
O local treatment charges must be selected with the later brief. No number here becomes the canonical spell cost or player-grace formula.
Contact/line/range and self/ally eligibility follow the selected named effect's EU1 evidence; do not assume all healing is touch or all targeting is remote.
An unknown wound can permit a paid diagnostic trial with uncertain applicability; preview cannot reveal the hidden cause as a free certainty.
Interrupted dressing leaves used stock and its actual partial effect. Interrupted casting follows that spell's startup/impact cost policy.
A clamp to maximum HP is not repair of every tissue, removal of radiation, resurrection or restoration of an erased source.
Ordinary care and magical healing can cooperate if they treat actual compatible states. Duplicate “health restored” receipts must not mint extra fuel or rewards.
Grace recovery belongs to the supported user/context. E2c death/Stake return is not universal NPC immortality or an outsider's entry benefit.
A dead witness cannot satisfy the O ferry's living-consent rule merely by granting positive HP. Any revival needs EU3 and an explicit policy.
Retention: wounds/treatment history and spent supplies persist; a learned incantation remains after losing support; returning does not replay a unique treatment reward.

## Comparison 3: support the same bearer on a hostile crossing

Task: distract or defeat the fixed patrol while the urn bearer crosses. “Help” must name actual combat/access output and separate consent obligations.
O `er.variant.hired_guard` is a real living contractor; `er.variant.spirit_cover` uses selected source-qualified spirit assistance; `er.variant.solo_cover` uses personal strength.
E2b requires the represented nearby-monument context and one spirit type at a time. Exact calling costs, durations and upgrade rules stay EU2/EU4-gated.

| Use | Hired Guard | Qualified Spirit Cover | Solo Cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | Available person accepts paid terms and supplies their actual arm/body | Actual acquired Ash/bell/support permit the selected call in valid context | Player uses owned arm/method and real stamina/fuel |
| Developed | Trained pair coordinate known signals with separate reserves | Practitioner learns timing with the selected spirit's supported behavior | Developed body/skill protects the bearer while managing reach/recovery |
| Mature | Powerful paid ally can decisively beat the lesser patrol | A sufficiently strong qualified spirit can be strictly superior to a weak hired guard at this combat task | A much stronger player can win unaided and retain the fee/calling stock |

A spirit's strong combat function stays strong. The local O living-witness requirement is a different task, not a universal spirit ineffectiveness rule.
Do not invent summon restraint, consent, cargo ownership or autonomous jobs from the calling category. Verify actual entity behavior and support first.
The contractor owns their gear, wounds, knowledge and reserves. Hiring them never acquires their technique or replicates their body.
Spirit acquisition, source development and calling execution are distinct. Summoning does not mint another permanent Ash or living companion.
Calling preview shows known context and supported costs. Invalid monument support rejects before spend; it need not erase lawful ownership.
Leaving the supported context affects execution under the exact qualified rule; no unsourced universal “instant disappearance at a boundary” is asserted.
Interruption, expiry and entity defeat require the selected call's termination contract. Neither calling nor reloading automatically repairs the bearer or urn.
One represented spirit type does not imply every world has a universal party slot. Living allies and summons need their own compatibility policy.
Observers see actual assistance and aftermath. They do not automatically learn hidden Ash provenance, the caller's private reserve or spirit cosmology.
Sufficient enemy power can defeat every currently available route. A truly overwhelming encounter need not promise escape or eventual victory.

## Evidence, authority and retained sources

An original copyist can preserve a witnessed manifest at paper/labor cost; its value is testimony, not magically acquiring a witness's identity.
Recovered paper condition and authenticity differ. Repairing the sheet can leave missing text, disputed custody and forged claims unresolved.
An authenticated record can outperform an uncertain memory for registry acceptance without outperforming a fighter at combat.
O learned Dry-join stays an ordinary process; observing a divine rune cannot promote it into Great Rune authority.
Frontier drive can supply an actually supported brace after local binding; it does not refill FP or qualify a catalyst/monument.
Outside qualified support, retain actual knowledge, source, item and prior receipts. Restore lawful support without re-awarding acquisition.
Claims/titles operate under real local issuer/jurisdiction. Neither title theft nor a spiritual-looking imported power universally rewrites testimony.
NPCs use their own actual means; private player spell use requires observation/report before it becomes an institutional response.

## Mastery and technique subprogression

O facets: target recognition, grip/casting control, action timing, actual output, efficiency and supported recovery; these are local tests, not new canon ranks.
A learner can know a spell but fail stable casting; a powerful arm can exceed a threshold while its handler still mistimes protection of an ally.
Paid facet trials can qualify one narrow execution, then a supported transition to guard, then a compatible bearer/caster combination.
A new function needs source-qualified support; ordinary mastery does not manufacture an Ash, catalyst, monument, grace or divine jurisdiction.
Variants can become simply stronger as body/source/control develop. No required weaker-method niche or globally fixed mastery cap is imposed.
Keep refit/reinforcement stages in the catalogue; these records describe what the learned method can actually execute and retain after interruption.

## Bounded implementation gate

Existing foundations: paid command/response, energy, conditions, custody, knowledge, claims and local power allowlists. ENGINE_PACKET owns exact engine gaps.
Missing: local FP/catalyst/seal/Ash/item publication, monument/entity support, native combat generalization and independent destination/absence state.
First brief should use fixed authored equipment/method variants with one named source-qualified effect, avoiding an invented dynamic Ash graph.
Preview must expose known requirements/uncertainty, with no RNG/spend, reward or free hidden-target disclosure.
Compare the same fixed threat and eligible wound with lower/developed/mature means, including straightforward superiority on a suitable task.
Reject absent support/body/fuel, ineligible arm/Ash, wrong target, stale holder state and occupied helper before costs.
Interrupt after legal paid use; retain wounds, spent FP, source and custody. Repeated call/return cannot duplicate Ashes, unique seal or shipment rewards.
Reload/import/failed-write cases must preserve those facts; unsupported content revisions must reject/quarantine rather than silently grant a new source.
An integrator rebuilds actual visible execution and save/reload later. Design review establishes no new combat, grace, travel or human acceptance.
