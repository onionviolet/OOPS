## Progression and assembly constraints

Martial path: genuine strength, reach, stamina and load can change field results. Canon stat thresholds and source-protagonist level-up services are not automatically assigned to an outsider.
Sorcery path: actual catalyst, learned method and fuel are separate. A new support mechanism can be an original paid experiment, but a generic staff does not prove compatibility with every sorcery.
Incantation path: exact sacred seal, faith/source restrictions and named effect need primary item evidence. Healing/buff presence in E4 proves categories, not resurrection or a universal cure.
Weapon/artifice path: E2d/E7 support eligible skill changes and source-qualified reinforcement. Exact item/affinity combination still needs its own inspection; armor must not inherit a weapon reinforcement ladder.
Craft/support path: ordinary carriers, wrapping and resin repairs are original. Source craft categories E5 do not verify a factory for Spirit Ashes, a divine rune or portable grace.
Institution path: learning/attestation/custody can support noncombat progression. The waystation's living witness is a declared local `O` rule and cannot override all supernatural testimony elsewhere.
Summoning path: ashes, bell, fuel and monument support remain distinct. A temporary summoned entity is not automatically an autonomous consenting companion with permanent possessions.
Transfer path: unsupported local execution can pause while retained owned method/source persists. FP is not automatically refilled by a Frontier exercise, chakra or a Skyrim potion.
