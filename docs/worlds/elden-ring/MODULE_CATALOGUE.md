# Elden Ring module catalogue: arms, preparation and witnessed craft

Status: Develop/Research proposal r1, 2026-10-03. Sole local module owner: progression/granularity GM. Authoring records only; no runnable equipment registry or save extension.
Read [campaign](CAMPAIGN.md), [entry brief](WORLD_BRIEF.md), [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md) and [shared assembly contract](../PROGRESSION_AND_ASSEMBLY.md). Campaign owns routes/people; ledger owns source rulings; this file owns families, operating stages and builds.
Boundary: 2022 base-game setting, divergent visitor campaign. No Shadow of the Erdtree, Nightreign, Tarnished Edition equipment or automatic protagonist grace/immortality.
Evidence E2a-E2d: [official starter guide](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/elden-ring/news/elden-ring-starter-guide-tips-know-playing-the-game) establishes distinct HP/FP/stamina, catalysts and restricted spirit-calling contexts. Its prelaunch balance is not an exact current patch claim.
Evidence E7: [official combat guide](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/es_mx/news/elden-ring-introduction-part-4-combat-guide) supports weapon requirements, weapon-type restrictions on Ashes of War, unique-skill exceptions, Whetstone Knife use, affinities/whetblades and distinct ordinary/Somber upgrade supplies.
Evidence E4: [official stats guide](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/es_mx/news/elden-ring-introduction-part-1-stats) separates FP, stamina/equipment load and spell-related requirements. Exact weapon, spell, cookbook and consumable prerequisites need ledger evidence.
P means original work application, stage or budget. Sura's craft may improve packing, service or witness preservation; it does not forge a universal divine suit or grant Great Rune authority.

Evidence marks inherit the ledger: C primary fact, A official adaptation corroboration, I inference, O original extension, U unresolved. P in this catalogue is explicitly an O proposal, never an official adaptation mark.
[Ability variants](../ABILITY_AUTHORING.md) permits stage/context differences and genuinely superior methods. The lower-cost roles illustrated here are concrete options, not a universal rule that every weak variant must survive or every mature method must pay a compensating penalty.

## Independent dimensions

- Proposed family IDs use `er.module.<family>` revision 1. Specimen identity is distinct from weapon name, Ash identity and owned technique.
- Structural stage identifies actual function: a plain held arm, a compatible skill-bearing arm, or a specialized researched loadout.
- Source weapon reinforcement numbers remain source reinforcement, not this catalogue's new-function ladder. A stronger + weapon alone may change output without opening a new action.
- Grade/rarity records source-certified class or availability; it supplies no universal comparison to Skyrim/Fallout stage names.
- Quality records ordinary fitting, sharpening or batch handling where the campaign proposes it. Canon special arms are not assigned an invented crafting-quality multiplier.
- Condition records wet wrapping, damaged ordinary fitting and supply state. Canon weapon durability/degradation is not assumed; any proposed wear applies to the authored work gear explicitly.
- Mastery records handling, spacing, spell execution and situational judgment. Acquiring a weapon, catalyst or Ash gives no field training for free.
- Source/body records actual strength/dexterity/spell prerequisites, usable body, learned spell and available FP. Ownership and execution are separate checks.
- Institution records actual smith access, teaching and witness legitimacy; a recognized carrier is not automatically a sorcerer or Lord.
- Research records inspected recipe, eligible arm/affinity combinations and tested target response. Knowing an item exists is not obtaining it.
- Owner/holder records physical cargo and lawful custody. An Ash installation cannot leave an unreserved duplicate loose item for another arm.
- World authority, spirit calling, grace and body renewal remain their actual source requirements; a workshop cannot manufacture them from generic metal.

### Central-stage operating records

- Arm / plain: an actual spear, grip and practiced spacing support reach against a fixed ordinary foe; simple service remains useful when specialist supplies are absent.
- Arm / handling: body-qualified two-hand/one-hand choices change reach, shield use and recovery opportunities. Exact source requirements cannot be waived by calling it mastery.
- Arm / specialized: a distinct source-qualified arm opens its supported skill envelope. A unique arm keeps its own restrictions rather than inheriting every interchangeable Ash.
- Armor / light: clothing frees equipment-load margin for supplies and a catalyst; it provides only its actual supported protection.
- Armor / mixed: select sourced pieces for the route and check complete burden. A heavy chest piece cannot be treated as weightless because boots are cheap.
- Armor / specialist: heavy defense or light mobility can truly surpass earlier preparation on its suitable task. Neither universally dominates the other.
- Ash / native: practice the arm's existing skill before budgeting field use. A cheap native skill remains usable without a large research collection.
- Ash / refit: source-qualified installation changes skill access on the eligible arm. The removed Ash and arm preserve actual identity under the future implementation.
- Ash / alternatives: maintain several actual acquired options and choose before a trip. Switching during a commitment requires an evidenced valid site/action.
- Affinity / Standard: preserves a known baseline and is valuable against uncertain targets or limited body support.
- Affinity / selected: a verified change shifts supported damage/scaling interactions. It can worsen the same arm's performance for a mismatched body.
- Affinity / researched: compare target evidence and choose among valid options. An invalid Ash/affinity pair rejects; collecting a whetblade is not universal compatibility.
- Sorcery / first spell: staff, spell, body and FP jointly enable casting. A staff remains carried property when the caster lacks usable FP.
- Sorcery / ranges: learned alternatives trade timing, reach and reserve. A shorter cheaper spell can remain optimal for routine scouting.
- Sorcery / school specialist: source-qualified catalyst/spells permit stronger fixed-threat engagements. Losing teaching access does not erase learned methods.
- Incantation / narrow: one verified healing or buff action can support a carrier without matching a demigod's output.
- Incantation / alternatives: support and offense compete for the same actor's actual FP and casting opportunity.
- Incantation / mature: compatible practiced methods coordinate with a real partner or prepared site. Canon authority is not invented by the ritual label.
- Craft / single batch: known material and recipe produce a useful preparation; cheap wrapping and ordinary supplies keep field work possible.
- Craft / differentiated: separate item batches answer different threats. A generic ingredient pouch cannot stand in for every recipe.
- Craft / workshop: preserved stock and trained staff support repeatable kits. Facility throughput and rare ingredients, not a universal rarity cap, constrain output.
- Records / dry manifest: linen, binding and an actual witness preserve evidence; this works before exceptional magical power is acquired.
- Records / archive: measured copying and protected storage allow a second claim route if the original report is lost. A copy is evidence with provenance, not a second unique entitlement.
- Records / waystation: trained packer, smith liaison and live witnesses operate separate jobs. More combat power cannot replace a missing truthful witness.

## Failure, counter and service

| Family | Test/discovery and failure | Repair/refit/salvage / permanent constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Arm | Observe reach/recovery in a practice trial; missing body support denies intended handling | Service ordinary grip; source reinforcement uses proper supplies; smithing does not invent a new unique arm |
| Armor | Check actual load and supported protection; an unsuitable burden changes mobility envelope | Repair authored wrapping/fittings; canonical armor wear is not assumed without source rule |
| Ash | Review eligible arm and actual skill; unsupported pair rejects | Refit at valid source-supported site, preserving acquisition; no generic Ash cloning or instant battle swap |
| Affinity | Test same target profile with declared baseline; resistant target may favor Standard | Refit only legal combination; previous knowledge retained and actual support still required |
| Sorcery | Wrong catalyst/body, exhausted FP or obstructed aim prevents action | Restore FP under selected entry rule; no staff repair can create learned spell knowledge |
| Incantation | Unsupported seal/spell, interruption or incompatible source blocks effect | Retain method, seek valid support; workshop cannot repair missing divine/ritual authority |
| Craft | Bad ingredient identity or wet batch fails the recipe test | Replace consumed stock, preserve failure record; do not refund test materials |
| Supply | Route blocked or outsider lacks source recovery access | Pay ordinary support or alternate route; a lost flask is not restored by a paper inventory entry |
| Spirit | Invalid calling context or absent bell/Ash blocks the attempt | Seek a valid source context; summoned spirit is not salvageable into ordinary component materials |
| Records | Missing live witness or ruined original weakens custody claim | Copy with actual provenance where possible; dead witness/expired opportunity need not have a substitute |

- Reinforcement, affinity choice, skill installation and training are different operations. Larger reinforcement alone is not presented as a new-function tier.
- Consumables remain finite; a mature loadout can defeat smaller threats outright without acquiring unlimited field recovery.
- Record restoration never recreates an unwitnessed fact. A stronger arm cannot force the registry to know unseen cargo history.
- Cancel untouched preparations differently from consumed batches; source-specific unique items need exact salvage rules before any destructive action.
- Known packing batches may idle with stock/bench capacity; first ritual tests, spirit commitments and unknown routes stay active decisions.

### Family salvage and irreversible constraints
- Arms: inspect surviving P grips/wrapping; canonical arm destruction and material return require exact source rules, never an assumed full stone refund.
- Armor/load: removed actual pieces remain owned/carryable; authored ruined straps yield only inspected material, with no automatic source armor degradation.
- Ash/skill: source-permitted refit retains actual acquisition; duplication or loss is governed by that specific source, not template cloning.
- Affinity: keep research and valid support after reverting configuration; no generic elemental battery is extracted from an arm.
- Sorcery: catalyst remains actual property if intact; learning survives, while fuel or body loss needs genuine recovery, not scrap conversion.
- Incantation: retained spell evidence is knowledge; lost source/authority is not recovered from a seal's ordinary housing.
- Craft: unused materials remain owned; consumed or failed batches yield only declared residue and evidence, not fresh recipe inputs.
- Supply: recover unused actual preparations and compatible containers; consumed supplies and unsupported outsider recovery cannot be refunded by station repair.
- Spirit: Ash/source and called entity follow verified rules; no ordinary salvage of a spirit into material, companion or reusable workshop fuel.
- Ritual records: preserve legible fragments and actual witness provenance; destroyed original, dead witness or expired exclusive claim may remain unrecoverable.

## Multi-component assembly: ferry preparation workshop

P assembly `er.assembly.ferry_workshop`, revision 1. Source-compatible smith/craft support plus original ordinary packing, not a magical vehicle or divine suit.
| Component/support | Actual input/output | Conflict or replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Rented measured bench and hand tools | Ordinary fitting, wrapping and cargo repair | One occupied bench cannot serve another job; rent another or defer |
| Owned plain arm, eligible Ash, actual Whetstone Knife | Source-qualified skill refit | Unique-skill arm can reject the operation; retain it unchanged or use eligible plain arm |
| Source-qualified whetblade support | Valid affinity configuration on that eligible arm | Invalid combination fails review; Standard remains a useful fallback |
| Smith and appropriate reinforcement stock | E7 supported reinforcement of actual weapon | Ordinary/Somber supplies stay distinct; no fabricated exchange rate |
| Crafting support and verified recipe record | Real ingredients become the selected preparation | Scarce ingredient reserved for another batch is unavailable |
| Linen, binding, dry box and retained control sample | Weatherproof manifest/cargo and batch evidence | Packing uses load/space; lighter wrap gives less tested protection |
| Catalyst/seal, learned method and qualified caster | Actual body/FP/spell supplies the optional action | Researching apparatus supplies no spirit, grace or spell |
| Witness and paid carrier with separate tasks | Moves cargo and verifies what was actually observed | Witness can leave or die under future selected policy; carrying a signature is not their continuing presence |

Proposed packing throughput: one kit per bench interval; smith and witness appointments are separate reservations. No independent construction clock is added.
Proposed field-load budget: ten units, arm/shield four, light armor two, supplies two, evidence box two. Adding a staff requires replacing cargo or using a carrier.
Proposed FP fixture: eighteen reserve, narrow spell three, prepared field method six; two field uses plus one narrow spell leave three contingency.
These are authored comparison units, not canonical item values. Spirit use has its own verified cost and location gate, not the spare three-unit remainder by assumption.
Flask/rest replenishment requires selected outsider eligibility; ordinary food and a workshop do not create grace.

## Three worked loadouts

### Poor specialist: witnessed ferry packer
- Owns plain sourced spear, light clothing, knife, linen, binding cord, dry box and one known ordinary packing method; no spell or spirit source assumed.
- Inputs: gathered/purchased cloth, paid carrier space, one rented bench and a willing live witness. Gear handling requires prior practice.
- Budget P: ten load units, one packaging batch and one carrier window; food/rest bought from actual local stock.
- Action unlocked: preserve the urn and its evidence through a practical transfer route without defeating the ferry's strongest threat.
- Decision: carry a better shield or retain dry evidence capacity; the latter can win recognition despite lower combat output.
- Conflict: taking an extra weapon displaces the control sample; choosing transport solves volume at monetary/relationship cost.
- Counterplay: route closure, soaked record or hostile witness invalidates the plan; ordinary packing cannot summon another witness.
- Replacement: redo wrapping with retained box, hire another lawful carrier if available, or keep cheap spear for known manageable reach threats.
- Lasting result: proven Dry-join handling and testimony route; consumed cloth and missed carrier window remain spent/lost.

### Developed field build: adaptable arm and caster
- Owns eligible arm, shield, actual Ash/Whetstone Knife support, verified affinity access, light mixed armor, catalyst and separately learned spell.
- Acquisition chain: source gear/records, body qualification, paid refit, reinforcement with proper stones and field practice; none is bundled into item purchase.
- Budget P: ten personal load units, eighteen FP, one compatible arm configuration, finite known preparations and a paid external cargo carrier.
- Action unlocked: change damage/skill behavior for observed threats, defend or cast with actual hand/load conflicts, then preserve the cargo independently.
- Decision: Standard/ordinary handling for uncertainty or a verified affinity for observed resistance, accepting its different body requirements.
- Conflict: shield skill can change which arm skill is available under E7; choose supported No Skill/two-handed handling only after source validation.
- Counterplay: interrupted casting, depleted FP or a fixed superior threat still defeats a careless plan; equipment quality cannot substitute for timing.
- Replacement: retain plain arm or cheap spell for routine work; safe refit changes the real configuration without resetting training or duplicating Ash.
- Lasting result: qualified loadout and research evidence; actual stock, injuries/claims under selected policy and failed delivery consequences persist.

### Mature project: Liurnia waystation and specialist expedition
- Owns/leases dry archive, staffed packing bench, smith arrangements, sourced specialist arms and separate catalyst/seal stock for qualified practitioners.
- A spirit route is optional only with actual bell/Ash, supported body/cost and valid calling context; expedition workers are ordinary people with their own reservations.
- Budget P: two packing benches, one booked smith, three finite mission kits and one emergency retained kit; actual FP and load remain per actor.
- Action unlocked: a powerful sourced combat build defeats lesser fixed threats while the staffed waystation supports simultaneous records/cargo recovery.
- Decision: spend rare reinforcement stock on personal arm, a partner's actual arm or another protected archival route.
- Conflict: a specialized unique arm may outperform ordinary gear yet reject the desired Ash; it is a distinct branch, not a universal upgrade.
- Counterplay: greater supernatural threat, missing witness, inaccessible calling context or destroyed archive can block different parts of the project.
- Replacement: maintain cheap Standard arm for unfamiliar targets; buy transport or research another supported spell instead of increasing every weapon number.
- Lasting result: real specialist gear, taught methods, source-qualified powers and waystation relationships; recognition remains the actual registry's local claim.

## Worked production chain and meaningful failed hybrid

1. Gather source-qualified ingredient stock and buy wrapping, acquire actual crafting support/recipe, then reserve Sura's bench and the intended cargo.
2. Research source arm/skill compatibility and cargo dampness with a retained sample. Decide which scarce supply goes to combat preparation versus evidence preservation.
3. Produce one known consumable batch and ordinary dry wrapping. Wrong ingredient trial consumes material and records failure without inventing a cookbook unlock.
4. Refit eligible Ash/affinity at the valid site, then reinforce with appropriate supplies. Do not convert reinforcement into free handling mastery.
5. Test ordinary wrapping with a wet control and grip/skill with safe practice. P failed wrapping test: resin stiffens the fold and breaks under load despite looking sealed.
6. Replace only binding/fold, retain box/arm identities, and retest. Successful field deployment spends actual preparations/FP and respects witness availability.
7. Use the completed kit for ferry recovery. A lost living witness can leave the cargo physically saved yet the original legitimacy objective failed.
8. Lasting consequence: tested method, configured arm and preserved cargo history; consumed resin, lost testimony and rare reinforcement input do not regenerate.
Hybrid P: import a Fallout-made dry container; pay one comparison sample, one fitting interval and a carried-load test. It can preserve ordinary cargo if material behavior works.
Failed test P: install a Skyrim inscription onto a staff to imitate a unique Elden Ring skill. A physical mark supplies no compatible Ash, FP method or authority; material/time spent, staff retained as actually altered.
Second hybrid P: use an existing imported strength method on the urn. Test body/load and footing; actual lifting can succeed while lacking the live witness still blocks recognition.
Compatible mundane craft can be useful without converting another world's fuel to FP or copying a source-bound spirit.
