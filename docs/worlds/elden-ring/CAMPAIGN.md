## Bounded source and original continuity

Selected baseline: the 2022 *Elden Ring* base-game premise before a chosen ending. Exclude Shadow of the Erdtree, Nightreign, Tarnished Edition additions and copied assets/dialogue.
E1: Marika's realm, shattered Elden Ring, demigod Great Runes, the Shattering and returning Tarnished are official background. [Publisher premise](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/games/elden-ring).
E2: HP, FP and stamina differ; sorcery requires an equipped catalyst; spirit ashes need nearby monuments; the source player has grace/Stake recovery with rune loss. [Official starter guide](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/elden-ring/news/elden-ring-starter-guide-tips-know-playing-the-game).
E4: Limgrave/Liurnia and unequal regional/night danger are official contexts. [Official gameplay overview](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/news/elden-ring-introduction-part-3-gameplay-overview).
E3: FP supports spells, skills and spirit calling; endurance affects stamina/load; incantations include healing/buffs. [Official stats guide](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/es_mx/news/elden-ring-introduction-part-1-stats).
E5: Stormveil is a Limgrave landmark; Renna and the Twin Maiden Husks are source bell-acquisition anchors. [Official setting guide](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/elden-ring/news/what-elden-ring-about).
These are reference facts, not current balance/patch claims. Exact catalysts, spell recipes, affiliations and character biographies need the source ledger.
The player begins as an outsider of unknown grace eligibility, not automatically a Tarnished, lord or endlessly reviving protagonist.
Every funeral law, workshop, original person, commodity, regional annex and local historical event below is original fan continuity, O.
Body/catalyst/spirit-support careers are O adaptations of bounded facts. Their specific training and logistics are proposed gameplay.
Living-witness custody is an O local institution. It is not a universal law of souls, grace or death in canon.

## Promise and campaign horizons

Survive a hostile ruined landscape by becoming useful, then decide which records, people and institutions deserve your protection.
Early play preserves an urn, learns custody distinctions and obtains a paid repair contact.
Middle play acquires an actually supported martial/magical specialty and restores a route to the register-house.
Mature play can confront genuinely lower threats, maintain several witness/repair sites and enter a regional authority dispute.
A clerk can acquire consequential access while remaining physically vulnerable; a great fighter can carry cargo while lacking its lawful recognition.
Every larger arc returns to tangible objects, memories and surviving witnesses rather than an abstract universal reputation score.
The character can leave a project, take another career or decline a demigod-scale intervention; not every opportunity stays available.
Early cloth/resin repair stays useful for expeditions and paid stock even after exceptional combat advancement.

## Assemblies without a forced magic suit

Component/technique families: fitted armament, movement training, FP reserve, sorcery method, catalyst, incantation support, bell, ashes, local monument context, dry wrapping, custody seal and authenticated record.
Their exact stage/compatibility rules are in [the module owner](MODULE_CATALOGUE.md). No global tier number equates these functions.
O courier ensemble combines dry linen, carrier, armament, optional supported catalyst and finite preparations.
Its garment is ordinary protective cargo equipment. It cannot grant a spell or copy a demigod's authority.
O witness workshop combines sheltered bench, genuine stamp, dry originals and willing registry participants.
O spirit-supported field build combines legally acquired source, the actual summoning support and trained handling of the encounter.
Removing catalyst support can disable its spell while preserving martial training and actual unused sources.
Leaving monument range can block the supported summoning function while retaining ownership and knowledge.
Damaged record authenticity and repair condition differ. Repaired paper can remain incomplete or legally contested.
An owned seal has one physical identity and local custody history; its image in an inventory does not create another seal.
Condition restoration costs real materials. A consumed/destroyed original may leave testimony without regaining the original object.

## Three worked careers

### Poor record porter

1. Meet Sura and exchange gathered intact resin for paid linen service rather than buying a magical profession.
2. Reserve the dry bench and repair the housing. Inspection grants only supported evidence, not an accepted delivery.
3. Fund Kest's actual cart/stock and choose public transfer with displayed custody/loan terms.
4. Return Ilen's actual seal and learn Dry-join wrapping once after legitimate witnessed delivery.
5. Become a paid copyist/courier and support a register-house retrieval with cloth, testimony and route work.

Means: linen/resin, a local rune purse, real record, available living participants and lawful cargo custody.
New functions: preserve a carrier, settle a witnessed delivery, weatherproof useful stock and compare entries.
Weakness: cannot outfight the Warden by literacy or assume an outsider's death will return them to grace.
Tradeoff: service/evidence career avoids expensive power sources but depends on consent and supported access.

### Developed catalyst courier

1. Acquire a sourced casting method, usable FP policy and legitimate compatible catalyst; observation alone grants none.
2. Practice one chosen specialty actively, then learn paid reserve recovery under its actual policy.
3. Add fitted armament and dry cargo harness; test the combination instead of assuming simultaneous casting/hauling support.
4. Retrieve flooded records with actual protective/physical means or buy a bearer service where spell function does not apply.
5. Acquire spirit assistance only through a separately verified scene and use it in a genuinely supported context.

Means: actual catalyst/source, local fuel, trained control, repaired clothing/weapon and route/monument evidence.
New functions: studied field execution, useful expedition combinations and greater real power against suitable threats.
Weakness: fuel and support limits; a spirit is not Ilen's living consent and magic does not authenticate a forged record.
Tradeoff: substantial independent capability versus equipment/fuel investment and less cargo capacity.

### Mature witness-circle protector

1. Recover counterpart testimony from actual survivors and choose which originals to publish or protect.
2. Fund separate artisan, porter and registry contributions at their own facilities.
3. Restore the archive, shelter and route records through paid dependent phases.
4. Train or hire genuinely capable protection; choose central public recognition or a distributed circle.
5. Face a regional confiscation crisis through sufficient power, decoy sacrifice, evacuation or negotiated relinquishment.

Means: real records/participants, several sites, transport, fuel, paid collaborators and scoped recognition.
New functions: broader testimony access, supported regional retrieval and durable independent services.
Weakness: simultaneous attacks and permanent loss of witnesses/originals; local recognition cannot command demigods.
Tradeoff: public legitimacy exposes routes; privacy preserves selected people but can make future claims harder.

## Five escalating arcs

### 1. The Unclaimed Ferry

Occurrence: a cart beam fails and opens a played-time departure offer. Observation reveals existing remaining time; it does not restart it.
Choices: actual physical support spends rope/resin and compatible drive; paid public transfer spends local runes and Kest's actual stock.
Shared rite: recovered urn, matching legitimate entry/custody, loaned seal and Ilen's available living consent settle one claim.
Aftermath: intact private versus public record, different costs and memories; Dry-join study unlocks once.
Abstention/expiry: Kest follows his emergency policy and can lose cargo; later testimony recovery preserves loss and costs different supplies.
Accepting creates separate frozen delivery terms. Declining creates no loan, cosmic debt or reward.

### 2. The Empty Toll

Trigger: witnessed collection or a supported report establishes the Warden's seizure operation.
Choices: spend cart/linen on a decoy, surrender indexed cargo, seek paid alternative testimony, or use actual sufficient force.
Aftermath: decoy consumes production stock; surrender loses private control; force can open a road but leaves witnessed conflict.
Abstention: patrol seals the crossing using real means. The resulting cargo loss is discovered later through testimony.
Later consequence: intact originals, willing witnesses and surviving transport determine register-house access.

### 3. The flooded counterpart

Trigger: a retained stamp/record reveals a supported lead to Liurnia's O register-house.
Choices: paid dry-room restoration, risky direct retrieval with sufficient means, or preserve surviving testimony without an original.
Aftermath: recovered original grants different recognition from a copy; damaged evidence can remain disputed despite repairs.
Abstention: Nessa's funded expedition may recover a sample first or lose it, independently of player timers.
Later consequence: archive custody sets public-service versus private-circle opportunities.

### 4. Whose name survives?

Trigger: actual counterpart records contradict a local monopoly on testimony.
Choices: publish the original to a public registry, entrust a private witness circle, or retain it without institutional privileges.
Aftermath: public recognition exposes some routes; private recognition protects selected people with narrower access; holding the record keeps control and delays services.
Abstention: officials act on the evidence they actually possess, not on hidden player discoveries.
Later consequence: a lost or saved witness materially changes eligible claims and the circle's future methods.

### 5. The road beneath greater powers

Trigger: separately sourced regional conflict reaches the campaign's actual route and funded institutions.
Choices: enter with genuinely sufficient power, rescue selected people, relinquish a threatened site, or honor a narrow retrieval contract and leave.
Aftermath: powerful builds overcome lower threats; the larger political conflict need not end because one contract succeeds.
Abstention: each original uses their owned means; an exposed site or person may be permanently lost without a repair route.
Later consequence: surviving records and people select the mature institution, while canonical ending decisions remain separately unselected.
Lethal resolution, grace eligibility and rune-loss quantities require an explicit failure profile. No outsider immortality is inferred from E2.

## Visits, harshness and acceptance

Physical entry retains living body, wounds, methods, actual sources/reserves and named cargo, without a fresh chosen-hero avatar.
Frontier `field.load_step` mastery two with real `field.drive` can brace the cart at output three for three reserve units and strain, once local binding exists.
A stronger supported import can carry or defeat suitable threats. It does not refill FP, forge consent or create an unearned local source.
Monument-dependent execution can stop outside supported context while ownership stays; compatible support restores it without relearning.
Dry-join knowledge can export to a paid Frontier linen/resin recipe after an explicit item/workshop adapter; registry authority remains local.
Return needs actual route, safe boundary and resolution/cancellation of work and loaned seals. Emergency return/capture losses remain separate policies.
Severe unfairness permits unwinnable threats and permanent local losses; exact lethal, revival, run-retention, aging and destructive offline rules remain unselected.
Inspected paid jobs, custody, sites, knowledge, energy and power checks supply groundwork, not destination isolation, FP/catalyst/monument combat or a resurrection system.
The [staged packet](IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md) owns source rebuild, two-route enter/return/reload proof and negative/save cases.
Document checks establish design coverage only. Human fiction, danger and balance acceptance remains pending; the first code milestone stays one realm/one prestige.

## Bounded expedition composition

A Paradise-style assignment can select one existing arc with declared objective, local entry means, paid costs and settlement/extraction terms, while the broader campaign supplies coherent people and aftermath. This is an O structural proposal, not verified novel mechanics.
A longer residence uses the same local history for work, training, relationships and projects; later visits do not reset its people or unique claims. A new era or parallel history requires a genuinely separate authored instance.
The shared issuer/hub owns mission admission and settlement, while this campaign owns local causes, source rules and lasting outcomes. Completing an extraction can leave the local conflict unresolved or lost.
