## 1. er.interaction.wrap: the fold that matters

Participants/place/time: player, Sura and available Kest at a sheltered packing bench before an actual cargo departure; bench/stock/custody required.
Opening, Sura: “That fold sheds rain. This one invites it to stay.”
Known evidence: handled wrap and actual cargo dampness; Sura is an artisan, not an automatic reader of hidden urn contents.
| Choice | Actual cost/condition | Distinct result and owner |
|---|---|---|
| Pay for carrier repair | Actual pall resin, linen and reserved sheltered bench under quote | Same carrier's repaired wrapping/condition and work receipt |
| Copy a known packing method | Permission, observed completed procedure and actual study/practice supplies | Learned work evidence only when paid study completes; no free item |
| Ask about the bad fold | Visible wrap and available ordinary exchange | “The rain has excellent manners. It uses the opening you provide.” Read-only response |

Refusal/exit: keep the existing wet wrap; no new debt or guaranteed weather loss. Accepted repair is cancelled only through its frozen reservation policy.
Interruption: another real booking closes unaccepted work; consumed failed samples remain spent while untouched reservations follow the job rule.
Afterwards/return: Sura comments on an actually inspected fold, not unseen research; familiar greeting can be “Still turning the seam outward?”
Nonfarming/reload: re-reading instructions neither repairs nor awards study; new repair consumes new labor/stock, old carrier identity remains.

## 2. er.interaction.entry: a seal is a loan, not permission

Participants/place/time: player, Ilen, available Kest and actual willing witness at the ferry during the occurrence's remaining departure window.
Opening, Ilen: “I can lend the seal. I cannot lend you another person's yes.”
Known state: actual urn/entry match, cargo holder, posted transfer terms and directly expressed consent. The living-witness rule is E6 `O`, not a universal ban on spirits.
| Choice | Actual cost/condition | Distinct result and owner |
|---|---|---|
| Support the cart physically | Actual rope/resin, compatible supported drive three and available participants | Paid job/energy and intact private cargo path; no free entry yet |
| Accept paid public transfer | Declared local rune fee, Kest's actual cart/linen/time and loan terms | Public custody path with different spent stock and directed service memory |
| Complete the shared rite | Recovered urn, matching lawful entry/custody, actual loaned seal and Ilen's living willing consent | One settled registry claim/Dry-join access; matched loan return separately verified |

Refusal: “Then no entry today. Your property remains yours where you actually hold it.” Declining creates no loan, cosmic debt or reward.
Exit/interruption: closing talk does not return a seal; consent loss/participant departure blocks unsatisfied rite, paid work/loan remain according to their terms.
Afterwards: “The entry is accepted. The seal is still on your side of the table” only if that is current custody; permission is not inferred from affection.
Nonfarming/reload: one rite across both paths, one actual loan; expired offer stays expired and a forged/unmatched seal rejects without spend.

## 4. er.interaction.toll: the Warden's actual reach

Opening, Warden: “Indexed cargo stays. You may argue from this side of the barrier.”
Known state: displayed local writ and seen patrol/reach; hidden motives, distant patrols and player's concealed power stay unknown.
| Choice | Actual cost/condition | Distinct result and owner |

Nonfarming/reload: surrendered cargo stays gone, damaged barrier stays changed, original patrol means are not rescaled or respawned for another reward.
