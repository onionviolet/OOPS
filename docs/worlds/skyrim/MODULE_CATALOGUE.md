# Skyrim module catalogue: work, spell and enchanted equipment

Status: Develop/Research proposal r1, 2026-10-03. Sole local component owner: progression/granularity GM. Authoring data only; no assembly API, saved fields or playable Skyrim system is introduced.
Read [campaign](CAMPAIGN.md), [entry brief](WORLD_BRIEF.md), [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md) and [shared assembly contract](../PROGRESSION_AND_ASSEMBLY.md). Campaign owns people/plots; ledger owns canon adjudication; this file owns local families, specimen rules and worked builds.
Boundary: Skyrim base game, ordinary visitor, divergent local campaign. DLC, Creation Club, Dragonborn status, Shouts and inherited guild authority are not entry grants.
Evidence S1: the [official PC manual](https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/72850/manuals/skyrim_gfw_manual-07.pdf?t=1466708637), printed pp. 3, 8, 16-19, verifies Magicka casting, destructive enchantment study, filled gems, weapon recharge, persistent worn enchantments, ingredient-effect mixtures and ordinary production stations.
Only that bounded source summary is asserted as verified here. Exact enchantment compatibility, soul sizes, ward behavior and material/perk recipes require the ledger's item-specific evidence before implementation.
P means original proposed work application, stage or measurement. A proposed regulator is a caster's practiced restraint or ordinary mechanical limiter, never a claim that canon has interchangeable magical circuit boards.

Evidence marks inherit the ledger: C primary fact, A official adaptation corroboration, I inference, O original extension, U unresolved. P in this catalogue is explicitly an O proposal, never an official adaptation mark.
[Ability variants](../ABILITY_AUTHORING.md) permits stage/context differences and genuinely superior methods. The lower-cost roles illustrated here are concrete options, not a universal rule that every weak variant must survive or every mature method must pay a compensating penalty.

## Local identity and independent dimensions

- Definition IDs use `skyrim.module.<family>` at revision 1; stage suffixes identify authored functions, not canonical skill ranks. Specimen IDs are separate proposed identities.
- Structural tier records the supported task: a boot that fits a worker, a weatherproof boot, or a reinforced expedition boot. It does not decide enchantment grade.
- Grade records source-certified effect/material category where evidence supports one; rare availability does not establish higher strength or capacity.
- Quality records fit, seam consistency, edge alignment or verified enchanting execution for this specimen. No automatic quality-to-rarity conversion is selected.
- Condition records wet lining, cracked sole, drained weapon charge and damaged fasteners independently. A drained blade remains a blade.
- Mastery records the person's known effects, casting control, alchemy evidence and smithing practice. Buying boots supplies none of these.
- Source/body records actual Magicka support, physical anatomy and acquired filled gems. Knowing Soul Trap does not imply having captured a soul.
- Institution records bench tenancy, College access, teaching permissions and military obligations. A College contact is not a combat multiplier.
- Research records inspected recipes and test results, including failed samples. A copied label without ingredient evidence is an untested recipe.
- Owner and holder differ for borrowed tools. Installed parts remain in the same owner's outfit; a hired caster owns their own method and reserve.
- Portability records actual clothing/tools, retained knowledge and local facility leases separately. The enchanter station does not fit in a travel pack.
- An item with an enchantment is not dismantled into a reusable independent effect card. Destructive study removes that item and leaves knowledge under S1.

### Central-stage operating records

These are local authoring constraints, not exported commands. Each line declares input, output, support and a reason an earlier stage remains useful.
- Substrate / fitted: leather and labor produce a wearable boot. Light weight serves quick couriers; a loose advanced shell can perform worse on that task.
- Substrate / reinforced: fittings and dry stock produce abrasion protection and stable tool grip. Added bulk can obstruct careful hand work.
- Substrate / expedition: custom measurements and specialist metalwork support heavier protection. Repair needs a smith, not only cloth patches.
- Effect / first pattern: destroy the lawful ring and record the learned compatible effect. The cheap single-effect item serves everyday labor without consuming escort stock.
- Effect / complementary: acquire a second independent effect and test the outfit's actual functions. More labels cannot bypass equipment restrictions.
- Effect / research: propose new work applications of eligible effects, then test those applications. Discovery can reject a hauling benefit even when combat use works.
- Soul / single purchase: inspect one source-qualified filled gem. This is inexpensive project access, not a permanent rechargeable reactor.
- Soul / separated stocks: tag creation and recharge lots. An emergency escort can deplete weapon stock while leaving teaching supplies intact.
- Soul / contracted supply: verify provenance and delivery rights. Mature capacity depends on actual arrivals; a closed supplier stops future batches.
- Regulation / measured: one spell and one stop condition reduce task ambiguity. A practiced ordinary light remains cheaper than continuous defensive casting.
- Regulation / alternating: learn which action releases a hand and which reserve remains. Low Magicka pauses casting rather than erasing known methods.
- Regulation / coordinated: fund another caster and agree handoff signals. Two actors supply two actual reserves; one person is not counted twice.
- Housing / cover: a removable leather cover permits dry storage. It contributes no magical resistance.
- Housing / liner: replace the wet liner without remaking the shell or effect. Refit changes comfort and weather behavior, not enchantment ownership.
- Housing / harness: secure separate tools and clothing pieces. A stuck buckle delays removal; a damaged belt does not drain a ring's effect.
- Interface / inspection: acquire and practice one verified spell. Seeing a crack does not teach structural diagnosis.
- Interface / switching: practice tool-to-spell transitions with unloaded gear. Incorrect hand use cancels the attempted sequence before the dangerous load.
- Interface / team: appoint a caster and load handler with distinct means. Losing the caster removes cover while the handler's craft skill remains.
- Weapon / ordinary: repair edge and hilt, train reach and guard. It still cuts when the charged alternative is unavailable.
- Weapon / charged: obtain an eligible enchantment and pay charge. Reserve planning chooses which enemy merits the scarce effect.
- Weapon / paired: carry a plain backup and charged specialist. This costs weight and readiness time; no automatic instant swap is assumed.
- Craft / brace: seasoned wood, iron and labor produce one tested brace. The simple design can be repaired away from an enchanter.
- Craft / jig: pay for dry storage and a measured jig. Repeated units fit the same cart but cannot automatically fit every cart.
- Craft / depot: train inspectors and maintain separate spare stock. A depot can outperform a lone powerful adventurer at simultaneous civilian repairs.

## Failure, test and service rules

| Family | Failure/counter and discovery | Repair, refit, salvage and irreversible edge |
|---|---|---|
| Substrate | Poor fit causes slipping; prove walking and loaded stance before enchanting | Repair ordinary seams; destroyed or sacrificed substrate cannot be returned as a fresh item |
| Effect | Ineligible item/effect pairing rejects at review; compare a plain control specimen | Lost study item stays lost; knowledge retained; removing an effect without destruction is not assumed |
| Soul | Empty or unsuitable gem fails the planned recipe; inspect source and fill state | Spent source stays spent; no routine soul recovery or new ethical/lawful capture permission |
| Regulation | Sustaining unfamiliar protection exhausts reserve; test duration with a spotter | Rest restores eligible reserve; failed practice consumes time/fuel and retains observed result |
| Housing | Water penetrates a seam; heat or abrasion damages lining | Replace liner at labor cost; salvage dry buckles if inspected, retaining their condition |
| Interface | Hands needed for tool cannot simultaneously perform incompatible casting | Retrain sequence; repair glove fit; observation grants no new spell by itself |
| Weapon | Charge empties or adversary resists the verified effect | Recharge under S1, sharpen plain blade; no free mastery or fresh condition from switching |
| Alchemy | Unknown pairing yields no useful preparation; batch contamination invalidates sale | Discard consumed trial material, clean vessel; preserve evidence, never refund tested ingredients |
| Craft | Wet brace warps under load; raw strength cannot straighten the whole cart reliably | Dry/remake member, retain reusable fittings; a failed delivery leaves its local consequence |
| Workshop | Queue, missing lease or absent specialist prevents the next job | Reschedule or rent elsewhere; facility expansion costs stock and labor, not faction rank |

- A repair restores a retained function after its cause is fixed; it does not relearn an enchantment or raise social rank.
- A downgrade to a plain backup preserves its own identity, quality and condition. It does not overwrite the charged weapon.
- Enchantment creation and ordinary removable attachment fitting are separate commitments. Cancellation before consumption differs from stopping after destructive study.
- Unknown alchemical or magical tests remain active choices. Known safe brace batches may idle with finite inputs and capacity.
- No unattended gem capture, dangerous escort or irreversible study is enrolled by reading this document.

### Family salvage and irreversible constraints
- Substrate: condemned leather/metal yields only inspected ordinary scrap; a sacrificed enchanted specimen yields learned evidence, not its original gear back.
- Effect: retain understood method and failed application evidence; no removable inscription salvage or transfer to another blank is assumed.
- Soul support: unused owned filled inputs remain portable; consumed sources produce only source-verified residue, never an automatically refillable gem.
- Regulation: retain practiced control across gear refit; rest recovers supported reserve, while lost body/source execution needs its own restoration rule.
- Housing: recover intact buckles and dry lining only; cut or contaminated pieces retain that condition and require inspection before reuse.
- Interface: retain learned switching sequences; damaged glove can yield cloth/fasteners, never a new spell or caster.
- Weapon: surviving physical parts remain source-eligible salvage; emptied charge cannot be recovered as fresh fuel, and source-specific unique weapons need exact rules.
- Alchemy: failed/used mixture is not fresh ingredient stock; preserve tested effects as knowledge and discard unusable material under the selected batch rule.
- Ordinary craft: retrieve inspected iron fittings and sound timber from a dismantled brace; warped stock remains downgraded or unusable.
- Workshop: unused owned stock can leave the facility; consumed construction does not return full inputs, and lease/access remains with its actual holder.

## Multi-component assembly: Lower Road work outfit

P assembly `skyrim.assembly.lower_road_outfit`, revision 1, combines ordinary removable gear and one source-eligible worn enchantment. It is neither a new canonical artifact nor a powered suit.
| Part / support | Binding and contribution | Conflict or replacement choice |
|---|---|---|
| Fitted boots with a verified compatible work effect | Owned worn item; effect eligibility checked before creation | Preserve an existing useful ring, or destroy it for knowledge and pay for new boots |
| Reinforced gloves and plain forearm guard | Ordinary installed fit/lining; protects grip | Thick guard limits fine work; remove at a safe halt for precise assembly |
| Leather harness, brass/iron buckles, two tool loops | Actual installed physical mounts carry hammer and gauge | More cargo occupies space and weight; no universal magical slot limit |
| Dry liner and spare liner | One installed, one carried; condition histories separate | Wet replacement costs downtime; spare cannot also be sold during commitment |
| Plain weapon, separately charged escort weapon | Two real items with distinct readiness and charge | Carry both or leave one in named storage; depleted charge keeps ordinary physical use |
| Learned casting sequence and present caster | Body/source and training support cover an inspected task | Hired caster can depart; outfit remains useful for ordinary hauling |
| Runa's bench, Orven's enchanter, supplier appointment | Facility access with real leases and capacity | Departure takes gear, not rented machinery or another person's source |

Proposed task budget: six carried load units; outfit/tools use four, two spare units remain. Units are prototype accounting, not canonical kilograms.
One hauling sequence occupies hands for the load; casting requires a safe support/set-down or another actor. Neither action is free.
Proposed local Magicka reserve twelve units; inspection costs one, guarded crossing costs four per declared interval. Two crossings plus inspection leave three contingency units.
This is one tested spell-policy proposal, not a canon universal reserve. If the ledger rejects the chosen protective effect, the crossing uses ordinary support or a different validated method.
P spell-policy throughput is four output units: protection uses four and inspection one, so simultaneous solo use rejects while sequential use remains possible.
Worn enchantment has no invented per-minute soul drain under S1. Weapon charge, caster Magicka and outfit wear are different budgets.

## Three worked loadouts

### Poor specialist: dry-stock joiner
- Owns plain fitted boots, gloves, a repaired hammer, two wedges, gauge cord and one dry liner; knows brace fitting and inspection only.
- Sources timber from a paid/gathered lot, iron fittings from Runa and cooked food from a real supplier. Bench tenancy is one shift.
- Budget P: five load units, three labor actions before rest, one brace per booked shift. No gem, spell or charged weapon is assumed.
- Action unlocked: diagnose and shore a minor cart failure with measured geometry, rather than attempting a dangerous escort.
- Decision: use scarce dry timber for a precise brace or buy a shorter civilian service, retaining timber for later craft.
- Conflict: a heavy guard reduces precision; this specialist leaves it in named storage and retains dexterous cheap gloves.
- Counterplay: saturated stock, an obstructed route or an overwhelming raider can defeat the plan. Ordinary repair never implies combat parity.
- Replacement: patch the existing liner or purchase another; hammer repair preserves its identity and condition history.
- Lasting result: delivered tested braces earn a specific supplier relationship; a failed promised delivery retains its grievance.

### Developed field build: prepared work-caster
- Owns the assembled outfit, plain blade, one source-eligible charged blade and two separate recharge inputs. Knows compatible effect, inspection spell and practiced hand switching.
- Source chain: lawful enchanted specimen to destructive study, owned blank boots plus filled gem at an enchanter, then separate paid training.
- Budget P: six load units, twelve Magicka, two guarded intervals and one inspection; weapon charge is measured independently by the eventual validated effect.
- Action unlocked: inspect and support an exposed cart, then escort through an identified manageable threat with preserved plain backup.
- Decision: keep three contingency Magicka or spend another protected interval on a delayed civilian cart. The next risk changes visibly.
- Conflict: heavy tools and dual weapons leave no room for a full ingredient crate. Choose supplier delivery or a smaller personal stock.
- Counterplay: resistant target, broken buckle, bad stock or loss of safe footing can make the same preparation insufficient.
- Replacement: plain blade for charge conservation; liner refit for dampness; a paid second caster for duration, each with actual costs.
- Lasting result: method and completed gear persist; consumed gems, lost cargo and private memories retain their actual outcomes.

### Mature project: staffed road-and-research depot
- Owns or leases dry rack, forge access, alchemy table booking, two work outfits, separate charged escort stock and measured supply records.
- Staff provide distinct trained roles: joiner, ingredient tester and caster. Contracts buy their time, never permanent ownership of their abilities.
- Budget P: two concurrent benches plus one separately booked enchanter; three source lots, one held reserve lot, finite wages and food for the current shift.
- Action unlocked: repair two civilian loads while maintaining a prepared escort, including threats that truly overwhelm the poor specialist.
- Decision: teach a tested work application for shared road access, keep it private for profit, or accept a militia allocation obligation.
- Conflict: simultaneous customers compete for timber and appointments even when the mature caster can defeat a local bandit outright.
- Counterplay: supplier closure, a stronger dragon or loss of lawful depot tenure creates real limits; enemies are not rescaled to match.
- Replacement: hire a second joiner instead of enchanting another outfit; retain cheap braces for repairable routes and advanced stock for exposed ones.
- Lasting result: depot access, employed people and public/private test evidence persist in their instance; travel exports only declared owned cargo/methods.

## Worked upgrade chain and failed import

1. Gather timber and hides legally, buy iron and one filled gem, inspect moisture and custody. Ledger evidence must verify the intended boot effect before purchase commitment.
2. Research Runa's cart dimensions and Orven's effect teaching. Decide whether sacrificing the owned ring is worth losing its current benefit and resale option.
3. Produce dry braces and blank fitted boots at paid stations. Poorly fitted boots fail walking inspection before the scarce gem is consumed.
4. Begin destructive study, removing the actual ring once; retain learned effect and consumed-input receipt through cancellation/reload in the future implementation.
5. Enchant eligible boots, practice hauling with an ordinary control pair and record the actual work benefit. A failed work hypothesis does not undo successful enchantment.
6. Refit a wet liner and loose buckle, returning the same removed parts in current condition. Replace only what inspection condemns.
7. Use braces/outfit on a committed delivery. Allocate spare Magicka to inspection or protective service and retain the missed customer's actual aftermath.
8. Lasting consequence: repeatable measured work, owned worn item and local route access; sacrificed ring and consumed gem remain unavailable.
Hybrid P: import a Fallout-made metal buckle into the harness, using one sample, one fitting shift and local load tests. Imported fabrication can improve fit without altering magic.
Failed test P: a Fallout cell wired to a teaching station supplies electricity but no verified Magicka or soul. Consume test labor and retain the rejection; do not claim the gem was refilled.
Second experiment P: a trained imported strength method reduces supported lifting demand using its own fuel. Test footing and brace stress; excess force can crack timber instead of creating a better route.
Knowledge, parts and test evidence persist after failed compatibility; acquisition never supplies casting or smithing practice for free.
