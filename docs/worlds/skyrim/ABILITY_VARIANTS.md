`C` means the ledger's bounded source fact; `O` means our authored adaptation; `U` means an unresolved source question. All IDs and fixture values below are proposed.

## Comparison 1: keep the same loaded cart upright

Task: move Runa's repaired cart through one uneven road segment without spilling its cargo. Repair and handling are separate outputs.
Observed inputs: cart geometry, load, footing, harness condition and the handler's tested support. Unknown road damage stays unknown.
O methods: `skyrim.variant.load_set`, ordinary trained bracing; `skyrim.variant.workbound_wear`, an eligible worn enchantment application; `skyrim.variant.holdfast`, an original Magicka-fed support spell.
Workbound Wear is conditional on SU1's actual effect/item eligibility. Holdfast is explicitly invented, not a named Skyrim spell or a claimed source-game industrial system.

| Use | Load Set | Workbound Wear | Holdfast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | One rehearsed stance steadies one known load; real stamina and free grip | Acquired, fitted eligible boots supply their verified benefit; poor training still mishandles a cart | Qualified caster supplies one narrow contact brace while another person handles cargo |
| Developed | Correct known jolts and transfer grip without losing balance | Trained handler integrates worn benefit with harness and footing | Trained caster follows a measured changing load within paid output/control |
| Mature | Experienced handler can carry much larger actual loads within body capacity | Better eligible effect plus strong handler can simply exceed ordinary bracing | Sufficient supported output holds the cart with less bodily effort and greater force than both lower methods |

Acquisition: Load Set requires taught/tested practice; boots require actual item custody, fitting and lawful creation; Holdfast requires a learned O spell, compatible body and Magicka.
Execution never awards the acquired boots or spell anew. It moves this cargo and retains the method's actual expenditure.
O proof tuning: ordinary handling costs two stamina and three minutes; a selected boots fixture reduces exertion to one; Holdfast costs six local Magicka and one minute.
Those numbers are balancing proposals, not manual costs, cross-world conversions or currently available command values.
S1c supports no recharge for worn enchantments; it does not prove this boot benefit or immunity to garment damage. Creation still consumes the selected real gem.
Boots need no invented drain merely to make Load Set competitive. An excellent eligible specimen can be strictly better on this task.
Holdfast startup establishes contact with the cart; sustained use requires the same supported load, range, control and caster availability.
Counter/failure: broken cart structure, lost footing or interrupted casting changes support. An ordinary mud puddle is not automatic anti-magic.
Termination: reaching the road's end, deliberate release, reserve failure or lost contact stops the brace; spilled cargo remains spilled.
Retention: learned handling/spell remains after stopping; unused reserve and worn equipment retain current state; loss of the item removes only its contribution.
Ordinary craft can fix the cart; no handling method automatically manufactures a new axle or authenticates ownership.

## Comparison 2: acquire a repeatable effect or procedure

Task: determine whether a work-support benefit can be reproduced on a new eligible specimen. Looking at the old ring is insufficient evidence of its construction.
O ordinary observation `skyrim.variant.workyard_read` and O documented reconstruction `skyrim.variant.process_trial` compare with S1b destructive enchantment study.
S1d alchemical experimentation is a separate source-supported evidence route; it produces ingredient-effect knowledge, not enchanting knowledge by analogy.

| Use | Workyard Read | Process Trial | Destructive effect study |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | Watch visible handling and record a stance fragment | Inspect a partial workshop record and propose a test | Inspect ownership/equipment state and disclose sacrifice; no effect acquired yet |
| Developed | Interpret repeated demonstrations and practice their bodily sequence | Pay for materials/time; a successful jig trial yields a tested ordinary procedure | Complete lawful study of the actual enchanted specimen; learn its eligible effect while destroying it |
| Mature | Independently improve the understood handling procedure | Reconstruct a lost ordinary design and qualify new tolerances | Maintain several separately acquired effects and craft supported equipment with actual gems/benches |

The comparison concerns repeatable knowledge, not equal output. Destructive study can provide magical effect knowledge that ordinary observation never supplies under this proposal.
No unsupported universal spell-copy prohibition is asserted. SU3/SU4 require exact evidence before a particular exceptional method is added.
Observation output is a recorded lead/fragment, then an understood practiced method after testing. The donor retains their skill.
Reconstruction output is a tested process revision with observed failures. It can recover an ordinary method without seeing its full original performance.
Destructive study output is learned effect knowledge plus permanent removal of the owned study item. Its prior owner/holder history remains recorded.
O local study fee/timer follow the campaign's Orven proposal; no new price or reusable effect cartridge is selected here.
Required means: lawful unequipped specimen, available instruction/station and complete eligibility evidence; a loan cannot be sacrificed by its holder.
Commitment freezes the exact ring/key, effect revision and custody. A sold or equipped specimen invalidates the preview before any spend.
Interruption before committed study follows its declared policy; after destruction, cancellation or return does not recreate the ring.
Copying an ordinary taught method records demonstrator, evidence and trial revision. It does not copy the demonstrator's Magicka, physique, institution or property.
Repairing a damaged manual restores readable material only; missing content remains unknown unless another actual source supplies it.
Same-instance reload/return must retain the ring's absence and learned effect once. A different historical counterpart cannot refund that property.

## Comparison 3: protect the worker from a named exposure

Task: shield a handler from measured flying debris while preserving the load route. Heat, cold, spells and physical debris are distinct hazards.
O `skyrim.variant.cover_shell` is fitted physical cover; `skyrim.variant.screen_cast` is an original directed spell; a `qualified_worn_protection` remains SU1/SU3-gated.
The cast screen is not a canonical Ward. Exact source Ward mechanics are unresolved, so its local behavior is entirely O.

| Use | Physical cover | Directed screen | Qualified worn protection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower | Sound panel blocks debris from its covered direction | Learned O spell blocks one declared debris path at startup | Actual eligible effect reduces its specified exposure while worn |
| Developed | Better fit covers moving work without obstructing grip | Measured control redirects protection between known paths | Supported handler combines real protection with ordinary panel geometry |
| Mature | Stronger materials genuinely stop greater impacts | Sufficient output protects several eligible workers under explicit range/fuel limits | A superior verified specimen can give broader continuous protection without a forced cast-like upkeep |

Panel acquisition is fabrication/purchase; screen acquisition is taught spell/control; worn effect acquisition is creation or purchase of an actual eligible item.
None grants awareness of an incoming hidden attack. Detection and defensive reaction timing require separately supported evidence.
Physical cover costs carried mass and repair stock; O screen costs real Magicka/strain; worn effect has only source-supported costs.
Startup before impact matters. A shield cast after damage does not erase the wound or its witnessed cause.
O screen targets self or consenting allies within its declared contact/range rule; no generic `$party` tag establishes all those conditions.
A panel's hole admits the actual projectile; losing concentration removes future cast protection without deleting learned knowledge.
Stacking proposal: covered trajectories use the applicable panel, then remaining impact reaches the specified screen/garment. Protection does not multiply merely because labels overlap.
A verified immunity applies where its mechanism fits. The author cannot add a special debris immunity bypass after seeing a successful build.
The hidden attacker can be sufficiently strong to break the real support. Numerical force and durability retain meaning.

## Cross-source use and knowledge

Actual item holder, learned method owner, caster and protected worker can be different people; reserve each participant and legal input explicitly.
S1a establishes local Magicka use; it does not convert Frontier drive, Fallout charge, FP or chakra. Cross-source trials stay O and paid.
Suppression of a caster faculty disables that spell; boots and ordinary learned handling persist unless their own source is affected.
An imported superior lifting function may trivialize the physical segment if support is real; certification, custody and delivery still have actual owners.
Runa observes cart movement and delivery; Orven observes the agreed study/casting; neither automatically knows an unseen imported mechanism.
Performance can be reported as an estimate with date/evidence. A good result does not reveal exact reserve, hidden source or every possible counter.
Acquired method records are independent of later donor development. A new donor achievement does not rewrite the learner's old copy.

## Mastery within the selected method

O facets: load diagnosis, bodily alignment, stable contact, output control, reserve efficiency and interruption/recovery; test only facets relevant to this method.
Knowing an effect and applying it efficiently are different progress. A practiced handler may still lack enchanting knowledge or actual Magicka.
Paid practice records a specific supported result, such as correcting a measured jolt, rather than raising every facet from one generic XP grant.
Mode refinements can favor precise grip, sustained support or quick release. Later mastery may improve all three; equal permanent tradeoffs are not compulsory.
An actual new function needs tested method/source support. Lower fuel use alone does not create area protection or revive a sacrificed specimen.
Keep these semantic facets here while the catalogue retains structural/tool/source stages; no new universal mastery ceiling is selected.
