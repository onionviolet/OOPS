## Entry and connected local life

Fantasy: Build a useful local life through craft, magical study and freight, then choose who benefits from the roads you create.
Signature rule: Destructive magical study consumes real owned property. A lasting work enchantment and a fueled weapon/service create different investment and supply decisions.
Starting situation: Inspect Runa's damaged cart. Work ordinary paid shifts, destroy a legally owned eligible ring and make reusable boots, or keep the ring and pay Orven for a finite escort. Serevyn supplies real preparations; no route grants a canonical hero identity.
Ordinary paid loop: A four-minute mill shift spends two stamina for three proposed septims. Ring study costs two septims/two minutes and consumes the ring; later craft reserves plain boots, a filled gem and three septims/four minutes. Escort costs ten septims, an owned dose and Orven's six Magicka units/six minutes. All are original proof values; the specific ring/boot effect is O until ledger SU1 confirms canon eligibility.
Connected pursuits: Immediate brace/shift income feeds dry stock and a warded outfit; the medium refuge connects civilian/military supply; the long archive/road-compact arcs decide research access and regional control.
Recurring people/anchors: Runa, Serevyn and Orven retain their opening roles. Odda, Vela and Teren extend mills, refuge and archive disputes with finite means. Vilkas/Jorrvaskr and the College are bounded canon anchors; scenes remain source-gated.
Career scope: Body/martial practice, spell study, enchanting, alchemy, ordinary master craft, quartermaster work and archive exploration.
Era/parallel choices: `opening_crisis` and explicitly original `prior_generation` have different people/leases/resources. `civilian_compact` and `exclusive_corridor` are causal divergences, not rewinds.

Detailed plots retain trigger, actual occurrence, observation, response, refusal/expiry and aftermath in the campaign. Contracts can compose with living situations; refusing an offer creates no accepted obligation.
Local capacity, paid NPC means, actual participant availability and material custody determine whether a project can happen. Standing is access/duty, not a universal damage rank.
Known public offers/jobs belong in native actions and Notebook Here; supported reports/people belong in Events/People; only accepted work projects into Quests. No author-only outcome, hidden build or future revelation is a tooltip.
