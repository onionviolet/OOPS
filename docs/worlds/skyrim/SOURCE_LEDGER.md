## Constraints for progression and assemblies

Training path: practiced weapon/movement methods can grow independently of purchasing enchanted gear. Exact source skill/perk milestones need later inspection; original load-handling applications can proceed as `O` without claiming source-game parity.
Magic path: record actual known spell, supported body, Magicka and targets. Custom sustained industrial protection, heat control or spell combinations require bounded testing and an `O` label unless exact source scenes support them.
Craft path: ordinary fitted tools, dry stock and jigs have useful functions before magic. A magical housing cannot imply canon electrical circuitry or a universal enchantment socket system.
Enchanter path: select an actual eligible effect/item pairing. Destroy only legally held study goods under the project's custody rule; keeping the ring is a different route, not a future destruction refund.
Alchemist path: knowledge does not create ingredients, supply a diagnosis or guarantee a cure. Specific healing/protection mixtures require source-qualified effects or explicit original recipes.
Institution path: Companions/College existence does not create unlimited instructors, benches, subsidies or political immunity. Source-compatible anchors can surround invented suppliers/annexes.
Assembly path: one outfit can combine separately owned pieces, condition and training. No evidence here permits extracting reusable enchantment modules, re-enchanting every already enchanted object or freely transferring unique artifacts.
Cross-world test: Magicka is not automatically FP/chakra/electric supply. Keep the actual method/source while explicitly testing support and interface in an original branch.
