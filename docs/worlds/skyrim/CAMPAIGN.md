## Source and invention boundary

Selected baseline: the base game of *The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim*, ordinary visiting character, no selected ending.
Exclude DLC, Creation Club, copied dialogue/assets, fan-wiki dates and an automatic Dragonborn role.
S1: spells spend Magicka; enchanting needs known effects, eligible gear and filled gems; destructive study consumes the enchanted specimen. Worn enchantments and charged weapon effects have different fuel behavior. Alchemy combines shared ingredient effects. [Official PC manual, printed pp. 8, 16–17](https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/72850/manuals/skyrim_gfw_manual-07.pdf?t=1466708637).
S2: Whiterun's Jorrvaskr houses the Companions; the College at Winterhold teaches magic; Stormcloaks oppose Imperial rule. Vilkas provides a membership test in the source game. [Bethesda introduction](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/the-elder-scrolls-v-skyrim-make-your-mark).
S3: the source protagonist is Dragonborn amid a political crisis. Our visitor is a different person. [Bethesda opening introduction](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/the-elder-scrolls-v-skyrim-welcome).
Use only these bounded facts until the source ledger supplies more. No exact civil-war date, dragon biography or main-quest result is assumed.
All local people, annexes, projects, research procedures, economics and arcs below are original fan-continuity proposals, labelled O.
Career applications of S1 are O gameplay adaptations of bounded source facts; the source game does not contain these authored hauling contracts or workshop histories.
Canon anchors can be met only through separately verified scenes. They are neither guaranteed teachers nor automatic allies.

## Campaign promise and playable horizons

Make a living, acquire useful methods, then decide who gets the roads and magical infrastructure that your work makes possible.
Early play earns a bench, a tested mixture and one dependable contact through paid work and inspection.
Middle play develops an expedition outfit, a specialist study route and a politically contested depot.
Mature play can sustain several routes, defeat genuinely lesser threats and build an institution without becoming ruler of all Skyrim.
There is no mandatory guild sequence. A joiner can reach mature infrastructure play while remaining a mediocre duelist.
The opening supply cart remains a meaningful first chapter; its sacrificed ring or preserved ring shapes later opportunities.
Progress comes from new functions, access and combinations. Longer shift timers alone do not create a campaign.
Local coin proposal is scoped septims; no automatic exchange with Frontier coins is selected.

## Progression careers

Each chain separates possession, method, mastery, specimen condition and institutional access.
Local structural tiers and exact costs belong to [the catalogue](MODULE_CATALOGUE.md); the chains below specify campaign functions.

| Path | Acquisition and first function | Developed function | Mature function | Cost, weakness and useful return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O adaptation: body and martial practice | Paid drill, footwork, weapon handling; escort one cart segment | Read formations, protect another worker, sustain loaded movement | Lead a prepared convoy through fixed lesser threats; specialize in reach or close defense | Stamina, injury and practice time; poor reconnaissance still exposes the caravan; workyard fitting improves protection |
| O adaptation: spell practitioner | Legitimate spell instruction, demonstrated control and real Magicka | Sustained inspection light, situational protection and precise field application | Coordinate researched techniques over several workers or defeat suitable lower threats | Fuel, concentration, teacher access and target defenses; casting does not grant engineering judgment |
| O adaptation: enchanter and equipment maker | Buy or own a specimen, destroy it to learn an effect | Fit a durable work item and manage a separately charged tool | Design supported combinations and maintain a regional armory with specialist facilities | Lost specimen, gems, fitting labor and repair; knowledge alone never fills a gem |
| O adaptation: alchemist and field medic | Purchase known ingredients and supervised trials | Distinguish recovery, protection and valuable but unsafe unknown combinations | Establish tested supply standards and provide expedition-specific preparations | Samples are consumed; wrong evidence wastes batches; no universal cure or guaranteed rare ingredient |
| O ordinary master craftsperson | Joinery, drying, iron fitting and honest invoices | Build jigs, weatherproof carriers and reusable inspection fixtures | Architect a pass refuge and train an independent repair crew | Timber, capacity and paid collaborators; fabrication can be excellent without enchanted gear |
| O quartermaster and negotiator | Fulfill a declared small order; learn local claims | Reserve scarce capacity, bargain for mixed civilian/military access | Maintain a multi-site compact and allocate crisis transport | Deposits, reputation with particular people and incompatible duties; rank is not combat output |
| O archive explorer | Interview witnesses and map real foundations | Test old techniques against current materials | Recover lost process knowledge and establish a durable public or private archive | Evidence, travel and specimen custody; a remembered past can be wrong in this branch |

Paths cross at concrete jobs. A fighter protects a sample expedition; the sample gives an alchemist a new test, and the alchemist's paid dose funds a longer mage escort.
No path must learn every other path. Buying a service remains a meaningful alternative to acquiring its whole career.
Body mastery can reduce exertion or open movement. It cannot replace an enchanting station or authenticate a lease.
Institutional rank changes teachers, permissions and obligations. It does not silently multiply sword damage.
Exceptional source-dependent routes such as Shouts remain unscheduled until their lore and acquisition are verified.

## Components and assemblies in campaign use

Campaign-facing component families: load harness, fitted boots, plain weapon, charged weapon effect, worn enchantment, filled soul gem, spell method, Magicka reserve, tested preparation, dry-stock jig, archive evidence and route charter.
Their identity, tier track, mounting and upgrade rules remain in [MODULE_CATALOGUE](MODULE_CATALOGUE.md).
The O warded convoy outfit combines fitted boots, supported clothing protection, load harness, a separately charged weapon and named recovery stock.
It is equipment plus training, not a powered technological suit. Each item retains one physical identity and actual condition.
Removing the charged weapon removes its fueled offense; it leaves boot utility and learned martial practice intact.
A stronger harness opens safe load distribution; polish/quality changes fit and wear within its actual structural capability.
Replacing a worn piece can preserve pattern knowledge while removing the item's benefit until another eligible specimen is made.
Enchantment study destroys owned gear deliberately. Borrowed or equipped goods fail the initial research check.
Repair restores material condition. It does not grant a new pattern or turn a consumed ring into recoverable salvage.
Experimental cross-world housing/fuel is an O paid research project, never an asserted Skyrim crafting recipe.

## Three worked careers

### Resource-poor joiner and courier

1. Take the four-minute mill shift for three proposed septims, paying two stamina; repeat only while means/capacity permit.
2. Inspect the cart and fit ordinary timber with Runa's paid bench access. Learn load distribution through a completed, observed job.
3. Preserve the enchanted ring if owned; buy a finite escort rather than destroy property before the player can afford replacements.
4. Earn particular delivery memories, then invest in a dry-stock rack and a jig that improves repeatable brace production.
5. Use the refuge as a supplier/courier base. Reliable stock and known route warnings provide a niche beside stronger fighters.

Dependencies: sound timber, paid bench time, bodily stamina, actual customers and inspected route.
New functions: fit a carrier, reserve repeat orders, teach a jig procedure and support a convoy's preparation.
Weakness: cannot force an exposed dragon crossing or cast a ward from the ring's observed appearance.
Tradeoff: keeps valuable personal gear and earns local infrastructure, but pays specialists repeatedly.

### Developed warded field artisan

1. Acquire a lawful enchanted specimen and verify what studying it will destroy.
2. Pay Orven's two-septim slot and complete nonrefundable two-minute study; retain a knowledge receipt and the ring's absence.
3. Reserve plain boots, an actual filled gem and three septims for four-minute fitting/enchantment; craft cancellation refunds only these untouched reservations.
4. Train movement and learn a paid recovery mixture. Add a separately charged weapon only when charge supply exists.
5. Complete a supported northern sample expedition and choose public College study or private salvage rights.

Dependencies: specimen ownership, teacher, enchanter, fitting competence, gem, local fuel/recovery and destination access.
New functions: repeated hauling support, supported hazardous inspection and a maintained armed escort build.
Weakness: excellent work boots do not prevent every kind of magical attack; charge depletion can leave the weapon ordinary.
Tradeoff: lasting function and transferable knowledge cost an irreplaceable specimen and continued maintenance.

### Mature road-compact architect

1. Return with tested archive evidence, not a memory sold as a guaranteed ancient design.
2. Fund separate smith, alchemist and mage contributions; each starts only when that person receives actual supplies/payment.
3. Build the refuge in phases: foundations, dry stores, protected work area and public route records.
4. Train crews and establish paid reserves; allocate capacity under a published civilian/military charter.
5. Prepare a fixed-threat intervention with genuinely sufficient power or choose evacuation and cargo sacrifice.

Dependencies: several facilities, real collaborators, tested materials, sustained supplies and actual jurisdiction.
New functions: concurrent locally supported services, larger expeditions and chosen public/private access policies.
Weakness: the compact can lose members, supplies or an exposed site; large personal strength does not guarantee simultaneous rescues.
Tradeoff: spends scarce capital on shared capacity rather than concentrating every rare component in one outfit.

## Five escalating arcs

Each arc separates occurrence, observation, accepted commitment and aftermath. Numbers below remain proof/balance proposals.

### 1. The Lower Road supply choice

Trigger: inspect a damaged cart; a public supply offer has a 24-minute played-time window.
O author truth: the cart needs sound repair and supported hauling. The visible briefing shows damage and known service prices.
Choice A: destructive study and durable boots; loan boots to Runa's named hauler under custody and support the six-minute delivery.
Choice B: keep the ring, pay ten septims and an owned recovery dose; Orven spends six real Magicka units on a six-minute escort.
Aftermath: both settle the same shipment once, but one retains pattern/boots and the other preserves ring/property with a service memory.
Abstention: Runa funds the longer road, Serevyn redirects a shipment; later repair restores access without the expired fee or erased history.
An accepted default records a broken promise; refusal creates no debt. Consumed study/property/service inputs follow their separately disclosed policies.

### 2. A yard for whom?

Trigger: actual competing civilian and militia orders exceed Runa's bench capacity.
Evidence: reported bookings, owned stock and buyer terms; buyers' unspoken strategy stays hidden.
Choices: expand at timber/labor cost, prioritize one customer, or let Runa choose the best funded order.
Aftermath: civilian tools, escorted freight or expanded shared capacity change the next available contracts and specific memories.
Abstention: funded orders proceed according to Runa's policy. No automatic reputation loss with people who never learned the decision.
Later consequence: Orven may leave artillery work and offer paid study instead; Serevyn may retain or move the medicine route.

### 3. The salt archive

Trigger: delivered samples reveal a recoverable older support design in the coastal depot.
Choices: public sample store, exclusive financed retrieval, or independent testing with a smaller specimen set.
Aftermath: public access attracts apprentices; exclusivity provides capital but limits shared study; independent work stays costly and uncertain.
Abstention: the financer funds a rival expedition from actual means; some exposed evidence may be lost permanently.
Later consequence: tested design changes refuge foundations and the claimant's argument; a fabricated memory can ruin purchased stock.

### 4. The winter passage

Trigger: a real disrupted supply route meets an armed exclusive-delivery offer.
Choices: accept exclusivity and its explicit obligation, negotiate a paid civilian exception, or run a genuinely prepared independent convoy.
Aftermath: regional access and stock ownership change; supply contracts do not confer civil-war victory or authority everywhere.
Abstention: another financed carrier takes available rights. Civilians can lose supplies while the player succeeds elsewhere.
Later consequence: the refuge stores either military freight, shared reserves or private stock, influencing the dragon crisis.

### 5. The sky over the depot

Trigger: supported reports or direct sight establish a fixed dragon threat near the exposed route.
Choices: fight with actually sufficient means, divert below cover, evacuate workers, sacrifice cargo or decline intervention.
Aftermath: combat can genuinely defeat a suitable threat; evacuation can preserve people while losing sites; no option guarantees every rescue.
Abstention: Vela uses her actual shelter/scouts and may lose an exposed group; reports disclose only known survivors and damage.
Later consequence: surviving people, burned stock and maintained routes determine whether the compact can be rebuilt. Some losses remain permanent.
Lethal resolution is reserved for a separately selected severe profile; current native recovery is not silently replaced.

Reject an era composition with later teachers, leases or free goods that its initial snapshot cannot support.
The original Dragonborn plot can continue independently only where separately authored evidence supports it; this campaign does not fix its ending.
An intervention that saves a particular worker invalidates their expected death. Later scenes must read actual life/knowledge, not force history back into place.
Travel retains actual body, wounds, owned methods/sources, remaining fuel and named cargo; companions need real consent and compatible passage.
Frontier's learned `field.load_step` at mastery two can brace a supported cart with output three, paying three `field.drive` units and strain. Destination binding is missing.
A truly stronger imported body or levitation method can move greater cargo within actual capacity; no arrival nerf or adaptive dragon scaling is selected.
Unsupported recovery/fuel blocks execution without deleting ownership. Valid support restores use without relearning.
Local leases and legal authority remain instance-bound; real boots or ordinary fitting knowledge may export through an explicit native adapter.
Return requires a valid route, safe boundary and settlement/cancellation of jobs and borrowed goods. Interruption/capture is not a free escape button.
Draft absence/browser-closed freeze is still a proposal needing an adapter; death, run end, full-life retention and offline loss are explicitly unselected.

## Bounded expedition composition

A Paradise-style assignment can select one existing arc with declared objective, local entry means, paid costs and settlement/extraction terms, while the broader campaign supplies coherent people and aftermath. This is an O structural proposal, not verified novel mechanics.
A longer residence uses the same local history for work, training, relationships and projects; later visits do not reset its people or unique claims. A new era or parallel history requires a genuinely separate authored instance.
The shared issuer/hub owns mission admission and settlement, while this campaign owns local causes, source rules and lasting outcomes. Completing an extraction can leave the local conflict unresolved or lost.
