# My Hero Academia interactions

Status: Scene and interaction proposals r1. All original people, dialogue, schedules and numerical values are `O`. Canon-character knowledge requires a selected-era source and an observed or reported event.

## Evidence and knowledge contract

The authoritative incident state is separate from each actor's observations and reports. Store observer, target, time, sensory/tool channel, source, confidence and recipients. A police report, camera, Quirk detection, rumor and direct sighting are different evidence. The GM view must not leak hidden conditions into labels or character dialogue. A character who learned a fact keeps it unless a selected memory effect changes it.

## Interaction matrix

| ID | Situation proposal | Available response | Costs and consequences | Knowledge boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mha.scene.blocked-route` | Evacuation path is obstructed while roll call is incomplete | Clear obstruction, guide a group by a longer route, request licensed responder, or decline | Time, route capacity, tool stock, property risk and responder schedule | The player knows only observed people/hazards; cause remains unknown until evidence |
| `mha.scene.quirk-accommodation` | A worker's body/Quirk fit conflicts with a task or space | Adapt schedule, change equipment, reassign task with consent, or leave unsupported task | Work delay, fit cost, agency or employer response | Do not infer diagnosis from appearance |
| `mha.scene.copy-demo` | A temporary copied ability is proposed for a rescue drill | Verify contact/expiry and accumulated prerequisites; choose compatible test or stop | Lease timer, physical conditioning, partner availability | Copied function does not disclose source's private training or stock |
| `mha.scene.training-feedback` | A trainee's repeated use causes an observed drawback | Stop, adjust output, seek care, use tool support or continue with explicit risk | Fatigue/injury, session time, coach and equipment | Hypothesis about cause is labeled until tested/reported |
| `mha.scene.public-claim` | A viral clip accuses an unlicensed intervention | Preserve evidence, ask witnesses, make a qualified report or repeat rumor | Reputation, privacy, legal review, trust | Clip shows framing and angle, not full event intent |
| `mha.scene.trigger-exposure` | A substance exposure is suspected | Evacuate, identify route/formulation, seek qualified care, notify appropriate authority | Exposure, time, legal/safety consequences, uncertainty | Do not infer permanence from another formulation or case |
| `mha.scene.support-repair` | Rescue item is damaged before a shift | Repair, borrow with custody record, replace from stock or proceed without it | Funds, workbench time, owner permission | Repair status proves no new Quirk or factor |

## Original local roles proposed

`O` proposes one dispatch coordinator, one independent support maker, one trainee, one licensed responder and one neighborhood witness. Give each finite schedule, goal, resources, relationships and current knowledge. No named franchise character is assigned these duties. Canon figures enter only when the campaign row and source locator establish era, role and contact.

## Consequence rule

Each scene records occurrence, discovery, available response, accepted obligation, costs actually paid and aftermath. An NPC can outperform the player if their actual means support it. An adequate player action works when compatible. Refusal has no unaccepted obligation; abstention may lose an authored opportunity. A changed or randomized canon outcome is a sealed-slot route, never disclosed in a public index.
