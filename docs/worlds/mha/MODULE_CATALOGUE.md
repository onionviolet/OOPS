## Coverage rule

Use a specific Quirk function definition, not a universal Quirk skill tree. A row can describe innate mechanism, trained control, external support or changed condition, but these are different state owners. An advanced compatible power can simply outperform a lower one on a suitable task. Do not invent a counter, cost or weakness to force a preferred puzzle.

Continuity axes stay explicit: manga, anime, film, Vigilantes, novel, game and guidebook. A claim with unclear page-level provenance remains unclear. The 2024 ending is outside the selected snapshot.
