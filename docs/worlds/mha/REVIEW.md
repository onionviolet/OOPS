## Fixes, in priority order

1. **Legal route before first-slice authoring.** Specify that the proposed civilian-helper path does not itself grant public Quirk-use permission. The wiki states an unlicensed public-use ban even during rescue, then gives a self-defense exception, a Commission permission example, and only an *inference* about a Pro Hero's temporary grant to a protégé. The eligible supervised path needs an actual provisional license or a separately evidenced exception. `CAMPAIGN.md:20,28`, `SYNTHESIS.md:99`, and `IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md:7` currently leave that distinction implicit. Evidence: A9 `Heroes` r407817 @21041–22000; shard A `NOTES.jsonl` line 9.
2. **Separate Copy access expiry from effect persistence.** The proposed `CapabilityAccess.expiresAt` and “copy lease” wording must not make every copied effect vanish on switch or expiry. The Copy page says non-body-restructuring effects can remain, with altered object Size as its example. Add an independently owned effect/end condition to future rules. Evidence: A132 `Copy` r407112 @990–1220, @1987–2160; shard A `NOTES.jsonl` line 132. This changes rescue aftermath and save behavior if Copy enters a playable slice.
3. **Do not generalize Erasure across mutant bodies.** One section says mutant appendages remain while factor-dependent function can be impaired; another calls mutant fighters immune. Record the narrower function-level rule and the page's tension before authoring suppression or treatment. Evidence: A31 `Erasure` r389772 @2091–2310, @3778–3840; shard A `NOTES.jsonl` line 31.
4. **Keep primary and later-source gates explicit for canon use.** The pack already lists the 2024 ending, transfer/copy scenes, Trigger permanence and era-specific law as gaps (`SYNTHESIS.md:103-115`). Resolve the selected era and those primary scenes before treating the first slice as canon-verified. This is a research gate, not a reason to relabel the current proposal as failed gameplay.

## Supported details the pack underuses

- **Quirk registration is distinct from a hero license.** The wiki says citizens register Quirk details and may revise them as they learn more, with major or frequent revisions constrained. A future awakening or uncertain profile therefore needs a registration record, not just `Permission` and `QuirkFactor`. A17 `Quirk` r409871 @30220; shard A `NOTES.jsonl` line 17.
- **One For All donor residue and Copy consequences need separate clocks.** The shard A notes record finite post-transfer donor use for One For All, while the Copy page allows some effects to outlast access. Both reinforce the pack's proposed distinction among ownership, access, stock and effects. A35 `One For All` r409338 @18428–18800; shard A `NOTES.jsonl` line 35; A132 `Copy` r407112 @990–1220.
- **Institutional routes have concrete boundaries.** U.A. has written and practical admissions with rescue credit; the Heroes page distinguishes an observation internship from work-study after a provisional license. These can give the applicant and trainee proposals real eligibility checks once the era is chosen. A8 `U.A. High School` r401755 @8347; A9 `Heroes` r407817 @12938–14150.

## Fixes applied

1. **Applied: legal route before first-slice authoring.** Updated `CAMPAIGN.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`, `IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md`, and `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`. Civilian help does not itself authorize public Quirk use; a Quirk-using route needs an applicable license or separately evidenced exception. The Pro Hero temporary privilege remains labeled as an inference.
2. **Applied: separate Copy access expiry from effect persistence.** Updated `SYNTHESIS.md`, `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md`, and `IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md`. Added independently owned effect state and effect-specific end conditions; switching or access expiry does not automatically end every effect.
3. **Applied: qualify Erasure for mutant-type bodies.** Updated `SYNTHESIS.md` and `IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md`. Preserved both the function-level description and the same page’s conflicting immunity claim; no universal outcome is selected.
4. **Applied: explicit primary and later-source gates.** `SYNTHESIS.md` and `SOURCE_LEDGER.md` continue to identify the 2023 snapshot, post-snapshot ending and primary-scene checks as unresolved canon gates; the proposal is not relabeled as gameplay or canon-verified.
5. **Added supported registration detail.** Updated `SYNTHESIS.md`, `CAMPAIGN.md`, and `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`: Quirk registration/profile revisions are separate from hero license status.
6. **Added separate donor-residue and Copy-effect clocks.** Updated `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`, and `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`; donor-side One For All residue is finite and distinct from recipient access, while Copy-created effects can persist independently.
7. **Added institutional eligibility boundaries.** Updated `CAMPAIGN.md`, `SYNTHESIS.md`, and `SOURCE_LEDGER.md`: written/practical admission and rescue credit, plus distinct observation-internship and provisional-license work-study routes. Era-specific criteria remain open.

No review finding was declined. Evidence remains secondary snapshot evidence, not primary-canon verification.
