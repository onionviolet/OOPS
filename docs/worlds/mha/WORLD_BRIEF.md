# My Hero Academia world brief

Status: Develop/Research proposal r1, 2026-10-14. This pack is not accepted game design or implemented gameplay. Weibao placed MHA in the canon-first tier. The first milestone remains one realm and one prestige layer; this pack proposes one Japanese urban region and a bounded hero-work arc.

## Authority and boundaries

- User direction: [canon-first world priority](../../USER_VISION.md#world-depth-and-canon-first-world-priority-2026-10-13), including parallel/AU and relationship/start-point variants. Details here are proposals.
- Research base: WikiTeam Internet Archive snapshot dated 2023-11-28, secondary My Hero Academia Wiki. The manga ending (2024) and everything after the snapshot are gaps. See [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md).
- Default continuity proposal: original manga claims only where the input identifies manga evidence. Anime, films, Vigilantes, novels, games, guidebooks and unclear citations remain distinct. No blanket combined canon.
- Scope proposal: one living-world city region, civilian life, U.A.-adjacent public institutions, licensed hero work, support gear and Quirk development. Other realms, afterlife, nested timelines and crossovers are out of first slice.
- Franchise-public revelations can appear as canon facts. Any changed or randomized truth uses an opaque slot ID and discovery route only; see [secrets rule](../WORLD_SECRETS_AND_DISCOVERY.md).

## World identity

This is a society built around bodily differences, public safety, licensed intervention, education, media and everyday accommodation. A Quirk is not a class choice or universal mana pool. It is an individual capability with a body, trigger, usable envelope, condition, training history, social context and sometimes special transfer or alteration rules. Hero status, legal authority, public reputation and raw capability are separate records.

The game should make small ordinary work matter: a crowded evacuation route, a support item repair, a school exercise, a neighborhood complaint, a witness who saw only part of an event, or an agency shift with limited staff. A stronger Quirk can solve a task when its actual mechanism fits. A clever plan, equipment, coordination or refusal can also matter without a fabricated universal weakness.

## Proposed playable boundary

The first slice proposal lives beside a local hero office and a public training institution. Player chooses an eligible start and one actual Quirk profile, or a Quirkless profile. A district emergency asks the player to help track people and clear a blocked route. A legal supervised route, an ordinary civilian route and a support-gear route lead to different costs, permissions and witness knowledge. No canon protagonist needs to be a required quest giver.

Desktop/laptop target: a readable district panel showing current incident, available people, legal authority, Quirk/gear envelope, costs, and witnessed versus reported facts. Mobile optimization is deferred under standing UI direction.
