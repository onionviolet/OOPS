Status: Develop/Research proposal r1. Local story, cast and consequences are original proposals. Source evidence is in [ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md); this is not a canon-event chronology.

## Composition contract

Proposed run identity is `(world family, continuity, era, region, entry, scenario, Quirk/source profile, failure policy)`. Freeze the selected composition and seed at Begin. A revisit continues the committed instance. An earlier entry is a separately authored branch, not a rewind. Character foreknowledge is a belief until current evidence confirms it. Canon occurrence, revelation, actor belief and player knowledge have distinct records.

Default source window proposal: manga material represented by the 2023-11-28 snapshot, with chapter locators only where the secondary page supplies them. The 2024 manga ending is outside scope. No worker should fill that gap from memory. Anime adaptation differences, films, Vigilantes, novels, games, gag manga and guidebooks require explicit version selection.

## First local arc proposal

`mha.arc.route-and-rollcall`: an evacuation route is blocked and the dispatch desk has an incomplete roll call. The player can take a supervised licensed response if eligible, help as a civilian under responder instruction without using a Quirk publicly, or use a support item to clear one obstruction. A civilian-helper label grants no public Quirk-use permission. Quirk use requires an actual provisional license or a separately evidenced exception that applies to the actor, action, jurisdiction and time. The snapshot describes self-defense as an exception and a Commission permission example; its suggested temporary privilege from a Pro Hero is an inference, not an established general route. The job has finite responders, route capacity, equipment, played-time deadlines and witnesses. A successful rescue does not automatically identify who caused the emergency or confer hero status. Government Quirk registration remains separate from licensing; keep a revision history when new knowledge changes the registered profile, and do not assume unlimited major or frequent revisions.

Consequences are tied to actual actions: people reached, route opened, property damaged, equipment spent, obligations accepted, facts observed and reports received. Declining an optional duty creates no hidden debt. A deadline can close an opportunity or worsen a local situation only if the cause and schedule were authored in advance.

## Eligibility and public-use constraints

`O`: A civilian start or helper role does not authorize public Quirk use. Route civilian assistance through non-Quirk actions under responder instruction, or require an actual provisional license or a separately evidenced exception that applies to the actor, act, jurisdiction and time. The snapshot describes a self-defense exception and a Commission permission example; its Pro Hero temporary-grant explanation is an inference.

`O`: Keep government Quirk registration separate from hero licensing. Record the registered profile and its sourced revisions; the snapshot says people may update details as they learn more, while major or frequent changes are usually constrained. Applicant eligibility checks the selected institution’s written and practical assessments and any applicable rescue credit. An observation internship and licensed work-study are separate routes.

## Failure, return and persistence proposal

The pack does not select permadeath, Quirk erasure recovery, offline NPC advancement or cross-copy merging. Keep body injury, Quirk/source state, equipment, reputation, legal status, knowledge and obligations separate. If later persistence changes, choose a migration or explicit compatibility decision before implementation. Personal saves remain untouched during research.
