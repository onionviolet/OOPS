# My Hero Academia ability variants

Status: Develop/Research proposal r1. IDs and function examples are YAIRPG proposals. Source anchors and limits are in the [ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md). A named Quirk is not a transferable generic power profile.

## Comparison A: move one trapped civilian to safety

| Proposed mode | Valid means and useful envelope | Cost / limit | Information result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mha.variant.lift-and-carry` | Compatible trained body and safe grip, with a clear path | Fatigue, load, injury risk and time | Rescuer knows what they personally saw |
| `mha.variant.quirk-rescue` | A selected Quirk can lift, bind, phase, redirect or create a route only within its authored target and body conditions | Quirk-specific reserve/drawback and target compatibility | Effect reveals only what the actor can perceive |
| `mha.variant.support-tool` | Actual owned or issued tool performs its tested function | Charge, stock, fit, condition, legal authority | Instrument result is not innate perception |
| `mha.variant.team-chain` | Available partners each perform a compatible task and relay an actual report | Schedule, consent, communications and finite capacity | Shared knowledge retains speaker/source |

No mode is the universal best answer. A genuinely stronger compatible Quirk may solve the task directly. A tool or team can outperform an incompatible ability without granting a new Quirk.

## Comparison B: learn or use another capability

| Route | State change | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `mha.variant.practice` | Develop control or technique for the learner's own Quirk/body | Training is not factor transfer; retain fatigue, coach and practice evidence |
| `mha.variant.copy` | Temporary access to the selected copied function after its actual trigger | Copy page says 5 minutes then up to 10; accumulated resources and conditioning do not transfer with the factor (`Copy`, rev. 407112) |
| `mha.variant.inheritance` | Specific donor/recipient factor transfer with intent and procedure | One For All is an exceptional composite route; DNA and holder intent conditions remain (`One For All`, rev. 409338) |
| `mha.variant.theft` | Factor leaves donor under the specific stealing method | Touch/proficiency/body constraints and damage remain; do not model as normal item trade (`All For One (Quirk)`, rev. 409862) |
| `mha.variant.gear` | Tool supplies or shapes an effect | Actual custody, compatibility, repair and stock; gear never silently grants a factor |

## Comparison C: defend against one observed hazard

`O` proposal: select the hazard's actual domain and delivery first. Body resilience, a Quirk defense, a barrier, armor and evacuation alter different state. Each defense records coverage, activation, duration, interruption, fuel, affected targets and residual harm. If a genuine source protection covers the hazard, let it work. If the effect is stronger and compatible, let it overpower a lesser defense. Do not invent a universal weakness or immunity.

## Facets and evidence

Proposed development facets: mechanism recognition, trigger control, safe timing, target selection, reliable range, dual-task coordination, recovery and tool integration. These are local training dimensions, not a canonical ranked ladder. Advancement needs a real practice outcome and consumes actual time/support. Proficiency does not grant new target classes, source ownership, legal authority or another person's memory.

Every executable definition needs a source/version, actual owner, body/factor identity, trigger, target domain, range, output, cost, condition, counter/evidence, end condition, interruption and persistence rule. Unsupported values remain open. Every table row is proposal `O`.


## Cross-cutting consequences

`O`: Copy access expiry and effects created through that access have separate clocks. Switching or losing access does not automatically end every effect; the snapshot describes some non-body-restructuring effects as persistent. Record an effect-specific end condition and verify the chosen mechanic before gameplay use.

`O`: One For All donor residue is also separate from transferred ownership and recipient access. After transfer, the previous user retains residual power that declines with continued use; model this as a finite donor-side reserve, not as recipient stock. The snapshot does not establish a universal numeric depletion rate.
