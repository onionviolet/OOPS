## Spell, NP, command and vow audit

The digest's earlier E8 mapping is explicitly unverified. Its DF1–DF3 review supports only a bounded Fuyuki model: command authority and energy supply are separate; the command has a delivery carrier; specificity and agreement affect enforcement; a Servant is an autonomous counterparty; relationship may remain after compulsion no longer applies. It does **not** establish a universal vow or oath schema.

| Question | Proposed card field | Default status |
|---|---|---|
| Who owns the method or authority? | source, holder, controller, jurisdiction | Required per selected profile |
| What activates it? | trigger, spoken/physical action, carrier, timing | Unknown until source gives it |
| What can it affect? | target domain and compatibility | Unknown until source gives it |
| What pays? | reserve, authority charge, external supply, material, injury, obligation | Separate ledgers; no bundled cost |
| What can stop it? | interruption, resistance, incompatibility, loss of supply, counter | Unknown is explicit |
| What persists? | body, source, artifact, instance, contract, event record | Separate carriers |
| What does the actor know? | observation, report, inference, belief | Actor-specific; author truth does not leak |


### Fuyuki Master state after Servant loss

`W` Master r398975 says Master status can persist while the Master retains a Command Spell, even without a Servant; an unclaimed Servant can then be contracted. `O` Keep Master status, active contract, living instance, and remaining charges as separate state. Exact eligibility and transition behavior still require a selected primary-source check.

`W` Command Spell r404906 says three is the initial Fuyuki grant, not an absolute lifetime cap: more may be stolen or granted by the overseer under circumstances. `O` Do not hard-code a three-charge lifetime maximum or assume replenishment.

### Chaldea resummoning memory

`W` Servant r405040 describes Chaldea Saint Graph storage as an exception where defeated Servants may return with memories from prior Chaldea summonings, subject to circumstances. `O` An FGO mission must declare whether this local persistence rule applies; do not assume either universal reset or universal memory retention.

| Growth form | Proposal | Avoid |
|---|---|---|

| Class / graph change | Create a versioned new instance or form with an explicit source and effect set. | Replacing a heroic record, body or history silently. |
| Rebirth / restoration | Require continuity-specific carrier, cause, cost, and proven conditions. | General immortality, universal resurrection, or a free prestige loop. |
