# Independent Fate / TYPE-MOON world-pack review

**Verdict: usable with fixes** as a secondary-source design proposal. The central authority, supply, record, vessel and continuity distinctions survive direct payload checks. It is not canon-authenticated, implemented or playable. The fixes below concern a false media option, a wrong revision, and source/inference labels; they do not require discarding the pack.

**Method and locator key.** I read the pack, `AGENTS.md`, `PROJECT.md`, the source deep-dive playbook, both wiki-lane spot-checks, and the relevant Fate reader notes. `P Title rNNN @A–B` means character offsets in the retained `text` for that title and revision. Resolve the exact private `read_file` through `typemoon_SOURCES.json` under `docs/research/power-systems/wiki-digests/full-dumps/`; the selected text hashes of the checked payloads matched their manifest `read_sha256`. `N` means the matching title/revision entry in the Fate `NOTES.jsonl` files under `workers/fate-full/` or its `r2-2026-10-13/shard-{a,b,c}/` directories. Payloads are dated wiki compilations with unexpanded references, not primary TYPE-MOON works. File line numbers below refer to the pack as reviewed on 2026-10-15. **Supported** means the bounded wiki claim survives this check; **qualified** means its scope or label needs repair; **incorrect** means the named source contradicts it.

## Fixes in priority order

1. **Remove the Apocrypha “game version” option** from `CAMPAIGN.md:15`, or label it as an abandoned planning concept that cannot be selected as a source edition. The page's game development section contradicts the current wording.
2. **Repair the evidence labels and direct citations in the rule table.** `SYNTHESIS.md:23` should say Command Spells carry distinct authority/charge and can themselves supply magical energy in specific uses. Rows 25–29 and 31 are cross-page inference or authoring principles, not direct `W` assertions. Row 30 needs the remake revision, and row 31 needs at least one payload case instead of only shard summaries. This prevents a proposed engine ontology from being presented as a wiki rule.
3. **Correct `SOURCE_LEDGER.md:57` to r403127.** The current r392162 does not match the retained manifest or paired note. Also specify at `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md:24` that three is the initial Fuyuki grant, with extra charges possible through named routes.
4. **Keep media fields exact when a scenario is chosen.** The pack generally separates Zero/stay night, hollow ataraxia, EXTRA, FGO events, original/remake Tsukihime, Redline/GUDAGUDA, manga, anime, films and games. Its broad Prisma and other adaptation entries still require a particular source edition before mechanics are authored. This is already listed as a gap in `SYNTHESIS.md:120–127`; retain that gate.

## Supported detail the pack leaves unused

- **Master state after Servant loss:** Master r398975 @2450–3230 says status can persist with Command Spells after the Servant is gone, and a new pact can be made with an unclaimed Servant. A future Fuyuki state model should separate Master status, active contract, living instance and remaining charges.
- **Command Spell acquisition is not only the initial grant:** Command Spell r404906 @1300–2200 allows more through theft or the overseer under circumstances. That changes any charge-cap or replacement rule.
- **Summoning memory has a Chaldea exception:** Servant r405040 @48000–48650 describes Saint Graph storage and conditional memory retention after resummoning. The pack's general record/instance split is sound, but an FGO mission needs this local persistence rule rather than an assumed reset.

**Other checks.** The pack labels its starts, engine fields and first slice as proposals; I found no accepted-gameplay or primary-canon claim. Its changed/randomized truth references give only a slot/route policy and reveal no sealed truth. A quote scan found no quoted passage above 15 words; a 16-word normalized overlap scan across all nine pack files and all 6,371 retained payloads found no copied run. These checks cannot authenticate the wiki's embedded citations. The design-changing gaps in `SYNTHESIS.md:114–129` are materially honest, including primary-source procedures, route timing, cross-work architecture, FGO history conflicts, transclusions and native persistence. No build or gameplay check is claimed by this review.
