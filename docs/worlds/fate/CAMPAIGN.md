## Connected play arcs

All arcs below are proposed, original local plots. Source people are optional anchors only when their version and era support presence; the player cannot inherit their unearned secrets or powers.

1. **Workshop account:** reconcile material, labor, fuel, and evidence for a small commissioned effect. Alternatives: do it personally if eligible, hire a qualified operator, or refuse. Consequence belongs to the actual customer and supplier.
2. **The borrowed catalyst:** custody is disputed after a source object changes hands. Track holder, owner, provenance, and what each witness saw. A catalyst is not a guaranteed summon or an ability grant.
3. **The silent familiar:** investigate a missed report by checking its carrier, route, operator, signal, and interruption. Do not give the player the familiar's viewpoint without a delivered observation.
4. **War-adjacent shelter:** an ordinary household must decide whether to shelter an endangered participant, call institutional help, or protect its own supply. A refusal can remain consequential without inventing a debt.
5. **Branch-specific crisis:** a known local event is altered in one selected continuity. Record only changed causes and downstream state. A randomized changed truth is an opaque sealed slot with a route, never a visible candidate list.

## AU and access proposals

Existing world-depth proposals support in-world route divergence rather than a franchise-wide frontier. The `QBK4` model proposes route-specific event graphs; `QBW10` explicitly limits that approach to stay night and binds hollow ataraxia separately. The older QW10 suggests a Grail War as a main quest with the Church as referee, but this is a proposal and not a required player role.

| Access / AU proposal | Entry condition | Continuity rule |
|---|---|---|
| Route divergence (`QBK4`, `QBW10`) | New-game route choice, or an earned supported divergence event. | Each stay night route gets its own event graph, cast state and consequences; hollow ataraxia is separately scoped; no live route is rewound. |
| New-game selection (`AC1`, `SM2`, `SM4`, `SM9`) | Select a known/unlocked work and compatible start; preview does not commit or reroll. | Confirmed selection persists on reload/import; sealed/unknown variants stay fogged. |
| In-world door (`AC2`), invitation (`AC10`), carrier (`AC9`) | Actual place, NPC, or owned route item with supported means, cost and destination. | Reveal only what the door, host or carrier can establish; crossing does not merge instances. |
| Notebook or achievement route (`AC11`, `AC12`) | Acquire a route fact or prior profile discovery. | Opens access, not hidden destination truth or knowledge the character has not learned. |
| Storyteller choice (`AC7`) | A hub or eligible offer presents a few fogged, public-level options. | Hide canon names until arrival when needed; do not imply story familiarity or player foreknowledge. |
| Fall-through / accident (`AC15`) | An authored failed crossing or unstable route. | The character sees actual surroundings; copy identity may be unknown only if the scenario establishes that uncertainty. |
| Fuyuki historical branch | Explicit historical-entry capability and a new branch instance. | Selecting 1994 is not time travel that changes a 2004 instance. |
| Other Fate work | Validated access route plus exact destination profile. | Arrival carries only declared body, memory, methods, owned sources, fuel, cargo, companions, obligations, and return limits. |
| FGO mission | Chaldea permission and selected operation. | A mission projection is not a franchise-wide merge or another world's permanent state. |
| Sealed alternate truth | Discovery route in selected instance. | Public source canon remains public; changed/randomized world truth only appears through its sealed ID's route. |

No automatic cross-world portal, global quest line, universal time travel, or free copy of unique people/items is selected. Those remain implementation/design decisions for the integrator.
