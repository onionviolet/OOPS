## Identity and promise

**[O-PROPOSAL] World family:** `fan.type_moon`. Each entry must select exactly one work-specific continuity, rules profile, era, location, role, and scenario. An entry in one branch does not imply that the same person, event, power, or rule exists in another.

**[O-PROPOSAL] Campaign promise:** live inside a world where identity, training, ritual access, energy supply, authority, information, and historical possibility each have their own limits. A person can be a capable magus without being a Master; a contract can carry obligations without granting unlimited control; a famous heroic record is not the same thing as its summoned instance.

**[O-PROPOSAL] Signature distinction:** preserve the chain `source / person / vessel / contract / fuel / executable action / observed result`. Possession of one does not silently grant the others.

**[O-PROPOSAL] First candidate:** a small Fuyuki-adjacent workshop or household campaign in a fixed *Fate/stay night* route branch, with ordinary paid work, one bounded magical investigation, and a consequential choice about whether to aid a war participant. The proposed player is not Shirou, Rin, Sakura, or a seventh hidden hero. It is a new local person whose real means and knowledge are authored. This is only a later candidate; the first proof in the [packet](IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md) uses authority/resource distinctions in a safer, smaller contract scenario.

## Source boundary

**[EVIDENCE]** Current source basis is reader notes from the dated 2026 Type-Moon Wiki archive plus `DIGEST_fate-series.md`; the wiki is secondary and revisions are cited in [SOURCE_LEDGER](SOURCE_LEDGER.md). Readers report complete reading of their assigned retained selected payloads, not every full article, nested transclusion, or primary source. *Fate/stay night*, *Fate/Zero*, *Fate/EXTRA*, *Fate/Grand Order*, original and remake *Tsukihime*, *Kara no Kyoukai*, *Mahoutsukai no Yoru*, *Fate/Apocrypha*, *Fate/strange Fake*, *Prisma Illya*, *Fate/Prototype*, *DDD*, and game/event branches remain separately indexed.

**[O-PROPOSAL]** Do not copy prose or source assets. Keep quoted text out of the tracked pack. Public franchise revelations may be named. Any invented, changed, or randomized truth gets an opaque sealed ID and a discoverable route; this pack records no sealed truth.
