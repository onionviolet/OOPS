# Harry Potter candidate source ledger

Status: bounded candidate-source review, 2026-10-03. Master owner. This supports an origin/campaign proposal, not a full seven-book audit or selected sixth deep adaptation. [Brief](WORLD_BRIEF.md) owns local proposal; [origins/timelines](../ORIGINS_AND_WORLD_TIMELINES.md) owns shared start rules.

## Proposed boundary

Default source snapshot: J.K. Rowling's original seven Harry Potter novels, English Bloomsbury series, including their authored endpoint. Film, Cursed Child, Fantastic Beasts, games, later television material and supplemental writings are separate source sets. No recent release schedule, shopping choice, copied text, panels or assets is relevant to this design.

The publisher synopsis was inspected; complete book/chapter text was not acquired. Official companion/author pages below are reference evidence whose range is stated separately. A claim being verified on a companion page does not mean its details were cross-checked against every original novel.

## Questions before a direct implementation

- Exact magical-source acquisition, mundane learning limits, source inheritance and adult admission/training routes under the selected books.
- A sourced first-year window, earlier generation and post-ending cast/body/institution snapshot; do not invent exact canonical dates.
- Selected wand/catalyst, potion, charm, transfiguration, herbology and nonhuman-body rules, including limits and irreversible consequences.
- Family/guardian/school recognition, permission, wages/property and age-specific access; exact wizarding law is not checked here.
- Specific canon character voice/schedule/power and source scenes before writing a direct consequential meeting.

These source gaps do not prevent labelled original scenario design. A deliberate changed rule needs a divergence label; a verified source fact can remain a constraint. Full source fidelity and numerical tuning are open, separate from technical document review.
