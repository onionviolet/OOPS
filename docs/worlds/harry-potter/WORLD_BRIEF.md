Exact placement/history is `O`; canonical location labels need source inspection. The contexts exchange real care, stock, evidence, knowledge and access.

A powerful spell can solve a suitable physical obstacle; secrecy, support, timing and simultaneous needs can still change a mission.

## Original people and connected life arc

**Mara Fen, caretaker:** wants a reliable livelihood and the child's safety; owns household stock and practical skills. Knows what she actually saw, not private past-life memories. An unusual signal may prompt curiosity, reassurance or suspicion depending on witnessed history.

**Elias Brook, nursery worker:** wants healthy stock and a fair supply contract; owns tools/seeds and observed growing records. Can tend or interpret a declared tree signal after learning it; cannot automatically hear every plant or identify the player's soul.

**Nessa Vale, apprentice craftsperson:** wants a teaching place and a tested design; has a rented bench and limited samples. Remembers real contributions and borrowed tools. Favors are particular obligations, not universal school reputation.

Initial arc: household work or age-appropriate signaling creates trust and a known local need. Plant/craft work supplies a supervised lesson; lesson evidence opens a useful study or supply role. A later original registry/supply dispute forces a choice between private family advantage, disclosure and independent support. A crisis can divert the nursery into medicine/refuge supply or destroy a scarce sample. Intervening, refusing and selling unverified foreknowledge leave distinct material/social history.

Two adult counterparts can remember the same expected future but disagree about what current evidence proves. One may become a rival supplier, share methods, expose a false prediction or retire. They need actual observation/resources, not access to the player's private UI.

## Visits, craft and first proof

Imported observation/precision can help keep records or fit a carrier where the actual body/tool supports it. It does not grant a wand bond, magical source, verified prophecy or every local method. A learned preservation/fitting procedure and genuine carried materials can be useful back in Frontier after its actual recipe/compatibility binding exists.

Proposed first proof compares an ordinary entrant's paid nursery/craft contribution with a child entrant's supervised observation and limited-body action. Both can earn a particular trusted instruction opportunity with different costs/evidence, followed by a reviewed authored age/chapter advance in the child fixture. A tree variant is a later actual body-specific proof, not a renamed human.
