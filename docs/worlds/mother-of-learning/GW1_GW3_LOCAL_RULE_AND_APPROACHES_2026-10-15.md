## GW1-CY: one local rule with a functional consequence

**Branch operating rule:** knowing a magical formula, possessing a compatible
personal source, supplying actual energy, owning a functioning device and having
trained control are separate conditions. A visitor without an established
compatible personal source does not gain casting just by reading. An actually
supported external device or willing local specialist can provide another route.

| Distinction | Action changed in this existing branch | Consequence to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge versus personal source | Study reveals understanding/known procedures; it does not mint personal mana or turn a book into a cast. | Missing personal compatibility redirects the method, not every possible solution. Actual other starts/means may differ. |
| Artifact capacity versus stored charge | Nora reconstructs an actual lamp; calibration separately supplies stored device fuel from her finite attuned reserve. | An empty built lamp is still owned equipment, not a charged spell source. No passive reload/absence refill. |
| Control versus ownership | Xvim's actual held-tool drill spends time/charge and improves the operator mastery read by the field action. | The device is not fully usable merely because its item label is in inventory. Knowledge, practiced control and material custody are all checked. |
| The caster is an actor with means | Taiven's supported action spends her source, staff-backed ability, effort, attention and field supplies. | Cooperation solves a real problem without copying her talent, granting permanent escort or running a free helper effect. |
| Location/support and portability | The keyed device's supported operation is scoped to its authored Cyoria/body binding. | Carrying its material or remembered procedure elsewhere does not prove the device still works there. No universal power suppression is inferred. |

The useful verb is therefore **prepare/operate a supported tool or coordinate an
actual specialist**, rather than selecting “cast” from an unearned spell list.
This need not become a new global magic compiler or extra mana bar for the visitor.
Known prerequisites belong in existing qualified previews. Unknown compatibility
or deeper causes stay unknown until actual assessment/research reveals them.
