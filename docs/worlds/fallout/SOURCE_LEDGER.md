## Progression and assembly constraints

Physical/operator path: train scouting, carrying and equipment control through actual practice. A suit's possession does not give piloting, diagnosis, aim or treatment knowledge. Exact S.P.E.C.I.A.L./perk adaptation remains separate.
Weapon path: own a compatible weapon, actual ammunition and any supported modification. Named attachments, per-shot costs and repair behavior need an inspected source or explicit `O` rule.
Armor path: F2 supports maintenance/customization in general. It does not verify interchangeable T-series stages, all model availability, electrical output or manufacture of fusion cores.
Utility path: pumps, laboratory fixtures and a Waterline Reclamation Rig can be original engineering projects with tested functions. Label thermal/pressure/cargo budgets `O`; realistic-sounding values are not canon specifications.
Medic path: distinguish contaminated sample, radiation exposure, ordinary injury and actual therapy. Filtering water does not automatically remove accumulated bodily radiation. Original diagnosis can guide play without claiming real-world technical or medical precision.
Institution path: volunteering/refuge/service can open local access under invented terms. Faction standing and material capacity remain separate; an Institute contact grants neither a reproducible body nor every research secret.
Assembly custody: one frame/actuator/plate belongs to one actual assembly/holder. Salvage preserves inspected condition; installation and refit do not duplicate intact parts or refund spent fuel.
Portability: retain ordinary skill and actual suit cargo/support, while checking interfaces and fuel. Neither magical fuel nor Fallout 76 mechanics become a base-game core charger by analogy.
