# Nonhuman protagonist mechanics, 2026-10-14

Status: research and proposals, not accepted design or implemented gameplay. Monster-protagonist lane under [COMMON](../gm/dispatch/nonhuman-starts-2026-10-14/COMMON.md); [checkpoint](../gm/dispatch/nonhuman-starts-2026-10-14/monster/CHECKPOINT.md). This is a mechanics palette, not a reading list. The [start-as-X catalog](../research/START_AS_X_SOURCE_CATALOG_2026-10-14.md), especially SA14–SA18, owns source selection and holdings.

Authority: [User vision](../USER_VISION.md), [PROJECT](../../PROJECT.md), [E1–E15 engine owner](NONHUMAN_STARTS_ENGINE_REQUIREMENTS_2026-10-14.md), [origins](ORIGINS_AND_WORLD_TIMELINES.md), [genetics/brood G1–G4, G9–G10](../GENETICS_CONSTITUTIONS_AND_BROOD_PLAY.md), [identity/control L1–L5, K1–K5](PLAYER_CHARACTER_IDENTITY_AND_CONTROL_2026-10-13.md), [intro CR4 and SA14–SA19](../INTRO_FLOW_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md), and [foundations U01/U03/U12, X01/X06, V06](../SHARED_FOUNDATIONS_AUDIT_2026-10-12.md). These owners distinguish person, body, controller, coherent history, actual carriers, observer recognition and care. A source resemblance grants none of a protagonist's exceptional powers automatically.

**Evidence convention.** SHOWN means a narrated event/result, STATED names the speaker or secondary source, INFERRED is an interpretation, MEMORY is outside-text recall, UNKNOWN is unresolved. Local SHOWN/STATED claims below reuse the cited dossiers' located readings; this lane read those notes, not whole novels anew. Wiki audits' older CANON labels are restated as STATED (wiki), never upgraded to primary-text proof. All engine shapes, verbs proposed for YAIRPG, minimal state, defaults and dials are **INFERRED proposals**. No direct source quotations are reproduced.

**Scope.** Tensura baseline is Fuse's Japanese WN, Syosetu n6316bn, supplied members 1–304. Member numbers are collection IDs, not printed episode numbers. Main story ends at 256; bonuses/side stories have distinct provenance. The older wiki audit merges adaptations and mostly LN material. Keep that boundary. Public metadata/wiki inspection for nonlocal comparisons ran on 2026-10-14, without source-body downloads. Some wiki full-page fetches failed; search excerpts are explicitly identified below. No sealed YAIRPG files were opened.

**Default + Dial + Reasonably per world.** Default: a body has useful distinctive verbs, ordinary idle routines and coherent local relationships. Dial: solitary, partner, territory, collective or established ruler; low or high starting strength; canonical route or declared AU; transformation optional. Reasonably: a supported means can expand the available choices, including communication, a carrier, research or an earned new body. No universal penalty for a strange body, compulsory human end form, or hunger death. Meals help; a source's starvation pressure is not imported as YAIRPG's default. One realm and one prestige layer remain the first milestone. Desktop/laptop presentation is primary; mobile is deferred. This document changes no UI and claims no viewport/browser evidence.

### TM1: Slime limits produce new verbs

SHOWN: Rimuru begins blind/deaf, explores by touch, dissolves/absorbs herbs, and does not need food or sleep there. STATED (Great Sage): magicules sustain the body without oxygen; cell division distributes thought/rest. SHOWN: water-jet experimentation produces movement, a collision damages the body despite pain immunity, and regeneration repairs it. Later magic perception needs teaching and Sage-assisted processing; language comprehension does not itself produce speech. T1 members 2–4, 7–9, headings “Testing what I can do,” “First contact,” “First conversation,” “Acquiring skills,” “First battle,” “Acquired abilities.”

SHOWN/STATED (Rimuru tests): biomass supports larger mimic forms; copied breath has shorter reach in slime form than snake form; speech develops through an anatomical reference and practice. T1 members 8–9. SHOWN/STATED (Great Sage/Rimuru): the early print bonus cannot supply taste, intoxication or hands from insufficient anatomical data. T4 member 282, “Night Butterfly,” F282a. This is a bonus-stage limit, not a claim about every later form.

| Engine shape, proposal | Minimal state | A useful idle result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amorphous body with channels and functions | `bodyId`, mass, integrity, shape, available senses, movement methods, manipulation methods, fuel source | Queue herb sorting or dissolution through touch; earn a usable ingredient without pretending to hold a knife |
| Practice creates control | practiced method, compatible anatomy, available water/material, procedure progress | Repeated supported water propulsion improves control and opens a route |
| Regeneration without compulsory sleep | damaged structure, repair method, reserve, recovery job | Repair while a compatible analysis job runs; attention/fuel sharing follows actual means |

Default: contact sensing, engulfing and local water movement make a small slime playable before human imitation. Dial: acquired remote sense, speech, tendrils or a humanoid form. Reasonably: preserve the slime route as a full life rather than treating human shape as graduation. E1–E6, E9–E12, E15. A pain-free body still takes damage; no extra pain meter is needed merely to express that distinction.

### TM2: Predator is a pipeline, not a kill reward

STATED (Great Sage): Predator has intake, analysis, stomach, mimicry and isolation. Conscious targets resist intake; copies need materials, mimicry needs analyzed information, and stored contents are unaffected by time. TC C01, member 2, body char 4789, anchor `解析`. SHOWN: consent permits ingesting Veldora and his prison after direct seal attack fails. T1 member 5. SHOWN: hunting yields heat sensing, poison breath, threads, ultrasound and armor; partial anatomy changes potency. T1 members 8–9.

STATED (Rimuru): the early claim that consumption yields no knowledge is later qualified by unreliable memory fragments. TC C10, members 41/49, chars 4388/3074. STATED (Great Sage): Gluttony's Supply grants partial abilities within recipient capacity and Food Chain returns gains; technique/knowledge are excluded from automatic sharing. TC C07, member 44, chars 565/644. SHOWN: later integration changes or removes functions rather than retaining every earlier interface. TS F7/R3–R4 and TC C41.

| Stage, proposal | Minimal state | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Obtain and ingest | target/item ID, owner/holder, access, intake method, contest/consent if relevant | A defeated creature and its usable specimen are distinct; custody transfers once |
| Analyze or isolate | sample ID, observations, analysis job, contamination/isolation state | Unknown material stays unknown; hazard handling follows this method |
| Store or manufacture | container/body ID, capacity, contents, preservation policy, recipe inputs | Timeless storage is an acquired power, not every inventory's default |
| Express/mimic | analyzed template, installed faculty, compatible functions, mass/fuel, mastery | Blueprint knowledge does not automatically grant authority or expert movement |
| Supply linked gains | giver/recipient, link, granted faculty, recipient capacity, payment | Training XP is shared only if the selected mechanism actually carries it |

Default: one specimen becomes one new usable faculty after analysis and rehearsal. Dial: instant integration, long research, partial forms, reliable memory transfer or stronger sharing as explicit mechanisms. Reasonably: scavenging, trade and consenting study can feed an original absorption route where the world allows them; those alternatives are proposals, not statements that every WN skill can be obtained that way. E2–E5, E8–E9, E13–E14. An idle queue should say what it is digesting and what input ends it, with no invented endless prey supply.

### TM3: Naming, bonding and evolution have separate outcomes

SHOWN/STATED (Veldora): reciprocal Tempest naming marks equal friendship; Rimuru observes no immediate visible evolution. TC C04, member 5, chars 3002/3376. SHOWN: mass naming exhausts Rimuru into conscious low activity with predicted recovery. TC C05, member 12, char 3558. STATED (Veldora): his reserves secretly paid early deficits and delayed his release. TC C17 and T2 F77b, member 77, char 796.

SHOWN/STATED (Rimuru): refugee orc naming recycles otherwise fading energy, evolves high orcs and creates no domination relation because his own power was not used. TC C08, member 45, chars 367/3102. STATED (Benimaru): magicule loss can be permanent. TC C09, member 49, char 1667. SHOWN: late souls substitute for magicules in Ramiris's naming of four dragon kings. TC C32, member 170, char 4418. These cases do not establish a universal zero-risk ambient payer or renaming formula.

SHOWN: goblin/ogre and wolf routes have different evolutionary neighbors; pack propagation is a specific case. T1 members 13, 29–30, 39, 76; TS “Race paths actually supported by the reads.” SHOWN: class assignment alters Hakurou while evolution is settling; the universal mechanism remains Rimuru's hypothesis. TC C06, member 30, char 5130.

**Engine shape, proposal:** a naming event records `participants`, `name`, `payer`, `energySource`, `cost`, `resultingLink`, `authorityTerms`, `bodyTransition`, and `observedResult`. Evolution edges separately record requirements, functions gained/lost, equipment fit and carried knowledge. A social name, official title, command relationship and body species remain different fields.

Default: earned recognition/bonding can open one route, with explicit payer and outcome. Dial: equal friends, imposed authority, gifted naming, refugee integration, pack change or ordinary growth without naming. Reasonably: retain citizen/apprentice play before and after evolution; naming is neither a compulsory loyalty meter nor proof every NPC becomes a puppet. E8–E10, E12–E14; X01/X06 apply to families and dependent young. A naming preview is not a committed expenditure or another free evolution after reload.

### TM4: Great Sage is an information and delegation system

STATED (Great Sage/Rimuru): early faculties include thought acceleration, analysis/appraisal, parallel calculation, chant cancellation and comprehension of encountered non-concealed phenomena. Knowledge is not omniscience; early Rimuru calls the skill reactive and without ego. TC C02–C03, member 3, chars 1632/41/687. SHOWN: combining Sage's parallel calculation with Predator speeds an actual production/analysis task. T1 member 3. SHOWN: Raphael's promotion uses Degenerate and a Harvest blessing after failed attempts. TC C15, member 73, char 5915. SHOWN: naming Ciel produces an independent Manas within Rimuru's soul, distinct from Raphael's shell, with no separate bodily person established by that scene. TC C40, member 197, char 10793.

**Engine shape, proposal:** `guideId`, host/controller IDs where the premise needs them, observed inputs, learned procedures, requested task, permitted control, active calculations, and reported uncertainty. Advice, routine automation, possessing a body and an autonomous person are different contracts. Ordinary UI queue helpers need none of the fictional ontology.

Default: a guide explains body functions and repeats authorized known procedures. Dial: reactive advisor, independent partner, shared controller, separately playable guide (SA17), or no in-fiction guide. Reasonably: let guide autonomy develop or remain narrow; don't force all starts to become Ciel. Existing ordinary controls stay usable without a spirit explanation. E2–E3, E8–E10, E14. A forecast can say “insufficient information”; it cannot reveal sealed truths or grant the host a power merely by describing it.

### TM5: Harvest Festival is a body/power event, not the prestige definition

STATED (Great Sage): Rimuru already has a demon-lord seed and needs 10,000 human souls for this awakening. TC C14, member 71, char 2202. SHOWN: the army encounter meets his threshold; awakening disrupts action and causes irresistible sleep; slime becomes Demon Slime, skills integrate and gifts propagate along soul genealogy. T1 members 72–74; TC C15–C16. Revival of Shion needs a surviving soul, repair and reconnection, not simply corpse healing. TC C16, member 74, char 7883.

SHOWN: later Gabiru completes while awake, Diablo completes externally at once, and Benimaru has a personal lineage duty. TC C31, members 168–169. TS F8–F10 rejects treating provisional EP, a universal three-day helpless sleep or one subordinate restriction as WN-wide laws. Political demon-lord office, seed and true awakening remain separate. TS R6 and T2 member 135.

**Engine shape, proposal:** `candidateId`, eligibility evidence, typed contributed resource and provenance, threshold rule for this route, event phase, actual interrupted jobs, resulting body/skill changes, affected links and aftermath. Keep permanent identity, public title and prestige/run continuation separate.

Default: ordinary evolution milestones can be the early loop; Harvest Festival is an optional source-specific later route. Dial: canon destructive acquisition, explicit AU resource substitution, another supported awakening, or no awakening. Reasonably: stronger starts may begin after a declared event without invented player-earned deeds. Automatic jobs should not silently enact an unselected mass-killing commitment. E6, E8–E9, E12–E14. If a transition changes a queued craft's anatomy, adapt or pause that job based on its actual method; no universal downtime penalty.

### TM6: Monster society makes power productive

SHOWN: rebuilding, teaching crafts, roads, medicine production and trade create work beyond fighting. Mixed patrols exchange monster strength for human tactics and recipes; independent makers and trainees reduce reliance on Rimuru. T1 members 22, 30, 46, 50, 53–57. SHOWN/STATED: staff/equipment determine potion throughput and distinct contracts/prices. T1 members 50/54; TC C12 verifies the WN twenty-medium-potion experiment rather than the wiki high-potion recipe.

SHOWN/STATED: the dungeon has supplied floors, settlement and tourist services. Floor 95 initially houses treants/dryads; revival depends on recognized subordinate or bounded visitor-bracelet permissions inside the supported domain. TC C21–C22, members 104–106/119. SHOWN/STATED: later wages, crews, schooling, transit and hobby chips exist, but do not verify one timeless Effort Point economy. T4 members 257–259, 265, 268, 281; TS F15/R12. Nation building is not evidence for a shared player consciousness.

**Engine shape, proposal:** reuse a facility/project with `siteId`, workers, duties, owner/access, recipe and finite stock, queued job, output custody, transport and agreement. Add civic standing, services and dated jurisdiction only where they affect actions. Territory reach belongs to a core/tree body if that is the premise; a slime citizen is still a mobile person within a nation.

Default: one monster craft/repair/delivery routine changes a real service or earns access. Dial: apprentice, merchant, guardian, founder or established ruler; peaceful production and martial work both stay available. Reasonably: society scales through actual NPC capacity and institutions, not mandatory brood control. E3–E5, E7–E11, E14–E15. Dungeon construction and visitor recovery are distinct jobs; revival isn't free outside because it exists inside.

### TM7: Humans observe bodies through institutions and evidence

STATED (Veldora): allegiances affect whether some demi-humans are categorized as monsters or humanity. T1 member 4. STATED (Nicolaus viewpoint): the Western Church treats monsters as doctrinal enemies. T1 member 6. SHOWN/STATED (Falmuth council): the safe Tempest road harms tariff/lodging income; conquest aims at monopoly, knowledge and monster labor, while other council members propose diplomacy/tourism. T1 member 68. SHOWN: cooperative humans do not endorse the assault, and residents' restraint affects defense. T1 member 70. Those scenes do not establish universal human hostility.

SHOWN: specialized sensors expose a disguise ordinary perception misses. T1 member 53. SHOWN/STATED: foreign observers mistake Beretta for a machine/weapon using measured output without knowing its mechanism. T4 member 284, F284b. SHOWN: later publicity, schooling and conflicting national histories influence understanding without giving every observer the private truth. T4 members 278–280.

**Engine shape, proposal:** `observerId`, perceived form, observed act/channel, identity claim, confidence, remembered encounter, local law/faction and resulting access. A guard might refuse entry for size, demand a guild sponsor, recognize an artisan by work or welcome an ally. These are different causes, not one “monster reputation” score.

Default: world/era-local reactions to observable conduct. Dial: open monster personhood, ambiguous recognition, disguise, hostility, established treaties or AU coexistence. Reasonably: speech, testimony, sponsorship and useful trade can alter access when the local institution responds to them; no automatic soul recognition or instant reconciliation. E2–E3, E7, E10, E15; X01 is the recognition seam. Known creation effects follow CR4 without disclosing a changed world's sealed identity.

Skills should distinguish controller knowledge, body coordination, anatomy and borrowed faculty. Cheats can change acquisition or execution where they actually supply a mechanism: absorption is not a universal replacement for learning, and naming is not a universal replacement for relationships. Structured starts compose body, world/era, memory, family, custody and opening actions (U03), rather than granting the whole canon protagonist kit. Strong, dependent, object, spirit and infant starts remain reasonable options, each with supported agency.

Realm travel needs explicit U12 carriers. A slime's analysis archive, a sword's shared skills, a spirit's anchor, a brood member and the institution left behind do not cross merely because the player changes screens. Tensura's low-magicule destination can disable some powers while other methods still function (T4 members283–284; TS R1/F5). Default: declare retained source/functions and required support. Dial: supported conversion, temporary suppression, alternate vessel, destination repair, or explicit AU compatibility. Do not erase working faculties solely to manufacture a penalty.

Brood extends NM11 when a link actually carries commands, sensation or skills. A pack name, familiar bond or nation does not establish a hive mind. X06 requires actual caregivers/parents/offspring; budding or cloning does not automatically continue the same controller. The first milestone need not implement every scale to prove one nonhuman life.
