## Reading the palette

All body and cross-cutting rows below are **INFERRED original content**. Suggested state names are conceptual read inputs, not claims that these fields already exist. Default: derive a line from admitted body functions, observable facts and an actual event. Dial: species/culture/material, warmth or strangeness of voice, seasonal intensity and detail preference. Reasonably per world: skip a row whose anatomy, means or premise is absent. No universal animal instinct, ghost chill, rust, wet penalty or discomfort tax.

**T = pure text:** no inventory, XP, regard, condition, time, route, material wear or command outcome changes. It may describe an existing settled outcome but cannot establish a new clue or promise secretly. **R = changes a result:** a selected command/event must settle the named change; the line follows that receipt. R is not implemented. Missing semantic state means omit or use a neutral line, never fabricate wetness, a witness, a part, a meal or a partner. Display controls remain usable regardless of fictional faculties.

Each kind has eight cheap moments, identified `MD-BKkind.number`. Cost means small authored text/read inputs; result rows and remembered preferences cost more than derived text. No row requires a new meter. Examples describe fictional variants, not a biological reference guide.
