## Authority, evidence and engine shorthand

The [engine owner](NONHUMAN_STARTS_ENGINE_REQUIREMENTS_2026-10-14.md) owns E1-E15. The [shared audit](../SHARED_FOUNDATIONS_AUDIT_2026-10-12.md) owns U01 person/body/controller, U03 coherent origins, U12 carriers, X01 recognition, X06 care and V06 exact retention. [Origins](ORIGINS_AND_WORLD_TIMELINES.md), [genetics/brood](../GENETICS_CONSTITUTIONS_AND_BROOD_PLAY.md), [identity/control](PLAYER_CHARACTER_IDENTITY_AND_CONTROL_2026-10-13.md) and [intro CR4, SA14-SA19](../INTRO_FLOW_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md) remain their owners. This report extends their palette without editing their requirements.

SHOWN means an existing reader dossier reports a demonstrated scene, with its original locator; it does not mean this lane reread the novel. STATED names the speaker or secondary source making the claim. INFERRED names the deduction. MEMORY is recollection outside the inspected evidence, with a retrieval lead rather than a verified citation. UNKNOWN means the inspected material does not resolve the question, not that canon forbids the possibility. A wiki assertion is STATED (wiki), even if the wiki calls its evidence demonstrated. Source confidence and implementation status are independent.

Every start below is a **Proposal**. **CF** means a new player scenario consistent with the bounded evidence, not a canon protagonist or an existing canonical player route. **AU** names a deliberate changed premise. **CF?** needs the named source check before being advertised as canon-faithful. No predecessor mastery or property arrives merely because the player selects its appearance. Existing-body, reincarnation and earned transformation versions remain reachable where their actual carriers support them.

**B = E1 + E2 + E14 + E15 for every start:** authoritative body, function queries, versioned persistence/migration and world admission. Tables list additional engine requirements. E3 actions; E4 equipment; E5 upkeep; E6 rest/defeat; E7 presence/reach; E8 controllers/bodies; E9 skills; E10 communication/recognition; E11 combat; E12 life stages; E13 transitions. These are dependencies proposed by the owner, not assertions that every piece is wholly absent.

Default for this palette: keep selected canon physiology and institutions, declare workable first actions and exact initial means. Dial: change source scope, continuity, embodiment, autonomy, social treatment or power through an explicit branch. Reasonably per world: keep alternative experiences reachable without pretending incompatible source rules are identical. Hunger never kills; meals help. No universal strange-body penalty. A canon life-threatening appetite is an explicit adaptation question, not a silent override of the food direction. No browser-closed progression is implied by an idle queue. Target for any future UI proof: rebuilt desktop/laptop at 1366×768; mobile optimization deferred.
