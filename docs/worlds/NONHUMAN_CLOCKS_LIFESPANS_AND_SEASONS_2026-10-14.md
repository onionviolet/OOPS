It does not let a mouse collect a year's wages while its owner experiences a minute. **Dial:** source-supported stasis, accelerated growth, dilation, reincarnation or independent branches. **Reasonably per world:** record the mechanism and who is exposed; a long-lived label alone supplies none of these powers.

### Three ways to move ahead

**INFERRED:** ordinary supported idle time executes existing chosen work; a reviewed chapter commits a finite authored state transition; a summarized era reports only the domains its adapter can resolve. These are selectable tools, not three currencies. No chapter should claim it simulated unrepresented ordinary days minute by minute.

A chapter preview identifies source state/revision, destination phase/date, affected body and people, world instances that remain frozen, actual work and stock budgets, learning recipients, expected known losses, unresolved contingencies and the next stop. Unknown hidden outcomes remain qualified or sealed. Freeze the selected seed/outcome and commit once; inspection, cancellation and import do not reroll a favorable century. If unsupported events intervene, shorten to a supported boundary or offer a separately described authored chapter. Do not pretend the existing minute scheduler has already simulated a century safely.

An original Village example: a sapling spends supported idle time establishing roots, then offers “next growing season.” Preview says the caretaker can tend one funded period, ordinary unscheduled Village history is outside this summary, and the cartographer's current invitation expires if local time crosses its deadline. The player can finish that invitation first, accept a missed event, or postpone the skip. CF1/CF8 supply calendar/weather seams; PLAY BK3 owns the proposed plant verbs. This is not an implemented Village walkthrough.

## 3. Seasons as useful idle phases

**INFERRED default:** a body phase has a useful continuing activity and a known exit/stop condition. **Dial:** seasonal, age-triggered, habitat-triggered, voluntary or source-compelled cycles. **Reasonably per world:** weather can influence a trigger but season names alone do not prove physiology. Migration and rut are suggestions/benefits unless a selected premise discloses compulsion (N8/TX7). No hunger death, including renamed fuel or winter starvation. Unsupported growth can pause while signaling, planning or other supported agency continues.

| Phase | Active choice / positive unattended progress | Future opted-in offline settlement and stop | Retained consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hibernation / dormancy | Choose refuge, caretaker and wake policy; supported recovery, bud preparation or memory organization continues | Integrate only declared rest-compatible work, bounded by refuge support, wake date and relevant outside decision. No generic sixfold sleep XP or automatic harvest while dormant. | Recovery/growth and actual supplies used; caretaker time and information remain theirs. |
| Molt / metamorphosis | Prepare shelter/materials, practice signaling while movement is limited; gradual repair or new-function preparation | Stop at new anatomy/gear reconciliation or an unapproved evolution choice; automatic completion is possible only if its exact destination was preselected. | One uniquely caused shed item under TX3; real compatible fittings and retained skill provenance. No reload molt farming. |
| Migration | Choose route/companions and known stopping sites; travel familiarity or suitable escort work progresses | Advance segments only with actual carriers/habitat/support, pause before unknown junctions, separation or new obligations. No double-credit simultaneous stationary labor. | Changed actual location, observed route and contacts; transit is not a teleport or automatic discovery of the whole region. |
| Bloom / fruiting | Choose support/output allocation or cooperative tending; produce a finite supported batch | Seasonal window, light/water/support, storage and demand cap eligible production. No annual output for every skipped year without a modeled/prescribed causal batch. | Flower/fruit/sap stock belongs to its actual owner; pollination or a seed does not automatically create a controlled child. |
| Rut / reproductive season | Optional courtship, territorial signaling, nest preparation or a non-reproductive alternative | Continue pre-agreed signals/preparation only; pause at a new partner decision, conception/offspring commitment or contested encounter unless selected terms resolve it. | Actual bilateral encounter/choice and care plan; no involuntary partner consent, unconditional bond XP or automatic heirs. |

**SHOWN (Village source):** `src/data/locations.js:1930-1941` fieldwork has a 120-minute period, hours 06–20 and Spring/Summer/Autumn availability; `:1955-1962` swimming excludes Winter. **INFERRED:** these are useful existing demonstrations that work availability changes with calendar context. A new winter rest/root-growth routine should show its own output and next milestone when fieldwork closes; it must not silently receive fieldwork wages. CF8's weather cycle optimization cannot bypass a bloom's one-time stock, a molt's gear change or a migration's location change.

## 4. Mixed clocks in a party or family

**INFERRED default:** everybody in a shared exposed interval experiences its actual duration under their own body policy. The shortest relevant participant boundary stops an unreviewed skip. **Dial:** continue through disclosed expected endings using an explicit chapter policy, leave a member in source-supported stasis, appoint another caretaker, retire a seat or keep open mortality. **Reasonably per world:** freeze is an instance absence policy or a sourced effect, not a free pause applied only to inconvenient friends while extracting their labor.

| Case | Decision before the skip | Grief, memory and succession afterward |
|---|---|---|
| Human owner outlives dog | Preview known life-stage/support boundaries without claiming an exact date of death when the policy does not establish one. Offer ordinary time, care, a shorter chapter or a selected ending chapter. | Keep the dog's named shared deeds, home and objects. Mourning can be conversation, memorial, caring for another animal or quiet routine. A new dog is a new person with its own bond; it does not inherit affection numerically. |
| Elf outlives human friends | Pin promises and known invitations; identify which friends are actually exposed to a decade chapter, and which absent instances freeze. Stop before an unselected consequential meeting/loss. | Distinguish the elf's lived memory, letters, hearsay and public archive. Friends' descendants can know a story without having the same friendship. Old relationships remain historical rather than being erased or decayed into hostility. |
| Short-lived member with long-lived partner | Pick useful paired segments and separate jobs. A switch of control seat changes neither partner's biological cursor nor known commitments. | Surviving partner can become a caretaker, memorial witness or playable seat. Generational handover releases the old member's work and seeks fresh authority for the successor. |
| Tree beside successive Village caretakers | Preview care capacity, land/custody terms and next appointment; chapter does not conscript every descendant into the old contract. | Tree may remember the first caretaker while the new one learns from records. One named memory per meaningful era can remain retrievable without storing daily greetings forever. |
| Ghost or construct with mortal family | Separate no-senescence body from expiring manifestations/leases. Retain that person's role without assigning ownership of the family's estate. | Select executor, item holder and next relationship separately. An inherited sentient blade can retain its own mind/history while its new holder forms a new bond. |

**INFERRED grief policy:** make mourning an available life activity with causal remembrance, support and optional reflection, not a universal stat debuff or compulsory waiting period. Dial: a selected world/premise can have specific grief effects or a sourced return route. Reasonably: no requirement to “get over” a person to resume play; repair/revival/copying follows TX9, NX3 and PB4. A returned friend need not erase the history of loss.

A durable remembrance references actor/body identity, cause/date domain, observed or reported provenance, shared milestones, a small selected keepsake set and the actual successor/estate receipt. Rolling chatter can remain bounded, but retained named people cannot exist only in CF10's last 32 messages. A century summary can aggregate anonymous cohorts while preserving named friends and exceptional causes. Private motives and sealed truths stay out of public memorial text.

## 5. Engine shape and save safety

This is an **INFERRED extension of ENGINE_SPEC's body schema**, not a second clock service or final serialized format. Fields can be split across existing person/body/world owners rather than duplicating their authoritative values.

| Owner / proposed field group | Meaning and constraints |
|---|---|

| `ageAnchor` | Biological age at an actual common-clock anchor, with units, world mapping ref and provenance. Store accumulated eligible exposure plus fractional residue if required. Existing enrollment bornAt is not automatically a historical birth date. |

### Settlement through one route

**INFERRED sequence:** validate the current body/world/job terms and selected elapsed policy; quote a finite interval against actual remaining means; stage common-clock advancement with body/phase/dependency boundaries; integrate eligible portions and retain fractional progress; validate the whole result; durably publish costs, stock, clocks, memories and once-only receipts together. Failed publication retains the previous durable state and the unsettled request. CF9 is a useful staging precedent, but cannot be treated as an already generalized implementation.

Online and opted-in offline work use equal eligible game time and costs (NP2/NP3, PG7). Wall time is input to a selected mapping, not age on its own. Backwards/invalid wall timestamps give no credit; large forward jumps hit declared caps and review. Paused, unsupported and discarded windows earn nothing and must not reappear on resume. Two linked jobs share the same exposed interval, each using its real executor and means; they do not advance world time twice. Switching bodies or tabs cannot restart a lifetime, cycle or budget.

Split at season changes, stage changes, source expiry, actual work completion, support loss, relevant mortal endings and player decisions. Equal-time boundaries need deterministic ordering: CF6 currently completes due jobs before lifespan cleanup. Preserve that ordering for old content; explicitly select any new species/content exception and test it. If a boundary or work budget prevents full consumption, settle only supported partial work under its declared contract, or reject the staged request atomically. Do not grant a full output after consuming only part of its time. CF9's exact-full-consumption rule remains suitable for that finite batch.

For centuries, closed-form cycles are safe only over homogeneous supported intervals with no material unresolved boundary. Use bounded cohort/era summaries where authored rather than looping over every minute, every offspring and every NPC pair. Persist resumable work position if a bounded chapter requires chunks; previews/chunks must not each mint a new year of profit. Cost ceilings and broader queue scheduling stay OT13/OT15 owners' scope.

### Compatibility and verification contract

**INFERRED default:** migrate missing clock fields to the old save's actual behavior, retain chronicle opt-in mortality, reedling/Tern/home-body state and existing elapsed packing receipts; no retroactive offline credit or loss. **Dial:** pin older content rules or offer a disclosed migration decision for incompatible authored clocks. **Reasonably:** unknown/future clock profiles suspend affected progression with an explanation, preserving the saved person and stock. Do not enroll services merely to install clock structure (ENGINE_SPEC B21), or silently reinterpret an adult's enrollment stamp as birth.

Fresh saves and imports validate finite units/ranges, profile revisions, interval ordering, body references, unique receipts, stage consistency, no future cursor beyond committed exposure, and output/escrow conservation. No subtraction of whole years from an integer age field per tick. Preserve fractional maturation/passive XP where tiny positive progress applies under the [food owner](../HUNGER_AND_BODILY_NEEDS_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md), while bodies exempt from nourishment remain exempt.

**Future gates, not checks run:** deterministic equal-eligible-time online/offline/chunk results; no duplicate costs/output across reload/repeated import; exact season/molt/death boundary ordering; interrupted save leaves prior state; invalid/backwards/over-cap time; same-time paired work without double aging; parked-body return; old mortal/open saves; a short life with useful choices; centuries summarized without log loss or unbounded execution. Then rebuild and exercise a fresh desktop origin at **1366×768**, with 1920×1080 follow-up where the larger layout is used, and save/reload/export/literal-file import for changed persistence. Show focused body, world date, known life stage/phase, routine progress and stop reason beside Local actions. Mobile deferred. No speculative date-of-death meter or hidden lifespan truth exposed without an observation rule. Human pacing/feel acceptance remains separate.
