This lane read analytical dossiers and selected claim records, not books. A SHOWN label does not claim fresh primary verification. Original design rows are marked **ORIGINAL**, including adaptations suggested by source patterns. No source prose or quotations are reproduced. No sealed files were opened.

## 1. What each source shows about villain or villain-adjacent starts

VS1 splits **role**, **reputation**, **history** and **intent**. A source character's demonstrated intent is evidence about that character, not an instruction to the YAIRPG player. A publicly feared office may grant useful authority; a villain organization can also contain a captive, ordinary employee or unwilling dependent.

The direct answer to “do they just not exist?” depends on the operation. Ordinary unplayed seats exist as people under OS12; a copied person or prior embodied character needs their actual fate preserved. A projection can be only a vessel, a loop can recreate templates without erasing source people, and a counterfactual story need not be a reachable universe. Neither regression nor identical memory alone proves a surviving alternate self. Keep that uncertainty in source research; choose a clear operation for an original playable premise.
