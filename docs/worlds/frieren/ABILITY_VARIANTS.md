# Frieren ability variants proposal: different means, comparable outcomes

Every comparison below is a **proposal** for authoring and review. It does not claim these abilities are interchangeable in canon. Source pointers use page title and revision; detail is in [SOURCE_LEDGER](SOURCE_LEDGER.md).

## 1. Learn or acquire a usable spell

| Route | Means and condition | Cost / failure | Source anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mentor training | A qualified teacher, time, practice and compatible learner | Time, teacher access; failure can expose misunderstanding | "Flamme", rev. 14594; "Fern", rev. 14749 |
| Grimoire study | Actual grimoire, language/access and study time | Content may be incomplete or inapplicable; ownership is not execution. The wiki estimates around half of grimoires use ancient Elvish; this is not a universal access gate. | "Spells", rev. 14938; "Heiter’s House", rev. 7845 |
| Serie transfer | Eligible first-class mage privilege or authored transfer; Fearvelia grimoire | Serie forgets the spell transferred; no free duplication | "Serie", rev. 14764; "First Class Mage Exam", rev. 14828 |
| Observation and research | Observe the target phenomenon and formulate an applicable spell | Search can take months; observation does not guarantee generalization | "Turk Region", rev. 14484 |
| Public technical diffusion | Analyze repeated use and publish countermeasures | Others gain the counter too; old reputation may outlive old effectiveness | "Beyond Journey’s End Arc", rev. 14281; "Timeline", rev. 14231 |

## 2. Resolve a hostile magical effect

| Means | What it can address | Key restriction |
|---|---|---|
| Defensive magic | Eligible offensive magic | Not a universal answer to curses; exact spell matchup matters ("Beyond Journey’s End Arc", rev. 14281; "Spells", rev. 14938). |
| Curse-specific Goddess magic | Wiki-described curse cases | Exact curse class and dispelling requirements are inconsistent or underdescribed ("Spells", rev. 14938; "Laub Hills", rev. 7232). |
| Mana concealment | An observer relying on mana detection | Does not hide ordinary physical evidence; some observers detect instability ("Frieren", rev. 14471; "Fern", rev. 14749). |
| Physical force | Some barriers and material objects | Only exceptional force is reported to damage Böse’s barrier; not a general anti-magic property ("Böse", rev. 14765). |
| Will resistance | Auserlese control | Partial defiance/release is described, but threshold and duration are unknown ("Aura", rev. 14618). |

## 3. Obtain safe access through an obstacle

**Proposal:** compare preparation, a compatible spell, a qualified person, a tool, a formal permit and a route around. Do not make “magic” one universal key. For example, exam barrier exit is disqualification, while a northern transit barrier has official passage in the early arc synopsis; different barrier purposes need distinct rules ("First Class Mage Exam", rev. 14828; "Beyond Journey’s End Arc", rev. 14281).

## Copying, transfer and imitation

**Proposal:** keep these verbs separate. Learning by study creates personal knowledge; reading a Fearvelia grimoire is a specific instant transfer; Übel’s spell-copying is an empathic condition with its own bounds, not a general learner function ("Serie", rev. 14764; "Übel", rev. 14742). Imitating movement or technique does not automatically copy a magical source. Every authored variant must specify user eligibility, target, source, condition, actual cost, failure behavior and counter.

## Mastery and subprogression (proposal)

Track exposure, understanding, practice and reliable execution as separate states. More mana can improve available scale or endurance only where the authored spell supports it. A research record may increase counter knowledge without increasing the researcher’s output. An ability’s displayed name or rank does not prove mastery. Unknown source limits stay unquantified.
