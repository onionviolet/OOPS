# Frieren campaign proposal: routes across a changing age

All local stories, mechanics, careers, consequences, quantities and start profiles below are **proposals**, not canon or accepted content. Source facts and the 2023-12-12 snapshot boundary are in [SOURCE_LEDGER](SOURCE_LEDGER.md).

## Snapshot and campaign promise

**Proposal:** maintain a bounded manga-referenced continuity. Keep the Hero Party’s ten-year quest, its later reunion fifty years after victory, and Frieren’s later travel as source anchors, while never assuming the player has canon protagonists’ memories or destiny ("Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End (Manga)", rev. 13288; "Timeline", rev. 14231). The wiki timeline is rough, contains fan-made era names and explicitly marks some events unordered or spin-off-only.

**Proposal:** campaign identity is a road network of seasonal travel, small exchanges, study, dangerous encounters and the slow change of public knowledge. A route should remain useful after the immediate reward because it changes what the character can recognize, who will teach them, or which local work becomes possible.

## Progression careers (proposals)

1. **Field mage:** acquire spells through teaching, grimoire, research or exam privilege; practice execution; learn counters from failures and public adaptation. Personal spell ownership and mana are separate.
2. **Priestly support:** study scriptures and Goddess magic; develop healing, survival support and curse response; keep the theological source claims distinct from verified world mechanics ("Spells", rev. 14938; "Heiter", rev. 14835).
3. **Warrior / protector:** train movement, weapon handling, pain tolerance, timing and protection of companions. Do not convert a named technique into a universal stat rank ("Stark", rev. 14497; "Eisen", rev. 14925).
4. **Researcher / archivist:** compare spell behavior, record countermeasure diffusion, preserve grimoires and separate observation from interpretation ("Timeline", rev. 14231; "Serie", rev. 14764).
5. **Guide / naturalist:** read habitat, weather, fauna and plant cues, prepare supplies, and negotiate local knowledge exchanges ("Turk Region", rev. 14484; "Ecology", rev. 14559).

## Three worked career sketches (proposals)

### Early: village guide

The guide has local routes, a pack, basic tools and a herbalist contact, but no combat spell. They can map a safe approach to a specimen, gather forage or earn coin escorting a trader. A dangerous creature can end the expedition; help or retreat costs time and trust. The payoff is route knowledge and a reusable contact, not invented mage rank.

### Developed: apprentice mage

The mage has one taught utility spell, a grimoire lead and a mentor. They can practice, observe a local effect, or join an Association preparation group. Mana spending competes with safe defense and travel; spell interpretation can be wrong. Exam access is gated by the cited qualification and proctor rules, not a hidden XP threshold.

### Mature: field researcher

The researcher has a small catalogue of tested spells, field records, trusted contacts and an institutional credential. They can investigate a changed defense, teach a countermeasure, or preserve a unique source. Public dissemination can make yesterday’s rare defense more common. Powerful foes remain fixed and may outmatch them.

## Escalating local arcs (proposals)

1. **The missing flower:** observe habitat, trade labor for local vegetation knowledge, and choose whether to keep the find, share it or restore a public memorial. Source anchor: "Turk Region", rev. 14484.
2. **The late cure:** identify a venom exposure and find qualified help while early treatment remains possible; no cure recipe is authored as canon. Source anchor: "Alt Woods", rev. 14478.
3. **A spell changes hands:** a mentor may teach, lend a grimoire, or use a transfer method with a permanent personal cost. The source’s Serie transfer makes the giver forget the transferred spell ("Serie", rev. 14764); local terms are invented proposals.
4. **The examination route:** a party must coordinate around explicit time, membership and escape rules; proctor-specific rules are not generalized beyond the snapshot’s described exam ("First Class Mage Exam", rev. 14828).
5. **A defense becomes ordinary:** a researched offensive spell gains countermeasures through public learning; encounter tactics and future threat expectations change without retroactive deletion of old records ("Beyond Journey’s End Arc", rev. 14281; "Timeline", rev. 14231).

## Temporal and branch policy (proposals)

Use distinct authored snapshots for historical starts; do not rewind an existing instance. For alternate histories, label the divergence as invention. Do not use the timeline’s fan-made era labels as canon names. Keep occurrence, revelation, actor belief and player knowledge separate. Events after the snapshot are gaps, not presumed continuations.

## Visit, return and failure (proposals)

Before any later travel runtime, define body, known methods, mana/resources, cargo, companions, obligations, return route and absent-world clock. Default proposal is freeze while absent. Defeat, injury, failed commission, capture, death and return interruption are distinct outcomes; no lethal mode or irreversible offline loss is selected here. A fixed demon or monster can overwhelm an underprepared traveler; a compatible imported means can matter only if its actual function applies.
