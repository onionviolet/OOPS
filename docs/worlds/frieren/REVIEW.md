# Frieren world-pack independent review (2026-10-14)

**Verdict: usable with fixes.** The pack is usable as a source-bounded design proposal, not as accepted canon or playable content. The checked historical and ability claims generally match the retained 2023-12-12 wiki payloads. The engine-rule table promotes one useful design separation into a source law, and it omits two concrete mechanics that would affect resource and detection design. No pack file was changed for this review.

## Method and evidence key

I read `AGENTS.md`, `PROJECT.md`, the source deep-dive playbook, both wiki-lane spot-checks, the nine pack files, the Frieren worker notes and ledger, and the manifest `docs/research/power-systems/wiki-digests/full-dumps/frieren_SOURCES.json`. Each locator below is a page title, revision and zero-based character offset in that manifest row's decompressed private `read_file` **text**. The manifest resolves to `~/.codex/research/wikis/frieren.fandom.com/dump/full-reading/cited-pages/`. I opened the cited payloads themselves, not only the worker paraphrases; SHA-256 of the `text` matched each checked row's `read_sha256` (28 distinct pages). “Pass” means the dated secondary wiki payload supports the bounded report, not that manga primary text was verified. “Qualified” identifies a source/design boundary needing clearer wording.

## Fixes needed, in priority order

1. **F1: Keep engine inference out of the source-law column.** In `SYNTHESIS.md:23`, replace “knowledge and execution are separate” as a sourced law with the two facts the payload gives: mana use and possible conferral/recording. Keep separate knowledge/execution state in the proposed engine shape. Likewise mark `SYNTHESIS.md:26,30` as case-derived design choices rather than universal research or travel laws. This matters before a spell schema is treated as canon complete.
2. **F2: Add the source's defensive coverage cost and tactical narrowing.** The arc note and payload say broad defensive coverage rapidly drains Fern's mana and that covering the threatened area at impact is the response. `SYNTHESIS.md:19–30` and `SOURCE_LEDGER.md:31–39` currently lack that concrete counterweight. Preserve it as a described training case, not a universal numeric cost: “Beyond Journey's End Arc” r14281 @22800–23500; worker `NOTES.jsonl` row 24, “Training / defense.”
3. **F3: Preserve the second detection route and Heiter's stated duration.** `SOURCE_LEDGER.md:33,39` reports Heiter's mana sensing and priest support but omits his circle spell that prevents mana detection, distinct from trained concealment. Its support summary should also carry the stated **up-to-two-month** duration without extrapolating a general priest spell duration. “Heiter” r14835, Abilities @2530–2920; worker `NOTES.jsonl` row 5, “Survival spell” and “Stealth spell.”

## Boundaries and other supported material

- **B1: Proposals and continuity.** Starts, local jobs, costs, routes, data shapes and alternate branches are labelled proposals. `CAMPAIGN.md:53–55` separates historical snapshots and AU divergence; `GM_PROMPT.md:12` separates manga, anime, tie-ins, anthologies and fan chronology. I found no film, game or parallel-world claim imported as manga law. This is a separation check, not a review of those media. The ecology exception and the timeline's spin-off markers were confirmed in “Ecology” r14559 @1150–1410 and “Timeline” r14231 @170–1160.
- **B2: Sealed secrets.** No changed or randomized truth is exposed in these nine pack files. `WORLD_BRIEF.md:37` states the opaque-slot and discovery-route rule; no secret payload appears in the pack. Public franchise history is treated as public. This satisfies `AGENTS.md` for this document-only proposal.
- **B3: Prose and quote limit.** I inspected the tracked pack and compared all nine Markdown files against 16-word token sequences from the retained Frieren payloads: zero matches. I found no raw source prose or quotation longer than 15 words. The private page text remains outside the pack.
- **B4: Design-changing gaps.** `SYNTHESIS.md:69–76` honestly lists post-snapshot works, missing manga-primary verification, curse scope, spell costs, time/afterlife and continuity choice. The playbook's M3 fan survey was not run and is disclosed at `SYNTHESIS.md:54`; it is not needed to use this pack as bounded research. The pack is not implementation-ready and makes no gameplay claim.
- **B5: Further material the notes support.** The worker's Heiter row 5 and arc row 24 support F2–F3. The “Spells” row 103 also reports that roughly half of grimoires use ancient Elvish (“Spells” r14938 @3310–3410), a useful constraint for the proposed grimoire/language route in `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md:10`; it should remain a wiki-reported estimate, not a universal access gate.
