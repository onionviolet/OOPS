# Frieren interaction proposals: knowledge, time and unequal means

All authored people, dialogue, outcomes and interaction rules below are **proposals**. Source characters are not assigned new motives beyond cited wiki statements. No sealed truth is included.

## People and means

**Proposal:** each actor can act only through available body, skills, tools, access, information, time and relationships. Distinguish what happened, what an actor observed, what they believe, and what the player’s Notebook records. A mage can misread hidden mana; a report does not grant the receiver the observer’s skill.

| Actor role | Typical means (proposal) | Blind spot / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Local herbalist | Habitat knowledge, samples, patient observation | Cannot identify an unobserved species by magic unless separately authored |
| Apprentice mage | One or more taught spells, mentor access, practice | Can misjudge counters or lack execution under pressure |
| Veteran mage | Broad study, detection, institutional access | May still fail to read concealment or a new interaction |
| Priest | Scripture and Goddess magic where eligible | Do not assume every priest has every spell |
| Warrior | Physical protection, weapon technique and field judgment | Does not gain magical targeting or mana reserves by role label |
| Association proctor | Local exam authority and chosen stage rules | Authority is stage- and institution-bound |
| Demon opponent | Individual magic and mana means | Do not assign every demon identical tactics or knowledge |

## Reaction matrix (proposals)

| Situation | Player can know | Other actor can know | Response options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mana concealment | Own signal and any observed failed/successful reads | Only what their perception and evidence allow | Reveal, maintain concealment, bait a read or withdraw |
| Research exchange | Stated terms and observed work | What the local collaborator has seen or been told | Share result, keep it, trade labor or decline |
| Spell teaching | Teacher’s offer and declared price | Learner’s demonstrated practice, not hidden future mastery | Accept, negotiate, refuse or request a demonstration |
| Dangerous travel | Visible terrain, known forecast and personal supplies | Local guide’s route knowledge | Pay for guide, prepare, wait, take a risk or turn back |
| Exam | Published/current stage rules | Proctor may know more about authored hazards | Coordinate, conserve resources, withdraw under stated penalty |
| Old rumor | Source and confidence of report | Witness knowledge and bias | Verify, repeat with caveat, or withhold |

## Worked interaction sketches (proposals)

### The specimen and the memorial

A proposed village herbalist offers local vegetation knowledge for help restoring a neglected memorial. The apprentice can accept the work, search independently or earn supplies elsewhere. A real specimen unlocks only the specific observation-led study route. The herbalist receives credit only for what they observed or were told.

### The concealed reading

A proposed examiner’s aide reports that the apprentice’s mana looked stable. A witness with better perception disputes the reading. The action log preserves both observations and their source; it does not declare the apprentice’s true level to every actor.

### The transfer offer

A proposed senior mage offers a uniquely useful spell through an authored transfer method. The terms explicitly state what the giver loses. The learner can refuse without hidden debt. If accepted, the saved state changes only after the transfer and record publication succeed.

### The storm shelter

A proposed guide asks travelers to share a scarce shelter. The party can pay, help repair the roof or continue into the storm. NPC choices use their actual supplies, forecasts and obligations. The game does not promise every branch survives.

### The public countermeasure

A proposed research group publishes a defense after repeated observations of an offensive spell. Local actors gain it only if the record reaches them. The original mage can adapt, conceal technique or accept that an old advantage has spread.

## Relationship and time model (proposal)

Trust, instruction, shared work and witnessed reliability are distinct relationship facts; none is a universal affinity score. Long-lived and short-lived actors experience calendar spans differently only where a source supports it. Actor-specific aging, deadlines, absent-world clocks and historical divergence remain open runtime/design requirements.
