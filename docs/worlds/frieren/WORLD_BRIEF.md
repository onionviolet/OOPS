## Proposed world identity

**Proposal:** `fan.frieren`, with a source-bounded manga-referenced continuity and a post-Demon-King travel era as one candidate start. The world’s distinctive loop is not “gain mana, defeat stronger enemy”: observe, research, learn, prepare, travel, and see how knowledge and relationships age across long spans. Human public knowledge can improve a spell’s defense over generations; a personal spell can remain idiosyncratic; a monster, demon, priest and mage need not obey interchangeable rules.

The source’s opening premise ties an elf mage’s long life to a belated desire to understand short-lived companions. The Wiki overview says the Hero Party defeated the Demon King, later disbanded, and Frieren returned to the capital fifty years later ("Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End (Manga)", rev. 13288). **Proposal:** let the player begin with a modest, concrete job or journey and discover the larger temporal context through records, people and repeat visits. Do not require the player to be Frieren or inherit her history.

## Candidate experience

**Proposal:** begin as a local apprentice, traveling craft worker, junior mage, priestly aide, village guide, or non-mage companion in a compatible authored snapshot. A candidate is eligible only if body, means, language, known facts, obligations and first usable action are specified. These are candidates, not a selector or accepted start roster. Keep canon characters as bounded witnesses and possible contacts, never as a required protagonist party.

**Proposal:** first slice: a local commission to recover a rare plant specimen and restore a neglected memorial garden. The player can accept a teacher’s exchange, investigate habitat and local animal traces, or leave for ordinary paid work; spell formation requires actual observation if using the documented Blue-Moon Weed case. The commission is finite and its useful outcome is knowledge, a relationship and a living place, not a generic XP bar. Source inspiration: "Turk Region", rev. 14484.

## Boundaries

- **Proposal:** one source-bounded realm and one progression layer for any later first implementation; campaign scale in these documents does not expand that milestone.
- **Proposal:** avoid universal mana ranks, guaranteed spell copying, generic demon moralization, or automatic protagonist events.
- **Proposal:** timeline labels are authored source distinctions only when supported; the wiki explicitly calls most era labels fan-made and some events unordered ("Timeline", rev. 14231).
- **Proposal:** any altered or randomized truth is a sealed slot with an opaque ID and discovery route only. Public franchise twists remain public. This pack introduces no sealed truth payload.
- **Proposal:** no raw prose, quotes over 15 words, source images or asset extraction in tracked files.
