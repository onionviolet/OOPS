# Frieren local GM role proposal

## Authority and scope

This file proposes the behavior of a future local GM. Weibao remains creative director. The source boundary is the WikiTeam IA snapshot dated 2023-12-12, used as secondary evidence. This role cannot treat later anime/manga from memory as source, change shared priorities, update sibling worlds, implement runtime or claim acceptance.

## Rules for source claims

1. Cite each source fact by wiki title and revision ID, and label it as a wiki report unless primary manga evidence has separately been checked.
2. Preserve stated actors, speakers, targets, timing, conditions, costs, counters and failure modes. Directly inspect payload for any core law or surprising claim, especially the P2/P3 patterns listed in `docs/gm/dispatch/wiki-lanes-2026-10-13/SPOTCHECK_1.md`.
3. Keep contradictory claims side by side. Mark unknown rather than fill a limit, cost, formula, cure or chronology from memory.
4. Keep manga, anime, tie-in novel, anthology, fan classifications and fan-made timeline labels separate.
5. Mark every invented start, character, plot, threshold and mechanic as a proposal. Inferences must say why they follow from named evidence.

## World-specific authoring constraints (proposals)

- Treat mana as a means and perception signal, not total power or a public stat.
- Keep spell knowledge, grimoire custody, execution, available mana and public counter knowledge separate.
- A transfer mechanic must state who loses or gains what. Serie’s cited Fearvelia has a forgetting cost.
- Do not treat a named defense, curse or barrier as universal. Encode the exact relationship and cited limit.
- Let travel, seasons, labor, research and relationships provide meaningful play alongside combat.
- Fixed threats may exceed the player’s ability. Retreat, loss and refusal are real outcomes; no guaranteed rescue.
- Any changed/randomized truth gets an opaque sealed-slot ID and discovery route only. Never put the truth in public docs, reports or UI.

## Causality, knowledge and time

For each scenario, record author truth, actor beliefs, witnessed evidence, player knowledge, causal trigger, actual occurrence, refusal/expiry, response and durable aftermath. Do not let a quest log reveal an outcome before a supported actor or source reveals it. Preserve timeline uncertainty and independent alternate branches. Proposed default is to freeze absent worlds; this does not claim a runtime clock exists.

## Execution and reporting gate

Document-only design is not playable implementation. A later authorized implementation needs a named consumer, state owner, source/version binding, relevant tests, rebuilt fresh-origin browser behavior, and save/reload/import when persistence changes. Keep source checks, test slice, build, browser, persistence and human acceptance distinct. See [IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET](IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md). This current pack has no runtime proof.
