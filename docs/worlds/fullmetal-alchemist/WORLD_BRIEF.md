# Fullmetal Alchemist: world brief

Status: proposal. Source scope is revision-backed FMA wiki synthesis, not a completed primary manga/anime audit. Read the [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md) before using any mechanic. This world pack follows the canon-first priority in [USER_VISION](../../USER_VISION.md#world-depth-and-canon-first-world-priority-2026-10-13); that priority sets order, not implementation scope.

## Identity and boundaries

| Status | Field | Proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal | World | Fullmetal Alchemist: industrial-era states, alchemical craft, military institutions, prosthetic engineering, and competing accounts of life and exchange. |
| Proposal | Primary continuity options | Manga, Brotherhood, and 2003 anime are independent selectable branches. The 2003 sequel film stays attached only to its branch. |
| Proposal | Design promise | Let learned method, actual material, body condition, tools, information, authority and moral choice combine into useful outcomes. |
| Proposal | First milestone | One realm, one prestige layer; build one small alchemy-and-investigation slice after source-primary verification. No whole-series runtime claim. |
| Proposal | Audience knowledge | Public franchise twists may be presented. A changed or randomized truth uses an opaque sealed slot ID and discovery route; its truth stays in the sealed owner. |

## Source-grounded rules to preserve

| Status | Rule | Evidence anchor | Proposed world behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Transmutation is a learned, staged operation. | Alchemy r78024; Scar r77728. | Knowledge, available substance, array/tool, execution and result are separate state. A failed or partial operation can be legible without becoming arbitrary. |
| Proposal | Equivalent exchange tracks matter/composition and energy, not a universal coin-price meter. | Alchemy r78024; Philosopher's Stone r78123. | Track actual matter, energy, stored source, bodily cost or external payer that a selected continuity establishes. Unknown payer remains unknown; Stone use depletes its reserve. |
| Proposal | Rebound is a dangerous result of imbalance, not a harmless fizzled action. | Alchemy r78024, Rebound. | A later system may model a source-qualified risk band; exact thresholds stay unknown until primary review. |
| Proposal | Specialization comes from method, training, tools and source access. | Roy Mustang r77805; State Military r76667; Amestris r77354. | Do not assign elemental birth classes or universal ranks. State certification grants institutional permissions, not automatic power. |
| Proposal | Taboo and technical limitation differ. | Taboo r62677; Edward Elric r78039. | Human transmutation and other taboo cases need source-specific consequences; ordinary failed craft is not automatically the same metaphysical event. |
| Proposal | Soul carriers and bodies have distinct properties. | Alphonse Elric r77623; Edward Elric r78039. | If used, record identity, carrier, bond, senses, bodily support and vulnerability separately. No generic immortality flag. |
| Proposal | Alkahestry is a distinct knowledge tradition. | Dragon's Pulse r77465; May Chang r77825. | Model sensing and prepared transfer as a separate tradition. May's soul-origin explanation is her theory; manga/2009 Amestrian energy-source claims conflict between Alchemy and Father, while the 2003 account remains separate. |
| Proposal | In manga/2009, Father's network can halt Amestrian alchemy while alkahestry and Scar's alkahestry-based array remain effective. | Father r78035; Scar r77728. | Keep branch-specific; do not generalize to 2003. |
| Proposal | Political violence has causal aftermath. | Ishval r76903; State Military r76667; Amestris r77354. | Military authority, occupation, public belief, harmed communities, surviving evidence and personal responsibility are distinct world state. |

## Proposed entry menu

| Status | Start candidate | Native activity and means | Continuity constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Rush Valley apprentice | Repair automail, source parts, negotiate workshop time, learn from an engineer. | Manga/Brotherhood or explicitly authored adaptation; no imported 2003 event. |
| Proposal | East City trainee | Study material theory, perform safe bench trials, pursue State certification or refuse military service. | Institutional details are locally versioned; no instant protagonist rank. |
| Proposal | Xingese traveling practitioner | Read local life-energy signs, use prepared tools, treat an injury within known limits and build access through relationships. | Alkahestry source evidence stays attributed; May's explanation is not settled truth. |
| Proposal | Ishvalan survivor or aid worker | Secure water/medicine, protect testimony, choose between concealment and public record. | War timeline and culpability follow the selected continuity; danger is explicit where known. |
| Proposal | 2003 Central investigator | Track restricted research and the brothers' early case trail. | This is 2003-specific and may connect to its separate Gate/world-crossing route. |
| Proposal | Random suspenseful entry | Choose among eligible authored snapshots with usable first actions and known body/resources. | Do not require a Frontier run; commit the chosen profile and preserve it on reload/import. |

All entry candidates above are designs, not sourced characters or registered starts. Original local actors and situations need source-bounded authorship review.

## Truth, belief and discovery

| Status | Knowledge layer | Proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Public facts | State institutions, common craft, open consequences and public events can be shown. |
| Proposal | Actor belief | A character may have a theory about souls, energy or a conspiracy. Keep the speaker and evidence attached. |
| Proposal | Author truth | Canon branch truth follows that branch's source. If the world changes or randomizes it, assign an opaque sealed slot and route. |
| Proposal | Player discovery | Research, witnesses, artifacts and experiments reveal only what the actor could establish; failed tests do not certify the theory. |
