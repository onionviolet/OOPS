# Fullmetal Alchemist interaction proposals

Status: every case is a proposal. These are authored test journeys for later design review, not implemented game behavior. Use fixed threats and actual means. Do not create a second owner for shared time, inventory, knowledge or relationships.

| Status | Case | Start and state | Action alternatives | Observable consequence proposal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Partial array at the bench | Apprentice has a known sample, one safe bench period and a copied partial array. | Identify composition first; attempt decomposition; ask a qualified teacher; stop and preserve the sample. | Knowledge confidence, sample condition, bench access and learning source update separately. A partial effect cannot secretly do the unlearned stage. |
| Proposal | Stone-funded emergency | Care team has one documented Stone reserve and a patient with a treatable injury. | Use the reserve with informed knowledge of its source; use ordinary care; decline the operation. | Actual reserve consumption and payer are recorded. A life saved does not erase the source cost. |
| Proposal | Automail repair under deadline | Engineer has a damaged limb, compatible parts and a fixed repair queue. | Pay, barter parts, take a supervised repair, or defer and use a mobility aid. | Part ownership, work time, fit, function and queue change. Account for costly/painful surgery, recovery, oiling and painful reconnection, barometric pain, heat discomfort, cold oil failure/frostbite risk and growth impact (Automail r77396). No free full repair from a repair skill alone. |
| Proposal | Alkahestry diagnosis | Practitioner senses a flow pattern but has no verified cause and limited supplies. | Treat a bounded symptom, gather another observation, or refer to a physician. | The theory remains attributed; response and patient state provide evidence but do not prove the cosmology. |
| Proposal | Conflicting atrocity records | Witness, official ledger and community account disagree about a past event. | Protect witness, authenticate records, disclose publicly, or withdraw. | Custody, actor belief, public claim and safety risk remain distinct. Each actor needs actual means. |
| Proposal | Military certification | Candidate has trained for an exam and faces an institutional offer with obligations. | Accept commission, decline, or request terms and records first. | Authority/access and obligation change; no alchemical power is granted by rank alone. |
| Proposal | Fire in adverse conditions | Flame specialist has damaged gloves or uncertain ignition conditions near allies. | Repair/replace, reposition, use a different learned method, or abstain. | The action checks the method's actual trigger and ally geometry; no unsupported weather threshold is invented. |
| Proposal | Branch-specific taboo attempt | Character has source-supported warning and a dangerous, irreversible preparation. | Proceed only with explicit informed commitment, interrupt the setup, or refuse. | Consequence follows selected continuity; ordinary craft failure is not substituted for taboo aftermath. |
| Proposal | Soul-carrier damage | A bound person relies on a carrier mark known to be vulnerable. | Protect carrier, retreat for a qualified repair, or continue at known risk. | Bond condition, identity, carrier and body function stay separate; no generic heal restores them all. |
| Proposal | Prepared theory is wrong | Researcher brings a plausible but unverified method to a fixed test. | Run a small controlled trial, ask a specialist, or publish as hypothesis. | Result can falsify only what the test covers; uncertainty and reputation change according to evidence. |

## Interaction joins

| Status | Join | Required ownership proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Knowledge to execution | Knowledge owner records source, confidence and tested scope; ability owner checks valid means. |
| Proposal | Craft to inventory | Existing inventory owns actual matter and tools; crafting owns transformation consequences. |
| Proposal | Injury to automail | Body condition, prosthesis function, repair work and mobility aid remain separate carriers. |
| Proposal | Institution to plot | Office access is a permission; campaign state owns orders, evidence and consequences. |
| Proposal | Soul to body | Identity, carrier and bodily support require distinct persistence fields if implementation is ever authorized. |
| Proposal | Canon to AU | Continuity selects causal facts; divergence records changed events and leaves unsupported futures unknown. |
