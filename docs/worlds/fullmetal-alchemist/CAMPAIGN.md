# Fullmetal Alchemist campaign proposals

Status: all campaign rows are proposals. This is a menu of authored campaign shapes, not a registered plotline or runtime implementation. Use only within the continuity named by the entry; consult [SOURCE_LEDGER](SOURCE_LEDGER.md).

## Campaign frame

| Status | Campaign | Entry and pressure | Meaningful choice | Durable aftermath proposal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proposal | A Rush Valley repair season | A traveler reaches an automail workshop with a damaged component and limited funds. | Take paid shop work, barter a useful part, or leave before promising a delivery. | Workshop stock, trust, repair queue and local access change according to actual contribution. Automail recovery, painful reconnection, oiling, weather discomfort and cold-related oil/frostbite risks can shape the deadline; the wiki's average recovery is about three years, with six months its fastest cited example (Automail r77396), not a universal game timer. |
| Proposal | East City materials inquiry | A trainee studies an anomalous failed transmutation and has access to a supervised bench. | Repeat under controls, ask a specialist, or report the attempt and lose access. | Knowledge is evidence-specific; access, reputation and material stock change without granting a universal level. |
| Proposal | Central restricted archive | A researcher finds incompatible records about an alchemical project. | Follow official chain of custody, copy evidence, or conceal it to protect a witness. | Custody and actor beliefs diverge; disclosure can alter institutional danger. |
| Proposal | Ishval relief route | An aid team has medicine, transport and disputed testimony after military violence. | Prioritize immediate care, preserve evidence, negotiate safe passage or withdraw. | People helped, supplies spent, testimony reliability and local trust remain separate outcomes. |
| Proposal | Xingese field study | A practitioner observes a local flow pattern and has a prepared tool but incomplete theory. | Treat a limited injury, map the pattern, or defer rather than promise a cure. | A supported technique can grow through observation while an unproven explanation remains attributed. |
| Proposal | 2003 Gate investigation | An investigator tracks a branch-specific anomaly with uncertain costs and dangerous access. | Secure a lead, interrupt a procedure, or abandon the route. | Preserve the 2003 branch's actual costs and consequences; no Brotherhood/manga substitution. |

## Connected pursuits

| Status | Pursuit | Connects | Costly ordinary loop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Workshop supply chain | Mining/scrap sources, component inspection, fabrication, fitting, maintenance. | Spend hours and scarce parts to restore a usable limb or machine; quality and fit depend on skill and evidence. |
| Proposal | Material literacy | Study, sample, identify, calculate, prepare array, execute and document result. | Consume a limited sample and bench access to distinguish a composition hypothesis from a verified one. |
| Proposal | Evidence and testimony | Witness care, records, chain of custody, safe transport and public claim. | Protect one source or preserve one record while other evidence decays or becomes harder to reach. |
| Proposal | Local treatment | Practitioner, diagnosis, method, supplies, patient condition and recovery. | Treat within known limits; no knowledge-only cure or generic potion result. |

## Arcs and outcomes

| Status | Arc beat | If player intervenes | If player abstains or fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Entry and first need | Solve one grounded repair, research or aid task using actual means. | The local need continues; no penalty is invented for refusing before commitment. |
| Proposal | Evidence conflict | Compare claims, trace authorship and preserve or disclose evidence. | Actors proceed on their own beliefs and resources; the record may be lost or distorted. |
| Proposal | Institutional pressure | Use lawful access, social ties, craft leverage or public challenge. | Officials, rivals and affected communities act only with established means; danger does not auto-scale. |
| Proposal | Branch-specific crisis | A prepared and source-supported method can alter a fixed threat or consequence. | Failure may leave a permanent local loss; recovery is not guaranteed. |
| Proposal | Return and reassessment | Revisit changed people, work and sites; fulfill existing obligations. | Missed deadlines and lost access remain real where previously committed. |

## Direct adaptation and AU policy

| Status | Mode | Proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Proposal | Canon route | Reproduce only source-supported events and information available to the player at that point. No private protagonist knowledge. |
| Proposal | Start-point AU | Begin before/after a known event, with a separately authored snapshot and causal ledger. An earlier era does not rewind the live branch. |
| Proposal | Relationship AU | Change relationship state only as a declared branch; do not imply canon source evidence. |
| Proposal | Original-character route | The player has an ordinary compatible role, body, means and knowledge. They do not replace protagonists by default. |
| Proposal | Divergence | A witnessed, materially possible intervention changes downstream claims; preserve which canonical events remain possible, altered or unknown. |

The pack includes no new randomized truths yet. Any future change to canon truth must be stored as a sealed slot with route-only outward references.
