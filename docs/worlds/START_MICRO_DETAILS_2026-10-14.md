Reasonably use the body's actual senses and available objects; a sword may have a sheath rather than pockets. The SA palette and entry palette are separate axes: six details per kind, not 216 required SA-by-entry combinations. Compose a compatible pair, remove contradictions and keep its authored text stable with the resolved record. Born can begin at a later childhood anchor; woke does not itself mean amnesia; returned can mean travel or a supported regression.

**Cost legend, applied to each row:** `T` = pure authored text, no new system; `D` = data/copy in an existing description or text slot, no new system; `H: …` = needs the named trigger, state, rendering or gameplay hook. T/D means the detail itself is cheap once a compatible start exists, not that its underlying ghost, collective or patron start is implemented. A static prop in prose is scenery; making it lootable, inspectable or useful changes its cost to H. Personal history on one item needs an instance binding; a shared template description is only shared flavor. UI-tagged T/D is copy in an existing surface, not a new control or automatic selection.

**Default + Dial boundaries:** PN1-PN9 keep past people ordinary and belongings with their owners; optional recognition follows IX/world law. Fresh canon has no imported previous-life residue; a continued branch uses its actual known changes. Villain tells distinguish office, reputation, attributed history and chosen intent. Mentor/elder details do not imply forced decline. Food scent suggests comfort, not hunger death. Harshness cues accompany a reachable first step where supported (EH4/EH5); they do not supply an unimplemented rescue or protection window. A detail that offers an action needs that action. Hidden-layer truth remains behind its discovery route.

Presentation proposal: desktop/laptop **1366×768** primary, with 1440×900 and 1920×1080 useful later comparisons. Visible outcome would be one readable opening cue and a concise start card beside creation controls; additional texture stays in ordinary text surfaces. Mobile optimization, new audio/VFX, extra panels and a signal registry are deferred. No rebuilt UI evidence is claimed here.

## Beyond MZ1-MZ18: events, substitutes, branches and endings

Default: texture follows an observed cause, even if the reason remains uncertain. Dial: no extra tell, one close-look clue, several corroborating clues or clearly communicated aftermath, reasonably per world.
