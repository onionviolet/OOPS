## CN-S: cheats that show nonhuman things

Default: acquire an actual means, sample through its supported channel, resolve
AP depth/precision and record a qualified result. Dial: self-only diagnostics,
consenting partner, contested target, delayed/biased system, exact revelation or
world-law access. Reasonably per world: exact reading can be a real strong power;
ordinary life keeps cues and useful decisions without it. A hound smelling flour
is ordinary body function; an impossible perfect trail may be a cheat (WR1-WR8).

**Sealed safety shared by every row:** author truth stays outside UI unless the
specific acquired means succeeds through its authored discovery route. An exact
reveal may legitimately discover a secret in play, but this report records only
ID/route/status. Do not populate secret strings in hidden DOM, tooltips, ARIA,
search/settings lists, exports of public status cards or debug-style log text.
Even a silhouette, branch count or “blocked bloodline” can reveal existence;
show it only if that existence was discovered. A denial reason may itself be
unknown. Save/export controls stay available; a gameplay save is not promised
to be a spoiler-redacted share card.
