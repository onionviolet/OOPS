## O1: The Borrowed Floodwarden

**Distinctive life:** powerful wrong-soul resurrection, inherited public fear and the freedom to turn an instrument of coercion into civic or personal work.

**World and ordinary life.** The Lattice Coast consists of inhabited tidal shelves. Certain human bodies can speak to mineral gates, opening real channels or holding water at an embankment. Gateworks belong to harbor cooperatives, estates and municipal trusts; bodily command is an operating faculty, not automatic ownership. Water courts hear disputes with measured channel records and witness testimony. Reservoir leases, salt gardens, ferry tolls and dry-workshop rent support an economy whose weekly markets move between elevations. Families keep tide calendars; children learn safe crossings; evening social life happens on roof promenades.

**Entry and occupant.** The feared Floodwarden Seret died after years of punitive channel closures. A medical resurrection restores Seret's healthy adult body and its practiced neural patterns, but summons your continuing subject instead of Seret. The selected premise establishes Seret as departed, with no dormant co-occupant. You receive accurate procedural and contextual memory, including language and gate operation. Your actual outside memories remain reliable. You do not inherit Seret's desires or owe a redemption arc.

**Strong starting means.** The body has exceptional breath endurance, mineral resonance and fluent engineering mastery. You begin in Seret's personally owned house with tools, liquid funds, archive keys and a capable paid staff willing to hear the next order. Public gateworks remain under their actual titles. Outsiders know a familiar face and reported conduct.

**First scene and alternatives.** At breakfast, a ferry cooperative asks whether today's already scheduled punitive closure will proceed. You can cancel it using the intact control faculty, authorize the earlier schedule, negotiate a limited maintenance window, or resign command and hand the operating keys to its qualified keeper. Cancellation immediately preserves the ferry route and livelihoods. Resignation creates an independent succession decision.

**Research ambition.** Could the gate faculty hold a stable pocket of quiet water inside moving surf, allowing delicate surgery aboard a rescue boat? Outside ideas meet inherited gate mastery, test basins, a surgeon and volunteers in a supported first experiment. The player may instead investigate acoustics, create floating gardens or devote the established technique to ordinary transport. A successful rescue method changes what the harbor can do, without requiring a new siege to keep it useful.

**Independent relationship.** Iven, the ferry cooperative's elected mechanic, lost income under Seret's policies and wants the cooperative to own its route controls. Iven can work with you, refuse your patronage or sell a design elsewhere. If you disclose the transfer, inherited memories do not retroactively make Iven your old friend. New trust grows from your actual conversations and work; restitution and friendship remain separable. Iven may prefer decentralizing your power even while liking you.

**Continuity, home and maturity.** The resurrection apparatus carries your subject and learned memories into a deliberately empty replacement body if you choose a prepared return upon bodily death. Neural expertise transfers as an operating pattern; mineral command transfers only with compatible resonance organs, which the available workshop can prepare. Equipment and property stay in the harbor unless actually moved. The retained world is not rewound.

The proposed entry leaves your original home body alive in a rite-supported dormant state, with no conscious subject remaining in it. Your one continuing subject acts in the Floodwarden body; it has not been copied. Departure freezes the represented home calendar and the dormant body’s aging until return. Constructing a passage can carry your subject back into that original body, leaving the Floodwarden vessel empty, or carry your current body home while the original remains dormant by choice. Each transfer moves the same subject rather than awakening two selves. Closing the game performs neither operation. Local time advances only through played or explicitly chosen chapter spans. You may settle, return, keep two homes through the completed passage, or end the story while alive. Mature play includes rescue engineering, arbitration, music, boating, workshops and relationships. Stable prosperity is a valid sustained state.

**Conditional beats.** The ferry blockade occurs only if the scheduled order remains and its operator acts. Canceling or replacing the order removes that event. A dispossession hearing occurs only while a claimant files and maintains a contested title; settlement ends that hearing. Iven's bid for cooperative ownership continues while desired and feasible, even if Seret's old conflict ends. None requires an assassination or flood as a substitute.

## O2: Two Voices in the Orchard City

**Distinctive life:** cooperative shared embodiment, reciprocal expertise and a partnership that can mature into voluntary separation without deleting either person.

**World and ordinary life.** Vel follows a seasonal migration of living cities: enormous orchard organisms walk between rainfall belts. Residents lease rooms grown into their branches; food and construction compete for growth allocation. Cultivation councils bargain over routes, fruit yields and public space. Independent graft houses make tools, medicines and furnishings from responsive living material. Payment mixes coin and transferable harvest shares. Residents hold balcony dinners, exchange flowering cuttings and visit neighboring limbs by suspended tram. A city can simply enjoy a good season.

**Entry and occupants.** Lio, an established graft surgeon, voluntarily uses an invitation rite to seek an outside collaborator. You arrive as your continuing subject alongside Lio, who remains fully conscious. Two people share one adult body. Reciprocal access accurately conveys agreed memories and procedures; private recollections stay private by choice. You immediately understand local language, anatomy and Lio's consenting technical instruction. Neither is an alter ego or a copy of the other.

**Control and starting means.** The pair begins with fluent graft mastery, an excellent body, a licensed workshop, savings, apprentices and an unusually strong faculty for sensing living tissue. Lio has volunteered standing consent for ordinary workshop control; new major promises, bodily alterations and shared-fund commitments require both people's agreement. Either can speak under their own name, while joint speech is identified. Known procedures need no synchronization minigame. Lio offers goals and can decline a plan, rather than secretly sabotaging success to manufacture drama.

**First scene and alternatives.** The workshop receives a request to turn an unused limb into a public reading garden. You can co-design flexible shelter, propose household rooms instead, teach apprentices to handle the commission, or decline and take a walk with Lio. The council chooses among proposals rather than granting unlimited land. Your outside idea about modular construction becomes usable through Lio's local execution; neither contribution is erased.

**Research ambition.** Can a living neighborhood carry a library of *smells* that reliably evokes specific memories without dictating emotion? It could aid navigation, storytelling and preserving recipes. Lio supplies plant physiology; you supply chosen conceptual ideas; a perfumer contributes reproducible blending. Tests ask what participants actually recall. The project can stop at a pleasant public archive.

**Independent relationship.** Eska, a tram builder and Lio's longtime friend, wants a new route that avoids dependence on one graft house. Eska already knows Lio but meets you as someone new. Joint work, a separate conversation with you or a refusal to socialize are meaningful possibilities. Their friendship with Lio is not assigned to you; a bond with you has its own shared history. Eska can accept the reading-garden commission, leave for another city or criticize a design while maintaining affection for either person.

**Continuity, home and maturity.** The invitation thread carries your self and knowledge through a later departure; Lio keeps their self and everything genuinely learned. Bodily tissue-sense stays with the body. A grown empty vessel can receive you with a deliberately prepared equivalent faculty and retained procedural patterns, so separation need not mean returning to novice status. No new person is evicted. If the shared body dies while the prepared two-seat refuge is active, both subjects continue separately; that protection does not recreate lost workshop tools or rewind the city.

The proposed invitation rite leaves your original home body alive in a protected dormant state. No second conscious you remains there: your single continuing subject is present alongside Lio. The invitation thread can deliver information to a home-side receiver; communication does not awaken or manufacture a second self. Home calendar time advances equally with represented local life, while the rite suspends the dormant body’s biological aging. These are separate clocks, so people and circumstances at home can change without your unattended body aging. Bodily return requires a compatible passage that transfers your subject back into the original body; Lio remains themselves, and any empty vessel you leave has no independent you. Absence freezes both represented calendars unless another mode is selected. Closure can mean staying together, separating locally, returning home after building passage, or leaving a flourishing workshop to successors. Mature play includes shared domestic life, touring orchards, teaching, independent commissions and experimenting for pleasure.

**Conditional beats.** A tram dispute occurs only if two proposed routes still compete for the same branch capacity. Rerouting or increasing capacity removes the conflict. A shared-control renegotiation happens when either actually requests changed terms, not at a mandatory betrayal chapter. Harvest loss depends on relevant crop and weather conditions; solving those conditions leaves a good harvest, not a replacement pest.

## O3: The Many-Door Observatory

**Distinctive life:** one outside continuing self, replaceable non-person avatars, a persistent local home and relationships sustained across different embodiments.

**World and ordinary life.** The Lantern Reach contains pocket landscapes connected by physical door stations. Local physics differs: one district carries sound through stone, another has long low-gravity terraces. Station guilds maintain routes; town councils govern destinations; survey colleges certify safe navigation. Trade centers on compatible instruments, visitor services and goods actually carried through doors. Residents garden, rent rooms to travelers and attend cross-district concerts.

**Entry and starting means.** Your original self remains outside, connected to a grown instrument-body with no independent subject. The link continuously transmits experience and player-supplied outside information. A station sponsor provides an adult capable vessel, fluent language, a reliable procedural atlas, survey mastery, a private room, travel funds and access to an observatory. Starting knowledge includes documented routes, not undiscovered landscape rules. The atlas can truly grant immediate supported execution.

**First scene and alternatives.** Observatory engineer Neri asks what to do with a newly opened door that leads to an apparently empty terrace. You can survey it together, send a consenting qualified crew, keep it closed while reviewing available records, or use the afternoon to furnish your room. Exploration needs appropriate instruments but no compulsory novice-labor phase.

**Research and relationship.** Could music composed across districts become audible to people whose senses cannot perceive any single component? Neri wants to develop accessible public instruments and independently pursues a cooperative workshop. You can investigate signal translation with them, fund their project without owning it, or choose a different field. Neri's work and other friendships continue under the chosen clock, with negotiated shared commitments. Their interest in you can survive vessel changes because the known link signature, witnessed by an available station instrument, provides reliable recognition.

**Death, homes and closure.** Vessel destruction ends its bodily activity; you continue outside with all link-carried experience and learned knowledge. A ready replacement restores supported mastery through the procedural atlas. Bodily capacities depend on the selected replacement design; cargo stays at the destruction site unless recovered. Neri remembers what actually happened. A home anchor returns the connection to the same observatory and retained people, never a silently reset counterpart.

Proposed home time is stopped during connected visits; local experienced time advances through direct play and chosen bounded chapters. Disconnecting freezes the represented local world. Ending a session is still a player action; fictional disconnection is a separate chosen operation. You can return outside permanently, retain a recurring local household, retire from exploration or invite Neri to travel through a genuinely built person-carrying door. Mature play offers concerts, instrument design, mentoring, household life and visits with friends after route safety is established.

**Conditional beats.** A rescue attempt requires a missing expedition, evidence of its location and an actor able to reach it. A successful early recall removes that rescue. A threatened station closure requires an unresolved structural fault; repair makes closure unnecessary. Neri considers moving their workshop when local opportunities suit that plan, not because avatar loss automatically dissolves affection.

## Comparison and recommendation

| Candidate | Best distinctive promise | Main design work still required |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | Immediate power; a familiar feared face can choose a different life | Distinguish bodily access, legal title, predecessor history and newly formed bonds |
| O2 | A competent local person is an active partner inside shared action | Represent two subjects, speech, standing consent and independent relationships clearly |
| O3 | Comfortable home/return and long relationships through changing bodies | Bind controller, vessels, retained instance, clocks and transferred capacities consistently |

**Recommendation D1:** choose O3 for the first literal-self demonstration if dependable continuity is the central pleasure. Choose O1 instead when the exciting premise is waking with substantial power and social history. O2 is the strongest cooperative experience, with more interaction authorship rather than a mandatory weakness.

**Decision questions:** Q1: Is the main attraction inherited authority, another competent person beside you, or a persistent home across bodies? Q2: Should original-home access be live, delayed until a passage exists, or an optional final ending? Q3: Do you want bodily powers to survive through prepared equivalent vessels, or prefer selecting materially different bodies while retaining reliable expertise? Answers select a candidate and carrier, not acceptance of every detail.
