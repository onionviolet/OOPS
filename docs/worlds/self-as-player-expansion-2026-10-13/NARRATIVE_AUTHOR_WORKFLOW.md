These are workflow gaps, not evidence that the existing GM fails at them. The author role adds sustained prose and cast development to established design contracts.

## Inputs and ownership

An assignment needs the desired lived experience and tone; selected world/origin/continuity; source range or original-rule brief; current scene snapshot; accepted choices and open questions; relevant state-owner pointers; and an exact exclusive output area. Include player-declared outside knowledge only when supplied for this purpose. A literal-self premise does not authorize inferred biography, expertise or preferences.

The author owns the assigned draft scenes and cast elaboration. The world GM owns reconciliation with campaign rules, playable scope and other writers. The researcher verifies borrowed claims. The implementer binds selected effects to actual systems. These can be sequential roles of one agent. No permanent staffing hierarchy is necessary.

Reuse the world's existing brief, cast notes, timeline and checkpoint. For a new candidate, one small author packet can contain all of them until separate files become useful. Do not establish another universal lore registry. Changes to an accepted rule are explicit proposals for its owner; current facts are not silently rewritten to rescue a scene. A writer reservation ends with a named handoff or task closure.

## From research into original writing

Follow the [source playbook](../../research/SOURCE_DEEP_DIVE_PLAYBOOK.md) and [lore/timeline audit](../../research/LORE_TIMELINE_AUDIT_CONTRACT.md). Preserve work/version, inspected range, locator, source fact or attributed belief, inference and gap. Acquisition status and earlier full-read claims do not establish this question's inspected evidence.

For each borrowed situation, keep a short transformation note: dramatic function; actual source causes; selected mode (recreation, qualified AU or original inspiration); changed causes and social arrangements; resulting choices and aftermath. Original composition needs distinctive laws, institutions and participant aims, not replacement names. Several references can inform it without importing incompatible laws by accident. Raw prose and private source detail stay in their existing private owners; public synthesis can describe the reusable mechanism. Sealed discoveries use the existing secret-ID/route standard.

The author's ambition includes what follows the entry: research programmes, friendships, professional work, residence and voluntary closure. Every quiet detail need not become a quest. Food smells, teasing and an untidy desk can simply establish a place people inhabit.

## Writing a lived cast and conditional beats

A core cast note should say what the person wants now, what they know and how they learned it, their competence and resources, an existing tie, and a plausible next action without player assistance. Add one voice sample and one ordinary habit. Different wording should reflect different attention: a technician asks which reading changed; a councillor asks who can authorize access. Avoid making everyone a tutor, enemy or reward dispenser. Trust and cooperation can be established facts.

For an important beat, record its stable ID, situation, participants, eligibility, information channels, offered actions, resulting changes and later dependencies. Include refusal/delay/bypass where they matter. Distinguish occurrence from discovery and an actor's belief from author truth. Reader foreknowledge can remain useful; changed causes revise only relevant predictions. NPC independence means motives plus available means, not omniscience or compulsory opposition.

Use this cancellation rule: if a necessary cause disappears, remove or rewrite that beat; preserve unrelated plans. A defeated conspirator does not generate a replacement solely to deliver the planned attack. A research success can open collaboration rather than trigger a mandatory catastrophe. Authored deadlines use a selected clock; silence in chat or a closed browser does not imply world advancement.

The [storylet draft](../../emergent/E2_STORYLETS.md) offers useful needs/effects and retained-cast ideas, but labels its schema and cards proposals. Its dated source observations are not current runtime proof. Use its concepts only after checking the actual integration owner. No new deck engine is required for a small authored scene.

## Original demonstration: the empty restoration

All facts below are invented, public demonstration content. No hidden truth is specified.

In Grey Weir, worked silver stores a precisely measured heat difference until its engraved boundary is broken. The canal towns use it to keep pumps running between tides. The restoration society attempted to recover a dead engineer's working mind; instead it formed a new empty body and received your continuing self, with reliable declared outside memories. No occupant was displaced. The sponsor offers lodging and access to the engineer's public notebooks. You have no inherited personal memories or credentials.

The workshop smells of wet wool and hot metal. On the table, a silver strip curls beside two cups.

“Wrong person,” says Mara, the experimenter. She pushes a cup toward you. “Still a person. We can decide what to do after tea.”

Across the room, canal mechanic Iven measures the strip again. “It held twelve degrees yesterday. Eleven today. If you know why, I'd rather hear it than your title.”

Mara wants to understand the entry and offers voluntary tests. Iven wants dependable pumps before the next harvest and has a repair crew. They disagree about instrument funding but already share weekly supper. Neither needs the player to invent a life for them.

| Code | Player continuation | Coherent aftermath |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Propose a controlled heat-accounting experiment from supplied outside knowledge, then pursue a canal heat network. | Compare storage duration, transfer losses and engraving geometry using available thermometers, strips and Iven's pumps. A demonstrated result can support a pilot, workshop methods and later district cooperation. Remembering a concept does not itself establish local law. Strong starting mastery is allowed if separately included. |
| D2 | Explain the identity change and ask Mara to be a collaborator rather than an examiner. | She accepts that boundary in this version. Agreed tests and notebooks become shared work; supper, friendship and disagreement about priorities continue without a loyalty grind or required romance. |
| D3 | Accept a small repair job and make the town home. | With training or declared competence, maintain known pump designs, earn a living and attend supper. Iven's crew handles the larger network. Successful quiet play remains a complete chosen life, with later research optional. |
| D4 | Decline the project and leave with an agreed travel stipend. | Mara continues the permitted experiments with ordinary instruments; Iven repairs pumps. They can write if you exchange an address. The refusal creates no replacement crisis or compulsory return. |

These can combine. Research eligibility depends on access, consent and tools; withdrawing consent cancels that test. A supplier dispute can affect the pilot only if that supplier actually controls needed stock. Iven can procure an alternative using his crew's funds. The scene's meaningful difference is the local heat-storage law and civic infrastructure, not a renamed restoration council.

## State integration and durable continuity

Before implementation, annotate material effects: who owns the body, tools and notebooks; who granted access; what was demonstrated; which promise was made; which actor witnessed it; and what needs to survive departure/reload. Bind each to an inspected existing owner or mark a missing interface. Separate described possibilities from reachable actions. The author supplies causal intent and player-facing text, not invented APIs or speculative save fields.

At each accepted writing increment, save premise/content revision, stable actor/event IDs, occurrence order, knowledge channels, material changes, commitments, unresolved questions and the next eligible situation. Preserve superseded versions as history. On recovery, read these owners and the current checkpoint before writing. A synopsis can recover tone; it cannot replace exact ownership or a promise's terms. Review dependent beats after a change, using the [timeline owner](../../TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE.md) rather than recomputing an entire universe.

## Review rubric and first authoring unit

Score each dimension 0 (absent/contradictory), 1 (plausible but thin), or 2 (demonstrated). Scores are comparison aids, not mechanical acceptance.

| Code | Dimension | Evidence for 2 |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Distinctive world and useful life | One local rule changes actual decisions; work, research and relationships continue past the opening. |
| R2 | Agency and causality | Two different chosen aftermaths; refusal/bypass respects causes; relevant independent action remains possible. |
| R3 | Cast and voice | Recognizable dialogue, competence, non-player ties and knowledge-supported action. |
| R4 | Continuity and integration | Clear identity/retention, recorded consequences, knowledge channels and named state gaps. |
| R5 | Taste and evidence | Source/invention labels are honest; director can assess tone, attraction and pacing from an actual scene. |

A reasonable first unit is one original world brief, one entry snapshot, three core cast notes, one short opening and three conditional follow-ups, including quiet success. Deliver a continuity delta and state-gap list. Human review selects, revises or rejects tone, premise and desired pursuit; passing this rubric does not accept the world. Future implementation needs its own rebuilt browser and applicable persistence evidence.

Offline AI-assisted drafting can produce reviewed, frozen content. Possible future runtime AI would require a separate decision about state access, memory, determinism, latency and failure handling. This packet commits to neither architecture nor infinite simulation.

## OD7 writing and continuation chain, 2026-10-15

Reusable proposed workflow responding to [Weibao's request](../../USER_VISION.md#overarching-direction-discussion-od7-2026-10-15).
Accepted direction is authored plots/simulation by world and mode, with canon's
natural course unless changed. This workflow does not require a permanent writer
agent, runtime generator or full simulation of every possible dependency.

| Step | Finite output for the existing world owner |
| --- | --- |
| W1, baseline | Chosen source/edition/continuity/cutoff or original premise; event causes, participant motives/means, occurrence order and knowledge channels. Use the lore/timeline contract. |
| W2, disturbance | What actually changed, by whose action/means and when; separate intent, attempt, outcome and discovery. An AU premise is a starting difference, not necessarily an intervention inside the story. |
| W3, propagation | Which dependent events still qualify, change, delay or fail; preserve independent plans. Name a replacement actor's own motivation/resources/access rather than replacing a missing canon role by fiat. Record genuine uncertainty. |
| W4, playable beat | Current situation, readable leads, actual available actions, refusal/delay/bypass where meaningful, conditional prose and resulting state changes. Lore or narration alone does not implement effects. |
| W5, aftermath and resume | Durable delta, who knows what, surviving goals, resolved problems, pending events, selected clocks, next actionable scene and exclusive writer/state-owner pointers. Resume from this packet, not a copied synopsis that overwrites history. |

Before moving to the next beat, the writer asks whether the previous outcome
actually supplies its prerequisites. Changes in one dependency should not erase
unrelated canon. Off-canon characters continue with their motives and possibilities;
there is no requirement to recreate the same set piece with new names.

Books, author AUs and fanfiction can supply contrasting mechanisms: a changed
birth/family changes access and loyalties; foreknowledge changes decisions only
when communicated or acted upon; a different power changes which plans are
feasible; a loop changes retained knowledge and repetition. These are illustrative
patterns, not factual claims about newly inspected works. Keep source law,
fan premise, project counterfactual and director-accepted change distinct.

Storytellers can select/present eligible opportunities under the selected mode.
Fate manipulation, authorial intervention and narrative cheats can alter causes,
probabilities or constraints if that is their actual supported power. Record that
mechanism in W2/W3. Do not silently grant every storyteller unlimited world edits,
or impose a blanket ban on a world whose premise includes them. Per-world
storyteller/cruelty options already have prior accepted direction; this chain does
not revoke it or choose one global director policy.

A design checkpoint checks causal continuity, knowledge/secret boundaries and
available choices. Implementation later requires real bindings, fresh rebuilt
browser behavior and relevant save/reload/import proof. A good written scene is
not evidence that its gameplay consequences exist. No worker dispatched here.


### W2/W3 clarification: correction mechanisms, 2026-10-15

[OD8 answer](../../USER_VISION.md#overarching-direction-discussion-od8-2026-10-15)
explicitly permits heavenly will, fate, historical correction or other world
mechanisms to restore a course. In W2/W3 record the actual restoring cause,
its relevant trigger/reach and who knows it, just as for an intervention. This
may legitimately produce a substitute or recurring outcome; it is not prohibited
by the earlier warning against fiat replacement. Do not assign every world this
power or assume it succeeds/fails independently of its actual rules. Concealment,
illusions and knowledge-changing cheats also affect which causes actors believe
and act on; keep those beliefs separate from author truth and authorized views.
