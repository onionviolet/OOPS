**What the repo builds versus what the source has.** The built Cyoria is an original independent visitor in an ordinary third-year era: no loop, no marker, no personal mana for the player, frozen absence, same native body ([brief](../mother-of-learning/WORLD_BRIEF.md); `src/book_world_mol_state.js:24,44-47`). ## 1. Gate catalog

Template per gate (GTR5): kind and owner (GTR3), reads, enforcer, default and dial with the in-world reason (GTR1, WPR5), routes past it with cost (GTR4), failure cost, overflow (GTR16, GTR21), visibility (GTR10, GTR15, GTR24), evidence. All defaults are source-reading proposals; dials are mine.

### A. What the loop retains [L]

**MLG1 Persistence classes at the cut.** Kind: law (the loop's rule) over capacity. Reads: which class each asset belongs to. Enforcer: the loop itself (no actor). Default: soul-bound things carry (memory, learned spells, shaping skill, soul damage); template edits carry; body, items, money, injuries, other people reset. Dial: per asset, per loop version; the held-at-start exception for artifacts (MLX2). Reason: the soul is what the loop pulls back, and "magic is heavily connected to the soul". Routes: carriers for notes (memorize spell, memory packets, code phrase, MLG24). Failure: unwritten notes vanish at the cut. Overflow: n/a (a class, not a cap). Visibility: the character learns it by testing across restarts. Evidence: stated/inferred, Zorian's reasoning, Ch 7 para 73-75 (MAGIC_SYSTEM 1.5); LOOP_INERTIA 1.3 lists shown cases (notes vanish Ch 6; savings refill c014 p117; body ailments reset Ch 62; items reset).

**MLG2 Reserve: cap growth versus current fill.** Kind: capacity (body or vessel). Reads: two numbers, not one. Default: the current fill is body-side and refills at the cut (a spent reserve returns each restart, institutions pass section 6); the cap's growth is contested (MLC1). Proposed default: cap growth carries slowly, efficiency carries fully, fill resets. Dial: how much of growth is soul-side, per loop version. Reason: Zorian himself cannot separate reserve growth from efficiency (Ch 46 para 7). Routes: none needed; it is a retention row. Failure: n/a. Overflow: see MLG3. Visibility: character-measured as rod-shot counts (c008 p46), a unit the character has. Evidence: stated/inferred, both locators above. **The task prompt's framing "body-bound reserves" is one of two source readings and I keep both (MLC1).**

**MLG3 Reserve growth with age and practice (the roughly 4x ceiling).** Kind: maturation over capacity. Reads: inborn magnitude, age, and practice that empties the reserve. Enforcer: the soul ("protecting control"), no institution. Default: slow growth to about 4x the start, usually less (booklet, c008 p46; the 4x reading for Zorian and Zach is inferred, 232 shots over magnitude 50). Adults barely change in a month (Ch 86 para 31-33). Dial: gift, practice, age; the ceiling multiplier (3 to 6). Reason: a soul can hold so much safely (Zorian's inference, MAGIC_SYSTEM 1.2). Routes: the fast route is repeated exhaustion with mana-hungry spells, which permanently wrecks shaping (Taiven, Ch 22 para 55-57; stated). Gulping ambient mana in an emergency costs days of fever (Ch 22 para 69-74). Reserve-adding objects (a stored-mana crown, formula batteries) raise usable maximum without growing the soul (late, Ch 81 para 115). Failure: shaping damage, fever. Overflow: **pressure** (excess forced growth spills into the neighbouring track, MLG4, as lost control). Visibility: shot counts yes; ceiling no, the character can suspect it. Evidence: stated (c008 p46), inferred (4x).

**MLG4 Power versus control ceiling.** Kind: capacity pair (MLX6). Reads: inborn magnitude against the finesse a spell asks. Default: above roughly magnitude 20 by Taiven's guess, finesse spells (advanced mind magic, detailed illusion, complex alteration) become very hard; a magnitude-15 illusionist is fine. Dial: the threshold, per school; a world with a different soul law moves it. Reason: stated by one speaker as a guess, so it is a rumour with a number. Routes: the large-reserve mage takes years longer to certification; shaping-heavy fields suit small reserves (Kyron); a divine blessing doubles the max without harming shaping (late, MAGIC_SYSTEM 3.11). Failure: spell fails or does not reach the finesse tier. Overflow: pressure/converted (surplus reserve converts into training time, not skill: larger reserves speed practice per day but not skill, Ch 8 para 51-54). Visibility: guesswork. Evidence: stated, c022 p57-61 (institutions pass spot check 14: Taiven's guess, not a rule).

### B. Skill and spell progress

**MLG5 Shaping exercise ladder.** Kind: maturation plus knowledge. Reads: exercise order, repetitions and a mastery test. Enforcer: the teacher's test (Xvim's marbles) or the book's order. Default: later variations need earlier ones; most students finish about 6 a year (c010 p56, stated by Ilsa); a textbook lists 15 per base set. Mastery means flawless performance while interrupted with near-zero setup (Ch 2 para 203-212, shown). Dial: focus, teacher quality, restarts (Zorian did 15 in a month using many loops, Ch 10 para 19, 85). Reason: raw control is the foundation of every spell (Xvim, Ch 50 para 117-119). Routes: a harsh mentor multiplies speed (Ch 51 para 7); rural drills exist as an alternate track (Ch 27 para 75). Failure: a failed test costs a repeat, not a wall. Overflow: **banked** (a long sitting past one's limit is fatigue, but rehearsal under interruption digests it); diminishing returns appear as plateaus (Zach "as high as it will ever be", Ch 62 para 101; aranean mind lessons "low-hanging fruit picked", Ch 63 para 212). Visibility: the character sees which exercise comes next. Evidence: stated/shown as cited.

**MLG6 Spell efficiency.** Kind: maturation. Reads: repetitions of one spell, practised at low power then scaled. Default: practice cuts cost; the boundary bounds how far a spell can be overpowered (Ch 46 para 7-15). Dial: spell, school. Reason: a tighter boundary is cheaper and less forgiving (Ch 11 para 41). Routes: formulas as stabilisers, not multipliers. Failure: none beyond wasted mana. Overflow: converted (extra repetition becomes a cheaper cast, not a bigger cap). Visibility: shot count rises, visible. Enforcer: none; the boundary of the spell itself. Reason: stated above. Evidence: shown (about 35 shots at Ch 46 versus 8 to 10 at the start) and inferred (how much is efficiency).

**MLG7 Spell access: circle, library tier, see-once.** Kind: credential over knowledge. Reads: certification circle, library tier, and whether the spell has been seen. Enforcer: the academy, then the Guild; the library clerk. Default: first-circle certification at the end of year 2 opens the restricted stacks; deeper levels need Guild standing (c001 p75-99; Ch 2 para 3); knowing the chant and gestures is about 80 percent of learning, and a watched cast can teach (Ch 6 para 191, Ch 8 para 76-79). Dial: circle of the spell (circles 0, 1, 2, 3 and 7 are the ones named), library policy. Reason: students are trusted with weak spells first. Routes: ask a teacher for a priced service; illegal learning by theft or raids with exposure risk (MAGIC_SYSTEM 4.3). Failure: refusal, or exposure and Guild attention. Overflow: n/a. Visibility: the catalogue shows tiers; the circle ladder is mostly unknown (MLA2). Evidence: stated, shown.

**MLG8 Mentor and teacher terms.** Kind: social. Reads: mentor quota, trust and obedience, a price in errands or favours. Enforcer: the academy quota; each teacher. Default: one mentor per third-year, no transfer; a late visit lost Zorian his choice (c001 p88-96, shown). Teacher terms: Xvim wants dedication, Ilsa an apprentice contract plus assistant duties, Alanic evening attendance and courier errands (c032 p58-76, p74, p76; he can end it at once). Dial: quota size, the giver's incentive (offers carry incentives, institutions F10). Routes: another teacher, a book, a peer (Zach, Taiven), rural mages. Failure: lost slot, instruction ends. Overflow: **lost** (a missed slot does not bank); a teacher's earlier lessons stay. Visibility: visible. Evidence: shown/stated, V in the institutions pass.

**MLG9 Academy certification, year-3 gates and the key.** Kind: credential. Reads: exam result; the academy's record. Enforcer: academy examiners. Default: about a quarter of a year group did not return, civilian-born more often (c001 p80, c002 p64, stated by Benisek); year 3 adds 3 to 5 electives with a one-month switch (c001 p99) and a housing key that opens to the owner's mana (c001 p84-85). Dial: pass rate, number of electives. Reason: overbooked staff and pass-rate pressure. Routes: a Guild badge (MLG11) is a separate record; a patron vouches. Failure: the path ends; expulsion with a tuition dispute (c095 p15-27, late). Overflow: lost. Visibility: shown. Evidence: stated; the pass rate is unverified (institutions pass: U).

**MLG10 Job board and points.** Kind: resource/social. Reads: posted jobs, points, a team of four for sewer retrieval. Enforcer: the academy's administration; the posting mage. Default: payout "generally miserable", points required, most start in year 4 (c002 p80; points quota and unit not found). Dial: points required, pay. Routes: money need moves it earlier; rural boards pay in goods (c027 p42). Failure: a team slot goes to another (c002 p85). Overflow: n/a (jobs, not a cap). Evidence: stated/shown, V.

**MLG13 Library tiers and library work.** Kind: credential. Reads: circle, employment, trust. ### D. Soul, golem and blood gates

**MLG14 Soul perception (hard gate, several routes).** Kind: capacity plus knowledge. Reads: whether the body can perceive souls. Enforcer: none (a bodily fact); teachers hold the routes. Default: needed for sophisticated soul spells; without it the options are rituals up to 2 hours of casting that last weeks, or rudimentary spells (Alanic, c032 p58 opened; Ch 32 para 66-70 reader). Dial: which route, the time, the risk. Reason: souls are not visible to ordinary senses. Routes with costs (stated/shown): (1) months of daily meditation plus a weekly hallucinogenic potion that wrecks normal function for a time (Ch 32 para 82-95, reader unverified); (2) near-death or a dirge-moth potion drunk to the edge of death, 1 to 5 days unconscious (Ch 74 para 117-125); (3) a murder ritual (necromancers); (4) born with it ("ghost eyes"); (5) a faster, more dangerous soul-rip (Ch 59 para 122-179). Failure: death or lasting soul damage (MLG22). Overflow: n/a. Visibility: the route is known to the teacher; the character may not know the options. Evidence: stated/shown. This is the book's clearest "one goal, several costs" gate.

**MLG15 Soul magic access.** Kind: knowledge, social. Reads: perception (MLG14), a willing teacher, time. Enforcer: the Church (loosened after the gods fell silent, Ch 32 para 101). Default: Alanic teaches protective rituals first, perception training over months, one track at a time (c032 p58-76). Routes: Kael's basic soul-sight cloak spell (Ch 32 para 47). Failure: teacher ends instruction. Dial: time, the teacher's trust, one track at a time. Reason: the Church trains priests since the gods fell silent. Overflow: n/a. Visibility: the teacher states the options (shown); the character does not see the cost of the faster ones until told. Evidence: stated, V (c032).

**MLG16 Golemancy.** Kind: knowledge plus law. Reads: Houses' training, stabiliser glyphs, a crystal battery, steel. Default: Houses specialise; hard to learn; a licence above 1 m (Ch 29 para 127; Ch 44 para 138). Dial: licence height, battery cost. Routes: a House teacher (Edwin); no soul to attack, so mind routes fail (Ch 36 para 21). Failure: Guild action over unlicensed size. Overflow: n/a. Enforcer: Guild inspectors for size licences. Reason: Houses guard their trade and size is a safety line. Visibility: not shown (U). Evidence: stated/shown, unverified locators. Gate details thin (MLA3).

**MLG17 Blood magic and enhancement.** Kind: law plus resource. Reads: heritable anchors, ritual materials, life force. Enforcer: the Guild suppresses the texts; morlock practitioners hold the knowledge. Default: enhancement makes the target a magical creature needing ambient mana and costs a permanent 10 to 50 percent of max reserves; a toad ability cost about 8 percent (Ch 62 para 57-67; Ch 89 para 96, late). Dial: sophistication, materials, skill. Routes: reserve-anchored (stable, permanent cost), blood-anchored (heritable, instinctive), skin sigils (fragile). Failure: death or ruin; body-anchored enhancement is lost when the soul leaves the body (MLG28). **Overflow: converted** (reserve capacity is traded for the ability). Visibility: costs are told by Zorian, who had them from others. Evidence: stated. Partisan sources (MAGIC_SYSTEM gap 12).

**MLG18 Mind magic and the aranean gift.** Kind: capacity (innate) plus social. Reads: the Open gift; teacher; trust. Default: humans learn crude structured telepathy; the guild restricts mind magic; the aranea teach mind sense first, then shells, then attacks and memory work; some gifts are withheld until a full memory probe (Ch 21 para 41; Ch 37 para 6). Dial: gift, teacher. Overflow: **lost** (lessons saturate, Ch 63 para 212). Enforcer: the Guild for human mind magic; the aranea for their gift. Routes: aranean teachers for trade (information, relays, large payments); memory probe for withheld gifts. Failure: junk answers from wrong questions (Ch 43 para 28). Reason: the Guild treats mind magic as dangerous. Visibility: not shown whether the gift is detectable by the learner (U). Evidence: shown (late for the withheld rule).

**MLG19 Dimensionalism chain.** Kind: knowledge (prerequisites). Reads: dimensional boundary, then teleport (needs a visited destination), then the gate spell (very few can cast it; range short alone). Default: Ilsa teaches teleport; black rooms cost upfront and scale with volume (Ch 50 para 86, 131; Ch 23 para 159; late for the gate). Dial: reserve stamina for the gate and pocket tracks (Ch 78 para 86). Routes: beacons cut cost; paid teleports abroad (500 silver, c064 p173, late). Failure: teleport mishap; wards cause it to fail badly (Ch 26 para 283-284). Overflow: converted (time into mana-hungry training). Enforcer: academy wards key teleport hubs (Ch 6 para 187); teacher scarcity. Reason: wards disrupt dimensional spells, and the gate is rare. Visibility: the destination must be known, so the character sees its own limits. Evidence: shown/stated.

**MLG20 Simulacra count.** Kind: resource over capacity (MLX5). Reads: shared reserve, running cost. Default: 3 to 4 before regeneration goes negative; they drift after a day; the Guild watches (Ch 64 para 16; Ch 92 para 25; Ch 62 para 136, late). Overflow: **pressure** (over the limit, regen goes negative). Enforcer: the Guild watchlist (late). Dial: copy count, regeneration. Routes: golem simulacra are cheaper and tougher (Ch 93 para 36); a stabilising simulacrum can hold a damaged reserve (MLG22). Failure: negative regeneration, drift. Visibility: the caster reads their own regeneration (inferred). Evidence: stated/shown.

### E. Place and money

**MLG27 Foreknowledge half-life.** Kind: knowledge, decays (MLX3). Reads: days since divergence. **MLG28 Exit conditions.** Kind: world state, law. Reads: the Gate space (stated to reopen with the Key of five treasures), the Guardian, a death pact. Default: the keeper inspects the loop for anomalies and flags a non-Controller for deletion (Ch 90); exit by a death pact needs a kill switch and carries one person at a time (Ch 90); the heroes' exit sacrifices an orb, and the exiting soul leaves body-bound abilities behind (Ch 91-92). Reason: stated by the keeper. Visibility: the exit rules are learned late and from the keeper. Evidence: stated/shown, late. Cause and identity of the loop are not stated here (kept out of this file).

## 4. New gate shapes GTR1-GTR27 miss (c), proposed rule text MLX

GTR8 covers carrying power across worlds; none covers a repeating cut inside one world. Each rule below is a proposal, with default, dial and reason.

- **MLX1 Retention class at a cut.** A recurring cut (a loop restart, a lifetime rebirth, a season reset) reads each asset's retention class (soul-bound, template, body, nothing) and carries it by that class. Default: knowledge and skill carry, material and body do not. Dial: per asset, per world. Reason: the world's mechanism for the cut decides what it takes. Routes: carriers with capacity and decay.
- **MLX2 Held-at-start carry.** An effect that survives a cut when the holder starts the cycle holding its anchor; otherwise it lapses. Default: held artifacts keep marks, unheld ones lose them. Dial: which anchors. Reason: Guardian, Ch 83, 89. The gate reads the start-of-cycle inventory.
- **MLX3 Validity that decays with divergence.** A knowledge gate whose satisfaction decays with how far the world has diverged from the remembered one, not with the clock. Default: routine events hold for a few days after the first nudge. Dial: inertia per world. Reason: a nudge cascades. GTR13 closes a gate with a reason; this one thins continuously and reopens by re-observation.
- **MLX4 Shared finite budget outside the gate.** A budget (iterations, simulacra running cost) paid by every attempt in every gate, spent per cycle not per day, and visible to some holders (the marker holder). Default: attempts cost a cycle; idle cycles also cost. Dial: supply, cost per cycle. Reason: GTR26 limits attempts per gate; this one limits the whole cycle supply.
- **MLX5 Pooled capacity across bodies.** Copies that share one reserve read a pooled capacity, and the count limit is where regeneration turns negative. Default: 3 to 4 copies. Dial: copy cost.
- **MLX6 Trade-off ceiling between two capacities.** A gate that reads a pair, raising one lowers a neighbour (reserve versus finesse), and an overflow policy "spills into the sibling track as damage". Default: forcing growth wrecks control. Dial: the threshold, per school. Reason: the soul protects control. A GTR16 spill variant with a named victim.
- **MLX7 Mastery under perturbation.** A standing, repeatable mastery check passes when performance holds under a distractor, and fails otherwise, and unlocks the next ladder step. Default: flawless under interruption. Dial: distractor strength, setup time. This is a gate on a skill, not a one-shot attempt (GTR14, GTR26).
- **MLX8 Neighbour discount.** Progress in one family of drills lowers the cost or threshold of related gates (cross-benefit, foundation effect). Default: a small discount per mastered neighbour. Dial: family map. GTR2 reads the mechanism; this adds that mechanisms lower each other.
- **MLX9 Place-set rate.** A gate whose rate or ceiling is set by the place (well rank) rather than the character, with a downside of waste at the rich end (mages raised in Cyoria are wasteful and dulled). Default: Cyoria rich, frontier town poor. Dial: rank per place.
