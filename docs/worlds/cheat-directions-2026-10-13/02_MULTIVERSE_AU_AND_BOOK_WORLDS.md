## P1. Separate the world you enter from the power you bring

| Identity | Meaning | Cheat implication |
|---|---|---|
| Source family | A work or declared collection of works | Rules cannot silently mix books, film, games and fanworks |
| Rules profile | What this variant permits | A familiar spell name can have different mechanics |
| Continuity/universe | A persistent causal history | Counterparts have their own identities and relationships |
| Timeline branch | A history fork or alternative history | Foreknowledge can become stale; provenance matters |
| Era | The historical starting context | Teachers, stock, laws and institutions differ |
| World instance | The actual durable played state | Revisiting resumes this state rather than resetting rewards |
| Visit/life | This character's presence and embodiment | Body, custody and eligibility decide current actions |

A universe can have several branches. A branch can contain several realms. A pocket realm need not be a new universe. A start menu can create a new instance without granting the character multiverse travel. Character travel, player run selection, reincarnation and a cheat that constructs universes are distinct functions.

Library entries should identify the version of the cheat as well as the world. A prototype, repaired relic, stolen imitation and mature original can share a family without identical reach or authority. A random roll chooses from version-qualified candidates and commits the result; reopening, reload and import must preserve it.

## F2. Earth-11 is a useful example, with source boundaries

DC's [official Earth-11 description](https://www.dc.com/characters/earth-11) describes switched-gender counterparts and an altered historical influence from Amazons sharing their knowledge. Its [Teen Justice #1 listing](https://www.dc.com/comics/multiversity-teen-justice-2022/multiversity-teen-justice-1) identifies a particular Earth-11 series and roster. These bounded sources support the example; they do not establish an exhaustive or version-independent DC cosmology.

Design inference: an AU can alter identities, families, institutions, historical causation and available careers together. Gender-swapped cast alone should not force different personality or mechanics; select which consequences actually change in the authored premise. An Earth-style number is a convenient player label, not enough to define the world. Pin the source version and declared differences behind it.

## P2. Multiversal describes reach, not one strength tier

| Original cheat family | First useful function | Multiversal development | What remains a design choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Route sense | Find one reachable crossing | Chart unfamiliar worlds and return routes | Discovery versus authority to open a route |
| Translator | Interpret one unfamiliar magical notation | Rechannel methods across rules profiles | Semantic translation versus granting a power source |
| Traveling workshop | Keep one owned bench and its instruments | Maintain a workshop across lives/worlds | Cargo, staff, time, fuel and actual custody |
| Lineage seed | Leave a latent trait or prepared artifact | Deliver a creation to a selected next incarnation | Recipient identity and delivery reliability |
| Counterpart resonance | Sense one alternate self | Exchange selected experience, bodies or faculties | Consent, interference, fusion or explicitly adversarial theft |
| World garden | Sustain a small created habitat | Construct or develop independent inhabited worlds | Inhabitants, clocks, resource creation and exit rules |
| Record library | Preserve one's observations | Compare branches and share researched methods | Knowledge scope, uncertain reports and source revisions |
| Rule editor | Change one specified local rule | Negotiate, override or author broader domains | Exact authority and collision behavior |

A door that reaches many universes can be more useful for a trader than a planet-destroying attack, while being much weaker in combat. Offer tier labels for intuitive scale and optional rankings for fun, alongside separate reach, precision, autonomy, portability and authority. Do not infer unbounded power from the word multiversal.

Costs and counters are selected per cheat, not compulsory penalties. An exceptionless or overwhelming version is allowed if chosen. Where limits exist, make them follow the mechanism: a translator lacks samples; a door lacks a destination anchor; an imported creature lacks habitat; a leased network service loses contact. Arbitrary universal suppression would make travel feel like punishment.

## P3. Many Harry Potter AU premises can support different play

These are original premise families unless bound later to a named inspected fanwork. No claim is made that a specific fanwork implements them.

| Premise family | Difference to author | Cheat/acquisition opportunity | Meaningful first action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Household and relationships | Different guardians, kinship, friend/rival ties | Inheritance, access to records, borrowed artifact | Establish custody and earn or negotiate access |
| Different protagonist or parallel cast | Counterparts have distinct biographies | A latent trait or role-associated opportunity | Learn who this person is rather than assume canon trust |
| Historical divergence | An event's causes or outcome differ | Recover a lost craft tradition or research alternate methods | Compare a record with actual local evidence |
| Changed magical law | New ritual, soul, familiar or magical-source rule | Develop a new exceptional faculty | Test one small observable effect |
| Institutional AU | Schools, Ministry or secrecy function differently | Scholarship, public research, black-market tools | Choose an institution or independent route |
| Wider magical geography | Another regional tradition is central | Translation, compatible tools, cross-tradition research | Seek qualified instruction and compare techniques |
| Reincarnation or transmigration | A declared arrival/body/retention rule | Self-prepared seed, artifact, retained knowledge | Check what really survived and what needs relearning |
| Crossover arrival | An outside mechanism enters this rules profile | Imported system, companion, tool or bodily power | Demonstrate one home/outside difference |
| System or game AU | A game mechanism is real in that branch | Quests, crafting, analysis or a personal library | Use an actual first benefit rather than a decorative panel |
| Post-arc life | A branch's surviving society develops | Crafting, teaching, restoration or new research | Work on a real local need rather than await another villain |

Source distinction matters mechanically. Rowling's [Familiars note](https://www.harrypotter.com/writing-by-jk-rowling/familiars) distinguishes ordinary pets and postal owls from traditional familiars. A telepathic soul-linked familiar is therefore an explicit original/AU addition when using that source profile, not a default imported fact. Her [wand notes](https://www.harrypotter.com/writing-by-jk-rowling/wand-lengths-and-flexibility) describe the interaction of wand properties and owner experience. A cheat that appraises a wand should not automatically grant its bond, suitable source or mastery.

Multiple AUs may deliberately disagree. One can have strict familiar bonds, another temporary partnerships, another no such faculty. A named fanwork has its own author/version/chapter evidence; it is not proof for all Harry Potter worlds. A game-original variant may remix premises without representing itself as a faithful adaptation of that work.

## P4. Home and outside behavior need an actual crossing contract

Use the existing Works, Reread, Rechannel, Weakened and Blocked compatibility vocabulary. Expand the placement card with a worked first crossing rather than adding another universal conversion layer.

| Check | Questions to resolve |
|---|---|
| Carrier | Does body, soul, artifact, knowledge, remote service or local law sustain this component? |
| Destination | What world/instance is reached, and how is an unknown destination selected? |
| Source and fuel | Does power travel with stored fuel, replenish locally, convert or require its old network? |
| Information | Can the cheat read this rules profile? Does an estimate differ from verified appraisal? |
| Authority | Does it affect local law, only its holder, a selected target, or external worlds too? |
| Detection | Who could notice what trace through an actual faculty, instrument or witnessed result? |
| Counter | Which specific defense or interruption works, and under what conditions? |
| Custody and retention | Which owned objects, companions, commitments and memories cross? |
| Time | What happens to the origin world during travel and to a next-life package before arrival? |
| Failure | Does interruption strand the traveler, consume fuel, lose cargo or leave the origin unchanged? |

Outside arrival does not grant every NPC perfect detection. Conversely, concealment is not automatically perfect. A teacher may notice unusual precision without identifying an alien system; an instrument may detect energy but misclassify its origin. Social responses can include curiosity, employment, cooperation, exploitation, indifference or fear. Strong cheats need not always attract stronger enemies.

Knowledge has a portable carrier but not guaranteed applicability. Alternate rules and history can make predictions wrong without erasing the character's memory. Let the Notebook distinguish remembered source, observed branch fact, inference and tested procedure.

## P5. Prepare for the next life without a bestower

An original craft route can run: discover a retention mechanism, research a design, obtain materials, make a prototype, test a bounded transfer, designate delivery, then live with its actual outcome. The mechanism might be a soul-bound technique, an artifact cached for a future self, an inherited workshop, a preserved notebook or a self-authored system seed. These are alternatives, not five required stages of every cheat.

Preparation should provide a useful present-life application where appropriate. A storage seed can first preserve one instrument; a research archive can help current work; a teaching lineage can benefit successors even if reincarnation is uncertain. The game need not make dying immediately the best way to test every design. A verified small transfer can establish part of the process while identity-through-death remains unverified until the chosen mechanism operates.

A future incarnation is not automatically an heir, counterpart or memory copy. Specify delivery criteria, body/source compatibility, retained contents, control rights and whether the package can miss, be intercepted or be declined. A patron offer can be one route, while independent research, collaboration, reverse engineering and creation remain complete routes of their own.

## A1. A bounded playable candidate

Proposal: one original traveling research artifact in a selected HP AU. Obtain or craft it locally, use its limited record/analysis function, prepare one durable research capsule, and test it in one distinct authored variant. The two variants change one relevant magical rule, so a recorded method needs adaptation instead of silently working identically. A later death/reincarnation extension can use the same actual carrier if selected.

Required evidence: useful acquisition and first action; different declared behavior in both profiles; independently held mutable worlds; committed selection; justified observer/counter behavior; retained custody and records through save, reload and literal import; unchanged first instance after returning. Desktop/laptop first, with an explicit target viewport in a future implementation task. No browser claims are made for this report.

## P6. AU composition needs dependency checks

An AU overlay has a base revision, declared changes and affected dependencies. Changing magical education can alter teachers, qualifications and jobs; changing a death rule can alter inheritance and risk decisions. These consequences should be authored or deliberately scoped, not assumed to follow automatically from one toggle. Two overlays editing the same fact need an explicit composition result; silent last-write-wins can produce a world neither author intended.

Separate an allowed possibility from one actually selected into an instance. Every potentially surviving character need not survive in every branch. A counterpart link means correspondence, not shared inventory, trust or personal memories. A broad multiverse can reuse shared mechanics while retaining branch-specific history and versioned rule overrides.

World breadth is a content horizon; a selected playable region supplies its concrete consumers. Neither a one-region slice nor a metadata roster establishes a whole simulated universe. See [worked scenarios](08_WORKED_WORLD_AND_LIFE_SCENARIOS.md) for concrete original variants and their action/state boundaries.
