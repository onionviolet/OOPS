## S1. Harry Potter original AU: build a pattern glass

**Starting facts.** An adult original character rents a bench beside a nursery. This AU explicitly adds a craft mechanism that can record and replay a bounded magical stabilization pattern. The character has basic instruction, a cracked recording lens owned outright, two suitable blanks and one practiced stabilization method. The lens does not contain every spell, read minds or grant an absent magical source. Nursery workers need to transport fragile seedlings without losing their supported growing conditions. Exact magical mechanism, people and commercial terms are original.

**Playable sequence.** Inspect the lens, compare a known pattern with an ordinary magnifier, then choose a purpose. Repairing the existing lens produces a usable recorder. Buying a proven lens skips repair; borrowing one allows an experiment without ownership. Record the practiced stabilization method, test a nursery carrier, then decide whether to keep a personal instrument, sell a tested carrier, teach the design or prepare an heirloom version.

| Component | Concrete role |
|---|---|
| Script | Worker explains the transport problem; lens notes introduce the recording hypothesis; dialogue responds to actual test evidence |
| Rule | Recording requires a supported method the operator actually knows; replay affects the selected carrier under this AU's declared limits |
| RNG | This lens's recording capacity is sampled once at its generation; a noisy test can sample observation noise if selected |
| Timed work | Repair, calibration and established fabrication can queue; new test interpretation presents a decision |
| Choice | Purpose, purchase/repair/borrow route, testing conditions, disclosure and future holder |

**Useful effect.** A working carrier transports one actual seedling shipment that ordinary equipment cannot safely handle under the authored conditions. The recorder later supports another compatible procedure. Mastery can improve precision or target range rather than merely increasing a stat.

**Alternatives.** Refuse the research and use ordinary protective packing or hire a transport specialist. A failed calibration leaves the owned lens and an observation narrowing the viable range; it does not magically resolve the hypothesis. A failed fabrication has the selected consumed/salvaged quantities. A perfect craft variant succeeds immediately. A business version sells repeatable tools; a lone researcher keeps one private instrument; a school version teaches the method.

**Save and discovery boundary.** Keep actual lens identity, known capacity, hidden capacity, notebook evidence, reserved materials, pending job and carrier custody separate. Completing the job settles once. Reload preserves sampled properties. The player sees observed results and warranted hypotheses, not the undiscovered complete rule table. This scenario adds no sealed truth to shared reports.

## S2. Random innate cheat: resonance memory

**Starting facts.** A new character starts in an original coastal town. The selected random-life profile permits ordinary lives and several innate power families. This particular committed draw creates Resonance Memory: after deliberately examining a nonliving object, the holder can recall its physical pattern precisely. It neither learns its manufacturing method nor duplicates it by default. This character begins with the active power and a clear explanation, demonstrating that an interesting first cheat need not be dormant.

**Playable sequence.** Compare a familiar household key with a sketch, inspect a customer's damaged component, then choose a use: matching repair parts, cataloging collections, apprenticeship or investigation. Precision is immediately useful. Studying fabrication turns a preserved pattern into something the character can actually make. A declared evolution could later allow replaying the pattern into suitable material, changing the scarcity from observation to fabrication inputs.

| Component | Concrete role |
|---|---|
| Script | An original opening offers household, craft and archive applications without requiring a cosmic encounter |
| Rule | Allowed object classes, observation access, recall fidelity and actual execution abilities determine effects |
| RNG | Start generation picks the family and compatible parameters once; a later evolution rolls only at its declared milestone |
| Timed work | Ordinary repair training and cataloging can queue; recall itself can be immediate |
| Choice | Career, which objects to study, disclosure and selected development where this version permits it |

**Useful effect.** Distinguish near-identical compatible parts and repair a paid commission, or preserve a disappearing inscription for later comparison. A recollection is a character knowledge record, not an owned physical object.

**Alternatives and variants.** The holder can ignore the faculty and live an ordinary career. An absolute version records any encountered pattern perfectly; a dormant version first activates through an authored circumstance; a body-bound version is lost with that body; a soul-bound version retains selected records through the specified transition. Social discovery follows actual witnessed performance, not a universal signal announcing the cheat to everyone. No compensating enemy or drawback is required.

**Save and discovery boundary.** Persist generated card/version, backing, sampled parameters, records and actual known explanation. A player-facing random menu can show an eligible distribution without showing undiscovered character powers. Reopening a character screen cannot reroll the trait. An explicit reroll power uses its own supported operation and records which outcome it changed.

## S3. Multiversal translation: carry the function, adapt the method

**Starting facts.** An original traveler owns a pattern glass from S1 and a translation faculty. Destination B has a different authored stabilization law: patterned supports operate through a material lattice rather than the traveler's practiced magical source. The translator can derive a local implementation from inspected evidence. It does not automatically supply lattice material or fabrication skill. The travel edge freely carries this owned tool and its records; this scenario adds no toll.

**Playable sequence.** Cross into a distinct durable world instance. Try the tool on a declared test fixture and observe that the home procedure does not operate directly. Inspect a local stabilizer or obtain instruction, ask the translator for a candidate, fabricate or commission a compatible mounting, then test it on a useful local transport job. Return home and choose whether to retain two methods or pursue a genuinely unified design.

| Component | Concrete role |
|---|---|
| Script | Local craftsperson supplies a goal and offers paid teaching, collaboration or a finished mounting |
| Rule | Home and destination profiles determine translation eligibility, valid inputs and real effects |
| RNG | A chosen destination can be deterministic; an exploratory route may sample an eligible destination once on commitment |
| Timed work | Sample preparation, learning and known fabrication queue under each instance's selected clock |
| Choice | Investigate, hire, collaborate, translate, return or use an ordinary local solution |

**Useful effect.** The translated carrier completes a real destination shipment. The retained method is independently usable by a qualified local operator. A simple dictionary version only translates terminology; a powerful equalization version converts the source directly; an exceptionless version works unchanged. These are distinct cheat versions rather than arbitrary nerfs of one card.

**Failure and interaction.** Insufficient evidence produces an explicit unresolved candidate, not a confident fabricated recipe. A noisy translator reports its confidence and may need a second sample. Local observers can notice an unfamiliar tool or a successful transport without identifying the traveler as multiversal. A selected counter might disrupt the lattice; it affects this mechanism, not every foreign power automatically. Refusal leaves travel and ordinary commerce viable.

**Save and discovery boundary.** Preserve the same carried object, its custody and version, both methods, evidence, translation commitment and independent stocks/people in each instance. Return resumes the original world under its declared absence clock. Buying a counterpart lens is a second object, not duplication of the original. A failed crossing follows its selected cargo/return rules. Save/import must preserve destination choice and cannot reset first-visit claims.

## S4. Build a source for a future life: the continuity workshop

**Starting facts.** An original reincarnation AU explicitly supports a soul signature across bodies and a source capable of carrying a small workshop through that transition. The character has researched this mechanism and owns a modest repair bench. They do not yet have a finished traveling workshop. The player chooses a project intended for the same soul's later incarnation, with an optional successor fallback. This is different from an estate automatically recognizing the player account.

**Playable sequence.** Select one useful bench function, construct a continuity anchor, test cargo retention across an available bounded transition, then add either records, a compatible tool or an automatic lesson. Assign recipient and fallback rules. Continue using the workshop now. At an authored retirement/death/rebirth transition, resolve what actually transfers. The new body examines the received workshop and chooses to use, modify, hand over or decline it.

| Component | Concrete role |
|---|---|
| Script | Research leads explain known mechanism and unknowns; life-end manifest reports readiness; next-life discovery introduces actual contents |
| Rule | Carrier, soul recognition, destination compatibility, custody and selected transition define retained objects/functions |
| RNG | Unsteered next life can sample body/location at the transition's commitment point; an authored anchor may constrain or guarantee it |
| Timed work | Known construction, recording and provisioning queue; a workshop continues after death only if its actual operator/source supports it |
| Choice | Current-life utility, package, recipient, recognition policy, fallback and successor acceptance |

**Useful effect.** The current character completes repair work with the bench. The next compatible holder receives that actual tool plus a learned procedure recording, enabling a repair they could not otherwise perform. The recording may teach over time; it does not grant mastery unless the selected source explicitly does so.

**Failure and alternatives.** An unfinished anchor leaves an ordinary usable bench and notes in the old world. An incompatible future body may still read the record while lacking manual execution; it can seek a collaborator. A selected fallback can entrust the physical workshop to an apprentice. The recipient can decline the project without deleting its history. A perfect continuity source can carry the whole finished workshop immediately. A no-reincarnation variant delivers through an heir or institution instead.

**Save and discovery boundary.** Keep created, tested, preserved, designated, pending delivery, delivered and usable as distinct facts. A limited cargo test proves its own transfer, not rebirth survival. Persist next-life draw, recipient recognition and one-time transfer while allowing deliberate copying if a power actually grants it. Never both retain and transfer one physical workshop accidentally. The new character's explanation follows retained memory or records, not the player's recollection alone.

## P1. Compose AU overlays by their causal content

An authored composition can combine a household change, an earlier era and a new recording law. Each overlay declares its touched definitions/actors/events, prerequisites and intended effects. Compute the selected start snapshot and validate conflicts before creating the life. Do not silently apply whichever overlay happens to load last.

| Overlay interaction | Resolution example |
|---|---|
| Independent changes | New guardian plus recording law can coexist if each supplies its own valid starting facts |
| Shared resource | Two overlays both promise the same unique lens; author one custodian or deliberately create distinct counterpart objects |
| Conflicting truth | One law binds powers to bodies, another carries all faculties with souls; choose an explicit precedence or hybrid version |
| Removed prerequisite | A historical divergence removes the teacher used by an acquisition script; replace the route by authored research/another actual teacher, or mark that route absent |
| Changed causation | An institutional reform makes the old clandestine auction unnecessary; update its motive, route and participant plans rather than forcing the scene |
| Actor identity | An earlier-era character cannot inherit a later actor's age, reputation and training simply because their names match |

These resolutions are part of authoring, not a demand that the player fix data conflicts. A valid composite can still contain uncertainty, rivalry and contested knowledge as intentional play.

## P2. Bind named fanworks individually after inspection

A fanwork profile should identify title, author, supplied edition/version, inspected range, relevant premise, supported rule evidence and deliberate game additions. No named HP fanwork was inspected in this pass, so these scenarios must not be attributed to one. Naming a famous title from memory would not make its rules verified.

For a later inspected work, map each selected scenario to chapter-supported eligibility and effects. If its magic differs, create a distinct profile and card version. If only the protagonist has a unique device, do not give every unrelated entrant that device by default. A direct-adaptation mode can initialize its declared role; an outsider mode supplies a different route; a remix explicitly labels altered rules. Conflicting fanwork profiles remain separate until an authored crossover resolves their interaction.
