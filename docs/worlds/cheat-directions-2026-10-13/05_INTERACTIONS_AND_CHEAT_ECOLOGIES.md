## P1: Cheats may create, alter or reproduce other cheats

The important distinction is the resulting object. An inventor can discover a
method without owning the faculty that performs it. A copy can reproduce output
without transferring a donor's source. A fusion can add a mode while preserving
the original sources, or consume them to create a new source. These are different
acquisitions and deserve different provenance.

| Route | Concrete original example | Necessary local answer |
|---|---|---|
| Research | A lifetime of studying storage produces a method for making a portable pocket | Does the notebook carry sufficient instructions, or must a later life rediscover part of the process? |
| Manufacture | An artifact engraves another artifact with a supported faculty | Is it duplicating its own source, embedding a dependent extension, or making an independently powered item? |
| Editing | A wording power changes an existing cheat from "seen movement" to "understood movement" | Which definition can it edit, who authorized the change, and what happens to already committed uses? |
| Fusion | Appraisal plus a craft faculty makes a device that measures and repairs a material | Does each source remain independently usable? Can the fusion be undone? |
| Cultivation/evolution | A living cheat develops a new organ that grants another function | Who controls the result, and does inheritance copy the function or only its possibility? |
| Granting | A source-holder teaches, awakens or gives another person a power | Is this teaching, a revocable lease, a permanent gift or a dependent link? |
| Absolute creation | A declared authoring power creates a functioning cheat outright | What exactly is within its declared domain? No obligatory resource cost or loophole is added. |

Creation can be mundane craft, extraordinary research or immediate omnipotent
action in a chosen mode. Do not force all of it through patrons, currency, slots,
sacrificial materials or an invented cap. An unlimited creator can legitimately
produce unlimited independent copies if that is its actual premise.

For next-life preparation, preserve a **deliverable**: an artifact, method,
seeded bloodline, soul alteration, legal claim, living companion, prepared body,
world institution or transferable authority. Record its carrier and intended
recipient. A library entry documenting the design is not itself possession in a
future life. Some modes may explicitly make account-library selection the carrier.

## P2: Combination should name the operation

"I have both" need not mean "fused." Combinations can be independent, chained,
additive, multiplicative, highest-only, suppressing, translating, parasitic or a
new fused source. Choose only what the selected premise needs.

Original example: a multiplier and a clone power could multiply each clone's
output, multiply the creation of clones, divide one enhanced pool among them or
leave clones unaffected. None follows solely from the word multiplier. In an
unlimited variant every clone may receive the full unlimited function.

Recursive growth can be the intended pleasure. If a copied factory creates more
copying factories, represent useful stages and batches rather than iterating an
unbounded recursive call. Engine execution budgets are technical controls; they
must not quietly become a fictional energy bill or acquisition cap.

## P3: Time, fate and identity need particular collision answers

| Interaction | Decision that determines actual play | Observable original result |
|---|---|---|
| Rewind meets duplication | Which branch/body returns, and which memory carrier survives? | Both selves persist, one is replaced, or the copy only remains as a remembered life |
| Fate prediction meets free alteration | Prediction of a conditional future, fixed destination or authority enforcing events? | A forecast becomes stale after intervention, remains valid under stated conditions, or the destination still occurs by a changed route |
| Resurrection meets body theft | Which soul, controller and body does revival restore? | A restored body exists beside an occupied old body; possession is not silently erased |
| Soul-bound item meets ownership theft | Theft of possession, bond, legal claim or the source's own recognition? | A thief can hold an unusable item, acquire the real bond, or trigger a return depending on mechanism |
| Perfect concealment meets perfect appraisal | Exact domains, declared hierarchy and selected collision rule | One wins, domains do not overlap, or the scenario supplies an explicit unresolved/branch outcome |
| World rewrite meets a portable archive | Is the archive inside the rewritten domain? Is historical truth, memory or documentation changed? | The archive survives as a discrepant record, is rewritten too, or remains a record of a genuine earlier branch |

There is no universal correct answer to two incompatible absolutes. The scenario
must define how the declarations coexist before the encounter is settled. Do not
invent a last-minute immunity or choose the order that makes the plot convenient.
For an AU, document the changed collision rule as part of the AU premise.

## P4: NPCs and civilizations can build around cheats

Cheat-holders need not be enemies or rivals. A farmer, archivist, guild, city,
family, habitat or distributed institution can hold or operate one. Their access
and decisions follow their particular means and knowledge, not the player's
complete authoring record.

| Ecology | What changes | Play beyond combat |
|---|---|---|
| Reliable duplication | Reproducible goods become abundant; scarce land, attention or unreproducible services may still matter | Choose open distribution, a business, private comfort or selective aid |
| Revival infrastructure | Death and risk mean different things within the supported domain | Medical work, exploration, rescue, migration and negotiating access |
| Public divination | Forecasts become institutions and markets; actions taken on forecasts may change their basis | Test claims, sell advice, corroborate evidence or choose privacy |
| Distributed storage | Supply routes change where supported endpoints exist | Build networks, prepare refuges, repair nodes or live without the network |
| Broad granting | New capacities can spread through education, manufacture or direct gifts | Found a school, create a tradition, spread a technology or remain solitary |
| Absolute abundance | Some scarcity can actually end | Authorship, relationships, exploration and chosen projects remain possible without forcing a new shortage |

Civilizational consequences should follow reach, adoption and elapsed time. One
hidden household artifact does not instantly transform a continent. A publicly
available universal source can transform it quickly. A powerful holder can also
choose an ordinary quiet life; compulsory cosmic policing would be invented balance.

Off-screen holders should execute bounded supported policies, leave evidence and
retain actual consequences. Coarse simulation must not gift them every optimal
move or knowledge of hidden player choices. Detailed adversaries can use the same
declared commands when relevant; no continent-wide optimizer is required first.

## P5: Concealment, counterfeit and counters are optional particular functions

Separate the source's truth from an observer's belief. A false appraisal result
may be a genuine detector failure, fabricated report, tuned decoy or altered
target. These need different counter-investigations. A rumor of a weakness is
not confirmation that the weakness exists.

An artifact can be genuinely powerful but falsely attributed; counterfeit but
useful; empty; dependent on a hidden operator; or completely honest. Acquisition
without a being's offer can use salvage, ordinary trade, research, inheritance,
craft, emergent growth, accidents or direct selection in a chosen sandbox mode.
Neither fraud nor a secret drawback is compulsory.

A counter targets an actual operation: deny observation to a visual copier,
sever a required channel, alter a forecast's assumptions or remove a supported
revival domain. If a stronger variant supplies its own information/channel/domain,
that counter can stop working. Some powers can have no counter in the selected
world. Weak methods do not need contrived victories; their earlier accessibility,
history or sentimental value may be enough, or they may become obsolete.

## P6: Living and autonomous cheats have their own participation

Distinguish an interface voice, advice engine, persistent agent, living organism,
independent person and collective. A talkative panel does not automatically have
its own goals; a person does not become an obedient tool merely by being called a
cheat. Companions can honestly help without a betrayal arc.

Useful local questions: who chooses actions; what information it actually reads;
whether it can act while the holder sleeps or dies; whether it can refuse; what
it remembers after copying; and whether two copies are separate people. Delegated
authority is separate from physical custody and source ownership. A next-life
companion might wait, choose a new host or continue an independent life.

Keep the interface simple by default: request, advice, action permission and
result. Deeper programming is optional. Autonomous action should identify who
acted and what changed rather than hiding spending inside flavor text.

## P7: Discovery can stay deep without spoilers

Public library browsing can show learned cards, evidence, known routes and
unconfirmed claims. Authoring truth, sealed future variants and exact unseen
enemy capacities belong in a separate projection. Hidden counts, search matches,
disabled controls, tooltips and receipts can leak the same truths as prose.

An informative failure reveals only what that experiment could demonstrate.
"No usable response" does not prove a fake artifact when compatibility, support
or knowledge remain unresolved. A perfect detector can reveal exact truth when
the chosen power truly grants it; mystery is not an automatic defense.

## P8: Practical runtime contract and known gaps

Use the existing causal phases: eligibility, non-mutating preview, commitment,
ordered resolution, observer updates and aftermath. Retain source identity,
definition revision, actual holder/controller, chosen mode, causal receipt and
declared retention. Costs may be empty. Preserve optional unlimited/absolute modes
with semantic rules rather than JavaScript numeric Infinity.

Current `finite`/`natural` validators use finite safe numbers. Numerical overflow
is not evidence of a fictional cosmic event; a future huge-number representation
or symbolic unlimited effect needs an explicit data/save decision. Likewise,
current source grants reference registry definitions. General runtime cheat
editing, generated definitions, recursive provenance, autonomous policy and
cross-world identity/time collision need selected adapters and tests before
claiming them implemented. A definition update must preserve pending commitments
or explicitly migrate them; it cannot silently replace their meaning.

## S1: A finite suggested playable slice

**Original proposal: the pattern workshop.** In one selected world, acquire a
recording artifact through salvage or ordinary purchase. Use it to record one
supported material operation. Research or a preselected stronger artifact turns
the record into one useful implement. Borrowing and owning the original remain
different states. One NPC workshop uses its own instance and only its own evidence.
This proves acquisition, useful output, simple combination, custody, independent
holder and a durable next-life preparation artifact without implementing a
multiverse, second prestige layer or every cheat family.

Target viewport if implemented: 1366×768 laptop. Visible result: an earned
artifact action, a known pattern, a usable crafted implement and a compact
provenance/observed-result record in existing panels. Mobile, world-scale economy,
general cheat generation and full autonomous personalities are deferred.

Acceptance: fresh rebuilt origin; both acquisition routes or the selected route
plus genuine refusal; useful downstream operation; borrowed versus owned result;
no source grant from merely browsing the library; repeat-settlement protection;
and Save/reload/Export/literal Import of the actual resulting artifact. Future-life
handoff stays design-only until a selected supported carrier route can be exercised.
An absolute variant should demonstrate its declared superiority directly if chosen.

## P9: Define an interaction only at the domain it changes

A collision resolver should first identify effects that can actually interact: target, timing, authority domain, backing and changed state. Two impressive powers need no special collision if they affect unrelated things. Shared labels such as “time” do not prove overlap; stopping a body's motion and rewinding a world instance are different operations.

Declare invariants for the selected mechanism, not universal fairness rules. A unique artifact cannot be in two custody locations unless duplication or branching creates another supported instance. A settled action cannot publish twice. An absolute power can legitimately override ordinary defenses in its declared domain. A changed rule must specify whether it affects pending commitments, future actions or prior history.

General templates handle ordinary overlap; unusual interactions receive authored exceptions or a new resolver. An unsupported combination should be identified in authoring before it is advertised as playable. This preserves future possibility without fabricating the present capability. See [grammar and exceptions](07_POSSIBILITY_GRAMMAR_AND_AUTHORING.md) and [interaction coverage](09_COVERAGE_VALIDATION_AND_IMPLEMENTATION_ORDER.md).
