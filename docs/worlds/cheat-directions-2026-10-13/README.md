## Synthesis

**D1. A cheat library is a library of playable rule changes.** A name, rarity and bonus are insufficient for a cheat that adds simulation, reincarnation, clones, systems or territory. A reusable card needs its actual function, acquisition forms, backing, activation, information rules, growth, interactions, scope and presentation. The same family can support many distinct cards, and one card may belong to several families.

**D2. Keep several readings of tier.** Output, information access, reliability, autonomy, portability, authority and growth potential answer different questions. Named rarity grades and fun power ladders can coexist with those axes, including an actual absolute scale when a cosmology declares one. Being rare does not prove strength; crossing many worlds does not prove stronger attacks; rewriting one local rule does not prove universal authority.

**D3. Randomness selects coherent possibilities.** Select a start/world mode, eligible cheat families, compatible acquisition and backing, then authored parameters and growth. Validate the result against the intended profile. Preserve genuine outliers, ordinary lives and unchecked powers where selected; do not silently force equal difficulty. Store the generated instance and pending outcomes so restoration keeps the same power rather than rerolling it. Reveal details through actual known information.

**D4. Acquisition need not involve an offering being.** Nature, anomalies, bodies, research, crafting, accidents, predecessors, ruins, institutions and player action can supply powers. A patron can be generous, mistaken, transactional, manipulative or absent. Provenance, ownership and revocability differ: accepting an offer does not automatically create a revocable loan. Free gifts and costless powers are legitimate authored forms.

**D5. A multiverse is more than a world picker.** Each continuity can have different history, people, institutions and fundamental laws. Distinguish a separate universe, an alternate timeline and an instance of the same definition. Give each foreign power an actual outside behavior where needed. An AU change should propagate to consequences rather than leave every relationship and event unchanged. Canon, source-qualified fanwork, original AU and crossover are separate premises; none automatically overrides the others.

**D6. Preparing a cheat can be a life goal.** The player may build a relic, preserve a method, establish a school, engineer a body or soul faculty, create a system, or arrange a future opportunity. Separately define what exists, whom it recognizes, how it survives, where it is delivered and what remains unfinished. A fresh random protagonist need not be the recipient. A later stranger can find an old creation; a planned successor can refuse it; a former enemy can inherit it. These are authored possibilities, not mandatory failures.

**D7. Cheat creation can become recursive gameplay.** A copy power may help collect components for a self-made system; a simulator can test a succession plan; a forge may produce a future-life artifact; a multiversal library may exchange methods across variants. Creating, editing and interpreting rules are different capabilities. Runtime effect resolution must define relevant identity, event order, ownership, persistence and randomness rather than treating all powers as additive numbers.

## Connected illustration

An original AU character finds an unusual minor relic. Research reveals one useful function. Through work, teaching or crafting, the character improves it into a durable tool and prepares instructions for a later holder. The life ends or retires. A successor can claim it if the selected route supports that; a fresh stranger might instead encounter the old workshop, while another continuity contains a counterpart creation with different history and rules.

This connects random starts, artifact acquisition, research, cheat growth, future-life preparation and AU variation without requiring a patron or automatic reincarnation. It is one optional example, not the mandatory opening or an implemented journey. Other profiles can begin with a powerful active cheat, no cheat, a natural trait or an afterlife grant.

## How to script broad possibility without writing every combination

**M1. Author a vocabulary of actual operations.** Observe, learn, produce, move, repair, copy, bind, transfer and preserve need effect contracts with real consumers. Some operations already have bounded implementations; new temporal, identity or rule-authoring operations require selected engineering. A vocabulary is extensible, not a fictional ban on unsupported ideas.

**M2. Compose mechanisms into content cards.** A card references operations and declares participants, target rules, acquisition, backing, timing, chance policy, persistence and known information. Common patterns can compile to the existing registry shapes where compatible. The existing `compileStructuredPack` validates structured definitions; it explicitly does not provision actors/stock, create timers or sample a random start. A compact-card compiler and its runtime bindings remain future work.

**M3. Script meaningful decisions and unusual exceptions.** An investigation, inheritance dispute, breakthrough or patron negotiation can use a scene graph, existing narrative tooling where appropriate, and real commands. Each branch states what changes if accepted, refused, postponed, interrupted or solved another way where those choices belong to the premise. Do not manufacture five choices for every instant grant or simple tool.

**M4. Instantiate a coherent selected world.** Bind rules profile, source version, AU changes, world identity, actors, stock, clocks and start facts. Generate only selected unknowns, then retain them. Derived consequences need an explicit implementation or authored update; changing one premise is not proof that the entire world's causality updates automatically.

**M5. Verify behavior at the right layer.** A definition can be listed, source-grounded, validated, executable, playable and persisted at different times. Track these states independently. Template checks can cover many content instances; targeted tests must cover changed rules and critical collisions; browser proof covers connected player journeys. Neither a huge catalogue nor a test count proves all possibilities work.

### A practical authoring loop

Choose one intended experience; list its useful actions and outputs; bind a source or original premise; map each behavior to a supported operation; write its project/scene transitions; qualify RNG and clock boundaries; instantiate it; exercise its useful result and persistence. Add an operation only when a selected experience actually needs it. Then reuse the proven pattern in another genuinely different case.

Example: “leave a teaching artifact for a later stranger” combines creation, record preservation, physical custody, character knowledge and later learning. “leave a soul-linked capsule for your next incarnation” additionally needs identity recognition and an actual rebirth delivery edge. Shared steps are reusable; the extra mechanism must be real before the stronger card is advertised as working.

### What broad coverage means

The design space is open-ended. A finite current release can cover its identified families, profiles, entry routes and meaningful interactions, plus explicit gaps. It cannot exhaust every possible story, parameter combination or future source. Source census and world-completion owners define the selected world horizon; these notes do not shrink that horizon or turn every proposed multiversal feature into its immediate completion gate.

An unsupported concept stays on the design horizon with the missing operation named. A listed but nonfunctional promise is not a substitute. Conversely, a general operation can support many unenumerated combinations if its actual semantics cover them. The goal is reliable breadth with unique authored detail where it matters, rather than either infinite manual scripting or an empty generic framework.
