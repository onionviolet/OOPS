# O. Cheat origins without a required patron

2026-10-13. Design exploration for Weibao's request for first cheats, libraries, randomness, multiversal variants and preparing future lives. Proposals only. No gameplay, accepted policy, canon acquisition route or new implementation assignment is established here.

## O1. Recommendation and authority

Make exceptional opportunities part of a world's ecology. Beings can offer them, but so can unusual natural processes, surviving artifacts, institutions, research and things the player creates. A cheat does not require a chooser, quest issuer or hidden benefactor. An impersonal opportunity need not secretly become a patron encounter later.

Keep ordinary-life and cheat-focused openings possible. For a cheat-focused opening, provide a reachable early lead or an immediately usable starting power; refusal should remain meaningful. Exact distributions and timing are unresolved. Randomness can pick circumstances while choices determine investigation, acquisition and development. Do not make every technique a cheat merely because it was difficult to learn: distinguish locally ordinary competence, an unusually effective application, and an explicit rule-changing exception.

The accepted [rules](../../RULES_FROM_SCRATCH.md) allow free carry and generic conversion where the relevant world, edge, cheat or mode permits them. A physical deposit or paid handover is one possible next-life route, not a universal restriction. An authored direct grant, free transfer or immediate conversion needs no invented transport chore or tax.

## O2. Origins, mechanisms and stories are separate

An acquisition record should answer separate questions:

| Axis | Example | Why separate it? |
|---|---|---|
| Creation | A scholar constructed a lens | Explains how it came to exist |
| Previous custody | It belonged to an institution | Explains claimants and history |
| Acquisition | The player recovered it from a wreck | Explains how this holder obtained it |
| Backing | An internal reservoir and etched array | Explains what actually operates it |
| Fuel | Ambient local magic or carried charge | Explains operation away from home |
| Rights | Owned object; copied design licensed separately | Custody does not automatically convey every permission |
| Knowledge | Holder knows the lens works, not who built it | Avoids author truth leaking into the notebook |

A patron can sell a self-powered artifact. That does not necessarily make the artifact patron-backed. An apparently natural gift can actually draw remote fuel. A famous origin does not prove superior performance; an anonymous workshop can build something excellent. Actual provenance, in-world claims and demonstrated performance remain distinct. Unknown origin should still permit useful experiments and meaningful play.

## O3. Origin palette

| Code | Origin | Acquisition play | First useful benefit | Development and consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OA1 | Innate variation | Notice signs, test, train, seek diagnosis | One controlled unusual sense or action | Precision, new uses, changed body; family resemblance need not prove exact inheritance |
| OA2 | Natural phenomenon | Observe a cycle, prepare a site, survive or harness exposure | Brief local advantage or a durable change | Repeatable harvesting, stabilization, ecological depletion or migration |
| OA3 | Emergent interaction | Combine individually ordinary methods under an unusual condition | A new composite function | Discover why it works; reproducibility, compatibility and ownership differ |
| OA4 | Self-invention | Identify a problem, form hypotheses, build and test | A narrow working prototype | Efficiency, robustness, replication, portability; negative results remain useful evidence |
| OA5 | Lost or abandoned artifact | Recover, inspect, repair, establish usable access | Limited surviving function | Repair, modification, independent use; former owners may still have claims |
| OA6 | Inheritance or bequest | Receive actual belongings, notes, bindings or a prepared route | A working tool or actionable lead | Interpret the predecessor's work; distinguish inherited possession from inherited mastery |
| OA7 | Purchase, exchange or service | Find a real supplier, compare, pay or barter | An item, recipe, instruction or admission | Financing, maintenance and supply chains; money buys what that seller actually has |
| OA8 | Theft, scavenging or capture | Obtain custody and learn how to operate it | Whatever function remains usable | Locks, severable links, rivals, counterfeit parts; taking a holder's item need not copy their innate gift |
| OA9 | Collective creation | Join a laboratory, workshop, lineage project or mutual aid network | A shared facility or jointly authored method | Attribution, access schedules, succession, disagreements and spin-off inventions |
| OA10 | Impersonal rule or process | Meet an actual threshold, ritual rule or transformation condition | A known or initially uncertain result | Discover the process; decide whether it works for others and whether it consumes scarce inputs |
| OA11 | Cross-world contact | Compare incompatible local rules, recover transported material, construct a bridge | A bounded translation or combined technique | Import dependencies, conversion loss and new manufacture; crossing a border alone grants nothing |
| OA12 | Prepared future-life source | Deposit an object, preserve research, cultivate a carrier or arrange a real handover | A later character encounters the prepared means | Delivery, recognition and activation must each work; preparation does not prove receipt |

These are composable routes, not mutually exclusive cheat types. A natural crystal can become a researched artifact, be sold, stolen, inherited and eventually redesigned for another world. Its family remains defined by what it lets a holder do.

## O4. Opportunity ecology and economy

Where powers come from should change everyday life. A reproducible exceptional method can create schools, repair trades, ingredient supply, competitors and public infrastructure. A unique object instead encourages custody disputes, concealment, copying attempts and dependence on one holder. Neither outcome is universally preferable.

| Question | Proposed treatment |
|---|---|
| What creates supply? | Separate naturally occurring resources, manufacturable devices, teachable methods and one-off anomalies. |
| Why is access uneven? | Use actual geography, knowledge, capital, institutions, compatibility or scarcity. Avoid generic rarity as the whole explanation. |
| Can NPCs obtain it? | Use the same authored route and dependencies where applicable; do not imply universal access to singular objects. |
| Can it spread? | Record what can be copied: design, fuel, training, bond or actual power. A blueprint does not duplicate missing components. |
| Does mass adoption make it ordinary? | It can become standard locally while remaining an exceptional import elsewhere. Mechanical effects need not vanish with its label. |
| Can a monopoly be challenged? | Independent invention, substitute methods, second-hand supply and collective access can coexist with exclusive sources. |
| Is it always transferable? | No; item custody, learned knowledge, bodily transformation and linked authority have different handover requirements. |
| Are rivals automatically enemies? | No; another holder may be a seller, collaborator, teacher, dependent or competitor. |
| Does every acquisition attract retaliation? | No; attention follows observers, detectability and local motives. Quiet useful gains are allowed. |

Do not make every origin cursed or every gain demand an equal penalty. A cost can be already paid, negligible, manageable, nonmaterial or absent. Constraints should follow the particular premise and chosen mode rather than impose a universal fairness tax.

## O5. Reliable and random routes

Recommend randomizing the reachable opportunity ecology, then preserving its facts. Generate a life, applicable source routes, ownership, compatibility and clues together. Do not reroll an artifact's truth every time the player examines it or reloads a save.

| Profile | Randomness | Player influence | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cheat-focused opening | Starting source or early reachable lead varies | Pursue, decline, trade or redirect development | Another authored opportunity can remain available, without promising the same rare power |
| Ordinary life | Exceptional opportunities may be absent initially | Build resources, research, travel, join institutions | Useful ordinary competence supports continued play |
| High-variance mystery | Identity, mechanism and accessibility vary | Investigate and experiment; use partial functions | Named profile communicates variability without exposing sealed truths |
| Chosen premise | Player selects the desired source or function | Shape limitations, acquisition route and world fit | Selection is recorded as chosen access, not fabricated earned history |

Rarity of occurrence, probability of discovery, acquisition difficulty and strength are separate. A common source may be hard to use; a rare source may be modest. A reliable route can lead to the same useful function through a different mechanism, rather than handing out a duplicate unique object.

Preparation should affect relevant opportunities through real means. Joining a workshop changes contacts and equipment; surveying ruins finds clues; collecting samples improves a research question. It should not secretly increase every unrelated rare drop. Chance outcomes remain chance outcomes unless the particular cheat can alter them.

## O6. Offering beings are one route, not the default explanation

Treat trust and mechanical dependence separately. A kind teacher can have limited reach; an unfriendly merchant can sell an irrevocable working tool; a dishonest sponsor can still reveal some accurate information. Terms, actual backing, incentives, competence and demonstrated behavior all matter.

Before accepting an offer, show what the character can know: proposed benefit, visible obligations, known dependence, acceptance action and known exit terms. Unknown terms may exist in an appropriate premise, but the interface must not label the offer verified or safe without evidence. Refusal, negotiation, independent inspection, comparison and obtaining an alternative are possible content.

The [existing gift spectrum](../../CHEAT_COSMOLOGY.md#patron-gifts-what-can-and-cant-be-taken-back) already allows irrevocable gifts. Do not turn all grants into revocable leases. Conversely, deleting a patron's dialogue does not detach a power's operating link. Becoming independent can mean learning a method, replacing remote fuel, purchasing rights or rebuilding a source; each requires the actual mechanism to permit it.

## O7. Original connected acquisition journeys

These examples reveal no existing sealed truth and make no canon claims.

| Journey | Choices and first use | Further directions |
|---|---|---|
| Natural resonance survey | Ordinary work reveals a measurable recurring phenomenon. Compare sites, prepare equipment and tune a damaged recorder. First use identifies a material property useful in the same workshop. | A mobile recorder, shared survey service, self-powered design, better precision, environmental changes. No being needs to grant it. |
| The unfinished bequest | Receive notes plus a partly functional instrument. Use it as-is, fund repairs, learn to reconstruct it, lend it or sell it. First use solves a concrete local problem. | Complete a predecessor's research, challenge their mistaken theory, preserve the design for another life. Owning it grants no automatic expertise. |
| Independent method | Practice reveals a repeatable interaction between two known techniques. Test alternatives and demonstrate the resulting function on a useful task. | Keep it private, publish locally, teach, license or build a device. Others may reproduce it if the required means exist. |
| Prepared crossing | In one world, design a source for a particular later environment. Test local function and arrange transport or storage. | The next life must actually retrieve and operate it. Failed delivery, incomplete fit or successful adaptation produces distinct continuations. |

For an HP-like AU, a researcher-built inherited device can be an original addition under the declared AU premise. For canon-grounded HP, verify the relevant acquisition and magical rules with its world owner before selecting a mechanism. Reusing a world's names is not source verification. Exact franchise cases belong to the source-qualified world lanes.

## O8. Useful presentation and authoring checks

Keep an opportunity's notebook entry separate from a held cheat's control. A lead can say what was observed and which actions are available; an acquired power should show its useful action, known fuel, current source, usable/dormant state and relevant next investigation. Unknown origin can display as unknown. Loss of the object does not erase already learned facts or the player's acquisition history.

If later selected for UI implementation: desktop/laptop primary, proposed target 1440 × 900; show a known lead, investigation beside ordinary work, a useful first action and the record of its acquisition without an empty catalogue of future secrets. Mobile optimization is deferred. An alternate laptop viewport should be chosen from the active UI owner rather than adding a separate mobile gate.

Before a selected route is called playable, prove reachable means, acquisition once, useful outcome, cancellation/refusal where supported, retained investigation, and separate possession/knowledge after loss. Rebuild and use a fresh test origin; persistence changes require save/reload/export/import and an explicit compatibility decision. Preparing a future-life source additionally needs delivery, recipient and actual useful operation proof. These checks are future gates, not work performed here.

## O10. Script a supply process, not just an acquisition button

A source can be a fixed unique object, a recurring natural event, a manufactured product, a teachable method or a distributed service. Each has different depletion and recurrence semantics. A single find should not respawn on every visit unless its process creates replacements. A teachable method need not disappear when one student learns it. A reusable grant may deliberately create independent copies.

Record whether preparation changes supply, access, discovery or activation. Those are different causal effects. Buying surveying equipment may improve searching without increasing the world's artifact count. Founding a workshop may actually create new sources. An empty supplier can offer a future commission rather than produce fictional stock at checkout.

Offers and investigations become authored entry points into those processes. Source truth stays stable while clues and knowledge develop. Unknown provenance can remain unknown without preventing practical use. The [authoring grammar](07_POSSIBILITY_GRAMMAR_AND_AUTHORING.md) and [worked scenarios](08_WORKED_WORLD_AND_LIFE_SCENARIOS.md) develop these processes into explicit actions.
