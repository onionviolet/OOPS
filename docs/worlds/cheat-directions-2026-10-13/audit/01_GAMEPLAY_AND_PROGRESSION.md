# Gameplay and progression audit

2026-10-13. Independent bounded audit requested through Weibao's subagent request. Reviewed live PROJECT standing priorities, USER_VISION's anything-is-possible and balance directions, the synthesis and reports 01, 03, 04, 06, 08 and 10. Documentation only. These findings extend proposals; they neither select mechanics nor authorize a runtime implementation. No existing sealed content is described.

Authority: [PROJECT](../../../../PROJECT.md#standing-priorities-and-document-authority), [USER_VISION](../../../USER_VISION.md#anything-is-possible-2026-10-09). Canon-first priority, ordinary and dominant lives, costless powers, world-local absolutes, random entry, desktop/laptop priority and one-realm/one-prestige delivery remain intact. The existing proposals already preserve these directions. This audit found omissions and design tensions, not an established runtime bug or a demonstrated accepted-direction conflict.

## Findings and proposed dispositions

### F1. Worked content understates the intended range of play

**Location:** [08 S1–S4](../08_WORKED_WORLD_AND_LIFE_SCENARIOS.md#s1-harry-potter-original-au-build-a-pattern-glass), [03 O7](../03_ORIGINS_WITHOUT_PATRONS.md#o7-original-connected-acquisition-journeys), [10 PG5](../10_RULE_DRIVEN_PROCEDURAL_GENERATION.md#pg5-worked-original-generated-instance).

**Classification:** coverage gap, not a conflict. The examples repeatedly compare, record, repair or transport something around a workshop. These are concrete and useful, but another recording lens does not establish that the same authoring model handles combat power, exploration, social institutions, wish-like authority or deliberately dominant play.

**Counterexample:** a character starts with unconditional command over weather. Repairing a component or researching activation does not express that premise. Making them earn control through calibration would weaken the chosen cheat without authority.

**Disposition:** retain the existing examples and add contrasting vertical stories. Require a genuine difference in decisions, affected entities and consequences, not a different name. E1–E3 below exercise environmental authority, stochastic transformation and delayed inheritance. A later source-qualified combat or social scenario should be authored by its world owner, without claiming this audit inspected a franchise.

### F2. Backward-planned opportunities can make the whole world orbit the player

**Location:** [10 PG3–PG5](../10_RULE_DRIVEN_PROCEDURAL_GENERATION.md#pg3-construct-by-dependencies-rather-than-roll-everything-independently), [03 O4](../03_ORIGINS_WITHOUT_PATRONS.md#o4-opportunity-ecology-and-economy).

**Classification:** design risk and proposal. Generating a useful situation for a cheat helps onboarding. If every new town happens to need exactly the current power, however, the player stops exploring a world and starts following a succession of custom demonstrations.

**Counterexample:** every place visited by a perfect duplicator has one missing part. The duplicator never encounters ordinary recreation, a dispute copying cannot resolve, an already prosperous region or another duplicator's industry.

**Disposition:** distinguish context generation from optional matching. Generate independent local facts and actor goals first where the profile promises an independent world. Then identify opportunities supported by current powers. Author a deliberately tailored tutorial as tailored. Track unaddressed situations without making them mandatory chores. A strong character may simply solve an encountered problem; the game does not have to invent stronger resistance.

### F3. Every new observation should not become a queue interruption

**Location:** [06 D5](../06_AUTHORING_RNG_AND_IDLE_PROJECTS.md#d5-grind-should-have-a-function), [04 P9](../04_PREPARING_CHEATS_FOR_NEXT_LIVES.md#p9-idle-work-and-closure).

**Classification:** missing execution/presentation policy. Both notes recommend stopping for new evidence or questions. That can produce incessant interruptions in noisy experiments or a large catalogue.

**Counterexample:** thirty queued tests each slightly change confidence. Requiring thirty acknowledgements makes routine research an active clicking task, despite available evidence accumulation.

**Disposition:** let a selected queue policy distinguish routine observations, material discoveries and decisions. Continue within an approved method and resource bound; summarize observations at a chosen batch end. Stop for a new design choice, an unsupported target, exhausted allowed supplies or a stated player condition. Noise changing a number is not automatically a material discovery. An autonomous source can decide more if its actual policy grants it that authority. Do not infer browser-closed progress from a played-time queue.

### F4. Grinding can itself be the selected pleasure

**Location:** [06 D5](../06_AUTHORING_RNG_AND_IDLE_PROJECTS.md#d5-grind-should-have-a-function), [README practical authoring loop](../README.md#a-practical-authoring-loop).

**Classification:** taste qualification. Repetition that produces nothing is a useful diagnostic. Nevertheless, collecting, slow mastery, huge counters and repeated harvesting can be enjoyable without presenting a new choice every batch. USER_VISION also preserves grindy-game exploration; this audit does not replace it with a short-session ideal.

**Counterexample:** a player deliberately wants an ordinary alchemist who makes thousands of potions while slowly becoming excellent. Automatically compressing that career to one breakthrough removes the intended fantasy.

**Disposition:** evaluate repetition against the selected experience. Show useful production, mastery or collection progress; allow queues where appropriate. Provide richer decisions when the premise supports them, without requiring constant novelty. Distinguish duration, uncertainty and interaction burden. A long deterministic project can be comfortable; a short rare-drop loop can demand exhausting clicks. Exact rates remain an empirical pacing question, not resolved here.

### F5. Chance invention needs a coherent experiment economy

**Location:** [06 D3–D5](../06_AUTHORING_RNG_AND_IDLE_PROJECTS.md#d3-four-research-modes-can-coexist), [10 GP6/GP10](../10_RULE_DRIVEN_PROCEDURAL_GENERATION.md#pg6-deeper-considerations).

**Classification:** omitted choice consequences. Stable truth and meaningful negative evidence are correctly described, but not enough to choose between experiments.

**Counterexample:** every attempt costs the same and returns equally useful evidence. Choosing a method becomes decorative. Conversely, a rare success with no informative observation can intentionally be prospecting, but calling it deduction is misleading.

**Disposition:** state what each research mode buys: evidence, increased reproducible competence, a stochastic candidate, or known production. Experiments can differ in the questions answered, input consumption, precision, accessible targets and reusability of samples. Track observations so repeats can corroborate, measure noise or investigate changed conditions. Retain legitimate fresh-draw phenomena where the phenomenon actually creates a new sample. No universal evidence bonus, pity rule or forced research stage is implied.

### F6. Later-life preparation needs optional clean exits

**Location:** [04 P5/P9](../04_PREPARING_CHEATS_FOR_NEXT_LIVES.md#p5-iterative-construction-across-lives), [08 S4](../08_WORKED_WORLD_AND_LIFE_SCENARIOS.md#s4-build-a-source-for-a-future-life-the-continuity-workshop).

**Classification:** UX/progression risk. The documents allow refusal and specialization, but successive lives can still accumulate maintenance obligations, research queues and unresolved inheritances until every new life becomes administration of old ones.

**Counterexample:** six old workshops all expect the next character to supply materials. Choosing an unrelated ordinary life becomes economically punished by inherited upkeep, even where no actual obligation was transferred.

**Disposition:** define actual ownership and obligations at each edge, then provide supported delegation, donation, sale, dormancy, completion or abandonment routes. A new character's refusal does not destroy independent institutions by default. Some stories can intentionally inherit debts or unfinished duties; name that premise. Cross-life collection can archive historical work without implying current control. Technical UI archiving must not silently sell or erase objects.

### F7. Death-only rewards and delayed payoff remain legitimate selected fantasies

**Location:** [04 P1/P9](../04_PREPARING_CHEATS_FOR_NEXT_LIVES.md#p1-treat-preparation-as-a-project-that-exists-during-this-life), [README D6](../README.md#synthesis).

**Classification:** clarification, not an existing prohibition. Current text explicitly permits delayed payoff and death-triggered power, but its strongest repeated examples benefit the current life and treat death as delivery.

**Counterexample:** a soul garden only grows after the creator's death; or a declared death-based cheat generates one new faculty per death. Replacing either with retirement inheritance misses the premise. Early repeated death might be intentional play rather than an exploit.

**Disposition:** label the life-end model: legacy delivery, death-triggered reward, rebirth conversion, afterlife activity, player unlock or world aftermath. Define what is counted, which identity owns the result and how repeated transitions work. Offer ordinary and other continuation profiles alongside it. Do not install a universal death cooldown, minimum life length or penalty. A selected policy against accidental immediate-death optimization belongs to that profile and needs its own observable rule.

### F8. Acquisition variety should alter a life beyond the unlock receipt

**Location:** [03 O3/O5](../03_ORIGINS_WITHOUT_PATRONS.md#o3-origin-palette), [01 L2/L5](../01_LIBRARY_TIERS_AND_RANDOM.md#l2-types-describe-several-things-at-once).

**Classification:** extension opportunity. Purchase, theft, birth and invention are correctly separated. A content implementation could still converge to the same flag and forget everything else.

**Counterexample:** buying a tool includes instructions and a repair contact; recovering one from a wreck provides custody without those contacts; inventing it establishes knowledge and possible reproducibility. Their first action may be identical, but the later options should not be identical automatically.

**Disposition:** preserve relevant acquired facts, rather than attaching a universal reputation penalty or reward to route labels. Supply actual instruction, collaborators, provenance, obligations and reproduction rights where authored. Acquisition should not automatically change personality or attract pursuit. Use downstream consumers only for consequences the selected story actually promises.

## Three contrasting original playable examples

All three are proposed original content, not canon claims, hidden world truth or selected implementation assignments.

### E1. Weather Sovereign: start powerful and choose what to do

**Premise:** a generated character has immediate, costless, exact authority over regional weather in an original rules profile. No activation tutorial, fatigue cost or mandatory counter. The random start fixes a region and current season, not a combat challenge designed to neutralize the power.

**Play:** inspect available local knowledge; change rain over a selected region; run a known crop-production cycle; compare the resulting yield; choose a private garden, irrigation service, restored wetland or simply travel. Ignoring the power leaves ordinary play available. Broader ecological effects exist only to the extent authored and implemented; the interface must not promise a complete climate model.

**RNG/script/grind:** the initial region may be sampled; the weather command is deterministic. Authored actors can request service, but they do not automatically know its holder. Agricultural routines can queue. A schedule can deliberately hold chosen weather without repeatedly clicking the command. No rare-drop ladder is needed to make it interesting.

**Next life/AU:** transferring the faculty follows the chosen edge; a body-bound variant leaves only changed farmland and any independently operating infrastructure. A soul-bound variant can retain it. Neither outcome is inferred from the player's account. An AU changing weather's relationship to cultivation needs real consumer changes.

**Observable gate:** demonstrate immediate changed weather, one real production effect, ordinary refusal, saved command/schedule and useful subsequent activity. This does not prove global ecology or cross-life transfer.

### E2. Mutating Orchard: fresh randomness with cumulative authorship

**Premise:** an impersonal phenomenon produces new fruit variants each harvest under a defined local model. Fruit is an actual item with retained properties. A working fruit can grant one supported bodily or magical effect. There is no offering being and no automatic equalizing drawback.

**Play:** collect one fruit; test a sample; plant a viable seed; choose growing conditions; queue harvests; sort resulting fruit by known observations; retain a lineage, exchange fruit, cultivate a useful stable variety or pursue unpredictable novelty. A starting active transformation can skip the first search; an ordinary gardener can initially use ordinary fruit.

**RNG/script/grind:** fruit creation legitimately samples a fresh variant; reinspection does not. Breeding choices affect only declared inheritable properties. A stable cultivated line can be reliable. Stochastic wild variation can remain stochastic. Batch rules stop for a genuinely new phenotype or chosen inventory threshold rather than every small test fluctuation. Long cultivation is a valid career, not automatically filler.

**Next life/AU:** leave seeds, living trees, records or a portable growing space. Each preserves different things. An institution can maintain the orchard if it has actual workers and means; a frozen absent-world policy does not grow it. A universe without that phenomenon can still receive edible fruit while lacking reproducible cultivation, if its selected crossing law permits this.

**Observable gate:** grow and use one supported effect; show two genuinely different outcomes, deliberate selection influence and stable fruit identity; preserve lineages, sampled outputs and pending harvests. Do not claim a general genetics simulator from a small supported model.

### E3. Deferred Lantern: build something you cannot use yet

**Premise:** an original reincarnation profile permits a lantern to wait for a defined returning soul. Its function becomes usable only by a later body. This intentionally delays personal payoff. An optional alternative entrusts it to any willing successor instead; that is a different recipient rule.

**Play:** select one supported starting faculty for the later recipient; create or obtain its actual source; choose recognition and destination policy; test only the available components; deposit the lantern; continue the current life without a mandatory countdown to death. At the declared transition, receive the actual retained lantern and use its faculty, refuse it or pass it on under its permitted rules.

**RNG/script/grind:** construction can be reliable and queued. A chosen next-life destination can be certain if the source has that reach; an unsteered body can remain random. The game cannot treat a local recognition test as proof of an unsupported rebirth edge. A perfect creator can skip fabrication. A death-reward sibling might create the lantern at death instead, eliminating preparation rather than disguising it as completed work.

**Agency and escape:** the creator can change the intended recipient before the defined commitment; the successor can reject the planned career without losing unrelated ordinary options. Unclaimed objects remain according to world state. No universal debt or punishment follows.

**Observable gate:** preparation persists, the selected transition delivers once, recipient eligibility is correct and the new function has an actual useful effect. Test deliberate recipient change, refusal and unfinished preparation for this scenario. Rebirth itself remains unimplemented until that selected delivery mechanism exists.

## Suggested disposition and evidence limits

Retain the existing framework. Add contrasting worked experiences and explicit policies for opportunity matching, batching, research outcomes and inherited obligations before presenting it as a general gameplay solution. Select one useful narrow scenario for implementation only under the project's live ownership process; the examples above do not create a new required milestone.

This audit read the named live documentation and checked its own relative links and whitespace. It did not inspect runtime behavior, acquire book sources, execute tests/builds, use a personal save or exercise a browser. Existing source claims remain the original reports' claims. No probabilities, rates, first cheat, life-end policy or scenario were accepted by this audit.
