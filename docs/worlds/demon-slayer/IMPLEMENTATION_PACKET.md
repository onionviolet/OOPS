## Proposed slice

**[PROPOSAL]** First slice: the entry and one encounter in the seven-night Final Selection at a desktop/laptop target viewport of 1366×768. Visible outcome: the player can inspect local reports and supplies, choose to help/avoid/engage a fixed threat, see the actual consequence and continue the selection.
