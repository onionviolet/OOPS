**[PROPOSAL]** Authoring IDs: family `fan.demon_slayer`; continuity `demon_slayer.night_watch.branch`; era `taisho_manga_snapshot`; regions `sagiri_foothills`, `fujikasane_approach`, `mountain_settlement`; entry profiles are defined in the campaign owner. IDs are illustrative, not registered runtime IDs.

**[PROPOSAL]** Fantasy: survive and build a life in a covert human organization confronting demons whose bodies, regeneration and arts demand specific preparation. Training, allies, medical support, equipment and knowing when to withdraw matter alongside sword skill.

**[PROPOSAL]** Signature rules: Total Concentration Breathing enhances a human body through trained respiration; it does not itself grant supernatural elemental matter. Demons have distinct biological needs, growth and counters. Nichirin weapons, sunlight and wisteria have source-defined roles, with exceptions preserved. See [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md).

**[PROPOSAL]** Opening: a new trainee reaches a wisteria-marked selection mountain with basic supplies, no guaranteed mentor relationship, and a seven-night survival test. The player may prioritize rescue, evidence, concealment, or direct demon engagement. Other entrants and fixed threats retain their own capabilities.

**[PROPOSAL]** Connected play: earn food and lodging through ordinary work; train and qualify through a cultivator; pursue a Corps field role, support work, medicine, smithing or independent civilian life. A return visit follows the same continuity and its consequences.

**[PROPOSAL]** Source boundary: the complete original manga through its ending as represented in the WikiTeam Internet Archive snapshot dated 2023-12-19. The dump may not represent every page, template or later correction. Later anime seasons, later wiki changes, and claims not covered by the snapshot remain gaps. Separate anime, film, game, novel, fanbook and Academy material at claim level. No raw source prose is reproduced.

**[PROPOSAL]** Any changed or randomized truth must be stored only as sealed opaque slot IDs with discovery routes in the private pack. Canon public twists remain source truth. See [secrets standard](../WORLD_SECRETS_AND_DISCOVERY.md).

All gameplay mechanics, local people, jobs, relationships, incidents, and branching events in this folder are proposals. Source descriptions and design inference remain distinct. No world-completion claim is made.
