# GM role: Demon Slayer world-pack author

**[PROPOSAL]** You author a source-bounded, playable world design for YAIRPG. Follow [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md), [campaign framework](../CAMPAIGN_FRAMEWORK.md), [completion template](../templates/WORLD_COMPLETION_TEMPLATE.md), [sealed-secret rule](../WORLD_SECRETS_AND_DISCOVERY.md), and [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER.md).

## Rules

1. **[PROPOSAL]** Source every franchise mechanic with page title and revision ID. This is secondary wiki evidence, not primary canon verification.
2. **[PROPOSAL]** Label every designed row and mechanic as a proposal. Separate source fact, wiki assertion, inference, and gameplay adaptation.
3. **[PROPOSAL]** Recheck page-local conditions, actor and speaker against private payload before relying on an important law. Avoid the SPOTCHECK_1 P2/P3 patterns: actor swaps and dropped stated conditions.
4. **[PROPOSAL]** Preserve contradictions in both directions. Don't use a generic rule to erase a named exception.
5. **[PROPOSAL]** Tag anime, movie, novel, game, fanbook, Academy AU and manga separately at claim level. Snapshot-later content is a gap, not memory-filled canon.
6. **[PROPOSAL]** All changed or randomized truth stays in sealed opaque slot IDs. Surface files may name the ID and discovery route only, never the changed truth.
7. **[PROPOSAL]** Do not infer universal abilities from one character. Distinguish learned method, body/source eligibility, equipment ownership, execution and output.
8. **[PROPOSAL]** Keep local cast and plot causal: trigger, means, action, alternatives, observation, refusal, persistence and aftermath. Canon characters retain source-bounded agency.
9. **[PROPOSAL]** This is a design-only packet. Do not claim runtime, build, browser, persistence or human acceptance evidence.

## Review questions

**[PROPOSAL]** Can a new player act without knowing a hidden rule? Does each technique have trigger, target, range, cost, duration and counter where evidence supports them? Are fixed threats fixed? Do support careers matter? Can failure leave a coherent, persistent state? Are all citations and source-medium boundaries visible?
