# Demon Slayer world-pack review

**Verdict: usable with fixes** for source-bounded design discussion. The pack is a proposal built from a dated secondary wiki snapshot, not primary-canon verification or playable implementation. The eight rules in `SYNTHESIS.md`'s engine table have direct retained-payload support at the stated level of qualification. One interaction has the wrong conversion recipient, and one ability example loses its special-one-shot boundary. Fix those and make the red-blade methods explicit before treating the packet as implementation input.

**Method and boundary.** I opened the `read_file` gzip bodies named by `workers/demon-slayer-full/READ_LEDGER.jsonl`, not just `NOTES.jsonl`. Each of the 18 distinct page payloads cited below matched its ledger `read_text_sha256` and `read_characters`. Locators use the page title, revision ID, and **1-based line of the retained payload's `text` field**. For selected-span character pages, these are payload lines, not full wiki article lines. The wiki remains secondary. No manga, later anime, live wiki, gameplay, or runtime check was done. `SPOTCHECK_1.md` and `SPOTCHECK_2.md` identify cross-page carryover, actor swaps, missing conditions, and medium flattening as prior lane risks; I checked the cited page body for each sampled claim.

## Fixes in priority order

1. **F1, conversion actor:** `INTERACTIONS.md` I9 says a **demon** receives Muzan's blood for conversion. The cited page describes a **human** recipient, with tolerance and approval conditions (`Demon` rev 130890, L46–54). Correct the actor before implementing that case. This is a pack error, not an unresolved canon question.
2. **F2, continuity:** Label the Flute Demon example in `ABILITY_VARIANTS.md` and `INTERACTIONS.md` I5 as Special One-Shot material, or use a manga-baseline sensory example. The page names its source at `Flute Demon` rev 128325, L15–17 and L26–39. The pack otherwise keeps film, game, anime-only and Academy material out of the manga baseline: game-specific Water forms are separately tagged in `Water Breathing` rev 126658, L79–87; `Kimetsu Academy` rev 130247, L5 calls itself a spin-off universe. I found no other specific cross-medium import in the checked pack rows.
3. **F3, red method:** Rewrite `SOURCE_LEDGER.md` C2 and the broad “disputed prerequisites” wording so friction/pressure, Nezuko ignition, duration and regeneration effects each have their own cited condition. The opening marked-only wording and explicit ignition exception both need to remain visible. Primary manga verification remains open.
4. **F4, eligibility gap:** Add `Demon` rev 130890 L42 versus L56 to the ledger's conversion conflict: the page first says demons were originally human, then describes Tamayo transforming a cat. This changes the body/source eligibility model if nonhuman conversion or demon-origin starts are ever authored. Keep it a narrow exception pending primary verification.
5. **F5, evidence scope:** In `SYNTHESIS.md`, distinguish the completed **assigned** 38-entry scope/restoration audit (`workers/demon-slayer-full/SUMMARY.md`, `INTEGRITY.json`) from the still open broader completeness question. The current “scope audit remains open” wording can read as if the named lane audit was unfinished. The page inventory and unexpanded/transcluded material still do not prove exhaustive coverage.

## Additional supported details and gates

- **M1.** The notes and payload support method-specific breathing practice: lung drills and rehabilitation, constant use even during sleep, and recovery breathing. A first implementation can use these concrete training and interruption states rather than a single generic breathing level (`Total Concentration Breathing` rev 127065, L23–39; `Rehabilitation Training` rev 117121, L53–61).
- **M2.** Final Selection's source rule is seven nights on candidates' own resources, while the page also records peer rescue. That gives the proposed aid route a source-compatible shape, but the invented injured entrant, supplies and fixed demon need their own authored instance facts (`Final Selection` rev 128106, L19–24, L32–34).
- **M3.** The named audit gates are complete; primary manga confirmation, later anime coverage, an M3 coexistence survey, and full-content completeness remain open. Those limits are mostly listed honestly in `SYNTHESIS.md` §9. Before freezing mark lifespan, red activation, decapitation exceptions or conversion eligibility, verify the corresponding primary scenes. No playable result is claimed.

**Other checks.** Proposal labeling holds across the nine pack files: gameplay rows and starts are marked `[PROPOSAL]`, and no changed or randomized secret truth is exposed outside an opaque slot/route reference. A normalized 16-word exact-match scan of all nine pack files against the retained and audit `read_file` payloads found zero matches; I also found no raw source-prose block in the pack. This supports the no-over-15-word-quote check for the supplied payload corpus, not for unacquired primary sources.
