## Play promise

**[PROPOSAL]** Start as an ordinary person or qualified trainee in Taisho-era Japan. Earn access and means through work, training, trust, logistics and risk. A demon threat is not a level-scaled encounter. The Corps is one institution in a lived world, not the only available identity or career.

## Worked opening: the seven-night selection

**[PROPOSAL]** At the mountain boundary, candidate-owned equipment and supplies are consistent with the selected start; the source rule is survival for seven consecutive nights using candidates' own resources. The source also records a candidate rescuing another, so peer aid is compatible, but this proposed wounded entrant, aid supplies and outcome are authored instance facts. The player can spend time and carried supplies stabilizing them, mark the location for later help, refuse, or leave. Each choice changes actual time, danger exposure, trust and available supplies. The same demon remains a fixed threat. A second route might use concealment and terrain rather than combat. Passing selection is not guaranteed. Recoveries, deaths, lost supplies and incomplete objectives remain part of the instance. Source: Final Selection rev 128106.
