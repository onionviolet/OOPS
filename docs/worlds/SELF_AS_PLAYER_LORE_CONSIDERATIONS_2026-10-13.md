# Being ourselves in the fiction

Status: exploratory design report, 2026-10-13. Findings describe the live documents; recommendations and hypothetical premises remain proposals. Human acceptance is open. No universal identity model or runtime change is selected.

Weibao's request: “Send out a chat to consider lore reasons to be ourself as us accordingly?” The question is literal: can the human playing be present as their actual self, with their external knowledge, identity, agency and continuity, rather than merely choose an unrelated person's actions?

**Recommendation S1:** explore a continuing self who enters a world in a body, remembers their actual outside knowledge, and can continue into another life through a declared carrier. Use transported-self entry when keeping the original body matters; reincarnation when a new body and local upbringing matter. This gives research and relationships the same answer: the person who formed the idea and the bond is still you. It does not require a universal cosmic bureaucracy, a second occupant, an unreliable memory tax or menus that control reality. A connected avatar is the stronger alternative when live access to the human outside the game is central.

## What “actually us” promises

**I1: Literal identity within fiction.** The premise declares the continuing subject to be the actual human player. It models that presence through supported play; it makes no claim that software transfers a real consciousness. A transported person, reincarnated self or connected controller can all satisfy the fictional promise if their continuity is explicit.

**I2: Authored version or copy.** A character based on the human's preferences, or a newly created mind with a snapshot of their memories, resembles them but is not automatically their continuing self. A copy can have a valuable independent life. Calling it “you” should disclose whether that is narration, branching identity or literal continuation. Memory resemblance alone need not settle the distinction.

**I3: Outside-fiction convention.** The human selects an independent character's choices; no factual information crosses merely because a menu accepts a modern goal label. This remains a coherent alternative, but by itself does not fulfill a request for literal external knowledge inside the fiction. It should not be sold as reincarnation with everything inconvenient excluded.

No autobiographical profile of Weibao is inferred here. “Your knowledge” means the knowledge and preferences the player elects to supply. The game cannot know the entire contents of a human mind. A declared start and supported information inputs can represent that knowledge without mining private files, inventing qualifications or claiming an arbitrary invention simulator.

## The knowledge gap in both directions

**K1: Remembering accurately is different from knowing everything.** A carrier can preserve exactly what you knew at entry, including incomplete or mistaken beliefs. Perfect retention stops loss; it does not add a missing textbook, certify a theory or supply tacit technique. Conversely, if the declared grant includes full engineering mastery, allow it to bypass the learning it genuinely replaces. No mandatory apprenticeship follows merely because research exists.

**K2: Live outside access is a separate promise.** Under M1/M2, a later fact learned by the human at the keyboard is not automatically a memory of the embodied self. A supported external channel, ongoing M3 link, carried archive with updates, or explicit open information convention can admit it. A closed-memory mode need not police private reasoning: players may still choose hypotheses and attempts. It only limits which new outside facts the fiction establishes as received. A typed modern label may be interface translation; a detailed transmitted diagram may be an information event. Say which.

**K3: The local expert can know more than the human.** A reincarnated adult may have years of local craft experience; a cooperative host may know how to inspect an unusual machine or comfort an old friend. Show “you recall…” only for the self's established history, and “the host recalls…” for a separate person. Contextual reminders, meaningful action options and competent execution let that expertise work. The player does not need manual dexterity, obscure vocabulary or an invented personal childhood to activate it. Memory access can expose a recollection without making every detail authoritative truth.

**K4: Communicating an idea need not grant mastery.** Words can communicate a question or principle; diagrams can supply a design; a demonstration can teach a procedure; a memory-sharing power can transfer understanding; a mastery grant can supply actual execution. Select the operation rather than universally treating all channels as weak speech or all speech as instant expertise. Translation can make words intelligible without revealing intent, correctness or every cultural implication.

**K5: Prior knowledge is a legitimate means, not omniscience.** Remembered story events or a previous life can help recognition, preparation and intervention. Mark their provenance as a recollection of that source/era/history, rather than claiming a view of present hidden state. An exact password may still work if unchanged; preventing the appointment that created it may make it irrelevant. Intervening in one region need not invalidate unrelated facts everywhere. NPCs respond to observations, claims, evidence and their own faculties, not an automatic metagaming alarm.

The [technology fidelity palette](../TECHNOLOGY_AND_PROGRESSION.md#knowing-that-versus-knowing-how-tk) helps distinguish a concept from a recipe and mastery. It is a proposal palette, not a required data field for every remembered fact. Record detail where it changes an actual choice. Do not display a secret truth merely because author data can supply a certainty label.

## Playable hypothetical situations

These scenes describe supported choices a future prototype could offer. They are not implemented or playtested. The recurring person Mira and the Brass Estuary setting are original hypothetical examples, not existing world secrets.

### E1: “I want to research nuclear physics as a Victorian-style child”

Use M2 plus M7. You have reincarnated in an original pre-industrial setting with a supportive household, reliable selected outside memories and a new body. You remember that matter has internal structure, but the premise does not invent specialist training you never supplied. Local physics is an authored question; no claim about exact real Victorian experimental feasibility is made.

First scene: a tutor asks what you want to investigate. Choices include explain the remembered concept, ask what local science says, seek a skilled collaborator, or pursue another subject. If you supply a remembered principle, the character can actually tell the tutor through ordinary speech. If you merely enter a modern ambition, the interface can map it to an intelligible question without inventing a complete recipe.

The useful next result might be a research plan, instrument access or a contact. A wealthy household can immediately fund appropriate assistance. A gifted local collaborator can supply expertise you lack. A supported perfect archive can replace forgotten detail. The game should name an unsupported project boundary honestly rather than pretend every possible invention is simulated. Research remains useful through local validation and new questions after imported answers work.

Mira becomes a collaborator because she understands instrumentation. You contribute outside concepts; she contributes local practice. Later, when this body dies, M7 preserves your actual project experience. Instruments stay in the workshop unless carried. In the next body you may know more than your first self did, while still needing compatible hands or help. The same continuity that preserves this research memory will preserve the relationship in E2.

### E2: “Will Mira know it is still me?”

Same M2/M7 premise and same history, not a new cosmetic explanation. Mira remains alive in the retained world. You return in a new body at a declared later time through a supported passage. You remember your shared work and an ordinary promise to return. She remembers the person who made that promise, but does not automatically recognize the new face.

Playable choices: introduce yourself openly, recall a shared public event, show a surviving letter, seek a mutual witness, or use a known identity-reading means if available. Her response follows the evidence she can read. Reliable recognition can be simple and immediate where the premise provides a trusted carrier; no compulsory suspicion quest is needed.

After recognition, preserve the shared history, including affection, disagreements and work left unfinished. Changes during your absence can still matter. If Mira also traveled or reincarnated through an actual carrier, both retain what it brought. If instead you enter a parallel start containing a counterpart who never met you, only you remember that relationship. An AU can explicitly begin with mutual shared history, but that is a selected premise rather than memory you imposed on an existing stranger. Returning as yourself does not by itself make every counterpart the same partner.

### E3: “You know the answer, I know how to build it”

Use M6 instead. Your cooperative co-occupant is an established instrument maker with years of shared history with Mira. You supply an outside concept; reciprocal memory access lets both of you understand it. The local person notices a material issue you missed and offers a workable test. Choices include accept their method, ask for an explanation, spend within an agreed budget or change the goal. Cooperation is real from the start.

Mira asks who is speaking and whether the old promise still stands. You can answer as yourself, let the local person answer, or speak together. Their old relationship does not automatically become yours, although she can come to know both of you. If you later separate, the maker retains knowledge they actually learned; a borrowed faculty can leave with its holder. This concrete difference makes a shared-host model worth choosing when mutual contribution is wanted. It also gives it more identity bookkeeping than E1/E2.

### E4: “My avatar died; did our life together end?”

Use M3. The continuing outside you pilots a created, non-person vessel. You and Mira develop a research project and friendship through it. The selected live connection lets you communicate a new outside idea next visit; vessel assistance supplies a declared local procedure. No claim that modern human knowledge alone supplies that procedure is needed.

The vessel is destroyed on an expedition. Your continuing controller survives; the expedition world retains the destruction and Mira's experience. If the premise includes replacement and return, you can arrive in a new vessel and continue the relationship after the supported recognition. You do not acquire a rewind, recovered cargo or an unwitnessed rescue just because the controller survived. If the link can preserve Mira too, that is a separate shared carrier with her own participation terms. The model supports a powerful comfortable return loop without mandatory damage to memory or loyalty.

### E5: “The warning worked, so the future changed”

Under M1, M2/M7 or M3, you remember a particular earlier history in which a bridge closure isolated Mira's workshop. You warn the actual responsible person, who repairs the crossing. The recorded closure no longer happens because its cause was removed. Mira now attends a meeting she previously missed, and its outcome is unknown to your old recollection.

Choices remain prepare from still-relevant knowledge, ask what changed, observe the meeting or leave it alone. The game need not replace the bridge failure with a different catastrophe. Your memory remains accurate about its original history even though it no longer predicts this event. Neither Mira nor the road workers automatically know you are an outsider; they know you gave a useful warning and may draw their own conclusions.

## Existing bodies, failed rituals and villain starts

Follow-up, 2026-10-13: Weibao asks what happens when inheriting someone else's existing body, arriving through a ritual gone wrong, or starting as a villain, and requests related ideas. These are original hypothetical premises and further recommendations, not selected origins or claims about a particular franchise.

**B1: An existing body has several kinds of inheritance.** Your continuing self, the former occupant, bodily faculties, remembered expertise, social recognition and property can have different outcomes. A ritual can transfer one without transferring all. Declare what the selected start actually preserves instead of assuming every arrival loses all powers or receives an entire biography.

| Part | Possible starting condition | What changes in play |
|---|---|---|
| Original occupant | Already dead/departed; elsewhere; dormant; cooperatively sharing; forcibly displaced; integrated by an explicit fusion. | Determines whether another person can advise, act, return or have claims on this life. “The ritual failed” alone leaves this unanswered. |
| Anatomy and innate faculty | The real body, organs, senses, capacity and bloodline remain operative where their mechanism permits. | A powerful body can be powerful immediately. A new controller does not automatically erase an organ or supply a soul-bound source that departed. |
| Memory and practical competence | Full accessible memories; contextual fragments; procedural skill without autobiography; no retained expertise; shared access through the occupant. | Determines whether you recognize people, understand an old project and execute practiced work. Intact expertise can supply fluent competent action. |
| Desires and bodily responses | Hunger, reflexes, sensory preferences, conditioned associations or remembered attachments. | Can provide sensations and context without forcing the human's loyalties or choices. Full personality replacement is a separate disclosed premise. |
| Social identity | People initially recognize the familiar body, name, office or manner according to their evidence. | You can inherit expectations, access and suspicions without everyone discovering the transfer. Reliable soul recognition can work where an observer actually has it. |
| Assets and commitments | The person had belongings, contracts, dependents, collaborators, debts or disputed property. | Possession of the body can enable access, while actual custody and institutions decide title. Some consequences arrive through other people's existing plans. |

Knowing the former occupant's emotional memories is different from having personally lived their relationship. You can care about someone after receiving those memories, and a genuinely fused self can preserve a shared lived identity under its declared terms. A replacement self with copied memories should not silently claim the same continuity. This distinction can matter in one conversation without requiring constant metaphysical exposition.

**B2: A failed ritual should have a specific failure and a useful result.** An immortal's resurrection rite might successfully restore a healthy, magically capable body while calling your continuing self instead of the intended soul. You wake with the body and its retained procedural expertise; the intended occupant never arrived. That is a strong existing-body start with no compulsory possession struggle. Another ritual could recall both subjects, giving a cooperative shared mind. Another might swap two people between worlds, leaving both alive. Another could create a vessel while the human remains connected outside. Each failure explains a different model; it need not entail a curse, insanity, hunted status or reduced powers.

Make the first scene playable before explaining everything: someone asks you to finish an interrupted task, an ally expects an answer, or you can inspect the ritual room. Known control and starting capabilities should be understandable even if the ritual's ultimate cause remains a discovery. These are general hypothetical mysteries, not disclosures of actual sealed content.

**B3: Starting as a villain describes a situation, not compulsory evil choices.** Distinguish an infamous former occupant, someone falsely called a villain, a person expected to become the antagonist, and a self whose prior fictional life actually included harmful deeds. The last is an authored starting history to choose, not a claim that the human performed those acts or owes a redemption story. You can pursue reform, maintain selected ambitions, withdraw, change loyalties or build another life through supported actions.

People can continue fearing the familiar face, seeking restitution, keeping a valuable alliance or advancing a previously begun operation. Those reactions follow their own experience and evidence. A changed controller does not instantly erase the harm they remember; neither does it make every witness permanently incapable of revising their view. Relationships among the former person's friends, employees and enemies can differ. Retained power can provide substantial freedom instead of automatically triggering stronger enemies to cancel the advantage.

**B5: Recommendation for the most intuitive existing-body version.** Begin with the original occupant already departed, you arriving as the continuing self, operative bodily faculties and clearly declared retained local competence. It allows a strong start, outside knowledge, useful local history and complicated social circumstances without requiring another controller or compulsory penalties. Offer surviving-host cooperation, swap and fusion as explicit alternatives when their distinct experience is wanted. A villain identity can be layered onto any of these, with its actual history and other people's knowledge preserved.

Hidden causes can stay hidden through their discovery routes.

## Home, experienced time and choosing an ending

**H1: Separate who continues from what they retain.** A useful two-part promise is “this is the same continuing subject” followed by “these memories, capacities and possessions survive.” Perfect memory alone cannot distinguish transfer from a newly made copy. Conversely, a continuing person can lose an item, change anatomy or learn new values without becoming a different subject by default. Choose a workable fictional answer rather than require a universal philosophical proof.

| Operation | Who continues in this version | What it establishes, and what it does not |
|---|---|---|
| Same-person transport | One self departs home and arrives elsewhere. | Preserves the declared person/body/cargo. Home does not also contain an independently active original unless another mechanism is added. |
| Reincarnation through a continuing carrier | One self continues in a new body. | Can preserve memories and subject continuity while replacing anatomy. Whether the old body died, remains empty or is outside represented scope is a selected origin fact. |
| Connected non-person avatar | The outside self continues and operates a vessel. | Can transmit live knowledge and experience. The vessel is not a second subject in this version; losing it is not inherently losing the controller. |
| Copy while the original continues | Two subjects now have a common earlier history. | Can duplicate selected memories or faculties. Later acts, promises, aging and relationships belong to their actual participants unless a further operation joins them. |

For M1/M2 plus M7, recommend one continuing subject and a declared carrier rather than an unannounced copy. For M3, recommend one connected self and non-person vessels unless independent vessel personhood is the chosen attraction. Either can support a memory-only transfer or full supported mastery; continuity does not determine the strength of the grant.

Home can be reachable, remembered, communicating through a channel or outside represented scope. Named real acquaintances and a detailed biography are unnecessary. An abstract self with supplied knowledge and broad chosen preferences is enough to make the identity literal within the premise. A more detailed self-portrait is optional, and should be labeled when it changes the literal-self promise. No physical change to the actual human is asserted.

A reachable home needs concrete crossing terms: can the self return bodily, can only information return, what stays in the destination, and can the same retained world be revisited? A conversation link need not carry tools; a body passage need not rewind either world's history. Ending a session or closing the application remains a player action rather than an automatic fictional journey home.

**H2: Experienced years, body age and world calendars can differ.** Suppose an explicitly selected M3 scenario gives the controller one afternoon at home while an avatar experiences twelve local years. At return, the self can remember twelve years of collaboration with Mira, the vessel can have aged twelve years, and the home body can have aged an afternoon. That is a proposed unequal-time mechanism, not a measurement or current offline policy. It does not itself grant twelve years to another vessel or an absent world. Under M2/M7, the reincarnated body can age locally while continuing experience accumulates across several bodies; a former home body need not still be alive and running a second life.

A chapter summary can establish chosen research routines and represented relationships, but should not fabricate major promises, expenditures or private decisions. Use direct choice or bounded autonomy for those spans. Remembering a summary is not evidence that every minute was simulated. New interests and attachment are possibilities for the player to express, not mandatory trauma or a designer-authored replacement personality. An experienced self may still want the same goal, or choose a new one.

**H3: Simultaneous selves need a specific operation.** A single controller can switch attention between two avatars, use a supported unified awareness, or delegate known routines. None requires creating another person. Fully independent copies are different: each can learn, disagree and establish relationships while the other is elsewhere. Do not call their later reunion one shared experience until a declared channel actually shares it.

Concrete hypothetical: one independent copy promises Mira to return to the laboratory on a certain day; another promises a distant collaborator to remain for a conflicting observation. At reunion, sharing memories makes both promises known. It neither fulfills both nor silently removes one. If the subjects remain separate, each can honor its own promise. If a selected fusion leaves one subject and one body, the resulting self must resolve the commitments through supported travel, delegation or renegotiation. A strong merge that retains multiple bodies and concurrent action may genuinely keep both promises. Its breadth should be honored rather than countered with an invented synchronization tax.

Memory exchange, skill transfer and subject fusion are distinct. Accurate memory sharing can retain provenance so the receiver knows which self witnessed an event; a recollection can still contain a mistaken belief. A fusion must state which subjects continue, whether either can remain separate, and whether incompatible goals remain open choices. No mandatory madness, dominance contest or erasure follows from divergence. Mira's relationship with one copy does not automatically authorize the other to claim the same shared history as an experienced participant. She can willingly relate to both, or recognize a supported fused continuity, under the actual scene's facts.

**H4: Embodiment can change means while the same self continues.** If E1's returning researcher now has a body that senses material stresses directly but lacks familiar hands, retain the project knowledge and Mira's shared history. Offer actual means: local tool use, a compatible instrument, Mira's assistance, an adapted procedure or a declared body-control grant. The new sense may immediately make a previously difficult measurement easy. It is neither automatically weak nor automatically equivalent to perfect interpretation. If an adaptation grant includes control and understanding, let it work from arrival; otherwise show relevant orientation or practice where it changes an action. No universal rehabilitation ladder follows from changing bodies.

The same rule handles the relationship: a different voice or body can change recognition and everyday interaction without erasing the continuing person or automatically resetting affection. Mira contributes what she knows about the local body and customs. Her knowledge can become available through teaching or the selected shared-memory channel, while ordinary reminders express only the protagonist's own established competence. Research, affection and body use therefore keep the same carrier/access logic rather than each getting an unrelated exception.

**H5: Continuity can be voluntary.** Successful life can continue without compulsory death. A persistent self can stay, retire from travel, return home or choose a final ending where the premise allows it. These differ: retirement can preserve later visits or communication; ending the travel power can leave a lived local life; a final mortal ending can close that self's future without deleting the world's aftermath. Potentially unlimited rebirth does not require endless actual reincarnation. A playable life can also simply conclude outside-fiction without asserting metaphysical extinction.

Refined recommendation: a single continuing embodied traveler, reliable declared memories, direct agency, and world-qualified crossings is the clearest first comparison for “ourselves as us.” Make home communication/return and voluntary staying explicit alternatives when choosing that premise. Choose a connected avatar instead when maintaining a live original outside the world is essential. Treat copying as its own disclosed experience, rather than using it as an unnoticed substitute for either model.

These points deepen the identity discussion's “Further implications of being ourselves in the fiction,” refreshed again for the follow-up request. They remain proposals rather than accepted answers or implementation instructions.

**N3: Three useful ways to reuse a story.** Direct recreation preserves the selected source's setting and causal history, with player-caused divergence and explicitly labeled alternatives. A source-qualified AU changes a particular premise and works through its consequences. Original composition borrows situations, mechanisms or dramatic functions while creating its own world laws, participants, motives, institutions and outcomes. All are available under existing direction. Direct recreation follows the project's private-pack/public-fork separation in [PROJECT.md](../../PROJECT.md#accepted-decisions).

Copying an interesting beat can be a deliberate design choice. Record what is copied and whether the result is adaptation or inspiration. For an original world, changing names alone does not create a meaningfully different campaign. Change the causes, available responses, social relationships and aftermath where an original experience is intended. Several works can inform one world, but imported rules need an explicit compatible composition rather than assumed genre equivalence.

### Example: one beat, several real choices

Original hypothetical: a restored ruler awakens, an adviser announces their return, and a council expects the ruler to resume an unfinished undertaking. The dramatic function is recognition meeting changed identity and inherited authority.

The first scene offers useful alternatives: ask for a briefing, finish the work using retained competence, reveal what happened, renegotiate the undertaking or leave office. These alternatives can share the council scene while changing later relationships and access. A hostile takeover is not obligatory just because it makes a dramatic second act. If the council trusts the new ruler, cooperation can be the actual outcome.

In a direct recreation, a later attempt on the ruler's life happens only if its participants and causes still hold under the represented source/premise. In an original version, perhaps the undertaking is a research observatory rather than a conquest, and the returning ruler wants to study a remembered outside idea. The same opening beat then supports scientific collaboration, leadership, friendships and travel. The broader world still needs its own economy, ordinary residents, feasible means and independent plans. A premise is an entry into a lived world, not its entire content.

**N4: Generate coherent world candidates, then author their useful life.** Combine compatible choices of local rules, era, institution, origin/identity mechanism, retained knowledge, initial means, cast relationships and active situation. Some combinations can be templates; others need deliberate writing. Validate the important interactions before offering a start. A soul-reading society and an existing-body impostor can produce different play from a society relying on faces, while neither demands universal detection.

One premise can support several starts and arcs, reducing the need for a completely separate world per trope. Different worlds are worthwhile when their rules and everyday possibilities differ materially. An AI-generated setting is a draft; freezing a reviewed version and coherent starting state makes it a candidate for playable implementation. This is an authoring workflow, not a claim that a live AI can invent arbitrary actions, rewrite state or simulate every suggested science project during play. Runtime generative storytelling would be its own later architecture decision.
