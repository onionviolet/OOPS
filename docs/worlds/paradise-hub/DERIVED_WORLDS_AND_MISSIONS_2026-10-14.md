## Chronological catalog

This is an ordered destination/mission research catalog, not an exhaustive transcription of every main-task ring, hub return, origin visit or Void errand. It includes franchise-derived worlds, novel-native worlds and distinct nested trials because the engine must distinguish them. **Native/original** below means original to the novel, not original YAIRPG content. Descriptive names in parentheses are retrieval labels, not authenticated Chinese aliases.

Length is the **issued task window**, when known, rather than an invented observed stay. Team means people actually involved in that visit, not a permanent party assumed present everywhere. Carryback records an example retained acquisition or consequence; it never means the whole local shop becomes portable. Unspecified rewards, penalties, roster and actual elapsed duration remain UNKNOWN in that cell. Early rows are more detailed because their mission notices are more fully extracted. Later broad rows remain authoring leads pending exact panels.
