### Household accounts and service appointments

SHOWN/STATED(dossier, D/workers/b0009/windows/w1720-1729.md F01): companions operate a stall, split earnings and pay to repair shared space from their own money (sid1720 p1–23,p29–47). A later company starts with 50,000 capital, shares Bubtney 40%, Benny 40%, Amu 10%, Su Xiao 10%, then quickly goes bankrupt (sid1724 p15–27). The beings lane uses this to establish noncombat companion life. The economic consequence is narrower: distinct earners, contributions, household damage and business failure exist. UNKNOWN: a universal incorporation law, automatic loss-sharing or a profitable repeatable company mechanic.

PROPOSAL transaction sequence: **offer → accepted terms → reserved funds/materials → performed service → delivered object/benefit → invoice/fee → paid/contested/closed**. An auction adds **consigned → escrowed → listed → bid accepted → settled → collected/returned**. A household contribution transfers named funds to a defined repair project; percentage equity is not permission to spend another member's personal purse. Borrowing, lending, ownership and working at a shared stall remain different relations.

PROPOSAL service record: worker, actual location, supported skill, available attention, duration, quoted currency, queue capacity, cancellation terms, due time, next known departure and client pickup rights. Before recall, a player can collect finished work, appoint an authorized collector or accept a disclosed later delivery. Changing body/location/role does not silently finish the service or consume the teacher's stock again. Default one finite order with a visible useful result; Dial a cooperative workshop or business failure/recovery route; reasonably maintain the worker's own rest and obligations. This deepens CRP4/CRP9 and BS5 without requiring company simulation in the first slice.

## 8. Five suggestions, ranked by playable value

1. **S1: Room and neighborhood routine.** PROPOSAL: source-qualified room permission, two useful furnishings, meal/rest and one recurring staff relationship. Use one bounded hub era and show a saved change that matters on return. Default a small earned home; Dial later shared residence or AU guests; reasonably reuse room/service/custody foundations while keeping original Exchange available. Value: makes the hub a place to live quickly. Acceptance: furnish, invite or decline a known visitor, rest, depart and find the exact property/relationship intact after a supported return and save cycle.
2. **S2: One real market and repair circuit.** PROPOSAL: reconcile currency naming, consign one actual owned item, quote a repair or craft order, reserve payment/materials and settle an item with a truthful fee/net receipt. Default finite counterparties; Dial bargaining, suppliers and larger contracts; reasonably begin without unsupported tax percentages or universal conversion. Value: turns mission gains into useful household/work choices. Acceptance: seller and buyer custody change once; declined quote costs nothing; escrow and unfinished repair survive save/reload/import without duplication.
3. **S3: Distinct paid progression services.** PROPOSAL: separate own-method practice, image instruction and earned attribute allocation, then one item enhancement only when its card/failure terms are rechecked. Default early facilities; Dial later true points/talents; reasonably preserve the current ordinary craft route. Value: gives a meaningful preparation choice between time, money and scarce materials. Acceptance: paid teaching changes the player's own supported method, point allocation consumes exact earned points, and incompatible scroll ownership grants no executable faculty.
4. **S4: Team and recurring social commitments.** PROPOSAL: recruit one independent coworker for a stated role, share one finite order, retain payment shares and a remembered kept/broken appointment. Default friendship and trade trust; Dial permanent team/rivalry/explicit relationship AU; reasonably let solo play and support careers remain useful. Value: relationships change what a player can do between journeys. Acceptance: each person retains stock, knowledge and agency; group agreement does not erase an individual grievance or buy intimacy.
5. **S5: A bounded auction or cross-Paradise meeting.** PROPOSAL: one sourced event with real lots, ticket terms, event protection and two era-correct contacts; a rival visitor needs an actual meeting route. Default selected canon scene adapted around an independent player; Dial original event or AU attendance; reasonably defer full global economy. Value: combines leisure, reputation, bargains and rivalry after the smaller loops work. Acceptance: attendance, bid, escrow, win/loss and collection are separate; old feud survives event protection; no unknown commission or shared currency rate is fabricated.
