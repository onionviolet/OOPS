Labels (WPR4): **S** = stated by a system notice or a character, **H** = shown in a scene, **I** = inferred, **P** = proposed, **U** = unverified (helper claim not reopened by the coordinator). Where-from tags: **LOCAL** (the world's own institution), **PARADISE-PROVIDED**, **PARADISE-RESTRICTED** (a Paradise rule limits what a visitor may do there). Rule codes: WPR4-WPR5 (labels, defaults and dials), WPR20 (contradictions kept), AD5/AD38 (defaults, dials, mechanisms), AD22 (limits are adjectives), WA1/WA5 (mechanism plus data rows).
