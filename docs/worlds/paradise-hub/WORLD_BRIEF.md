## Residence connects to journeys

An optional earned passage/invitation leads to a durable settlement with lodging, workshop, practice, recovery, public records, traders and travelers with their own means. Residence is more than a mission-selection screen. A small owned place stores actual goods, teaches routines, supports research/refit and preserves specific relationships.

Proposed roles: specialist traveler, craftsperson, route researcher, broker, healer/support worker or institution builder. Missions can fund parts and discoveries; ordinary work can fund travel without becoming a combat career. A high-power character retains their strength while suppliers, source compatibility, useful research and people still create choices.

Existing original Exchange people stay their own characters: Iona's ledger authority, Venn's partial maps and Kes's competing route work are defined in the framework. A returned traveler can bring actual evidence about a danger rather than selling omniscient lore. Rivalry can close, become cooperation or continue under specific remembered events.

## Bounded scenario and deeper world are compatible

An assignment selects an existing local arc, entry profile, era/region, objective, known terms, extraction and settlement. The destination campaign supplies its own institutions, people, powers and consequences. Completing extraction may leave its civil conflict unresolved; the issuer's receipt cannot declare that every local person was saved.

An initial mission can represent one region. Later residence, discoveries and earned routes can expose more of that same instance; reentry does not regenerate its starting reward or erase its people. An explicitly different timeline is a different causal instance with source-qualified identity/age/body/support.

[Multiverse scope](../MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES.md) distinguishes cosmology, represented coverage and persistence. A bureau can reach genuine independent worlds, a region within one, nested realms or constructed trials; each selects its actual connection and state policy. Several independent networks can coexist. A small assignment is not evidence that its destination exists only for that assignment.

The full campaign dossiers remain useful for coherent places outside the represented region and future expansion. A small first delivered scenario does not require simulating every off-screen actor. Its scope is declared before commitment; an extraordinary action that reaches beyond that scope needs a real expanded adapter or an honestly scoped scenario rule.

## Distinct progression and project proposals

Training develops actual retained methods/control; a workshop develops tools/modules/refit; research tests cross-world materials/functions; licensed service develops local access and obligations; a home develops storage/collaborator capacity. None is a global compulsory progression ladder or automatic power-conversion marketplace.

A project chain: ordinary repair work funds a bench; the bench lets the traveler calibrate an acquired sensor; a funded visit returns a genuine sample and paid damage; inspection identifies an incompatible coating; testing creates a useful local seal recipe; that recipe improves a supported return mission. Another player can buy the calibration service and pursue a different career.

Strong acquisitions can be genuinely superior. Specialized local requirements still need actual source/body/fuel/authority; the hub cannot manufacture a soul-bound release by charging generic coins. Cross-world hybrids are paid original experiments with observable uncertainty and retained evidence.
