## Reading the result

**IG1: Conditional answers settle a named route, not every use of an operation.** D = simultaneous duplicated self; C = copied mind or representation; Sp = split; Sw = exchanged minds/souls; M = merged identities; P = possession/co-occupancy; R = identity-bearing remnant. Position swapping, copying power, forced obedience and looking alike do not by themselves establish soul exchange, mind fusion or a second person. A row marked resolved can narrow an UNKNOWN cell while leaving its unrestricted form UNKNOWN.

**IG2: Evidence labels retain their limits.** SHOWN means a local reader/dossier reports an event with a scene locator; this lane did not reread raw novels. STATED names the archived wiki, speaker or project brief. INFERRED marks interpretation/design. UNKNOWN remains a missing answer, not forbidden. No MEMORY claim establishes a law. Wiki worker notes are secondary reports even when their receipts say `read_complete`; index and hash metadata do not certify meaning. LOTM later worker notes have advisory audits but are beyond its parent-integrated cutoff. Keep those qualifications if promoting this report into an owner.

**IG3: Three independent defaults.** Round 8's None/Implied/Present/Playable concerns other versions of a person and supported control. Round 9's IX concerns prior played characters/bodies: persistent worlds reasonably keep IX2 background NPCs; BW1 proposes IX0 for a fresh canon story life, BW2 offers IX2 on a continued branch, and BW8 preserves source-native identity mechanisms. A new canon timeline is not a character's in-world soul-copy power. None is an ordinary-start experience filter, not a claim that a franchise forbids souls, ghosts or avatars. No resolved law below forces a previous player character into the current story. These remain proposals except the user's recorded lean. See [intro rounds 8–9](../INTRO_FLOW_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md#round-8-should-there-be-no-other-selves-2026-10-14).
