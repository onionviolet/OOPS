## A scene is a meeting with particular people

| Authoring group | Required frozen information |
|---|---|
| Identity | Scene/revision, world/instance/era, actual speaker/listener IDs and source snapshot |
| Place and time | Physical or supported remote contact, observed location, played-time availability and opening cause |
| Participants | Life/body, language/channel, consent (a default that coercion, possession or a cheat can bypass at a cost), accessibility and conflicting reservations |
| Goals and means | What each speaker wants, what they can offer/enforce, owned stock, learned methods and authority |
| Evidence | Who saw/heard what, where/when, report provenance, uncertainty and contradicted expectations |
| Directed history | Speaker-to-player and player-to-speaker memories separately; unresolved promises, loans and known deeds |
| Presentation | Visible clothing/equipment/body/condition, observer recognition, voice and ordinary detail |
| Choice | Intent, visible terms, prerequisites, material/time/attention cost, paid action and distinct result |
| Closure | Refusal, exit, interruption, default, aftermath, re-entry and unique/repeat settlement policy |

The author can know the true fault, hidden build or later event. Opening speech contains only information the actual speaker can have and choose to disclose.
A speaker can mislead, joke, misunderstand or withhold; record discrepancies and label uncorroborated statements. A mill foreman can recognize sound stock without diagnosing an unfamiliar spell.
Temperament affects phrasing/choices without a new psychology meter or neutral-choice punishment; social rank supplies particular access/duties, not learned power or proof of strength.
Threats need target access, target knowledge, actual force/authority and an objective. Failed intimidation can leave a witnessed grievance without summoning universal retaliation.

## Evidence travels through an observer

1. Establish the event or demonstration with its actual source, participants, targets, cost and scope.
2. Determine each observer's supported channel: direct sight/hearing, accessible record, named witness, instrument or declared faculty.
3. Record only supported content, time and provenance; preserve uncertainty or contradictory reports.
4. Let that observer interpret using known methods. Seeing a result need not identify its source or exact capacity.
5. Resolve disclosure through an authored interaction, then project only the player's learned record.

Observation, understanding, possessing a method/source and executing it stay distinct under [ability authoring](ABILITY_AUTHORING.md).
A private component, hidden reserve, dormant lineage, unseen origin, future outcome or another instance's relationship cannot enter a greeting through registry metadata.
A declared detector needs actual body/source, fuel, range, target/contact and counters. Detection of presence is not automatic identity, intent or full inventory disclosure.
Attire can suggest affiliation without proving it. A disguise changes ordinary evidence; recognition can come from a familiar voice, scar, manner or authenticated record under a stated channel.
Visible injury invites a question or accommodated task. It does not establish cause, prognosis, diagnosis or the owner's private body record.
A power demonstration reveals what happened in that test. An impressive lift can justify a larger load offer; it cannot certify water, reveal every spell or prove allegiance.
People views, unseen loadouts and other-world visits disclose nothing automatically. A parallel recollection belongs to the traveler; a counterpart can reject or test it without shared affection/debt.
Knowledge-aware options expose only known reasons. Secret prerequisites must not leak through unavailable-button text or a tooltip containing private target facts.

## Voices and reactions without a quest machine

Use concrete work, habits and stakes: fittings, a clean tray, a sister's record, a clinic slot or a roof before rain. Include purposeful ordinary exchanges with warmth, annoyance or humor and no material reward.
Repeated greeting selects first meeting, known return, occupied, unresolved commitment or changed-evidence wording from actual records.
Warmth can coexist with payment due; affection promises no free labor/companionship. Hostility can mean one refused bench or distrusted accusation without becoming combat.
Reactions can be read-only and award nothing. Recording a new disclosure or witnessed deed is a separate validated action; rendering cannot manufacture a memory.
If repeated work is allowed, each new batch pays its real inputs and serves an actual demand. Repeating a joke, apology or greeting never farms familiarity or unique rewards.
A changed reaction cites returned loan, demonstrated control, kept delivery or known harm/evidence. Choices say “Ask for the reading” or “Offer the spare seal”; engine IDs remain author-only.

## Availability, interruption and return

Schedules are original proposals tied to funded shifts, site capacity, travel and common-clock commitments. A speaker can depart or refuse contact; a menu reserves nobody and resets no deadline.
Before a paid choice, revalidate the current participants, location, life, consent, evidence, stock/custody and sequence. Stale or unavailable choices reject without spend.
Accepted work freezes its terms/revision/costs/participants and uses the real job/session owner. Closing the text pane is not cancelling the job.
Uncommitted exit leaves no debt/reservation/reward while funded actors can act and offers expire. Committed work separates refundable stock, consumed tests/study, partial work and persistent loans, disclosed before acceptance.
Interruptions name the cause and closure: participant departs, withdraws consent, alarm, injury or loss of contact. Future adapters must release reservations and preserve paid receipts.
Re-entry checks actual aftermath; it never restarts the clock, resurrects a speaker, repeats payment or turns a failed accepted promise into a clean refusal.
A witness dying or leaving before a consent-dependent rite can block it. No dialogue line supplies a replacement witness or a generic resurrection.
Native fainting/nonlethal recovery stays with its owner; severe losses need a selected failure profile. Absence/browser freeze remains a proposal, with no new offline aging, loss or schedules.

## Lifecycle and alternative bodies

Inherit the master's [origin/timeline owner](ORIGINS_AND_WORLD_TIMELINES.md), without assigning starts or Harry Potter content. These `O` interactions need that owner to define body, household, language, rights, support and time policy.

| Profile | Communication and interaction boundary |
|---|---|
| Ordinary scratch start | Learn names/language, gain introductions and buy or earn access; no remembered contacts or gear are invented |
| Random family | Record actual guardians/kin, household means and known ties; surname suggests a social role, not inherited powers |
| Infant body | Nonverbal signals and actual comprehension bound choices; a guardian can interpret uncertain needs and acts with their own means |
| Tree/nonhuman body | Declare sensory field, mobility, voice/signs and reachable contact; no hands, weapons, job or human language by renamed profile |
| Reincarnated successor | Retained recollection does not supply predecessor powers, anatomy, lawful property or other actors' recognition |

An infant's intent is not adult spoken bargaining. Show an available signal and the guardian's possible interpretation, with care/support costs owned by that actor.
A tree needs a tested sign system or qualified interpreter; wind/touch proves no speech, omniscience or consent. Language learning needs instruction/practice; named translation has availability/uncertainty and grants no fluency.
Schools, caretakers and institutions can recognize the current social role while refusing unsupported training, tools or missions.
Later-life remeetings use an actual elapsed-life transition selected by the master. The observer recognizes witnessed growth, authenticated identity or a learned sign, rather than reading the save profile.
“You used to tap twice for the red cup. Is that still yes?” requires actual guardian memory. An unrelated parallel guardian can ask “Who taught you that?” without inheriting the recollection.
No forced aging, browser-closed harm, inherited protagonist identity or adult equipment is added by these examples.

## Consequences have one authoritative owner

| Result | Existing foundation or required binding |
|---|---|
| Payment/trade/loan | Native carried inventory/currency bindings; simulation transfer/custody and exact owner/holder |
| Teaching or trial | Actual source/method eligibility, paid job, knowledge/progression/source owner; no ability from reading dialogue |
| Promise/favor/access | Actor facts, scoped institution/world facts, directed memory and accepted terms; exact campaign definition required |
| Observation/report | Observer-specific knowledge/disclosure; source evidence and uncertainty richer than current scalar stages need an adapter |
| Injury/threat/conflict | Actual body/vitals/encounter/pursuit owners and selected failure policy; dialogue cannot secretly apply damage |
| Unique settlement | Held/settled claim plus frozen cause/completion, retained beyond displayed-history pruning |

A favor can fund stock, lend an actual tool, authorize a bench or reserve teaching. Its consent, return terms and completion are explicit.
Refusal creates no offered cost/debt; accepted default or witnessed threat can change particular access. Private memory and public reputation stay separate; regional news needs actual transmission/audience.
Paying one service may grant useful evidence while leaving the cause unresolved. A successful narrow contract does not settle a larger war.
Durable social effects must stage with their material consequences in one validated save envelope. Failed persistence publishes neither payment nor changed speech/history.

## Set dialogue versus autonomous people, and relationships (2026-11-06)

Weibao asked whether somewhat autonomous NPCs mean writing set dialogue for every character to cover every option and result, and how relationships fit ([record](../USER_VISION.md#prose-rulebook-and-npc-dialogue-ideaboard-2026-11-06)). Proposals (AD32), ranked by how much they change the decision. Codes DA1–DA17; relationship rows reuse the RLA–RLAI owner above and [LSD126–LSD137](../LEAD_LIFE_SYSTEMS_DESIGN.md) for depth by tier.

Short answer: no. Set dialogue is for pivotal scenes of the top-tier cast. Everything else is rendered from state through a three-layer ladder, so an autonomous act never lacks a line and the writing load grows with verbs and thread types, not with characters times branches.

| Code | Idea | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DA1 | **Separate the act from the line.** An autonomous person's decision is state: job, place, mood, open threads, stance toward the player, memories. Dialogue is rendered from that state at the moment of talk. Three text layers: (a) situation templates, world-neutral ("busy at her bench", "still owed for the cart"); (b) a character's voice overlay, data not prose (register, two or three idioms, what they call you, one taboo); (c) hand-written set pieces. Only layer (c) is "set dialogue". | Existing greeting selector (first meeting, known return, occupied, unresolved commitment, changed evidence) is layer (a) already. SUI1: a scene is state, not prose. |
| DA2 | **Every autonomous verb ships with its telling.** When an NPC can do something on their own (take a job, move house, marry, refuse you, leave town), that verb lands with one greeting variant, one Reports line and one "heard about it" line, or it does not land. | RLT3's generic telling per event type, applied to talk. WR4: a generator ships only with a consumer. The telling is the consumer. |
| DA3 | **The greeting is composed, not picked.** Four slots: recognition (stranger, known, friend, enemy), current occupation (from the schedule), strongest open thread (a loan, a promise, a grievance, a deed they heard of), stance (from relationship axes). Each slot has a world-neutral template and optional world or character variants. | Replaces a per-character greeting tree with four small tables. `npc_schedule.js` and `relationship_view.js` already supply three of the four inputs. |
| DA4 | **Voice as data, by tier.** Tier 1 (the lead's close cast) gets hand lines for pivotal scenes plus voice data; tier 2 gets voice data over templates; tier 3 gets the role voice (a miller sounds like a miller). | Matches the accepted line that other people's lives are not all full. A thin canon stays thin on purpose. |
| DA5 | **Relationships are a few axes plus directed memories, already.** `relationship_view.js` holds axes (chronicle clamps to ±100), typed ties and up to 32 memories per source; `talk_bridge.js` holds rapport stages counted in things said. These are two systems reading as one feeling. Dialogue reads stance from the axes and mentions the strongest memory; rapport gates which topics open. | ACCOUNT FOR NOW: before a third meter appears, state which of the two a new feature reads. Rapport is familiarity, axes are feeling. No affection purchase (crush social content already refuses it). |
| DA6 | **Player options are shared verbs, gated by means and stance.** Ask about, offer, request, propose, apologize, confide, press, threaten, ask for help. Which verbs a person accepts, and how they answer, differs by character data and relationship, not by a hand-made tree per person. | AD6 means decide; social contests on the notch scale resolve pressed verbs. Fallen London and Crusader Kings are the references: qualities gate, text selects. |
| DA7 | **Results are state change plus a telling line.** A verb resolves through the existing contest or trade, writes a memory and an axis change, and the telling renders the line. Set dialogue is needed only when the result is a canon pivot. | Saves the line with the memory so reload never rerolls (DA13). |
| DA8 | **People reach out through the same ladder.** An NPC-initiated approach is a content request as data (event type, facts, cast by role, tone, seals), rendered through DA1's layers. | RLAI1; the absence and reach-out cards in [NPC absence](../research/NPC_ABSENCE_AND_REACH_OUT_2026-10-27.md). |
| DA9 | **A gap is logged, never shown.** When no variant exists for a state, the generic template plays and the state is logged to the authoring queue. The queue, not a full tree, is what gets written next, ahead of play where possible. | RLU1 and RLY2/RLY5. The player never sees an engine id (PRS6). |
| DA10 | **Threads, not meters, drive most talk.** Open commitments have four lines per thread type: open, kept, broken, lapsed. A loan, a promise, a grievance, a favour owed and a shared deed cover most of what people bring up. | Already the strongest signal in "Voices and reactions without a quest machine". Four lines per type beats four lines per person per type. |
| DA11 | **Second-order mentions travel with provenance.** NPC-to-NPC ties (RLF) change whose side someone takes and whom they mention; what Wen told Mira about you arrives through the channel it used, with its uncertainty. | B68 rumor rule. Lets autonomy show up in talk without new dialogue per pair. |
| DA12 | **Budget the lines before writing a world.** Hand lines needed ≈ tier-1 cast × pivotal scenes + thread types × 4 + verbs × response templates + voice rows. Per-character trees are cast × verbs × states, which is why nobody finishes them. | A world loop card (WR1) carries this count. Tingen's cast size makes the comparison concrete; see LATER. |
| DA13 | **Save what was said.** Any rendered line that carried a consequence is saved with the memory it created; reload shows the same words. | The existing proposer-validates-commit stance; keeps seeded saves reproducible. |
| DA14 | **Drift while you are away is content.** Village lives already court, marry, estrange and widow (`npc_lives.js`). The return greeting cites what changed since, from the person's own point of view. | The return note (UT3). Absence policy itself stays a proposal. |
| DA15 | **Kinds of relationship are typed ties with their own thread types.** Friendship, rivalry, mentorship, courtship, kin, employer each add a few thread types (a lesson owed, a challenge standing, a visit expected), not a separate meter or tree. | Ties already exist in the chronicle shape; crush social content is the courtship example. |
| DA16 | **The player chooses intents, not sentences.** Labels say "Ask for the reading" or "Offer the spare seal"; the character's own words are not written for them. Dial: a witnessed canon scene may offer verbatim lines. | Keeps trees small and matches existing label rules. |
| DA17 | **A language model, if ever, rewords within the ladder.** Rung one: reword a selected template line in a character's voice. Rung two: barks. Never decide the act. Templates are the fallback with the model off. | RLAI2/RLAI5 unchanged. |
