## Three independent decisions

**Cosmology** describes what exists and how places or histories relate. **Represented scope** describes the places, people and effects this delivered campaign can resolve. **Persistence** describes which actual changes survive leaving, failure, a cycle or another visit. World size, content coverage, mission length, danger and power scale are different axes.

A one-town assignment can take place in a genuine planet-sized world. Its mission ends; that world need not disappear. A tiny constructed realm can be permanent and support a home, ecology and generations. A vast setting can initially have only one implemented district. Calling a destination a subworld does not settle any of these choices.

The default proposal is durable histories and bounded first visits. Additional regions reveal more of an existing instance without restoring spent goods, dead people or unique rewards. A deliberately reset trial or new branch declares its different identity and retention rules. Neither reset nor permanence follows from a genre label.

## Cosmologies to compose

These are original authoring options, not claims about any cited franchise.

| Structure | What connects | Distinct useful play | Necessary boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary shared universe | Countries, planets or star systems share a causal history | Trade routes, ship construction, migration and distant conflict | Distance, transport, communication and local calendars; crossing a border does not clone a world |
| Neighboring independent universes | Worlds have their own histories and possibly different laws | Study compatible fuel, construct a passage, establish a cross-world workshop | Actual entry route, travelers/cargo, local body/source support and return conditions |
| Branching histories | Histories share an origin then diverge | Prequel intervention, alternative factions, people who took different paths | Fixed fork point and separate causal state; counterparts are distinct people after divergence |
| Nested/pocket realms | A larger setting contains bounded spaces with their own extent/rules | Grow a garden realm, explore a sealed academy, maintain a portable refuge | Interior/exterior ownership, exit, reach, capacity and what happens if its anchor is damaged |
| Layered realms | Physical, spirit, dream or other layers overlap under authored access rules | Different sensing, manifestation and interactions with shared locations | Which bodies/objects can contact which layers; metadata alone does not enforce native interaction |
| Expedition network | An institution or discovered route connects selected destinations | Live in a hub, accept assignments, settle or revisit independently | Issuer terms differ from destination truth; a contract is not the world's entire plot |
| Cycles and checkpoint histories | A region/history repeats under a specific cause | Knowledge-led research, rehearsed routes, different alliances | Reset domain, actual retained faculties/items, reset trigger and unique-claim policy |
| Constructed/story/simulated realms | An author, device, ritual or other source produces an environment | Modify a trial, rescue its inhabitants, investigate the controller | State reality and inhabitants' agency explicitly; an apparent simulation can still have real losses |
| Converged worlds | Portions of previously separate settings become connected or merged | Contested land, incompatible institutions, refugees and rebuilt supply | A declared merge event and actual ownership/history conflicts; by default no automatic universal law winner (a dominant world law or verse equalization can declare one) |
| Several multiverse clusters | Each network has its own connector, authority and compatible destinations | Build a passage beyond a known network; negotiate rival travel authorities | A cluster is not an extra power rank; access and source compatibility remain real requirements |

These structures can coexist. An independent universe can contain spirit layers, a pocket garden and a repeating city. A network can connect different multiverse clusters through one rare route. The master owns shared transport/time/identity contracts; a local GM owns that destination's laws. No arbitrary nesting depth is promised by the current engine.

## Visits and content coverage

For titles on worlds, regions, places and their organizations, [E4 C11-C13](../emergent/TITLES_ACHIEVEMENTS_AND_META_RULES_2026-10-15.md#c11-items-places-organizations-and-worlds-as-title-holders)
considers beneficiaries, borders, migration, splitting/merging and branch-world
identity. World achievements need not be the traveler's personal deeds or
grant identical benefits to every resident. These remain proposals.

| Delivered scope | Enough detail to be useful | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Encounter or small trial | Actual targets, rule, costs, choices, failure and aftermath | A whole living world or general conceptual-combat adapter |
| Bounded expedition | Entry, preparation, several connected sites/people, objectives, extraction and return | Complete geographic coverage or an automatic destination reset |
| Regional campaign | Institutions, recurring cast, careers, linked arcs, ordinary life and useful returns | Every distant actor being simulated |
| Multi-region campaign | Travel/supply between regions, consequences that cross them and longer local projects | All eras or all source material being adapted |
| Era/continuity expansion | More places or a separately frozen historical starting state | Rewinding or replacing an existing save |

These are content coverage stages, not character tiers. A bounded trial can contain overwhelming entities. A multi-region campaign can focus on ordinary craft and family life. Design can sketch the whole setting while implementation deepens one connected slice at a time.

Off-screen detail also has distinct resolution: authored background, a bounded aggregate state, or actual actors/controllers. Name which is available. Do not maintain a pretend global simulation or repeatedly generate contradictory off-screen news. When a background person becomes an actual participant, freeze their identity, means and prior commitments before acting.

## Other world and novel styles

Original seeds below are proposals. Novel comparisons have bounded primary sources in [candidate scouting](novel-worlds/CANDIDATES.md); genre resemblance is not source adaptation.

| Style | Original seed and ordinary life | Progression and distinctive conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Local fantasy or martial world | A floodplain town trades food, tools and instruction | Training, surgery/support where authored, craft and institutions; securing winter access can matter more than dueling |
| Cultivation and higher realms | A valley school owns a quarry and cultivable spiritual soil | Control, research, materials and bodily change; ascending leaves real people/projects and requires actual passage |
| Institutional expedition fiction | A route bureau shares a settlement with independent artisans | Missions, residence, service, research and source acquisition; issuer success can conflict with local welfare |
| Classes and everyday professions | An inn sits between hostile communities and supply routes | Cooking, hosting, mapping, trade and defense; social trust and actual stock affect who can be sheltered |
| System apocalypse | A coastal district loses its existing services after an authored change | Repair power/water, form households, develop faculties and salvage tools; competing reconstruction changes access |
| Tower or trial network | A vertical settlement relies on passages and inherited maps | Climb, supply, study, manufacture or administer; a clearance need not grant permanent rights to someone's home |
| Science fiction and technological ascension | An orbital repair habitat has scarce oxygen and rival fabrication claims | Research, pilots, modular suits, shipyards and automation; exceptional output still needs real supply unless its source removes that requirement |
| Horror and mysteries | An archive town loses people according to discoverable local causes | Investigation, protection, cooperation and occult craft; clues change safe actions while unknown does not mean automatically unbeatable |
| Superheroes and conceptual powers | A transit city has public protectors, private labs and contested identities | Training, experiments, equipment, reputation and actual function transfer; scope/contact/order create difficulty beyond HP |
| Nonhuman/ecological lives | A rooted grove trades shelter and seeds with travelers | Sensing, growth, symbiosis, craft through collaborators and authored avatars; mobile rivals can alter water/light while the tree has different reach |

Every seed needs its own sources or declared invented rules, local people, resources and progression before receiving a build packet. Do not register ten shallow reskins just because a genre table exists. Add depth to existing worlds and scout genuinely different decisions in parallel.

## Minimal new authoring records

The following are proposed records. They complement v1 composition without reinterpreting today's saved `worldId`.

- `CosmologyDefinition`: source boundary, structure, parent/layer/cluster relationships and law scopes. Connections need explicit types; a parent reference does not imply storage or universal control.
- `ConnectionDefinition`: actual endpoints, passage cause, eligible body/cargo, travel duration/exposure, visibility, cost, interruption and return. Reincarnation, summoning, physical transit and remote projection are different connections.
- `CoverageDefinition`: resolvable sites/actors/functions, background assertions, out-of-scope effect handling and expansion revision. Do not promise free-form reality rewriting outside supported resolution.
- `PersistenceDefinition`: exact durable domains, reset/revisit policy, unique claims, retained traveler state and local absence policy, linked to existing failure/transfer contracts.
- `VisitDefinition`: concrete instance, entry, scenario, represented coverage and frozen commitments. Visiting a place is not creating a replacement instance unless its declared policy says so.

Illustrative IDs are definition/instance/visit references, never display names: a `frontier-network` connection can lead to one `river-branch-1` instance; two paid visits refer to that same state. An earlier `river-branch-2` snapshot starts a separate history. The same source family can exist in multiple histories without merging their people or goods.

World laws specify where they apply. A imported method/source retains its real requirements; the host can supply those requirements or fail to support them. A genuinely independent faculty can work broadly. An authority tied to one issuer may have no claim elsewhere. Law conflicts require scoped, source-qualified interaction rules and effect order; never choose the winner from franchise prestige alone. Use [ability authoring](ABILITY_AUTHORING.md), [conceptual packet](conceptual/ENGINE_PACKET.md) and [assembly](PROGRESSION_AND_ASSEMBLY.md) for actual means and native limits.

## Three worked compositions

**Mission grows into residence:** the Exchange offers a paid water-line inspection in the existing Fallout branch. Mina and Imani have their own goals; finishing the issuer's inspection does not settle all water rights. A later earned residence reveals a second district in the same instance. The bypass, goods, debts and witnesses from the mission remain actual state. A new district needs content/adapters, not a fresh starting reward.

**A realm inside an original world:** a crafted seed-anchor supports a small interior grove. Its interior has declared water/light capacity and distinct access; the rooted occupant trades seeds through a collaborator. Moving the exterior anchor is not moving every interior tree through a human travel menu. Damage, eviction, clock relation, cargo and escape need authored consequences. This combines ecology, craft and nested space without asserting that tree reincarnation is canon in another setting.

**A repeating city inside a larger network:** an original fixed-duration city cycle has one paid memory-retention source. City claims and material goods reset under the selected rule; the traveler's supported retained knowledge does not reset. A remote hub receives no duplicated exported object merely because the city rolled back. If export is allowed, its actual custody and conservation policy must settle before reset. This is a proposed difficult engine extension, not current prestige or a universally available save-point.

## Sources, completion and coherent expansion

Completed stories and defined arcs are useful starting boundaries because their inspected endings can be held fixed. Completion does not prove that every rule/history was explained, or that later sequels share the selected continuity. Preserve an edition/range, actual source locators, C/A/I/O/U distinctions and unresolved rules using [lore/continuity](LORE_AND_CONTINUITY.md).

Original worlds can start with a frozen local rulebook instead of franchise canon. A living serial can start with an inspected chapter cutoff. A comic arc can use a bounded issue range. Prequels, aftermaths and what-if histories outside inspected material are original alternate continuities with explicit departures. No favorite-book list, purchase, whole-novel audit or claim that an entire comic universe is finished is needed to start original content.

## OD11 local economies and cross-world value, 2026-10-15

[Accepted direction](../USER_VISION.md#overarching-direction-discussion-od11-and-recovery-refinement-2026-10-15):
local economies, with means-based shared currencies/cross-world trade, arbitrage
and useful knowledge/items. Access and compatibility can grow. **Correction later
2026-10-15:** "selling cheats" means cheats centered on selling/trade, not sale of
the cheats themselves. No global market or universal conversion chosen.
[Clarification](../USER_VISION.md#anchors-trade-cheat-correction-and-discussion-workflow-2026-10-15).

### ACCOUNT FOR NOW: proposed implications, not a balance specification

- **EC1, relative abundance:** a common material, cheap energy source or ordinary
  technique can be valuable elsewhere. Preserve profitable discoveries as genuine
  rewards; don't automatically normalize prices or negate every trade advantage.
  Meaningful constraints follow actual transport, compatibility and market rules.
- **EC2, useful knowledge:** remembering a process, proving it, teaching it and
  operating it at scale are different capabilities. Tools, substrates, workforce,
  institutions and local laws of nature affect application. A cheat may remove
  these requirements where its actual power supports it; no universal technology
  suppression or compulsory patent/licensing system is selected.
- **EC3, demand and information:** price may reflect scarcity, buyer purpose,
  authenticity, trust, access or ignorance. Large trade may change supply/demand,
  attract partners/competition or leave prices stable under a supported market.
  These are options, not automatic punishment or a mandatory economic simulator.
- **EC4, commerce-oriented cheats (corrected):** possible powers include seeing
  buyers/demand, better sale terms, remote/system markets, appraisal, currency
  conversion, sale-triggered growth or rewards, and moving cheap goods to places
  where they are valuable. A system buyer may use its own valuation instead of
  local prices. Specify what counts as a sale, whether reward is coins, power or
  another benefit, and the actual conditions for repeat sales or buyback loops.
  Intended profitable loops are allowed; no universal diminishing-return cap,
  market tax or forced price normalization is selected.
- **EC5, history and claims:** shared currency needs an accepting issuer/network
  or conversion means; local legal ownership and actual custody remain distinct.
  Rewinding a sale, visiting a parallel market or importing capital needs the
  selected history/retention policy. Preserve intentional repeatable arbitrage;
  save/reload accidents are not the same as authored duplication powers.

Subtle future opportunities include brokerage, translation/appraisal, samples,
portable workshops, information businesses, trade-credit networks and production
chains spanning worlds. They are ideas, not a new implementation wave or required
prerequisites for a first useful trade. Prices, caps, taxation and market simulation
remain unselected. Documentation only; no source/build/save changes.

> EC4, cheat commerce: distinguish transferring ownership of a power, copying
