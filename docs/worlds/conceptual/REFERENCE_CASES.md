## Copying taxonomy: what is actually acquired?

“Copy” describes an acquisition verb poorly unless the resulting owner/state is explicit. Several routes can yield the same method, or wholly different outputs.
Weibao's follow-up explicitly asks for Sharingan-style copying versus kill/sacrifice copying, finer mastery and difficult powers such as time stop.

| O acquisition route | Actual output | Donor/source consequence | Receiver's remaining execution gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Observation | Timestamped visible evidence or fragment | Donor unchanged | Understand/test method; body, source and fuel remain real |
| Recording | Supported signal record, possibly high fidelity | Donor unchanged; actual privacy/discovery aftermath | Interpretation, complete evidence and trained execution |
| Motor mirroring | Temporary supported action pattern | Donor unchanged | Actual reach, anatomy, control and eligible contact |
| Taught/reconstructed method | Understood tested procedure revision | Teacher unchanged; trial specimens can be consumed | Owned method, mastery and current support |
| Output emulation | A declared temporary function supplied by the imitator's source | Donor can remain unchanged | Imitator's source, duration, target and paid support; no donor-body grant |
| Memory imprint | Specified remembered procedure/experience | Declared copied/removed memory, not assumed | Memory accuracy, understanding and bodily means |
| Victory-gated learning | Eligible method reward after actual qualified defeat | Defeated donor can remain alive and retain method | Proven victory receipt, eligibility and learner's own source/body |
| Kill-gated copy | A declared method/source output after an eligible actual death | Actual donor death; removal of source depends on the rule | Selected lethal policy, precise donor outcome and support |
| Sacrifice extraction | Declared function/method from a consumed actual specimen/token | Named input permanently consumed | Legal custody, extraction procedure, real recipient/source compatibility |
| Borrowed function | Temporary licensed or forcibly borrowed execution | Donor availability/loss follows the chosen rule | Loan/bond, expiration, support and restoration |
| Faculty/source theft | Actual specified source moves or exclusive execution transfers | Donor loses the specified source/use | Physical/soul/body compatibility, installation/binding, acquisition history |
| Blueprint duplication | A process description or newly manufactured device | Original remains; actual materials/labor consumed | Fabrication, calibrated specimen, fitting and operation |

These are original semantic categories, not claims that Sharingan or Universal Thief implements each row. Naruto NU2 and Bleach BU4/BU6 retain their source gates.
No one table row establishes copying every organ, anatomy, lineage, reserve, title or personal soul. Conversely, an explicitly source-qualified copier can be much stronger than ordinary learning.
The copy target can be a definition, personal learned revision, mode, faculty, physical source instance, stored reserve, memory or action result; select one before preview.
Acquisition trigger, extraction fidelity, transfer loss, possession, understanding and execution are independent. A kill condition alone does not settle them.
Family/function tags aid comparison; they never execute or make sources interchangeable. Identical looking attacks can have different contact, fuel and retention.

## Case 1: repair as an actual transformation

J1 supplies strong restoration with specific exceptions; it should not be flattened to a small healing bonus. Exact uninspected extremes remain U.
O adaptation `conceptual.variant.rejoin`: restore one actual damaged compatible mechanism to its recorded intact form after deliberate touch.
Authoring target: actual specimen ID, retained damage/form history, owner/holder, installation and currently recoverable components; an item key alone may aggregate several goods.
Output: the same object is repaired, not removed and replaced with a newly granted good. Custody, provenance and unique claims remain attached.
O low use fixes a simple known break; developed use handles moving assemblies; mature use can restore complex compatible damage quickly with sufficient real source/control.
An ordinary repairer needs parts/time/knowledge; a truly superior Rejoin can restore the same eligible object without those material costs if that is its declared function.
Do not impose ordinary replacement-stock costs just to balance a conceptual repair power. Its actual source cost and exceptions still need definition.
O fixture exception: no restoration of consumed fuel, destroyed extraction token, prior knowledge, dead actor or missing independent source; this belongs to Rejoin, not a new J1 canon list.
J1's self-wound/dead-life exceptions apply only to that source-bound reference. An original variant may have other rules if labeled and paid.
Startup establishes actual touch/form eligibility; completion checks whether the same object remains. Lost contact before impact follows a frozen cost policy.
Broken support interrupts use; completed repair persists. Stopping/reloading cannot award a duplicate repaired item or replay a unique acquisition.
Observers learn a repaired seam/result they witnessed. They do not automatically learn its entire prior damage, ownership or hidden source.
Counter: deny eligible contact, destroy/remove a genuinely required retained part under this O rule, or apply a source-specific transformation Rejoin cannot restore.
An applicable repair can invalidate a destruction plan. Do not invent “plot damage” immunity after the player acquires a working counter.
Engine gap: exact item-instance condition/form, identity-preserving repair, target contact and native condition publication; no general repair effect exists today.

## Case 2: information access and behavior writing

J2's information/behavior function is powerful; a faithful version cannot be reduced to a charisma modifier or ordinary interview.
O adaptation `conceptual.variant.ledger_hand`: contact an eligible actor, read declared authored memory domains, and write a supported behavioral constraint.
Target: stable actor/body identity and actual memory/constraint owner. Knowledge in the recipient and a command prohibition in the target are separate transforms.
O low use reads one supported recent event; developed use reads selected stored history and writes one tested prohibition; mature use reads broader actual records and coordinates compatible constraints.
That staged O envelope is not a claim that Heaven's Door itself begins with those limitations. J2 supports the reference's broad life-history description.
O cost: actual source reserve plus contact/control commitment; knowledge read and writing have separately declared scope/cost.
Constraint example: “cannot open this named gate during this encounter.” Store a typed action/gate restriction with cause, source and termination, not an executable sentence.
Natural-language UI can express the authored rule, but its meaning comes from a validated rule definition. Unsupported new sentences produce no authority.
Actual strong effect: the eligible target's forbidden action fails even if their ordinary desire is to perform it. More HP does not automatically overcome the prohibition.
Controller, player command, native action and routine all need the same supported restriction check. Blocking only one button is incomplete implementation.
Source-specific eligibility/resistance must be decided before encounter; no universal mental immunity or automatic soul control is inferred.
Termination: authored expiry, valid erase/restore mechanism or actual source-specific break. Removal restores lawful choices without erasing memories of compulsion by default.
Conflicts: two prohibitions can coexist; mandatory and prohibited actions need a selected priority/resolution rule. Do not pick the plot-favorable result.
Counter: deny the actual trigger, interrupt startup, or use an acquired eligible constraint-removal mechanism at real cost; ordinary disbelief is not a universal dispel.
Knowledge boundaries: read only the actual source-supported domains; no unrelated private actor builds leak through preview. Target knowledge of writing follows its declared experience.
Engine gap: private memory read projections, source/cause-bound constraints and native/controller enforcement. Existing knowledge/title effects do not implement this.

## Case 3: spatial removal changes the scene

J3 establishes removal plus spatial repositioning, with an unknown removed destination; it does not prove ordinary damage, a loot bag or an accessible storage realm.
O adaptation `conceptual.variant.palm_cut`: remove one eligible authored segment and join its declared neighboring positions under a fixed topology rule.
Target: one material segment or one spatial interval; those are different modes with explicit occupants, boundary/collision and eligible contact.
O low use removes a reachable thin barrier; developed use removes a selected gap to close distance; mature use can remove a larger eligible segment with real output/control.
A wall can cease blocking regardless of its ordinary durability where the declared erasure actually applies. Preserve that strength rather than using a normal damage formula.
Numerical reach, startup, output extent and body speed still determine whether contact/selection happens before a counter.
O cost: actual reserve/strain and chosen extent; no canon duration/range/cooldown is asserted from J3's short paragraph.
Removed items leave their actual owner/custody history with an explicit absent/removed state. They are not quietly granted to the caster as stolen loot.
The authored adjacent positions join once; movement and carried objects resolve against the selected topology before further impacts.
O unsupported destination remains inaccessible. Retrieval or restoration requires a separately selected source, not an assumed return from inventory.
Interruption before resolved removal follows startup rules; after completion, a stopped faculty does not automatically regrow the deleted wall.
Concurrent erasure/repair needs a precise selected order and restore eligibility. Erased versus broken are different states.
Counter: prevent eligible hand/path contact, leave declared extent before resolution or use a qualified spatial support mechanism. High wall HP alone is not an erasure counter.
Knowledge: visible disappearance/repositioning can be observed; removed destination and mechanism remain unknown without actual evidence.
Engine gap: spatial topology/occupancy, removed-object identity, native navigation and interruption. A world fact “wall gone” can approximate one authored scene only.

## Case 4: stopped time still contains actions

J4 supports stopped-time attack/escape and exceptional movement. It does not select a uniform number of seconds or make every time power equivalent.
O adaptation `conceptual.variant.held_beat`: suspend the selected scene's external motion while the supported caster gets a finite budget of internal action steps.
Scope is explicitly O scene-local for the proposed proof. A future global variant can be stronger, but needs a much broader native/world scheduler contract.
Freeze who/what may move, which objects become interactive on contact, range, due work, existing projectiles and later release before commitment.
O low use supplies one internal step; developed use supplies several controlled steps; mature use can sustain more actual work and overwhelm slower eligible opponents.
Do not describe this as merely “pause and gain speed.” Frozen opponents cannot act unless an actual supported exception applies.
Caster movement, damage, object manipulation and reserve spend still resolve inside the stopped interval. Time stop is not invulnerability or resurrection by itself.
External time and internal action budget are distinct. A paused common clock with all actors inactive is a player decision boundary, not this function.
O budgets pay startup reserve, strain and per-step support; exact duration/cooldown and exceptional mover rules require selected source evidence for a direct JoJo implementation.
Specify queued impacts: immediate within supported contact versus pending until release; projectiles cannot receive both damage receipts.
Multiple time effects need selected nesting/priority, shared affected set and bounded termination. Save/reload may not grant extra internal steps.
Ending the effect resumes external due times once according to the frozen policy. No double catch-up jobs, infinite paused cooldown or free recovery inside the stop.
Knowledge: frozen observers can later see changed positions/wounds; they do not witness every internal action unless their real source supports perception there.
Counter: prevent startup, survive the actual available impact, use a qualified exceptional mover or maintain a paid preexisting compatible defense. No invented universal “time immunity.”
An overwhelming supported time stopper can win against an unprepared weaker actor. Some encounters truly fail; exact lethal/offline policy stays unselected.
Engine gap: dual/internal clocks, actor action permission, object-contact/impact semantics, native timers and durable step receipts. Existing decision pauses supply none of those by themselves.

## Case 5: steal a move, not necessarily its means

U1 supports move theft as a category only. O `conceptual.variant.earned_imprint` qualifies an eligible defeated donor's method and acquires a specific learned revision.
Trigger may be victory, actual eligible death, contact or paid token sacrifice; these are alternative authored variants, not interchangeable flavor text.
Victory leaves an actual living donor where the encounter was nonlethal. Kill-gated acquisition requires true life state and a separately selected lethal policy.
Sacrifice consumes the actual owned token once. A copied technique and a transplanted source are different rewards even if the same target was defeated.
O lower use records a fragment; developed use acquires a tested narrow mode; mature extraction can faithfully acquire the declared whole method without inventing a weaker-method niche.
Donor loss is declared: copied method retains donor use; exclusive theft removes the method/use; neither automatically steals faculty, fuel or body.
Receiver needs actual support unless the chosen source supplies it. A stronger emulation variant can supply temporary missing support, with declared cost/termination.
Snapshot donor method revision, mastery policy, observed evidence and retained personal refinements; definition ID alone cannot capture every individual variant.
Counter/failure: invalid donor/output, incomplete eligible evidence or missing extraction input rejects; legal risky extraction can fail at the frozen cost/evidence policy.
Receipt keys bind donor instance, acquisition route and eligible output. Repeated visits, same donor death or reload cannot mint another unique acquisition.
Engine gap: personal method/source-instance fidelity, eligible defeat/death receipts and atomic donor consequences. Current copy/transfer has only narrower definition-ID semantics.

## Case 6: HP theft needs a declared conservation rule

U1 names HP stealing without an inspected exact formula. O `conceptual.variant.vital_siphon` transfers current health; maximum-health theft is a separate much stronger transformation.
Target: actual compatible living donor/recipient bodies, current/max health and life policy. “HP” is not automatically all tissue, lifespan, soul or identity.
O current-health transfer amount is bounded by eligible donor health, recipient missing health and actual ability output; select whether excess dissipates or remains with donor.
Conserved fixture: debit exactly the credited amount; full recipient gets zero transfer rather than draining the donor through independent clamps.
O lower use drains a small contact amount; developed use reaches more eligible targets; mature use can transfer substantial supported health and dominate weaker foes.
Maximum-health theft must explicitly reduce donor capacity, reconcile current health and increase recipient capacity under compatible native publication. It is not two ordinary vital effects.
Life consequences remain separate: reaching zero does not secretly select death, resurrection or a kill-copy reward in the current nonlethal game.
Cost/contact/interruption/cooldown are frozen; no double recipient credit from multiple callbacks, simultaneous siphons or reload.
Counter: deny eligible contact/source channel or use supported resistance. Stronger numeric output is meaningful where the siphon model actually scales with it.
Engine gap: paired conserved vital transfer and maximum/body capacity mutation; current independent clamps do not establish either contract.

## Case 7: identity has several state owners

U1 names identity stealing, but supplies no inspected social/body/relationship formula. O adaptations must select appearance, credential, office, claimed identity, body/source or soul identity explicitly.
O `conceptual.variant.borrowed_office` transfers a named local credential/authority under its actual issuer rule; it does not change the persistent actor ID.
O `conceptual.variant.face_cast` changes visible appearance; recognition requires an actual observer's evidence and supported detector, not universal NPC mind rewriting.
O low use imitates a visible mark; developed use holds one valid credential; mature genuine authority theft may really revoke donor access and grant recipient jurisdiction.
Those are distinct variants, not three canonical Universal Thief identity levels. A whole-body/soul replacement is a further consequential profile, not a stronger disguise checkbox.
Donor relationships, history, custody and debts stay with their actual owners unless the exact rule transforms them. Beliefs may remain mistaken or contested.
Title grant/remove alone is not exclusive office transfer or propagation of recognition. Define issuer, instance, uniqueness, recognition, loss and restoration.
Private memories stay private; forged appearance does not reveal the copied actor's thoughts or automatically persuade an observer who saw the theft.
Counter: actual authenticated evidence, supported perception, jurisdiction refusal or return of an exclusive credential. Do not invent universal “identity immunity.”
Engine gap: scoped appearance/recognition, exclusive authority lineage and body/soul transitions. Stable actor IDs must not be repurposed as costumes.

## Case 8: steal a concept through a named mechanic

U1 supports metaphysical theft generally, without proving that arbitrary words can transform anything. O `conceptual.variant.borrowed_fastening` moves one eligible latch's self-lock function into a real collector source.
Target: actual authored detachable fastening with one self-lock owner. Stealing its function opens the latch and prevents self-lock while the collector holds that exact function.
The latch remains the same owned object; collector custody, source capacity, contact, current function and restoration are separately authoritative.
O low use affects one touched simple latch; developed use handles a moving eligible mechanism; mature variant can handle greater eligible assemblies if its actual capacity/control grows.
This is a strong conceptual effect on an eligible mechanism regardless of ordinary material durability, not a minus-one defense debuff.
One collector may hold one such function in the small proof. A future superior source may hold many without forcing a new penalty; that would need its actual support/adapter.
Contact denial, alternative physical lashing, a real relinquished clasp or enough force to break/disarm the collector can be paid counters for causal reasons.
Restoration returns the specified function once, not the entire object, prior cargo position, lost stock or observer memories. Unsupported destruction/restoration stays a named uncertainty.
Engine gap: typed function ownership/lease plus contact and cause-bound restoration. Fixed authored fact keys can model this one scene without a universal concept interpreter.
The [engine packet](ENGINE_PACKET.md) specifies the two-route small proof, fixed threat, real costs, failure and abstention. No encounter is played or selected for runtime here.

## Granularity, mastery and subprogression

One technique can progress in evidence resolution, understanding, trigger reliability, contact precision, scope/output, duration, efficiency, recovery and combination control.
Only relevant facets are authored. Spatial removal needs extent/topology selection; repair needs form fidelity; information writing needs constraint correctness; time stop needs step/contact control.
Knowing the method, completing one facet, acquiring a source and owning an execution mode are separate. A successful output trial need not improve memory-reading fidelity.
Practice has an actual task, paid input, observation and resulting qualification. Genuine failed tests retain evidence/expense without awarding a false successful function.
Subprogression may unlock a narrow reliable use, then use on moving targets, then interruption-safe recovery or compatible multi-action integration.
Some improvements are numerical and meaningful: more output, range, internal steps or faster startup can reverse an otherwise fixed matchup.
Some improvements change rules: valid restoration of a newly qualified form, copied support rather than only motion, or ability to act during another time stop.
Do not invent such an unlock merely because a mastery integer rose. Name its actual source/practice evidence and precise transformation.
Branches can specialize but need not permanently exclude each other or carry equal penalties. A superior mature variant can improve several facets at once.
Structural/body/source stages remain with local catalogues; these are semantic learned-method/mode tests, not another universal module ladder.

## Ability forms and what can progress

Follow-up: Weibao asks for technique mastery, biological improvement/evolution and other advancement. This O semantic map links [shared progression](../PROGRESSION_AND_ASSEMBLY.md); its five rows are illustrative, overlapping starting points, with no canonical stage, universal ladder or runtime anatomy.
Classify purpose, source and development separately: an escape function can be learned footwork, wings, a bonded power, owned apparatus or passage granted by an ally, each with different support.
| Ability form | Distinct progression inside it | Result and authoritative owner |
|---|---|---|
| Learned skill, discipline or specific technique | Understand/acquire a method; practice mastery facets; refine its personal revision; discover modes; reconstruct missing parts; synthesize compatible methods | Broad competence differs from a specific practiced action. Knowledge, personal method/mastery and actual body/source support stay separate; copying theory grants no trained muscle |
| Biological trait, aptitude or active body faculty | Ordinary conditioning; maturation; capacity growth; exposure-driven adaptation; awakening a latent function; structural mutation/organ development; metamorphosis or a new supported form | Fictional physiology really changes reach, senses, output or permitted actions. Trained control, reserve and anatomy progress separately; changing form can retain learning while losing its old bodily execution |
| Personal source, lineage, spirit, pact or conceptual power | Acquire the actual source; awaken/activate it; develop capacity/output/control; deepen a source-specific relationship; evolve its architecture; qualify new target domains or combine supported sources | Source/body/bond and learned use have real owners. Larger fuel storage does not automatically improve throughput, precision, replenishment or authority; a new source function requires its actual mechanism |
| Equipment, implant, artifact or constructed system | Improve specimen quality; qualify a structural upgrade; fit/refit compatible parts; calibrate; develop operator mastery; research/redesign or replace the actual source | The retained item/body binding changes. Recharge/rest/repair restore current reserves/condition; they do not by themselves grant a new structural function. Installation is not possession or learned operation |
| Relational, institutional or environmental capability | Develop a companion's actual competence; build mutual coordination/trust; earn scoped office/access; learn tested designs; fund facilities/supply networks; acquire jurisdiction or local environmental support | Power can reside in another actor, issuer, site or environment. Better coordination does not copy their body/source; rank does not guarantee combat strength; a paid workshop can create genuinely new supported actions |
Every development can tune a quantity, change a function, broaden eligible targets, improve reliability, create a branch or fuse supported mechanisms. None requires a new name, universal rarity ceiling or mandatory replacement of the original method.
Acquisition trigger and reward differ: practice, insight, taught transmission, actual feat, research, exposure, material assimilation, surgery, pact or chance must cause a declared eligible change; eating something is no generic inheritance recipe.
Specify what grows, where it lives, what caused it, paid means, functional evidence, branching/irreversibility and retained/lost support. Numerical strength remains useful; genuinely superior development need not gain a forced penalty.
O example: one courier refines a sidestep's timing, conditions legs for greater output, grows a qualified new sensory organ, learns to interpret it and fits compatible gear. These are distinct changes that combine in one escape, not five automatic rewards from one XP event.

## Further families: future sight and other deep variants

Follow-up, 2026-10-03: Weibao asks for more ability families with substantial variation, especially future sight. Everything in this section is O design, without new franchise/canon claims.
Forecasting, perception/concealment, spatial positioning, defensive interference and bodies/proxies are five worked starting families. The inventory stays open; new mechanisms deserve their own actual target/state rules.

### Future sight: inference, prediction and changing fate differ

Task: avoid the same fixed sentinel's two-step attack while protecting a carrier. Sentinel timing, reach and force do not scale to the player's chosen sight method.
Information acquisition never supplies movement speed, stamina, physical contact, a shield or authority to change an event. Knowing an unavoidable strike can still leave the actor unable to survive it.

| O variant | What it reveals | What makes its result different |
|---|---|---|
| Pattern Read | Likely next action inferred from observed posture and learned habits | Present evidence, not literal future access; unfamiliar technique/deception can invalidate the inference |
| Near Echo | One actual upcoming committed action/result within a declared short horizon | True source-supported future information; freeze whose action, eligible event and whether later intervention changes the vision |
| Fork Lens | Outcomes of several explicit candidate courses under stated conditions | Conditional futures; changing a target, policy, input or other actor's response creates a different branch |
| Danger Sense | A warning of an eligible impending harm | Warning without necessarily revealing attacker, cause, exact time or a usable escape route; false positives follow its defined rule |
| Remote Omen | A symbolic future event involving an eligible person/place | Interpretation can be uncertain even if the vision is genuine; trigger, horizon, symbol meaning and target binding are separate |
| Clear Horizon | Detailed actual action sequences, including source-supported hidden causes across several participants | Can be simply more accurate, informative and broad than weaker methods; do not add forced blindness just to balance it |
| Sealed-Future Read | An event guaranteed by an actual authored fate mechanism | Knowledge of an existing guarantee, not creation of fate; the guarantee's enforcement, exceptions and domain must already be defined |

An outcome-editing power is separate: selecting a branch, forcing an event, rerouting probability or rewriting a cause changes state. Seeing several futures alone does not grant those actions.
For each variant select horizon, participants, resolution, trigger, update latency, certainty, cost, retention, causal coverage and what intervening use changes.
“Five seconds ahead” and “five actions ahead” behave differently when actors have different speeds or a time stop creates internal actions.
Personal future, target future, place future and all-future coverage are different scopes. Seeing one person's danger need not expose private cargo or unrelated conversations.
A vision can legitimately reveal hidden facts if its real source supports that information. Ordinary preview must not reveal the same facts for free through error text or branch labels.
Select mutable, conditional or enforced future before use. Do not quietly switch from accurate to vague when the player finds a successful counter.
If intervention changes a mutable future, the old vision remains an accurate dated reading of that earlier course. A new reading pays its declared cost and identifies changed assumptions.
For a fixed/guaranteed event, the actual fate mechanism must resolve contradictory attempted actions. A prediction label cannot force an impossible outcome or silently override another acquired authority.
O lower mastery may recognize a single clear image but misinterpret its cause; developed control reads selected moving-target sequences; mature control can maintain several genuinely detailed supported courses.
Facet tracks: trigger reliability, time-horizon stability, temporal resolution, participant discrimination, branching capacity, interpretation, update latency and response integration.
Practice can improve interpretation without increasing horizon. Greater horizon can increase useful information without improving the user's response speed.
New thresholds can open an actual action: distinguish a feint from its following strike, update after an ally intervenes, or inspect a second candidate plan with its own support.
Powerful sight can trivialize a lower predictable threat. Fast enough, broad enough and sufficiently supported foreknowledge deserves that advantage.
Counters work through actual reasons: faster attack than usable response, out-of-scope simultaneous events, genuine signal interference or source-qualified causality manipulation. “Boss cannot be predicted” needs its own real source rule.
Overlapping oracles require consistent snapshots, assumptions and authority order. Two reliable reads of the same course cannot disagree merely to create drama.
Ordinary NPCs cannot counter a private vision without evidence of the resulting action or a supported detector. A sensing enemy can react if its actual source permits it.
Interrupted reading retains completed paid evidence under its rule; termination stops new information, not already learned memories or wounds.
Engine need: distinguish reading a frozen committed outcome from inference and bounded forward simulation. The latter needs copied authoritative state plus native inputs/policies, private projections and no live RNG/time/save mutation.

### Perception and concealment

Detection can read reflected light, sound, body activity, energy, intent, identity or a specific mark. Each exposes different information and needs actual acquisition/body/support.
Invisibility can alter emitted/reflected light; camouflage changes appearance; attention diversion changes noticing; memory concealment changes later recall. These are different targets and counters.
A superior detector may defeat several weaker concealment methods. An accurate signature detector still needs its real signature/contact history unless its source supplies that knowledge.
Psychometry can read actual object history under its declared scope; seeing past custody does not transfer ownership, read every holder's mind or reveal future events.
Mastery can improve discrimination, coverage, noise tolerance and interpretation independently. Native sight, NPC reports, pursuit and private knowledge must all use the selected channel rules.

### Spatial positioning

Teleportation moves an actual actor/cargo; swapping exchanges actual occupants; a portal connects places; phasing changes supported contact; acceleration still traverses the intervening route.
Author destination knowledge, endpoint access, occupants, momentum, collision, carried/borrowed cargo, companions, interruption and return. A remembered coordinate can be stale.
Short sight-bound movement and remote anchor travel can share a purpose while having different prerequisites. A superior source may simply remove earlier limits when it truly supplies that function.
Moving inside cover, bypassing a lock and escaping a committed encounter have different effects on custody, occupancy and witnessed history; no automatic world reset follows relocation.
Mastery can improve landing precision, moving-target lock and compatible cargo handling. General native movement/scene topology remains a missing adapter, not a location-name edit.

### Defensive interference

Blocking intercepts an effect; absorption converts eligible input; reflection redirects it; suppression disables a specific execution; negation removes an eligible effect; adaptation develops a response after actual exposure.
Name the affected channel, stage of impact, capacity, eligible source/effect, information required, conversion loss, target selection and residual state. One “magic resistance” number cannot express every mechanism.
Reflection need not copy an attack's source or cause credit. Absorbed energy becomes usable fuel only through an actual supported conversion, with capacity and receipts.
Source severing, faculty suppression, method removal and removal of a current effect have different retention/restoration. A silenced caster can still possess their method.
Reactive adaptation needs recorded exposures and actual development; a genuinely immediate adaptive source is a different stronger variant. No mandatory universal immunity or eventual solution is assumed.
Mastery can improve timing, discrimination and eligible scope. Simultaneous reflection/negation loops need declared priority, bounded activations and one damage receipt per actual impact.

### Bodies, proxies and replication

Illusion presents evidence; projection supplies a supported temporary actor/interface; physical clone creates another actual body; summon brings an actual entity; transformation changes the existing body's supported functions.
Define body versus actor identity, independent minds/knowledge, source ownership, reserves, cargo, control, consent, death, persistence and return. A family tag cannot decide whether memories merge.
One shared mind with several bodies, several independent copies and remote puppets make different decisions. Parallel practice earns only the experience their actual source/learning-transfer rule permits.
Morphing a limb may enable reach or contact while retained equipment ceases to fit. Copying appearance need not reproduce anatomy, lineage, soul or a compatible fuel source.
Mature replication can genuinely multiply useful labor or combat power if real sources, control and supplies support it. Do not invent a universal clone penalty to protect solo balance.
Ending a proxy must settle actual transferred goods, damage, acquired knowledge and source costs once. Recalled cargo is not duplicated in both bodies on reload.

## Open-ended design and advancement

Latest direction: Weibao explicitly says there are likely many more ability/progression types and asks us not to be limited. The earlier tables organize examples; they are not an exhaustive ontology, compulsory roadmap or ceiling on imagination.
Investigate an unfamiliar mechanism on its own terms before classifying it. Allow overlapping, emergent, composite, externally supplied and entirely new forms rather than forcing every idea into a familiar row.
Additional O directions below are examples for exploration, not selected world rules or a replacement closed taxonomy.
| Directions to explore | Possible ability and progression behavior | Concrete question before implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Pacts, vows, taboos, reciprocal laws and ritual permissions | Fulfilled terms can expand a contract's actual rights; ritual comprehension can change valid execution; a restriction can fund a particular stronger variant | Who recognizes/enforces the agreement, what actual cause advances it, and what survives breach or return? Vow costs are local rules, not mandatory penalties for every power |
| Names, memory, identity, stories and inherited legacies | Recovering a true name can open a function; a recorded deed can change a sourced authority; selective inherited experience can progress differently from practical mastery | What information or identity actually changes, whose history supplies it, and what gives that record causal power rather than merely granting a title? |
| Consumption, assimilation, symbiosis, parasites and ecological growth | A compatible substance can change a real source; a symbiont can develop independently; seasons, habitat or an organism's distributed structures can enable new functions | What is actually acquired/digested/bonded, where do new functions reside, and what body/environment support and provenance remain? No universal consumption-to-inheritance formula |
| Fortune, debts, exchange and causality | An eligible accumulated claim can fund an effect; repayment can release an acquired function; authored causal interventions can alter which developments occur | Which actual owner, probability rule, obligation or event is transformed, and how are paid inputs, competing causes and irreversible consequences settled? |
| Domains, collectives, devotion and distributed existence | Territory can supply local authority; a network can gain functions through actual connected participants; several bodies or sites can jointly develop one supported source | Is progress held by a person, group, jurisdiction, environment or distributed source, and what happens when that real support is split, relocated or lost? |
Advancement can also depend on discovery, reconstruction, transmission, resonance, institutional legitimacy, environmental change, life-stage transitions, history or a source-specific event. Do not require practice XP, a breakthrough ritual or a generic level for every route.
Growth may be continuous, branching, cyclic, event-driven, reversible or lasting; latent potential, current access, available output and learned control can evolve at different rates. Greater strength and genuinely superior functions remain valid outcomes.
Develop new variants from concrete interactions: who acts, what changes, how the change occurs, who holds it and what can be learned or retained. Their mechanism can warrant a new adapter instead of being flattened into the closest existing stat.
An unusual source need not resemble human physiology, a soul-bound blade, a component assembly or a private individual. Verify source-derived rules when selected; keep invented mechanics labeled O and qualify their actual execution.
Novel ideas remain compatible with bounded delivery: choose one demonstrable interaction and integrate its real state owners without declaring every possible future mechanism implemented.

### Across the game

Weibao extends the open-ended direction to other aspects as well. Apply it across world/cosmology design, places/ecology, characters/forms of life, cultures/institutions, stories/timelines, crafting/economies, relationships, encounters/consequences, activities/play styles, pacing/automation and presentation/interactions.
Current lists, counts, templates and worked examples are navigation aids and initial coverage, not exhaustive required structures. An unfamiliar world or mechanic can require different actors, institutions, causal rules, narrative forms or ways to interact.
Allow new discoveries and emergent connections to change proposals; preserve coherent local rules, bounded source evidence, accepted decisions and actual saved consequences. Broader openness does not silently reinterpret an already committed event or erase its history.
The creative scope can stay open while each selected playable slice has definite behavior and an observable gate. This shared direction is captured here for coordinator integration; it assigns no additional file owners, runtime edits or new worlds.
