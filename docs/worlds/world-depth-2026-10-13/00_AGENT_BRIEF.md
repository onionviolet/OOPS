## What "deeper" means
Go below the headline power system into the life of the world. Per world, 12-18 considerations spread across angles like these (pick what fits; skip what doesn't make sense for that world):
- The world's one unique verb / loop that makes it mechanically distinct from the other worlds
- Ordinary daily life texture: work, food, lodging, money, chores, seasons, calendar, holidays
- Institutions, law, gossip, reputation, who notices you and what they remember
- Small devices/materials/crafts and their knock-on consequences (micro to macro chains)
- How time, idle/offline play and absence work there; what keeps running while away
- Death, injury, aging, succession and heirs in that world specifically
- What crosses in/out (items, knowledge, powers, people) and the in-world reason/cost
- Late-game usefulness: why a strong returning character still has reasons to be here
- Canon pressure points: where player intervention, refusal or abstention changes things
- Presentation: frame tint (V6), ambient header (V7), state edges (V8), log voice, VFX, sound, a panel only this world reveals
- Subtle look-alike pairs and little details a fan would notice
