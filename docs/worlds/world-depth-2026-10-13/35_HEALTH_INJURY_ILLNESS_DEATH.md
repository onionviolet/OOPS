**Status:** one home for health considerations, grounded in inspected source and existing lore records. This is a design palette, not an implementation dispatch, accepted tuning or a new death policy. Pick what fits this body, character, world and moment. All new mechanics below are **INFERRED proposals**, including defaults, unless explicitly labelled otherwise. Lists are illustrative and open-ended. No gameplay, tests, bundles, saves or private packs changed.

**Evidence labels:** SHOWN means inspected code in this pass. STATED means an existing document's claim, retaining its primary/secondary evidence and limits; it is not a new whole-book reading claim. INFERRED means proposed interpretation or original design. UNKNOWN means the inspected record does not establish the fact. No sealed files were opened. Public canon premises may be discussed; altered or randomized truths stay behind their discovery routes.

**Accepted direction:** useful food benefits, comfortable unfed ordinary play, no hunger death, passive underfed Famine XP and passive progression where a sustained condition or queued activity supplies a cause. Exact Famine effects/rates are still proposals, not source functionality. Old age stays **only in the existing explicitly selected optional mortal mode**. Desktop/laptop target is **1366x768**, mobile deferred. See `PROJECT.md` standing priorities, `docs/USER_VISION.md` food/UI entries and `docs/HUNGER_AND_BODILY_NEEDS_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md`.
