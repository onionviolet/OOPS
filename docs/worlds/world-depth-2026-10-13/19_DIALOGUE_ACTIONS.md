# Dialogue actions with impact (2026-10-13)

Weibao asked: "Dialogue actions with impact and more?" Proposals only; nothing here is accepted. Each row is a Default plus a Dial, with a Reasonably clause where cost or in-world reason matters. Numbers are illustrative.

Already covered elsewhere and not repeated: the scene contract, evidence-through-observer rules, refusal/interruption/return and the missing adapters D1 to D6 (`docs/worlds/DIALOGUE_AND_INTERACTIONS.md`); the observe/follow/help/intervene/leave dispositions and rumor-has-a-speaker rule (`docs/CHARACTERS_AND_WORLD_EVENTS.md:15,53`); threat preconditions (`DIALOGUE_AND_INTERACTIONS.md`, "A scene is a meeting"); IX4 beat tables and IX5 memory-based standing (`10_FULL_WORLD_METHOD.md:78-79`); PE5 memory receipts (`:65`); quiet skills palette SB13 and the SC micro/meso/macro frame (`14_RESEARCH_SKILLS_SCALES.md:95,125-151`); joke carriers HF1/HF20 and the no-lie joke PW2-34 (`15_JOKES_AS_HINTS.md:15,34,148`); OF4 lossy gossip (`01_HOME_AND_HUBS.md:21`); CL13 NPCs who remember across lives (`17_CARRYOVER_AND_RETROFIT.md:59`). This pass adds the verbs themselves, what each needs and changes, how words become obligations and evidence, and how talk looks on a desktop screen.

## 2. Dialogue actions (DA)

Columns are folded into the two cells: **Needs** (knowledge, skill, relationship, reputation, item, place), **Changes** (regard, knowledge stage, a world fact, a schedule, a price, an access, an event), **Witness** and **Remembered**. Default for every verb: it is a host-bound response (X4) or a beat (IX4) with an explicit audience (X11), and repeated delivery of the same cause writes nothing new (X8).

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| DA1 | **Ask.** Needs: the speaker present and willing (attention, X12), and a topic the player knows exists. Changes: a knowledge entry at `seen` attributed to the speaker; Dial: a well-asked question (skill, rapport) yields `interpreted`. Witness: co-located. Remembered: the speaker remembers what you were curious about. | The base verb. A remembered question is itself information: asking a guard about the vault tells the guard something. |
| DA2 | **Tell.** Needs: the fact in your knowledge. Changes: the listener gains a belief attributed to you, which can change their plan, price or schedule. Witness: audience setting. Remembered: listener holds "told by player, day N". | Lets the player move information. Telling a merchant about a road ambush can shift tomorrow's prices. |
| DA3 | **Lie.** Needs: a claim the player knows to be false (or does not know is true), and Lying skill vs the listener's reading-people skill or a detector. Changes: listener belief at `seen` with basis `told by player`; a private truth stored as in X9. Witness: anyone present can later compare. Remembered: the lie sits until a same-rank correction catches it (RS2). | Lies become objects with a shelf life. Default: no immediate "lie failed" pop; the cost lands when evidence meets the claim. Dial: a quiet tell line for passive insight (SB19). |
| DA4 | **Promise.** Needs: the means to plausibly deliver. Changes: an obligation record with terms, deadline and recipient (IC5). Witness: recipient plus audience; witnesses can vouch or testify. Remembered: kept, late, broken or released. | Turns words into the cheapest quest. The elder's archive duty is the shape (X10). |
| DA5 | **Threaten.** Needs: the contract's four preconditions (target access, target knowledge, actual force or authority, objective). Changes: compliance, a grievance, or a call for help; Reasonably: the threatened party may tell someone. Witness: often private by choice. Remembered: grievance tie and a fear axis. | A threat that works today can be a witness statement tomorrow. Private threats are cheaper to make and harder to prove for both sides. |
| DA6 | **Flatter.** Needs: knowledge of what the listener values (preferences entry, like `craftsman.preferences`). Changes: a small warmth delta per cause; Dial: wrong flattery (bad read) lowers respect. Witness: others hear it; the target's rivals remember it. Remembered: once per cause (X8), so no farming. | Rewards learning people, not clicking a compliment button. |
| DA7 | **Joke.** Needs: a shared reference or local humor knowledge. Changes: warmth, tension released, or offense; a joke can carry a hint (HF1). Witness: group jokes land on the group. Remembered: callback potential (HG14). | Jokes are social glue and a carrier at once. A bad joke at a funeral is remembered longer than a good one. |
| DA8 | **Confess.** Needs: something true you did that the listener does not know. Changes: the listener's belief moves to `understood` with basis `confession`; trust can rise while a grievance also opens. Witness: confessions in public become testimony. Remembered: "they told me themselves", which weighs differently from being caught (IC8). | Gives the player a way to defuse a future lie-catch at a price chosen now. |
| DA9 | **Apologize.** Needs: an existing grievance tie. Changes: Default resolves or softens the grievance only with a matching act (repair, payment) or the listener's temperament; words alone soften tone. Witness: public apologies change standing with the audience too. Remembered: apology receipt; repeated apology for the same harm adds nothing. | The contract already blocks apology farming; this makes the apology a real settlement step instead. |
| DA10 | **Bargain.** Needs: something to offer (item, coin, labor, information) and Haggling. Changes: a price, terms or a trade; Reasonably: a hard bargain moves the seller's regard down slightly and their next opening price up. Witness: market crowds learn your going rate. Remembered: "drove a hard bargain", read by prices next visit. | Prices remember people. Ties to the price owners already in simulation transfers. |
| DA11 | **Refuse.** Needs: nothing. Changes: the IX6 refusal branch declared by the offer; often nothing material. Witness: the asker. Remembered: refusal receipt, distinct from absence (IX6). | Refusal stays cheap and safe, with texture: a polite no and a rude no can be two responses. |
| DA12 | **Stay silent.** Needs: nothing; Dial: a world where silence is read (an interrogation, a court). Changes: the listener interprets silence by temperament (assent, guilt, respect). Witness: present. Remembered: "said nothing when asked about X", which can be evidence. | Silence as a verb is distinct from leaving. It keeps the scene open and hands the interpretation to the NPC. |
| DA13 | **Change the subject.** Needs: a second topic and some rapport. Changes: closes the current topic without a refusal receipt; Reasonably: a sharp listener notes the dodge (reading-people check). Witness: present. Remembered: a dodge marker per topic, so asking again later is pointed. | The cheapest evasive move, and a quiet place for reading-people skill to show. |
| DA14 | **Gossip.** Needs: a held belief about a third person and a listener who trades talk. Changes: the belief copies to the listener with basis `heard from player`, possibly blurred (OF4 drift); third party's standing with that listener moves. Witness: the third party is absent by definition. Remembered: you as a source; Dial: the third party can trace it back. | Words travel without you. The engine already stores attribution (X7), so a rumor's path is replayable. |
| DA15 | **Warn.** Needs: knowledge of a danger, and a channel to the person at risk. Changes: their schedule or route (they stay home, hire a guard); Reasonably: warnings from strangers are discounted (MOL6 shape). Witness: low. Remembered: if the danger was real, credit; if not, "cried wolf". | A warning changes an NPC schedule, which is the most visible proof that talk does something. |
| DA16 | **Recruit.** Needs: an offer (wage, cause, shelter), the person's need or want (PE4), and standing. Changes: a duty assignment and a meso ledger member (SC4); their old employer loses them. Witness: the old employer may hear. Remembered: who brought them in. | The village recruit (Iona, `src/npc_lives.js:46-51`) is the seam. Recruiting is a dialogue result with a schedule consequence. |
| DA17 | **Teach.** Needs: the method actually held, and the student's prerequisites. Changes: the student's knowledge or a lesson job, not instant ability (contract rule); the teacher may lose an exclusive edge. Witness: a classroom or a pair. Remembered: "taught by player", which can show later in the student's style. | Teaching is talk with a lasting product. A taught method spreading is a macro chain (IC12). |
| DA18 | **Comfort.** Needs: knowledge of the person's grief or fear (a death in `npc_lives` news, an injury), and presence. Changes: warmth and a shared memory; Dial: comfort can lift a short-term penalty on their work or mood. Witness: usually private. Remembered: a long-lived memory with slow decay. | The clearest no-reward exchange that still matters. Gives yearly village deaths a social follow-up. |
| DA19 | **Insult.** Needs: nothing, or knowledge of a sore spot for a sharper one. Changes: grievance, a challenge offer, or a laugh from the right audience. Witness: public insults move standing with the audience, private ones only the target. Remembered: grievance tie; Dial: some temperaments forget fast. | An honest antisocial verb. It can open the elder's provocation route without a fight. |
| DA20 | **Reveal a secret.** Needs: a held secret (yours or a third party's) at `interpreted` or above. Changes: the listener's knowledge, plus a world fact if the secret is public-relevant; the secret's owner gains a grievance if it was theirs. Witness: decides how far it spreads. Remembered: by the owner, permanently by default. | Sealed world secrets reach the player only by their routes; this verb is for in-world secrets the player already learned. Revealing a sealed one follows that secret's own discovery rules (by ID). |
| DA21 | **Keep a secret.** Needs: a secret entrusted to you (a confidence receipt). Changes: nothing visible until someone asks; refusal to tell under pressure (DA12, DA11) writes loyalty. Witness: the asker. Remembered: the owner learns you kept it only if someone tells them. | Keeping a secret is a choice the game notices only when tested. Pressure scenes make it real. |
| DA22 | **Ask a favor.** Needs: positive regard or an existing favor balance. Changes: the NPC spends their means (time, stock, access) for you; a favor-owed record on your side. Witness: low. Remembered: favor ledger entry. | Favors are currency between people, separate from coin. |
| DA23 | **Call in a favor.** Needs: a favor owed to you, with its original cause. Changes: the debtor pays in kind; the favor is settled (claim settle, `src/simulation_effects.js:298`). Reasonably: calling a small favor for a large ask strains regard. Witness: the debtor. Remembered: settled, or refused (which writes a grievance on your side). | Closes the loop: favors become an idle-safe economy that mints no money. |
| DA24 | **Overhear and eavesdrop.** Needs: position and Eavesdropping skill (`docs/SKILLS_AND_TALENTS.md`). Changes: a knowledge entry attributed to the overheard speaker without their consent. Witness: if caught, the speakers. Remembered: "caught listening" if detected. | The passive verb partner to gossip. HF20 covers the hint use; this row adds the getting-caught consequence. |
| DA25 | **Introduce.** Needs: two people you know. Changes: a new relationship between them seeded with your standing; Reasonably: if one wrongs the other, you share blame. Witness: both. Remembered: "met through player". | The player can build the social graph, not just sit in it. |

## 3. Impact chains (IC)

SC frame from part 14: micro = one conversation, meso = household/shop/squad/class over a week or term, macro = institution, city, era.

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| IC1 | **Micro: the line changes one record.** Default: every committed verb writes at least one of knowledge, relationship memory, fact or obligation, with cause and audience. Read-only exchanges are still allowed and write nothing (contract rule). | Makes "impact" checkable: a verb either has a receipt or is flavor by design. |
| IC2 | **Micro to meso: repeated talk becomes a pattern.** Default: N similar acts toward one group (helping the same shop's customers, joking in the same barracks) move that group's ledger (SC7). Dial: N per world. | A reputation inside a household or squad grows from many small talks, not one speech. |
| IC3 | **Meso to macro: someone reports up.** Default: an informed member carries a remembered statement to an institution (a sergeant, a prefect, a temple), which changes one decision (SC8). | Words reach institutions through people, so the player can intercept, persuade or bribe the messenger. |
| IC4 | **Delayed consequence.** Default: a verb can schedule a caused event (`src/simulation_effects.js:375-377`) that fires days later: the warned merchant hires a guard next week, the insulted noble's letter arrives at month end. Dial: delay range per verb. | Delay is what makes dialogue feel weighty. The caused-event owner already exists. |
| IC5 | **Promises as tracked obligations.** Default: a promise writes recipient, terms, deadline, witnesses and status (open, kept, late, broken, released). Breaking one writes a grievance; keeping one writes trust and a vouching witness. Dial: deadlines strict or soft per world. | A promise ledger is cheap, readable and drives return reports (IC14). The elder's archiveDuty is a one-off version (X10). |
| IC6 | **Lies that can be caught.** Default: a lie stores the claim on the listener and the truth privately (X9 shape). It is caught when the listener gains a same-rank conflicting entry: a witness, a document, a detector, a confession by someone else. Dial: whether the player sees a "they believe you" marker. | Lies become a timed risk instead of a skill-check coin flip. Catching happens through the world, which makes it fair. |
| IC7 | **Misremembered and retold words.** Default: when gossip copies a belief, it can drift one step (a number grows, a name swaps, a joke becomes a claim), stored as a new entry with the teller as basis. Reasonably: careful people drift less. | The player hears their own words come back wrong. Ties to OF4 and HG14, adding the per-hop record. |
| IC8 | **Confession vs caught.** Default: the same truth reached through a confession sets trust up and grievance moderate; reached through being caught sets trust down and grievance high. Dial: per temperament. | Gives DA8 a mechanical reason, and makes lying a real wager. |
| IC9 | **Words travel at a speed.** Default: gossip moves at walking pace through named talkers (OF4), letters at post speed, magical channels faster per world. A macro fact can lag a micro event by days. | The player can outrun news, or arrive after it, which is a plan. |
| IC10 | **Words set prices.** Default: bargaining memory (DA10), gossip about your wealth and a known generous streak shift opening prices for you in that town. Reasonably: by a small, bounded step per receipt type. | Ordinary talk touches the economy without a reputation integer per shop. |
| IC11 | **Words change schedules.** Default: a warning, a recruitment, an invitation or a threat edits an NPC's schedule block (PE3). | A changed schedule is visible on the map, so the player sees the talk land. |
| IC12 | **Taught words spread.** Default: a lesson or a method told to one person can be retold to their circle at meso speed, so the method appears in others' hands later. Dial: whether the method degrades in retelling. | Macro diffusion from one conversation; also how a player's invention becomes common (RS9 credit). |
| IC13 | **Words can close doors.** Default: a broken promise or a public insult toward an institution member edits that institution's service gate (IN2) for the player. Reasonably: other institutions can open as a result (the rival guild hires the man who insulted the first). | Every closed door opens another, which fits "everything possible, reasonably". |
| IC14 | **Return report reads talk.** Default: the SC17 three-block return report includes "said about you" lines from receipts that changed while away (a rumor arrived, a promise came due, someone asked for you). | Turns idle time into social news without simulating every chat. |
| IC15 | **Cut points recorded.** Default: each chain notes where it stopped (SC13): "the guard heard; the captain did not." | Keeps agents honest about how far a word reached. |

## 4. Conversation form and UI (CF)

Target viewport: desktop/laptop first; mobile deferred (standing UI priority).

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| CF1 | **Two columns: topics left, verbs right.** Default: the left column lists known topics (people, places, items, rumors the player holds); picking one shows the verbs that apply to it on the right (ask, tell, lie, warn, gossip). Dial: a compact single list for simple NPCs. | Topic-first respects knowledge (you cannot ask about what you have not heard of). Verb-second shows the range of action without a long menu. |
| CF2 | **Authored lines on top.** Default: when an ink scene or authored beat exists, its specific choices appear above the generic grid with the world's wording. | Authored scenes keep their voice; the grid fills the gaps (IX3 specificity). |
| CF3 | **Effect hints without spoilers.** Default: each choice shows a short known-effect line from the host preview (X6): "costs 2 crowns", "she will remember this", "others can hear". Hidden reasons stay hidden (contract rule). Dial: a "certainty" word (likely, unsure) from the player's reading-people skill. | Players choose with information, and skill shows as better hints rather than a bar. |
| CF4 | **Witness strip.** Default: a thin row above the choices shows who is present and close enough to hear. Choosing a public verb highlights them. | Makes audience visible, which is the main multiplier of consequence (X11). |
| CF5 | **NPC voice vs log voice.** Default: the NPC speaks in quotes in the scene pane, using voice tags and world cues; the log records the event in the player's notebook voice ("Told Hessa about the ford, in front of her son"). | Two voices let the log be scannable while the scene keeps character. |
| CF6 | **Interruptions as scene events.** Default: an arrival, an alarm or the NPC's schedule ending the talk shows as a line and closes or pauses the scene per contract; a third person can join and become a group. | Conversations live in time. The ink pause/resume path already exists (`src/ink_narrative.js:279-286`). |
| CF7 | **Group conversation.** Default: one speaker at a time, with a "turn to" selector among present people; verbs aimed at one person are heard by all present. Dial: side whispers (private to one, with an overhear check for others). | Groups make witness rules matter and give jokes, insults and toasts their natural home. |
| CF8 | **Letters and messages.** Default: a letter is an item with author, recipient, text summary, seal and travel time; reading it applies its knowledge with basis `letter from X`. Replies are composed from the same verb grid (ask, tell, promise, warn). Dial: interception and forgery per world. | Dialogue at a distance, with custody and delay, using the item owner. |
| CF9 | **Idle dialogue.** Default: while away, scheduled conversations happen between NPCs on the meso clock (gossip hops, a promised visit), and appointed helpers can carry messages. Results appear in the return report (IC14). Frozen micro by default (SC14). | The player returns to talk that already happened, which is the life-sim payoff of idle. |
| CF10 | **Where ink fits.** Default: ink for authored arcs and canon scenes; the verb grid plus IX4 beat tables for ordinary people; both commit through the same host bindings. Reasonably: a verb used inside an ink scene is a `response:` tag named after the verb (`response:warn_...`). | Keeps one consequence owner and lets most talk ship without a story revision. |
| CF11 | **Memory peek on the people card.** Default: the people card shows what you said to them and what they said to you, filtered to what the player witnessed (`src/people_knowledge.js` rules). Dial: a "promises" tab across all people (IC5). | The player can plan follow-ups. No NPC hidden state leaks. |
| CF12 | **Desktop keyboard flow.** Default: number keys pick choices, Tab moves between topic and verb columns, Esc leaves with a leave receipt only if the scene declares one. | Fast for repeat play on a laptop. Mobile layout deferred. |
| CF13 | **Tone as a modifier, not a meter.** Default: a few verbs offer a tone toggle (gentle, blunt) that changes wording and the temperament reaction, without a global charisma score. | Matches the contract's "temperament without a new psychology meter". |
| CF14 | **Dialogue VFX restraint.** Default: a small ripple on the witness strip when a public verb lands; a faint seal icon on a promise; no damage-number style popups for regard. | Social effects should feel like a life sim, not a combat log. |

## 5. Per world (DW)

Franchise facts are general knowledge unless cited. Sealed secrets by ID only.

### Harry Potter

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| DW1 | **Veritaserum and Legilimency as truth channels.** Veritaserum compels true answers to direct questions; Legilimency reads thoughts and memories, and Occlumency guards against it (general knowledge). Default: these are detector channels in IC6 that can catch a lie, each with real access and cost (a controlled potion, a trained reader, eye contact). Dial: whether a precise-wording dodge works under the potion. | A world where lies have specific, scarce counters. Occlumency becomes the quiet "keep a secret" skill. |
| DW2 | **Owls, Howlers and portraits as message channels.** Letters go by owl with travel time; a Howler shouts its letter in public; portraits relay talk between frames (general knowledge). Default: CF8 letters with a public variant (Howler audience = everyone in the Hall). | The same verb lands differently by channel: a scolding owl is private, a Howler moves house standing. |
| DW3 | **Unbreakable Vow as a hard promise.** Breaking it kills (general knowledge). Default: an IC5 obligation with a lore-absolute penalty and a required bonder witness. | A local absolute for promises, which makes DA4 terrifying in this world only. |
| DW4 | **Points for words.** Cheek to staff costs house points; a good answer in class earns them (general knowledge; HP1 owns points). Default: some verbs toward staff (insult, joke, confess) carry a points delta read by housemates. | House points turn individual talk into meso standing with your house. |
| DW5 | **Memory charms erase witnesses.** Obliviate removes what someone remembers (general knowledge; HP5 covers street exposure). Default: in dialogue terms, it deletes a listener's receipt of a verb, at a cost and with traces a skilled reader can notice. | The one world where "what is remembered" can be edited by force, with the ethics visible. |

Default: DA verbs map to those skills; the roll sets the hint certainty (CF3) and the listener's interpretation, not an auto-win. | Gives the verb grid native numbers in the world built on them. |
| DW7 | **Charm and compulsion magic with aftermath.** Charm Person makes a target friendly and the target knows it was charmed when it ends; Suggestion, Zone of Truth and Detect Thoughts exist (general knowledge, SRD).
