# Deeper per-world considerations: Bleach, Naruto, Elden Ring (2026-10-13)

Proposals only. Nothing here is accepted. Franchise facts marked "(general knowledge)" come from memory, not repo notes, and need a ledger check before any canon label. Source boundaries follow each world's ledger: original Bleach manga 1-74, original Naruto manga 1-700, Elden Ring 2022 base game. Rows read as Default (starting proposal), Dial (what a world or player can turn) and Reasonably (in-world reason and cost).

## Bleach, "Missing Street Record" (`docs/worlds/bleach/`)

The existing docs settle the perception/contact/passage split, the street opening and release-versus-tool rules. This pass goes into the afterlife's daily machinery: sending souls on, the chain clock, empty bodies, memory edits, spirit pressure as a neighborhood effect, squad culture and how the world looks to someone who can or can't see.

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters / micro-to-macro chain |
|---|---|---|
| BL1 | **Unique verb: send a soul on.** Default: a lingering soul in the street can be sent to Soul Society by an eligible Soul Reaper's soul burial (Konsō, general knowledge), or a civilian can help it settle the tie that keeps it here. Dial: how much a settled tie eases the later sending. | No other world's core loop is "help the dead leave." One sent soul becomes a Rukongai resident you may meet later in a soul-side start, so a street errand seeds a neighbor in another domain. |
| BL2 | **The chain clock.** Default: a lingering soul's Chain of Fate erodes over played time while you are in the world, and a fully eroded soul turns Hollow (general knowledge). Dial: the "world moves on" setting lets erosion run while you are away. Reasonably: it fits the idle rule (absent worlds freeze) and still gives the street a real deadline. | A rare world where waiting has a named victim. Each soul you do not reach in time becomes a threat to the same street, which feeds BL13's purify-or-destroy choice. |
| BL3 | **The empty body.** Default: when a soul leaves its living body or gigai (general knowledge), the body stays where it was. The player picks who runs it: nobody (it slumps, Fuyu or a passerby finds it), a swallowed artificial soul pill that acts on its own personality (general knowledge: Gikongan, Kon), or a paused routine. | Life-sim gold: your shift, class or date goes on without you. A pill-run body may skip work or charm the landlord, and witnesses remember what "you" did. Ties to CW23 identity and CW26 remote agents. |
| BL4 | **Memory replacement as a civic tool.** Default: Soul Reapers can rewrite witnesses' memories of a spiritual event with a device (general knowledge: Kikanshinki). The player can apply, refuse or ask for it. Dial: how cleanly an edit holds against a ledger that still records the event. | Jun's ledger can disagree with Jun's own memory. Refusing edits grows a town of people who half-know, which slowly raises BL5 sensitivity and Ritsu's jurisdiction claims. |
| BL5 | **Spirit pressure is loud.** Default: a strong spiritual body leaks pressure that wakes sensitivity in people near it and draws Hollows (general knowledge, inferred from Karakura's awakenings). Dial: wear a power limiter in the living world, the way captains do (general knowledge), at the cost of capped output. | Late-game reason to stay careful: a returning powerhouse makes the street both safer and more haunted. Neighbors who start seeing ghosts become new witnesses, new patients for Fuyu and new targets. |
| BL6 | **Hunger comes with power.** Default: most Rukongai souls feel little hunger, while souls with spiritual power get hungry (general knowledge: Rukia and Renji's backstory). Dial: per-body need table. | A body rule (rule 4) that reverses expectations: awakening adds a food bill. Rukongai food stalls and water become meaningful for gifted children, which explains why talented street kids push toward the Academy. |
| BL7 | **Rukongai district numbers.** Default: the four directions run from district 1 (orderly) to 80 (lawless) (general knowledge), and arriving souls land by lot, with strangers grouped as families. Dial: start district chosen, rolled or earned. | Lodging, safety and found family all hang on one number. Patronage that calms a high-numbered district is a slow regional project that changes which souls the Academy recruits from there. |
| BL8 | **Training in folded time.** Default: special spaces such as an underground training room or the Dangai can let more training happen than outside time allows (general knowledge; exact ratio unverified, BU-level). Dial: ratio, access cost, and what danger lives in the fold (the Dangai's cleaner, general knowledge). | Maps onto XWC4 extra study in an overlapping interval. Cost is real: access belongs to whoever owns the space, and the body still ages or tires by the selected rule. |
| BL9 | **Squad culture is reputation.** Default: each Court Guard squad remembers you through its culture (general knowledge): the relief squad is looked down on and gets chores like sewer duty, the fighting squad scorns Kidō, the research squad experiments. Dial: how far squad prejudice spreads beyond its members. | Joining relief work (Fuyu's career) brings low status but access to every injured officer. One squad's grudge does not become global hostility, in line with the existing "no single reputation bar" rule. |
| BL10 | **Soul Society's gossip press.** Default: a squad-run magazine and the officers' women's association (general knowledge) carry news and rumor through Seireitei. Your deeds reach print only through a reporter who heard of them. | A diegetic news channel that obeys rule 6: a column can be wrong, flattering or late. A magazine feature can open doors that a quiet career keeps closed. |
| BL11 | **The alert that arrives late.** Default: a Soul Reaper's issued phone-pager gives Hollow alerts with a location and some lag or error (general knowledge). Dial: alert accuracy by area and device quality. | Small device to big consequence: a cheap pager's late ping means a soul eaten, a ledger line struck and a grieving family. Upgrades are a research path for Seki Kuro, not omniscience. |
| BL12 | **Crossing costs a butterfly.** Default: lawful passage between the living world and Soul Society goes through a gate guided by a hell butterfly (general knowledge). Unguided passage risks the Dangai. A living body entering needs a conversion of its matter to spirit particles (general knowledge). | Gives the crossing rule (rule 5) a physical carrier and a permission owner. A butterfly is issued, so lawful trips leave a record, and unlawful ones leave a missing record, which loops back to the opening theme. |
| BL13 | **Purify or destroy.** Default: a Soul Reaper's blade purifies a Hollow and sends the soul on, while the Quincy method destroys it (B11 says the approaches differ; destruction upsetting a balance is general knowledge). Dial: whether the world tracks a soul balance between domains. | The kill method decides whether the victim exists later. A destroyed Hollow's family never meets them in Rukongai. Over many kills, balance pressure can become a canon-scale event. |
| BL14 | **Canon pressure: the first Hollow night.** Default: the early_living_district era runs toward the night Rukia transfers power (B10). Resolving that attack first, or keeping Rukia uninjured, removes the cause of the transfer and the canon path changes (C1-C6 lazy resolution). Abstention leaves canon by default. | One small street intervention reroutes the whole saga. The engine only needs to track the represented cause (Rukia's injury), not Ichigo's whole future. |
| BL15 | **Presentation (V6/V7/V8).** Frame tint: living Karakura warms Vivid's navy toward dusk orange; Soul Society shifts to ink black on paper white; Hueco Mundo goes bone white over black. Header: rainy-season clouds in June in the living town, the fixed crescent moon over Hueco Mundo (general knowledge). Edge: heavy spirit pressure gives the vitals panel a faint weighted edge; a fraying chain shows as a thin rust line on a soul's People entry. | Tells you which domain you are in before text does. The rust line gives BL2's clock a quiet visual without another meter. |
| BL16 | **Log voice follows sight.** Default: a character without spiritual sight reads unseen events as mundane effects in the log ("a sign fell," "the door stuck," "cold air near the clinic"). With sight, the same event names the Hollow. Optional sound: a low hum only sighted characters hear. | The panel only this world reveals is the unseen layer itself: a People and Places overlay that appears once sight is gained, with old log lines kept as written. Rule 6 made visible. |
| BL17 | **Late game: the street still needs you.** A strong returner can teach an Academy class, sponsor a high-numbered Rukongai district, fund Seki's sensor network, or patrol under a limiter (BL5). Each needs means and permission, not rank alone. | Gives power a reason to come back beyond fights: people you sent on (BL1) are now neighbors, and districts move up or down with your patronage. |

Smallest playable taste: BL16 with BL2. One lingering soul on Emi's street, a chain that frays in played time, and a log that changes voice when sight is gained. It uses the existing street opening, adds one clock and one observer rule, and shows the world's difference in a few minutes of play.

## Naruto, "River Carrier" (`docs/worlds/naruto/`)

The existing docs cover craft versus chakra, puppetry, medicine and the carrier opening. This pass adds the mission economy, the team and teacher loop, village law and silence, the shinobi body as a secret, shadow-clone time, training journeys and the shape of village life.

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters / micro-to-macro chain |
|---|---|---|
| NA1 | **Unique verb: split yourself, then remember.** Default: shadow clones (N2 corroborates training use) do parallel work and their experience returns when they disperse, while chakra and mental fatigue split between them (general knowledge). Access is a rare technique learned through real access, not a menu pick. Dial: clone count, fatigue share, whether clones can do chores as well as training. | Fits the idle game better than any other world: parallel lived time compressed into one body. Cost keeps it honest: a large chakra pool is needed, and many clones mean a hard crash. Ties to XWC4. |
| NA2 | **Missions are chores until they aren't.** Default: the mission desk ranks jobs D to S, with D-ranks being weeding, babysitting and lost pets, all paid in ryō (general knowledge). Dial: how often a client lies about rank, as the Wave bridge-builder did (general knowledge). | Life-sim and power fantasy share one board. A misranked C-rank is the world's signature risk, and village income from missions funds the Academy and hospital that the carrier opening already supplies. |
| NA3 | **Eating together.** Default: a regular stall (ramen, barbecue, dango, general knowledge) remembers your order and your team. A team meal after a mission settles mood and small debts. Dial: stall owners as information brokers. | Small ritual, real relationship state (CW06 hospitality). A stall closing in a war arc is felt more than a statistic. |
| NA4 | **The exam calendar.** Default: the Chūnin Exam runs on a recurring schedule, hosted by allied villages in turn (general knowledge). Foreign teams arrive, inns fill, and the finals are a public festival. Dial: frequency and host rotation. | A world event the whole economy feels. It is also the stage of a major canon attack in Part One (general knowledge), so a festival is also a canon pressure point (NA11). |
| NA5 | **Leaving can make you missing.** Default: a shinobi who leaves without permission becomes a missing-nin, enters the bounty book and draws hunter-nin (general knowledge). A visit to another world is booked as a sanctioned long mission. Dial: with "world moves on" set, an unexplained absence starts a missing-nin clock. | Gives the crossing rule an in-world law. A slashed headband is the visible mark (general knowledge). |
| NA6 | **The body is a secret.** Default: hunter-nin dispose of fallen shinobi bodies so their techniques and bloodlines can't be studied (general knowledge); the Hyūga branch-house seal is said to seal the eyes at death (general knowledge). Dial: how much a body reveals to a skilled examiner. | Death here has a custody problem. An heir's first job may be recovering a parent's body. Bloodline organs make bodies stealable goods (CW34). |
| NA7 | **The memorial stone and inherited will.** Default: names of those killed on duty are carved on a memorial stone that people visit (general knowledge). A teacher's goals pass to students as an inherited aim, not a stat. | Succession through teaching rather than blood: a student who takes a dead teacher's aim gets a quest and a relationship, while their own skills stay their own. |
| NA8 | **Decrees of silence.** Default: a village leader can forbid speaking of a matter, as the Third did about the Nine-Tails (general knowledge). Adults know and obey; children don't know and feel only the cold. | Gossip with a legal hole in it. A player who learns the secret through the right route sees why neighbors act as they do, and breaking the decree has a named punisher. |
| NA9 | **Tags, scrolls and paper.** Default: explosive tags and storage scrolls are ordinary war materiel made by paid workers; chakra paper that reacts to a person's nature is grown from special trees (general knowledge). Dial: tag supply as a regional economy. | Micro to macro: a paper shortage stops academy nature tests, and a tag boom before a war raises scribe wages. Carriers and puppets (existing docs) slot into the same supply web. |
| NA10 | **Contracts with a whole species.** Default: a summoning contract scroll is signed in blood and passes from signer to signer (general knowledge: the toad contract). Summoned animals have their own society, and a reverse summoning can carry you to their home (general knowledge). Dial: contract access route and price. | A contract is both an heirloom (succession) and a crossing (rule 5). Mount Myoboku-style homes become small worlds with their own food, elders and rules. |
| NA11 | **Canon pressure: the village falls and is rebuilt.** Default: canon attacks run toward the Part One festival attack and the Part Two flattening of the Leaf (general knowledge). Abstaining leaves canon; acting can change who dies. Either way, rebuilding follows: carpentry, carriers and clinics are in demand, and a wood-style user can raise houses fast (general knowledge). | Gives the civilian fitter career a war economy. Aki's yard is busiest after the worst day. A saved worker removes their expected death scene, per existing branching rules. |
| NA12 | **The training journey.** Default: a long journey with a teacher can be a deliberate absence: the village moves on while you train elsewhere, and friends rank up (general knowledge: the Part One to Part Two gap). Dial: journey length and what the village does meanwhile. | An in-canon "world moves on" mode that the player chooses. Coming home to changed friends is the payoff. |
| NA13 | **Late game: become a sensei.** Default: a strong returner can take a three-genin team, set their first test (general knowledge: the bell test), pick their D-ranks and keep them alive. Dial: a teacher's quality visible only through students' results. | The generational loop at the center of the source. Students grow into NPCs with their own careers and can outlive or outshine you. |
| NA14 | **Senjutsu as an import door.** Default: natural energy is ambient, so a foreign body might draw on it through sage training, with the risk of turning to stone if it fails (general knowledge). Exported tags and tools need chakra to trigger unless timed (inferred). | One plausible way an outsider gets power here without a chakra-born body, with a vivid failure. Exports stay mostly inert, matching rule 5. |
| NA15 | **Presentation (V6/V7/V8).** Frame tint: Leaf green-gold, Sand ochre, Mist grey-blue, Rain slate. Header: Rain shows constant rain, and in a ruler's rain the rain itself senses intruders (general knowledge), so the header can carry a faint "watched" glyph. Edge: chakra low flickers the vitals edge; an opened Gate gives a brief red edge. Log voice: mission report format (rank, client, result). The panel only this world reveals: a Mission Report and, once you've earned enemies, a bounty-book page. | Region tint tells you which village you are in. The Rain header makes weather a spy. |

Smallest playable taste: NA2. A D-rank board with three chores, one paid in ryō on completion, and one C-rank that turns out misranked. It reuses the carrier opening's clinic and yard, adds the world's signature risk, and costs only job definitions plus one reveal event.

## Elden Ring, "Unclaimed Ferry" (`docs/worlds/elden-ring/`)

The existing docs cover the funeral-custody opening, catalysts, Ashes, refits and records. This pass adds the rune economy, rest and respawn as idle, time at grace, messages and phantoms from other worlds, the Roundtable's ledger, regional ailments as ecology, caste bodies, rebirth, remembrances, the game's own new-cycle layer and points of no return.

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters / micro-to-macro chain |
|---|---|---|
| ER1 | **Unique verb: spend or grow.** Default: runes are both money and growth for the Tarnished (general knowledge; rune drop on death is E2c). Every purchase is a level not taken, and runes dropped at death can be recovered once from where you fell (general knowledge). Dial: outsiders without grace use runes as coin only. | No other world fuses wallet and growth. A ferry fee paid in runes is a real sacrifice, which gives Ilen's institution weight. |
| ER2 | **Rest resets the road.** Default: resting at grace brings ordinary enemies back while bosses stay dead (general knowledge), matching rule 11 (beaten obstacles stay beaten). Dial: which foes return. | A canon-born idle loop: an offline routine can be farming a known spot from a grace, with stop conditions. It also makes routes stay dangerous for porters like Kest. |
| ER3 | **Choose the hour.** Default: at grace you can pass time to morning, noon or night (general knowledge). Night brings different patrols and night-only threats (E3). Dial: time-of-day locks on certain merchants and spirits. | Time as a player verb, not a clock. Ties to V7: the header shifts as you choose the hour, and a night delivery has a different risk profile. |
| ER4 | **Cookbooks are dead people's notes.** Default: recipes come in cookbooks left by earlier travelers, each a few pages of someone's life (general knowledge). Crafting draws on gathered flora and animal parts, not shops. | Every recipe carries a small story and a provenance (CW09, CW11). | ER5 | **Mixing tears.** Default: a flask can be filled with two crystal tears to make one mixed drink with combined effects (general knowledge: Wondrous Physick). | ER6 | **Notes from other worlds.** Default: messages on the ground and bloodstains showing someone's last moments (general knowledge) come from your own earlier lives and heirs in this instance. Dial: messages from strangers, some of them lies. | In-canon support for rule 5 (a record is a carrier) and rule 6 (a note can lie). Your heir finds your warning where you died. |
| ER7 | **Phantom visits.** Default: in canon, other Tarnished are summoned from "other worlds" as phantoms who leave when the host's boss falls or the phantom dies (general knowledge). | ER10 | **Bodies born into a caste.** Default: some bodies are persecuted by law and custom, such as horned Omens whose horns are cut at birth and albinaurics hunted in hiding (general knowledge). A child born with horns in your line is a succession event, and protecting them is a long arc. |
| ER11 | **Rebirth and the mirror.** Default: a queen in her academy can remake your attributes for a rare larval tear, and a mirror can change your look (general knowledge). | ER12 | **Remembrances and the new journey.** Default: a fallen demigod leaves a remembrance that can be traded for one of two unique items or consumed for runes (general knowledge). After an ending, a new journey restarts the world while you keep your gear and growth (general knowledge: NG+). Dial: treat journey two as a separate branch instance with the same people reset. | The source has its own prestige layer. It maps to YAIRPG prestige as a world-local loop and gives a returning strong character a reason to stay: the other choice of each remembrance. |
| ER13 | **Points of no return.** Default: some acts reshape the map for good, such as burning the great tree, which turns the capital to ash and ends several stories (general knowledge). The world gives a premonition through a witness or omen before it happens. | Persistence made visible. NPC questlines advancing with your progress (E3) mean these moments cost people, and the funeral circle records who was lost. |
| ER14 | **Presentation (V6/V7/V8).** Frame tint: Vivid's navy warms toward grace gold over charcoal, with regional shifts (green-gold west, blue mist lake, scarlet east, white peaks). Header: the golden tree glows on the horizon at every hour, dimmer at night (ER3), with a rider glyph on nights a night patrol is known. Edge: rot red, madness amber, frost pale blue, building up as a slow fill. Log voice: terse item-text style for found things; a quiet death line rather than the source's screen. The panel only this world reveals: item descriptions as a lore codex that fills as you find things. | Lore here lives in item text, so the codex panel is the world's real story channel. Status edges show buildup, which is how the source handles ailments. |

Smallest playable taste: ER2 with ER1. One grace near the ferry, a rest action that brings back ordinary road foes, runes that fall where you die and can be recovered once. It turns the existing opening road into an idle loop with real stakes for Kest's cart, using one respawn rule and one recovery rule.
