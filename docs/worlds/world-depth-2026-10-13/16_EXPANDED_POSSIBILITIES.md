# P. Expanded possibilities: one idea, many forms (proposal, 2026-10-13)

Weibao: "Expanding each possibility and more? More to consider?" This is
aspect expansion of the possibility families raised this round, not a stop
point. Method: each family is treated as one idea, and its forms come from
moving one axis at a time (E8 axes: object, mechanism, trigger, certainty,
scope, price, limit, visibility, persistence, growth; simulator axes SA1 to
SA13 where they fit: subject, start, horizon, control, fidelity, carry,
refill, reach, failure). The axis moved is named in brackets after each form.

Sources read: `docs/worlds/WORLD_DEPTH_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md`, parts 06
to 10, `docs/emergent/E8_POWER_ASPECTS.md`, the SA section of
`docs/SIMULATORS_AND_CHEAT_IMPLEMENTATION.md`, and scratch file K (access and
starts). L, M and N were not present when this was written. Forms already in
PW, ST, DS, AK, QK, AC, UG, MR, DT, WI, IR and the skills "little stuff" list
were grepped and left out; where a form sits close to one, the row names the
neighbor and the one difference.

Everything is a palette of proposals: Default plus Dial plus a Reasonably
(in-world reason and cost). Nothing here is accepted, built or run. Sealed
truths appear only as IDs. Franchise facts from memory carry "(general
knowledge)". A Soldier's Life is a held world (paused since 2026-10-12); rows
that fit it say so and name no cast or places.

## 1. Parallel copy (PX)

The idea: another version of a world exists at the same time. Existing rows
cover provenance, tells, counterparts, drift, trade, endings and specific
premise copies (No-Statute, no guns, Shadowfell echo). These forms change
what the copy is made of and how it relates in time.

| Code | Form [axis] | Fits best | Reuses / needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| PX1 | **Lagging copy.** The same world running a fixed span behind yours (a week, a school year). You can watch your past choices play out without you. [scope: time offset] | HP: a copy one school year behind, where last year's House Cup is still open. | EI10 canon-course clock with an offset; frozen counterpart state from your own receipts. |
| PX2 | **Frozen-moment copy.** A copy stopped at one instant you can walk through, touch and search, but nobody moves. Default: one tableau per copy. Dial: it thaws slowly while you are inside. [persistence] | Shadow Slave: a Nightmare-like frozen battle (general knowledge for Nightmare framing); MoL: the city at one festival minute (public structure only). | Existing `absence:"freeze"` turned into a visitable state; inspect verb on a still pack. Cheapest true copy. |
| PX3 | **Hearsay copy.** The world as local gossip tells it: exaggerated heroes, wrong villains, inflated prices. Differs from M11 fanon (natives believe it) in that here the beliefs are true. [mechanism: belief made fact] | LOTM: Tingen as the tavern stories describe it. | OF4 lossy gossip outputs read as EI7 fact overrides. |
| PX4 | **Pocket copy.** A copy of only one building or district, cut off at its walls. Cheap because the census is tiny. [scope: place] | HP: a single classroom or shop that exists twice (Room of Requirement-like, general knowledge). | LC hierarchy; one district card plus a boundary rule. |
| PX5 | **Stitched copy.** Two worlds fused at one seam district where both packs' rows live together. [object: two packs] | TTKS B1 wizard market stitched to HP's Diagon Alley; D&D Sigil-like hub as the seam. | EI1 patch that imports foreign rows; RF9 carry rules at the seam. |
| PX6 | **Season-locked copy.** One holiday or season that never ends; the calendar loops inside one week. Differs from a loop world: nobody is trying to break it. [horizon] | HP: a winter-break castle copy with the hall half empty. | RF6 calendar with a pinned week; festival rows. |
| PX7 | **Inverted-allegiance copy.** Every institution's side flips at once. IN4 flips one institution; this flips the whole map. [scope: all institutions] | LOTM: churches and hostile orders swap public standing. | IN4 run over every institution card; reputation regions per copy (needs R9 fix). |
| PX8 | **Notebook copy.** A copy seeded from your own Notebook, so your wrong beliefs are true there. [subject: the player's knowledge] | Shadow Slave: a dream copy built from your memory; MoL by analogy. | Notebook facts as EI7 seeds; PW7 copy tags show which facts made it. |
| PX9 | **Rescaled copy.** Same people and places, one power rank higher or lower for everyone. [growth/scale] | D&D: Phandalin where the farmers are seasoned veterans; or a low-magic version. | AK4 rule swap on the level or rank table only. |
| PX10 | **Guest copy.** A copy that belongs to another traveler's branch; you visit as a guest and see their choices as settled history. [reach] | TTKS B3: another board member's world segment; LOTM by analogy. | Branch atlas, MR5 slot tree read-only, PW1 provenance "someone else's branch". |
| PX11 | **Draft copy.** A rough low-tier copy (T1 everywhere) that firms up where you look. Honest about being thin. [fidelity] | Any newly loaded world; sandbox first. | DT tiers as the copy's own texture; pairs with DTX seed-fill. |

**Look-alike pairs**
- **PX1 lagging copy vs regression.** Both show your past again. In PX1 you
  stand outside and watch it run; in regression you are back inside it and the
  watcher is gone.
- **PX2 frozen-moment copy vs a frozen absent world.** Same freeze code. PX2
  lets you in; an absent world's freeze is only a bookkeeping state.
- **PX3 hearsay copy vs PX8 notebook copy.** Both make beliefs true. PX3 uses
  the crowd's beliefs, so you can be surprised; PX8 uses yours, so every
  surprise is your own error showing.

## 2. Start (SXF)

The idea: where and as whom a life begins. ST1 to ST13, DS1 to DS16, QK5 and
the per-world start shifts are taken. These forms change the start's
relation to time, to obligations and to other people.

| Code | Form [axis] | Fits best | Reuses / needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| SXF1 | **Ending-first start.** Pick an end state ("a shop in Hogsmeade at forty"); the game offers starts that could reach it, without promising. [horizon set first] | HP: life-sim ending as the brief. | P3 selector run backward over start cards; ending tags. |
| SXF2 | **Stand-in start.** You cover someone's post for a fixed span (sick leave, a trip). Known end date; the owner comes back. [horizon] | HP: covering a shop counter for a summer; A Soldier's Life (held): covering a duty post. | Existing jobs plus a return event; regard from the owner on return. |
| SXF3 | **Executor start.** You must carry out a stranger's will: hand out goods to heirs you have to find. Differs from heir starts: you give, not receive. [object: someone else's estate] | LOTM: a Tingen estate with scattered heirs. | RF8 succession leftovers; custody records; people search. |
| SXF4 | **Wrong-luggage start.** You arrive holding someone else's bag; its owner, contents and trouble follow. [carry] | D&D: the torn bag slice from the other side; HP: a trunk swap on the Express (general knowledge setting). | Existing bag item and repair job; owner as a person card. |
| SXF5 | **Transfer start.** Join an institution mid-year from another one, with the old place's habits and gaps. [start: mid-calendar] | HP: a transfer from another school (general knowledge that others exist); Shadow Slave: Academy transfer. | Calendar rows; SU3 carried habits; partial class records. |
| SXF6 | **Last-of-a-trade start.** You hold a skill the world is giving up (a lamplighter as gas arrives). [growth: declining value] | LOTM: an old craft in industrial Backlund (general knowledge era texture). | Price drift per year; a skill card with falling demand. |
| SXF7 | **Falling start.** Start at peak wealth or rank just before the crash you can see coming. Differs from DS6 creditor start: you hold the wealth, and it is leaving. [price] | LOTM Backlund trade; HP: an old family on its last money. | Household ledger with a scheduled loss event. |
| SXF8 | **Shared-body start.** Two minds in one body from the first hour, with turns, quarrels and one shared schedule. Differs from DS11 swap (two bodies traded). [subject] | D&D: a patron riding along; TTKS B1 by analogy to replacement. | Two skill sheets on one body; a control dial (SA4). |
| SXF9 | **Short-lease start.** You know your remaining time from the start (a curse, an illness, a contract term). Dial: the lease can be extended in-world. [horizon visible] | HP: a cursed-object holder; Shadow Slave: a spell deadline framing. | Lifespan field shown in bio; extension routes as jobs. |
| SXF10 | **Witnessed-arrival start.** Someone saw you arrive and tells others before you speak; your first reputation is their version. [visibility] | LOTM, MoL: a neighbor at the window. | PE5 memory receipt on one NPC; IN6 gossip speed. |
| SXF11 | **Second-chance start.** Same world and body as a life that just ended badly, a day earlier, with only a feeling. Differs from regression (no memory, only a mood). [carry: almost nothing] | Circle of Inevitability (its own loop rules differ; general knowledge); any world as a gentle replay. | Snapshot of the last day; a "dread" state with no facts. |

**Look-alike pairs**
- **SXF2 stand-in vs SXF5 transfer.** Both drop you into a working
  institution mid-stream. The stand-in has a known end and an owner coming
  back; the transfer stays and builds its own place.
- **SXF3 executor vs heir (AC4).** Both open on a dead person's things. The
  heir keeps; the executor must let go, and the reward is who you meet doing it.
- **SXF7 falling vs DS6 creditor.** Both start rich on paper. The creditor's
  money is coming in late; the falling start's money is already leaving.

## 3. AU divergence (AUX)

The idea: a canon world changed at one point. AK1 to AK7 and QK1 to QK12
cover premise field, relationship flip, start shift, rule swap,
survivor/absence, sealed reroll and slot-taken. These forms change what
kind of thing diverges.

| Code | Form [axis] | Fits best | Reuses / needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| AUX1 | **Object AU.** One artifact exists or is missing. Differs from AK5 (a person): an object has no will, so others try to fill the gap. [object: item] | HP: a famous map or heirloom never made (general knowledge). | Item census row toggled; EV rows that depend on it. |
| AUX2 | **Timing AU.** A canon event happens early or late, by days or years; who is ready changes. [trigger: date] | HP: the letter comes a year late; D&D: the town rebuild runs behind. | EI10 canon clock with one shifted row. |
| AUX3 | **Moved-place AU.** A canon place sits somewhere else (a coastal Phandalin, a school in another country). [scope: location] | D&D, HP. | LC6 connections override; travel times recomputed. |
| AUX4 | **Early-invention AU.** One technology lands earlier and spreads by diffusion. Differs from PML11 (a war's winner): here it is one device. [growth: tech] | LOTM: radio or rail a decade early; HP: wizards adopt phones. | CW28 diffusion; price and job shifts. |
| AUX5 | **Custom AU.** One social norm flips (no house sorting, open guild entry, inherited ranks abolished). [limit: social rule] | HP: no houses; Shadow Slave: no Legacy clans (general knowledge). | IN cards with one rule changed. |
| AUX6 | **Genre AU.** Same cast and places, another genre's pacing and danger (mystery, horror, slice of life). [mechanism: tone] | HP as cozy mystery; D&D as horror. | V6 tint, log voice, encounter weights; content unchanged. |
| AUX7 | **Butterfly AU.** The player picks a tiny change (a shop closed, a letter lost); the engine runs consequences under the inertia rule. [scope: smallest] | A Soldier's Life (held): one supply shortfall; MoL: one stall gone. | TIMELINES inertia; abstention outcomes. |
| AUX8 | **Era-merge AU.** Two eras of one source overlap (a sequel's cast alive in the first book's years). Differs from QK7 heir-across-sequel: both eras present at once. [scope: time] | LOTM with Circle of Inevitability. | Two era packs, one counterpart map (EI3). |
| AUX9 | **Edition AU.** Same story under another official version (film vs book canon, a rules edition). PDD6 does rules; this extends to story canon. [fidelity] | HP film-versus-book details. | Fidelity tag (QK12) per row; small patch. |
| AUX10 | **Fanon-true AU.** Popular fan beliefs are real in this copy. Differs from M11 (natives believe them) and PX3 (local gossip): here the source is fandom-wide. [certainty] | HP has the deepest fanon; LOTM next. | Fanfic pilot findings as a patch list. |
| AUX11 | **Reversed-outcome AU.** One canon contest or verdict goes the other way; everyone lives with it. Changed-truth variants stay sealed (reserve an ID first). [certainty: outcome] | HP: a different Cup winner; Shadow Slave: a seat contest. | One EV row result; reserved sealed slot per QK10 if a truth changes. |

**Look-alike pairs**
- **AUX1 object AU vs AK5 absence toggle.** One missing thing, one missing
  person. People come with plans that others react to; a missing object
  leaves a hole that people quietly fill with substitutes.
- **AUX2 timing AU vs start-era shift.** Both put you near a different
  moment. In a start shift you move; in a timing AU the world's event moves
  and you stay.
- **AUX9 edition AU vs AUX10 fanon-true AU.** Both change "what is true".
  Edition picks among official sources; fanon picks what the crowd decided.

## 4. Relationship flip (RLX)

The idea: one tie changes kind. Kin, rival, mentor, enemy, partner, lodger,
roommate and cold stranger are covered (AK2, QK1, PML6-8, PTT6-8). These
forms change who knows, when it flips and what the tie rests on.

| Code | Form [axis] | Fits best | Reuses / needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| RLX1 | **Masked bond.** You know a canon figure only by a code name at a secret meeting, then meet them by daylight without knowing. [visibility] | LOTM: an anonymous club (general knowledge). | Two person views on one card; a reveal event. |
| RLX2 | **Mistaken identity.** They treat you as someone else (a look-alike, a namesake) and the regard is not yours. [object: wrong target] | HP: Polyjuice-style confusion (general knowledge); MoL by analogy. | Regard keyed to an alias; correction event. |
| RLX3 | **Unrequited regard.** A person admires or hates you, and you do not know they exist. Shows up as gifts or trouble with no sender. [visibility: hidden, one-way] | HP, LOTM. | Hidden regard value; anonymous events. |
| RLX4 | **Scheduled turn.** Regard is warm but set to flip at a known canon event unless you act. [trigger: event] | D&D: a friendly townsperson with a hidden debt; LOTM. | EI10 clock row plus a regard condition. |
| RLX5 | **Inherited grudge.** They resent what your body's last owner or your ancestor did. [persistence: carried by the body] | LOTM (transmigration body debts, general knowledge); Shadow Slave clans. | Predecessor record; regard seeded from it. |
| RLX6 | **Status inversion.** You employ the canon lead, or a canon figure works under you. Differs from QK2 slot taken: you are above them, not in their seat. [subject] | LOTM: hiring a private detective (general knowledge); MoL: paying a tutor. | Jobs with you as employer; wages paid out. |
| RLX7 | **Proxy rivalry.** You and a canon adult are friends while your kids, students or pets feud. [reach: through dependents] | HP: two households, rival children. | Household members' regard separate from yours. |
| RLX8 | **Copy-split bond.** Your rival in one copy is your friend in another, and you carry both memories. [persistence: across copies] | Circle of Inevitability; HP via PX copies. | Counterpart map (EI3) with per-copy regard. |
| RLX9 | **Familiar's choice.** A canon animal companion prefers you, with all the awkwardness. [object: nonhuman] | HP: an owl or cat (general knowledge). | Pet card with regard; owner's regard reacts. |
| RLX10 | **Leverage bond.** You both hold the same secret, so you are tied whether you like it or not. [price: mutual risk] | HP: both know a hidden passage; LOTM. | Shared knowledge fact; exposure risk on both. |
| RLX11 | **Passed-on tie.** A friendship moves to your heir in another shape (your friend becomes the heir's mentor). [persistence: succession] | HP generations; LOTM to Circle via heirs. | RF8 succession plus regard inheritance. |

**Look-alike pairs**
- **RLX1 masked bond vs RLX2 mistaken identity.** Both have hidden
  identities. In RLX1 the mask is chosen and kept on purpose; in RLX2 nobody
  chose it and it breaks by accident.
- **RLX3 unrequited regard vs RLX4 scheduled turn.** Both are hidden values
  that matter later. RLX3 just sits there; RLX4 has a timer.
- **RLX6 status inversion vs QK2 slot taken.** Both change canon's work
  order. QK2 puts you in their place; RLX6 puts them under you.

## 5. Earned option (EOX)

The idea: an option (a verb, a start, a dial, a route) opens through play.
UG2 to UG4 cover play, discovery and prestige unlocks; skills cover
practice and watching. These forms change what kind of deed earns it.

| Code | Form [axis] | Fits best | Reuses / needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| EOX1 | **Earned by failing.** Failing the same thing a few times opens a sideways approach (bribe, ask, go around). Differs from fumble XP: it opens an option, not a number. [trigger: failure count] | D&D: failed checks open other routes, which suits the tabletop feel. | Failure counter per verb target. |
| EOX2 | **Earned by refusing.** Turning something down opens a route (refusing a bribe opens the honest-clerk path). [trigger: abstention] | TTKS B5 gate shift; A Soldier's Life (held). | Refusal receipt (PE5) checked as a condition. |
| EOX3 | **Earned by teaching.** Teaching a younger person a method opens a lesson verb and a new kind of regard. [reach] | HP: tutoring a first-year; MoL by analogy. | Learning foundation; a teach verb on a skill. |
| EOX4 | **Earned by being remembered.** Once enough NPCs hold receipts about you, a "known face" option opens (credit, favors, being asked). [visibility] | LOTM Tingen; D&D Phandalin. | Count of PE5 receipts by town. |
| EOX5 | **Earned by waiting.** An option ripens offline: a planted tree, an aged brew, a letter answer. [refill: idle time] | Home world; HP herbology. | RF7 away-time; a ripening timer item. |
| EOX6 | **Earned by familiarity.** Visiting a place enough opens shortcuts and hidden doors there. [scope: place] | HP: castle passages (general knowledge); MoL Cyoria underground. | Visit counter per location; LC7 discovery. |
| EOX7 | **Earned by keeping time.** Doing a ritual at the right hours over days opens it. [trigger: calendar] | LOTM rituals (general knowledge); Circle of Inevitability. | RF6 calendar plus a streak record. |
| EOX8 | **Earned by losing.** Losing a contest opens a different option than winning would (cut from the team, invited to commentate). [certainty: consolation] | HP: Quidditch tryouts; Shadow Slave seat contests. | Contest result branches. |
| EOX9 | **Earned by cataloguing.** Completing a Notebook page (all kinds of a creature, all stalls in a market) opens a combo or a trade. [object: knowledge set] | Shadow Slave creature tiers (general knowledge); TTKS B1. | Notebook categories with a completion rule. |
| EOX10 | **Earned by asking.** Some options open only if you ask the right person outright. No deed, just nerve and the right regard. [mechanism: social] | HP, D&D. | Ask verb with a regard floor. |
| EOX11 | **Earned by carrying.** Carrying a thing long enough opens what it can do (a sword learns your hand). [persistence: item bond] | D&D magic items (attunement, general knowledge); HP wands. | Custody time on an item. |

**Look-alike pairs**
- **EOX1 failing vs EOX8 losing.** Both reward a bad result. EOX1 counts many
  small failures; EOX8 is one public loss with an audience.
- **EOX2 refusing vs EOX5 waiting.** Both earn by not acting. Refusing is a
  choice someone sees; waiting is time nobody notices.
- **EOX6 familiarity vs EOX9 cataloguing.** Both reward thoroughness. One is
  your feet in the place; the other is your notes about it.

The idea: how a cheat reaches its holder. Origins, grants, shops, gods,

| CAX3 | **Rented.** A cheat on a lease with a monthly bill; miss a payment and it dims. [price: recurring] | TTKS B5 (the power bill fits); Reincarnation Paradise. | Recurring cost; refill rule tied to payment. |
| CAX4 | **Issued.** An institution hands you a cheat as kit, logged and recallable. [persistence: owned by someone else] | A Soldier's Life (held): kit issue; Shadow Slave academy gear. | Custody record with an owner; recall event. |
| CAX5 | **Misdelivered.** The cheat was meant for someone else, and the right owner, or its sender, comes looking. [object: wrong holder] | HP: a letter to the wrong address (pattern); D&D. | Intended-holder field; pursuit (ENCOUNTERS). |

- **CAX4 issued vs CAX5 misdelivered.** Both cheats belong to someone else.

- **CAX3 rented vs CAX4 issued.** Both are on loan. You pay coin for a rental;
  you pay service for an issue.
- **CAX1 habit vs CAX2 wound.** Both come from the body's history. A habit is
  slow and chosen; a wound is sudden and not.

## 7. Research (RSX)

The idea: finding out something new. IR01 to IR08 cover hypothesis,
apparatus, controlled comparison, negative result, replication,
publication, institutions and cross-world translation. These forms change
where the evidence comes from.

| Code | Form [axis] | Fits best | Reuses / needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| RSX1 | **Field survey.** Count and map things in the wild over days; the answer is a pattern, not a recipe. [scope: area] | Shadow Slave Dreaming; D&D around Phandalin. | Gather verb with a tally; map notes. |
| RSX2 | **Oral history.** Ask many people and triangulate; each answer is biased in a known way. [mechanism: testimony] | LOTM Tingen; HP (elves and portraits, general knowledge). | PE5 receipts; witness bias per person. |
| RSX3 | **Archive dig.** Search a library whose catalog is incomplete; the search itself takes skill. [object: records] | MoL library (built location); HP restricted section. | Library location; search verb with catalog quality. |
| RSX4 | **Teardown.** Take apart an artifact to learn its method, losing the artifact. [price: the sample] | TTKS B4 ruins and the estate AI; D&D. | Item consumed; method card unlocked. |
| RSX5 | **Brute volume.** Many cheap tries run while idle, no control group, just numbers. Differs from IR03: volume replaces design. [refill: idle] | HP potion variants; home crafts. | Idle job with a seeded outcome table. |
| RSX6 | **Self-experiment.** Test on your own body with the risk that brings. [price: health] | LOTM (potion digestion framing, general knowledge). | Body conditions; risk roll. |
| RSX7 | **Oracle narrowing.** Pay a seer for a hint that rules out half the hypotheses. [certainty: riddle] | LOTM; HP divination. | E8 riddle oracle; hypothesis list. |
| RSX8 | **Race.** A rival team works the same question; publishing first matters. [reach: rival] | MoL academy; HP. | Rival with a research clock. |
| RSX9 | **Forbidden study.** Knowledge with legal risk; the research is also a crime record. [limit: law] | HP dark magic; LOTM. | RF12 reputation; MC6 law. |
| RSX10 | **Copy experiment.** Change one thing in a parallel copy and compare outcomes with the base. Differs from PW4 (a diff tool): you cause the difference. [object: a copy] | Any world with PX copies; MoL by analogy to loops. | EI7 instance facts; PW4 comparison. |
| RSX11 | **Apprentice notes.** Inherit a dead researcher's half-finished notebook, with their errors. [persistence: someone else's work] | HP, LOTM, D&D. | ST5 letter pattern applied to research state. |

**Look-alike pairs**
- **RSX5 brute volume vs IR03 controlled comparison.** Both test many
  variants. Volume finds what works without knowing why; comparison knows
  why and costs more per try.
- **RSX2 oral history vs RSX3 archive.** Living memory is biased but current;
  records are honest to their time but stale.
- **RSX6 self-experiment vs RSX10 copy experiment.** Both take a risk to learn.
  One risks your body; the other risks a whole copy and its people.

## 8. Subtle skill (SBX)

The idea: small skills that are not power. The skill families, hobbies,
signature tells and odd-use notes are covered in SKILLS_AND_TALENTS. These
forms are quiet competences that change how ordinary life lands.

| Code | Form [axis] | Fits best | Reuses / needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| SBX1 | **Queue reading.** Knowing which line moves fastest and which clerk says yes. [object: institutions] | TTKS B5 gate; A Soldier's Life (held) mess line. | IN3 duty rosters; wait times. |
| SBX2 | **Gift sense.** Picking presents that land. [object: a person's wants] | HP Christmas; LOTM. | PE2 wants; regard gain scaled by fit. |
| SBX3 | **Unremarkable.** Being seen and then forgotten. Differs from stealth: people look right at you. [visibility] | LOTM. | Lower PE5 receipt weight about you. |
| SBX4 | **Name recall.** Remembering names and small facts; people warm when you do. [persistence] | HP's big cast; D&D town. | Regard bonus on greeting known people. |
| SBX5 | **Hand reading.** Telling letter writers apart and spotting forged hands. [mechanism: sense] | HP owl post; LOTM letters. | Item author field; inspect check. |
| SBX6 | **Good timing.** Turning up when someone is free and in a mood to help. [trigger] | MoL household; D&D. | PE3 schedules shown as hints. |
| SBX7 | **Soothing.** Calming animals, children and frightened people. [object: nonhuman and young] | HP creatures; Shadow Slave evacuation. | Mood state on people and pets. |
| SBX8 | **Losing well.** Losing a game in a way that wins regard. [price: the game] | HP Gobstones (general knowledge); LOTM card tables. | Minigame result plus regard. |
| SBX9 | **Packing.** Fitting more into a bag and finding things fast. [scope: capacity] | D&D carrying; Fallout. | Custody capacity dial. |
| SBX10 | **Letter craft.** A well-written letter gets a better answer and arrives as you meant it. [reach: distance] | HP; LOTM; cross-world mail (SU10). | Letter item with a quality field. |

**Look-alike pairs**
- **SBX3 unremarkable vs stealth.** Both avoid trouble. Stealth hides you;
  unremarkable lets you be seen and makes the seeing not stick.
- **SBX8 losing well vs haggling.** Both are about value in an exchange.
  Haggling takes value; losing well gives value away to buy regard.
- **SBX1 queue reading vs SBX6 good timing.** Both beat waiting. One picks
  the right line; the other picks the right moment.

## 9. Scale crossing, micro to meso to macro (SCX)

The idea: one scale's events reach another. MI, WS and MA rows (crafting
research) and the brief's micro-to-macro chains cover the general shape.
These forms change the direction and the hinge.

| Code | Form [axis] | Fits best | Reuses / needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| SCX1 | **Decree lands in the kitchen.** A macro law arrives as a micro chore (a new rule changes what you can cook or carry). [direction: top-down] | HP decrees at school (general knowledge); LOTM. | Law row with micro effects on verbs. |
| SCX2 | **Zoom into the war.** A macro event can be entered as one small scene (a campaign as one tent's week). [scope: zoom in] | A Soldier's Life (held); Shadow Slave sieges. | Encounter scene bound to a macro event. |
| SCX3 | **Rumor sets into opinion.** Many small gossip receipts become a town-wide view of you. [growth: aggregation] | LOTM Tingen; D&D Phandalin. | PE5 receipts summed per region. |
| SCX4 | **One key, one city.** A single small object controls a macro outcome. [object: hinge item] | LOTM sealed objects (general knowledge); HP. | Item with a macro flag; custody matters. |
| SCX5 | **The household as an actor.** A family or guild acts between person and city, with its own plans. [subject: meso] | HP families; D&D factions. | Household card with goals (PE4 needs at group level). |
| SCX6 | **Scale-locked skill.** A skill that only works at one scale (a duelist versus a general). [limit: scale] | A Soldier's Life (held); Shadow Slave. | Skill card with a scale tag. |
| SCX7 | **Doesn't scale up.** A recipe that works for one fails in bulk; the fix is its own research. Differs from IR05 (another maker): same maker, more units. [scope: quantity] | HP potions; home crafts. | Batch size on recipes; failure rate curve. |
| SCX8 | **Echo across scales.** One pattern shows at three sizes (kids' feud, houses' feud, nations' feud). Presentation, not new rules. [visibility] | HP. | Log voice tags; event grouping. |
| SCX9 | **Cosmic flicker.** A huge event shows as a small tell (a candle bends, a dog whines). [visibility: micro tell] | LOTM, Circle of Inevitability. | SU11 animals; V7 header glyph. |
| SCX10 | **Notice radius.** How far your deeds are noticed sets which scale reacts to you next. [reach] | LOTM (being noticed from above, general knowledge); all worlds. | Rule 12 outlier reaction; reputation spread. |

**Look-alike pairs**
- **SCX1 decree vs SCX2 zoom in.** Both join big and small. In SCX1 the big
  thing comes to your table; in SCX2 you walk into the big thing.
- **SCX4 one key vs SCX5 household.** Both are hinges between you and the
  city. One is an object anyone can hold; the other is a group with its own will.
- **SCX6 scale-locked skill vs SCX7 doesn't scale.** Both fail at the wrong
  size. SCX6 is the person; SCX7 is the method.

## 10. Depth tier promotion (DTX)

The idea: a row climbs from T0 census toward T5 arc. DT6 (weight floor),
DT7 (demotion), DT9 (per facet), MC2-3 (attention heat) and QK12 (fidelity)
are covered. These forms change what triggers a climb.

| Code | Form [axis] | Fits best | Reuses / needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| DTX1 | **Seed-fill on touch.** A T1 thing gets generated T2 detail the first time you inspect it, then that detail is saved. [trigger: inspect] | D&D generated folk. | Generation tables (MC2-2); persist on first read. |
| DTX2 | **Generated becomes authored.** A W0 person who matters in play gets a hand card, freezing the generated facts. [growth: authoring] | D&D Phandalin; HP households. | Dev export of a generated person to a card. |
| DTX3 | **Clock-driven.** Rows near an upcoming canon event get promoted just before it. [trigger: calendar] | HP yearly set pieces. | EI10 clock plus the coverage printer. |
| DTX4 | **Regard-driven.** A person's tier rises as your relationship deepens (stranger T1, friend T3). [trigger: play] | MoL household; LOTM lodger. | Regard thresholds as tier hints for authors. |
| DTX5 | **Halo.** Neighbors of a T4 place get a bump to T2 so the edges are not bare. [scope: adjacency] | HP castle; MoL Cyoria. | LC hierarchy; a lint rule. |
| DTX6 | **Crossing promotion.** An item that crossed worlds gets more depth (provenance, wear). [object: travelers] | All worlds. | RF9 provenance fields. |
| DTX7 | **Group promotion.** A whole household or crew climbs together. [scope: meso] | HP families; A Soldier's Life (held) squads. | SCX5 household card. |
| DTX8 | **Per-copy tier.** An AU copy's rows can sit at another tier than base (the changed parts deep, the rest thin). [scope: copy] | Any AU. | EI1 patches carry tier fields. |
| DTX9 | **Asked-for promotion.** A local "tell me more" mark from Weibao queues a row for the next pass. [trigger: request] | Sandbox profile. | Dev flag in sandbox; WI8 feedback tag. |
| DTX10 | **Retire to archive.** A finished T5 arc steps down to T3 history: still remembered, no longer staged. Differs from DT7 (stalled): this is done. [persistence] | HP school years; MoL post-ending. | Chronicle entries; DT7 machinery. |

**Look-alike pairs**
- **DTX1 seed-fill vs DTX2 generated becomes authored.** Both turn generated
  folk into real ones. Seed-fill happens at runtime for anyone; authoring is
  a deliberate pick by a person.
- **DTX3 clock-driven vs DTX4 regard-driven.** Both promote just in time. The
  clock is the world's schedule; regard is yours.

## 11. World iteration pass (WIX)

The idea: a pass that deepens a world. WI1 to WI15 cover pass size, entry
and exit checks, ledgers, grids, feedback, parallel lanes, cluster reuse,
diffs, play-anchored passes and mechanical checks. These are pass kinds
with a different lens or reader.

| Code | Form [axis] | Fits best | Reuses / needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| WIX1 | **One-person pass.** Deepen one NPC across every facet (schedule, needs, voice, memory, trade). [object: person] | MoL household; D&D shrine priest. | PE card; DT9 facets. |
| WIX2 | **Day-in-the-life pass.** Walk one ordinary day hour by hour and fill each gap found. [scope: one day] | HP; D&D. | PE3 schedules; LC4 hours. |
| WIX3 | **Abstention pass.** Run the canon clock with the player idle; check that the world still moves and ends sensibly. [subject: world without you] | HP, LOTM. | EI10 abstention outcomes; SA1 "world without you". |
| WIX4 | **Fan nitpick pass.** A reviewer reads as a picky fan and lists canon errors. [mechanism: expert reader] | HP (largest fandom); LOTM. | Census locators; source ledger. |
| WIX5 | **Fresh-eyes pass.** A reviewer with no context reads only what the player sees. [mechanism: novice reader] | MoL, any first slice. | Player-visible text export. |
| WIX6 | **Subtraction pass.** Remove rows that do not fit the world's tone or rules. [growth: negative] | Researched worlds with many borrowed rows. | Ledger "not fitting, reason" cells (WI7). |
| WIX7 | **Senses pass.** Sound, smell, light and weather per place. [object: texture] | Shadow Slave; LOTM fog city. | LC4 ambient lines; V7 header. |
| WIX8 | **Coin pass.** Follow money through wages, prices, rent and debt; find leaks. [object: economy] | D&D Phandalin; A Soldier's Life (held). | Household ledgers; price tables. |
| WIX9 | **Death pass.** Ask what each death leaves behind in this world, for each kind of person. [persistence] | HP; LOTM. | RF8 succession; SUCCESSION_BY_WORLD. |
| WIX10 | **AU stress pass.** Apply each AK kit to the world once on paper and note which break. [scope: all variants] | D&D; HP. | AK1 to AK7; AUX forms above. |
| WIX11 | **Seal-route pass.** Check each sealed secret's tells form a solvable route that leaks nothing early (by ID only). [limit: secrecy] | LOTM, MoL seals. | SA6 sealed fields; RF19 seal lint. |

**Look-alike pairs**
- **WIX2 day-in-the-life vs WIX3 abstention.** Both walk the clock. One walks
  with the player doing small things; the other walks with the player gone.
- **WIX4 fan nitpick vs WIX5 fresh eyes.** Both are outside readers. The fan
  knows too much and catches canon errors; the newcomer knows nothing and
  catches confusion.
- **WIX8 coin vs WIX9 death.** Both follow what flows. Coin follows money
  through living hands; death follows things when the hand stops.

## Suggestions (cheapest first)

1. **PX2 frozen-moment copy.** The engine already has `absence:"freeze"`; a
   visitable frozen tableau of one built location (MoL) is the cheapest copy
   that feels like a parallel world.
2. **RLX4 scheduled turn on MoL.** One regard row plus one clock condition on
   an encounter that already runs; pairs with A2 (Taiven rival).
3. **EOX6 earned by familiarity.** A visit counter on the seven built MoL
   locations opening one hidden route; small state, visible payoff.
4. **SXF2 stand-in start.** Reuses existing jobs plus one return event; good
   first HP or D&D start-shift once those packs exist.
5. **WIX2 day-in-the-life pass.** Docs only, run against Phandalin cards before
   the breadth pass; finds gaps before anyone writes code.

## Shards (fragments that fit no family)

- A world's "quiet hour": one in-game hour each day when nothing scheduled
  happens and idle progress runs slightly faster.
- Lost property office as a hub service: items dropped in any world wash up
  there weeks later, tagged with where they were lost.
- A neighbor who keeps your spare key in every world you have lived in.
- A cheat that only works while someone else believes in it (pairs with E6
  legends, but personal, one believer).
- Photographs or sketches as items: a picture of a place shows it at the time
  taken, so an old sketch is a small window into another era.
- Weather that follows a person, not a place (a gloomy NPC whose rain is real).
- Debts that convert between worlds at a bad rate, like currency.
- An NPC who collects stories about you and sells them; the collection is a
  T3 item.
- Practice dummies that remember who hit them and how hard.
- The last line of each life's log reused as the first line of the next
  start's flavor text.
