# What relationship play needs first, and what else it unlocks (proposal, 2026-10-14)

Weibao (2026-10-14): "stuff required for relationship stuff and more?"

Proposals only. Nothing here is accepted. Part 20 says what relationships should do; part 28 says which layer is default, earned or cheat. This part lists the plumbing those designs assume, what already exists, and what other life functions ride on the same plumbing.

Read for this pass: parts 10 (PE3, PE4), 19 to 23 and 28; relevant User Vision, life-sim and engine owners; current source. Source-read, not browser-checked. The [2026-10-14 audit](../../gm/dispatch/relationship-prereqs-2026-10-14/AUDIT.md) owns the evidence and corrections. Sections 4 to 11 are inferred proposals, not accepted decisions or a build list.

**Short answer.** The relationship *store* already exists. People, locations, scheduled jobs, moods, social writers and some memory readers already exist. The gaps are reusable tie semantics, useful target-aware offers, sustained idle contact, durable social facts beyond recent memories, and explicit owners for continuity. General offline ties are not implemented; a narrow reviewed packing catch-up already exists. A store merge is an option, not a prerequisite for every slice.

## 4. Retention, scale and consumer contracts (RZ)

Inferred proposals. Shown limits: both memory lists retain 32 entries; the chronicle accepts at most 64 relationship owners (`src/simulation_contracts.js:250`; `src/simulation_effects.js:292`; `src/chronicle.js:34-39,69`). These are current storage bounds, not accepted limits on friends. No large-cast performance result is claimed.

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| RZ1 | **Recent memory is not durable truth.** Default: keep a short recent list plus durable causes for commitments, discovery and kind changes. Dial: detail and archive depth per world. Reasonably: summary keeps participants, basis, time and settlement. | Entry 33 must not erase a marriage, an unsettled harm or the proof needed by a memory reader. |
| RZ2 | **Receipt retention is separate.** Default: preserve delivery identity independently of visible memory. Dial: compact receipts by settled epoch when no command can replay them. Reasonably: old saves and imported work still have a declared disposition. | `simulation.causes` is separate from the 32-entry list; the relationship branch does not prune it. Removing memory is not permission to repay the same event. |
| RZ3 | **Byte budget, not a friend cap.** Default: measure serialized bytes, receipt count and hot edges. Dial: archive detail and dormant loading. Reasonably: version any changed bound; keep the existing 64-owner chronicle and 32-person household limits until compatibility work changes them. | Unlimited conceptual friendships need bounded working data, not silently rejected saves. |
| RZ4 | **Sparse active graph.** Default: create edges when there is a cause, and schedule only due people and contacts. Dial: simulation depth by active district or household. Reasonably: distant groups can use summaries with named clocks. | An all-pairs tick grows quadratically; many people need not all meet every minute. |
| RZ5 | **Catch-up preserves order.** Default: stop at choices and return unused time, reusing the shared scheduler. Dial: work chunk size and recap detail. Reasonably: queued completion, mood expiry and contact settle in a declared order. | The current 512-boundary return is a work budget, not evidence that all requested absence was consumed (`src/expansion_engine.js:626`). |
| RZ6 | **Combat and party readers.** Default: recruitment, rescue and refusal read a named directed tie plus current willingness, body, knowledge and means. Dial: bonuses and morale per world. Reasonably: recheck agreement at commitment and at any supported completion boundary. | Regard does not grant combat strength or consent; the existing braced contest already checks willingness (`src/party_battle.js:20-38`). |
| RZ7 | **Travel and service readers.** Default: travel reads exit permission and companion terms; traders and teachers read scoped offers at quote and settlement. Dial: favors, credit and escorts. Reasonably: show known withdrawal causes and keep paid commitments coherent. | A warm friend may lack a pass, stock, funds or permission to leave. A display-only discount cannot change the actual price. |
| RZ8 | **Compatibility matrix.** Default: plan old-save, missing-field, unsupported-schema, pack-missing, reset and import cases before persistent changes. | Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| RFM1 | **Agreement has scope.** Default: keep offer, acceptance, refusal, expiry and withdrawal by person and act. Dial: formal vows, casual arrangements or local customs. Reasonably: consent to a date, travel or a shared home does not stand for every later act. | Attraction and trust cannot replace the other person's decision. |
| RFM2 | **Attraction has several axes.** Default: liking, romantic interest, physical attraction, compatibility and commitment remain distinct where scenes use them. Reasonably: keep unused axes absent and unknown tastes undisclosed. | Friendship, unreturned love and partnership without romance stay complete paths. |
| RFM3 | **Courtship has an authoring route.** Default: invitations name place, cost, duration, participants and alternatives after a missed date. Reasonably: an idle standing plan can create fitting contact without automatic acceptance. | A route needs more than a heart threshold or a calendar trap. |
| RFM4 | **A household is an owner.** Default: record members, residence rights, shared stock, private property and agreed duties separately. Dial: communal, separate or several homes. | RFM5 | **Children and care are separate from marriage.** Default: parenthood, adoption, guardianship and apprenticeships use their fitting routes. | RFM7 | **Opt-out is a real path.** Default: romance can stay off while friendship, companionship and found family remain rich. Dial: route prominence and scene detail in settings. Reasonably: keep care, teaching and ordinary access reachable through fitting nonromantic routes. | The north star's opt-out must not become a poorer life or a hidden power penalty. |
| RFM8 | **Succession names the recipient and basis.** Default: keep kinship, chosen heir, executor, beneficiary and title successor distinct. ## 6. Passive contact and idle growth (RI)

Inferred proposals within the stated idle direction (`docs/USER_VISION.md`, food/Famine entry, 2026-10-13). Applicable sustained conditions or queued work can progress without clicks. This does not imply unconditional growth or general wall-clock accrual.

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| RI1 | **Presence can grow familiarity.** Default: eligible overlapping routines accumulate contact time with a named person. Dial: pace, saturation and cadence per world/person. Reasonably: actual local presence, availability and observer evidence supply the cause. | Makes working near the smith a friendship route without a daily greeting chore. |
| RI2 | **Shared work can grow respect and trust.** Default: reliable agreed work or care may deepen fitting axes while idle. Dial: response per person, task and history. Reasonably: the NPC experiences the work or receives a credible report. | SF4's form-letter default need not ban trust earned by actual repeated help. This is a proposal refinement, not an accepted override. |
| RI3 | **Upkeep has a tell.** Default: templated mail maintains contact with an attribution to its helper or routine. Dial: personal effort, helper quality and tell strength, following SF4. Reasonably: sincere delegated care can count differently from identical cards. | Large circles stay playable while people can notice impersonal contact. |
| RI4 | **Away has several meanings.** Default: distinguish idle with the game running, another local place, another world, paused play and browser-closed time. Dial: freeze, ratio, tending or reviewed catch-up by world. Reasonably: bind every tie cursor to one clock and instance. | Existing played-time jobs and the reviewed packing batch do not prove offline friendship. |
| RI5 | **A growth cause is not a replay.** Default: an elapsed contact interval gets a fresh cause scoped to pair, world instance, epoch and interval. Dial: event granularity. Reasonably: retries use the same cause; later valid contact uses another. | Current receipts block duplicate delivery, not future greetings or funded work (`src/simulation_effects.js:289-292`). |
| RI6 | **No silent new agreement.** Default: idle growth does not itself accept marriage, reveal a confidence, cross a border or spend unreserved assets. Dial: explicit standing authority for fitting acts. Reasonably: pause a new major request or report the NPC's independently authored decision. | Passive progression and meaningful agency can coexist. |
| RI7 | **Absence can be gentle.** Default: retained trust and a useful return recap; drift mainly changes recent familiarity. Dial: cozy, strict, immortal, frozen or crisis worlds. Reasonably: show known stakes before an irreversible deadline and offer fitting repair routes. | Sleep should not become an unexplained friendship tax. Harsh worlds can still have causal losses. |
| RI8 | **Prove elapsed equivalence.** Default: compare supported stepped time with supported catch-up for the same funded contacts. Dial: capped catch-up with unused-time return. Reasonably: fixture reloads, interrupted choices and pack removal must preserve exact cursors and results. | A future idle tie needs evidence beyond adding a timer field. |

| DV25 | **Prerequisites.** Shown versus true fields on people, items and places; per-observer knowledge of each layer (part 29 RQ10); perception checks that read means; reveal events; a lint that no shown-layer UI reads a true field. | What the engine needs before any of the above can ship. |

## 14. Addendum: bonds as power, conflict, rituals, lineage (BK, merged 2026-10-14)

Weibao: "more to consider while codex runs? Save and expand accordingly?" Source: [addendum 3](../../gm/dispatch/relationship-prereqs-2026-10-14/CLAUDE_ADDENDUM_3.md).

### 1. Bonds as a power source (BK1-BK6)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| BK1 | **Contracts with familiars and spirits.** Default: a contract is a tie with terms (what each side gives, what breaks it). Power flows by the terms, and the bond deepens with shared action. Dial: per world how binding contracts are (D&D warlock patrons, beast tamers, Shadow Slave Echoes). | Turns "summon a beast" into a relationship with leverage both ways. |
| BK2 | **Combination arts.** Default: two people who trained together unlock joint techniques or formations; strangers cannot do them. Strength scales with shared history (memory kinds), not one axis. | Gives a mechanical payoff to old friends and comrades in combat worlds. |
| BK3 | **Vows and binding promises.** Default: in worlds with them (JJK binding vows, HP Unbreakable Vow, Naruto oaths), a promise to someone can grant power or cost a life. Breaking it is a world-rule event, not just a grievance. | Obligation rows (RQ9) become power with real risk. |
| BK4 | **Protection as motive power.** Default: some paths grow faster or hit harder when protecting a bonded person (shonen "someone to protect", Naruto Will of Fire). Dial: per world, off in worlds where emotion does not feed power. | Matches genre fantasy, and it is a world rule, not a universal bonus. |
| BK5 | **Shared or lent power.** Default: lending a technique, mana, blessing or a true name to someone you trust. They can misuse it; you can take it back where the world allows. | Trust gets a stake measured in power, and betrayal can cost you abilities. |
| BK6 | **Bond loss as a wound.** Default: when a contract or soul bond breaks (death, betrayal, release), the survivor loses its power and gains a lasting mark: grief, a scar, a changed technique. | Keeps power bonds from being free. Links grief (RD15) to progression. |

### 2. Social conflict and persuasion (BK7-BK12)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| BK7 | **Negotiation as a scene.** Default: both sides have wants, a walk-away point and leverage. Knowledge of their needs (RQ6) and ties shifts the terms. No single persuasion roll. | Trade, treaties, recruitment and pleas all reuse it, and investigating someone first pays off. |
| BK8 | **Leverage and blackmail.** Default: secrets, debts and hostages are leverage. Using it gets compliance but writes fear and grievance, and the target looks for a way out. | A dark but legitimate tool with a built-in cost, fitting LOTM and court worlds. |
| BK9 | **Debate and public argument.** Default: arguing in front of an audience moves the audience's opinion more than the opponent's. Winning can cost a tie with the loser. | Separates convincing a person from winning a crowd, which matters for reputation (part 21). |
| BK10 | **Mediation.** Default: the player can step between two NPCs in a feud. Success needs trust from both sides; failure can turn both against you. | Uses NPC-to-NPC ties (RQ11) as content. |
| BK11 | **Factions inside friend groups.** Default: groups (RQ12) have internal ties; two friends who dislike each other make group events tense, and the player may be asked to choose. Reasonably: the player can refuse to choose, at a cost. | Friend circles become living things, not lists. |
| BK12 | **Boundaries and refusal.** Default: NPCs say no based on their values, mood and limits, even to close friends, and remember being pushed. The player can refuse requests too, with no hidden penalty beyond the person's reaction. | People with limits feel real; a friend who does anything you ask is a servant. |

### 3. Rituals, milestones and shared time (BK13-BK18)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| BK13 | **Personal dates.** Default: birthdays, anniversaries of meeting, a death day. Remembering one is a bond event; forgetting a close friend's can sting. Reasonably: player notes (CQ13) help. | Time gets personal markers, and the calendar gains social meaning. |
| BK14 | **Rites of passage as group events.** Default: coming of age, graduation, promotion, weddings and funerals pull ties together and write shared memories for everyone present. Forms follow world and era (CQ16, part 30). | Big moments for many ties at once, and a natural place for news and reveals. |
| BK15 | **Traditions that form.** Default: a repeated shared act becomes a tradition with a name ("Thursday dumplings"). Keeping it holds familiarity; breaking it is noticed. | The idle-friendly version of friendship: a routine that means something. |
| BK16 | **Keepsakes.** Default: gifts and found objects from people become keepsakes with the giver's memory. Losing or selling one is noticed by the giver if they find out. | Physical continuity for ties and a carrier across lives (part 29 RCL). |
| BK17 | **Shared spaces.** Default: a regular table, a secret spot, a shared workshop. A place tied to a person triggers memories when you visit after they are gone. | Places carry relationships, which makes returning home emotional. |
| BK18 | **Collective memory.** Default: a village, house or guild remembers what you did as a group story, which outlives individual members and drifts as it is retold. | Legacy at community scale, and the bridge to renown (part 21) and heirs. |

### 4. Teaching lines and lineage (BK19-BK22)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| BK19 | **Technique lineages.** Default: what you learn records who taught it. A teacher's style marks you; others recognize the lineage and react (respect, rivalry, old grudges). | Mentors become history, and schools of technique feel like families. |
| BK20 | **Students outgrow you.** Default: a student can surpass, reject or betray their teacher. The tie changes kind (RM12), with pride or bitterness. | A mentor arc needs an ending. |
| BK21 | **Secret teachings need trust.** Default: a master's best method opens only at deep trust and often with an oath (BK3). | Turns trust into a progression path parallel to grinding. |
| BK22 | **Your own school.** Default: teaching many students founds a lineage that persists after you, with descendants who carry your style into later lives or worlds. | Top-end social progression that fits legacy and reincarnation. |
