Proposals only. Nothing here is accepted. Parts 29 and 30 cover ties and life stages. This part covers what sits around them: how the character feels, where and with whom they live, and how a community moves news, help and pressure. Rows follow Default + Dial + Reasonably where a dial fits. Code IL checked free.

Two accepted directions shape every row: food gives benefits without hunger death ([USER_VISION](../../USER_VISION.md), 2026-10-13), and progression can be passive in an idle game. Inner states follow the same pattern: a good state is a bonus, a bad state is the absence of one, and nothing spirals into a forced loss.

## 1. Inner life (IL1-IL10)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| IL1 | **Spirits as a soft state.** Default: one mood band (low, steady, bright) moved by rest, meals, company, wins, losses and grief. Bright gives small bonuses to learning and social events; low removes them. Dial: per world how heavy moods are. Reasonably: never a fail state. | Makes daily life choices matter without a death meter, matching the food direction. |
| IL2 | **Loneliness and company.** Default: long stretches without contact lower spirits slowly; any warm tie, a pet or letters lift it. Dial: per temperament (a hermit cultivator barely notices). | Gives friendships (part 20) a quiet everyday payoff beyond favors. |
| IL3 | **Stress from load.** Default: overwork, danger and debts raise stress; rest, hobbies and confiding in a friend lower it. High stress slows practice; low stress is neutral. | A reason to rest in an idle game without forcing the player to babysit. |
| IL4 | **Coping styles.** Default: the character copes by temperament: training harder, drinking, prayer, crafting, talking. Each has side effects (a drinking habit costs money and trust; crafting gains XP). Reasonably: the player picks or changes the habit through routines. | Personality through behavior, and a passive way to gain skills under stress. |
| IL5 | **Hobbies.** Default: music, gardening, reading, games, collecting. Each lifts spirits, levels slowly and passively, and opens people who share it (part 29 social hooks). | Low-stakes progression and a natural way to meet people. |
| IL6 | **Dreams, ambitions and regrets.** Default: the character holds a few personal goals (open a shop, find a parent, master a sword) set at start or picked up in play. Progress lifts spirits; abandoning one writes a regret memory. | Gives a life direction that is not only power, and fits the life-sim north star. |
| IL7 | **Trauma and recovery.** Default: severe events (near death, loss, betrayal) can leave a mark: a fear, a nightmare, a trigger place. Recovery comes from time, support and facing it. Dial: per world tone. Reasonably: shown with care and never used as a joke. | Lets war, horror and grief worlds (A Soldier's Life, LOTM) carry weight. |
| IL8 | **Sanity and corruption as world rules.** Default: where lore has them (LOTM madness, Fallout radiation of the mind, eldritch exposure), they are their own tracks with their own recovery, separate from IL1 spirits. | Keeps world-specific mental rules distinct from ordinary mood. |
| IL9 | **Faith and meaning.** Default: belief, doubt and conversion are inner facts that change what rituals do for spirits and what temples offer (part 28 LF12). | Religion becomes personal, not only an institution. |
| IL10 | **Inner voice and self-knowledge.** Default: a short line in the log reflects the character's read of their own state ("You haven't laughed in weeks"). Hidden flaws stay hidden until found (part 29 DV22). | Makes inner states visible without meters, consistent with words-not-numbers. |

## 2. Household and home life (IL11-IL19)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| IL11 | **Where you live is a record.** Default: a home has an owner, rent or ownership, rooms, who else lives there and its comfort and safety. Moving is an event. | Rest, privacy, storage and status all read the home. |
| IL12 | **Housemates.** Default: family, roommates, lodgers, dorm mates, barracks, servants. Each shares space, noise, chores and gossip. Dial: per world living norms (part 30 LSE). | The most frequent ties in a life come from who you live with. |
| IL13 | **Chores and upkeep.** Default: cleaning, cooking, repairs and laundry can be done, shared, paid for or neglected. Neglect lowers comfort and housemate trust slowly. Reasonably: chores can run as an idle routine and give small passive XP. | Everyday life with gentle consequences, and the HP household start (part 07 BO4) already uses chores. |
| IL14 | **Comfort and rest quality.** Default: a good bed, quiet and safety improve rest, which helps spirits and recovery. A cold barracks rests worse than a home. | Gives home improvement a reason without a separate stat. |
| IL15 | **Privacy.** Default: shared rooms mean secrets can be found; a private room, a locked chest or a warded study protects them. | Ties into hidden identity (part 29 DV) and secret practice. |
| IL16 | **Household budget.** Default: rent, food, tuition, debts and dependents draw on income as a shared ledger. Shortfalls show as worries and choices, not sudden losses. | The life-sim money loop, with dependents making money meaningful. |
| IL17 | **Caregiving.** Default: caring for a sick parent, a child or an injured friend costs time and grows a strong tie. Paying for care trades money for time. | A real choice between progress and people. |
| IL18 | **Pets and animals at home.** Default: a pet lifts spirits, needs care and becomes a tie (part 20 RM15). Working animals earn their keep. | Cheap comfort content that fits most worlds. |
| IL19 | **Home as a hub.** Default: guests, dinners and gatherings at home build ties faster than meeting outside. A good home becomes a place friends come to. | Links home investment to social reach (part 28 LS4). |

## 3. Community and information (IL20-IL28)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| IL20 | **Gossip networks.** Default: news travels along ties at the speed of the world's contact clock. A gossip NPC spreads it faster. What you do in public reaches your friends before you tell them. | Makes NPC-to-NPC ties (part 29 RQ11) do something visible. |
| IL21 | **News and notice boards.** Default: town criers, papers, owl post, bulletin boards and rumors carry public news with a delay and some errors. | Gives the "hearing about distant events" direction (2026-10-02) a channel. |
| IL22 | **Neighborhood standing.** Default: the street, village or dorm has a local opinion of you, separate from wider fame (part 21). Small kindnesses move it; noise and trouble lower it. | Most of daily life happens at neighborhood scale. |
| IL23 | **Mutual aid.** Default: barn raisings, harvest help, fire brigades, funeral meals. Helping when called builds local standing; the community helps you back when you need it. | Community as a safety net, which fits a game with no forced death spiral. |
| IL24 | **Shared crises.** Default: a flood, plague, raid or famine hits everyone. How the player acts writes a memory in many people at once. | Big shared events give the whole cast a common story. |
| IL25 | **Local customs and calendar.** Default: market days, holy days, festivals, curfews and quiet hours, from part 30's era palette. Missing a custom is noticed. | Each place feels lived in, and the calendar shapes routines. |
| IL26 | **Outsiders and belonging.** Default: newcomers start at low standing and earn belonging through time and participation. Dial: how closed the community is. | Isekai and foreign starts get a real arc of becoming a local. |
| IL27 | **Social class in daily life.** Default: class shows in where you may sit, shop, drink and live, and who speaks first. Crossing lines is possible with friction (part 28 DS5). | Class becomes felt, not just a label. |
| IL28 | **Community memory outlives people.** Default: places keep stories, names on graves and plaques, and how the village tells your story drifts (part 29 BK18). | Legacy at the scale an ordinary life actually reaches. |

(3) Gossip: a public act at the Village reaches one known friend with a delay. | Each is small, readable, and shows the idea in the home world. |
