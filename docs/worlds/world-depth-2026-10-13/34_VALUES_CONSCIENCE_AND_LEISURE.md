# Values, conscience and leisure (proposal, 2026-10-14)

Weibao (2026-10-14, second time this hour): "more to consider? other stuff and more? save and extend accordingly Send out codex as usesful?"

Proposals only. Nothing here is accepted. Morality appears in pieces (open-world karma in CHARACTERS_AND_WORLD_EVENTS, Proto23 karma P2310, Fallout companion approval FO13) and leisure barely at all. This part gives both a home. Rows follow Default + Dial + Reasonably where a dial fits. Code VL checked free.

Ground rule: the game never grades the player's morality with one good/evil bar. People judge by their own values, worlds may have their own cosmic rules, and the character has a conscience the player shapes.

---

## 1. Values (VL1-VL8)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| VL1 | **Values belong to people, not the game.** Default: each NPC holds a few weighted values (loyalty, honesty, mercy, ambition, tradition, freedom, faith, family). Your acts are judged through their values, so the same act wins one friend and loses another. | Replaces a single karma bar with judgments that differ by person, which makes every choice cost someone. |
| VL2 | **The character's values.** Default: the player character has values too, set at start by origin and shifted by repeated acts. They color the inner voice (part 31 IL10) and which acts cause regret. | Gives a character a personality the player builds through play. |
| VL3 | **Cultures carry values.** Default: worlds, eras and institutions weight values differently (honor in a samurai house, profit in a merchant city, purity in a church, family in a cultivation clan), from part 30's era palette. | The same act reads differently across worlds, which keeps them distinct. |
| VL4 | **Cosmic morality only where lore has it.** Default: karma, sin, alignment, corruption or heavenly tribulation exist only in worlds that state them (D&D alignment and planes, cultivation karma and heart demons, LOTM madness from acts). Elsewhere, consequences are social and causal only. | Respects each world's rules and avoids a universal morality meter. |
| VL5 | **Grey choices.** Default: many dilemmas set two goods or two harms against each other (feed your family or keep a promise; save the stranger or the friend). No answer is marked right. | Gives choices weight without moralizing, matching Weibao's grey-areas rule. |
| VL6 | **Reputation is not virtue.** Default: what people think you did (part 21, part 29 DV19) and what you did can differ. A hidden good deed earns nothing publicly; a framed crime costs you anyway. | Keeps deception and rumor meaningful. |
| VL7 | **Values can shift.** Default: hardship, love, betrayal and faith can move a character's or NPC's values over time, with a remembered cause. Dial: how fluid values are per temperament. | Redemption and fall arcs become possible for anyone, including the player. |
| VL8 | **Oaths and codes.** Default: a chosen code (knight's oath, ninja way, a sect's precepts, a personal rule) gives benefits when kept and real costs when broken, enforced by people or by world rules (part 29 BK3). | Lets players adopt a playstyle as a moral identity. |

## 2. Conscience (VL9-VL14)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| VL9 | **Regret and pride.** Default: acts against the character's values write regret; acts that fit write pride. Each nudges spirits (part 31 IL1) a little, never as a punishment loop. | A soft, felt conscience without a moral score. |
| VL10 | **Atonement.** Default: regret can be eased by restitution, confession, service or time. Some harms cannot be undone, but the character can still change (part 29 RHE5). | Gives guilt a path, not only weight. |
| VL11 | **Hardening and softening.** Default: repeated harsh acts make the next ones cost less regret; repeated kindness makes cruelty cost more. Shown in the inner voice. | Shows character change through play, and lets darker runs feel different. |
| VL12 | **Witnessed by yourself.** Default: some acts nobody sees still shift the character's values and memories. | The character is someone even when alone. |
| VL13 | **Villain play stays allowed.** Default: the player can be ruthless, a villain start, an antihero (VILLAIN_STARTS doc). The world reacts honestly; the game does not lecture. Line: content still never sexualizes minors or glorifies harm to real people. | Respects player choice and the existing villain-start direction. |
| VL14 | **Moral injury in war worlds.** Default: in worlds like A Soldier's Life, orders that clash with values leave a mark (part 31 IL7). Dial: per world tone. | Lets war worlds carry their real weight. |

## 3. Leisure and play (VL15-VL24)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| VL15 | **Free time is a real slot.** Default: routines can include leisure. It lifts spirits, opens people (part 31 IL5) and passively trains a light skill. | An idle life that is not only work, and a reason to pick leisure in a routine. |
| VL16 | **In-world games.** Default: cards, dice, board games, sports, Quidditch, Gwent-style games, chess. Each has a light skill, people who play it and stakes. Reasonably: a simple text resolution, not a full minigame, unless a world earns one. | Taverns and dorms get activity, and games are a cheap social channel. |
| VL17 | **Gambling.** Default: gambling exists where the world has it, with real odds, house edges, cheats and debts. Dial: per world. Reasonably: odds stay honest; no player-facing real-money mechanics ever. | A classic risk-and-ruin story source. |
| VL18 | **Arts and performance.** Default: music, storytelling, painting, theatre. Performing builds renown; creating writes works that outlive you (part 31 IL28). | Non-combat fame and legacy paths. |
| VL19 | **Food and drink as pleasure.** Default: a good meal or drink lifts spirits beyond its stat benefit (food direction, 2026-10-13); taverns and feasts are social hubs. Drink has side effects in excess. | Gives meals social and emotional meaning. |
| VL20 | **Travel for pleasure.** Default: a holiday, a pilgrimage or sightseeing restores spirits and can surface rumors, people and places. | Rest that also opens content. |
| VL21 | **Festivals and holidays.** Default: from part 30 and part 31 IL25 calendars. Leisure is concentrated there: games, dances, contests, markets. | Seasonal highs everyone shares. |
| VL22 | **Collections.** Default: stamps, cards, books, curiosities, Chocolate Frog cards. Completion lifts spirits and can open a collector circle. | Idle-friendly long goals with zero power creep. |
| VL23 | **Vices.** Default: drink, gambling, rich food, idleness. Small doses lift spirits; habits cost money, health or trust. The player can break a habit with effort and support. | Realistic texture without moralizing, consistent with VL13. |
| VL24 | **Contests.** Default: tournaments, exams, races and cook-offs give public stakes and renown, and are a place to meet rivals. | Bridges leisure and progression. |

## 4. Prerequisites and cheapest slices (VL25-VL26)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
