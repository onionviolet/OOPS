# Micro moments and small signals (proposal, 2026-10-14)

Weibao (2026-10-14): "more to consider? other stuff and more? save and extend accordingly Send out codex as usesful? Subtle micro stuff too?"

Proposals only. Nothing here is accepted. Earlier parts set the big systems. This part collects the small touches that make them feel alive: one-line signals, tiny state changes and details that remember. Part 20 UF4 (small signals) and part 21 SU rows started this; here it goes across every system. Code MC checked free.

Rules for every row: one line of text or one small visual change, written only when it is true in state, never repeated so often it becomes noise (a per-signal cooldown that is a world adjective, not a fixed "once"), and never leaking sealed or hidden-layer truth (part 29 DV24).

---

## 1. People (MC1-MC12)

| Code | Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| MC1 | **They use your name, then a nickname.** Strangers say "traveler", acquaintances your name, close friends a nickname that came from a shared memory. | Familiarity shown in one word. |
| MC2 | **They remember your order.** The innkeeper pours your usual after a few visits. | The world noticing your habits. |
| MC3 | **A saved seat.** Friends keep a place for you at the table or in class. | Belonging without a meter. |
| MC4 | **Callbacks.** An NPC mentions something you did or said weeks ago ("still owe you for the rain cloak"). Reads the memory list. | Proves memories are real. |
| MC5 | **Mood on the face.** A line of body language before they speak: arms crossed, a tired smile, avoiding your eyes. From mood (part 29 RM10). | Lets the player read people like people. |
| MC6 | **Small lies with tells.** A pause, a too-quick answer, a changed subject, where a liar reads warm (DV9). | Fair clues for later reveals. |
| MC7 | **Gifts in use.** The scarf you gave appears on them; the knife sits on their belt. | A kept gift is a quiet thank-you. |
| MC8 | **Their own small lives.** A new haircut, a bandaged hand, a child's drawing on the counter, a new apprentice. From part 29 CQ6 life events. | NPCs change when you are not looking. |
| MC9 | **Inside jokes.** A shared joke from a memory resurfaces as a one-line callback. | Friendship texture that grows with time. |
| MC10 | **Distance in greetings.** A cooled friend greets you formally again; a forgiven one teases. | Tie changes felt before any panel says so. |
| MC11 | **Who sits with whom.** At meals and festivals, NPC-to-NPC ties show in seating and who leaves together. | The social graph made visible without a chart. |
| MC12 | **Children grow.** The kid who asked for sweets now asks about swords. | Time felt through others. |

## 2. Your body and things (MC13-MC22)

| Code | Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| MC13 | **Calluses, scars and posture.** Long practice leaves a mark mentioned in descriptions: sword calluses, ink-stained fingers, a soldier's stance. | Skills shown on the body; others may notice (DV recognition keys). |
| MC14 | **Aging details.** A first grey hair, stiff knees on cold mornings, by body age (part 30). | Aging becomes felt, not a number. |
| MC15 | **Wear on gear.** A notched blade, a patched cloak, a book with a cracked spine. Items gain small history lines. | Equipment with memory; old gear becomes precious. |
| MC16 | **Named items.** Items used long enough get a nickname from you or others. | Attachment to things, and a keepsake path (part 29 BK16). |
| MC17 | **Weather on you.** Wet boots, sunburn, frost on the eyelashes, a line after travel. | Travel and seasons feel physical. |
| MC18 | **Smell and dirt.** After a sewer job, people step back until you wash. | Small consequence, cheap comic beat. |
| MC19 | **Tired hands.** After long crafting, a line that your grip is shaky; quality dips slightly until rest. | Rest matters at micro scale without a fail state. |
| MC20 | **Food memories.** A dish tastes like home; a smell brings back a person. | Links food, places and memory. |
| MC21 | **Habits of hand.** The character taps a ring when nervous, hums while working; friends notice changes (TTKS habit tells). | Identity at the smallest scale. |
| MC22 | **Pocket clutter.** A pebble from a first quest, a ticket stub, a note. Inspectable trivia. | Personal history in the inventory. |

## 3. World texture (MC23-MC32)

| Code | Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| MC23 | **Time-of-day lines.** Bread smell at dawn, lamp-lighters at dusk, owls at night. | The clock felt, not just shown. |
| MC24 | **Seasonal details.** First snow, harvest dust, festival bunting going up days early. | Seasons announced by the world. |
| MC25 | **Places change.** A new shop sign, a closed stall, a repaired roof you helped with. | Your acts and time leave marks on places. |
| MC26 | **Overheard talk.** Short snippets in the street about real world state: prices, news, a rumor about you. | Information delivered as texture (part 31 IL20). |
| MC27 | **Animals.** The cat that follows you, the stray dog that learns your face, birds quieting before danger. | Life beyond people, and soft warnings. |
| MC28 | **Prices remember.** Bread costs more after the bad harvest; the line says so. | Economy felt at the scale of one loaf. |
| MC29 | **Traces of others.** Footprints, a cold campfire, a dropped glove, used before you arrive. | The world has other actors. |
| MC30 | **Signs of danger.** Shuttered windows, fewer children outside, guards doubled. | Soft warnings before threats (part 28 RC2). |
| MC31 | **Sound per world.** One recurring sound motif per world in text: a clock tower, distant bells, the hum of a ward, generators. | Cheap identity for each world. |
| MC32 | **Local words.** NPCs use local slang and terms the player learns; the notebook glosses them when learned. | Language as texture and discovery. |

## 4. Interface micro (MC33-MC40)

| Code | Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| MC33 | **Log voice variation.** Several phrasings per common event, picked by world, mood and context. | Stops the log reading like a machine. |
| MC34 | **Gentle firsts.** The first time something happens, a slightly longer line; later, a short one. | Teaches once, then gets out of the way. |
| MC35 | **Return recap in character.** After an absence, a short in-world note ("The garden ran wild; Maia left bread") instead of a stats dump (part 29 RUI6). | Idle returns feel like coming home. |
| MC36 | **Hover memories.** Hovering a person's name shows the last shared memory line. | Quick recall without opening a panel. |
| MC37 | **Quiet celebration.** A small flourish on milestones (first friend, first craft, first level) at desktop size, never a modal. | Progress felt, not shouted. |
| MC38 | **Per-world frame touches.** A tiny icon or border motif per world (part 07 RF1). | World identity at a glance. |
| MC39 | **Remembered UI state.** Panels reopen where you left them; the last-used routine is preselected. Settings, not character knowledge. | Removes small friction for a returning player. |
| MC40 | **Honest uncertainty words.** "Probably", "seems", "you're not sure" wherever the character's read is uncertain (part 28 SF1). | Small words carry the true-versus-shown rule. |

## 5. Micro stories (MC41-MC46)

| Code | Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| MC41 | **Two-line arcs.** A stranger asks for directions; days later they wave from a new stall. | Tiny payoffs for tiny acts. |
| MC42 | **Lost and found.** A dropped item turns up later with whoever found it. | The world returns things, with character. |
| MC43 | **Running gags.** A merchant who always overcharges by one coin; a door that always sticks. | Comic texture that becomes familiar. |
| MC44 | **Small favors.** Someone asks you to carry a letter or watch a stall for a minute. | Micro help that seeds ties (part 20 MF3). |
| MC45 | **Unseen consequences surfacing.** Weeks later, someone mentions the boy you helped is now apprenticed. | Shows your acts mattered without a quest marker. |
| MC46 | **Anniversary echoes.** A year after a big event, a line marks it ("A year since the flood"). | Time and memory tied together. |
