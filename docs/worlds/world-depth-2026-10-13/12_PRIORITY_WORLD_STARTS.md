# L. Priority-world start catalogue (proposal, 2026-10-13)

Weibao (2026-10-13): "Different starts, other stuff and more?" This file adds new starts for the eight priority worlds, in his order: Harry Potter, D&D, A Soldier's Life (book world 1, private), TTKS, Mother of Learning, then Lord of the Mysteries, Circle of Inevitability and Shadow Slave.

Everything here is a proposal. Nothing is accepted, built or run. Rows follow Default + Dial + Reasonably where a rule is implied.

**Checked against, not repeated:** parts 02, 04, 05, 08, 09 and 06 ST1 to ST13 in `docs/worlds/world-depth-2026-10-13/`; sibling K (`K_access_and_starts.md`, DS1 to DS16 and AC1 to AC17); HP O01 to O16, T01 to T10, I01 to I14 and O-ALT1 to 4 (private HP audit); D&D ST1 to ST3 and O1 to O5; MOL-ST01 to 08 and MOL-B rows (private MoL design); book world 1's 18 private snapshots W1-ST01 to 18 (compared only, paraphrase-free); TTKS deep-dive seeds and TT19 to TT23; LotM packet E1 to E4 and T1 to T8; SS-ST01 to 07, SS-ER01 to 05 and SS-OPEN01; the CoI timeline; `docs/STARTS_ENDINGS_AND_RANDOMNESS_PALETTE.md`; `docs/worlds/ORIGINS_AND_WORLD_TIMELINES.md`.

**Labels:** (G) general franchise knowledge, not repo notes. (P) paraphrase of a private repo note. (O) authored invention. (I) our inference. Public franchise twists are fine. Rerolled or changed truths appear only as a proposed sealed slot ID plus its route.

**Access routes** use K's AC names: picker (AC1), door (AC2), death or reincarnation (AC3), heir (AC4), loop (AC5), invitation (AC10), discovery unlock (AC12), summons (AC14), accident (AC15).

**Cost:** S reuses a built or specified slice with a new snapshot and a few lines. M adds one new place, role or rule table. L needs new source work or a new era.

Each world's rows are spread across era, social position, body, relation to the canon cast, premise, knowledge and resources. The axis tags in the first column show the spread.
