# More book worlds: canon spine and easy AUs (proposal, 2026-10-13)

Weibao widened the canon-first order: "Others too like LOTM, Shadow Slave,
other stuff and more? AUs that would change relationships, starting points
and more?" This file covers the book worlds outside the other agent's set
(HP, D&D, A Soldier's Life, TTKS, Mother of Learning in
`H_priority_canon_au.md`). Everything here is a proposal. Nothing is
accepted, built or run.

It builds on `E_researched.md` and does not repeat it. E already gives each
of these worlds daily-life rows, a smallest taste, two or three starts and
two parallel versions (for example LM8 to LM11, COI7 to COI10, SS7 to SS10,
GU8 to GU11, WRM7 to WRM10, LGM6 to LGM9, ORV7 to ORV10, RT7 to RT10, CR9 to
CR12). The AUs below are different ones, and they lean on E's codes, the
cluster defaults (CLI, CLL, CLH, CLC) and the private 2026-10-11 design
packets' start pools (named by ID, contents not copied).

Labels: (G) general franchise knowledge, not repo notes. (P) paraphrase of a
private dossier or design packet. (I) our inference. (U) source quality
unverified. O marks an authored invention. Canon names of main cast are
public franchise facts. Sealed truths appear by ID, route and status only.

How the copies work, for every AU row: the AU is a variant instance per
`docs/TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE.md` T4 and B1. Immutable definitions (items,
pathways, Gu, Aspects, places) are shared. Mutable state (actors, stock,
relationships, knowledge, claims, committed RNG) is copied or reset by an
explicit copy plan. "Carries" below means what that plan lets cross between
canon and the copy, and through what carrier.
