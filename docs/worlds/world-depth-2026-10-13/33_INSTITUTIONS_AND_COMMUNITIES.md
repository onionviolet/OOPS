## 2. Institution palette: kinds and terms (IKP, IOP)

**INFERRED original palette throughout this section.** An institution is organized continuity: people or other supported agents maintain rules, assets, functions and recognized authority across changes of members. A faction is an aligned bloc; it can be an institution, a wing within one, or an informal movement across several. A community is shared life and belonging (section 3). One place can contain all three without making them the same saved owner.

| Code | Kind | Typical entry and what it can do for / to a member |
|---|---|---|
| IKP1 | **Guild** | Apprentice, competence test, sponsor or paid registration; jobs, tools, prices, guarantees and mutual aid / dues, standards, complaints and market exclusion. A loose craft association or adventurer service fits where sourced; not every fantasy town has one. |
| IKP2 | **School** | Admission, guest study, scholarship, private pupil or staff hire; teaching, cohort, library and credentials / attendance, fees, exams, supervision and expulsion. Guest, pupil and teacher are distinct roles. |
| IKP3 | **Sect** | Recruitment, lineage nomination, donation or service; methods, protected grounds and cultivation resources / quotas, secrecy, seniority and rival branches. Paid admission may buy access while giving no breakthrough. |
| IKP4 | **Church or temple** | Congregant, volunteer, initiate, ordained role or lay employee; rites, care, records and sourced faculties / doctrine, tithes, discipline or loss of church access. Belief, employment and supernatural eligibility can differ. |
| IKP5 | **Court** | Petitioner, witness, clerk, advocate or office appointment; hearings, claims and rulings / summons, evidence duties, fees and sanctions under actual jurisdiction. A royal household court differs from a judicial court. |
| IKP6 | **Army** | Recruit, levy, contract, commission, specialist or civilian supplier; pay, drill, kit, protection and comrades / orders, deployments, custody and service restrictions. Desertion and release need local terms, not an ordinary cancel-job label. |
| IKP7 | **Bank** | Account, deposit, employment, loan agreement or recognized executor; custody, payment, exchange and credit / fees, collateral, recovery claims and account restrictions. A customer is not a governing member; a vault is not necessarily a loan service. |
| IKP8 | **House or clan** | Birth, adoption, household service, marriage alliance or nomination where fitting; home, patronage, property claims and methods / family expectations, contested succession and exclusion. Kinship does not grant every asset or inherited faculty. |
| IKP9 | **Secret society** | Introduction, invitation, test or infiltration; intelligence, contacts and hidden exchanges / secrecy, exposure and competing loyalties. A cell need not know its full parent roster. |
| IKP10 | **Company** | Hire, partnership, share purchase or founding; wages, productive assets, customers and division of work / contracts, targets, competition and insolvency. Employee, shareholder and director have different powers. |
| IKP11 | **Government** | Residency, citizenship, appointment, election or service; public goods, documents and protection / taxes, permits, conscription and enforcement. Local ward, city and central government can disagree. |
| IKP12 | **Gang** | Neighborhood contact, sponsorship, useful work, coercion or alliance; fences, protection and local influence / tribute, rivalry, intimidation and exit disputes. Membership is not a compulsory fight or automatic approval from all criminals. |

| Code | Model consideration | Default + Dial + Reasonably |
|---|---|---|
| IOP1 | **Scope and identity** | Default: stable institution ID scoped to world instance, era and region, with source/revision and function. Dial: branch chapters, mobile institution or multiverse charter. Reasonably: a same-name AU guild is a counterpart unless a route actually connects it. |
| IOP2 | **Membership is an agreement, not an affection bar** | Default: applicant, guest, probationer, member, inactive, suspended, resigned, expelled or former member as fitting states; issuer and effective date. Dial: inherited entitlement, compulsory levy or decentralized recognition. Reasonably: entry terms name real facts and who can accept them; friendliness alone grants no badge. |
| IOP3 | **Overlapping membership** | Default: allow fitting simultaneous memberships with scoped obligations. Dial: exclusivity, dual office, cover identity, exchange student or ally status. Reasonably: a conflict produces a known institutional dispute, not a global rule that the player must pick one organization forever. |
| IOP4 | **Ranks, offices and competence differ** | Default: rank expresses local standing; office names powers and duties; actual methods determine execution. Dial: elected, hereditary, bought, appointed or exam-earned roles. Reasonably: seniority can differ from age, sequence or combat threat. No global rank conversion is required. |
| IOP5 | **Dues have a destination** | Default: payer, recipient, currency/material, due boundary, grace and settlement. Dial: money, labor, tithe, quota, scholarship or waived dues. Reasonably: reserve the actual amount; inability to pay can yield negotiation, suspended service or departure, with local alternatives and no hunger death. |
| IOP6 | **Duties have terms** | Default: specific duty, authority, inputs, location, time, success/partial/breach and exit. Dial: standing routine, optional commission, compulsory order or elected responsibility. Reasonably: refusal, cancellation and desertion are distinct; no new irreversible duty is silently accepted during idle time. |
| IOP7 | **Privileges are scoped readers** | Default: enumerate which door, lesson, stock, service, vote or command the status authorizes. Dial: shared tools, credit, safe lodging, legal representation or delegation. Reasonably: a paid facility is useful without giving its customer's source, skill, consent or all private records. |
| IOP8 | **People exercise institutional power** | Default: registrar, officer, teacher or treasurer has a remit and actual means. Dial: council quorum, automated archive, god, or distributed signers. Reasonably: harming or replacing an incumbent changes service only through a supported successor or alternative; a title alone creates no stock. |
| IOP9 | **Internal politics** | Default: a few named goals and competing priorities such as safety, profit, access and prestige. Dial: cooperative, patronage-heavy, corrupt, reforming or divided. Reasonably: useful teachers and helpful institutions can remain helpful; betrayal or a stronger relative is not automatic. |
| IOP10 | **Factions within** | Default: students versus administration, officers versus veterans, research schools or reform bloc only when they affect a slice. Dial: several wings, secret cells or one consensus. Reasonably: a supporter in one wing may be disliked by another; institutional standing cannot stand in for every personal tie. |
| IOP11 | **Discipline, revocation and return** | Default: specific alleged breach, received evidence, decision-maker, revoked privilege and known remedy. Dial: hearing, pardon, appeal, restitution, defection, reinstatement or rival service. Reasonably: lost access does not erase already learned techniques or the ordinary save/log controls. Later genuine admission remains possible. |
| IOP12 | **Founding** | Default: purpose, actual members, place or contact channel, means and chosen terms; informal club can precede recognition. Dial: charter, outlaw order, virtual circle, splinter or revived institution. Reasonably: recognition, buildings and funding are separate outcomes; no universal founding level or fixed count cap. |
| IOP13 | **Between institutions** | Default: concrete trade, recognition, rivalry or disputed jurisdiction with a cause and communication route. Dial: alliance, treaty, schism, merger, absorption, war or neutral broker. Reasonably: friendly reputation with a rival does not automatically trigger global hostility; who knows and can act matters. |
| IOP14 | **Institutional assets** | Default: distinguish corporate purse, member goods, loaned tools, shared facilities, protected records and endowed funds. Dial: communal ownership, private property, conquest or exceptional abundance. Reasonably: delegation or inheritance transfers actual rights/assets under a rule; it does not clone them. |
| IOP15 | **What the institution remembers** | Default: enrollment, credentials, work, loans, dues, claims, discipline, appointments and founder legacy with author, date, recipient and basis. Dial: oral witnesses, archive, AI, sacred register or disputed copies. Reasonably: keep durable rights and unsettled causes outside recent-memory trimming; records can be incomplete, forged, corrected or lost. |
| IOP16 | **What it believes** | Default: separate actual event, received report, register entry and current policy response. Dial: surveillance, divination, public vote or rumor with bounded faculties. Reasonably: a robe or copied face may fool an observer without acquiring real authority, and no institution reads sealed truth just because state contains it. |
| IOP17 | **Continuity and collapse** | Default: vacancies, insolvency, destroyed facilities and dissolved charters change specific offers. Dial: caretaker, successor, diaspora, reconstitution or new regime. Reasonably: no surprise absent-world simulation is added; former members and surviving records can persist without a functioning hall. |
| IOP18 | **Outsiders have useful routes** | Default: public services, guest permits, contract work, independent study and ordinary trades exist where fitting. Dial: stricter exclusion, patron, translator, forged credential, illegal route or reform. Reasonably: each route has actual costs, support and consequences rather than requiring membership for all progression. |

## 3. Communities and belonging (ICP)

**INFERRED original palette.** Community is shared place, daily contact, mutual recognition and practical dependence. It need not have admission, dues or command authority. A barracks has a military institution around it; its roommates can still help a disgraced soldier. An institution can have distant members who have never shared a meal.

| Code | Community / consideration | Different life and belonging consequences |
|---|---|---|
| ICP1 | **Village** | Repeated faces, seasonal work, oral memory and shared facilities. Default: a newcomer may be welcomed, ignored or distrusted according to the actual village. Dial: kin enclave, open trade stop or quiet hamlet. Reasonably: residence and helpful participation can build familiarity without compulsory faction enrollment. |
| ICP2 | **City ward** | Many neighbors, work circles, landlords and overlapping audiences. Default: the player can belong to a street while unknown to the city. Dial: migrant district, wealthy enclave, mixed ward or controlled territory. Reasonably: one merchant's grievance is not every resident's belief. |
| ICP3 | **Dorm** | Cohort schedules, shared rooms, privacy, study groups and house norms. Default: school admission affects lodging, but closeness grows through contact. Dial: boarding house, independent residence or supervised youth dorm. Reasonably: house points and friendship are separate, and not every student is a confidant. |
| ICP4 | **Barracks or camp** | Shared drills, kit care, watches and risk. Default: command and comrade opinion are separate. Dial: temporary levy, professional unit, shipboard marines or civilian support. Reasonably: promotion or transfer can strain a tie without deleting it. |
| ICP5 | **Sect mountain** | Kitchens, farms, practice halls, archives and seclusion zones connect different roles. Default: peripheral worker, outer disciple and inner specialist meet different circles. Dial: public market, secluded lineage or traveling sect. Reasonably: locality is not a universal right to read the mountain's secrets. |
| ICP6 | **Ship** | Moving home, voyage roster, provisions, limited space and land contacts. Default: passengers, crew, captain and owners differ. Dial: navy, merchant crew, family boat or migrant convoy. Reasonably: authority at sea and ordinary belonging need scoped terms; ship inheritance does not transfer a living crew's agreement. |
| ICP7 | **Settlement** | Built shelter, water, clinics and work allocations. Default: contribution and residence shape belonging. Dial: frontier, refugee camp, fortress/citadel or habitat. Reasonably: services consume actual support; raids or shortage can create aid and alternatives without hunger death. |
| ICP8 | **Belonging has several bases** | Default: resident, familiar face, contributor, kin, citizen and accepted neighbor can overlap or diverge. Dial: newcomer welcomed immediately, earned trust, contested ancestry or chosen family. Reasonably: IL26's low-standing newcomer is one option, not a compulsory penalty at every arrival. |
| ICP9 | **Community memory** | Default: witnesses, meeting places, stories, graves and notices carry particular events. Dial: oral, print, magical mail or durable archives. Reasonably: IL20/IL28 need a carrier and clock; one community can remember gratitude while an institution records unpaid dues. |
| ICP10 | **Aid and exclusion** | Default: help is supplied by actual people/funds, refusal by actual actors or norms. Dial: inclusive, closed, stratified, fragmented or reforming. Reasonably: outsiders can make new circles, leave or negotiate; a community is not a single hostile mind. |
| ICP11 | **Shared events create contact** | Default: market, festival, repair, funeral or crisis creates observed shared history. Dial: sparse cozy routines or more intense settings. Reasonably: a repeated funded event can recur with a new cause; not every public act changes every person's tie. |
| ICP12 | **Community can outlive its place** | Default: dispersed alumni, veterans, refugees and former crew retain selected bonds. Dial: reunion, diaspora network or faded contact. Reasonably: institutional dissolution can leave a living community, and moving home need not erase all earlier belonging. |
