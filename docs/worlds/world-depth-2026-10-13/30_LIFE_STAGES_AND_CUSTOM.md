**Fixed rule:** romance and partner verbs, and any romantic or sexual content, require both people to be 18 or older **by body age**. This is not a dial. Childhood betrothals, dynastic contracts and historical marriage customs may be offscreen legal or political ties without romantic content until both are 18. A reincarnated adult in a child's body counts as a child. Local law, adult status, a mature appearance, supernatural power, an adult soul, species maturity, a cheat or an AU cannot lower this floor. Other boundaries, including work, school, inheritance, military service and coming of age, fit the selected world, era and lore. Passing the floor never supplies willingness or overrides a stricter local rule. Live reconciliation: [later appearance addendum](../../gm/dispatch/relationship-prereqs-2026-10-14/CLAUDE_ADDENDUM_2.md) DV1, also recorded in User Vision and part 29 RFM6, adds adult appearance as a separate fixed restriction. ## 2. Life-stage ladder: distinct clocks and meanings (LSL)

Illustrative human palette: dependent infant, exploring child, school/apprentice youth, locally independent adult, established worker/caregiver, elder, deceased remembered person. These are roles that can overlap or be skipped in a selected start, not a forced sequence. An ancestor is a social or spiritual relation after death, not automatically a living body's final stage.

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| LSL1 | **Separate body, experience, appearance and law.** Default: body age belongs to the actual body's chronology; experience/soul age belongs to retained life history; apparent age is an observer's read; legal age/status names a jurisdiction. Dial: several jurisdictions and uncertain estimates. Reasonably: do not compress them into one `age`. | A child with adult memories, young-looking elder and adult under guardianship need different rules. |
| LSL2 | **Two people, two body checks.** Default: each romantic/partner verb and content route requires both body ages established as at least 18, alongside willingness and fitting local terms. No age-floor dial. Reasonably: unknown age remains unknown; an introduction can establish it without displaying a private birth date. | The player being adult does not qualify the target. Text, mail, generated scenes and NPC-to-NPC news need the same floor as buttons. |
| LSL3 | **Local independence is a separate ladder.** Default: household release, contract capacity, school exit and military eligibility each have a local predicate. Dial: age, exam, guardian approval, rite, office or mixed proof where sourced. Reasonably: a child officer or ruler can hold an office without adult romantic content. | One coming-of-age flag would incorrectly unlock unrelated rights. |
| LSL4 | **Stages follow bodies, not power ranks.** Default: authored development/function changes and social stages are separate from cultivation realm, wizard grade or military rank. Dial: sourced breakthroughs that actually alter aging. Reasonably: power only changes biology through its declared method. | A strong child remains a child; an untrained elder remains an elder. |
| LSL5 | **Lifespan scaling stays local.** Default: body-specific growth, maintenance and senescence bands. Dial: short-lived, long-lived, ageless, seasonal, molting or constructed forms. Reasonably: longer lifespan does not imply proportionally later literacy or earlier romance. | Species-wide multipliers often distort both school stages and the fixed floor. |
| LSL6 | **Nonhuman adulthood never substitutes for 18.** Default: cub/adult/elder, seedling/grown/ancient or forged/awakened/repaired can govern nonromantic functions. Dial: sourced body histories and original forms. Reasonably: constructed or bodyless persons need a supported body-age definition before romantic routes can qualify; consciousness time or a mature avatar label alone cannot qualify them. | Many nonhuman starts remain fully useful through friendship, care, work and legacy without an invented age equivalence. |
| LSL7 | **Body replacement names what aged.** Default: retain controller/person history separately from the entered body's age. Dial: transplant, possession, restored old body or genuinely new body. Reasonably: borrowing an older body is not proof that the previous body aged; a cosmetic disguise does not change actual age, while DV1 still bars childlike appearance from romantic content. | Recognition and execution need the current body, while past experience stays with its carrier. |
| LSL8 | **Regression suspends romantic play.** Default: if actual rejuvenation, regression or replacement produces an under-18 body, retain history and legal ties without romantic/sexual content or partner verbs. No bypass dial. Reasonably: both bodies must qualify again before any new romantic offer, with present agreement. | A saved adult partnership cannot authorize child-body scenes after a later transformation. |
| LSL9 | **Transition has a basis and a date.** Default: record meaningful growth or institutional transitions through their owning fact, not only the recent-memory list. Dial: birthdays, exams, rites and delayed legal registration. Reasonably: genuine later transitions remain possible; receipts protect publication from duplication. | Losing an old memory should not revoke graduation or award it again. |
| LSL10 | **Freeze and time exposure are distinct.** Default: follow the existing selected clock/absence policy; no new browser-closed or absent-world aging. Dial: separately authorized local ratios, bounded work or chapter advances. Reasonably: map actual body exposure and local calendar explicitly through the shared scheduler. | Traveling for a week must not silently age a frozen family's children by decades. |
| LSL11 | **Chapter growth has a preview.** Default: meaningful childhood choices followed by a declared later snapshot, with changed people, rights, unfinished work and risks. Dial: detailed stages, short chapters or starting later. Reasonably: no required real-time years or retroactive earned training. | Keeps infancy reachable and playable without inventing a full childhood simulation. |
| LSL12 | **Care gives agency.** Default: observation, signals, requests, supported practice and later delegation with named caregivers. Dial: supportive, poor, busy, strict or solitary support situations. Reasonably: care uses actual means; hardship needs causal alternatives and does not introduce hunger death. | A dependent start should have useful decisions, not only disabled adult actions. |
| LSL13 | **Elderhood can add functions.** Default: accumulated expertise, teaching, mediation, archives and institutional influence. Dial: retirement, continued field work, disability support or renewed body. Reasonably: decline is specific to the body; age alone does not erase acquired knowledge or imply weakness. | Elders need a life to play rather than a universal penalty tier. |
| LSL14 | **Death and ancestry are distinct records.** Default: bodily ending, survivor knowledge, memorial and estate are separate; spiritual continuation needs its sourced method. Dial: ancestor rite, ghost contact, reincarnation or no return. Reasonably: a descendant is not a new body controlled by the ancestor by default. | Fits succession and brood autonomy without automatic resurrection or possession. |
| LSL15 | **Immortality leaves social time intact.** Default: no senescence where supported, while jobs, children, laws and mortal friends can change under their own clocks. Dial: dormancy, seclusion, frozen visit or active local life. Reasonably: returning to society grants no skipped experience or guaranteed welcome. | An immortal still has life stages in role and relationships even if their body stays stable. |

## 3. Era and custom palette (LSE)

Pair period with place, class, institution and a dated world branch. Mix or omit fitting elements; future eras do not inherently become freer or more restrictive. Customs can be disputed within one society. No period label authorizes under-18 romantic content.

| LSE10 | **Ninja-village palette.** Default: clan household, training cohort, command hierarchy and mission availability. Dial: civilian household, academy, field team or wartime relocation. | LSE11 | **Post-apocalypse palette.** Default: shelter, ration/work access, guardians and survival institutions. Dial: organized city, roaming group, legacy enclave or rebuilding frontier. | LSE12 | **Kinship and household are different maps.** Default: parentage, caregiver, guardian, household member and legal heir are distinct. Dial: clan, nuclear, multigeneration, communal or chosen family; matrilineal/patrilineal/bilateral/local descent rules where fitting. Reasonably: do not infer household power from sex or surname. | Adoption, fosterage and separated families need more than two parent slots. |
| LSE13 | **Matchmaking and courtship have local forms.** Default: family introduction or personal invitation according to the selected custom. Dial: matchmaker, festival, letters, visits, public service or freely chosen meeting. | LSE14 | **Dowry, bride price and gifts name ownership.** Default: author payer, recipient, owner, conditions and return terms; absent where unsupported. Dial: household settlement, ceremonial exchange, reciprocal gifts or none. Reasonably: transferring goods grants no person's consent or ownership of them. | Family debt, a spouse's separate property and wedding expenses are different ledgers. |
| LSE15 | **Inheritance has several objects and authorities.** Default: separate estate goods, office, name, land tenure, secrets and source eligibility. Dial: eldest, shared children, nominated/adopted successor, household council, merit or no family claim where fitting. Reasonably: transfer only actual leftovers under the relevant jurisdiction. | Winning an heir contest cannot recreate a destroyed archive or confer another person's mastery. |
| LSE16 | **Widowhood, divorce and remarriage have aftermath.** Default: separate ended partnership, surviving property rights, care duties and public standing. Dial: remarriage, household continuation, separation, religious release or contested settlement. Reasonably: mourning is not a universal timer; renewed adult relationships are not capped. | A legal status change should not erase children, history or independent means. |
| LSE17 | **Class, caste and gender expectations need specific readers.** Default: state whose custom restricts which act and what institution enforces it. Dial: compliance, resistance, reform, migration or different circles with actual access. Reasonably: contextualize prejudice rather than making it a universal stat penalty. | Wealth, gender, ancestry and legal status can affect opportunities differently within one era. |
| LSE18 | **Apprenticeship and schooling need separate terms.** Default: teacher, admission, fee/support, schedule, taught means and exit rights. Dial: household trade, guild contract, school, sect or self-study. Reasonably: youth work is nonromantic; acquired practice remains separate from a certificate. | A talented child can learn while still needing permission to travel or sell. |
| LSE19 | **Military age is not adulthood in every domain.** Default: enlistment/conscription, rank, duty, pay, custody and release use sourced local rules. Dial: civil support, cadet, field service, emergency levy or exempt status. Reasonably: command authority supplies neither romance eligibility nor personal willingness. | A youth's service and an adult veteran's dependency can coexist. |
| LSE20 | **Retirement, elders and ancestors have local functions.** Default: work capability, care, pension/household support, teaching and ritual role are separate. Dial: active elder, retired artisan, council member, recluse or remembered ancestor. Reasonably: no automatic pension, universal infirmity or magical ancestor power. | Old age can change how someone contributes without removing them from life. |
| LSE21 | **Custom can change in a living world.** Default: a dated law/reform/war/migration event changes relevant future rules while retaining past acts and unsettled claims. Dial: gradual norms, formal decree, contested authority or unchanged enclave. Reasonably: a new period does not overwrite every household's beliefs. | History becomes play through changed institutions rather than a costume switch. |
