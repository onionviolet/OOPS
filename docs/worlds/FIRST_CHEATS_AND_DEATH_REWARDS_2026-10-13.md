# First cheats, death rewards and book-world integration

2026-10-13. Weibao asks about rewards after death, first-time acquisition through random characters, artifacts and other sources, and integration into book worlds.

Status: proposals, not accepted mechanics or implemented gameplay. Continues [feature acquisition process](FEATURE_UNLOCK_AND_CHEAT_ACQUISITION_PROCESS_2026-10-13.md). Standing random-entry direction is in [USER_VISION](../USER_VISION.md); carry and conversion are per-edge dials under [accepted rules](../RULES_FROM_SCRATCH.md). Free carries are possible when the relevant world, hub, cheat or mode allows them. The current heir implementation is not a limit on future designs.

## D1. Recommended default: random circumstances, authored opportunity, player-directed development

Randomize a coherent life rather than independently rolling world, body and power and hoping they fit. Each eligible start has an authored set of possible opportunities. The first cheat can be active, latent, accessible through an early lead, or absent from the opening under an ordinary-life profile. The distribution, rarity and reroll policy remain proposals.

For a cheat-focused first-run profile, recommend a reachable early opportunity, not an unavoidable powerful grant. Its timing follows meaningful play rather than a universal minute deadline. The player may investigate, refuse, negotiate or pursue another route. A guaranteed opportunity need not announce itself as a guaranteed cheat.

Separate player knowledge from character knowledge. A random start can hide the identity of a latent power without hiding known abilities the character has used for years. Keep randomized truths sealed outside their discovery routes.

## D2. First acquisition forms

| Code | Form | First experience | Suitable variation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Innate or latent | Begin with a known trait, or notice signs and learn what awakens it | Bloodline, unusual body, soul trait, prior experiment |
| A2 | Artifact | Find, inherit, borrow, buy, craft or steal an object; identify and activate it as needed | Useful immediately, damaged, unidentified, sentient, conditional |
| A3 | Learned or invented | Teacher, text, observation, research and practice lead to a new function | Ordinary training that reaches an exceptional application |
| A4 | Granted or contracted | A patron, institution, system or storyteller makes an offer | Gift, loan, bargain, sponsorship, unwanted obligation |
| A5 | Event or aftermath | An ordeal, breakthrough, ending, death or return changes the available routes | Awakening, reconstruction, selection by an observer, rebirth |

These overlap. A family relic may contain a teacher; a random character may already hold a contract; a death reward may arrive as an artifact. Acquisition form, power function and backing should be independently authored.

## D3. Death rewards: distinguish recipient and effect

| Code | Kind | Recipient and result | Is it a cheat? |
|---|---|---|---|
| DR1 | Player discovery | Player unlocks a start, scenario, account option or recorded experience | Meta progression; not automatically a character power |
| DR2 | Legacy | Heir, student, companion or later finder receives belongings, records, standing or unfinished work | Ordinary continuity unless an exceptional carrier adds more |
| DR3 | World aftermath | Survivors and the world change because of the death | Consequence; can create new opportunities without any personal reward |
| DR4 | Afterlife offer | Dead character can negotiate another life, employment, a power or a price | A cheat when it provides exceptional access; could be ordinary in an afterlife setting |
| DR5 | Death-triggered power | Holder rewinds, revives, copies, evolves or retains something | Usually exceptional; its authored trigger and limitations decide |

A death recap should separate “what you learned”, “what remains in the world”, “what others inherit”, and “what you may carry or choose next”. It must identify who actually receives each benefit. A player knowing something does not automatically grant that knowledge to a fresh character.

Recommendation: rewards primarily recognize the life lived, discoveries and meaningful outcomes. Death resolves or transfers them. Repeated identical early deaths should not produce unlimited generic upgrade currency by default. Deliberately using death as a strategy remains appropriate for a cheat whose premise depends on it. Retirement, completion and succession can also resolve rewards where relevant, so death need not be the only productive ending.

Decide for each death power whether it existed before death, awakens at death, or is newly granted afterward. These produce different stories and cannot share one unexplained “reward” label. Borrowed power, debts and curses may also survive according to their own terms.

## D4. Integrate into book worlds through a declared mode

| Mode | Placement |
|---|---|
| Canon-grounded ordinary life | Use source-supported local acquisition routes and constraints. The character may never receive an outside cheat. |
| Original character within canon | Start circumstances and opportunities are authored additions compatible with verified rules. Do not quietly give away a unique canon possession. |
| AU or crossover | Declare the changed premise. An outside artifact, system, soul or patron can be the divergence, with an explicit world interaction profile. |
| Sandbox | Choose desired powers and access directly; keep chosen access distinct from earned history. |

Illustrative adaptation proposals, not claims of verified canon implementation:

| World | Candidate first route | Death/continuation question |
|---|---|---|
| Harry Potter | A family object, lessons, or an original magical curiosity with a defined provenance | What passes through the estate, and what would require an explicitly exceptional survival mechanism? |
| D&D | Treasure, apprenticeship, a bargain or a species/class-related opportunity, under a selected rules profile | Is return available through the selected rules, who provides it, and who pays? |
| A Soldier's Life | A start-compatible gift or an opportunity encountered through service, constrained by the private source owner | Who receives effects, records, obligations and any transferable source? |
| Mother of Learning | A selected entry premise involving learning, research or a loop-related opportunity | Which reset mechanism applies, and what does this character actually retain? |
| LOTM / Circle of Inevitability | A source-verified local power route, artifact or patron offer; outside grants belong to a declared variant | What survives as material, soul, contract or world consequence under that route? |
| Shadow Slave | A source-verified awakening or acquisition route; a special outlier is an explicit premise | Is the proposed benefit native, an authored exception, or an outside cheat? |

Before implementation, the world owner must verify the relevant source rules. These examples do not establish exact canon acquisition, resurrection or inheritance behavior.

## D5. Further considerations and first slice

| Code | Question | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Does every start have a cheat? | Offer cheat-focused and ordinary-life profiles; keep distribution configurable rather than making one universal promise. |
| C2 | Is first-time access a tutorial tax? | Keep a short understandable first benefit. Complex powers can develop later; inherited usable tools need no repeated attunement chore without a reason. |
| C3 | What is randomized? | Prefer coherent authored packages; roll strength, restrictions and growth only inside compatible ranges. Preserve genuine weak or unusual lives without accidental impossible starts. |
| C4 | Can the opportunity be missed? | Distinguish temporary offers from reliable routes. A missed offer can leave a clue or alternative; some rare events may remain genuinely missable. |
| C5 | What does a death reward know? | Reward rules may track simulation truth, but presentation stays within known information and sealed discovery routes. A reward name must not leak a hidden cause. |
| C6 | Who selects the reward? | World judgment, patron choice, seeded draw, player choice or a mixture are all possible. Display which mechanism is actually operating where known. |
| C7 | Does a new cheat destabilize canon? | Define who can detect it, local counters, compatibility, and whether canon events can change. Preserve the declared mode instead of forcing canon outcomes after meaningful intervention. |
| C8 | Can small cheats become deep systems? | Begin with one function, then grow reach, precision, fuel, autonomy or transferability. A modest scouting benefit can later support a full exploration system. |
| C9 | What carries across boundaries? | State scope and carrier per power and edge. Allow full, partial, transformed, dormant or blocked transfer where authored, including free carry where permitted. |
| C10 | Is random selection rerollable? | Declare reroll and replay behavior; persist pending choices so reload does not accidentally redraw outcomes. Repeated death strategies remain power-specific. |

First candidate: one coherent random-start package with one discoverable acquisition route, a useful first action, and an end-of-life manifest showing one genuine consequence or carry. This is a proposed slice, not authorization to implement it. Source work, rebuilt desktop gameplay, save/reload/import, compatibility, and director acceptance remain distinct gates.
