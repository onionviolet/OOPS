## C1 scope and reading key

The [root palette](../COMPANIONS_FAMILIARS_AND_PETS_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md) owns A/B/C codes. [User vision](../USER_VISION.md#world-depth-and-canon-first-world-priority-2026-10-13) and [PROJECT](../../PROJECT.md) keep canon-first priority and the paused Skyrim/Fallout/Elden Ring work intact. This catalogue adds research, not a new implementation queue. It does not reopen the Foundation chain or require a companion engine before another slice can ship.

**E1 SHOWN** means an inspected text or a local dossier's located account of an enacted event. The register distinguishes direct primary checks from dossier-mediated checks. **E2 STATED** means attributed author, rules, system, character or wiki wording; a fandom wiki is secondary, not official canon. **E3 INFERRED** covers every proposed start, cheat, player role, fit judgement and Default/Dial below. **E4 UNVERIFIED** means the reviewed evidence does not settle the claim. No unchecked MEMORY is promoted to canon.

**K1 codes:** A1 pet; A2 working animal; A3 mount; A4 familiar; A5 summon; A6 contracted spirit/devil; A7 shikigami/shadow beast; A8 made being; A9 bound dead; A10 object spirit; A11 system/voice; A12 swarm/colony; A13 plant; A14 place spirit; A15 hatchling. Codes describe candidate functions and can overlap. A human ally is outside this A palette. A sapient animal, aranea, spirit or constructed person can also be an independent ally. Coding its body does not imply ownership.

**K2 codes:** B1 buy; B2 adopt; B3 tame; B4 raise/hatch; B5 contract; B6 ritual; B7 chooses you; B8 inherit; B9 craft; B10 rescue/steal/win; B11 cheat; B12 crosses with you. Friendship or paid cooperation with an independent person is a social route outside these acquisition codes; do not relabel it taming.

**K3 codes:** C1 trust; C2 affection; C3 obedience; C4 understanding; C5 terms; C6 shared channel; C7 respect. These are separate axes. C3 can be coercion rather than friendship; C6 is not proof of consent. A companion's game-specific invulnerability or menu slot is a rules-profile fact, not a universal bodily law.

For every row, **Default** is a fitting modest entry at the stated era, **Dial** changes access or power with a named source, and **Reasonably** keeps care, fuel, agency and local history causal. A high-power start is possible where authored; no universal pet cap, compulsory weakness or forced novice opening is proposed. Playing **as** the being belongs to [nonhuman starts](NONHUMAN_STARTS_BY_WORLD_2026-10-14.md) and [bonds and parties](NONHUMAN_BONDS_AND_PARTIES_2026-10-14.md).
