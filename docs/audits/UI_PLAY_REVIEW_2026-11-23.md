# OOPS UI play review · November 23, 2026

The current desktop UI has a coherent look and a readable combat surface. The biggest remaining weakness is knowing where to act. The Village makes local people and destinations legible through Here, but the wide action column can sit mostly empty while next-step guidance lives in Reports and feature entry lives in Here or All panels. The road fight puts decisions and feedback in more predictable places. Brewing reaches a real outcome in two worlds, with a busier worktable and a less obvious route in.
