## What "full implementation" means

The standard already defines it. A world is fully implemented at **C4** for its
frozen represented scope: every selected section (S00-S14), start, route and
ending works end to end on a current rebuild, with save/reload/import proof.
**C5** is Weibao's own acceptance of fiction, pacing and feel. One working room
is C2, not a finished world. Freeze a finishable scope per world, not the size of
the vision.

**Where things stand:** no world is above C1. Frontier, Paradise, Proto23,
Mother of Learning, LOTM and the D&D ST2 slice are C1 (specified or
implemented-unverified). Every other world is C0. Some older browser evidence is
C2-shaped but was taken on builds that have since changed.

## Patterns across worlds

- **WGP1, aftermath is missing.** Powers are well specified; what happens after
  use (who saw it, who pays, who remembers, what it costs in daily life) is not.
  Groups 2, 3 and 4 found this independently.
- **WGP2, time joins.** Most gaps sit on the one played clock: night, season,
  weekday, term, post latency, report age, strain. Calendar and climate code
  exist, but few world rows use them. "Freeze while absent" is the default with
  no stated dial or in-world reason for when time should move.
- **WGP3, knowledge that can be wrong.** S09 is a principle in most worlds but
  rarely produces an output that can be stale, misattributed or misread.
- **WGP4, nothing above C1.** The cheapest way up is the AI playthrough harness:
  turn each done check above into a stepped-time journey (C2 evidence), then
  freeze section criteria per world (C1 to C3).
