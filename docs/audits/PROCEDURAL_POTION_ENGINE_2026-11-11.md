# Procedural potion engine · architecture and play evidence

Design comparison under AD38, AD49, AD51, AD52 and MR29–MR36. This records implementation evidence and a proposal for further work.

## Architecture

| Option | What the playable routes show | Judgment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Universal batch resolver | The same pure composition calculation can bind Seer power and hazard and resolve Hogwarts precursor or binder reactions. Polyjuice binds a living donor at the glass, and an unstirred suspension retains solids for the next pour. Both operate on state after the initial reaction has settled. | Keep composition in the resolver. A universal lifecycle would have to model different custody, timing, and dose semantics as well. |
| Shared contracts, local rules | Hogwarts and LOTM both call [the composition resolver](../../src/craft_resolver.js) through [Hogwarts reaction rules](../../src/hp_potion_reactions.js) and [the Seer binding](../../src/lotm_seer_brew.js). Hogwarts uses the same [demonstrated-method contract](../../src/craft_reference.js) for batch recipes and a separately paid classroom assay. | Preferred. Share a module at the boundary two different callers actually exercise. The composition evaluator already meets that test. |
| Largely independent engines | [The Hogwarts bench](../../src/hp_brewing_state.js) and [LOTM cast host](../../src/book_world_lotm_cast_host.js) already express their different ownership. Independent composition and experiment code would repeat behavior already exercised by current callers. | Keep separate rules where a shared contract would lose behavior, such as the recipient's transformation or institutional stock. |

Additional boundaries: [Village mixture](../../src/mixture_native.js) demonstrates atomic publication in its native host. [Gu keeping](../../src/book_world_gu_keeper_domain.js) and [creature habitat care](../../src/habitat_care.js) have living participants, needs, and products that keep their own identities. [Spoilage](../../src/spoilage.js) reads age without owning custody or time. A shared stock or stage executor still needs two genuinely different play callers exercising the same behavior before extraction.

## FG2. Magical contribution, delivery and persistence

Foundation audit under AD38, AD49, AD51 and MR29–MR36. Scope follows [Round 6](../CROSS_WORLD_SYSTEMS_TRANSFER.md#round-6-foundations-goals-by-existing-chat). The chemistry proposal owns conservation, vessel history and kinetics. This comparison freezes the preparation trace except where a brewing contribution is itself the controlled variable. Published cases establish what was shown or said. The untried branches below are paper checks for possible game rules.

**Cross-owner settlement check.** A preparation can be valid and held while a delivery condition changes. Quote a ready dose with a valid source and recipient, then withdraw the live contribution before administration. An interruption before ingestion leaves the dose in custody. If actual ingestion already occurred, record the debit and settle the local exposure with the contribution that was actually present. Keep batch, receipt, recipient outcome and notebook observation linked by references to their respective owners. Save/reload should preserve the same decision at the same boundary.

**Open gaps.** Hogwarts wandwork is source-backed in general. Its exact place and effects in the individual preparation remain unresolved. Polyjuice still needs evidence for sample-form differences, partial dosing and overlapping transformations. LOTM needs separate evidence for which recipient facts change the same dose and whether specific ritual substitutes carry the required effect. The relay and its assay are original proposals. A measured response, onset, persistence and later digestion are separate observations. The existing implementation evidence above applies to the routes named there.
