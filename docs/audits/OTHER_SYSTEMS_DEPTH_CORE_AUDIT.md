**Foundations and truth, SHOWN.** `drainage_site.js` stores physical layout seed,
movable-object/support facts and a finite material balance in world facts.
`validateDrainage` checks remaining + removed + washed = 4. Knowledge is separate:
entrance/route/room observations and a local learned handling method. Its view
filters rooms and edges through held observations. This is a bounded original
gallery with meaningful physical interactions, not arbitrary dungeon generation.
No generated room truth is listed here.

**Producers and consumers, SHOWN.** Surveying creates a known route; inspection
creates an observed room; actual wood and rope support a gate; moving a physical
object uses adjacency, observed route and actual effort; searches consume a
finite face and can produce native Iron ore. Attended observation reduces local
search risk, and failed field work feeds an existing bounded learning reader.
Installation removes carried supply, recovery returns installed rope, and
depletion has an explicit loss destination. These are useful outputs beyond a
“site completed” flag.

**Stages, SHOWN.** `site_projects.js::compileSiteProject` compiles phase
dependencies into shared world-fact requirements and jobs/claims. The observer
view uses held observations or the actor's own completed work. It does not make

idle/prepared/inside/resolved/returned/ended phases over ordinary commands,
discovery gating, approach choices, withdrawal, repeatable extraction after
another attempt, treatment and an explicitly labelled lethal branch. It owns no
second inventory, timer, RNG or reward dispatcher.

**Failure/recovery/persistence, CHECKED at fixture scope.** Structured tests
reject phase skips and unknown discovery, prevent repeated extraction from the
same attempt, allow another supplied attempt after treatment, distinguish
unconsciousness from true death, preserve pending work and reject durable-save
failure without live publication. Drainage tests cover finite stock, support
custody, knowledge, attended learning and weak-body effort. These are synthetic
headless/source journeys, not fresh-life native progression or browser proof.

**Depth gap, INFERRED.** The useful next expansion is a book-world place whose
discovery gives one real choice, whose output changes an existing life, and
whose failure has recoverable aftermath. More random room names would not close
that gap. Generic metadata currently assumes at least two approaches and an
injury/treatment structure. Those are the contract of this descriptor, not a
world law requiring two buttons and a medical loop at every place. Keep simpler
sites simple and use bespoke existing verbs where they fit.

**Reference gap:** no source-qualified book location, map edition or renewal law
was selected by this audit. Before building, use the named world's existing
source owner to settle access, what a lead actually tells the character, what
can be extracted or learned, and why it renews or stays depleted. No new map,
site ownership, faculty or resurrection should be inferred from a genre label.

## Deep pass B: body, conditions and bounded ecology

**Foundations and truth, SHOWN.** Native health/stamina, simulation condition
records, body facts, faculty activation and knowledge are distinct owners.
`body_progression.js` has real training/recovery and visible lasting changes;
`bodyFacts` derives tender hands from unexpired conditions. A permanent acquired
mark survives condition expiry. `bodyFacultyActive` checks the saved and current
body identity/form, owner/controller and faculty state for its selected reader.
This does not automatically bind every generic condition to a body instance.

**Producers and consumers, SHOWN.** Drills spend stamina and grant actual XP;
tender hands change work duration; trained grip supplies capabilities used by
existing work; visible demonstrations create observer knowledge without
inventing ancestry. Status care projects selected conditions into native stat
effects and rain accumulation. `book_care_facts.js` requires explicit Paradise
visits and same-native-body support and offers a narrow carried-grip consumer;
it does not silently widen every old care effect. Worker care uses existing
people, independent native bodies and controller appointments.

**Stages and failure/recovery, CHECKED for body/habitat; SHOWN for care adapters.**
The body suite checks drill cancellation, earned conditioning, temporary injury
recovery, lasting marks, observer knowledge, actual work-time consequences,
failed publication and body-dependent weathered-skin expression. Batched and
reloaded recovery agree. The habitat suite checks paid ownership, actual feed
and attention, partial supported-time pause/resume, bounded yield, serialization
and failed-publication rollback. The care/Paradise adapters were source read,
not independently exercised in this six-file run.

**Ecology boundary, SHOWN.** `habitat_care.js` projects a specific sheltered
owned animal's output while actual feed and attention overlap. Missing support
pauses production; it does not enact an unchosen death/neglect simulation.
`harvest.js` separately records specimen damage, time-based freshness,
integrity/completeness, skill/tool contributions and finite processing routines.
Neither example establishes food webs, disease transmission, seasonal breeding
or regional resource recovery. Those need a selected use and world evidence,
not a universal ecology backlog. Making/material-process details remain with
the parent owner.

**Depth gap, INFERRED.** The most consequential extension is an explicit
carrier/expression decision for one selected book-world harm or recovery route.
Ask of that route: what was harmed, what means detect it, what the treatment
repairs, what still limits function at full HP, and what happens when the body,
world support or pending work changes. An actor-keyed record can be sufficient
for a fixed body; a new cross-body route may need an owner-bound identity or
explicit transition. No generalized migration is recommended without that case.

**Reference gap:** this pass establishes no new clinical, biological or canon
healing rule. Per-world flesh, magical injury, mental harm, foreign power and
construct repair differences need the corresponding source/law and existing
body owner. An HP healing example or LOTM extraordinary condition is not
interchangeable merely because both show a health bar.

## Deep pass L: learning, knowledge and useful records

**Foundations and truth, SHOWN.** Rat-step separates observation, understanding,
proficiency, ownership and current executability. Guide analysis and self-study
reach the same understanding by different paid means; a physical manual and
completed ordinary reading are actual requirements of self-study. Practice
changes proficiency; the route consumer reads execution/mastery. It is a
bounded technique example, not a general every-skill progression schema.

**Diagnosis and recovery, CHECKED.** The clearance-manual family provides a
particularly useful existing model: an attempt can identify a specific missing
comparison, produce study fatigue, and stop before learning. Rest or expiry
allows comparison; paid guidance can prepare directly; separate reading learns
the procedure. Losing the copy after learning does not erase the procedure,
while a later reference-supported job still needs its actual copy. The suite
checks held stock, worn tools, costs, teacher effort, support loss, cancellation,
pending reload, stale requests, mutation rejection and failed durable save.
These are synthetic supplied-stock/body fixtures, not proof of acquisition pace.

**Teaching and use, SHOWN.** `world_completion_learning_runtime.js` freezes the
accepting body and sample/measurement/teacher causes into jobs, requires
successful common completion before recording evidence, and stamps physical
output with its cause. Player and a selected NPC participate through incumbent
jobs and inventory. This is a strong source example of causal teaching and
physical evidence, but it is local datum work rather than an established shared
learner resolver for all book worlds. Its full runtime suite was not run here.

**Knowledge versus feedback, SHOWN.** `world_knowledge.js` reveals authored
world identity through held facts, with unknown identity in old state rather
than automatic disclosure. `recent_learning.js` reports actual XP awarded in
the current session and deliberately is not saved. A session XP row is not a
durable lesson record, causal proof or away-time recap. `observation_channels.js`
has its own activation/sample/notice/identity and receipt mechanisms; this audit
does not certify every channel's play route.

**Depth gap, INFERRED.** Existing learning is deeper than its simplest XP view.
The missing broader proof is a selected book-world skill going through a useful
outside application with its own source, body/tool support and feedback. The
same-day dojo proposal already owns one combat version of that idea. A fitting
spell, investigation method or practical lesson may use fewer stages where
the mechanism supports it. Do not turn every skill into a compulsory sequence
or require a teacher where testing, observation or self-study can work.

**Reference gap:** the pack's local authored lesson is not proof of a canon
syllabus, exact training duration, institutional certification or universal
transfer. Existing `book_hp_school_extension.js` explicitly labels its assessed
repair tasks original proposals, separates means/mastery from assessment, and
gives the resulting pane to a real recipient. Credit that work before proposing
another HP classroom continuation. Source review should resolve one actual
teaching/access/transfer uncertainty, not reread every novel to add one lesson.
