# char-fidelity: Characters: named cast, crowds, rivals, companions, their state over time / source fidelity, original-content boundaries, sealed secrets and attribution

1. Source fidelity drift in long-term state
Named characters may evolve in ways that contradict the original source material as time passes without explicit checks. A builder would compare current character traits against a frozen snapshot of source canon at regular intervals. M

2. Original content boundary leaks
New companion behaviors might inadvertently mimic copyrighted expressions from source games or books. A builder would scan generated dialogue and actions for unique phrase patterns found in protected works. L

3. Sealed secret integrity failure
Plot twists involving rivals or companions could be revealed to the player through unintended data exposure. A builder would audit all save files and UI logs to ensure secret flags remain opaque until triggered. M

4. Attribution gaps for hybrid characters
Companions blending original and source elements may lack clear credit to their inspirations. A builder would verify that every hybrid character entry lists its source lineage in the metadata. S

5. Crowd behavior source inconsistency
Background NPCs might act in ways that defy the logic of their source world during idle phases. A builder would test crowd reactions against source-defined social norms during random events. M

6. Rival state persistence issues
Rivalries may reset or degrade when saving and loading across sessions, breaking narrative continuity. A builder would track rival relationship values through multiple save load cycles to confirm stability. S

7. Companion loyalty decay mechanics
Companions might lose loyalty without clear feedback, leading to sudden departures that feel unfair. A builder would review the decay formula and ensure decay rates are visible or hinted to the player. M

8. Source fidelity in idle actions
Characters performing idle animations or background tasks might violate the physics or magic rules of their source world. A builder would cross-reference idle action logs with source world constraint files. L

9. Attribution in UI tooltips
Tooltips describing character origins may omit source titles or authors when mixing original and canon content. A builder would inspect all character tooltips for complete source citations. S

10. Sealed secret leak via AI
AI-driven companion dialogue might accidentally reference plot points that are supposed to remain hidden. A builder would run automated tests where AI is prompted to reveal secrets and check for leaks. L

11. Original content in named casts
Newly created named characters might lack distinct personalities, making them feel like generic placeholders. A builder would evaluate new character profiles for unique trait combinations and backstory depth. M

12. Crowd source fidelity in events
Mass events involving crowds might ignore source-specific cultural or historical constraints. A builder would simulate large-scale events and check crowd composition against source demographics. L
