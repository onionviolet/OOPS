## 1. Overall direction

### Recommendation, within one page

Build **a useful canon-to-capability desk**, then use it to choose and finish foundation work. The atlas should let Weibao recognize something from a book, see what OOPS can actually do with it, and move its next missing part into an owned task. That directly serves the provisional [goal](../reviews/OOPS_GOAL_DISCOVERY_2026-11-24.md): satisfying growth, new possibilities and visible creative progress. A giant graph or a complete encyclopedia is not the first success criterion.

**O1, first:** let the active provenance/codex work land through its owners. Current untracked `prototypes/world-atlas/codex.mjs` already supplies scoped keys, aggregates, pages of at most 100 rows and separate evidence fields. Review and extend that work; do not commission another ingestion layer. Use Gu as the first actionable catalogue and LOTM potions as the contrasting case. A canon entity and a coverage case are different objects: connect source observations, catalogue identities and mechanism rows rather than declaring every ledger line a wiki article.

**O2, alongside O1:** build the design kit from the real components, in Graphite, with three contrasting specimens: a Gu record, a potion preparation and a living creature disclosure. Keep shared controls, meanings and typography; let composition follow the mechanism. A shared fold is useful where it shares actual behavior, not because every thing needs body/thoughts/appetite slots. The kit should expose blocked, unknown and changing states, not just attractive full panels. Neither a kit-wide refactor nor removal of all style debt is a prerequisite to a useful atlas page.

**O3, next:** finish one gap-to-proof cycle through the atlas. Diagnose a Gu blocker using existing facts first, qualify a named source join, draft a case or owner handoff, execute the relevant engine check and show the changed evidence back on the same row. Include one unresolved or rejected mapping so the route demonstrates honest uncertainty. This is a better platform pilot than Watchcraft: it exercises the foundations problem. Watchcraft remains an optional taste check for the immediate progress loop, not a dependency.

**O4, then:** resume foundation waves from the reconciled current checkpoint. Prefer mechanisms needed by an existing lead's day, fight or making loop, while retaining OR1's engine-only breadth exception. Report engine outcome/route fit separately from reachable player actions, ordinary-play observations and persistence. Pick by qualified capability value and consumer need, not raw blocked-row totals. Make a useful improvement visible without resuming story windows prematurely or inventing a Village-first requirement.

**O5, expand after the pilot:** add a blocker work queue, bounded relation canvas, unlock/first-use lens and receipt comparison. Keep the current atlas camera for aggregates; share pure game components and small figure previews rather than coupling the whole atlas to the game's shell. The unusual option worth retaining is a wiki with almost no graph until a relation question needs one. A renderer rewrite and all-world skins first lose to this sequence because neither proves a faster route from a book possibility to working behavior.

## 3. Atlas as a wiki and developer platform

### The data contract

Use one **addressable record page with several evidence tabs**, not a single progress badge. The durable key for coverage is `(ledger path, row.id)`; an entity gets its own existing game/reader identity and can link many cases. Preserve many-to-many joins, aliases, editions and observations at different story times. A join stores its basis (explicit ID, reviewed source match, inferred candidate), source locator and input revision. Conflicting observations stay visible; uncertain matches are not silently merged.

The current `codex.mjs` is a useful first adapter: aggregate counts, SHA-based inputs, composite keys and bounded pages exist in source. Its `moduleReachable`, `playerAction` and `persistence` fields are currently null rather than inferred, which is appropriate. However, `parseRows()` silently skips malformed JSON. The platform should display validator errors and a partial-data flag, not present the remaining rows as complete. This is a proposed follow-up for that file's owner, not a fix in this lane.

Keep evidence as independent dimensions: source qualification; entity binding; case execution and basis; static module reachability; reachable player command; observed use; persistence; UI receipt; installed candidate; optional human response. Each observation needs candidate/input identity, time, route/scenario, command and result. Missing means **not recorded**, distinct from failed, stale, explicitly deferred or not applicable. A `runs-canon` row proves none of the later dimensions by itself.

Show the ingredients, not an unexplained precision score. A high-count blocker may lose to a small prerequisite enabling an actual loop. Include “not selected yet” and “none by design” so the atlas does not turn the whole canon into a compulsory backlog.

### One gap to a draft and back to proof

**P1 · Select and diagnose.** On Gu kinds, open a rank/effect-unknown row. Show the source observation, catalogue match candidates, blocker note and current case. Read held evidence before requesting new reading. The developer chooses a scoped task such as binding an existing qualified rank, not “implement all Gu.” Capture IDs and input hashes. A concrete candidate is the `ice-blade` rank binding identified in the concurrent [Gu blocker sample](GU_UNKNOWN_BLOCKERS_SAMPLE_2026-11-24.md): that report says the chapter tier already records its rank. Recheck the cited identity/evidence before using it; this lane did not reread the novel or approve the binding.

**P2 · Draft in the owning shape.** Produce a scratch join/case/pack candidate or a lane brief. A case names its existing mechanism, source basis, `expect` world change, normal input and a meaningful failure/near miss. The brief names owner files, consumer or engine-only purpose, test and proof route. Missing source facts remain explicit. The atlas can populate known fields; inference stays marked for review.

**P3 · Validate and hand off.** Use the actual owner validator/compiler, then run the selected case and relevant focused test. Existing coverage `--check` validates rows but does not replay every case. Adoption rechecks file hashes; a concurrent change yields a conflict/diff for its owner rather than an overwrite. Runtime changes go through the owning lane. New source parsing does not execute embedded commands.

**P4 · Prove at the appropriate level.** Engine-only work reports case/held-out results and the intended consumer. A player slice adds the reachable action, visible cost/refusal/result, rebuilt fresh-origin browser receipt and affected save/reload/export/import under AD43. `scripts/effect_landing_count.mjs --start <start> --minutes <N> --actions <N>` can measure a scoped ordinary-play sample; its seed/policy/budget travel with the result. It is not a complete playability census. The script's default mode writes a shared receipt, so it is not a background refresh action.

**P5 · Refresh the same record.** The owner/coordinator adopts the reviewed change under current authorization, records its receipt and refreshes the atlas snapshot. Invalidate affected evidence when its relevant inputs change; do not invalidate the entire project for an unrelated file edit. The row shows before/after evidence, remaining gaps, its current queue owner and what now works. Preserve search, selected ID, camera and folds. A disappeared row becomes an explicit removed/renamed result, not a blank panel.

The first pilot is complete when a person can traverse P1–P5 on one real row and inspect one unresolved sibling without a false “playable” claim. No new universal process gate is implied.

### Additional aspects the note does not settle

| Aspect | Proposed treatment and reason |
|---|---|
| Identity and wiki completeness | Distinguish entity, observation, variant, capability and case; a single potion can have several observations/cases. Ledger totals measure coverage rows, not all things in the world. |
| Evaluation contamination | Mark tuning versus held-out membership. The workbench must not silently turn a failing held-out case into training data and continue calling the score held out. |
| Concurrent drafts | Save drafts separately from snapshots, with base hashes and owner reservations; refresh does not discard an unfinished candidate. Show a stale-base conflict before adoption. |
| Failed/partial refresh | Keep the last valid snapshot with its age; show malformed/missing inputs and failed jobs. A zero count after a failed supplier is not an empty world. |
| Tool execution | Use named bounded jobs with fixed arguments, cancellation, logs and status. Opening a source/row is read-only; an “inspect” click should not start a long test or mutate generated reports. |
| Information boundaries | Produce separate developer and observer-safe player projections before delivery. Do not ship private book text or developer secrets and hide them with CSS. Private local source links can stay local. |
| Performance | Lazy pages and aggregate graph nodes first; measure load, memory, pan/zoom and refresh growth on the intended hardware. Reuse the preceding audit's proposed envelope as a test starting point, not a permanent product cap. |
| Platform usefulness | Measure time to find the correct owner, false/stale gaps, draft rework and completed gap-to-proof cycles. More rows or nodes alone do not show that development became easier. |
| Transfer into play | Export supported identities and projections to Known things through its actual suppliers. An atlas import is not discovery, inventory possession, a learned ability or a player-save migration. |

### What must not be automated by inference

**B1 · Authority:** an atlas diagnosis does not accept a design, assign another writer's files, commit/push/deploy, or authorize paid reads. These actions require their actual session authority. A future explicit authorization can change the workflow; the UI should not infer one from a green badge.

**B2 · Canon and proof:** matching names does not establish identity; reader output does not establish an AD49 anchor; static imports do not establish play; stored receipts do not certify new inputs. Preserve uncertainty rather than auto-promoting any of these.

**B3 · User state and privacy:** preview/validation does not mutate a live save, publish private sources or disclose developer truth in play. Test fixtures and scratch candidates have explicit boundaries. Runtime adoption remains an owned operation.
