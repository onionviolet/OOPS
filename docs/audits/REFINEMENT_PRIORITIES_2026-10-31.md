## Ranked proposals

### A1. Finish a connected lead-life day and its recovery

Make the next runtime slice an ordinary, repeatable chain: a fresh lead starts with a legible immediate situation, reaches a person or place, learns or obtains something, uses it in a source-supported action, sees an effect, and can save and return. Include one blocked or interrupted action with an explanation and an alternative. Use the selected route's existing systems, rather than making every below-floor lane a prerequisite.

Why now: the [fresh-player walk](UI_CAPABILITY_GAPS_2026-10-30.md) found an empty-seeming lead opening and missing orientation. The [floor ledger](../gm/dispatch/sonnet-wave-2026-10-30/LEDGER.md#floor-wave-o1) shows divination can resolve an unsupported question as unclear, but the supported answer was seeded and no in-run route earned its grounds in the tested opening. The same ledger records the unresolved death/restart and travel-halt probes. Before building around death or a timed journey, reproduce the relevant failure on current inputs; finish the selected slice through its actual consumer and persistence route. The first supported divination case need not be forced into an earlier chapter that cannot supply its grounds.

Account for now: waiting, opening hours, scheduled meetings and travel quotes must agree; the world can advance independently, but distance should have a per-world impact rather than dictate quality of play. A return should say what changed, what is due, and where action is possible without inventing knowledge. Exit, reload and literal Import belong in the slice when its saved state changes. The [floor ledger](../gm/dispatch/sonnet-wave-2026-10-30/LEDGER.md) and [time representation](../representations/TIME_AND_CHRONICLE.md) own the details.

### A2. Make the world knowable without making everyone omniscient

Use the same event or action to join place, calendar, person, knowledge, relationships and reports. A person can learn from witnessing, being told or finding evidence; memory and trust should affect a later response where that information matters. Give the player ways to investigate an uncertain claim and show known, guessed and unknown as distinct states.

Concrete correctness candidate: [known_things.js](../../src/known_things.js) constructs its places from `location.isUnlocked()`, while its own description promises places already met. The [October 30 walk, CG2](UI_CAPABILITY_GAPS_2026-10-30.md#gaps) found that this can list unmet places across worlds. Check the route on the current build and use a visited/known source appropriate to each world; decide migration/compatibility if a new saved record is needed. The [relationship web](../representations/RELATIONSHIP_WEB.md) also records a who-knows view with no registered live source in its round-3 receipt. Join existing owners instead of duplicating a separate social truth.

Later: a doubt ledger could make a guess actionable by showing a known way to check it; rumour trails and character sheets can deepen the same system. These are opportunities, not a requirement for A1. Knowledge is observer-relative, so protect future events and sealed discoveries across search, map, dialogue, notifications and save/return.

### A3. Give the Luminous interface useful, distinctive forms

Continue the blue Luminous Gothic direction with the existing multi-panel desktop workspace. Make each surface answer its main question at a glance, offer a concise reason for unusual states, and keep full explanations reachable by click or keyboard. Say a fact once; do not turn every category into the same glowing box. Preserve readable names and full text access as visuals become more compact.

The [gallery audit](GALLERY_FORM_AUDIT_2026-10-30.md#what-is-generic-in-codes) measured repeated text, nested frames and weak hierarchy. Its [housing comparison](../representations/evidence/housing-variants/housing-variants-composite.png) makes the tradeoff visible: H1 has clear labelled slots; H2 feels more like a place but hides some meaning in hover; H3 reads status quickly but hides which fitting is missing. Proposed default: H1's named slots plus H3-style status only when useful; retain the H2 idea for a world where the form serves a decision. This is a design proposal, not a selected user preference.

For travel, consider a compact departure list **alongside** the journey map: the former answers time and availability, the latter location and discovery. The [comparison](../representations/evidence/places-variants/walks-composite.png) shows why a list alone would lose spatial context. Remaining variant families cover time, progression, body/making and fights ([queue](../gm/dispatch/ui-refactor-wave-2026-10-29/GALLERY_FORM_AUDIT_BRIEF.md#round-2-queue-other-aspects-with-the-same-gap-weibao-2026-10-30-we-need-to-consider-similar-runs-like-this-but-for-other-aspects-too-that-have-similar-gaps)). Choose forms by actual information need and live data, then prove the selected live surface in the rebuilt app. Avoid making every proposed variant a prerequisite for A1.

### A4. Finish progression in context, then exercise a different world

Mount the existing pathway card in the actual lead-life view when its owner can do so. Connect known requirements and real actions, keep unknown details hidden, and remove duplicate facts only when the shared view demonstrably replaces them. The current [checkpoint](../gm/dispatch/book-ui-wave-2026-10-30/PATHWAY_CARD_CHECKPOINT.md) notes sparse authored routes, higher-rung stubs and proof-page-only evidence. Weibao selected the card, ladder and journey map; their quality still needs a live implementation.

Make the next distinct-world slice serve its own play. A Harry Potter school interaction could join learning, practice, a real cast, a meaningful fizzle and consequences; the [HP lane](../gm/dispatch/book-ui-wave-2026-10-30/ideas/bk-hp.md) says its fizzle is displayed as planned but does not yet draw, and flags pack/save compatibility. Mother of Learning offers another useful check: its [access lane](../gm/dispatch/book-ui-wave-2026-10-30/ideas/bk-mol.md) can display an expired pass while its host still checks possession, so the privilege and retained skill can disagree. These are alternative next slices after the selected current slice, not gates on all future worlds.

Account for now: progression includes skill, knowledge, gear, relationships and resources, and can be a ladder, several branches or no rank at all. Share only the underlying mechanisms that actual worlds use; let each world select its systems, wording and representation ([AD46](../USER_VISION.md#each-world-chooses-its-own-systems-and-their-ui-standing-rule-2026-10-30), [world examples](../representations/PROGRESSION_BY_WORLD.md)). Path switching and lives outside the lead remain long-term directions, while present data should not hardcode their impossibility.

### A5. Deepen consequences where they change a real choice

Carry the existing combat work into an earned encounter: the player can read what is known, compare a meaningful move, see a hit or injury change the next action, and try to withdraw or get help. A wounded hand should shift accuracy, force or speed by severity; an extreme injury may block what the body cannot perform. Tie damage types to relevant protection and healing, companions to their goals and orders, and witnessed events to social memory where applicable. Keep idle and more involved fights as options when they fit the world.

Evidence: the [lead-life guide](../LEAD_LIFE_GUIDE.md#6-implementation-order-and-cost) records fixture-seeded road-contest proofs for tempo combat, allies, hit location and withdrawal; the [combat receipt](../representations/COMBAT.md#round-3-receipt-2026-10-30-implemented-below-floor) still identifies a seeded route. The goal is an earned, connected encounter with legible aftermath, not simply more damage fields or a universal combat model. Reuse the existing health, body, combat and memory owners.
