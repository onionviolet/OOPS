- **G15. Built but unreachable** (supervisor-confirmed). `src/away_ledger.js` and `src/anchor_snapshots.js` have tests, but nothing in `src/` imports them. The time bank and the anchors therefore don't exist for the player.
  **Resolution (2026-10-30, agent-verified, not human-reviewed).**
  - *Away ledger: wired.* `src/away_bank_domain.js` (save slice `awayBank` v1) calls `computeAway` on load and banks the real time since the last save as game minutes at one per tick, capped at 7 days with the excess dropped and reported. Nothing is applied on return (AD16, AD38). The player spends 60 minutes at a time from the "While you were away" row; the native clock runs through `fastForward`, stops at waiting choices, and only the minutes it actually consumed are debited. Capacity and the 60-minute batch are proposals, and `ports.capacity()` is the AD18 improvement hook. Compatibility: a save without the slice starts at an empty bank, so old saves never get a retroactive bank. The last-seen time is the save's own `saved_at` stamp, so the slice holds no wall time and byte-equal save checks and the durable writer's unchanged-bytes skip still work. Importing an old file credits the time since its `saved_at`. Patient Furnace rule: while its routine is assigned the Furnace accrues that wall time, so the ledger only rebases. Proof: `tests/away_bank_domain.test.js`, `scripts/away_bank_browser.cjs` (1920x1080, fresh origin, away, return, spend, save, reload, literal import).
  - *Anchors: waits, nothing wired.* `anchor_snapshots.js` derives world facts from an authored event graph at a time (with branch overrides and a knowledge ledger). It does not capture or restore player state, so it cannot be the CA4/CA7 rewind checkpoint (initial fallback plus one replaceable checkpoint). bk-mol does not use it: its restart consumes the L9 restart model and `loop_table.js`. Unblock condition: a world ships an authored event graph (`events` and `anchors`) that a runtime consumer needs derived at a time, and that consumer calls `deriveSnapshot` or `deriveIncremental` directly, with no wrapper. Separate gap, not built on this module: a player save-checkpoint store for CA7, which needs its own persistence decision.

## UI at 1920

This section draws on a headless pass at 1920x1080 using about 235 screenshots. It covered a fresh Village life, a fresh Tingen life, a populated save and the figure gallery. Full tables are in [ui_cov.md](gap-audit-2026-10-30/ui_cov.md), with findings F1-F12 and proposals COV1-COV15.

- **U1. Almost nothing is drawn, and what is drawn is circles.** On a fresh life, 0 of 48 Village panels and 0 of 40 Tingen panels contain a figure. On a populated save, 2 of 57 views do. Every drawn view is a node-link graph: a radial web, a rail or a hub map. Across the gallery's 45 sections, 72% of the drawn shapes are circles and none are rects. The forms the view docs themselves prefer have no gallery home. Those are cards, tables, timeline bands, chip rows and bars with a number.
- **U2. Walls of text.** 25 of 57 views run over 80 words and 10 over 200. The worst are Effects & sound (877 words, 2,900 px tall), the Tingen workroom Here tab (26 sentence-long buttons, 369 words), Vows and trials (351), and Standing and ledger (233, which should be a table).
- **U3. Balance.** The three side columns take 1,615 of 1,920 px, leaving 721 px for an opened panel. The inventory column widens by 290 px and still shows 2 rows. The Reports column holds 3 lines for the first hour.
- **U4. Dead tabs (bug).** Journal, Data and Knows open a 0x0 panel on fresh lives. This reproduces on a clean copy of HEAD. Routed to ui-defects.
- **U5. Duplication.** Five views answer "where am I". Condition and Inventory each appear in five places.
- **U6. Labels.** Four buttons have no name, 48 disclosure toggles are bare, and disabled controls give no reason.

The ranked fixes are COV1-COV15: dead tabs, reclaiming width, turning text into tables, non-circle forms, merging the place views, label and empty-state passes, a paper-and-ink second skin, and the read models behind gated figures. COV1 went to ui-defects. COV2-COV15 went to the General UI coordinator's round.
