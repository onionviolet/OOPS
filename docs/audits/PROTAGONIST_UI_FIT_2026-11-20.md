## Which rules help

- **UR93 and UR94** give the strongest starting point. Use a table when comparing several mouths to feed, an ordered list when swapping two operations changes a pane, and a target with a preparation clock when a threat creates a response window. Reuse focus, type, disclosure, time formatting and cancellation. [Distinct surfaces](../UI_RULES.md#distinct-reactive-and-pleasing-surfaces)
- **UR72, UR157 and UR175** answer what deserves space. Show what the actor can observe where it changes the decision. Early identification or a precise hidden success roll would give Fang Yuan knowledge he has yet to earn. Once settled, the spend and the specimen's identity are fair to show. [Descriptions and knowledge](../UI_RULES.md)
- **UIV1, UR100 and UR102** catch omissions that a successful click misses. “Can't submit” may mean missing materials, no ordered steps, a running job or missing instruction. Put the applicable reason beside the control. A read-only inventory count needs different state coverage from a command. [Element sheet](../UI_RULES.md#every-element-the-element-sheet)
- **UR166 and UR167** cause trouble when a familiar shape becomes the goal. A reserve meter suits quantities with a known scale. A missed opportunity, an unknown cause and a change of custody each need their own words and placement. Give a fight's timing and exits the room the encounter demands. **UR158** prevents duplicate controls. It still permits the inventory to answer “what do I have?” while the result answers “what did I spend?” [Cohesion rules](../UI_RULES.md)
- **UR94 and UR176** test visual identity and fit beside neighbours. A reader can identify Hogwarts with labels covered and still fail to repair glass. Pair that review with an uncoached task. Use a developer replay for correctness, and a fresh reader for discovery and preference. [UI rules](../UI_RULES.md)

Use a competing layout when both versions plausibly improve the decision.

In the Infested Field capture the opponent, its HP and attack timing sit below that place, with area progress and the return route underneath. **D1 · Proposed trial.** Retain a compact cross-panel line for place, time, running work and a consequential interruption. Give critical read-only/save warnings priority. Move routine suggestions to their owning activity and keep planned events in the day view. Give a focused activity a stable subject header with one changing fact that affects the player's decision. At the academy that could be “Wild Gu · refining” with time left. Let the same specimen become the named result on settlement. At the forge the header should identify the actual workpiece and selected part. Reports can keep recording outcomes while the player inspects either. Stable placement, tab edges and familiar controls can unify these panels even with equally strong borders. Keep player layout choices. A broad chrome or palette rewrite would leave the same information-placement problem.
