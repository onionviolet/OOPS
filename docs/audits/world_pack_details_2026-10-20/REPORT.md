## More to consider, with dispositions

**ACT NOW, done:** corrected current owners and evidence labels, named-formula framing, desktop targets, first-slice versus later-depth routing, and the private HP locator/spelling records. The primary lesson from worker review is to keep canon assertions separate from original scenario fields, rather than requiring primary evidence for authored invention.

**ACCOUNT FOR NOW:** a report describes a target/lot at an observation time, not permanent truth. Knowledge can survive expiration of permission, destruction of a recipe-bearing item or return of a tool; the physical item, learned method, usage right and recipient's belief have different owners. Time effects follow the selected instance clock, so a frozen absence is not automatically a missing season or decay bug. A substitute works through its actual mechanism, not through an allowed-material list. A change of world is not by itself a reason to degrade a learned method or manufacture a new source.

**LATER / DELEGATE:** select one pair of operations where these distinctions affect play, rather than build universal infrastructure for all packs. Examples remain with their owners: sample/retest at Fallout, ring study/custody at Skyrim, paid operator reserve at Naruto, site calibration at Proto23, delayed care/news at HP, report valuation at Paradise. Level/effort and done checks are local; parked packs remain parked. These are proposals, not new implementation mandates.
