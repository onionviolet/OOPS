# Card-world Workbench trial

Preflight, AD36 / AD38 / AD43 / DSG5 / UR70–UR75. Requested: a small playable comparison of ordinary and +1 starting land against identical opponents, ward costs, collection, orders and observed dialogue. This is a hypothetical balance proposal in the developer Atlas, separate from the native Quiet Faculty slice. Numbers are authored trial values, not source facts or accepted world rules.

Home: Workbench → Card-world trial, desktop 1366×900. Shared Atlas sheets, controls and typography; mobile deferred. Seam: one independent browser/Node module owns the deterministic state model and view; narrow index/serve registration. No main-game combat, private corpus, default game build or save format changes. Voice sample: the existing Atlas says “Finding linked records…”; this trial uses direct outcome and cost text.

Mechanism: one action settles one turn, then land refreshes energy. Ordinary starts with one land; comparison starts with two. A two-energy spillway move is immediately affordable in the latter. The comparison can instead select one extra opening energy without extra land; it then refreshes to one energy. The Warden's own printed ward is 999; its maintained aura gives each teammate ward 3. Dislodging costs one plus the actual target's ward, so targeting the Warden costs 1000 and an ally costs 4 while the source remains. The caravan can also cross by operating a spillway and using a found side-gate key; these effects do not target opponents. Opponents do not scale to the start.

Collection: searching earns one concrete key. Spend it to open the side gate, or trade it for two land and pursue the guarded road; its use changes the later choices. Orders are explicitly selected key-route, trade-and-clear, or bounded repeats with an energy ceiling. Each step revalidates cost, target, key custody and encounter state. Stop retains settled moves and spending. The eight-turn nightfall ends an unfinished encounter. Observed dialogue follows resolved actions; it grants no hidden knowledge.

Failure checks: 999 energy must fail against a 999-ward source whose effect costs one; no spending on failed actions; removing the source ends its maintained ally tax; collecting twice or spending one key twice fails; budget and unavailable-repeat orders stop without advancing; manual and ordered play agree. Rejected defaults: a separate combat engine and automatic enemy scaling add work without answering this short encounter; an empty collection counter would not change play.
