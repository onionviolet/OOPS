# Village patrol and Watchcraft

The normal Village entry loaded a schema 1 career with four completed patrols, four returned kits and no promotion. This fixture lets the player ask Tessa for the first promotion immediately. Growth initially showed one role. Two idle ticks left the second slot empty. The player chose Watchcraft, studied with Tessa, then rehearsed during Village meditation. A fifth completed patrol paid 65 coins. Returning its kit made the Mentor promotion available.
