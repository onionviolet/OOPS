# Potion bench visual spec

Target is the ordinary Potioneering panel at 1920×1080, using the game's default Gothic look. The bench stays in the expanded center with its Back control and the Reports column.

The vessel has a narrow neck, sloping glass shoulders and a square table rule beneath it. An empty or unconfirmed vessel has no assigned liquid colour. Stir marks form an ordered line of small rectangular direction markers, with text available to read the sequence. Dark navy and plum panels give the ledger and glass distinct materials without adding a second heading or pretending the exercise has more controls.

The work surface covers ready, blocked, in progress and handed in. The player sees bench supplies, purse and entry fee before choosing how to prepare the bean. Blocked controls use their live quote. The record keeps the addition, stroke order and observed cues on reload. Hand-in replaces the active flask with the last assessed sample and allows a fresh attempt.
