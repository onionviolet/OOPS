# Village growth points and ability loadout, 2026-11-27

The runs load the rebuilt bundle from the normal game page at fresh local origins. The Village fixture starts in the existing Shack with Sleeping at 999 XP and health and stamina missing. All growth and choices are earned through ordinary gameplay after load. The test clock advances time without touching the save or dispatching choices.

## SM1

Sleep crosses level one. Reports shows an earned recovery point and links to the one Growth queue. Sleep progresses with the point banked. Allocate it to Deep rest for up to four additional health per sleep minute. The same minute can restore up to one less stamina, scaled to the extra healing. The Village can reset the allocation at an available bed.

Both sizes record the offer and the actual Reports effect. Save, reload, Export and Import preserve the earned pool and allocation. [SM1 proof](proof-sm1.json)

## SM5

The same level crossing makes the sleep ability slot available in Growth. Ordinary sleep is the default. Choosing Steady breath instead of Gentle position trades the option for extra health recovery for a stamina effect. Growth shows a 12 game minute swap. Sleeping and XP continue with Ordinary sleep while it waits. After the deadline, Reports records Steady breath recovering three extra stamina while sleeping.

The slot resolves from the saved game clock, so a jump and the same elapsed time in steps use the same deadline. Both sizes preserve the settled slot after Save, reload, Export and Import. [SM5 proof](proof-sm5.json)
