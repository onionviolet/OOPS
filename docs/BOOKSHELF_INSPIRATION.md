# Bookshelf research: powers, people and connected campaigns

Research date: 2026-10-03, America/New_York. Status: source observations, design interpretation and original proposals. The request authorizes researching and absorbing these references; it does not select a book world, complete mechanic catalogue or implementation order.

## Scope and evidence

The three requested shelves were inspected in the signed-in Chrome browser: [TTKS](https://ttks.tw/novel/user/bookshelf_direct), [69shuba](https://www.69shuba.com/modules/article/bookcase.php), and [TWKAN](https://twkan.com/bookcase). TTKS and TWKAN showed the same 56 book identities; 69shuba showed 18 additional titles. This pass reviewed all 74 titles, opened 21 public synopses, and read chapters 5–6 of one copying-power reference. It is a bounded discovery audit, not a reading of 74 novels. Shelf presence does not establish favorite status, reading completion or endorsement of every motif.

Only titles, public source links and our summaries belong here. Account names, bookmark positions, shelf IDs, authentication tokens and chapter text are excluded. Site recommendations are separate from the user's shelf. Site genre labels and update dates are not treated as reliable descriptions of the story's mechanics or publication state. TWKAN's translated English shelf titles were checked against TTKS's Chinese titles.

Evidence levels: **P** = author/publication-platform premise inspected; **M** = synopsis inspected on the supplied mirror, with exact canon still requiring verification; **C** = a bounded chapter scene inspected on that mirror; **T** = title inspected only. An M or T reference can suggest an original design question without establishing its answer. Fanfiction's invented rules do not establish the canon of Naruto, Marvel, Warhammer or any other underlying setting.

[PROJECT.md](../PROJECT.md#books-and-original-worlds) owns adaptation policy. [Genre tropes](RESEARCH_GENRE_TROPES.md), [genre sweep](RESEARCH_GENRE_SWEEP.md), [powers](RESEARCH_LITRPG_SYSTEMS.md), [characters](CHARACTERS_AND_WORLD_EVENTS.md), [origins](RUN_DEFINING_ADVANTAGES.md) and [world journeys](DIRECTION_SYNTHESIS.md) retain their existing detail ownership. ## Main interpretation

The shelf supports a broader design vocabulary than a cultivation rank ladder: exceptional acquisition, overlapping histories, specialized collaborators, institutional power, strange conditional abilities, cultivation satire and worlds with complementary needs. This is an interpretation of the inspected premises, not a measured statement about the user's tastes or the prevalence of those tropes across fiction.

Preserve the pleasure of becoming exceptionally powerful. Some powers should give large, real advantages, and earned strength should make earlier obstacles easier. Limits can come from a particular source's operation, available information or a new kind of objective. Every gift does not need a curse, every older enemy does not need scaling, and every path does not need the same practice sequence or slot budget.

## Five ways to absorb the tropes

These are optional feature families, not five concurrent work orders. Familiar tropes can be played straight, reconstructed or subverted when the resulting choice warrants it.

| Family and related tropes | Proposed player experience | Deeper variant and campaign value |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Acquired powers with distinct rules.** Golden fingers, copying eyes/panels, stolen talents, inherited methods, body evolution, living techniques. B01–B03/B09–B11/B21. | Discover a source; identify what it can acquire; choose a target or acquisition route; use the new function in work, exploration or an encounter. An exceptional copy may grant competence immediately; another method may require reconstruction and practice. | Separate method, mastery, body compatibility and actor ownership. Copy can leave a donor intact; transfer or extraction can change them. An ordinary specialist can possess something worth acquiring. Do not add a compulsory drawback or practice bar to every gift. |
| **2. People who know a different story.** Regressors, false prophecies, unreliable systems, wrong timelines, hidden identities and misunderstood strength. B03–B05/B08/B15/B16. | A person makes a prediction or responds to a remembered event. Investigate through clues, a trial or conversation. Choosing a new intervention changes their next plan and what is still knowable. | The NPC has an agenda independent of being a player tutorial. Facts, claims and predictions remain separate. An NPC can be honestly wrong; suspicion can soften without erasing its origin. Repeated face-slapping does not have to replace a relationship arc. |
| **3. Power through institutions and useful roles.** Academies, sects, mentors, elite background, officials, patrons, cultivation capitalism and public recognition. B06/B08/B13/B14/B19. | Earn access through field competence, service, a sponsor or payment; use a real teacher/facility; decide how to handle an obligation or public dispute. A combatant, craftsperson and investigator can qualify differently. | Authority, actual capability, affiliation and reputation answer different questions. Some institutions can be helpful and competent. Rank and wealth may dominate a contest without solving every objective. A patron's help can be valuable without guaranteed betrayal or permanent debt. |
| **4. Progress through collaborators and projects.** Many selves, talented disciples, summons, followers, found family, a hidden master and inherited workshops. B07/B11–B13/B17/B18. | Assign known people to different paid jobs, gather materials, compare outcomes and build a project that opens another useful activity. A learner can later help the teacher in their own specialty. | Workers have means, schedules and interests. A copy need not be hostile; trust and pooling rules are authored. Automation should reduce repeated input while preserving choices about scarce capacity. Avoid making each character an extra mandatory maintenance meter. |
| **5. Worlds that change the strategy.** Portals, dimensional networks, game-becomes-real, alternate lives, industrial uplift, ruined civilizations, divine conflict and return journeys. B04/B07/B11/B12/B16–B19/B21. | Choose a destination for a resource, teacher, role or project; develop locally; return with something that changes the home campaign. A powerful portable ability should keep useful strength when its defined operation permits it. | Some worlds are alternate paths, others are higher tiers. Fuel, bodies, resources, information and local institutions can differ. Preserve relationships and projects on return. A mortal world can remain valuable through contacts or materials without inflating every inhabitant's level. |

The comedy also supplies texture: officious power loans, systems using the wrong vocabulary, a supposedly insignificant expert, an earnest institution with bizarre specialties, or a mundane trade unexpectedly solving a grand problem. Use short state-dependent lines and occasional scenes. Descriptions can hide the explanation of a mystery while keeping a commitment's known costs, duration and consequences legible. Self-aware humor should not make every NPC a genre commentator.

### 1. Acquisition, development and the identity of a power

B01/B02/B03/B09/B10/B11/B21 suggest asking what actually changes when an ability is obtained. A source may reveal a method, install practiced competence, alter a bodily faculty, recruit an independent being, grant authority over a domain or provide an object that executes an effect. These can coexist without becoming interchangeable inventory entries.

| Acquisition distinction | Proposed state and operation | Consequence that should be playable |
|---|---|---|
| **Procedure versus practiced command** | Store learned instructions separately from owned executable power and retained mastery. Teaching, reconstruction, observation and exceptional installation can settle different subsets. | Someone can explain a method they cannot currently perform. A trained teacher can correct timing; an exceptional source may install a useful amount of competence immediately. |
| **Faculty versus technique** | A body/energy/sensory source has its own acquisition and functional development; a technique can declare which faculty it needs. | Seeing a movement does not always reproduce its physiology. A source expressly able to acquire physiology can supply that missing function and visibly change the available action. |
| **Copy versus transfer/extraction** | Record donor, acquired revision, retained mastery policy and donor aftermath. Copy and transfer use different effects; destructive extraction additionally needs an authored loss/life policy. | A willing teacher retains their method after sharing. A true transfer removes the declared asset from the donor. Extraction can provoke a specific remembered grievance or witnessed response. |
| **Personal execution versus an independent holder** | An intelligent technique/artifact/companion gets a stable actor and its own means when its agency is real. Otherwise treat it as an ordinary power with contextual prose. | A living holder can offer instruction, disagree, remember a promise or perform a distinct task. Preferences change actual cooperation instead of supplying a renamed affection bar. |
| **Growth versus replacement** | Preserve the acquired origin and actual demonstrated progress. A new application, larger capacity, more efficient operation or bodily evolution can extend the same lineage. | A once-small skill remains recognizable after extraordinary growth. Refining it should not erase earned practice, while an explicitly consuming fusion can replace constituents coherently. |

Acquisition does not need one global sequence. An inherited manual can require restoration; a school can teach a reliable complete method; a copy source can supply useful mastery; a talent can awaken through an event; an authority can depend on office or local law. The player should be able to tell which route they are pursuing and what its known immediate result is. Different results may justify different commitments without being reduced to equivalent XP prices.

For a selected source, define its eligible targets, access method, observation requirement, cost or recovery policy, acquisition result and repeat behavior. Then define the acquired ability's execution, development, combination and retention separately. A copying eye's development is not the copied method's mastery. Its range or discrimination can improve while an acquired technique develops through its own local rules. A broad collector can eventually acquire multiple asset classes, but each class still needs its own transaction and materialization policy.

Do not infer physical item creation from a method-copy operation. A blueprint, an actual tool, a bodily adaptation and a building have different means and owners. Likewise a lucky source may change exposure, eligibility, outcome weights, guarantees or explicit rerolls; the word "luck" does not specify all of them. Preserve the earlier [RNG/source dossier](RNG_ENCOUNTERS_AND_SOURCE_DEVELOPMENT.md) and [identity/fate dossier](RIVALS_IDENTITIES_AND_FATE.md) as those policies' owners.

An original conditional ability can be strong because its predicate is unusual rather than because its numeric coefficient is small. Write down the relevant observer, distance, environment, timing or declared target property. Once a supported condition is met, its operation can be dependable. Test edge cases and one meaningful combination before creating a combinatorial catalogue. Literal loopholes can be intended rewards when the source promises them; unsupported tags or prose do not create new runtime effects.

### 2. Histories, beliefs, recognition and independent characters

B03/B04/B05/B08/B15/B16 motivate several distinct information sources: firsthand observation, a person's account, remembered prior history, a conditional forecast and a system assertion. Authorial truth answers what actually happened; the player's record answers what they have learned; an NPC's belief answers why that person acts. Those answers can disagree coherently.

| Information distinction | Original presentation | Proposed consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Observed fact versus report** | Record who supplied the report and when, and retain any later corroboration or contradiction. | Hearing a danger rumor opens an investigation lead; it does not reveal the hidden build of every enemy or update every faction's knowledge. |
| **Prior history versus the current life** | Preserve a remembered event with its history context. Show only the portions the speaker discloses or the player discovers. | A regressor may distrust the player before a current betrayal exists. A kept promise can change their present plan without deleting the earlier memory. |
| **Forecast versus scheduled occurrence** | A forecast declares its source and dependencies; an actual occurrence has its own committed identity and state. | Intervening can invalidate a prediction. A displayed expectation never guarantees a future reward or spends a chance draw. |
| **Actual strength versus reputation/appraisal** | Keep a coherent actual build private until a permitted observation or reveal exposes evidence. A public rank describes its own local scale. | An underestimated artisan may hold an excellent method. An elite title may provide institutional backing without proving superiority in every matchup. |
| **Similarity versus shared identity** | Copies, branch counterparts and impersonators retain distinct actor IDs and history provenance unless an explicit identity policy joins them. | A person can mistake the traveler for another counterpart. Proof, conversation or conduct can alter the belief, while possessions and rewards remain tied to their real owners. |

NPCs can want different outcomes from the same event. A peer may seek qualification, a teacher may preserve a tradition, a collector may seek access, a refugee may need safety, and an official may need accurate records. Cooperation, rivalry and obstruction follow those concrete goals. Some people can remain helpful, competent or trusting; every mentor does not require betrayal and every recurring rival does not require a fatal confrontation.

Give a recurring character continuity through changed means, relationships, availability and plans. A defeat, public demonstration, shared rescue or disputed share can create a causal memory. Later development must have an authored cause and enough time/resources, rather than instantly scaling to the player's latest level. A formidable opponent may remain formidable. An eventual overwhelming player victory can close one threat while opening a different institutional or personal decision.

Foreknowledge creates interesting asymmetry when it changes someone's priorities. It need not turn every interaction into a lie detector or let the player demand omniscient explanations. Offer observed mismatches and actionable leads first. Mystery can remain vague while an actual agreement states its known payment, timing, ownership and withdrawal consequences.

The scenario below needs no global reset. A later chosen rewind still requires a specific history snapshot, retention scope, dependent-cause restoration and RNG policy. Rebuilding a relationship from retained knowledge is a different result from retaining its former trust. The [character owner](CHARACTERS_AND_WORLD_EVENTS.md) retains directed memory and autonomous-situation design; [world/transition direction](DIRECTION_SYNTHESIS.md) retains the history policy.

### 3. Institutions, professions and social power

B06/B08/B13/B14/B19 support institutions as working parts of a campaign. A school, sect, guild, survey office or research group can supply knowledge, facilities, collaborators, protection, demand and duties. Its name or membership badge should connect to at least one actual activity or choice.

| Institutional distinction | Proposed access or interaction | What advances |
|---|---|---|
| **Entry and practical competence** | A field result, craft contribution, examination, fee or sponsor can supply a scene-supported qualification. | Different builds gain usable access through different accomplishments. Not every institution must offer every route. |
| **Instruction and transmission** | A teacher can share a full method, a licensed subset or a personal variant with disclosed terms. | Knowledge and demonstrated command improve; the teacher's own method and interests persist. Withheld material has a specific reason. |
| **Facilities and production** | Membership permits using an actual bench, archive, garden, laboratory or training site subject to capacity and means. | Projects and methods become feasible. A facility supplies a function instead of a generic faction XP multiplier. |
| **Office and public recognition** | An appointment, charter or local title can grant specific legal commands and responsibilities. | Authority, audience reputation and obligations change independently from personal combat power. |
| **Funding and exchange** | Work, patronage, trade, a commission or an optional loan funds a selected pursuit. | The player chooses a credible resource path. Economic pressure can define a particular world without making debt compulsory everywhere. |

Institutional membership can be a lasting pursuit. Prepare, undertake a meaningful field/craft/service task, gain access or recognition, use the resulting support, then address a changed regional problem. Rival institutions can specialize rather than differ only in rank. A politically connected novice may have leverage a stronger outsider lacks; a capable independent practitioner can still succeed through an authored alternate route.

Reputation belongs to audiences with a reason to know. A private lesson does not automatically become public fame; a public demonstration can establish a witnessed result without revealing the secret acquisition source. A uniform, credential or inherited role can create expectations, but the player's actual history remains distinct. Benefits and obligations should be visible when the role is accepted.

Satire can expose how a setting works. An advertisement can dress a loan as a gift, an examiner can care about the wrong metric, or a prestigious academy can teach a very useful mundane specialty. Preserve actual contracts and useful alternatives beneath the joke. A good teacher, fair institution or supportive patron remains a valid played-straight choice. Numeric prices, loan terms and promotion thresholds require later balance work.

### 4. Collaborators, multiple selves and growing places

B07/B11/B12/B13/B17/B18 connect character growth to collective capacity. A few people with complementary specialties can accomplish something the traveler alone cannot efficiently sustain. The character may still become a powerful individual; group projects add goals and choices beyond distributing damage bonuses.

| Collaboration model | Original rule to specify | Distinct reason to choose it |
|---|---|---|
| **Hired specialist** | Real payment/materials, a local task, independent availability, output ownership and collection. | Useful work continues while the traveler pursues a compatible activity. |
| **Teacher and student** | What is taught, whose competence grows, commitments and when the student chooses a new goal. | Knowledge becomes a living tradition and later cooperation. Do not assume an automatic teacher-rebate mechanic. |
| **Summon or construct** | Creation/bond costs, autonomy, command scope, maintenance or reclaim policy where applicable. | Purpose-built functions differ from employing a human. Their supported operation can be excellent without pretending they share all human motives. |
| **Copy or alternate self** | Body/identity origin, resource owner, shared versus separate memories and what pooled expertise actually means. | Parallel specialization can unlock a different strategy. Trust is authored; hostility is one option rather than the default. |
| **Domain or institution** | Owned place, access, labor, material sources, storage and staged useful facilities. | A home project opens new methods or supports local people, then creates expedition or regional goals. |

Expose relevant work without demanding constant upkeep. The player chooses a goal and allocation; the supported routine handles repeated execution and stops for missing means, a new choice or an agreed bound. A completed project grants its function once. Returning goods, collecting rewards or transferring a loan must use the actual owners and claim identities.

Distributed agency needs a scale decision. A small roster of fully modeled actors, a larger population summarized through institutions, and a world-spanning network of selves are different engineering scopes. The earliest proof should use a few persistent participants. Later aggregation can preserve meaningful bottlenecks and outcomes without ticking thousands of full NPCs. Scheduler budgets, save size and shared-resource contention need measured fixtures before adoption.

Base development can remain optional and low-maintenance. A workshop supports crafting, a garden supplies a chosen research path, an archive helps compare methods and a shelter supports expedition preparation. Layout is an optional view over actual facilities. An inherited ruined place can be a discovery/recovery campaign rather than a second compulsory management game. Local storage stays local unless a portable-domain rule expressly changes that.

### 5. Distinct worlds, portable strength and sustained campaigns

B04/B07/B11/B12/B16/B17/B18/B19/B21 supply reference questions for multiple forms of access and scale. A portal, chat network, reincarnation, life simulation and inherited position do not create the same player relationship to a world. Physical travel, remote contact, acquiring a local role and historical replay need their own commitments and information boundaries.

For every proposed world specify how methods are acquired, how they grow, what fuels them, which resources and institutions matter, what an ordinary visit accomplishes, what can return home and which consequences stay local. A supported imported power should operate at its meaningful defined strength. Incompatibility needs a real local reason, such as unavailable fuel or a jurisdiction-dependent authority; it should not be invented anew to neutralize every advantage.

| Candidate original campaign profile | Local loop and distinctive rules | Useful return and sustained objective |
|---|---|---|
| **Frontier schools and practical traditions** | Ordinary work, field discoveries, manuals/teachers, personal methods, contracts and regional institutions. Strong fundamentals support multiple professions. | Export practical expertise and tools. Deepen routes, schools and remembered people across a substantial home campaign. This is the closest expansion surface to current play. |
| **Industrial cultivation society** | Cultivation methods interact with laboratories, production, credentials, patrons and resource access. Financial satire is an optional tone. | Export a method or process with declared fuel/material requirements. Pursue reliable independence, a research project or institutional reform as well as personal strength. |
| **Bonded wilderness and changing bodies** | Creature observation, habitat access, bonding or personal adaptation, partner development and ecological objectives. A creature and a copied faculty differ. | Export a compatible partner, biological capability or researched material. Discover lineages and routes while preserving partner agency and local ecology where modeled. |
| **A route between a refuge and a hazardous ruin** | Home-side learning/craft/support complements ruin-side salvage, danger and unusual resources. Logistics, return windows and locally useful methods drive visits. | Recovered resources enable home projects; better home tools open deeper expeditions. A two-way benefit can exist across an unequal power scale. |
| **An inherited domain and wider stewardship** | A bounded place grows through projects, followers, research and negotiated access before later regional or cosmic decisions. | Export specific research or support; grow stewardship or infrastructure as an optional journey. Domain-scale power requires its own authored institutions and performance scope. |

These profiles preserve breadth and are not a selected world roster, reference adaptation or delivery promise. A direct fan setting additionally needs an exact source pack and selected entry/divergence policy. Original low-scale stories can borrow structural questions without inheriting a reference's named cast, universe or late powers.

A substantial campaign can cycle through local routine, a new clue, preparation, a consequential visit, aftermath and another changed pursuit. Some arcs emphasize combat; others emphasize instruction, recovery, construction, rescue or research. Time spent should regularly produce useful differences: a technique becomes reliable, a route opens, a facility works, a person changes their response, an old obstacle becomes trivial or a new objective becomes reachable.

World return should preserve local people, possessed goods, contracts and meaningful outcomes. A previous area can remain relevant through a unique material, teacher, trusted contact or project. Do not turn relevance into a compulsory daily maintenance tour. Interrupted visits need the selected recovery/retention policy: ordinary defeat, bodily death, an ended life, voluntary prestige and historical reversal remain different events. The existing one-realm/one-prestige first milestone sequences delivery of this broader vision.

## Five original scenario cards

These are detailed variants of candidates A–E, not new selected work orders. Names, costs, timing, lore and numerical effects remain review proposals. No card requires a forced opening reset, personal-save access or another prestige layer.

### A. A method acquired in two genuinely different ways

**Working scene:** a known specialist has a method for keeping a loaded frame steady on uneven ground. Original working name: **Load-bearing Step**, separate from the existing Rat-step proof and the earlier Split Reed proposal. Its first application is an existing-style work or expedition objective, with an ordinary equipment/preparation approach still usable.

**Teaching route:** meet the holder, agree to disclosed instruction/work terms, learn the procedure, and complete a bounded applied practice. **Exceptional route:** after discovering an appropriate source, inspect the eligible holder and acquire the declared method with a specified retained amount of command. It may shorten or bypass practice when that is the source's promised operation. The source does not grant every hidden technique simply because the person exists in a registry.

**Choice and payoff:** compare instruction's reliable access/support with the exceptional route's source-specific acquisition opportunity. Use the method to stabilize an actual task or gain a new supported route response. Costs and resulting competence differ observably; both can remain worthwhile for different builds. A separate bodily faculty can enable a further loaded application if actually acquired, while the ordinary subset remains useful.

**Aftermath:** preserve the holder's method for a true copy, the acquired origin/revision and actual paid commitment. Teaching can leave a useful directed memory. If a later extraction variant is selected, explicitly author its donor loss and consequences rather than changing this scene silently. Do not make the initial copy an unannounced theft.

**Implementation and gate:** compose a real donor power, acquisition command, execution requirement and staged practical effect. Existing copy/transfer primitives help, but source-specific eligibility and native application still need content/binding work. In a rebuilt fresh-save journey, both routes produce their advertised results; the holder's declared aftermath, owned power, costs and applied function survive reload/import and a failed save publishes no partial grant.

### B. A visitor remembers a promise you have not made

**Working scene:** a returning traveler is wary because they remember a failed agreement involving a person they identify as the player. Their remembered context could be an earlier history, a counterpart or an honest identification error. Select authorial truth before implementing the scene; its first presentation need not reveal which explanation is correct.

**Choices:** ask for concrete details, investigate a local discrepancy, offer a small bounded act of help, decline an obligation, or keep a new explicit promise. Inspection and conversation can expose evidence without committing a quest. Any accepted payment/deadline/resource transfer stays explicit.

**Payoff:** a current intervention changes the visitor's next plan, available information or willingness to cooperate. They can retain the prior memory while recognizing the player's present conduct. A resolved mismatch need not erase uncertainty about every other prediction.

**Implementation and gate:** give the visitor a stable actor, disclosed report, private authored belief, directed memory and one causal follow-up. First prove that factual outcome, NPC belief and player discovery can differ. An uninformed player sees no private timeline label; two current outcomes yield different later meetings; duplicate delivery grants no extra reward; reload retains the same actor and actual result. General belief inference and historical rewind remain separate extensions.

### C. Two useful ways into an institution

**Working scene:** an optional town field school or guild workshop offers access to a practical teacher and shared facility. Connect it to the current town permit/ledger chapter when authoring the scene; do not declare that the existing permit already includes every new privilege.

**Entry options:** a field-focused character submits a relevant demonstrated result; a craft-focused character contributes a useful inspected component or completes a disclosed commission. A later institution may offer payment or sponsorship, but the first card only needs two actually working routes. Each route records its real contribution once.

**Payoff:** access permits one method, paid facility task or collaboration that the player can use beyond the entrance scene. The institution recognizes the supplied evidence. A private acquisition source can remain secret unless observed; institutional status is not a universal power-rank declaration.

**Implementation and gate:** use actual native materials/skills/access and staged unlocking, with an authored bounded obligation if selected. Martial/explorer and craft-oriented synthetic characters qualify through different means and then use the facility. Costs, ownership, disclosure and return usefulness survive reload. Refusing entry leaves ordinary progression viable; later roles and promotions are separate content.

### D. A project that needs two different specialists

**Working scene:** improve a discovered home workshop or route-support site using one craftsperson and one field specialist. The project needs measured parts and a verified local survey. Connect to existing work/home services rather than inventing a parallel job writer.

**Allocation:** supply real material, reserve capacity, choose which work to start or defer, and let each eligible actor execute their paid local plan. The traveler can pursue compatible work. A shortage or blocked participant pauses the affected phase under a declared policy; it does not invent resources or discard a finished contribution.

**Payoff:** the completed facility opens a useful production/preparation action or changes its supported operation. The player can inspect who supplied each contribution and what remains. A collaborator's later availability or interest may create another commission, without compulsory upkeep or automatic friendship.

**Implementation and gate:** declare a unique project, actual owners, dependency phases, reserved inputs, supported interruption/refund rules and single settlement. Save during each phase, resume with exactly one completed facility, and verify no duplicate outputs or resource contention. This proves specialist cooperation and a growing place; shared clone consciousness, a whole settlement population and native party combat require separate scope.

### E. Foreign tools become useful back home

**Working scene:** a later isolated destination offers a material/process the home campaign lacks, while home-side craftsmanship or preparation improves access there. A local contact and return route give both places a reason to matter. This card follows the first realm milestone and requires actual world isolation before it is playable.

**Journey:** learn a credible lead, prepare a supported travel commitment, visit, use a locally useful approach, acquire the declared resource or procedure, and return to apply it in an existing home project. A powerful portable method can solve an earlier obstacle directly when compatible; another objective may require local knowledge or a teacher.

**Payoff:** the home action gains a real useful capability, and the foreign destination retains its own changed possessions, people and history. The return does not clone goods, restart quests or collapse branch counterparts into the same NPC. Direct contact/chat alone does not create physical travel.

**Implementation and gate:** implement native world-state bindings, departure/arrival ownership, pending-job/session arbitration and the selected transfer/retention rules. Validate one complete round trip with independent local progress, a useful return interaction, an incompatible case explained from known rules, interrupted-visit recovery and generated-save roundtrip. Metadata and generic transition planners are prerequisites rather than this journey's acceptance result.
