# Starts, endings and randomness palette

2026-10-11. Spoiler-free design proposals responding to Weibao's request for
possible starts, ending possibilities, randomness and further considerations.
These expand the palette, not accepted rules or implemented gameplay. Actual
world-specific candidates, hidden conditions and outcomes remain with sealed
owners. No runtime, build, browser or save verification was performed here.

Existing owners: [completion standard](WORLD_COMPLETION_STANDARD.md),
[campaign framework](worlds/CAMPAIGN_FRAMEWORK.md),
[origins](worlds/ORIGINS_AND_WORLD_TIMELINES.md),
[timelines](TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE.md).
Apply this palette to S02, S09, S11, S12 and S14 rather than creating competing
world rules. Source-world possibilities need source/premise qualification.

## P1 Starting possibilities

Compose compatible axes instead of rolling every field independently.

| Axis | Possibilities to consider |
|---|---|
| Entry | Birth and upbringing; reincarnation; physical arrival; summoning; awakening; return after absence; possession where supported; inherited identity |
| Life stage | Child, apprentice, established professional, veteran, elder; an existing life interrupted by a change |
| Body/agent | Ordinary person, unusual body, nonhuman creature, artificial being, spirit, collective, system guiding a host; each needs actual supported actions |
| Social position | Family member, outsider, worker, student, officer, dependent, ruler, prisoner, fugitive, debtor; powerful or comfortable beginnings are valid too |
| Situation | Ordinary day, first employment, journey, rescue, injury recovery, siege, political transition, failed expedition, aftermath of an important event |
| History | Before, during or after a major era; coherent existing relationships and responsibilities; authored background separated from player-earned history |
| Knowledge | Local familiarity, outsider ignorance, incomplete memories, retained prior-life knowledge, mistaken beliefs; disclosure follows actual means |
| Exceptional means | None, latent, immediately usable, earned later, inherited, borrowed, transferred or supported by a host; no compulsory drawback or rarity ladder |

Each start should specify observable surroundings, usable opening actions,
possessions/access, obligations, known facts and initial time pressures. A
dependent or immobile agent needs a meaningful way to act, communicate or
experience progression. Unequal starts are allowed; they need coherent causes.
Birth need not require clicking through years of passive waiting.

## P2 Ending possibilities

An ending has a scope, a cause, an aftermath and a continuation policy.

| Scope | Possible closure | Continuing questions |
|---|---|---|
| Task/relationship | Completed, abandoned, expired, failed, transformed into a different commitment | Who remembers? What remains owed or possible? |
| Career/arc | Mastery, retirement, departure, reconciliation, defeat, chosen ordinary life | Can daily life continue? Can another pursuit begin? |
| Body/life | Aging, bodily death, sacrifice, destruction, supported resurrection or changed form | What survives, by what carrier, and who receives it? |
| World/era | Settlement, collapse, rebuilding, displacement, major historical divergence | Does the world continue? Who lives with the consequences? |
| Run | Chosen closure, terminal loss, succession, rebirth, supported loop or new beginning | Which exact history/state is retained, archived, inherited or reset? |

Allow partial and mixed outcomes. Achievement, happiness, loyalty, survival and
the world's condition need not agree. A peaceful life can be satisfying closure.
An ending can emerge from accumulated conduct rather than a final menu choice.
Do not equate every death with prestige or assume every finale stops simulation.
Sealed concrete outcomes stay out of public catalogs and player menus.

## P3 Randomness possibilities

| Layer | What may vary | Design question |
|---|---|---|
| Beginning | Qualified world, era, place, role, body, relationships, means | Which axes are chosen, constrained, or unknown to the player? |
| World creation | People, resources, institutions, local tensions, eligible hidden variants | Which facts are authored and which may vary without breaking source rules? |
| Ongoing events | Timing, encounters, offers, weather, opportunities | What existing cause makes this event eligible now? |
| Action resolution | Combat, crafting variation, discovery, uncertainty | How do actual skill, preparation, tools and circumstances affect the result? |
| Long-term history | Actor choices, competition, succession, cascading consequences | What comes from simulated decisions rather than arbitrary event rolls? |

Randomness should change decisions and stories. Repeated interchangeable rewards
do not provide much variety. Correlate connected facts: a privileged upbringing
can explain education, contacts and duties together. Preserve real outliers
without requiring every strong benefit to receive a balancing penalty.

Possible selection policies include weighted surprise, equal world chances then
local selection, preference-filtered surprise, and optional anti-repeat history.
These have different distributions; more start records should not accidentally
make one world dominate. Filters can describe experience preferences without
revealing the candidate catalog. Do not secretly force a lucky result after bad
rolls unless that policy is selected.

Commit generated facts at their defined creation/commitment point and persist
them. Reload, import, previews and UI refreshes must not reroll them. Intentional
reroll, reproduction by seed, content-version differences and missing-pack
behavior need explicit policies. A single seed alone does not promise identical
results after content or generator changes.

## P4 Additional things to consider

| Area | Useful questions |
|---|---|
| Agency and idling | What can be delegated, queued or interrupted? What happens during travel, absence or browser closure? Are these policies clear before a commitment? |
| Other lives | What do NPCs pursue independently? Can they compete, teach, inherit, retire or disappear through supported causes? |
| Ordinary continuity | After becoming powerful, are home, work, relationships, making things and caring for others still useful pursuits? |
| Knowledge and surprise | What can each actor establish? Can a mistaken reading be revised? Can the player act before certainty without exposing author truth? |
| Recovery and changed goals | Can failure redirect a career, place or relationship? Where does the local world permit recovery, and where is loss terminal? |
| Legacy | Who actually holds items, records, obligations or memories after departure or death? Heirs do not automatically receive everything. |
| Pacing | How soon does a start offer a consequential action? What can be skipped, summarized or continued without erasing meaningful history? |
| Consequence | What changes when the player refuses, delays, leaves or succeeds overwhelmingly? Do other actors have actual means to respond? |

## P5 Suggested next design exercise

Qualify a small set of genuinely different starts inside one selected world.
For each, trace the first meaningful action, an ordinary livelihood, a dangerous
situation, one changed-goal/failure route, and a closure with aftermath. Preserve
larger horizons; this comparison is a first slice, not a cap on the world.

Record eligibility and compatibility privately. Before implementation, decide
the selector distribution, randomness commitment points, reroll policy, ending
scope and retention policy. Bind selected details to existing native consumers
and state owners. Gameplay acceptance then requires rebuilt fresh-origin
journeys and relevant save/reload/import and old-save compatibility evidence.


## Content reconciliation B300: start choices, scoped closure and source-qualified variance

The full original 107-line palette was read. P1's eight compatible axes, P2's five closure scopes, P3's five variation layers, all selection-policy options and P4/P5 considerations remain useful proposals. Unequal/comfortable/powerful, ordinary, child/elder, dependent/nonhuman and retained-knowledge starts need coherent actual means and meaningful actions, not a universal disadvantage or compulsory years of waiting. A language, body, social position or latent faculty is not automatically all the other axes. A generated biography is not player-earned history.

Current [developer-route scope](WORLD_ENTRY_RULES.md#developer-build-through-and-coverage-beyond-the-lead-2026-11-03) distinguishes a protagonist build-through from the default player start. Forced-canon policy can serve that development run while ordinary/alternative lives retain their own knowledge and choices. Lead-path coverage does not close the whole-world denominator. A source-world variation fills qualified unstated facts under its local dial rather than replacing established canon/observed history because a random-start button was pressed.

Task, relation, career, body, world and whole-run closure can differ. Survival, happiness, loyalty, goal and world condition can disagree, while quiet continued life remains possible. Departure or bodily death does not itself trigger prestige, whole-world reset or automatic possession of a defeated person's means. Actual leftover property, custody, rights, obligations and knowledge need their source-supported carrier/recipient. Partial closure and a changed pursuit are useful results. Current activity abstraction can compress repeated spans while retaining consequential choices/history; it does not impose a short campaign or loss of a long life.

World-first and row-first random selection produce different distributions. Preserve optional preference/anti-repeat/pity ideas without silently selecting them or disclosing the sealed candidate catalog through filters/counts. Creation truth, current occurrence eligibility, per-attempt execution and simulated actor choices use their own causes and commitment points. A seed does not promise unchanged values after generator/content revisions; retain committed facts and actual source/definition identity. Preview/load/import is not an implicit reroll. Deliberate reroll and missing-content behavior require their selected local policy.

P5's comparison exercise is a possible bounded design method, not a prerequisite to every small original start or a request to launch new worlds. Old-save proof wording follows the current explicit compatibility decision; current-format consistency and relevant persistence still need real evidence when behavior changes. This palette supplies no source-world candidate eligibility, hidden ending/menu catalog, live selector, stored branch or playable route proof. No new distribution, ending policy, unlock ladder, schema, runtime or source reader is selected here.


## Content reconciliation B397: fan selectors and narrative references remain local examples

The full fan interactive-fiction and CYOA surveys were read, alongside the existing B325 quest-survey qualification. Their opened-page, listing-only, snippet, partial build-data and hub observations retain different evidence depth. A fan build, a human-run vote quest and an engine-assisted story are different kinds of reference. Lack of results in a bounded title/host search does not establish absence, and listing status/prices/dates are October snapshots. No linked source story, fan-build data, live site or game was newly read in this reconciliation.

Retain useful questions: coherent era/body/means choices, constrained combinations, optional difficulty or budget selectors, power-plus-mentor bundles, origin-related access, calendar training, conditional storylets, practical inventories and distinct relationship dimensions. Those can inform an optional premise, an original mechanism or a readable control. Their point budgets, lifespan pressure, action-energy bank, drawback rewards, stat thresholds, mandatory mentors, class caps, finite endings and fiat assumptions are donor-specific choices. They do not establish canon law or a global cost, distribution, short-campaign target or character benefit from a player-layer selection. A nonnegative budget allows points left over; it does not prove a balanced-zero exchange mechanism. Large serialized data may be assets rather than mechanical depth.

Twine, ChoiceScript and Ren'Py tags do not by themselves prove the presence or absence of maps, combat, migrations, background simulation or offline work. The recorded sample can suggest a scheduling, discovery or relationship comparison, but site text, review claims, source implementation and actual play need separate evidence. Current lead-first priorities retain independent lives later, AD51 shares reasonable seams without flattening the worlds, and AD52 allows long play with useful compression. No new acquisition wave, fan-text import, dependency, point-buy start, energy throttle or mandatory romance/death unlock is selected.
