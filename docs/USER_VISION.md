## Design principles

- **Anything is possible, reasonably** (AD5). "anything is possible should be a line and in uservision". No mechanic or outcome is ruled out by default; "reasonably" means it fits its world's lore and has a sensible cost or condition. Balance is not imposed by default; a world, heaven or person may push back when lore gives a reason ([2026-10-09](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#anything-is-possible-2026-10-09)).
- **Things work by how they work** (AD38). "potions work by virtue of how they work, if it's methodolocgy reasonably wotks it works". A stated limit, canon included, describes the ordinary case; exceptions exist with an in-world reason, cost, rarity and record. Inputs shift odds and quality rather than gate; a hard gate needs a world's stated lore ([2026-10-17](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#extensible-panels-and-layout-seams-2026-10-16)).
- **Limits are adjectives** (AD22). "Nothing has to only work 'once', the power of adjetives is vast". Each limit is a per-world dial; he is essentially the sole player, so out-of-fiction anti-abuse is very low priority ([2026-10-10](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#jjk-world-adaptation-and-a-testing-world-2026-10-10)).
- **Means decide, both ways** (AD6). "Alot of things should be decided by means, on both sides" ([2026-10-10](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#text-vfx-bluffs-knowledge-and-means-2026-10-10)).
- **One underlying record**, with truth, observer knowledge and representations kept separate. Hidden properties are real and act whether or not anyone knows them: knowledge changes what you can predict, not what happens ([2026-10-15](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#proto23-visual-baseline-reaffirmed-2026-10-15), [2026-10-09](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#anything-is-possible-2026-10-09)).
- **Information appears when it comes up.** Unknown rows and future panels stay hidden; hints disclose known clues and nothing more; a new opponent starts with impressions and `???`, and estimates come from real data and say so ([2026-10-03](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#discovery-aware-overview-menus-2026-10-03), [2026-10-05](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#complete-scoped-worlds-useful-skills-and-discovery-ui-2026-10-05), [2026-10-16](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#r7-panelreadability-steering-2026-10-15)).
- **Ideas that open possibilities are welcome; limits run by him first** (AD56). "I like basically all the ideas that follow the core rules, and is interesting and leads to more possiblities, anything that limts those should run by me first" (2026-11-10). Agent ideas that follow the core rules and widen play become adjustable working defaults; a line that narrows or removes options waits for his answer ([2026-11-10](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-11.md#ideas-that-open-possibilities-are-welcome-limits-run-by-him-first-2026-11-10)).
- **No forced weakness, anywhere.** "The idea that there is no forced weakness should honestly apply to everything" (2026-11-10). Nothing must carry a weakness, drawback, counter or tell by rule; limits are per-thing adjectives and pushback comes from in-world agents with reasons. Costs a mechanism really has still apply ([2026-11-10](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-11.md#levels-of-fate-cheats-further-no-forced-weakness-anywhere-2026-11-10)).
- **Difficulty from mechanics and interaction**, not pure numbers. The game can be very unfair, "like caves of qud": overwhelming threats and irreversible consequences are allowed ([2026-10-03](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#jojo-universal-thief-and-mechanical-difficulty-2026-10-03), [2026-10-03](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#running-the-master-gm-for-modular-game-worlds-2026-10-03)).
- **Core game inspirations: Caves of Qud, RimWorld and Shadow of Mordor.** Weibao (2026-11-09): they "would and should be core inspirations". Qud for hand-written story inside generated history and places, RimWorld for people and events that come from rolled systems with a storyteller pacing them, Shadow of Mordor for opponents who remember and rise. Existing research and code: [Qud systems audit](research/qud-systems-audit-2026-10-15/REPORT.md), `rival_memory.js`, the storylet deck ([2026-11-09](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-11.md#fleshing-out-the-world-and-core-inspirations-2026-11-09)).
- **Threats grow through their own causes**, not by matching the player. Run-ending threats can be turned to advantage by a capable enough character (AD50) ([2026-10-15](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#proto23-shell-variant-over-yair-systems-2026-10-15), [2026-11-03](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-11.md#run-ending-threats-a-capable-character-can-use-2026-11-03)).

Our systems are calibrated top down until they reproduce the lore's stated quantities, and the same deterministic, rule-based fit extends to money, combat, relationships, personality, tags, knowledge and time. Story walks are where that fit is tuned: a system is built so its outcomes reproduce the walked canon events, and a system can be visible before it changes anything ([2026-11-03](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-11.md#match-the-lores-numbers-by-calibrating-our-systems-2026-11-03), [2026-11-03](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-11.md#reproducible-lore-fit-across-domains-2026-11-03), [2026-11-08](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-11.md#story-walks-calibrate-the-systems-and-more-crafting-reputation-and-homes-2026-11-08)).
- **Variance only fills what the source leaves unstated**, mainly to make parallel versions of a world differ; how much varies is a dial. Skills keep chance while results converge through the available actions ([2026-11-03](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-11.md#variance-abstraction-and-procedural-work-2026-11-03)).
- **Reasonable things get abstracted** (AD51). Where several worlds reasonably use the same mechanism, design it once as a shared seam; world numbers, laws and differences stay local ([2026-11-03](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-11.md#reasonable-things-get-abstracted-2026-11-03)). Mechanisms a book needs are built as general mechanisms other worlds can use ([2026-10-28](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#cast-lives-build-everything-the-leads-life-needs-choose-later-for-other-seats-2026-10-28)).
- **Each world chooses its own version of each system and that system's UI**, with nuance; no world is the template. Progression is any lasting improvement: skills, knowledge, stats, equipment, relationships, standing, wealth (agent reading of his words, [2026-10-30](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#abilities-no-hardcoded-path-2026-10-30), [2026-10-30](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#each-world-chooses-its-own-systems-and-their-ui-standing-rule-2026-10-30)).
- **Complexity is welcome**; the question is how to keep depth while managing implementation cost. Guardrails CX1–CX8 pace when depth arrives, not how much the game may have ([2026-10-16](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#r7-panelreadability-steering-2026-10-15), [2026-10-16](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#architecture-rules-and-modular-seams-run-2026-10-16)).
- **Not everything has to matter.** Scenic detail, casual lines and incidental people need no XP or payoff, but major adversaries need source-grounded identity, motive and capability ([2026-10-05](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#world-texture-loreful-adversaries-and-parallel-timelineprogression-authoring-2026-10-05)).
- **Titles, tags and cheats.** Titles can grant actual powers per world; tags may change rules themselves. Many types of one ability can coexist, some better or worse ([2026-10-15](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#titles-achievements-and-tags-on-rules-2026-10-15), [2026-10-03](gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-10.md#ability-variants-and-fine-detail-roles-2026-10-03)).
