## Outcome

Make the existing depth easy to reach, useful to pursue, and worth continuing. A player should be able to enter a coherent life, find a goal through what the character knows, obtain means, act, observe a consequence, recover or change approach, and continue after saving. Idle routines should support chosen plans and show why they stop. New content should open decisions, places, capabilities, relationships and consequences beyond the opening.

“Super playable” is a proposed acceptance lens, not a claim about today's experience:

| Code | Player-visible result | How to judge it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | I can start and understand my immediate situation | A natural supported entry provides usable actions, known costs and observable context without a developer command or out-of-world checklist. Starting-world policy remains with its accepted owner. |
| P2 | I can pursue different kinds of useful lives | Ordinary work, making, learning, exploration and world-specific power have connected uses where the world supports them. A peaceful pursuit can continue without obligatory combat or prestige. |
| P3 | Progress changes what I can do | A new rung or learned method has a real consumer and opens a fitting opportunity; a renamed meter or longer timer is insufficient. |
| P4 | I can understand and recover from an interruption | Missing means, unavailable people, cancellation, injury and exhausted stock give supported feedback and fitting alternatives. Hidden truth stays hidden. |
| P5 | I can stop, return and keep going | Save/reload/import retain the selected history, custody, earned progress and pending work. Chosen absence policy governs time. Director pacing/taste feedback is recorded separately. |
