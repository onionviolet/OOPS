# Run-defining advantages: trope and novel comparison

Date: 2026-10-01, America/New_York. Research and original proposals only. No starting roster, canonical artifact or implementation is selected. This expands the origin discussion in [Direction synthesis](DIRECTION_SYNTHESIS.md); [PROJECT.md](../PROJECT.md) owns accepted direction.

## The important distinction

A modifier improves the existing loop. A defining advantage changes its rules: what persists, how capabilities are acquired, which resources constrain action, which objectives matter, or who the player controls. Both can be useful, but they need different scope and acceptance tests. A deliberately exceptional advantage need not be reduced to a small bonus for balance. Its promised strength should remain visible; obstacles, consequences and progression can evolve around it.

Origins and worlds are independent design axes. A temporal origin in a cultivation campaign should not produce exactly the same decisions as one in an industrial campaign. Not every pairing needs bespoke content, but every supported pairing must have coherent acquisition, compatibility, event and retention rules. Avoid committing to the full origin-by-world-by-ending matrix before measuring authoring cost.

The subsequent requested [rival, identity and fate audit](RIVALS_IDENTITIES_AND_FATE.md) expands luck/fate manipulation, ability appropriation, social usurpation and body/soul replacement into distinct future gameplay contracts. It also covers the persistent people who can hold, lose or contest those advantages. Its catalogue and proposed stages do not select a starting origin or change the current runtime.

## Additional trope search directions

The wider taxonomy should also examine information/appraisal systems, consumption or ability copying, reincarnation with remembered expertise, future simulation, portable worlds, luck/fate manipulation, patron contracts, nonhuman evolution and institutional inheritance. These are discovery categories, not sourced exact mechanics or a commitment to implement them. Group candidates by what rule they change rather than the artifact's appearance. A ring may provide storage, rollback or a pact; those are different mechanics despite the shared item shape.

Prior dossiers already support some narrower comparisons: the [alternative-power dossier](RESEARCH_ALTERNATIVE_POWER.md) examines Gu supply/maintenance, artifact drawbacks and institutional access; the [systems dossier](RESEARCH_LITRPG_SYSTEMS.md) examines classes and acquisition/retention contracts. The live Reverend Insanity chapter fetch failed in this pass, so its chapter details are not newly verified. For a future exact copy/absorption origin, inspect an accessible original case before assuming unrestricted theft, compatibility or stacking.

## Subtle rules that can change a whole journey

Define the scope of retained experience versus physical improvement, remembered relations versus current trust, and known procedures versus executable powers. Differentiate material creation, maturation, duplication, quality improvement and recovery: each produces a different economy. Identify whether a system observes, predicts, grants power or creates objectives; calling it a system does not answer those questions.

Origins can unlock different goals and endings, not merely alternate combat solutions. A supplier might rebuild a settlement; a temporal traveler might prevent a disaster; a networked character might establish several footholds. Shared campaign milestones can coexist with these different ambitions. NPC responses should follow what they could observe, not automatically reveal a hidden origin. Secrecy, sponsorship or scrutiny can be authored consequences without simulating every witness.

Some origin interactions may deliberately be extremely powerful. Separate limited prototype scope from permanent fictional weakness. Later growth can remove an early restriction, and some combinations can be intended rewards. Avoid an endless pattern of enemies conveniently nullifying every advantage. If a power removes resource scarcity, move meaningful decisions toward allocation, discovery, commitments or consequence rather than artificially restoring the same grind.

For every selected origin record activation/ownership, acquisition, operating rules, growth, failure/recovery, compatibility, stacking and end-of-journey retention. Repeat-visit RNG must match that identity: fixed replay draws suit one bounded anchor, while an explicit fate-manipulation origin could permit rerolls. Neither should become a universal hidden policy. Multi-actor or private-world origins need a larger architectural test than an added inventory item.

## Selection test and next research

An advantage passes the definition test if the player can name a new strategy, a different meaningful objective or a changed transition rule. Show a short example route before writing a large tree. Test two origins in the same chapter and one origin in two circumstances to distinguish origin identity from encounter-specific convenience. Compare preparation time, unique opportunities, safety, repeat work and save correctness; do not require identical DPS.

The earlier ring-versus-vessel comparison remains a useful proposal, not a locked starting roster. Resolve the bottle source gap if pursuing that inspiration. A crafting origin is another low-to-medium integration candidate; distributed agency is a later structural experiment. User creative preference and playable evidence should select the pair. No new book purchase is necessary for the publicly verified examples; exact later rules need a bounded source pass when selected.
