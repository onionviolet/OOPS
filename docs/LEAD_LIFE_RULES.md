has accepted, with the date and his words or the AD code that records it. It adds no new
rule codes: details live in the LSD lines it cites, and those lines stay proposals until he

[LEAD_LIFE_GUIDE](LEAD_LIFE_GUIDE.md). Dated history and the proposals:

## These rules are defaults, not laws

Nothing here is absolute. Each rule is a default with a dial and a reason, and a better idea
with a reason can change or replace it (AD5, AD38, AD45: what is built is the current best,
not a final shape, and "it was written this way" is not a reason). To change one:

1. State the better idea and the reason (what it gets that the rule does not).
2. If it changes direction, it is Weibao's call; record his words in
   [USER_VISION](USER_VISION.md) and the owner here, and keep the old wording as dated
   history. Agent proposals stay proposals (AD32).
3. If it only refines how a rule is carried out (a different curve, a different tag route),
   a lane may do it with a note in its checkpoint and an update to the guide.
4. A world may differ from a default when it has an in-world reason and cost; say so in the
   world's pass.

## C. Decided under delegation (he can override)

**Grains versus reachable steps** (decided by the advisor under "We need advisor to decide #1
contradiction", 2026-10-29). Both lines stay. Floor: every canon step the lead takes is
reachable in the canon run as a playable step, a card or part of a montage day, with untaken
options shown locked. Depth: beats get step controls and real choices first, days run as a
montage, narration becomes journal and knowledge. A card or montage counts as reachable only
if it keeps choices, inputs and outputs, time and order, who was there, place, outcomes and
what was noticed (CLR6); a lossy fold is a gap. The day-zoom dial lets a player follow all
options. Progress reports reachable share over all steps, playable share over great beats,
the fold-loss count, distinct usable verbs and emergent canon rate; step-order fidelity stays
a diagnostic. Detail: LSD167, LSD209, LSD210 (qualified in the LSD doc).
