# Activity abstraction rules

Standing defaults requested 2026-11-04. Authority: Weibao, after the activity and cross-world findings: “turn findings into rules accordingly, and then” reconcile-project, then “Then consider long term ideas like gameplay, types of palystyles, and much more?” [Recorded direction](USER_VISION.md#activity-abstraction-rules-and-long-term-playstyles-2026-11-04), AD52. The request adopts the findings as operating principles through this rulebook; the agent-authored wording remains adjustable under AD32. Specific activity examples, field lists, numeric policies, implementation order and later playstyle ideas are not blanket adopted.

Applies when designing or implementing repeated activities, compressed periods and their controls in any applicable world. Compose with AD5/6–8/15–18/38/44/46/49, MR18/MR29, domain laws, AR3/7/8 and AD36/43. AD51 governs shared software mechanisms; this owner governs compressed gameplay. Neither requires a universal activity engine, full offscreen simulation or all-world rollout.

## Rules

| Code | Default | Dial and reason |
|---|---|---|
| ACR1 | **Compress repeated work around a chosen plan and useful consequence.** Use a session, batch, route leg, recurring order, encounter policy or period plan where it fits. | Choose the unit per activity, life and world; combine or omit forms. Familiar work gains convenience without forcing each setting through the same ten activity types. Compression reduces repeated handling; it sets no short total-play target. Long growth, sustained pursuits and time spent enjoying a place remain valid ([length clarification](USER_VISION.md#long-play-cross-world-pursuits-and-quests-2026-11-04)). |
| ACR2 | **A plan uses actual means and local laws.** Bind the relevant actor/body, method, source/support, place, inputs, clock and result consumer through existing owners. | A simple activity needs the relevant facts, not every possible field. A plan, saved preset or progress bar does not supply stock, knowledge, authority, labor or competence. |
| ACR3 | **Depth is chosen for the activity and situation.** Quick, guided and detailed views use the same applicable resolution rules; view changes preserve work and history. | Familiarity, novelty, stakes and player preference set detail. Better interventions can change results through their real mechanisms; extra clicks alone are not a competence or reward bonus. MR18 remains the making-specific owner. |
| ACR4 | **Keep decisions that change consequences, and preserve character.** Retain meaningful choices of objective, method, budget, commitments, disclosure, risk, stopping and response. | Summarize routine exchanges; play consequential moments more closely where useful. Reports can carry texture without mandatory choices. Personality, motives, knowledge and voice survive summarization; no universal affinity meter replaces them. |
| ACR5 | **Show the kind of progress the character can know.** Distinguish effort, known coverage, completion, success and waiting for support. | A bounded process can show a percentage; an uncertain inquiry can show elapsed effort or observed coverage. Display does not promise a solution or disclose hidden evidence/danger merely to fill a bar. |
| ACR6 | **Keep the result's real lifecycle and use.** Work completed, usable output, delivery, recipient approval and payment remain distinct when their terms differ. | Immediate actions may settle together; longer contracts may separate stages. The actual item, capability, learning, service or evidence has a consumer. A label or observation receipt alone is not proof that every named domain effect happened. |
| ACR7 | **Interruption settles what happened.** Return untouched reservations and retain actual spend, partial work, output and consequences under the selected operation's law. | Refund, forfeit, spoilage, retained intermediates and recovery vary by stage and world. Existing transaction/job owners settle once; a two-way refund/forfeit option does not automatically model staged consumption. |
| ACR8 | **Compression advances the declared clocks and relevant causes.** Batch stable stretches and split at a material condition or decision boundary. | Body, world, space, passive process and absence clocks can differ through means. Notification or awaiting input does not itself promise a safe world pause. Existing unsupported absence policies remain explicit; a routine summary does not enable offline play. |
| ACR9 | **Compress presentation without rewriting knowledge or chance.** Keep truth, attributed evidence and each observer's knowledge separate; preserve relevant draw/history contracts when work is grouped. | Repetition can improve fluency while new understanding needs informative means. Reopening detail, splitting a block or reloading does not grant free new evidence or reroll established outcomes. Source-fixed facts stay fixed; variance follows the source's unstated space and local law. |
| ACR10 | **Parallel and delegated work name their actual performers and support.** Shared stock, attention, tools and capacity determine what can coexist. | Compatible passive work, independent workers or world-specific bodies can operate together; there is no universal one-job rule. A delegate keeps their own means, motives, knowledge and reporting route. Their finished search is not instant player knowledge or delivered goods. |
| ACR11 | **Preserve committed plans and work through saves and revisions.** Use the existing state/save owners for spent inputs, pending work, exact receipts and settled outcomes; declare compatibility when changed. | Saved plans can require a fresh quote when reused; drafts are not commitments. A new content version must preserve or explicitly migrate committed terms. Runtime proof follows AD36/43, including affected persistence; document adoption is not delivery. |
| ACR12 | **Each world and life keeps its mechanisms; effective powers can remove chores.** Select relevant activity forms and local defaults rather than impose one economy, progression law or human life pattern. | A source, power, tool or arrangement can bypass an old step for its actual reason and cost. Do not add replacement busywork or automatic opposition scaling to cancel earned capacity. The remaining choice may be how to use, share or conceal that capacity. |
| ACR13 | **Change scale without losing established identity or causality.** Pools, cohorts, rates and summaries may represent unneeded detail; meaningful individuals and committed causes retain their bindings. | Promote detail when an actual choice uses it, under SSD73/GR. A named carrier, possession, worker, promise or completed delivery does not become rerollable because its view changed. No fully simulated population is required. |
| ACR14 | **Separate canon policy, life scope and delivery evidence.** A developer protagonist route can constrain its own sequence/outcomes without substituting for ordinary or alternative-life coverage. | Keep source-qualified local mechanics and supported alternatives. A one-time story event is not automatically repeatable practice; compressed canon scenes do not certify open-world careers. Select and prove a real consumer at the existing floor, not all worlds at once. |
