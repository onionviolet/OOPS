## Weibao's answer: intensity

Heart-demon and other trial intensity **depends on the world; every level
is possible**: abstract (a dark figure), personal (real names from your
receipts), raw (your actual worst moments replayed), and anything between.
A world sets its range; a trial picks within it.

## The trial model

### What starts a trial

| Trigger | Example |
|---|---|
| Threshold | A stat, level, realm or Concept crossing a line |
| Access | Touching a power above your soul's grade forces a test |
| Chosen | You enter a gate, an exam, a tomb |
| Scheduled | The yearly exam; a coming-of-age rite |
| Patron or system | A god or system issues a test with a deadline |
| Social | A duel challenge; a clan's demand; a court summons |
| Event | The world itself tests everyone (a tribulation season, a calamity) |
| Inherited | Your bloodline's trial at a set age |
| Debt | Breaking a vow or skipping duties opens a penalty trial |

### The pending queue

- Trials go into a **pending queue** visible in an existing panel, with
  what is known about each (which may be little, rule 6).
- **Bypasses, all honest:** prepare; start early on your terms; delay with
  barriers, rewards or vows; seal the stat that triggered it; change race
  or body to move the threshold; rewind and keep less.
- **Delay is never cancel** by default. A world can allow true
  cancellation through an outlier (rule 12) or the obscure way (rule 1).
- **Interaction:** two pending trials can merge into a harder one, or a
  passed trial can satisfy part of another.

### What a trial contains

| Kind | Example | Idle fit |
|---|---|---|
| Combat waves | Hold out against waves | Strong: runs on your triggers |
| Endurance | Survive N hours of heat, cold, hunger, poison | Strong: an idle trial by nature |
| Riddle or rules | A sphinx; a rules puzzle | Needs the player present |
| Craft | Make something good enough | Good: crafting already idles |
| Moral choice | Save one of two; break a vow or lose | Needs the player present |
| Heart demon | Face a generated self ([G6 and G7](SELVES_AND_ECHOES.md#g6-and-g7-shadow-and-worst-selves)) | Either |
| Memory or karma | Relive receipts; the past judges you | Either |
| Perfect self | Beat your [G1](SELVES_AND_ECHOES.md#g1-and-g2-perfect-selves) | Combat |
| Social | Win allies, pass an interview, survive politics | Mixed |
| Team | A trial for the party, each with a role | Party combat |
| Exploration | Reach the center; find the hidden objective | Mixed |
| Sacrifice | Give something up to pass | Present |

### The trial space

A trial is usually a **place with its own rules**, using the same place
rule layer as domains ([JJK brief](worlds/jujutsu/WORLD_BRIEF.md#domains-in-detail)):
- Time ratio (a day inside is an hour outside, or the reverse).
- Items suppressed, or only some allowed.
- Death blocked, softened, or real.
- Mind at risk: sanity, memory, identity.
- Simulated or real: simulated events can still leave real marks.

### Outcomes

| Result | Possible effects |
|---|---|
| Pass | The breakthrough, a rank, titles, a reward menu |
| Pass with distinction | First finisher, hidden objective, perfect clear: rarer menus |
| Partial | Breakthrough with a flaw; reward without the title |
| Fail, survive | Injury, flaw, a mood, a retry later (harder or easier), lost access |
| Fail, die | Death per world rules: revival, rewind, an heir, or the end |
| Refuse | Allowed where the world allows; the trial stays pending, or the power is withdrawn |

**Reward menus with different kinds**, from the reference: guidance, a
hypothetical future (a [G3 projection](SELVES_AND_ECHOES.md#g3-and-g5-projections)),
a guarantee for a later trial, a rewind, a perfect fight, an item, a title.
Choosing the kind is the decision.

## Idle and life-sim fit

- **Trials as checkpoints.** Idle growth can pile up until a trial gates
  it. You prepare while away and start when present.
- **Forced trials while offline.** The big design call. Options, all
  reachable per world:
  - **Wait** (default proposal): forced trials hold until you return; growth
    past the threshold pauses or overflows into a stored pool.
  - **Auto-run** on your saved triggers, with the outcome waiting for you.
  - **Harsh worlds** fire them anyway; you prepared or you didn't.
- **Long lives.** Childhood rites, adult exams, elder trials; a trial
  history per life in the atlas.
- **Never pointless.** A failed trial still pays evidence, a mood, a lesson.

## More to consider

- **Trials of others.** Allies, rivals, your children and your
  followers take their own trials; you can help, sabotage, watch or bet.
- **Examiners as people.** An examiner has taste, grudges and bribes
  (E3). The same trial differs by who runs it.
- **Audiences.** Some trials are shows: fame, sponsors, viewer rewards,
  and pressure to be entertaining.
- **Cheating trials.** Possible and judged by the world: a smuggled item,
  a bribed examiner, a leaked riddle. Getting caught is an odd record.
- **Trials that test the wrong thing.** A combat exam won with a trick may
  pass you into a role you can't do.
- **Trial lore.** Famous trials have legends, guides, survivors' tips and
  wrong rumors (rule 6). Guides go stale when the trial changes.
- **Trials that adapt.** A repeatable trial that learns your strategy
  (E8E).
- **Trials across worlds.** A pending tribulation follows your soul, your
  body, or stays behind, depending on what triggered it (rule 5). Leaving
  a world to dodge one is a real option with real costs.
- **Trials made by you.** A sect founder sets trials for disciples; a
  Culling-Game-like rule system lets players add trials.
- **Trials as content glue.** One trial engine serves exams, tribulations,
  dungeons, rites, duels and heart demons.

## Expansions (2026-10-10)

### Generating trials
- **Authored** trials for set pieces (a sphinx, an exam).
- **Assembled** trials from parts: trigger plus kind plus space rules plus
  examiner plus reward menu, seeded per world (E1), so the same world
  makes consistent trials.
- **Personal** trials built from your receipts and generated selves.
- **Escalating** trials whose next tier reads how you passed the last.

### Trial economy
- Entry fees, sponsors, bets, prep markets (potions before tribulation
  season), survivor guides that may be wrong.
- Guilds that sell coaching; examiners who can be bribed; insurance
  against death in trials where revival is a paid service.

### Trial grades and records
- Grades by speed, style, casualties, hidden objectives, mercy.
- A trial history in the atlas per life; famous clears become legends;
  rivals study your record.

### Abuse limits
See [abuse and limits](ABUSE_AND_LIMITS.md#per-system): escalation on
repeats, delay is not cancel, committed seeds on entry, diminishing reward
menus.

## Engine notes

Groundwork that exists: conditions and facts, paid actions with outcomes
and seeded chances (see [technique_content.js](../src/technique_content.js)),
timed opportunities with delay and lifetime, frozen encounters, party

## More expansions

- **Trial adjectives.** Every trial is a noun with adjectives
  ([limits are adjectives](ABUSE_AND_LIMITS.md#limits-are-adjectives)):
  a *rare*, *personal*, *lethal*, *timed* trial; a *daily*, *gentle*,
  *public* one. Changing one adjective makes a new trial.
- **Repeatable by default where it makes sense.** Arenas, waves and
  exams can repeat as often as the world says; escalation and
  diminishing yield are adjectives too, not hard caps.
- **Trial chains.** Passing one unlocks the next; failing branches to a
  different chain, not a dead end.
- **Trials that remember.** An examiner or a tribulation recalls how you
  passed last time and asks for something else.
- **Inverted trials.** You are the examiner, the sphinx, the heart demon
  for someone else.
- **Trials as relationships.** A patron's test is also a conversation;
  passing changes how they treat you.
