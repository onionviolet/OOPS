# Effect, power and time-ability UI standards (EU1-EU22)

*Proposal, not in force (AD32), 2026-11-18. Design and audit only; no runtime change. Target viewport: desktop and laptop, 1920x1080 first, 1366x768 second; mobile deferred (standing UI priority). Weibao asked how to consider UI standards for the effect lanes. Each line is a default with a dial and a reason, adjustable under AD56; none removes an option, so none waits on a limit decision.*

Where a standard restates an existing UI rule, it cites it and adds only the effect-specific part: UR83 (forewarned losses), UR84 (a fact shows its source), UR85 (a means adds a readout), UR86 (unchosen acts show), UR87 (lives one click away), UR88 (notices by stakes), UR91 (one settled event, several renderings), UR92 (history per aspect), UR95 (surfaces react before and after commit), UR111 (tooltips), UR114 (toasts), UR123 and RPT1-RPT14 (Reports), UR134 (facts reach the keyboard), UR136 (unknown is not zero). Rule index: [UI rules](../UI_RULES.md), [cheat rules](../CHEAT_RULES.md), [time rules](../TIME_RULES.md), [world entry rules](../WORLD_ENTRY_RULES.md).

## 1. Audit: what a player sees today (F1-F8)

Method: `yairpg-game` MCP on the live worktree build (`claude/effect-ui-fef517`), seed 7, fresh session, `game_look` and `game_click`, one `game_screenshot` at 1920x1080 (`private/effect-ui-2026-11-18/workshop-effects-1920x1080.png`, gitignored). Limits: the screenshot is a saved-state mirror and does not show the open Workshop run view or transient panels, so layout claims below come from the text views and source, not pixels. Reproduced: Capability runs panel, "Workshop · Effects" run, three effects (splash, ward, derived formula), Reports and Condition reads. Not reproduced: the inspector on an effect, a power sheet, any time ability (none is wired to a player surface yet), the Worlds lives list.

- **F1. An effect lands as one flat Reports line, `Kind · result`.** "Brew splash · the brewer is swollen for 30 minutes." and "Ward · the ward took 4 of 4 and the wearer lost 0." Observed. The line says what changed. It does not say why (which law, which reach against which resistance) or from what source (whose flask, which pack). No nested detail.
- **F2. Engine vocabulary reaches player text.** "Derived formula · Seer (Fool 9), 9 steps; power: bind thing, self, lasting; hazard: transform mind, self, delayed." Observed in Reports. That is the effect grammar's own tokens (RPT11, PRS lint scope). The Workshop is a dev-facing run, so this may be intended there; the standard below is for the ordinary surfaces the lanes will use.
- **F3. A landed effect did not appear in Condition.** After the splash line ("swollen for 30 minutes"), the Condition panel still read "No active effects" (headless look, same build; confirm in a rebuilt browser before treating it as a defect). If it holds, a timed effect has a log line and no standing home (UR83, UR92).
- **F4. The same sentence is written three times in the run view.** After each act the compact diff showed the result line in the run's own "What happened" block, a seen-count block and Reports. Same text, three places, no link from one to another (UR29, UR147).
- **F5. No effect line links to a detail.** Reports rows in this build are plain text blocks with no source link, so UR29 and UR111 have nothing to attach to.
- **F6. The seen-count, "0 of 13 / 3 of 13", is the one progress cue seen here**, and it counts acts tried, not effects understood.
- **F7. No surface exists for taken or lent powers, rule changes, awareness, time ability or foresight.** Nearest owners found: `cheat_ui.js` (the "Continuing insight" panel, read-only, one cheat), `ability_ladders.js`/`ability_ladder_figure.js` (ladders), `book_world_lotm_lives.js` and `src/ui/lives_figure.js` (lives), `src/ui/inspector.js` and `check_inspector.js`/`material_inspector.js` (typed inspectors), `src/ui/gu_moves_view.js` (move readiness). None reads a timeline, an awareness level or a rule object.
- **F8. Notice weight today:** everything is a log line; no toast or interrupt was seen in play (consistent with UR88). So the density problem is not here yet; it arrives when the lanes land many small effects per tick.

## 2. Peer evidence (what transfers, what does not)

Written from the maintainers' own published behavior as I know it; not re-fetched this session, so treat details as the pattern, not a quote. Names, art and wording stay ours.

| Peer | Pattern | Transfers | Does not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caves of Qud | Status lines with duration; a message log that names source and number ("You are stunned by X"); look-mode inspector with stacked effect rows | One-line cause plus a look-mode detail (EU1, EU4) | Qud's raw stat deltas are too dense for a life-sim idle log |
| Dwarf Fortress (Premium) | Announcements by category with a settings-level filter; a unit sheet with thought and need lists; reports jump to the unit | Category filters, jump-to-subject (EU1, EU17) | DF's announcement flood is the anti-pattern for EU16 |
| RimWorld | Alert stack by severity; hediffs and "Needs" list with a tooltip stating cause and numbers; letters (interrupt) versus messages (log) | Tiered interrupts and a cause breakdown tooltip (EU3, EU16) | Colonist-scale lists; one hero is the focus here |
| Crusader Kings 3 | Tooltips nest inside tooltips: a number's tooltip shows its modifiers, each modifier hovers to its own source; hold Alt to pin | Pinned nested detail on demand (EU1, EU4); keyboard pin is a requirement, not a bonus (UR43) | Nesting past two levels without a pin loses the reader |
| Slay the Spire, Monster Train | Intent icon over each enemy with a number; keyword glossary on hover that is itself keyworded | Telegraphed outcome plus keyword tooltips (EU12, EU20) | Fixed one-turn horizon |
| Into the Breach | The enemy's next action is drawn on the board before the player moves; the result is deterministic | The model for precognition: show the outcome in the place it lands, with a way to undo the player's own choice (EU12) | Perfect certainty; ours carries a confidence level and a "can change" marker |
| Outer Wilds | Ship log: a map of known facts as nodes with rumor and explored states; loop memory lives in the log, not the character | Memory held in a visible record; "what carried" is the log (EU9, EU10) | A fixed 22-minute loop |
| Deathloop | Per-loop knowledge (a visible lead list and times of day) the player carries; world resets, knowledge stays | Who carries what, shown per lead (EU10) | Single protagonist, single loop |
| Braid | Rewind with a visible scrub; objects marked by how time touches them (green glow: unaffected by rewind) | Per-object time marking; scrub as a view (EU8, EU11) | Real-time scrubbing; ours settles in discrete steps |
| Baba Is You | Rules are objects on the board you can push; the rule text is the rule | A conceptual rule change is a visible object with a scope (EU13, EU14) | Puzzle-grid layout; ours is a panel or a place chip |
| Disco Elysium | Skills speak in the log with a name, a check result and a voice; passive checks show their roll and difficulty on demand | A power or skill labelled by its own voice and the check it made (EU2, EU3) | Skills as personalities; use only where DSG13 voices already exist |

## 3. The standards

Each is checkable: the **Check** column names a pass or fail a reviewer or a test can apply at the target viewport. "Landed effect" means an effect settled by the engine (RPT8: written once, by the settling event).

### Reading an effect

| Code | Standard (default, dial, reason) | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU1 | **One line says what, why and from what.** Default: every landed effect that changes the player's world, body, knowledge or standing writes exactly one Reports row of the shape `<what changed> · <why> · <source>` in the game's own words, with the source name linking to its home (UR29) and a "Details" disclosure for the nested view (EU4). Dial: word order and length per world voice (DSG13); a hidden cause stays hidden and the row says "cause unknown" rather than omit the slot (UR80, UR136). Reason: F1, F5; peers (Qud, RimWorld) name cause and source in the line. | Pick any landed effect; the row has three slots, the source link opens the right home, the row has no engine token (EU3) |
| EU2 | **The source is a means, named as the world names it.** Default: a source slot reads "your Moonlight Gu", "the Vitality Leaf", "Old Hesk's charm", "your insight", never an id, a pack key or "effect". Taken or lent powers add "on loan from X" or "taken from Y" (EU7). Dial: per world. Reason: UR85, DSG15; Disco Elysium labels skills by their own voice. | Source slot text passes `check:rules` vocabulary lint and has a hover and keyboard route (UR134) |
| EU3 | **No engine vocabulary in the player line.** Default: the grammar's tokens (`bind thing, self, lasting`, `transform mind`) never reach Reports, tooltips or cards; they live in the inspector's engine view (EU5). Dial: a dev-only toggle. Reason: F2, RPT11, PRS lint; the Workshop line is the counterexample to fix when it graduates from a dev run. | Search Reports and card text for the effect-grammar token set; zero hits outside the debug view |
| EU4 | **A nested detail on demand, at most two levels, pinned by keyboard.** Default: "Details" on the row opens a pinned panel (UR43) with: the number or state that changed, before and after; each modifier with its own source link (CK3 pattern); duration and how it ends; what the player can do about it. A modifier's own detail is the second and last level; deeper facts open their home panel. Alt-hover is a quick peek only; Enter or click pins; Esc closes first (UR132). Dial: levels, per surface. Reason: CK3 nesting works only with a pin, and hover-only fails UR134. | Tab to a row, press Enter: pinned detail appears; Tab inside it works; Esc returns focus to the row |
| EU5 | **The inspector shows why an effect resolved, apart from the debug view.** Default: the player inspector (`src/ui/inspector.js` family) shows for the selected effect: the laws consulted (named in the world's words), reach against resistance as two labelled numbers with the margin, the roll or the fixed rule that decided, and what would have changed the result. This is a player view limited to what the character could know (UR80); a separate dev/debug view shows the engine record with ids and grammar tokens. Dial: how much of the margin shows (exact, banded, "narrowly"), by means (UR85); unknown reads as unknown (UR136). Reason: the existing inspectors are typed per domain and none explains a resolution (F7); Disco Elysium shows check and difficulty on demand. | Select a resolved effect: the panel lists laws, reach, resistance and margin; the debug toggle is a separate control that adds ids and tokens and is off by default |
| EU6 | **A standing effect lives in Condition, not only in the log.** Default: a timed or lasting effect shows in the Condition panel with name, source, time left in its own clock (TMR3) and how it ends, and leaves when it ends; the log row is the receipt (RPT3). Dial: a hidden effect shows its felt signs only (UR80). Reason: F3; UR83, UR92. | After a 30-minute effect lands, Condition lists it, counts down, and removes it at expiry with one "ended" row |

### Powers, loans and terms

| Code | Standard (default, dial, reason) | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU7 | **Taken or lent powers show their terms.** Default: a power card for an absorbed, copied, negated, borrowed or lent power shows: its holder and origin ("copied from X on day D"), the terms (charges, duration, cost, who can recall it, what negates it, what stops it working in the next world), and its current state (ready, spent, suppressed, on loan). Terms use the cheat card's adjective form (CHT12). Dial: how many terms show at a glance; the rest sit under Details (EU4). Reason: CHT16, CHT19, CHT20, CM7 (cheat inspector, proposed); absorb, copy and negate lanes otherwise ship a power with no visible cost. | A copied power's card lists origin, terms, state; a negated power shows who or what negated it and until when |
| EU8 | **One power sheet shape for every means.** Default: powers, cheats, skills and borrowed abilities share one row anatomy (UR119: glyph, name, key fact, trailing action) and one card anatomy, in the means' own dress (DSG15, UR94). Dial: dress per world. Reason: today the cheat panel, ladders and Gu moves each have their own form (F7). | Place three powers from different sources side by side with labels covered: row anatomy is identical, dress differs (UR94 two tests) |
| EU9 | **Cheats and system interfaces have a panel and an entry warning.** Default: a cheat or system interface (status window, store, quest list, ring spirit) has a named home panel under the player's own words for it, listing its card, state and reach (CM7); crossing into a world shows the CHT16 line in the WER confirm step ("Your insight carries. Your system store does not work here until [condition]"), one line per cheat, sourced from the same card data, read-only over existing cards. Dial: how loud, per world; a hidden cheat stays hidden until discovered (CHT10). Reason: CHT16 says a silent stop is a hidden loss. | Cross into a world with one carrying and one stopping cheat: the confirm step lists both; the panel afterward matches |

### Time abilities

| Code | Standard (default, dial, reason) | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU10 | **Who remembers is shown, by level.** Default: any rewind, loop or time effect states for the player and for each affected named person one awareness level from a short fixed scale (does not know; felt something; remembers the other time; remembers and can act on it), as a chip with a word, not a color alone. The scale is shared; the world words it (KNW3). Dial: levels shown per means (UR85); an unknown level reads "unknown" (UR136). Reason: Outer Wilds and Deathloop put memory in a visible record; WER9 and CHT14 need "what carries" on screen. | After a rewind: the player's chip and each witnessed person's chip appear on the timeline and in that person's view |
| EU11 | **A rewind or loop has a timeline view.** Default: a center view (UR89) in the UR79 timeline shape: past points as dated nodes, the current line marked, abandoned branches kept visible (SUI5), the chosen return point previewed with what would be undone before commit (UR95, UIV4). Braid pattern: objects the effect does not touch are marked. The view has a text list form for keyboard and screen readers. Dial: depth of history shown; branches per world. Reason: no surface exists (F7); TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE needs one; UR92. | Trigger a rewind in a fixture: the view opens, lists points, previews undo, and the committed result matches the preview |
| EU12 | **What carried is a ledger on the return.** Default: after a rewind, loop restart or crossing, one recap row ("Carried: your memory, your Gu, the ledger entry on X. Lost: the unspent coin, the debt to Y") lists items, knowledge, state and ties that carried and did not, from the same carry data the engine used (AD11, CHT14, UR81). Dial: grouping. Reason: Deathloop and Outer Wilds make carry the point of a loop. | The recap's two lists equal the engine's carry set in a test |
| EU13 | **Frozen or held actors are marked where they stand.** Default: during a time stop, hold or slow, each affected actor in the scene carries a visible state chip ("held", "slowed to 1/10") with its clock scope (TMR3) and its end condition if the character can know it; the scene header says whose clock is running. Actors outside the effect read normal. Dial: chip form per world. Reason: Braid marks time-immune objects; without a mark, a stopped room looks like a stuck game. | In a stop fixture, every actor has a chip or the header says "all but you"; time passing shows no tick for held actors |
| EU14 | **Precognition is a telegraphed outcome, with a confidence level and a "this future can change" marker.** Default: a foresight readout draws the predicted outcome in the place it would land (a line on the Here panel, a ghost row on the target's card) in the foresight's own form (UR85), with: a confidence word (certain, likely, possible) tied to the means' stated accuracy (CHT19), a dated horizon, and a marker "can change" that is true by default and drops only for a fixed event; it names what could change it when the character could know. Acting on it shows the result of the change next to the forecast, so a miss reads as the future changing, not the game lying (Into the Breach's telegraph, with our uncertainty added). Dial: horizon, confidence bands, per means. Reason: UR85 lists "foresight odds" as a readout without a form; TMR15. | A forecast shows outcome, confidence, horizon, marker; after the player changes it, the old forecast is marked "no longer coming" and kept in history |
| EU15 | **Time effects settle at stable moments and never replay on navigation.** Default: a skip, rewind or loop writes one settled event whose receipt carries the stop reason (TMR10), and re-opening the timeline view or panel writes nothing (RPT8, UR91). Reduced motion keeps the change as a static mark. Dial: effect intensity by stakes (X1-X7 ladder). Reason: UR91, TMR11. | Reload and re-open: no new Reports row; reduced-motion on: meaning intact |

### Rules as objects

| Code | Standard (default, dial, reason) | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU16 | **A conceptual rule change is a visible rule object with its scope.** Default: when a power changes a rule of the world (what counts as a lie here, who may lie, what fire does in this room), the change is a rule object: a short sentence in the world's words, a scope chip (this room, this scene, these people, this world, until sunset), its holder (the power and who wields it), a duration, and a "what it overrides" line. Rule objects list in a "Rules in force here" section on the Here panel and in the scene header; each opens a detail with its source and history (EU4, UR92). Baba Is You makes the rule the object; we keep it a panel row so idle play stays readable. Dial: how visible to those not aware (EU10); a hidden rule shows its felt signs only. Reason: F7; mechanism foundation covers rule powers and has no surface. | Apply a scoped rule change in a fixture: a row appears with sentence, scope, holder, duration; leaving the scope removes it from Here and keeps it in history |

### Density and attention

| Code | Standard (default, dial, reason) | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU17 | **Density limits for idle play.** Default: (a) at most one new Reports row per landed effect (EU1); (b) effects from the same source within one band fold into one row with a count ("Moonlight Gu: eased fatigue x6"), the newest value shown and the full list in Details; (c) at most three effect rows per game hour reach Reports at the default notice level, the rest fold into one "N smaller effects" row that opens a list; (d) during compressed time, one summary row per span (TMR12, RPT6) with a stop-list item standing out (UR88); (e) a repeating, unchanged effect writes nothing after its first row. Dial: the numbers, per player and per kind of notice (UR88). Reason: DF's announcement flood is the anti-pattern; F8 says the flood arrives when lanes land many small effects. | Run a 24-hour compressed span with twelve ticking effects in a fixture: Reports gains no more than the cap, folded rows carry counts, nothing is lost from the nested lists |
| EU18 | **Interrupts are only for stop-list stakes.** Default: only a choice that needs the player, a known event, a lethal edge or an exhausted supply may interrupt (UR88, TMR8); a landed effect, however strong, is a row, a Condition entry or a dot (UR13). A big effect earns a larger rendering only through the X1-X7 ladder (UR91) and always has its text row. Dial: per kind. Reason: RimWorld separates letters from messages; UR88 was accepted by Weibao ([log](../gm/USER_VISION_LOG_2026-11.md#notice-tiers-adopted-2026-11-14)). | Land a large effect with no choice pending: no modal, one row, one dot |
| EU19 | **Coming effects are forewarned once, in their home.** Default: a known coming effect (a duration ending, a debt, a loop reset, a forecast event) shows what, when, why and exits in the subject's home and the under-way list (UR83), and becomes a log row once settled. Dial: how early. Reason: UR83. | A scheduled effect shows in the under-way list before it lands and not in Reports until it does |

### Quality

| Code | Standard (default, dial, reason) | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU20 | **Keyword tooltips are keyword objects.** Default: a defined term in an effect row or card (reach, resistance, held, suppressed, on loan, certain) is a focusable keyword that opens one short definition with its numbers; the definition is the same text everywhere the term appears (UR117, UR135, Slay the Spire pattern). Dial: glossary depth. Reason: one term, one meaning. | The same keyword in Reports, a card and the inspector gives byte-identical definition text |
| EU21 | **Accessibility basics for effects.** Default: every state chip, marker and level has text, not color or icon alone (UR121, DSG); every effect row and timeline node is reachable by keyboard with a visible focus ring (UR126, UR7); live updates use a polite `role="status"` that does not repeat on reload; contrast meets UR37 and UR46 in all dress variants; reduced motion and a motion slider keep meaning (UR91); effect text has a screen-reader form for figures (UR98); no countdown needs reading faster than the player's own pace. Dial: none for the floor. Reason: UR126, UR134 and the accepted effects setting. | Tab-only walk through one effect, one timeline view and one rule object; axe-style check on contrast; reduced-motion on |
| EU22 | **The same line in three places is one line with links.** Default: a landed effect's text is authored once in the owning event row and every place that shows it (run view, Reports, Condition, history) reads that text or a link to it, not a re-rendered copy (RPT3, RPT8, UR147). Dial: none. Reason: F4. | Change the authored string in one place; every surface changes |

## 4. Lanes: which standard each must meet in its slice

All five lanes are in flight on their own branches (`claude/entry-points-89eb18`, `claude/director-cards-6ec212`, `claude/power-target-7da3a1`, `claude/timeline-5cc994`, `claude/mechanism-foundation-304cb3`). The "floor" column is the least a slice must show to be called UI-complete under AGENTS.md's UI gates (rebuilt browser evidence at the target viewport); the "next" column is what can follow.

| Lane | Floor (meet in this slice) | Next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| entry-points | EU9 (CHT16 line in the confirm step, from card data), EU12 (carried and lost ledger on return), EU2 (source names the method and the body), EU10 (does the world know the outsider; WER12 dial as a chip) | EU11 for loops that begin at entry |
| director-cards (cards carrying effects) | EU1 and EU22 (one authored line, three slots), EU3 (no tokens), EU17 and EU18 (fold and no interrupts unless stop-list), EU19 (forewarn the coming effect) | EU4 nested detail; EU5 once the inspector can read a card's resolution |
| power-target (absorb, copy, negate) | EU7 (terms, origin, state on the card), EU8 (the one power-sheet shape), EU1 and EU2 (source naming), EU6 (a standing negation in Condition) | EU5 (why a negate resolved: reach against resistance) |
| timeline (rewind, loop) | EU11 (timeline view with undo preview), EU10 (awareness chips), EU12 (what carried), EU15 (no replay on navigation), EU13 (held actors, if the slice has a stop) | EU14 for the forecast side of a loop |
| mechanism foundation (time, space, mind, chance, rule) | EU16 (rule objects with scope), EU14 (precognition form), EU13 (held actors for time-scope), EU5 (inspector explains resolution through laws consulted), EU21 (accessibility floor for every new surface) | EU20 keyword glossary once terms stabilise |

Cross-lane floor (every lane): EU1, EU3, EU17, EU21, EU22.

## 5. Gaps and how this proposal is tested

- Not reproduced: any time ability, power sheet, inspector explanation or Worlds lives list, because none is wired to a played surface on this build. Each standard's Check is written to be run in a DEV world fixture after its lane lands (AD36 item 11).
- F3 needs a rebuilt-browser confirm before it is filed as a defect; the headless text view may lag a standing-effect refresh.
- Mobile is out of scope (standing priority). Controllers and touch pin routes for EU4 are deferred with it.
- Peer notes in section 2 are from recall of the published behavior, not fresh sources; each transfer decision stands on the check column, not on the peer.
- The recommended first five, in order: **EU1** (the one-line shape), **EU17** (density caps, before more lanes land), **EU11** (timeline view, the largest missing surface), **EU7** (terms on every taken or lent power), **EU9** (cheat and system panel plus the entry warning).
