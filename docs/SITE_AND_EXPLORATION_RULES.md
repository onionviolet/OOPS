# Site and exploration rules

Standing design/decision rules requested by Weibao, 2026-10-16, for ruins,
natural sites, maps, discovery and restoration. [Original request and scope](USER_VISION.md#standing-site-rules-and-more-site-types-2026-10-16).
These capture the logical principles of the requested world-first approach.
They authorize no implementation, feature queue or universal site mechanic.
World/site examples and numeric balances in the linked palettes remain proposals.
Later user direction and source-qualified world laws retain authority.
Complexity remains welcome under the [current direction](USER_VISION.md#complexity-and-implementation-difficulty-2026-10-16):
bounded implementation is a sequencing method, not a ceiling on desired depth.

Use the applicable rules when deciding a concrete site; do not build every
possible supporting system. “Declare” means state the meaningful behavior in its
existing owner, not create another framework or fill a universal schema.

## Identity, world fit and scope

| Code | Standing rule | Prevents |
|---|---|---|
| SR1 | **Bind a place to its world, instance, era and existing geography.** Original regions can host original sites; canon fill stays within permitted places and source boundaries. | Invented canon landmarks and maps leading to the wrong history |
| SR2 | **A site's purpose, past and present condition must fit together.** Generate only consequential detail needed for the chosen experience; an unknown past may remain unknown. | Unrelated loot, apparatus and history; requiring a simulated civilization |
| SR3 | **World law changes supported actions and outcomes.** Theme does not grant a resource role, supernatural power, revival or reset. Label canon evidence versus original proposals. | Every world playing as a renamed dungeon; invented canon mechanics |
| SR4 | **Use the existing owners.** A site joins geography, objects, resources, making, history and knowledge; it does not create parallel stock, RNG, clock or truth stores. NPC-heavy simulation and generated layouts remain deferred unless separately selected. | Rebuilding the same systems under a site label or silently lifting deferrals |

## Discovery, information and maps

| Code | Standing rule | Prevents |
|---|---|---|
| SR5 | **Keep truth, evidence, claims and interpretation separate.** Preserve already committed causes and observed facts; refine only uncommitted detail within those constraints. A world law that actually changes reality is a separate operation. | Choosing a convenient past after a player's guess; treating every account as truth |
| SR6 | **A lead, location, entrance, surveyed extent, understanding and usable method are distinct.** An explicit supported action may establish several together; no need for artificial mandatory steps. | Finding an entrance revealing all rooms, or excessive ceremonial clicking |
| SR7 | **Maps are scoped knowledge artifacts.** Retain work/edition/copy or digital-equivalent identity, source, date, coverage, coordinate frame and relevant interpretation means. Markings are claims; copies retain their source lineage. | Infinite fresh truths from copies; unrelated maps counted as independent evidence |
| SR8 | **Buying information grants what the artifact/channel supports.** Possession does not itself prove markings, grant passage, ownership, understanding or survey completion. Validated actionable directions can guide travel without requiring a prior visit. | Omniscient paid reveals and maps that cannot usefully direct a first visit |
| SR9 | **Knowledge uses actual means and safe projections.** Reading, sensing, testing, translation and powers establish their supported scope. Protect sealed facts across names, prices, sorting, counts, previews and accessibility. | A map price or disabled button becoming an unsupported identification power |

## Exploration and useful actions

| Code | Standing rule | Prevents |
|---|---|---|
| SR10 | **Preparation answers an observed need or declared uncertainty.** Provide reachable clues to requirements, useful assessment or an honest warning of incomplete knowledge. Do not require a hidden packing list for ordinary advertised work. | Blind errands and unexplained refusal |
| SR11 | **Approaches follow actual body, tools, skills, resources and powers.** Support alternatives where those means exist; do not negate capabilities just to protect a puzzle or promise unsupported verbs. | Universal humanoid kits, arbitrary anti-power walls and fictional options |
| SR12 | **A meaningful site has a real reader for its output.** Materials, objects, knowledge, access, shelter and local functions can all count. A clue can point to a real later opportunity; not every visit needs immediate loot. | Empty restoration messages, write-only discoveries or compulsory treasure |
| SR13 | **Keep action costs and commitment legible through known information.** Inspection/previews consume no stock or draws; committed work follows declared costs and uncertainty. Record observations before destructive work when supported. | Hidden consumption, preview rerolls and accidental commitment |
| SR14 | **Declare continuation, withdrawal and aftermath for the selected activity.** Ordinary exploration must not introduce an undisclosed permanent trap or harsher loss policy. Source-qualified danger and chosen harsh play remain possible under their actual rules. | Surprise loss-policy changes or a universal harmlessness guarantee |

## Resources, learning and restoration

| Code | Standing rule | Prevents |
|---|---|---|
| SR15 | **Track actual custody, allocation and conservation.** Installed parts, carried objects, harvested batches, catalysts and residues have declared destinations. One resource cannot support mutually exclusive uses at once. | Duplicate salvage, free installation refunds and infinite material from processing |
| SR16 | **Separate intrinsic properties, condition and external support.** A site's field, moisture or power can matter without becoming an item's permanent trait. | Extracting unlimited local power or rewriting collected material truth |
| SR17 | **Learning and function are separate results.** Possession, readability, comprehension, demonstration, mastery, repair and reproduction need their own supported links. | An unread book granting mastery; repairing a machine granting its entire blueprint |
| SR18 | **Restoration has an explicit purpose.** Stabilization, original operation, preservation, repurposing and habitat recovery can compete. No original state is universally best. Model only selected consequences. | A compulsory perfect-restoration checklist or penalties from unimplemented ecology |
| SR19 | **Access, discovery and repair do not establish ownership or issuing authority.** Respect existing permission and custody rules without requiring a new society simulation. | A temple automatically granting a priesthood, or an abandoned mine becoming owned on entry |

## Time, persistence and decision discipline

| Code | Standing rule | Prevents |
|---|---|---|
| SR20 | **Renewal and change need a declared cause and clock.** Salvage, deposits, fluid supply, living growth and magical renewal can use different laws. Returning, reading a map or reloading is not a replenishment cause. | Refilled ruins and blanket once-ever limits |
| SR21 | **Use accepted world-time, pause and routine policies.** No silent job acceptance, caretaker debt or wall-clock absence punishment. A return can honestly show no change. | Mandatory daily maintenance or invented offline simulation |
| SR22 | **Persist commitments and knowledge independently.** Preserve identity, revisions, terms, observations, installed parts, removed stock, pending work and outcomes through the supported save path. Corrections retain provenance; updates cannot silently reroll a site. | Forgotten repairs, paid map loss, erased evidence and duplicated results |
| SR23 | **Rules constrain a selected slice, not demand all adjacent features.** Use bounded authored causes and transitions where adequate. Extract shared infrastructure only for actual consumers. Distinguish proposed dependencies from real blockers. | A temple requiring a theology engine or a mine requiring full geology |
| SR24 | **Prove the claimed layer.** Document proposals as proposals; later verify actual discovery, use, change and persistence for the selected slice. Player-facing work follows UI rules and rebuilt desktop checks. | Documentation, test counts or screenshots being presented as playable acceptance |

## Applying a rule without another design interview

For a concrete decision, identify its world/site authority and actual reader,
apply the relevant SR rule, then choose the smallest coherent reversible behavior.
Keep unsupported certainty unknown and special world mechanics explicitly scoped.
Escalate only a real conflict, consequential direction change or irreversible
choice requiring the player's decision. Do not ask the director to decide every
book shelf, mineral adjective or ordinary restoration step.
