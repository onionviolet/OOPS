## The means principle

**What anyone can do is decided by the means they actually hold, on both
sides.** Not by what would be convenient for the plot, not by what their
world allows in general, and not by what the player's UI happens to
show. A world sets which means are common; a character either holds one
or doesn't.

A means is anything on hand that makes an action possible: a skill, an
innate power or cheat, an item, a contact, access to an institution,
prior knowledge, money, a body that can do it, or simply being there.

### Domains decided by means

| Code | Domain | Means that enable it | What happens without them |
|---|---|---|---|
| MN1 | **Knowing** | Appraisal, senses, informants, archives, prior contact | Impressions only (see knowledge doc) |
| MN2 | **Finding** | Tracking, scent, scrying, a lock-on mark, bounty networks, informants in each town | They can't find you. Leaving no trail is a real defense |
| MN3 | **Reaching** | Roads, mounts, ships, flight, portals, teleport anchors, a world-crossing means | Distance protects. An enemy in another world can't touch you without a crossing |
| MN4 | **Communicating** | Letters, messengers, sending stones, telepathy, a sect's signal fires | News travels as fast as their means. Reinforcements only come if someone can call them |
| MN5 | **Harming** | A weapon that can touch the target: cursed energy for curses, spirit weapons for ghosts, a sure-hit for Infinity | No means, no damage. Some enemies are untouchable to you until you get one, and the reverse |
| MN6 | **Defending and escaping** | Barriers, armor, escape talismans, a safehouse, a fast mount, a hidden identity | You stand and take it |
| MN7 | **Healing** | Medicine, healers, regeneration, reverse cursed technique, rest in a safe place | Wounds stay; scars and lingering conditions follow |
| MN8 | **Making** | Tools, workshops, recipes, materials, a skilled hand | You can describe it but not make it |
| MN9 | **Persuading and coercing** | Money, leverage, reputation, rank, a hostage, a contract, charm | Words only, and they can say no |
| MN10 | **Hiding** | Concealment skills, masks, seals, forged papers, a crowd, a remote place | Anyone with a finding means finds you |
| MN11 | **Enduring** | The right body, gear or power for heat, cold, poison, miasma, vacuum | The environment wins |
| MN12 | **Timing** | Clocks, calendars, prophecy, a regressor's memory, a lookout | You learn when things happen as they happen |
| MN13 | **Remembering** | A notebook, records, a perfect memory, a scribe | Details fade; old knowledge goes stale faster |
| MN14 | **Legitimacy** | Papers, seals, titles, registry entries, a sponsor | Doors stay closed; guards ask questions |

### What follows

- **Means are the plan's preconditions.** An NPC (or the player) can only
  attempt an action whose means they hold. A villain who hates you still
  can't send assassins across worlds without a crossing means, and can't
  send them at all without money or loyalty.
- **Means are targets.** Break their scrying bowl, kill the messenger,
  burn the archive, seal the appraiser's eyes, steal the crossing key.
  Cutting a means is often better than fighting the person.
- **Means are loot.** Defeated enemies drop what made them dangerous: a
  truth lens, a sending stone, a map with your hideout marked on it.
- **Means asymmetry is strategy.** You can see them and they can't see
  you; they can reach you and you can't reach them. Each mismatch is a
  situation with obvious moves.
- **Means cost something.** Charges, money, favors, fatigue, time,
  attention, exposure. A free unlimited means is a cheat and should feel
  like one.
- **Means can be noticed.** Using one is an action in the fiction, and
  sensitive targets can feel, trace or block it.
- **Idle time follows means too.** What happens while the player is away
  is limited by what NPCs could actually do in that time with what they
  hold. An enemy with no finding means doesn't track you down overnight.
- **The player's UI is a means.** In worlds where the system or Notebook
  is part of the fiction, losing it (the system dies, a memory curse)
  degrades what the player can see and do.

## Appraisal levels

A ladder of depth. Each level includes the ones below it. A means has a
maximum level, and the contest (observer factors versus concealment and
relative rank) decides how much of that maximum a given reading reaches.

| Code | Level | Reveals | Example output |
|---|---|---|---|
| AP0 | **Glance** | What eyes see, no skill | `a tall woman in a grey coat` |
| AP1 | **Identify** | Category and name or label | `Human. Name: Ilse Varr` (or the name they're registered under) |
| AP2 | **Estimate** | Rough tier, relative to you | `stronger than you` / `far beyond you` / `weaker` |
| AP3 | **Measure** | Numbers or grades within scope, in bands at first | `level 40 to 45`, later `level 43` |
| AP4 | **List** | Names of skills, techniques, conditions | `Skills: Swordsmanship, ???, Concealment` |
| AP5 | **Detail** | Rules of a listed thing | `Concealment: hides level from observers below rank 5` |
| AP6 | **Hidden layers** | Traits, curses, bloodline, seals, status effects | `Sealed bloodline (dormant)` |
| AP7 | **History** | Origin, past events, where an item has been | Psychometry; `forged in the north, used to kill twice` |
| AP8 | **Intent and fate** | Goals, grudges, fate threads, future risk | Rare and costly; ties to hidden intent |
| AP9 | **Rules** | The laws the target runs on | Seeing how a world, a domain or a god works. Extremely rare |

Subtleties of the ladder:
- **Precision before depth.** A low-rank Measure gives bands (`40 to 45`);
  rank narrows them. Two appraisers at the same level can differ in
  precision.
- **Relative readings are cheap and honest.** AP2 is what most fighters
  actually have: a gut sense of who would win. It needs no numbers and
  fits almost every world.
- **The ceiling.** Most means can't read past the observer's own rank
  (the Kairos rule in the contest section). Above it, results degrade to
  AP2 (`far beyond you`) or worse.
- **Levels can be skipped.** A physician's eye jumps straight to AP6 for
  poison and disease while knowing nothing of the patient's skills.
- **Self-appraisal has blind spots.** Reading yourself can stop short of
  what others with a different scheme would see.

## Appraisal types

Appraisal isn't one skill. It varies along several axes, and a means
picks a value on each.

### By target

| Target | Typical specialist | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| People | Fighter's sense, appraisal, soul sight | Strength, skills, condition, intent (rare) |
| Creatures | Hunter's eye, beast lore | Species, weak points, habits, danger |
| Items | Smith's eye, merchant's eye, identify | Quality, material, enchantments, curses, value |
| Food and medicine | Cook, physician, poison taster | Freshness, poison, effects, dose |
| Money and documents | Moneychanger, clerk, forger | Counterfeits, forged seals, real issuer |
| Texts and manuals | Scholar, sect elder | Whether a technique manual is real, complete or sabotaged |
| Places and formations | Geomancer, formation master, scout | Wards, traps, ley lines, how a formation works |
| Contracts and vows | Lawyer, binding-vow expert | Hidden clauses, loopholes, penalties |
| Markets | Merchant | Real price, scarcity, who's buying |
| Weather and omens | Sailor, farmer, diviner | What's coming, how soon |
| Groups | General, spymaster | An army's real strength, morale, a sect's politics |

**Specialists beat generalists in their domain.** A generic appraisal
skill reads a sword's grade; a master smith reads its flaw, its maker
and how long until it breaks. A merchant's eye reads value to the coin
and nothing about danger.

### By channel

| Channel | Notes |
|---|---|
| Sight | Ordinary looking, trained or not |
| Energy sight | Mana, qi, cursed energy, holy, void; blind to things without energy (a heavenly-restricted fighter reads as nothing) |
| Soul sight | Sees through bodies and disguises; blind to soulless things |
| Smell, touch, taste, hearing | Physical senses at skill rank (E7) |
| System data | A status window reads fields its schema has; foreign powers show as `???` |
| Divination | Reads fate and likely futures; hostile beings distort it |
| Memory reading | Psychometry, mind reading; costs and risks |
| Records | Registries, archives, rumors: someone else's past reading |

Each channel has its own blind spots, which is why concealment works
against some observers and not others.

### By mode

| Mode | Options |
|---|---|
| Activation | Passive (always on, shallow) or active (chosen, deeper, noticeable) |
| Time | Glance, careful look, study over days, ritual |
| Range | Touch, sight line, area, anywhere with a mark |
| Scope | One target, a scan of everyone nearby, a continuous lock-on |
| Cost | Free, fatigue, mana, charges, money, lifespan |
| Notice | Silent, felt by sensitive targets, felt by everyone |

### By output

| Output | Fits |
|---|---|
| Numbers | Status-window worlds, scientific worlds |
| Words | `strong`, `wounded`, `lying`; most worlds |
| Colors and auras | Nine-Eyes-style worlds; `colorless` means unreadable, not safe |
| Comparisons | `stronger than you`, `like a mountain` |
| Feelings | Dread, warmth, pressure; ordinary people's appraisal |
| Visions | Psychometry, divination |
| Lists | System windows, sect registries |

Output also shapes VFX: numbers get the status-box look, feelings get the
emotion effects, visions get the ghost-text look.

### By schema

What the means thinks matters: levels, realms, grades, price, danger
rating, karma, morality, bloodline purity. A means only reads what its
schema has, and maps everything else to its nearest field or to `???`
(schema mismatch, in the contest section).

## Little subtleties

| Code | Subtlety | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| LS1 | **What `???` means** | Different failures look different: `???` (beyond scope), `[blocked]` (concealment), `cannot be measured` (too far above), `error` (schema mismatch), blank (nothing there, or a perfect concealment). Each tells the reader something, and the VFX should differ |
| LS2 | **Snapshot timing** | A reading is of that moment. Reading someone mid-buff, injured or drunk misleads later |
| LS3 | **Base versus equipped** | Is that strength theirs or their armor's? Better means separate them |
| LS4 | **Which name** | The registered name, the true name, the name they believe is theirs, or an alias. Each means reads a different one |
| LS5 | **Observer bias** | The appraiser's prejudice tints the words: a snob reads commoners as weaker. Novices are overconfident |
| LS6 | **Revealing your means** | Using an active appraisal can tell a sensitive target that you have one, and sometimes which kind. Your means is now known to them |
| LS7 | **Two-way channels** | Some readings open a link both ways: you look into them, they look back |
| LS8 | **Consent** | A friend who lets you read them gives a full, easy reading. Contracts can require disclosure |
| LS9 | **Surface versus core** | A disguise changes the surface reading; deep channels see through it. A clever disguise layers both |
| LS10 | **Items that lie** | Cursed or tricky items report false properties to ordinary appraisal |
| LS11 | **Context** | Fighting leaks more than sitting; strong emotion slips concealment; sleep drops it |
| LS12 | **Fatigue and overuse** | Headaches, nosebleeds, temporary blindness after too many deep reads |
| LS13 | **Etiquette and law** | Appraising a noble is an insult or a crime; shops and cities can have anti-appraisal wards |
| LS14 | **Aggregates** | Reading a crowd or an army gives totals and outliers, not individuals |
| LS15 | **Appraisers appraised** | Items or beings that read you back the moment you read them |
| LS16 | **Confidence shown** | Good means say how sure they are; poor ones sound sure when they're wrong (the dangerous "wrong but confident" result) |
| LS17 | **Units and translation** | A realm in one world maps badly to a level in another; conversions lose detail |
| LS18 | **Habituation** | Reading the same person often gets cheaper and catches changes faster |

## Wider subtleties

### Society shaped by its common means

If a means is common in a world, society organizes around it.
- **Readings as status.** Where everyone can see levels, people are sorted
  by them: jobs, marriages, seating at dinner. Hiding becomes normal and
  rude to question, or suspicious.
- **Certificates and authority.** An appraisal guild's certificate is
  trusted over the truth. Forged certificates are a crime and a trade.
- **Test stones and mis-sorting.** Entrance tests read only what they were
  built for, so talent outside the schema gets thrown out (the classic
  "trash talent" opening).
- **Courts and evidence.** Some readings count as proof. Others don't,
  and a world can argue about which.
- **Professions.** Appraisers, forgers, concealment tailors, mask makers,
  information brokers. Each is a fitting NPC or a life-sim job.

### Arms races over history

Concealment and appraisal improve against each other over a world's
history. An old concealment technique fails against modern appraisal;
an ancient one nobody remembers how to read beats it. A regressor's
knowledge of future techniques includes future means.

### Information hazards

Some targets hurt the reader: eldritch beings, gods, sealed things,
rules too big for the mind. Higher-level reads raise the risk (AP8, AP9).
The contest's backlash outcome covers this.

### Means across worlds

- Means travel with their holder. An appraisal skill from a system world
  read in a cultivation world maps through translation (LS17), or fails.
- Locals react to foreign means: a status window glimpsed by a
  cultivator is a curiosity, a threat or a treasure to steal.
- Some worlds suppress foreign means; some amplify them. That's a world
  rule, not a judgment on the character.

### Means and fairness

- The player is never denied what their character's means should show.
- Anything hidden has some route: a means you could acquire, borrow,
  buy or build.
- Enemies don't get means for free because a fight needs to be hard.
  If they're dangerous, it's because of what they hold, and that's
  findable.

## Implementation considerations

| Code | Topic | Proposal |
|---|---|---|
| IMP1 | **Means records** | `means = {id, kind, holder, domains (MN codes), max level (AP), channel, target types, mode, range, cost, notice, counters, schema}`. Skills, items and contacts that are means carry this as a capability on their existing records |
| IMP2 | **One gate function** | `can(actor, verb, target, context)` checks means for every MN domain. The player's actions and NPC planning use the same function, so both sides follow one rule |
| IMP3 | **One reading function** | `read(observer, target, means, context)` runs the existing contest and returns a graded outcome at some AP level, with confidence and possible error |
| IMP4 | **Knowledge records** | The reading writes to the observer's knowledge record with the means id, level reached, timestamp and confidence. Ordinary NPCs keep impression tags; record-keepers get fuller records |
| IMP5 | **Knowledge filter** | All text and VFX render through the viewer's knowledge (F3 in the [cheat feasibility audit](audits/cheat-implementation-2026-10-10/readers/C_feasibility.md), VP levels in the presentation doc). Truth strings are referenced by id only |
| IMP6 | **Failure wording** | LS1's distinct failure strings come from the outcome type, not ad hoc text |
| IMP7 | **NPC means budget** | NPCs get means from their role, faction and world commonness, generated once and saved, so a bandit doesn't gain a scrying bowl just because the scene needs one |
| IMP8 | **Persistence** | Means on existing records are capability data; readings and impression tags are new save data. One migration with empty defaults, plus an old-save import test |
| IMP9 | **Tests** | No means, no information; a means never reads past its max level; failure strings match outcomes; no truth text in any output below the level that reveals it (also guards sealed secrets) |
| IMP10 | **Native realm** | The native enemy tooltip already shows stats (`display.js` around 5061 to 5188). Proposal: model it as a built-in AP3 means everyone in the native realm holds, which keeps current behavior and fits the principle. A change would be a recorded compatibility decision |

### A first slice (proposal)

1. Means records for one skill (a basic appraisal at AP2 max) and one
   item (a lens that reaches AP4 for items only).
2. `read()` with the contest and three outcomes (relative tier, a band,
   `[blocked]`).
3. Impression tags for one ordinary enemy type that remember your last
   notable move.
4. One `can()` check on an NPC action: a bandit can only follow you to the
   next location if he has a tracking means.

Acceptance: from a fresh origin, appraise with and without the skill,
see the lens read an item but not a person, see the blocked string
against a concealed target, and watch the tracker follow you while the
plain bandit doesn't. Save, reload and import an old save.

## Expanded: the life of a means, counters, places and more (2026-10-10)

### The life of a means

| Code | Stage | Routes | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ML1 | **Gaining** | Learn, train, awaken, inherit, buy, rent, borrow, steal, loot, build, be granted, bond (a familiar, a spirit) | Each route leaves a trace: a teacher who knows you have it, a seller who remembers, an owner who wants it back |
| ML2 | **Keeping** | Charges, repair, feeding a familiar, practice to avoid rust, paying an informant monthly, renewing a license | Upkeep runs while idle, so a neglected means degrades (the idle decay VFX) |
| ML3 | **Losing** | Stolen, broken, sealed by a curse, confiscated at a city gate, forgotten (memory loss), outgrown, a contact dies or defects | Losing a means changes what the UI shows: numbers turn back into words, a dossier stops updating |
| ML4 | **Recovering** | Reforge, re-learn faster than the first time, ransom it back, find a replacement contact | Re-learning is cheaper (the skill remembers), which softens losses without removing them |
| ML5 | **Passing on** | Gift, sell, bequeath, teach, betray | Heirlooms can carry their former owners' habits or marks |

### Means that are people

A contact is a person with their own goals, not a tool. They:
- **Can refuse**, overcharge, or want something besides money.
- **Can be wrong**, and their mistakes look like confidence.
- **Can be turned**, so the other side feeds you false reports through
  them (planted intel).
- **Can be found out**, which puts them in danger and makes your network
  smaller.
- **Can die**, and that's a loss in the relationship sense, not just
  the capability sense.

Borrowed means come with obligation. Borrowing a sect's archive access
means owing the sect.

### Combining means

Two means together can unlock a domain neither covers alone.

| Combination | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| Finding + reaching | Hunting someone down |
| Knowing + communicating | Warning allies, or calling for help |
| Informants + sending stones + an archive | An intelligence network, a faction-level means |
| Appraisal + a map | Scouting a region's threats from a distance |
| Hiding + legitimacy | Living under a false identity for years |
| Timing + reaching | Being where things happen before they happen |
| Harming + finding | Assassination |

Factions are largely bundles of combined means. Taking out one piece
(the stone, the archive, the courier) breaks the combination.

### Qualities beyond level

Two means at the same appraisal level can differ on:

| Quality | Range |
|---|---|
| Reliability | Always works / sometimes fails / fails under stress |
| Speed | Instant / minutes / days |
| Stealth | Silent / felt by sensitives / obvious |
| Range | Touch / sight / region / anywhere with a mark |
| Durability | Permanent / wears out / single use |
| Attunement | Anyone can use it / owner only / bloodline only |
| Legality | Allowed / licensed / contraband |
| Condition | Fine / damaged / unstable |

This is where most of the flavor between look-alike means comes from.

### Counters are means too

Every means has counters, and the counters are means held by the other
side.

| Means | Typical counters |
|---|---|
| Appraisal | Concealment, masks, anti-appraisal wards, false status displays, being far above |
| Tracking | No trail, water, crowds, false trails, a portal |
| Scrying | Wards, moving often, a blank spot in fate |
| Informants | Paying them more, turning them, never meeting in public |
| Sending stones | Jamming, intercepting, killing the courier who carries the pair |
| Reaching | Distance, a sealed border, a hidden location |
| Harming | Immunity, a barrier, untouchability, not being there |
| Legitimacy | Exposing the forgery, a rival claim, a purge of the registry |

Counterplay is then a matter of reading which means the other side holds
and which counters you hold, which keeps fights and intrigue grounded.

### Places change means

Place adjectives modify what works there:
- **Dead zones:** no magic, no system, no cursed energy. Everyone falls
  back to physical means.
- **Warded buildings:** a bank, a palace, a sect hall block appraisal and
  scrying inside.
- **Noise and crowds:** hearing-based and energy-based sensing blur.
- **Fog, darkness, deep water:** sight fails; smell and sound matter more.
- **Amplifying places:** a ley line, a temple, a domain. Some means get
  stronger, others go wild.
- **Legal zones:** a city where carrying a truth lens is a crime; a
  border where foreign means are confiscated.

### Organizations escalate means

When a junior fails, a faction sends someone with better means. A
sect's escalation chain:

1. A disciple who sensed something odd (AP2, no follow-up).
2. An outer elder with real sensing and a messenger (AP3, communicating).
3. An inner elder with a tracking artifact (finding, reaching).
4. The sect's archive and spymaster (knowing, timing).
5. The patriarch, whose means are a world event.

Each step only happens if the previous one learned enough to justify it
and had a way to report it. Killing the disciple before he reports ends
the chain at step 1 (and a missing disciple is its own clue).

### Reading someone's means

Appraising **what someone holds** is its own useful read:
- Visible kit gives it away: a scrying bowl on a desk, a sending stone
  on a cord, a sect token.
- Behavior gives it away: they always know where you went, so they have
  a finding means.
- An AP4 or better reading can list means directly.
- Knowing their means tells you what they can do next, which is more
  useful than knowing their level.

### Asking instead of reading

The cheapest appraisal is asking. People answer with:
- **The truth**, when it costs nothing or they want to impress.
- **A rounded version** ("about level forty").
- **A lie**, when it's useful.
- **A refusal**, which is information too.

Self-reported numbers are a social means with its own reliability.
Résumés, sect rank badges and adventurer cards are institutional
versions, and they can be forged (LS and the wider subtleties).

### Canon, the natural course and means

Canon characters' means are part of why canon events happen. A canon
hero finds the villain because of a specific means (an item, a contact,
a skill). Take that means away, for example by buying the item first or
befriending the contact, and the canon event can't happen as written.
That's a disturbance in the [timelines](TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE.md)
sense, and a clean way for the player to change a story without
fighting anyone.

### Showing means in the UI

- **A capabilities view.** What your character can do by domain:
  `Find: tracking (novice), informant in Ashford`. `Reach: none beyond
  walking`. It makes the principle legible.
- **Missing-means hints on actions.** A disabled action can say
  `You'd need a way to find him`. This has to follow the existing
  disclosure rule in `src/action_legibility.js`, which only shows
  requirements the observer qualifies to know and never reveals a hidden
  alternative. A means the character has never heard of stays unnamed.
- **Means as inventory.** Items that are means show a small domain tag.
- **Means loss shows.** When a means is lost, what it provided visibly
  fades from the UI with a short line saying why.

### More appraisal angles

| Code | Angle | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| AA1 | **Growth paths** | Use-raised appraisal branches into specialties (smith's eye, physician's eye) or fuses channels (energy plus soul sight) at higher ranks |
| AA2 | **Progressive reveal** | Fields fill in one per level, each with its own confidence; the reading improves visibly with repeated looks (LS18) |
| AA3 | **Misfires** | A failed read that returns something absurd (`Species: chair`) in comedic worlds; a dangerous wrong read in serious ones |
| AA4 | **Readings that change treatment** | Already in the contest section; add that treatment follows the *reader's* conclusion, so a misread sets prices and suspicion wrongly too |
| AA5 | **Appraising the player** | NPC readings of you show up only through behavior and dialogue (knowledge works both ways), unless you hold a means to see how you're read |
| AA6 | **Shared readings** | A party's readings pool into one picture. A companion with a different channel covers your blind spot |
| AA7 | **Reading for others** | Appraisal as a service: you read items for a merchant, people for a matchmaker, suspects for a guard |
| AA8 | **Appraising without looking** | Records, rumors and testimony as remote appraisal, with distance and staleness penalties |

### Suggestions

- **G1.** Make the capabilities view and the missing-means hint the first
  visible piece. They show the principle to the player cheaply, and
  `action_legibility.js` already has the disclosure rules they need.
- **G2.** Add a "means" field to [world cards](WORLD_CARDS.md): which
  means are common, rare or absent there, and which place effects apply.
  That's how a world says how readable it is without a separate system.
- **G3.** Prototype one organization's escalation chain in the native
  realm (a guard post that can call for help only if a runner gets
  away). It tests finding, communicating and the chain with very little
  content.
