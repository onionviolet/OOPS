## F1. Result and comparison standard

**Multiverse Loot Hunter remains the strongest documented direct fit. RetroMUD and 4Dimensions deserve much more attention than their visibility suggests. Lost Souls and Genesis are particularly useful for combining acquired capabilities with institutions.** No inspected game establishes the entire requested combination of deep independent worlds, unlimited cumulative powers, persistent companions/home consequences, and offline idle play.

This is a ranked mechanism scout, not a claim that no other exact match exists. The strongest new direction beyond the seed report is to read actual MUD travel, advancement and affiliation rules. Chinese searches also found two unusually close advertised premises, but both are unreleased.

The five independent criteria are:

| Code | Question | Look-alike that does not establish it |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Does the same character continue through the transition? | An account multiplier or a replacement hero |
| C2 | Do destinations change meaningful rules, institutions or ordinary activity? | Biomes, enemy skins or larger numbers alone |
| C3 | Can something acquired there survive departure and have a consumer elsewhere? | A local quest buff or a separate minigame's currency |
| C4 | Can acquisitions combine, and under what actual restrictions? | A list of mutually exclusive classes |
| C5 | Does returning change a home, relationship, job or later expedition? | A menu that merely starts the next map |

**Evidence labels:** D = documented by developer/store/help; S = source inspected statically; W = community-authored material on a game-hosted wiki, qualified as such; I = inference or original YAIRPG proposal; U = unresolved/unavailable. **Tested gameplay: none.** Public pages and source files were read; no game was launched, no save was created and no MUD server connection was attempted. A platform listing is not proof of operation on this Mac.

Direction reconciled against [PROJECT.md](../../../PROJECT.md), [USER_VISION.md](../../USER_VISION.md), [multiverse structures](../../worlds/MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES.md), and [carryover considerations](../../worlds/world-depth-2026-10-13/17_CARRYOVER_AND_RETROFIT.md). These games supply comparisons, not a new requirement for universal suppression, obligatory resets, fixed costs or a home-first opening. The structures owner's dated implementation readback was not re-audited as current runtime truth.

### F3. Details worth extracting from the strongest text games

**G02: world difference can be functional before it is huge.** RetroMUD's [Wysoom world help](https://www.retromud.org/proxy/help?=Worlds/wysoom) connects an underwater environment with knowledge carved into durable materials, equipment culture and portal ships. Separate environmental description from enforced simulation: the prose alone does not establish hydrodynamic weapon penalties. Its [transport help](https://www.retromud.org/proxy/help?=Movement/interplanetary_transport) describes different planetary transport mechanisms and differing destination reliability. The reusable idea is an authored transport ecology: learning a route, acquiring a suitable body or arranging a carrier can each solve a different part of travel. Idle/offline advancement is not established, and multiplayer party assumptions should not become YAIRPG requirements.

**G03: class history is not the same operation as destination travel.** The public [4dmud repository](https://github.com/4dmud/mud) describes the matching four-era setting. At revision `d2ee92671693f2f0eac88d7d5f9d86bebc17d89c`, [remort.cpp](https://github.com/4dmud/mud/blob/d2ee92671693f2f0eac88d7d5f9d86bebc17d89c/remort.cpp#L52) resets level and experience, shifts current/prior class slots, and has a class-membership predicate accepting the current and three remembered classes. A code comment discusses broader mastery; that comment alone is not executable proof of all-skills access. This repository was not matched to the running server. [Quest catalogue text](https://4dimensions.org/wiki/index.php/Quests) documents regional trading and occupations; no puzzle solutions are reproduced here. Useful question: can a livelihood earned in one era finance a persistent house while the same traveler explores another? Exact live retention remains a help/runtime gate.

**G04: acquisition, membership and capability need separate records.** The Lost Souls [Travelers help and notes](https://wiki.lostsouls.org/Travelers_(of_Ganesha)) distinguish recurring and permanent bestowals and describe navigation marks, invocations and affiliation conflicts. This page mixes reproduced help with player comments, including conflicting accounts, so it is W evidence rather than a current server trace. The broader mechanism worth studying is multiple independent grants with explicit eligibility. A lesson learned, an active institutional favor and permission to use a facility need not disappear together when a relationship changes. That last sentence is an original YAIRPG proposal, not a claim about every Lost Souls guild.

**G06: secondary careers can connect combat and ordinary life.** Genesis [craft guilds](https://www.genesismud.org/craftGuilds.php) show that a membership slot can represent a trade rather than a combat class. Read particular admissions, tools and products before treating all listed trades as equally complete. For YAIRPG, adapt an institutional offer that teaches one useful procedure and recognizes its student elsewhere, rather than importing an entire guild system. No live permissions or cross-world recognition were tested.

## P1. Useful subtle interactions for YAIRPG

All rows are original proposals, not accepted product direction or new implementation requirements.

| Code | Interaction | Concrete player consequence and important distinction |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Foreign acquisition × home consumer | A recruited foreign specialist can teach, operate a workshop or assist a later expedition. Owning their contract, their consent/presence, having tools and knowing their method are separate states. G01/G06 suggest the loop; existing NPC/workshop owners supply YAIRPG foundations. |
| P2 | Transport × cargo × learned ability | A fast portal can have authored cargo limits while a carrier route transports equipment. Learned technique retention should be evaluated independently. G27 supplies a concrete transport distinction; do not generalize its naked-portal rule to every world. |
| P3 | Body × environmental reach × institution | An aquatic ally, a breathing method or local transport could each enable a route. Entry does not automatically grant citizenship, guild admission or underwater practice. G02/G07 are useful comparisons. Avoid turning all environment differences into universal suppression. |
| P4 | Local lesson × portable mastery × active patron | Keep a trained capability, a lent tool and a patron-supported effect distinct. If support stops, evaluate actual dependencies; do not erase a whole biography. G04/G06 motivate the separation, but their restrictions are local rules. |
| P5 | Imported power × existing practical skill | A new thermal power can change native smelting or cooking efficiency if an actual consumer accepts it. G13/G16 suggest explicit conversions and triggers. Combinations need readable effect order and caps where authored, rather than a generic synergy bonus for every pair. |
| P6 | Career × world time × away progression | Leaving a workplace can pause work, delegate it, or resolve a bounded receipt under that world's rules. MUD server clocks, G11 banked time and G18 offline gains are different models. Choose per activity and show what progressed. |
| P7 | Return × changed local access | A foreign expedition's result can change settlement stock, a job offer or a faction welcome. G21/G23 suggest useful consumers. Distinguish durable history from respawning combat supply and from a new branch instance. |
| P8 | Accumulation × legibility | Preserve actual acquisitions while offering named preparations, source-aware filters and clear current blockers. A bounded active loadout can be one authored option; it is not a reason to make the traveler forget everything else. G03/G13/G15 expose different composition choices to compare. |
| P9 | Home development × opportunity cost | One imported material can support an instrument, a gift, a sale or a facility. A home matters when it changes future choices, not just because its storage count increases. Use existing custody/craft consumers before inventing another estate currency. |
| P10 | Discovery × reproducibility | Preserve the learned route or method, not just a completed marker. G11's route automation suggests routines that stop on changed prerequisites and retain the last useful receipt. Re-evaluate plans after destination rules or equipment change. |
