# RetroMUD and 4Dimensions: useful absorption into YAIRPG

2026-10-14, America/New_York. **Proposal-only synthesis requested by Weibao.** Read alongside the [donor findings](RETROMUD_AND_4D_DEPTH.md) and [reference library](MUD_SOURCE_GUIDE.md). This pass checks existing coverage and proposes refinements; it does not implement gameplay, accept a new rule, change world priority or dispatch work.

## F10. Recommendation

The strongest gains are **more useful applications of acquired methods, service careers with real customers, and carryover whose present limits are visible**. YAIRPG already contains much of the necessary vocabulary and several concrete examples. The MUDs help sharpen connections between those pieces.

In particular, do not restart “foreign knowledge becomes useful at home” as a new system. Current source already gives the Cyorian notebook a mundane home-bench consumer. It also separates a permanently learned workshop method from leased assistance. The useful next question is which additional task, person or destination should read an existing acquisition, and what actually makes that application possible.

All world examples below are **original gameplay proposals** within the selected world's existing source contract. They do not establish new canon facts, automatic foreign-power compatibility, current playable routes or a need to acquire more source material. Sealed truths and discovery solutions are excluded.

## MA01–MA05. Highest-value refinements

### MA01. Let an acquired method open another specific application

**Disposition:** E, deepen P03/P04/P09/P14. **Donors:** RetroMUD's shared practical abilities across worlds, 4D's retained/remembered repertoire. **Existing coverage:** COV01/02/07.

**Player journey:** after using the existing notebook-to-bench adaptation, a later supported repair or material-handling job recognizes the already-earned procedure. The player compares a familiar principle with the current material, spends actual test stock/time where needed, then gains a new application. They do not relearn the original technique or gain the source teacher's personal faculty.

**What is added:** one additional consumer and a record of what was established for that application. The current notebook route remains a concrete incumbent. For a different source, use its real learned owner rather than treating a fragment, object label or traveler-view entry as mastery.

**Subtle interaction:** a foreign procedure can help selection, control, inspection, fitting or teaching even when its original supernatural output is unsupported. A sufficiently capable imported power can also perform the full task where its actual rules allow it; adaptation must not become a mandatory weakening ritual.

**Save/acceptance:** preserve one learner/method identity and its provenance, plus the specific new application if it needs state. Show a useful output, a genuine incompatible control and a supported alternative. Travel/reload/import must not duplicate the lesson or replace the earned proficiency. Do not globally remove Rat-step's existing world requirement to obtain this example.

### MA02. Give an imported tool a useful home treatment

**Disposition:** E, deepen P02/P17/P18 and the current furnishing/refit paths. **Donor:** RetroMUD's power room changes wand-recharge risk; merchant fitting keeps valuable gear useful. **Existing coverage:** COV05 and the inspection/refit chain, not an absent repair system.

**Player journey:** bring an actual tool home, inspect its disclosed construction and choose cleaning, fitting, mundane repair or qualified local specialist work. A built facility changes one supported cost, precision or risk. The repaired object then performs a useful later job.

**What is added:** an item-specific compatibility reader and one facility treatment, only where the selected object/source permits it. A home workshop is not automatically a mana source or universal recharge station. Cosmetic resemblance and an identical item type are insufficient to identify the particular object being treated.

**Subtle interaction:** repairs, resizing, charge restoration and changes to attunement are different services. Ordinary casing repair can be possible while a magical bond or spiritual function remains untouched. Borrowed gear also needs permission for the particular modification, independent of permission to transport it.

**Save/acceptance:** bind quote and work to the actual instance/condition, consume the reserved stock once, preserve owner and provenance, and prove the changed condition is read by a subsequent task. Failed publication retains prior goods. No destructive failure or upkeep timer is introduced merely because RetroMUD has one.

### MA03. Turn existing skilled work into a customer relationship

**Disposition:** E, deepen P06/P07/P18/P25. **Donors:** RetroMUD merchant services; 4D buyer confidence and value-added trades. **Existing coverage:** Neri's finite service, named worker means, native market saturation, duty/claim patterns.

**Player journey:** a known customer requests a particular repair, preparation or batch with disclosed terms. The player does it, commissions a consenting skilled person, or declines. The customer checks the actual product/service, settles the agreed currency and remembers that specific outcome. A later offer changes because a real customer received a useful result.

**What is added:** one customer-specific acceptance/continuation consumer. This is richer than selling anonymous stock, but does not require another business simulator. Delegating an output neither trains the player nor transfers the worker's entire craft repertoire.

**Subtle interaction:** the best market sale price and the best customer outcome may differ. Reputation from trustworthy completion must not be minted by every resale or duplicated by reopening a receipt. A commission might offer board, a lesson, repair access or goods rather than coins; the offer declares the actual payer and stock.

**Absence:** Iona's patrol and Neri's current work have specific local support/clock conditions. A “shop earns while I travel” extension needs an explicit supported absence policy. Existing work must not silently continue through frozen worlds, dead workers or unavailable kit.

**Save/acceptance:** preserve accepted terms, producer, actual owner, receipt and the particular customer's memory/next offer. Show player-made versus commissioned output with different mastery attribution; unpaid, wrong-product and withdrawn cases do not settle. Only a genuinely new supported order can pay again.

### MA04. Display retention, eligibility and current execution together

**Disposition:** E for presentation; mechanics largely covered. Extend P01/P04/P05. **Donors:** both games make “I learned it” insufficient to explain every present result.

**Player journey:** at an existing skill/work/travel view, the player sees a compact statement such as “You retain this method; this task needs the current sample,” or “The lease ended; your learned ordinary method still works.” A known alternative appears beside the reason.

**What is added:** clear projection of real existing owners. Do not create another persistent status ledger or show hidden world laws, source truths or unknown routes. Use known facts and the command's actual admission/completion checks.

**Subtle interaction:** a world lock, missing resource, lost permission, changed body, occupied tool and absent external support require different remedies. “Remembered but unusable” must not imply “forgotten.” Current generic technique text can omit a location/world condition even when the underlying fact checks it; that is a concrete legibility question to verify in the chosen view.

**Save/acceptance:** begin with zero new save fields. At 1366×768, show current task, useful result, actual blocker and known next action at readable size; mobile optimization remains deferred. Rebuild and exercise the selected view before claiming UI behavior. Human taste stays separate.

### MA05. Let preparation change a declared travel problem

**Disposition:** E, deepen P02/P05/P13/P14. **Donors:** RetroMUD's route/arrival differences and 4D's cargo-specific restrictions. **Existing coverage:** cargo snapshots, scoped permissions, preservation and boundary evaluation.

**Player journey:** before a particular crossing, compare taking an ordinary item, preparing a permitted protected form, arranging lawful transport, leaving it in storage or commissioning the result at the destination. Preparation matters only if the selected crossing actually has a relevant rule.

**What is added:** a consumer that reads the selected item's existing preservation/classification or a real negotiated permission. The native cargo snapshot defaults to class `carried` unless classifications are supplied; the conventional wrapper's borrowed-goods refusal remains its current scope. Do not advertise a general lender-permission route merely because a detached evaluator accepts it.

**Subtle interaction:** a protective container may solve a material condition without solving ownership, prohibited cargo, creature consent or an arrival hazard. Treat transformed cargo as the same accounted stock with declared changes; no free duplicate or invented sealed version appears at the border.

**Save/acceptance:** full actual inventory and equipment remain bound to the quote; current route, arrival, ownership, expiry and permission are rechecked. Reject or transform only what the authored route supports. No universal organic-goods ban, naked-portal rule or global spoilage timer is imported.

## A3. Practical selection and proof

The best immediate use is to **audit and present the existing notebook/assistance chain clearly**, then choose one unmet customer or application consumer. A new universal guild, profession, inventory, remembered-skill or world-affinity engine is not justified by this pass.

| Candidate | Why choose it | What remains to authorize/establish |
|---|---|---|
| MA04 over an already-earned method | Lowest new-state cost; fits P01's selected work-feedback direction | The selected view must actually omit/misstate a consequential reason; then rebuild, fresh-origin desktop behavior and changed-state persistence evidence as affected |
| MA03 over an existing funded resident/service | Adds livelihood and remembered local consequence | Name the one customer/order/outcome not already represented; preserve worker, funding and absence rules |
| MA01 or MA02 for one additional real application/item | Strongest cross-world payoff | Confirm the existing consumer is insufficient; qualify actual source/body/material compatibility and use one existing record/stock owner |
| MA05 for a declared crossing problem | Makes preparation/carryover meaningful | A real route rule and actual native classification/permission consumer; no detached-prototype completion claim |
| MA11/13 for larger world or estate consequences | Good long-term breadth | Needs explicit causal state/clock or succession scope; do not make it a prerequisite for the smaller slices |

For any later selected slice, preserve the actual producer, learner, recipient, owned item and current source. Decide missing-save defaults and carrier lifetimes before adding persistent state. Prove the useful result and a blocked/alternative case, then save/reload/Export/literal-file Import where persistent behavior changes. Pending work, interruption and failed publication must preserve the declared stock and rewards. Natural acquisition and a debug-seeded edge test are different evidence. Mobile checks cannot replace 1366×768 desktop proof.
