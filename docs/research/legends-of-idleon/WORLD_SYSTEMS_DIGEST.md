# World systems digest

STATED unless marked SHOWN. Sources: [Skills](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Skills), [Worlds](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Worlds), and the individual articles below. These are successive regions of one game account, not prestige resets or separate universes.

| World | New work and distinctive decisions | Connection to the existing roster |
|---|---|---|
| W1 Blunder Hills | Mining, Choppin and Smithing: gather, smelt, manufacture. | Class specialties divide supply work; storage connects outputs. |
| W2 Yum-Yum Desert | Fishing, Catching, Alchemy: assign brewers, trade fill for discovery or boosts, spend liquid and materials. | Old drops become upgrade inputs; small bubbles are shared, large bubbles equipped personally. |
| W3 Frostbite Tundra | Trapping, Worship, Construction: scheduled traps, tower-defense records/charge, refinery and build board. | Cogs and character construction levels feed infrastructure. 3D Printer and later collection utilities reduce repeated gathering/visits. |
| W4 Hyperion Nebula | Breeding, Cooking, Laboratory: pet battle/forage teams supply spices; kitchens turn them into recipes and meal levels; Lab is a geometric connection board. | Uploaded characters power shared nodes but occupy their ordinary activity. Chip slots/effects remain personal. |
| W5 Smolderin' Plateau | Sailing, Gaming, Divinity: manage captains/routes/chests, grow a garden and spend bits, train divine links. | Fleet/garden progress is shared. Divine blessings apply broadly; links and skill levels preserve individual choices. |
| W6 Spirited Valley | Farming, Sneaking, Summoning: crop evolution/markets, stealth-floor teams with detection risk, active slime battles backed by growing resources. | Sneaking uses unlockable ninja counterparts rather than requiring the real character to stop ordinary combat. Shared rewards keep earlier systems relevant. |
| W7 Shimmerfin Deep | Research observations/lenses/grid and Spelunking teams/tools/stamina/escape decisions; also reef and other feature boards. | Class levels earn account Legend Talent points. Spelunking has node-based AFK page gathering and separate team dives. The wiki applies special inherited-bonus restrictions. |

## Selected joins and friction

W2: liquid upgrades, bubble upgrades and purchases compete for one capped liquid supply. Cauldron discovery and cauldron strengthening compete for brewing fill. This is a spending choice within one system, not simply another XP bar. [Alchemy](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Alchemy).

W3: build progress and material payment are distinct. Reaching DONE does not buy the upgrade. Multiple queued buildings each receive the full listed build rate, according to the article. Therefore a generic shared-work-pool model would misdescribe this system. Character board/shelf assignment is separate from their normal AFK destination. [Construction](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Construction).

W4: pets earn access to territories and forage spices. Kitchen recipe search consumes spice combinations; existing recipes can reduce the chance of finding a new recipe. Meals raise lasting buffs. Kitchen AFK produces personal XP and ladles; kitchens also produce meals without those characters present. A ladle advances cooking time and does not create a full character AFK claim. [Breeding](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Breeding), [Cooking](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Cooking).

W5: a captain is a fleet unit, not another player character. Ship routes combine time, loot and artifact odds. The garden distinguishes growing capacity from upgrade spending. Divine links can change how old systems use a character, rather than simply increasing damage. [Sailing](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Sailing), [Gaming](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Gaming), [Divinity](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Divinity).

W6: a crop can keep accumulating overgrowth after maturity; a full/ready state is not always wasted time. Sneaking separates farming currency, untying another ninja and breaking doors: the latter tasks forgo normal currency and XP until resolved. Detection adds recovery downtime. Summoning attempts have daily limits; that is a source fact, not a recommended YAIRPG solo limit. [Farming](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Farming), [Sneaking](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Sneaking), [Summoning](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Summoning).

W7: Research unlocks observations through level requirements and daily discovery rolls, then spends points in an adjacency-revealed grid. Its retrieved table contains 43 observations and 60 grid entries, not proof that all are currently available. Spelunking distinguishes gathered pages, discovered objects, upgrades and risky dives. A cave rope can permit escape with earned rewards, but continuing can remove it. These are local representations and rules, not a mandate to port daily limits or hidden global multipliers. [Research](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Research), [Spelunking](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Spelunking).

The W7 article says generic skill efficiency/XP are heavily reduced for Spelunking and generic skill AFK bonuses do not normally apply. The current code has a conditional conversion term for generic AFK bonuses, so later exceptions matter. Exact unlock and modifier evaluation is an open trace, not a blanket assertion that such bonuses never apply.
