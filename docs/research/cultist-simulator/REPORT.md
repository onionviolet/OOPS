# Cultist Simulator: finite source pass complete

Useful source is copied and hash-matched: 1,307 files, 58.8 MB, build 22090405. The managed engine was decompiled from the private copy with ILSpy. The installed files have no remaining research consumer, so this game qualifies for Steam uninstall now. Parent owns the uninstall dialog and observed removal.

F1: Earning money, paying expenses and treating illness compete for time and the same resources. Eligibility checks do not automatically spend a resource: local effects and card transformations do.

F2: Temporary states have different consequences when ignored. Fatigue returns to Health; untreated illness becomes Decrepitude; missed work becomes demotion with a recovery route. Decay is more expressive than a generic timer.

F3: Occult progress becomes a recurring obligation. The power route consumes Funds to advance and regresses or hurts the body when unsupported. Ghoul hunger is a mutation on the committed desire, with different feeding consumers.

F4: Endings can still offer a relationship choice after earlier costs have been paid. The next life is selected from ending-dependent origins plus alternatives, not established here as a copy of the whole previous character.

Seven connected chains and exact private source hashes are in FINDINGS.json. Five original proposals for the experience GM are in IMPLEMENTATION_PACKETS.md. All managed dependencies and shipped definitions, including DLC and localization inputs, are retained privately. Most content, save internals and DLC campaigns remain unread; no game was launched and no YAIR gameplay was implemented or verified. Source pass completion is not whole-game coverage.
