# NV-D4: What a living universe means here

Pin `f8982244800e86553217a2dba555a0f41c2b87ba`. Shown means inspected static source, not runtime proof.

Faction XML references spawn, equipment, standing and AI scripts. The spawn common library rejects unsuitable origins, considers local/neighbor presence, picks fleets and returns a future delay, with extra delay under excessive presence. Shared behavior and faction settings produce different encounter patterns. Sirius is aggressive with its own scan/hail voice; Za'lek distinguishes human ships and drones and retreats earlier for fragile crewed ships. Standing rules have source-specific effects and progression caps. Ports check actual services, domination, temporary bribe, explicit allow/deny, hostility and local standing in a defined order. A story permission is not identical to friendship, cargo custody or military access.

The economy uses commodity metadata and periodic price functions. It supports regional differences and temporal trading decisions, but price movement alone does not show merchants transporting conserved stock between persistent households. News can comment on calculated prices without simulating the named cause. Map and faction traffic give a living setting; authored mission consequences give durable change. Keep those evidence layers separate when adapting it to worlds where residents live independently.
