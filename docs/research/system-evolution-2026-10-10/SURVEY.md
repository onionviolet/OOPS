# System evolution in web fiction: survey

Date: 2026-10-10. Reader: research subagent for YAIRPG. Scope: stories where a System or cheat itself changes (levels, upgrades, merges, wakes up, breaks, gets patched, changes owner, is made or hacked by the protagonist) and world-level systems that change (integration, resets, updates).

## Method and honesty notes

- Route 1: all 14 pages of Weibao's Royal Road history, 680 fictions, synopses and tags fetched from each `/fiction/<id>` page and filtered by keyword. Keyword filtering finds synopsis wording only. A work with no signal in its blurb may still evolve its System (for example The Primal Hunter shows no such wording in its blurb).
- Route 2: 18 Royal Road title searches ("evolving system", "system upgrade", "system maker", "sentient system", "glitched system", "system merge", "system hacker", "system administrator", "system creator", "system integration", "system evolution", "broken system", "corrupted system", "system fusion", "system patch", "system update", and two companion queries). Top ~8 per query, 143 unique fictions, synopses only. Search is title-biased, so it over-returns titles with the word "system" in them and says little about quality.
- Route 3 (web): WebSearch returned spam PDFs and no usable list pages. The only web-sourced items are those in section A2, flagged as recalled from memory with no link verified. Do not treat A2 as checked.
- Labels: STATED = the synopsis says it. INFERRED = deduced from title or tags, or from memory of the work.
- Reddit was not used. No reviews or comments were read, so section C is mostly inference from tags and synopsis wording.
- No account state was changed. One browser tab was opened and closed.
- Link form: `https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/<id>`.

## B. Taxonomy of system-evolution mechanics

Each entry lists the Table A1 numbers that show it (A1 only).

1. Leveling the System itself (the tool, not the player). Shipwright core evolves ships (#48); Upgrade System (#62); Corpo-style "system upgrades" (RR 75446, not tabled). STATED in synopses.
2. Merge, fusion, absorption of skills or systems. Skill Eater (#13), Apocalypse Me (#14), Ascension of Chaos (#12), Broken System Archivist (#39), Skill Fusion Lottery (#57), Supreme Merge (#59), System Fusion (#58).
3. Sentience and personality of the System. Aggravated Defense (#30), Undying System (#31), Ultra A.I. (#21), Non-Player Character's AI (#23), I Am A Table (#26).
4. Brokenness and corruption: the System starts defective and changes as it heals, spreads or learns. Broken System Trilogy (#9), Corrupted/Glitched/Scrambled/Malfunction (#34-37), Broken System Apocalypse (#40), Corrupted Cardsmith (#41), Madman Apocalypse (#4), System Corruption: Patch (#33).
5. Creator or author reveals: someone built the System. Homegrown System (#1), Reaper's Resurgence (#2), Dan's Dungeon (#16), Symphony (#7).
6. Admin and dev access: protagonist holds admin rights or works inside the machine. Unwilling Eldritch Horror (#5), God Shards (#11), Corporate Core (#54), Zenith Zone (#45), Fantasia (#55), Otherworld Volunteer (#6, admins as antagonists).
7. System maker and hacker: the protagonist writes or alters systems. System Maker (#43, #44), Game Creator System (#47), World-Builder (#46), System Override hacker (#56).
8. World-level phases, patches and resets. Hard Reset (#52), System Integration (#53), Patch Monday (#50), System Update (#51), Reaper's Resurgence (#2), Intelligent Design (#17), Ashvattha (#49).
9. Owner change and mismatch: System misreads or rejects its host. Incursion Protocol (#10), world traveler (#32), Prophecy Approved Companion (#22, NPC viewpoint).
10. Species or monster evolution as the System's own mechanic. #17-20.
11. Cost of System growth on the host. Broken System Trilogy (#9) burns identity; Shadowborn (#35) mutates the body.

## C. Pitfalls readers raise

No reviews were read. Everything below is INFERRED from tags, synopsis wording and author notes.

- Genre flood of "System" titles. 143 unique hits for 18 near-synonym queries, many with "SSS-Rank" and "Creator" names. Inference: low-effort variants are common and hard to tell apart by premise.
- Maker and creator cheats invite power inflation. Synopses that stress "SSS" tiers and "limitless growth" (#43, #62) suggest the cheat becomes the whole plot. INFERRED.
- Broken-system premises often become cosmetic. Many glitch titles (#34-37) stack "glitch" on a normal power fantasy. INFERRED from blurbs that list mutations and strong abilities rather than a rule the reader must reason about.
- Hiatus and cancellation risk is visible in titles: #51 is on long-term hiatus per its own title, and several history items carry "Stubbed" or "Cancelled" notes. STATED in titles, not about system mechanics.
- Author notes admit weak craft in some entries (#62 apologises for English and says it is written chapter by chapter). STATED.
- Tag "Reader Interactive" and "Technologically Engineered" appear on the odder systems (#17, #3). No conclusion drawn.
