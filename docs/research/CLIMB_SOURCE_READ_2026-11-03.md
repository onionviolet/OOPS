## Findings

- **F1. Rune blessings exist,** but only as a permanent rune slot (see table). The build offers blessings at two floors only (floor 1 and floor 5 guardian rooms).
- **F2. First Blood is not a dummy award.** It fires on the first kill of any enemy; the playtest's first kill was a dummy, so GAP_AUDIT G5's phrase is literally true but misleading about why it fired. The dummy-specific award is a 1000-kill resonance that needs an awakened Training Sword.
- **F3. No offline progression.** Time advances only through worker ticks while the page runs, plus the bed's x5 speed and a skip-to-morning jump. YAIR's away bank is the opposite design, not an equivalent.
- **F4. Awakening is a three-stage item arc** (rank by use, feed duplicates, awaken) whose payoff includes a permanent account-level stat achievement and NPC reactions. YAIR has gear-use hooks but no gear arc of this shape.
- **F5. Heirloom carry is a power budget with an opportunity cost.** Summed item power must fit the blessing's limit, and selected items keep rank, enchants, exp and awakening. The blessing UI (`20:298716`) applies one chosen blessing and its action ends in rebirth, so each rebirth grants one blessing: choosing heirlooms gives up that rebirth's stat, rank, workshop or rune bonus. That cost, more than the power budget, is the tradeoff.

## Comparison summary

Already present in YAIR (SV): death and rebirth carry rules by scope, estate nomination, unique named items, relic tiers keyed to use, role attachments, profile achievements, titles with a first-kill title, away bank.

Genuinely new from this read (proposals, none selected):

1. **Rank from use for all equipped gear,** automatically, not only at naming.
2. **Feed-duplicates-to-awaken,** with capacity growth and a deed on completion.
3. **Power-budgeted carry choice** for the compact (a tradeoff, not a list).
4. **Resonance deed:** the proposed default counts an action while a specific item is held, against a repeatable target.
5. **NPC reaction to an awakened item** (the smith's scene at `20:27288`, which grants the `iron_sword_resonance` achievement `17:142347`), a cheap way to make item history socially visible.
