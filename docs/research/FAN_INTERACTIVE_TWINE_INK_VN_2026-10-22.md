# Fan and original interactive fiction survey (2026-10-22)

Research only. Web search plus page fetches; nothing downloaded, no text reproduced. "V" = I opened the page. "U" = search snippet only. This is a catalog and proposal, not accepted direction.

## Headline finding

Fan interactive fiction for our listed worlds is thin and mostly short. No fan game turned up for Mother of Learning, Lord of the Mysteries, Shadow Slave, Reincarnation Paradise, Fullmetal Alchemist or Bleach (text/VN). Fan work exists for Harry Potter, Jujutsu Kaisen, Hunter x Hunter, Naruto and Fate, but almost none has real stat, calendar or inventory systems. The mechanically useful material is in ORIGINAL games: Academagia, Long Live the Queen, Fallen London, Xianxia Simulator, Four Heavenly Valleys, The Magician's Burden.

## Short notes on mechanics

**What VN and ChoiceScript games usually cover**

- Stats: ChoiceScript uses named numeric variables with opposed pairs and a stats screen; Ren'Py scheduling games (Long Live the Queen) use many trainable skills plus a mood multiplier. Growth is by choice, rarely by elapsed time.
- Items: usually booleans or small counts. Real inventories exist (Magician's Burden, Academagia) but are dozens of entries, not hundreds.
- Skill checks: threshold or opposed-percentile checks against a stat. Long Live the Queen shows deterministic threshold checks with foreknowledge as the skill.
- Calendars: chapters or fixed weekly slots. Academagia (three slots a day) and Long Live the Queen (40 weeks) are the strong cases; most ChoiceScript titles have no clock beyond chapter breaks.
- Relationships: per-NPC numeric meters (approval, trust, romance flags) gating scenes. Mirror's approval points are a typical small case.
- Saves: engine-level only; Twine game state lives in browser storage and breaks across updates (Dao Ascension complaints).

**Where they are weak versus our idle/life-sim**

- Authored, linear, finite: content is one-shot passages, so replay value comes from branching, not systems.
- No ongoing simulation: nothing runs while the player is away, no offline progress, no background NPC schedules.
- Shallow economy: items rarely decay, craft, trade or compound.
- Weak long-horizon pacing: no automation, no throttled energy (Fallen London is the exception) and no many-world meta-progression.
- Fragile persistence across updates, and almost no migration story.
- Twine/ChoiceScript are poor at maps and combat (forum consensus, U).

**Best stealable ideas (proposals only)**

- Qualities as one namespace for stats, items, progress and menaces, with storylet gating (Fallen London).
- Action bank refilling in real time as the idle throttle (Fallen London).
- Visible-schedule training with mood multiplier (Long Live the Queen).
- Lifespan and realm percent bars with reincarnation carryover (Four Heavenly Valleys, Xianxia Simulator, Mystic Samsara).
- Daily three-slot school day with exams as scheduled gates (Academagia).

## Gaps

- No fan game found for Mother of Learning, Lord of the Mysteries, Shadow Slave, Reincarnation Paradise, Fullmetal Alchemist or Bleach text/VN.
- IFDB, AO3 and the Choice of Games forum were not searched directly (searches returned no hits; WebFetch of one itch.io tag page and choiceofgames.com returned 403/404 once each, so I switched routes).
- Many candidates are search-snippet only (marked U). Mechanics of Blackstone Academy, Saint Spell's, Witch Time stats and Xianxia Simulator internals were not confirmed.
- Licences were rarely visible; none of the entries state an open licence, so treat all as read-only references.
- Adult content is common in Harry Potter and JJK itch results; I excluded the explicit titles.
