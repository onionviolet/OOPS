# BitLife: mass-market text life simulation

2026-10-13. RESEARCH COMPLETE at public-page level, with wiki and current-version gaps. All design suggestions are PROPOSALS, not accepted decisions or implemented gameplay.

**Answer first.** BitLife makes a whole life easy to read: choose actions from familiar life domains, advance age, meet an unexpected event, then see the resulting history. Death gives the life a compact identity and a reason to try another path. YAIRPG already has calendars, dated invitations, relationship memories, biographies and estate carryover. The strongest added reference is how to present these together, especially a life-end appraisal, an honest succession preview and optional goals that combine existing systems. Yearly pacing, birth rolls and heirs are already covered here and should not become duplicate implementation lanes.

BitLife is a reference for pacing and presentation, not authority for fiction-world rules. HP, D&D and the priority book worlds retain their own laws. Lifespan, stat ranges, action limits, repeat rules and carry sets are per-world adjectives. Desktop/laptop is primary; mobile optimization remains deferred.

## How YAIRPG compares

These are **verified (code)** observations from the live working tree, HEAD `9d6dc6a1ad9c4ae6bd12cc8e528923ae3c941e5f` plus existing dirty edits. HEAD alone does not pin this snapshot. No runtime claim or whole-repo absence claim follows.

| Area | Actual local source read | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Time and mortality | `src/chronicle.js:3`, `createChronicle`, `ageYears`, `lifespanDeadline`, `nextChronicleBoundary`, `advanceChronicle`; `src/game_time.js:18-41` | Chronicle uses elapsed minutes and a 360-day year. Its current original Frontier defaults are age 18/lifespan 80, with open or mortal mode. Scheduled events can become missed; old age ends only mortal lives. These are current local defaults, not world-wide constants. A blind annual jump can cross real deadlines. |
| Life stages and appraisal | `src/life_experience.js` functions `lifeStage`, `lifeLearningMultiplier`, `lifeEpitaph`, `lifeGrave` | Youth learning, elder teaching and evidence-based epitaph already exist. Extend this owner for a recap rather than create a competing death/reward system. No broad ribbon collection was seen in this module. |
| Biography | `src/life_record.js:1-82` | Explicitly lossy and non-authoritative. Stores recent milestones, yearly folds and older counts. Migration records the loaded present, not invented history. A recap may display it; a reward predicate cannot rely on prose or retained highlights. |
| Inheritance | `src/heir_inheritance.js:32-98`; `src/chronicle.js` `createHeirChronicle` | Estate carries actual money, goods, chest/home access, selected papers/teaching, standing and unresolved duties. Debt settles first. A pristine heir keeps their own body/skills; the chronicle creates a new age-18 owner and chooses apprentice versus kin from teaching, not a roster of already-aged playable children. BitLife F3 exposes that specific distinction. |
| NPC lives | `src/npc_lives.js:5-42`, `addVillageRecruit` | One bounded optional household has seeded death years and authored birth/marriage events. The view hides future members. This is not proof of player parenting or child possession; a succession picker needs an explicit identity bridge. |
| Relationships and news | `src/chronicle.js` `relationshipChange`, `commandChronicle`, `finishEncounter`; `src/people_knowledge.js` `knownPeopleView`; `src/event_feed.js:1-60` | The elder has axes, ties, dated memories, invitation gates and a grievance. People/news views restrict private or unobserved state. Preserve those information boundaries instead of exposing BitLife-like full NPC stats. |
| Career gates | `src/village_career.js:91-111` | Promotion already gives unmet reasons and links full shifts, returned kits and debt. Use as a good existing example; this lane does not audit all careers or education modules. |
