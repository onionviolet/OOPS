# Monarch Lotus in ordinary play: lore and acquisition options

**Status: proposal (AD32), 2026-11-24.** Scope: `gu_kinds:fandom:Heavenly_Essence_Treasure_Monarch_Lotus`. This is an acquisition design, not an accepted rule or an implemented route. Novel claims below are paraphrased from the local Reverend Insanity reading copy; `ri:chN:pM` locators identify the evidence. Prices, schedules and caps proposed for OOPS are dials, not canon values.

## What the novel establishes

- **Identity and place.** The Monarch Lotus is a rank four Gu in the Heavenly Essence Treasure Lotus advancement series, not a Gu House or a free-standing resource point (`ri:ch308:p82`, `ri:ch558:p9`, `ri:ch2235:p2`). Its rank three precursor is described as a flower or grass Gu refined in a natural spirit spring; a cultivated specimen can be carried in a Gu Master's aperture (`ri:ch163:p11`, `ri:ch163:p16`, `ri:ch182:p58`, `ri:ch187:p59`, `ri:ch187:p62`). Applying that physical form and carrying method to the rank four specimen is an **inference**, since the cited Monarch scenes do not depict its body or aperture placement.
- **Known holders and protection.** Ju clan held a Monarch Lotus recipe and offered it at an auction; the opening bid was 500,000 primeval stones, and Wei Yang bought the recipe after bidding 670,000. These are prices for knowledge, **not** a demonstrated price for a living Monarch Lotus (`ri:ch308:p86`, `ri:ch308:p100`, `ri:ch308:p105`, `ri:ch309:p1`). Shang clan city's living treasure door held a Monarch Lotus and guarded its stock under ancestral rules (`ri:ch315:p43`, `ri:ch315:p45`). The rank three precursor refined by Flower Wine Monk occupied the Gu Yue clan's spirit spring; taking it ruined that spring and risked clan pursuit (`ri:ch163:p16`, `ri:ch163:p55`, `ri:ch182:p58`, `ri:ch182:p59`). The last event is evidence for a **possible contested acquisition pattern**, not a recorded Monarch theft.
- **Making and losing one.** The rank four recipe requires a rank three Heavenly Essence Treasure Lotus as its main ingredient (`ri:ch308:p90`, `ri:ch308:p93`). Fang Yuan acquired that recipe but deferred refinement because he needed multiple spirit springs and the undertaking was large (`ri:ch309:p16`, `ri:ch345:p26`). The earlier explanation specifies seven spirit springs to advance to rank four; its account of rank three creation also requires a sufficiently full natural spring and says refinement destroys it (`ri:ch163:p21`, `ri:ch163:p24`). This supports a costly advancement chain, not a recipe that conjures a Monarch Lotus from money alone. No passage found in this name/alias sweep shows a Monarch Lotus being sold, stolen, fed, dying or lost in action. Those outcomes remain design possibilities, not canon events.
- **Production and limits.** The rank three through rank five Lotus Gu produce primeval essence and improve recovery (`ri:ch463:p2`). The rank three example produces natural essence in the aperture, with stone formation as a further consequence; at its poorest level it still helps recovery, but its daily stone output cannot match an ordinary spring (`ri:ch187:p60`, `ri:ch187:p61`, `ri:ch187:p66`, `ri:ch163:p26`). Fang Yuan expects the rank three version to become less useful once he reaches rank four (`ri:ch309:p19`). **Inference:** a rank four Gu should matter at the corresponding power stage without becoming an unbounded source. The cited text supplies no Monarch output rate, feeding material or interval, activation charge, storage limit, or exact stage compatibility. OOPS must author these openly. The spring cost is a **refinement input**, not evidence of continuous upkeep (`ri:ch163:p24`, `ri:ch345:p26`).

## Ranked options

**O1. Earn the precursor, buy or learn the recipe, then advance it at seven springs. Recommended.** Lore: the recipe consumes the rank three Lotus, whose initial refinement already needs a strong spring; rank four advancement requires seven springs and was too large for Fang Yuan to undertake immediately (`ri:ch163:p21`, `ri:ch163:p24`, `ri:ch308:p93`, `ri:ch345:p26`). Reuse the keeper's exact Gu title and care, staged refinement, and aperture accounting. Add a finite, located spring reserve with a named custodian, an acquired rank three specimen, recipe knowledge, and a rank four refinement job. The player secures access by purchase, patronage or negotiated rights; consuming a spring changes that site's available essence and its owners react. Gate on a compatible rank four aperture or an explicitly costly lower-rank use, plus the precursor, knowledge and all seven spring inputs. **Proposed upkeep:** a visible living-Gu food or care contract, with the precise diet left open because the passages do not state one. **Proposed output:** periodic recovery into the holder's own compatible pool, capped by that pool and the Gu's production rate; no stone sale loop until measured and balanced. This makes access a substantial expedition and social cost while avoiding infinite primeval essence or free money. It also makes the existing effect case reachable from an ordinary acquisition and use route.

**O2. Obtain an existing Monarch Lotus through guarded treasury custody.** Lore: Shang clan city's living treasure door held one and guarded it by ancestral rules (`ri:ch315:p43`, `ri:ch315:p45`). Reuse keeper claim transfer and local trade, adding treasury inventory, permission, price or earned standing, and a concrete transfer receipt. Rank four control remains gated; propose the same living-Gu care and pool/rate cap as O1, without pretending the novel names either value (`ri:ch308:p82`, `ri:ch463:p2`). A legal transfer could exchange a comparably rare asset or a major obligation; a theft branch would need pursuit and custody consequences, since the rank three spring removal shows such fallout (`ri:ch163:p55`, `ri:ch182:p59`). Balance risk is earlier access to sustained essence without paying the seven-spring refinement cost; the treasury's price and standing gate must be comparable, not a routine shop purchase. This is smaller mechanically but needs a believable world route to the treasury.

**O3. Unusual: find an unfinished precursor in a clan spring and decide whether to repair, leave or take it.** Lore: the Flower Wine Monk precedent puts an immature rank three Lotus in a clan spring; primeval stones repair its damaged leaf, premature plucking fails, and extraction disables the spring (`ri:ch163:p16`, `ri:ch163:p31`, `ri:ch163:p37`, `ri:ch163:p38`, `ri:ch163:p55`, `ri:ch182:p39`, `ri:ch182:p59`). The rank four outcome still needs the recipe, rank three precursor and seven-spring advancement (`ri:ch163:p24`, `ri:ch308:p93`). Reuse wild discovery and claim transfer, then add a spring maturity clock and visible reserve/owner response. The player can preserve the public spring, compensate its custodians, or take the future Gu at an immediate social and resource cost. Gate full Monarch use at rank four; propose the same care and pool/rate cap as O1. This gives the spring an interesting choice but is the largest new scene and risks turning a settlement's core resource into a farmable pickup unless each spring and its loss persist.

All three options are **proposals**. O1 best fits the novel's explicit Monarch recipe and difficulty, and yields a repeatable player path with real costs. O2 can be a rare parallel path once treasury access exists; O3 is a distinctive encounter, not the default drop table.

## Ledger and proof consequence

The present row already has the authored case and workshop action. The evidence ladder separates **(1)** case success, **(2)** module reachability and actual consumer, **(3)** player-action scope, and **(4)** engine restore, game save/reload, export/import and browser proof (see `docs/gm/dispatch/atlas-platform-2026-11-24/pilot/HANDOFF_fandom_Heavenly_Essence_Treasure_Monarch_Lotus.md`). Any chosen option would fill the *ordinary-play acquisition and activation* scope at rung 3 only after a player can obtain a particular specimen and use its effect from a normal start. The acquisition, control, spring cost, production cadence and cap need their own game state and observed receipts. Rung 4 then needs separate restore, save/reload, export/import and fresh rebuilt browser checks. A changed `gu_kinds` ledger state or stronger atlas badge follows those receipts; this document alone changes none. O1 would additionally prove refinement and spring consumption; O2, treasury custody and transfer; O3, maturity, extraction and persistent spring loss.
