## 1. Two funded resident shifts

**Player action:** after meeting an existing Village worker, fund up to two displayed local shifts, allow other work to proceed, then buy or collect one actually produced Prepared survey kit per completed shift. Inspection and refusal remain free. The finite limit avoids silently making the active GM's explicitly finite merchant supplies renewable.

**Prerequisites, costs and aftermath:** real willingness, local work window, usable hands/health/stamina, legal bench access and free capacity; reserve one actual log, the agreed payer's existing wage and worker effort per shift. Output belongs to the declared worker/commission owner until a separate paid transfer. No funded input or missed window creates output; completion rechecks support and returns reservations if it is lost. A retained receipt changes the later public work report without exposing private worker state.

**Owners and missing binding:** `simulation.js` already dispatches controllers and finite programs; `world_rhythm.js` derives recurring windows; `feature_expansion_society.js` has funded independent Orr work; `field_workshop.js` already consumes actual kit stock. Author one schedule fact adapter and two finite shift commands/claims. Do not create a population simulator, invent wages or refill Eda's sleeve/salt inventory. M3/M5 provide the comparison.

**Persistence and later acceptance:** retain explicit funding/shift claims, frozen jobs, output ownership and published observations in their existing owners; old saves remain unenrolled. Later demonstrate work continuing during another supported activity, off-hours blocking, lost-support refund, one owned output/one transfer, kit use and reload with no second production.

**Prerequisites, costs and aftermath:** own or receive the declared build right; supply two real logs and agreed labor/payment, complete site construction, then observe the fixture. It grants no regional happiness, global speed bonus or free stock.

**Owners and missing binding:** extend `site_projects.js`, `field_workshop.js`, `everyday_life.js` and society claims/permissions; add one declared fixture world fact with an actual work predicate/duration consumer. Elin's Hobby home/destination/bed-quality/affinity consumer and M4 capacity path motivate useful local surroundings; a complete furniture-quality or needs simulation is not required.

**Persistence and later acceptance:** construction remains one existing project/claim; no retroactive existing-home bonus. Validate any fixture fact through the owning state contract. Later show real material spend, a distinct wet-weather before/after, an unchanged unrelated job, inaccessible-fixture refusal, and retained fixture/right after reload.
