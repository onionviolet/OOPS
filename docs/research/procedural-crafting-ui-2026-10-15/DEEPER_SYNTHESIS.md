## P6: Give an ingredient several roles, then let the use choose

Original proposal extending earlier P1/P2, not an accepted material system. Separate four questions:

| Question | Retain | Show at the decision |
|---|---|---|
| What is this actual lot? | Identity, amount, condition and only meaningful provenance | Owned/free/held quantity and observer-permitted description |
| What can this operation use? | Exact identity, functional fit, structure, pattern or other local requirement | Eligible role and known reasons for fit or uncertainty |
| What happens to it? | Spent input, recoverable allocation, catalyst/tool, transformed intermediate or installed component | Consumption/hold/recovery terms |
| Who uses the output? | Actual body/object/job/service/pattern consumer | Useful consequence and relevant use conditions |

HP's direct-use/ingredient example, Minecraft's fuel/component example, MoL's processed steel/battery and Warbreaker's carrier/resource all fit without forcing them into one chemical average. A material may qualify in multiple ways while only some facts are known. “Known uses” is not “all uses.” Aggregated ordinary stock can remain compact; keep an individual lot when its differences affect the choice.

**New selection example:** an original resin can seal a vial, bind a tool or carry a pattern if the selected world gives it those functions. The interface lists those supported uses and the actual reserve each requires. Sealing suitability says nothing by itself about whether the pattern can execute. Do not add every property to the resulting object's strength.

## P7: Compose means at the step that they affect

| Means | What it might change under a selected law | What it does not automatically grant |
|---|---|---|
| Herbal knowledge/diagnosis | Which fraction or observable cue matters | Stock, apparatus or universal true identification |
| Skilled manipulation | Repeatability, preparation or a precise operation | A different material identity |
| Heat/pressure/space power | Supplies the actual process condition or reach | Knowledge of what operation to perform |
| Telepathic control | Directs a compatible remote carrier | Casting through it, unless separately supported |
| Apprentice/machine/construct | Performs a known instruction with its own means | Player mastery or every faculty of its maker |
| Analysis/creation cheat | Whatever its chosen rule actually reveals or produces | An invented generic restriction to preserve ordinary chores |

MoL's animation requirement is a local example, not a reason to force all players to master a task manually before delegating. A machine with an existing procedure, a knowledgeable assistant or a selected creation power can supply a missing part. Show a short explanation such as “the furnace supplies steady heat; your technique determines separation.” Preserve chosen involvement per activity/life. A fully capable power need not carry a fabricated drawback.

## P8: Recipes should record what was demonstrated

A recipe may be a teacher's claim, an authored canonical formula, a discovered concrete method or an inferred family of methods. Keep the distinction visible. Save the actual tested procedure and a new revision when it changes. Generalize acceptable substitutions only as far as the character's evidence or authorized means supports; an experiment route can still try an unknown candidate.

Meaningful discoveries need not always create a new effect. They may reduce cost/time, use local stock, remove a required tool, improve stability/precision, change delivery, combine supported functions, scale a method, or reveal another consumer. MoL explicitly supports several of these; the rest remain original examples where not separately sourced. Unexpected discovery can reveal a real previously unrecognized cause. Do not assign random unrelated effects to every combination or present an attempted combination as a success.

Keep the design surface proportional: a short ordered method for process work; component graph for assemblies; instruction/trigger view for a command carrier; spatial layout only when relationships in space matter. One interface shell can host these without pretending every domain is chemistry.

## J2: A stronger original candidate journey

1. Inspect one herb lot at an accessible apothecary. Compare two known uses and one hypothesis, with actual free stock.
2. Choose a specific research goal: a cheaper local preparation, rather than “discover anything.” Change one ingredient or operation; use the character's available measuring/control means.
3. Run an experiment and apply the output through an appropriate existing consumer. Record the observation, cost and uncertain cause; don't require drinking to be the only assay route.
4. Save a concrete method revision. A second attempt tests whether the change generalizes; an unexplained difference can become a useful research question rather than a universal failure penalty.
5. Personally run, supervise or delegate the method through available means. State substitutions/reserves and stop conditions. Production does not discover hidden facts beyond what its actual observations permit.

This is a proposal, not a dispatch. Existing mixture observation/method/repeat functions remain the incumbent. Adding more fields or names without changing a real choice is not the completion gate.
