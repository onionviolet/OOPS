**B1 synthesis:** this source supports a craft path through technique, experiments, useful goods, collaboration and dissemination. It does not supply numerical recipes or an executable chemistry. Kael's smaller optimizations are especially suitable for meaningful discovery without authoring a new potion for every experiment. A quieter personal laboratory can have a full path without becoming a factory.

Do not generalize the loop into free research or automatically carry every partner's understanding between lives. The passages themselves distinguish retained records from having to relearn prior work. YAIR's accepted continuity/knowledge owners determine its selected behavior.

**B2 synthesis:** an item can be a recipe ingredient, a directly used remedy or a harmful exposure under different conditions. Known uses belong to the same substance, not separate unrelated inventory items created for each menu. The game still needs separate consumers for each route. These are fictional source rules, not real medical advice or a reason to map the named plants onto real chemistry.

Original adaptation: an ingredient detail can show “known preparation,” “known direct use” and “handling observation,” each with its own evidence and amount. The observation must not reveal unsourced universal potency. A recipe catalogue is still valuable when a direct substitute is unavailable or unsuitable.

## B3: Warbreaker, commands are methods over a real carrier

Refreshed primary author-hosted text: [prologue](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/warbreaker-prologue), passages beginning with the straw figure's construction through Breath recovery; [chapter46](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/warbreaker-chapter-forty-six), rope experiments from initial recovery through the longer-rope advice. Complete fetched HTML is private, with URL/hash receipts. No other chapter or full-book reading claimed.

**B3a, prologue, shown/narrated:** a shaped carrier, focus, color, Breath and an intended instruction contribute different roles. Deployed Breath changes the user's available awareness; one created servant's Breath can be recovered. Shape affects the stated requirement. This does not establish recovery from every possible Awakened entity.

**B3b, chapter46, shown:** Vivenna tests instructions on one rope, observes an unintended grip, recovers power and tries more specific or conditional instructions. Some proposed instructions fail; a taught conditional command works. Recovering a useful thrown rope becomes a practical design issue. Her causal explanations remain hypotheses unless the scene separately confirms them.

**B3 synthesis, proposal:** save a functional method as carrier + power allocation + instruction + trigger + recovery/control arrangement. This is a different domain from dissolved potion fractions. A reusable physical item can be transformed for a task, then returned to ordinary use. Keep “holds” and “spent” resources separate; a recoverable resource is not automatically free at the moment it is deployed. Do not copy these costs/limits onto unrelated YAIR cheats.

## B4: New-recipe discovery has several meanings

| Discovery kind | Example supported here | Retained knowledge | Separate game question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learn an authored method | Teacher/instructions; a known antidote route | Where it came from and what it claims | Does the character have access and means? |
| Optimize a method | MoL cheaper input or shorter process | Actual tested revision and conditions | Is it valid beyond the tested apparatus/scale? |
| Find another use | HP ingredient versus direct use; harmful exposure versus remedy | Route-specific observation | Does the other consumer actually exist? |
| Compose a new supported function | MoL golem control; attempted combined medicines; Warbreaker conditional command | Parts/interfaces, supported effect and unresolved limits | Was this success shown, hypothesized or merely attempted? |
| Infer a principle | Compare methods and observations | Scope and confidence, rather than omniscient truth | Which further test would distinguish competing explanations? |

These categories coexist. No source here proves unrestricted creation of arbitrary new mechanics. Procedural variety becomes useful when the output reaches a real consumer. Original proposals belong in the [deeper synthesis](DEEPER_SYNTHESIS.md), with the current one-realm/one-prestige and Proto23 constraints unchanged.
