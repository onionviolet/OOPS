The concrete benefit for YAIR is richer causality. A person can be qualified but occupied, titled but without supplies, loyal but medically unable, or experienced yet reluctant after a particular event. A facility can be unlocked but unbuilt, built but reserved, supplied but not staffed, or productive while leaving a waste/care obligation. Those combinations create useful choices without adding another generic XP bar.

Two version traps matter. Older Sins patch notes described a level-10 cap, while the developer's [1.5.0 notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1313140/view/1822556746159558) say it was removed. The current level-up and function consumers were inspected separately, so uncapped progression is not mistaken for uncapped productivity. Also, readable legacy names such as GetXP, GetDevotionGeneration and Happiness contain empty or constant implementations. Source anomalies, including a repeated missionary-tier condition and conditional productivity asymmetries, remain evidence limits rather than rules to copy blindly.

## Broader original methods beyond the selected proposals

**Progression, bodies and retained history.** Keep acquired skill, preparedness, practical benefit, institutional office, bodily support and fuel distinct. A high retained level may improve some functions and leave others unchanged; an appointment may grant one useful duty without a new supernatural source. Preparation before a transition can influence later complications. A former resident, dead person or offspring should retain the identity and causal history required by later consumers. Ordinary defeat, death, GameOver and optional prestige need independent rules.

**Materials, care and production.** Let materials pass through storage, custody, reservation, processing, useful output and actual consumption. Care can change a selected ongoing condition and expose a different treatment cost or an incomplete cure. Construction must become a functioning facility, not just a completion flag. Feeding, warmth, harvest and waste can join a small local economy; exceptions and cancellation should state whether input was never spent, returned, transformed or lost. Do not port frame-dependent chance, free special-feed branches or cinematic callback spending as transaction architecture.

**People, institutions and information.** Traits and received events can alter priorities, eligibility and later actions, while remembered relationships remain separate from willingness. A local charter can preserve policy versions, membership, independent response and specific agreed duties. A warning can precede a proportionate consequence and a repair route. YAIR's existing knowledge rules are stricter than this source's broad thought/cult-trait propagation: retain actual witnesses and received accounts, and do not give every region private actor knowledge. The original project also requires actual willing cooperation; the source's cult authority is not a reusable consent model.

**Encounters, ecology and changing opportunities.** Expeditions should use real participants and change future home capacity. Actual return can deliver supplies, a report and a bounded experience or injury consequence, with another meaningful choice afterward. Useful growth can create a new pressure without erasing previous benefits. Optional habitat and animal systems could connect care, cargo, materials and lineage in a distinct world, but that is a larger future scope with missing body/time/spatial bindings, not an implied new home-world requirement.

**Simulation and authoring.** Content definitions, current state, actor/site allocation, commands, clocks and save publication matter more than switching presentation engines. A renderer can project the same person in active or unloaded form; a visual scene must say whether it introduces reach, heat coverage, enclosures, paths or sight. Source callbacks sometimes couple payment/progress to animation or UI, whereas YAIR should keep its durable command owner. A future ideaboard can link each of these findings to current owners, a proposed experiment, implementation and later acceptance without storing proprietary code.
