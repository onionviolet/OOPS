Three selected proposals from47 findings/16 finite source paths. These are decision-ready original adaptations, not implemented gameplay or director acceptance. The fuller methods remain in[FINDINGS](FINDINGS.json),[coverage](COVERAGE.json) and[report](REPORT.md).

Same realm/one prestige. Explicit optional enrollment and versioned definitions; earlier absence/old jobs unchanged. Same inventory/body/clock/custody/save publisher. No source names, decompiled code, raw story or media copied into gameplay. Current unit/browser/gameplay/save/reload/import/failure/regression/keyboard/director gates remain deferred. Build/syntax/import/link checks may be used after an authorized implementation.

## LAMB-P01: A voluntary repair-circle charter with real consequences

At the existing home/work circle, choose equal access to the existing workbench or reserved specialist hours. Each existing consenting worker responds from their own current means, duties and received information. An unjoined resident may refuse or leave the offer open.

**Decision and commitment.** Explain and fund either declared local policy; Ask a named worker for their response; Decline or defer without implicit membership. Freeze one policy version, actual admitted member IDs, accepted duty, paid native materials/coins and exact facility capacity through the existing durable command path. No render-side policy effect or free replenishment.

**State and later consumer.** Use current world/actor facts, directed received knowledge, relationship receipts and controller predicates. Policy changes which eligible job a consenting member prioritizes and what stock their accepted service can reserve; it grants no new power or private omniscience.

**Failure and aftermath.** An unavailable/unsupported actor, occupied site or insufficient own stock refuses before commitment. Unfulfilled agreed work retains the actual promise and later response; a new charter version does not erase an older paid job.

**Current owner/gap.** Current received-response and resident-controller owners may already express individual parts. The proposed missing slice is one explicit charter/version/member/duty relationship joined to those actual consumers, not a second faction engine. Reuse `src/simulation.js`, `src/simulation_contracts.js`, `src/site_projects.js`, `src/audit_remainder_home_content.js`, `src/promise_content.js`, `src/people_knowledge.js`.

**Later acceptance.** Player can choose, fund, hear independent acceptance/refusal, see one actual changed worker decision and return to its retained outcome. Check current saved old/absent policy and insufficient-stock/publisher-failure cases in the later permitted phase.

Source findings: LAMB-F06, LAMB-F10, LAMB-F11, LAMB-F18, LAMB-F19. Exact private source hashes/spans are preserved in FINDINGS/COVERAGE.

## LAMB-P02: Earn an office that performs a useful service

An existing resident can become the circle’s safety adviser after completing actual funded inspections or learning the relevant existing method. A separate accepted appointment permits one funded practical review or care advice for another consenting actor.

**Decision and commitment.** Continue ordinary paid instruction/work; Offer the qualified person an appointment; Ask for one supported paid service or decline it. Require actual completed receipt IDs/learned execution method, supported body, accepted appointment, recipient acceptance, the adviser’s own attention and current stock/facility. Appointment fees, if chosen by the director, are original provisional costs.

**State and later consumer.** Persist learned competence, title/office, membership and actual service receipt separately using current facts/knowledge/controller APIs. The office controls one specific review/service choice; it does not mint broad stats, body capabilities or copied powers.

**Failure and aftermath.** A title without means cannot complete service. If the office is relinquished, earlier earned competence and already delivered receipts survive while future institutional access changes. Treatment claims must match the selected actual condition.

**Current owner/gap.** Current Neri/Eda learning and inspection/care receipts are already implemented-unverified. Reuse their ownership; add only the office-to-service admission/legibility join if it is still absent. No cloned person, new save domain or generic XP bar. Reuse `src/simulation_contracts.js`, `src/simulation.js`, `src/world_completion_learning_content.js`, `src/audit_remainder_home_content.js`, `src/status_care_content.js`, `src/people_knowledge.js`.

**Later acceptance.** Compare equally titled but differently prepared/occupied actors; the qualified willing actor spends real means once, gives a useful received service, and retains separate competence/office history after return and reload.

Source findings: LAMB-F15, LAMB-F16, LAMB-F17, LAMB-F22, LAMB-F44. Exact private source hashes/spans are preserved in FINDINGS/COVERAGE.

## LAMB-P03: A resident dispatch that changes home work and the returning person

Offer an existing consenting worker one supplied field inspection while the player pursues ordinary activity. That exact worker is unavailable for their home shift until return. They deliver the actual funded material/report, then retain one bounded fatigue or experience consequence affecting the next work choice.

**Decision and commitment.** Fund and agree the finite inspection; Let the worker keep the current home duty; Withdraw before commitment or use the declared supported cancellation route. Freeze the actor, accepted supplies/custody, supported-time lease, destination, departure/due state and chosen failure policy in the existing job/transition owners. If random resolution is selected, freeze the eligible draw under the current authored RNG policy rather than rerolling on viewing.

**State and later consumer.** Existing actor job/cargo/site/knowledge and returned receipt own delivery. A current condition or remembered method changes the next existing controller choice, available care or later inspection. Home and field share one actor’s attention/body/stock.

**Failure and aftermath.** No supplies/consent/body support means no dispatch. Missing destination/recipient, interruption or incomplete field work follows the explicit current lease/refund/custody rule. A report alone creates no inventory output; no universal browser-closed loss is selected.

**Current owner/gap.** Current source already has funded independent work, willing instruction/travel, native-return leases and changed return. Inspect current dispatch/return owners first; extend the exact unavailable-at-home and returning-person consequence if absent, and avoid duplicating an already delivered journey. Reuse `src/simulation.js`, `src/party_content.js`, `src/native_realm_traveler.js`, `src/realm_clock_leases.js`, `src/world_completion_learning_content.js`, `src/audit_remainder_home_content.js`, `src/status_care_content.js`.

**Later acceptance.** One journey demonstrates home capacity unavailable during field work, actual supplied expense, supported player activity in the meantime, once-only delivered cargo/report, one retained return consequence and a meaningful next work/care choice. Later save/reload and failed publication checks remain required.

Source findings: LAMB-F29, LAMB-F30, LAMB-F31, LAMB-F32, LAMB-F33, LAMB-F35, LAMB-F41. Exact private source hashes/spans are preserved in FINDINGS/COVERAGE.
