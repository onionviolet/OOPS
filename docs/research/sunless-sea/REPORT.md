# Sunless Sea: source audit

Sea makes death a choice about what the next captain receives. A rival can take a weapon, a shipmate an officer, a correspondent the chart. A child unlocks a second selection; a will protects the home and stored heirlooms. These are **seen in data or code**, not a claim about a playthrough.

**Seen in data or code:** its stories are authored events with numeric qualities, eligibility ranges, choices, outcome changes and links to other events. Hunger and terror feed those stories as well as the sailing loop. A successful investigation can make terror worse. This is a useful alternative to treating every successful check as an uncomplicated reward.

**Inferred:** the strongest YAIR proposals are an inheritance receipt based on actual surviving things, story choices that explain their locks, and danger that opens several response stories. Sea also contradicts parts of our proposed life sim: most story progress does not carry, successors receive abstract stat fractions, and map retention is a selection rather than automatic world history. Those are Sea's rules, not mistakes we should rewrite in the report.

**Seen in text:** Failbetter reports feedback that Sea's death penalties were excessive. That is reported feedback, not a universal player verdict: [developer comparison](https://www.failbettergames.com/news/sunless-sea-vs-sunless-skies-death-legacies-and-repetition).

The assigned source pass is complete. Runtime behavior, every story and every DLC branch were not tested or exhaustively read. No game launch, YAIR implementation, build, commit or uninstall occurred. See [findings](FINDINGS.md), [coverage](COVERAGE.md) and [ranked proposals](IMPLEMENTATION_PACKETS.md). Skies is the next audit, only after its installation completes.
