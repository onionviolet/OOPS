# Tabletop systems as multiverse research

2026-10-14. Requested research and direction capture. The source books have been acquired/prepared where recorded in CATALOG.md, not read in full. The mappings below are research hypotheses from publisher descriptions and existing design contracts. They do not implement gameplay or adopt a tabletop rules engine.

## D1. Several kinds of multiverse can coexist

Weibao: “We can incorporate different world systems as different types of multiverses for yair”. Accepted as a broad creative direction. Particular cosmologies, adapters and rules remain proposals. Preserve the continuing traveler and earned faculties; a source's transformation, local authority or cost is an authored property, not a universal reason to erase progress. This extends [the multiverse owner](../../worlds/MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES.md), without reprioritizing the first playable slice or deferred content.

A setting, a cosmology and a resolution system are separate things. Two settings can use Cypher while having incompatible metaphysics. One setting can contain several magical traditions. “5e compatible” describes rules compatibility, not where a world exists. Do not turn every book or dice engine into a different universe automatically.

## O1. What a boundary must settle

Do this for one actual connection and power interaction before building a universal converter. A portal should answer who travels, which body/cargo travels, how long travel takes, whether return is possible and what persists behind them. A power should identify its owner, carrier, source, required contact and supported effects. A local law should state where it applies and which interactions are actually resolved. These are independent records in the existing owner, not new mandatory taxes.

For example, learning a skill may persist as knowledge while a ritual needs material left behind. A body-specific faculty may travel when the body travels. A remote patron might retain contact, lose contact, or act through an established relay. No one outcome is the default for every world. Show the known reason when a power cannot function, and preserve unknown causes for their discovery route.

Local worlds also need ordinary lives: places to stay, work, study, relationships, resources, institutions and useful revisits. Their absence clocks and unique claims must remain causal. A new region should not silently respawn rewards or reset people. Keep knowledge, physical property, learned techniques, licenses and authority distinct.

## O2. Text is particularly well suited, but not unique

Design judgment: text makes unusual powers, changing bodies, social relationships, nested cosmologies and large catalogs comparatively cheap to represent. We can add a materially different action without first animating every body and environment. It also works well with queued study, crafting, travel and asynchronous consequences.

Text does not itself create a simulation. State, prerequisites, outcomes, scheduled jobs and persistence still need explicit resolution. Unlimited generated descriptions with no durable consequences would make the world feel less real. A human-GM rulebook often leaves judgments to the GM; those gaps need bounded procedures or declared unsupported actions in a computer game.

For YAIR, use a desktop text-led interface with visible current activity, people/place panels, ability details, concise event summaries and useful maps/diagrams. Spatial tactics, geometry, timing and resource comparisons often benefit from visuals. Avoid burying routine actions in paragraphs or requiring a chat prompt for every repeated action. Descriptions can carry atmosphere while controls and logs expose cause and progress.

## O3. Research order and a useful comparison

Start with Ars Magica core plus Covenants for one seasonal study/invention cycle and a persistent home. Compare WWN/SWN for one settlement/faction situation, Godbound for one world-changing project, then Eclipse Phase for one identity/body transition. These are reading priorities, not permission to implement five slices at once.

For each source, save exact private page/heading locators, rules, full tables and conditions; write public paraphrases with attribution. Separate source rule, inferred design opportunity and proposed YAIR behavior. Compare one example across systems: learn a method in world A, acquire its actual means, visit B, use it on a supported target, return, reload and revisit the same state. Success, unsupported use, interruption and failed storage all matter. No build or browser gameplay acceptance is claimed by this library work.

## F1. MCG and editions

Monte Cook Games is a strong shelf for varied settings and compact concepts. “Most robust” depends on the need: precise ability construction, magical research, economics, body continuity and procedural sandbox generation call for complementary references. This is a design judgment, not a measured publisher ranking. See the [source-linked sweep](../TABLETOP_BOOK_SWEEP_2026-10-14.md) for verified publisher descriptions.

In this context 5e usually means D&D Fifth Edition compatibility. Non-5e includes unrelated engines, not one alternative system. Ars Magica Fifth Edition is its own game. Keep D&D SRD 5.1 and 5.2.1 separate, and compare the actual version's content rather than merging similar names.
