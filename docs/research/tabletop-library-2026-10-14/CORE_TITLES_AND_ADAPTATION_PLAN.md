## D2. Recommended composition

Use existing YAIR runtime and persistence for one coherent playable state. Use a selected book or book family for a world's local rules and institutions. Author a concrete local progression network from source-inspected mechanics. Then use procedural generation for bounded variations inside that network: people, resources, jobs, research opportunities and conflicts that satisfy actual prerequisites. This avoids requiring a whole-world generator before a useful slice exists.

A rulebook is neither a complete world database nor executable code. Many procedures depend on human GM judgment. Preserve actual source rules separately from inferred or invented connective detail. A direct canon world needs a selected edition/continuity and inspected coverage; an original world can declare its own frozen rules using source mechanisms as inspiration. Different dice engines need not become incompatible YAIR runtimes or automatically separate universes.

An illustrative original chain: discover a method, get access to a teacher or text, acquire the actual means, queue a supported study/practice/research activity, use the learned faculty in a meaningful local situation, then use it in a supported encounter across a boundary and revisit the same durable aftermath. These are possible links, not universal prerequisites. Alternate routes, free discoveries, exceptional starts and independent faculties remain possible. A book's calendar or session-based downtime is not automatically the player's real waiting time.

## O6. Consequences to account for now

Keep chains as connected opportunities with branches, not only a fixed quest order. A world should support ordinary residence and repeated use of skills after the first unlock. Separate knowing a technique, having the bodily/tool/material means, having permission and having an authority's actual reach. Do not impose a universal loss of powers when crossing worlds merely to normalize balance.

Persist generated people, unique items, resolved outcomes and the generation revision, not just a seed. Reload and revisit should retain earned state. Generators must produce reachable, supported situations; an evocative paragraph is not enough. Idle progress should advance through supported queued activity or sustained conditions. New book acquisition is research readiness, not source reading, implemented gameplay or native acceptance.

The smallest useful proof is one deep local progression route, one materially different alternate approach, one supported cross-world use, and a return/reload/revisit with actual consequences. This is a proposed evaluation example under the standing slice priorities, not dispatch authorization or a new completion gate for unrelated work.

## Reusable adaptation constraint, 2026-10-18

A tabletop point budget, rest/session cadence or GM adjudication procedure is
source-qualified evidence, not automatically a global idle-game rule (AD1/AD5/
AD16/AD38). Keep source wording/provenance separate from the modeled mechanism
and the character's knowledge. When selected, one useful native action and its
actual resource/clock/custody consumer prove a slice; the illustrative cross-world
proof above is a later seam test when crossing is in scope, not required for every
local prototype. Use current AR/MR/SR/UR and compatibility decisions. This audit
rechecked no external product claim, source book or current library contents.
