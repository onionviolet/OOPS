## Proposed batch laws

Use a small reaction network with real intermediate amounts. OpenStax's distinction between a net reaction and its steps suggests tracking intermediates that are produced and later used. Its kinetics summary suggests giving rate and exposure their own fields, and distinguishing a catalyst's pathway from a consumed reagent. These are design analogies. The Hogwarts reactions, units and thresholds below are invented. [OpenStax Chemistry 2e, §12.6](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/12-6-reaction-mechanisms), [Chapter 12 summary](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/12-summary)

| Rule | State and operation | Default, dial and reason |
|---|---|---|
| **C1. Extract and transfer** | A lot carries identity, quantity, form, provenance, custody, preparation and revision. A preparation step transfers measured constituent amounts to an available form or a separate extract. Keep the spent solid and any remainder in their actual containers. | Where the law describes extraction, the tool and technique change yield. Crushing a bean opens more of it in the example. Any independent tool-material contribution needs its own evidence. A receipt identifies what was prepared, when, how much moved and where the remainder went. |
| **C2. Evaluate in event order** | The batch carries present constituent amounts, volume, vessel, thermal state, stage/work progress and any retained residue. Settle elapsed work since the last event, then admit the exact added lot, change the fire, or apply agitation and run the reactions enabled at that boundary. A later addition sees the current state. | Use an explicit on/off-fire condition where the scene distinguishes that action. Add temperature and residence where cooling, heating rate or a hot hold changes a decision. Turning the fire off immediately changes fire contact, while the liquid cools over time. A reversal can read accumulated directional work. Symmetric agitation can have a direction-independent effect. |
| **C3. Convert by available amounts and conditions** | Reaction rows name reactant and product ratios, a trigger or exposure window, extent, required apparatus where relevant and any retained participant. Compute extent from the least available reactant and cap it by work or exposure. Carry unreacted stock, intermediates, waste and escaping mass forward. | Prefer authored finite steps and monotone progress over continuous tick simulation for the cases here. Temperature, duration, concentration and contact affect the particular row that reads them. Different pathways may share a reactant. When a measured or authored condition favors one, encode the reason and apply it to the same finite stock. Where both run together and competition matters, apportion the available stock by their authored relative rates over a defined interval. Keep unknown competition visible in forecasts. A catalyst provides a pathway and is recovered unchanged in this abstraction. A consumed binder enters the product balance. P2 leaves the real added component's role between those possibilities open. |
| **C4. Project cues, classify and deliver** | Project sensory cues and assays from actual state. A recipe records a known method and expected signature. A prepared product has concentration, available volume, typed effects and applicable routes. Applying a portion binds the actual target, dose and exposure. | A familiar cue supports a stage judgment. A lesson can grade a flask, oral administration can affect a patient's sleep or poisoning, and a spill can expose a body or damage a vessel. Each consumer reads the facts relevant to it. Doubling all amounts at the same concentration doubles available portions when apparatus and work can support it. Adding carrier alone reduces concentration and can change contact or heating. |

The book leaves most ingredient functions open. Record named participation first. Add a reusable property when a source isolates it or an original test needs it. Candidate selection can use the character's demonstrated reactions, available lots and the stated goal, then call the same evaluator as a manually entered method. A preview can say that the assay identified two ingredients and their combined response is untested. Committing the same method uses the actual material truth and records the observed response. Quick repeat, guided and deep brewing differ in which decisions are prompted. An interrupted brew retains its actual state and spent inputs, so resuming, decanting and discarding have consequences that follow what remains.

## Three derivations and an independent check

### T1. Replay the Draught of Living Death

Use a known prepared base and one sopophorous bean. Asphodel, wormwood and the classroom use of valerian are source fields. Their independent roles and the base's makeup are unresolved. For the first trace, assign the prepared base a six-unit precursor pool and take its other 94 units as carrier. Assign a bean 12 units, of which the demonstrated flat crush transfers eight units of juice to the cauldron. Account for four units left in the solids. When juice enters, one unit of it and one precursor unit yield two intermediate units. The resulting 108-unit brew holds 12 intermediate units and two unused juice units. A lilac cue triggers at 9% intermediate before stirring.

Give the intermediate two ways to settle under agitation. In the example, eight counterclockwise strokes settle up to two units. Seven counterclockwise strokes build enough directional work for a reversal that settles up to eight units on the next clockwise stroke. That reversal spends the directional work. A pale-pink cue triggers at 7% settled material at the checkpoint. After the first full seven-plus-one cycle, eight of the 108 units are settled, four intermediate units remain, and the cue appears. The class judges the result from the flask. Sleep potency, dose, and the cue at the eventual endpoint can be calibrated separately when a consumer and evidence are available.

The step order, lilac, pale pink and class judgment are checked against P1. All arithmetic, the proposed intermediate, ordinary settling, and the precise cue thresholds are original. A narrower alternative would encode each printed or marginal instruction as a named operation. That would replay the scene cheaply, while offering little explanation when a player reverses too soon. A larger kinetic model would be useful when timing or heat measurements can distinguish two explanations. The proposed first fit uses explicit amounts and a short directional history.

### T2. Change one process input at a time

The paper model gives slicing three extracted units from the same 12-unit bean. An equal-work checkpoint compares eight counterclockwise strokes with seven and a reversal. Keep the prepared base, tools, heat and elapsed work fixed. The table is a controlled prediction, with the combined annotated case serving as calibration. Hermione's run supplies a comparison scene with uncontrolled differences.

| Bean preparation | Stir motion at checkpoint | Intermediate before stirring | Settled after eight strokes | Predicted cues |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Slice | Eight counterclockwise | 6 | 2 | Below both cue thresholds |
| Crush | Eight counterclockwise | 12 | 2 | Lilac on admission, below pale pink at this checkpoint |
| Slice | Seven counterclockwise, one clockwise | 6 | 6 | Below both cue thresholds at this checkpoint |
| Crush | Seven counterclockwise, one clockwise | 12 | 8 | Lilac on admission, then pale pink |

An early reversal should fail to deliver the eight-unit conversion when the accumulated work is short. Subsequent stirring can still change the batch. A doubled batch should preserve the same cue sequence if the vessel and maker deliver equivalent extraction, heat and work per amount. Those are proposed counterchecks. The eight-stroke comparison gives no final grade for the printed method, which tells the student to continue to an observed endpoint.

### T3. Resolve an unlisted blended antidote

Create a supervised assay fixture with two original poison components. They leave two units each of individual burdens A and B and two units of a joint burden G. Set up two separate exposure controls with A and B in isolation. Scarpin's spell reveals the constituent identities in the fixture. The joint burden is a distinct proposed observation through the test target. The bench carries four units each of reagents that address A and B. Those functional fields come from the controls. Add two units of an original binding reagent with a demonstrated ability to join both antidote fractions. At the supported hold condition, each reaction consumes one unit of each of the three reagents and yields three units of the joint antidote. Its target effect is one unit of G cleared per three units delivered. Hold carrier, receiver, baseline and administered portion constant across runs. For this toy arithmetic the test portion carries all listed active units, with carrier omitted from the table.

| Method evaluated by the same rules | Free A reagent / free B reagent / joint product | Burden remaining A / B / G |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mix the separate antidotes | 4 / 4 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 2 |
| Add two units of binding reagent at the supported hold | 2 / 2 / 6 | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| Add the same amount after a hot hold that degrades one binder unit | 3 / 3 / 3 | 0 / 0 / 1 |
| Use four binder units at the supported hold | 0 / 0 / 12 | 2 / 2 / 0 |

In the hot run a competing reaction converts half the binder to inactive residue before it can bind. In the four-unit run the joint reaction uses all the free reagents, leaving none to clear the individual burdens. This gives the player a reason to measure or stage the two fractions. All ingredients, effects and amounts here are original. P2 constrains the need for a blend-level response. P4 keeps room for a separately delivered remedy that works by its own mechanism. The fixture makes no claim about the composition of the poison that Ron drank.

### T4. Check the Cure for Boils against the representation

Use the same lot of quills and one addition operation in a replay of P3. The default order simmers the prepared base, removes the cauldron from the fire and adds the quills. The contrast adds them while the fire is still on. Under an original hot-contact law, the active quill fraction competes for the dissolved cure fraction and creates a corrosive product. The hazard can damage the vessel. Spill volume and actual contact then determine the exposure delivered to a person. This fits both P3's observed causal order and the successful written path represented by the repo. It also exposes an implementation trap: two batches with the same total heating can differ when the quills arrived at different times. End-of-batch heat arithmetic alone loses that distinction. The threshold for a ruined vessel and Neville's exact dose remain open.

### ACCOUNT FOR NOW: proficiency, strength and self-modification

**C5. Give proficiency a job.** Under MR21, understanding makes it easier to infer why a cue changed, choose the relevant measurement, adapt a written method and recognize when to stop. Practice can steady a stir, improve extraction, reduce set-up time or increase the chance of hitting a narrow window. Retain book instructions, hands-on familiarity and understanding as separable evidence in existing knowledge and skill owners. Treat current focus, fatigue, physical form, reach and dexterity as facts about the maker that the operation can read when relevant. An apprentice with excellent notes and a steady apparatus can produce the same dose as an expert when both actually achieve the same result.

**C6. Reduce steps according to what the step does.** Collapse repeated player inputs into one paid action that still produces the same work and ordered changes. A timed stir, trained helper, or self-stirring vessel can carry out work in the background. A pre-prepared lot moves its preparation out of the current session and brings along the actual receipt. Expertise can remove a redundant check when the maker already knows the relevant fact. A better method can reduce the underlying work when its process reaches the needed state earlier. A maturation period, late identity binding, or narrow reaction window remains a real part of the route as long as the mechanism reads it. A new means can change that mechanism if it actually supplies what the old step achieved. Let quick, guided and deep use the same settlement logic and give the player an intervention point when their available choices change the outcome. [Activity abstraction rules](../ACTIVITY_ABSTRACTION_RULES.md)

**C7. Give strength a place to come from.** Record batch yield, concentration, selectivity, stability and unwanted effects independently. At use, bind serving size, route, recipient and timing to the supported response. A maker's understanding can yield a stronger result when they choose a better extraction, raise conversion, avoid degradation or tune a magical operation that actually affects the product. A precise result may also be gentler, last longer, have fewer side effects, or give more doses at the same strength. For a maker-linked potency rule, name the step, contribution, source and resulting property. A self-modified brewer might gain a steadier hand, a useful sense for a cue, greater heat tolerance, or access to a magical contribution. Any of those changes can matter when a step reads that capability. If a method draws on stored energy, a practitioner, an artifact or the environment, account for the actual transfer and any resulting state. The books do not establish a numerical Hogwarts-wide energy pool or a universal conversion from personal power to potion strength. The supplement's lightning is a condition of that method. A rule that uses the storm as a measured charge source would be original.

**C8. Keep tags descriptive and put the reaction in a rule.** Use [making traits](../../src/making_traits.js) and the [tag registry](../../src/tag_store.js) to identify candidate forms and functional roles. Keep quantities and declared units on [catalogue axes](../../src/craft_materials.js), and custody, collection conditions, preparation and exact parent lots in [material lineage](../../src/material_lineage.js) or the existing Hogwarts lot owner. A hidden functional tag can help the evaluator select a local rule without revealing a secret to the character. A plant tag can narrow a search to botanical stock. An observed profile and local law determine what that stock does in the mixture. Bind the actual conditions where a profile acts, including relevant substrate, temperature, concentration and target. Preserve the distinction between a defaulted axis, an untested axis and a demonstrated absence when projecting a forecast. Link the magical operation through the existing [spell seam](../../src/spell_seam.js) and the delivery through its condition, body, or ability owner. [Power grammar](../../src/power_grammar.js) can represent separate magnitude and duration where applicable. It supplies no evidence for a Hogwarts-wide energy budget.

#### Source basis and the common account

IUPAC describes adsorption as accumulation at an interface. It motivates separate liquid and surface reservoirs. The USGS filtration study found that pore size, filter material, volume processed and sample composition can affect the apparent dissolved result. Its operational measurement is a useful counterexample to a universal mesh-only rule. Cotton dyeing experiments distinguish bath exhaustion, dye fixation and wash-off. Work on Reactive Red 195 shows that wash liquid can receive loose dye and, under some conditions, dye released from previously bound material. These observations justify separate reservoirs and conditions. The following dye and fixture values are original YAIR proposals. [IUPAC adsorption](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/A00155) · [USGS filtration study](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/effect-membrane-filtration-artifacts-dissolved-trace-element-concentrations) · [Cotton dyeing experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9781206/) · [Reactive dye wash study](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/15589250231223466)

Use one conserved constituent, **blue-tracer equivalent**, from the verified cauldron trial. This unit counts the blue component through physical partition and the proposed dye forms. If a reaction consumes a binder or substrate, its amount belongs in the total-matter ledger independently. Keep carrier volume, energy and elapsed time as separate quantities. A material can enter a bottle, adhere to equipment, remain in an in-flight batch, leave in a named waste stream, or remain on a workpiece. A measured change in color alone does not identify which destination received the material.

#### Interruption and the boundary between owners

- **Liner:** stop before the committed wash settles and leave the previously retained tracer in place. A cancellation policy determines whether the water reservation returns. The present workroom has a pending rinse that survives save and settles once. It has no partial-wash cancellation law.
- **Filter:** stop after the first five volume units and keep the remaining feed, first filtrate, and any captured material as distinct holdings. Continue from the measured cursor using current equipment. Changing the mesh during the interruption can legitimately change what the second portion yields. At the handoff from apparatus to stock, the filter stage publishes one exact retained lot and one filtrate lot or leaves the corresponding material in the machine.
- **Dye bath:** stop after uptake and before fixation. Already transferred dye remains loose on the identified cloth. The retained bath, wet carrier on removal, spent heat and elapsed exposure remain with their owners. Resuming checks temperature and remaining means again. Rinsing is a new transfer that can recover loose material. It cannot undo an already spent stage.

The integration discriminator is **wash jar → filtration job → cloth lot**. The workroom hands off an actual measured lot, and the receiving equipment resolves current mesh and capacity before accepting it. A rejected admission leaves the wash in workroom custody. Once a stage moves a portion, the old owner records only the remainder and an event reference. Quantities and identities can then be checked without pooling the same tracer in two inventories.
