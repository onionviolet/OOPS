## Evidence and root rules

| Rule | Observed evidence and boundary | Proposed resolver consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CR1 · Identity outlasts a change of stage or keeper | Held English *Philosopher’s Stone*, ch. 14, B1 prepared copy pp. 187–191. Hagrid gets an egg, keeps it by the fire, and quotes a book about care after hatching. The hatchling grows about threefold in length in a week, its care interferes with his work, and Charlie's group arranges a timed transfer. Each stage can supply a different clock, diet, body hazards and habitat requirements. Capacity includes effects on the place and keeper's time. Keep legal permission, possession and the keeper's beliefs separately addressable. |
| CR2 · Habitat support is a network of actual supplies | Held English *Reverend Insanity*, ch. 826, local ri9 chapter paragraphs 8–12 and 54–59. The text shows an affordable charcoal-stone habitat and describes the grass → rabbit → fox supply relationship in developing the blood-marked land. The [wiki index](https://reverend-insanity.fandom.com/wiki/Volumes) independently locates the chapter title. A fox-equivalent availability calculation cannot determine whether an Immortal Gu can exist. |
| CR3 · Heritable potential, expressed condition and harvested quality are separate | A [CSIRO selection experiment](https://connectsci.au/cp/article-abstract/11/5/836/29684/Efficiency-of-conversion-of-food-to-wool-I?redirectedFrom=fulltext) found differences in wool output per food eaten in offspring of two selected Merino families on its trial ration. The latter is a game precedent, and its evaluator bodies were unavailable in the digest. Let elapsed care and conditions alter actual output measurements such as quantity, fiber diameter and integrity as local laws warrant. A separate administration step binds the specific target, route, amount and exposure. The creature resolver decides response over time from its biology and current state. Separate outside rain, moisture at the wound interface and dressing fit. The pig evidence gives no sheep remedy, universal moisture rule or game recovery rate. |
| CR5 · Engineering needs a bound interface and a test under load | The shipped reed construct has a 300 g bare frame, a fitted 450 g capacity contribution and a 600 g parcel. This proves a simple capability threshold with actual custody. The [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook appendix](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/system-engineering-handbook-appendix/) treats interfaces and their verification as explicit parts of system definition. It supplies a verification pattern, while the Village numbers are game dials. | An assembly binds part, attachment, load and operating environment. The author can use a capacity sum for the proven case. A strained assembly may instead depend on weakest connection, force direction or a changing load. A fitting may help one job and alter gait or skin contact in a biological carrier. The structural resolver owns those predictions. |

## Shared contract and one testable feedback loop

Use the existing [creature methods](../../src/creature_methods.js), [creature lifecycle](../../src/creature_lifecycle.js) and [craft working](../../src/craft_working.js) boundaries. Shared operations bind actual subjects and means, carry units and provenance, quote conditions, keep custody during work, and hand outcomes to their owners. Each domain supplies its causal evaluator. Keep observation records with the character who made them. A reproducible method needs its law revision and enough recorded conditions to repeat the result.

**First experiment.** In the proposed [Village dressing trial](../COMPANIONS_FAMILIARS_AND_PETS_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md#making-that-changes-the-next-creature-outcome-2026-11-11-ideaboard), compose a contact pad and separate rain cover from ordinary materials. Compare moving the wax from the outer cover to the contact layer with the same wound and weather. The observation to compare is whether rain penetrates the dressing and interrupts the actual care interval. Any contact-material effect needs its own Village rule and observation. Wet surroundings and wound moisture are separate variables. Start with a six-minute recovery fixture. Interrupt at minute four, save and reload, and compare healing and first usable wool time with the shelter or effective-cover control. The existing injury state carries two owed minutes where the interval ended early. Wool from that animal goes through native weaving, and the cloth can feed an existing sling. None of these experimental timings are real treatment advice or book facts.

**Contrasting proof.** Reuse the same evidence and custody structure for the Ridgeback egg. A hatch changes body, diet, safe space and work demand. Then move the same hatchling to a new keeper with a known travel route. Test a warmed egg, a failed support condition and a saved mid-transfer animal. Keep the reported growth anchor distinct from invented time curves. For Gu-adjacent ecology, vary prey or substrate while holding the enclosure and local time fixed. Confirm the consumer count responds for the causal reason and that the Gu owner still decides unique emergence.

**Hard case.** A living graft may have its own appetite, custody and response while modifying the host's care. A preparation can change both. Use one atomic time-boundary settlement across both existing identities, with explicit resource debits and a preserved attachment reference. Compare host-only supply, graft-only supply, and removal while the host still owes recovery. A second-order consumer such as fleece should see the settled host condition. Do not infer that a part that behaves like material in one operation has lost its own identity.

**Evidence accounting.** Targeted local reading sampled B1 ch. 14 (three windows, about 400 words), B6 ch. 9 (one window, about 100 words), and targeted paragraph matches in RI ch. 826 (about 120 words shown in the read, plus the existing chapter synthesis). External spot checks used HTML source/index and article landing pages. No PDF bodies were read. Full creature catalogues, comparative animal behavior, full veterinary evaluation, and comprehensive Gu, potion, chemistry and engineering corpora remain outside this bounded pass. The first two new proofs are proposals, with the fleece and courier receipt as the existing runtime evidence.

## Counterexamples, interference, scale and teaching

November 11 follow-through. These are decision tests for the proposal. Existing Village code supplies the paid care interval, injury remainder and wool gate. Rain and dressing effects are proposed inputs to that interval.

**CF5 · Separate two explanations.** A successful wrapped ewe fits both “the outer cover prevented rain from reaching the pad” and “waxed material against the wound improved recovery.” The bench observation that water beads on wax fits both explanations. Vary placement and exposure independently. Hold fabric, fit, hay, wound and ewe traits fixed.

| Matched trial | Predicted if outside water entry explains the benefit | What would change the rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary sheltered care, untreated pad | Six supported minutes suffice. This is the baseline available in the current Village loop. | If care stalls here, diagnose supply or fit before attributing anything to rain. |
| Shelter, swap a waxed contact pad for an untreated one | The barrier-only explanation predicts no gain from waxing contact under shelter. | A consistent change here needs a separate contact-layer explanation. |
| Shower, waxed outer cover over untreated pad | Less water enters at the exposed face. With open seams and stable fit, recovery can continue. | A wet pad despite a sound cover points to seams, movement, or a second water source. |
| Same shower, same wax applied to contact pad instead | Cover performance can fall even though the waxed contact sample beads water on the bench. Under the proposed fixture, an interrupted interval retains the unfinished recovery. | If both placements keep the contact layer equally protected and recover equally, drop placement as the cause in this fixture and look for another controlling variable. |

The book precedent for this method is the cut-versus-crush extraction observation in CR4. Use a near miss and a later use to isolate which step mattered. The wound studies justify distinguishing the interface from the surrounding weather. They supply no predicted result for this proposed ewe fixture. [Source comparisons](#evidence-and-root-rules)

**CF6 · Make interference visible and reversible.** A continuous wax coating might help against rain while blocking a pad's ability to draw in fluid. Tightening the binding might reduce slipping while changing contact pressure. For a specific material failure, compare wiping grime from the exterior with a cleaning method that also strips its wax. Record both outcomes on the same covering. If the cleaner leaves the pad untouched, retain its previous preparation record. During a shower, test whether a sound spare cover or a roof restores support. The care owner settles progress at the change boundary, the material owner retains which layer changed, and the character can inspect what they actually saw. The engine should explain why the replacement worked and preserve any residue or unfinished care.

**CF7 · Scale elapsed work and available means.** Repeat the same dressing on several animals using the available cloth, dry workspace and worker time. Compare a first and last fitting, a roof with fewer stalls than animals, and an advance split at the moment the dry stock runs out. An idle order can proceed through earned repetitions and pause on a condition that changes the decision. Keep completed fittings, reserved supplies, the precise affected animal and elapsed progress through reload. Larger lots can use grouped settlement where conditions and outcomes are equivalent. Split groups at a real boundary, such as a late arrival, stock exhaustion or a creature needing individual treatment. A saved method should retain the observed ranges and arrangement, while each run binds the present means. This is the creature counterpart to checking a changed vessel size, heat distribution or mixing condition before replaying a vial experiment at vat scale. [Activity abstraction rules](../ACTIVITY_ABSTRACTION_RULES.md) · [Reaction rate factors](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/12-2-factors-affecting-reaction-rates)

**CF8 · Teach observations and repeatable conditions.** A breeder's log can separate inherited fleece potential from what a particular season produced. An apprentice's failed dressing can show which seam admitted water. A mechanic's rubbed sling can locate an overloaded attachment. The note should preserve the observed symptom, actual method revision and conditions, what the author inferred, and what would falsify that inference. Another character can reproduce the arrangement with locally equivalent material properties or get a different result and add a new observation. A reader who only saw the pad bead water should not learn the hidden recovery result. Use the existing knowledge owner for who knows each observation, the item or method record for reproducible steps, and the creature record for what happened to the animal. [Knowledge rules](../KNOWLEDGE_RULES.md) · [Making rules](../MAKING_RULES.md)

Proposed proof order: matched shelter and shower controls, then one cleaning interference, then repeat across animals with a known supply boundary, then hand the notes to a second keeper. Each step should reuse the first experiment's existing care and wool consumer. This extends the research proposal without changing runtime.

## Foundation-first pass

November 11 steering. Expand the design space and establish rules before choosing further playable slices. The [terrapin regulator receipt](../gm/dispatch/living-regulator-2026-11-11/CHECKPOINT.md#shared-living-state-and-feedback-proof) is evidence for one feedback loop, with Village-specific values. Use it to find seams and counterexamples. The following is a working map of the broader possibilities, with unresolved mechanisms marked as questions for the foundation pass.

| Axis | Coverage to design for | Test that distinguishes competing rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Identity and organization** | An individual, body, body part, host and symbiont, lineage, colony and cohort can be related without sharing the same identity. Include development, replacement bodies, fission, merging, inherited traits, learned behavior, reversible attachments and lasting transformations. | After a host changes bodies, can an old friend recognize it? Does its implant travel too? The world must answer separately for identity, the material implant and who can know the change. |
| **Properties and operations** | Distinguish intrinsic properties, contributions, current conditions, expressed capabilities and observed effects. Account for position, order, tools, dosage, alternative means, compatibility, removal and interactions. Share typed bindings, units and receipts. World-specific resolvers handle mechanisms where the shared operations do not explain the evidence. | The same carried mass may fit two harnesses on a flat road, then only one survives a crosswind or a wet fastening. The same coating may protect an outer face and block absorption on the contact face. Both decisions use the position of the part and the actual environment. |
| **History and time** | Track material preparation and residue, growth and recovery, charge or hunger, intervention, delayed effects and causally committed facts. Describe which changes are continuous, which settle at a boundary, and what can be undone. | A washed vessel and a never-used vessel can look identical yet produce different traces. A shell signal can be queued when the player switches the inlet to manual. Specify what the change cancels, what persists, and how a reload at that instant reproduces it. |
| **Scale and procedural composition** | Define when individuals, cohorts, devices or sites can settle together, when to split them, and what a planner may vary. Use repeatable search from legal means, evidence and local laws, with entity, time and operation budgets. Preserve individually committed obligations when aggregating. | A treatment may cover one ewe but fail for a herd when dry stalls run out halfway through. A vessel heated evenly on a small bench may develop hot and cold zones at vat scale. The method can be reused if its premises still hold, or revised for the new capacity. |
| **Discovery and comparison** | Retain observations, controls, inferences, law revision, and what can be taught or reproduced. Include accidental discoveries, contradictory sources, failed trials, delegation and tools that reveal only part of the state. | If water beads on a pad, the player can record the bench observation. Only a timed animal comparison establishes what it did to recovery. A second keeper can repeat the method without inheriting the first keeper's private evidence. |

The shared contract should answer five questions for each proposed rule: which objects and actors it binds, which owner supplies each fact, what event or elapsed interval changes it, what can observe or use the result, and what example would falsify it. State the ordinary default, the local dial and its in-world reason. Distinguish existing behavior, source-supported claims, inferred explanations and original design proposals.

For interacting systems, name the direction of each dependency and the time at which it takes effect. The terrapin supplies shell position, the inlet supplies airflow, and airflow changes the humidity used for the next response. That establishes a delayed loop. An instant loop, several creatures driving the same inlet, or an external override needs its own order or settlement law. For history, give one owner authority over each change and let other systems read projections rather than storing a second truth. For invention, generate or suggest candidates through those same laws and discard proposals that fail the actual binding or consumer test.

**Deliverable for this pass:** a source and contradiction matrix, a vocabulary with ownership and time semantics, and a ranked list of contrasts that would expose weak rules. The map should include areas still unresolved. Prioritize gaps where different interpretations predict meaningfully different play. New playable slices can be selected from that evidence later.

### Ownership and time contracts

Foundation goal active. This section is design work. A future implementation may extend an existing owner or introduce a separate one where a counterexample demonstrates the need. The cross-domain map should use these interfaces and link back to the creature cases.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Ordinary keeper, authorized handler and present controller can differ. |

A snapshot valid at combat entry may need a refresh if the encounter itself changes the body or environment. |

**Cross-lane handoff.** The creature lane owns living state, exposure and biological response. The crafting/material lane owns properties, lots, fitting inputs and preparation history. Potion and chemistry lanes own dose composition, transformation and preparation. Engineering owns assembly and actuation; a living component can contribute a signal and read the changed environment without engineering owning its appetite or wound. The Gu lane owns refinement, Gu-specific upkeep and emergence/uniqueness. Derive and Implement World Rules can synthesize where the same facts and settlement vocabulary cross these boundaries. Beam Catch, frayed tether and replacement Immortal Gu already supply contrasting technique, time and custody cases for that map. Further playable routes are deferred.

### Contradictions that change the creature contract

November 11 follow-through. This bounded pass adds one held English book window and three contrasting experimental sources. Status labels apply to the claim in each row. The proposed rule is an adjustable design.

| Code | Source claim and rival reading | What the contrast decides | What remains open |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CR6 · Form can change affordances and recognition independently | **Stated in-world:** Lupin recounts in *Prisoner of Azkaban*, ch. 18, B3 prepared copy pp. 1013–1014, that his friends could approach in animal forms and the smallest could reach the Willow's knot. He also reports becoming less dangerous in their company. A body-only reading explains the physical access and might explain initial safety. It cannot by itself explain whether familiarity changes later behavior. | Keep form-specific fit and sensory affordances separate from identity, recognition and relationship. Compare a familiar and unfamiliar animal with equivalent bodies in the same setting. Then change the familiar actor's form without removing their history. Fit, perceived identity and behavior each need their own observed outcome. | How much the relationship mattered in canon is a report by a participant. A new game's values and repeatability remain original design. |
| CR7 · Development can carry forward some learned behavior | **Shown:** In Blackiston, Silva Casey and Weiss (2008), tobacco hornworms trained late showed adult odor avoidance; those trained earlier showed avoidance after larval molts but did not show it as adults. Washing conditioned pupal cases and adding odor to naïve cases separated the observed result from a surface-residue explanation. Figures 2–4 are the direct contrasts. [Primary experiment](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0001736) | Preserve historical identity and training evidence, while the local development transition determines which response remains available in the new form. Represent cue, sensory channel, learned response and stage separately. Compare early/late training and wash/odor controls rather than zeroing or copying every response when a body changes. | The finding is for a specific species, task and life-stage comparison. The proposed neural explanation is not proven by the behavioral result. |
| CR8 · Failure to respond can depend on context | **Shown in an animal experiment:** Free-operant behavior can renew after extinction when context changes. The paper discusses competing accounts of how context gates response. [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3059840/) | A single failed cue is evidence of failure under those conditions. Preserve learned history and record current cue access, context, incentives and body constraints. Compare a sound cue blocked by noise with the same cue heard at home. Compare changed incentives with the cue still audible. A training record can stay intact while the creature does something different. | Moss currently saves chase practice and a keeper-scoped bond. This proves persistence and transfer. It does not measure context-dependent recall or adopt an extinction rule. |
| CR9 · A collective decision can depend on local participation | **Shown:** Seeley and Visscher's honeybee nest-site experiments contrast consensus among advertising scouts with attendance at a candidate site. The work motivates separating candidate-site visits from reports about that site. [Primary study abstract and text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/225491722_Quorum_sensing_during_nest_site_selection_by_honeybee_swarms) | A colony may own a decision while local scouts supply its evidence. Test identical total scout counts distributed across two sites versus concentrated at one. A single global enthusiasm meter would predict the same outcome where a local quorum might not. Persist evidence and the decision boundary independently from anonymous stock. | Quorum thresholds are local to the chosen species and circumstances. This experiment does not model a magically shared mind or grant all colonies individual agency. |

**Learning vocabulary.** Distinguish the cue emitted, cue perceived, action the body can perform, prior association, current context and observed response. Keeping these separate supports the familiar returning to a prior keeper, a newly transformed being responding differently, a false cue that diverts a swarm, or a maker changing material residue without changing what an animal learned. Keep irreversible biological changes and external memory alterations explicit in the world resolver. A context-dependent absence of response can be represented without a forgetting rule.

### Birth, coupled bodies and powers

The broad foundation needs separate operations for a change to an existing subject, a new descendant and a relation joining existing subjects. The current sheep brood is one timed carrier route. Its two parents and categorical inheritance describe Village data. They do not determine the shape of the following contracts.

| Code | Source and competing reading | Reusable rule and counterexample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CR10 · Reproductive roles are part of the method | **Shown:** genetic fingerprinting found offspring from two isolated female Komodo dragons, and one subsequently also bred sexually. The investigators suggest mate absence as a trigger. The occurrence and proposed trigger have different evidentiary strength. [Watts et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/4441021a) | A reproduction event binds a local method, contributors, gestating or incubating subject, exposure and birth or hatching boundary. Contributors need not be two co-located adults. Compare the same female in two supported reproductive routes, then remove a necessary incubation condition. Save a committed embryo or egg when the local method creates it. A failed condition may delay development or produce loss according to the world law. |
| CR11 · Division allocates a body and creates lineage edges | **Shown:** planarian fission depends on mechanical interaction with the substrate and yields distinct fragments. The study measures where separation occurs and investigates asymmetric mechanics. It does not address fictional personal continuity. [Malinowski et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5642676/) | Capture body allocation at separation, bind descendants and assign identity by the local world rule. Keep pending restoration or regeneration on the right fragment. Compare full fission, regrowth of one missing part, and a detachable living graft. The same quantity of living tissue can leave three different identity and recovery outcomes. A split cannot duplicate a scarce implant, assigned load or custody claim. |
| CR12 · Coupling changes conditions on both sides | **Shown:** fungal and algal growth responded differently to media in experimental co-cultures. Contact and layered structures appeared under some conditions, and the technique did not accelerate primary lichen symbionts in that study. [Muggia et al.](https://doi.org/10.1093/femsle/fny115) | A living attachment can be a relationship with exchanged resources or effects, plus its actual attachment and environment. Keep both participants, their individual needs and any shared store addressable. Compare a host and partner grown alone, then joined with equal available light or feed. A candidate law may change one participant's performance while leaving the other's unchanged. Separate material-only attachment, living partner and collective as different readings of the same apparent assembly. |
| CR13 · A harvested power may carry more than composition | **Stated in-world:** Firenze describes unicorn blood sustaining life and a curse in *Philosopher’s Stone*, ch. 15, B1 prepared copy p. 208. His account ties the cost to slaying a unicorn. It does not isolate whether the material, the manner of obtaining it, the drinker's act or their combination determines the curse. | A power-bearing lot can retain acquisition history and relevant claims as well as material properties. The potion or material resolver returns what was delivered. The world's power and creature owners evaluate target fit, duration, cost and any continuing relation. On paper compare otherwise equivalent lots taken through different means. Preserve both readings until another scene or authored choice settles the law. No freely obtained blood outcome is asserted as canon. |

The vocabulary for these cases can use `contributor`, `carrier`, `offspring`, `host`, `partner`, `part`, `lot`, `attachment`, `recipient`, `exposure`, `claim` and `effect source` as bound roles. The same ID may fill more than one role when the mechanism warrants it. Roles describe what an operation needs. The local law decides whether that actual binding fits. An egg kept in a communal nest may have different contributors, current keeper, heat source and future caretaker. A manufactured living construct may bind a chassis, an existing inhabitant, a donor and a fabricator. The design must record whose continuity is affected before and after the act.

**Consequences across domains.** A power can end at the next missed feed, persist in a detached lot, or survive as a learned method when the original carrier goes away. Each case needs an explicit sustaining source and consumer. Two effects that look similar on screen can depend on different sources, so they can fail differently when a part is removed or a dose is withheld. The shared record keeps bindings, current custody, time and evidence. It leaves the local owners responsible for how biology, material history, magical control or engineering conditions produce the result. ### Procedural proposals and rejection evidence

| Bind the question | Intended effect, relevant terrain and clock, permitted observations, targets, tools and resources. **Paper case · An inlet that runs a drying rack.** A maker sees a terrapin's chamber inlet cycle and proposes linking it to a drying rack for short pulses. The existing terrapin law offers a timed signal, but a rack needs an independently measured airflow and a load-bearing linkage. A planner should propose the known linkage plus an observed duct, then ask engineering whether it can move the intended air and ask creature care whether the altered chamber still supplies what the animal needs. The rack's bulk airflow reading alone cannot prove safety or adequate drying. Replacing the fitting with one that reduces movement lag might stop the pulses while improving humidity control. If the terrapin leaves for care, a manual bypass can supply steady air, with its own authority and costs. This is an original candidate, beyond the regulator proof. A declared control tests identical rack loads with the terrapin linked, the inlet fixed and the inlet manually pulsed. It distinguishes biological signaling from airflow as the direct drying cause.

**Additional high-value contrasts.** After the first ranked identity and behavior cases, compare a single contributor and a paired birth under different incubation windows (CR10), allocate one identifiable attachment across a split without duplicating it (CR11), and remove a living graft while its host still owes care (CR12). For material-linked powers, use the same target and dose with changed acquisition provenance (CR13). A failed fit, a conserved boundary and an observable downstream effect are required in each paper case. The engine must leave room for a world that supplies a better explanation.

Evidence accounting: one targeted held B1 paragraph, plus primary article HTML or abstracts for Komodo dragons, planarian fission and fungal–algal co-culture. No full book or PDF read. Next audit remaining gaps in shared clock semantics, multi-party authority, ecology scaling and the synthesis handoff, then reconcile the ranked proof list against all covered families.

## FG1. Persistent individuals across birth, promotion and transfer

November 11, Round 6 of [the cross-domain foundations map](../CROSS_WORLD_SYSTEMS_TRANSFER.md#round-6-foundations-goals-by-existing-chat). This scoped pass sharpens the earlier foundation and uses this document as its sole write owner. Two domestic-cat paper abstracts and the existing creature and regulator owners bound the source pass. No bulk reading, paid credits or runtime changes.

**Read the creature in layers.** Inherited potential belongs to the individual and records how it was settled. Development interprets that potential against the current stage and the conditions that shaped it. Practice and learned associations belong to the individual, with the relevant cue, handler and context. Present condition is projected from actually elapsed care and exposure. The consumer asks what the creature can do now given its body, perception and situation. Then the action host separately asks who can do or request it. A state change may affect more than one layer. For example, a stage boundary can change hearing and reopen a learned response without changing the evidence of training. A person with permission may still need the animal to perceive the cue, have the physical means and be willing to act. Preserve each cause so a later observer or handler can distinguish it.
