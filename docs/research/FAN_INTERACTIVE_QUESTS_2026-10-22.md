# Fan interactive quests catalog (2026-10-22)

Scope: forum "quests" (vote-driven collaborative stories) in the target worlds, as design references for stats, items, sheets, dice, time loops and build systems. Catalog only. No threads were downloaded or scraped, and no story text is reproduced. Research used WebSearch plus one WebFetch per thread page. Thread counts come from each page's own statistics panel at fetch time (2026-10-22); the pages disagree with themselves in places (header threadmark count vs statistics panel), so treat sizes as approximate.

Legend: V = verified (page opened and the mechanic read there). U = unverified (search snippet only). CRUNCHY = explicit stat blocks, sheets or published rules docs.

## Notes

- **Crunchiest, verified:** #1, #2, #3, #4, #5, #7, #13, #15, #19, #20. Projects wanting formulas should start with #13 (XP-per-rank ladder), #15 (priced point-buy and grade capacity) and #19 (point currency plus lottery).
- **Calendar and loop:** #3 is the only time-loop quest found for a target world. #8 shows a per-turn action budget on a month calendar. #6 shows a deadline-driven school year.
- **Items and inventory:** #1 (expiring consumables, artifacts with quirks), #7 (weight-capped inventory), #15 (cursed tools and seals as priced items), #24 (Memories and Echoes).
- **Omake and plan voting:** #13 awards XP for omakes, art and discussion. #9 and #10 run structured plan-voting schedules. #6 and #8 use tallied vote threadmarks.
- **Caveat on freshness:** thread-page dates are as shown at fetch time. Several "Ongoing" labels are stale (see #2, #5, #7, #10, #17, #23).
- **Rules docs are off-page:** #9 and #10 keep their rules in linked Google docs, which were not opened. Numbers there are not verified.
