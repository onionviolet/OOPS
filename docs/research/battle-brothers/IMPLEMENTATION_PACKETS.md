# Ranked Battle Brothers proposals

All eight are original proposals for the north star. They are not decisions or implementation authorization. Ranked by useful fit to present YAIR; no runtime changes were made.

Shared future gate: rebuild selected changes and exercise them from a fresh test origin, including save/reload/import for durable state. Preserve vanilla opening and native unconsciousness. Numbers, fiction and balance below are proposals.

## P1. Continue one named helper agreement

**Source:** BB-FL05/06/07/08; W14/16. **First result:** Extend the existing src/helper_content.js pathfinder commission with an opt-in follow-on agreement: named wage/provision terms, one preference, warning before missed work and a visible departure/custody choice.

**Owners:** Existing actor/resources/relationships/life status, src/everyday_life.js conditions, common clock and native money/items. Do not add a parallel party inventory or generic mood currency.

**Cost and failure:** Pay from actual job earnings; missed terms interrupt only the helper service. Keep native solo work available.

**Proposed proof:** Hire, complete first commission, accept continuing terms, meet/miss a due payment, remedy or let helper leave; verify costs once, tools custody and save/reload/import. Migration must leave old commissions valid.

## P2. Explain local standing with a short cause ledger

**Source:** W2/3/4/13. **First result:** One Village contact explains a recent favor or grievance; an explicit town-owner link carries only an authored consequence. Capability and trust can differ in dialogue.

**Owners:** src/reputation.js and reputation_state.js own native totals; existing simulation relationship receipts own personal causes; life_record remains biography only.

**Cost and failure:** No universal moral currency or every NPC instantly learning an event. One positive and one harmful native quest result have distinct consumers.

**Proposed proof:** Verify both results, recipient knowledge, one service response and bounded reason retention after reload/import; do not replay rewards on restoration.

## P3. Earn a practical specialist through ordinary work

**Source:** W17; E5. **First result:** A repair or care specialist offers one service after actual Smithing/care work. Display missing proof and concrete service benefit in the center menu.

**Owners:** Native skills/crafting/equipment service and actor agreements; reuse existing unlock/condition framework.

**Cost and failure:** Fee and supplies are real; replacement rebuilds derived effects, with no accumulating bonuses. No five-slot roster imported wholesale.

**Proposed proof:** Demonstrate unmet/met requirements, real service effect, insufficient supplies and replacement; save/reload/import keeps one active effect.

## P4. Let prior work shape a recurring social scene

**Source:** BB-FL01/02/16/17; E4. **First result:** An existing named helper or elder mentions prior fieldwork or patrol life, then offers one lesson or comments on an actually supplied meal.

**Owners:** src/activities.js ordinary skill practice; existing dialogue/conditions/relationship owners; native food identity.

**Cost and failure:** Occupation is not an extra global power tier. Current inventory is current evidence; a repeated-diet claim needs actual saved history.

**Proposed proof:** Exercise eligible/ineligible scenes, one visible skill or relationship consequence, cooldown and duplicate prevention after reload/import.

## P5. Keep useful work available during recovery

**Source:** BB-FL10/11/13. **First result:** An optional authored companion injury changes one job restriction and exposes a recovery estimate, while study or workshop assistance stays possible.

**Owners:** Existing status/care and job requirements must be inspected before implementation; NPC life truth is separate from lossy biography.

**Cost and failure:** Ordinary native defeat stays unconsciousness/recovery. Lasting wounds and true death require separately selected campaign rules.

**Proposed proof:** Observe blocked physical task, valid alternative, treatment cost, elapsed recovery and save/reload/import. Unselected vanilla saves receive no injury.

## P6. Make one local crisis change the place

**Source:** W9/10/11/12; E1/2. **First result:** One authored road/supply crisis has traces, pressure and aftermath. It changes a specific route or institution and ends coherently with active commitments.

**Owners:** Existing scheduled simulation events/controllers and event_feed knowledge; common clock, authored enemy/route owners.

**Cost and failure:** Warning is information, not automatic level scaling for every threat. No world war before the local slice works; do not translate BB50-60/20-day timings into YAIR balance.

**Proposed proof:** Play warning, intervention and nonintervention branches, route consequence, active-contract boundary and aftermath; long advance/reload/import cannot skip or duplicate phase effects.

## P7. Record an enemy power as an observed method

**Source:** E6. **First result:** One native enemy encounter reveals a source and restriction through witnessed behavior; Notebook distinguishes observation from a guessed counter.

**Owners:** src/adventure_notebook.js knowledge, actual encounter/skill owners; no universal monster ability-copy system.

**Cost and failure:** Original enemy example must have a real eligibility check and a paid action; defeating it does not automatically grant its power.

**Proposed proof:** Observe tell, try an ineffective response, research/prepare a working counter and verify only learned facts persist. This remains a proposal, not existing combat integration.

## P8. Give service and loss an individual ending

**Source:** BB-FL14/15/18; E7. **First result:** A voluntarily finished helper agreement or known loss writes one named milestone and an authored survivor response. Older people may teach rather than disappear.

**Owners:** src/npc_lives.js birth/death truth, existing departed status and src/life_record.js loss/legacy entries.

**Cost and failure:** No general combat-level aging. No deletion of YAIR saves or mandatory ironman. Source epilogues do not demonstrate simulated succession.

**Proposed proof:** Verify departure versus death, possessions and surviving speaker eligibility; one milestone across reload/import. Any future player retirement/true death needs separate accepted scope.
