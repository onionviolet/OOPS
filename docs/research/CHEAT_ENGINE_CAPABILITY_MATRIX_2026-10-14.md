## ER15-F1 Scope and meaning

Read the [dispatch rules](../gm/dispatch/engine-runtime-2026-10-14/COMMON.md), AGENTS.md, PROJECT.md, user preferences, the eight requested cheat owners, [root palette O–Q](../ENGINE_RUNTIME_PERFORMANCE_AND_STACK_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md#o-cheats-that-show-the-engine-ecx-round-5), [save/scale E1–E10](../ENGINE_SAVES_AND_SCALE_REVIEW_2026-10-11.md) and [offline/clock audit](OFFLINE_TIME_AND_BRANCH_CLOCK_AUDIT_2026-10-14.md). This extends their mappings rather than selecting their proposals as requirements. All dependencies and effort ranks below are **INFERRED**. Source support is **READ** at the linked line, not tested or browser-accepted here. Source-game names retain the uncertainty of their owners; no new canon claim is made.

The matrix covers the library's 49 base families, all 18 CT types, all 18 SH lenses, and distinct named temporal/simulator/self mechanisms. Named examples share a family row unless their mechanism changes its dependencies. [Cheats by world](../CHEATS_BY_WORLD.md) is a source/example inventory, not proof those powers are implemented here. Family rows do not claim an exact source adaptation. A card's standing remains relative to its destination world. A normal status window can be world law there and a cheat here.

**N = needed** for the row's stated scope; **H = helps** with scale, reuse or clarity; **- = none** for that scope. Not every variant needs every dependency of its family. Workers are H for bounded futures, not a prerequisite to every tiny dream; UI rendering needs no worker. Full replay needs RT5 and RT9; an isolated checkpoint need not replay commands. Static rule edits need not reload executable modules.

Columns use the root palette codes, not same-named codes in the cheat library: C=SB5 single clock owner; R=RT9 named saved streams; Q=RT5 serializable command log; S=LB3/DA2 snapshots; V=RT4 read models; K=AI6/E9 knowledge filter; L=RT7 LOD; W=TS4 workers; D=DA5 content as data; M=OE3 module/reload contract. **S=N means restoration/fork state is needed; structural sharing/chunking helps its cost and is not intrinsically required for a small snapshot.** None of these ten columns captures all ownership, transaction, body or hook needs.

Support labels describe the promised cheat, not whether its foundation exists: **exists** means a narrow named behavior has a source implementation; **partial** means reusable code exists but the full row is not established; **missing** means the stated engine operation was not found in the inspected routes. Absence claims are scoped inferences, paired with the closest READ seam. New card hooks are not executable just because their schema accepts an array (`src/cheat_cards.js:1,32,66`).
