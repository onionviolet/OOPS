# Three high-payoff deeper systems

## D1: interruption is a domain policy

Shown E1-E5: barrel early unseal clears timestamps; drying resets on sampled rain or wet additions; kiln invalidity consumes fuel while preserving current contents; pot cold state resets boiling work. Shared duration alone does not predict recovery. PROPOSAL P1 extends the existing receipt with one stage's real policy and leaves other stage types selectable later.

## D2: part identity and workmanship have separate carriers

Shown E6-E7: casting chooses metal-specific output from mold/fluid recipe; assembly chooses a metal-specific finished item. A selected head passes its forging ordinal through a modifier. Actual speed, damage and durability hooks read it. This is stronger than a decorative quality tag, but weaker than universal provenance or arbitrary component transfer. PROPOSAL P2 requires one useful consumer and explicit old-save defaults.

## D3: clocks and causal catch-up

Shown E1/E2/E9: playerTime is the processing clock, calendarTime provides month/day climate; sleep and set-time paths advance both. Barrel catch-up temporarily rewinds completion context; pot catch-up accounts for finite fuel. Drying has only saved last-reset time and sampled weather checks, so no historical rain ledger was shown. PROPOSAL P7 should freeze job policy and avoid inventing weather history or full output from elapsed wall time alone.
