## Findings

- **F1:** The strongest adaptations are a truthful breakthrough planning surface, a person-centered cultivation book, mechanisms that distinguish inherited aptitude from current embodiment, a physically bonded growing implement, and a powered spiritual home with accountable workers. Catalogue size is useful reference material, not a parity gate.
- **F2:** Current YAIR already implements more than the October 24 audit's early snapshot: inherited stat effects, root-dependent practice, vessel laws/geometry, graded ordeals, enforced vow routes, charged runes, a household school, medicine and a creature brood. The matrix below credits current producer/consumer paths rather than copying old absence claims.
- **F3:** Advertised features and shipped admission differ. The body/guide/changelog describe three Inner World trials; the checked 0.2.106 state selector and request route admit Question the Heart, while disabling Shatter Core and Stabilize Void through that route. Likewise, pill/failure bonuses depend on difficulty rather than a universal 5%. These contradictions remain visible.

The downloaded enum declarations contain 137 techniques and 154 spells, and cultivation progress is separate from current Qi. A bundled test asserting 144 spells is stale relative to its enum, not runtime proof.
