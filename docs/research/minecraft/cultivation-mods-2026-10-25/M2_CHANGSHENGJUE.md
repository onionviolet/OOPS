# M2: ChangShengJue (长生诀) absorption audit

Status: static absorption audit complete, 2026-10-25. Documentation only. No gameplay changes, commit, installation or launch. Source-spot-checked and rules-reconciled; ordinary Minecraft play and YAIR browser acceptance were not performed.

Pinned archive: `1.20.1-0.7.13-beta`, 11,121,376 bytes; Modrinth SHA-512 verified. Private evidence: `../private/cultivation-2026-10-25/changshengjue/`.

Rules: AD5/6–8/32/33/34/36/38/41; WPR1–5/8–23/27/29/31; AU1–12/14–15; MR1–23; SR1/15/19/22; UR35/40/59–64. Shared COMMON scope overrides generic owner/index edits. Coordinator will link/fold this report.

## Findings

**F1. The useful contribution is a lived martial society.** Manuals become usable through appropriate activity; skill mastery changes combat, movement, trades and work. Gangs, village assistance, local reputation, inns, food, clothing and regional buildings connect that growth to ordinary life. This is an absorption palette, not an instruction to copy its catalogue.

**F2. Wuxia and xianxia need separate claims.** The project page advertises both themes but identifies wuxia/adventure/agriculture/decoration as the current feature set. The pinned release establishes martial techniques, not a demonstrated immortal realm ladder or general inner-energy reservoir. “Internal kungfu” is a technique category; “breakthrough” in translated tooltips often means technique mastery. Names invoking immortals or meridians do not establish a cultivation topology.

**F3. Local reputation is real; global jianghu fame remains unestablished.** Three effects write positive/negative vanilla villager gossip about the player UUID. A helper initially missed the reputation labels; source spot-check corrected its private notes before integration. Do not turn that narrower finding into a whole-world fame/rank simulator.

**F4. Release/source drift matters.** GitHub HEAD declares 0.7.8-beta, while Modrinth supplies 0.7.13-beta. Newer mental techniques and release repairs must be read in the jar. Eleven custom screen registrations were found; a texture named `cultivation.png` is not proof of a twelfth screen.

**F5. Existing native mechanisms cover much of the foundation.** This audit grades 24 mechanism rows: **A 2 / P 18 / M 1 / U 3**. These are bounded static comparisons, not percentages, exact parity, runtime proof or an accepted backlog. The clearest new family is physical minting with material returns and seigniorage. The largest useful extensions concern activity-specific mastery, faction consequences and food-technique interactions.

### Martial arts, internal methods and meaningful practice

SHOWN, S1–S5: learning a manual is distinct from comprehension, and comprehension from mastery. External styles use attacks; internal styles refer to practice stakes; movement and interactions train other categories. `AbstractionExternalKunfu.comprehendKungFu` checks a probability while uncomprehended at level zero; `isReady` checks cooldown, started state, comprehension and positive level. `addExp` advances to the configured maximum and resets experience at a level transition. Config exposes individual caps, experience thresholds and cooldowns. This is not a single universal character realm.

Tooltips distinguish damage multipliers and weapon-specific bleed, launch/knockback, critical or stun bonuses. SHOWN, S4: a Dugu cast uses hunger and a configured cooldown; tea and wine effects alter the same execution path. Biluochun can restore health on cast, Longjing can return food, Fenjiu increases damage while charging extra food, tribute wine subtracts cooldown, and Shi Li Xiang reduces cast food cost. These are scoped resource interactions. A separate inner-energy reserve was not established by the inspected capability/base classes and locale pass. Native energy capacity/output/control/strain should therefore be treated as our existing mechanism, not attributed to this mod.

SHOWN, S4: trauma's application helper inflicts 7% of maximum health on a successful proc, then applies a debuff; the effect class supplies attribute penalties. Internal injury separately increases damage received by 5% per effect level and periodically checks dizziness. Do not collapse both into a generic damage-over-time label. Armor's trauma mitigation is checked before the trauma roll. Exact combat feel, stacking edge cases and all recovery methods remain unplayed.

### Gangs, NPCs, quests, reputation and information

SHOWN text/data/code, S1/S6: gang leaders offer trades and quests; the corpus includes gang recruitment/fundraising/supplies, village defense and relief, curing a villager, hostile targets and automatically tracked conduct. Bangpai (帮派) is better rendered “gang/faction” here than silently equated with an immortal sect. The inspected implementation does not establish a disciple-management institution, sect research economy or paired cultivation/sect rank ladder.

SHOWN, S6: `QuestManager.tryAcceptQuest` obtains an offered quest from the actual gang leader and associates it with the player. Submission rechecks `canComplete`, takes requirements, issues rewards/effects, records completion and removes a conflicting quest before synchronizing. That is an actual mutually incompatible commitment/settlement mechanism. The 28 JSON definitions support repeatability, prerequisites, conflicts, generated targets and time/target parameters. Each quest's full trigger chain was not traversed.

SHOWN, S6: `InstantCharmEffect` targets villagers within `32 × (amplifier + 1)` range and writes major/minor positive gossip about the player; `InstantDisfavorEffect` writes negative gossip. `VillagerCharmEffect` updates nearby villagers periodically. A treatment quest explicitly grants `instant_charm_effect`. These establish local reputation effects through Minecraft's villager gossip API. Broad rumor propagation, global celebrity tiers and all downstream vanilla price behavior are not independently exercised here.

SHOWN, S8: structure-intelligence items distinguish six location types and cache a found position in item NBT, with search progress/messages and bounded server work. This is a material information service that persists a discovery, not omniscient map access. Native proposals should expose supported known routes and preserve hidden truths.

### Crafting, food, medicine and architecture

SHOWN, S7/S8: the recipe inventory contains **405 woodworking, 263 brick kiln, 59 tailoring, 27 forge-block**, plus 52 shaped, 53 shapeless, 13 smelting, nine blasting, four smoking, four campfire and one conditional recipe. These counts describe definitions, not independent production systems. Sampled kiln recipes combine clay/kaolin inputs with color; woodworking uses material-specific wood and furniture variants. Full ingredient/output tables remain private.

SHOWN, S8: ordinary foods cover crops, cooked meats, dishes, soups and drinks. Tea food definitions have guaranteed 1,200-tick effects. A Zabing manual is a learned support method in code, not evidence for a pill furnace. No alchemy recipe family, pill station or realm-granting medicine was established by the recipe/locale/class inventory. That bounded absence does not imply that cooking, cures or health effects are absent.

SHOWN, S7/S9: roof tiles and ridges, masonry, wooden structural members, fittings, furniture, beds, doors/screens and clothing form a large material/visual catalogue. Guide text and worldgen distinguish courtyard, cave-dwelling, Hui/Su and fortress contexts. Woodworking and kiln menus organize that breadth into work categories and color/material variants. Furniture and worldgen existence do not prove that every building has a simulated household, heat field or social function.

SHOWN, S8: coins and bullion are carried items. Seven loose copper coins craft into one string-of-coins item in the jar recipe; do not substitute a historical exchange rate. `CastingMoldsBlockEntity.craftItem` calculates a 24-coin batch, quantizes defects in groups corresponding to three coins per returned ingot, and consumes the input. Its release/output path subtracts five coins as tax, with particles/sound. The economic rationale for the tax collector is not established by that effect. Bullion minting details remain less completely traced. Treat exact source mint arithmetic as evidence, not an endorsed native balance model.

### UI absorption proposals against native rules

**UI-P1.** Adapt catalogue → selected preview → explicit action, including truthful required/held materials and output quantity. Target YAIR's existing crafting/gear surfaces at **1920×1080**, also the user's laptop and desktop floor under UR rules. Selection stays free under UR35; commitment rechecks stock and the saved quote. Preserve current context and combat visibility.

**UI-P2.** Adapt contextual art and material/category filters as data-driven presentation within the shared shell, UR37/59–64. Keep labels alongside color/icons. A source-like 512-pixel texture atlas is not a reason to shrink YAIR type or install Minecraft-shaped windows.

**UI-P3.** Adapt before/after mastery descriptions and a compact cooldown/availability summary only for discovered methods. UR40/AD6–8 constrain what the actor can know. A source manual may explain its known skill; a universal future catalogue could spoil discovery.

Visible outcome for any later slice: inspect a known method or craft, understand its current effect/inputs/blocker, commit once, and see the resulting owned state in the existing desktop UI. Mobile optimization, a new shell, copied assets and a broad screen rebuild remain deferred. These are proposals; no UI implementation or browser proof is claimed.
