# Royal Road trope atlas (2026-10-10)

Research, not decisions. Every status, gap and ranking below is a proposal
for review. Titles and premises are paraphrased from public Royal Road
listings; no blurb text is reproduced.

## Summary

- **Sample:** 1,543 fictions harvested from Royal Road ranking pages on
  2026-10-10 (best-rated, trending, rising, weekly, active, completed, plus
  the top 20 by followers and top 20 by rating for each of 94 tag
  filters). 1,515 carry usable tags; 28 rows were scraper noise with no
  premise data and are excluded from examples and tag counts.
- **Atlas:** four readers proposed 351 shard tropes; merged and
  deduplicated, that gives **161 tropes** in ten families (156 premises plus
  5 publishing or format conventions).
- **Against project coverage:** 99 covered, 47 partial, **10 new**, 5 not
  applicable (publishing conventions).
- **Main signal:** the big premises (System apocalypse, isekai,
  regression, dungeon cores, monster evolution, cultivation, academies,
  kingdom building) are all already in the project. The gaps are mostly in
  the middle of a life: careers with ranks (soldier, athlete, thief),
  households (noble estates, poverty starts, disability), and specific
  social worlds (fae courts, cosmic bureaucracy, transported towns).

## Method and caveats

1. **Readers.** The harvest was split into four shards of about 386 rows.
   Each reader listed tropes with the fiction ids that fit, plus variants.
   Readers 1 and 2 matched ids with broad keyword rules, so their counts
   for broad tropes run high (a blurb that says "portal" or "war" counted).
   Readers 3 and 4 tagged by hand and were stricter, so their shards are
   undercounted by comparison.
2. **Merge.** Shard tropes with the same premise were merged into one row;
   the count is the **union of fiction ids** across readers. A `*` marks a
   count of 25 or more where at least 80% of ids came only from readers 1
   and 2 and the matching RR tag does not back it up. Treat starred counts
   as inflated.
3. **Tag cross-check.** Where a Royal Road tag matches the trope, its
   harvest count is shown as `(tag N)`. A tag count higher than the trope
   count means the readers missed works; lower means the readers counted
   blurbs that do not carry the tag.
4. **Examples** are the three highest-follower works in each trope's id
   set. Follower order favors older, finished, popular serials.
5. **YAIRPG status** was checked by grep and section headings, not full
   reads, against `docs/RESEARCH_GENRE_TROPES.md`,
   `docs/RESEARCH_GENRE_SWEEP.md`,
   `docs/research/far-field-tropes-2026-10-08/PART_2_TROPES.md`,
   `docs/IDEA_INDEX.md`, `docs/CHEAT_LIBRARY.md`, `docs/CHEATS_BY_WORLD.md`,
   `docs/LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md` and `docs/RESEARCH_LITRPG_SYSTEMS.md`.
   **covered** means a section designs or catalogs the trope; **partial**
   names what is missing; **new** means no section found. A grep miss can
   hide coverage written in other words, so "new" is a lead to verify.
   Other project docs (for example the cultivation and alternative-power
   dossiers) were not checked.
6. **Sample bias.** This is a ranking-page sample, not the whole site.
   Per-tag pulls cap every tag at about 40 entries from its own lists, which
   flattens the frequency of big tags and inflates small ones. Popular
   works dominate. **The signed-in account used for the harvest has a global
   filter that hides Female Lead works from tag searches** (confirmed
   afterwards: the Fantasy top 20 drops three Female Lead hits with the
   filter on; best-rated and other ranking pages are unaffected). So every
   per-tag list under-samples women-led works, and the Female Lead count
   here is an artifact. A filter-off supplement of the top 20 Female Lead
   works is in [REPORT](REPORT.md#female-lead-supplement). Content-warning filters (profanity, sensitive
   content, AI-assisted) were pulled but are not displayed tags.

global filter hid that tag from tag searches. See the
[supplement](REPORT.md#female-lead-supplement).

For a life sim, the useful reading is: sports careers, interactive or
community choice, women-led and queer romance, non-humanoid bodies and
otome-style social plots are under-supplied on popular RR pages. The
project already covers several of these at idea level (see the atlas
statuses), so the gap is in depth, not absence.
