# Royal Road audit, 2026-10-10

Weibao: "RR audit and all tropes and interesting ideas and more?" RR is
Royal Road, as in his 2026-10-02 ask. Research and proposals only. Nothing
here is an accepted decision or implemented gameplay.

## What came out

**F1 The big premises are already in the project.** 161 distinct tropes
across ten families. 99 are already covered, 47 are partial and 10 are new.
System apocalypse, isekai, regression, dungeon cores, monster evolution,
cultivation, academies and kingdom building all have homes already. Full
table: [TROPE_ATLAS.md](TROPE_ATLAS.md).

**F2 The gaps are in the middle of a life.** The thinnest partial tropes are
careers with ranks (soldier, athlete, thief or assassin), households (a
noble estate, a poverty start, a disability or chronic illness start) and
the sci-fi worlds (space opera, cyberpunk, hard sci-fi). Of the new tropes,
these add the most to a life sim:

1. A whole group or town transported together.
2. The morally grey lead, read differently by each audience.
3. Fae courts, where etiquette and bargains are the mechanics.
4. Archaeology as a career.
5. Power carried as visible brands or tattoos.

**F3 There are 275 ideas, 104 of them new to the project's docs.** Full list
with links: [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md). The top five:

- I135 Every Level Erases Me: each level deletes a memory, a relationship
  or a skill you choose, and you can refuse to level.
- I173 My Children From Another World: you raise strange children while
  you age.
- I077 Just a Bystander: you're the Chosen One's classmate and catch the
  fate pressure meant for him.
- I057 The QuestWright: you write quests for other people and get paid by
  how many they finish.
- I136 The Celestial Auditor: qi is a loan with karmic interest.

**F4 All four readers found the same holes in Royal Road.** These are aging,
parenting, long marriage, decades-long institutional careers, real costs on
cheats, and money, housing or illness as the main progression. Others are
domestic nonhuman lives, loops over an ordinary week, a relationship as the
cheat, and cozy lives that stay cozy. They are opportunities a life sim can
fill (G1 to G10 in IDEAS).

**F5 Royal Road's own habits can be presentation ideas** (RR1 to RR13 in
IDEAS):

- A life log split into serial chapters and volumes.
- Stubbing as a way to compact old logs.
- Offline progress returned as "3 new chapters", with a hiatus banner after
  a long absence.
- An in-world author's notes at the end of chapters.
- In-world reader comments, which can carry rumors but never sealed truths.
- A finished life summarized as a fiction listing card.

**F6 Women-led works are under-sampled.** The signed-in account used for
the harvest hides the Female Lead tag in search, so every per-tag list
skipped women-led works. Ranking pages were unaffected. The supplement
below covers that gap.

## How it was gathered

- **Royal Road blocks scripts.** Direct requests get HTTP 403, and an
  earlier lane was refused on 2026-10-10. This pass used the built-in app
  browser, already signed in to Weibao's account. Requests ran one at a
  time from the page, about 2.5 to 4.5 seconds apart, with no challenge
  pages, logins or workarounds.
- **The sample is 1,543 fictions.** It comes from best-rated pages 1 to 5,
  plus the trending, rising stars, weekly popular, active popular and
  complete lists. It also includes the top 20 by followers and the top 20
  by rating for each of 94 tag filters, and the account's own reading
  lists.
- **This is a ranking-page sample, not the whole site.** Per-tag caps
  flatten tag frequencies.
- **Four readers each went through about 386 listings**, and two synthesis
  passes merged their work. Every fiction id was checked against its
  quarter, and a spot check matched premises to blurbs.
- **No chapters were read.** Hooks reflect what a listing promises, not what
  a book delivers.
- **The raw data stays private.** The harvest, catalog, quarter outputs and
  the reading-list taste profile with a deep-dive shortlist are in the
  gitignored folder `docs/research/novels/royal-road-2026-10-10/`.
