# Royal Road ideas, 2026-10-10

Research synthesis. Proposals only; nothing here is an accepted decision or implemented gameplay. Idea docs in this project are palettes of considerations for fitting characters at fitting times, so treat each line as an option a world or origin may use, not a spec.

**Input.** A harvest of 1,543 Royal Road fictions (best-rated, trending and top-40 tag lists, pulled 2026-10-10) was split across four readers. They flagged 296 interesting entries with a hook, a reason and a YAIRPG use, plus notes on trends and gaps. Raw harvest files stay in the private research folder. Hooks below are paraphrased from listing blurbs; no book text was read.

**What this file does.**
1. Dedupes the 296 picks into **275 ideas** across 22 themes, coded I001 to I275. Merges happened where two listings were the same work or the YAIRPG use was the same (for example several "game master as dungeon" stories became one idea).
2. Checks each idea against existing coverage: the life-sim north star (headings plus targeted greps), [CHEAT_LIBRARY](../../CHEAT_LIBRARY.md), [IDEA_INDEX](../../IDEA_INDEX.md), [far-field part 3](../far-field-tropes-2026-10-08/PART_3_NEW_IDEAS.md), [living beside protagonists](../LIVING_BESIDE_PROTAGONISTS_2026-10-10.md) and the 2026-10-09 web-novel scout (private notes, cited by entry ID only).
3. Ranks a top 40, then turns the readers' notes into gaps and presentation ideas.

**Novelty labels.** "New" means this specific decision or mechanic was not found in those files. "Extends" means the family exists and this adds a distinct operation. "Already present" means the existing docs already name it. The check is keyword and heading based; it is evidence of absence in these files, not proof of originality.

## Top 40 for YAIRPG

Ranked by two tests: how lived a single character's life would feel, and how mechanically distinct the idea is from what YAIRPG already has. Every line below is a proposal, not a decision. "Adds" names the specific life-sim decision or consequence; "Lane" says where it fits (world, origin, cheat or career).

**1. I135 Every Level Erases Me** ([191481](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/191481))

- Hook: each level makes him stronger and permanently deletes something.
- Adds: at every level the player picks what goes (a memory, a relationship, a skill), and refusing to level is a real path. Lost people still remember you, so a deleted friendship becomes a stranger who grieves you.
- Lane: cheat card in the "cursed gift" family; strongest in a long human life with many named ties. Novelty: extends [NS: Cheats that hurt](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#cheats-that-hurt-proposal-2026-10-08).

**2. I173 My Children From Another World** ([51532](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/51532))

- Hook: a retired adventurer raises children whose souls and gifts are strange, in a world that would eat them.
- Adds: each child has a trait that needs schooling, hiding or a special teacher, and the parent ages while choosing between their own climb and the kids' protection. Children leave with what you taught them.
- Lane: origin for a late-life parent; ties into heirs. Novelty: extends [NS: Heirs and families](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#heirs-families-and-life-beyond-the-first-character-proposal-2026-10-10).

**3. I077 Just a Bystander** ([32123](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/32123))

- Hook: a student arcanist enrolls next to the prophesied Chosen One and slowly realizes he is the side character.
- Adds: fate pressure splashes onto you (kidnappings meant for the hero, rivals who test you first), and you choose to stand beside, compete, or step out of the story's blast radius.
- Lane: origin in any world with a protagonist; uses the canon clock idea. Novelty: extends [NS: Protagonists as other characters](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#protagonists-and-cheaters-as-other-characters-proposal-2026-10-07); extends [Living beside protagonists](../LIVING_BESIDE_PROTAGONISTS_2026-10-10.md).

**4. I057 The QuestWright** ([121243](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/121243))

- Adds: you write quests, NPCs decide whether to accept, and your reward scales with completion. Matching a task to the right person is the craft; a badly matched quest gets someone hurt and lowers your standing.
- Lane: system-job origin, or a late career for a System-as-character start. Novelty: extends [NS: Starting realms](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#who-or-what-you-start-as-realms-of-play-proposal-2026-10-10).

- Lane: world law for one cultivation world, not a universal rule. Novelty: extends [Cheat library: more types](../../CHEAT_LIBRARY.md#more-cheat-types-2026-10-10); extends [NS: Cheats that hurt](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#cheats-that-hurt-proposal-2026-10-08).

## And more

### Gaps Royal Road under-serves (opportunities)

All four readers independently reported the same holes. The listings mostly use these subjects as backstory, if at all. Each is a place where a life-sim can offer something the genre's readers rarely get.

| Code | Gap | What the harvest shows | Opportunity for YAIRPG |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Aging and old age | A handful of elder leads (I170, I182 grandpa sect leader); most leads are young transmigrators or retired heroes | Elder starts and a real late-life stage, where the body slows and memory, students and reputation carry the weight. Extends [NS: Life progression defaults](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#life-progression-defaults-proposal-2026-10-07) |
| G2 | Parenting | Children appear as quick milestones (I168) or rare premises (I173) | Children with their own natures who need years of choices: schooling, protection, letting go. Extends [NS: Heirs and families](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#heirs-families-and-life-beyond-the-first-character-proposal-2026-10-10) |
| G3 | Long marriage, divorce, widowhood | Romance is mostly tags, harem or a subplot; long partnerships are rare; one widower start (I169) | A marriage that changes over decades: shifting roles, quarrels with history, divorce or loss as a life event that keeps echoing. Extends [NS: Romance](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#romance-and-dating-mechanics-proposal-2026-10-08) |
| G4 | Institutional careers over decades | Bureaucracy and work show up as comedy (I066, I213) or a short arc | A clerk, guild member, teacher or town official whose rank, colleagues and pension accumulate over a whole life; municipal politics. Extends [NS: Work](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#work-proposal-2026-10-08) |
| G5 | Costs on cheats | Readers counted few explicit costs; the standouts were debt (I136), memory (I135, I139), dying first (I137), one question a day (I138) | Make the cost a recurring life decision, not a fixed tax. Extends [NS: Cheats that hurt](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#cheats-that-hurt-proposal-2026-10-08) |
| G6 | Money, housing and illness as the main progression | Economy works are mostly merchant power fantasies; rent and illness appear only in a few (I039, I206, I205, I185) | Solvency, a lease or a chronic condition as the spine of a life. Disability is almost always a cure arc on Royal Road; a life where the condition stays and the person adapts is the open space |
| G7 | Domestic nonhuman lives | Nonhuman leads are cores, slimes, skeletons, hives and swords, almost always combat-led | Bodies with chores, pets, feeding and seasons as daily life (a house cat, a shop crab, a hidden dragon household). Extends [NS: Every family in every body](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#every-feature-family-in-every-kind-of-body-proposal-2026-10-10) |
| G8 | Daily-life loops | Time loops are almost all academy or dungeon fare; one idle-style loop (I149) | A loop over one ordinary week of work and family, where the puzzle is a relationship rather than a murder |
| G9 | A relationship or reputation as the cheat | Very few works make the edge a bond or a name (I265, I148 kinship binding) | Cheats powered by who you know and how they see you |
| G10 | Non-combat lives that stay non-combat | Cozy-tagged works usually end in a dungeon or war | Let a cozy life stay cozy all the way to its ending; threat can come from rent, weather or a family feud |

Smaller signals: romance-forward and queer progression-light stories are a growing newer strand on the site; deckbuilding magic is a recent mechanical trend (about 14 titles) with mental costs on some cards; nearly all franchise fanfiction uses canon knowledge as the cheat, which matches the foreknowledge findings in [living beside protagonists](../LIVING_BESIDE_PROTAGONISTS_2026-10-10.md).

### Platform conventions as presentation and fourth-wall ideas

Royal Road's own habits (serial chapters, stubbing, schedules, author notes, ratings) are a ready-made fourth-wall vocabulary. The [presentation doc](../../PRESENTATION_AND_FOURTH_WALL.md) already has the Narrator, the Clerk, footnotes, tab-title effects and status-window VFX, but none of these platform conventions. Each item below says which existing entry it builds on. All follow that doc's safety lines: never touch a real save, never read personal data, and every effect has a plain version and an off switch.

| Code | Convention | Proposal | Builds on |
|---|---|---|---|
| RR1 | Serial chapters and volumes | Group the life log into numbered, titled chapters; a life stage closes a volume ("Book 2 complete"). Chapter titles come from the biggest event in that span | New; pairs with [the Narrator](../../PRESENTATION_AND_FOURTH_WALL.md#a-character-who-does-cool-stuff) |
| RR2 | Stubbing | When a life ends or a world is left, its early chapters collapse to a stub: the blurb and a few lines. The full record stays in the Notebook. This doubles as in-fiction log compaction | New |
| RR3 | Release schedule and hiatus | Offline progress returns as "3 new chapters" on a cadence the world sets (daily, Mon/Wed/Fri). A long absence shows a hiatus banner that the character's life also reflects | New; pairs with [Patient Furnace](../../CHEAT_LIBRARY.md#2-patient-furnace-offline-progress) |
| RR4 | Author's notes | An in-world author character signs off chapters with thanks, apologies and schedule notes, and is sometimes wrong about what comes next | Extends the Narrator and narrator's footnotes |
| RR5 | Advance chapters | A foresight cheat framed as reading chapters a few days early, with a chance the author revises them before "release". In-fiction only; no real payment, no real paywall | New; pairs with foreknowledge states in [living beside protagonists](../LIVING_BESIDE_PROTAGONISTS_2026-10-10.md) |

## Method and limits

- Inputs were four reader outputs over listing metadata (title, rating, followers, tags, blurb). No chapters were read, so hooks show what a listing promises, not what a book delivers.
- Newer entries (2026 IDs above about 185000) often carry 15 to 40 tags and thin blurbs, and some look AI-assisted; readers favored older or clearer listings, which biases the picks toward established premises.
- Some harvest rows held no premise data and were skipped.
- The harvest account's global filter hid Female Lead works from tag searches (ranking pages were unaffected), so women-led premises are under-sampled here. See the [Female Lead supplement](REPORT.md#female-lead-supplement).
- Novelty was checked with headings and targeted greps, not a full read of the 430 KB north star. A "new" label can be wrong where a different word was used for the same thing.
- Rankings are editorial judgment against the two stated tests, not popularity.
