Status: research proposals, not accepted or implemented. Preserve default vanilla opening, recovery defeat and first one-realm/one-prestige scope. Source mechanics below inform original scenarios; no Kenshi characters, prose or setting are proposed for copying. Experience GM owns playable selection; foundation GM owns shared persistence choices.

| Packet | Original smallest connected slice | State and consequence | Acceptance gate after current verification deferral |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1: Two mouths at the workshop | Opt into hiring one apprentice for a bread-making project, or work alone. Inputs come from existing gathering/trade; apprentice takes a declared wage and shares the production role. | One project, worker assignment, input accounting and relationship memory. Interrupted supply pauses work and gives a reason in the log. Keep bodily survival optional to this scenario. | Wages/materials spend once; finite capacity; stop on unavailable input; save/reload restores assignment; cancellation has an explicit policy. |
| P2: A fixed-term escort | Pay for protection on one dangerous supply trip; compare shorter protection versus deferring the journey. | Existing clock expiry, scoped ally contract and one contact-dependent end event. Expiry removes only the hired service, not permanent friendship. | Cannot double-charge, reward or renew on replay; expiry while elsewhere leaves a discoverable message; offline policy must be selected explicitly. |
| P3: A person changes the town | A workshop foreman is dismissed, imprisoned or restored through a bounded authored event; the same workshop changes its available contract and workers. | Distinct person status, institution status and local knowledge. Default services persist until the opt-in scenario changes them. | Alternate outcome revisits show the right contracts; uninformed people do not know hidden outcomes; one durable transition prevents duplicate reconstruction. |
| P4: Origin is circumstance | An optional later-life start has tools and a contact but an unresolved obligation. A poor start has a companion and a shared project instead. | Reuse origin composition and one biography entry; no universal power ranking or reset-policy change. | Origin can explain its practical differences before commitment; existing vanilla saves and default start stay compatible. |
| P5: Summarize absent workers | Foundation proposal: represent an established routine by assignment, last simulated time, capacity and interruption reason, with milestones on boundary changes. | Separate observed detailed work from declared coarse absence policy; retain only relevant person/place milestones.
