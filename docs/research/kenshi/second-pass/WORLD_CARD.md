# Draft world card: The Red Basin

2026-10-08. **Original proposal, not accepted or implemented.** Inspired by the source relationships in this pass, with original names, institutions and fiction. Uses the [world-card format](../../../WORLD_CARDS.md). A later campaign would need its own selected world brief; this card does not add a destination to the roster or change Frontier saves.

## Premise

A dry basin surrounds the remains of an industrial sea. The Meridian Houses own wells and toll roads, the Kiln League controls fabrication, and the Votive March treats mechanical bodies as evidence of a broken oath. Beyond their towns, hungry labor bands, wreck pickers and independent caravans compete for the next workable site. You arrive with no title. An ordinary wage, a trustworthy partner and a place to repair a pump can matter more than winning your first fight. Strong people and established armies already exist; they do not scale to your arrival.

## Signature laws

1. **Your body is infrastructure.** Injury changes available work and weapons. Parts can restore or exceed a natural function but need maintenance, fitting and a supply chain. Learned skill and installed capability remain separate.
2. **A settlement is an address others can act on.** A roof, a well and several workers change which territorial authority notices you. Money collectors, provision levies and ritual visits have different terms. Concealment affects discoverability; merely leaving does not erase obligations.
3. **Power vacuums have inhabitants.** Removing a custodian changes eligible workers, shops and road groups according to explicit local facts. Personal responsibility controls some reprisals. No faction is assumed to learn every hidden event immediately.

## Absences

No native spell energy or concept-granting world will. No native resurrection, natural limb regrowth or automatic post-defeat safe return. No level-up stat-point allocation. These absences describe this world's native routes; an imported ability can provide a real exception when its mechanism works here. Do not impose a universal magic ban simply because the locals have none.

## Dials

| Dial | Proposed local setting |
|---|---|
| Danger | High on exposed roads, very high near known war bands; settled streets have guards and their own coercion |
| Defeat/death | Wounds, unconsciousness, rescue or captivity when the actual cause permits; true death possible. No guaranteed rescue. Apply only after a separate harsh-life/world-entry policy is selected |
| Time ratio | 1 initially, no accelerated aging or browser-closed death by implication |
| Fusion pressure | None for concepts; devices interact through mechanical fit, capacity, loads and repair access |
| Carry | Ordinary skill, compatible equipment, memories and companions can cross if the door supports them; followers choose rather than become cargo |
| Stat source | Use-raised skill plus separately tracked body/part performance; no free points from an origin |
| Offline | Established safe work may be summarized only under a selected input/capacity/interruption policy. Pursuit, injury, hunger and final death while closed remain unresolved |

## Display

Keep the familiar skill/equipment numbers. Describe injury by function: a weak grip, an unusable two-handed weapon, a limp that slows a journey. Show part quality, fit, wear and known maintenance costs where they change a choice. Character and faction descriptions expose only discovered information. A patrol's menace comes from observed formation, kit and behavior, not a universal cultivation rank.

## Growth

Use, work, instruction, challenging practice, research and crafted replacements are active families. Proposed local craft titles are Learner, Hand, Specialist and Master; they describe proficiency, not total personal strength. A laborer can become a fabricator, medic, scout, caravan partner or settlement founder. Hard practice can train defense while adding genuine risk. Aptitude can improve through teachers, practice and parts; parts do not automatically improve learned knowledge. No local prestige ritual is selected.

## Stance

There is no conscious world judge. Institutions classify visitors through observed body, clothing, documents, relationships and conduct. A machine-friendly smith and a suspicious official can react differently to the same arm. Exile status, escaped captivity and public bounty are distinct facts. Friendly reputation is local evidence, not a pass that every faction recognizes.

## Scarcity

Clean water, reliable repair stock, safe transport, trained workers and permission to use a site. Knowledge of wells, faction patrols and old machines is valuable. Hoarded wealth without access can be less useful than one trusted local mechanic. Cheap repairs exist as a proposed economic floor for basic work; availability must be authored rather than guaranteed everywhere.

## Deck

A stranger who accepts shelter but declines membership; an apprentice seeking a new trade; a collector who returns with a different demand; a storm that hides a crossing; a pump that needs an unfamiliar bearing; a repaired worker proudly showing their first good grip; a patient and healer arguing over an old belief; a lost caravan's equipment in a captor's warehouse; a captive workplace reopening as a market; a successor patrol still short of food. Recruitment, dangerous work and captivity are authored events with distinct means, costs and outcomes.

## Doors

Proposal: entry through a derelict transit lock at the basin rim. The lock opens inward using a disposable seal; ordinary exit needs its missing regulator rebuilt, a caravan route to a different lock, or a compatible traveler ability. This is an original one-way-method problem, not recovered Kenshi travel. Do not advertise a guaranteed return before an exit is qualified. An alternate world may restore a damaged body through genuine medicine; that possibility must be discovered and authorized by its own rules, not inserted as automatic regrowth here.

| Incoming power | Proposed compatibility | Practical reason |
|---|---|---|
| Sword skill, language, engineering knowledge | Works where the practice and tools apply | Ordinary learning remains owned; local materials/history may require study |
| Powered prosthetic | Works if mechanically/fuel compatible; otherwise Rechannel | Fit, spare parts and the actual energy source matter |
| Locally supplied mana technique | Blocked until fuel exists | The basin supplies no mana by default; carried/self-produced fuel is a separate case |
| Ability with its own fuel and no incompatible law | Works | Preserve its actual output and reach, including overwhelming strength |
| A blessing requiring another world's institution | Case-specific Blocked or Weakened | That source's authority may not reach here; never blanket-nerf all travelers |

Body, equipment, belongings, companions and obligations require explicit transition handling. An obligation can remain with its local institution while a pursuer needs a legitimate way to track or cross. Time spent away does not silently resolve or destroy a settlement.

## Places

| Place | Override and reason to stay |
|---|---|
| Cistern Ward | Lower road danger, wages and rented rooms; civic inspection and toll debts matter |
| Solder Reach | Repair and fabrication services, machine-friendly customs; unreliable supplies constrain high-end parts |
| Pale Causeway | Open travel with little shelter; storms change observation and sound, armor and water planning matter |
| Quiet Quarry | A possible home site with a weak seam and disputed well; relocation competes with improving the site |

These are local dials and pursuits, not four independent worlds or permission for four simultaneous implementation slices.

## Swap test

Here, a stronger arm can expand your work while barring a temple service, and a better workshop can draw demands that a rented room never did. Winning a fight can remove an oppressor yet worsen the road until another institution takes over. The characteristic decision is how to assemble a viable life through bodies, tools, people and local authority. Replacing those relationships with a concept ladder or potion sequence would change the decisions, not just the names.

## First possible slice and open decisions

Draft one safe-town encounter: a wounded traveler needs a part fitted, you can work for the price or obtain a favor, and the traveler decides whether to accompany you. It proves one body/work/social connection before base founding or faction wars. Proposal O1 can first demonstrate the social part in Frontier without accepting this world's harsher rules.

Open: selected harsh-life loss/retention policy; injury/body compatibility and migration; local tracking and door-crossing authority; absent-player and offline consequences. This card resolves none of those shared policies by itself. Source support is [people](PEOPLE.md), [settlements](SETTLEMENTS.md) and [bodies](BODIES.md); all names, transit laws, scale, titles and numerical dials here are original choices.
