## F4. Correct the retention comparison

[Bruce Cordell's official translation article](https://www.montecookgames.com/your-game-bodies-in-translation-gear-and-foci-vs-stat-pools-in-the-strange/) describes bodily/mental adaptation between recursions, changing equipment/foci and generally retaining current stat pools. [In Translation](https://www.montecookgames.com/store/product/in-translation-the-strange-character-options/) likewise explicitly describes foci shifting between limited worlds.

The Strange is therefore a strong reference for identity continuity and world adaptation, not a default rule that every acquired power stays simultaneously usable everywhere. YAIRPG can retain its requested accumulation premise while borrowing selected world-authoring techniques. Also distinguish the publisher Monte Cook Games from a sole author: The Strange is associated with Bruce R. Cordell and Monte Cook; several relevant supplements credit Cordell as designer.

## A1. Recommended order and closeout

First trial G6, inspect G8's guild/world joins, and read the already downloaded L03/L07. Then prioritize B1/B2/B3; B4/B5 broaden the setting and authoring tools. These are research recommendations, not an implementation assignment or final source roster.

The high-value extraction questions are: what remains the same person; what survives departure; what is learned versus granted by a destination; what happens when the traveler returns; and which brought-back item/skill/ally actually changes home play. Each later full-source read should preserve exact private tables, conditions and exceptions under the project's existing private-research standard, while public notes paraphrase and cite.
