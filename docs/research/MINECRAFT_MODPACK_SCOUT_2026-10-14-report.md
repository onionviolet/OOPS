## Materials and particular equipment

**M1, Silent Gear:** the author documents blueprint crafting and datapack-defined materials/parts. A pinned aluminum definition separates crafting eligibility, categories, salvage, ingredient tags, part substitutes, appearance and per-part properties. It includes a trait condition tied to material ratio. A separate trait file defines gear-type conditions and numeric effects. These are specific authoring structures, not a measured alloy model. The older CurseForge description includes grading and mixing, while the SG4 guide identifies changed behavior. Do not import the old synergy formula as current. [Source](https://github.com/SilentChaos512/Silent-Gear), [SG4 guide](https://github.com/SilentChaos512/Silent-Gear/wiki/Starter-Guide-%28Silent-Gear-4%29), [author description](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/silent-gear).

**M2, different equipment models:** Tetra's versioned documentation describes items with module slots and JSON definitions under paths such as `tetra/modules/sword/basic_blade.json`. Tinkers' own design index separates material tiers, traits and modifiers. Compare part replacement, modifier application and material processing as distinct choices. Do not combine every donor's upgrades into one universal bonus stack. Exact honing, settling, repair and trait-combination behavior still needs edition-specific reading. [Tetra](https://tetra.mickelus.se/wiki/1.16/tech/modules), [Tinkers](https://slimeknights.github.io/docs/design/).

**M3, material proposals for our game:** distinguish composition, preparation, knowledge of properties and the final object's actual function. A tested metal could be useful for a spring but unsuitable for a ward conductor; a coating could preserve a tool in one environment but complicate repair. Those examples are original proposals, not claimed Minecraft features. Batch identity and knowledge should matter only where a consumer uses them. Use existing stock, job and item owners.

## Roguelike and expedition ideas

**R1, Apotheosis:** the author's page documents data-driven affixes, gems, sockets and tier-related customization. Downloaded examples confirm readable affix/gem data under `src/generated/resources/data/apotheosis/apotheosis/`. The changelog documents targeted affix editing and preserving older affixed gear through certain upgrades. A useful donor question is whether upgrading a known item competes meaningfully with salvaging unfamiliar loot. Runtime roll ordering, costs, compatible gear and preservation rules remain unread. [Author page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apotheosis), [developer changelog](https://github.com/Shadows-of-Fire/Apotheosis/blob/26.1/changelog.md).

**R2, Vault Hunters:** the official Third Edition page describes an input-funded entrance crystal, procedural dungeons, character abilities and research-gated mod access. Study the cycle of preparation, expedition, findings and improved home capability. Do not automatically import death, compulsory food pressure or repeated entrance grinding. Its Remastered listing is a distinct fresh-start pack, not a verified compatibility upgrade. No pack payload or internal research formula was read. [Official pack](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/vault-hunters-1-18-2).

**R3, dungeon variety:** Roguelike Dungeons advertises generated underground layouts and loot, and its author explains the old version boundary. It is a candidate for connected room roles and encounter/loot constraints, not evidence that a Java structure generator can be dropped into our browser game. Original proposed text scenes could include a workshop, blocked passage, clue and encounter whose order matters. Seed reproducibility and persistence must be demonstrated separately. [Author page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/roguelike-dungeons).

## Research, magic and small discoveries

**K1, Botania/Patchouli:** downloaded challenge entries have category, ordering, read state, advancement qualification and quest-page metadata. The descriptions are localization keys, so those small JSONs alone are not full readable manuals or executable success checks. The useful proposal is a discovered notebook that explains an experiment, then links to an actual process. [Botania source](https://github.com/VazkiiMods/Botania), [Patchouli](https://github.com/VazkiiMods/Patchouli).

**K2, Hex Casting:** the versioned manual describes stack-based composition, typed inputs and specific errors. That is a good research target for composing a learned operation with a tool/carrier and understandable failure feedback. A menu of known methods may capture the useful choice without requiring a player programming language. The exact current repository was not verified after a guessed URL failed; use the successfully opened versioned documentation instead. [Manual](https://hexcasting.hexxy.media/v/0.11.4/1.0/en_us/).

**K3, Thaumcraft and Ars:** Thaumcraft remains a worthwhile candidate for studying the distinction between discovering evidence and performing a researched technique, but its official page does not establish a full research algorithm or open-source implementation. Ars has a public code repo and an author-linked wiki; spell composition and practical magical assistance are named follow-up questions, not source-proved findings here. Pin editions before comparing them. [Thaumcraft](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/thaumcraft), [Ars](https://github.com/baileyholl/Ars-Nouveau).

## Quest packs and easy acquisition

**A1:** above-and-beyond is the correct official repo name, discovered through its CurseForge Source link after the guessed name failed. The snapshot contains quest SNBT and KubeJS scripts. Market quests include reset-tag rewards; the server script schedules a reset for those rewards. Exact payment atomicity and repeat behavior have not been exercised. Its author describes profession-funded trade and seed-dependent alchemical research. [Official pack](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/create-above-and-beyond), [source](https://github.com/simibubi/Above-and-Beyond).

**A2:** Enigmatica's snapshot contains 38 chapter SNBT files and 817 files under the inspected server-script path, plus manual data. These are file counts, not completed feature counts. The sampled automation chapter has both checkmark and item tasks. A sampled expert recipe script is gated by `global.isExpertMode`, with transitional items and repeated processing steps. Normal and expert semantics must stay distinct. [Repo](https://github.com/EnigmaticaModpacks/Enigmatica6).

**A3:** Nomifactory's downloaded `overrides/config/betterquesting/DefaultQuests.json` parses as JSON with 845 quest-database records, prerequisites, visibility/task/reward settings and consumption fields. Its recipe overrides are `.zs` files. The count proves file readability, not a validated dependency graph or runtime behavior. Do not substitute Nomifactory CEu or Monifactory for this 1.12.2 dev snapshot. [Repo](https://github.com/Nomifactory/Nomifactory).

| Downloaded JAR | Resources can be read as archive entries; Java behavior may need source/decompilation | Packaged resources; not gameplay parity or public-source licensing |

**A4, reuse boundary:** Silent Gear's repo declares MIT, Nomifactory distinguishes LGPL content from reserved branding, Botania uses custom terms and Patchouli declares CC-BY-NC-SA. FTB Quests exposes source but its inspected metadata says All Rights Reserved. No license was established for the Enigmatica snapshot. This pass recommends original mechanic translations, not copying their code/assets. [Silent Gear](https://github.com/SilentChaos512/Silent-Gear), [Nomifactory](https://github.com/Nomifactory/Nomifactory), [Botania license](https://github.com/VazkiiMods/Botania/blob/1.20.x/LICENSE.txt), [Patchouli](https://github.com/VazkiiMods/Patchouli), [FTB metadata](https://github.com/FTBTeam/FTB-Quests/blob/main/fabric/src/main/resources/fabric.mod.json).
