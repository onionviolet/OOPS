# Events, encounters, fortune and continuity

E1: Task contains 393 definitions: 121 main, 101 side, 99 offers and 72 friendship tasks. Trigger contains 413 rows, including explicitly invalid examples. Selected valid rows combine quest history, elapsed days, friendship and house state with command arrays that alter dialogue, schedules, messages and scene access. This is evidence of authored reactive conditions, not unrestricted procedural storytelling.

E2: Prayer is specific rather than one vague luck meter. Pray has 11 benefit families: double-yield processes for crops, gathering, cooking, fishing, monster drops, products, animal drops and alchemy; banquet friendship; autofarming; magic-plant energy. Each family names three buff levels and an initial level. GodnessLevel has 12 upgrade rows, extra chances at levels 6 and 12, configured critical rate 0.1 then 0.2. GlobalSettings.PrayBaseCriticalRate is another value, 0.2. Native combination and duration remain unread, so do not add or substitute these rates by guesswork.

E3: Automation is earned authored tooling. AutoWater distinguishes manual physical-cost rows and Auto=True zero-cost rows, with two ranges. This is a useful comparison for progression changing the shape of routine work. It does not prove offline progress, free seed stock, unattended harvest or an actor agreeing to work.

E4: Mystic/collection zones have many named tables and drop packages. Only layout/table discovery and delegation references were read. The [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1201230/?l=english) states seasonal farming, shared rebuilding, skill growth, classes and tests, companion stories/shared dwellings, and more than ten Mystic Realms. These are stated features; full realm encounter semantics are open. No in-game exploration occurred.

E5: Conflicting breakthrough sources must remain visible. Installed Level LV2 has Exp 28000 and BreakTimeSpend 0; the [wiki](https://immortallife.wiki.gg/wiki/Breakthrough) lists a Qi middle threshold 32000 and six-hour cost. Its stage catalogue also differs. Installed Breakthrough has nine rows and four puzzle maps in BreakthroughMaps/BreakthroughMapConfig, while Level LV1–6 carries monster waves. ClassSchedule includes modern classroom examples; LifeSkill contains a test skill. None may be dismissed as dead or declared live solely from the payload.

E6: Inheritance and next life remain open. No dedicated rebirth/legacy/lifespan schema was identified in the targeted type search and key/text scan of parsed nonlocalization tables. A full method-name/content audit was not done. Mythic language about immortality or reincarnation is not an implemented next-life carry contract. Compare with the explicit permanent meta ledgers in Cycle/Infinite Dao and Path, and preserve YAIRPG's current heir code as current implementation rather than a future design limit.
