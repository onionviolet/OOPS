## Roblox boundary

Roblox says the client receives replicated runtime data, and client-replicated scripts can be decompiled; server-only scripts in ServerScriptService and ServerStorage do not replicate. Streaming can mean only part of the world is present. A release thus may expose a partial implementation, not the complete place or server rules. [Client/server](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/client-server), [confidentiality](https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/security/access-control). Roblox Studio's normal full-copy route requires the experience to allow copying: [support](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/203313900-How-to-Copy-an-Experience). Observe playable mechanics and public materials first, seek source when shared, and distinguish inspecting client material from reusing assets or publishing code. Actual access and content remain untested for these experiences.

Follow-up when selected: compare a specific Gu interaction to the novel and relevant current engine seam, then choose one end-to-end experiment. No gameplay files were changed by this scout.
