## 8. Method limits

Search results are metadata, not file checks. "Found" means a result with a
plausible title; sizes and years are listed so bad editions can be avoided. The
holdings list is built from Calibre plus bounded file-name checks and docs, so
items stored elsewhere may have been missed. Re:Zero and A Soldier's Life Book 4
statuses are the two known ambiguous cases. No existing file was edited and no
download or job was started.
