# Secondary source extensions: exceptions and later continuities

Checked 2026-10-11 through the web reader. These are wiki-reported mechanics,
not directly inspected manga scenes. Permanent revisions below were obtained
from each rendered page's footer. No raw manga or wiki dump was downloaded.
They supersede the assumption that every current reading route is blocked;
the old scripted-fetch failure remains a valid historical record.

Each source summary is deliberately bounded. The implementation implications
are original proposals, not extra canon rules. None reveals a sealed game truth.

## Coverage and uncertainty

W1 spans examples from several parts; W2/W4 give selected Part 7 mechanics,
W3/W5 selected Part 8 mechanics and W6/W7 selected Part 9 mechanics.
This is not an exhaustive catalog of any part. The series overview was opened
for orientation but contributes no additional mechanics claim here.

The original plan's M3 fan premise/generator survey remains unperformed.
It can yield optional origin/authoring ideas, but cannot settle canonical laws.
Likewise, a wiki reference to a chapter is a locator for follow-up, not proof
that the chapter was read. Latest publication coverage is not established by
the checked revisions. Pin the actual represented chapter cutoff before
authoring a direct Part 9 campaign.
