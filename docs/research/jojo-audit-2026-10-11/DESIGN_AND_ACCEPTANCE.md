## D1 Two horizons, one delivery boundary

The audit's broad horizon is a represented JoJo world or several explicitly
separated continuity/era scopes, including ordinary lives, institutions and
exceptional powers. The next implementation horizon is one connected original
fixture in one realm, with no new prestige. A playable conceptual fixture does
not complete a JoJo campaign or establish full-series source coverage.

Canon questions below need continuity, medium and scene locators. `O` denotes
original proposals; a canon question is not resolved by an O adapter. The four
bounded official-anime anchors already recorded in the conceptual dossier are
retained only within their stated limits. None establishes universal visibility,
one-power ownership, shared damage, range tradeoffs or mandatory weak points.

Whole-world planning can retain more than this first fixture. Each selected
detail needs one actual owner, one native consumer and one observable criterion.
Section tags are navigation, not imposed gameplay meters. No copy of raw source
prose or sealed world truth belongs in this public report.

## D3 Starts, means and development

Random candidate design must include ordinary starts as well as unusual ones.
Do not equate random place, random era, random role and random faculty. Each axis
needs a selected policy, then a compatible joined snapshot. Eligibility follows
installed content and functioning initial actions, not the presence of a design
document. Candidate details that would reveal world signature rules belong in
sealed owners; player entry shows only observable surroundings and usable means.

An ordinary worker can know tools and customers without knowing a hidden power.
An investigator can possess a fitting instrument or contact without having its
target's private history. An unusual source-holder may possess great output but
not know every application. These are O candidate shapes, not three mandatory
classes or equal-difficulty starts. They require no Frontier prerequisite.

Means qualify both sides at commitment and at any declared sustained boundary.
Knowledge, finding, reaching, harming, healing, making, persuasion, communication
and institutional legitimacy need their own held support. Learning that an enemy
has a power does not supply the means to block it. Strong means can solve a
problem outright; the audit must not invent a weaker alternative's exclusive
counter to protect balance. An unsupported hostile suspicion can exist as a
belief, but cannot grant accurate hidden identification or impossible pursuit.

Growth has separate possible dimensions: practical mastery, higher supported
output, wider understanding of an unchanged function, new mode, changed source,
body development and genuinely evolved rules. Each selected change records its
cause, actual changed function and retention. Repeated ordinary work can improve
a method without evolving its source. A source event can evolve a function
without awarding generic practice XP. No universal energy, cooldown, strain,
rarity or breakthrough system follows from this audit.

## D4 Cheats, copying, carriers and continuing consequences

| Distinction | Required actual output | What cannot be silently granted |
|---|---|---|
| Observed/recorded method | Evidence or a learned method revision | Donor source, anatomy, fuel, mastery or immediate execution |
| Temporary imitation | Declared function for a declared period/source | Permanent ownership or donor loss |
| Teaching | Learner's actual progress through the teaching means | Automatic learner compatibility or donor depletion |
| Transfer/theft | Named exclusive source/function moves under custody and body rules | A duplicate at donor and recipient, or transfer of unrelated identity |
| Duplication | A source-qualified newly made instance and its provenance | Free replication of all memories, carried property, souls or reserves |
| Function lease | Declared function held elsewhere with expiry/restoration policy | Return of consumed materials, erased history or already paid work |
| Foreknowledge/forecast | Supported information with source, horizon and stale policy | Guaranteed execution, free rerolls or authority to alter history |

These distinctions are O implementation semantics, not claims that every JoJo
source has all these modes. An exceptional copier may truly supply more when
source evidence establishes it. Victory, killing, sacrifice, contact and licensed
borrowing are different triggers; selection of one does not settle the output.

Carriers preserve their own domains. A learned method travels as retained
knowledge; its execution still needs suitable body/source/local support. A
transferred faculty travels under its own actual binding rule. Goods and
companions need custody and travel means. A record of a prior person does not
prove the current target is that person. Direct canon interpretation and original
cross-world conversions stay separate, with no universal exchange rate.

Everyday utility needs a closed causal chain: source use changes a real service
or good; work is accepted or rejected; actual pay/stock/custody changes; named
witnesses can form impressions or pass supported reports. Macro effects need
participants, demand, supply and transmission. One witnessed repair does not
automatically advertise the player's entire faculty to every government.

## D5 Recommended first proof: C2-JF

Reuse the existing conceptual packet's original single-scene proof, qualified
through the sealed specification rather than starting another disconnected
realm. Status: O recommendation, no new prestige, no runtime assignment or
accepted JoJo campaign. The agent-only exact brief and fixtures are in
`docs/research/novels/jojo/audit-2026-10-11/SEALED_C2_DESIGN.md`.

The public category is an everyday delivery/service consequence joined to a
conceptual interaction, actor-specific discovery and real retained ownership.
One coherent journey has two causally different response routes, optional
supported direct action and withdrawal. Routes JF-A and JF-B change different
actual retained state; their exact actions, triggers, private intent and solutions
stay sealed. Source availability, local inputs, actual costs and outcome rules
must be frozen before coding. Do not add a universal meter or balance ceiling.

| Step | Public journey contract | Durable outcome obligation |
|---|---|---|
| JF01 | Enter through a qualified initial route; inspect visible surroundings | Selected scene/start remains stable, with no fabricated prior player actions |
| JF02 | Read the ordinary work offer; use a held fitting investigation means | Only permitted evidence enters character knowledge; known work obligations are readable |
| JF03 | Choose and complete supported route JF-A or JF-B, direct action or withdrawal | Actual property, function, work and relationship results follow sealed local rule; routes do not collapse into different dialogue for identical state |
| JF04 | Complete the ordinary task and obtain its report | Acceptance/payment claim, custody and supported observations settle once after successful persistence |
| JF05 | Revisit the participants and affected object/service | Continuing consequences retain their actual owners and causes |
| JF06 | Save, reload and import a generated fixture save | Same identities, paid steps, retained evidence and claims remain without replay |

NPC and player use the same source/contact/custody/target validators. NPC policy
uses held evidence, with no special access to player-private state. A generated
test actor may hold selected means for symmetry comparison without granting
that means as a live reward. Strong supported direct means can resolve the
problem; no invented immunity should force the investigation route.

First proof uses authored response boundaries, revalidating declared sustained
invariants at resolution. Same-boundary competing transitions follow the frozen
stable order and fresh checks, with no duplicate exclusive ownership. General
continuous geometry, simultaneous combat, time domains and arbitrary concepts
are outside this proof.

Until a distinct autonomous policy is selected/tested, offline time chooses no
new scene response, acquires no private evidence and starts no uncommitted
conceptual use. Committed ordinary work follows its explicit clock/access
policy; an unresolved decision stops the scene's next action. This is an O local
fixture policy, not a global world pause or current-runtime claim.

Native panels show only target descriptions, known inputs, acquired evidence,
chosen actions and witnessed results. Unknown rules, private intent/capacity
and other actors' histories stay absent from previews, DOM and rejection detail.
Natural-language feedback binds registered transformations, not executable
free-form player sentences.
