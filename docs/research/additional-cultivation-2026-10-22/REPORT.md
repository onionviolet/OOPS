## Source findings

### C7: learned techniques have a real resource and combat reader

**C7-F1 implemented and tested.** Training spends current Qi to increase capacity; insufficient Qi switches to meditation. Advancement checks capacity, then offers aspect and technique choices. Ordinary play reached Fire Bolt and selected it against a 40-HP goblin. Continuing changed enemy HP to 37 and current Qi from 5 to 0; capacity also rose. This proves a combat consumer and payment, not superiority over an ordinary attack or a balanced build.

**C7-F2 source-traced, alternatives unplayed.** `tickMethods.ts` trains selected mastery. `store.tsx::tickMana` decreases cost toward a floor with mastery and scales cost steeply with intensity. Its damage readers distinguish target damage, sustained area damage, weapon enhancement, accumulated enhancement, thorns and increasing target count. Combat cleanup and continuing costs have handlers. This is more than an aspect label, although only the Fire Bolt path was trialed.

**C7-F3 partial.** The smithing component is a heading and incomplete prompt. The advancement-method file is commented-out code, while actual advancement lives in the store. A computed meditation regeneration contribution is not returned by the general passive-mana getter, so declarations do not certify the intended bonus. Offline training directly credits capacity for bounded elapsed real time rather than replaying the active action sequence. Do not infer a complete crafting system or foreground/offline equivalence from filenames.

Pinned primary source: [store](https://github.com/shlocko/CultivationIdle/blob/63a70dcf7b9b6af9f5ca37a68cbdb231f642d144/src/state/store.tsx), [tick consumers](https://github.com/shlocko/CultivationIdle/blob/63a70dcf7b9b6af9f5ca37a68cbdb231f642d144/src/functions/tickMethods.ts), [combat](https://github.com/shlocko/CultivationIdle/blob/63a70dcf7b9b6af9f5ca37a68cbdb231f642d144/src/functions/combatMethods.tsx).

### C8: complementary idle power and active acquisition, currently documented

**C8-F1 advertised, not gameplay verified.** The creator distinguishes meditation progress from equipment and herbs earned through active play, describes limited slower offline accumulation, and describes lifespan-driven disciple succession separately from voluntary reincarnation. A quest-gift pill is described as helping rather than being required for Foundation. These are useful distinctions to investigate, not verified service behavior. [Creator FAQ](https://idlecultivation.com/)

**C8-F2 blocked within scope.** The apparently guest-friendly button opens registration, including optional email and a required name. No name was entered or submitted. Direct HTML capture returned HTTP 403; inspection switched to the functioning browser route without repeated fetch variants. Account creation was excluded, so no ordinary active/offline loop or method consumer was tested. The current served script filename is a locator, not a source revision or source-read receipt. No paid-content conclusion rests on this trial.

### C9: body cultivation changes different operations

**C9-F1 implemented, early reach tested.** Body costs require both blood and stones plus the matching Qi realm. The opening hunt earned blood while Qi continued accruing. The body panel accurately blocked the first purchase on the Qi Condensation requirement; therefore no claim of a completed body/qi interaction is made from play alone. First ordinary stage advancement and saved continuation were observed.

**C9-F2 source-traced consumers.** Recovered `body.gd` supplies functions read by the main loop: lifespan, hunt duration, longevity recovery through `laws.gd`, fading, tribulation deflections/loss, extra beasts and Qi gain. Body strength is suppressed below its matching Qi realm. Rebirth restores a bounded portion of best body progress, and save/load records current and historical progress separately. This is retained qualification plus current activation, rather than one permanent number affecting everything.

**C9-F3 implemented late layer, unplayed.** `laws.gd` has contemplation costs, focus and retained law progress; tiers feed authored effects. `genesis.gd` checks selected law thresholds and Qi, then advances epochs and eventually produces faith for decrees. These are executable progression systems, not merely planned headings. They are also not a simulated society of independent mortals or arbitrary world-law execution. Generated law names do not imply 3,000 independent mechanics. [Exact served build](https://html-classic.itch.zone/html/19173761-2155396/index.html?v=1792348683)

### C10: expedition personnel are real reservations, previews are only baselines

**C10-F1 tested opening.** Buying the breathing upgrade changed tap gain from 1 to 11. A first breakthrough spent 100 Qi and advanced to layer 2; reload preserved the layer and upgrade. Sect Management explicitly required Foundation, so later disciples were not granted or bypassed for the trial.

**C10-F2 source-traced.** Expedition start requires an available disciple, permits one expedition per type, records a named worker and deadline, snapshots the computed base reward and saves. Completion applies risk, reward, pity/rare bonus, frees the reservation and optionally repeats. Research has realm/prerequisite/currency checks and production consumers. Story choices feed persistent modifier pools, not a demonstrated branching social simulation. [Deployed source](https://html-classic.itch.zone/html/17892856/Cultivation_World_v6_19/main.js)

**C10-F3 qualifications.** The displayed reward uses definition values while start applies research/class modifiers; displayed duration also omits some modifiers. Failure and pity bonuses further change settlement. Consequently the creator's exact-preview claim is stronger than the inspected implementation. Bonuses use the sect's class totals rather than just the sent disciple, and reservations use names rather than robust person IDs. Description comments and formulas also disagree in places. Preserve the worker-conflict lesson, not those shortcuts. Later gameplay remains implemented-but-unplayed here.

### C11: power opens geography; environmental cultivation is explicitly incomplete

**C11-F1 tested.** The opening shows one member of a six-slot party, individual Qi controls, total party power and area requirements. Cultivation spent Qi; later observed capacity and total power reached 10. At that cap the action required breakthrough first. Reload retained the 10-power state, and selecting/entering the 10-power woodland revealed a further area. Exact advancement causality was not instrumented, so the observations do not establish its formula.

**C11-F2 retired/partial, not a current complete feature.** The final-major-update post says weather, area element and Qi density were removed, with some background breakthrough effects remaining. A subsequent post reports expanded areas/cultivation and a dismissal-save fix. Do not recycle older comments as current proof of either complete environmental laws or current save bugs. [Final update](https://marrow-tap.itch.io/cultivation-quest/devlog/897913/final-major-update), [later changes](https://marrow-tap.itch.io/cultivation-quest/devlog/903237/update-balancing-optimization)
