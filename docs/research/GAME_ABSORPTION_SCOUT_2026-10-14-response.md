## Completed external response

YAIRPG: Next game absorption research

Research date: October 14, 2026 · External source review · Recommendations, not implementation authorization

Executive conclusion

YAIRPG's next research should concentrate on mechanics that connect systems already present, rather than collecting more isolated progression systems.

The supplied audit establishes substantial source-level adoption, but not full playable parity. The strongest remaining opportunities are at the boundaries between existing systems: learning something and acting on that knowledge; having a profession and belonging to an institution; owning materials and transforming a particular object; developing a distinctive power and making meaningful tactical choices; living in a world and encountering consequences that did not originate as player quests.

I would prioritize five references across those boundaries:

The ranking weighs incremental value, compatibility with YAIRPG's existing consumers, distinctive player experiences, and research payoff. It does not rank the games' overall quality.

Three guardrails shape the recommendations:

World rules remain local. Wuxia sect succession, archaeological interpretation, material processing and triggered powers should not automatically become universal systems across Harry Potter, Fate, LOTM, original worlds or parallel variants.

Automation preserves decisions. Queued practice and work can advance naturally; the player should intervene when evidence changes, a meaningful choice appears or a support assumption fails.

A playable result outranks architectural elegance. Each recommendation below has a small, observable connected journey. A convincing diagram, generated description or source-only consumer is insufficient evidence of gameplay.

1. Ranked top five: what to absorb
1. The Scroll of Taiwu: institutions that change what learning means

Queued reference · High confidence in advertised mechanics · Highest incremental social/world-system value

Documented: ConchShip Games describes fifteen martial sects, learnable crafts, relationships and feuds, village industries, aging NPCs, and the transmission of martial knowledge between generations. 
Official developer Steam description
 
store.steampowered.com

Exact YAIRPG gap: A resident can already remember a promise, teach a profession and perform paid work. What remains distinct is institutional standing that grants and constrains access to particular knowledge, equipment, responsibilities and successors. These cannot be reduced to affection or a skill level.

Connected player journey (proposed adaptation)

A traveling apprentice reaches a mountain school. They can learn a basic technique from a friendly instructor, but the school's advanced method requires a sponsor or contribution. They spend ordinary days repairing ceremonial equipment and practicing. A teacher later offers supervised access to a restricted manual. If that teacher leaves, the obligation remains recorded by the institution, but personal support changes. Another qualified teacher may take over.

Small details worth absorbing: distinguish an instructor's personal willingness from the institution's permission; treat lending, gifting and selling as different transactions; make a sponsor's departure matter without erasing earned knowledge; and permit an outsider to learn through a legitimate alternative where the fiction allows.

Dependencies: Existing NPC commitments, profession teaching, retained work, manuals and access restrictions. Succession is a conditional future extension, not a required aging simulation.

First playable proof: Two qualified paths produce the same useful technique, but differing costs, obligations and later access. Removing one instructor changes the offer without deleting progress.

Exact gap: YAIRPG has discovery, diagnostic study, manuals and identified objects. The missing interaction is retaining the difference between what was observed, what was inferred and what a character currently believes, with later evidence capable of changing a practical decision.

Connected player journey (proposed adaptation)

A player finds an inscription on a damaged workshop mechanism. Two plausible readings suggest different uses. A local scholar offers an interpretation, but cannot guarantee it. The player chooses a cautious repair, searches for corroborating evidence or experiments with a limited component. Later evidence changes their interpretation, possibly opening a different repair method. Their earlier work remains part of history rather than being silently rewritten.

Useful details include confidence without compulsory numerical percentages, character-specific interpretations, provenance of observations and a journal that distinguishes fact from hypothesis.

Dependencies: Existing discovery records, Book of Hours-style study, identified-object custody and world-specific knowledge rules. Avoid a universal glyph language or mandatory translation minigame.

First playable proof: One artifact admits two plausible interpretations, two reachable choices and one subsequent revision. A mistaken interpretation changes an action without permanently blocking progress.

Exact gap: Existing mastery and preparation modify useful functions. The additional value is a composable action-trigger relationship that changes which actions a player prefers and how powers interact with equipment, timing and limited resources.

Connected player journey (proposed adaptation)

A practitioner learns a defensive technique triggered by holding position and an offensive technique triggered by attacking. The first rewards patience under pressure; the second creates openings but consumes a limited charge. A crafted item reinforces one technique. An encounter that interrupts stationary concentration encourages the player to reconsider preparation instead of merely increasing damage statistics.

Important details: distinguish initiating an attack from a triggered extra hit; specify whether effects originate from the player, an ally or equipment; expose resource consumption; and place clear limits on recursive effects.

Dependencies: Actual combat or another relevant action consumer, retained equipment properties and event ordering. Work queues should not generate unlimited trigger activations.

First playable proof: Two preparations using comparable resources produce visibly different successful actions in the same encounter, with correct costs and an understandable event log.

The older v0.4.7 dossier should not be treated as current. Published changes through v1.4.3 establish material design evolution, but the searched announcements do not independently establish every later build rule.

Exact gap: YAIRPG already has paid-input refinement, real tool use and a retort with downstream products. The next valuable consumer is a particular unfinished or completed object's recoverable transformation state, not another recipe or inventory subsystem.

Connected player journey (proposed adaptation)

A smith acquires an irregular metal blank, prepares it for a ward fitting and begins shaping it. A cooling interruption stops that particular workpiece. Reheating permits continuation at a resource cost; recovering scrap preserves some material but loses progress. The finished fitting modifies an existing named item. Later, its usefulness changes when the supporting ward loses power.

Absorb intermediate-item identity, retained partial work, tool-specific requirements, explicit recovery yields and distinguishable completion quality. None requires importing voxel manipulation.

Dependencies: Existing material quantities, actual tool consumers, object identity, paid work and optional persistent-ward behavior. Preserve stock conservation and distinguish recoverable matter from irreversible treatment.

First playable proof: One workpiece can finish, pause and resume, or be dismantled. All branches preserve legal material totals; a completed fitting has a useful effect on one existing item.

The wiki's smithing page identifies v1.20.6 as its verified version, so it should not be represented as a complete account of all later Vintage Story revisions.

Exact gap: YAIRPG has causal NPC memory and negotiated duties. Warsim suggests small generated circumstances whose distinct properties make several concrete responses sensible, rather than randomly paraphrased offers.

Connected player journey (proposed adaptation)

A traveler arrives with a disputed route chart and needs a repair. The player can pay to inspect it, repair the instrument in exchange for access, seek a guild witness or decline. Which options work depends on the visitor's resources, provenance and what services already exist locally. The resulting chart or obligation creates a later usable opportunity.

Small details worth absorbing: visitor origin, reason for arrival, finite means, encounter variants, consequences of delegation and reports that identify the actual changed relationship or resource.

Dependencies: Existing duty negotiation, professions, local workshop problems and promise records. Avoid converting this into automatic kingdom management.

First playable proof: One seeded visitor problem has three legitimately reachable solutions. Each produces a different persistent change that the player can later use or observe.

This yields more value than pursuing millions of generated species or enormous branching text counts. For YAIRPG, causal usefulness per variation matters more than variation count.

The less obvious findings deserve attention. Medieval Merchants 2.0 directly resembles YAIRPG's presentation constraints, while Moonring demonstrates how a changing world condition can influence available powers without needing an enormous simulation. Cataclysm: DDA is unusually useful because public documentation exposes exact relationships between skills, proficiencies, tools, recipe stages and practice.

3. Five combinations with particularly strong potential

1. Heaven's Vault + Shadows of Doubt + YAIRPG's existing study

A found object carries observations, interpretations and chain of custody. Research or conversation changes the interpretation; a subsequent action tests it. This gives discoveries useful consequences beyond unlock notifications.

Risk: information overload and tedious evidence bookkeeping. Only surface distinctions that affect a plausible choice.

2. Taiwu + Discworld MUD + existing profession/household systems

A person can be a skilled teacher, family member, guild officer and private friend, with different powers in each role. Succession could eventually change an institution's available service without resetting everything.

Risk: vague claims of autonomous society. Require actual retained relationships, obligations and observable consumers; avoid simulating every person's entire life.

3. Vintage Story + Potion Craft + existing retort and tool systems

Transform a particular material through discrete, meaningful preparation stages, then sell, equip or modify the result. A specialized alchemist might care about preparation quality where an ordinary consumer simply buys the finished medicine.

Risk: chore creep and duplicate crafting interfaces. Keep routine production queueable and active intervention optional or genuinely consequential.

4. Path of Achra + Siralim Ultimate + canon-specific power systems

Conditional effects could make a JJK technique's restrictions, a Fate artifact's conditions or another world's supernatural rules materially different. Existing identified and charged objects provide natural carriers.

Risk: exponential reward inflation, recursive effects and canon flattening. Keep finite trigger limits, understandable costs and powers confined to compatible world rules.

5. Warsim + Roadwarden + Idle Loops + existing saved work

A local workshop or expedition proceeds through predictable work until a visitor, clue or support failure creates a meaningful decision. The player returns to a useful state with distinct options, not a punishment for being away.

Risk: incompatible time models, excessive interruptions and unsupported emergent-world claims. Browser-local advancement should reconcile finite work and pause at decisions it cannot reasonably resolve.

4. What to defer

Proto23's original and mod, basic discovery staging, semantic colors and craft panel design are already heavily researched. Revisit only a specific unresolved browser or human-taste gate, not another general UI absorption pass.

Similarly, Factorio-style batch production, Book of Hours-style tool-supported study and Amazing Cultivation Simulator-inspired duty are already represented by meaningful selected consumers. Their next reviews should concern concrete gaps, not generic counterparts.

NGU Idle, Antimatter Dimensions and broad prestige-tree references have comparatively lower immediate incremental value. The existing first-sequence milestone needs credible ordinary play and distinctive world interactions more than additional numerical layers.

HOI4's global logistics and Taiwu's full population simulation should also wait for a narrower locally useful consumer to justify further scale. Spatial scenes, richer scripting, succession and AU convergence remain valid future possibilities, not prohibited categories.

5. Practical research order and open questions

A short next research sequence would be:

Taiwu plus Discworld: Determine exactly how a specific institution's teaching access differs from personal goodwill. Compare eligibility, cost, obligations and what survives the teacher's departure.

Heaven's Vault plus existing discovery: Determine how uncertain interpretations are represented and revised without requiring confirmation or spoilers. Inkle's public ink writing manual is useful for examining state tracking and branching, but it does not expose Heaven's Vault's private game rules. 
GitHub

Path of Achra plus Siralim: Establish exact event ordering, source attribution, resource payment and recursion limits for a small set of interacting powers, then compare the result with YAIRPG's existing functional mastery.

Vintage Story plus CDDA: Compare retained physical workpieces with recipe-stage requirements and specific proficiencies. Determine which distinction creates a new useful choice rather than a duplicated abstraction.

Warsim plus a smaller text trading reference: Investigate whether different origins, resources and institutional relationships actually yield different reachable outcomes, rather than merely different descriptions.

Unresolved evidence boundaries: No local repository was inspected. There was no game installation or native play in this review. Closed-game developer descriptions establish advertised systems but not all internal algorithms. The older Achra dossier, hosted Magic Research 2 demo, bounded Soulash 2 research and incomplete MUD coverage remain version or completeness limitations. Public GitHub documentation describes accessible files, not a fixed historical executable snapshot.

The most important question for each deeper dive is therefore not how many mechanics the source contains. It is:

What one additional decision becomes possible, what retained state makes it different, and which existing YAIRPG consumer can demonstrate that difference without compromising current saves or the first playable milestone?

That is the standard I would use before elevating any of these research recommendations into an implementation proposal.
