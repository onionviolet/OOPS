## Wider implications for original YAIR

**Capabilities need a physical basis.** A faculty, tissue/material, reach, timing, learned method and current source support can all change execution. Keep the learned knowledge when a body is unsuitable. Finite functional injuries fit YAIR before a full anatomy simulation.

**Production is a graph of competing uses.** One plant or material may have several outputs and an identity-preserving treatment. Use actual native items, preserved tools, source-specific conversions and useful byproducts. Existing finite reserves should choose a recipe before committing stock.

**Information is paid work with provenance.** Physical records, qualified observers and offices can improve knowledge precision without omniscience. A current stock report should be a sampled estimate or actual counted receipt, not a second inventory. Teaching can change capability only after an eligible learner performs the work.

**Institutions are actors and rights.** Culture permissions, appointment, succession, policy, personal belief and worker willingness differ. A school or guild can change what its own officer offers with actual facility/funding and a meaningful refusal; membership is not mind control.

**Development can change danger.** Production, extraction and wealth can alter which inhabitants notice a site. Tie finite threats to visible world changes and actual informed actors, with practical responses and retained aftermath, rather than secretly scaling every opponent to the player.

**Causality survives transformation.** A curse, interrupted visit or changed body should retain origin and target identity, distinguish treatment from complete removal, and change only its supported clocks. Specific culpability/observer records prevent generic retaliation at everyone.

**Content pins are part of persistence.** A save depends on body/action/material definitions and compatible revisions. YAIR already validates exact frozen jobs; new content should keep old meanings and migrations. Snapshotting inputs is retention, not proof that new gameplay saves work.

**Graphics uses rules rather than replacing them.** Keep body/state, discoverable description and rendering distinct. A later visual workshop should present the same work tile, rights, custody, interrupted work and produced identity. This report selects no replacement engine and copies no art.
