# Decoded rule chains and cleanup limits

All locators below resolve through [READ_COVERAGE.json](READ_COVERAGE.json), which pins exact files and hashes. `S01:170–202` means source S01, inclusive lines 170 through 202. These are static reads of shipped source/data. Numeric token fields remain token fields unless their native interpretation is independently recovered. No implied milliseconds, guaranteed action, success rate or gameplay proof.

## DF-EM01: actor → blood → eligible actor

**Producer.** `interactions.curse.major.vampire(idx,tok)` emits a syndrome-bearing interaction. Its additional ingestion source describes consuming another actor's tainted blood. The target must have `CAN_LEARN` and `HAS_BLOOD`; `NOT_LIVING` and `SUPERNATURAL` are forbidden. It also excludes `WERECURSE`, `VAMPCURSE`, `DISTURBANCE_CURSE`, `RAISED_UNDEAD`, `RAISED_GHOST` and `GHOUL`. This is a composability boundary: visible power families do not imply all combinations are legal on one body. [S02:53–95]

**State.** Added `VAMPCURSE` syndrome gives `BLOODSUCKER`, no aging, sterility, not-living state, no exertion/pain/breathing/stun/nausea/dizziness/fever, paralysis immunity, no ordinary food/drink/sleep and no physical-attribute gain/rust. Strength, agility and toughness use declared pairs `200:0`, and the material-force multiplier is `1:2`. Native arithmetic and precedence are not recovered here, so these are not silently restated as additive stat points. [S02:79–85]

**Propagation join.** `CE_BODY_MAT_INTERACTION` binds reserved blood to the same supplied `tok`; the following syndrome tags permit ingestion and injection. The route therefore points back to a real interaction identity rather than a new prose-only disease. This is a static recursive-capability declaration. Actual blood item production, retaining source attribution through containers, dose/concentration, attack injection, route eligibility, duplicate exposure and transmission execution are missing native consumers. [S02:86–89]

**Information/decision.** Display tile/name can be hidden. Tooth length modifier has a drinking-blood counter trigger requiring at least one. A creature-class sense declaration also appears. These fields do not show who notices a vampire, whether an observer learns its identity, whether an NPC chooses victims, or how legal/social response works. A player-readable raw must not become actor omniscience. [S02:90–94]

**Cleanup.** Transformation effects have `START:0:ABRUPT` without END. This establishes the absence of an emitted timer, not impossibility of all cures. No remove-syndrome, cure, source-death revocation or blood-cleaning consumer was recovered. Exclusion classes prevent some applications; they do not remove existing applications. Binding an actor's blood to a source interaction also raises a persistence question about retained definitions and identity after that actor dies, which remains unread.

## DF-EM02: corpse → actor → specific retaliation and further corpses

**Entry.** Disturbance targets a corpse at the context item, rejects not-living and six curse/raised classes, and requires fitness for resurrection plus learning and speech. It emits RESURRECT with an immediate syndrome bearing both `DISTURBED_DEAD` and `DISTURBANCE_CURSE`. Attribute pairs are strength `300:1000` and toughness `300:1000`. It adds extensive undead/needs/movement-independence tags, removes blood/trance/mischievous tags and changes anxiety/trust/vengefulness facets by `50/-50/100`. These are declared state operations, not proof of memory preservation or an executed emotional model. [S04:65–92]

**Target scope.** Raised actor gets three capability declarations. Curse has LOS plus `DISTURBER_ONLY`, range25, one target, verbal source and wait20. Ordinary corpse animation has LOS range25 and wait10. Intelligent resurrection has LOS range10 and wait10. Only the first declaration is disturber-only. The broader capability set could change the scene beyond the culprit, but actual action selection and responsibility lookup remain native. [S04:93–134]

**Different corpse consumers.** `basic_animation(regional)` selects context region when regional, otherwise context item. It requires `FIT_FOR_ANIMATION`, rejects `NOT_LIVING`, and uses ANIMATE with weekly versus immediate effect. It adds ZOMBIE and `OPPOSED_TO_LIFE`, changes strength/toughness, removes blood, and can emit speed percentage60 or20. Its exact random-branch probabilities depend on the native `one_in` binding, not executed here. [S01:170–202]

`basic_lieutenant` instead requires `FIT_FOR_RESURRECTION`, learning, not-living exclusion and six syndrome exclusions. RESURRECT produces `RAISED_UNDEAD`, then merges generated capability declarations and additional interaction records. Animation fitness and resurrection fitness are separate token requirements, so this audit does not equate every corpse fragment with a valid intelligent actor. [S01:239–270]

**NPC use.** Ice/propel capability definitions use LOS range25, one target and wait50 with ATTACK hint and a GRASP body requirement. Vanish uses SELF_ONLY, GRASP, DEFEND/FLEEING hints and wait50. Fog requires GRASP, outside/no-thick-fog hints, DEFEND/FLEEING hints and wait500; effect changes weather. These are intended decision annotations, not recovered AI scoring. [S01:13–168] Availability is additionally limited by DF-EM06's map traversal concern.

**Feedback and cleanup.** A raised actor possessing valid powers could create further actors from corpses, but corpse input removal, corpse reservation/exhaustion, item-to-unit identity, faction/hostility, death-to-corpse and repeated-raising behavior are all native gaps. `NOT_LIVING`/syndrome exclusions limit declared target eligibility but do not establish a global no-loop theorem. The disturber curse has `IT_CANNOT_TARGET_IF_ALREADY_AFFECTED` and a no-END skill-roll effect. Retarget exclusion is not expiration or a cure. [S04:28–47]

## DF-EM03: regional emission → material route → anatomy → persistent aftermath

**Material production.** Cloud names carry a selected material state: GAS, SOLID_POWDER or LIQUID. The material disables ignition/melt/boil/heat/cold damage points and fixes temperature10000. It enters blood and declares injected/contact/inhaled/ingested exposure for `GENERAL_POISON`. One branch emits living-death transformation with permanent-form tags and blood removal. The other calls `add_base_poison_effects` with seventeen candidate effects, severity100 and up to five selected effects. [S06:25–109]

Rain instead has solid/liquid/gas state names, the same four exposure routes and eight candidates: pain, oozing, bruising, blisters, fever, coughing blood, nausea and dizziness. Its helper arguments use severity10, max3 effects, and different timing/resistance/size fields. Cloud and rain therefore differ structurally even before native weather transport is known. [S06:144–186]

**Effect generator.** `add_base_poison_effects` chooses `trandom(max_eff)+1` candidates without replacement. It computes `start=trandom(max_start)+min_start`, `peak=start+trandom(max_peak)+min_peak`, `end=peak+trandom(max_end)+min_end`. A `one_in(terminal_chance)` branch suppresses END; it does not prove instant death. Field `terminal` is helper naming for no emitted finite end, not a clinically measured outcome. Additional branches append RESISTABLE, SIZE_DELAYS and SIZE_DILUTES. Exact handling of zero arguments to `trandom` and `one_in` lives in an unrecovered binding; numeric probability claims are withheld. [S09:69–84]

Anatomy qualifiers differ: some effects are optionally LOCALIZED; swelling/oozing/bruising/blisters/bleeding select vascular scope and sometimes muscular scope. Necrosis can select skin, fat, muscle, eye, nerve, brain, lung, heart, liver, gut, stomach, pancreas, spleen, kidney or ALL. Impairment has its own selection. These are body-sensitive declarations, not a single universal damage type. Actual tissue matching, absence of a vascular system, overlapping effects and healing precedence require native consumers. [S09:1–67]

**Region join.** `generate_random_interactions` references material IDs produced earlier with token-prefix/index. GAS maps to WEATHER_CREEPING_GAS, LIQUID to WEATHER_CREEPING_VAPOR, other selected states to WEATHER_CREEPING_DUST. Rain and usable blood materials map to WEATHER_FALLING_MATERIAL. Effects emit weekly. Region entries declare EVIL_ONLY, SAVAGE_ALLOWED, ANY_TERRAIN and frequency100. Animation and material can both be merged into one region record; source selection distinguishes no materials, animation selected, and material selection. [S07:591–652]

**Combinations and limits.** The same region declaration can expose living actors to body-changing material and contain a separate corpse animation rule. This establishes compatible authored ingredients for a feedback loop, not observed epidemic or endless army. In particular, cloud transformation can add NOT_LIVING while corpse animation forbids it; a specific subsequent actor/corpse path may close rather than multiply. Native death/corpse tags and eligibility must resolve that boundary.

**Cleanup.** Leaving the emitting region is not an emitted remove-syndrome effect. Stopping emission, removing material from a body, ending a temporary physiological effect, reversing damage already caused and undoing a permanent transformation are distinct unresolved operations. Shelter/path avoidance, fluid volume, contamination on gear, washing, weather expiration, seasonal/inactive-region catchup and native syndrome clocks were not inspected.

## DF-EM04: finite crop → material choices → writing medium → capability access

**Competing outputs.** Pig tail structural material maps to drink, seed and paper-press thread material. The THREAD material names solid, paste and pressed forms; PAPER_SLURRY class is attached to it. Summer/autumn planting, subterranean-water biome and depth1–3 are declared. Material identity allows both agricultural return and multiple outputs rather than a universal recipe currency. Native planting/growth yield is not recovered. [S11:65–110]

**Production.** Brewing requires an unrotten plant with DRINK_MAT, plus an empty FOOD_STORAGE_CONTAINER. Container is preserved and does not determine product amount. Product is five drink units, dimension150, placed into the selected container, plus one seed product mapped from plant SEED_MAT; skill is BREWING. These raw counts are product declarations, not measured stack/yield/reservation semantics. [S10:265–280]

Paper from selected plants requires PAPER_PLANT and returns seeds. Slurry needs an unrotten plant with PRESS_PAPER_MAT at quern/millstone; returns a paste glob and seeds. Pressing needs150 unpressed PAPER_SLURRY glob at screw press, produces pressed sheet. Making a quire consumes10000 sheet material and produces a quire. Scrolls additionally consume rollers and emit a specific rollers improvement. Separate material routes mean paper, cloth and drink can compete for plant stock. [S10:372–420]

Binding requires an already-written quire, permits artifact/location-reserved input, a binding and15000 non-web thread. Output book material derives from binding; page improvement material derives from quire. TRANSFER_ARTIFACT_STATUS is explicit, and the shipped note says writing transfers into pages. Item definitions identify scroll and quire as CONTAIN_WRITING, while binding is a hard-material incomplete component. These details distinguish knowledge-bearing content, physical carrier and protective part. [S10:422–437; S13:247–278]

**Institution/learning join.** Dwarf entity permits paper/binding jobs, screw press and listed reactions. A material/reaction existing globally does not imply every civilization permits its jobs. [S12:230–254] Necromancer secret separately advertises supernatural learning, mundane research/teaching and BOOK_INSTRUCTION recording. Target must be mortal, learn and speak. Acquired syndrome grants animation capability, needs/aging changes and possible experimenter role. Manufacturing a blank book is not the acquisition step. [S05:104–159]

**NPC and information.** Manager, medical chief, broker and bookkeeper are site offices with different responsibility declarations. Descriptions distinguish production validation at population-dependent scale, overall-health visibility, item appraisal/trade and count precision improved in an office. These are shipped author descriptions and role tokens, not recovered dynamic accounting algorithms. Vacancy, replacement, actual report staleness and whether the actor knows a true count remain native questions. [S12:636–689]

**Cleanup.** PRESERVE_REAGENT means the container is declared reusable, not proof of cancellation refund. HAS_WRITING_IMPROVEMENT constrains binding input; moving writing into book pages is not deleting an actor's learned knowledge. A borrowed/destroyed book, interrupted teaching, forbidden reaction, unavailable worker, altered body or transformed reader each has a distinct possible consequence. No native job reserve/commit/cancel, reading/teaching scheduler, knowledge validation or ownership consumer was recovered.

## DF-EM05: timed effect ≠ physiological repair ≠ actor expiry

**Temporary changes.** Bad luck has skill-roll fields PERC0/PERC_ON20 and END8400. Beast curse uses END8400 on body transformation and associated no-aging/no-food/no-drink tags. Candidate body is natural animal; excludes small race, large predator, air-incompatible, immobile-land, learning and several supernatural/major-beast categories, and includes fast-effortless-gait qualifier1000. Good luck uses PERC150/PERC_ON50 and END8400. The shipped comments call8400 a week; this audit does not infer a native frame or browser-time conversion. [S02:1–50; S03:1–19]

**Indefinite capabilities.** Vampire, necromancer and disturbed-dead effects omit END. Ending the originating encounter therefore has no shown automatic timer-based revocation. This is not proof that every effect is irrevocable. Body/material/needs/appearance/learning changes must be tracked independently to understand a future cure. [S02:79–94; S04:83–123; S05:145–159]

**Summons.** Bogeyman/nightmare action waits are100 and12000 respectively. Their unit-generation effects use a random nearby location within5 and IE_TIME_RANGE200:300. Availability depends on world parameters and creature categories. A useful qualification: definition records are emitted on `bogeyman`/`nightmare` branches even though the granted CE_CAN_DO_INTERACTION is guarded by `summon`; a visible record can be orphaned from this actor's capability. Ghost routes explicitly use `if false and ghost`. Do not report dormant records as reachable powers. [S05:108–124,188–301]

IE_TIME_RANGE is an actor/summon-related declaration, separate from syndrome END and action WAIT_PERIOD. Native disappearance, carried items, generated corpses, ownership, targets pointing at an expired actor, loaded-region boundaries and save/reload semantics remain unread.

**Healing.** The healing blessing emits twelve effects, each SEV100, PROB100, START0, PEAK0, END12, ABRUPT: stop bleeding, close wounds, heal tissues, heal nerves, reduce pain, reduce swelling, cure infection, reduce paralysis, reduce dizziness, reduce nausea, reduce fever and regrow parts. [S03:101–129] The list demonstrates separate channels. It does not establish damage-history deletion, cleansing every syndrome, full restoration of all body connections, revoking a vampire curse or retaining equipped items after regrowth. An effect ending at12 could stop applying a healing process while already-repaired tissue remains; exact native semantics are unrecovered.

**Companions and physical items.** Pet blessing emits a nearby SUMMON_UNIT with natural-animal/large-predator selection and several exclusion flags, followed by MAKE_PET_IF_POSSIBLE. The qualifier leaves an explicit conditional outcome. It has no IE_TIME_RANGE in that shown declaration. Holy-equipment blessing instead creates an artifact item; improved equipment changes quality by2. Those operations must not inherit temporary-summon cleanup just because they share a blessing source. [S03:21–99]

## DF-EM06: availability and control-flow qualification

The four powers are a keyed Lua table, while the selector loops over `ipairs`. A recursive search of all shipped Lua found those definitions and the selector but no table conversion or override of `ipairs`. Under ordinary Lua the power loop contributes no entries. It can still terminate because the retry loop separately sometimes assigns1,3or5 curses. This means the zero-curses call to `add_curses` is not automatically reached in the unmodified ordinary-Lua case. [S01:11–168,205–236]

If a mod or native binding supplies numeric entries, the loop may finish with powers and zero curses. `add_curses` then returns no values at num<1, while the caller assigns its return values to `tbl,end_tbl,power_index` before appending power output. That is a conditional nil-state concern, not a demonstrated retail crash. [S09:209–251]

Other source oddities remain questions rather than defects: vanish declares target A but MAX_TARGET_NUMBER for C, and propel assigns a string to `end_tbl` before `add_generated_info`. Neither branch was executed or followed through the engine bindings. The trustworthy conclusion is narrow: actual generation and native consumption remain missing. Visible authoring intent, parser acceptance and executable behavior are separate evidence layers.
