# Comic worlds as playthrough targets

*Status: research and proposals, not accepted. Written 2026-10-10 by Claude
Code at Weibao's request ("specific alternate worlds as possible playthrough
targets, what about Invincible and other comic worlds"). Follows the
[comics wiki pilot](REPORT.md). Direct recreations would live in the private
pack; the public fork uses original versions.*

Evidence labels: **shown** = checked live through the wiki API on 2026-10-10;
**known** = general knowledge of the story, not pulled this session;
**title** = name only, not checked.

## What makes a good target

A comic world works as a playthrough when it has:

1. **A bounded snapshot.** A finished run or a single reality page, so
   canon has an edge (see [LORE_AND_CONTINUITY.md](../../worlds/LORE_AND_CONTINUITY.md#bounded-comic-comparisons)).
2. **A natural course.** Something terrible happens if you do nothing. That
   becomes the world's clock under the "natural course unless disturbed"
   rule.
3. **A rule no other world has.** Otherwise it is a reskin.
4. **Room for an unchosen person.** A role for someone who is not the
   series lead.

## Targets

### T1 Invincible (original comic #1-144)

- **Fantasy:** matter in a world where a hidden empire owns the strongest
  bodies, and the friendly hero next door is its scout.
- **Natural course (known):** the father's betrayal, then decades of war
  with the empire, then the far-future ending.
- **Signature rule:** bodies decide nearly everything, so the counter-list
  matters. The wiki's "Viltrumite Weaknesses" category (shown) lists 18
  counters: a virus, a sonic device, a kill switch, a tech suit and
  specific people. *Inferred:* a run for a normal human is about collecting
  and protecting counters, not out-punching anyone.
- **Entry eras:** before the reveal, after it, the war, the ending.
- **Paths:** the three in LORE_AND_CONTINUITY (trained body, maintained
  suit, institution) plus a counter-collector.
- **Source caveat (shown):** the Invincible fandom wiki is mostly about the
  TV show (episode codes, season and game-tie-in categories). Comic facts
  should come from the Image Comics Database, which has 145 pages under
  "Invincible Vol 1".

### Titles worth a later check (title only)

  Powers (homicide detectives).

## More to consider

- **C1 Era as a run option.** The same world, entered before, during or
  after its key event. JJK already uses this.
- **C2 One-variable worlds.** Red Son and Earth-65 change one fact. They
  are the cheapest targets: reuse a base world and flip one switch.
- **C3 A natural-course clock per world.** Sentinels, the empire's war,
  the apocalypse. It runs unless you disturb it.
- **C4 Worlds that are lies.** House of M and Age of Apocalypse know, or
  half know, that they should not exist. This connects to "readings can be
  wrong" and to memory as a resource.
- **C5 Your variants.** Peter Parker's page tags a "Spider-Army" and a
  "Galactic Alliance of Spider-Men" (shown in the pilot). Meeting yourself
  across targets is a ready mechanic.
- **C6 Time law per world.** Most Marvel realities branch when you change
  the past; Earth-6160 overwrites (shown). Each target states its own time
  rule.
- **C7 Worlds that end.** The Wastelands was destroyed by an incursion and
  its pieces were reused (shown). A world can die, and its scraps can seed
  a patchwork world.
- **C8 Rank ladders as careers.** Hero association ranks, Worm's threat
  ratings, the corps' 3,600 sectors (shown in the pilot). These map onto
  the village career ladder.
- **C9 Who owns heroes.** The state (Invincible's agency, Earth-712), a
  corporation (The Boys, 2099) or nobody (Watchmen vigilantes).
- **C10 Non-hero roles.** Civilian, henchman, cleanup crew, journalist,
  villain. Every target should offer at least one.
- **C11 Snapshot discipline.** Fandom wikis mix TV, film, game and comic
  versions. Each card names one continuity and one source.
- **C12 Tone and power ceilings.** Zombies and The Boys are grim; Tangent
  and Earth-11 are light. Earth-X and cosmic worlds break normal balance,
  so each target sets its own ceiling.

## Recommended first three (proposal)

Every target above stays reachable; this is only build order.

1. **T1 Invincible.** Already the preferred first comic audit, finite, with
   a strong clock and a clear counter-collecting role for normal people.
2. **T3 Days of Future Past.** Small, with a fixed bad end and a
   send-it-back loop that reuses Mother of Learning's loop thinking.
3. **T19 Worm.** A finished serial with a built-in power taxonomy, trigger
   origins and a city full of factions. It suits our dense-stats vision.

Cheapest experiment: **T14 Red Son style** flips one variable in an existing
world.
