# Scaling glossary and game translation

Community vocabulary, paraphrased. These are descriptions and proposed uses, not shared character ranks. Source families: [wiki tiering](https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/Tiering_System), [wiki glossary](https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/VS_Battles_Glossary), [WWW hierarchy discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/whowouldwin/comments/1impbv5/the_www_feat_hierarchy_and_why_you_should_read_it/), [PowerScaling disagreement](https://www.reddit.com/r/PowerScaling/comments/1rrs89g/dimensional_scaling_is_dumb/). Wiki access is excerpt-only; see [limits](SOURCES.md).

| Code / term | Meaning and recurrent failure | Proposed game use |
|---|---|---|
| G01 Street / building / city / planet | Shorthand for an effect's demonstrated scale. Defeating a city-destroyer does not establish that every attack destroys cities. | Notebook describes a specific feat and affected place, without promoting the actor's whole build. |
| G02 Universal / multiversal | Affecting one universe or several independent continuums; distinguish space from its full history. A pocket called a universe needs context. | Record named histories affected and the actual operation: enter, protect, alter, create or destroy. |
| G03 Dimensional scaling | Some frameworks rank effects on higher-dimensional structures. Another realm named a dimension may have no extra spatial axis. | Let extra axes permit a specific contact or navigation behavior; author the advantage rather than multiplying stats. |
| G04 Hyperversal / outerversal | Framework-specific labels for dimensional or qualitative hierarchies beyond ordinary multiverses. Not scientific units. | Rival scholarly schools use disputed terms. Actual local law defines whether anyone can touch the described being. |
| G05 Feat | Something actually accomplished, with version and circumstances. Missing context changes its meaning. | Record observer, target, equipment, fuel, assistance and aftermath. |
| G06 Statement | Narrator, authority or character assertion. Lies, metaphor, translations and limited knowledge differ. | Show speaker and confidence. A reliable teacher can explain an unperformed ability without forcing a destructive demonstration. |
| G07 Anti-feat | A failure or apparent low performance. Injuries, restraint and counters can explain it. | Preserve failed predictions and investigate their causes. Do not silently shrink an ability to match one failure. |
| G08 Outlier | An exceptional result outside typical portrayal; may be a one-off boost or inconsistency. | Preserve the event and conditions; do not grant an automatically repeatable action. |
| G09 Attack potency / destructive capacity | Ability to harm a target versus how much surroundings an attack destroys. | A precise curse harms an individual while leaving the workshop intact; a blast ruins it. |
| G10 Hax versus stats | Abilities such as binding, memory editing or time control can bypass ordinary contest variables. | Resolve contact, eligibility, method and counter before physical endurance. |
| G11 Durability negation | An effect bypasses a particular defense, not necessarily every defense. | Armor fails against one soul attack; a soul ward may still work. |
| G12 Resistance / immunity | Protection against a specified operation under stated conditions. | Track resisted mechanism, source and scope. Fire protection does not imply mind protection. |
| G13 Speed | Travel, perception, reaction, attack and activation speeds differ. | Crossing a world instantly may still require an interruptible ritual. |
| G14 Infinite / immeasurable speed | Community labels involving endless distance or nonstandard time. They do not settle turn order by themselves. | Explicit local clock and activation ordering; teleportation is a route, not reflex mastery. |
| G15 Range versus reach | Distance versus legitimate access to a target across layers or barriers. | A curse reaches marked souls across a cluster but cannot find an unmarked stranger next door. |
| G16 Scaling chains | A beat B, who beat C, therefore A exceeds C. Matchups are not generally transitive. | Scholars can make this mistake; actual resolutions retain source, preparation and counter differences. |
| G17 No-limits fallacy | Treating an untested ceiling as proof that an ability has no ceiling. | Unknown is unknown. Preserve explicitly established infinity; do not invent a finite cap to simplify balance. |
| G18 Plot armor | Perceived author favoritism, not an automatically transferable faculty. | A selected fate gift needs an actual source and effect; player status alone supplies none. |
| G19 Composite character | Mixing powers across versions or continuities. | Freeze era, edition and body state; any original composite needs an explicit adaptation boundary. |
| G20 Avatar versus true body | A manifestation may have different powers, exposure and defeat consequences. | Show whether destroying the avatar harms its remote source. |
| G21 Reality warping / authority | Altering a rule or invoking permission is not identical to energy output. | A world owner's license works on its own domain, unless a broader source is established. |
| G22 Preparation / bloodlust / equalization | Matchup assumptions alter knowledge, conduct and system compatibility. | A board labels its hypothetical conditions; rumors cannot silently equalize real world laws. |
| G23 Conceptual existence | An entity or power operates through a defined abstract mechanism. The word concept alone proves little. | Author how identity is anchored and which actions can affect it. |
| G24 Infinity / cardinal / ordinal | Endless extent, number of elements and order type are distinct notions. | Use authored topology or sequence puzzles, never aleph-number damage ratings. [Infinity](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2026/entries/infinity/). |

## Reading the familiar tier ladder

Broad vocabulary moves from local structures through celestial bodies and universes to multiverses, then framework-specific dimensional and ontological categories. In the indexed VSBW descriptions, 2-C uses a small finite group of continuums, 2-B a larger finite group, and 2-A a countably infinite group. The current indexed 1-A description uses qualitative superiority; older blogs and other sites may use different definitions. This is a dated index read, not a verified revision archive. [Tiering system](https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/Tiering_System).

For YAIR, retain the **question** each label asks: what changed, how many histories, through which mechanism? Discard the automatic inference that the next label defeats every previous one.

## Common argument failures to turn into investigations

| Code | Mistake | Useful player investigation |
|---|---|---|
| F6 | The word dimension becomes an infinite power jump. | Determine whether it means another place, an extra axis or a different rule domain. |
| F7 | Creation, survival and destruction are treated as equivalent. | Establish the exact mechanism of each alleged feat. |
| F8 | One defense protects against every unfamiliar effect. | Compare the attack's contact route with the actual ward. |
| F9 | Borrowed power and rare equipment become baseline strength. | Find the patron, consumed artifact or preparation site. |
| F10 | A fight result becomes a total ordering. | Reconstruct knowledge, terrain, intent and cooperation. |
| F11 | An enormous statement crowds out contradictory evidence. | Preserve competing observations until context resolves them. |
| F12 | Physical equations are imported selectively into magical feats. | Use a local reproducible rule or a labeled approximation, not false precision. |
