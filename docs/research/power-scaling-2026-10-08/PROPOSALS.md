# Eight ranked proposals for the north star

All O proposals, ranked by value to ordinary play, compatibility with local ladders and size of the first proof. This is a reference inventory, not eight actions to implement. No shared owner is changed. Sources and boundaries are in [SOURCES](SOURCES.md); broader structure in [COSMOLOGY](COSMOLOGY.md).

| Rank / code | Proposal and player choice | Dependency / cost | Observable acceptance gate if selected |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 / O1 | **Feat notebook.** Record an event, observer, version, source, target and conditions; compare a claim with later evidence. | Attributed knowledge plus event history; actual Notebook bindings need implementation review. Keep hidden author truth out of player records. | Witnessed feat, hearsay and failed prediction remain different entries; correcting a claim changes a real available action. |
| 2 / O2 | **Hub arguments with consequences.** Publish, correct, ignore or investigate an NPC rating. Boards are institutions with agendas and fallible methods. | One author, one board audience and one downstream job consumer. Social influence must not write combat strength. | A sensational claim attracts a client; a documented correction changes that client's request while the actor's powers stay the same. |
| 3 / O3 | **Cross-world capability dossier.** Research how one faculty finds, contacts and affects a target, and what supplies it. | Actual ability source and one explicit edge/destination rule. Higher authoring burden than metadata alone. | A known independent faculty retains strength; one dependent faculty fails for a disclosed missing source; no scalar franchise conversion is consulted. |
| 4 / O4 | **Outgrow obstacles, keep a home.** Show familiar tasks becoming easy and open voluntary teaching, workshop or stewardship projects. | Milestone effects, recurring characters and useful work. Avoid upkeep that forces travel back. | A once-dangerous obstacle becomes easy; returning home offers a new choice and preserves a prior relationship. |
| 5 / O5 | **Barrier field science.** Measure one boundary, track attributed forecasts and choose repair, study or passage. | Edge-local filters, time and leakage consumers. Do not imply a running closed-browser disaster policy. | Two edges react differently; one measured change affects a visible local service or passage; observation is distinct from causation. |
| 6 / O6 | **Precision authority contests.** A powerful being can govern one domain; craft, diplomacy or research supplies another route. | Source-qualified effect order, contact and actual protections. New resolution behavior likely required. | A precise operation bypasses one relevant defense but fails against a different protection for an authored reason; raw strength still resolves a suitable physical obstacle. |
| 7 / O7 | **Infinite Archive as a bounded expedition.** Request a coordinate, retrieve one useful method or rescue one inhabitant, and preserve the relationship. | Finite authored expedition, indexing rules, cargo and anchors. Infinite background is not infinite simulation. | Three selected destinations are coherent; losing an index blocks retrieval; no repeated portal reward duplication. |
| 8 / O8 | **Stewardship beyond one world.** Fund passage workers, teach a portable principle or repair a damaged shared service rather than chase another enemy rank. | Multiple persistent regions, beneficiaries, time and resources. Far outside the first proof. | A real distant consumer benefits, retains its own choices and history, and does not become a stat extension of the player. |

Suggested first packet: O1 with one O2 consumer, the seal-maker example in COSMOLOGY. Before implementation, the assigned integrator must inspect live Notebook, attributed-knowledge, event-feed and command consumers. This research does not certify those interfaces, reserve their files or reopen another implementation lane.

The later proposals preserve real overwhelming powers and local asymmetry. They add types of work, not a mandatory counter to every ability. No proposal changes prestige, death or retention by implication. Gameplay, migration and fresh-origin save/reload/import gates belong to a subsequently selected implementation.
