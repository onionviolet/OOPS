# Proposed cosmology: larger scope, distinct rules

All O proposals. This preserves the accepted direction in [north star progression](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md#progression), [world cards](../../WORLD_CARDS.md#shared-rules-across-all-worlds) and [GM shared design](../../GAME_MASTER_PROMPT.md#make-worlds-change-decisions): no conversion of local power trees into a universal character rank. PROJECT.md itself does not currently spell out that sentence, despite the assignment's attribution; the live linked owners do. No owner is edited here.

## Four scopes, several relationships

| Code / scope | What it describes | What the player might grow able to do | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 World | A persistent history and authored rule domain; may contain planets, dreams and pockets. | Live, master a craft, gain a local faculty, protect a region, possibly change a world law. | Every world is one planet, one rank or one difficulty. |
| C2 Cluster | Worlds joined by known routes and some common infrastructure or authority. | Maintain anchors, travel, trade compatible fuels, shelter refugees, create a route. | The connector controls all inhabitants or ranks their powers correctly. |
| C3 Multiverse | A specified collection of histories or clusters, finite or infinite according to its own cosmology. | Act across several histories, protect a network, transmit a method, contest a shared source. | Visiting it supplies an attack affecting every member. |
| C4 Between multiverses | Connections between otherwise independent cosmologies with different time, identity and power assumptions. | Discover a coordinate language, translate one operation, broker a passage or construct an original domain. | A final omniverse score, all possibilities being reachable, or every rule agreeing. |

Pockets use **inside**, branches use **diverged from**, dreams use **overlaps**, routes use **connects**, and rule-making domains use **governs this operation**. Those relations are not interchangeable. A tiny pocket can host an overwhelming entity; a huge multiverse can contain ordinary farmers. A rule-maker may lack influence outside its domain.

The [multiverse owner](../../worlds/MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES.md) already separates cosmology, represented content and persistence. Keep that separation: a claim about infinite worlds does not require enumerating them or simulating infinite actors. Only authored sites and actual consumers receive resolved gameplay effects. A cosmic operation outside represented scope can be clearly authored background, or unavailable until a bounded resolution exists. Do not hide invented simulation behind a grand description.

## Fit to the current cards

| Card | Proposed cosmic connection that preserves its identity |
|---|---|
| Frontier | A barrier observatory starts with weather, interrupted trade and one visiting scholar. Ordinary skills remain native; cosmic awareness is optional. |
| Crucible | Concepts keep their separate stage and tier axes. Its candidate outer connections do not turn Tier 0 into a franchise comparison score. |
| Gu Wilds | Passage depends on actual Gu, fuel, custody and local authorization. An aperture is a nested domain, not automatically a separate universe. |

| Dreaming | Dream access, body access, Aspect and Flaw stay distinct. Dream knowledge alone grants no transport ability. |
| Paradise | The hub can run routes, contracts and disputed boards without owning the destination's laws or being the strongest cosmology. |
| Bay City | A precise imported faculty meets specific local physiology, sensing and defenses. Public fame is separate from actual reach. |

Cards remain proposals. Their memory-qualified book claims are not independently certified by this matrix. The Frontier's blanket imported-power weakening and the north star's ascension rank-reset idea are design tensions with functional transfer. Proposed resolution: apply only an authored edge filter or destination requirement. Preserve an independently supported faculty at its actual strength. Do not nerf arrivals or inflate locals merely to restore challenge.

## Infinity without fake mathematics

**Mathematical facts.** Countable infinity can be numbered like the integers. The real numbers are uncountable. Cantor's power-set result gives a strictly greater cardinality than any given set; there is no largest set cardinal. Adding finitely many elements to an infinite countable set leaves its cardinality unchanged, although placement can change its order type. [Infinity](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2026/entries/infinity/).

The continuum has size 2^aleph_0; identifying it with aleph_1 is the continuum hypothesis, not an unconditional fact of standard set theory. Finite-dimensional real spaces R and R^n have equal cardinality for positive finite n even though their geometric dimensions differ. More axes therefore do not mathematically prove more energy or combat strength. [Basic set theory](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/set-theory/basic-set-theory.html), [continuum hypothesis](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2019/entries/continuum-hypothesis/).

**Fiction conventions.** A setting may decree that each higher layer governs lower ones, or that a creator can revise a realm's rules. That is a declared causal relationship, not a theorem about set size. Outerversal is community terminology, not a unit in mathematics. [Wiki tiering](https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/Tiering_System), [FAQ](https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/Tiering_System_FAQ).

| Code / original setting | Player decision and limit | Why the infinity matters |
|---|---|---|
| C5 Numbered Archive | Select a finite set of numbered shelves using a reliable index; a lost coordinate stops retrieval. | Countably endless holdings make selection and indexing more useful than brute-force browsing. |
| C6 Continuum Coast | Choose a specified coordinate in a continuous family of histories; the route's accuracy determines destination. | A numbered tour cannot enumerate every point. Only selected histories are authored and visitable. |
| C7 Ordered Stair | A local ritual reaches a successor landing; a declared limit passage reaches the far boundary. | Order and route operations matter even where collections share a cardinality. No literal completion of infinitely many clicks is required. |
| C8 Rule Foundry | Negotiate permission to change one named operation in one domain, then test it. | Qualitative authority changes what actions mean; it does not give universal control. |
| C9 Axiom Gardens | Another cluster supports a different account of identity or continuity. Carry one object through an explicit compatibility experiment. | Different rules create research and choices without pretending they are a larger damage number. |

A concept route could progress locally from cutting material to separating a mark, a tether or an authored boundary. Each adds a function with defined eligible targets, rather than promoting the player to universal Law Tier 4. Frontier need not support that route at all. The original Skill/Technique/Intent/Concept/Law/Origin proposal and ANC's own stages/tiers are different systems; neither is imposed on every card.

## From local mastery to cosmic play

Proposed pacing: establish an ordinary life and one local method; show a known obstacle becoming easy; discover a route or condition that method cannot solve; earn new access through research, alliances or a genuine faculty; make a lasting choice in the new scope; return to people who remember the old life. This is a sequence of proof and consequence, not compulsory linear ascension.

The tempting failures are repeated rank resets, old companions becoming disposable, resurrection treating counterparts as interchangeable, infinite resource farms with no custody rules, and instant omniscient solutions. Keep the source's real power intact. Stakes can instead involve several simultaneous needs, someone refusing help, uncertain target identity, a severed route or a home that requires care. When a power really resolves a problem, allow the victory. Do not invent a counter on every use.

After multiversal reach, the player can choose stewardship, teaching, creation, exploration or rest. A god repairing a friend's home is still using earned power. Severe threats can remain fixed and unfair; this research changes neither native unconsciousness nor death, offline loss or save-retention policy.

## Smallest proposed proof

One Frontier witness saw a visitor stop a flood using an acquired seal. A hub scholar claims the visitor can suppress every world. The player checks the report, learns the seal works only on a named river authority, and can sell the sensational rumor, correct the board or help the seal-maker earn a commission. A later client reacts to the attributed public claim; the actual seal never gains power from the board's rating. This tests evidence, reputation and local authority in one realm. It is a hypothetical walkthrough, not runtime evidence.

## Cheat descriptors and absolute words

The north star's Knack/Gift/Golden finger/Fate-class/Origin-class labels are proposed descriptions of a gift's scope, not a universal combat order. Its later human direction explicitly allows genuinely drawback-free, overpowered cheats. Preserve that permission: a perfect-memory gift need not secretly consume lifespan, attract a compulsory hunter or acquire a balancing flaw.

Use Works/Reread/Rechannel/Weakened/Blocked only where a world card and a particular ability actually establish that mode. Some neighbors simply support the same method. A genuinely absolute navigation gift may solve the archive's route problem immediately; offer another pursuit instead of quietly disabling it.

The north star's suggestion that the higher tier wins when absolutes collide is a design question, not our resolution rule. Proposed alternative: first state the domains and activation conditions. If both absolute claims really apply to the same event, the local author must declare precedence, a narrower intended meaning or an explicit unresolved/impossible interaction. Do not invent a universal Knack-to-Origin victory table. A local story may select such a hierarchy, but that decision has no automatic authority in another world.
