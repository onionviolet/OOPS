# Twenty-four small touches

Original O ideas, not book facts or implemented behavior. Proposed surfaces are descriptive; no present API support is claimed. Most can enrich one home region before any cosmic travel. Inventory exemption permits more than five rows.

| Code | Touch | Player-facing consequence / proposed surface |
|---|---|---|
| T01 | A tavern board rates a carpenter above a visiting hero because it ranks useful work. | Reveal the board's criterion before accepting its advice. |
| T02 | The same scholar publishes separate rankings for duels, rescues and repairs. | Match a specialist to the actual job. |
| T03 | An old claim gains a correction in different handwriting. | Follow the corrector's witness lead. |
| T04 | Witnesses agree a wall vanished but disagree whether it was moved or destroyed. | Investigation opens different salvage choices. |
| T05 | A supposed limitless healer declines to treat a soul wound. | Learn the faculty's real target class. |
| T06 | A borrowed relic has its lender's name in the legend entry. | Remember a debt without making it a universal curse. |
| T07 | A saint's avatar buys a chair sized for its actual body. | Ordinary furnishings reflect embodied identity. |
| T08 | A god waits for a local baker to finish a favorite loaf. | Give the baker agency and a recurring appointment. |
| T09 | The first old enemy becomes effortless to defeat. | Let progress visibly pay off without forced enemy scaling. |
| T10 | That enemy's child asks for a lesson rather than a rematch. | A remembered outcome becomes a teaching choice. |
| T11 | A fragile souvenir survives travel because someone packed it well. | Ordinary craft contributes to an extraordinary journey. |
| T12 | A border fish develops one unfamiliar trait before scholars agree on the cause. | A small field observation precedes cosmic exposition. |
| T13 | Two clocks disagree near a passage. | Record measurements instead of assuming a universal ratio. |
| T14 | A surveyor's forecast includes yesterday's failed prediction. | Confidence becomes inspectable and revisable. |
| T15 | A smuggler sells coordinates that reach a place but provide no return anchor. | Read the actual service terms. |
| T16 | An endless library has a very small staffed help desk. | A librarian's expertise beats an unstructured search. |
| T17 | A numbered shelf preserves a letter from your first teacher. | Infinite scenery retains a personal point of attachment. |
| T18 | A continuous-history map can locate one city but not an exact door. | Pay or research for greater coordinate precision. |
| T19 | Two counterparts dislike being introduced as copies. | Distinct histories shape distinct relationships. |
| T20 | A returning traveler remembers a joke the local counterpart never heard. | Learn a branch through lived conversation. |
| T21 | A conceptual weapon can sever a tracking mark while leaving clothes intact. | Preview the selected target and consequence. |
| T22 | A historian asks whether the feat occurred before or after acquiring a patron. | Version boundaries affect which claim is trustworthy. |
| T23 | A world-scale protector still asks workers where they want the new bridge. | Power enables construction; locals choose its use. |
| T24 | After a cosmic victory, home has a warm meal and one unfinished promise. | Let the player choose ordinary life without losing earned strength. |
