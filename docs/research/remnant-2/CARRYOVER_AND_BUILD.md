Accepted YAIRPG OD2 already keeps memory and the base cheat across lives and makes other carriers depend on world and means. Do not replace it with Remnant's equipment portability. A technique, source energy, body, artifact, legal credential and another person's trust need separate applicability checks. Nothing here proves Remnant implements that broader metaphysical model.

R2-S01..R2-S03 are source-discovery labels only. No hidden faculty unlock instructions or item routes are published in this dossier.
