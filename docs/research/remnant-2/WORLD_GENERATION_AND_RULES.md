# World generation and authored differences

Evidence codes resolve in SOURCE_LEDGER. SHOWN below is a bounded static read, never native play.

## Generation layers

| Layer | Read evidence | Limit / YAIRPG implication |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign | B3 has three generic world subquests plus direct Labyrinth and Root Earth references; NumWorldBossesDefeated carries SaveGame metadata in bytecode property references | Parent campaign progress and local visit state are different owners. Complete native generator not decoded. |
| Adventure | B4 subquest filters are Story+Fae, Story+Jungle, Story+Nerud | Supports world-targeted story selection as structure. No verified weights or eligibility evaluator. |
| Story | Index exact-name pattern finds 11 story definitions, including DLC-era paths; examples span Fae, Jungle, Nerud, Labyrinth and DLC folders | This is a filename match, not 11 playable current outcomes. DLC payload is not entitlement. |
| Tile / connector | Quest components include RemnantQuestTile, zone links, subquests and waypoints; B5 tile sets contain dimensions, transition tags, spawn tags and music references | Separate authored required roles from optional inserts. Do not infer connectivity success or solver rules. |
| Side content | Exact directory/name patterns find 35 injectable definitions, 23 side-dungeon definitions and 9 boss definitions | Lower-bound naming signals only. Other conventions and miniboss families are outside those counts. |
| Rewards | SpawnTable, SpawnTableItem, account-award and reward-call exports exist | Several item/default records fail parsing. No accepted chance, duplicate conversion or pity formula. |

B3 function trace: `IsWorldValid` gets the subquest and applies the engine's object-validity check. `ActivateFirstValidWorld` calls it for World1 at instruction 0, branches at 32 to World2 at 74, then World3 at 148. Successful calls activate the matching waypoint and jump to return at 338; all invalid reaches a diagnostic at 222. This is a checked activation fallback, not proof of weighted world order.
