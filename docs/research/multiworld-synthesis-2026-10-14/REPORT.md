# Multi-world synthesis and audit

Different places become different play when local rules change a useful action, its cost, the means it needs or its consequence. A traveler can retain learning while a destination changes its usable form, fuel, equipment support or social authority. No sampled source proves the whole YAIRPG combination of careers, persistent residents, local power systems and changed revisits. Combine specific mechanisms through existing owners rather than adopting one game's cosmology.

## What to combine, and what must stay distinct

| Code | Synthesis inference | Existing owner / packet |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Start with one local verb whose legality or outcome differs for a reason. Noncombat work and careers count as much as combat. Author means and useful alternatives, then connect another place when the current slice closes. | K2/K3/K8/K13, P14/P03/P01; MWA-P01..03. |
| D2 | Retention needs named carriers: self/body/control, memory/method, source/fuel, goods/custody, credentials/recognition, debts/promises and allies. A source can transform one and retain another. Observation/contact/pass-through are different routes. | K6/K7/K9/K15/K22/K29, nested-world owner, P05/P07; MWA-P05/06. |
| D3 | Keep same-instance revisit separate from newly generated/parallel instance. Do not replay initial templates over spent stock, resident memory, settled reward or story outcome. Seed plus recipe revision supports new content, not reconstruction of lived state. | K12/K17/K18/K33, OD5, integration map IR1/IR4, P12/P15/P16/P24. |
| D4 | Show player-known context and usable next action beside retained growth; details can expand through existing center navigation. Origin, custody and usability remain separate labels. A bank meter is not earned-work evidence. | K11/K14/K30/K34, UI_RULES, P01/P04/P13; MWA-P07/08. |
| D5 | Authored definitions, native mutable owners and rendered text have different lifetimes. Graphs/templates suggest structure; effects must enter the existing staged native publisher. The research selects no shared schema, new global scheduler or profile migration. | K1/K18/K33/K34, integration map IR1/IR2/IR4, P16/P22/P23/P24; MWA-P09. |

Relationships are the weakest shared reference. The Strange gives jurisdiction limits, MUDs give faction/credit contracts, KH gives local cast/episode selection, and Siralim gives patrons. These do not establish cross-world personal relationships, recognition under a changed body, companion consent or retained property. YAIRPG should use its existing person/knowledge/custody owners and prove one changed useful return, rather than cite these games as a finished social solution.
