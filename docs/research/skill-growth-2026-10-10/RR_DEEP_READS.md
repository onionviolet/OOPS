# C1: Royal Road growth deep reads

Date: 2026-10-10. Status: completed bounded C1 audit, with source gaps stated per book. Research only, no gameplay changes.

## Method and evidence

The assigned route is FanFicFare 4.62.0, one process at a time. The existing Royal Road configuration sets a six-second delay with the client's random variation. Raw EPUBs, metadata and the download ledger stay in the gitignored `docs/research/novels/skill-growth-2026-10-10/sources/` folder. The session cap is about 300 chapter downloads. Existing local files are reused.

READ means chapter text inspected in this run. STATED means a blurb, metadata, glossary claim about unseen events, or an earlier audit being summarized, with that boundary named. MEMORY means unverified recall. No earlier audit's READ label becomes this run's READ. Proposals are explicitly separated from evidence. Codes use the merged G1 to G40, L1 to L23 and S1 to S14 in [SKILLS_AND_TALENTS.md](../../SKILLS_AND_TALENTS.md). Its merged numbering supersedes the independent numbering in the two earlier audits.

Executed sampling: twelve early public entries, six to twelve middle entries and six to twelve latest entries for long works, plus a relevant glossary/index when outside the bands. The total is 274 saved public entries, including notices and side stories, plus a conservative twelve-entry reserve for the interrupted initial attempt: 286 against the approximately 300-entry budget. An EPUB is saved per band; none of the completed on-disk bands was refetched. Automatic retries were disabled after the interrupted attempt. Rift-Runner was read in full through all 54 public chapters by the three named readers, with root integration and consequential spot-checks. Public ordinal positions are not necessarily the chapter numbers printed by the author. Samples establish their own range, not complete publication history or a universal level ceiling.
