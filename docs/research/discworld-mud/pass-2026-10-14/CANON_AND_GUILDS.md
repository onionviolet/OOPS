# Canon and guilds

## F20: What “living book world” means in the evidence

| Evidence | What it supports | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| [Wikipedia full article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discworld_MUD), revision 1368383791 | Stated: an expanding fan world, mixed chronology and play-driven departures. Its canon section has an explicit sourcing warning. | Formal licensing; a unified evolving book timeline. |
| [Founder interview, archived 2010 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20100924002936/http://www.armchairarcade.com/neo/node/364) | Stated by designer: place, mood, detailed interactions and social organizations are central. | Current state or every NPC's behavior. |
| [Karlsen, 2008](https://gamestudies.org/0801/articles/karlsen) | Historical observed quest conversations involving Bethan and Cohen's household. Some events reenact books. Puzzle obscurity is a documented weakness. | Current quest routes or a globally advancing chronology. |
| [PC Gamer tour, reissued 2022](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/saturday-crapshoot-discworld-mud/) | Historical observations: ambient routines, recognizable NPCs and retained Holy Wood. Continued access can outweigh a novel's final state. | That today's server uses the same rooms or routines. |
| [Official features](https://discworld.starturtle.net/lpc/about/features.html) | Stated: player-run businesses, newspapers and councils; different magical professions; object interaction. | Player choices rewriting every source event. |
| [Official AshkEnte announcement](https://discworld.starturtle.net/lpc/blog/blog.c?action=filter&blog=recent%20developments&id=422) | A 2012 mechanical expansion is introduced through in-world discovery and research. | A replay of a specific novel's plot or the complete modern implementation. |

**Inference:** distinguish source scenes, authored game events and player history. A novel event can become a local interactive scene; a game patch can arrive as a discovery; a guild election creates game history. They can coexist without sharing one chronology. For YAIRPG, P2 proposes explicit start-era and divergence state. That is a proposed authoring method, not a recovered Discworld engine.

No franchise licence document was located. The historical account of permission to continue is not a reusable licence. No recommendation here depends on copying Discworld prose, code or assets. The README's noncommercial code restriction is recorded separately in TOOLING.md.

## F22: Archived Thieves' Guild, stated by players

[2026-08-18 snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20260818154834/https://dwwiki.mooo.com/wiki/Thieves%27_Guild), page revision 75199, describes a licence quota, a 12-hour played-time accounting cycle and a guild share of theft. It reports fines for exceeding quota, falling short and overdue payment, with NPC enforcers. The page also separates commands exclusive to thieves from shared ones. Leaders named on the snapshot are historical and were not checked live.

The lesson is two-sided. An object can serve as an account statement and professional identity; obligations give institutions teeth. But enforced minimum activity creates chores. P5 proposes visible voluntary contracts and an NPC-backed solo path, rather than importing this punishment scheme. No modern quota implementation appears in the cut-down public source.

## F30: Recovered priest and witch pages, stated by players

[Priests, 2026-08-19 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20260819183350/https://dwwiki.mooo.com/wiki/Priests%27_Guild), read in full, describes a high-priest election initiated by a schism, with followers and priests voting. The office can excommunicate and appoint ministers with chest access. Fourteen primaries cover the faith family. That is a specific form of political authority, not proof every guild replaces a canon leader.

[Witches, 2026-09-21 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20260921083806/https://dwwiki.mooo.com/wiki/Witches%27_Guild), read in full including its cottage/service and spell tables, says Granny retains authority and there is no elected player hierarchy. It describes social trials, brewing as income, novice transport, later broom travel, learned spells, invented tricks and portable imbuements. Skill level, spatial services and social performance are different kinds of progress. The page states a female-character joining requirement and routes to change character gender. That is a source rule, not a restriction proposed for YAIRPG. Several command-acquisition details are marked as uncertain; listed commands do not prove their full implementation.
