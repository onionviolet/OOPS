# Citizen Sleeper: assigned source pass

**Inferred synthesis:** Citizen Sleeper makes bodily dependence part of choosing how to spend a day. Work, food, medical access, friendships and pursuit overlap. Its strongest contribution to a construct start is that repairing the body and becoming free are different projects.

**F1, seen in text:** the shipped guide describes one segment of condition decay per cycle, two segments of energy loss and additional danger from starvation. This artificial body still needs food. Sabine describes biosynthetic organ rejection and corporate-controlled stabilizer; Riko and Hypha offer another social/material context for medicine. This does not support a universal “maintenance replaces food” model.

**F3/F6, seen in text:** worse condition means fewer newly rolled action dice. A chosen die receives a skill modifier; cloud extraction instead uses die matching. Action clocks fill from work, while cycle clocks advance once per cycle. The guide says action clocks sit below their actions and cycle clocks appear on location icons. These are two different causes of progress, not two names for a timer.

**F7/F9/F10, seen in text:** a breakdown leaves a broken skill that needs a separate upgrade repair. Personhood, medical access and corporate retrieval remain distinct. Feng’s tracker solution falsifies the company’s assessment of the body, connecting freedom to what the pursuer believes. None of these narrative accounts proves a fully simulated society.

The unbiased limits matter. The source has authored branch restrictions, including a Purge commitment that closes unfinished drives. A dated individual player comment also criticizes the original’s eventual ease; this is reported experience, not a current balance finding. [Player discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2442460/discussions/0/600769217628750565/). Manual cycle advancement is a poor direct fit for unattended idle time unless a YAIR world defines an adaptation. No offline policy was established here.

Six ranked original options are in [IMPLEMENTATION_PACKETS.md](IMPLEMENTATION_PACKETS.md), with 24 smaller options in [SMALL_TOUCHES.md](SMALL_TOUCHES.md). They cover one optional body, progress versus pressure, alternative repair access, personhood/claims, safe routines and a bounded dice experiment. They are proposals, not accepted decisions.
