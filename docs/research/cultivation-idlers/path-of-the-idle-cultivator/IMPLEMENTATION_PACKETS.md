## P1: An attempt can succeed at a lower grade

Owning docs: [cultivation paths](../../../RESEARCH_CULTIVATION_PATHS.md), [engine](../../../ENGINE_EXPANSION.md). Candidate seam: existing staged practice in `src/techniques.js`, with world-specific costs and consequences.

Smallest playable result: one earned cultivation attempt has three visible pressure steps. A player can settle the current outcome or continue; exhausting trial capacity resolves the earned low grade. Distinguish cancelled, graded completion and bodily death. Do not select Path's numerical tuning or impose a universal lightning trial.

Save the committed attempt, costs, current pressure, result grade and resolved state. Confirmation applies the saved result once, even after reload. A future rule may retain ordinary HP, use trial capacity or allow lethal stakes, but must state that choice before commitment. Repeat attempts remain reachable when their causal resource/time requirements are met.

Acceptance: a fresh rebuilt 1440x900 desktop origin shows settle/continue/refuse reasons; exercise a low and high grade, cancel, reload while awaiting confirmation, and Save/reload/Export/literal Import. Costs and gains publish once. No reroll or accidental life reset. Human grade/pressure feel remains an acceptance gate.
