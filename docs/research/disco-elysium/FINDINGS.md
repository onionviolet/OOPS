## Contradictions, costs and weak spots

C1 **Inferred from F9 and the current north star:** a strict rule that everything displayed is something the current body knows would remove Disco's literary cutaways. YAIR could allow that presentation in a guide mode without granting the character actionable knowledge. This is an option, not a new rule.

C2 **Inferred from F4–F5:** “failure as content” is not equivalent to “every failure rewards progress.” A retry gate and a lasting fork create different decisions; either may frustrate a player. F20–F21 establish concrete authored costs and consequences.

C3 **Seen in text (F6–F7):** slot scarcity and initially hidden results are intentional parts of the thought design. **Inferred:** copying these into YAIR can create surprise and build curation, but can also make research feel like an uninformed commitment. Do not import the scarcity as a global limit.

C4 **Seen in text (F8–F11):** better faculties do not become neutral truth narrators. **Inferred:** their success labels must not be treated as confirmation that their advice is correct.

C5 **Seen in text:** the developer explicitly marks some examples as invented or pre-release, including the hand-stabbing illustration in S2 and “Soul Reaver” in S1's thought post. Those examples are not installed features.

C6 **Inferred from F12:** handcrafted scene quality has authoring cost. A source pass cannot establish that generating many generic voice lines reproduces it.

Player criticism **seen in text**: a [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/632470/discussions/0/3758852249522256931/?ctp=2&l=english) includes frustration with high-probability failures and white-check retry spending. Other participants defend the failures or describe interesting failure branches. These are individual reports, not measured consensus or verified bugs. No community claim is used as technical implementation evidence.

C7 **Seen in text:** the 2019 thought post describes 53 thoughts; the current [Final Cut store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/632470/Disco_Elysium__The_Final_Cut/) advertises 60. These source descriptions differ. Neither number establishes the installed table's count. The store also describes full English voice acting and political vision quests; those are Final Cut claims, not proof that the old developer design matches the installed build.

## Identity qualification

F13 **Inferred:** the north star's proposal that amnesia hides skill names is not evidence that Disco does so. Installed F22–F24 below establish a selective memory-loss presentation, not a procedural rebirth or a transfer-between-lives system. No such system was recovered in this targeted pass.

## Installed contradictions and limits

C8 **Seen in data or code:** dialogue item `timeLeft` and project `researchTime` disagree. Examples: Precarious World 300 versus 240; Actual Art Degree 240 versus 90; Waste Land of Reality 720 versus 1200; Hobocop 180 versus 260. Item description/bonus text also differs from serialized effects. **Inferred:** item fields contain stale design material and are unsafe as the sole balance source. The serialized component is stronger configuration evidence, but actual runtime initialization/overrides remain unplayed.

C9 **Seen in data or code:** both recovered thought sources contain 53 records, despite the store's 60-thought claim. This discrepancy does not prove the game lacks every other possible hidden record, nor a player-facing bug. No additional seven projects were recovered in the scanned asset-file headers.

C10 **Inferred from F22–F24:** Disco's start is an authored person's selective loss of memory with surviving competence, testimony and records, rather than the north star's proposal to empty all knowledge layers. It supports an amnesiac origin option, not a universal memory-loss policy. Labeled faculties and explicit numeric check data are still present in the captured sources; this pass did not demonstrate skill-name concealment in the UI.

C11 **Seen in text/data:** the physical faculty descriptions and the fan scene can give dismissive or unsafe advice about the body. **Inferred:** they dramatize character perception and are not reliable diagnostic readouts. The example belongs to the source's fiction; no real-world rule follows.

C12 **Inferred from F15/F25 and player criticism:** retry spending and residual chance can create preparation followed by disappointment. Authoring interesting failure does not remove that cost. No claim of measured player consensus, broken RNG or a current defect is made.
