# Disco Elysium small touches

At least twenty small options, mapped to native YAIR. **Every T entry is inferred and proposed**, based on the linked finding, not a claim of implementation. Installed-source cross-checks are F14–F28 in FINDINGS: private speakers/checks F14–F16, study and time F17–F19/F28, failure F20–F21/F25, identity F22–F24, preparation/text F26–F27. No copying of names, dialogue or art is proposed.
