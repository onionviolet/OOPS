# Path of Achra public demo source audit

This is a finite source reading of the **HTML5 v0.4.7 demo**, not an audit of the 2024 retail release. The inspected Godot 3.5.2 pack is SHA-256 `fca9ac051ee9c6c237619404875d2e37b8d58d605988d17008a0dd296cc71662`. Private input and token/line renderings live in `~/.codex/research/path-of-achra/`; no game payload or reconstructed source is in Git. `FINDINGS.json` gives exact line spans and SHA-256s.

The pack index has 3,234 entries. All 126 extracted `.gdc` scripts have token/line renderings with full byte consumption, but those renderings are **not buildable reconstructed GDScript**. Five connected paths were semantically read. The remaining scripts, loose data, scenes and assets are recovered or indexed, not semantically audited.

| Path | Source finding | Actual limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1 Turn clock and queue | Player, allies and enemies accrue speed toward a 100 tick threshold. Eligible units feed a queue. Pending effects are pulled before a queued action, and `add_effect` uses `push_front`, so chained effects can precede older pending effects. | `search_for_turn` has a bounded 100 iteration clock-search loop. It does not bound pending effect chains; no timing or ordering was measured in a running game. |
| F2 Invoke economy | The invoke button checks level, death and positive charge, spends one charge, calls the invoke router, then runs the queue. Router traits can enqueue buffs, bonus and healing. Event keyed recharge and `lose_charge` are distinct paths. | Numeric balance depends on per-invoke definitions and runtime state; this trace proves routing, not all invoke costs. |
| F3 Buff consequence | A queued `add_buff` reaches `ToolMagicMaker` and `event_apply_buff`. A unique repeated buff extends duration; a different existing form queues removal; fresh buffs append to target state. Trait checks enqueue consequences, including Shapeshifter healing and Priest damage. | Front insertion means removal/application timing deserves runtime inspection; a clone and event consumer were read, not every buff definition. |
| F4 Damage to heal | Damage runs resistance and trait checks, then reduces HP. FireHealing and other traits enqueue heal effects for the attacker producing the matching damage. The effect dispatcher requires living healer and target; actual healing requires missing HP, modifies amount, consumes a player heal keyed invoke charge where relevant, then clamps at maximum HP. | Exact resistance math and death aftermath are outside this path. Triggered heal can be skipped after a death or at full HP. |
| F5 Movement consequences | A legal player/unit move reaches `event_move`; waiting takes a separate branch. Movement checks Entangle/Bleed and queues typed damage scaled by buff duration, then updates residence and checks adjacent Vigilance attacks. The queued damage has a live target guard. BloodRetort and ChannelDeath producers also require a nonempty enemy list, which is distinct from an every-enemy-alive test. | No observed browser movement, swap, wait or enemy turn was tested. |

The public demo is from 2023 and predates the May 7, 2024 full retail release. The live retail executable, later balance and content, DLC status beyond the checked Steam metadata, native runtime behavior, scene wiring, and a Godot rebuild were not verified. Fourteen loose `Data/*.json` tables are available but were not fully read. The optional `game-data.json` reporting `EXISTS: false` does not mean those tables are absent; the 126 script count proves no table reading. Existing YAIR mechanics are not changed. The five original proposals in `IMPLEMENTATION_PACKETS.md` are unimplemented research options.
