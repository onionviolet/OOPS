# Nonhuman fiction research brief (2026-10-10)

Weibao: "expanding on each idea and more, looking at the relevant fictions
or book worlds around them and more to be inspired and learn from?"

Context: Yet Another Idle RPG is becoming a text life-sim across many worlds.
docs/LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md proposes nonhuman starts ("Who or what you start
as", line ~3916) and a body-by-feature-family crossing ("Every feature
family, in every kind of body", end of file). Each body is six slots:
verbs, senses, clock (time per action), needs, channel (how it reaches
people), ending.

Feature families to cross against: combat, crafting, work and money, social
and romance, family and heirs, power path, minigames, city building,
exploration, research, collections, expeditions, crime and politics,
writing and teaching, challenge runs.

## Per cluster, deliver one file in this folder

For each body type in your cluster:
1. **Sources.** 6 to 12 works (web novels, light novels, books, manga,
   anime, film, games, myth and folklore, tabletop). Include the obvious
   ones and at least two less obvious ones (literary fiction, non-English
   web fiction, folklore, indie games). Title, author or studio, medium,
   one-line premise.
2. **What each source does mechanically.** How the body's verbs, senses,
   time, needs, social channel, growth and ending actually work there.
   Concrete examples (named skills, evolution stages, rules, scenes
   summarized in your own words). Label each claim **shown** (in the work),
   **stated** (by author, wiki or review), or **inferred** (yours).
3. **What to learn for a text idle life-sim.** Steal-worthy mechanics, what
   goes wrong in those works (pacing, power creep, boredom, the body
   becoming a human in disguise too fast), and how idle/offline time fits.
4. **The feature-family crossing.** For each family, the best idea this
   body suggests, grounded in a source when possible.
5. **Adjacent bodies and twists** the sources suggest that our lists miss.

End with: the 5 strongest ideas for this cluster, open questions, and a
source list with URLs actually consulted.

## Rules

- Unbiased lens: report sources as they are, including what contradicts
  our ideas. No invented facts about works; if unsure, say so.
- Use WebSearch and WebFetch (wikis, Royal Road/NovelUpdates pages,
  reviews, TV Tropes, game wikis). Do not download books or games. No raw
  prose: paraphrase, at most one short quote per source.
- Write plain English, no em dashes. Headings and tables where they help.
- Write only your own file. Do not edit any other repo file.
