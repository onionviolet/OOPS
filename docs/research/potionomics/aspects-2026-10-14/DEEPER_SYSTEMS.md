# Deeper systems by payoff

These extend the feature questions and remain research, not selected implementation.

## DS1 / highest: Relationship reward and load repair

Shown: dialogue grants cards at stable row IDs; the native/Blueprint profile stores relationship state; a separate card library and loadout exist. `BPL_SaveLoad.GenerateUnlockedCardsFromRelationships` walks relationship records to recreate unlocks, and LoadGame contains repair branches. This suggests a useful compatibility strategy when old saves lack a newer derived unlock, but does not establish that every repair is safe or duplicate-free.

YAIRPG P03 owner: explicitly decide whether learned knowledge is authoritative or derived. Retaining a learned fact is preferable when acquisition can occur through several routes; deriving every lesson from current friendship could erase study or experimentation. Accept only a concrete old-save fixture and natural new grant that both preserve usable knowledge without duplicate payment.

## DS2 / second: Capacity and composition are different constraints

Shown: ingredient tables contain channel quantities and traits; cauldron UI separately reads ingredient count and magimin capacity. Potion data supplies tier/star bands while stability supplies possible quality modifiers. This permits substitutions to affect several distinct outcomes rather than simply passing a recipe ID check. No universal quality reducer is established.

YAIRPG P02/modular chains owner: compare one reagent across two consumers with real unit and composition readers. A tag registry is a selector, not a chemistry engine. Preserve exact batch identity where downstream consumers need it, and avoid generic averaging or inferred legacy composition.

## DS3 / third: Price and pressure have distinct continuation

Shown: price aggregates named percentage channels and rounds once; patience limits the exchange; retained stress changes future draw risk. Boundary evaluation gives stress failure precedence and supports closer success at a patience boundary. These are separate kinds of consequence and should not be collapsed into one relationship meter.

YAIRPG P19 owner: one original paid response can change price and leave a later tactical or social consequence. A later-offer reader must be implemented and proven separately; the donor's direct relationship/offer penalty remains unproved. Use current staged dialogue and host publication, not a new card system.

Other inventoried economies, adventuring, gardening, aging, shop customization and endless-mode variants remain reachable for later research. None becomes a gate for these three proposals.
