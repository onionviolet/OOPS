Unlock rows declare `ToInheritPreviousLoop=TRUE`. Visibility, guardian availability,
condition interpretation, side effects and click execution still have native
consumers. These rows establish authored gates, not a tested unlock sequence.

Greed's normal regional row 1 is always eligible. Rows 2 through 6 require
Reincarnation 2 through 6. Rows 7 through 11 additionally require matching
A8 through A12 first dungeons. A13/A14/A15 rows require Reincarnation 12/13/14
plus their first dungeon. Research purchases are further current-stock decisions.
A life counter alone is therefore insufficient to claim the player has the
benefit. Rare/Guardian rows have their own unlock and research/dungeon gates.

## A lasting lesson from a temporary answer

Shown joins: Past actions pathID 115, Astral purchases pathID 130, skills pathID
65. Two answer stages each lock their sibling, creating four local paths:
maintain formation, switch formation, collect materials, or collect research.
Question/answer spends are 40, 45, 50, 55 Love; the teaching reply spends 60.
Each step adds Love capacity. Relevant skill books then cost 900 Inspiration
and require Rank 60, the chosen answer and teaching reply.

All four books are declared inherited and grant distinct skills. `loveDissection`
uses 11 Stamina, interval 2.8, physical base parameter 37, Earth attribute, two
Collection effects and an attack effect. `loveHug` uses 11 Stamina, interval 4.2,
parameter 51, Fire attribute, two Luck effects and a defense effect. Help ties
Collection to resource drops excluding chests, and Luck to research drops,
when the ally defeats an enemy in the relevant formation. Exact runtime damage,
stacking and drop chance remain unreviewed.

Recognition is not just inferred from flavor: `A10EventL_Reply2` checks already
owned matching skill-book history and leads to `Reply3`, which grants a mastery
title at Rank 65. `Reply4` checks all four books plus earlier mastery history,
and `Reply5` grants a higher title at Rank 70. Each recognition path has its
own Love costs. Source data permits accumulating alternatives across lives;
a complete four-life route or minimum-life proof was not performed.
