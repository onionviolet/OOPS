## 1. A bounded kit reserve for a journey

**Player interaction:** At Eda's existing fitting bench, opt into keeping a small reserve of native Prepared survey kits for the next few outings. Show current owned, available kits, the chosen material route, each next batch's real costs, and a maximum of three accepted batches. Pause at four kits; consider the next batch only at two or fewer. Show batch overshoot explicitly. Completion, cancellation, departure and exhausted inputs each have an honest reason. The routine expires after its finite scope; it creates no permanent daily obligation.

**Source basis:** RW-JOB-01–03 and RW-MATERIAL-01–02. RimWorld distinguishes current eligible stock from historical production, separate pause/restart thresholds from repeat count, and reservations from consumed materials. A material/quality definition is useful only through a real output consumer. These are source observations; the kit policy, numbers and interface are original proposals.

**Reuse:** `src/feature_expansion_work_plan.js` owns finite cursors, accepted job receipts, revision checks and current-context revalidation. `src/simulation.js` owns reservations, completion support checks and refunds. `src/simulation_effects.js` exposes native item counts and stages costs. `src/reference_gameplay_content.js:61` already sets resin and binds two actual kits. `src/feature_expansion_objects_host.js:43` already excludes equipped/borrowed tool stock. Existing native storage, acquisition, publication and persistence remain the only stock/save owners.

**Actual missing consumer:** The inspected goal cursor completes permanently when its goal is met and has no lower-stock resumption policy. Its goal is currently restricted to the optional reference gameplay reader. Add one bounded stock policy to this existing owner with the integrator's compatibility decision. Count available owned kits from the current native context, normalize absent quantities to zero, and never count historical receipts, chest/NPC stock, equipped goods or borrowed goods as player supplies. Select existing crafting commands at the next commitment boundary; a stale preview cannot reserve the next batch.

**Costs/state/save:** Each batch pays the existing route's materials, stamina, time and site commitment. Reaching the stock target spends nothing. Persist policy revision, upper/lower thresholds, accepted-batch budget and cursor state, not a second inventory or copied native context. A new optional policy needs a validated additive cursor version or explicitly compatible reader. Old cursors retain their behavior and old saves remain unenrolled. Departure freezes/pauses this local plan unless the current owner expressly supplies a supported clock; no implicit offscreen crafting.

**Later acceptance:** In a rebuilt generated-save browser run, stock at/above target pays nothing; stock below restart accepts one real batch; failed support refunds only reserved costs; depleted materials pause clearly; an existing batch's output changes native kit count once. Spending/transferring a kit affects the next quote, with custody and other owners respected. ## 2. Recent friction expires; an actual promise remains

**Player interaction:** During one witnessed exchange with Iona, the existing parcel clerk, choose a brusque interruption or a considerate reply. The involved pair gets a caused record. A recent interruption temporarily closes one optional concession; waiting or a specific repair conversation can reopen it. The People view reports the witnessed exchange and later response. **Source basis:** social-01, with body-01 supplying the distinction between state, eligibility and action. RimWorld social memories retain their counterpart, feed directed opinion, can fade before expiry and affect later interaction selection. Its memory duration and insult probabilities are not YAIR balance recommendations. This packet uses a simpler authored temporary gate; it does not select a global mood/opinion simulation.

**Reuse:** `src/simulation_effects.js:185` already deduplicates caused directed relationship effects and saves bounded history. Its conditions have played-clock expiry. `src/simulation.js` supplies expiry boundaries, current response prerequisites and caused settlement. `src/people_knowledge.js:51` already projects known Iona records. The craftsman's existing irritation predicate in `src/field_workshop.js:15` is prior evidence that basic temporary irritation already exists.

**Actual missing consumer:** A new pair-specific concession must read an active temporary condition tied to the witnessed exchange, rather than treating every old memory as a permanent opinion delta or broadcasting one NPC's private state. Register one fixed-pair condition and one service predicate. Keep historical memory, active friction and outstanding obligation separate. Gradual numeric fading is an optional later extension with its own consumer, not required to implement this packet.

**Costs/state/save:** Ordinary conversation need not cost money. A proposed repair is a short conversation while both people are available; unrelated debt/payment rules stay with their current owner. Persist cause, pair, history and the ordinary condition expiry. Use existing typed records if sufficient; any extra expiry fields require additive validation/compatibility. Duplicate causes cannot farm concessions, and repetitive exchanges have an authored cap. Old memories acquire no retroactive timers or changed promises.

**Later acceptance:** Exercise the two replies, waiting and repair paths; verify only Iona's specific optional concession changes, directed attribution is correct, repeated causes are inert, history remains after expiry, and the actual parcel obligation survives. Verify conversation availability, mid-timer reload and a failed save without publishing the reply or concession. Deferred here.

## 3. A frost warning with one actual herb outcome

**Player interaction:** At the existing Village field association, explicitly establish one finite herb plot from an actually supplied planting allocation. A later frost warning offers covering it with a real log and short work, harvesting early, or leaving it alone. The report shows the observed state, decision and eventual native Cooking herbs received. This is one local plot and crop cycle.

**Source basis:** RW-STORY-01–05. RimWorld filters event candidates against current conditions, checks eligibility again at execution, registers a timed condition and consumes it through temperature into actual plant growth. ColdSnap's seasonal eligibility is distinct from cell temperature. Growth reductions are supported; universal crop death or a fixed yield loss is not. Plot, planting supply, costs, window and output are original proposals.

**Reuse:** `src/simulation.js:302` owns saved occurrences, disclosure, response requirements and settlement. `src/simulation_contracts.js` and `src/simulation_effects.js` already support world facts, scheduled effects and timed actor conditions. `src/field_workshop.js:33` supplies a concrete reported local situation and response pattern. `src/items.js:1810` defines the existing native Cooking herbs item. Inventory grants pass through the existing acquisition/publication/persistence boundary.

**Actual missing consumer:** The inspected field/simulation owners have no cultivated plot whose growth/harvest depends on an environmental state. Add one explicit plot-stage/deadline/outcome consumer. Use existing world facts or a minimal validated optional plot record; do not attach a world hazard to an invented invisible person merely to reuse actor conditions. Bind eligibility to the owning world's actual supported season/weather facts or clearly authored local trigger, rechecking at occurrence. A noticed warning is knowledge, not automatic accepted work. Covering, early harvest and expiry must converge on one final settlement.

**Costs/state/save:** Author a finite planting source and exact ownership before granting a crop; no free repeated seeds or herbs from reading a report. Proposed cover costs one real log plus time; early harvest ends growth with a lower authored output; neglect yields its stated result without consuming supplies. Preserve plot stage, branch, deadline, exposure and settlement cause. Missing records remain inert in old saves; new structured fields need an additive reader. No offline or cross-world season synchronization is inferred.

**Later acceptance:** From a generated save, establish the plot and exercise all three responses plus timeout, including an ineligible frost candidate and eligibility lost between scheduling and occurrence. Verify distinct actual herb counts, one final report/receipt, no duplicate harvest, and supported local clock behavior. Reload before warning, mid-window and after settlement; verify failed publication cannot charge supplies or grant herbs. Deferred here.
