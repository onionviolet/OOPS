## S01: Body parts become usable capacities

**SHOWN model and authoring:** BodyDef is a tree of BodyPartRecord references to BodyPartDef, with tags naming functions. PawnCapacityDef names a worker, minimum and awake restrictions. The raw census has 48 bodies, 147 part definitions, 25 part tags and 11 capacities. These are authoring records, including abstracts. HediffSet holds actual acquired injuries, missing/added parts and conditions. Hediff records definition, body-part identity, severity, age and source attribution. `Data/Core/Defs/PawnCapacityDefs/PawnCapacity.xml` binds Manipulation to its worker and zeroes it when the pawn cannot be awake. A body shape label alone is not a functional body.

**SHOWN rules:** `CalculateCapacityLevel` first handles the awake-zero rule, calls the anatomy-specific worker, and, only for positive base capacity, adds condition and active-gene offsets, multiplies post-factors, and applies the smallest set-max. It then enforces the capacity minimum and rounds to hundredths. Condition factor scaling can itself use a pawn stat. Manipulation is limb efficiency with digit weight 0.8 times Consciousness. Moving is zero for an always-downed life stage or functional-limb fraction below 0.4999; otherwise it multiplies limb, pelvis and spine efficiencies, blends Breathing and BloodPumping at 20% each, then multiplies by min(Consciousness, 1). `CalculatePartEfficiency` reads replacements on ancestors, missing parent parts, direct prostheses, stage offsets and health fraction. Thus grafted carrier, current injury and effective function are separate.

**SHOWN connections and save:** the same capacity workers expose an impactor list for explanation, including parts, conditions, genes and dependent capacities. Health stores the HediffSet and immunity deeply; HediffSet saves acquired hediffs deeply, while static body definitions and part paths reconstruct anatomy. `Hediff.ExposeData` saves the affected part and source, rather than a screenshot or an explanation string as the body model. Capacity is a derived reader. Exact damage selection, armor, surgery success and every body worker remain unread.

**INFERRED transfer:** extend G17/G22/G37 with one existing hand/tool-dependent task whose rate or admission changes for a named injury and returns after treatment. Use actual body means and an explained modifier; do not add a universal anatomy simulator. Cross-world suppression belongs to a separate world-law reader. Payoff rank 1 among new source gaps because life, crafting, combat and nonhuman bodies can share one honest function reader.

## S02: Treatment, disease severity and acquired immunity

**SHOWN model and authoring:** HediffDef combines stages with Immunizable and TendDuration components. ImmunityRecord retains disease definition, source and immunity. TendDuration separately retains quality, accumulated quality and remaining tend ticks. Example raw Flu in `Data/Core/Defs/HediffDefs/Hediffs_Local_Infections.xml` declares lethal severity 1, a 12-hour tend duration, tended severity/day -0.0773, nonimmune severity/day 0.2488, sick immunity/day 0.2388, immune severity/day -0.4947 and nonsick immunity/day -0.06. These values are RimWorld game rules, not medical advice or YAIRPG constants.

**SHOWN exact rules:** `CalculateBaseTendQuality` clamps `(doctor MedicalTendQuality * medicine MedicalPotency + current-bed offset) * self-tend factor` between 0 and medicine quality maximum. Missing doctor uses 0.75, missing medicine potency 0.3 and max 0.7; self-tend factor is 0.7. Each TendDuration component then adds a uniform -0.25 to +0.25 and clamps again. A nonpermanent treatment adds TendTicksFull to max(0, remaining ticks); while tended it contributes `severityPerDayTended * tendQuality`. This is one severity component, not the entire disease.

`ImmunityTickInterval` adds per-tick change times delta and clamps to [0,1]. Sick immunity gain is authored daily gain times ImmunityGainSpeed times a deterministic per-disease factor in [0.8,1.2], divided by 60,000. Its seed combines disease loadID and world persistent random value while isolating global RNG. Nonsick loss uses its authored daily rate. Nonflesh bodies gain zero here. `HediffComp_Immunizable` changes severity according to immunity >=1, its retained random severity factor and other specified hediff multipliers. Better treatment and faster immunity influence different curves.

**SHOWN connections, save and limits:** TendUtility selects tendable conditions and invokes their tending callbacks; medicine is a real input in that route. Immunity records, treatment timer/quality and disease severity/random factor are serialized in separate owners. A load must preserve the illness race and prior treatment, not roll a fresh case. No treatment job, surgery, infection-spread route or native recovery was exercised. Full disease catalog and timer/component call ordering remain open.

**INFERRED transfer:** G31 can offer a paid clinic/study path with diagnosis, care and gradual recovery represented independently. Comfortable ordinary play remains authoritative; this donor does not authorize hunger death, forced disease or a medical upkeep tax. Queued recovery can advance through an explicit clock policy. Payoff rank 2.

## S03: Substances, tolerance, dependency and use policy

**SHOWN model/authoring:** ChemicalDef points to separate tolerance and addiction HediffDefs; ThingDef comp properties describe addictiveness, minimum tolerance, need refill, overdose and existing-addiction offset. Eight raw ChemicalDefs and four DrugPolicyDefs are indexed. Psychite's raw chemical is in `Data/Core/Defs/Drugs/Psychite_Yayo.xml`; full tables and conditions remain private.

**SHOWN rules:** `CompDrug.PrePostIngested` only enters its addiction branch for addictive drugs and flesh bodies. Existing addiction severity increases by the authored offset. Otherwise a random draw must be below adjusted addictiveness AND prior tolerance must meet the minimum. `DrugStatsUtility.GetAddictivenessAtTolerance` multiplies base addictiveness by a curve through (0,1), (0.5,5), (0.8,15) when a tolerance definition exists and tolerance is positive; pawn genes further modify chance. A chemical need refill uses its own tolerance/body-size modifier. `Hediff_Addiction.CurStageIndex` returns withdrawal stage 1 when its chemical need is in Withdrawal, otherwise stage 0. Addiction presence, current withdrawal and acute high are not one state.

`PostIngested` separately evaluates overdose gene factors, possible large overdose and ordinary accumulated overdose. A large-overdose branch sets severity into [0.85,0.99] under its conditions; ordinary offset is divided by body size. It records use timestamps/history and notifies the policy tracker. Safe-dose UI calculations are an authored approximation, not a universal guarantee against all overdose paths.

**SHOWN save/connectivity:** health saves high, tolerance, addiction and overdose as hediffs, with chemical needs owned by needs. ## S04: Relationships, romance and family references

**SHOWN model/authoring:** the 28 raw PawnRelationDefs configure direct/implied/reflexive relations and workers. Pawn_RelationsTracker retains directRelations and virtualRelations, pregnancy approaches, romanceEnableTick and related actor references. Biological genealogy is not the same as a love relation or an attributed opinion memory. `Data/Biotech/Defs/PawnRelationDefs/PawnRelations_FamiltyByBlood.xml` is one raw family table; the spelling is the actual path.

**SHOWN conditions and formulas:** automatic romance selection rejects tutorial mode, juvenile participants, an inhumanized initiator, an existing love partnership, secondary romance factor below 0.15, or either directed opinion below 5. The weight is 1.15 times gender/trait, normalized attraction, normalized initiator opinion, existing-partner willingness and orientation factors. This donor includes hard-coded gender/orientation multipliers; they describe its model and are not recommended universal person rules.

`SuccessChance` rejects an inhumanized recipient; an eligible psychic-bond case can return 1, with quest-helper exceptions. Ordinary success is clamp01(base 0.6 * recipient's secondary attraction toward initiator * inverseLerp(5,100,recipient opinion) * partner factor). An active directed TryRomance job uses base 1, not guaranteed success after other factors. Partner factor has lover/fiance/spouse branches, opinion and attraction toward the existing partner, subject to belief willingness. Interacted stamps a 900,000-tick directed-romance cooldown and on success changes prior love relations, removes selected failed-romance/breakup memories, adds the new relation and produces aftermath. Rejection has separate memories. These are mechanical social effects preceding cosmetic grammar.

**SHOWN save and integrity:** AddDirectRelation rejects implied, self and duplicate relations; invokes the relation worker, records start tick and adds reciprocal edges only when the Def is reflexive. It notifies both actors and their health conditions. ExposeData saves relation lists, pregnancy approach references and romance cooldown, then rebuilds reverse indexes and removes null participant edges. That repair is lossy, not proof family continuity across deleted actors. Birth genealogy, every relation worker, death and inheritance law remain open.

## S06: Companion training, retained learning and command means

**SHOWN exact rules:** CanBeTrained requires current steps below authored total and every prerequisite both learned and fully trained. The Odyssey AttackTarget action requires its learned trainable, a master, and that master spawned on the same map. The chosen target must lie within 25.9 cells of the master. WorkGiver_Train requires a same-faction animal, a trainable target, interaction admission and usable food when that animal needs it. It can first return a food-gathering job. ## S09: Trade quotes, settlement stock and restocking

**SHOWN model/authoring:** Tradeable retains colony/trader thing references and intended transfer count; price data are initialized and cached for the session. TradeDeal owns preview/resolve behavior. Settlement_TraderTracker owns a ThingOwner stock, last generation tick and ever-generated marker. Twenty raw TraderKindDefs contain stockGenerators, category filters and price types. A stock list is not evidence that a settlement manufactured it.

**SHOWN exact pricing:** silver buy is max(0.5, MarketValue *1.4*trader-price multiplier*join-as factor*(1+difficulty loss)*(1-negotiator gain-settlement gain)). Sell is max(0.01, MarketValue *0.6*trader multiplier*item SellPriceFactor*human-pawn factor*(1-difficulty loss)*(1+negotiator+drug+settlement+animal-product gains)). Values above 99.5 are rounded. Favor currency instead reads RoyalFavorValue in the sell path. Tradeable clamps sell to buy if sell>=buy. These are route-specific formulas with item condition and market value upstream; they are not quantity-responsive supply/demand curves.

**SHOWN stock lifecycle:** default settlement regeneration period is 30 game days. Accessing StockListForReading generates stock when null OR empty. TraderTrackerTick discards existing stock after strictly more than 30*60,000 ticks since generation; the next access materializes new stock through TraderStock/TraderKind generation. The getter therefore mutates stock. Empty-stock access differs from merely waiting for NextRestockTick. `RestockedSinceLastVisit` is ever-generated plus null stock, not a complete replenishment receipt. Humanlike pawns use world-pawn/reference handling; other stock can be destroyed on discard. No offscreen production inputs, transport or market clearing were shown here.

**SHOWN save/commit:** stock is deep, world pawns are referenced, generation tick and ever marker are values. Tradeable's ExposeData saves counts and both reference lists, not the cached prices in the reviewed method. TradeDeal separates funds acceptance and ResolveTrade, with social/history calls interleaved. This is not proof of durable atomicity across all goods and goodwill.
