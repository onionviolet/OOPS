# Ideology: institutions as shared rule readers

**SHOWN F6:** MemeDef supplies exclusions, required/selected precepts, required rituals/buildings, role connections, starting research, styles and apparel rules. PreceptDef carries worker/comps plus stat offsets/factors, opposed work, role requirements, required/disabled work tags, granted abilities, expectation changes and other fields. These declarations alone do not prove every consumer. The current pass traced concrete readers: Ideo.RecachePrecepts rebuilds possible thoughts, goodwill, buildings/buildables, roles, mental breaks and needs; StatWorker reads precept stat modifiers and conditional affecters and a pawn's role effects. A belief record is reused by ordinary systems.

**SHOWN roles:** Precept_Role supplies disabled-work projections and temporary ability construction with sourcePrecept attribution. Assignment/unassignment notifies ability and disabled-work caches. Precept_RoleSingle activates/deactivates against separate believer thresholds, can unassign invalid holders, grants/removes holder state, and the leader path updates the faction leader and removes competing leader assignments. Assign logs an invalid pawn but continues its body, so ValidatePawn is not by itself proof that every caller rejects bad assignment. UI/caller admission must be checked before copying this pattern.

**SHOWN belief continuity:** Pawn_IdeoTracker keeps personal belief/certainty and conversion machinery. Ideo is shared rather than cloning a complete religion into every pawn. Personal knowledge, current office and faction membership are different state. Moving a pawn preserves its tracker, but universal recognition of office in all places is not demonstrated.

**SHOWN F7 ritual structure:** Precept_Ritual contains a behavior, obligations/triggers, target filter, outcome worker, last-finished tick and optional attachable outcomes. It distinguishes anytime/date/event delivery. Repeat-quality penalty applies only to applicable repeatable anytime rituals, so repeatable is not equivalent to equal immediate rewards. RitualBehaviorWorker checks start and role/target admission and constructs participating group work. The quality outcome worker combines completion progress and authored components, selects among eligible outcomes, applies extras/attachables/development, sends a report and grants source-attributed memories to qualifying participants. Positive outcome weights scale by quality; this is a weighted outcome model, not certainty of success.

**INFERRED YAIRPG:** RW-D5 extends P07 with one rule bundle read by a service and an existing relationship/response reader, K7/K8/K21/K24. Keep permission, office, personal belief, know-how and local support separate. A traveler can keep private practice but lose workshop access, or retain a role whose authority has no local jurisdiction. A ritual should consume actual participants/time/means and leave a real aftermath, K12/K29/K32, P10/P12. Membership should not silently rewrite body or technique competence. Reasonable repeat routes stay reachable; this game's population thresholds and ritual penalties are examples, not mandatory solo-player rules.

**Unread:** all meme/precept component consumers, every conversion/social thought, fluid reform/reward edge, all ritual outcome classes and actual UI/save behavior. Declaration inventory is not full semantic coverage.
