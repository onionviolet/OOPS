# Biotech: carrier, expression, heredity and development

**SHOWN F4:** Pawn_GeneTracker owns endogenes, xenogenes, xenotype metadata and hybrid/name/icon state. SetXenotype clears xenogenes and adds definition genes to the channel selected by the xenotype's inheritable flag; SetXenotypeDirect changes the label/reference without doing that construction. Do not use the latter as evidence that a body's genotype was rebuilt. AddGene updates lists, conflict overrides, ability/trait readers and caches. `Gene.Active` is false when overridden, below minAgeActive, or disabled by a current mutant profile. Inherited presence is not the same as present usable faculty.

**SHOWN inheritance path:** PregnancyUtility.GetInheritedGenes enumerates parental Endogenes, excludes ordinary melanin handling and archite genes from that pass, gives a candidate present in one parent 0.5 chance and a shared candidate 1.0 chance, shuffles, samples, removes missing prerequisites, and checks the metabolism sum of non-overridden candidates. It retries at most 50 times and reports success separately. Skin color, hair fallback and inbreeding have additional paths. The returned list alone is not proof that success was true; every birth caller/failure response remains unread. TryGetInheritedXenotype preserves a shared inheritable type in supported cases; other cases can be hybrids. This is a game model, not a claim about biological genetics.

**SHOWN persistence:** ExposeData serializes both gene lists deeply, xenotype by Def, and hybrid/name/icon metadata. Invalid gene references can be removed and missing lists rebuilt. This permissive repair is not an acceptable default migration for valuable YAIRPG powers without an explicit compatibility choice.

**SHOWN F5 learning:** Need_Learning has decay, thresholds and frozen/deathrest handling; NeedInterval adds growth via Pawn_AgeTracker when canGainGrowthPoints. GrowthPointsPerDay reads learning level times a young-age factor and child aging multiplier, has a growth-vat path of 2, and returns zero for suspension. GrowthUtility has nine tiers, thresholds from 0 to 162, and three growth birthdays at 7, 10 and 13. Better tiers expand trait choice sets and passion options/gains. These source-specific ages, budgets and maximum tiers are not global YAIRPG limits.

**SHOWN milestone handling:** BirthdayBiological creates a growth choice letter with tier/trait/passion data and newly allowed work types; pending choice sets canGainGrowthPoints false. ChoiceLetter_GrowthMoment keeps offered choices in save data, avoids applying archived choices and resolves actual trait/passions. Full choice UI and timeout behavior are not runtime-tested. A birthday is not ordinary skill XP, and passive learning fulfillment is not automatically mastery of the watched recipe.

**SHOWN learning means:** Pawn_LearningTracker keeps up to two desires and a 20,000-40,000 tick refresh interval. Lessontaking checks a usable teacher, desk and student place plus basic needs. Workwatching requires a nearby reachable adult doing a relevant task whose skill is not disabled for the child. The activity givers were read; every learning job's full XP/need accounting remains unread.

**INFERRED YAIRPG:** RW-D2 maps G1 layers, G18 genetics, G39 modification, G40 reproduction, G43 world physics, G61 heredity and G73 storage. BW1 already distinguishes blood/teaching/item channels; Biotech adds NEW evidence for germline/implant/expression rather than correcting Taiwu. Preserve a donor source, body carrier, active reason, transmission rule and usable consumer independently. RW-D6 extends P03/P06/P09 through G21/G59: one queued learning action should prepare an existing method, while development broadens future choices. No universal flaw budget, compulsory childhood track or heir-only traveler growth is proposed.
