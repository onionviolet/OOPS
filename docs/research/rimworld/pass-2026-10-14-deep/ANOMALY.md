# Anomaly: a subject, a study job, retained notes and useful research

**SHOWN F8:** CompStudiable owns studyEnabled, studyPoints, lastStudiedTick, studyInteractions and anomalyKnowledgeGained. CurrentlyStudiable rechecks permanent admission, switch, frequency and subject-specific holding/prison rules. StudyUnlocked also checks destruction, deactivation and applicable monolith progression. StudyManager caches candidates per map; cached inclusion is not current admission. Study adds capped subject points, calls Notify_Studied and sends a global signal. CompStudiable.Study separately routes positive Anomaly knowledge into StudyAnomaly, learns Intellectual and sends completion quest signals/letters on a completion transition.

**SHOWN jobs:** WorkGiver_StudyInteract handles ordinary subjects and rejects knowledge-category subjects. WorkGiver_DarkStudyInteract handles Anomaly knowledge-category targets, resolves platform-held subjects, reserves them and requires current study eligibility. JobDriver_StudyInteract reserves both platform and held subject where applicable, fails when the subject becomes unavailable, performs up to five interactions, retains partial count, and sets the cooldown tick after the study batch. Study duration reads ResearchSpeed or EntityStudyRate; knowledge per interaction reads StudyEfficiency and the subject's adjusted value. A captive is a real participant/target, not an abstract encyclopedia row.

**SHOWN retained notes:** CompStudyUnlocks stores resolved thresholds, nextIndex, progress and retained choice letters. Thresholds can be authored or rolled at setup and are saved. OnStudied can cross several thresholds; RegisterStudyLevel advances the index before letter delivery and preserves a dated copy. Reading the note is separate from the study callback. Other subject-specific subclasses were not exhaustively read.

**SHOWN research:** ResearchManager stores ordinary progress, project knowledge and currently chosen project per category. ApplyKnowledge sends amount into the chosen project; completion returns remainder, which can flow to a linked overflow category. With no current project, only an available overflow route receives that amount. There is no demonstrated universal bank of unassigned research points. ResearchProjectDef ties category, costs/prerequisites and unlock admission to research; physical means remain separate. StudyManager's ExposeData primarily handles older study-progress compatibility, while modern subject and research state live in their own owners.

**SHOWN F9 containment:** Holding-platform target mode must be Study. ContainmentUtility compares actual strength to required strength. Separate curves change escape interval and knowledge multiplier; a tiny negative surplus produces sharply different results than meeting the requirement. Escape interval also reads moving capacity, time in captivity and optional cold bonus. Longer interval means less frequent expected escape, not a literal certainty timer. CompHoldingPlatformTarget periodically samples escape eligibility and chance. Exact building strength aggregation and every escape AI were not closed here.

**INFERRED YAIRPG:** RW-D3 is a NEW bounded subject-study packet extending P03/P11/P20 and technology GR4 plus RW5/RW7. Start with one safe original material/object and a supported useful method, rather than importing Anomaly captivity or mandatory hazard. Keep sample custody, actor observation, note delivery, method knowledge and manufacturing permission separate. A known containment problem may explain a blocked study job; hidden subject truth must remain behind its discovery route. K9/K11/K13/K18 plus K2/K10/K22 for optional risky routes. Save one paid study receipt, retained threshold decisions, revealed note and method together. Reopening the record grants nothing.
