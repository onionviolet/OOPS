F1. Ordinary eligible victims can enter a private queue, leave their cell/inventory/equipment context and become inactive before any destination is selected. Important, interesting, player and nontrifling player-led actors select an explicit destination instead. An explicit entrant can therefore flush earlier ordinary victims before its own contact event can veto entry. The classification of one actor changes the outcome for other actors.

F2. The independent audit traced the actual stock part serializer. It skips `NotSerialized` fields in both directions, so the queue and stored victim locations are omitted. ActionManager also removes queued actors from the action and ability collections. This closes two concrete preservation candidates. It does not establish that every victim lacks all other serializable references elsewhere in the game.

F3. Temporary expiry has no queue-specific rescue in the inspected path. It visits event-defined contents, removes contents, obliterates the owner and removes its context. The vortex class does not expose its queue as contents or override destroy/save hooks. Zone cells and the ordinary action queue do not provide an automatic fallback for the queued actors in the traced routes. A whole-game save/root proof and native reproduction remain open.
