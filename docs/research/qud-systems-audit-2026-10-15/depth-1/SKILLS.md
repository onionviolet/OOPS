# Skills: execution and interaction detail

2026-10-15. Static reading of the pinned 2.0.211.56 assembly. Full declarations, descriptions and prerequisites remain in the [original skill/power tables](../private/POWERS.json); this supplement follows selected runtime consumers. Numeric costs below are arguments passed by the handler, before shared energy/cooldown modifiers. A class with little code may be a marker consumed elsewhere.

## S1: axes, armor and anatomy

Cleave runs on `AttackerAfterAttack`; it rejects gas/no-damage targets and mismatched weapon skills. Governing mental attributes select mental armor, otherwise physical AV. Its positive odd attribute modifier is rounded upward when halved for the base penalty ceiling, with a minimum of one. Charging plus Charging Strike raises each application from one to two. Existing penalties and event-adjusted ceilings are checked before application. The default chance is 75; the decompiled call to the special-effect chance event does not assign its returned value in this method. That differs from the Dismember path, which does assign it. Treat the discrepancy as source behavior to verify, not a proven live bug. [Axe_Cleave](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Axe_Cleave.cs)

The effect is more than a displayed debuff. On an NPC, ShatterArmor owns an actual negative AV stat shift, merges penalty into an existing effect, extends duration to the longer value, and restores its shifts on removal. On a player it may instead choose equipped positive-AV armor and apply ShatteredArmor to that object; an intrinsic-armor route remains possible. Repair removes the actor effect. Thus target ownership and equipment matter, and the armor item's downstream ShatteredArmor behavior is still a separate open consumer. [ShatterArmor L122-203](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Effects.ShatterArmor.cs)

Berserk gives a default five-turn effect, modified by ImprovedBerserk, starts a 100-turn ability cooldown and puts Dismember on 30. Its command requires a primary axe and rejects an already cooling Dismember. The AI offers it only with a primary axe, usable ability and a sufficiently challenging target. The effect's promise of dismemberment still passes through actual attack code: there must be a penetrating hit with a real axe, and a body part must permit severing. The ordinary passive chance is 3%, doubled for charging with Charging Strike and doubled for a two-slot axe; Berserk or the explicit dismember command chooses the stronger branch. Severing can fail at the body API. A successful sever adds bleeding and clears Hooked. Heads require the decapitation option; a `BeforeDecapitate` veto can stop the attempt, and death checks remaining mortal parts rather than assuming every head is fatal. [Axe_Berserk](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Axe_Berserk.cs) [Berserk](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Effects.Berserk.cs) [Axe_Dismember L67-185,280-339](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Axe_Dismember.cs) [Axe_Decapitate L1-130](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Axe_Decapitate.cs)

## S2: short blades and effect-driven penetration

Bloodletter is an after-damage hook for short blades. It rolls 75%, or 100% for an attack carrying Juking. The target's number of Bleeding effects must remain below one plus the attacker's Agility modifier. The application uses damage `1d2-1`, save difficulty 20 plus that modifier, and `Stack=false`. An effect-count limit and the effect's own stacking semantics are separate constraints. [ShortBlades_Bloodletter](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.ShortBlades_Bloodletter.cs)

Shank counts effect instances matching bitmask 33554432. Each counted instance adds two to both penetration modifier and penetration cap modifier for the selected short blade. This is not a count of distinct UI labels or every undesirable condition. The helper shows no separate bonus ceiling; downstream penetration and immunity rules still apply. A primary short blade with Expertise pays 750 energy, otherwise 1000; the primary cooldown is 10 versus 20 for the alternate-hand route, tagged Agility. The command checks a usable body/extremity weapon and target. AI admission compares a random integer 0..4 with the qualifying effect count, alongside distance, movement, weapon and readiness gates. This supplies an explicit preference for already afflicted targets. [ShortBlades_Shank](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.ShortBlades_Shank.cs)

Jab adds an attack attempt for a nonprimary short blade in the early melee-chance event, but explicitly excludes `Flurrying`. Flurry therefore does not automatically multiply the Jab benefit. [ShortBlades_Jab](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.ShortBlades_Jab.cs)

## S3: multiweapon and single-weapon modes

Multiweapon Proficiency, Expertise and Mastery add 20, 15 and 15 to the intrinsic nonprimary attack chance. These are chance points at the event layer, not a guaranteed number of extra blows. Flurry overrides the chance: intrinsic, nonconsecutive attacks get 100; other candidates get zero. It starts at cooldown 60, reduced by ten each for Expertise and Mastery, carries an Agility tag, and sends a 1000-energy melee attack with Flurrying. Cooldown starts before the combat call, so a later refusal does not itself prove a refund. The AI wants adjacency, usable extremities, readiness and a nonburrowed actor. [Multiweapon_Proficiency](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Multiweapon_Proficiency.cs) [Multiweapon_Expertise](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Multiweapon_Expertise.cs) [Multiweapon_Mastery](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Multiweapon_Mastery.cs) [Multiweapon_Flurry](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Multiweapon_Flurry.cs)

Single-weapon skills share a toggle controller. While enabled it sets intrinsic nonprimary attack multiplier to zero, with very early ordering. Weapon Expertise and Mastery then affect primary intrinsic attacks by 50% and 100% through another event. Penetrating Strikes adds one penetration under its target condition, rather than a flat damage number. Do not sum these into a universal damage multiplier: attack selection, chance, repeated attacks, penetrations and damage are different stages. Combat iterates chance above 100 with subtraction by 100; its attack-attempt count is another axis. [SingleWeaponFighting_Ability](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.SingleWeaponFighting_Ability.cs) [SingleWeaponFighting_WeaponExpertise](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.SingleWeaponFighting_WeaponExpertise.cs) [SingleWeaponFighting_WeaponMastery](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.SingleWeaponFighting_WeaponMastery.cs) [SingleWeaponFighting_PenetratingStrikes](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.SingleWeaponFighting_PenetratingStrikes.cs) [Combat L713-780](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Combat.cs)

Opportune Attacks runs on a critical hit while that mode is enabled. It selects at most two cooling abilities tagged for the weapon's skill or Shield, checks whether each can refresh, then refreshes selected entries. Berserk supplies a refresh veto while its effect is active. A critical hit therefore does not reset every martial ability. [SingleWeaponFighting_OpportuneAttacks](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.SingleWeaponFighting_OpportuneAttacks.cs) [Axe_Berserk](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Axe_Berserk.cs)

## S4: cudgels and shields

Bludgeon normally rolls 50% for Dazed; Conking, charging with Charging Strike, or Smashing Up selects 100%, with an ImprovedBludgeon branch otherwise and an assigned special-chance hook. Conk plus an already stunned victim can produce 30..40 rounds of sleep. Conk requires a head and usable primary cudgel, attempts a marked melee attack, then pays 1000 energy and cooldown ten. AI checks adjacency, extremities, weapon and readiness. [Cudgel_Bludgeon](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Cudgel_Bludgeon.cs) [Cudgel_Conk](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Cudgel_Conk.cs)

Dazed requires a brain. Its actual owned shifts are Intelligence -4, Agility -4 and MoveSpeed cost +10. Reapplication can turn into Stun. The `DontStunIfPlayer` condition is narrower than its name: its boolean also tests whether the player already has Stun. Duration decreases at action start only while not stunned. Removing the effect restores its shifts. [Dazed L1-150](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Effects.Dazed.cs)

Shield processing selects a real shield and usable extremities. It records `BlockedWithShield` before the chance roll, so the per-turn counter concerns attempts, not only successes. Default block chance is 25, with +25 from Block, +25 from Deft Blocking, and the ImprovedBlock contribution. A hand-held Shield Wall sets 100 before a veto/chance event. Successful blocking adds the shield's AV; it does not universally erase the incoming attack. Swift Blocking permits a second attempt; Shield Wall bypasses that count filter. Staggering Block has its own strength-based stun chance and save. These marker skills do their work in Combat. [Combat L60-136,648-704](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Combat.cs)

## S5: ranged marks, utility and terrain

Draw a Bead stores an actor-owned target reference and applies a RifleMark carrying that marker. Setting a new mark clears the old one after successful application. Confusion and suspension clear tracking; player visibility loss and NPC LOS/range loss have distinct validation checks. The command pays 1000 energy after selecting a cell, even if that cell yields no combat target. AI admission additionally wants distance at least three, a ready compatible bow/rifle, visibility and movement. The mark is not a universal shared-team debuff. The exact downstream aim benefit remains a MissileWeapon consumer question. One Shot's own class mainly owns a serialized command cooldown; its firing behavior lives elsewhere. [Rifle_DrawABead](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Rifle_DrawABead.cs) [RifleMark](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Effects.RifleMark.cs) [Rifle_OneShot](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Rifle_OneShot.cs)

In the selected firing windows, Sure Fire suppresses one aim-variance term for Beacon Fire or the One Shot combination; later movement and other deviations remain. It is not proof of an unavoidable hit. The pistol disarm branch occurs before a later zero-penetration test in the read window, so penetration must not be casually imposed as its prerequisite. Swift Reflexes prevents one -5 combat DV adjustment, while immobility can override DV farther downstream. Full firing-stack ordering remains open. [MissileWeapon L1780-1820,2775-2808,3355-3388](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.MissileWeapon.cs)

Meditate can start after ten passive Pass actions if not asleep, in stasis or already meditating. Active use starts cooldown max(200 - 40×Serene, 5); no energy cost appears in that handler. The effect resets a per-round damage budget, subtracts actual damage, ends at zero, and ends on nonpassive/non-Pass energy use. It accelerates selected finite negative durations by up to two extra decrements, excludes duration 9999, and gives bleeding recovery another opportunity. Its text says triple regeneration, but the healing consumer is outside this read. There is also a concrete description mismatch: the skill collector can display threshold zero, while the effect clamps its threshold to at least one. [Discipline_Meditate](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Discipline_Meditate.cs) [Meditating](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Effects.Meditating.cs)

Staunch Wounds changes bandage performance in two passes: default +1 in pass one, then ×2 in pass two, only when the owner is the checked actor. Global configuration can change those defaults. Bandage payment and the final healing consumer remain separate. [Physic_StaunchWounds](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Physic_StaunchWounds.cs)

Deploy Turret consumes one inventory missile weapon with `FiresManually`, generates a host through IntegratedWeaponHosts, places and activates it, then costs 2000 energy. It rejects the world map, unusable extremities, blocked deployment and excavatory terrain. This command uses player pickers and has no AI admission in the read class; an NPC owning the skill is not evidence it autonomously deploys turrets. The host's ammunition, energy, ownership and recovery behavior remains open. [Tinkering_DeployTurret](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Tinkering_DeployTurret.cs)

Terrain survival subclasses inherit real behavior from BaseTerrainSurvivalSkill: matching travel class adds 100 to encounter PercentageBonus, 95 to GetLostChance PercentageBonus, and 100 to travel-speed PercentageBonus. Those are event fields, not direct final percentages. Fasting Way doubles the stomach's cooking increment and a water-cost divisor; Mind Over Body multiplies those by six, yielding twelve together in those paths. The final world-travel and starvation policies must still be followed before claiming universal safety. [BaseTerrainSurvivalSkill](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.BaseTerrainSurvivalSkill.cs) [Stomach L73-88,263-280](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Stomach.cs)
