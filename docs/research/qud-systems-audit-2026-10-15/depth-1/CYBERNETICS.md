# Cybernetics: bodies, power, computation and medical automation

2026-10-15. All63 supplied Cybernetics-prefixed types are captured. [The broad inherited blueprint catalog](../private/CYBERNETIC_BLUEPRINTS.json) has91 candidates, including abstract/inherited records; it is not91 usable implants. The first audit's terminal, skillsoft and support-loss traces remain authoritative at their own scope. This supplement follows additional concrete consumers.

## C1: two power suppliers with different budgets

Biodynamic Power Plant is a transient per-action source, with default ChargeRate5000. At the actor-action boundary it records prior charge, clears current charge and refills if ready. It is not an accumulating battery. Its implanted event registrations provide query, test, use, production and connector participation; unimplant unregisters them. Query compatibility includes transient/live flags and readiness. Partial charge use clamps the remainder rather than guaranteeing every requested device succeeds. Shared IPoweredPart status, liquid supply and item configuration still determine readiness. [CyberneticsBiodynamicPowerPlant](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsBiodynamicPowerPlant.cs)

Electrical Generation instead combines stored biological charge and a per-turn allowance with AdvancedCharge debt. Integrated Power Systems forwards charge events between subjects it considers active, checking boot/power/AI readiness. Do not assume the two suppliers stack for every device merely because each can answer a charge event. Query, Test, actual Use, source ordering, active subject, transient inclusion and biological inclusion must all match. The source for the forwarding system is retained, but every mixed-source ordering is not semantically closed. [IntegratedPowerSystems](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.IntegratedPowerSystems.cs) [ElectricalGeneration](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.ElectricalGeneration.cs)

## C2: compute is an event total with integer consumers

A head-installed Palladium Electrodeposits supplies +2 base Intelligence and +20 compute in the owner event. Unimplant removes its owned stat shifts. No powered readiness gate appears in its compute handler. GetAvailableComputePowerEvent resets Amount to zero, dispatches on the actor and returns the accumulated result. Multi-subject overloads sum subject contributions. For integer values, AdjustUp computes amount×(100+compute)/100; AdjustDown defaults to max(amount×(100-compute)/100, amount/2). Integer truncation and the cooldown floor matter.

At a total of20compute, base radius10 becomes12; stasis coverage6 becomes7; base cooldown100 becomes80. Those examples are arithmetic evaluations of the read helper, not observed gameplay. Compute changes only consumers that query it, not every implant universally. [CyberneticsPalladiumElectrodeposits](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsPalladiumElectrodeposits.cs) [GetAvailableComputePowerEvent](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.GetAvailableComputePowerEvent.cs)

Micromanipulator Array removes certain combat tinkering/repair restrictions when operational. Its energy handler requires WasReady, a Skill Tinkering type, and specifically excludes Build, Mod and Repair. For matching other operations the reduction is max(existing, min(40+compute/2,60)). Deploy Turret's energy tag is a concrete candidate that matches the string filter, but complete device-readiness and event sequencing remain unplayed. The Sifrah bonus branch adds one when actor==implantee without an IsReady guard in that handler. Do not summarize this implant as a blanket discount to crafting. [CyberneticsMicromanipulatorArray](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsMicromanipulatorArray.cs) [Tinkering_DeployTurret L98-104](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Tinkering_DeployTurret.cs)

## C3: anatomy-producing implants own real parts

Motorized Treads adds two managed, integral Tread parts and changes an implant slot's lower-body fields. Removal deletes managed parts even if severed, restores saved slot fields and requests reequip. Its BeforeDismember gate protects the part currently holding that implant. These lifecycle details do not prove the complete movement speed, terrain or footwear interaction; those may be supplied by other parts/data. [CyberneticsMotorizedTreads](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsMotorizedTreads.cs)

Gun Rack creates two managed integral Hardpoint slots and removes its managed parts on unimplant, including severed ones, then requests reequip. Equipment Rack adds an actual EquipmentRack body part near Back and similarly cleans up managed parts. These are changes in legal equipment locations, not flat carry-weight bonuses. Tibular Hydrojets subtracts default200 from swimming MoveSpeedPenalty in its event. SyncJump calls the Jump ability synchronizer on implant/unimplant; that alone does not establish a free skill grant or exact jump-range value. ## C4: temporary fabrication has support and failure order

Precision Force Lathe creates an actual nonstacking force knife, requires an unequipped compatible hand/thrown slot and associates the result with an ExistenceSupport provider. Its activation consumes charge before a reality-distortion check. Failure then obliterates the provisional knife; the selected body has no refund. Receive/equip failure also destroys the output. A replacement-throw route can consume charge again. Continued existence depends on matching blueprint, operational source and equipped subject, with per-turn validation. Its AI offers offense only when the actor is an active subject, ready, can hold the output and passes advisability. A generated object is therefore a leased capability with its own support lifecycle. [CyberneticsPrecisionForceLathe L102-183](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsPrecisionForceLathe.cs)

## C5: medical automation uses actual consumables

Automated Internal Defibrillator ticks only on an implanted subject with CardiacArrest. Readiness spends charge before a default50 chance adjusted by compute to remove that specific effect. It is neither a general HP heal nor a universal death interceptor. [CyberneticsAutomatedInternalDefibrillator](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsAutomatedInternalDefibrillator.cs)

Medassist has physical inventory, default capacity8, load/eject actions and a toggle. AttemptMedicalAssistance first checks operational status while ignoring charge, inventory and tonic-effect capacity. It chooses the usable nonbroken/nonrusted tonic with the strictly greatest positive GetUtilityScoreEvent result. A tie retains the first encountered candidate. Actual power readiness then spends charge; dosage is queried, and zero/negative dosage stops afterward. ApplyingTonic can veto, then ApplyTonic can veto. On success it destroys the tonic and notifies newly added tonic effects. Thus charge can be spent before downstream dosage/application failure, and no medicine materializes from the implant itself.

The BeforeApplyDamage hook records phase compatibility, attempts assistance, then vetoes damage if the tonic changed compatibility with the incoming phase, attacker or prior subject phase. This is a real prevention route, beyond after-the-fact healing. Exact tonic utility scores, ingredients, effects and overdose policy still require their own consumers. `Overdose="No"` is passed into this event; it does not prove an unrestricted safe-tonic system. These mechanisms are actor-generic; UI messages are player-specific. [CyberneticsMedassistModule L262-428](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsMedassistModule.cs)

## C6: perception and phase do not imply NPC knowledge

Penetrating Radar's BeforeRender handler explicitly requires the implantee be the player, plus readiness, toggle, usability and a local cell. It adds Radar light at compute-adjusted radius, default10. Night Vision similarly requires the player, a usable on toggle and no breakage/rust/EMP, then adds Darkvision light, default40. Neither read handler grants NPC omniscience or changes Brain target selection. NPC equipment presence and player visual benefit must not be conflated. Phase Harmonic Modulator creates a default-on toggle, force-applies Omniphase keyed by the part name, and removes only that source-keyed effect on toggle-off/unimplant. No IsReady or charge gate appears in this class's toggle handler. Forcefield Nullifier increments/decrements a property on implant/unimplant and vetoes a missile-pass event under actor/projectile carry-over conditions. The meaning of that veto in the final forcefield consumer is not closed here. ## C7: Stasis Projector has distinct offense and retreat policies

Defaults are coverage6, cooldown100 and duration6..8, adjusted through compute helpers. Offensive AI requires distance1, valid actor/target, active subject, readiness and reality advisability. Retreat AI scans hostile visible creatures, chooses the nearest within coverage, then offers the ability with that target. NPC execution builds its own field path/coverage rather than invoking the player's picker.

Execution checks active subject and uses charge before reality initiation and before the player selection can yield no cells. After a nonempty selection it pays1000energy, starts cooldown and deploys. Each cell independently checks accessibility, creates a Stasisfield, sets Forcefield.Creator, adds Temporary(duration+1), carries actor phase and places it. Intended coverage is therefore not guaranteed actual field count. The Stasisfield's final freezing/collision effect still belongs to the blueprint/forcefield consumers, which are not claimed reviewed. [CyberneticsStasisProjector L60-94,114-180,244-305](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsStasisProjector.cs)

## C8: NPC generation performs an actual implant operation

CyberneticsHasRandomImplants runs after object creation. It rolls its configured chance, raises license capacity to at least the configured value, then tries up to30 candidates while spent cost is below capacity. Each population result must exist, declare implant slots and fit an unoccupied matching body part. It creates the item and calls BodyPart.Implant when remaining budget covers actual Cost; an unaffordable created item is obliterated. Chance success is not a guarantee every license point is filled or every slot can accept a candidate.

The authored creature fixtures include three Templar records with chance25/tableCybernetics4/minimum6 and one commander record with chance60/tableCybernetics5/minimum8. These are table-backed authoring examples, not measured spawned distributions. [Exact complete fixture nodes](../private/depth-1/NPC_FIXTURES.json) retain IDs, inheritance and source indexes. [CyberneticsHasRandomImplants](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsHasRandomImplants.cs)
