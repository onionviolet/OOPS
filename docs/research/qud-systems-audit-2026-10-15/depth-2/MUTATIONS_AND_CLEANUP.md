## M10: Sunder Mind is an interruptible channel

The command spends1000 energy and starts80 cooldown before BeginSunder. It rejects a mindless target, an existing psychic battle and reality-distortion failure where enabled. Psychic sensing can permit target selection, but an unidentified sensed mind is rejected. AI admission requires distance<=80, a brain, no MentalShield, no existing channel/battle, readiness and either LOS or SensePsychic within its radius. Thus sensing and tactical eligibility have explicit code consumers. [SunderMind L43-50,425-507](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.SunderMind.cs)

BeginSunder initializes accrued damage, applies the defender's battle effect, sets ten rounds and a reference, applies the attacker effect and immediately ticks. Subsequent ticks occur at the attacker's BeginTakeAction. Nonpassive/non-Pass energy use cancels; incoming Mental or Psionic damage also cancels. Tick rejects specified actor effect masks, invalid target, zone separation and missing battle state. Cancel/End clear activeRounds, release the object reference and remove matching-role battle effects. [SunderMind L52-148,172-247](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.SunderMind.cs)

Each ordinary blast rolls damage once per penetration: L1 uses1d3, L2 uses1d4, later odd ranks1d3+floor(L/2), even ranks1d4+floor((L-1)/2). Blast scales by100/Magnitude and adds that positive amount to accruedDamage **before** firing the target's TakeDamage event. The terminal tick uses that accrued amount, potentially modified by psychic Sifrah, for Mental/Psionic/Unavoidable damage. It is not necessarily the sum of final HP lost after all prevention. A damage veto does not subtract it back in this method. [SunderMind L96-110,249-294,343-422](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.SunderMind.cs)

Two source details remain open: the second battle-effect lookup inside Tick is on ParentObject again, rather than the target, and Unmutate removes the ability without explicitly calling EndSunder. The full base removal, MemberOfPsychicBattle cleanup and damage-event dispatch must be followed before asserting a leak or exploitable channel. No native reproduction was attempted.

## M11: Mental Mirror consumes readiness across several attack routes

When its cooldown is nonpositive, CheckActive synchronizes a3+L MA bonus; when inactive it removes owned shifts. BeforeMentalDefend ignores self-attacks. If reflectable and incoming penetrations<=0 it rolls reflection against the attacker's combatMA using max(Willpower mod,L)+event modifier. A positive result redirects Defender to the attacker and marks Reflected. A penetrating attack instead shatters the mirror. That handler then starts50 cooldown. The distinct mental-melee route can reflect a failed penetration through another melee call; Mental projectile reflection has an80% branch and also consumes readiness. These are not one universal reflection percentage. Unmutate explicitly removes the stat shifts and ability. Exact stat-query ordering during cooldown transitions remains in shared mental combat. [MentalMirror L41-130,150-210](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.MentalMirror.cs)

Mass Mind refreshes other cooling abilities whose class is exactly Mental Mutations, adds one GlimmerModifier, synchronizes glimmer/rank, spends1000 energy and starts max(100,550-50L) cooldown. Its own ability opts out of Willpower scaling. AfterMassMind then provides the previously traced Light Manipulation recharge reset. FungalVisionary blocks active use and changes the passive exhaustion response. Passive exhaustion is player-only in the read branch, checks a local nonworld-map cell, and uses a strict random1..10000 comparison with (0.13-0.005×WPmod-0.0065×L)×100; it is not a simple13% chance. On triggering, enabled mental abilities receive a scaled cooldown based on8d10. Refresh vetoes and AddScaledCooldown units remain shared consumers. ## M12: Beguile and Proselytize have separate support budgets

Beguile's ordinary mental attack uses attacker level+max(Ego mod,mutation rank),1d8 and defender-level adjustment. It refuses copy relationships and the original player body, influence vetoes and an already owned charm. It spends1000 and starts50 cooldown before the attack/Sifrah result. Success still requires penetration and effect acceptance. The Sifrah route has separate competition scoring. Neither read recruitment class registers a combat AI admission event, so generic applicability to NPCs is not autonomous NPC recruitment proof. [Beguiling L159-205,224-241](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.Beguiling.cs)

Beguiled requires a valid beguiler and target Brain, multiple acceptance events, then sets allied leader and an actor-specific companion flag. Its real bonus changes Hitpoints.BaseValue by5×the difference between current and already applied rank. It separately adds an OpinionBeguile. Nonindependent effects sync to the supplier mutation on action start; losing that supplier sets duration0. Independent effects skip mutation sync, but still check follower support. Removal clears leadership/goals only if that leader owns the target and no other support under exclusion-mask13 remains; it removes the specific allegiance, reverses applied HP and clears the supplier reference. Copy-without-effects callbacks temporarily reverse/reapply the bonus. Edge case: UnapplyBeguilement removes its opinion only inside LevelApplied!=0, so rank-zero/independent paths deserve a separate consumer check. [Beguiled L68-218](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Effects.Beguiled.cs)

Proselytize contributes one companion limit only while its ability ID exists. Difficulty adds the positive target-level excess, existing recruitment conditions and Beguiled.LevelApplied. The non-Sifrah attack uses1d8-6 with its own mental-attack parameters, and spends1000 plus25 cooldown after the attempt. Its effect replaces an existing Proselytized effect, records an opinion and allied leader, then syncs the supplier's companion table. It validates support at BeforeBeginTakeAction. Dismissal verifies the directing actor, removes the effect, charges companion-direction energy100 and fires DismissedFromService. Removing the skill removes its ability and syncs targets. [Persuasion_Proselytize L47-54,93-140,200-235](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Persuasion_Proselytize.cs) [Proselytized L54-132](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Effects.Proselytized.cs)

Each supplier sync filters a distinct party flag, orders by PartyMemberOrder and removes excess entries before adding the new target. Removal need not immediately erase every other effect; its next support check matters. Proselytized.Remove preserves leadership when other support survives exclusion-mask14 and removes AllyProselytize; it does not explicitly remove OpinionProselytize in that method. Full opinion lifetime belongs to its class, still unread. Multiple relationships therefore cannot safely be modeled as a single follower boolean.

## M13: added anatomy has owned cleanup and rank distinctions

Multiple Arms supplies7+3L chance on intrinsic nonprimary attacks **only for body parts carrying its additions manager**. It adjusts from the shared base secondary chance rather than adding that whole percentage to every arm. Rank increases can call AddMoreArms repeatedly. Unmutate removes managed additions even when dismembered, repairs specific changed laterality only when consistent, and requests reequip. Interaction with Flurry and the single-weapon multiplier stays at the shared event layer. [MultipleArms L23-55,132-185](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.MultipleArms.cs)

Multiple Legs writes a base MoveSpeed shift of-20L and changes carrying capacity. Its carry event passes (1+(L+5)/100)×Rank into AdjustWeight. Rank and Level are distinct fields: AdjustRank merely updates Rank in this method, while body addition happens in Mutate. Unmutate removes managed parts including severed ones, restores eligible laterality, removes shifts, sends carrying-capacity change and requests reequip. The final AdjustWeight implementation remains open; do not label that argument a direct additive percentage. [MultipleLegs L45-86,122-142](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.MultipleLegs.cs)

Wings installs owned ChargeRangeModifier2+floor(L/3) and JumpRangeModifier1+floor(L/3), replacing the prior applied values on rank update and syncing Jump. Replication syncs flight state; involuntary posture/mode change fails flight while voluntary change stops it. EndTurn maintains flight; entered cells check environment. ## M14: Force Bubble owns movable supported field objects

Activation starts100 cooldown, marks toggle on, sets duration10+3L, creates fields and spends1000. Each direction holds a distinct object in CurrentField. It checks per-cell accessibility, configures Creator/MovesWithOwner/RejectOwner, attaches ExistenceSupport validating every turn, carries phase and attempts placement. Push force is5000+500L, with a reentrancy guard and phase/passability filters. Field count can undershoot at boundaries or accessibility restrictions. [ForceBubble L128-211,349-368](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.ForceBubble.cs)

BeginMove removes fields from context without destroying them. EnteredCell, MoveFailed and EndTurn try to reattach them around the owner; failed placement obliterates and removes the direction entry. EndTurn decrements duration and syncs phase. Validation removes invalid references; destruction obliterates all field objects and clears the dictionary. OnDestroyObject calls DestroyBubble. Write/Read explicitly serialize direction keys and objects. Support succeeds only for a tracked object within distance1 of the owner. NPC admission avoids use when a neighboring combat object is allied; follower distance increases to at least2 while active. Unmutate only removes the ability in this class; explicit early DestroyBubble is absent there, so base removal and surviving support need further reading. [ForceBubble L30-127,214-330,340-345,373-424,448-485](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.ForceBubble.cs)

## M15: Fugue cleanup and general existence support

Temporary rejects trading, zeroes adjusted value, marks WontSell and a temporary flag. Derivation and replacement copy lifetime state. The Fugue mode expires after AfterAfterThrown and can be destroyed by reality stabilization. TurnTick decrements its clock; activation/thaw subtract elapsed game turns since LastTurn. This is catch-up expiry, not proof of full offscreen simulation. [Temporary L50-208](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.Temporary.cs)

Expire enumerates contents and recursively expires each child that has Temporary, then calls RemoveContents, obliterates the parent and removes context. For a real TurnInto blueprint it places a replacement at the saved cell or enclosing object's inventory; *fugue is not such a replacement. RemoveContents moves nonnatural surviving contents to the enclosing object or cell, but returns without a valid context. New permanent items acquired by a clone are therefore different from originally copied temporary gear. The complete content cascade and no-context edge still depend on GetContents/context ownership. [Temporary L211-285](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.Temporary.cs) [GameObject L9584-9610](../private/source/XRL.World.GameObject.cs)

AddHierarchically dispatches MakeTemporaryEvent. GameObject's handler gives descendants duration-1 and an ExistenceSupport dependency on the root; the root keeps the requested duration/dependency. This event declares cascade271; full descendant enumeration by body/inventory/implant consumers is not independently closed. ExistenceSupport stores a supplier ID with a cached reference, validates on zone activation/deactivation/thaw/synchronization and optionally every turn. Invalid support calls Temporary.Expire if present; otherwise removes contents and obliterates. The support event's inverted convention is now explicit: a valid supplier handler returning false means supported.
