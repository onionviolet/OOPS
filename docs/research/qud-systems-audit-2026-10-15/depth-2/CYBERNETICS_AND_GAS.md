# Remaining implants, gas effects and NPC environmental response

2026-10-15. Pinned-source research only. These traces add actual effect and removal consumers; item blueprints can override defaults or compose several parts. Class defaults are not final item configurations.

## C9: immunity, suppression and medicine are different mechanisms

Bionic Liver zeros Poison-attributed BeforeApplyDamage and sends an immune notification. On implant it registers legacy poison, poison-gas poison, disease and disease-onset acceptance/application vetoes; unimplant unregisters the same hooks. The selected handlers have no powered readiness check. It does not scan and remove already active diseases or poison effects in the implant method, so prevention and existing-status cleanup are distinct. [CyberneticsBionicLiver](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsBionicLiver.cs)

Reactive Cranial Plating registers CanApply/Apply Dazed and Stun vetoes, then unregisters on removal. This supplies a concrete counter to cudgel status chains, without claiming removal of existing Stun. Generic EffectSuppressor instead has a configurable comma-list of effect class names and Chance default100. Implanting removes matching existing effects without consulting Chance/readiness in that loop. CanApply only pre-vetoes when Chance>=100 and ready without charge consumption; Apply rolls chance and checks readiness with charge use. Partial chances therefore do not claim deterministic immunity during prospective queries. [CyberneticsReactiveCranialPlating](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsReactiveCranialPlating.cs) [CyberneticsEffectSuppressor](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsEffectSuppressor.cs)

TonicDurationModifier is a subject-or-actor event modifier, default pass2, chance100, Percentage100 and optional type/healing filter. It requires the right checking side, active subject and readiness, then adds BaseDuration×Percentage/100 to Duration. It does not multiply an already modified current duration. Two matching modifiers could therefore differ from naive repeated doubling, subject to shared event dispatch. Removal restores no prior tonic duration in this class: it affects duration construction, rather than owning an ongoing duration lease. [CyberneticsTonicDurationModifier](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsTonicDurationModifier.cs)

## C10: Inflatable Axons owns both a boost and a consequence

Defaults are+40 Quickness for10; compute scales both using integer (100+compute)/100. Activation applies AxonsInflated and starts100 cooldown; no energy cost or IsReady gate appears in this IPart command body. AI admission requires the implanted actor, distance<=1, readiness, target damage/challenge tests unless corresponding AI override properties exist. The damage predicate is specifically !target.isDamaged(0.2,inclusive:true), not a plain currentHP>20% claim. [CyberneticsInflatableAxons](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsInflatableAxons.cs)

AxonsInflated applies an owned Speed shift. At action start it ends on no cell, world map, invalid source item or source no longer implanted in this actor; otherwise it decrements. Removing it clears the shift and attempts AxonsDeflated(10,10,Source). Deflated supplies-10 Quickness, restores on removal, and decrements at action start; it has no continuing-source validation. Removing the implant therefore does not simply leave a free permanent boost or automatically erase the aftereffect. Application vetoes can still prevent either effect. [AxonsInflated](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Effects.AxonsInflated.cs) [AxonsDeflated](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Effects.AxonsDeflated.cs)

## C11: movement denial and teleport are not one escape system

Anchor Spikes default on. While toggled, CanBeInvoluntarilyMoved and IsRootedInPlace events return false for the implantee; the latter event's exact boolean convention must be read before interpreting its name. For saves applicable to Move it adds Difficulty to Roll and ignores natural1. The command and acceptance handlers have no IsReady gate in this class. It counters paths that honor these events; it is not source proof every teleport, scripted displacement or voluntary retreat is denied. [CyberneticsAnchorSpikes](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsAnchorSpikes.cs)

Matter Recompositer selects a random explored cell empty of solids for the actor in its current zone. No world-map use; no eligible cell or reality-transit veto stops early. After attempting TeleportTo it still announces teleport and starts a compute-reduced100 cooldown even if TeleportTo returned false. No energy payment is shown in this handler. AI movement uses distance>=40 and10% admission; offense>=40 and5%; retreat allows missing target cell or distance>=5, with readiness/identity/reality checks. The requested tactical destination is not directly selected by the command body. [CyberneticsMatterRecompositer](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsMatterRecompositer.cs)

Onboard Recoiler uses inherited AttemptTeleport, pays1000 and starts compute-reduced50 cooldown only on its success. The dynamic command checks actor==implantee but passes ThePlayer to ActuateTeleport; its item-action route passes E.Actor instead. This player-specific command choice is important before claiming NPC parity. No explicit AI admission appears in the class. Destination validation, danger restrictions and transmission are inherited ITeleporter work, still unread here. [CyberneticsOnboardRecoilerTeleporter](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsOnboardRecoilerTeleporter.cs)

## C12: social compute creates a separate companion limit

Social Coprocessor adds a ready Proselytize limit bonus, default1 adjusted upward by compute. Integer truncation means20compute still yields1;100compute yields2, before other suppliers. It does not increase Beguiling's limit through this handler. Losing operational support can therefore reduce one recruitment route's budget, with eviction enforced when the supplier's sync runs, not a demonstrated immediate unimplant purge.

Water-ritual reward handlers are explicitly player-gated. They compare current bonus with Record.LastSocialCoprocessorBonus and award only a positive difference, recording the higher award. Reinstalling the same bonus does not by itself promise infinite awards. Persistent negative WaterRitualUse costs get default20% reduction adjusted by compute; prospective readiness does not consume charge, actual changes can. The SocialSifrah setup handler adds Rating5 and Turns1 without an explicit readiness/owner guard in its own body. Final event routing remains a shared question. [CyberneticsSocialCoprocessor](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsSocialCoprocessor.cs)

## C13: automatic gas and liquid output has its own lifecycle

Anomaly Fumigator ticks when implanted, local and toggled on, picks0..2 distinct empty neighboring cells and creates NormalityGas with a random10..120 density adjusted by compute. It is an IPart handler with no readiness/EMP/charge check shown there. It does not set Creator, Level or carry phase in this method, unlike mutation gas generation. Unimplant removes the toggle ability but does not sweep already emitted gas from the world. Gas lifetime and normality effects remain owned by those generated objects. [CyberneticsAnomalyFumigator](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsAnomalyFumigator.cs)

Fire Suppression ticks while the subject is aflame and toggle on, applies a real LiquidCovered("gel",2..4) and splash. It does not directly clear a fire flag or temperature in this body. The gel/liquid/thermal consumer determines final extinguishing. No explicit readiness gate appears in this class. Unimplant removes the ability without removing existing coating. Automation can be NPC-generic even where messages differ. [CyberneticsFireSuppressionSystem](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.CyberneticsFireSuppressionSystem.cs)

## G1: poison gas has immediate and lingering stages

GasPoison requires respiration, Creature tag, phase match, shared gas acceptance, poison-effect acceptance and positive respiratory-agent performance. Exposure removes an existing PoisonGasPoison, applies a fresh1..10 duration with Damage=GasLevel×2, then separately deals max(floor((performance+1)/20),1) inhaled Poison/Gas damage. GasLevel is the behavior's own field here; do not assume mutation Gas.Level automatically overwrites it without the composition/sync consumer.

Lingering PoisonGasPoison ticks at BeforeBeginTakeAction only if the current cell lacks any GasPoison part. It deals its configured damage, then decrements its own finite duration. While still in that gas, the lingering handler itself does not count down or deal its separate damage. Recuperating sets duration0; general amnesty clears Owner attribution. The cell test does not filter gas creator in this body. Bionic Liver intercepts both effect admission and Poison damage, providing a connected counter; respiration and phase supply other gates. [GasPoison](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.GasPoison.cs) [PoisonGasPoison](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Effects.PoisonGasPoison.cs)

## G2: sleep/confusion exposure and navigation

Sleep gas applies on cell entry and TurnTick. It needs a Brain, respiration, phase, gas eligibility, several sleep acceptance events, no existing Asleep, and positive respiratory-agent performance. It then tests Toughness at difficulty5+Gas.Level+performance/10 and applies Asleep4d6+Gas.Level on failure. Confusion similarly applies on entry/tick, tests the same difficulty, and applies Confused(4d6+level,level,level+2,"ToxicConfusion"). It checks its own acceptance events; the sleep class contains the exact legacy token CanApplySleegas. These are retained as exact private code, not silently corrected names. [GasSleep L73-165](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.GasSleep.cs) [GasConfusion L72-150](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.GasConfusion.cs)

Both gases contribute actor-sensitive navigation weights. Smart navigation asks whether the actor can be affected, marks the result uncacheable and scales hazard weight with density/level; unbreathing, phase mismatch or IgnoreGases bypass these selected branches. Sleep uses a main-cell weight capped by min(50+5L,70), confusion by60. Adjacent hazard weights are smaller. Density-step changes flush navigation caches. Poison has its own similar smart/ordinary weights. This is concrete NPC environmental response, but no proof the planner always avoids a lethal cloud. Exposure, immunity, path cost and AI command choice are four distinct consumers. [GasSleep L29-71,95-101](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.GasSleep.cs) [GasConfusion L30-100](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.GasConfusion.cs) [GasPoison L28-74](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.GasPoison.cs)

## C14: item armor damage now has a closed stat consumer

ShatteredArmor requires an equipped Armor item, caps each incoming Amount to current armor.AV and refuses nonpositive penalty. It subtracts Amount directly from Armor.AV. Reapplication temporarily restores the old amount, adds the new amount and reapplies; duration becomes the maximum. Removal adds the accumulated amount back. Consequently the selected player Cleave route damages equipment AV, not only an actor's displayed condition. Repair events and value/name changes are retained in the full file, while equipped AV-cache propagation and every unequip/repair order remain unexercised. [ShatteredArmor L107-183](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Effects.ShatteredArmor.cs)
