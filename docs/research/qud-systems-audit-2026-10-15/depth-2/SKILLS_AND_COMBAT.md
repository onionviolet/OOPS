# Remaining skill consumers: stances, reactions, ranged effects and resistance

2026-10-15. Static source evidence from assembly2.0.211.56. Costs are handler arguments before shared modifiers; target/effect gates can still refuse the result. Prior skill acquisition and support ownership remain in the [first supplement](../depth-1/SKILLS.md).

## S6: long blades share one controller

LongBladesSkillBase requires LongBladesCore when a supporting skill is added. Removing a skill asks whether other support remains; the core removes itself only when the support event does not find another provider. Removing the core removes its two base stance abilities and all three stance effects plus En Garde. This is reference-count-like support by events rather than one independent controller per skill. [LongBladesSkillBase](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.LongBladesSkillBase.cs) [LongBladesCore L168-175,217-224](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.LongBladesCore.cs)

With a primary long blade, aggressive stance gives +1 penetration, or +2 with its improvement, and -2 to hit, or -3 improved. Those hit/penetration hooks also allow ShortBlades attacks when the primary long-blade requirement holds. Dueling gives +2/+3 to hit through the same family condition. Defensive stance owns a +2/+3 DV stat shift, removed before recalculation when leaving or refreshing defensive stance. Equipment/primary-limb events refresh stance state. These bonuses do not require every attacking weapon to itself be the primary long blade. [LongBladesCore L75-87,254-267,300-355](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.LongBladesCore.cs)

| Action | Aggressive | Defensive | Dueling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lunge | Requires a clear intermediate combat cell and a target two cells away; DirectMoveTo must succeed; adds +2 penetration modifier and cap | Attacks an adjacent target only if phase and flight match, then tries two backward pushes and gravitates | Adjacent attack with Autohit, Autopen, Lunging and +1 penetration modifier/cap, still phase checked |
| Swipe | Attacks targets around all directions when brainless or hostile towards the actor | Pushes adjacent matter-phase1 targets, then Agility/Strength save difficulty30 against Prone | Attempts Disarm with difficulty25 and Strength/Agility, then an Autohit/Autopen melee attack |

The defensive Swipe loop has no hostility filter in the read body, unlike aggressive Swipe. Friendly displacement is therefore a source-level consideration, not a measured friendly-fire frequency. Lunge/Swipe paths normally pay1000 energy and start cooldown15, but while Ultmode>0 the cooldown calls are skipped. En Garde requires the primary long blade, sets Ultmode10, starts its own100 cooldown with Agility tag and directly zeroes existing Lunge/Swipe cooldowns. Its visible effect has duration9999; the controller's action-start counter removes it. Deathblow's own skill class refuses cooldown refresh while that effect is present. No energy cost appears in the En Garde command branch itself. [LongBladesCore L410-760](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.LongBladesCore.cs) [LongBladesDeathblow](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.LongBladesDeathblow.cs) [LongbladeEffect_EnGarde](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Effects.LongbladeEffect_EnGarde.cs)

AI chooses aggressive by default, defensive when `isDamaged(0.6)`, or dueling for challenge>=0.8 if the skill exists. It offers a stance change at weight10. At adjacency, a ready En Garde against that challenge returns early from the AI handler; otherwise it can offer Lunge in nonaggressive stance and Swipe. At distance2 it offers aggressive Lunge. This is actual admission policy, not observed tactical mastery. [LongBladesCore L91-165](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Parts.LongBladesCore.cs)

## S7: Hobble and Rejoinder have different budgets

Hobble requires a primary short blade, movement of extremities and a selected combat target. It temporarily sets Hobbling, attempts a melee attack with Autopen/Maxpen1, then pays1000 or750 with ShortBlades Expertise and starts30 cooldown tagged Agility. Its after-damage hook applies Hobbled for16..20; the temporary flag is cleared in a finally block, including exceptions/cancellation. The AI offer checks adjacency, extremities and ability readiness but leaves the primary-blade requirement to execution. [ShortBlades_Hobble](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.ShortBlades_Hobble.cs)

Hobbled actually adds50 to MoveSpeed cost, extends an existing duration to the maximum and restores owned shifts on removal. It decrements at BeginTakeAction. Its description says -50% speed, but this implementation is an additive50 shift in that stat. The Apply guard is also unusually written: it combines missing MoveSpeed with an ApplyHobble event using AND, not an unconditional immunity veto. This is a pinned-source observation, not a live bug finding. [Hobbled L47-86](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Effects.Hobbled.cs)

Rejoinder resets Checked at the owner's BeginTakeAction. The first DefenderAfterAttackMissed sets Checked before toggle/readiness, the60% roll, movement or weapon checks. Failure there spends that reaction opportunity for the interval. It gathers equipped/default short blades from body parts excluding Thrown Weapon, randomly selects one and attacks the aggressor. No explicit UseEnergy call occurs in this reaction handler. Its default-on toggle and generic actor path permit NPC reactions without an active AI command. Extra short blades change the candidate pool; this is not one rejoinder per blade or every miss. [ShortBlades_Rejoinder](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.ShortBlades_Rejoinder.cs)

## S8: rifle modes and pistol cost stack in consumers

The skill command classes mostly validate a ready bow/rifle and call the shared firing path. Actual mode effects occur in MissileWeapon and require Defender==AimedAt and a combat object in the selected hit branch. Suppressing/Flattening Fire, or One Shot with Suppressive Fire, attempts Suppressed for3..5. With Flattening Fire and its criterion, it also attempts Prone and Disarm. The criterion counts at least six solid neighboring cells, so terrain is part of this upgrade. [Rifle_SuppressiveFire](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Rifle_SuppressiveFire.cs) [Rifle_FlatteningFire](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Rifle_FlatteningFire.cs) [MissileWeapon L1855-1885](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.MissileWeapon.cs)

Wounding/Disorienting Fire, or One Shot with Wounding Fire, applies nonstacking Bleeding with the current damage variable as its damage string and save difficulty20+maximum base-damage roll. Disorienting Fire additionally tests absence of another positively shared allegiance in a wide visibility scan, then applies Disoriented5..7 with parameter4. That is a faction/neighbor condition rather than a simple distance-to-an-ally test. The exact Disoriented/Suppressed effect bodies remain unread. [Rifle_WoundingFire](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Rifle_WoundingFire.cs) [Rifle_DisorientingFire](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Rifle_DisorientingFire.cs) [MissileWeapon L1886-1912](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.MissileWeapon.cs)

Draw a Bead's downstream benefit is now located: for compatible bow/rifle shots, a mark whose Marker==Attacker subtracts one from the selected aim-variance accumulator. Other adjustments still follow. The benefit is owner-specific. [MissileWeapon L2762-2775](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.MissileWeapon.cs)

Empty the Clips applies a21-duration instance for an advertised20 rounds, starts200 cooldown tagged Agility and disables its own ability at action start while the effect remains. Duplicate effects retain the longer duration. AI offers it at50% with a pistol, valid extremities and readiness. The effect class does not itself contain the firing discount; MissileWeapon multiplies pistol energy by0.5, then by0.75 for Fastest Gun, then by (100-PistolEnergyModifier)/100, and truncates the final EnergyCost product to an integer. For base1000 with only the first two, that arithmetic yields375. This is a cost example, not observed firing throughput or free ammunition. [Pistol_EmptyTheClips](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Pistol_EmptyTheClips.cs) [EmptyTheClips](../private/depth-2/source/XRL.World.Effects.EmptyTheClips.cs) [MissileWeapon L3537-3563](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.MissileWeapon.cs)

## S9: defensive resistance and fear

Shake It Off reduces Poison-attributed damage to three quarters with integer truncation and vetoes zero damage. Its stun/daze application veto and later tick removal roll use Toughness-10, modified by an event on actor and body parts. It can independently attempt removal of Dazed and Stun. The tick routine does not repeat Amount times for batched time. This is neither blanket poison immunity nor a fixed50% status defense. [Endurance_ShakeItOff](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Endurance_ShakeItOff.cs)

Intimidate checks neighboring hostility in either direction and performs mental attacks with1d8, modifier Ego mod+2×the relevant property, and6d4 duration argument. It pays1000 only upon encountering the first qualifying hostile during that call, then avoids paying again for other neighbors. Its command starts50 cooldown, reduced by10 for Horrifying, even when no qualifying target is reached. The actual invoked callback is Terrified.OfAttacker; the local Terrify helper is not automatically the used path. AI admission is adjacent, brain-bearing target, readiness and10% roll. Full fear movement/cleanup remains a downstream effect question. [Persuasion_Intimidate](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Skill.Persuasion_Intimidate.cs)
