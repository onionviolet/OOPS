## Q16: forcefield passage and material effects

CanPass checks IgnoresForceWall and ForcefieldNullifier before Unwalkable. It also permits a moving field's own creator before that restriction. Other passage routes include an explicit object list, allowed factions, player control when that faction token is present, a tag/string-property match and the creator when RejectOwner is false. A faction membership threshold is checked through actual allegiance, not only a displayed label. [Forcefield L175-227](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.Parts.Forcefield.cs)

Missile passage is different: an actual projectile may have IgnoresForceWall or satisfy CanMissilePassForcefieldEvent **before** MissileOpaque. It then tests lists/factions/tags/creator. The event's final result is inverted: actor legacy/typed event veto becomes Check=true, which the field treats as passage. This closes the nullifier event-protocol question left open in depth1. The implant's carryover projectile/actor conditions still matter; not every shot automatically inherits every actor property. Force Wall's owner-passage configuration therefore has a direct consumer, while nullifier traversal is a separate override. Fields additionally request100% liquid-exposure reduction. Reality stabilization can discharge1d8 electricity with random voltage1..4 toward a neighboring cell, then destroy the field. Collapsing a barrier is thus a world action with a secondary consequence, not just removing collision. ForcefieldMaterial implements the same selected liquid/stabilization behavior for objects composed with it. Moving-owner fields have property-attack/anger consumers, and ShouldAttackToReachTarget tells an obstructed actor to attack the field when it cannot pass and is not the creator. This links a local obstacle rule to NPC action admission and social memory, without proving the planner successfully breaks it. ## Q17: stasis is same-cell support, with several interaction vetoes

Stasisfield processes at EndTurn and cell-entry events, copies the cell's object list, and ForceApplyEffects Stasis on eligible real objects sharing its phase. It avoids its own object, missing/currently removed occupants and actors already in stasis. ForcefieldNullifier makes an actor ineligible. The field's own Creator does not create an exemption inside ProcessStasis. The entangler's exempt cells come from its deployment selection, not this generic field consumer. [Stasisfield L80-98](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.Parts.Stasisfield.cs) [Stasis L230-244](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.Effects.Stasis.cs)

Stasis.Apply requires a matching Stasisfield in the actor's cell and forfeits the current turn. Its early action handler prevents action, its AI handler vetoes action, and it vetoes damage, new effects, replication, dismemberment, involuntary movement and conversational response. It also blocks temperature return-to-ambient and gives extreme kinetic resistance through an event. These are specific hooks; they are not proof every possible direct stat edit or thermal API honors stasis. [Stasis L30-158](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.Effects.Stasis.cs)

Maintenance runs at EndTurn and early action. It requires the actor remain real, valid, without ForcefieldNullifier, in a cell containing some phase-matching Stasisfield. On field destruction, ShutdownStasis calls maintenance with the destroyed field as Skip while its cell is still available. Another matching field can sustain the same effect, so deleting one supplier need not release the actor. No matching field removes the effect. This connects Temporary field expiry to actor release through the ordinary destroy event. [Stasisfield L64-67,101-111](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.Parts.Stasisfield.cs) [Stasis L198-244](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.Effects.Stasis.cs) [GameObject L4137-4147](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.GameObject.cs)

One source asymmetry remains: Stasis declares resistance-shift helper methods, but normal Apply/Remove do not call them. Its copy-without-effects callbacks do. Do not count the declared+1000 resistances as normal application merely from the helpers' existence; that copy path deserves a separate stat-state check. ## Q18: destruction tears down context; it is not recursive content disposal

Obliterate delegates to Destroy with an Obliterate flag. Destroy can be vetoed by BeforeDestroyObjectEvent. Player/checkpoint/body-control branches can return early, distinct from ordinary object teardown. On the ordinary path it zeroes energy, sends OnDestroyObject **before** physical teardown, clears sidebar target and calls Physics.TeardownForDestroy. [GameObject L4046-4147](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.GameObject.cs)

Physics teardown force-unequips the object, clears default-slot references, removes it from an enclosing inventory and cell, removes active-object registration and adds it to a zone or global graveyard. The unequip loop has a100-iteration guard. It requests existence synchronization when leaving a cell if the dying object can provide support. This body has no blanket recursive destruction of the dying object's inventory or every child. [Physics L3862-3929](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.Parts.Physics.cs)

The earlier Temporary no-context issue is consequently narrower but still open: RemoveContents returns when there is no usable enclosing/cell context, and ordinary teardown does not visibly rescue every remaining child. Specific OnDestroy listeners or later graveyard handling may still own those objects. This pass does not claim an orphan leak or duplication exploit. Complete parent/child/graveyard serialization and all destruction listeners remain unread.

## Q20: flight loss can trigger a terrain-dependent fall

Flying itself flushes navigation caches on apply/remove, offers a companion stop action and lands when entering Engulfing/Engulfed movement. In its electrical-conductivity event, a nonflying source is given conductivity0 against the flying object on pass1. Shared flight has already been traced through source-count-aware stop/failure and Gravitate. Gravitate checks IsSubjectToGravity, then dispatches a forced downward Gravitation event to the current cell. This is not automatic HP damage on ordinary ground. [Flying](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.Effects.Flying.cs) [GameObject L17897-17902](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.GameObject.cs) [GravitationEvent L56-79](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.GravitationEvent.cs)

StairsDown with PullDown consumes gravity/cell-entry events, checks CanFall and BeforePullDown, obtains a landing and may shift it to a nearby passable cell within2. It calls SystemMoveTo with forced/zero energy, then applies same-phase, low-matter-phase collision damage to occupants. Each occupant receives1..4 Crushing; on a short fall the falling actor also gets1..4 at the last such collision. ## Q22: Burrowing is an actor effect with its own exit contract

Burrowing activation checks posture/mode/world-map, applies Burrowed with move-cost penalty100-10L, spends1000 and starts max(15-L,5) cooldown. While burrowed, BeforeTakeAction disables/restarts the ability cooldown. Bored AI may automatically burrow unless its librarian/target condition prevents it; village initialization disables the bored behavior. The separate defensive admission offers it while not already burrowed and usable. [Burrowing](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.Burrowing.cs)

Burrowed hides Render.Visible and owns a MoveSpeed shift plus a Stop Burrowing ability. Removal restores visibility, removes its owned shift and stop ability. It prevents ordinary attacks, voluntary extremity/posture/mode changes and long-distance travel. External nonbleeding damage with another owner removes the effect and returns false from LateBeforeApplyDamage; self/no-owner/bleeding paths are different. Direct movement/teleport also removes it; boredom can bring an untargeted NPC to the surface. Copy-without-effects callbacks unapply/reapply the visibility/stat state. Restoring Visible=true is a direct assignment, so overlap with another invisibility source is not established as ownership-safe. Unmutate in the supplier only removes its entry ability and does not explicitly remove Burrowed. [Burrowed](../private/depth-4/source/XRL.World.Effects.Burrowed.cs)

## Q24: a vortex is an independent timed object with deferred victims

Spacetime Vortex costs1000 and cooldown max(550-50L,5), validates a target path distance<=5, then creates a Space-Time Vortex on a randomly selected neighbor of that cell; player casting also includes the selected cell in the candidate list. It assigns Temporary duration15..18 plus L-10 above rank10. The source mutation does not retain that object for cancellation on unmutation. [SpacetimeVortex L91-174](../private/depth-1/source/XRL.World.Parts.Mutation.SpacetimeVortex.cs)

The object rejects scenery, nonreal targets, phase4, excavatory terrain and explicit ignore tags. An explicitly tracked player/important/interesting or nontrifling player-led actor forces destination resolution. Other eligible objects can be removed from context, made inactive and placed in a deferred Queue with their original coordinates while destination is unresolved. Resolution later teleports valid contextless victims or obliterates them if no destination/landing exists, then clears the queue. Both Queue and Locations are NonSerialized, and the read class has no OnDestroy queue flush. Vortex expiry or save interruption before resolution is therefore a concrete unresolved retention/cleanup path, not a proven world-deletion bug.
