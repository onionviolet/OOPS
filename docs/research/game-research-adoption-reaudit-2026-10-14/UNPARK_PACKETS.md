# Unpark packets, 2026-10-14

**PROPOSALS. No implementation or owner change is authorized by this document.** These packets make old parking decisions reviewable. The ranking is payoff for the next playable slice, not a new world priority list. HP, D&D, priority book and anime worlds keep their accepted order. Every option below may be authored in a fitting world; none must become a global rule.

Sizes are planning estimates: S, one leaf content/host/view change; M, several receiving owners or optional saved state; L, a new local execution model or transition contract. They are not delivery promises. Receiving owner means the system to consult, not a claim that a named agent has accepted ownership.

All packets share this later done check: reconcile live ownership and accepted direction, finish one connected paid/useful result, run focused source checks, rebuild the selected checkout, exercise at 1440x900 with a 1366x768 fit check from a fresh disposable origin, and inspect actual costs, output and changed downstream action. Persistent changes require Save/reload/Export/literal-file Import into a second fresh origin, an explicit old-save compatibility decision, and failure/cancellation conservation. Label natural and seeded-native evidence separately. Keep private saves untouched. Existing failed/stopped Foundation attempts stay exact and are not silently retried. No copied source/game data, new general inventory/scheduler/publisher, hidden-truth disclosure or mobile gate.

A settled receipt cannot pay again when rendered, loaded or replayed. That is transaction idempotency. A new supported occurrence may recur wherever its world's stock, conditions and rules permit. Do not turn a first proof's finite stock into a universal lifetime “once” law.
