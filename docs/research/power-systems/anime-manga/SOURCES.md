# Lane A sources

Checked 2026-10-10. Mechanics paraphrased in original words. No panels, raw chapters, prose corpus, logins or purchases were used.

Most evidence is a public wiki extract surfaced by web search, not a chapter read. JO1 was opened and its main manga sections inspected. DA4 and FF1 are licensed distributor descriptions, not proof of every fan-wiki detail. Reference-verified is deliberately narrower than primary-canon verified. A source ID on an unverified claim is a follow-up pointer, not an evidence upgrade.

Direct JJK Binding Vow open failed with 402. Frieren Magic open returned an internal fetch error. Switched to indexed references and alternate pages; no retry of failed fetches. Requests were made serially; no chapter crawler or same-host concurrent fetches were run. Search-result spam PDFs, unofficial full texts, AI chat sites and fan games were excluded. JoJo spin-offs and JJK sequel entries on mixed pages were excluded.
