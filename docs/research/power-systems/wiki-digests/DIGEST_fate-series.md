## Fandom dump revision review

Evidence: dated wiki snapshots, not a primary book/manga/game audit. Only the sections cited below were reviewed; archived catalogs and character histories were not certified complete. Every model mapping and takeaway remains a proposal.

**DF1 Command Spell / Obtaining Command Spells; Usage.** Fuyuki grants three command charges per Master, including a Master with several Servants. Specificity, available energy and agreement affect enforcement. Broad orders can be resisted. [Command Spell r404906](https://typemoon.fandom.com/wiki/Command_Spell?oldid=404906).

Proposal: Separate authority charges, resource support and counterparty consent; do not promise success for every command.

**DF2 Servant / Characteristics.** The ritual supplies a class vessel for a copy drawn from a heroic record. A vessel selects a subset of the hero's abilities rather than reproducing all of them. [Servant r405040](https://typemoon.fandom.com/wiki/Servant?oldid=405040).

Proposal: Keep the source record, summoned instance and enabled kit as separate entities.

**DF3 Command Spell / Usage.** A spoken command and its bodily carrier can be interrupted. Losing all charges does not make every Servant relationship end in the same way. [Command Spell r404906](https://typemoon.fandom.com/wiki/Command_Spell?oldid=404906).

Proposal: A rule power needs an executable delivery path, while social loyalty can persist after compulsion ends.
