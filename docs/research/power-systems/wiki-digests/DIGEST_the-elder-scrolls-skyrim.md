### Law lore for E9

DS1 to DS3: shouts give words mechanical effects, while hold-based bounties give civic authorities local jurisdiction. A title, witness or guild route changes enforcement. Word magic is not proof that every civil decree edits reality. This digest uses Skyrim-tagged or Skyrim-named pages only.
