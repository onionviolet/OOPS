## Fandom dump revision review

Evidence: dated wiki snapshots, not a primary book/manga/game audit. Only the sections cited below were reviewed; archived catalogs and character histories were not certified complete. Every model mapping and takeaway remains a proposal.

**DO1 Devil Fruit / Introduction; Origins.** Eating a fruit grants a distinct power with a swimming drawback. Its power normally has one bearer and returns to fruit after that bearer dies; the page records an exceptional multiple-power bearer. The proposed origin theory is not certified fact. [Devil Fruit r2129490](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Devil_Fruit?oldid=2129490).

Proposal: Keep a source's reuse cycle and exceptional carrier explicit, and label an in-world origin theory as a theory.
