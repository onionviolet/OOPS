## Fandom dump revision review

Evidence: dated wiki snapshots, not a primary book/manga/game audit. Only the sections cited below were reviewed; archived catalogs and character histories were not certified complete. Every model mapping and takeaway remains a proposal.

**DC1 Sponsors / Getting Sponsors; Sponsorship Tab.** A sponsorship fee and auction position do not guarantee useful aid. Ratings affect patronage, and a sponsor's bid can alter the price of later gift boxes. The interface allows one direction of direct messages but not the other. Proposal: Separate nominal support, purchasing budget, delivered aid and allowed communication channels.

### Ultra-Stabilized Size-Adjustable Race-Adjustable Alleviating Sleep Apparatus

[Revision 21885](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Ultra-Stabilized_Size-Adjustable_Race-Adjustable_Alleviating_Sleep_Apparatus?oldid=21885).
Two hours of sleep grants full rest and 30 hours of Good Rest. Tier-one upgrade, 250,000 gold or a coupon; later replaced by Instacot.

### Upgraded Shower

[Revision 15496](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Upgraded_Shower?oldid=15496).
Adds ten percent to all base stats and lowers detectability for 30 hours. Precise stacking/reset and detection rules unspecified.

### Food Box

[Revision 15377](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Food_Box?oldid=15377).
Three meal cabinets give daily adaptive options and Full buff. Each cabinet can be used once per day; repeated choices can lock the menu to one option, described as buggy rather than a reliable planned upgrade.

### Enchanted Right Back Atcha Personal Shield

[Revision 17804](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Enchanted_Right_Back_Atcha_Personal_Shield?oldid=17804).
Physical interception depends on dexterity; magic-bolt interception on intelligence. After actual buckler interception, reflection chance is 75 percent; after successful reflection, doubling chance is 25 percent. Do not apply these conditional rates to every incoming attack.

### Bopca

[Revision 25805](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Bopca?oldid=25805).
Alien race supplying safe-room attendants by contracts exchanged for spared homeworlds. Restricted information access: safe rooms/personal spaces permitted, finer dungeon points barred, newsletter instead of live newsfeed.
Proprietor reward incentives resist discounting; negotiations can lower stated prices by up to 25 percent and yield extra skill experience. No breach sanction or contract exit procedure is given.

### Welcome to the Neighborhood! Achievement

[Revision 25390](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Welcome_to_the_Neighborhood%21_Achievement?oldid=25390).
Purchasing a personal space triggers achievement, then entrance opening and communal naming. The infobox says Ninth Floor but body/story/category say Fourth Floor; unresolved internal mismatch. Ownership rather than a loot prize is the stated reward.

### Cleaner Bot

[Revision 27428](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Cleaner_Bot?oldid=27428).
Cleaning includes small-fire foam, rescue of a fragile book and later disintegration/sorting. A looted bot can enter inventory and be reset/reassigned. Personality and refusal to clean crafting tables are described.
Weight-based utility storage accepts a crawler in a pet carrier that normal personal inventory rejects. Listing the loaded bot for sale lets a standing purchaser transfer it to an outside holding area; sequence, carrier timing, capacity and buyer order are essential. Resale locks the new owner out for thirty days.
The article reports Li Na as a successful first test, then 227 evacuated via several units; one holds forty people and another 105. These are instances, not a fixed universal capacity. Evacuation does not certify cure or final freedom; later visibility/fate is uncertain. Book-nine spoiler wrapper contains Book-eight assertions, a markup/content mismatch.

### Doggie Door

[Revision 26360](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Doggie_Door?oldid=26360).
Shared access travel uses previously used doors on the same floor; opening/story also describe party-location travel. Usual tier-three availability is ninth-floor; early received instances occur eighth-floor. Guild-wide installation is 25 million gold. Do not infer cross-floor or arbitrary-location travel.

### Instacot 60

[Revision 21889](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Instacot_60?oldid=21889).
Full rest after sixty seconds; Great Rest replaces Good Rest. The standalone page calls the thirty-hour duration presumptive, so keep uncertainty even when another page supplies a confident buff summary.

### Personal Space

[Revision 26775](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Personal_Space?oldid=26775).
Safe-room rules forbid crawler violence/theft and teleport attacking mobs. Later blood-bar access consumes roughly ten hours per full bar and requires kills to refill before re-entry. Private whitelists and level-dependent guest access are separate from protection.
Purchase has a stated price floor, negotiable margin and possible proprietor liability for a below-floor agreement. Coupons can grant spaces; combining requires same party and 5,000 gold per union. Two level-three spaces yield four, three yield five; only one merger participant needs level three.
Upgrades share benefits but retain purchaser ownership, depart with that owner and disappear on death. Coupons cap at 250,000 and cannot combine for one purchase. Sixth-floor guild sharing need not require one party.
NPCs need crawler escort into specialized rooms. The generated entry vestibule routes only to accessible rooms. An additional internal storey is Mordecai’s speculation, not an implemented world rule.
AI compensation coupons counter a corporate reward veto and shift production/sponsor spending; gift/controller authority and corporation/sponsor economics are distinct.

### Social Media Board

[Revision 21873](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Social_Media_Board?oldid=21873).
50,000-gold running comment feed replaces appointed corporate media manager but is heavily System-AI moderated. Access to audience speech is not unrestricted communication.

### Sun Shrine

[Revision 25927](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Shrine?oldid=25927).
Choosing Emberus worship creates a personal-space shrine. One drop of blood every 30-hour day and five percent of looted/received gold are separate recurring obligations; shrine displays accrued payment and accepts church-loan payments. Bloody water is crafting input. Breach penalty/release unspecified.

### Vocal Coach Training Room

[Revision 15384](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Vocal_Coach_Training_Room?oldid=15384).
Holographic coaching trains Singing, supporting Bard spells and substantive illusions. Available from boxes/marketplace; no numeric improvement or universal limit stated.

### Royal Palace of Princess Donut

[Revision 25136](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Royal_Palace_of_Princess_Donut?oldid=25136).
Reported three level-three spaces combine to five, with workshops upgraded by floor and Katia taking Makeup Table when leaving. The page labels the start as Third Floor while Personal Space’s detailed story says Fourth Floor; preserve cross-page chronology conflict.

### Enhanced Crafting Studio

[Revision 24754](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Enhanced_Crafting_Studio?oldid=24754).
255,000-gold space upgrade permits crafting tables and occupies about a quarter of a level-five space; specific craft success/rate rules absent.

### Training Room

[Revision 15556](https://dungeon-crawler-carl.fandom.com/wiki/Training_Room?oldid=15556).
Level one permits one hour daily on one nonmagical skill. Explosives require level two/Bomber’s Studio; generic introduction mentions skill/spell training without a full higher-level spell schedule. Exercises simulate skill-specific challenges.

### Law-lore and model proposals

FDC1: Personal-space protection, guest permission, price floors, proprietary upgrade custody and kill-refilled access time have separate rule owners and triggers. Protection against combat does not imply indefinite access. NPC escort rules differ from crawler whitelisting.
FDC2: Bopca corporate contracts exchange labor for protection of homeworlds; the source gives information restrictions and incentives but no generic supernatural breach penalty. Sun worship has periodic blood and income obligations, without a cited universal sanction or release rule.
FDC3: A cleaner bot exploits a mismatch between utility storage, crawler inventory, carrier containment and marketplace shipping. A successful transfer to an outside holding area is not a court ruling, a completed crawl or a certified cure.
PDC1, proposal for E8: preserve interception, conditional reflection and conditional doubling as successive events; do not grant the final probability to every attack. PDC2, proposal for E8B: separate vendor incentives, storage carriers, delivery channels and effect families. PDC3, proposal for E8C: keep housing level, upgrade tier, party/guild access and skill training separate. PDC4, proposal for E8D/E9: record the actual obligation, deadline, information rights, access policy and known sanction independently; leave untested breach consequences unknown. These are research proposals, not accepted design changes.
