## Fandom dump revision review

Evidence: dated wiki snapshots, not a primary book/manga/game audit. Only the sections cited below were reviewed; archived catalogs and character histories were not certified complete. Every model mapping and takeaway remains a proposal.

**DT1 Covenant / Changing covenants.** Leaving and being expelled have different consequences for contribution progress and re-entry. Absolution and a living covenant leader can matter. The bonfire switching route is labeled Remastered. [Covenant r362364](https://darksouls.fandom.com/wiki/Covenant?oldid=362364).

Proposal: Separate release, breach, expulsion, accumulated offerings and edition-specific re-entry.
