### The Nightmare Spell

[The Nightmare Spell revision 802](https://shadow-slave.fandom.com/wiki/The_Nightmare_Spell?oldid=802).
The Spell infects young people, usually around ages 16–18; infected people become very sleepy and are transported to an individually faced First Nightmare after falling asleep. The Spell appeared after disasters and resource wars. Its nature is described as unknown; the article relays debate about whether it is sentient.
Nephis attributes fate-manipulating behavior to the Spell; distinguish her statement from the wiki’s neutral explanation that its nature is unknown.
Trials contain humans and monsters described as illusory and a central conflict to resolve. Their conflicts are believed to reconstruct ancient Dream Realm events. Infection causes persistent fatigue/sleepiness, then an unnatural coma; infected people who die during sleep become Nightmare Creatures.
Each trial is tailored to its Aspirant and typically contains one creature, often Awakened Beast or Monster, sometimes Demon, rarely Devil. Role selection is partially near the participant’s real body, but principles are unknown. The page advises checking Attributes and Aspect and says environment/survival is commonly a central challenge.
Seeds form in the Dream Realm and multiple Awakened may challenge them. Success grants an additional Aspect Ability and Master title. Ascended-rank people are barred from challenging.
Multiple Awakened may challenge; Saints are barred while lower-rank Awakened can challenge. A successful challenger can become Saint directly. Skipping Master may produce a physically weaker Saint because the person has not filled the core as much. The article says humanity has conquered through the Third Nightmare.
From the Second Nightmare onward, if challengers die or fail to find the Seed before it matures, a Gate opens in the real world and monsters pour through. Gates have ranks and potentially admit any creature type; the described result is major destruction and civilian losses.
Creature power has a seven-rank ladder from Dormant to Unholy, based on core quality, and a separate seven-class ladder from Beast to Titan, based on one to seven Soul Cores. The page says Beasts are strong but mindless, Monsters have rudimentary warped intelligence, and Tyrants can make minions.
The Spell communicates in a dead Rune language, which has fine semantic distinctions that cannot be reliably flattened by translation.
Limits and conflicts: The Spell’s agency, selection logic, Nightmare construction, success evaluation, and full creature class abilities remain partly unknown. The page gives no precise Gate rank calculation, maturity timer, or challenge coordination rules. Its “fairness” comparison says Nightmares are constrained relative to the open Dream Realm but does not enumerate all trial limits. Character dialogue attributes broad perception and fate play to the Spell, while the article separately says its sentience and agency cannot be known; keep these as attributed belief versus open system question.

### The Awakened

[The Awakened revision 798](https://shadow-slave.fandom.com/wiki/The_Awakened?oldid=798).
Awakened are people who completed the first trial and have a Dream Realm connection and an Aspect; their role includes seeking Nightmares to prevent Gates. Those infected but not yet through the First Nightmare are called Aspirants.
If a Sleeper/Awakened dies in the Dream Realm and the soul is not destroyed, the person can become a Hollow. If an Awakened’s body dies while they are in a Nightmare or Dream Realm, the page says they become Reverse Hollow and remain trapped there.
After First Nightmare, the carrier can perceive/interact with Soul Cores and gets a unique Aspect and Flaw, but lacks a Dream Realm connection until the winter-solstice transport. The explanatory text says the body slumbers while the spirit is in the Spell; duration may be days, weeks, or months.
After first return from Dream Realm, core advances to next rank, Aspect gains a second ability, and the person can perceive/interact with Soul Essence. They enter Dream Realm in spirit whenever asleep, near their anchored Gateway.
By choosing and succeeding in Second Nightmare, the person becomes Master, core advances to Ascended, and Aspect gains a third ability. They can travel physically between worlds and enter only by choice.
Conquering Third Nightmare advances core to Transcended and grants a transformation ability. Saints can travel between realms and take others with them; they are rare and described as demi-god-like.
Limits and conflicts: The page lists only the first four human rank labels despite saying there are seven Soul Ranks; it does not define the later human ranks. The page does not detail the attribute, power, or Flaw of any specific character. Number of Saints is an approximate statement in this dated wiki.

### The Dream Realm

[The Dream Realm revision 799](https://shadow-slave.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dream_Realm?oldid=799).
Dream Realm is described as vast, larger than Earth, ruined, mostly unexplored and inhabited by Nightmare Creatures, corrupted ecosystems, and human visitors. Ancient ruins indicate former civilizations. Sky configuration varies by region and the sun is unpredictable.
The open Realm has no forced story and allows free movement subject to survival; most people stay near Citadels. It has no native ordinary people according to the page, though creatures can imitate humans. Death here does not spawn a creature in the real world, unlike the First Nightmare account. No fairness cap limits what creature can be encountered.
Hollow Mountains are a huge northern-border mountain region and Death Zone; even Saints are said not to return. Death Zones host Great, Cursed, or Unholy creatures.
Human fortresses are built around conquered Gateways.
A Gateway is both an anchor/entry point for Awakened and a route to exit. Sleepers escaping through one return to reality, become Awakened, advance their core and gain a second ability; thereafter sleep returns them to the Realm near their anchored Gateway.
Limits and conflicts: The Realm’s time/astronomy rules, exact travel, geography, and Gateway ownership are not specified. “No ordinary people” is the article’s broad rule; the page acknowledges human visitors and creature mimicry.

### Law-lore and model proposals

FSS1: Dark City combines a weekly shard admission charge, armed law enforcement, labor castes, patronage and an informal Memory market. The wiki gives no universal court, default penalty or appeal procedure. Authority is social and armed here; no general reality-editing legal power is established.
