### Law lore for E9

DJ1, DJ4 to DJ8: self-vows, mutual pacts, imposed bodily exchanges, rule-adding ritual and one court technique have different authority and enforcement. The court bans violence on both sides; evidence shown to a caster differs from the judge's knowledge. Its sentence can route to a tool or a particular soul. This supports an E9 procedure with jurisdiction and sanction routing, not a generic omniscient court available to every sorcerer.

### Finger Bearer

[Revision 73010](https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Finger_Bearer?oldid=73010).
Species created after a cursed spirit consumes a Sukuna finger and gestates as a womb; special grade and visible to nonsorcerers even during gestation.
On birth it manifests an incomplete innate domain without a technique, warping the birthplace. It fires unrefined energy and forms barriers despite lacking a technique.
Two instances differ substantially in strength within the same special grade. A second instance dispels Megumi’s incomplete domain by explosive energy but is then killed by a diverted shikigami attack.
Finger recovery/reconsumption transfers the specific carrier after death; rank is not a universal outcome predictor.

### Fly Heads

[Revision 61878](https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Fly_Heads?oldid=61878).
Curses below grade-four/unranked standards can be contained in talisman-wrapped boxes and used as curse-visibility tests.
Toji stores a swarm in his inventory curse and uses their energy signatures as sensor clutter to conceal his own zero-energy presence. Range, duration and exact sensor policy unspecified.

### Sorcerer Clan/Zenin Clan

[Revision 79324](https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Sorcerer_Clan/Zenin_Clan?oldid=79324).
Hereditary technique valuation drives exclusion, gendered coercion and grade-promotion obstruction. Clan power does not equal every member’s battle strength.
Naobito’s will has conditional succession: Gojo incapacitation substitutes Megumi for Naoya. Megumi initially refuses then accepts at Maki’s urging; political recognition and literal control of assets diverge.
Megumi grants warehouse access, but Ogi removes inventory and takes a hostage. A key opens the physical custody barrier; permission does not guarantee remaining stock or safety.
A plan to execute three relatives uses the accusation of plotting Gojo’s release. A disciplinary room contains grade-two-or-lower curses; punishment uses a local hazard, not a formal omniscient verdict.
Hei eligibility is semi-grade-one and above. Kukuru is compulsory martial training for male members without innate techniques; Maki’s former membership is an explicit tension with that stated general male criterion. Akashi have techniques but lack Hei qualifications.
After clan destruction, two clans seek to remove its Big Three status but Headquarters holds the decision. Battlefield destruction, successor designation and institutionally recognized status remain separate.

### Law-lore and proposals

FJFS1: The Zenin succession clause responds to Gojo’s incapacitation, then passes through acceptance and political resistance. A named successor, permission to open a storehouse and control of its contents are separate. Rival clans can propose removing clan status while Headquarters delays the decision.
FJFS2: A training/disciplinary pit and execution accusation are coercive clan practices; this source does not establish a supernatural vow, fair trial or universal procedural code. Hei/Kukuru/Akashi membership follows different capability and status criteria.
FJFS3: Fly Head swarm signatures conceal a zero-energy carrier by sensor clutter. Finger Bearers can form an incomplete domain without an innate technique, and the same special grade covers different strengths. Grades and sensed energy are incomplete evidence, not universal outcome guarantees.
PJFS1, proposal for E9: keep successor designation, accepted office, asset access, physical custody and institutional recognition separate. PJFS2, proposal for E8: let detection respond to surrounding signatures and the carrier’s own absence of a signature. PJFS3, proposal for E8C: preserve organizational eligibility, learned skill and battle power separately. No design decision is accepted here.
