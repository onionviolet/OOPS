## Fandom dump revision review

Evidence: dated wiki snapshots, not a primary book/manga/game audit. Only the sections cited below were reviewed; archived catalogs and character histories were not certified complete. Every model mapping and takeaway remains a proposal.

**DM1 Philosopher's Stone / Composition.** The stone spends stored human souls rather than proving that equivalent exchange has no cost. Its apparent rule exemption shifts the payer. [Philosopher's Stone r78123](https://fma.fandom.com/wiki/Philosopher%27s_Stone?oldid=78123).

Proposal: Record who actually pays for a rule-bypassing effect, including a third-party stored budget.
