## Fandom dump revision review

Evidence: dated wiki snapshots, not a primary book/manga/game audit. Only the sections cited below were reviewed; archived catalogs and character histories were not certified complete. Every model mapping and takeaway remains a proposal.

**DX1 Nen / Nen and Limitation / Conditions.** A stolen ability can retain its creator's definition of an eligible target. The example accepts an artificial corpse replica while rejecting the actual corpse. [Nen r421055](https://hunterxhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Nen?oldid=421055).

Proposal: A copied power carries its semantic predicate, not merely the current user's tags or intent.
