# New mechanics and concrete refinements

Ranked by the change in player decisions and the gap in E8 to E8D, not by franchise power. All takeaways are proposals. These are 15 candidate deltas from two acquired wikis, not 15 newly verified series or accepted engine requirements. No blocked series supplies a fresh claim.

## Fold-in proposals

**D1 E8:** add carrier/payload identity, applied versus noticed time, reserved versus spent resources, event kind and fidelity of copied effects. These fields refine axes without forcing a shared law.

**D2 E8B:** add participant retention, replacement families, local aggregate interference and named guardian provenance. Keep labels separate from behavior checks.

**D3 E8C:** give merged paths an activity router; keep collective admission and local graders distinct from task completion. Item growth can alter its execution gate.

**D4 E8D:** breach hooks consume typed events, not only matching power tags. Keep an unknown sanction unknown; the Inn vow section does not certify JJK-like pricing or punishment.

Not ranked as new: typed composition (P01), mastery versus system grants (W04), versioned reward weights (W12), and imitation fidelity (W13) already have close owners in the survey fold-in or system-patch proposals. They are useful supporting examples, not a second invention.
