## Fandom dump revision review

Evidence: dated wiki snapshots, not a primary book/manga/game audit. Only the sections cited below were reviewed; archived catalogs and character histories were not certified complete. Every model mapping and takeaway remains a proposal.

**DH1 Unbreakable Vow / Casting; Rules.** A vow is sealed clause by clause by two parties and a third wand-bearing witness. Breaking the accepted terms is fatal. The page mixes book evidence with film and game exceptions. [Unbreakable Vow r1991314](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Unbreakable_Vow?oldid=1991314).

Proposal: Keep the witness ritual and clause acceptance; choose an edition before importing an exception.

**DH3 Fidelius Charm / Introduction.** A secret can be concealed in a living soul and disclosed by its keeper. The effect hides access to a place or fact, beyond merely hiding an object from eyesight. [Fidelius Charm r1933855](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Fidelius_Charm?oldid=1933855).

Proposal: Treat disclosure authority, concealed fact and physical target as distinct carriers.

**DH4 Wizengamot / Introduction; Functions.** The described body has both legislative and judicial roles; proceedings and testimony are institutional acts, not a guarantee that every judgment is magically true. [Wizengamot r2019998](https://harrypotter.fandom.com/wiki/Wizengamot?oldid=2019998).

Proposal: Keep civic jurisdiction separate from an automatically enforcing magical vow.

### Law lore for E9

DH1 to DH5: magical promises, secret-keeping magic and civic courts coexist. A book-supported Unbreakable Vow has a binder and fatal breach; the wiki's film/game exceptions need a selected version. The Goblet refusal sanction remains untested in the cited case. Wizengamot legislation is institutional authority. Gamp's law is a transfiguration constraint, not a wizarding criminal statute.
