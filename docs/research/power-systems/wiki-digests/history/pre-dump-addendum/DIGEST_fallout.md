## Fandom dump revision review

Evidence: dated wiki snapshots, not a primary book/manga/game audit. Only the sections cited below were reviewed; archived catalogs and character histories were not certified complete. Every model mapping and takeaway remains a proposal.

**DA1 Karma / Background; Characteristics.** Karma and faction or town reputation are different measures. Fallout games do not all use the same combination; the tabletop rules are also a separate version. [Karma r4146440](https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/Karma?oldid=4146440).

Proposal: Bind institutional readings to a named game and group instead of one universal morality score.

**DA2 Reputation / Fallout; Fallout 2; Fallout: New Vegas.** Reputation describes how particular groups regard the player. It is an institutional and social measure rather than a reality-enforcing oath. [Reputation r4507693](https://fallout.fandom.com/wiki/Reputation?oldid=4507693).

Proposal: Separate a body's abilities from the standing a particular group reads.
