# Ranked mechanics after the dump pass

These 15 rows rank concrete gaps and verified refinements by the decisions they change. Rows marked existing are supporting examples, not claims that the family or idea is new. Live E8 and E9 were compared, including E8D's J1 to J10 corrections and its witness/evidence fields. All takeaways are proposals; no E8 file was edited.

Proposal: Separate judge knowledge, disclosed evidence, verdict, sanction routing and the soul legally targeted. |

## Fold-in proposals

**DP1 E8:** retain the source carrier, the effect payload, target-selection policy, event kind and the original semantic predicate. These refine existing axes; they are not mandatory shared world laws.

**DP2 E8B:** define each method's allowed substitutes and whether its conditions are tested once or continuously. Grants, upkeep and delivered support can have different owners.

**DP3 E8C:** keep learned words, understood words, execution means and grades separate; a world-rule commit is not ordinary level growth.

**DP4 E8D/E9:** keep fulfillment history, verdict and sanction target distinct. A judge's knowledge is not necessarily what its caster receives. Existing witness/evidence fields remain the owner; add the case-specific routing rather than creating a universal court.
