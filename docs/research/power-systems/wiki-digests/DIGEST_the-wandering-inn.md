# The Wandering Inn wiki digest

State: revision_digest_available. Checked 2026-10-10 UTC.

This is a mechanics research record, not an accepted design or gameplay change. Wiki statements are secondary evidence. All E8 mappings are analyst inference; all takeaways are proposals.

## Revision-reviewed mechanics

**W01 Classes / Leveling.** A level can take effect before its sleep-time announcement. Some classes and bodies have different notice rules. [Classes r128402](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Classes&oldid=128402).

Mapping: E8 visibility: notice timing. Proposal: Keep the change, the notice and the recipient as separate events.

**W02 Classes / Class Consolidation.** After certain classes merge, work in one part can advance the whole; maid duties can advance a merged combat path. [Classes r128402](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Classes&oldid=128402).

Mapping: E8C branching: activity routing after fusion. Proposal: A merged path must state which activities earn shared growth.

**W03 Classes / Limits and Consequences.** A total of one hundred across several classes is not a proven ceiling; the page records people above that total. [Classes r128402](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Classes&oldid=128402).

Mapping: E8C ceiling: measurement basis. Proposal: Store a per-class threshold separately from a total-level estimate.

**W04 Skills / Learning Skills.** A person can first perform a method unaided and then have it recognized as a Skill without gaining a level. [Skills r128100](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Skills&oldid=128100).

Mapping: E8C gate: demonstrated execution. Proposal: Allow execution-first recognition instead of always reward-first learning.

**W05 Skills / Combination Skills.** Some joint techniques later work for one contributor; others keep requiring the original team. [Skills r128100](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Skills&oldid=128100).

Mapping: E8B composed/shared: participant retention. Proposal: A combined technique needs an explicit participant set and a rule for later solo use.

**W06 Skills / Mischief Skills.** Some prank-related Skills bank the results of antics and spend that credit on stronger effects. [Skills r128100](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Skills&oldid=128100).

Mapping: E8B source: banked acts. Proposal: Let completed jokes supply a stored local currency, rather than merely pricing a silly vow.

**W07 Skills / Ward Skills.** A ward can receive abilities tied to a designated guardian. [Skills r128100](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Skills&oldid=128100).

Mapping: E8B granted/shared: guardian provenance. Proposal: Record the named provider and the relationship from which each borrowed effect comes.

**W08 Skills / Vows.** Declared vows can grant Skills outside normal leveling; monastic examples include benefits that deepen with time. The section does not give a general breach penalty. [Skills r128100](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Skills&oldid=128100).

Mapping: E8D benefit and ramp established; consequence unknown. Proposal: Separate growth from keeping a vow from its unverified breach law.

**W09 Conditions / Background.** Some conditions are hidden from their bearer, with notice arriving only after removal. [Conditions r122477](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Conditions&oldid=122477).

Mapping: E8 visibility: retrospective notice. Proposal: Allow discovery to lag behind both application and cure.

**W10 Aspects / Overview and examples.** This wiki uses Aspects for body-changing Skills, including a temporary iron-like body. [Aspects r121217](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Aspects&oldid=121217).

Mapping: E8 vocabulary: source term distinct from model axis. Proposal: Keep source terms namespaced so a body effect is not mistaken for one of our ten descriptive axes.

**W11 Quests / Requirements.** Quest posting needs knowledge of a real objective and a guaranteed tangible reward; not every imagined target qualifies. [Quests r128731](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Quests&oldid=128731).

Mapping: E8B quest giving: admissibility before posting. Proposal: Separate knowledge eligibility, reward custody and posting authority.

**W12 Grand Design of Isthekenous / Overview.** The system can move experience from one activity category to another and alter its multiplier; chess is an example. [Grand Design of Isthekenous r128387](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Grand+Design+of+Isthekenous&oldid=128387).

Mapping: E8C currency: versioned activity weights. Proposal: A local system may revise reward routing while retaining a dated rule version.

**W13 Grand Design of Isthekenous / Overview.** The system distills feats into Skills; the copy need not match the original feat. [Grand Design of Isthekenous r128387](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Grand+Design+of+Isthekenous&oldid=128387).

Mapping: E8 source/mastery: imitation fidelity. Proposal: Record the source demonstration and the fidelity of the granted version.

**W14 Trials of Levelling / Overview.** A species can finish the trials and still be rejected at the later judgment. Knowledge of the trials is suppressed for many system users. [Trials of Levelling r117469](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Trials+of+Levelling&oldid=117469).

Mapping: E8C gate and grader: collective eligibility. Proposal: Keep task completion, admission and ability to know the gate separate.

**W15 Artifacts / Magical Dissonance.** Several active artifacts near each other can interfere. Inactive items in a holding bag avoid this; balancing leakage changes the safe load. [Artifacts r125944](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Artifacts&oldid=125944).

Mapping: E8 limit: spatial aggregate interference. Proposal: Use active leakage and proximity rather than a fixed count of carried items.

**W16 Artifacts / Enchantment Degradation.** Magic-producing monsters can preserve nearby enchantments; leaked magic also benefits the monsters. [Artifacts r125944](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Artifacts&oldid=125944).

Mapping: E8 persistence: ecological carrier support. Proposal: Preservation can create a useful but dangerous local ecology.

**W17 Horror Ranks / Background.** Horror advancement degrades ordinary classes. This page says lost ranks can restore levels, while Classes and Skills leave restoration unresolved. [Horror Ranks r105597](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Horror+Ranks&oldid=105597).

Mapping: E8C risk: competing paths with disputed recovery. Proposal: Keep conflicting witness records until a primary scene resolves which losses return.

**W18 Magic / Categories of magic.** The page distinguishes mana-based casting, emotion-based witchcraft, community-based shamanism and faith-based miracles. [Magic r128694](https://wiki.wanderinginn.com/index.php?title=Magic&oldid=128694).

Mapping: E8B source: different budgets and counters. Proposal: Make fuel and suppression rules depend on the actual source.

## Fresh mapping amendments

E8C: activity and insight feed a class ladder that can branch, merge, lose permission and restore it. System appraisal is separate from public rank and sleep-time notice. No cumulative-100 ceiling is established. Risks include class loss, conflicting paths and active artifact leakage.

E8B: trained mastery, system grants, body effects, shared powers, item effects, banked antics and community fuel are distinct means.

E8 axes: object includes each recipient and carrier; mechanism includes system recognition; trigger can be a joint act; certainty retains page conflicts; scope can follow a linked group; price includes banked acts and fuel; limit includes admission and leakage; visibility includes hidden conditions; persistence follows restored class access or artifact ecology; growth follows tasks, insight, fusion and local rule changes.

E8D: vow bearer, restriction, benefit and time growth are described in Skills / Vows. Witness, breach punishment, release, coercion, inheritance and exact interpretation remain unknown. Do not fill them from the general E8D template.

Rule abuse: merged maid/combat classes route maid work into combat growth (W02). This is a described interaction, not a checked gag scene.
