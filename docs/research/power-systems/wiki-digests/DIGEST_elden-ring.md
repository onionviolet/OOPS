## Fandom dump revision review

Evidence: dated wiki snapshots, not a primary book/manga/game audit. Only the sections cited below were reviewed; archived catalogs and character histories were not certified complete. Every model mapping and takeaway remains a proposal.

**DE1 Law of Causality / Description; Effect; Notes.** An incantation sets up automatic retaliation after qualifying damaging hits. Perfect blocking can prevent its trigger. Its title does not establish unlimited control over causal history. [Law of Causality r67313](https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Law_of_Causality?oldid=67313).

Proposal: Define a reaction by the event it counts, not by the breadth of its lore name.

**DE2 Law of Regression / Description; Effect; Notes.** The spell removes positive as well as negative effects and exposes mimicry. It uses an Intelligence requirement despite being classed as an incantation. [Law of Regression r67314](https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Law_of_Regression?oldid=67314).

Proposal: A cleansing action can erase helpful effects; a technique family need not use one universal attribute gate.

**DE3 Mending Rune of the Death-Prince / Description; Effect.** This rune offers an ending-level change to Order involving life within death. It is used at a specific mending event rather than as an ordinary combat buff. [Mending Rune of the Death-Prince r74273](https://eldenring.fandom.com/wiki/Mending_Rune_of_the_Death-Prince?oldid=74273).

Proposal: World-rule editing needs a carrier, an eligible authority and a commit site.

Proposal: Keep a world-level rule, its carrier and a religion interpreting it separate.

### Law lore for E9

DE1 to DE4: Order has lore and material rule carriers, including confined Destined Death and mending runes. Law-named incantations have bounded effects: retaliation and broad dispelling. A mending rune is used at a particular ending event. These source hooks are distinct from the project's original funeral-custody law; they do not retroactively make that prototype canon.
