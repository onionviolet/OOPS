# Lane B: web-fiction power systems

2026-10-10. Research complete for the assigned survey scope: 16 main titles, two bridge notes and five system-novel subtypes. **All absorption ideas are proposals.** No gameplay, E8 owner file, build or accepted decision changed.

The strongest addition is a way to keep a power’s parts apart. Fuel, vessel, technique, source custody, permission and public grade often follow different paths. A new rank can leave a fighter poorly trained. A skilled teacher can lack the old body. A bought artifact can grant both strength and a binding duty.

[Structured rows](SERIES.json) contain all eight E8C path parts, family/source mappings, subtle E8 variants, E8D slot mappings, distinctive mechanics, rule abuse and limits for every entry. [Source ledger](SOURCES.md) records access mode, local input hashes and source bounds. This is a survey of existing dossiers and public references, not a fresh full-book reading. “Verified” means the cited reference was inspected, not that every novel claim is authenticated.

The lane adds a shared comparison, not new duplicate book dossiers. Reverend Insanity, ORV, Shadow Slave, Gluttony, ISSTH, Renegade Immortal, Mother of Learning and Fated Villain retain their existing source owners. Three qualifications belong beside that row: acting speeds assimilation rather than being the only possible digestion; a higher gate may also need a ritual; and neighboring swaps depend on the group and exact step. Do not turn “adjacent” into free mixing. The ordinary high-sequence threshold has a LOTM-group exception. The local dossier has only partial reviewed coverage, so its early paired-swap teaching does not certify every late pathway. E8D can describe them, but should not imply that all were voluntarily sworn or that every restriction earns more power. ## P2. Proposed changes for E8 through E8D

Claude owns integration. These amendments are decision-ready text proposals; this lane did not edit the owners.

### P2.1 E8: add qualifiers beside the ten axes

Keep the ten axes. Add an optional **carrier/substrate** reference so persistence names what actually holds the power: body, soul, tool, imprint, service or record. “Soul-persistent” alone cannot tell whether losing a tool disables recall. RI’s permanent body gains and Shadow Slave’s service loss show why this matters.

Make **certainty** name its target: objective fact, sincere belief, visible evidence or prediction. A sincerity reader can be exact about belief while wrong about reality. Keep the observer’s interpretation separate from the power’s actual rule.

Treat **growth** as a reference to a path record and a facet/skill record. A rank promotion, improved control, digestion and source acquisition need not occur together. Counter-powers should target the relevant mechanism; they can reverse one axis without deleting the whole gift.

### P2.2 E8B: qualify acquisition and composition

Add source metadata `owner`, `carrier`, `grantor`, `authorityScope` and `serviceDependency` where the world needs them. A gift, donation, borrowed authority and full ownership are four possible events, not one generic “granted” flag.

Separate recipes that **create/consume a source** from moves that **coordinate existing sources**. Add food, activation fuel, stress tolerance and control burden as separate costs when the source needs them. No global upkeep tax or slot limit is proposed.

Give inheritance a local mode: **coexist**, **absorb**, **copy method**, **grant source** or **teach**. Prior wills and debt travel only when an actual carrier or rule takes them. An inherited book, remembered lesson and bond with a speaking remnant should not be interchangeable.

### P2.3 E8C: make branching and ceilings explainable

Preserve the eight path parts, but let `currency` be a list of purpose-tagged budgets. Add parallel tracks or related path IDs for body, mastery, realm, class, tool grade and institutional standing. Show them only where useful to the world.

Represent a gate as prerequisites, consumed inputs, risk and resulting function. That supports seven-input assembly, role digestion, vulnerable conversion, a personal severing and clone-body advancement without forcing them into a shared XP threshold.

For branch links, record from/to stage, compatible source group, costs, retention and chapter scope. Add `ceiling.reason` and `ceiling.exceptions`: method defect, source scarcity, world limit or an observed endpoint. Record an outlier without rewriting the ordinary rule.

Replace the short LOTM row with: “Sequences 9 through 0; acquire and assimilate the next source. Acting speeds digestion while preserving the actor’s identity. Higher steps add path-specific rituals. Switching uses group- and step-specific links; it is not free path mixing.” This is proposed wording, with its source qualifications in F1.

### P2.4 E8D: distinguish rule kinds and their enforcers

Add `kind`: **chosen vow**, **mutual contract**, **authority permit**, **imposed task**, **intrinsic burden** or **causal obligation**. Only the first two necessarily enter a swear/draft lifecycle. A body burden can be present before consent; an imposed task can punish without offering a negotiated quote.

Add `enforcer`, `substrate`, `activation`, `jurisdiction`, `dependencies` and `tamperPolicy` where needed. A signer may have no active mark, lack the trigger memory, lose patron permission or stop matching a true-name relationship. These changes differ from honestly satisfying the release clause. Price the actual world rule; do not require the law to be fair or tamper-proof.

Add per-party terms and `collectiveMode`: **independent**, **shared budget**, **quorum**, **joint liability** or **role duties**. Do not infer joint punishment from merely belonging to a sect or army. Specify quorum membership, contribution shares and who can revoke support.

Keep `interpretation` from E8D, but separate **term meaning** from **observed proof of breach**. A detector can read sincere belief or be suppressed. A literal objective can admit insects. Courts, patrons and magic need not agree on what happened. Memory, coercion and release remain world-specific.
