# Cultivation: paid preparation, distinct bottlenecks and lasting quality

The selected ACS law makes preparation consequential in three different ways: ordinary practice reaches a hard gate, a Chance gate spends inputs before a probabilistic attempt, and a Gold gate converts a finite energy reserve into lasting quality. These are code-established rules from build17074448, not observed gameplay or a claim to have read every cultivation path. Exact private source paths, line spans and SHA256 values are in [cultivation.json](cultivation.json); raw recovered code stays outside Git.

## Original YAIRPG candidate: optional quiet-hand trial

After learning the existing Measured cooling source and completing its three supervised trials, offer an optional self trial at the Village bench. A proposed attempt costs one actual salt packet, two stamina and three played minutes. The player can try during an ordinary hour or wait for a disclosed quiet-hour preparation bonus. Success retains a narrow steadied-control application. A completed failure still spends its inputs and records retry history plus brief hand soreness. Cancellation or loss of required support uses the current refundable reservation policy and grants no result. Values and fiction here are original balance proposals.

The application needs a useful consumer: an additive steadied-hook command spends the same owned charged sleeve and existing effort, then gives the existing hook field five played minutes instead of four. The current four-minute command remains available. This does not grant a new rank, source, item supply, forced cultivation path or prestige layer. The actual original method/tool/body requirements continue to matter.

Reuse `src/reference_gameplay_content.js:30–98` for acquired cooling/tool/mastery and the current hook effect, `src/world_rhythm.js:5–30` for the saved-calendar window, `src/simulation.js:118–221` for costs/jobs/outcomes, `src/simulation_effects.js:43–79,212–266` for retained progress/condition/knowledge and `src/power_models.js:5–43` for capability legality. These owners were read and hashed in the JSON, but shared integration is active and their hashes must be refreshed before implementation. The energy owner already has finite integer reserve/strain semantics, although this initial proposal can stay with current salt/stamina costs.

The precise gaps are the quiet-hour fact/preview provider; additive trial and outcome definitions; a paid-attempt/application receipt validator; the five-minute hook consumer; and a notebook/bio presentation of costs, preparation snapshot, outcome and retained consequences. No current cooling consumer uses an environmental window. Avoid a generic bonus with no downstream function.

YAIRPG currently freezes outcome weights/effects at `startCommand`. The first proposal should explicitly snapshot preparation at acceptance and reuse those frozen jobs. ACS live-per-dt quality conversion would require a different job policy and is not silently selected here. Old cooling jobs and commands retain their original timing/outcomes; optional absence stays inert, any new receipt relations are explicitly admitted by the current owning validator, and the existing inventory, clock and durable save publisher remain the owners. The shared integrator01a10fe7 owns implementation; this leaf owns no gameplay files.

The later playable gate is a disposable-state journey comparing ordinary and quiet-hour quotes, completing reproducible paid success/failure attempts, checking stock/stamina/time and retained application/soreness/retry records, then using the new five-minute hook method beside the original four-minute method. Reload mid-job and after settlement must not duplicate cost or reward, and old absent/frozen states and save-failure atomicity need the existing owner's acceptance.
