## Selected medicine chain and contrast

NoHunger1 is a Normal recipe, Medicine level5, base success addition0.8 and base yield9. The item is Rate6, with declarations of3Fruit,9Ginkgo,9Healroot and200work. The UI requires a player Dao disciple eligible for treatment/magic, formula visibility from Normal status or actual learned knowledge, sufficient Medicine level/energy and an available RefiningDan station. Selecting a station records the exact Maker and formula in its production queue. `CommandMakeItem` permits that maker, requires fuel/workspace/path and a task. P02:27–34; P04:633–688; P06:725–793; P11:3544–3627; P12:1399–1407,2846–2858; P22:11–45.

Ingredients are requested at their current scaled `rcount`, locked, then spent on first work. Entry spends `200×max(1,output.Rate−5)³`Ling, so this Rate6 pill costs200Ling per entry. Maker speed, room FengShui, formula success addition, maker success/yield properties, station-output element relation and runtime difficulty variables affect the completed batch. Born relation adds0.2success and1.5yield; Contrary subtracts0.2 and halves yield; Same adds0.1 and1.2yield. Base count9 is multiplied and floored, with a minimum output count1 on success. P21:359–420; P24:46–67,106–160; P34:33–42.

Missing stock is a waiting path before manufacture. A completed failed batch has already spent its inputs, then creates a selected failed-pill type, practice experience, obsession change and a failure event. Failed-pill effects are unread. `Loop=-1` or a finite loop count queues a new batch at completion, including failure, and still needs new ingredients and maker time. Building progress is serialized and may survive an actor's higher-priority interruption. Since each toil entry charges energy, repeated-entry and reload behavior is a later runtime question, not a proven once-per-batch charge. P21:78–100,350–356,364–414,507–523; P24:70–81,224–240.

`ToilEatItem` locks one actual medicine during a short consumption action and debits it only after completed `EatItem` succeeds. In the selected normal eligible actor route, its Food declaration restores50nutrition and its Elixir applies Dan_NoHunger1. The modifier declares MaxStack1,1200world-time units and−0.5 additive proportions for NutritionConsumeConstant/FatigueConsumeConstant. Actual consumers are `FoodNeed.GetSubValue` and `RestNeed.GetSubValue`; severe hunger or fatigue can interrupt ordinary jobs. `ModifierBase` expiry checks world TolSecond against actual application times, and reapplication refreshes its clock. This is a temporary labor-preparation choice with downstream work consequences, not simply a pill count or HP tooltip.
