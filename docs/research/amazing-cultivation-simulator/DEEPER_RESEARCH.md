## Deeper principles already established

Progression can retain quality and failed-attempt experience, not only a level. Material properties can reach actual combat functions. Medicine can alter needs and conversion rates, while failures consume stock and leave byproducts. Institutions can retain access independently of a person's learning, body, fuel or equipment. Autonomous work must compete for the actual worker/site/means; temporary mood and lasting relationship state can diverge. Discovery, account-level collection, personal knowledge and executable permission are distinct owners. Source interruption and timing policies vary by system.

The Wudang supplement adds a stronger example: two learned progression components change later study costs and ratio-specific combat/mind modifiers; an extreme or balanced build can have different uses. Bamboo fishing adds equipment/environment-qualified yield, a speed-adjusted reaction window, escape and dangerous catches. These are source-backed rules, with explicit unread runtime/acquisition/consumer limits. They are not two more selected gameplay packets.
