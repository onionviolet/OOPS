# Original YAIR implementation packets

Four ranked proposals, 2026-10-06. This research selects no runtime work. All names, numbers and fiction below are reversible original designs. References explain the interaction pattern; no Rogue Legacy source, disability names, classes, prose, assets or setting would be copied.

The integrator must refresh [YAIR_INPUTS.json](YAIR_INPUTS.json), check the current shared writer, preserve the vanilla opening and existing field renewal, and use the current durable native publisher. Build/syntax/import/link checks remain permitted; all playable/save/failure/director gates below are later and **unperformed**.

## 1. Choose a useful physical preparation before one encounter

**Action and result:** Before an explicitly enrolled original sluice attempt, choose a traversal preparation using your actual Survey rope, or a braced preparation made from one Prepared survey kit. The rope changes a paid escape action; the brace changes a paid self-guard action. Choosing one excludes the other for that encounter, creating a local mobility-versus-protection choice without a new global equipment slot system.

**Prerequisites and proposed costs:** Inspect the sluice; finish current work; own the required item with valid custody. Reserve one rope until settlement, returning that exact item at settlement/withdrawal. Consume one kit only when committing the brace. Proposed effects: traversal permits one two-step escape for two stamina; brace permits one stronger self-guard for two stamina. Both are one-use functions, not permanent stat bonuses. Recheck the physical item at admission; preview spends nothing.

**Aftermath:** Actual wounds, stamina, spent kit and ordinary passage result remain. The reserved rope remains unavailable for other consumers until released. An ordinary unprepared attempt remains viable. A repeated view, miss or unsupported action grants no extra charge.

**Reuse and actual missing consumer:** Native inventory/custody and `prepareNativeEquipment` remain owners. `featureBattleView`, `previewFeatureBattleAction`, `commitFeatureBattleAction` and the feature host already support paid guards, kit lashing, escape, snapshots and NPC gear functions. The missing binding is an explicit player preparation choice at admission with distinct one-use escape/guard consumers. Existing kit lashing, fitting tools and defense-earned counter windows are already implemented and are not this gap. See `functional-kit-choice` in [YAIR notes](readers/yair.json) and `YAIR.durable-publisher` in [root owner notes](readers/root_yair.json).

**Source interaction:** [P3 equipment/runes/spells](FINDINGS.json) distinguishes discovery, purchase, equip and a real movement consumer; P5 distinguishes shield-facing defenses and counterable actions. Translate that distinction into the existing text/native encounter.

**Save and retention:** Version only new enrolled encounter payloads, with a frozen chosen item/function, reservation identity and charge receipt. Existing schema-3/4 sessions retain their prior rules. No loadout appears on old saves or from opening a menu. The kit/rope still uses native stock and the same save envelope. Field renewal keeps item ownership; an active session already prevents renewal.

**Later acceptance:** Rebuild; choose each preparation in disposable generated state and see its declared action/outcome. Verify wrong custody/stock rejects, one charge is consumed once, withdrawal returns only the reserved rope, and save/reload retains the same preparation/charge. Verify ordinary and preexisting frozen sessions retain their declared behavior. No such check has run.

## 2. Let existing body aptitudes change a specific maneuver

**Action and result:** In the same authored encounter, choose a sure-footed crossing step using awakened Natural balance, or a grip-and-rope step using Conditioned grip. Both reach the same near-bank position by different paid means. The player can use the ordinary slower retreat without either source.

**Prerequisites and proposed costs:** The balance step requires the actual acquired, awakened, active, unsuppressed source and recovered hands; proposed cost two stamina for two retreat progress. The grip step requires the existing trained hand-control capability and an unencumbered owned rope; proposed cost one stamina for one progress and no consumed rope. Ordinary retreat remains its current cost/result. These effects cannot stack with packet 1's traversal charge on the same committed action.

**Aftermath:** Spend only the selected maneuver's effort; preserve innate-versus-trained provenance, current soreness, wounds and encounter receipts. An aptitude does not teach the grip method, erase a condition or grant an inherited family story.

**Reuse and actual missing consumer:** `bodySources`, `bodyCommands`, `bodyFacts`, capability/faculty projection and existing battle response gates are owners. Natural balance already affects expedition outcome weights; Conditioned grip already shortens packing, and tender hands already block specific frame/material actions. The new binding is a discrete native maneuver reading those sources together with real rope custody, surfaced in the current battle action menu. This is not a missing general traits engine. Exact reuse is `YAIR.body-functional` and `YAIR.body-combat-consumer` in [root owner notes](readers/root_yair.json).

**Source interaction:** P1's functional trait consumers and class/trait incompatibilities, P3's rune-to-movement consumer and P5's action-specific defensive exceptions. Preserve executable compatibility checks rather than applying every trait to every action.

**Save and retention:** Use existing body/source/condition owners. Save only new maneuver receipts in the opted-in encounter revision. Missing aptitude remains absent; no retrospective awakening, body change, hereditary reward or new prestige. Freeze existing sessions. Packet 1 and this packet need one integrator for their common response/admission schema.

**Later acceptance:** Rebuild; compare dormant/active/suppressed balance, trained/untrained grip, tender/recovered hands and actual rope custody. Commit each supported response and observe its paid effect. Preview/reload cannot supply missing sources or refund spent effort. Existing packing and expedition consumers remain useful. Unperformed.

## 3. Compare three retained, funded pathfinder offers

**Action and result:** Ask the existing association for a finite set of three candidate pathfinders, compare their actual local means, and commission one or decline all. Example original profiles: a careful observer with extra focus, a strong carrier with extra stamina, and a practiced packer with a useful work method. Their chosen command policy creates different paid route/work outcomes rather than merely different portraits.

**Prerequisites and proposed costs:** Meet the craftsman and know the workbench. Creating the offer set takes one played minute and no fee; it freezes three unique candidate identities/profiles exactly once for this solicitation. Hiring keeps the current helper price of three coins plus one transferred log. A retained association service earned through the existing supply commission can later qualify the practiced-packer profile; the exact roster is a proposal. No candidate gets free supplies or unsupported abilities.

**Aftermath:** Only the selected person receives the contract and real stock; their controller pays its own effort and returns an actual output. Record selection, funded work and later collection under that durable person. Other candidates remain declined/uncontracted records with no ticking duties or invented relationships. A new solicitation needs an explicit finite policy, not rerolling by reopening the menu.

**Reuse and actual missing consumer:** `helperDefinitions` already commissions a funded actor, executes a local controller and transfers one real returned kit. `prepareSimulationEffects` freezes a spawn template and stable generated ID; `startCommand` freezes chosen outcomes and cost-bearing jobs. The missing consumer is a saved offer list with compatibility/eligibility filters, comparison UI and a selection command that binds one listed profile to the existing contract. No general actor factory, controller engine or heir system is missing. See `YAIR.helper-offers` in [root owner notes](readers/root_yair.json).

**Source interaction:** P1 freezes three successor offers, rejects incompatible class/trait/spell combinations and limits rerolls; P2 retained unlock purchases change the future offer pool. Reuse the choice structure as living NPC commissioning, with no forced player death, replacement identity or another prestige.

**Save and retention:** Add an explicit optional, validated solicitation record containing candidate IDs, template revisions, profile facts, generation cause/seed and selected/declined disposition. Existing hires and frozen jobs keep their prior template/identity. Existing absent records remain inert. Persist generation and selection before publication; reloading must not redraw. Candidate source ownership and NPC work/stock stay actor-scoped. Current field renewal retains the offer/commission records.

**Later acceptance:** Rebuild; compare distinct offers, decline without payment, hire one exactly once, inspect funded independent work and collect only that person's actual output. Save/reload preserves all offers, selected identity, ownership and outcome; stale selection/cancel/failure cannot create a second person or free log. Unperformed.

## 4. Put a useful recipe behind a new optional objective

**Action and result:** Accept a separately declared workshop-recovery objective before an attempt: recover a marked loose fitting and complete passage by raising the gate, keeping the fitting intact. Combat victory alone, bypass, withdrawal or loss does not complete this objective. Return the fitting to its consenting owner to learn one original brace-wrap recipe; the recipe then enables paid creation of a usable guard preparation.

**Prerequisites and proposed costs:** Inspect the encounter and hear the owner's offer; at most three explicitly enrolled attempts. Recovering the fitting costs one stamina and an authored action opportunity, not a new real-time reaction requirement. Returning it transfers the actual recovered item. Proposed recipe work uses one log, one resin and two played minutes to make one brace preparation; exact materials depend on refreshed item ownership. Existing no-strike/prepared-team challenges stay recognition-only.

**Aftermath:** Completion unlocks knowledge/recipe once, not a free item or repeated payout. The recipe still requires stock and time. Failed/withdrawn attempts retain spent effort and ordinary passage/wounds while leaving the owner’s recipe unlearned. Declining imposes no debt or deadline. The fitting's custody must survive recovery, loss and handoff rather than being a display-only quest token.

**Reuse and actual missing consumer:** `validateAttempts`/`advanceReferenceGameplay` already bind finite attempts to actual sessions and settlement; `finish` records objective/win outcomes and one route XP payment. Feats already recognize committed results. Native item transfers and unlock publications remain owners. The missing binding is a NEW objective-specific recovered-item receipt and idempotent recipe-learning handoff claim. No generic challenge, achievement or objective engine is missing. See `optional-objective-payout` in [YAIR notes](readers/yair.json); its suggested material payout is refined here into a conserved item handoff and useful recipe.

**Source interaction:** P4 fairy conditions have actual fail/succeed gates and unlock a missing rune; P3 still requires rune purchase/equip before its function works. The original YAIR equivalent distinguishes challenge accomplishment, recipe knowledge, paid product and actual use.

**Save and retention:** Version a new optional objective record instead of changing existing challenge contracts. Persist enrollment/session ID, fitting stock/custody, attempt result, handoff and learned recipe in one current save envelope. Old saves gain no reward; earlier wins do not invent objective evidence. Recipe remains character-retained under the existing field renewal. Never copy Rogue Legacy's render-driven checker or its separate-file save design.

**Later acceptance:** Rebuild; decline normally, enroll, recover the actual fitting, complete only the declared objective, transfer it and learn the recipe once. Craft and use its real output. Wrong result, stale handoff, repeat completion, insufficient stock and failed save cannot unlock/pay twice. Generated save/reload preserves attempt, custody, recipe and aftermath. Unperformed.

## Existing support and deliberately unselected scope

Retained dry rack, ordinary → braced → sealed fitting improvements, finite challenge recognition, contextual NPC profiles, defense-earned next-choice windows, funded helpers and one field renewal already have current consumers. Keep those owners. A second worksite, a generic procedural dungeon generator, a body/heir replacement lifecycle, forced permadeath, a universal rune grid, a gold confiscation rule and a second prestige are not selected by this research.
