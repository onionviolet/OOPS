A generic tag sum cannot represent all six. The same preparation infrastructure can host world-specific rules without making different worlds' energies interchangeable.

## Twenty-three material and mechanism cards

Each card pairs a bounded source fact with an **O proposed consumer/contract**. An amount, yield or compatibility rule absent from its locator remains unknown.

### A Soldier's Life

**M01. Extracted essence: targeted potential.** S, Volume1 T24/S108, Book1 chapter33 P5–9,39–48; D/source-dossier: essence source determines the affected trait, and distribution has custody/family consequences. **O:** essence consumption targets a named attribute/potential field, never all related skills. Preserve source/trait and holder; extraction quantities and complete formulation remain gaps. Consumer: bodily progression alongside trained work.

**M02. Glowstone: contextual utility.** S Book5 chapters314–315; D/shown combat-grounded summary: glowstone interferes with lightning; light also counters dark-ogre darkness advantage, chapter296 P17–32,55–64. **O:** evaluate illumination and lightning interaction separately, under environment/attack contexts. Consumer: lamp or shielded workroom. Do not infer conductivity, recharge life or universal lightning immunity.

**M03. Tattooed spell-form carrier.** S Book5 chapter308 P13–30; D/dossier: tattoo forms broaden usable affinity expressions while sacrificing core flexibility and stressing small cores. **O:** inscription structure, body carrier, operator core and available aether are separate fields. Consumer: executable spell, not a pigment granting trained mastery. Ink identity and application formula are unestablished.

**M04. Preservation under a storage law.** S Book5 chapter282 P50–54; chapter310 P48–53 and311 P4–14,22; D: permanent storage time-stops contents, but exceptional opening damages glassware and spills potions. **O:** storage suspends eligible lifecycle clocks, not ownership/conservation. On spill, allocate actual spilled volume and remaining vessel stock. Consumer: supply transport. The ordinary temporary-space ejection rule, Book1 chapter5 P179, is a different law.

### TTKS B5: information, living carriers and fragments

**M05. Rune fragments as executable information.** B R10, members0221/0360/0396/0408; D: a rune requires carrier/material/fuel; partial fragments can function. **O:** pattern coverage is a set of supported operations rather than a global completion percentage. Consumer: inscription apparatus. A copied diagram neither supplies fuel nor creates its carrier.

**M06. Fire-bearing substrate and shared modes.** B0408, heading chapter403; T/SHOWN panel and protagonist reasoning: absorbed fire characteristics feed wall, weapon and giant expressions; giant mode requires full accumulated fire. D adds the fragment-count/shared-pool interpretation. **O:** one reservoir powers every applicable mode and records acquired fire traits. Consumer: alternate artifact modes. Claiming four independent full charges would duplicate energy; precise reservoir arithmetic needs more primary windows.

**M07. Emotion-bearing plant artifact.** B0437, heading chapter432; T/SHOWN in selected displayed sections: a supernatural plant mask fuses with same-source resentment/jealousy flames and its uses/effects change. **O:** define an explicit affinity relation, owner-derived imprint and directional consumer rule. A property helping its source can harm a coerced wearer. Consumer: original cursed equipment, sealed truth until discovery. This exceptional collector process does not establish ordinary botanical chemistry.

**M08. Organ fragment versus artifact fragment.** B0437–0438; T0437 selected ending shows three heart fragments combining into one ninth of an artifact; D0438 supplies independently functioning ninths. **O:** distinguish the three-piece intermediate set from the nine-part final artifact. Consumer: fragment reconstruction. Three thirds cannot be interpreted as a complete nine-part weapon, or summed like grams of ore.

**M09. Infection source as rule-writing substrate.** B0658 and R-source catalogue; D: virus-mediated rules make infection sources usable resources with activation, gifts, debts and removal conditions. **O:** a living/infective material carries transmission routes, host capacity and ongoing obligations. Consumer: symbiosis or source-powered equipment. “Purified” must name the removed contamination; it cannot silently erase the rule-bearing identity.

### LOTM and COI: conserved identities and exceptional substances

**M10. Main material replaced by characteristic.** L0142 v1 ch140;0214 v1 ch212;0221 v2 ch5, D/SHOWN/STATED: a same-pathway characteristic can replace potion main material; auxiliaries remain distinct. **O:** slot substitution checks pathway/sequence content and identity lineage. Consumer: advancement potion. Physically similar herbs cannot synthesize the characteristic through tag arithmetic.

**M11. Converged characteristic content.** C0295, D/STATED: a Sequence7 characteristic includes lower Sequence9/8 content. C0028–0029 states conservation. **O:** preserve a contained-identity ledger; splitting is only available through a declared supported law. Consumer: advancement or artifact fabrication. Counting the outer characteristic plus its contained identities as independent inventory stock duplicates resources.

**M12. Imprint and contamination are different.** L0357 v2 ch138, D/SHOWN: former-source impulse persists; L0254 v2 ch38 and0491 v2 ch268, D/STATED/INFERRED: external corruption is a separate material hazard with exceptional removal mechanisms. **O:** selective treatment can remove one condition without declaring all spiritual residue gone. Consumer: potion assessment or artifact handling. Similarity to an old owner is a dossier inference, not a guaranteed cleanse.

**M13. Formula carriers: blood, powders and liquid.** L0461 v2 ch238, D/SHOWN: ordered Faceless brewing combines blood, plant preparations, hair and main ingredients. **O:** auxiliary lots have volume/mass/prepared-state requirements while the main characteristic stays countable. Consumer: potion batch. Complete ratios are recipe-specific; this scene does not authorize procedural replacement of every canon ingredient.

**M14. Blood and aromatics as ritual media.** C0060–0061, D/STATED/SHOWN: own blood and specified aromatic preparations support a ritual drawing on sealed power, bounded by body/spirit strength. **O:** source identity and invoked grant are separate from chemical composition. Consumer: ritual access. More incense cannot exceed the body's capability ceiling or create an absent bestower.

**M15. Temporary extraordinary goods.** C0030, D/STATED: charms, lotions and weapons without characteristics differ from characteristic-bearing mystical items; they decay, and charms can be single-use. **O:** track charged/expiring preparation separately from conserved identity. Consumer: consumable coating or weapon treatment. Empty/depleted remains do not automatically yield a characteristic.

**M16. Stored characteristics and container upkeep.** C0495, D/STATED manual: relocation/storage rules apply; Traveler's Bag needs periodic resealing and can lose contents to the spirit world. **O:** loss from a holder moves the identity to an unavailable location, rather than deleting conserved essence. Consumer: storage and recovery. Verify the manual's exact scope before implementing blanket decay.

**M17. Indivisible exceptional identity.** C1089–1091, D/STATED: sefirot/Uniquenesses have exceptional nondivision/noncopy rules and named exceptional authority. **O:** generation can create original ritual carriers but cannot roll additional canon unique identities. Consumer: high-tier accommodation. An apparent loophole requires explicit local law, not “rare enough” quality.

### Shadow Slave: mineral anchors, networks and soul-bound construction

**M18. Soul shard as physical anchor.** H613/618/695–701, D/STATED/SHOWN: shards anchor essence-string weaves; rank supports intensity and anchor count supports tier. **O:** shards are countable installed components with rank and a node role. Consumer: Memory assembly. Splitting a crystal by mass does not create two supported anchor nodes unless a rule permits it.

**M19. Name and pattern constrain assembly.** H695–701, D/STATED/SHOWN: names are woven into nature/binding, incompatible enchantments can fail despite capacity, and a bad weave can destroy anchors. **O:** typed connections, semantic compatibility and construction order precede output publication. Consumer: generated original enchantments. Capacity alone cannot authorize every combination; naming arithmetic remains unknown.

**M20. Fabrication pipeline and operator means.** H2048, D/STATED;1605, D/SHOWN: forging, infusion, inscription and Memory formation are distinct, and mundane metal/leather/tailoring matter. **O:** each stage has its practitioner/tool/Aspect ceiling and intermediate output. Consumer: equipment manufacture and repair. High-grade metal cannot substitute for access to a missing creation capability.

**M21. Heart-derived alloy, binder and living anchor.** H2056–2062, D/SHOWN/STATED: a particular soulbound construction uses heart-derived alloy, obsidian dust, ashes and blood, with a living-soul anchoring aim. **O:** composition, layered forging, weave and bound recipient persist separately. Consumer: original soulbound equipment. This is an exceptional evidenced recipe, not permission to declare all blood a universal binding agent.

### Two materially different additional book worlds

**M22. Living implanted seed and soul fruit.** X0158, T/SHOWN narrated implantation and STATED explanations: 蓮心種子 grows in the cultivator's dantian, draws personal mana and supports cultivation; 九星古魂果 has a soul-stabilizing role and an advantageous consumption stage. **O:** distinguish a living persistent implant from a fruit consumed at a stage. Consumer: cultivation support. Reader estimates of growth time are conditional, not every host's timer. Preservation-box claims are stated, not a measured million-year experiment.

**M23. Environmental processing versus technical energy.** X0209, T/STATED/SHOWN targeted passages: trained alchemy can use environmental heat/cold; 祭丹之術 trades multiple prospective pills for one improved pill. M660/703:p82, D/STATED/SHOWN: high-tech energy sources require knowledge, and machinery can lose operation when its live force source is cut. **O:** facility heat, process skill, yield sacrifice, battery energy and operator power are distinct inputs. Consumer: an original refinery or powered workshop. No one “potency” score pays all of them; the yield sacrificed is explicit, and a blueprint alone cannot supply fuel.

## Proposed contracts that make these cards procedural

**P1. Resource type controls allowed operations.** Mixtures support partition by measured amount; identity tokens support transfer and explicitly licensed conversion; assemblies support remove/install; patterns support copy with access limits; grants support revoke/expire; living substrates support growth, host requirements and death. Each process declares which operations it accepts. Potions can contain both a divisible carrier and a countable characteristic, so a sip requires an authored delivery law. Never infer that ten aliquots grant ten copies of advancement.

**P2. Conservation includes location and deferred outcomes.** Every rule names consumed stock, installed identity, recoverable residue, emissions, retained catalyst and destination. Identity lost into another realm remains unavailable, with its ledger preserved. Failed crafting can destroy a mundane carrier while conserving a spiritual token according to its world's law. Delayed separation creates pending extraction, not immediately lootable duplicate stock.

**P3. Separate material grade, quality, potency and trained means.** Grade may constrain supported intensity; quality describes the constructed product; concentration governs divisible dose; suitability depends on the actual consumer. Maker knowledge/precision/Aspect access remains a gate. A low-grade substrate can outperform an expensive one for a particular use without rewriting its rank.

**P4. Local compatibility is executable and directional.** An import retains origin facts, but the receiving law decides whether it conducts, binds, fuels or remains inert. Matching “mana,” “blood,” “crystal” or “soul” names proves nothing. World variants name which laws change. A local energy adapter records conversion payer, efficiency and waste; a universal free conversion is an explicit original proposal.

**P5. Generate coherent originals within a declared family.** Resolve family, ecology/source, compatible fractions, power payer, processing tolerances, lifecycle and useful consumer once. Generate correlated properties within that law, not arbitrary independent buffs. Named canon ingredients remain authored exemplars; discovered originals get stable IDs/versioned resolved truth. Generated identities cannot multiply finite canon stock or confer protagonist-exclusive methods. Knowledge reveals evidence-specific observations; it does not reroll truth.
