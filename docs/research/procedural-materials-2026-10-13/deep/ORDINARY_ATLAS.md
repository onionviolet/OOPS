# Ordinary material atlas and intake contract

2026-10-13. Second-round research, with 24 representative family/specimen cards. These are schema-grounding examples and original procedural-rule proposals, not an exhaustive substances catalogue, accepted gameplay specification or implemented chemical simulator. [Round scope](README.md) and [prior synthesis](../REPORT.md) remain controlling context. Living materials and canon laws have separate lanes.

## What must remain distinct

F1: **Composition, phase, structure and geometry are different facts.** Composition says what is present and how much. Phase describes each relevant constituent's state at declared conditions. Structure includes grains, pores, layers, fibers, interfaces and processing history. Geometry belongs to the owned piece or assembly. A powder is a collection of particles, not automatically a liquid; a slurry contains solid and liquid fractions. A coated sword has a substrate and a surface layer, not an averaged new metal.

F2: **Hardness, stiffness and toughness must not become one strength tag.** For proposed gameplay, hardness answers the declared scratching/indentation interaction; stiffness answers elastic deformation; toughness answers an authored fracture/impact response. Yield resistance is another field when permanent bending matters. NIST's data-evaluation guide identifies hardness as dependent on its measurement procedure, including indenter geometry and load. NIST's ceramics record separately reports hardness and fracture toughness, with specimen structure and orientation. These sources support qualified measurements, not a universal ranked material ladder. [R5/R6 below]

P1: Material stiffness and the stiffness of a finished handle differ: section shape, length, joints and load direction can matter. A knife-edge consumer may read surface hardness and edge geometry; an impact consumer may read fracture response and defects; a brace may read stiffness and connector support. Only introduce a field when a useful consumer reads it. “Strongest metal” is not a sufficient recipe requirement.

P2: **Unknown, absent, unsupported and inapplicable are different states.** A missing assay is unknown, a measured zero may be absent under its detection limit, an unavailable rule is unsupported, and a liquid's wood-grain direction is inapplicable. Preserve unidentified fractions. Never interpret an omitted conductivity or contamination field as zero. Player knowledge remains separate from authoritative material facts.

Sources ground distinctions and intake routes. Most cards below deliberately propose bounded game behavior rather than assert exact real processing, thermal limits or specimen statistics. Numerical adoption requires its own measured record and conditions. No real medical, extraction or hazardous mixing recipe is supplied.

These cards permit manufactured intermediates and byproducts to stay ordinary owned materials. Some outputs conserve composition while changing structure; others transform constituents with declared products and environmental exchanges. “Reversible” must specify conditions, resource costs and what can be recovered. Reversing a displayed arrow cannot restore original workmanship, lost heat, dispersed gas or forgotten provenance automatically.

## Intake and procedural generation

P3: Suggested intake record: `id`, `world`, `family`, `specimenOrBatchScope`, `source/edition/revision`, `claimLocator`, `evidenceKind`, `adaptationStatus`, `composition(amount, unit, basis)`, `phaseByConstituent`, `structure`, `surfaceLayers`, `geometryRef`, `condition`, `measurements`, `processEligibility`, `routeResponses`, `exceptions`, `consumerIDs`, `unknowns`, `resolverRevision`. Leave optional fields absent only with a stated interpretation; observations live in separate observer records.

P4: A measurement record needs property/method, value or interval, unit/scale, temperature/pressure, moisture or sample preparation when relevant, direction, basis and uncertainty. Density in mass/volume differs from powder bulk density including packing voids. Material modulus may use Pa, local hardness may use a named ordinal test, fracture toughness may use MPa√m, and masses/volumes use declared units. Do not silently convert between incompatible hardness scales or compare a simulated ordinal with SI. Counts require a defined specimen conversion before mixture arithmetic.

P5: Generation chooses a coherent family branch, then correlated composition/structure/condition within its constraints. Save the resolved batch with revision and provenance. Regional ore variation changes eligible yield/impurity fields; it does not randomly invent unrelated plant effects. Soil fractions sum on their declared basis; porosity, coating coverage and phase are not additional mass fractions. Alloys require allowed composition and supported processing branches; a random metal percentage vector is not guaranteed to form useful stock.

P6: Generate only contrasts with real consumers: one ore easier to refine but lower yield; one board suited to a brace but poor for a wick; one carrier retaining a coating longer but costing more to acquire. Make categories overlap without inheritance accidents. An added ceramic layer must not grant the substrate every ceramic ability, and a trace constituent must not dominate all bulk mechanical responses through tag union. Unknown imported materials can remain owned while unsupported processes decline before spending.

## Adversarial probes to specify before implementation

| Probe | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| T1 Same fractions, different structure | Granite-like and sandstone-like stock need not share all responses; alloy treatment or fiber orientation can change only declared consumers |
| T2 Same substrate, different surface | Wearing/removing a coating exposes the retained substrate; surface property does not rewrite bulk composition or duplicate stock |
| T3 Same name, different conditions | Wet versus dry board, packed versus loose powder, and gas at different conditions remain distinguishable where rules read them |
| T4 Unknown versus zero | Unassayed salt/ore never passes an “absent impurity” guarantee; unsupported imported feed is preserved on rejected processing |
| T5 Identity versus shared trait | Ore and bar, cured binder and uncured resin, salt A and salt B cannot bypass exact process eligibility |
| T6 Reversible versus recoverable | Dissolution/precipitation, remelting and recycling account for retained/escaped fractions, actual energy and recovered quality without free loops |
| T7 Mixture versus assembly | Powder blend does not spontaneously become bonded composite; fibers/layers need declared interfaces and actual processing |
| T8 Unit/basis mismatch | Mass fractions, soil size fractions, surface coverage and gas volume cannot share one sum or silently coerce incompatible measurements |
| T9 Saved revision and split/merge | Processed or coated variants retain their complete record across transfer, split, merge and reload; catalogue update never changes old truth silently |

Priority remains one useful first slice, then one different material consumer. The biggest extension is **structure and surfaces beside conserved composition**, with qualified measurement and explicit process eligibility.
