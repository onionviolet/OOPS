# Deeper materials and procedural rules: integrated proposal

2026-10-13. Six user-authorized deeper subagent lanes completed. This report extends the [first synthesis](../REPORT.md), rather than replacing source/design owners. [Assignment and writers](README.md) owns scope. No runtime implementation, accepted law change or new gameplay is claimed.

## Outcome and coverage

N1: The right shared foundation is a **typed product envelope**, not a universal bag of tags. It can reference composition, physical structure/surfaces, assembled parts, magical patterns, exact identities, living state and ongoing grants independently. Rules select the representations they need. Heat, light, sound, ambient energy, place conditions and authority are context/resources unless that world's law explicitly makes them material stock.

N2: The atlas contains **85 representative family/material/process cards**, including overlapping categories and multiple examples from one source. This is not 85 distinct substances, an exhaustive world inventory or 85 implemented features. The framework can admit more material cards without inventing a new subsystem for each one. Completeness means adequate data for selected consumers and declared coverage gaps, not filling every possible property for every object.

## What the deeper coverage changes

| Code | Additional requirement | Concrete distinction |
|---|---|---|
| N3 | Composition is separate from structure, surface and geometry | Same constituents can form powder, layered material or a connected part; melting need not preserve its assembly benefit |
| N4 | Functional properties are consumer-specific | Hardness, stiffness, fracture response, absorption and conductivity answer different questions; price/grade is not a universal fitness score |
| N5 | Living producers are separate from harvested stock | A seed/cutting/culture can develop under a declared law; an extracted potion cannot reproduce merely because it contains a plant tag |
| N6 | Heterogeneous stock cannot always be averaged | Different seeds, hides or anchored cores retain relevant members/sublots; homogeneous portions split proportionally only under a supported mixing model |
| N7 | Non-fungible identity differs from divisible contents | Aliquoting a characteristic-bearing potion cannot create several characteristics; contained identity is not additional free stock |
| N8 | Local effect/grant and charged object differ from conserved identity | Expired coating, discharged crystal, revoked grant and unavailable conserved identity have different transitions and recoverability |
| N9 | Similar appearance/name is not compatibility | A “mana” carrier needs an actual receiving-world coupling/conversion law; origin alone also does not forbid use |

The ordinary lane draws on BGS, USDA/NRCS, NIST and university references, with exact read boundaries. Coordinator independently opened [BGS rocks/minerals](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/discovering-geology/rocks-and-minerals/), confirming its same-constituent/different-arrangement example, and [Maryland grafting](https://extension.umd.edu/resource/graft-failure), confirming the distinct scion/rootstock roles. ## Proposed resource types and allowed operations

| Type | Permitted operations under its law | Avoid this accidental inference |
|---|---|---|
| Divisible composition | Mix, partition, separate and transform accounted fractions | Splitting creates active matter or erases trace material |
| Piece/layer/assembly | Shape, install, remove, coat, repair and disassemble real parts | Shared ingredient tags spontaneously create a bonded structure |
| Pattern/information | Copy, learn, connect and validate typed nodes | Copying a blueprint supplies substrate, energy, anchors or skill |
| Exact/conserved identity | Transfer, contain and explicitly supported conversion/extraction | A physical-property match duplicates a canon singularity |
| Living producer | Enroll, grow, propagate, harvest or lose viability | Passive inventory reproduces without inputs, site or supported time |
| Charge/exposure/grant | Spend, decay, expire, revoke or renew through explicit sources | Every depleted magical item yields a permanent characteristic |

A single product can contain several of these. A structured enchanted weapon might reference metal fractions, head/handle roles, surface coating, glyph graph and bound identity. A purification process names what it removes; it does not erase every curse, imprint, contaminant or obligation because the output says “pure.”

## A procedural rule needs enough data to execute

P1: Each rule declares identity/revision, local scope, trigger/operation/route, typed selectors, actual bound roles and quantities, process/environment conditions, transformation operator, destinations, accounting law, conflict policy, timing and executable consumer. Effect descriptions and wiki metadata are not executable consumers.

The rule lane proposes selectors for identity, family, property, constituent amount/concentration, phase, provenance, structure, affinity and condition. Roles distinguish reagent, carrier, catalyst, tool, energy source, anchor and living/ritual participant. Exact sample provenance or a pattern edge is categorical, not a percentage of brew mass.

P2: Use separate truth and knowledge states. Applicable material truth can match/mismatch; inapplicable properties and unresolved model/data are separate. Observer knowledge can be known, suspected or unobserved. Unobserved material truth still operates. Missing authoring data must fail validation or invoke explicit unresolved semantics; never treat it as zero, safe or absent.

The revised rule contract now defines `all`, `any` and `not`: a definite failed conjunct determines false, a definite successful disjunct determines true, and negation never turns unresolved/inapplicable into evidence. Positive admission still requires complete binding quantities for the actual selected branch. `someConstituent` binds eligible amounts; `allConstituents` checks its full declared domain, including traces; whole-batch properties need a derived law. One useful fraction cannot certify the whole vial.

P3: Quantities have dimensions and declared bases. External decimal strings can normalize into exact integer/rational units; canonicalization records conversion/remainder policy. Mass, volume, count, elapsed time, local charge and indivisible identity are not interchangeable. Soil texture, surface coverage and mass composition are different denominators. Absolute temperature and temperature difference are different types. Measurements keep relevant protocol, conditions, direction and uncertainty; knowledge measurements remain separate from internally authored truth.

P4: Resolve immutable snapshots in order: validate references/access → bind exact allocations → apply process steps → select reactions/allocate extents → transform and assign leftovers/byproducts → schedule relevant timed changes → derive consumer payload → validate ledgers/references → stage a durable delta. The host revalidates current ownership/sequence and commits once before publishing effects, discovery, XP or UI.

Composition transforms and structural transforms can be different operators. Simultaneous reactant competition needs declared priority/partition or a kinetic law. Equal-priority ambiguous consumption rejects before spending. Contact uses actual edges among participants; sharing a room is not automatic contact. Instant loops must terminate; timed/self-sustaining effects require supported source/rate/clock semantics. Computation limits cannot silently discard constituents or weaken a supported extreme lore effect.

P5: Save material definitions, generated resolutions, products, accepted procedures and active exposures under their separate retained revisions. Restore validates all holder references, including containers, storage, vendors, installed parts, pending jobs and effects whose source vial was already spent. No session-only registry or silent template fallback can preserve generated behavior. Current implementation gaps remain in the [first code audit](../IMPLEMENTATION.md); this proposal does not fix them.

## Generation should produce coherent, usable possibilities

P6: Generate at explicit boundaries: species/family, region, deposit, batch and processing outcome. Resolve eligible family branch → shared latent axes → correlated properties/fractions/structure → supply/context → compatible process/consumer → validation → saved truth and receipt. Rain can change batch moisture without rerolling species. Moving or previewing stock changes no generated truth.

The worked original families demonstrate an herb with correlated restorative/sedating fractions, an ore with composition/processing and a layered product, and an arcane pigment/crystal with local energy and pattern compatibility. These are separate fictional fixtures, not real chemistry or standardized properties for named canon materials.

P7: Generate hidden recipe roles and conditions once with a feasibility witness when the result is promised playable. Check ingredient availability, station/tool/energy, simultaneous requirements, process path and useful output. Catch circular acquisition gates without an initial route. Unknown-material trials must not require the very appraisal ability the recipe would unlock. Historical/dormant/lost recipes may intentionally remain unavailable, but label their status rather than advertise them as presently playable.

P8: Content lints reject dimension errors, duplicate identities, missing transformation destinations, invalid interfaces, contradictory family constraints, undefined property consumers, disconnected patterns, absent energy payers and silent revision loss. A rare superior material need not have a forced downside; correlation and tradeoffs follow the family law. Weird lawful combinations remain possible. Canon generation must not randomly manufacture additional exclusive identities or capabilities without an explicit local exception/AU rule.

## Broad scope and remaining source gaps

The atlas covers ordinary and fantastical families, but “all materials” remains an extensible coverage method. Additional cards should enter through source/edition/revision, exact locator, shown/stated/inferred evidence, relevant identity/amount/state/structure, process/use law, observer limits, unknowns, proposed consumer and an acceptance case. Original inventions carry explicit original-law references. Canon sources outrank fan summaries; a wiki is useful for discovery and qualified gap filling.

| Gap | Why it matters before selecting that slice |
|---|---|
| Ordinary specimen-specific numerical properties | This pass grounds categories and distinctions, not a full handbook, species-level assay catalogue or real metallurgy/chemistry simulator |
| HP broader wand woods/cores, creature products and complete formula census | Selected crafting scenes establish 10 cards, not every magical material or complete quantities/compatibilities |
| D&D beyond selected 2024 Equipment | Magic items, special materials, individual spell components and other editions need their own chosen source contracts |
| MoL and Soldier manufacturing details | Full extraction/treatment/essence catalogues, charged-carrier yields and apparatus values remain incomplete |
| TTKS/cultivation mirror snapshots | Exact versions, glyph damage, later coverage and exceptional copying/creation rules require selected checks |
| LOTM/COI and Shadow Slave fine arithmetic | Formula substitution, cleanse scope, identity handling and pattern/anchor capacity must stay recipe/world-specific |
| World compatibility and closed-browser time | Names do not establish conversions; queue/growth proposals do not establish offline behavior |

Research breadth does not make these all required first-slice gates. The approved one-realm/one-prestige, laptop-first implementation direction remains; original mixtures can prove the shared foundation before selecting another world or special resource type.

## Concrete acceptance cases and recommended sequence

| Case | Required outcome, specified not run |
|---|---|
| Split a trace-bearing brew | Both portions retain accounted active and trace fractions; rounding does not erase them |
| Process equal composition into different structure | Only declared structural/surface consumers change; melting/removal clears the corresponding structure benefit |
| Select a living propagule versus extracted stock | Only the eligible propagule enters an actual growth process; dead reagent cannot clone itself |
| Split a potion with a conserved spiritual identity | Delivery follows its world law without duplicating the identity or its contained resources |
| Compare unknown versus unresolved | Unknown-to-player effects operate; undefined required rule data rejects before spending |
| Generate a hidden recipe | A saved stable definition and witness support the promised route; trials never reroll it |
| Persist and restore consumed-product exposure | Exact payload/revision/timing survives after vial consumption and reference cleanup |

A1, recommendation: choose exact normalized quantity units, canonical product references, one competing-rule policy and one effect family, then implement the complete Village mixture journey already proposed. A2: prove different reuse with one surface/structural product. A3: introduce one coherent local family generator and learned repeat production. Biological producers, magical identities and book adapters remain selected later slices, with their own consumer proof.
