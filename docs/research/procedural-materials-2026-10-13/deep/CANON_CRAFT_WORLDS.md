# Canon material and process cards: HP, D&D and Mother of Learning

2026-10-13. Second-round research, not accepted design or implementation. Sole changed file: this report. Twenty representative cards cover materials, preparation, apparatus, charged fuel and constructed substrates. They are not exhaustive catalogues. No runtime edits, saves, builds, tests, commits, source-library changes or new acquisition. No original/generated sealed truth is disclosed.

**F1: Recipe presence establishes participation, not an ingredient's universal elemental or medicinal property.** Separate physical composition, symbolic identity, condition/provenance, prepared structure, charge and local permission. An exact-ID selector and a functional-property selector can coexist, but substitution needs a supported local rule or explicitly original proposal.

## Harry Potter: ten cards

### H01 Dried nettles and crushed snake fangs

**Shown:** B1 ch8, PDF111, L3743–3749, CCF03. Pupils weigh dried nettles and crush snake fangs for Cure for Boils. This establishes species/part and prepared state, not separate healing strength, extraction chemistry or toxicity. **Quantity:** unknown. **Policy:** exact prepared ingredient slots for this recipe; profile alternatives require an authored variant. **Consumer:** procurement -> weighing/crushing -> reserved prepared lot -> brew stage. **Missing source:** exact amounts, permitted particle size, drying process, complete formula. **Check:** crushing transfers stock into one prepared lot without creating a second raw copy; good preparation alone cannot produce the finished potion.

### H02 Stewed horned slugs and heat-sensitive quill addition

**Shown/stated:** B1 ch8, PDF111–112, L3743–3768, CCF03–04. Stewed slugs are demonstrated; damaged cauldron/spill injury is shown; Snape attributes failure to porcupine quills added before heat removal. **Quantity:** stew time and amounts unknown. **Policy:** preserve exact named animal-part roles and prepared states; do not generalize the teacher's diagnosis into a universal quill heat law. **Consumer:** ordered brew with heat-off checkpoint, separate vessel damage/exposure state. **Missing source:** entire stage sequence and salvage law. **Check:** declared order changes outcome once; tool repair cannot retrospectively restore destroyed contents.

### H03 Full-moon fluxweed

**Stated:** Hermione, B2 ch10, PDF433, L4155–4168, CCF06; primary window rechecked. Polyjuice fluxweed has a specified harvesting condition. **Quantity:** unknown; lunar condition known. **Policy:** exact plant identity plus harvest provenance, not “moon affinity” averaged into a mixture. Authentic purchased/prepared stock may qualify under an authored custody rule. **Consumer:** sourcing -> recorded harvest event -> recipe admission. **Missing source:** freshness/shelf life and effect of wrong provenance. **Check:** unknown provenance is presented as uncertain; only supported evidence authenticates it. A failed qualifier does not invent an unsourced alternate potion effect.

### H04 Twenty-one-day lacewing preparation

**Stated:** same B2 ch10 passage, CCF06. Lacewings must be stewed for twenty-one days; the estimated month includes acquisition uncertainty. **Quantity:** elapsed preparation duration known, lacewing amount and heat regime unknown. **Policy:** exact prepared lot, with process age separate from calendar harvest provenance; no inferred requirement to stew every insect for this duration. **Consumer:** finite timed preparation -> ready lot -> Polyjuice stock. **Missing source:** interrupted heating, spoilage and finished-brew stability. **Check:** reload retains elapsed stage state; a ready-prepared purchase can satisfy the same declared state without charging preparation twice.

### H05 Powdered bicorn horn and shredded boomslang skin

**Stated:** B2 ch10, PDF432–433, L4132–4153, CCF06; rechecked. Both are named Polyjuice materials, with preparation forms and distinct private-store access. **Quantity:** unknown. **Policy:** identity/part/preparation selectors; “horn”, “skin” or expensive-material tags do not prove substitution. **Consumer:** specialist supply or authorized store access -> batch reservation -> brew. **Missing source:** independent ingredient behavior, full quantities and legitimate retail availability. **Check:** knowing a formula grants neither ownership nor store permission; shredding a titled skin changes its form while preserving custody and amount accounting.

### H06 A bit of the intended person

**Stated:** B2 ch10, PDF432, L4139–4150, CCF06; added last. This is a target-identity input, unlike the physical fractions in H01. **Quantity:** unspecified sample amount. **Policy:** require sample provenance pointing to the actual donor; a same-species generic substitute cannot target that donor. Do not broaden “a bit” into every body fluid or arbitrary manufactured identity copy without source. **Consumer:** identity-binding final stage. **Missing source:** accepted sample types, contamination, storage and age limits. **Check:** actual donor and player-believed donor remain separate; labels never rewrite the sample's origin. Unknown sample identity stays concealed until a supported discovery route.

### H07 Mandrake seedlings, earmuffs and maturation

**Shown/stated:** B2 ch6, PDF352–355, L2315–2395, CCF05. Moving seedlings are repotted with damp compost, pots and completely covering earmuffs; Sprout describes restorative use and young-cry unconsciousness. **Quantity:** four students per tray is shown, not a harvest-yield rule. **Policy:** retain living developmental state, hearing/exposure route and equipment fit; no inferred bottled healing fraction. **Consumer:** protected greenhouse handling -> institution-owned growing project. **Missing source:** mature processing formula, maturity duration/yield and efficacy scope. **Check:** ear protection affects this exposure route; tending school stock does not create privately owned duplicate harvest.

### H08 Bubotuber pus, gloves, dilution and hospital supply

**Shown/stated:** B4 ch13, PDF1300–1301, L4824–4855, CCF10. Dragon-hide gloves are used while collecting pus; Sprout calls it an acne remedy and warns against undiluted contact; corked bottles go to the infirmary. **Quantity:** dilution ratio/yield unknown. **Policy:** exact secretion identity and concentration-dependent topical rule, not automatic oral healing. Gloves provide the sourced handling role without universal immunity. **Consumer:** protected extraction -> titled bottles -> infirmary delivery. **Missing source:** dilution method, therapeutic dose and shelf life. **Check:** delivery debits the pupil-held school lot once; any original dilution rule preserves total active amount and carrier volume.

### H09 Sopophorous bean, silver dagger and mechanical preparation

**Shown:** B6 ch9, PDF3070–3073, L6472–6573, CCF14. Flat-dagger crushing releases useful juice and advances a particular brew cue better than the printed cutting instruction. Tool is a silver dagger, but metal causality is not independently isolated. **Quantity:** juice amount/particle threshold unknown. **Policy:** exact method revision initially; functional crushing-tool substitutions are proposals requiring comparison. **Consumer:** pressed intermediate -> revised Living Death brew method. **Missing source:** silver necessity and complete formula/effect test. **Check:** splitting the comparison isolates cutting versus crushing; “silver” alone cannot be credited as the cause or confer the same bonus on unrelated recipes.

### H10 Pewter cauldron, brass scales, glass/crystal phials

**Shown:** B1 ch5, PDF52–53/64, L1760–1785/L2154–2165, CCF01. School kit specifies pewter size2 cauldron; scales and phials are actual apparatus. **Quantity:** cauldron size category known; capacity/accuracy unknown. **Policy:** exact school-kit compliance where required, functional vessel/weighing roles only where an authored recipe permits. Gold price is not a potion-quality score. **Consumer:** owned/loaned station access, measuring, bottling. **Missing source:** chemical compatibility, calibration and substitute vessel performance. **Check:** a loan reserves availability without consuming the tool; container repair preserves its owner while leaving lost solution lost, supported separately by CCF15.

## D&D 2024: five cards

For D01–05, canonical facts below are terse summaries of the linked official Equipment subsections. Proposed alternate material profiles are explicit extra mechanics, not prescribed 2024 rules.

### D01 Acid vial

**Rule, Acid:** one thrown vial can deal 2d6 Acid damage; no chemical identity/concentration is specified. **Quantity:** vial dose known; molarity unknown. **Policy:** exact equipment effect under this edition; a generated corrosive liquid needs an explicit adapter. **Consumer:** ranged object/creature exposure. **Missing:** synthesis and structural-corrosion law. **Check:** the consumed vial cannot also remain tradeable; damage type does not imply universal metal dissolution.

### D02 Oil flask, lamp fuel and surface exposure

**Rule, Oil:** one flask fuels a lamp/lantern for six cumulative hours; dousing has separate burn rules. **Quantity:** flask count and fuel time known; chemical composition unknown. **Policy:** exact equipment law; procedural fuel efficiency is an original variant. **Consumer:** fuel store or coating/ground application. **Missing:** mixing chemistry and residue. **Check:** extinguishing preserves remaining cumulative fuel time, while pouring redirects finite stock into an exposure owner rather than retaining a full flask.

### D03 Holy Water

**Rule, Holy Water:** a flask can deal 2d8 Radiant damage to a Fiend or Undead. **Quantity:** flask dose known; consecration details absent here. **Policy:** exact blessed-item identity, not generic water purity. **Consumer:** creature-type-dependent exposure. **Missing:** manufacture and desecration law. **Check:** water-like physical properties alone cannot supply the conditional effect; a creature's actual local type governs response separately from the observer's belief.

### D04 Healing potion and Herbalism Kit

**Rule, Brewing Potions of Healing:** kit/proficiency, 25 GP materials and eight hours; potion heals 2d4+2. **Quantity:** budget/time/effect known; herbs unknown. **Policy:** abstract permitted material budget, preserving canonical output; herb chemistry would be added design. **Consumer:** timed manufacture -> administered potion. **Missing:** particular ingredients/purity. **Check:** adequate raw-material value without real kit access cannot start; a kit is reusable, potion dose consumable. Improved skill does not silently increase this named canonical effect.

### D05 Scroll materials and prepared spell

**Rule, Scribing Spell Scrolls:** prepared spell and appropriate proficiency are required; consumed spell components debit on completion. **Quantity:** level-based schedule exists; item-level component amounts depend on the spell. **Policy:** exact required component selectors; generic ink similarity supplies no missing spell. **Consumer:** staged inscription. **Missing:** a selected spell's complete component contract and any extra material recipe. **Check:** ordinary substrate, reusable supplies, knowledge/preparation and consumed component owners remain distinct; cancellation follows an explicitly chosen contract instead of duplicating components.

## Mother of Learning: five cards

### M01 Crystallized mana

**Stated:** ch39 *Suspicious Coincidences* ¶54; trains use it, ch1 ¶133. Stable solid ambient mana is a durable battery but harder to use directly than ambient mana. **Quantity:** charge per crystal and conversion efficiency unknown. **Policy:** exact local energy carrier; same “mana” label elsewhere is insufficient. **Consumer:** device/train fuel; storage and commerce separate from personal reserves. **Missing:** purity grades, spent crystal residue and recharge process. **Check:** a device debits one declared charge owner; no automatic equal-volume crystal-to-personal-mana conversion or invented inert residue.

### M02 Alchemically treated steel

**Stated/described in reader:** golem entries ch43 ¶76/ch46 ¶23 identify treated steel, glyph stabilization and crystal battery. **Quantity:** treatment quantities and steel specification unknown. **Policy:** prepared substrate identity, not raw-metal strength alone; substitute structure needs an authored validation. **Consumer:** durable constructed frame. **Missing:** treatment formula, geometry tolerances and failure modes. **Check:** ordinary steel and treated steel are separate states; supplying either never automatically supplies glyphs or charged battery. Structural repair restores only the declared component, not energy or control pattern.

### M03 Stabilizer glyphs and telepathic relay

**Stated/shown through reader:** ch43 ¶76/ch46 ¶23 supplies stabilization; ch48 ¶13–17 grounds remote-control relay and inability to cast through it. **Quantity:** glyph count and relay range unknown here. **Policy:** typed structure/pattern input, not dissolvable affinity fraction. **Consumer:** assembled golem control, with local access and interference. **Missing:** actual glyph schematics and inscription medium. **Check:** a relay can provide its declared control function without granting every spellcasting route. Copying a picture of a glyph does not prove an operational inscription or bypass knowledge/execution prerequisites.

### M04 Dirge moth chrysalis and soul-perception potion

**Stated/shown through reader:** Silverlake/narration, ch74 *The Return* ¶117–125; ch75 *Soul Stealer* ¶190, identifies chrysalis or other soul-attacking creature parts in a dangerous soul-perception route. **Quantity:** dose/recipe unknown; one-to-five-day unconsciousness is recorded, not manufacturing duration. **Policy:** sourced family-level alternatives only after the recipe's actual qualifying requirement is recovered; no generic insect substitution. **Consumer:** specific bodily/soul effect, separate from laboratory knowledge. **Missing:** full formulation and eligible alternative parts. **Check:** acquiring a chrysalis alone grants neither the potion nor soul sight; actual preparation and use must publish the corresponding body state.

### M05 Ward material, geometry and power budget

**Stated/described through reader:** ch3 ¶4 defines warding through material, geometry, surrounding magic and power. Ch30 ¶74 distinguishes entrance-only coverage; ch30 ¶96–99 describes different ward-breaking approaches. **Quantity:** material amounts and numeric budget unknown. **Policy:** installed structure has typed connections/coverage plus energy dependency, not aggregate enchanted-material tags. **Consumer:** one situated boundary or alarm. **Missing:** exact drawn patterns, material catalogue and operating thresholds. **Check:** an entrance-only ward's declared coverage leaves walls outside that protection; equivalent material composition with different topology cannot silently inherit full-room coverage.

## Local-world adapter proposals

| Code | Adapter and generation constraint | Narrow specified-not-run check |
|---|---|---|
| A1 HP | Recipe admits exact ingredient/preparation/provenance predicates, ordered stages and observer-specific cues. Personal sample identity binds separately. **F2: Generated original variants need authored law bounds.** Generate correlated plant/ore families with declared extraction responses, prepared states and meaningful consumers. Preserve full constituent amounts when composition matters; preserve exact identity when symbolic origin matters; preserve topology when shape matters. No arbitrary “healing” tag follows from participation in a cure recipe. A copied pattern is information, not installed means. Canon exceptions remain explicit rather than averaged away.

**F3: Conservation claims must be local.** HP CCF15 supports repaired bowl versus lost essence, not universal impossibility of magical creation. D&D's selected costs are crafting contracts, not proof of a physical conservation theorem. MoL's crystal and structure descriptions establish dependencies, not a numerical universal energy equation. Spent-container residue is presently unknown across these cards; do not fabricate it to fill an atlas column.

Missing gates remain source specificity, selected procedural extensions, runtime ownership/implementation and rebuilt native use/persistence proof. The 20 cards and adapter checks are proposals with explicit evidence limits; none is executed gameplay.
