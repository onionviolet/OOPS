## Main conclusion

F1: Build reusable material, process, product and discovery records, then attach world-specific rules and useful consumers. Derived composition can generate potions and salves; component structure can generate equipment; pattern structure can generate inscriptions/devices; exact identities can support ritual or soul-material systems. These share ownership, persistence and production infrastructure without requiring the same law for every material.

F2: Specific books, primary game sources, wiki research and original ideas can all contribute. Preserve source/edition, confidence and adaptation status. Canon constraints remain exact where the source is exact; functional substitutions remain available where the local rules permit them. Original worlds can define new coherent laws. A shared name such as mana, steel or healing does not establish cross-world equivalence.

F3: Procedural generation reduces per-combination authoring, but we still author behavior primitives, process grammars, material families and exceptional laws. An invented tag is inert until a consumer or rule reads it. New material families should expose useful choices, not a long list of decorative statistics.

## Four kinds of product logic

| Code | Logic | Suitable uses | What must survive |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Composition and transformations | Potions, salves, dyes, fuels, foods, alloys | Constituent amounts, trace fractions, phase/condition, extraction and conversion history, byproducts |
| P2 | Parts and interfaces | Tools, weapons, clothes, fixtures and installed devices | Part roles, relevant geometry/connectors, material contribution, condition and installation ownership |
| P3 | Patterns and channels | Enchantments, rune inks, formations and constructed magic | Pattern identity/structure, carrier eligibility, energy source, channels/anchors and shared capacity |
| P4 | Conserved or exact identity | Canon potion cores, singular materials, ritual offerings and world-specific soul substances | Identity, quantity or indivisibility as appropriate, provenance, eligible consumer and the relevant world's law |

These are complementary. A powered blade can have an alloy composition, head/handle structure, inscription pattern and an exact bound core. Do not reduce the four into one additive tag-strength calculation. A conceptual offering need not have mass, temperature or liquid dose; define only the properties its world actually gives it.

## Three distinct procedural modes

P5: **Derived outcomes.** Actual ingredients, ratios, preparation and environment determine the product. The same complete facts reproduce the same chemistry. Skill controls execution through declared precision/yield mechanisms. A failed preparation may leave a changed mixture, recoverable intermediate or waste rather than erase the stock.

P6: **Generated material families.** Resolve regional species, deposits or batch variations once under coherent constraints. Related specimens share useful structure. Rare affinities require a compatible branch; canon objects retain their selected identity. Save resolved properties and generation revision, not merely a seed that changes meaning after an update.

P7: **Generated discovery recipes.** Select hidden roles/conditions once at an explicit world/life boundary. Save the resolved recipe and any exceptional law. Testing discovers that truth; it does not reroll it. Distinguish a functional role, an exact ingredient and an abstract canonical material budget.

Example from the fictional rule lane: a leaf contains restorative, sedating and inert fractions. Extraction transfers selected amounts; filtration removes specified residue; a binder can bind one fraction; dilution lowers concentration without creating active matter. A single mechanism yields several brews without a separate item template for each. The written example accounts for products and waste and does not claim real chemistry.

Official rules/source are primary where available. A fan wiki supplies leads and qualified secondary evidence, not automatic canon admission. Existing book acquisition routing applies if further text is needed; this pass collected no new books.

Coverage includes plant/wood, animal/biological, fibers/textiles, minerals/metals, ceramics/glass, liquids/gases, charged/magical substrates, and source-supported soul/conceptual identities. These intersect: blood can be biological and liquid; rune ink can be fluid and charged; a wooden enchanted handle has several roles. ## Reconciled implementation proposal

P9: **Explicit allocation → pure resolution → validated saved product → durable publication → observer-filtered discovery → route-specific use.** Generated products need native decoder support and complete saved references in every holder. Input selection must preserve exact batch keys rather than silently aggregate cheapest variants. Active effects need saved payload/family/timing and instance-aware application; the current template-duration loader cannot prove that.

Current source already supplies complete-key transfers, acquisition staging, crafting rollback, finite reservations and jobs. Reuse them with a deliberate generated-product envelope, not another inventory or scheduler. Current WorkPlans references validated authored commands; saved player procedures require an adapter rather than arbitrary additional cursor fields.

P10: **Learned procedures support idle production.** Save operation order, allowed substitutions, apparatus requirements and shortage/variation stop conditions. Freeze each admitted batch's actual terms. Queue support does not establish closed-browser production. Use supported game-time boundaries first; offline equivalence becomes a gate only if an offline policy is selected.

Rules must declare quantities, retention/transformation, route, conditions, competition and relevant timing. Support exact/profile/mixed roles and multiple participants. Independent reactions can commute; competing consumption needs authored precedence/partition. Instant cycles must terminate or reject before commit; timed/reversible cycles use explicit clocks. Computational limits must not silently truncate products or cap a permitted lore effect's scale.

Knowledge changes prediction, not material truth. Internal cause traces are filtered before display; some observations reveal only the tested trait. Research is not free full identification. Do not leak a sealed truth through a generated name, price, tooltip, comparison or hidden-property preview.
