## Ten transferable patterns

### P1 HP: exact identity and harvest provenance

**Canon, attributed:** CCF06, B2 chapter 10, compilation PDF432–433, TXT4132–4168: Hermione specifies full-moon fluxweed, prolonged lacewing preparation and a final person's sample. Generic plant affinity is insufficient evidence of substitutability. The scene does not give complete quantities or a production calendar.

**Proposal:** Slots can require `(material identity, prepared state, source condition)` alongside functional properties. In original mixtures, broaden a solvent role by dissolution behavior while keeping an exact identity role for an authored ritual. Do not randomize canon Polyjuice requirements as if they were established canon.

**Proof:** Two mechanically similar plant lots differ only in required provenance; the exact recipe distinguishes them, while a separately authored functional recipe permits both. Unknown provenance remains uncertainty, not proof of failure.

### P2 HP: order, tool action and observation context

**Canon shown/attributed:** CCF04, B1 chapter 8, PDF111–112/TXT3749–3768, shows cauldron damage and exposure; Snape attributes failure to quills before heat removal. CCF14, B6 chapter 9, PDF3070–3073/TXT6472–6573, shows a particular crushing/stirring improvement. CCF09 and CCF04 use green in different successful/failed contexts.

**Proposal:** Processes transform prepared lots through ordered operations. Outcome depends on relevant heat/tool actions, not a generic skill roll overriding hard procedure. Observation records recipe, stage and modality; skill supports control and diagnosis.

**Proof:** Replay identical stock with heat removal before versus after the declared addition. Different results survive reload, and an observed green cue cannot identify another recipe's hidden truth.

### P3 D&D: an abstract crafting rule is a valid local law

**Official 2024 rules:** Healing-potion manufacture requires Herbalism Kit proficiency, that kit, 25 GP raw materials and eight hours of work. General nonmagical crafting also requires tools, material availability and time; scroll inscription preserves required components, consuming the specified components on completion. [Equipment](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/equipment).

**Proposal:** A D&D adapter may use the canonical abstract material budget without pretending the rule specifies a herb formula. A richer procedural laboratory is an explicit additional rule/variant. Reusable tool access, spent ingredients and consumable magic components remain different resource roles.

**Proof:** A timed canonical healing-potion job publishes one usable potion under those conditions, while its cancellation policy preserves the separately declared spent/unspent resources. Ingredient chemistry is not required to pass that edition's recipe.

### P4 A Soldier's Life: a material changes potential, not learned mastery

**Dossier-grounded canon summary:** skills and essences occupy separate axes; the dossier does not give a complete essence formulation catalogue. Book5 chapter308 paragraphs13–30 specifically grounds tattooed spell-form breadth versus core flexibility/exhaustion. Storage also has explicit rules: Book1 chapter5 paragraph179 versus exceptional Book5 chapter310 paragraphs48–53 and chapter311 paragraphs4–10.

**Proposal:** Material consumers target named bodily/power axes, rather than granting every related skill. Preservation depends on container/storage law, not rarity. Ordinary field supplies, medicines and inscriptions can share lot records but use separate effect adapters.

**Proof:** One authored essence raises a supported potential field without granting trained alchemy; one preserved batch retains state only under its specified storage law. Exact canon essence amounts/effects need a further scene check before implementation.

### P5 TTKS: blueprint, carrier and fuel are independent

**Source-dossier rule:** B5, Wasteland Border Inspector R10, members0221/0360/0396/0408, describes rule-information runes requiring material carrier and fuel; partial fragments can work. Member0408 supplies four expressions sharing one charge pool. These are captured-edition facts, not universal magical physics.

**Proposal:** Craft an artifact from a structural carrier, inscription/pattern and energy supply. Procedural variants change carrier suitability, efficiency or supported expressions without treating a stored blueprint as a finished item. One energy pool can feed several modes.

**Proof:** Two supported modes debit the same saved charge owner; a copied pattern without a suitable carrier/fuel cannot publish an operational artifact. A fragment retains only its authored supported function.

### P6 TTKS: assay instruments have blind spots

**Source-dossier rule:** B5 member0038 says a green rot-light result means no known match, not absence of unknown infection. B4 R1 members0003/0018/0031 separates detector ceiling from true measured condition. These are biological/inspection cases; applying them to material assays is an **original adaptation**.

**Proposal:** Each assay asks a limited question: identity, concentration, affinity, contamination family or provenance authentication. Instruments can saturate or miss unmatched substances. Purity is the amount of a specified target fraction, separate from overall quality and suitability.

**Proof:** An unrecognized trace constituent survives a negative family assay and affects use under its actual rule; a second appropriate assay reveals only what it detects.

### P7 Mother of Learning: experimental recipes become routines

**Reader-grounded canon:** Alchemy does not require shaping (chapter7 paragraph160; chapter84 paragraph78); measured modern methods differ from improvisational traditional methods (chapter28 paragraph137; chapter74 paragraphs214–221). Kael uses repeated experiments to optimize recipes/costs (chapter83 paragraphs55–58).

**Proposal:** Save a procedure with tolerances and achieved outcomes. Improvisation explores choices; validated production executes them using actual inputs and capability. Control precision and ingredient efficiency matter without continually rerolling material truth. Specific ratios and tolerances remain authored because these passages do not supply them.

**Proof:** An experiment establishes one useful method; an idle repeat consumes matching stock and reproduces the output, stops on shortage and retains its procedure revision after import.

### P8 Mother of Learning: construction is a network of material functions

**Reader-grounded canon:** Golem manufacture uses stabilizer glyphs, crystallized-mana batteries and alchemically treated steel (chapter43 paragraph76; chapter46 paragraph23). Warding considers material, geometry, existing magic and power budget (chapter3 paragraph4). Crystals are stable fuel, not identical to freely usable personal mana (chapter39 paragraph54).

**Proposal:** Extend composition to treated metal, conductive inks and installed assemblies. A chassis, channel, control pattern and power source fill different roles. Cross-world imports are tested against the particular consumer's local requirements; a shared word “mana” does not prove interchangeability.

**Proof:** One original powered lamp consumes its fuel record while its frame remains; substituting an authored compatible fuel works, an unmatched affinity does not silently convert. This proves the adapter, not canon golem completeness.

### P9 LOTM/COI: conserved magical identity survives transformations

**Dossier-grounded canon:** L2 members0209/0221/0232 tracks persistent characteristics; COI C0295 records folded lower sequences. L7 members0018/0033/0165–0167 identifies specific loss-of-control causes. Recipe auxiliaries and a characteristic are not equivalent generic reagents.

**Proposal:** Use unique conserved characteristic records with pathway, contained sequence history and applicable contamination. Potion preparation changes carrier and exposure, preserving conserved identity. Compatible-looking mundane herbs cannot synthesize that identity without an explicitly sourced or authored law. Cross-world operation remains a compatibility proposal.

**Proof:** Moving one characteristic through preparation, inventory and an eligible consumer never duplicates it; a wrong-pathway check follows the selected local rule. A matching physical-property substitute supplies no characteristic.

### P10 Shadow Slave: artifact generation needs a structure grammar

**Reader-grounded canon, chiefly stated:** chapter613 describes essence-string/nexus spellweaves; chapters695–701 distinguish pattern, name, anchors, strength and incompatibility. Chapter2048 describes a staged mundane forging/infusion/inscription/Memory pipeline and replication before invention. A scalar tier alone cannot establish an enchantment fits.

**Proposal:** Generate artifacts from constrained pattern components and typed connections, not an arbitrary list of buff tags. Carrier/material, anchor rank, thread strength, naming and practiced method can each constrain assembly under this world's adapter. Original tools can reuse the structural idea without borrowing privileged lineage access.

**Proof:** Two equal-capacity frames accept different authored enchantments; a valid assembled artifact preserves its anchors and effects through save/import, while incompatible assembly does not consume stock during preview.
