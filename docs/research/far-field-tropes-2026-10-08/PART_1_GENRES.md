# Part 1: far-field mechanics

Research date: 2026-10-08. All YAIR mappings are original proposals. Linked developer, publisher and official rule pages establish the specific source features named below; this is a documentary pass, not an installed-game audit. No balance values or exact thresholds are imported. NS means [the live north star](../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md), whose status remains proposal.

## Dating and visual novel design, beyond an affection bar

**F1 Different games answer different questions.** Tokimeki's finite school life emphasizes allocation; Monster Prom combines self-development with high-pressure choices; Stardew's gift and status systems make relationship stages explicit; Hades lets repeat encounters develop a relationship through ongoing story. Those are source contrasts, not a mandate that YAIR use all four.

**O1 Proposed relationship state:** save a few significant experiences, expressed preferences, invitation/commitment status and unresolved disagreement. Affection can coexist with incompatible plans. Public standing can coexist with private trust. This adds to the existing layers and flags without pretending a universal attraction equation can model every NPC.

**O2 A concrete route:** a cartographer invites you to check a river crossing. Join, suggest a workshop afternoon, or decline warmly. The shared work reveals that she wants a traveling life. Later, she asks whether you want a relationship; agree on traveling together, discuss separate bases, or remain friends. A failed survey can still be a remembered afternoon. A rare gift helps only where its meaning fits. The confession result records what both characters chose, rather than converting enough gifts into entitlement.

**O3 Timing and multiple endings:** a route can end with partners who travel together, partners who live apart, enduring friendship, a respectful separation, or an unresolved connection. Those are proposed outcomes with callbacks. A missed invitation can offer a changed later scene, not necessarily permanent lockout. Schedule pressure comes from game time the player advances; no wall-clock calendar is proposed. Existing calendar/favorability/route ideas are already in the north star and are not counted as new ideas in Part 3.

## Source depth limits

No game was launched, purchased, decompiled or installed for this task. Product descriptions support loops and named broad mechanics; they do not establish numeric tables, exact route flags or late-game edge cases. Persona social links, Harvest Moon, Pokemon and Werewolf are comparison leads, not individually completed audits. The requested genre coverage is provided by the named inspected examples above.
