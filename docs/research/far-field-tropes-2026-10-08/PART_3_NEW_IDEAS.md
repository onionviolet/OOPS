# Part 3: 24 new interaction ideas

2026-10-08. Original design proposals, not accepted decisions or implemented features. “New” means the **specific interaction** was not found in the live north star during this pass. It does not mean no game or novel has ever used it. Every idea builds on an existing direction; that connection is stated explicitly. Broad romance, factions, weather, museums, ghost companions, loops and curriculum-free “teaching as power” are already present and do not count as new here. IDs remain stable across this packet.

## People and shared life

**I01 The invitation comes from them.** An NPC asks you to join a practical outing because of their own current goal, not because you clicked a relationship button. Accept, counteroffer a time/place, or decline warmly; the NPC then makes their own plan. A later callback recognizes the answer without requiring affection loss for every refusal. **NS: Romance and dating mechanics; Random events.** New delta: player refusal and counteroffers resolve NPC-originated social commitments. G01/G11 provide the encounter structure; initiative and its consequences are our invention.

**I02 Same affection, different shared futures.** Two characters like each other but one wants a stable workshop and the other wants seasonal travel. Negotiate a home base, agreed periods apart, or friendship. The result changes visits, work arrangements and the route ending; a bigger affection value cannot choose the arrangement for you. **NS: Romance and dating mechanics; Work.** New delta: practical compatibility is an explicit authored negotiation separate from relationship stage and cross-world distance.

**I03 A gift acquires a history.** A cheap cup given after a first survey becomes significant because of that scene. Years later, repairing it, replacing it or giving it to an apprentice produces different reactions. Its importance comes from named prior events, so buying another identical cup does not reproduce the memory. **NS: Little stuff; Romance and dating mechanics.** New delta: an item's relationship meaning changes through its individual provenance, beyond fixed liked/disliked categories or a favorite food. Item histories are already proposed; the added operation is renegotiating an individual object's social meaning.

**I04 Public allies, private disagreement.** Your partner supports your school publicly but privately objects to its admissions policy. Choose a candid private discussion, invite a public review, or postpone the issue with a stated next meeting. Their public support and personal opinion can change separately. **NS: Romance and dating mechanics; Followers and factions.** New delta: a disagreement has an audience and a response venue, rather than becoming an undifferentiated trust penalty.

**I05 A household exchanges shifts.** During one season, either person can cover care, paid work or an expedition, with the other taking a different role next season. Agree on a schedule and see who gets a training opportunity; the household's plan may change at review. **NS: Work; Progression.** New delta: household labor substitution trades actual opportunities between named people. This is a bounded authored arrangement, not a new global population simulation.

**I06 Your friend introduces two strangers.** A recurring friend offers to introduce a merchant and a scholar through you. Choose a workshop meeting, a meal or a public demonstration. The venue reveals different things and may create an NPC-to-NPC connection that later operates without you. **NS: Little stuff; Followers and factions.** New delta: acting as a host changes a relationship edge between other actors, rather than simply earning another favor toward the player.

## Teaching, work and institutions

**I07 Teach in an order that matters.** Two apprentices can learn observation before procedure or procedure before observation. A later ambiguous repair case exposes the difference: one recognizes the cause, the other completes the familiar process faster. Let them retrain after the field result. **NS: Followers and factions; Progression.** New delta: curriculum ordering creates different operational competence, while “students carry your techniques” already belongs to the north star. No universal pedagogy claim is intended.

**I08 A student sets the graduation goal.** An apprentice wants to keep an elderly relative mobile, while you prefer producing a tournament-winning swordsmith. Accept their practical final project, propose a compromise or redirect them to another teacher. Their demonstration can establish mastery through service and change their later workplace. **NS: Followers and factions; Work.** New delta: negotiate a learner's authored endpoint rather than awarding the teacher's standard progression badge. G17 inspires the finite training horizon.

**I09 A guild runs anonymous demonstrations.** Craftspeople submit one repair without their names attached. Judge the result and supporting explanation, then reveal who made it. Reputation and actual performance may disagree, offering a concrete reason to hire a dismissed specialist or challenge a trusted master. **NS: Research as a standard system; Work.** New delta: temporary identity concealment changes evaluation, not power. The useful outcome is an appointment or method comparison, not a new universal rank.

**I10 The clinic is limited by handoffs.** One healer can diagnose quickly but needs an assistant to prepare supplies and another to follow up. Pick assignments for three cases; the post-shift report identifies which stage delayed recovery. Hire, train or reorganize based on the report. **NS: Work; Followers and factions.** New delta: service capacity depends on process stages between people rather than just staff stats. G03 supplies the flow inspiration; fantasy treatment effects remain locally authored.

**I11 Let patients choose whether to wait.** A small clinic offers an immediate modest intervention or a slower specialist appointment. Explain the difference and ask the NPC's preference; their work and care obligations can make the “best” treatment impractical. A referral can succeed without the player retaining the fee. **NS: Work; Random events.** New delta: a service recipient's decision affects scheduling and reputation. This is fictional clinic design, not medical advice or a claim about realistic treatment.

**I12 Certify a routine by handing it over.** An apprentice watches a process, performs it with you, then completes one supervised independent order. Sign off only the demonstrated routine, which now runs without your repeated clicks; a novel material calls you back. **NS: Progression; Followers and factions; Work.** New delta: delegation unlocks after a concrete transfer-of-competence event, rather than assigning arbitrary automation at a skill threshold. It fits an idle game while giving teaching a remembered moment.

**I13 A kitchen chooses its social purpose.** A communal dinner can prioritize inexpensive plentiful meals, unfamiliar dishes that bring outsiders, or one time-consuming dish shared between rival groups. Ingredients and service capacity limit the menu, and the guest mix changes the night's conversations. **NS: Work; Little stuff; Random events.** New delta: menu composition shapes encounter eligibility and who meets, not just food buffs or cash yield. G04 provides the preparation and capacity structure.

**I14 A fair has a program, not only a date.** Organize three slots: apprentice demonstrations, trader consultations or public storytelling. Their order changes which visitors stay for later encounters. A spectacular opening draws a crowd but can exhaust the staff needed for a quiet recruitment session. **NS: Little stuff; Followers and factions.** New delta: festival sequencing shapes a visitor cohort and service opportunities. Existing festivals remain the container; this is the interaction inside one.

## Evidence and collective choices

**I15 Record how a clue reached you.** A witness handled a relic after its discovery; another only heard the witness's account. The Notebook stores those chains, letting you distinguish two independent observations from two repetitions of one story. Ask for a fresh test, inspect the object or proceed with uncertain evidence. **NS: Knowledge and discovery; Research as a standard system.** New delta: evidence dependence and contamination affect corroboration, beyond rumor/confirmed labels or a case board.

**I16 Observation windows change the available proof.** A ruin's inscription is visible only while a pool is partly drained. Arrive early to study the surroundings or later to read it; preparing a rubbing lets you preserve the inscription for comparison. A recurring tide means a missed window remains recoverable. **NS: Access, research, events and quests; Knowledge and discovery.** New delta: the timing of observation changes evidentiary access, not a generic timed quest reward. G10 inspires this without requiring time travel.

**I17 A warning network has actual reach.** Establish a three-person relay between a village and two camps. Decide who carries warnings and which message takes priority; travel time and each messenger's real knowledge determine who hears before the event. A missed warning can lead to a repair project rather than unexplained global ignorance. **NS: Followers and factions; Random events.** New delta: network topology determines detection time, separate from the existing idea that NPCs know different things.

**I18 Council support is committed together.** Before an irrigation vote, privately promise labor, timber or political backing. Other actors also commit, then all commitments are revealed and the project resolves from their combination. You cannot watch their final choice before making yours. **NS: Followers and factions; Knowledge and discovery.** New delta: simultaneous commitment creates coordination uncertainty without NPC mind-reading. G07's Diplomacy structure supplies the inspiration; use an authored council rather than pretending multiplayer negotiation has been reproduced.

**I19 One last statement from the dead.** In a later world that explicitly permits it, every deceased council member retains one witnessed statement or vote and must choose when to spend it. Ask a ghost about an accusation now, or preserve their limited intervention for succession. **NS: World cards under Every idea somewhere, not everywhere; Ancient monsters and plans from the past.** New delta: finite posthumous political agency, distinct from already-proposed ghost companions. G07 supplies the one-use vote analogy. It does not alter Frontier defeat or death.

**I20 Repealing a law leaves cohorts.** A sect ends a costly initiation requirement. Existing members paid it; new members did not. Decide compensation, honorary recognition or a common replacement trial, then see particular members' reactions. The policy can be good for recruitment without instantly repairing all loyalties. **NS: Followers and factions; Work.** New delta: law change tracks differently affected cohorts, not merely a faction opinion modifier. G13 supplies the link between policy and population experience.

## Bodies, records and local rules

**I21 Raise the same creature in two homes.** A partner and the player share seasonal care of one animal. One home trains crowded-market calm; the other trains wilderness recall. Choose which behavior to consolidate before a deployment, while both histories remain attached to the individual. **NS: Followers and factions; Progression.** New delta: shared care produces a mixed learned repertoire; feeding, breeding and evolution are already existing directions. G12 supplies the raising structure, not a claim about real animal genetics.

**I22 A chorus is your apparatus.** A rite needs three people to sustain different phases: pulse, response and silence. Rehearse substitutions, then deploy the group for one storm warning or forge process. A virtuoso cannot fill all simultaneous roles alone unless a declared power actually permits it. **NS: How to represent powers and hidden things in text; Progression; Followers and factions.** New delta: a power uses phase coordination among people as its apparatus. G09 inspires sequence recognition; no compulsory real-time rhythm test follows.

**I23 Restore an exhibit, challenge its label.** The north star already proposes a museum of your old possessions. Here, restoring one displayed tool reveals it was made by an uncredited apprentice, while the exhibit attributes it to a famous founder. Present evidence, keep the public myth or add a disputed label. Each choice changes the apprentice's family and the institution's standing. **NS: Little stuff; Knowledge and discovery.** New delta: curatorial provenance adjudication, not the already-existing museum premise or memory trigger.

**I24 A process preserves a season.** Make a batch from the spring harvest whose maturation schedule depends on its chosen storage site. Decide to use it now, reserve it for winter guests or donate it to a later teaching exercise. Its history changes the available recipe and encounter, rather than simply granting an age multiplier. **NS: Work; Little stuff; Progression.** New delta: processing preserves a specific seasonal opportunity and gives it a later social use. G02/G04 inspire planning; this is a fantasy recipe, not food-safety guidance.

## Novelty boundary

The live north star already names layered affection, route flags, gifts with taste, calendar competition, cross-world romance, NPC goals, promise ledgers, teaching, museums, ghosts, weather, clocks, traitors, decks and creature care. These ideas are explicitly **extensions** of those families. Their added decisions are: who initiates; which shared future; what an individual object means; where disagreement occurs; whose shift replaces whose; which NPC edge forms; instruction order; learner-defined assessment; blind evaluation; handoff capacity; recipient choice; competence transfer; a menu's guest mix; a festival's sequence; evidence independence; observation timing; message reach; simultaneous support; one-use afterlife action; policy cohorts; split care; coordinated phases; contested exhibit attribution; and delayed seasonal use.

Novelty was checked against the live owner, not just this assignment's earlier summary. [INPUTS.json](INPUTS.json) fingerprints a mid-pass revision. Shared owners changed during research, so [CHECKPOINT](CHECKPOINT.md) records the final drift check. These 24 are not a claim of exhaustive originality across every project document.
