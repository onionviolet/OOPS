## One proposed scene, concretely

**P01 opening:** a cartographer is preparing a river crossing survey and asks you to come because you repaired her measuring pole. The menu offers “Join the survey,” “Offer a workshop afternoon,” and “Decline for now.” The survey costs an afternoon and provisions; the workshop costs materials and shares a different experience; declining costs neither. She pursues her own survey in the declined branch. The successful expedition establishes a useful route; a weather-disrupted expedition can still reveal her ambition and produce a changed later survey opportunity. After a later visit, both characters discuss their preferred home/travel arrangement. No quantity of cups substitutes for that conversation.

This is our proposed fiction, not a transplanted game's scene. It fits the Frontier's ordinary skill/calendar card without adding a realm or reset layer. The first prototype could use an ordinary friend with identical initiative and commitment machinery if the director prefers that tone.

## Capability boundary

Read-only source checks in this pass inspected `src/game_time.js`, `src/npc_lives.js` and `src/life_record.js`. The calendar provides month/season/day/time operations; the village model schedules a bounded family history and news. The life record explicitly says it is lossy and not an authority for rewards, relationships or quests. None of that demonstrates a generic romance, clinic, curriculum, diplomatic-commitment or evidence-chain implementation. These would need authoritative gameplay state, not reverse-parsing biography text.

Source inspection is not a browser pass. Before any selected proposal is called gameplay done, its implementer must rebuild and exercise the changed behavior from a fresh origin. Persistence work also needs save/reload/import and a migration or explicit compatibility decision. No such checks are required or run for this research-only packet. The director's selection is the next design gate; implementation remains a separate authorized task.
