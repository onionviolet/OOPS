# Original YAIR implementation handoff

Four ranked proposals, 2026-10-06. None is accepted, implemented or playtested by this research. [Findings](FINDINGS.json) pin the KeeperRL causes and current YAIR inputs. Names, durations and prices below are reversible examples, not KeeperRL rules or selected YAIR balance.

The runtime integrator must refresh current source and ownership first. This research reserves no runtime files. Use current actor, stock, native item, job, site, source/body, observation and durable publication owners. Vanilla continuity, existing saved jobs and the one optional field renewal remain intact. Explicitly enroll any new content; absent old-save fields grant no room, person, material, practice or retaliation. Browser-closed advancement and absent-world clocks retain their current policies.

## 1. Complete one room, then choose a funded resident invitation

**Player action and outcome.** Commission a small original guest room at an already supported Frontier site. When its last real construction phase finishes, inspect one willing local helper's terms and choose Invite or Decline. Acceptance occupies the room and enables that same person to perform one useful funded material service. Building alone grants no person, free stock or completed service.

**Prerequisites and costs.** Use `compileSiteProject` phases with actual owned logs, a kit reservation, native coin costs and played time. A possible first room uses two logs and four coins, with its counted kit returned rather than duplicated; exact requirements remain proposed. Invitation requires completed construction, one unoccupied residence claim, supported local contact, a live willing helper and current funding. A proposed two-coin starter purse is transferred from the player's existing purse, plus one actual kit if the selected role needs it. Prospective actor definitions begin with no unearned tool or wage. Refusal spends no invitation funds and no slot.

**Capacity and aftermath.** Start with one residence slot. Keep occupancy distinct from simultaneous job capacity. Reserve a unique room-occupancy claim only on accepted terms; preserve stable person identity and its own stock, fatigue, method and later observed account. The helper may decline unsupported work. An occupied room cannot be dismantled; an explicit once-only departure releases occupancy while preserving the person and prior results. Do not add daily hunger, rent or automatic refill. The service must have an actual consumer, such as creating one owned native survey kit from supplied wood, which existing inspection/packing functions can use.

**Current reuse and actual missing consumer.** `site_projects.js` already compiles construction to claims/jobs/facts; `field_workshop.js` already funds independent specialists and spawns `fieldSurveyor` before assembly. `simulation.js` already stages participant/site reservations; `feature_expansion_society.js` supplies funded work, independent choice, consent and reports. The missing join in these inspected owners is completed room -> residence occupancy -> funded Invite/Decline -> useful resident work. No general recruitment engine is required. KeeperRL K4 supplies the completed-attraction and acceptance-recheck pattern; its group-cap exception is not a desired YAIR rule.

**Save and retention.** Declare an optional content/definition revision and explicit compatibility before adding occupancy/arrival/departure records. Use the current actor/claim/owner envelope; preserve identity and spent balances across local travel and the existing renewal policy. No second save namespace, auto-enrollment or person respawn on reload. Frozen accepted jobs retain their original definitions. Native binding for the new person and any physical site must be real before calling the feature playable.

**Later playable acceptance.** Complete construction with actual material loss; unfinished/cancelled construction grants no slot. Compare funded invitation, unaffordable/full/unsupported invitation and decline. Observe the accepted helper spend their own means on a useful job, then inspect its real output once. Confirm the defined occupied-room/departure rule and generated save/reload/import/failure behavior without duplicate identity, stock or occupancy. These gates are unperformed.

## 2. Allocate one scarce toolkit between two useful independent jobs

**Player action and outcome.** After the existing household introduction and supported teaching, inspect Orr's and Vale's current offers. Choose to transfer one actual player-owned kit to one willing worker, or keep it for your own field work. The funded worker selects and performs the eligible commission through their own controller. One branch makes an owned survey kit from actual wood; the other performs a local bench-drying job that removes the existing wet-work restriction. Use one finite shared commission, not a permanent worker-order dashboard.

**Prerequisites and costs.** Require the worker's own learned operation where relevant, current consent/rights, attention, stamina, supplied materials and one available work slot. A reversible sponsorship example transfers one kit, one log and two coins from the player; the drying branch may additionally need an actual sap portion. The sponsored coins become the resident's means; any fittings expense and earned wage must conserve that purse. No automatically replenished attention, tool, wood or payment. An exhausted worker's offer stays unavailable unless separately authored recovery exists.

**Capacity and aftermath.** Two live offers contend for the same finite commission claim and one supported workbench slot. The player changes available means rather than granting competence or directly puppeteering a body. Worker/controller previews still reject unsupported work. Tool ownership really transfers; this proposal is a gift/sponsored transfer, not a custody loan silently consumed by generic item costs. Finished production creates exactly its declared new product from its material input; a reusable kit reservation returns exactly once to its actual owner. Bench drying changes `world.workbench.wet` only for that local bench; it grants no permanent weather immunity. Later accounts distinguish the selected result, the worker's retained tool/wage and the unselected work.

**Current reuse and actual missing consumer.** `simulation_effects.js` supplies conserved transfers, equipment/custody guards and claims. `simulation.js` already chooses eligible controller actions and blocks capacity collisions. Orr/Vale funding, teaching and retained results already exist in `feature_expansion_society.js`; real output/tool functions already exist in `feature_expansion_objects.js`, `field_workshop.js` and `feature_expansion_powers.js`. The new consumer is the player's allocation of the same scarce tool/funding between alternative actor-owned jobs, followed by an actual kit or wet-bench outcome. K1/K2 support this causal pattern; copying KeeperRL's automatic equipment reassignment or refund exceptions would be unnecessary.

**Save and retention.** Freeze offer/selection identity, transferred ownership, accepted job inputs, output owner and reported result in the existing domains. Keep old funded household work and finite demand unchanged. Explicit activation may add a new finite commission; merely opening a report must not mint funds or reset resident attention. Transfer across worlds, equipped goods and encumbered goods follow current restrictions. Existing field-renewal retention applies only after the runtime owner declares how the new job/claim fits it.

**Later playable acceptance.** Choose each branch in separate generated characters and keep the kit in a third. Only the chosen worker receives it; no two jobs claim one slot or budget. See actual production support a native field action, or actual drying allow the previously wet-blocked harness work. Cancel or lose consent before completion and inspect the declared exact refund/ownership result. Reload mid-work and after reporting; no repeated outputs, payment or hidden knowledge. These gates are unperformed.

## 3. Give earned Conditioned grip one small combat consumer

**Player action and outcome.** After the existing two grip drills, acquisition and recovery, choose a four-minute brace practice for two stamina. Completion creates one prepared grip receipt and applies the existing twelve-minute soreness condition. Recover, enter the next eligible local sluice session and choose whether to use the preparation on one ordinary self-guard, reducing its cost from two stamina to one. Ordinary guarding remains available.

**Prerequisites, costs and body limits.** Check the player's actual active Conditioned grip source, living supported body, recovered hands and current practice/actor availability. Owning a trait does not waive soreness or grant Lio's body/gear/control. Practice cancellation forfeits its stated effort and awards no receipt. Prevent preparation stacking; sore or suppressed/unsupported hands cannot spend the benefit. This adds no dismemberment, generic anatomy engine, new method currency or universal mastery cap.

**Aftermath and current missing consumer.** `body_progression.js` already owns earned grip, soreness, recovery and carry/work-time use. `feature_expansion_battle.js::costOf` currently prices an ordinary player guard at two without consulting that trait; commit spends the quoted stamina and creates the actual guard/action receipt. `feature_expansion_powers.js` already has distinct trained functions, body gates and Lio's harness/release training. The new join is this existing trained body change -> one qualified guard-cost consumer. K3 illustrates that bounded training must reach an eligible physical action; Qud/DCSS/DDA's already delivered practice/defense findings remain comparison owners.

**Save and retention.** Declare a small versioned preparation record with ready -> reserved for actual session ID -> spent/expired phases. Reserve only on an explicitly eligible new session; consume on the accepted guard action. End/withdrawal expires unused session preparation without removing the permanent body trait. Preserve a spent receipt across reload/import and failed publication. Old/absent fields and existing earlier session schemas remain inert; do not retroactively give old sessions a discount. Cross-world eligibility and field-renewal preparation lifetime need explicit decisions.

**Later playable acceptance.** Compare untrained, sore, recovered/prepared and source-suppressed players. Preview spends nothing. The accepted guard receives the exact one-use reduction; a second guard pays normal cost. Session withdrawal/end, reload and failed saving cannot restore spent preparation. Confirm the native stamina and displayed quote agree. These gates are unperformed.

## 4. Let one retained grievance fund a finite collection response

**Player action and outcome.** Extend the original Saltworks outlet grievance only after a real committed seizure and local attribution. On a supported later visit, observe a named resident pay for one collection visit to the landing. Choose to pay a stated deferral fee, perform the existing material restitution, decline future trade, or use a separately authored nonlethal contest if that actor has an actual supported encounter profile. Basic departure stays available. Source-pressure alone never creates an unavoidable global raid.

**Prerequisites, costs and means.** The commissioning actor must retain the relevant grievance/knowledge, actual local credit, available agenda/body and a declared willing collector. Reserve the collector's actual effort and local capacity before dispatch. A proposed one-credit commissioning fee must come from a present purse; a proposed two-credit player deferral pays a declared owner and does not convert native coins to local credits. No collector is silently spawned with unlimited funds, gear, combat skill or access. If funds, body, contact, route or supported local clock are missing, no visit launches. Existing absent-world time stays frozen.

**Aftermath.** Payment stops this funded visit and changes only declared short-term access terms; the earlier forced outlet and repair duty remain. Actual restitution uses the current two logs and rope and can end the demand. Refusal leaves a recorded local trade/access consequence; contest/withdrawal retains actual effort and injuries under a nonlethal authored policy. One visit consumes its finite commissioning opportunity; no repeated fees, respawned collector or restored resources. Source K5 specifically shows why cancelled attack and erased grievance are different outcomes.

**Current reuse and actual missing consumer.** `feature_expansion_nested_chains.js` already has the forced-outlet grievance, funded issuer audit, work restriction, actual restitution and changed later accounts. `causal_situations.js`, the shared event/controller/job services and nested local clocks supply causes, response admission and finite timing. The additional consumer is an actual funded named-person movement/collection task with payment/withdrawal/settlement affecting retained access. Its native person/location projection and any contest need explicit integration; a renamed existing audit is insufficient. No universal faction engine or random raid catalogue is justified.

**Save and retention.** Keep commissioning cause, reserved costs, collector identity/location/job, local deadline, observed notice, chosen response and spent fee in existing owners, with a declared optional compatibility revision. Do not change old grievances or old missions on load. Use the local clock/contact owner rather than global/browser-closed elapsed time. Preserve prior export/payment/welfare accounts, whether the collection is paid, withdrawn or superseded by restitution. Current renewal is not faction amnesia.

**Later playable acceptance.** An actual attributed seizure enables the case; preview, abstention and unsupported/private information do not. A funded eligible collector launches once, an unfunded one does not. Observe real movement and the public notice before responding. Compare deferral/restitution/refusal and any supported contest, revisit the changed terms, then generated save/reload/import/failure checks. Verify normal departure and existing mission settlements still work. These gates are unperformed.

## Delivery boundary

Implement these only under a later explicit runtime assignment and sole integrator. Reuse current modules rather than importing source/data/assets or registering another dungeon world. Build/syntax/import/link checks can establish source correspondence; the project's deferred gameplay/save/browser/director acceptance remains a separate later backlog. Source reading supplies design evidence, not completed gameplay.
