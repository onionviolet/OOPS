**Witch Hat-specific candidate loop, D:** select a known design, acquire real tools/material, construct its sigil/sign/ring structure, inspect the known current draft, and complete the activation stroke. Preserve physical artifact, authored structure/revision, maker competence and actor interpretation separately. A qualified copied design may work without revealing its purpose to the character. Inspection does not itself activate or teach every hidden effect.

**Default prototype choice, D:** structured component placement first, with optional freehand later. It serves spatial construction without making mouse precision the character's entire skill. Rejected as the initial approach: treating the existing linear rune strip as a complete drawn-spell evaluator; it has different semantics. A freehand route remains reasonable when its recognition and accessibility cost is justified by play.
