MEMORY means externally recalled, unverified metadata. Native spellings explicitly marked MEMORY are unverified even when the English premise is VERIFIED. UNKNOWN means not established. Every fit judgment and teaching suggestion below is **INFERRED**; priorities are this lane’s proposals. No whole-work reading or authenticity claim follows from a synopsis.
