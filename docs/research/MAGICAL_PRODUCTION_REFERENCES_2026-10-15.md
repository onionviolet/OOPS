## Strongest bounded candidate, not dispatched

Extend the existing apothecary proposal: make a useful preparation once, record a demonstrated method, repeat it through available apparatus, reserve enough for personal use and direct surplus to one real service. An alternative preparation can trade scarce inputs for time or equipment where local rules support that trade. Show the blocked stage and known cause when stock or capacity runs out.

The observable benefit is choosing between personal supply and another useful output, with a working production method that saves attention. Do not require a conveyor grid, another world, universal remort or an immortality subsystem for this first example. A later immortality route needs a specified effect on aging/death and its continuity conditions; producing an elixir item alone cannot establish immortality.

If selected later, recover sole-writer ownership and inspect incumbent runtime consumers first.
