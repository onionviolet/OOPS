C1: Taiwu is the strongest of these three dossiers for executable NPC decision mechanisms. This says more about recovered evidence than comparative game quality or simulation cost.

C2: Do not combine the games into a universal cultivation schema. Taiwu martial breakthrough is knowledge/method development; Tale's realm advancement and Immortal Life's work-prepared trial have different bodies, materials and timelines.

C3: A first YAIRPG slice can combine an original bounded NPC need with a facility/teacher route and a persistent missed-opportunity aftermath. It need not launch full marriage, aging, population or reincarnation at once. World rules decide which are appropriate.
