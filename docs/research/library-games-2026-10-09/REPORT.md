## Review candidates

**LG-P1 Practical study.** Read a native manual, practice its method, use the required tool and see a concrete difference in gathering or crafting. Theory of Magic's study/furnishing chains support the inspiration; Bitburner's prerequisite/effect chain supports legibility. Audit existing home-study and workshop work before selecting new content. Future proof: native reading, useful tool-dependent action, then save/reload/import.

**LG-P2 Clear routine stops.** Extend the existing counted routine row with target, remaining count, progress and exact stop reason. Theory of Magic's runner and Bitburner's active-script UI support this proposed improvement. Keep manual pause, exhausted inputs and unavailable access distinct. Future proof: each stop cause, resume after repair, no unintended repetition or double reward.

**LG-P3 A working home.** One furnishing changes one task while used at the Shack, and leaves a modest biography entry. Theory of Magic supports a causal workspace benefit. This may overlap existing Proto23/home-workshop content; reconcile those owners first. Future proof: before/after rate, leaving the location, saved installation.

**LG-P4 Research with a downstream consumer.** One local method changes an actual recipe, tool or work result. Bitburner's research settlement connects prerequisites, one debit and a concrete effect. Its automatic industry-wide sharing is a source fact; requiring a lesson or delivered copy for YAIR is an original proposed choice.

**LG-P5 Explicit retention.** Before any future single prestige layer is selected, name what is kept, reset, copied or recreated. Theory of Magic's non-resetting Hall and Bitburner's distinct reset paths are alternatives, not a combined universal rule. Travel, unconsciousness, true death and prestige remain separate under current owners. Design decision required before persistence implementation.

These proposals are not authorization to build. Each completed game dossier retains its full five-to-ten ranked proposal set and twenty-plus original touch mappings. Acquisition-only dossiers have empty sets rather than invented source-backed suggestions.

**LG-P6 A contact opens real work.** A person met through ordinary work offers a later job or useful rumor. Kill the Lich's authored social nodes connect conversation with work access and threat information. YAIR can deepen native dialogue and job availability without claiming autonomous NPC simulation. Future proof: return after a relevant job milestone, get a new offer once, defer it without losing the old livelihood, then verify saved aftermath. [KTL packets](../kill-the-lich-idle/IMPLEMENTATION_PACKETS.md).

**LG-P7 Preparation changes a specific encounter.** An ordinary skill, a learned fact and an equipped means each affect one known threat in different ways. Kill the Lich's confrontation consumes a prepared resource state separately from its Amulet reset. For YAIR, use native combat and existing defeat rules; the source does not select forced death, a reset or a graph combat model. Future proof: compare prepared/unprepared outcomes and persist aftermath without replacing the vanilla opening.
