The central reading loop is more than a timer that grants XP. **The Book of Cinders** has a Forge-6 mystery and a particular lesson output. A successful first mastery mutates that book and grants Pyroglyphics; later reading supplies Foresight. The lesson can create a skill when absent or become an expendable ingredient in improvement. The skill has two principle values, conditional memory slots, crafting compatibility and two wisdom choices. Permanent book mastery, learned skill, expendable memory, soul availability and wisdom commitment therefore remain separate states.

Under-strength reading is a connected failure system. Three opportunity draws can expose a compatible memory, comfort or other support slot. Ongoing recipe prediction rechecks alternatives as inputs change. Exhausting the opportunities chooses a failure recipe with fatigue, consolation and specific malady links. A malady is not just fatigue with a longer timer: the inspected Phost malady has weaker useful aspects and a Nectar-5 treatment requirement. Curing returns fatigued Phost; dawn or a separate recovery restores fresh capacity. This creates practical recovery work and preserves the earlier failed attempt.

Crafting connects text hints, trained means, facilities and actual materials. Pyroglyphics with Liquid and Forge 10 produces Iotic Essence. Inks of Power with that essence and Forge 15 produces Orpiment Exultant. The liquid prototype responds to fatigue by becoming dregs, while the soul becomes tired. The intermediate essence already has useful principle values. The great ink is also encaustum, which reaches a high-end journal History consumer. The sampled ending chain separately requires a discovered text, its Ascent Numen, Forge 25, the journal and Numa. These requirements cannot be collapsed into a larger crafting level.

Assistant preparation makes library growth functional. A memory contributes its actual boost values and is spent, with a category marker preventing repeated same-category use that day. Soul collaboration and tool support are separate categories. Room unlock preslots require assistance and one listed sufficient principle. **Reading Room needs Rose 5 or Lantern 5**, not both: SphereSpec checks all essential requirements but accepts any positive required criterion. The newly available desk then constrains its own soul, skill, memory, paper and ink/tool inputs. Room fiction is descriptive, while access and workstation capacity have concrete consumers. The entire scene map was not extracted or reviewed.

The calendar changes usable resources and decisions. Daybreak triggers soul recovery, ordinary memory forgetting and assistance departure separately. Weather is chosen through season-specific routes and its card carries useful principle values. The same garden yields Lenten Rose in Spring and Moly in Numa. A committed skill has a different boundary. This pass establishes these particular authored transitions, not a universal closed-app simulation or punishment policy.

## Larger design and implementation implications

These are inferences for original YAIR design, not claims that every Book of Hours subsystem works this way.

**Knowledge can be an economy of functions.** A physical source, comprehension, a reusable reference, an expendable observation, a learned procedure and an authored report can all serve different decisions. One skill should reach several qualified consumers, such as preparing work, interpreting an object, teaching a bounded method and creating a report. Existing Eda/Neri instruction already distinguishes learned method, body and actual product; this source suggests a useful further carrier and recipient layer rather than restarting that implementation.

**A discovery should have more than one timescale.** Immediate reading can provide a present-use clue, mastery can retain understanding, a crafted intermediate can unlock a new process, and a transmitted account can change a later case. The source's daybreak and Numa triggers show why temporary support, permanent understanding and retained history need separate expiry rules. YAIR should continue using its existing supported played clock and explicit absence policy. The source does not authorize offline loss or hidden aging.

**Facilities and people should contribute actual means.** The source's slot semantics and assistant categories are a useful authoring pattern. YAIR's actor ownership and willingness model needs more than the source's token invitation retrieval: known address cannot imply consent, travel, co-location, capacity or owned supplies. A richer adaptation can preserve those actual prerequisites while avoiding a universal generic helping action. Room access, body capability, tools and interpretation remain separate gates.

**Consequences should compose across a finite case.** The casket chain's strongest lesson is that two completed interventions may conflict. A later resolver can evaluate retained causes and keep an unfinished remedy, without erasing the original event or returning both victories. Finite incident decks also allow exceptional business to become quieter ordinary life after the relevant history is exhausted. This can support deeper continuing worlds without an endlessly growing checklist of mandatory daily chores.

**Small social systems can reach institutional work.** Hosting, truthful reports and directed exchange history can make an actual later decision possible. A charter should state what each person accepted, supplied and can do. Opposed purposes can yield a narrower service, a delay or closure. The source's institution ending is an authored narrative result; an original YAIR circle can instead continue ordinary play through one practical service and changed return, while leaving current life/prestige rules intact.

**Authoring should make distinctions inspectable.** The interpreter separates local requirements, extant requirements, per-object group conditions, slot acceptance, linked alternatives and effect order. Completion then applies mutation, xtriggers, global xpans, draws and quantity changes in a specific sequence. A future idea board can expose these same categories alongside prerequisites, owner, spent input, output fate, downstream consumer and failure/aftermath. A visual room should consume that authoritative state and emit commands, with persistent IDs independent of its graphical nodes.

**Foreign engine architecture needs qualification.** The saved reconstruction envelope covers root population, characters, environment and version, while UI presentation remains separate. The inspected writer uses direct WriteAllText and later backup writing. This is useful evidence about reconstruction, not an atomic persistence template. YAIR keeps its current durable publication/inventory/body/clock owners; copied assemblies or command code are not an integration shortcut. Cancellation, broad offline catchup, rollback and complete serializer behavior remain open source questions.
