# Implementation packets: PROPOSALS

Unaccepted and unimplemented. Rank is suggested order, not authorization. Use original names and IDs; actual truth for a divergent secret stays with its sealed owner. Do one playable slice before the next. Limits, reset periods and carry are per-world adjectives, never a fixed global "once" rule.

All packets need source checks appropriate to their change, a rebuild, and a fresh-origin desktop browser exercise. Persistence needs Save/reload/Export/literal Import, an old-save compatibility decision and no duplicate receipts. Target 1366×768 and 1920×1080; show useful independent notes and actions at comfortable text size. Mobile optimization is deferred. Source proof and human experience acceptance stay separate.

## P1: Connected field notes (F1, F5, F7, F9)

**Smallest result:** one existing location gains an original clue that yields a rumored destination record linked to its actual source. An observation at the destination adds evidence to the same thread. No graph of undiscovered content is drawn.

**Seams:** `src/simulation_effects.js` knowledge records, `src/causal_situations.js` basis, `src/adventure_notebook.js` and the existing workspace module. Any new projection should consume observer-approved records, following `src/world_knowledge.js` and `src/people_knowledge.js`.

**Player path:** hear the clue, open its note, inspect the source, visit the known place by a real route, compare the new evidence. A small node list and linked detail pane is enough; a freeform canvas is optional.

**Acceptance:** untouched origin shows no clue, title or edge. Each discovery appears only after its actual event. The source remains inspectable after the second discovery. Out-of-order exploration produces valid notes. An NPC's uncommunicated discovery does not appear for the player. Save round-trip preserves graph and attribution without revealing extra nodes.

## P2: Unfinished leads without hidden counts (F4, F5, F6, F15)

**Smallest result:** one known note can show "heard about", "examined" or an authored "a lead remains" state. Each hint must have an explicit native disclosure policy; it never derives from the total private fact catalogue.

**Seams:** P1 projection, `src/known_routes.js` for the boundary between rumor and usable route, `src/native_discovery_policy.js` only for a settled cue. Persistent reading state belongs in save-owned records, not the cue's Set.

**Player path:** select a known question, read one remaining public lead, revisit it, and see its status change after actual evidence. Keep closed-window records readable; name changing availability only if the character knows it.

**Acceptance:** adding a sealed undiscovered node changes no public count, layout, badge or hint. Entry and child suppression work independently. Rereading clears attention without creating observations. A disproved hypothesis remains in history with a correction. The section does not disappear when no current action is available.

## P3: A remembered visit window (F2, F7, F10)

**Smallest result:** one original recurring local passage becomes usable during a world-defined phase. Observation teaches the condition; tested knowledge makes a future plan available, with an actual safe waiting or queued visit option where the world permits it.

**Seams:** `src/world_rhythm.js`, native action preview and knowledge receipts, `src/known_routes.js`. Do not replace genuine route eligibility with a notebook flag.

**Player path:** arrive when closed, observe a change, infer a window, test a later visit, then plan another visit while doing ordinary idle work.

**Acceptance:** clock and real access determine eligibility. Learning changes explanation and planning, not the physical law. A world-allowed experiment can succeed before formal certainty. Missed windows recur according to world rules. Reload and offline handling do not invent discoveries or reroll the schedule. Show what the actor learned, not an omniscient countdown.

## P4: An incomplete record becomes useful (F8, F11, F12, F14)

**Smallest result:** an original existing document has a visible gap. A separate evidence route explains one useful mechanism and allows a controlled test. Avoid an automatic truth reveal from merely collecting two note IDs.

**Seams:** native knowledge effects and situation basis, existing content commands, P1's source links and sealed-secret stage projections.

**Player path:** inspect the damaged record, follow one of two fitting routes, compare the later observation, try the supported method and record its actual consequence. No secret's answer is specified in this packet.

**Acceptance:** clues are reachable in ordinary play through both routes. Readings remain guesses until evidence warrants certainty. An incorrect method has a world-appropriate observed result. A correct experiment is permitted under the world's character-knowledge rules, even without all graph nodes filled. Browser, log and errors leak no sealed text before the route. Save round-trip retains evidence and correction history.

## P5: One world permits knowledge-only reset (F3, F9, F13, F15)

**Smallest result:** an isolated original test scenario declares a reset policy and an actual memory carrier. One route is learned in an iteration and usable in a later iteration with restored physical state. Do not reset the main life or promote a new prestige tier.

**Seams:** transition contracts in `src/simulation_contracts.js`; actual transition runtime must be located and read before implementation. Knowledge effects, native state/save owners and `src/heir_inheritance.js` supply boundary examples, not a ready reset implementation.

**Player path:** learn through a real act, trigger the world-supported reset, inspect what carried, and use the remembered method. A character without the carrier can re-find or test the method through a fitting route, without free truth publication.

**Acceptance:** declared physical state restores exactly; knowledge carries only via the specified mechanism. Other actors retain only permitted memory. Jobs, claims and costs settle without duplication. A reload during an iteration resumes the same phase. Export/import preserves iteration, carrier and remembered evidence. Explicit departure and disabled-reset states obey their own rules. No claim of runtime completion until the complete changed behavior is exercised.
