## Strongest two transfers

**Ensemble separates asking from accepting.** An initiator's selected social intent supplies a bid; the recipient's stance determines the accepted/rejected action branch; authored conditions choose a concrete action and its effects. Trigger rules can produce later consequences. Upstream selection itself does not consume stamina, money, a site or played time. YAIRPG must pay for the actual bid/work, demand affirmative authored willingness where needed, recheck current participants/means and persist a specific branch receipt. Ensemble's shared social record is unsuitable as an NPC's omniscient motive input. Author-only truth remains in legal checks; actor planning receives only its own goals, supported knowledge and directed memories. The [full source chain](../traces/ENSEMBLE.md) distinguishes this transfer from an untested general social simulation.

**Unciv separates discovery from execution.** Its selected prerequisite research queue consumes accumulated science over turns; treasury deficit reduces subsequent science, while choosing a queue is not itself a gold purchase. A completed technology affects reveal/eligibility, but an actual unit still needs the required resource, production and a valid placement. Its selected mutually exclusive policy blocks the alternative; conditional policy effects feed later science consumers. These distinct gates are the useful transfer. YAIRPG already has work-funded research and exclusive specialization, so the [original packet](FIRST_PASS_IMPLEMENTATION_PACKETS.md#es-02-known-prerequisites-to-a-useful-field-method) adds a finite prerequisite forecast with real downstream means, rather than cloning a research currency or technology tree.

Both accessible candidates have source pins, line locators, costs/absence-of-cost, actual mutation consumers and failure/aftermath bounds in their trace pairs. Their two [original implementation packets](FIRST_PASS_IMPLEMENTATION_PACKETS.md) are bounded one-realm proposals, with no extra prestige layer. Neither source reading nor a future tooling trial counts as delivered gameplay.

## Authoring many worlds and consequential endings

Keep immutable content definitions separate from one saved domain model. Use stable namespaced command/person/method IDs, explicit world/instance/continuity/scene scope, pinned definition revisions, declared knowledge basis, actual paid effects and named consumers. The existing registry and Ink retained-content identity implement much of this discipline already. New social rules can select a host action; new world prose can report its durable result. Neither needs its own actor graph or second campaign save.

An ending should consume achieved or failed goals, paid losses, specific relationships/promises, and what each character actually heard. Do not derive it from a dialogue cursor or a global favorability sum. Original closure content must preserve ordinary life and recovery alternatives where authored. CK3 suggests constraints tied to office and leverage; Ensemble supplies bid/response structure; Unciv supplies distinct knowledge/use prerequisites. These are complementary finite transfers, not a replacement campaign engine or proof of broad reference-game parity.

Spatial presentation remains conditional: use it when the positions/topology change supported action choices or knowledge. A diagram of the same command list alone is no migration benefit.
