## Oxygen Not Included

**Documented interest:** Klei's [official page](https://www.klei.com/games/oxygen-not-included) and [developer Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/457140/Oxygen_Not_Included/) describe linked heat production/control, gas/liquid plumbing, power sources/overloads, oxygen/carbon-dioxide conditions, waste recycling and different colonist stress reactions. This supports considering **production -> useful output plus environmental burden -> habitability/worker response -> revised production choices**. The page supplies no inspected conservation equations, fluid topology, scheduling or colonist utility algorithm.

**Broader original YAIR direction:** a workshop, greenhouse, clinic or sect should have actual operating conditions alongside useful production. Cooling one apparatus might move heat elsewhere; a waste stream might become another process's input; choosing which room or worker receives limited service could change possible research and care. Keep heat, breathable medium, liquid, electricity, labor and waste as different carriers with their own consumers. A single “base efficiency” score would discard the decisions. These are original directions, not verified ONI rules or selected YAIR architecture.

**Overlap and gap:** [Mindustry](traces/MINDUSTRY.md) already supplies a source-traced power/work reference; Factorio owns selected material/logistics patterns; Qud's deeper liquid/material path remains with root. Current [PROJECT](../../../PROJECT.md) also records native weather/exposure/care delivery as implemented-unverified. ONI's remaining lens is their coupling to a persistent inhabited place and people's practical environment, rather than duplicating the cooling-drive packet or another personal status effect. Existing clock, stock, jobs, site capacity and durable publisher remain the incumbent owners.

**Edition/DLC boundary:** official Klei/Steam metadata currently names five gameplay add-ons: [Spaced Out!](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1452490/) (multiple planetoids/resource transport); [Frosty Planet](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2952300/); [Bionic Booster](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3302470/); [Prehistoric Planet](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3655420/); and [Aquatic Planet](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4310080/). Klei describes their high-level themes; actual base/DLC joins, inputs and exception behavior are unread. Steam separately lists [Neutronium cosmetics](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4157740/) and [soundtrack](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1244160/), which do not count as gameplay coverage. Bundles combine products rather than add mechanics. Current executable build, code/mod access, enabled DLC, ownership, compatibility and cross-asteroid absence policy are unknown here.

Two deeper questions remain unassigned:

1. For one gas/liquid/heat-producing process, what actual inputs, conserved outputs/byproducts, transport and worker/environment predicates determine progress, interruption and useful recovery? Which quantities persist across unloading or absence?
2. Across one declared base/DLC join, do bionic portable power or inter-planetoid transport change who can work and which stock/environment supports them? What rejects unsupported work, and what survives an interrupted transfer?

## Dwarf Fortress

**Documented interest:** [Bay12's live release log](https://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/) identifies Classic 53.16, released 2026-08-05. Its notes make drinking restraint personality-dependent, prevent guild demonstrations trapping people with unmet social needs, and disable the distant-family need until actual contact routes exist. Earlier 2026 notes expose accepted guild/temple building petitions and residency/citizenship status, retain remembered friends/grudges, and add messenger diplomacy with trade affected by profits/negotiations. These are concrete reported changes, not an inspected decision algorithm or proof of every institution's rights.

March 31 notes discuss labs/procedural magic development alongside a later magic/army/diplomacy release. **Those work-in-progress notes do not establish that new magic is playable in 53.16.** Keep already released supernatural content and future lab/procedural-magic work separately qualified.

**Broader original YAIR direction:** schools, guilds, temples, households and settlements can ask for facilities, recognize different kinds of belonging, transmit teaching and negotiate external support. A member's unmet need can interrupt an otherwise legal lesson; a distant relative needs an actual means of contact; an accepted petition needs a built, usable place and a declared operator. Material provision, individual motives, collective decisions and recognized rights should remain distinct. Repeated small history can matter on return without making every person a universal favorability bar or requiring the player to manage a fortress.

**Overlap and gap:** the [society owner](../../gm/world-implementation/second-pass/society/REPORT.md), [family/history extension](../../gm/world-implementation/second-pass/society/FAMILY_GENEALOGY_HISTORY.md), [bases/institutions owner](../../gm/world-implementation/second-pass/bases/REPORT.md) and historical [CK3 comparison](traces/CK3.md) already cover related rights, paid work, kinship and collective pressure. DF's useful additional question is how personal needs, institution activities, petitions and actual contact/diplomatic work affect one another. Those planning reports are historical scoped audits, not current runtime authority; live PROJECT owns delivery state.

**Edition/DLC boundary:** Classic 53.16 is the visible developer release label. Steam's separate [graphical edition](https://store.steampowered.com/app/975370/) lists [Fortress soundtrack](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2232370/) and [Adventure soundtrack](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2937200/) as the two current DLC IDs. This is edition/music metadata only, not a claim of binary equivalence, universal historical DLC absence, installed Adventure mode or source access. Released raws, procedural Lua, DFHack integrations, executable source availability, save compatibility and platform behavior are uninspected. No download link was followed to acquire a game payload.

Three deeper questions remain unassigned:

1. What actual need/personality and occupancy/support conditions choose or interrupt a guild demonstration, social activity or family-contact attempt? How do durable relationships differ from temporary need satisfaction?
2. What changes between a requested facility, accepted petition, built location, assigned operator and resident/citizen/member? Which actions and stock-use rights consume those distinctions?
3. How does an appointed messenger's funded journey, mutually exclusive mission, negotiation result and retained history change later trade access? Which rules are released now versus only announced for future magic/diplomacy work?

## Book of Hours

**Documented interest:** Weather Factory's [official page](https://weatherfactory.biz/book-of-hours/) and [developer Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1028310/) connect acquiring/restoring/cataloguing books, study, crafted inks/memories/tools, library restoration and helping visitors with investigations. **Study -> practical scholarly means -> visitor assistance -> library/world consequences** is the useful dependency hypothesis. The descriptions do not establish exact recipe/aspect checks, a memory-expiry algorithm or ending predicates.

**Broader original YAIR direction:** a learned interpretation could change a recipe, reconstruct a method or answer a particular visitor; a restored room could provide a real kind of research or hospitality. Teaching, borrowing, retaining a physical text, understanding it and producing a useful object should have different carriers. Institutions could grow from repeated investigations and chosen collaborators, with histories transmitted through actual people and documents. Preserve the possibility of substantial noncombat scholarship and visitor campaigns, rather than limiting this reference to a single book-reward button.

**Overlap and gap:** Book of Hours was already a proposed reference in [broader idle-game research](../../RESEARCH_IDLE_GAMES.md#broader-inspiration-for-a-text-first-rpg-2026-10-02) and the [reference catalog](../../NICHE_GAME_REFERENCES.md). Reuse that selection, current knowledge/skill/source/material owners and Ink. [Papers, Please](traces/PAPERS_PLEASE.md) owns evidence under current institutional rules; [Ensemble](traces/ENSEMBLE.md) owns offered bids and recipient stance. BOH adds the larger scholarly-crafting and visitor/institution dependency, not another testimony scene or dialogue engine.

**Edition/DLC and authoring boundary:** [House of Light](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2834350/BOOK_OF_HOURS_HOUSE_OF_LIGHT/), released 2024-09-26 and requiring the base game, is documented as a story expansion with invited visitors, food/drink/ambience/timing, social events, deeper visitor stories and selecting an institute's founders. Their actual prerequisites, guest decisions, resource consumption and world aftermath are unread. The other current Steam DLC ID is the [soundtrack](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2340780/); Anthology combines these products.

The official site links a [locmod creation reference](https://weatherfactory.biz/book-of-hours-locmod-creation-reference/). That checked document supports localisation metadata/text/image replacement and explicitly distinguishes it from full official mechanics-mod support. It is **not** an inspected general recipe/simulation API. Its linked [professional localisation document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1epX9-lye6ST1TJLePwEEEdV19kvYMltfrVf4_4c5lg4/edit?tab=t.0) returned a JavaScript-only shell here; its body remains unread. No log, account, save, private library, content archive or asset was inspected/acquired. Current game build, ownership, source/data access and mod compatibility remain unknown.
