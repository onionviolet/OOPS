## Connected source path

1. **Choose a vessel and an actual amount.** `LiquidVolume` holds total volume and component proportions, rather than treating a colored vial label as its contents. `PerformFill` checks seal/stasis/frozen access and actual capacity, selects carried or equipped collectors, and can ask whether to empty existing contents first. Taking liquid from another owner can prompt and broadcast a help event; that is a source consequence, not proof that consent was obtained. Filling can preserve the old mixture instead. Its selected source windows do not establish a turn cost for every pour or every fill. (`LiquidVolume.cs:6509–6645`, `6162–6218`, `946–1015`.)
2. **Mixing changes the material used by consumers.** `MixWith` first offers target and component callbacks a veto, then splits the specified amount from its source, computes volume-weighted thousandth proportions, clamps capacity, invokes filling/mixing hooks and updates properties. Normalization and rounding matter, including preservation of small incoming components. The base callbacks allow mixing without extra behavior, but specialized callbacks can differ. A return value is not a general atomic transaction guarantee: selected `MixInto` partial/full branches differ, and `PerformFill` does not use `MixWith`'s boolean result. (`LiquidVolume.cs:1371–1588`, `1664–1733`, `5938–5957`; `BaseLiquid.cs:357–364`.)
3. **A configured fuel cell rejects contamination.** Base definitions declare a Combustion Cell with oil fuel, capacity eight drams and 750 charge per dram. Its `EnergyLiquid` requires positive volume and `IsPureLiquid(configuredFuel)`. Oil plus another component therefore has volume but supplies no charge to this oil-configured consumer; a matching component in a mixture is an invalid input, and absent/wrong fuel is a missing input. More liquid alone does not repair that failure. The helper has an exception: a comma-separated authored recipe can match an exact multi-component composition. Consequently “all mixtures are unusable” would be false. (`Items.xml:10552–10569`; `LiquidFueledEnergyCell.cs:18–90`; `LiquidVolume.cs:1239–1369`.)
4. **Slotting retains the physical cell and has its own cost/failure.** `EnergyCellSocket` forwards relevant events to its cell, and `SetCell` assigns the cell's `SlottedIn` back-reference. Inserting into an empty socket spends 1000 actor energy unless overridden, then attempts inventory removal and placement. Inventory removal can fail after energy was spent. Before-remove/replace and compatibility gates also exist. No copied “always atomic reload” guarantee follows from this code. (`EnergyCellSocket.cs:155–180`, `349–372`, `417–500`, `630–691`, `721–740`.)
5. **A real consuming action and a separate useful light share that charge.** Gaslight weapon declarations combine an energy-cell socket, a charged-only radius-four `LightSource`, and a `Gaslight` part with configured draw 100. `Gaslight.HandleEvent(GetWeaponMeleePenetrationEvent)` calls `UseCharge`, then refreshes its powered state. Activation/deactivation changes actual melee skill, damage and penetration fields, not only its name. Combat dispatches that event in the hit branch after the defender has armor-value support. This is not a demonstrated cost on every swing. The dispatcher targets the weapon; an adaptive-penetration branch of `Brain.PreciseWeaponScore` also calls it. The activation scope of that branch and other callers are unread, so this report does not promise one mutation per attack or a harmless AI preview. (`Items.xml:868–902`; `Gaslight.cs:158–162`, `193–250`; `Combat.cs:1079–1104`; `GetWeaponMeleePenetrationEvent.cs:63–65`; `IMeleePenetrationEvent.cs:61–108`; `Brain.cs:3231–3272`.)
6. **Partial expenditure becomes lasting material aftermath.** Charge is `volume × chargePerDram − ChargeCounter`. A draw increases the counter, consumes whole drams only at the threshold, and resets the counter when consumption empties the last dram. `SameAs` also compares the counter. The pass-two charge event spends the available amount even when it cannot cover the entire request; caller failure is not proof of zero material loss. Gaslight ignores the charge call's boolean and then checks its current readiness. A low-charge item may lose its powered weapon profile while its passive light still passes the smaller `TestCharge(1)` threshold. When the final fuel is consumed, `CellDepletedEvent` notifies the cell/socket; it does not itself grant a refill or destroy the vessel. Closed-vessel emptying clears contents, whereas a normal open puddle can be destroyed. (`LiquidFueledEnergyCell.cs:93–116`, `193–213`, `280–329`; `UseChargeEvent.cs:58–105`; `LightSource.cs:53–91`; `CellDepletedEvent.cs:16–49`; `LiquidVolume.cs:4283–4305`, `4719–4792`.)

The oil-cell/weapon/light configuration is grounded in copied base declarations and matching C# consumers. Standard XML 1.0 parsing of the entire copied Items file failed on control characters in the earlier intake. This audit read specific declaration windows and did not execute the game loader, construct an item or verify spawned distribution. It also did not prove all configured values are unchanged by inherited parts, modifiers, transient providers or later events.

One bounded algebra check illustrates retained expenditure: the cell helper with two valid oil drams, 750 charge per dram and counter zero reports 1500. A direct helper draw of 100 leaves two drams, counter 100 and charge 1400. This follows from the selected source arithmetic. It is not eight-attack benchmarking, event-protocol execution or gameplay proof.

## Current YAIRPG fit and one original trial

The live incumbent already has paid cooling/refinement, actual charged-to-empty sleeve work, resin-to-kit use, Eda lesson/practice, single-use reed-oil identification/sharpening/scent and a closed-batch wash-versus-oil assay. The assay expressly rules out a third mixture and its rinse recovers a real fitting pin. Fresh status care adds saved warmth/lining/grip/tired/focus, native wet/rain behavior and ordinary/precise/lined kit work. Current PROJECT labels these deliveries **implemented-unverified**. The old “no feats” or “no physical outcomes” premise is stale. Selected native hashes/ranges are frozen in the JSON; this report does not audit every carrier.

The useful remaining question is **a retained multi-dose physical carrier whose composition and partial expenditure change an actual tool function across uses**. A recipe, cooling buff or new global energy bar would not answer it. Adapt the mechanism through original code and content; do not copy the proprietary classes or move the game to Qud's component/event runtime. The supplied export is evidence, not a redistribution license; no source code, art or proprietary prose is included here.

An unselected bounded trial could add one owned inspection lamp, one closed multi-dose fuel reservoir, one permitted dark cavity and one finite lodged fitting. Metered transfer either preserves pure fuel or contaminates it; a real lamp-guided inspection consumes a disclosed fractional dose and reveals the fitting's reachable position only to the inspecting actor. A separate deliberate extraction consumes actual effort/time and moves the one fitting from the cavity into that actor's inventory. The existing fitting/resin/stave kit route is its useful downstream consumer. Use a new retained cavity stock, not a duplicate claim on Eda's existing two pins. A slower consented dismantling route provides the same physical retrieval without lamp fuel. Names, amounts, duration and stock are original proposals, not Qud balance or an accepted packet.

This needs three genuinely missing joins: native per-carrier quantity/composition and partial-use persistence with exact custody; a scoped lamp-readiness/inspection consumer, because the present text game does not gain spatial lighting from source research; and a once-only extraction that conserves the cavity's stock and teaches only observed position. Reuse the native item/job/knowledge/body/site owners, current stale-command gate, common integer played-minute scheduler and durable publisher. Keep authoritative mixture truth for legality; NPC motives and reports use only their own observations or received testimony. `inspectActorChoices` returns actor knowledge, while `acceptActorProposal` rejects effects/costs/source text and rechecks a registered command. `simulationFacts` contains broader engine truth and is not a universally safe observer context.

Do not equate Qud actor energy or local `TurnTick` with YAIRPG played minutes. Prefer explicit authored command-completion dose events for this trial; rendering, previews, AI scoring and passive light views consume nothing. The shared scheduler settles one disclosed dose when an actual inspection completes. A later ongoing lamp mode would need its own explicit minute-boundary approximation and interruption rules, not silent tick conversion. Use one item representation and publisher, with documented cancellation/refund treatment; no second inventory or fuel clock.

Effort is medium rather than a content-only recipe: a builder must define and validate the carrier's native reconstruction/save representation, prevent stacking away composition or partial debt, implement transfer and reservation semantics, and add the visible consumer. Old absent-carrier saves must remain inert; old full/empty vials and frozen jobs keep their meanings. New IDs/fields need an explicit compatibility admission and later save/reload/failure checks. Exact serializer/key encoding and integration cost remain unaudited, so no migration-ready claim is made.

The later observable gate has five checks, all **unrun**:

1. A player transfers a measured amount, sees the retained reservoir/remaining stock, uses only their own or explicitly permitted carrier, and cannot create fuel by rendering or canceling work.
2. Adding a contaminant retains its material while disabling the lamp inspection; draining/replacing fuel spends or returns disclosed real material and does not grant automatic purification.
3. Two paid inspections use different portions of the same carrier. Remaining fuel and composition survive save/reload without converting the reservoir into a fresh full item or a second inventory.
4. Successful observation reveals only the actual actor-known fitting position. Extraction removes that one cavity stock once; the recovered fitting can feed the existing resin/stave kit consumer, and manual dismantling is an actual alternative.
5. Lost permission/body/site support, stale proposals, wrong-realm work and failed publication follow declared cancellation rules. No partial fuel payment or retained knowledge is silently refunded, duplicated or generalized to other actors.
