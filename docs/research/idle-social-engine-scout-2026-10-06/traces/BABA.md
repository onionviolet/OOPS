## Incumbent, missing consumer and original trial

YAIRPG already has typed declarative effects, multiple power scope/cost models, designed-function capacity, jurisdiction/termination predicates, real item/custody owners, world instances, paid sequenced commands, completion support and one publisher/clock. `realm_boundary_rules.js` explicitly identifies detached prototypes; it is design evidence, not proof that all its boundary rules are native gameplay. The proposal must join actual commands and native consumers.

**One local formation with physical clauses.** Acquire a small set of owned reusable plates and specific site permission. Pay actual setup effort/time to install one of two declared arrangements on one field device. One arrangement routes a finite site reserve into a useful heat/work operation; the other supports a particular safe crossing or protective operation while foregoing that output. Only the actual installed plates, current device/body/source and local scope can enable its declared consumers. Removing one supporting plate disables future operation; it does not reverse already spent energy or completed stock. Numbers, fiction and cast remain proposals.

This is not selected source acquisition, a learned recipe reward or another menu specialization. The missing slice is **conserved physical arrangement -> derived local property -> two genuinely different environmental actions**. Show the known arrangement and resulting supported choices in the current native UI before choosing a spatial renderer. A button that changes a label or an unseen modifier does not meet the gate.

Keep only a fixed typed clause set, finite declared targets and actual cost/support checks. Do not evaluate user-authored JavaScript, rewrite custody/ownership, redefine victory, mutate unrelated worlds or grant arbitrary powers. Configuration needs a current revision and cannot be altered during dependent reserved work without an explicit cancellation/support policy. Persistent plate placement/retained definition identity needs the shared owner's validation and compatibility decision. No copy of Lua, levels, sprites or names is proposed; the recovered archive establishes no redistribution license for copying those assets/source.

Integration effort is qualitatively medium-to-high for arrangement, conservation, observers, useful consumers and recovery. Importing a general grammar plus opaque host semantics is higher and not selected. Existing ES-module commands and Ink can present known clauses and their durable consequences. Exact authoring speed, balance and player appeal are unmeasured.

## Retained broader implications and later gate

Physical knowledge can make new laws available for deliberate local experiments. Property configuration and identity conversion need different conservation rules. Local law, source/fuel/body support, world jurisdiction, observation and interruption can interact without creating another universal stat scale. A reversible puzzle move is not historical campaign reversal. Retail meta/conditional rules could change the scope further, but their algorithms are unread.

Later observable gate, unrun: two conserved configurations alter a useful action and its cost/risk; removing support blocks only future use; invalid/unknown/out-of-scope clauses cannot execute effects; the player sees only learned or delivered evidence; and a generated-fixture save/failure check retains exact paid setup and stock when that phase is authorized. No current project unit/browser/gameplay/save/regression/director gate was run. The trial remains proposed and unimplemented.
