# Ensemble: adapt the social bid, keep the incumbent

**Recommendation: adapt.** Ensemble supplies a useful distinction between an initiator's motive, a recipient's stance and the resulting social action. YAIRPG already has directed relationships, attributed knowledge, cause receipts, independent controllers, Ink, paid commands, a common clock and native durable publication. The remaining useful slice is an authored bid, independent response and observed aftermath, not a replacement social graph. This is a research recommendation and original trial proposal, not accepted gameplay or dependency integration.

## Complete bounded mechanism trace

**Prerequisite.** The example loads schema, cast, trigger/volition rules, actions and history before calculating choices (`loversAndRivals.js:352–378`). Feelings are **directed**, default 0 and bounded 0–100; the two directions are independent. Undirected attributes describe one person. Reciprocal schemas automatically mirror a relation, rather than establishing consent by themselves. The sample cast/history gives hero strength 0/intelligence 5 and explicit role traits. Source: [schema:4–56](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/examples/loversAndRivals/data/schema.json#L4-L56), [history:1–47](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/examples/loversAndRivals/data/history.json#L1-L47), `socialRecord.js:504–602`.

**Initiator motive and recipient stance.** Matched rules add intent weights and retain rule-name/condition explanations; each pair and self-directed goal has its own score. In the initial authored state, the hero's increase-closeness intent toward love derives 20 while the reverse intent derives -10. The reverse stance rejects below 0, but **no matching reverse rule defaults to acceptance**. That is an explicit engine policy, not evidence of action-specific willingness. Source: [volition rules:5–88](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/examples/loversAndRivals/data/volitionRules.json#L5-L88), [acceptance:111–139](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/ensemble/Volition.js#L111-L139), `RuleLibrary.js:625–710`. The numeric result is derived, not observed in a running game.

**Choice and real cost.** `getActions` matches the intent to `RAISECLOSENESS -> WRITELOVENOTE -> accept/reject terminal`. It binds roles, requires conditions and matching acceptance polarity, applies action influence and excludes negative terminals. The player clicks an already selected refusal or acceptance variant; the recipient does not make a fresh interactive decision on that click. **The note consumes no money, material, stamina or paid duration.** Only one turn of player choice passes. This absence applies to this example, not to every possible authored Ensemble domain. Source: [action traversal:322–377](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/ensemble/ActionLibrary.js#L322-L377), `ActionLibrary.js:956–1014`, `loversAndRivals.js:123–200`.

**Mutation.** An accepted note adds 10 to both directed closeness values. A rejected note adds 10 only to the initiator's closeness and writes `romantic-failure(initiator,responder)`. `doAction` loops through these effects and immediately calls `ensemble.set`; it does not recheck current prerequisites, budgets or a stale sequence, and has no durable transaction. Numeric `set` arithmetic clamps at the schema bounds; direction mirroring occurs only for reciprocal categories. Source: [note outcomes:230–271](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/examples/loversAndRivals/data/actions.json#L230-L271), [execution:42–51](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/ensemble/ActionLibrary.js#L42-L51), `socialRecord.js:504–602`.

**Actual aftermath consumer.** Immediately after the note, the sample runs triggers. A rule binds the rejected suitor, recipient and another distinct character, tests that the other has no matching romantic-failure event toward the recipient, then adds 10 to **recipient -> other** closeness. With the three-person initial cast, the other is the rival. There is no witnessing or knowledge prerequisite. Trigger processing deliberately retains offstage actors for perfect information. This supplies a real third-party consumer, but its causal rule is unsuitable for direct copying into YAIRPG. Source: [failure trigger:61–90](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/examples/loversAndRivals/data/triggerRules.json#L61-L90), [trigger engine:481–525](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/ensemble/RuleLibrary.js#L481-L525).

**Next decision and ending.** The UI shows the terminal's refusal/success message, rereads feelings and moves icons from closeness. Ending checks use love->rival 90 or mutual hero/love closeness 90. The next turn copies lasting facts, omits duration 0 event labels, decrements temporary boolean moods and recalculates choices. The rejected note's durable closeness changes can therefore affect future intent and endings after its event label vanishes. The sample displays attraction but its inspected action/volition/ending definitions operate on closeness. Its main loop commits **hero choices only**; the engine's per-pair/self-desire API does not establish an autonomous NPC scheduler or multi-step goal pursuit. Source: [UI/action loop:164–229](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/examples/loversAndRivals/loversAndRivals.js#L164-L229), `loversAndRivals.js:390–411`, [timestep:168–232](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/ensemble/socialRecord.js#L168-L232).

## Integration, save and maintenance cost

A dependency would add a singleton social record, a cached `main` volition set, turn-indexed history and direct mutations alongside current state. The public record-copy/history API is not a save migration or atomic bridge. It would need compatibility mappings for identities, axes, cause receipts, conditions, clocks, seeded decisions and native resources, plus rollback/reconstruction after failed persistence. Avoid this duplicative cost in the initial trial.

A small original adaptation can retain named influence rules and their explanations while ranking only registered command IDs. It still needs action-specific recipient willingness and knowledge-safe explanation disclosure. Friendship, attraction, romantic agreement and rivalry are separate concepts; automatic reciprocal state does not prove mutual agreement. Existing true-state legal checks remain separate from each participant's reasons.

[Binding selection](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/ensemble/ActionLibrary.js#L1104-L1132) uses Underscore random; [rule binding](https://github.com/ensemble-engine/ensemble/blob/8b74bdec4ba2ef4e14795b7591df3b5d73f283e3/ensemble/RuleLibrary.js#L81-L159) enumerates distinct cast assignments; timestep advancement copies state. Determinism, large-cast cost and history growth are unresolved integration risks. No benchmark, browser, runtime, build or security claim is made. ## Original bounded player trial

**After-work route invitation.** In the home world, two explicitly adult original actor templates have met through an observed work exchange. One independently wants to finish a real funded route-report shift. The player can invite a friendly three-minute walk, separately express romantic interest through an explicitly authored willingness route, cooperate on the shift, compete openly for its finite claim, or defer. Names, cast, numbers and dialogue remain proposals.

The invitation uses three played minutes of the initiator's scheduled time, no currency/material and no purchase of acceptance. An accepted meeting reserves both people for the same timed job. Busy or unwilling recipients can refuse or defer without an automatic attraction/reputation penalty. Respecting the answer records one cause-scoped memory. A later local scene consumes that memory and the independently completed work. Unknown observers gain no report. High trust cannot force a romantic answer; actual funds/stock/body/contact and completion support remain command gates.

Later acceptance criteria, all **not run**:

1. An unobserved report known to only one actor changes only that actor's motive. A delivered reply can inform the other; private goal/attraction scores never leak through UI explanations.
2. Friendship, romance and competition use separate directed stance/outcome state. Explicit unwillingness refuses romance even with high trust, and the later scene respects the answer.
3. The NPC performs its funded work on the existing clock while the player does something else, then can accept/defer a meeting; exact costs and one completion are conserved.
4. Changed sequence, missing stock/contact/support or replayed causes reject or resolve through existing command/refund gates, with no duplicate rewards, forced intimacy or second scheduler.
5. When the deferred gameplay/save gate is authorized, generated opt-in and old unenrolled saves on an isolated origin retain jobs/memories exactly once; a failed durable write publishes nothing. No personal save is needed.

Research does not select an engine switch, state schema or gameplay integration. Root's future shared-owner packet can use this trial and its missing consumers. Current source delivery remains implemented-unverified under the project's deferred unit/browser/save/regression/director gates.
