## One bounded original prototype proposal

**A storm-damaged teaching bench:** the player holds an older work authorization, a new posted closure and the keeper's recollection that the bench remains usable. The player's identity can match all three while the currently valid permission differs. Offer explicit comparison of the old authorization against the dated closure, a paid physical inspection, clarification with the actual keeper, refusal or other ordinary work. Merely clicking every card reveals no private truth or reward.

The closure permits one scoped brace-inspection/repair exception. Once supported evidence and the keeper's actual consent establish it, the host grants only that work right, reserves real wood/kit/time and the keeper's finite fee, and begins the supported repair. Teaching stays unavailable until actual repair completion changes the bench. Payment occurs once from the reserved purse for completed work; cancellation or failed support follows the existing declared refund policy. An identified but invalid old permit starts no paid job. Refusal grants no fee and preserves independently chosen alternatives. Specific actors, numbers and balance are unselected original content.

A wrong access request receives a specific explanation from the keeper's actual closure record, rather than exposing private truth or inventing an omniscient fine. The practical aftermath belongs to people: after completed repair, a willing learner can use the genuinely reopened bench under their own teaching agreement; while it stays closed, they can pay for another available teacher or defer. Their purse, time, knowledge and choice remain independent. Personal liking alone neither reopens the bench nor awards the contractor's wage.

This changes access to useful teaching and material work, so it is more than a bureaucracy reskin. Preserve the witnessed authorization and completed repair receipt separately from display memories. The original case does not select mandatory dependents, an offline death clock, global suspicion or arbitrary fines. Any new durable rule/evidence carrier requires the integrator's compatibility decision; old/absent state stays inert.

Later observable gate: hold identity and liking constant, then change only the applicable dated rule or observed brace condition. The same old paper must lead to different real work eligibility. Show one permitted repair with conserved inputs/funded wage, one invalid claim with no paid job, one retained correction and one independent learner choice. Save/return/failure verification and director play review remain deferred. The familiar opening and first one-realm/one-prestige milestone remain.
