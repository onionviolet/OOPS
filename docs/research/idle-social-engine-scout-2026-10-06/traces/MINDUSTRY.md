# Mindustry: retained power infrastructure and work under shortage

**Recommendation: trial an original finite site model.** Mindustry connects startup reserve, changing supply/demand, retained machinery and partial production. That creates a useful choice beyond YAIRPG's current paid jobs: decide whether a small battery can support startup with another load attached, isolate that load, then reconnect when the source is ready. Keep the character-centered campaign and existing state, item, clock and native publication owners. No Java engine dependency or factory-management campaign is selected.

## Strongest connected trace

**Build and configure actual retained infrastructure.** An impact reactor's ordinary base requirements are lead 500, silicon 300, graphite 400, thorium 100, surge alloy 250 and metaglass 250. A silicon smelter costs copper 30/lead 25; a small battery costs copper 5/lead 20 and stores 4000 power units. Costs are rule-scaled, with separate infinite-resource branches. Placement first validates the site; construction checks/removes actual core items and can wait for missing residual inputs. Successful construction installs the block, applies configuration/previous state and invokes `placed()`. Node taps configure real links; connecting merges power graphs, while disconnecting rebuilds their components. These are paid spatial choices, not automatic rewards from research metadata. Sources: [reactor declaration:2650–2663](https://github.com/Anuken/Mindustry/blob/067c720a8817c1c9fb586c03898a7d948caaed56/core/src/mindustry/content/Blocks.java#L2650-L2663), [construction:278–332](https://github.com/Anuken/Mindustry/blob/067c720a8817c1c9fb586c03898a7d948caaed56/core/src/mindustry/world/blocks/ConstructBlock.java#L278-L332), `ConstructBlock.java:72–109,151–155`, `PowerNode.java:60–109,362–371,419–435`. Technology acquisition and campaign unlock gates were not traced.

**Startup has its own demand.** The impact reactor consumes 25 power units per tick before its generation ramps toward 130. It also requires blast compound and cryofluid. Warmup rises only with mandatory efficiency at least 0.9999 and power status at least 0.99; its output efficiency is `warmup^5`. Blast compound is consumed on the 140/timeScale timer inside the valid branch. Cryofluid drains at 0.25 times the building's scaled efficiency delta. Insufficient power/fuel/coolant makes warmup decay, potentially leaving a diminishing generation tail. An attached load competes with startup on the same graph. Source: [startup:83–110](https://github.com/Anuken/Mindustry/blob/067c720a8817c1c9fb586c03898a7d948caaed56/core/src/mindustry/world/blocks/power/ImpactReactor.java#L83-L110), `ConsumeItems.java:43–53`, `ConsumeLiquid.java:36–47`, `PowerGenerator.java:208–210`. These tick constants are evidence, not proposed YAIRPG prices/rates or a measured startup duration.

**Storage bridges deficits and retains useful surplus.** Each graph sums actual producer output and active consumer requests using each building's delta. Enabled batteries hold `capacity * status`. A production deficit draws only available stored energy; excess generation charges available capacity. Ordinary consumers receive shared coverage, so shortage can slow them rather than deleting the machine or granting a binary completion reward. A battery does not create energy. Sources: [balance and buffers:95–256](https://github.com/Anuken/Mindustry/blob/067c720a8817c1c9fb586c03898a7d948caaed56/core/src/mindustry/world/blocks/power/PowerGraph.java#L95-L256), `ConsumePower.java:38–59`.

**Other inputs and output room determine demand.** The building chooses the minimum mandatory-consumer efficiency. Missing a non-power input turns off active power demand; a disabled or output-blocked machine does no effective work. A crafter advances partial progress with efficiency, consumes item stacks only at completed craft, offloads actual products to accepting neighbors or local stock, and tries to dump them later. Full output stops production. Sources: [efficiency/demand:1950–2010](https://github.com/Anuken/Mindustry/blob/067c720a8817c1c9fb586c03898a7d948caaed56/core/src/mindustry/entities/comp/BuildingComp.java#L1950-L2010), [crafter:204–333](https://github.com/Anuken/Mindustry/blob/067c720a8817c1c9fb586c03898a7d948caaed56/core/src/mindustry/world/blocks/production/GenericCrafter.java#L204-L333), `BuildingComp.java:1002–1019,1077–1119`. Item output is not automatically a global construction balance; actual delivery matters.

**The product changes future infrastructure.** A silicon smelter consumes one coal and two sand per silicon, needs 0.5 power/tick and has craftTime 40. Delivered silicon enters actual core inventory. Construction can then remove 30 silicon, 50 lead and 20 titanium to install a large battery with 50000 capacity. This joins output logistics to retained storage, which changes later startup/outage choices. Core storage/full-incineration rules can reject or discard a delivery. Sources: [smelter:1070–1084](https://github.com/Anuken/Mindustry/blob/067c720a8817c1c9fb586c03898a7d948caaed56/core/src/mindustry/content/Blocks.java#L1070-L1084), [battery:2510–2521](https://github.com/Anuken/Mindustry/blob/067c720a8817c1c9fb586c03898a7d948caaed56/core/src/mindustry/content/Blocks.java#L2510-L2521), `CoreBlock.java:744–750,886–911`, `ConstructBlock.java:400–458`. ## Ordinary shortage and genuine danger stay separate

Impact starvation alone does **not** explode. Destruction of a sufficiently warm impact reactor can explode when `reactorExplosions` is enabled; its declared minimum warmup is 0.3. This is different from shortage/restart.

The thorium reactor has a real hazardous shortage route. While enabled and holding fuel, it increases heat according to fuel fullness and consumes fuel faster as heat rises. Its manual coolant loop removes heat; a missing coolant supply does not prevent heating. At heat 0.999 it calls `kill()`. A killed building invokes destruction and removes its tile; the inherited generator can inflict area damage under the explosion rule. Nuclear explosion eligibility additionally requires at least five fuel items or heat 0.5. Disabling/no fuel instead allows ambient cooldown. Sources: [thermal failure:114–150](https://github.com/Anuken/Mindustry/blob/067c720a8817c1c9fb586c03898a7d948caaed56/core/src/mindustry/world/blocks/power/NuclearReactor.java#L114-L150), `NuclearReactor.java:169–172`, `PowerGenerator.java:123–145`, `BuildingComp.java:2249–2258`. No radiation mechanic is inferred. Stored battery energy also contributes to destruction effects under a separate damage-explosion rule (`BuildingComp.java:1490–1518`). This hazard is retained research, not selected for the first original trial.

## Original trial: Eda's shared cooling drive

Propose one optional installation at the existing `feature_objects.fitting_bench`, owned by the existing Eda actor `feature_objects.miri` in `base.frontier`. Its five-minute installation consumes two actual Sound repair staves and five native coins under an explicit owner agreement. Construction alone is existing behavior. The new choice is the retained source/buffer/load interaction afterward.

Illustrative minute rules, all untested tuning proposals: prime with two stamina/two played minutes for a four-unit **site** buffer; fund at most four played minutes with four real Tree sap transferred for Eda's disclosed burner use. Source upkeep is two utility/minute. Its startup has integer stages 0/1/2; full supply raises a stage, shortage lowers one. Generation is zero until the **pre-boundary** state is stage 2, then four utility/minute while its finite fuel/support holds. An attached ready recharger asks for another two utility/minute. Isolate it during startup or spend the small buffer on both loads and risk a stalled start. This original staged rule replaces the source curve rather than approximating it silently.

Static derivation of these **original** rules, not a runtime result: with the recharger isolated, minutes 1 and 2 spend two buffer units each and reach startup stages 1 and 2. Reconnecting for minutes 3 and 4 uses generation 4 against demand 4 and completes the two recharge stages. Connecting immediately spends the whole buffer on minute 1, reaches only startup stage 1 and one recharge stage; minute 2 has no supply, startup falls to 0 and partial recharge remains stalled. Every enabled funded minute spends the disclosed fuel even when the attempt cannot progress. Stop/window exhaustion ends generation/fuel draw; supported played-minute stage decay reaches 0 without rebuilding the rig, while reserve/partial work stay retained.

A recharger reserves a real player-owned empty sleeve and salt packet through current staged ownership, keeps two stages of partial work, and yields exactly one charged sleeve after two fully supplied work boundaries. Missing input, output room, support or power stops that consumer. Unused held inputs return once on cancellation; burned sap, priming effort and elapsed time remain spent. The existing `set_resin` action then discharges that sleeve using real sap, returns the empty sleeve and produces real Set repair resin. `resin_kits` combines that resin with two sound staves into the existing survey kits. The useful endpoint remains a person's gear and journey.

Eda can accept, defer or refuse service using her actual availability and own controller/means. Her learned cooling energy, inventory, body, consent and purse remain separate from machine utility. Other actors receive only locally observed or delivered status snapshots. `simulationFacts` still supplies true stock/support for legal gates; it is not safe motive evidence for unobserved private situations. No new inventory, character, global currency or invisible automatic supply is invented.

Compatibility requires an explicitly opted-in, validated optional record for installation/source stage/reserve/owner/held inputs/partial progress and a defined boundary order. Old absence stays inert; old frozen jobs retain their meanings. Registry/clock/state/native/UI integration belongs to the shared integrator. ## Retained depth, cost and precise limits

The broader map retains five distinct joins: topology/startup competition; proportional ordinary work versus threshold startup; local output delivery into paid infrastructure; ordinary shortage versus source-specific thermal/destruction risk; and serialized state versus derived runtime/timing. Battery replacement can transfer stored energy but clamps to new capacity (`Battery.java:88–95`), so a capacity downgrade is not lossless. Missing inputs and output backpressure remove demand differently from merely having a small power reserve. Rule multipliers/infinite resources and game-mode hazards change outcomes. Erekir heat/beam systems, remote-sector production, transport/research/raids and further world/institution/actor implications remain retrieval questions, not claims of a whole-game audit.

The fixed-site trial is moderate shared-interface work: retained state/validation, deterministic boundary ordering, support/cancellation, observed UI and native publication. A Java graph port is high effort and duplicates existing host contracts. Authoring speed and runtime performance remain unmeasured.
