## ES-03: startup, isolation and retained useful work

**Player result.** An optional retained cooling drive has one source, one small buffer and one actual sleeve recharger. The player can isolate the recharger during startup, connect immediately and exhaust the same reserve, or keep the existing manual method. Interruptions preserve the apparatus and partial work while actual burned fuel stays spent. It must change useful charged-sleeve production and existing resin/kit options, not merely display power bars.

**Source binding.** [Mindustry trace](traces/MINDUSTRY.md) and [JSON](traces/MINDUSTRY.json) bind stablev160.5/`067c720a8817c1c9fb586c03898a7d948caaed56`: impact-reactor paid startup/warmup, graph deficits/surplus/battery coverage, actual production/build consumers, separate thorium cooling failure and retained per-building state. Source tick rates, fractional efficiency and nonlinear warmup are evidence, not YAIRPG tuning. Acquisition of every fuel/technology/campaign gate and full save/crash guarantees remain outside the selected reading. Ordinary impact starvation does not justify copying an explosion.

**Incumbent and new consumer.** [Personal energy](../../../src/energy_resources.js) already has integer actor reserve/output/control/strain; [jobs](../../../src/simulation.js) already freeze costs/effects/due times and apply explicit failure policies. Neither inspected path is a changing shared-site supply/demand/storage balance with retained partial production. Keep current [site construction](../../../src/site_projects.js), actual ownership/custody, stock/native commands, [one clock](../../../src/expansion_engine.js) and [publisher](../../../src/expansion_persistence.js). Add one fixed site consumer rather than duplicate them or port a generic Java/Arc power graph.

**Original optional installation.** Under an explicit agreement, build a retained drive at Eda's actual `feature_objects.fitting_bench`, owned by her actual `feature_objects.miri` identity. Proposed construction consumes2 Sound repair staves and5 native coins over5 played minutes. Separately prime it with2 stamina and2 minutes to store4 site-utility units. Transfer/reserve4 actual Tree sap for one disclosed maximum4-minute source window. This allocation has one physical payer/holder/cause and cannot appropriate Eda's unrelated matching sap, refill on menu/revisit or invent free personal energy. These integers and fiction are reversible design choices.

**Original fixed-minute rule.** At each supported integer played-minute boundary, read generation from the source's pre-boundary startup stage. An enabled fueled source draws2 utility; it generates0 before stage2 and4 thereafter. A connected supported recharger draws2 more. The4-unit buffer covers a deficit or stores surplus up to capacity. Full coverage raises startup stage by1 up to2; shortage lowers it by1 toward0. Only full coverage advances one of the recharger's two stages. An enabled funded boundary spends its disclosed sap even when starvation prevents useful progress. No real-time timer, browser-closed evolution or claim of Mindustry tick-curve parity is made.

| Static proposed comparison, not execution | Startup/buffer consequence | Useful consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Isolate recharger for first two source boundaries | Draw2 twice: buffer4 ->2 ->0; source reaches stage2 | Connect for the next two boundaries: generation4 covers total draw4 and advances both recipe stages |
| Connect recharger immediately with identical supply | First draw4 empties buffer at stage1; next has no generation/buffer and loses startup | Progress pauses; there is no free output. Recovery requires an actual later prime/fuel/support decision |

**Actual material path.** Reserve one actual player-owned Empty cooling sleeve and Cooling salt packet under the accepted service's ordinary ownership/cause guards. Completion consumes the salt and replaces the empty sleeve with one actual Charged cooling sleeve exactly once. The existing [reference recipes](../../../src/reference_gameplay_content.js) can consume the charged sleeve plus actual sap into Set repair resin while returning its empty form, then consume resin plus two actual Sound repair staves into existing Prepared survey kits. Preserve the ordinary manual `reference_gameplay.recharge` route. Nothing in paid construction, a source label or a UI meter grants a charged sleeve.

**Ownership, time and failure.** Eda may accept, defer or refuse through her current availability/means; apparatus state is separate from her own learned energy and body. Actual source/consumer/held-input support and output room are rechecked. Loss of fuel stops generation; missing salt/tool/contact/output room stops only the supported consumer. Infrastructure and partial progress survive a pause. Cancelling returns exact unused held sleeve/salt once, while burned sap, elapsed priming and spent effort remain. Stop/funding withdrawal must settle remaining supplied stock with the rightful holder. A generic whole-job refund must not erase already consumed flow inputs. Unsupported local-clock context freezes the unspent finite window under a declared policy; no private offline loss or inferred debt appears.

**Missing joins and compatibility.** Need one validated versioned site record for owner/installation/source stage/buffer/input allocation/partial accepted consumer, one deterministic boundary join and legal connect/isolate/fund/stop/cancel commands, and an observed native view with real demand/reserve/startup/stop reasons. Derive temporary flow from current records; do not persist a duplicated graph or native context. Old absence is inert, old jobs retain their original meanings, and the shared owner selects schema/validation/old-reader disposition. The saved cursor and real native input/output publish in one existing envelope before live state/DOM. An actor's motive receives only locally observed or delivered reports, not full `simulationFacts` truth.

**Larger reach and exclusions.** Retained utilities can connect several distinct professions, household services, school access and actor goals later. Buffer allocation can favor immediate work or resilient startup without turning every character into a factory manager. Arbitrary topology, fluids/pipes, Erekir heat, proportional tick simulation, team stock, radiation and explosions are unselected. A separately selected hazardous apparatus would need its own actual heat/body/location/damage and shutdown/recovery consumers.

**Later observable gates, unrun:**

2. Fuel, input, contact or output-space loss pauses the right component. Refill/reconnect resumes retained work; cancellation returns unused inputs once, preserves spent fuel and never explodes or duplicates output.
3. One real charged sleeve reaches the existing resin/kit recipes with conserved salt/sap/staves and correct ownership. Eda's funded service can proceed while the player takes another eligible activity.
4. In the later authorized phase, split/common-clock advances and generated reload/export/import/failing-write cases retain exact stage/buffer/input/progress totals, obey returned unspent time and publish nothing on durable failure.
5. Old unenrolled/current opt-in fixtures preserve manual recharge and unrelated energy/jobs; no forced enrollment, personal-energy conversion, changed frozen work or private save use. Director reviews service choice and tuning.

**Static future delivery gate.** Exact registered content/site/native item IDs, one selected site-state owner/boundary, input reservation/cancellation/publication consumers and current syntax/import/definition/build correspondence. Mark code implemented-unverified until its deferred behavioral gates run. A buffer calculator, machine installation or drawn network alone is not the useful result.

## ES-04: an evidence pair changes a scoped work right

**Player result.** The same identified person and the same old authorization can yield different work eligibility under a newly applicable rule. The player identifies a supported discrepancy by comparing two known pieces of evidence, verifies the relevant physical condition, and chooses a real scoped exception, refusal or ordinary alternative. A resulting repair enables a useful service and pays its actual worker once. Identity, document validity, current permission, practical readiness and liking remain distinct.

**Evidence binding.** [Papers, Please trace](traces/PAPERS_PLEASE.md) and its [JSON](traces/PAPERS_PLEASE.json) bind creator2012–2013 design records and the2022 mobile explanation of discrepancy selection. Current exact pay/fine/family/campaign algorithms remain closed and unread. The public localization packer source proves a separate document-authoring process, not those gameplay algorithms. This is an original functional translation of a creator-described decision structure, not a reproduction of legal constants, narrative cases or a general inspector engine.

**Existing implementation to preserve.** [Promise content](../../../src/promise_content.js) already compares a slip with a mistaken identification and supports paid independent verification. [Relay content](../../../src/feature_expansion_subtlety_scene.js) already distinguishes honest/claimed credit, reallocates a finite invitation and has compensation paths. [Town content](../../../src/town_content.js) already gates permits and material work. [Directed knowledge/claims](../../../src/simulation_effects.js), [situated evidence basis](../../../src/causal_situations.js), [Ink](../../../src/ink_narrative.js) and [publication](../../../src/expansion_persistence.js) remain authoritative. The missing consumer is a **player-selected evidence pair evaluated against a particular versioned current rule -> specific real permission/job/payment/person options**. It is not another favorability or correction flag.

**One original optional case.** A posted rule suspends one newly offered storm-check service at a teaching/work strip. The player has an older service authorization; the keeper remembers admitting them before the suspension. Those can all be authentic while failing to authorize the same present task. The new rule permits an explicit bracing-inspection/repair exception. Specific actor/site/fee/material/time values are proposals. Scope this closure to the new optional service so it does not silently revoke unrelated existing bench rights, recipes or native quests.

**Causal flow.** Obtain the old authorization, current dated posting and keeper testimony through real observation/conversation. The player explicitly selects authorization versus posting, or testimony versus the observed brace condition. An authored valid pair can reveal only the supported conflict or agreement and unlock its actual clarification/inspection. Identity match alone grants no right. Paid inspection checks the current body, site, tools and condition; the keeper independently agrees or refuses the offered exception. A valid exception grants one task-scoped work claim and accepts a funded job with real materials/time/participants. Teaching stays unavailable until actual repair finishes. Completion changes only the actual supported service, moves the finite fee to its rightful worker, and records that a particular learner can now attend or pursue another option. A visible “approved” label without that consumer fails the packet.

**Cost and failure.** Pair selection may be a pure information action or paid focused comparison; author and display one policy, with no hidden charge. Material inspection and repair have their own actual paid costs. Wrong/insufficient evidence provides no omniscient verdict or free private truth. Clarification can provide a new report instead of solving every case. Superseded scope, missing actual tools/body/site support, unavailable participant or changed sequence blocks before a new reservation. Accepted work uses existing named completion-support/refund/forfeit policies; cancellation cannot create permission, a completed brace or wages. No automatic global suspicion, fine, mandatory family upkeep or offline loss is selected.

**Missing durable/native joins.** Define immutable evidence IDs and their author/recipient/basis/time, task-scoped rule identity/revision and a bounded comparison table with stable host response IDs. Keep true site condition and institutional authority separate from the player's known evidence. Only an actual observer/reviewer can deliver a corrective finding. Persistent permission/closure/payment must use durable causes/claims/receipts, not32-entry display memory. A case cursor must not copy native inventory or full truth/context. If new records are needed, the shared owner chooses optional schema/validation, retained rule/content revisions and old-reader recovery. Rendering/loading cannot enroll, post a rule, grant knowledge or fund actors.

**Presentation and larger reach.** Two readable evidence cards and a current-rule reference can express the useful comparison in the existing menu/Ink flow. No raster document tooling, freeform desk, immigration fiction or engine port is required. Later regions could give different institutions conflicting task scopes, recurring witnesses, paid verification or changed rules with remembered commitments. Those are broader design implications, not generic law simulation or source-qualified campaign parity.

**Later observable gates, unrun:**

1. Hold identity and liking constant; changing only the applicable rule revision or supported brace condition changes real optional work eligibility. An old authentic document does not silently become current permission.
2. Only a selected valid evidence pair delivers its supported discrepancy/confirmation. Unknown/private evidence stays absent until an actual report/inspection; random clicking grants no stock or right.
3. A consenting exception and funded repair conserve actual material/tool/time/worker fee, then enable one useful service and change the learner's actual choices. One actor may refuse while ordinary alternatives remain.
4. Stale sequence, superseded scope and support loss follow current acceptance/completion policies. Prior finished repair/payment remain retained; no repeated cause or dialogue revisit farms another fee/right.
5. In a later authorized generated-fixture acceptance phase, old/unenrolled and opted-in saves retain exact evidence/rule/job/claim/payment across return, reload and export/import; failed durable publication changes neither cursor nor native state. Private saves remain unused.

**Static future delivery gate.** Exact registered command/evidence/rule IDs, current participant/right/stock consumer, optional validation and retained-content bindings, plus source syntax/import/link/build checks by the owning integrator. Mark implementation implemented-unverified until its deferred player/save gates run. An evidence-card UI or localization-tool setup alone is not this result.
