## F1 Existing evidence and corrections

The 2026-10-10 plan says JoJo has "only a blocked wiki digest". That is
incomplete: the 2026-10-03 conceptual dossier already records four bounded
official-anime references, with explicit limits. Reuse those anchors rather
than starting from zero. The wiki digest remains `blocked_no_revision`, and
source request S24 remains open in the local records. No full manga audit
or article revision is established by those records.

These are official adaptation summaries, not manga scene verification.
The Hand and stopped-time anchors retain the earlier dossier's dated evidence;
they were not freshly checked in this pass.

The old plan's "one per person", shared damage, visibility and range/power
tradeoff must be audited as questions with exceptions, not installed as
universal laws. "Stand as one rule" can be a concise authoring entry point;
it must allow multiple functions, modes, conditional effects and evolution.
Do not turn every power into a mandatory weakness puzzle. An unresolved
boundary stays unknown rather than receiving an invented balancing limit.

## Proposed additions

These are research questions and original acceptance fixtures, not canon claims.

| Code | Audit section | Questions and required output |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Power families and exceptions | Separate innate manifestation, acquisition, learned control, evolution, transfer and loss. Examine Hamon, Spin and bodily/nonhuman systems independently of Stands. Record each supported visibility, damage-sharing, autonomy and post-user-death rule by continuity and example; include disconfirming scenes. |
| P2 | Everyday life and long-term growth | Examine repair, medicine, cooking, transport, investigation, making goods and earning a living alongside fights. Distinguish more output, finer control, newly understood applications and genuinely changed abilities. Ask whether a strong power replaces labor, creates a service, changes supply or attracts attention. Keep ordinary skills useful through concrete applications. |
| P3 | Discovery and asymmetric knowledge | Separate what the power does from what its user and each observer believe. Record visible clues, mistaken explanations, safe experiments, actual identification means and consequences of acting before certainty. Opponents receive the same evidence rules. UI previews must not reveal unknown activation conditions or private actor history. |
| P4 | Interaction and time semantics | For each pair, identify actual targets, contact, order, ongoing effects and source-supported counters. Distinguish stopped, skipped/erased, accelerated, rewound and predicted time before mapping them to game clocks. Define travel, work, automatic powers, projectiles, memory and simultaneous effects only where evidence supports them; do not invent a single generic time spell. |
| P5 | Lived campaigns, starts and continuity | Audit an ordinary day's places, work, money, family, factions, travel and institutions. Qualify starting points by part/continuity, identity, actual means and knowledge; include ordinary/non-user starts as candidates. Track how changed events affect local schedules, canon momentum and foreknowledge. Treat cross-world carriers and conversions as explicit proposals, separate from source canon. |

## Evidence and delivery contract

Each audited rule needs: part and continuity, medium/edition, chapter or
episode locator, shown versus stated versus inferred, target/trigger,
observability, persistence, change history, exceptions and unresolved cases.
Wiki revisions are secondary evidence and fan premises occupy a separate
proposal column. Official summaries can establish bounded baselines; they
cannot settle every extreme. A blocked route does not prevent work from
accessible primary sources. After one failed fetch, change routes.

Deliver a coverage ledger so a representative selection is never described
as an exhaustive Stand catalog. Preserve disagreements and absent evidence.
Detailed direct-world research belongs in the private research area; public
authoring guidance can describe original mechanics without raw source prose.

## Proposed first proof after source selection

One local encounter with an everyday consequence, a discoverable effect,
two genuinely different ways to respond and an opponent using only held
means. The player may investigate, exploit a supported interaction, withdraw
or overpower the opponent where the actual rules allow it. Do not guarantee
that every power has a mundane counter. The fixture is original and does
not choose a JoJo campaign or alter canon.

| Case | Observable gate |
|---|---|
| A1 Hidden rule | A fresh start exposes clues but no private rule truth; an acquired relevant means changes what can be learned |
| A2 Interaction | Two supported responses cause different durable outcomes; identical conditions resolve consistently for both sides |
| A3 Everyday consequence | The same effect changes an actual object, work task or service, with custody and history retained |
| A4 Idle behavior | A prepared policy responds only to evidence it can obtain; offline progress never grants omniscience |
| A5 Persistence | Rebuilt fresh-origin play, save/reload/import preserve knowledge, ongoing effects, target identity and settled rewards without duplication |

These cases are specified, not run. The follow-up audit includes existing
engine support, native UI consumers, actual target/ownership/contact semantics,
NPC symmetry, scheduler/offline interactions and save compatibility, together
with concrete implementation order and browser acceptance. Full source coverage
and playable acceptance remain separate gates in the report.
