## F6. What 5e and non-5e mean

In an MCG product selector, **5e** means written for Dungeons & Dragons Fifth Edition. It does not mean fifth-tier quality or the fifth volume of that book. **Non-5e** simply means some other rules system; Cypher, GURPS, Ars Magica and Warhammer do not share one alternative engine. An unrelated game's own fifth edition, such as Ars Magica 5th Edition, is not D&D-compatible merely because it says 5th Edition.

[D&D's official SRD page](https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd) distinguishes SRD 5.1 from the revised 5.2/5.2.1 material. Version-lock a source rather than silently combining 2014 and revised 2024-era rules. The open SRD is a rules subset, not every published setting, class, creature or expansion.

The [official Planar Character Options comparison](https://www.montecookgames.com/store/product/planar-character-options/) provides a concrete example:

| D&D 5e edition | Cypher edition |
|---|---|
| Species, subclasses, backgrounds, feats, spells and magic items | Species/planar descriptors, foci, abilities, cyphers and artifacts |
| Fits D&D's character and spell structures | Fits Cypher's character-option structures |
| Useful for defined tactical packages and spell examples | Useful for compact concepts and modular character descriptions |

Shared setting concepts do not imply identical mechanical content. Choose one version first for lore/world structure. Compare both contents pages when the question is character abilities; inspect previews before buying duplicate coverage. Neither version provides native YAIRPG code or ready-made idle progression. Conversion must preserve the actual function and conditions before selecting local numbers.

## F7. Is Monte Cook Games the most robust?

Not as a universal conclusion. Robustness has several different meanings: coverage of genres, precision of powers, depth of ordinary life, procedural world tools, setting detail, ease of reading, and the amount of work left to a human GM.

| ID | Research need | Strong candidate | Reasoned disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Varied dimensions, strange encounters, compact concepts | MCG: Strange, Numenera, Planebreaker, The Weird | Keep as a major inspiration library. The earlier B1-B7 queue remains useful. |
| C2 | Constructing precise supernatural abilities | GURPS Basic Set + Powers + Thaumatology | Prioritize alongside MCG. More explicit construction vocabulary, with more system-specific detail to translate. |
| C3 | A life of magical research, teaching and laboratory work | Ars Magica core + Covenants and selected supplements | Highest-value new family for the research/home/progression direction. |
| C4 | Creating populated sandbox worlds and faction situations | Worlds/Stars Without Number and related tools | High-value world-authoring family. Distinguish generated prompts from implemented simulation. |
| C5 | Careers, institutions, material culture and dense authored setting | Warhammer RPG lines; RuneQuest; HarnWorld | Strong complementary sources. Their tones and default constraints are not automatically YAIRPG rules. |
| C6 | Consequences between adventures, organizations and long timelines | Blades, Pendragon, Kingdom, Ironsworn | Targeted procedure references rather than universal replacement systems. |

Publisher evidence for C2: [GURPS Powers](https://warehouse23.com/products/gurps-powers) describes using Basic Set machinery for superheroes, wizards, wuxia, psionics and gods. [Thaumatology](https://warehouse23.com/collections/digital-downloads/products/gurps-thaumatology-1) explicitly covers different magic systems, freeform magic, material magic and combining systems. That is a more targeted match for different worlds having different power rules than another generic monster collection.

## O3. Warhammer: several useful libraries

Warhammer Fantasy/The Old World, Age of Sigmar and Warhammer 40,000 are distinct settings/eras and RPG lines. Do not merge them into one cosmology or treat the RPGs, miniatures rules and novels as interchangeable evidence.

| W7 | [Imperium Maledictum](https://cubicle7games.com/en_US/our-games/warhammer-40k-roleplay-imperium-maledictum) | Patrons, factions, influence, access, investigation and institutional service. | Strong people/institution reference, not a free-travel power collector. |

| W9 | [Warhammer: The Old World Roleplaying Game](https://cubicle7games.com/en_GB/our-games/warhammer-the-old-world-roleplaying-game) | A separately named RPG using C7D10, not the same rulebook as the WFRP d100 library. | Compare later rather than assume duplicate rules. |

Edition caveat: the live Winds of Magic page displayed a WFRP Fifth Edition preorder banner. The linked fifth-edition page returned 429. This pass recommends the inspected WFRP supplement library for its contents, not because fourth edition is established as the newest edition. Any acquisition should record exact title, edition and errata. WFRP fifth edition is also unrelated to D&D 5e.

The [official Warhammer fiction overview](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/topics/black-library/) identifies Black Library as the story source. Novels can supply viewpoint, daily life and demonstrated exceptional events; RPG books supply explicit play procedures. For YAIRPG's present design questions, start with relevant RPG books before indiscriminate codex/novel collecting. If a specific Warhammer world is selected for direct adaptation, canonical sourcing is a separate deeper task.

## D2. How to absorb these sources usefully (proposal)

Choose books by the missing activity they explain. A source that makes one ordinary profession playable can be more valuable than another thousand powers. Keep a small active reading queue while retaining the larger inventory.

| ID | Consideration | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | Character-scale and institution-scale procedures differ | Trace how a learned skill changes a workshop, household, guild or expedition. A faction stat alone does not simulate its people. |
| K2 | Different resolution units | Combat rounds, scenes, downtime actions and research seasons cannot be pasted directly into idle ticks. Select an actual action, duration and retained result. |
| K3 | Imported means retain distinct causes | Learned technique, body modification, artifact, patron authority and local environmental grant need different acquisition/retention handling. No universal suppression or cost follows from studying another system. |
| K4 | Numerical scales are system-local | D&D level, Cypher tier, GURPS point cost, Warhammer career and divine Word are not interchangeable world power rankings. Preserve function, scope and conditions first. |
| K5 | Human-GM interpretation is unresolved implementation work | A freeform spell or oracle answer needs a supported consumer and persistence consequences before it becomes computer-resolved gameplay. |
| K6 | Sources have different authorial jobs | Core books teach systems; supplements deepen activities; adventures demonstrate sequencing; gazetteers show place; fiction shows lived experience. Collect complementary kinds. |
| K7 | Reference versus redistribution | Open text, purchased PDF, private extraction and public shipped content are different uses. Record source/license/version when actually adapting material; this sweep makes no blanket licensing conclusion. |
