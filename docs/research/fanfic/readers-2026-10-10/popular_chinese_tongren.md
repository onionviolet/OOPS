## (c) Cheat/system families and their economies

F-A. Main God Space points economy (无限流 core): works 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 17, 34. Points earned in tasks, spent on item/ability exchange. Work 17 SHOWN: 1 luck = 1 energy = 1 point; world travel 500 points per trip; max 10 target worlds, initial rank cap A; S/SS/SSS need paid upgrades; catalog split into Techniques, Items, Bloodlines, Skills, Other, each by Xianwu, Magic, Technology, Mystery. Work 9 SHOWN: 100 m3 safe storage, one free item per world, free item capped by current level. Work 6 SHOWN: points spendable only inside the space; items and abilities cannot leave, skills can.

F-B. Simulator (模拟器): works 6, 19, 20, 21, 22. See (e).

F-C. 签到 sign-in: works 12, 28. Work 12 SHOWN: one card per day; card copies an ability from a touched person; max 3 uses on the same person; star grades; can fail. Work 28 SHOWN: sign in once per heroine; reward is a skill (a "financial investment mastery" example). INFERRED: sign-in economies are rate-limited by time or by a finite set of NPCs, not by price.

F-D. Shop with harvested currency: works 15 (negative emotion from living beings, spent in shop; starter dragon fruit and 10 m2 backpack), 23 (EXP from living and fighting; magic points from killing/absorbing creatures; store sells spells, creatures, items; upgrades cost a lot), 16 (chakra to points; basic system costs 100; targeted delivery priced by target strength, top figures 100,000), 2 (fishing pulls, no price described on page).

F-E. Quest system with penalty: work 14 SHOWN: failing a main quest costs 10 years of lifespan; first quest is a 10-day fitness test for 10 EXP and a martial set; each rank promotion gives one random draw; hidden Easter-egg quests; starts at 100 Berries, empty skill panel. Work 8 SHOWN: points tied to "merit" by rumor; first-level trial can be farmed once a week for small reward.

F-F. Rebate / cashback multiplier: works 24 (rebate multiplier starts at 0.1, points from training, caring, teaching the target, spent to raise multiplier; ceiling advertised as very high), 26 (1:1.2 rebate on in-game spend only; first top-up doubled). INFERRED: the multiplier is the whole game; the "work" is feeding the target.

F-G. Teaching / delivery (give powers to others): works 10 (teach moves when affection is full, in a teaching space), 16 (deliver systems; host copies the recipient's highest ability and takes some chakra).

F-H. Gacha / cards / skill cards: works 1 (manual drawn from system gacha), 12 (cards), 27 (skill cards turn learned skills into system skills that grow with use).

F-I. Observer/behind-the-scenes: works 29 (shares protagonists' senses; can cut the link), 30 (chat group built from a world core). INFERRED: these are story engines, not economies.

F-J. 词条 trait entries with color grades: UNKNOWN. I found no book page showing one. Only the word "grade" appears as star ranks on cards (work 12), S-ranks on worlds (work 17), and "destiny trait" rewards (work 20). Do not assume a Qidian standard.

## (h) One game option per family for an idle RPG

- A (Main God Space points): a hub tab where one currency converts world "luck" into points; world tier caps (A to SSS) unlocked by paying upgrades; a fixed per-trip toll. Cheap, and gives a hard sink for the idle loop.
- B (Simulator): a "preview" action that runs a world's life headlessly for free but shows only a result band, with a refill clock (per 7 days or per kill). Good for the heirs/time-loop theme.
- C (签到): a daily check-in per NPC, each NPC giving at most one gift, so the cap is the cast size, not a timer.
- D (shop + harvested currency): world-specific currency (e.g. a world's "negative emotion" analogue) that is spendable only in that world's shop. Fits "each world makes its own rules".
- E (quest penalty): main quest with a lifespan stake that fits an heir system (failure spends years of the current life).
- F (rebate multiplier): a nurture bond where actions feed points that raise a multiplier on a passive income, an idle-native loop.
- G (teach/deliver): the player gives a system to an NPC and receives a copy of their best skill, a trade loop with scaling price by target strength.
- H (cards): sign-in or gacha yields cards, each usable at most N times per target, with star grades.
- I (observer): optional "watch only" world that grants shared senses from a protagonist at the price of no intervention.
- J (词条 traits): UNKNOWN evidence; design from scratch if wanted.
