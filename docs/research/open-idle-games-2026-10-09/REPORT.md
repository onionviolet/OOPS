# Open-source idle games research, 2026-10-09

Start with **one ordinary career that changes the character's life**: fieldwork leads to better work, a colleague knows you, and a promotion leaves a biography entry. This is the strongest first proposal, followed by clearer routines and staged research. No gameplay implementation is authorized in this assignment.

**F1 Careers and lifetimes.** Original Progress Knight combines a chosen job and skill with task-specific learning bonuses across two rebirth layers. Quest changes the game to simultaneous task training and five layers. Neither provides rich age-stage lives or personal followers. Borrow promotion clarity and explicit carryover, while keeping YAIR's long-life proposal and native opening. [Source dossier](PROGRESS_KNIGHT.md).

**F2 Routines.** Idle Loops separates the current action sequence from the next plan. A loop restores supplies and raw stats while keeping talents, skills and town knowledge. Reload also discards the current run. Its useful lesson is a repeatable plan with a visible failure reason; mandatory short loops would be a separate design choice. [Source dossier](IDLE_LOOPS.md).

**F3 Research and reset boundaries.** Evolve reveals a large technology catalogue through prerequisites, changes costs through species traits, and lets different endings retain different context and reward currencies. Its population/job counters do not establish individual follower lives. Its thirteen surveyed ending routes are not thirteen nested prestige layers. [Source dossier](EVOLVE.md).

**F4 Offline means different things.** Original Progress Knight has no closed-tab catch-up. Quest replays up to one hour of ticks, advances age and stops at death. Idle Loops and Evolve bank acceleration for later simulation. None settles YAIR's offline-aging policy. Operation cards must declare production, age, history and outside-clock behavior separately. Exact formulas and fork differences are in the dossiers.

**F5 Compare against live YAIR.** The fork already has single-command counted routines, an optional lifespan clock, a bounded household, a compacted life record and an origin-wide 4 MiB save guard. The north star's older missing-save-guard claim is stale. Recommendations deepen those owners. [Verified source baseline](YAIR_BASELINE.md).

Eight ranked proposals and their future playable proofs are in [PROPOSALS.md](PROPOSALS.md). All remain proposals; the accepted one-realm/one-prestige milestone stays in place. [SMALL_TOUCHES.md](SMALL_TOUCHES.md) selects thirty practical adaptations; the three dossiers contain fifty-eight source-linked mappings in total, with overlap across games.

Seven repositories were shallow-cloned outside YAIR, using about 204 MiB. [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md) records each license or its absence; [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json) pins full hashes, dates and paths. The original-author repositories came first, and continuation evidence is distinguished from active-maintenance claims.
