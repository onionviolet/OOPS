# Ranked north-star proposals

2026-10-09. All P codes are proposals, not decisions or an implementation queue. Read [REPORT](REPORT.md) first. Ranking favors one lived life in the native center menu, existing owners, and the accepted one-realm/one-prestige milestone. Source anchors live in the linked game dossiers. Source inspection is not runtime proof.

| Rank/code | Proposal and player decision | Source lesson | Existing YAIR owner, missing part and cost | Smallest future playable proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 / P1 | Give fieldwork a visible career ladder: apprentice, dependable worker, mentor. Choose earnings now or practice that qualifies for different work. Add a colleague and career milestone so ranks mean something in a life. | [Progress Knight](PROGRESS_KNIGHT.md): work and training advance together; promotion prerequisites keep skills relevant. | `src/activities.js`, `src/data/locations.js`, skill owners and `src/life_record.js` exist. Add one local promotion definition and native dialogue consequence. Low to medium cost; avoid separate career XP or mandatory upkeep. | From the vanilla opening, work a normal shift, see the next promotion's actual unmet requirement, meet it and get changed pay plus a colleague's response and one saved milestone. |
| 2 / P2 | Let a solved everyday task become a saved routine with an explicit stop reason. First support one task repeated to a target; consider named multi-action schedules only after that proof. | [Idle Loops](IDLE_LOOPS.md): editable plans distinguish learning a route from replaying it; loop failures identify bad planning. | `src/simulation.js:311-329` already has counted single-command programs; its completion path pauses for missing inputs. Native activity bridging and clear center-menu controls need work. Medium cost; no parallel executor. D2b remains draft. | Repeat one craft until three items or an input shortage; interrupt, inspect the exact reason, reload and resume without duplicate payment or outputs. |
| 3 / P3 | Make one research project expose stages and useful partial understanding, then require practice to use the result. Study a better agricultural tool or ordinary alloy before any multiverse-sized tree. | [Evolve](EVOLVE.md): technology dependencies open new functions; [Idle Loops](IDLE_LOOPS.md): enduring learning and immediate execution are distinct. | Existing simulation commands, skill/book owners and native recipes are the incumbent. Add authored subject/stage/output definitions using that clock. Medium cost; dependency graph needs cycle/reference validation. | A text or mentor opens a project; one completed stage changes a preview; final understanding unlocks a recipe, but tool use still exercises the relevant native skill. |
| 4 / P4 | Present an operation card before any selected rebirth, succession or rewind. List the affected body/history, anchor, retained fields, reset fields, outside clocks and repeatable rewards. | All three dossiers show that similar-looking resets have different persistence boundaries. | `src/chronicle.js:273-280` constructs an heir chronicle and explicitly leaves body reset to the host. Existing carry policies are useful owners, not evidence of a universal reset. Medium design/integration cost. Keep native defeat as unconsciousness. | In a disposable opted-in mortal life, show a concrete before/after preview, perform exactly one selected operation, then save/reload/import and prove the advertised retained and reset fields. No hidden reward limits. |
| 5 / P5 | Make old age open teaching or stewardship, alongside authored bodily changes and life extension. Preserve long sessions and voluntary later-life projects. | [Progress Knight](PROGRESS_KNIGHT.md) makes age a strategic deadline, but its rapid repeating lives are a poor timing model for the north star. | Calendar, chronicle lifespan and last-year reflection already exist; specific life-stage work modifiers and mentor consequences need authoring. Medium cost. Do not adopt wholesale XP slowdown or shorten lives for prestige speed. | The same character gains a visible teaching option at one authored stage; a student improves, the bio records it, and the player's ordinary work remains possible. |
| 6 / P6 | Choose and declare offline-time semantics before adding catch-up: banked acceleration is a candidate, not an already-selected rule. Separate wall-time credit, routine production, biological aging and event clocks. | [Evolve](EVOLVE.md) and [Idle Loops](IDLE_LOOPS.md) bank acceleration; [Progress Knight Quest](PROGRESS_KNIGHT.md) replays capped ticks including mortality. | Shared clock/chronicle alignment is already enforced. Could instead pause simulation until credit is deliberately spent. | Use generated timestamps: close/reopen, inspect the credit and cap, spend a bounded amount, and verify outputs, age, deadlines and stopping at a choice exactly match the card. |
| 7 / P7 | Improve compact saves through definition/state separation and measured compression only if bounded representative lives justify it. Keep the quota guard and compatible export decoder. | [Evolve](EVOLVE.md) compresses state; [Idle Loops](IDLE_LOOPS.md) reconstructs action definitions from durable identifiers. | `src/save_files.js:166-190` already measures UTF-16 origin occupancy and preserves the previous save on quota failure; `src/life_record.js:41-47` compacts 64 recent/200 annual entries. Remaining gain is payload design/compression, not adding those safeguards again. Medium to high migration cost. | Synthetic short/century/multiworld records measured before and after; old import accepted, Unicode intact, corrupt payload rejected, quota failure preserves last good save. No private save samples. |
| 8 / P8 | Support one follower's declared work assignment, capacity and upkeep before population simulation. A person can train, refuse or leave for authored reasons. | [Evolve](EVOLVE.md): scarce workers and production bottlenecks create allocation decisions. Its population counters do not demonstrate personal follower lives. | `src/npc_lives.js` is a bounded authored household; inventory/NPC/simulation owners exist. Assignment and personal motivation semantics need integration. Medium to high cost; do not add one ticking loop per follower. | Hire or recruit one known NPC, choose field help or study assistance, inspect actual costs, pause for missing supplies, and observe one personal consequence instead of only a yield multiplier. |

P1 is the best first trial: one ordinary career with a meaningful changed relationship. P2 and P3 can deepen the same place later. P4-P8 require decisions or integration work and are not authorized by this research assignment. Multiple prestige layers are reference material for the broader vision; the current milestone still has one.
