## D2: Operations compose routes while keeping risk and mission outcome distinct

Why this system: prior politics/AI digest covered planning and operations at preference level. This pass reads `common/operations/_documentation.md`, `common/operation_phases/_documentation.md`, rescue definitions and infiltration routes. SHOWN: 39 operation declarations, 82 phase declarations and 117 phase-group blocks within operations. Those 117 groups are authored alternatives, not 117 independent missions.

SHOWN, `common/operations/00_operations.txt:6`: rescue work takes 35 declared days, one operative and network strength 30. It requires an actually captured operative in the target country, and completion frees operatives and retains a country flag. Three phase groups choose infiltration, rescue approach and exfiltration. Diplomatic infiltration has a peace-conditioned boost, while air approaches are suppressed without a transport aircraft. STATED operations docs divide declared duration evenly over selected phase groups; native exact scheduling is unverified.

SHOWN, `common/operation_phases/lar_infiltration.txt`: paradrop needs one plane and declares return_on_complete. Submarine infiltration checks coast access; border infiltration checks a neighboring route not at war with either actor; diplomatic infiltration reserves one civilian factory for 15 declared days. The phase has its own name/description/outcome art keys and costs. These are concrete means and geography, rather than only a difficulty roll.

STATED: phase validity is checked weekly for planned operations and invalid plans can be reseeded. Risk handling concerns assigned operatives, while successful/extra mission outcomes have their own handlers; the docs explicitly call outcome_execute once per operation. `outcome_potential` exposes prospective output separately in actual content. Exact native risk scale, reservation timing, partial refund, reseeding RNG retention and arrival after a target change remain unverified. The docs' example chance magnitudes differ from actual sample fractions, so do not copy one probability normalization across fields.
