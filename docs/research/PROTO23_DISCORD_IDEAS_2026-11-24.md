## Coverage and limits

The coordinator supplied two topic-sweep JSON files and 25 `proto23_search_*.json` files in the gitignored `docs/research/proto23/private/` folder. I read all supplied plain-text message lists, extracted **504 distinct message IDs** across those successful files, and deduplicated repeated hits by ID. `proto23_topic_search_2026-11-24.json` contains a failed argument-validation response, not messages. Timestamps are shown by the bridge in America/Chicago. A search hit may be a reply with missing context; its author's intent or the referenced game's behavior is not independently verified. Attachments, linked media and thread history were not opened in this lane.

The topic sweep showed 200 of 248 unique hits before its request budget; its searches covered the first 50 each for `idea`, `suggestion`, `feature`, `would be cool`, and `wish`, plus all 37 `should add` hits. The second topic sweep showed 48 unique messages from the first 25 each for `mechanic` and `system`, then stopped at a Discord 429 on `balance`; `feedback`, `improve` and `UI` did not run. The separate pages reached approximately the first **175 of 1,555** `idea` hits and **170 of 419** `suggestion` hits. They reached the full reported **37 `should add`**, **76 `would be cool`**, and **111 `would be nice`** matches, subject to the bridge's displayed scope and page warnings. These are query counts, not a count of all channel messages, and overlapping terms are not additive.

This is a sample of a channel active since **2021**, not a complete review of its history, threads, attachments or all potential ideas.
