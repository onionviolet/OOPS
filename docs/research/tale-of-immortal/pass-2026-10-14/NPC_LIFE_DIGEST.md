**Inference:** the source represents multiple stages and consequences of social life, not simply a friendship bar. **Not established:** autonomous initiation probabilities, complete NPC practice/realm progression, marriage transaction, personal disciple teaching, childbirth, final lifespan death and whole-world scheduler cost. Prior declarations in N4/N6 are reported history, not freshly revalidated native evidence.

**Proposal consequence:** reuse YAIR's current actor, memory, household and event owners rather than claiming they do not exist. A meaningful extension needs an actual decision, consumed resources, persistent result, later consumer and an observed report. The source's apparent overflow-death policy is a caution, not a recommended YAIR population rule.
