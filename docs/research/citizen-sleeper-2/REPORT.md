# Citizen Sleeper 2: assigned source pass

**Inferred synthesis:** Citizen Sleeper 2 adds a crew and a bounded job around the daily dice decision. Preparation changes which risks the group can survive, and a failed job can leave bodily damage and specific story aftermath. This is a scoped source contrast with Citizen Sleeper, not a ranking.

**F1/F14, seen in text:** the attempt to escape stabilizer dependence is interrupted, damaging memory. Serafin remembers a relationship that the Sleeper cannot fully recall. Laine seeks to retake control from inside the frame. Freedom therefore involves more than buying better parts or leaving a station.

**F2–F6, seen in text:** stress marks dice faces that cause damage on the next roll; each die has three health. Fully broken dice can be repaired at the Rig, using components, with glitches as possible aftermath. Contracts remove that repair access. Push trades stress for a class-specific intervention, and its configuration changes at hubs. Food remains necessary; starvation now produces stress. Exact lookup tables, repair quantities and all upgrades were not decoded.

**F7–F11, seen in text:** each crew member has two contract dice and an individual stress limit. Supplies sustain the group; exhaustion strains player and crew. Contract stress is a separate failure track, and a crisis can add pressure every cycle until addressed. The guide puts damage-face markers above stress and a glitch clock beside the dice. Docked crew can pursue a chosen resource focus at the next cycle. This is not evidence of offline work.

**F13, seen in text:** difficulty changes bodily recovery rules. Safe allows broken dice as 1s and disables death; Risky describes a permanent-glitch penalty after terminal damage; Dangerous describes a run-ending consequence and explicitly offers continuing on a lower difficulty. The install also contains both “cannot be fixed” and “cannot be fixed easily” permanent-glitch messages. Their active binding remains unresolved. These are contradictions and local options, not a global rule about what YAIR death must mean.

**F17, seen in text:** January2025 players report that stress cascades and contract restrictions felt like losing access to play. Publisher replies describe a crisis-focused intent and suggest adjustable difficulty. This is dated reported experience, not a representative survey or current-build bug proof. [Player discussion and publisher responses](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2442460/discussions/0/600769217628750565/). **F20, inferred:** a YAIR preflight ledger could disclose known repair restrictions, crew availability and supply consequences while leaving story twists unknown.
