# Godville

2026-10-14. Official public descriptions only; no account or play. All adoption is PROPOSAL.

**GV-F1, STATED:** The hero fights, loots and earns gold automatically. The developer sells the joke and the low time demand, not a claim that the world has no controls. Optional influences, adventures and community submissions add active layers. [Developer listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.godvillegame.android), [official blog](https://godvillegame.com/blog/).

**GV-F2, STATED:** The April-July 2026 blog describes daily anomaly layouts with hero-specific zone ordering, effects discovered through experience or intervention, one-event alerts, contextual help and fresh community-written content. August adds automatically updated hero IDs recording achievements. **INFERRED:** interest comes from a persistent personal subject, short amusing reports, occasional surprises and visible milestones. This is a design explanation, not a retention study. [Dated developer posts](https://godvillegame.com/blog/).
