# Iron Realms, Achaea first

2026-10-14. Public-document pass, no play or server-source access. SHOWN means a inspected artifact; STATED means an official rule description; INFERRED means our analysis. All adoption below is PROPOSAL.

**IR-F1, STATED:** Achaea connects professions, goods, offices and places. Tradeskills are separate from class. Its help lists 17 trades, including Transmutation, while the June 2026 feature page calls its 16-row list complete and omits it. Prefer the help list for this census, without claiming runtime availability. Two skill slots open at levels 30 and 50; further permits and rising lesson costs allow expansion. Several creative trades also require paid permits. Free reading does not mean every in-game feature is free. [Help](https://www.achaea.com/game-help?what=tradeskills), [feature page](https://www.achaea.com/game-features/crafting).

**IR-F2, STATED:** Housing is a room/exit/door graph with access permissions and useful room upgrades. Ownership does not override a city's power to bar access to its subdivision. The current help converts wood and stone into room credits; the old manual's credit prices and Development ministry name are historical. [Housing](https://www.achaea.com/game-help?what=housing-summary).

**IR-F3, STATED:** Mining links prospect reports, mine size, working squads, storage, upkeep and military capture. A stronghold gets a share when mine goods are claimed. The commodity market has unit-price listings, maximum-price buying, cancellation, expiry and storage routing. These are stated rules, not inspected market algorithms. [Mining](https://www.achaea.com/game-help?what=mines-and-mining), [market](https://www.achaea.com/game-help?what=the-commodities-market).

**IR-F4, STATED:** City government varies, including dictatorship and divine appointment. Do not turn every city into a universal election template. The publisher marks Achaea/Aetolia active and three sibling games legacy. Its comparison also calls 13 trades the widest catalogue despite Achaea's larger list, so those rankings are unreliable. [Cities](https://www.achaea.com/city-states), [comparison](https://www.ironrealms.com/games/compare-games/).
