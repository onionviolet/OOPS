# Comprehensive tabletop settings: forum survey (2026-11-04)

Weibao asked: "check reddit or other forms for comprehensive worlds like
pathfinder and stuff". This survey collects forum opinion on which tabletop
settings are the most detailed. Before anything is fetched, the candidates go
on the [download wishlist W11](DOWNLOAD_WISHLIST_2026-10-30.md#w11-comprehensive-settings-and-full-pf1-rules-data-2026-11-04)
(DL83-DL91). Every line here is forum opinion unless it says otherwise. Nobody has
counted books across these lines. Depth and volume are different measures:
forum posters say Hârn wins on depth, and the Forgotten Realms and Golarion
win on volume.

The sources are web search, a Reddit mirror (reddit.sentinel-team.org) and
EN World threads, mainly
["What is the most detailed setting available?"](https://www.enworld.org/goto/post?id=8488372)
and ["Did you realize The Forgotten Realms is the most written fantasy world ever?"](https://www.enworld.org/posts/9882498).
Reddit itself was not opened directly. The settings below are grouped by
evidence, not ranked.

| Setting (line) | What forums say | Held now | Obvious next sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Golarion (Pathfinder 1e, 2e; Starfinder) | Biggest by volume: "about 3 books a month for 12 years", plus a very detailed fan wiki | PF2e Player Core 1-2, GM Core, Monster Core; scoped PF1 and PF2 rules data | Inner Sea World Guide (PF1), Lost Omens World Guide (PF2), pathfinderwiki.com |
| Forgotten Realms (D&D) | The most material in total. A forum post claims 208 RPG products (25,240 pages) and 302 novels, not checked. The detail is down to alleys and shops, but posters call it thin on politics | 5e core three, Xanathar's, Tasha's, Fizban's, Monsters of the Multiverse | Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide, 3e Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting, Grand History of the Realms, the Forgotten Realms Fandom wiki |
| Hârn (Columbia Games) | Named for depth again and again. One poster says the Realms "can't hold a candle" to its minutiae | none | HârnWorld, the kingdom modules |
| Glorantha (RuneQuest, HeroQuest) | Called the best setting, in print since 1975. Its encyclopedia is the two-volume Guide to Glorantha, and a ten-book mythology and cults series is planned | none | Guide to Glorantha, RuneQuest: Roleplaying in Glorantha, Cults of RuneQuest |
| Warhammer (WFRP; 40k) | Spread across wargame, RPG and magazine material | none | WFRP 4e line, the Lexicanum wiki |
| Mystara (BECMI D&D) | Its regions, factions and common people are well developed. Gods are replaced by Immortals | none | The Gazetteer series (GAZ1-14) |
| Tékumel (Empire of the Petal Throne) | Very old and very detailed, but obscure | none | Empire of the Petal Throne |
| Legend of the Five Rings (Rokugan) | Paired with Hârn for amount of lore | none | L5R 5e core and sourcebooks (also on the rulebook buy list) |
| The Dark Eye (Aventuria) | One poster guesses it may rival the Realms on word count. Much of it is German-only | none | English Dark Eye 5e line |
| Eberron, Planescape, Spelljammer (D&D) | Not in these search results | Eberron: Rising from the Last War, Planescape, Spelljammer | Keith Baker's Exploring Eberron |
| Ptolus | 672 pages on one city and its dungeon | none | Ptolus: City by the Spire |
| BattleTech, Middle-earth (The One Ring), Star Wars | Very detailed, but most of BattleTech's lore is in its 200+ novels and wargame books | none | |
| Eclipse Phase, Ars Magica | Praised for being thought through | Eclipse Phase 2e quick-start; Ars Magica open corpus | Eclipse Phase 1e core (DriveThruRPG) |

Not checked: World of Darkness did not come up in these searches, though its
line is known to be large. No fan wiki article counts were found. For a size
check, open each wiki's `Special:Statistics` page.

Fit with the project, as inference: Golarion, the Realms and Mystara add
volume of places and factions to settings already like ours. Hârn, Glorantha
and Tékumel would be mechanically distinct worlds: Glorantha's cult and
myth-based magic, and Hârn's low-magic social detail. Those are the many,
distinct worlds PROJECT.md asks for. This is a proposal, not a ranking.


## Content reconciliation B314: forum leads and setting boundaries

All13 grouped setting rows, source boundaries, W11 routing, missing checks and world-fit inference were content-reviewed. Keep the useful depth-versus-volume distinction. Forum superlatives, product/page/novel totals and a planned series are attributed claims with no independent count here. A wiki's article count would be a navigation statistic, not proof of depth, primary canon or useful mechanical coverage. The survey's wording about Reddit describes a mirror/search route, not a direct Reddit read.

Held now/none and source availability are this report's dated inventory assertions, not a fresh account/library check. W11 and DL83–DL91 retain acquisition routing; the survey itself authorizes no purchase, download, wiki crawl or library change. Source leads remain useful without becoming selected worlds or prerequisites before current work.

Keep a rules edition, setting, era and medium distinct. Golarion, a rules-data export and a Starfinder product line are not automatically interchangeable source truth; Warhammer fantasy and40k are not one pack because they share a table row. A social-detail reputation does not certify a magic law. The proposed mechanically distinct fit needs source qualification for the actual selected behavior, and multiple detailed settings may offer different ordinary lives even where a shared engine serves them. No external source, PDF, sourcebook, wiki or gameplay was inspected by this local content review.
