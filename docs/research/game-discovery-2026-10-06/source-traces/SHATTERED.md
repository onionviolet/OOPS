## Later interaction and persistent aftermath

Lightning starts from an actual hit character and recursively finds reachable characters through nonsolid cells. Base arc distance is1, or2when that character's cell is water; LightningCharge can add another1. After ordinary secondary allies and LightningCharge actors are filtered, multiple targets normally use `0.4 + 0.6 / affectedCount` damage multiplier. A wet primary collision cell sets the multiplier to1. The caster, if reached, receives half of that damage and can die. The hero is excluded from arcs beyond distance1, so a farther caster can exploit wet reach while adjacency is dangerous. The inspected function tests the water cell directly, with no separate flying/grounded predicate. [Water-sensitive lightning and self-damage](https://github.com/00-Evan/shattered-pixel-dungeon/blob/e9defd0444c96d2fce3de5ec297c3398be8b7c55/core/src/main/java/com/shatteredpixel/shatteredpixeldungeon/items/wands/WandOfLightning.java#L62-L103)

Actual wand eligibility checks magic immunity and charge; the normal cast callback calls `onZap` then `wandUsed`, which debits the charge and spends the turn. This does not grant a wand by learning about rain. [Charge prerequisite and expenditure](https://github.com/00-Evan/shattered-pixel-dungeon/blob/e9defd0444c96d2fce3de5ec297c3398be8b7c55/core/src/main/java/com/shatteredpixel/shatteredpixeldungeon/items/wands/Wand.java#L150-L164)

After rain expires, terrain water and the deleted trap are still Level state. `storeInBundle` saves MAP, TRAPS and BLOBS; restore reads map/trap/blob records and rebuilds cell flags. `updateCellFlags` derives water from terrain. Dungeon saves energy and the level; potion identification has its own saved handler. No source-save or YAIR save was exercised here. ## Original YAIRPG packet

**SPD-P01 proposes one optional Village-side conduit fixture in the same home realm.** Use a few named nodes and explicit adjacency, with no tile grid, new world or combat-schema change. The player chooses personal precursor use that leaves the hazard intact, or refinement that destroys the actual same-fixture trap and retains water. The later test-charge action reads that retained surface: dry reach affects the near terminal; wet reach also opens a farther cache latch. Near wet use causes disclosed native health loss, while the dry safe-bank node avoids it. Fixed node reach, grounded exclusion if selected,2HP prototype damage and health-floor policy are original adaptations.

The proposed finite costs are in SHATTERED.json:3counted new reagent units plus an owned conserved empty vial,1stamina and3minutes for the precursor; one held precursor plus a supported `field.focus` output1/control1 and3minutes for refinement; one wash vial,1stamina and1minute for pouring. Present native focus charges2reserve and1strain for that effect. This is not an equivalence between SPD alchemy energy and native focus. The personal bypass costs the actual precursor plus1stamina/2minutes and returns the empty vial. A pulse spends one actually owned counted test charge plus1stamina/1minute. All values, new fiction and stock allocation are reversible proposals.

The observable end is **guarded cache retrieval into native inventory**: the opened farther latch allows a declared local node action, then exactly one existing Prepared survey kit transfers from explicitly allocated finite owner stock to the player. A latch fact does not conjure the kit. Stock, nodes, hazard, substrate, knowledge, vial return and actual injury must retain their owners and causes. Source knowledge grants no tool, charge or body. The ordinary existing sluice bypass stays intact. `feature.battle.routeCleared` is a settlement/gating flag; setting it would not prove native relocation or this new consumer.

Reuse current `previewSimulationCommand` and `commitSimulationCommand`; `applySimulationEffects` for owner-bound items, resources, vitals, facts and knowledge; the actual acquired `field.focus` energy policy; and the single `persistExpansionPlan` envelope. For a retained instance, `planWorldInstanceUpdate` requires declared typed facts and a revision guard. Existing sealed/soaked kit and `repositories.dry_kit` can support an optional cargo consequence, but that cannot replace the new conduction/self-risk interaction.

**Missing consumer:** no inspected current YAIR owner executes a persistent liquid/trap/conduction rule. The useful delivery therefore includes a real local fixture, pulse reach/self-risk and guarded item retrieval, not only a recipe or decorative water fact. Existing oil assay/scent, charged cooling sleeve/resin, native pump routes, and ALL19 wet-bench drying already cover related weaker patterns and stay excluded.

Initial implementation may use only new otherwise-unreserved `src/game_absorption_*.js` and builder docs. Shared/native/state/UI/registry/build/canonical integration remains with01a10fe7 until a live release. ALL19, causal and artifact lanes stay reserved. Optional absent state is inert; old jobs, inventories, vials, original opening and frozen battles3/4 retain behavior. No new inventory, scheduler or durable publisher is proposed.
