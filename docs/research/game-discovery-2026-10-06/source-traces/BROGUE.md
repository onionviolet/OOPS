## Original finite consumer proposal

**One optional Frontier salvage task: identify a destructive wash before spending the only fragile charcoal route rubbing.** Fiction, quantities, prices and timing below remain reversible proposals.

A willing original waypost steward supplies a finite lot of four unknown wash appearances: fixative/rinse conserve paper, while etchant/bleaching wash strip it. A received catalogue describes these public classes and observable reactions. The steward initially owns two full bottles of each lot, two classification strips, one fragile rubbing and five real coins for one accepted preserved-note delivery. Enrollment provisions once, and revisits replenish nothing. Do not reuse the pale reed-oil vial or its facts/projector.

- **Obtain or refuse.** Buy a real bottle for two native coins or a strip for one coin. Transfers debit actual seller stock and credit that actor's purse. Accepting salvage terms transfers the unique rubbing, not its successful outcome.
- **Classify.** Spend one strip, one stamina and two played minutes on a sealed bottle; retain that bottle. This reveals conserving/stripping only. Both classes still contain two candidates.
- **Release a specimen.** Spend a full bottle, one stamina and two minutes on a glazed chip, retaining an empty container. Etchant reacts; fixative leaves film; bleach/rinse splash harmlessly. Harmless alone stays ambiguous. Stripping+harmless proves bleaching wash; conserving+harmless proves rinse. An earlier negative receipt can become decisive after later classification. Completed ambiguous experiments remain paid.
- **Choose the real application.** Spend a remaining bottle plus the unique fragile rubbing, two stamina and three minutes. Fixative creates one native Preserved route rubbing; bleach/etchant creates one Spoiled route rubbing; rinse returns the still-fragile rubbing without preservation. Physical truth owns the result. Inference helps avoid destroying the only deliverable; calling a bottle safe does not make it safe. Unknown application remains an explicitly uncertain gamble without leaking its exact frozen outcome in previews.
- **Keep or deliver.** Preserve the actual output in inventory, or transfer it to the willing steward for five coins deducted from that actor's actual funded purse and credited to native player money once. Spoiled output cannot claim payment. No replacement rubbing, repeated escrow reward or restoration after destructive use.
