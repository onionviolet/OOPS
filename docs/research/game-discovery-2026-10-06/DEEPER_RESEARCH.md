## Standard for subsequent relevant passes

| Lens | Evidence to preserve before choosing a small packet |
| --- | --- |
| Mechanic map and dependencies | Show how actual knowledge, body/source, equipment, energy, materials, people, institutions, combat and environment connect. Record which state belongs to whom, which clock advances, and what loses support. The map may be partial; label unread joins. |
| Real decisions and consequences | Trace prerequisite → player/NPC decision → actual expenditure → mutation → actual downstream consumer → time/persistence → aftermath. Keep plausible alternatives, competing investments and practical ordinary uses, rather than stopping at a numeric modifier or recipe label. |
| Failure and later exceptions | Retain refusals before spend, paid ineffective attempts, interrupted/refunded/forfeited work, wrong or incompatible targets, loss/suppression/expiry and later high-stage rules. A successful early example does not establish every body, status, class, item or DLC combination. |
| Knowledge and coverage | Separate private truth, rumor/instruction, observation, inference, learning, ownership, access and execution. Declare actual pinned version/files/functions/ranges read and reviewed. Include relevant available DLC/base-game interactions while retaining installed-unread/documented-only/unavailable and cosmetic/artbook/soundtrack distinctions. |
| Original adaptation and selection | Preserve the larger YAIR implications and exact missing consumer beside the source findings. Then choose an affordable original packet against live native owners. Keep source readiness, disjoint code, native wiring, current build and deferred play/save/director acceptance distinct. |

Apply this standard to appropriate current or subsequently assigned game/engine scouts. Reuse completed reports, pins, raw snapshots and source coverage; select a specific unreviewed join. A new label, an installed game, an export or a README promise does not create semantic coverage. Neither a small implementation budget nor a completed first packet should erase deeper observations. Equally, unresolved questions do not make the delivered finite scope incomplete.
