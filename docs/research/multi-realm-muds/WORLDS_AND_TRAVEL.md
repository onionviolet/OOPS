## W7: Local transport is already a gameplay grammar

The official guide differentiates fixed endpoints, changing destinations, daylight availability and inspecting a portal. The XML help adds dangerous false pods, guarded shafts and alignment-sensitive statues. These are stronger mechanics evidence than a six-world marketing count. [retro-newtransport](https://newbie.retromud.org/gameplay/world-transport), [retro-transportxml](https://www.retromud.org/proxy/helpxml?=Movement/interplanetary_transport).

**Conflict retained:** the guide describes Welstar statues as fixed in place and destination; XML help describes randomized statue placement. The guide grants newbie transport through level 18, while the indexed Paladin help warns of losing it beyond level 10. The latter was only found as a search result, not re-fetched or treated as current authority. A later native audit must resolve actual thresholds. The 2014 archive is historical evidence only. [retro-archive-started](https://web.archive.org/web/20260000000000id_/https://www.retromud.org/proxy/help?=Basics%2Fgetting_started).

## W8: One origin can stay relevant abroad

Homeworld help gives a 3% skill/spell benefit, travel endurance relief and better merchant/stable prices; the opposing planet applies comparable penalties. This is an explicit local modifier, not evidence that all powers are disabled away from home. Pairings, exact arithmetic, exception order and observer disclosure are unread. [retro-worldbonuses](https://www.retromud.org/proxy/helpxml?=Worlds/homeworld_bonuses).

## W9: 3 Kingdoms separates realm theme from guild mechanism

Pinnacle connects Fantasy, Science and Chaos. The page advertises Fantasy's 250+ areas, and quest totals 42/9/27; these are not enumerated live counts. Milestones states that the realms open at level 5, qualifying the overview's broad accessibility statement. Names like cyber cities or cartoon worlds do not prove different resource, death or item-transfer laws. [3k-realms](https://3k.org/realms/index.php), [3k-milestones](https://3k.org/support/help/fetch-help.php?topic=milestones).
