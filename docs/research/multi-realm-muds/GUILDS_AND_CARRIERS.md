# Guilds, carriers and retained identity

## G1: RetroMUD guilds build a path, not a universal skill buffet

STATED: primary/secondary/tertiary training is cumulative within the adventurer-level budget. Path history controls eligibility; Paths of Power permits starting a secondary after primary level 14 and describes exceptional double-secondary wishes. The newer guide warns that taking another primary as a secondary can lose access to specialized tertiaries. Do not treat the common 20/14/9 sequence as the only legal path. [retro-helpxml-started](https://www.retromud.org/proxy/helpxml?=Basics/getting_started), [retro-paths](https://www.retromud.org/proxy/helpxml?=Guilds/paths_of_power), [retro-progression](https://newbie.retromud.org/gameplay/progression).

STATED: training has a place, not just an unlocked button; appropriate guild rooms supply it. Race determines skill/spell aptitude, body slots and vision. What is learned, what the current body supports and what institution can teach are different questions. [retro-progression](https://newbie.retromud.org/gameplay/progression), [retro-creation](https://newbie.retromud.org/gameplay/character-creation).

## G2: 3K guild machinery, professions and Ascended are separate

STATED: the guild page separates starter, four core, eighteen full and two advanced entries. These are page categories, not a proven runtime registry. Guild help confirms joining full guilds from level 5. Professions layer passive, situational and active benefits. Ascended explicitly preserves the mortal guild; no read source establishes arbitrary simultaneous guilds or free retention after switching. [3k-guilds](https://3k.org/guilds/index.php), [3k-guild-help](https://3k.org/support/help/fetch-help.php?topic=guilds), [3k-professions](https://3k.org/support/help/fetch-help.php?topic=professions), [3k-ascended](https://3k.org/support/help/fetch-help.php?topic=ascended).

STATED: Mage uses spells/summons with physical and equipment tradeoffs; Cyborg requires an installed interface and adequate constitution; Sii joins consciousness to prepared forms. These are power sources and body relations, not three cosmetic skill labels. Source rules for replacing a form, transferring implants and functioning in every realm are unread. [3k-mage](https://3k.org/support/help/fetch-help.php?topic=mage), [3k-cyborg](https://3k.org/support/help/fetch-help.php?topic=cyborg), [3k-sii](https://3k.org/support/help/fetch-help.php?topic=sii).

## G4: Ownership, usability and survival are different

STATED: RetroMUD threading enables owner use and autoload; a loan still needs safe storage through reboot. 3K's character save records stats and quest information, while milestones and special autoload help qualify gear retention. Neither establishes atomic saves or exact restoration of random/crafted instances. [retro-threading](https://www.retromud.org/proxy/helpxml?=Equipment/threading), [retro-equipment](https://newbie.retromud.org/gameplay/equipment), [3k-save](https://3k.org/support/help/fetch-help.php?topic=save), [3k-milestones](https://3k.org/support/help/fetch-help.php?topic=milestones), [3k-autoload](https://3k.org/support/help/fetch-help.php?topic=autoload).

INFERRED: reuse P05/P16/P24 for a boundary preview and stable item instances. Do not import daily equipment destruction, paid retention exceptions or tier caps into solo YAIRPG without selected lore and explicit direction. The useful lesson is precise retention disclosure.

## G5: Relationship transfer has the weakest evidence

STATED faction attitude is visible after contact in 3K. RetroMUD dues link conduct to service/leadership eligibility. That supports institution-specific memory, not global friendship or automatic cross-jurisdiction title recognition. [3k-reputation](https://3k.org/support/help/fetch-help.php?topic=reputation), [retro-progression](https://newbie.retromud.org/gameplay/progression).

INFERRED: adapt P07/P12 with one institution that remembers an actual deed and changes one service on return. Preserve NPC/manual alternatives to MUD player-service dependencies. Keep knowledge, permission, property and execution distinct under P04.

**G6, retained contradiction:** the creation guide broadly promises assets retained through reincarnation; the progression guide specifically warns that non-autoloading items do not survive it. Prefer the explicit item qualification for this research, while leaving native behavior unverified. [retro-creation](https://newbie.retromud.org/gameplay/character-creation), [retro-progression](https://newbie.retromud.org/gameplay/progression).
