# Trimps and Antimatter Dimensions source audit

2026-10-13. Research complete at the static scope below. All adaptations are PROPOSALS, unaccepted and unimplemented.

**Answer first:** borrow how later progress changes the player's job. Trimps retains a Portal-run economy while adding investment, equipment, timed expeditions and automation around it. AD nests Infinity, Eternity and Reality resets, then uses Celestials to change the rules and purpose of that same engine. Early production becomes routine infrastructure; late restrictions can make it a fresh choice again. The best first YAIRPG candidate is a world-aware routine that survives as a plan but pauses with a clear reason when its tools are forbidden. Keep the accepted one-realm, one-prestige milestone. A full ladder belongs to future design, not this research's acceptance gate.

## Reuse and cost

AD's MIT notice permits reuse with its copyright/license notice included in copied or substantial portions. Bundled libraries and assets still need their own checks. YAIRPG's MIT license does not erase upstream attribution.

Trimps is copyleft, with the version mismatch in the source table. Do not paste its code into an MIT-only distribution. If actual reuse is selected, resolve the notice mismatch and the combined-work license before distribution. The [GPLv3 summary and text](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/gpl-3.0/) describe source, notice, change and same-license conditions. Here we recommend original implementations of the observed ideas, with original names. No copied source or assets are in this lane.

For YAIRPG, the full AD ladder would add separate currencies, reset combinations, automation permissions and save migrations. The useful small substitute is one existing routine with a visible, world-specific blocked reason, then a run-purpose panel if selected. Canon rules remain the authority in HP, D&D and book worlds; an idler reference cannot define their metaphysics.
