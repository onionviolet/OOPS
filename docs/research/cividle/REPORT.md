# CivIdle source audit (2026-10-13)

**Answer first.** CivIdle splits the save into two halves. **Run state** (`GameState`) is thrown away whole on rebirth. **Meta state** (`GameOptions`) persists: permanent Great People, Age Wisdom, rebirth history and the offline-time setting. Carry across rebirth is a few explicit, capped exceptions, each granted by a named wonder (F1).

Four more mechanisms matter for YAIR:

- **Ages are derived** from the most advanced tech's column, and each age-up queues Great Person choices (F2).
- **Prices and tiers are derived** at load from the recipe graph, with authoring asserts (F3).
- **Workers are a per-tick flow resource**, spent greedily by priority on production and transport (F6, F7).
- **Absence is replayed** through the normal tick for a player-chosen share of a 4 h cap; the rest is banked as a capped reserve that later buys speed-up (F5).

Most YAIR value is in the derived value pass (F3), the absence split (F5), earned capped carry (F1), and era-up choices with diminishing stacking (F2, F9, F10).

Findings and packets are proposals, not accepted decisions. Labels: verified (code) = read at the pinned source; inferred = this lane's judgment.

## How YAIRPG compares

- **F1 partial.** `commitCampaignTransition` resets a declared domain list and keeps the rest (`src/simulation.js` L566-L575, `src/simulation_contracts.js` L193); heir leftovers in `src/heir_inheritance.js`. Missing: carry granted by an earned structure, with a cap. Overlaps [Modding Tree](../modding-tree/IMPLEMENTATION_PACKETS.md) P1; merge rather than add a parallel packet.
- **F2, F9, F10 new.** `advancePowerProgression` (`src/simulation.js` L339-L352) has tracks and exclusive branches but no derived era, era-up choice queue, diminishing in-run stacking or set-completion bonus.
- **F3 new.** Native crafting computes success and quality (`src/crafting_recipes.js` L80-L96), but item values are hand-set (about 319 `value:` fields in `src/items.js`) and nothing checks chain consistency.
- **F4 partial.** `applySimulationEffects` applies effects with causes; rebuilt, source-labelled bonuses with a stable-only subset are not an explicit pattern (inferred).
- **F5 new as a policy.** YAIR's default is no offline advancement (`docs/DESIGN_DEFAULTS_AUDIT.md` row 9). The boundary scheduler already drains a minutes budget faithfully (`advanceChroniclePlan` in `src/main.js` L1217-L1240). CivIdle adds the percent split, banked remainder and before/after diff.
- **F6, F7 partial.** Enrolled people and finite work plans exist (`src/repository_absorption_workplan.js`, `src/feature_expansion_work_plan.js`). "Idle labor makes knowledge" is new. Conveyance and named stalls are already in the [Factorio report](../factorio/REPORT.md).
- **F8 covered** by the Factorio lesson in [technology and progression](../../TECHNOLOGY_AND_PROGRESSION.md).
- **F12 split.** Take the startup asserts; skip the closures, since YAIR forbids code in content.
- **F13 partial.** Per-world starts exist (`chooseSimulationOrigin`, `src/simulation.js` L609); meta-level gating of start variants is new.
- **F15 covered and stronger** in `src/save_files.js`. One idea to take: never persist in-flight state; settle or refund it at save.
