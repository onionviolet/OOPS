# Small touches, proposed menu

Twenty-four options, not a current build checklist. Source IDs point to the
digests or foundation rules. Each needs an existing reader and world fit.

| ID | Touch | Visible effect | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| ST01 | Role-aware name opening | A formal officer and a bandit use different name grammar slots. | G7 |
| ST02 | Shared family vocabulary | Related people share a root pool without sharing an ID. | G9 |
| ST03 | Epithet with known cause | A new epithet can cite a witnessed receipt rather than inventing a feat. | G10 |
| ST04 | Rumor as attributed speech | Display who said a biography claim, separate from established history. | G3/G14 |
| ST05 | Known trait in offer text | Explain the local rule behind a refused deal when it is known. | L6 |
| ST06 | Unknown terms remain unknown | A forecast does not reveal an undiscovered policy or secret. | GR5 |
| ST07 | Retained portrait identity | A face detail persists when the same person moves between systems. | G1/G4 |
| ST08 | Portrait from body fact | A known missing eye changes presentation from the same body record. | G4 |
| ST09 | Faction symbol in receipts | Show the known flag on a treaty or resource-transfer report. | G1 |
| ST10 | Currency as surface | Local money names do not imply an unimplemented exchange economy. | G12 |
| ST11 | Worker warning before levy | Show work displaced beside force gained before confirming deployment. | L2 |
| ST12 | Return to work notice | Surviving deployed workers reappear in the work ledger. | L3 |
| ST13 | Wage quote per period | State unit count, rate and pay period together. | L1 |
| ST14 | Policy tradeoff visible | Longer work shows benefit and supported risk before enactment. | L1 |
| ST15 | Tribute-free response | A payer can recognize a no-tribute policy without forcing a one-time reward. | L5 |
| ST16 | Warning before revolt | A known warning references the policy and actor causing it. | L5 |
| ST17 | Trade route closure receipt | Show which agreement ended and which income changed. | L4 |
| ST18 | Infrastructure joins several flows | Roads can affect actual transport readers, with one modifier receipt. | L1 |
| ST19 | Champion record with identity | A competitor keeps name, results and cause across appearances. | L7/L9 |
| ST20 | Stake and payout labels | Distinguish money risked, gross return and net change. | L7 |
| ST21 | Budgeted visitor backlog | A delegation queue has a visible useful exception and finite catch-up cost. | L8 |
| ST22 | Quiet petition outcome | Nothing urgent today is an eligible result rather than filler. | GR11/GR17 |
| ST23 | World profile preview | Show known differences before selecting a proposed content profile. | G5/G6 |
| ST24 | Name collision without erasure | Two actors may share a label while receipts preserve separate IDs. | GR4/GR15 |

Do not copy Warsim prose, identity pools or art. Desktop/laptop first. None of
these touches was implemented or browser-tested by this lane.
