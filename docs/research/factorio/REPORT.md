## Larger system map

| Joined system | Source-supported distinction | Original YAIR implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resources, work and research | Inputs become actual products, science items feed labs, and some unlocks require actual crafted work. | A working sample can teach a useful process without supplying mastery, equipment or free stock. |
| Machines, transport and buffers | Crafting underway differs from progress advancing; the API exposes shortages and full outputs. Belts, robots, receivers and carriers have distinct state. | Improve the existing finite work plans with an actual receiver and explicit stall causes. |
| Fuel, energy and consequences | Reserve capacity differs from flow; burner heat, hot fluid, electricity and nutrient fuel are separate. | A workshop's fuel/effort, reserve and supported output should have observable costs and waste. |
| Materials, quality and salvage | Quality affects selected functions and item identity; reverse processing is lossy and has exclusions. | Keep native continuous quality and exact keys. Improve one real function, then make salvage conserve custody while losing material. |
| Perishables and regional processes | Freshness reaches production, handling and expiry; environmental conditions and local resources change feasible methods. | One opt-in volatile ingredient family can create a handling choice, a recovery route and useful spent material. |
| Travel and later progression | Platforms connect persistent production; advanced science still consumes earlier products and perishable cargo. | Home preparation and return routes can remain useful after new destinations open, without another reset. |
| Authoring and time | Startup definitions, runtime state, events and migration have separate contracts; exposed update time is not an inferred closed-game catch-up system. | Keep one inventory/body/clock/publisher and make absence/suspension an explicit policy. |

The detailed source chains, exact locators, exceptions and later consumers live in [FINDINGS.json](FINDINGS.json). The following are the most consequential joins, rather than a claim of full-game coverage.

**Production and knowledge.** A gear/science/lab chain connects materials to actual research. Machine work seconds and research units are different costs. The exposed lab input list also bounds which science it can consume. A faster machine still needs material, power and usable output space. YAIR should show the binding shortage rather than display a fictitious completion ETA. [Base research definitions](https://github.com/wube/factorio-data/blob/374934f81f82b09ec9fc459f92a316bd642ddd82/base/prototypes/technology.lua#L87-L125), [Lab contract](https://lua-api.factorio.com/latest/prototypes/LabPrototype.html), [actual entity state](https://lua-api.factorio.com/latest/classes/LuaEntity.html#is_crafting).

**Automation and custody.** Robot requests, incoming targeted goods and owned inventory differ. Current full train/platform schedules require the schedule interface; their simplified table omits interrupts/groups. A supplier order cannot count as delivered goods. Historical developer explanation adds the useful pattern of a temporary refuel or depot route, qualified by the current API. [Logistic network](https://lua-api.factorio.com/latest/classes/LuaLogisticNetwork.html), [logistic points](https://lua-api.factorio.com/latest/classes/LuaLogisticPoint.html), [train schedule](https://lua-api.factorio.com/latest/classes/LuaTrain.html#schedule), [schedule interrupts](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-389).

**Quality and reverse processing.** Installed stable quality has per-function consumers and explicit fluid exceptions. Stable Recycler Lua inside Quality generates reverse recipes under specific eligibility rules, returning only a portion of item inputs and no recreated fluid input. Some items use separate self-recycling instead. Stable explicitly excludes many smelting, chemistry, organic, metallurgy and cryogenic processes, with named exceptions. Its generated duration also differs from experimental result-count division. This supports an authored salvage decision rather than an automatic inverse of every YAIR recipe. [Quality definitions](https://github.com/wube/factorio-data/blob/374934f81f82b09ec9fc459f92a316bd642ddd82/quality/prototypes/quality.lua), [stable reverse generator](https://github.com/wube/factorio-data/blob/ce6741a54c3199caad4ed147e9987fd8d5653fdf/quality/prototypes/recycling.lua), [recipe productivity contract](https://lua-api.factorio.com/latest/prototypes/RecipePrototype.html#maximum_productivity).

**Time and meaningful failure.** Installed item deadlines and **recipe-level** always-fresh/reset-freshness exceptions connect custody to useful production. A pentapod egg can become an enemy, including spatial exceptions. A weak assembling-compatible nutrient route supplies a practical restart path. Stable does not have the experimental ingredient `spoil_weight` or per-product freshness-reset fields; exact native freshness aggregation/stack merging remain unread. [Installed item contract](https://lua-api.factorio.com/2.0.77/prototypes/ItemPrototype.html#spoil_ticks), [stable recipe freshness](https://lua-api.factorio.com/2.0.77/prototypes/RecipePrototype.html#reset_freshness_on_craft), [stable egg definition](https://github.com/wube/factorio-data/blob/ce6741a54c3199caad4ed147e9987fd8d5653fdf/space-age/prototypes/item.lua#L1148-L1215).

**World identity through operations.** Vulcanus metal processing exposes coproduct disposal; Fulgora reverses normal production order through scrap and stores captured lightning; Aquilo links cold operation to continuing heat and imported material. A hot reagent is not automatically a warm site. Platforms then connect these production families, with distinct weight, propulsion, schedule and loss contracts. These are useful original region rules rather than reskinned ore tiers. [Foundry and energy machinery](https://github.com/wube/factorio-data/blob/374934f81f82b09ec9fc459f92a316bd642ddd82/space-age/prototypes/entity/entities.lua), [Aquilo environment](https://github.com/wube/factorio-data/blob/374934f81f82b09ec9fc459f92a316bd642ddd82/space-age/prototypes/planet/planet.lua#L610-L685), [platform state](https://lua-api.factorio.com/latest/classes/LuaSpacePlatform.html).

**Probability is a versioned rule.** Installed scrap has per-product `probability` fields and a stable zero-or-count/expected-value contract. Experimental 2.1.21 instead supplies disjoint shared-roll intervals. The native stable roll correlations were not inspected, so the experimental 0.40 no-match range is not an installed conclusion. A finite YAIR batch should preserve its realized result/cause while averages stay estimates. [Stable product probability](https://lua-api.factorio.com/2.0.77/types/ItemProductPrototype.html#probability), [stable scrap definition](https://github.com/wube/factorio-data/blob/ce6741a54c3199caad4ed147e9987fd8d5653fdf/space-age/prototypes/recipe.lua#L2093-L2129), [experimental probability contract](https://lua-api.factorio.com/latest/types/ProductPrototypeBase.html#shared_probability).
