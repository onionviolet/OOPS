# What OOPS can borrow from existing games

2026-11-24 · Donor research for the [OOPS design review](../reviews/OOPS_DESIGN_DIVERGENCE_2026-11-24.md). The [project](../../PROJECT.md) and [vision digest](../VISION_DIGEST.md) own direction and build order.

**Start with a shift that changes tomorrow's work.** Village Watchcraft already connects a lesson, practice, tools, pay and a colleague who can use what you taught her. Tingen has a native shift, a separate wage ledger, inquiry and named colleagues. The missing connection is a shift result that gives the player a choice and changes a later offer from someone who actually learned about it. Once that works one action at a time, try the same shift in a finite routine. For cross-world play, use Tidemark as the comparison for an earned lesson and return before calling the proposed Village–Tingen exchange proven.

## Ranked borrowings

| Rank | Borrowing | Existing OOPS route and work remaining |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **A1 · Proto23's learned verb, with a consequence in another pursuit** | Reuse Village **Find work → patrol** as the control and Tingen **Office hours → inquiry** for the new integration. Village already proves the lesson/practice/tools/pay chain. Tingen needs one shift observation with a real inquiry consumer and a later response. |
| 2 | **A2 · Idle Loops' inspectable plan, with OOPS's stop-for-choice rule** | Reuse Reports' bank spend and the existing finite routine controls as references. Add a bounded shift/research plan through the native clock and surface its cursor, last result and next decision. Office hours already advances time in one step and needs a job or plan adapter for progress and cancellation. |
| 3 | **A3 · YAIR's economy feeds the next means of working** | Reuse existing Village watch → standing → trade, or the clan's patrol → merit → contribution store. Make one existing purchase or supply decision improve the next job. Tingen can use the existing salary terms while the shift result earns a separate opportunity. |
| 4 | **A4 · Mordor's remembered encounter, scoped to the people who know** | Reuse Tingen's colleague offers, directed ties and observed people records. Bind one dated, attributable result to an actual later offer. A wider circulation model can follow after witness and handoff behavior is shown. |
| 5 | **A5 · Arkivia's quote that matches the clock** | Reuse Ways out and the shared native journey quote. Verify the current run/walk promise, then try a single route where choosing to carry a load by hand or arrange a cart changes the terms. The current pace provider reads Running and its guards. |

A1 and A4 should share one experiment. Start from a supported Klein life once the foundation order allows the tie-in. At opening time choose a shift or a personal lead. On the shift path, produce one ordinary observation. Let Klein investigate it or tell a colleague. Next day, offer different help through the colleague who learned it. Compare the two paths, then compare live actions with the finite routine under the same conditions. Keep a small crossing as a separate follow-through. ## Mechanisms and checks

### F1 · Learn something you can actually do

**Proto23 primary source.** In the pinned original HTML, Walking level 1 invokes `giveAction(act.demo)`. Its action grant announces Run and reveals the Actions tab. Run has location and occupation guards, improves Walking and consumes resources while active. Completing the hunter's encyclopedia opens Bestiary, and the tea book grants a recipe. The interface teaches the change at the moment of acquisition, with a separate place to use or inspect it. This is static source evidence from the atlas copy, SHA-256 `a43e55e2eeb455f1c1ae589e3fc074a588813fe6cd57cfba827dcf923f3755d5`, lines 1859–1872, 5351–5365 and 8996–9059. [Original game](https://23html.github.io/) · [Pinned mechanism review](proto23/SOURCE_MECHANISMS_AND_ADOPTION_2026-10-13.md#f4-the-strongest-pattern-is-a-chain-from-experience-to-capability-to-ui).

**OOPS seam and adaptation.** The Practice panel already exposes Run from Running progress and explains fallback. Watchcraft is the stronger comparison: learn from Tessa's notes, rehearse during meditation and apply on a full patrol with loaned kit. The historically rebuilt browser showed a known-only patrol paying 50, a practiced equipped patrol paying 60, and one first-use life entry. The separate Mentor journey showed that a taught recruit can do her own shift and credit her teacher. Keep knowledge, practice, current tools and permission visible where the player chooses the work. For Klein, let a method learned through reading or field practice expose a particular follow-up investigation. Read the actual evidence and means through inquiry. [Practice code](../../src/reveal_chain.js) · [Study browser checks](../gm/dispatch/village-study-2026-10-09/REPORT.md#checks) · [Mentor receipt and provenance](../gm/dispatch/village-mentor-2026-10-09/REPORT.md) · [Tingen inquiry](../../src/book_world_lotm_inquiry.js).

**In-game check.** Run one full Village patrol before rehearsal and another with rehearsal and the issued kit. The row should explain the difference before the work, and the receipt should show the use after it. In Tingen, offer the same clue to two otherwise identical saves with and without learned practice. Compare the available method and its stated means. Remove a required tool or access grant and check that the known method stays readable with its blocker.

### F2 · Compress the wait and keep the decision

**Arkivia and Idle Loops primary source.** Arkivia v0.26.1 quotes arrival from remaining distance, Walking, load and energy. Its short-trip skip spends the quoted in-world minutes, credits Walking and distance, logs the skip and enters the destination. The ordinary branch advances a walk in ticks and can update arrival as conditions change. Those branches establish the visible contract, with exact parity across every clock event untested. Omsi's Idle Loops keeps the executing list and editable next list separate, supports counts and displays invalid actions. Its option to pause on an incomplete loop operates at restart. For OOPS, meaningful decisions should stop execution when reached. Arkivia snapshot SHA-256 `451cd4b2be202aa5c75f7ab4e690cc596b4939cdf79c7ccf89302672fda0cb0b`, `game.js` lines 1759–1779, 2424–2429, 3539–3540 and 5204–5215. [Arkivia](https://just-pointless.github.io/arkivia/) · [Omsi executing list and failures](https://github.com/omsi6/omsi6.github.io/blob/fa21477d424f3aa1f1bf6deb9f6f83c2569770ab/loops/actions.js#L13-L193) · [Omsi pause and restart](https://github.com/omsi6/omsi6.github.io/blob/fa21477d424f3aa1f1bf6deb9f6f83c2569770ab/loops/driver.js#L101-L149).

**OOPS seam and adaptation.** Native finite routines and work-plan slices already track selected steps, reserved inputs, refunds and explicit resume. Main has specific consumers, including a finite Frontier routine and a cooling plan. The general away bank spends through fast-forward and debits only clock time actually played. A historical Patient Furnace browser walk separately exercised finite harvest output, stop, reload/import and carry. Klein's shift is callable from **Office hours** under the LOTM panel. The current host requires the full time plan before writing its observation, and the separate **Find work**/Pay view still reads wages. Give the routine three visible facts: what is running, what just settled and why it stopped. Bind only the stable stretch into the plan and preserve the choice of what to do with the result. [Native command bindings](../../src/main.js#L2439) · [Work-plan boundary tests](../../tests/routine_jobs.test.js) · [Bank spend](../../src/away_bank_domain.js) · [Office host](../../src/book_world_lotm_host.js#L439) · [Office rows](../../src/book_world_lotm_ui.js#L322) · [Pay view](../../src/lotm_work_day.js) · [Furnace browser receipt](../gm/dispatch/cheats-2026-10-25/FURNACE_BROWSER.json).

**In-game check.** From one save, do the selected shift and follow-up live, then take the planned path with the same seed, inputs and clock. Compare spent time, rent/duty boundary, observations, purse and opening of the choice. Try stopping early, changing the next plan, and saving/reloading before the choice. A follow-up requiring a colleague who has left should stop with that reason while leaving unspent time available.

### F3 · Let the job purchase a means, earn access or create an obligation

**YAIR, Proto23 and Arkivia primary source.** YAIR's pinned `e3356432ad5112f30842cfe6a6b5c3a4ced77de9` defines Fieldwork as a location-specific job with pay and activity skill. Trading tracks sale saturation by region and group, then recovers and trickles at day boundaries. Clothing and weather already give route preparation a practical use. Proto23's market gate guard job advances Patience until 20:00, completes its quest and announces the wage, with occasional observations of passersby. Arkivia caches rolled vendor stock for the day, a simpler clock-linked shop pattern. Proto23 HTML lines 12308–12331; Arkivia `game.js` lines 2882–2901. [YAIR location rules](https://github.com/Miktaew/yet-another-idle-rpg/blob/e3356432ad5112f30842cfe6a6b5c3a4ced77de9/src/locations.js#L2321-L2347) · [Market recovery source](https://github.com/Miktaew/yet-another-idle-rpg/blob/e3356432ad5112f30842cfe6a6b5c3a4ced77de9/src/market_saturation.js#L76-L120) · [Upstream changelog](https://miktaew.github.io/yet-another-idle-rpg/changelog.html) · [Proto23](https://23html.github.io/) · [Arkivia](https://just-pointless.github.io/arkivia/).

**OOPS seam and adaptation.** The October Village browser receipt shows watch completion changing cash, Perception, regional reputation and trade terms. The later documented standing route lets the elder offer the same parcel relay that the courier’s pamphlet can open. The separate Watchcraft career already uses custody and debt. Tingen's office shift counts and leaves a note, while the wage follows its contract; the clan's patrol has a report and merit ledger that feeds local purchases. Harry's classes and marked homework already feed the exam rules, with a historical class/homework reload receipt. Choose the next input or privilege by the world's terms: Village equipment/courier access, clan supplies, or a Tingen colleague's next assignment. Reuse the existing account and spell out the resulting opportunity beside the payout. [Village route and browser receipts](../gm/dispatch/village-loop-2026-10-27/VILLAGE_LOOP_CHECKPOINT.md) · [Lead daily-loop evidence](../audits/LEAD_CORE_FLOW_AUDIT_2026-11-13.md) · [Institution and standing owners](../AREA_GUIDE.md#standing-and-reputation).

**In-game check.** Earn two Village watch completions via the native work route, inspect the trade price breakdown, then start the elder’s standing-gated parcel offer at its current threshold. Compare with an interrupted shift and confirm that completion rewards do not publish. For the lead-world experiment, show one real follow-on purchase, instruction or offer and prove it changes the next outing. Verify the receipts and balance after reloading.

### F4 · A named person knows, and an institution has a reason to act

**Shadow of Mordor and Qud primary accounts.** Mordor's GOTY manual documents enemies returning with scars, killers gaining power, and ordinary Uruks becoming captains after killing the player. The Army screen shows rank and known relationships, with information earned through encounters or intel. The useful unit is a specific encounter that changes one person's behavior and position. Qud's developers describe generated village histories, factions, local resources, named people and quests alongside authored Joppa and a shared main quest. Local history reaches the player through storytelling and practical local quests. Use that connection to give one generated local request a reason rooted in its supplier, people and place, within canon's unstated space. [Mordor manual](https://www.feralinteractive.com/en/manuals/shadowofmordor/latest/steam/#saurons-army) · [Freehold's village update](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?enddate=1531257647&feed=steam_community_announcements) · [Freehold GDC session](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1025914).

**OOPS seam and adaptation.** Iona already has a bounded, historically browser-tested memory that feeds solo work and terms. Tingen has named tie offers, an expense-claim consumer and a news/heard-reputation reader. Person memory and standings have owner-specific stores and observer-aware reads. The office host currently writes a player's last shift note and count. Give one observer or explicitly told colleague a dated fact and let it change their next offer. The player-facing reason belongs in that offer, with any source limits respected. Start with a quiet day and one optional follow-up. RimWorld's official description supports adjustable incident pacing as a later reference if the cadence of such offers becomes a problem. [OOPS people and news owners](../AREA_GUIDE.md#people-and-ties) · [Mentor evidence](../gm/dispatch/village-mentor-2026-10-09/REPORT.md) · [RimWorld storytellers](https://rimworldgame.com/index.php?lang=en).

**In-game check.** Tell colleague A the result and later ask A and B for help separately. A can refer to the report and change the offer; B only acts on independently learned information. A missed shift or late handoff should produce its own ordinary outcome. Repeat the contact and reload to check that the same incident pays, changes standing or consumes an offer only once. Test one authored task and one generated local task through the same observation and inquiry verbs.

### F5 · Travel, rewind and succession each retain specific things

**4Dimensions and Idle Loops primary source.** 4Dimensions documents travel between four eras, Tradepoints for advancement, and housing with persistent storage. The pinned public remort code resets level and experience and retains a bounded history of classes. Once the slots are full, the prompt points to the score sheet and asks which slot to discard. The source and destination guides offer separate examples of moving a continuing character and starting again with a recorded past. Server parity is unverified. Omsi's restart clears immediate resources and raw stats while town knowledge, talents and learned skills survive. A later trip still requires its local supplies. Use these to ask what stays usable, what becomes a hint and what needs to be acquired again. [4Dimensions features](https://4dimensions.org/features/) · [Pinned remort code](https://github.com/4dmud/mud/blob/d2ee92671693f2f0eac88d7d5f9d86bebc17d89c/remort.cpp#L52) · [Omsi restart](https://github.com/omsi6/omsi6.github.io/blob/fa21477d424f3aa1f1bf6deb9f6f83c2569770ab/loops/driver.js#L135-L149) · [Retention details](open-idle-games-2026-10-09/IDLE_LOOPS.md#f2-loop-operation-exact-retention-and-bonus-layers).

**OOPS seam and adaptation.** Native passage has an actual learned Tidemark method owner and cargo checks. A historical continuous browser journey paid for the local lesson, used owned kit, collected and returned. The separate visitor receipt exercises return, revisiting the same instance and literal save import from a retained-visit fixture. Treat those as route-specific proof. The proposed Tingen–Village method exchange still needs its taught method, Village consumer and return consequence joined. Reuse the passage terms for carried knowledge and cargo. At the destination, have the action quote require the local material, body or permission it actually needs. ### F6 · Use the same travel quote for the label and the actual trip

**Arkivia source spot-check.** `getMovementSpeed` multiplies `50 + 3 × Walking level` by a load ratio capped at one and a low-energy factor. Walking practice and energy changes can update the arrival display. Mining and felling include both tool efficiency and energy. Crafting and hunting include energy. Alchemy and exploration read their respective skill alone in this revision. This narrows the broader AK18 wording in the earlier discussion. The primary combat Speed stat is outside the movement formula. [Arkivia v0.26.1](https://just-pointless.github.io/arkivia/) · [Prior source read](../gm/dispatch/play-surface-2026-11-14/PROTAGONIST_WALKTHROUGH_UI_ELEMENTS_2026-11-20.md#arkivia-source-read-travel-stats-and-daily-life-discussion-2026-11-21).

**OOPS seam, adaptation and check.** Ways out and the two timed native paths now read the same journey quote. The Running multiplier is 0.8 with a two-stamina cost on ordinary timed travel, and the quote UI can show the cause, total and arrival. Other travel hosts have their own terms. Prove one route while walking, running, below the stamina floor and across midnight. Then try a load where walking remains possible but a paid cart changes the time or what can be hauled. Record the actual arrival and elapsed minutes both ways. This offers a distinct transport choice while the quoted effects can still come from the existing provider.
