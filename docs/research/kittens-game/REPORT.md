## Source mechanisms and corrections

F1: [core.js:1324](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/core.js#L1324) computes `(cost - held) / (net per tick * UI ticks per second)` if net production is positive, detailed prices are enabled and stock is short. ETAs under one second are omitted. Missing craftable inputs expand into known raw ingredient recipes, rounded up to complete craft batches; wood is intentionally excluded. [js/resources.js:1060](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/resources.js#L1060) tests direct cap limits and cached craft limits, and allows enough already-held overcap stock. The tooltip cap marker does not share that overcap exception, so do not treat a marker as authoritative refusal.

F2: [js/resources.js:695](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/resources.js#L695) scales conversion by the scarcest input, removes inputs immediately and returns an activity fraction. [js/buildings.js:2787](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/buildings.js#L2787) maps a non-full fraction to `lackResConvert`; [core.js:2101](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/core.js#L2101) maps it to a class. It does not return an exact input ID with a suggested fix. [game.js:4769](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/game.js#L4769) explains nonzero rate contributions and time to zero/cap. Zero contribution rows are omitted, so a zero-worker explanation is weaker than a named blocker. [js/jsx/left.jsx.js:127](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/jsx/left.jsx.js#L127) warns near 75%/95% capacity. Reaching cap clips accumulation; output capacity does not universally switch every converter off.

F3: [js/buildings.js:2828](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/buildings.js#L2828) separates permission (`defaultUnlockable`/`unlockable`) from exposure (`unlocked`, often a fraction of base price). [core.js:2145](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/core.js#L2145) separates owned `val` and active `on`; research has `unlocked` and `researched` instead. [js/science.js:2306](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/science.js#L2306) saves the latter and replays researched unlocks after load. Affordability is derived from current resources; optional cap highlighting is separately derived. These are useful state distinctions but not evidence for institutional/actor permission rights.

F4: [js/village.js:811](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/village.js#L811) charges upkeep to catnip. [js/workshop.js:2116](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/workshop.js#L2116) crafts wood from catnip and beams from wood. [js/buildings.js:350](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/buildings.js#L350) prices fields in catnip; [js/buildings.js:1071](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/buildings.js#L1071) smelters consume wood/minerals. Thus both catnip and wood compete across uses. Separate UI surfaces require the player to assemble the comparison. Borrowing lethal catnip maintenance would conflict with YAIRPG's accepted comfortable unfed play and no hunger death.

F5: [js/time.js:2048](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/time.js#L2048) separately rejects an ETA when storage is insufficient or the computed net rate is nonpositive. [js/time.js:2684](https://github.com/nuclear-unicorn/kittensgame/blob/781e379f79f1e7512d168849cba21ed111502644/js/time.js#L2684) reads structured purchase results to wait or skip a queue entry. This is stronger scheduling logic than the tooltip, but does not supply the complete visible cause and next useful action requested by P01. See [deeper systems](aspects-2026-10-14/DEEPER_SYSTEMS.md).
