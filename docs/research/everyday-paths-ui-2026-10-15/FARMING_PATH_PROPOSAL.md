# The courtyard grower: one farming life

2026-10-15. **Unselected original design proposal**, extending EP1/EP3/EP4/EP5. All new beds, area figures, methods, powers, people/agreements and growing laws below are examples, not implemented content or accepted tuning. Existing one-plot MG06 behavior remains unchanged. See [existing-system evidence](EXISTING_SYSTEM_FIT_AND_PROPOSALS.md), [fishing counterpart](FISHING_PATH_PROPOSAL.md) and [area/UI model](LAND_AREA_AND_INVOLVEMENT_UI.md).

## CG1. Purpose and existing anchor

The proposed character wants a small productive garden, ordinary food for people they know, and space to understand one interesting plant. They might remain a local grower, become a seed keeper/teacher, specialize in cultivation, or support a household. None requires acquiring an estate. A large ordinary farm is also valid if that is the chosen life.

MG06 already supplies a paid plot right, actual seed packet, greens/grain choice, planting, required tending, played-time growth, one harvest and useful food/trade consumers. That is a meaningful foundation. Its current plot is a single abstract capacity with player-held crop stage/type. It has no square-metre figure, six-bed layout, passive growth, environment simulation or new water-power consumer. This proposal describes possible extensions without retroactively assigning those properties to existing saves.

## CG2. What the garden looks like

**Illustrative future holding:** 48 m² total, with six 4 m² growing beds (24 m² cultivated),16 m² access paths and8 m² shared water/work space. The numbers explain why “six plots” alone is ambiguous; they are not an agricultural yield model. Beds A/B grow greens, C/D grain, E an ordinary useful herb if a real consumer is selected, and F holds a controlled comparison. The herb/comparison are candidate content, not existing MG06 crops.

A small place plan might show labelled bed rectangles and a water point. Each bed has an identity and one current crop/cohort; a crop drawing conveys stage while text gives known conditions, amount and work. Position matters only for an actual consequence such as access, shade or water connection. It is acceptable for visually adjacent beds to be mechanically equivalent. The interface should not imply a simulation it does not have.

The existing first slice can remain one clearly drawn plot card. A multi-bed layout is a later option, not required to make that first crop meaningful. “Plot” is an access/management parcel; “bed” is a cultivation unit; a plant/specimen is an individual when its state matters. Naming these distinctly avoids turning six pots and six fields into equivalent capacity.

## CG3. A concrete cycle

| Moment | Example choice | What must actually matter |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare | Keep one bed in familiar greens or use the existing grain alternative; inspect seed stock and known output uses. | Pay/reserve real seed and any actual effort. A seed packet's coverage is defined for that crop/method; one packet is not universal area or density. |
| Establish | Plant personally by a known method or assign a supported planting task. | Actor, bed, seed identity/count and completion belong to the same work record. A cancelled planting follows its defined refund policy and leaves no ghost crop. |
| Care | Personally tend the crop; use a learned efficient technique; later use actual irrigation or a fitting power. | Each means performs the named care. Repeated inspection does not count as tending. Water bookkeeping is added only if water availability/coverage makes a real decision. |
| Grow | Follow known growth stage and any represented condition; pursue other life activities only if the selected time/concurrency model allows. | Current MG06 requires an explicit grow command consuming played time. Independent biological progress is a separate proposed behavior change, not a UI improvement. |
| Harvest/use | Keep the planned food, sell a selected portion to an actual buyer, or retain the chosen sample. | Harvest creates real items once; allocations compete for those items. Existing meal/hospitality consumers can supply meaning without inventing another currency. |

No basic loop needs soil exhaustion, pests, pollinators, plant genetics and market simulation at once. Add a condition only if it changes a worthwhile choice in the selected world. A peaceful grower can use stable known methods without constant crises.

## CG4. Skills, powers and discoveries

| Example | Useful proposed behavior | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Read a crop, Farming competence | Interpret a represented readiness/care sign and explain the next useful intervention. | No hidden chemistry revealed from a single glance; no need to invent a disease solely to justify diagnosis. |
| Practiced tending, learned technique | Perform one actual care step with less effort or better control under a defined rule. | Not an automatic global speed/yield bonus. A practiced routine can use it too. |
| Plan the planting, learned knowledge | Match a known crop/method to an available bed and known calendar window. | No clairvoyant forecast or list of undiscovered plants. |
| Guided water, optional power | Deliver the required care to selected valid beds/connected irrigation within its real scope. | Does not grant growth energy, nutrients, harvest work or Farming mastery unless that power actually does those things. |
| Warmth/shade control, optional means | Supply a represented environmental condition so a selected plant can grow at that site/time. | No penalty added merely to preserve ordinary seasonal limits. Exact scope follows the world. |
| Accelerated growth, optional stronger power | Advance the biological process its law actually governs. | Does not automatically fill a whole seasonal simulation or mint harvested inventory; if the world genuinely allows material creation, model that as a different effective route without arbitrary obstruction. |
| Combined cultivation | Plant knowledge identifies a condition; trained control applies water/warmth accurately; the power supplies the relevant means. | No universal “magic farming” level or mandatory pairwise combo recipe. Attribution can distinguish knowledge, control and supplied effect. |

**One proposed discovery:** under this fictional garden's chosen law, a particular cover delays drying without reducing the required exposure. Compare uncovered and covered sections with matched crop/stage and available observations. The useful result is a known care method that reduces a real watering need, not a guaranteed higher-grade harvest. This example requires an actual moisture/exposure rule; it is not already MG06 behavior. If that rule is not selected, keep experiments to represented variables instead.

A helper can notice that one part remains damp, or a routine can retain a surprising sample. The notebook records actor/source, condition, intervention, observation and confidence. One good explanation can teach a principle without demanding repeated tests each life. A treatment's effect on this crop does not prove heritable seed change. A rare result can be worth preserving without claiming its cause is known.

**Illustrative power/area comparison:** a selected irrigation power can serve12 m² through a connected set of beds, while the example holding cultivates24 m². Its care view can show three 4 m² beds supplied and three outside that selected scope. This number is proposed capacity, not assumed magic tuning. Area alone does not determine reach or connection. A later stronger power might cover all six beds, removing that chore entirely. The point of the comparison is an intelligible consequence of actual scope, not a requirement to make every power insufficient.

## CG5. Choosing involvement, without changing the crop's identity

| Choice this session | Player-facing view | What continues and who learns |
|---|---|---|
| Tend one specimen personally | Selected bed/specimen detail, visible stage, actual condition, available interventions and known method comparison. Optional placement view where it matters. | Personally performed work counts as personal qualifying practice; opening detail is free. |
| Personally follow the usual routine | Garden summary: crop groups, next work, real inputs and player occupation. Individual bed detail remains reachable. | The character remains busy under current rules. Routine is not automatic helper labor or free concurrency. |
| Investigate one comparison | Keep A/B treatments and known observations beside each other. Pin the question rather than every growing statistic. | Research can coexist with routine care where the actual action model permits. No automatic confirmation from a single result. |
| Delegate ordinary chores | One helper assignment with named crop/area/chore, actual ability, inputs, availability, custody and stop policy. Exception row links to the bed. | Helper performs actual work and receives qualifying practice. The player learns from a real report/study route, not the helper's unshared knowledge. |
| Use a device/power | Coverage/target overlay only when the means has spatial scope; otherwise a plain active-care row with actual effect and end condition. | Chore executes only through that means. No fictitious worker or residual chore is added after a power genuinely solves it. |

The player may personally cultivate F while a helper harvests A/B and irrigation cares for E. These are assignments on the same garden, not three disconnected minigames. Inspecting F does not silently stop the helper. Taking over a claimed bed is an explicit handoff: finish or cancel/release the old job before accepting conflicting work. Opening a detailed display never creates a new crop or rerolls its result.

**Sample agreement, proposal:** “Harvest the ready greens in A/B into the household store. Keep the reserved study sample. Do not harvest E/F, buy seed or replant. Stop when these two beds are settled or access is lost.” The agreement does not include watering, seed purchase or ownership transfer unless explicitly offered. A worker being skilled does not mean they consent, are available or know the chosen method.

**Expertise follows the task:** careful diagnosis may use one expert's judgment; a bulk harvest may use combined real labor subject to space/access; teaching needs a learner and teacher; a ritual may require one operator controlling it throughout. Taiwu motivates this distinction, ACS motivates actual job execution, and Immortal Life motivates powers that cover selected chores. No donor's entire economy is required.

## A garden life after the first crop

Possible depth: a reliable kitchen supply, a favorite plant, a taught method, a locally useful preparation, a remembered shared meal or a garden inherited under existing continuity rules. If chosen, an unusual cultivation problem can make one bed deeply involving while the rest stays calm. A large farm can use grouped area/crop summaries; a small garden can stay richly represented. Neither screen density nor acreage measures path depth.

The unresolved implementation choices are recorded in [area/UI model](LAND_AREA_AND_INVOLVEMENT_UI.md): time/concurrency, area/density/seed coverage, what conditions exist, work handoff, capability scope and knowledge. These remain proposals; no save receives area, beds, water debt or new obligations in this pass.
