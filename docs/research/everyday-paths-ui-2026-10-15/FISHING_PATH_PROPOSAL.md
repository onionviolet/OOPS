# The river keeper: one fishing life

2026-10-15. **Unselected original design proposal.** Concrete examples flesh out EP2–EP5 in [existing-system fit](EXISTING_SYSTEM_FIT_AND_PROPOSALS.md); they are neither implemented YAIR content nor donor-game claims. Numbers, technique names, world rules and events below are illustrative. Existing accepted D2–D4 still govern: involvement chosen per activity/life, viable whole pursuits, repeatable world-specific principles and room for unexpected discoveries. No implementation authorized.

## RF1. A life worth pursuing at one river

The proposed character wants to know this river, supply useful fish and pass on a reliable practice. They can remain an excellent local fisher, become a patient naturalist, teach someone, support a kitchen, or develop a powered fishing method. A fleet, estate, rare monster or global collection is unnecessary. These are possible commitments, not a class choice that locks out other lives.

**Existing anchor:** Village fishing already has Fishing skill, an equipped pole, seasonal availability, skill-scaled cycle time/chance/quality and actual stock. A large-fish→fillet recipe exists; not every caught species qualifies. This proposal keeps that routine. The particular dinner/request route below is candidate content and needs an actual consumer, rather than assuming every existing fish is edible or fillet-compatible.

**Place proposal:** distinguish three river reaches only if they change a decision. The shallow reed margin suits one presentation; the moving seam suits drifting; the shaded bank offers a different known condition. Access, depth/flow class and nearby cover can be coarse attributes. No simulated fluid field or metre-by-metre river is required. Their effects are proposed world rules, not facts already encoded in the base catch table. Unknown fish/relationships stay undisclosed.

## RF2. A concrete outing, with optional depth

| Moment | Player experience | Consequence and source boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Choose “fish for today's meal” or simply “spend time fishing.” Inspect known catches/uses and actual gear before starting. | A purpose can suggest a method or stopping rule; a request is not required for a satisfying outing. Specific meal requirements are proposed, not a live quote. |
| Preparation | Pick the moving seam and a learned drift presentation, or keep the ordinary known routine. | A method has actual conditions and stock requirements. Existing generic fishing does not currently consume bait, so any bait method must add that real debit before saying it does. |
| Personal decision | After a visible sign, compare a shorter controlled drift with letting the presentation pass farther downstream. | One choice can trade selectivity, reach or handling difficulty. It spends the stated activity time and any real inputs once. Opening the diagram is free. No compulsory twitch timing. |
| Ordinary result | Catch an eligible fish, miss, or obtain another supported result. Inspect what actually happened. | One attempt is not proof of a causal law; a miss is informative only if the character observed a relevant sign. Existing cycles can award multiple species, so a new single-cast unit needs a stated compatibility decision. |
| Continuity | Keep a specimen for an experiment, allocate an eligible catch to a real meal, or record the visit and return later. | Stock has one custody path. A “keep for study” allocation prevents a routine from silently selling/consuming that copy. A notebook does not conjure specimens or knowledge. |

This is a complete proposed small loop. Deeper expertise grows around it without turning every outing into research or management.

## RF3. Skills, powers and combinations

These methods would use existing competence/learned-function mechanisms. They do not need separate numeric skills for every observation.

| Example | Proposed useful function | Boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Read the seam, Fishing supported by relevant perception | Recognize an observable feeding sign and select a suitable known presentation. | No automatic species census or private exact probabilities. Perception provides evidence; Fishing interprets a learned pattern. |
| Controlled drift, learned Fishing technique | Keep the presentation in a particular reachable band, improving selectivity or repeatability under its actual rule. | Still requires a suitable means and valid place. Routine resolution can apply the learned technique too. |
| Gentle landing, practiced handling | Safely retain/release a catch where living condition matters to an actual consumer. | Do not add fish-health bookkeeping until living specimens/release make it useful. It need not be in the first slice. |
| Surface sense, optional world-fitting power | Detect movement/position within its defined sensing scope, including something eyesight cannot establish. | Movement is not identity, intention, a complete catalogue or guaranteed catch. A stronger actual power may legitimately disclose more. |
| Hold an eddy, optional water-control power | Create a usable presentation zone or make an otherwise inaccessible flow fishable under the world's power law. | It changes flow/access, not the existence of fish. A forceful use might differ from a subtle use only if that consequence is part of the selected world, not an automatic penalty. |
| Combined approach | Sensing locates movement; Fishing chooses a presentation; water control holds it where intended. | Each contribution has a real consumer. No bespoke “Fish Sense + Water Magic” skill is necessary. Costs/limits follow actual powers, with no invented balancing tax. |

Practical learning belongs to the actor doing qualifying work. A personally performed and a personally followed routine both count as practice where appropriate. Watching a diagram gives no extra XP. A helper learns their own handling; the player can learn a principle by inspecting evidence or receiving a useful explanation. A teacher, inherited method or fitting power can provide an alternate route without mandatory manual grinding.

## RF4. One discovery with a repeatable cause

**Proposed fictional local rule:** drifting a known preparation through a particular feeding band changes which eligible fish are attracted. This is a possible law to author, not a reveal about YAIR's actual hidden world.

| Record | What the character can honestly know | What changes next |
|---|---|---|
| Observation | During this outing, catches/signs clustered after this preparation reached the feeding band. Conditions and actual results are retained. | Add a question, not “recipe discovered.” A helper/routine can report this observation too. |
| Hypothesis | Preparation or location might explain the pattern; timing is another possible cause. | Compare one variable using the same known method and reasonably matched conditions. No requirement for a fake scientific significance meter. |
| Supported method | Repeated evidence, a credible teacher, or an appropriate diagnostic power establishes a useful relationship. | Save the method with known conditions, uncertainty and real required stock. A method can be useful before its mechanism is fully understood. |
| Further surprise | A result outside the familiar pattern occurs under a changed condition or genuine world-supported variation. | Retain the actual specimen/report and uncertain cause. Do not invent a hidden explanation merely because the roll was rare. |

Understanding need not mean a guaranteed catch. Stable rules can yield variable results. A new method can be a better substitute, different access, useful selectivity or a taught procedure, not only a new species or larger payout. Reopening details or changing involvement never rerolls an already committed outcome. Cross-life persistence follows existing identity/continuity rules, not automatic omniscience.

## RF5. Hands-on choice and matching UI

These are independent attention/execution choices, not unlock tiers. A player can choose them per outing and per means available.

| Chosen involvement | Fishing UI | Execution and learning |
|---|---|---|
| Personally decide each useful step | Shared center detail shows a simple bank/reach diagram, visible signs, selected presentation and one consequential choice. Selected gear/catch detail remains adjacent. | Explicit action advances time. Optional paused choices follow the normal pause policy. No hidden reward multiplier for leaving this view open. |
| Follow a known routine personally | Compact current-work row: “You · drift presentation · [known reach] · [actual work remaining].” Open details or stop without resetting progress. | Character remains occupied under existing activity rules and gains qualifying personal practice. It is not free concurrent work. |
| Watch for selected discoveries | Show current routine plus pinned question, changed variable and last relevant observations. “Retain unusual catches” is a real inventory/custody policy. | Routine proceeds only under supported instructions. An informative result can become an obligation/notice; it need not interrupt every cast. |
| Ask an available helper | One assignment card: known helper, place, agreed method, supplies, destination, stop condition, actual status/report. | Actual actor work, agreement, availability and stock apply. Player can do something else only if concurrency permits. Reports teach only what was observed/communicated. |
| Use a fitting autonomous means | Show device/construct/power target, scope, current operation, destination and termination/expiry if it has one. | An unattended technique must actually execute; opening an automation panel does not create autonomy. A passive power can genuinely remove labor. |

**Sample instruction, proposal:** “Use this known method at the reed margin while access holds. Keep any unfamiliar catch for inspection. Stop when the meal's allocated requirement is met or supplies run out; do not buy substitutes.” The meal's real item requirement and capacity must be defined before execution. A routine never silently changes bait, method or sales policy.

**Illustrative result card:** actor and method → observed condition → actual catch/stock destination → qualifying practice → new observation, if any. Unknown causes remain uncertain; ordinary results can be summarized rather than spamming the full-height log. Notices link to the source detail.

A little bank diagram is useful for meaningful reach, flow, cover or placement. If none affects the chosen activity, three named reach cards are clearer. Zoom is inspection; involvement changes only through an explicit action/instruction. Full UI/area principles are in [land and representation](LAND_AREA_AND_INVOLVEMENT_UI.md).

## Route to a whole life, not compulsory escalation

Possible enduring results: a reliable local method, a favorite place and its remembered visits, a taught apprentice, a useful preparation, respected community supply or a notebook others can use. Commercial scale is one optional direction. The same river can remain interesting through improved understanding, relationships, different world-fitting means and changed personal purposes.

Implementation questions stay bounded: choose one existing spot, resolve Riverbank's source XP/display discrepancy if using it, select one method and one useful output consumer. Then native behavior and persistence would need their own evidence. No actual skill names, powers, discoveries, dialogue, recipes or UI controls were added here.
