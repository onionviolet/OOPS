## What their players want

- W1. Make cultivation stage and rank visibly change the same action. Support diverse Gu, path-specific choices and useful moves outside damage.
- W2. Give refinement multiple supply routes and meaningful preparation. Rare drops, repeated bandit fights and passive waiting draw complaints.
- W3. Give a big map enough to discover and let borders, movement Gu and local events affect the journey.
- W4. Keep loss consequential and readable. Players debate newcomer protection, harsh resets and valuable Gu stuck on inactive characters.
- W5. Make the world legible to newcomers, keep effects proportionate to what moves do and favor cosmetic extras over paid progress or gacha.
