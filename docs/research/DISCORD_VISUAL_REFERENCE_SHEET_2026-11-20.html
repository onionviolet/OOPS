VR01 · P1

Put the observed effect on the affected creature

Actually visible. Corc’s Green Slime has two effect icons and an expanded Liquid Metabolism card: rain enjoyment, HP 19/s and an infinite-duration symbol. YAIR’s frog log names bare-hand contact as the cause of toxins between attack lines.

Try. Attach a learned or currently observed effect to the selected individual, beside its posture. Reveal the cause only through observation or an earned explanation. Keep an unknown cause as an unanswered observation, not a guessed stat.

Test. Use one supported encounter with and without its actual condition. Inspect the same opponent before and after learning the cause; confirm that the result stays attributable in Reports after leaving and returning. Do not invent a counter or weakness to make the example work.

Provenance, existing OOPS support and owners

The slime caption only says “but look”; several reply previews are unavailable. The frog caption explicitly discusses reusing the damage colour. Neither picture establishes a general weather or contact-trait engine.

Already in OOPS. OOPS has living kinds/body/layers, observer-filtered opponent facts and combat situation views. This review did not demonstrate a runtime slime/rain or frog/contact encounter.
