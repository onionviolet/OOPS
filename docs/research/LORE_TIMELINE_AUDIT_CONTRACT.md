## L1 Source identity before canon labels

Each claim records work, medium, edition/language, continuity, represented cutoff,
exact locator, evidence type and actual inspected coverage. A wiki's chapter
reference is a retrieval lead until the chapter is read. Official episode
summaries establish what the summary states; they do not establish every scene,
line or power boundary. Downloaded is distinct from read and reconciled.

Keep these independent:

| Field | Values and purpose |
|---|---|
| Evidence | Direct scene/narration; attributed statement; official summary; secondary report; inference; memory; unknown |
| Continuity | Main version, adaptation, author alternate/IF, spinoff, fan work, original project branch |
| Claim scope | Historical fact, rule, belief, prediction, exception, disputed account |
| Project status | Research reference, proposed extension, director accepted, implemented, exercised |

An author alternate can be valid evidence for its own continuity without being
baseline history. A fan premise can inspire an option without establishing a
source law. Do not silently fuse WN/LN/manga/anime or treat an adaptation's added
scene as main-version history. No universal medium hierarchy replaces a world's
declared source selection.

## L2 Build two timelines and a knowledge ledger

Maintain occurrence order separately from revelation/publication order. Record
an explicit date only when supported; otherwise keep relative order or a bounded
interval. Chapter numbers alone are not in-world dates. Preserve uncertainty
instead of manufacturing an exact calendar.

For each event record:

| Event field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Identity | Stable event ID, source binding, continuity and occurrence interval |
| Causes | Participants, intentions, means, location, material/institutional support, prior events |
| Baseline effects | Actual changes to people, objects, institutions, access and obligations |
| Information | Who witnessed it; who learned through what channel and when; errors and withheld facts |
| Dependencies | Which later events need these exact effects, versus independent plans that merely overlap |
| Disturbance | What intervention changes, invalidates, delays or leaves unaffected; label counterfactual inference |

Separate world truth, narrator access, reader foreknowledge, actor belief and
remembered history from a discarded branch. A later revelation can correct an
earlier belief without rewriting an earlier event. Do not give NPCs the research
ledger or make public logs disclose sealed truths.

## L3 Expand ideas through causes

For each source-grounded expansion provide a before/during/after starting
snapshot, one useful first action, an ordinary-life route, an intervention route,
an NPC response using actual means, and downstream consequences. Name the
specific current idea it extends. Preserve existing proposal IDs when possible.

Classify each departure as player-caused divergence, an explicitly selected
alternate premise, a remix with declared imported laws, or an original extension
into an unestablished gap. Unwritten future and prehistory are unknown source
territory, not implicit permission to label invention canon.

Source events remain eligible only while their causes hold. Similar outcomes
may recur through surviving independent causes. Fate or historical repair needs
world-specific evidence and a selected contract; genre expectations are not
evidence. Keep loops, historical starts, temporal travel and parallel instances
distinct under the timeline owner.

## L4 Acquisition and coverage

Reuse verified local texts first. Where a gap needs new material, acquire
accessible chapters, source excerpts, official guides or supplied files. Save
downloads read-only under the ignored research area with URL, retrieval time,
hash, edition, language, member count and missing ranges. Do not modify the
library. After one failed fetch switch routes; retain the failed receipt.
An inaccessible volume stays a gap rather than being filled from memory.

Audit corpus inventories for mixed continuities, indexes, referral stubs,
afterwords and incomplete translations. Preserve full reader receipts when
using an existing full read; report a targeted recheck as a targeted recheck.
Raw text, direct-recreation identities and changed/randomized secret truths stay
private. Public reporting uses world/event/route IDs and coverage status.

## L5 Completion gate

An audit closes only its frozen source/event range. Deliver a source manifest,
claim ledger, occurrence/revelation timeline, cause/dependency map, actor knowledge
ledger, version/contradiction register, source-connected expansion proposals and
remaining retrieval gaps. Every major causal claim needs a directly inspected
locator or an explicit lower-confidence label. Conflicting testimony remains
attributed unless evidence resolves it.

Before implementation acceptance, exercise baseline, successful disturbance,
failed attempt, delayed arrival and changed knowledge in an isolated playable
slice. Check that invalidated events do not fire and unaffected independent plans
can continue. Persistence work also needs save/reload/import, selected source
revision compatibility and spoiler-safe presentation. These are future gates;
this research pass does not claim gameplay verification.


## Content reconciliation: chronology-contract applicability, 2026-11-04 (B355)

L1–L5 remain useful for selected source/lore work: evidence quality, continuity, claim type and project acceptance are independent; occurrence, discovery, publication and actor knowledge must not be collapsed. The stable event/causal map records actual support and uncertainty rather than inventing exact dates or a world law from genre expectation. A causal expansion is a labeled proposal until its actual authority and implementation say otherwise. A linked source/reference remains unread unless its own content was inspected.

The opening acquisition scope and local research path describe October 11 work, not an instruction to collect books during this reconciliation. Current library/coverage/tool owners govern any future authorized acquisition; inaccessible material stays an explicit gap. Public franchise information and private altered/randomized truths follow current sealed-secret rules, while raw source/recreation material retains its private storage boundary. No new source corpus, private report or lore claim was inspected here.

L5 closes its frozen inspected range, not an entire work or every future world. Baseline/disturbance/delay/knowledge scenarios and persistence checks apply where a selected implementation actually changes those behaviors. They do not create a new all-world event engine, require every research pass to implement gameplay or supersede current priorities. Source manifests, locators and exact proof states must remain with their owners; no research-completion or runtime acceptance is newly asserted by this fold.
