### S22. Balance, eighth pass: enemy model wrong fields (L1698-1978)
- Early check: first tutorial fight was unwinnable (player STR 1, dummy 24; 99% of landed hits dealt zero; 63,000 swings to kill), whereas the unmodded game reads 15 to kill, 15 to die (L1705-1715). Higher tiers were mutual one-shots (L1717-1728).
- Cause: damage is subtractive both ways, defender STR is armour, so scaling enemy STR raised offence and armour together; scaling agility changed both hit rates (L1730-1744). The earlier "die 10-22" came from a proxy ratio that ignored the subtraction (L1746-1750). The tutorial exemption never worked (L1752-1755).
- New model: three separate fields, hp multiplier for time-to-kill, a damage multiplier in the damage wrapper for time-to-die, agility solved to a target hit rate; enemy STR left at base (L1759-1768). Nothing replicated: each spawn runs the game's real damage function in both directions and scales to the result; side effects (XP grants, crit flag, DOM) parked during the probe (L1796-1801). Raw damage treated as a shape: divide by a slow average, multiply by target, damped 0.6, zeros excluded (L1803-1810).
- Cost admitted: the player's defence stats no longer change incoming damage (L1812-1816).
- Why not analytic: ill-conditioned (2% replica error becomes 150% result) and the defence bracket goes negative (e.g. -12.3 at character level 92), so a level 59 golem with 57 STR hit for 190 million; cause is a shared "dummy" equipment object that all monsters and empty slots share, which also gives enemies a hidden ~2.1x attack by level 20 (L1773-1794). Same shared-object bug as audit section 6 (L1786-1793).
- Targets: kill 8 swings, die 20, hit rate 0.45, margin 1.7 (L1858); tutorial 8/22, W forest 5-12/18-27, arena IV 9/19, Vigil 4-9/18-28 (L1862-1872). First tutorial fight kill 12-13 vs die 14-15 (L1876-1877). Dials must be two-sided (flooring at 1 made a lv 3 dummy take 21 swings to kill while dying in 15) (L1835-1843). A margin floor stops band spread and creature identity compounding (L1845-1849). Arena bosses get their own factors (L1851-1854).
- Verification: 2,490 matchups (21 areas x every creature at both band ends x 10 tiers x 3 builds), all winnable (L1889-1892); later 31 areas and 4 builds = 3,720 (L1894-1895). Test-harness bug: probes were levelling the player while measuring (max HP moved 2.5x); fixed by parking XP grants (L1909-1916). Kill-side estimator replaced: stratify on crit roll (normal swing varies 0.9-1.1x, crit about 8x), recombine with the known crit rate; samples 64 to 32; 12 clean runs of 2,490 (L1933-1946). Damage heavy-tailed: at cap 110 crit rate 33%, crit multiplier about 8x, median swing 159M vs mean 479M, crits about three quarters of damage (L1964-1970). End-to-end smoke test of nine real fights per area (L1954-1972). Obsolete proxy scripts deleted (L1974-1978).
- YAIRPG: NOT-RELEVANT mostly (YAIRPG combat is data-driven, groups, no shared dummy). The testing philosophy (measure real functions, stratify crits, matrix test every area x tier) is ADAPT for YAIRPG's own balance checks.

- Original catalog: 120 titles across 93 skills; 73 of those come from story events, so most skills grant none (L1992-1994 area, the "too few" text at L1989-1990 reads: 120 titles across 93 skills, 73 from story events). Four titles in the whole game carry a `talent` (a real bonus) (L1991).

- Rank in the original (`rar`) ran 0-5 and was assigned by feel; rank 3 titles were being given out at skill level 8 (Runner at Walking 10, Rookie at Fighting 15, Dissector at Disassembly 8; eight early ones) (L1984-1988).

### How titles are earned
- Each skill milestone grants its title; the grant is folded into the milestone already at that level, since two milestones at one level would be a duplicate the save cannot tell apart by index, and the audit script rejects it (L2013-2016).
- For the 47 titles a skill milestone grants, the rank level is read by scanning the milestone function's text for a `giveTitle` call so it stays correct if the grant moves (L2001-2003). The other 73 are story-event titles with no level; the author's 0-5 stretched over free ranks 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 (L2003-2005). Nothing story-granted reaches rank 9 or 10 (L2005-2006).
- Flagship skills also get appended deep tiers at lv 55/60/70/75 ending in a title; levels must sit above the existing top milestone (L618-638).
- Titles are held (counted) and can be worn; a Renown level also confers them passively (see below).

### Ranks
- Ten ranks derived from skill level: rank 1 at level 1, 2 at 8, 3 at 18, 4 at 30, 5 at 45, 6 at 58, 7 at 70, 8 at 82, 9 at 90, 10 at 110 (L1997-1999). Rank 9 means level 90 and rank 10 means level 110 and nothing else (L2005-2006).
- The rank also underlies cultivation realms: realm thresholds use the same level thresholds as title ranks (L145-146), and the rank ladder of duels floors the Power rank (L168-169).
- The five grant levels (25/50/75/90/110) map to ranks 3/5/7/9/10 (L2010-2011) although rank 3 starts at level 18 and rank 5 at 45, which means a grant at 25 is a rank 3 title: that is, a title's rank is the highest rank threshold at or below the level that grants it (INFERRED from the two tables).

### Bonuses and sizes
- Original: four titles only carry a talent; rest flavour (L1991-1994).
- Mod title effect: each title raises its own skill's exp rate, 5% at rank 3 up to 30% at rank 10 (L2025-2026). Only 5 and 30 are stated; the intermediate values (ranks 5, 7, 9) are UNKNOWN in this range.
- Applied via the skill's exp-rate multiplier field, not from milestone code, because that field is restored from the save after milestones fire and the write would be lost; so the tick reconciles it against a record in saved flags (L2029-2034). Test checks no stacking over 30 ticks and across a reload (L2033-2034).
- Wearing: only the worn title applies by default, giving the picker a purpose (L2039-2041). Renown level N makes every title of rank N or lower apply permanently, worn or not; the worn title always applies whatever its rank; renown 10 makes the whole collection passive (L2042-2045).
- Earlier flat bonuses attached to the flagship milestones, e.g. +22 stat; Executioner etc. are unlocks at lv 55-75 (L618-630).

### Renown
- A skill levelled by titles held, not XP; capped at level 10; exempt from the story level cap and from the XP path (L644-645, L659-662).
- Original ladder (123-title pool): titles needed 1, 3, 5, 8, 13, 20, 31, 48, 74, 100 for levels 1-10 (L647-649). Verified stepping exactly (L666-667). Rescaled to the 585 pool: level 10 now wants 550 titles (L2045-2047). The intermediate levels for the new pool: UNKNOWN in range.
- Bonus: first +1.5% to all four stats per level, +15% at maximum (L654-655); then changed to compound multiplicative x1.05 per level on every stat: x1.05 / x1.10 at 1 / 2, x1.28 at 5, x1.63 at 10 (L672-680). Applies last among permanent effects (L681-682). Verified 113 to 184 STR, ratio 1.628 vs expected 1.629 (L683-684).
- Balance: with all 100 titles it mostly buys survivability (Long Vigil die in 27 becomes 42) (L686-687). At max Renown margin worth about 50% (L1310-1311). Combat tables "with Renown 10" show kill/die improving, e.g. 4/16 to 8/16 at cap 20/110 etc. (L1566-1572).
- Described later as "load-bearing" rather than decorative (L2046-2047). (Quote is the mod's; our project bans that phrase in our own prose.)
- Design intent: a generalist reward for broad play rather than deep grinding (L655-656).

### UI: button, picker, grouping
- A Renown button next to "Recheck clears" in the skill panel prints the ladder and progress, how many titles you hold and which are missing; the full list of 123 names goes to the console because they would not fit the message log (L689-694). Console commands `modTitles()` and `modRenown()` exist (L47, L694).
- Summary: the picker and the wiki group titles by skill and into ladders (L164-165). Detail of the picker layout: UNKNOWN in range (a later section likely covers it).

### Save handling
- New ids 201-215 chosen above the game's highest so saves by id stay safe (L605-606). Granted-title flags rely on milestone array index; edits to milestones keep index order; levels appended above the top; reordering would misalign saved flags (L612-616, L632-638).
- Title count read from the mod's own `ttl` catalog, not the game's `global.titles.length`, because load rebuilds that array and then appends a second list (`titlese`), which can double-count (L663-665). This is the "list doubling on load" bug; the effect is only stated as "can double-count" (SHOWN) not measured.
- The title bonus: reconcile on tick against a record in saved flags (L2029-2034).
- Merging skills: dropped two flagship titles from the earnable set but kept them defined (L1352-1354).
- Save size: 19.3 KB with everything maxed at 123 titles (L669-670); 585-title save size UNKNOWN in range.

### Balance numbers (titles)
- Skill-level thresholds above; 5% to 30% own-skill exp; Renown x1.05 per level; ladder 1,3,5,8,13,20,31,48,74,100 for the 123 pool, 550 at level 10 for the 585 pool.
- Estimated reach: INFERRED that wearing one title with 5-30% exp on a single skill is small, so the collection (Renown) is where power sits.

### Takeaways for O1 (proposals, not decisions)
- T1. A counter-driven catalog (our O1) can be data: id, trigger (counter, threshold), rank. The mod's formulaic names show a cheap path to wide coverage (L2018-2022).
- T2. Derive rank from the trigger value, not by feel; the original's hand-set rank gave rank 3 titles for level 8 (L1984-1988).
- T3. Count titles from your own catalog and dedupe, never from the saved list length (L663-665).
- T4. Keep granted state as ids (or index-stable); never reorder; append only (L612-616, L632-638).
- T5. If titles give a bonus, reconcile applied amount against a saved record each tick instead of mutating a saved stat (L2029-2034). YAIRPG note: the base fork's existing field is an object (`src/main.js:4392`), so a bonus can be computed from it with no mutation at all (INFERRED).
- T6. Do not give titles a power curve before the player has a reason to want them: the original's titles were flavour only (L1991-1994).
- T7. The worn-title versus passive-collection split (worn applies fully; Renown makes lower ranks passive) gives the picker a job without making it mandatory (L2039-2047).
