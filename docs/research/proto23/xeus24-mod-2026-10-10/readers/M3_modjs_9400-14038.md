# M3: Xeus24 mod, mod.js lines 9400-14038

Coverage: lines 9400-14038 (end of file) read in full with the Read tool, in
chunks of 400-450 lines. Source: mod.js (PR 3, head b27d07a, v4.7), unlicensed;
this note paraphrases and quotes no code beyond identifiers. Labels: SHOWN =
stated in the lines cited; INFERRED = my reading of how the code behaves;
UNKNOWN = defined outside this range or not visible. Two kinds of outside
reference appear. Facts about the original game come from a grep of
`index.html` (cited as `index.html:N`, SHOWN there). Three mod constants at
lines 4180-5790 were seen only as grep hits, not read (marked "grep only").

Range structure: it opens mid-way through section 34 (rank ladder, wiki page
only) and runs through sections 35-51 plus an unnumbered "settings inside the
save" block and a final "LAST" line. Everything is patched in at runtime on
top of the original globals. The common patch technique is capture-and-wrap:
store the old function in a `MOD_..._before_x` variable, reassign the global,
call the old one first, then do the mod's work inside a try/catch so a failure
never breaks a tick, load or draw. Wrapped globals in this range: `ontick`
(many layers), `allbuff`, `load`, `save`, `area_init`, `dscr`, `formatw`,
`update_db`, `update_m`, `printDamageNumber`, `m_update`, `dom.d6.update`, the
three bar `.update` methods, `chss.lsmain1.sl`, `chss.t3.sl`, `chss.mbrd.sl`,
`act.mod_qi.use`, `you.onDeath`, `MOD_renderSlots`, and the internal
`MOD_dmg_calc_original`. One function is replaced outright: `MOD_breakthrough`
(11139).

## 1. Systems

### S34 (tail). Rank ladder wiki page (9414-9509)
- SHOWN: the wiki page is built from `MOD_RANK_LADDER` and `you.rank()`, not
  from copied numbers (9414-9417). The displayed Power rank is the better of
  the stat-derived rank and a "held" rank written by winning ladder duels
  (9473-9475); a held rank shows in gold (9475, 9482). Rank is derived on
  display, never stored (9441). Held rank cannot be lost (9495-9497).
- SHOWN formula as the page prints it: a ceiling of a big constant times a
  root of the stat sum, divided by the squared stat total; rank 1 is the floor
  (9442-9446). Measured rank by skill level, full builds: 90 -> 18, 95 -> 11,
  100 -> 7, 105 -> 4, 108 -> 3, 110 -> 1 (9451-9461).
- SHOWN: ten challengers in the Hall of the First Gate. Rank N opens only
  after rank N+1 is taken, and each needs a character level (9463-9467). Each
  duel is a protected single-enemy area scaled to your power at entry; rungs
  step up in length, not incoming damage (9468-9472). The table columns are
  rank, challenger, needed level, their level, a step multiplier and a
  payout in exp and coin (9477-9492). The definition table and
  `MOD_rankReward` are UNKNOWN (before line 9400).
- Patch: page is spliced after "Progression" by popping and re-inserting into
  `MOD_WIKI_PAGES` (9501-9509).
- Relevance: NOT-RELEVANT for YAIRPG as built (no Power rank). The idea of a
  "held rank" that floors a derived number is ADAPT at best; skip.

### S35. Crafting ladder, 2 to 5 stars (9512-10184)
- SHOWN audit of the original: 62 recipes; by output rarity 44 / 17 / 1 / 1 /
  0 for 1-5 stars, of which 35 / 7 / 0 / 1 / 0 are obtainable (9519-9524).
  Nineteen recipes have no `giveRcp` anywhere (9532-9538). The mod reports
  rather than wires them (9538).
- SHOWN constraints worth noting as design lessons: item ids are grouped by
  namespace and restore wrongly if in the wrong block (9554-9559); `rar`
  cannot exceed 6 because a colour table has seven entries (9560-9563); armour
  `str` barely affects damage (9564-9569).
- Gather tuning (9577-9580): 2% chance per tick at skill 0, +0.12% per level
  summed over Mining, Geology and Foraging (9934), cap 14%. Material sell
  anchors 6 / 18 / 54 / 162 coin for tiers 2-5 (9593), chosen so gathering at
  cap earns roughly six coin a second, better than combat's one coin a second
  but without loot or character exp (9585-9592).
- Four tiers (9599-9620): Ironwrought 2 star at "The Stone Hollow" (after the
  deep forest, flag `mod_t_deep`); Bluesteel 3 star at "The Iron Seam"
  (catacombs flag `mod_t_cata`); Skyiron 4 star at "Ashfall Quarry" (golem
  arena IV flag `trne4e1`); Starfall 5 star at "The Deep Vein" (The Long Vigil
  progress at or over `MOD_REQ_VIGIL`). Gates are read live, so an old save
  that already cleared the content opens the node on load (9597-9598).
- Twelve materials (9635-9649): ore, weave, essence lines at each tier, ids
  5200-5211, stype 5 (cheap pricing class, 9659-9664).
- Gear per tier (9676-9771): weapon (all swords), body armour, shield,
  accessory, tonic. Stat table (9676-9682): weapon str 34 / 72 / 124 / 205;
  weapon durability 45 / 65 / 95 / 140; armour str 14 / 30 / 52 / 86;
  shield str 10 / 22 / 40 / 68; accessory +3 / +7 / +14 / +26 on agl and int
  with spd about a third of that; tonic heals 200 / 800 / 3000 / 12000.
  Roughly times 1.7 per rung; the 4 star sword sits beside the base game's
  best and the 5 star sword clearly above it (9669-9674). Affinity and class
  arrays are a gentle linear ramp (9688-9729). Accessories use plain stat
  fields, not equip/unequip hooks, so there is nothing to unwind on load
  (9734-9736). New ids: items 9200+, weapons 10101+, armour 20101+, shields
  30101+, accessories 40101+ (9556-9559); recipes 201-220 (9803).
- Recipes (9779-9817): each rung consumes the previous rung's matching piece
  (except tonics) plus its own materials; main-material count is 4, 5, 6, 7 by
  rung (9785). Output 1 piece, or 2 tonics. Crafting skill exp 1.2 for
  weapons, 1.0 armour and shield, 0.8 accessory and tonic, via `onmake`
  (9788-9813). Recipes are taught by `MOD_learnTier` the first time a rung's
  material is found (9819-9829, 9978-9979); the base `giveRcp` refuses
  duplicates so no save flag is needed (9975-9977).
- Location: "The Diggings" screen id 981 reached from the Village Center
  through a wrapped `chss.lsmain1.sl`, gated on `mod_t_deep` (9840-9915); node
  screens 982-985 (9878-9898). These set no tier flag, because an added area
  that sets one would hand out a level cap on arrival (9836-9838).
- Action "Gather" id 910 (9938-9989): grants 0.5 exp per second, 0.8 / 0.6 /
  0.4 skill exp to Mining / Geology / Foraging per tick, costs +0.06 per
  second energy use, one material of three picked uniformly per hit. The
  action is granted on the tick when you stand at a node, not via a milestone
  (9991-10002).
- Value model: re-indexes `MOD_VAL.madeBy` for the new recipes and sets
  anchors for materials, then clears the price cache (10033-10057). Reason
  (SHOWN): without it a 5 star sword priced at 1,161 coin while its ingots
  priced at 13,000 each (10026-10031).
- Save fields: none new of its own. Items and recipes are saved by the base
  game's own id lists (INFERRED from 9554-9559 and 10346-10351).
- Relevance: ADAPT. YAIRPG already has component and quality crafting
  (`src/crafting_recipes.js`, `src/items.js`) and a saved
  `game_stats.gathered_materials` (`src/main.js:1249`), so the ladder itself is
  ALREADY-HAVE. The reusable ideas are the "previous rung is an ingredient"
  upgrade chain, gathering skill chance formula, and anchoring raw material
  price against the actual income rate rather than deriving it from rarity.

### S36. Raw Meat and the Hunter's quest (10187-10339)
- SHOWN: the mod's longer kills cut meat income tenfold while the meat rots,
  so a "hold ten at once" quest became unfinishable in summer. Rot is per
  game day: `item.rwmt1.rot` as four numbers (10207), gain per event divided
  by a season modifier 0.5 summer, 2.5 winter (10209-10212). Steady state
  stock formula: (gain per day times days per rot cycle minus one) divided by
  mean loss fraction (10214-10220, 10268-10277). Worked numbers: normal 15.6,
  summer 4.3 (fails), winter 51 (10228-10231).
- Fix: raise Raw Meat drop to 18% for every creature that drops it, solved
  backwards for a steady state of 25 in the worst season (10239-10250,
  10253-10257). Applied by scanning all drop tables, idempotent (10285-10303).
  Later made conditional on the Fights setting (13574-13581).
- Relevance: NOT-RELEVANT as content. INFERRED lesson: if YAIRPG ever pairs
  perishable goods with a "have N at once" quest, check the worst-season
  steady state. Note the mod's own admission that the bug was its own (10233-10235).

### S38. The cultivator's road (10473-11387)
Constants block `MOD_CULT` (10524-10573). Sourced from a public wuxia glossary
(10480-10484).
- Spiritual Root (10599-10652): five grades with roll weights 40 / 28 / 18 /
  10 / 4, cultivation speed 0.85 / 1.00 / 1.15 / 1.35 / 1.70, breakthrough
  odds -5 / 0 / +3 / +7 / +12 points (10600-10604). Rolled once, flag
  `mod_root` (10618-10628), when the Circulate Qi action exists (11290). Speed
  effect is reconciled every tick against stored `mod_rootxp` by multiplying
  `skl.qic.p` by want over have, because `p` is saved and restored after
  milestones fire (10637-10652). Never a dead end: a bad root costs 15% speed
  (10592-10596).
- Layers (10655-10723): nine per realm, derived from Qi Circulation level
  between this realm's threshold and the next (10688-10692), no saved field.
  Stats +2% per layer above the first (x1.16 at peak), body +3% (x1.24)
  (10528-10529, 10715-10723). Stage words early / middle / late / peak
  (10695-10700); "Half a step to X" at a bottleneck (10703-10713).
- Insight (10726-10791): a plain count in flag `mod_insight`. Need for a
  realm is the max over all lower realms of two numbers: floor 1 + 2 per
  realm, or climb seconds times 0.05 times 0.004 (10758-10766, 10531-10543).
  Sources: 0.4% per second of Circulate Qi but only while at a wall
  (10795-10812); 25% chance on a kill made under 20% health, via the game's
  `callback.onDeath` hook id 9380 (10816-10833); +1 the first time you enter
  an area, tracked in the `mod_seen` map (10838-10856).
- Closed Door Training (10859-10938): action id 905, exp 0.3 per second,
  Qi Circulation 1.1, Meditation and Patience 0.5 each, counter `mod_seclu`,
  1.2% insight chance per second (10876-10909). Consolidation = seconds sat
  over a full amount, with full = max(900, insight need divided by 0.012)
  (10773-10776, 10919-10923). Gives up to +30 points of breakthrough odds and
  shelters you from Qi Deviation if you attempt from inside it (10859-10869,
  11170-11174). Unlocked on the tick at the first wall (10930-10938).
- Qi Deviation (10941-10997): flag `mod_qidev` holds an end time on the saved
  `time.minute` clock, so it survives reloads (10956-10959, 10978). Duration
  max(720, full seclusion) in-game minutes. While active stats x0.55, body
  x0.70, no new attempt (10556-10561, 10893, 11154). Sets HP to 1; never kills
  (10952-10954, 10983).
- Heavenly Tribulation (11000-11051): from realm 8, a fraction of max HP is
  spent in a number of bolts: 55% then +15% per realm (55 / 70 / 85), bolts 3,
  5, 7 (10568-10572, 11020-11026). Failing mid-way sets HP to 1 and starts a
  deviation (11039-11046).
- Pills (11077-11125): Root Cleansing (rar 5, value 26,000, Herbalist price
  31,000, 18% stock chance, 1 max) raises root one grade; Qi Settling (rar 3,
  value 4,200, Herbalist 5,200, 50% chance, 1-2) ends deviation at once.
- Rebuilt attempt (11139-11217): odds = min(0.55 + 0.05 per level over the
  requirement + consolidation x 0.30 + root bonus, 0.95) (11179-11182).
  Insight is spent win or lose; the seclusion counter resets; failing
  sheltered costs the pill, failing in the open also gives deviation
  (11189-11196). On success it sets the realm flag, recomputes stats, prints
  a realm line and a random flavour line, calls `giveTitle` for a realm title
  and `MOD_updateRenown` (11205-11216; see TITLES).
- History: last 20 attempts in `global.flags.mod_bthist`, each with time,
  target realm, odds and outcome (11227-11245).
- Wrappers: `allbuff` multiplies str/int/agl/spd by the layer and deviation
  factors and publishes body multipliers through `MOD_BODY` (11256-11280);
  `ontick` rolls the root, applies its bonus, clears expired deviation
  (11287-11302).
- Save fields added (all in `global.flags`, SHOWN): `mod_root`, `mod_rootxp`,
  `mod_insight`, `mod_seen`, `mod_seclu`, `mod_qidev`, `mod_bthist`. Old saves
  simply lack them and every reader defaults (10613-10616, 10741-10744,
  10961-10966, 11238-11240).
- Relevance: ADAPT. YAIRPG has cultivation content
  (`src/feature_expansion_book_cultivation.js`, `src/body_progression.js`), so
  the realm ladder exists in some form. Useful ideas: bottleneck needs an
  earned non-purchasable resource (insight) plus a purchasable one (pill);
  a "shelter" choice that trades time for safety; effect timers keyed to the
  saved game clock so they survive reloads; reconcile-not-set for saved
  multipliers. UNKNOWN whether YAIRPG already has any equivalent, I did not
  read those files.

### S39. Skill rarity and convergence (11390-12109)
- Rarity (11482-11529): a skill's rarity is the rank of its level via
  `MOD_rankForLevel`, the same ladder that ranks titles (11405-11409). Ten
  names Novice, Common, Practised, Capable, Skilled, Expert, Masterful,
  Renowned, Peerless, Transcendent (11486-11487). Colour set on the row's name
  element style, which survives the game's per-second innerHTML rewrite; a
  `data-mod-grade` attribute plus CSS `::after` adds a non-colour marker
  (11500-11517, 14023). Repainted each tick (11523-11529).
- Converged skills (11532-11590): ten aggregate skills, ids 2201-2210, one per
  skill section (Warfare, Constitution, Pathfinding, Livelihood, Workmanship,
  Fortitude, Attunement, Provision, Maintenance, Fellowship; 11553-11564).
  Created unconditionally at load at the end of `skl`, because the save writes
  skill exp positionally; conditional creation would shift every later slot
  (11533-11544). No milestones, so no titles come from them (11544-11547).
- Folding does not remove a skill; it marks it in `global.flags.mod_folded`
  (a key map) and the row is hidden, because shortening `you.skls` breaks
  panel rebuilds, re-adds the skill on next level-up, and breaks the save
  (11433-11456). A folded skill keeps level, exp, perks and buffs.
- Jade Slip (11686-11698): item rar 4, value 225. Price at the Slip Archive =
  900 x 1.055 to the power of slips used, capped at 4,000,000 (11466-11468,
  11705-11708); slips used in `mod_slipsused`. One slip folds one skill, lowest
  level first, as many as slips allow (11741-11783). Unfold is free, slips not
  refunded (11785-11806).
- Convergence bonus (11470-11479, 11670-11681): each full section is x1.03 on
  all four stats; a partial section gives half of that, scaled by fraction
  folded; applied in an `allbuff` wrapper (11898-11908), never written into
  base fields. The converged skill's displayed level is the best folded skill
  in its section; its exp bar shows folded over total (11640-11662).
- UI: "Slip Archive" location id 974, drawn from a wrapped dojo lobby screen
  `chss.t3.sl` behind the same flags as Level Advancement (11820-11893). Wiki
  "Skill handbook" page (11986-12093).
- Save fields: `mod_folded`, `mod_slipsused`; plus ten extra skill slots
  (exp and multiplier) at the end of the skill list. Old saves: the extra
  slots are absent in an old save and the base loader's positional read of
  those missing entries is UNKNOWN (the load code is outside this range).
- Relevance: ADOPT-IDEA (display only). The skill list is long in YAIRPG too
  (about 69 skills). A rarity word and colour derived from level, plus a
  "hide folded" list, are cheap. The stat bonus for folding is not needed.
  The positional-save hazard does not apply to YAIRPG if skills are saved by
  key (UNKNOWN; not checked).

### S46. Three combat and death bug fixes (13380-13540)
- Death energy: original multiplies energy by 0.55 x (1 minus Death skill
  use), which makes the skill harm you and goes negative past level 10
  (13388-13397). Mod replacement: keep fraction = 1 minus 0.45 over (1 + use);
  55% at 0, 78% at 10, 96% at 110 (13399-13402, 13446-13449). Detects real
  death by `global.stat.deadt` rising (13403-13404, 13516-13520).
- Shield Mastery: defence bracket goes negative at high levels so defence adds
  damage; fix floors the bracket at zero by temporarily setting the skill
  level for one call (13404-13420, 13464-13477, 13497-13499).
- Luck: a block-scoped variable meant luck never reached the crit roll. Mod
  applies luck exactly but caps its crit contribution at 50% (13422-13434,
  13444, 13451-13462). Temporarily adds to `you.mods.crflt` and restores the
  exact prior value afterwards to avoid float residue in a saved field
  (13440-13442, 13481-13508).
- Relevance: NOT-RELEVANT (original bugs). Lesson only: temporary override
  then restore in a `finally` block.

  `p23_mod_lastdownload` (13741).
- Messages are delayed until after window load because `msg` prints nothing
  while the game is loading (13720-13722, 13778-13782).
- Relevance: ADOPT-IDEA for the Safari export reminder. Weibao uses macOS;
  YAIRPG's packaging target and browsers UNKNOWN to me. Everything else
  NOT-RELEVANT.

### S49. Catch-up for a hidden tab (13785-13845)
- SHOWN: the original loop is a chained `setTimeout`; hidden tabs are
  throttled to once per second and after five minutes to once per minute, so
  about 98% of away time is lost (13788-13791).
- Mechanism: wraps `ontick`. On each call it computes missed ticks as
  floor of elapsed milliseconds times `global.fps` over 1000, minus one for the
  current call; if more than 2 are missed it clamps to the limit and replays
  the previous `ontick` chain in a loop, also calling `use()` on the running
  action each tick, except when a fight was active at the start (combat is
  never replayed) (13815-13845). A `running` flag prevents recursion. Message
  "While the tab was in the background, N minutes passed." (13837-13840).
- Limit: default 3600 game seconds at the current speed, `setCatchUp(sec)`
  caps at 86400, 0 turns it off; stored in `p23_mod_catchup` and in the save
  settings block (13794-13813).
- INFERRED: the dishes check (S50) is installed after this wrapper (13888) so
  it runs once, not per replayed tick; wrappers installed before this one
  (HUD, small notes, convergence) run for every replayed tick.
- Relevance: ADAPT. `src/backlog_expansion_time.js:82` has a wall-clock
  catch-up that refuses a backwards clock, so some offline credit already
  exists; my grep for `visibilitychange` or `document.hidden` found no
  hidden-tab replay. Whether YAIRPG's own loop suffers throttling is UNKNOWN.
  The "never replay combat" rule is the part worth copying.

### S50. Dishes nothing taught (13847-13893)
- SHOWN: 15 cooking recipes (Bacon and Eggs through Mushroom Soup) have no
  `giveRcp`. The mod scans the page source with a regex for any `giveRcp`
  mention, sorts the orphans by the cooking exp they grant (the author's own
  complexity measure), spreads them across Cooking levels 6-44, and teaches
  each when the level is reached, checked when the level changes
  (13859-13893). Marks them as mod-taught for the wiki's reachability count
  (13873).
- Relevance: NOT-RELEVANT. Technique note: reading its own page source at
  runtime is fragile, and YAIRPG does not need it.

### S51. Shared creature gear (13895-13940)
- SHOWN: all creatures start with the same dummy equipment object, so
  per-creature affinity writes overwrote one shared object; the player's fists
  are that object and load writes the player level into it (13898-13911).
  Mod gives each creature its own copy and re-reads the author's intended values
  from the page source with a regex (13916-13940).
- Relevance: NOT-RELEVANT, but it confirms audit item section 6 ("creatures all
  share eqp.dummy").

### Settings inside the save (13942-14009)
- SHOWN: on every save the mod writes a `mod_settings` snapshot into
  `global.flags` (13989-13993) with pacing, fights, number format, speed, xp,
  coin, free actions, rarity filter and catch-up (13951-13955). On load, once
  per browser (guarded by `p23_mod_settings_seen`), it adopts settings only
  for keys this browser has not set; pacing goes first because a preset sets
  several boxes (13959-14009). Purpose: a save moved to a new browser keeps its
  display and pacing settings (13668-13671).
- Slot-switching detail (SHOWN 13999-14001): later loads must not apply an
  old slot's snapshot, because adoption writes keys that every other slot
  would inherit.
- Relevance: ADAPT. Settings-in-save is a small, safe pattern. YAIRPG has
  validated envelopes (`src/save_files.js`, `src/save_migration.js`), so any
  such field would need a migration entry per AGENTS.md. Probably a
  display-settings subobject, not gameplay flags.

### Closing items
- Number-input CSS: hides webkit spinners and adds the `data-mod-grade` marker
  rule (14015-14026).
- Last line: re-installs the skill XP curve on every skill, because skills
  created after section 19 (the converged skills and masteries) would

## 2. TITLES for O1

What this range contains about titles. The title definitions, generator,
picker and Renown live before line 9400 (another reader). I list only what
this range shows, plus original-game facts I confirmed in `index.html`.

### 2.1 Finishing the author's unfinished titles (12469-12579)
SHOWN in range:
- The mod found five unfinished titles; four were completed (12471).
- Shopper ladder: `shpt1` "Third-Rate Shopper" is the first rung. The mod
  names `shpt2` "Second-Rate Shopper" (desc: five thousand purchases, 12554)
  and `shpt3` "First-Rate Shopper" (ten thousand things bought, 12557). Joke
  source: the three-tier "third / second / first rate" grading of fighters
  (12476-12477).
- Thresholds: counter `global.stat.buyt` at 5,000 and 10,000 (12561, 12564).
- Money ladder: `mone2` already named "Peasant" and blank; the mod sets only
  its description (ten gold coins passed through your hands, 12567-12568) and
  names `mone3` "Merchant" (a hundred gold, 12569-12571). Counter
  `global.stat.moneyg` at 10 and 100 times `GOLD` (12573, 12576). The first
  rung "Beggar" is at 1 gold (index.html:7277, SHOWN there). The author had
  commented the checks out at 1 gold, a placeholder; the mod chose 10 and 100
  because "his other ladders step by five to ten" (12478-12482). That step
  choice is the mod's guess, not the author's.
- Left alone: `ttl.ddcd`, name and description are the literal string "null",
  nothing grants it, mod refuses to guess (12484-12489). It is not the missing
  first rung of the stay-at-home ladder, because `ttl.neet` exists
  (12485-12487).
- Mechanism: the base game has per-topic check arrays (`global.shptchk`,
  `global.monchk`) whose functions are run from event sites; the mod just
  pushes new check functions onto them (12560-12576). Guards read `have ===
  false`, then call `giveTitle`.
- Everything is wrapped in try/catch so a missing title logs a warning rather
  than breaking load (12578).

### 2.2 How the original grants titles (index.html, SHOWN there, not in range)
- `giveTitle` (index.html:13760-13766): if not held, pushes into
  `global.titles` (and `global.titlese` when id is not 0), sets it as the
  worn title (`you.title`), sets `have`, runs a one-time `talent()` if present,
  runs `onGet`, then runs every function in `global.ttlschk`, and prints "New
  Title Earned!" unless an `lv` argument suppresses it.
- Meta titles `ttsttl1/2/3` at 10 / 25 / 50 titles held (index.html:7283-7285).
- Counter ladders confirmed there: kills 50,000 / 200,000 / 1,000,000 /
  5,000,000 (`kill2`-`kill5`, index.html:7270-7273); cat pats `cpet1` at 9,999
  (index.html:7293); hunter challenges 100 / 250 / 500 (7296-7298); time at home
  `neet` 1 year, `neet2` 5 years, `neet3` 10 years (index.html:7301-7303),
  fed by `global.stat.athmec` (index.html:8255, 8267).
- Check call sites: `monchk` run at index.html:10903; `shptchk` run at 11327
  with the author's note "put it here for now". Shopper and money checks
  therefore fire only at the moment of a purchase or a coin gain, not on a
  timer (INFERRED from those two call sites).
- Original title rarity field `rar` and `rars` flag: shown on the Title
  objects (e.g. index.html:695, 699). `shpt2/3` set rar 2 and 3.

### 2.3 Other title touchpoints in this range
- Realm titles: on a successful breakthrough the code grants
  `ttl['mod_realm' + realm]` if it exists, then calls `MOD_updateRenown()`
  (11214-11215). One title per realm number 1 up to the top realm; the title
  text and rarity are UNKNOWN here.
- Rank-of-level scale shared with titles: skill rarity names use
  `MOD_rankForLevel` and `MOD_RANK_AT`, "the same that ranks titles"
  (11405-11409, 11949-11951, 12004-12005). The ten rank colours are the same
  as the title colours (11407). Rank-at-level thresholds seen in a grep line only
  (not read): lines 5706-5709 give ten thresholds starting 1, 8, 18, 30, 45,
  58, 70, 82, 90, 110.
- Skill-title generator (grep only, 5764-5817): rungs at skill levels 25 / 50
  / 75 / 90 / 110 with title exp bonuses 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 30%. In this
  range that is confirmed only by 11545-11547 ("section 24's title generator
  skips anything without milestones") and 10641 ("section 24 learned that with
  title exp").
- Lesson on static rarity: the mod notes the base game "handed out a rank 3
  title at skill level 8", so it derives rank from level instead of hand-
  assigning (11410-11414).
- Death-linked title: a title named `ndthextr` exists and is tied to the death
  count (10954-10955); its trigger and level are UNKNOWN.
- Title exp is a saved skill multiplier: `skl.qic.p` is saved and restored
  after milestones fire, so title-granted exp cannot be set once and forgotten
  (10637-10641). Direct design consequence for O1 if titles ever give bonuses:
  derive the bonus at read time from held titles; do not write it into a saved
  multiplier.
- Converged skills deliberately add no titles because they have no milestones
  (11545-11547).

### 2.4 Titles and the save
- SHOWN: nothing in this range adds a save field for titles. Held titles are
  saved by the base game's own list (INFERRED: the mod only calls `giveTitle`
  and checks `have`). A title object keeps `have` as a boolean (12561).
- Mod title definitions are created at load with fixed ids (grep only: ids
  start at 3000 for generated titles, 5787). Old saves lack them and the base
  loader is expected to restore by id; the exact behaviour is UNKNOWN to me
  (load code outside range).

### 2.5 Takeaways for O1 (proposals, not decisions)
- T1: A title is a data record: id, name, description, rarity tier, one
  counter key and one threshold. All counters in the examples (`buyt`,
  `moneyg`, `athmec`, `akills`, `cat_c`) are plain running totals, matching
  YAIRPG's `game_stats` style (`src/main.js:1249`).
- T2: Ladders step by large factors: shopper 500 / 5,000 / 10,000 (index.html:
  7288 and 12561-12564), money 1 / 10 / 100 gold, kills 50k / 200k / 1M / 5M,
  home 1 / 5 / 10 years. Tier names can carry the joke (third-rate, second-
  rate, first-rate).
- T3: Grant on the event that moves the counter, not on a poll (call sites at
  index.html:10903, 11327). Grants must be idempotent (`have` guard).
- T4: A grant message with a suppress flag for silent back-fill on load
  (index.html:13766 `lv` argument). Useful for O1 when an existing save already
  exceeds a threshold.
- T5: A meta-title for "titles held" at 10 / 25 / 50 (index.html:7283-7285) is
  cheap and fits a catalog.
- T6: Titles for deathless levels, mentioned as an O1 candidate in the audit,
  have only a hint here (`ndthextr`, 10954); no formula in range.
- T7: Do not store title bonuses as saved multipliers (10637-10641).
- T8: The task says "picker" and "renown": neither is implemented in this
  range. The only picker-like thing here is the original's `you.title`
  assignment on grant (index.html:13762). Renown is only called by name at
  11215.
