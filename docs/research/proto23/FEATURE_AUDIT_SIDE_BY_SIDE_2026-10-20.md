# Feature audit and side-by-side: Corc / Proto23 and OOPS

2026-10-20. Round 3 of the [design-lesson audit](DESIGN_LESSONS_FOR_OOPS_2026-10-20.md),
requested by Weibao: “Feature audit further, looking at each little thing, and
then do a side by side.” **Research complete for the bounded pass below;
incorporation remains proposed.** This changes no gameplay or accepted default.

The useful gain is often a connection or a small explanation. OOPS already has
many individual ingredients. A break after an exchange differs from a break after
a battle; a related-recipe link differs from a global ingredient search; a new-row
badge differs from recent XP on an existing skill. Those distinctions are the
point of this comparison.

## Scope and evidence

Authority remains [USER_VISION](../../USER_VISION.md), [PROJECT](../../../PROJECT.md)
and the [rules index](../../RULES_INDEX.md). Applies AD6–AD8, AD17, AD22,
AD32–AD36, AD38, AD40; UR35/38/43/45/48/49/52/56/58; MR1/MR5/MR21–MR23/MR28–MR31;
SR5/SR9/SR21. Protective safety stops retain the accepted domain exception:
they can inspect real consequences without revealing hidden causes. They are
not equivalent to player-configured routines predicting hidden exact expiry.

Future visible UI outcome: at **1920×1080**, the player can identify the subject,
understand the relevant comparison, act and return without losing their place.
Actual 14px/21px text, the other current desktop sizes and reduced motion follow
[UI_RULES](../../UI_RULES.md). Mobile optimization remains deferred. First
milestone remains one realm and one prestige layer; the broader complexity and
many-world direction stand. NPC-heavy simulation and generated layouts remain
deferred. No reference feature silently changes those priorities.

This is an atomic inventory of **the reviewed reference evidence**, not every
feature in either game. Round 1 read all 308 indexed captions and the 623 unique
actual messages in 17 saved context windows. Earlier rounds reviewed selected
larger images and sampled ten animation sequences. This round enlarged the first
available representation for **90 additional indexed records** and revisited
**17 ambiguous records**. These are record counts, not unique scenes or complete
attachment/frame coverage. Some were jokes, historical prototypes or experiments.
Small unreadable text remains unresolved. The collection owner retains the
[missing bytes and provenance limits](CORC_MEDIA_AND_DESIGN_2026-10-20.md).

Every reference cell states what was shown, stated or questioned, with a dated
saved-post locator. Locator links were not reopened live. A capture is not proof
of a released feature, its complete rules or current behavior. No downloaded
source HTML was executed. Original media/context remain private and unchanged.

OOPS cells report focused source inspection and reuse earlier qualified source
findings. They are **not rebuilt browser evidence**. The dirty checkout includes
concurrent candidate UI work, particularly the UI-extras modules. Their source
presence is not current installed, saved, human-accepted or committed behavior.
“Not established” means this bounded inspection did not prove the route; it is
not a whole-repository absence claim. This avoids repeating older stale absence
claims from the 2026-10-10 comparison.

The independent [UI/playful review](UI_AND_PLAYFUL_PRESENTATION_OPPORTUNITIES_2026-10-20.md)
adds label backing, comparison subjects, transfer orientation and stable focus.
Its existing candidate screenshots retain their own dated receipt; neither
report claims a fresh visual comparison of today's dirty source.

## How to read the disposition

| Code word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Present | A relevant OOPS source counterpart exists. Coverage and delivery limits still apply. |
| Partial | Some of the route exists; the named missing connection or presentation is not established. |
| Different | Different boundary, owner, accepted direction or an OOPS capability to retain. It is not a defect score. |
| Candidate | A concrete possible addition; the inspected OOPS seams do not establish it. No implementation authorized. |
| Unresolved | The reference or counterpart is too weak to conclude parity or a useful new mechanic. |
