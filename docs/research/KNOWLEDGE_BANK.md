# OOPS knowledge bank

Use this bank to answer design questions across books. Each case keeps one source locator and evidence status. A case can support several mechanics. Start with the question, follow the case IDs, and reopen the relevant text before implementing a rule. [Source library](LIBRARY_INDEX.md), [scenario reading runs and edition coverage](SCENARIO_SOURCE_SHELVES_AND_BATCHES_2026-11-19.md).

| KB1 | Can a surprising method fulfill a scenario objective? | S01, S02, S08 | Describe the target outcome in native facts. Handle institutional aid and cheats through the methods already available. S01 shows an adjudicated success. S08 ends at sandbox entry. |
| KB2 | Can a character know something while appearing unaware? | S02, S03, S07, S15 | Track what a character perceives, what they reveal and what the enforcer can observe. Carousel shows a power ending after visible acknowledgement. *48 Hours a Day* shows a hub restricting identification by default. *Terror Infinity* offers a briefing claim about overheard disclosure for a future test. |
| KB3 | Which clock controls the objective, danger and return? | S01, S04, S06, S10 | Give each clock its owner. Survival through dawn can satisfy a mission, while a completed objective can also leave the player in the world to practice or intervene. Compare announced extraction terms with the actual clock after powers apply. |
| KB4 | How do individual and group results combine? | S03, S05, S09, S13, S16, S19, S23 | Evaluate the actual terms, store individual causal receipts and itemize costs by payer. A rescue, another job at the same scene, and a labor payment can have different participants. |
| KB5 | What can a player bring through or unlock? | S01, S05, S08, S09, S12, S20, S21 | Distinguish permission to leave, the effect leaving has on obligations, what crosses, what a sponsor grants, and what needs follow-up to become usable. |
| KB6 | How do we preserve limits and conflicting evidence? | S02, S03, S04, S06, S10 | A character's guess, a stated rule and an observed result have different evidentiary weight. Preserve the counterexample when a method gives a partial answer or a preview ends before assessment. |
| KB7 | Can an opportunity recur while history persists? | S01, S04, S08, S17 | Track the identity of each opening and retain actual world state. S04 shows recurrence with local consequences intact. S17 shows a different rule, where written notes reset and memory survives. Test each boundary through its own carry rules. |
| KB8 | What defines the edge of a scenario? | S06, S08, S11 | A boundary can attach to a place, a scoped encounter, or a named moving actor. Record the trigger and who owns it. Test a boundary crossing and, for a moving anchor, what happens when the anchor changes. |
| KB9 | Who can offer a result that changes your standing? | S01, S05, S14, S19, S22, S24 | Treat rank, permission to join, and continued access to a site as changes an authority owns. Record what proof they asked for, what they received and what they actually granted. |
| KB10 | What can another actor agree to do for you? | S01, S07, S14, S18, S22, S23, S24 | Treat cooperation as each actor's decision. Compare a negotiated alliance with commanded work and a choice constrained by debt. |

## Bring reading batches into the bank

1. **Index per edition.** The existing five passes produce census, scenes, rules, people and texture. Keep the source hash, declared chapter window, paragraph locators, missing material and read status. Merge per world with `merge_shards.mjs`; use `cite_paragraphs.mjs` to reopen key evidence. [Batch workflow](BATCH_READING_WORKFLOW.md).
2. **Group across books by a question.** For a question such as how somebody satisfies a trial through help, collect cases that speak to the question. Preserve differences in whose law applies, what triggers it, the scope of the effect, what observers know and how the outcome is carried or paid. Keep failed attempts, exceptions and unknowns beside successes.
3. **Check a proposed shared rule.** Cite an example that motivated it and a genuinely different or held-out case. Compare the prior state, actor, means, authority, scope, clock, proof and consequences. New evidence can qualify a group and its design implications without rewriting the underlying source cases.
4. **Connect the design owner and test.** Keep this bank as a source-facing index. Design proposals belong in the [scenario proposal](../worlds/SCENARIO_WORLDS_AND_CHEATS_2026-11-18.md) or the applicable rules owner. The first test candidate combines KB1 and KB2 in the ferry scenario and checks real ally help, concealed discovery and exposure. [Ferry comparison fixture](SCENARIO_SOURCE_SHELVES_AND_BATCHES_2026-11-19.md#off-script-action-comparison).

Start with the existing reading lenses: procedures and time, institutions and law, progression and powers, knowledge and detection, economy and custody, body and hazards, and places and long play. Add a new group when several cases need the same lookup. Cross-link related work through the [procedural core](../PROCEDURAL_CORE.md), [cross-book law and making](../design/CROSS_BOOK_SEAMS_LAW_AND_MAKING_2026-10-30.md), and the [power mechanism catalog](power-catalogues-2026-11-16/MECHANISM_FOUNDATION_2026-11-18.md). Keep generic discovery summaries and exact private reading indexes in their existing homes.
