# Trait substitution and alternate methods, 2026-11-11

**Proposal.** Share how a method binds actual means and reports what they contribute. Let each world's laws decide whether those means work together and what they change. A replacement preserves a role in an existing operation. An alternate method can produce the state a downstream step needs by carrying out a different operation. Equivalence has a scope, such as cleaning this cauldron with one dose or keeping this workspace enclosed through a particular rite.

This extends [TSW1–TSW10 and PC7–PC14](../CRAFTING_ENGINE.md), [SUB0–SUB4](../systems/MAKING_ITEMS_ECONOMY.md#5-recipes-slots-and-substitution-the-swappable-ingredient-layer), [GKM5–GKM10 and GKM17–GKM22](../GU_ABILITIES_AND_KILLER_MOVES_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-11-10.md), and [DR1–DR5 / DT2 and DT4](GU_AND_HOGWARTS_RULE_DERIVATION_2026-11-11.md). AD6–AD8, AD38, AD49, AD51 and AD56 govern truth, evidence, mechanism, sharing and design authority. WPR3, WPR7, WPR19, WPR21, WPR27 and WPR31 govern this pass. ## Shared binding proposal

1. **Name what the next step reads.** A role declares its target, relevant operation and required conditions. Use exact identity when the mechanism reads identity, such as a particular contracted party. Use tag, property or conjunction selectors for regular material predicates. Let an operator or completed action contribute a verified state transition, such as enclosing the marked workspace. A planned walk and a drawn boundary are different evidence.
2. **Bind real means before estimating contribution.** Attach exact holdings or actor/tool handles, amounts and units, relevant state, scope and the step that uses each. A cutting tool can contribute a usable edge and reach with wear. A ritual tool may also need preparation or a provenance that matters to the local law. A Gu contributes its operation while ready, held or lent, with the actual operator and fuel. A catalyst can be retained while a different tool, charge or consumable pays for the operation. Respect simultaneous commitments. Reuse one thing across steps if it is still available, or across functions if one act physically supplies both.
3. **Evaluate the relevant dimensions under a versioned world law.** Check essentials such as closure, phase, sufficient charge or matching identity before scoring a contribution. Carry potency, reach, coverage, duration, risk, quality, noise, time and material consequences as separate results where a consumer reads them. Use the stated conversion and basis because different measures can describe different contributions. Drops require a known volume per drop in context, and a Gu count cannot stand in for essence. When quantities combine, keep excess bulk, waste and side constituents. A symbolic or ownership requirement combines only where the world has such a mechanism. Extra effect can plateau, dilute or interfere. Keep preparation, host world, origin, transport, contact and current custody distinct so an unknown field stays unknown.
4. **Separate the plan, settlement and observation.** Preview without spending. At use, confirm actual means and state again. Record spend, retain, hold, release, harm and leftover stock through the existing owners at the step that causes them. A missing requirement discovered before use and a late failure can have different consequences. Record what happened and what the character could observe separately. A witness might see powder traces or changed cauldron residue while remaining unsure about the law or a hidden source. A learned method can store demonstrated conditions and revisions and prompt a fresh comparison when a consequential condition changes.

The shared output can start with an ordinary record of the selected means, per-role facts and uncertainties, committed changes and evidence references. Keep the current batch arithmetic, Gu support logic and LOTM rites as separate evaluators. A universal fit percentage would rank a redundant Little Light or a nearly closed wall without explaining either outcome. An explicit pair list is useful for source calibration and mechanism-specific exceptions. Candidates can otherwise be assembled from owned means and supported operations, then run through the same evaluator as player-built plans. Search can bound work and report examined candidates and open branches. A partial search leaves the remaining options open.

**Unlisted composition prediction.** Hold operator, core, target and readiness constant. Normalize base Moonlight power to 1. Evaluate Moonlight alone, one and two Little Lights, and the refined Moonglow through their respective activation or refinement rules. The book comparison gives power 1, 2, 2, 3. Next submit Moonlight with a Little Light and Light Source. If the world law finds compatible activation and confirms that Moonlight qualifies for the discount, predict power 2 and Moonlight cost M/2. Compute total spend from that term plus each support's actual applicable cost. If only Rainbow Light has a supported discount binding, report Moonlight eligibility as unresolved in the forecast. A successful power prediction leaves the unknown cost visible. A second Little Light still contributes no further power under the sourced rule. The combined result is a paper prediction.

## Integration contract

Start with an adapter around each procedural evaluator. Callers can share the shape below while inventory, execution, and persistent state remain with their present owners. The proposed fields describe the data that crosses the boundary. Each adapter can derive them from its existing rows and state.

| Boundary | Fields and behavior |
|---|---|
| **Request** | `methodRef` with revision, actor, target, and selected inputs by actual handle. Each input carries a role, the step that uses it, a typed amount if measured, and the player's explicit choice. Goals use a named outcome, target, scope and window. Example: workspace isolated inside this boundary until the petition finishes. |
| **Resolved bindings** | Per-role rows with the provider handle, operation and phase, required conditions, and actual contribution. Report each condition as met, unmet or unknown in the observer's view. In the authoritative result, preserve an unsupported rule as unresolved. Keep disposition explicit: spend an amount, hold or retain a provider, release a commitment, or change a state. |
| **Evaluation** | Return expected effects, per-step costs, relevant side effects, and any unresolved fields. Identify the law and revision that supplied each result. A fully bound plan can still predict a weak result or a hazard. A partial prediction can give a supported attack-power change and an unknown total fuel cost. |
| **Commit and observation** | Recheck source availability, overlapping commitments, location and conditions at the relevant step. Use the owner's existing transaction or job and its identity to settle an expense once. Retain the selected bindings, actual step outcome and any changed state that later steps read. Project observations through existing knowledge rules. A simulation result may carry author-facing details that a player-facing adapter withholds. |

**Keep three comparisons separate.** First check that a provider can perform the role. Then run the full method and compare the result against the goal. Finally check whether the method qualifies for recognition as a named recipe or technique. A positive tag query or perfect fit score establishes neither equal effects nor recipe recognition. Compare equivalence against specific fields on the same target and window. In T1, one dose's change to the same residue is the equality check. Record stock, cost and provenance differences alongside it.

**Costs follow the step.** The admission check can quote a cost or reserve a provider. Settlement records the actual debit or state change. A cancelled step releases its reservation, and completed steps keep their committed changes. If a route scatters powder before failing to close, the spent portion and any supported trace survive. If the current job refunds all of that step's costs on failure, use explicit settlement for the step that actually scatters, then check closure afterward. This matters because [`compileProcedure`](../../src/making_procedure.js) puts command costs on the first timed step and configures refunds for failed jobs. It can carry ordered checks and effects, while the local action owner handles irreversible expenditure.

### Reuse the evaluator for search and direct play

For a replacement, freeze the method and the other bindings, enumerate plausible providers for the selected role, then evaluate each complete plan. For a different route, index world operations by the state they can produce. Take the requested state and scope, retrieve applicable producers, assemble each producer's prerequisite steps from available means, then evaluate the whole route. For example, the search for `isolated(workspace, petitionWindow)` can discover both perimeter operations by their outputs. It still has to bind a prepared dagger or actual powder and prove that the chosen method covers the requested workspace.

A direct request and a search result use the same evaluator. Supply no expected outcome with the request. Keep a stable search order from method revision and provider handles. For the first standalone fixture, evaluate at most 32 complete plans and follow at most four levels of prerequisite producers per route, with an explicit incomplete-coverage result at either limit. These are proposed search defaults, chosen to keep interactive evaluation bounded. A directly supplied valid plan still gets evaluated. Retain candidates with uncertain fields for deliberate trials, and rank known fits against the player's goal and observed tradeoffs. Search cannot silently swap the selected provider at commit.

A completed run can return `enclosure.active` for the workroom, a one-portion spend at the scatter step, and a trace carrying the same lot handle. A run missing the west segment leaves closure unmet. Under an original test rule that spends powder when scattering starts, it retains the spent portion and any observed trace. The dagger path binds a retained tool and contributes the same scoped enclosure through its own operations. Exact spirituality values stay open until set by the local fixture. Once both routes evaluate, use a custom plan that encloses the adjacent room to verify that a real effect on the wrong scope still fails the petition's condition.
