## Character exceptions and deeper pacing

The stage enum itself runs Adversary→Nuisance→Frenemy→Acquaintance→Friendzoned→AwkwardBesties→Crush→Sweetheart→Girlfriend→Lover. These are named progression levels; their labels do not establish player-authored relationship terms. Selected phone answers save reply indices used for alternate lines/images, but both callbacks continue to the same NextMessage. That differs from Peanut’s branching scene and QPernikiss’s persistent ending choice.

Pamu demonstrates why prose cannot establish a mechanic: selected lines describe taking diamonds or threatening exclusivity, while her actual rows use Diamond balance checks and the inspected debit loop handles DiamondConsume. No corresponding roster lockout was found in those traced responses. QPernikiss supplies a contrasting real ending/achievement/phone consequence. Bearverly/Alpha transformations and selected state-dependent dialogue must likewise be separated from a general NPC agency model. Peanut’s scene offers genuine authored choices, but local mood and achievements do not alone establish an enduring compatibility or commitment system for every character.

Late pressure is an inter-system problem. Hobby requirements grow exponentially; huge gifts/date counts, retained cash thresholds, specific job levels, gild counts and boost requirements can coexist. Favorite skills improve hearts while serving job access. Promotions can release blocks, enabling a date or additional training. QPernikiss removes derived available capacity, whereas all-gild requirements demand spending without freeing capacity. Catara compresses exact outstanding gift/date repetition, leaving those other gates intact. Its visible cash comparison is strict greater-than, unlike ordinary equality-allowed purchases, and its consumer assumes UI validity rather than repeating every affordability check. These details argue for honest current quotes and authored stops, not a universal one-click social solution.

The installed main Polly table requests FreeTimeBlock while the recovered Resources table lacks it. Under an unchanged load, the builder can throw and leave a heart-only partial requirement list that cannot advance. This is a qualified static consistency concern, not a confirmed live bug: updated/downloaded/PES data may differ. Paid SkipReset also leaves pending prestige untouched in the traced consumer, unlike ordinary prestige. No repeated-use behavior or intent is asserted. Reset/claim/purchase methods mutate before save/network responses; this review establishes no universal atomic rollback or live failure result.
