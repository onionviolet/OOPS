# Idle Reincarnator: installed-build research, 2026-10-14

Research only. Proposals are unselected. Status: PARTIAL, with the requested core systems traced in static source and explicit gaps in [CHECKPOINT.md](CHECKPOINT.md). No game launch, save access, runtime edit, build, staging or commit.

The original premise needs correction. This installed build supports **one connected setting, twelve exploration groups, parallel activity slots, and two live reset operations**, rebirth and regression. It does not establish several independently simulated realms sharing one incarnation. Its best lesson is how **personal learning, civilization progress and permanent time powers survive different reset depths**. Shared Gold and Renown fund the ordinary life; Temporal Essence comes from regression and buys powers that change later lives.

Evidence labels: **shown** means decoded installed code or parsed tables, not observed play. **stated** means source-authored text. **inferred** means analysis. Source locators use original `res://` paths and function or table IDs; local recovery paths are in [TOOLING.md](TOOLING.md). Decompiled text is a token rendering for inspection, not original source with comments or a runnable reconstruction.

## Answers to the four questions

**F1, realms in one life: shown plus bounded inference.** `assets/explorations/data.json` has twelve groups. `PlayerState` has one age, one current exploration and shared resources. `AssetLoader.CONSTRUCTORS` has no realm model; group contents share one `Game.tempData` registry. Jobs, training, lifestyles, spells, technology and exploration coexist as systems in this life. Normally exploration suspends active job/training gains. A purchased concurrency effect lifts that limit, and passive job income continues. This is a useful parallel-systems reference, but no independent realm clocks, bodies or currencies were found. The assertion that it is the only such idler is not established by this lane.

**F2, carry: shown.** Ordinary action levels and XP reset on rebirth; best levels within the regression cycle produce an XP multiplier of `1 + best / 10`. Skills override that multiplier, so do not describe every skill as directly receiving it. Training and jobs can accelerate their skill outputs indirectly. Heroic Potential explicitly skips both reset handlers. Technologies survive rebirth, usually reset on regression, and can later be preserved by a time skill. Exploration records distinguish this life, this regression and lifetime. Purchased Temporal skills survive rebirth; regression offers retaining their allocation or clearing it. See [RESET_AND_LIFE.md](RESET_AND_LIFE.md).

**F3, legibility: shown/stated.** Action detail UI exposes past best level and its multiplier, but hides the multiplier field for skills. Cards show requirements, XP and unlock notices. A diary connects progression milestones to feature unlocks. A manual regression preview breaks essence gain into rebirth-derived and boss-derived amounts. A warning before specified explorations explains forced regression until the relevant boss is defeated. Source wiring establishes these intended surfaces, not their actual layout or clarity in play.

**F4, unlock order and currency: shown.** Start at 20 with Beggar and Meditate, one job slot and one training slot. Beggar 10 opens Farmer; Farmer 5 opens physical training; Strength 5 opens the knight branch. Grasslands leads toward Woods, Crypt, a magic-research branch and Sea, then Tournament and later regions. Technology, professions, skill levels and diary events cross-gate one another. Statistics opens after the first lifetime rebirth, loadouts at ten, job/training automation at fifteen. Speed limits expand through diary milestones. Gold/Renown/Miracles reset per life; Temporal Essence spans regressions with special award/allocation handling. The resource JSON uses reset categories, not regional wallets. [SYSTEMS_AND_UNLOCKS.md](SYSTEMS_AND_UNLOCKS.md) gives the routes and limits.

**F5, offline/catch-up: shown versus stated contradiction.** Loading stores elapsed wall time plus a previously banked value, without replaying missed lives. The popup describes a 2x speed benefit. The speed controller has no bank check or offline multiplier; the bank is reduced per running frame when fast-forward is selected, without `delta`. This cannot establish a working duration-based bonus. Pause/death stop bank spending. Runtime behavior and old scene wiring remain untested. [OFFLINE_AND_CLOCKS.md](OFFLINE_AND_CLOCKS.md) traces this precisely.

**F6, life simulation: shown.** Fixed birth ages, normally 20 and 12 with Temporal Youth, base lifespan 50. Lifespan has additive year effects, boss-derived day rewards, accumulated daily gains/losses and a multiplier. Death checks age first and then exploration mortality. Miracles can absorb exploration death rolls; a purchased power enables a scoped Press On route. No per-life random talent/trait or family roll was found in state, reset handlers, content schema or save fields. Randomness in the inspected gameplay scripts is hazard rolls and hint wording, not generated lives.

**F7, data/save: shown.** Seventy-five JSON files parse, with 651 content rows including eight resource descriptors. Content is keyed by category, subgroup and stable ID. Constructors consume typed numeric, effects and requirement fields. The live save is JSON compressed with GZIP and base64 encoded, written to `user://savedata/save.sav`, with temporary and backup files, plus legacy paths/loaders. The save includes version, player, content state, statistics, automation, loadouts, clocks and preferences. No realm-state map or trait seed is present. [DATA_AND_SAVE.md](DATA_AND_SAVE.md) supplies inventories and caveats.
