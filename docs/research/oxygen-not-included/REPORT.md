## Wider system map

### Material, physiology and useful production

The important loop is material definition → owned payload → conversion → heat/waste → body/habitat/work → a later useful consumer. Element YAML expresses heat capacity, conductivity, phase thresholds, secondary products and state. ElementLoader checks the mounted substance table; SimMessages serializes those properties into a native solver table. Brine's declared high-temperature path includes steam and 30 percent salt. The managed interface does not reveal the complete native phase or flow algorithms, so the dossier claims definition/interface coverage only. ONI-01; E01–E04.

ElementConverter checks all active required inputs, computes a limiting factor, removes actual mass, combines input heat capacity/temperature and disease, and creates stored or emitted outputs. The electrolyzer's specific recipe joins water, 120 W, hot oxygen/hydrogen and a pressure ceiling. Pipes retain identity, limited capacity, temperature and disease across removal/admission and versioned load. EnergyGenerator consumes a real fuel recipe and emits useful power plus byproducts. Power allocation and overload therefore reach actual work support and eventually wire damage. ONI-02–04; E05–E10,E61.

Breathing selects a usable provider and consumes real nearby or carried material. Body air availability, breath debt and recovery target are separate. Shortage can interrupt work, reserve a breathing station, incapacitate and permit rescue before a death call. WarmBlooded separately models simple heat production versus homeostasis, including a mode without calorie impact; TemperatureMonitor reads smoothed actual body temperature rather than just ambient weather. Hungry/starving/depleted need states and meal schedules are another labor constraint, though the final ordinary-dupe calorie death consumer was not read. ONI-05–08; E11–E17.

Original YAIR implications extend beyond a crafting patch: heat can make the same material useful or hazardous; useful outputs can also leave local waste; medicine/lining/support can change conversion and recovery rather than become permanent traits; a biological body, vessel and projection can need different resources while sharing one actual identity. None of this selects real thermodynamic equations, fatal offline upkeep or a new universal physiology owner. Current native item custody, body conditions, energy policies and publication remain the actual integration gates.

### Progression, labor and welfare

MinionResume separates aptitude, learned/granted mastery and skill experience; AttributeLevels holds a different per-attribute training domain. Prerequisite skills, points and forbidden chore groups are hard mastery gates. Morale shortfall is a warning, not a CanMasterSkill veto. Learned non-granted skills increase expectations; aptitude can increase morale. QualityOfLifeNeed turns the current morale gap into difficulty-dependent stress modifiers, and StressMonitor/StressBehaviourMonitor turn high stress into response chores and reprieves. Exact destructive or comfort chore implementations remain unread. ONI-09,12; E18–E19,E25–E27.

Work eligibility also checks permission, assignment, schedule, perk, route reach, pickup privacy and priority. Emergency red alert can override a schedule predicate. The model's lesson is that a facility recipe cannot conjure a suitable worker or eliminate personal needs. Source permissions are not human consent; YAIR's willing worker and property rules remain a separate authored requirement. ONI-10; E20.

ResearchCenter consumes owned material during real qualified labor and converts mass into typed points with a fractional remainder. Research owns the prerequisite queue and typed payment; complete tech, practical POI unlock and personal researcher qualification remain distinct. This preserves research as a situated action with competing labor and samples rather than just another XP bar. Specific recipe cancellation and progress retention should remain local policy, not copied into every YAIR job. ONI-11; E21–E24.

The bionic booster bridge is especially instructive: a physical installed upgrade grants/removes competence and adds wattage support. Yet UngrantSkill also calls UnmasterSkill in the same mastery dictionary. That direct branch does not prove a separately learned copy survives removal. An original borrowed tool should contribute derived execution support while earned learning stays in its own owner. ONI-F33,39; E18,E50–E51.

### Ecology, sources and continuing life

Mealwood joins ambient atmosphere/pressure/temperature/drowning, actual dirt and growth. Wilt conditions and custom stalls stop supported growth; wild and cultivated growth rates differ. Crop creates a physical harvest carrying the plant's temperature and actual yield. Nutrient arrays in PlantElementAbsorber can be alternatives, while ElementConverter active inputs are jointly required. Authoring must preserve that distinction. ONI-13; E28–E32.

CreatureCalorieMonitor remembers diet-qualified consumption and creates species-specific material, possible disease and even solid tiles. Wild hunger and tame starvation differ; pause explicitly repairs starvation timing. Fertility can be blocked by confinement/paused tags, uses saved normalized breeding weights and creates an actual egg with copied wildness. Ecology is therefore production, lifecycle, habitat and lineage, not generic passive income. Predation, complete diet/egg/mutation catalogues and population-level stability remain unread. ONI-14–15; E33–E34.

Original YAIR possibilities include paid tending versus supported wild growth, feed choice with functional byproducts, growth stalls rather than automatic death, reproduction that competes for actual habitat, and scarcity that motivates exploration or trade. Existing Nell crop is deliberately finite and does not auto-replant. Any repeatable husbandry proposal needs its own optional lifecycle and actual renewal inputs; it cannot repurpose her old receipt as renewable stock.

### Time, persistence and authoring

GameClock advances simulation dt into 600 second cycles and emits cycle/autosave events, with separate render-played time. Creature starvation uses that game clock, but a meal-to-poop delay checks Unity Time.time. A source can contain several clocks even inside one need system. These selected methods do not establish all unobserved-region, pause, offline or renderer scheduling policies. ONI-16; E33,E35.

SaveLoader combines settings/root metadata, native cell bytes and managed objects; load reconstructs material tables and validates native/managed results. Source files also retain schema/type-directory and current-save DLC context. File writing builds contents in memory, then opens the target with FileMode.Create; inspected IO/permission catches show failure, but the method does not demonstrate a temp-file atomic replace. YAIR should preserve its existing durable publication instead of copying this write pattern. No personal save was read or actual save/reload performed. ONI-17; E04,E36.

DlcManager separates subscription, settings, required/forbidden content and directory mapping; current save context and mounted assets add further gates. Installed depot/bundle presence proves local access, not ownership, enablement or mechanic coverage. Definitions and useful rule data are retained privately for later original authoring; a visual scene/editor is a consumer of authoritative state, not another stock, clock or reward owner. ONI-18; E02,E36–E37.
