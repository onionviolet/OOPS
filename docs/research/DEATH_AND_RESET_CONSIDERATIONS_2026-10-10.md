# Death and reset considerations (2026-10-10)

*Research proposals only, not accepted design. This page pulls together six
deep dives from the death, regression and tower batch. Each book has a
private dossier with chapter locators:
[SSS-Class Suicide Hunter](novels/sss-class-suicide-hunter/DEEP_DIVE_2026-10-10.md),
[All You Need Is Kill](novels/all-you-need-is-kill/DEEP_DIVE_2026-10-10.md),
[Absolute Regression](novels/absolute-regression/DEEP_DIVE_2026-10-10.md),
[The World After the Fall](novels/world-after-the-fall/DEEP_DIVE_2026-10-10.md),
A Knight Who Eternally Regresses and Second Life Ranker. The last two had
only their reader files done when this was written, so their entries are
interim. A palette of considerations, not specs. Each fits some
characters, worlds and times and not others.*

## The one thing to take away

Across six books, a "reset" turns out to be a bundle of separate dials, not
one mechanic. If YAIRPG models the dials, one engine covers every book here
and its own original worlds too. The first milestone's single prestige layer
is the first instance: prestige is a reset with one setting on each dial.

## R. The reset profile (proposal)

| Dial | Range seen in the batch |
|---|---|
| R1 Trigger | own death (SSS, AYNIK); blackout or any loss of consciousness (AYNIK); failing the day's obstacle, the "wall" (Knight); a ritual you spend a lifetime building (AbsReg); none, because the "reset" item is fake (TWATF); someone else's life written down (SLR diary) |
| R2 Anchor | a window trailing you by 24 hours, walked deeper by chained deaths (SSS); a span tied to an event, not the calendar (AYNIK); a point that follows the wall (Knight); a random distance into the past (AbsReg); location saves made by sleeping at a save point (SSS) |
| R3 Carry set | memory and trained skill but not body, wounds or items (AYNIK, AbsReg); conditioning needs real lived days (Knight); copied skills picked from the killer (SSS); cooldowns that keep counting on lived time (SSS) |
| R4 Cost | reliving the killer's trauma, heavier with rank (SSS); food stops tasting, novelty fatigue (AYNIK); a whole first life, an eye and an arm, paid up front (AbsReg); memories of the old life fading (AbsReg); refusing the reset, which costs comfort (TWATF) |
| R5 Witnesses | the enemy loops too (AYNIK); rival regressors who resent visible loops (SSS); a lover who shares rewinds while the love is mutual (SSS); the overseer of the loop turns out to be earlier loopers who stayed (Knight); fooled "regressors" as NPCs (TWATF) |
| R6 Exit | kill the targets in the right order (AYNIK); clear the wall (Knight, and the loop may never end); refuse every offer (TWATF); a scope limit that is both a gain and a cage (SSS); a farewell instead of a final battle (AbsReg) |

Per-world adjectives set each dial. No dial needs a "once ever" limit:
AbsReg's single regression is a cost (a lifetime of materials), not a cap.

## F. Foreknowledge as a resource that decays

- **F1. Sources differ.** Personal memory (AbsReg), a dead sibling's diary
  (SLR), a loop count (AYNIK), dreams of other loopers (AYNIK), a copied
  memory from a killer (SSS). Each source goes stale in its own way.
- **F2. Staleness has causes.** Events you don't touch replay on their old
  dates (AbsReg). Enemy details shift with your actions (Knight). The big
  enemy reroutes around you (AbsReg). A higher record supersedes the diary
  (SLR).
- **F3. Memory fade as a slow clock**, slowed by writing things down
  (AbsReg). Pairs with the existing journal and notebook systems.
- **F4. The end of foreknowledge as a milestone.** After the last event you
  remember, you play blind, like everyone else.
- **F5. Repeat lines as tells.** An NPC saying the same words as last time
  shows which parts of the world are fixed.

## L. Loopers as a kind of person

- **L1. A population, not a protagonist.** Every book had other loopers,
  regressors or reset-holders: mentors, rivals, jailers, the fooled.
- **L2. Mentor who is also the lock.** A graduated looper is your best
  teacher and the reason your loop can't close (AYNIK).
- **L3. Loops can be inherited** or handed over (AYNIK, SLR's split
  inheritance from a dying god).
- **L4. Stuck loopers become the system.** The overseer is made of people
  who stopped trying (Knight). A cautionary end state for an idle player.
- **L5. Regression detectors.** Someone who reads death counts or sees time
  reverse turns your loops into suspicion (SSS).

## C. Counterfeit and conditional cheats

- **C1. The fake cheat.** An item sold as regression that is really a
  comfortable dream (TWATF). Its users leave gear and debts behind.
- **C2. Cheats with strings.** An inheritance that steers who you become
  (SLR). A tower that turns out to be a restraint on the strong (SLR).
- **C3. Refusal as a stat.** Turning down offers stacks into power (TWATF).
  An honest path for players who never prestige.
- **C4. Earned resets.** The reset is crafted from materials and judged by a
  patron on effort and character (AbsReg). A crafting goal at life scale.

These sit well with the sealed-secrets rule: a world can hide whether its
cheat is real until the player finds out.

## G. Repetition as progression (idle fit)

- **G1. The untalented grinder.** About 500 repeated days to make a knight
  (Knight); 211 loops (AYNIK). The repeat counter is the progress bar.
- **G2. One move past the system.** Billions of uses of one basic stab
  become the first step of a higher path (TWATF).
- **G3. Lived-time clocks keep farming honest** without banning it (SSS):
  cooldowns, aging and hunger run on your own clock.
- **G4. Time rooms with a price.** Faster training that ages the body until
  mastered (AbsReg), with temptation to stay too long (TWATF).

## B. Bonds across resets

- **B1. Same person, different life.** You use their first-life fate to
  give them a better one (AbsReg).
- **B2. Debts only you remember.** You owe people who don't know it.
- **B3. Love as a shared mechanic**, with a failure state when it stops
  being mutual (SSS).
- **B4. Bonds that survive without memory.** Friends come back with their
  feelings but not their memories (TWATF).
- **B5. Relationships as keys and power.** Final floors gated by being
  remembered, prayed for and forgiven (SSS); a world becomes real when
  enough people believe in it (TWATF).
- **B6. Death as how you meet people.** Die to someone, live their life,
  then summon them with that memory (SSS). A household of former killers.

## D. Where death sends you

- **D1. Back** (most of the batch). **D2. Down** a layer, into a realm with
  a corruption meter that ends in a monster (TWATF). **D3. Into someone
  else**, through their trauma (SSS). **D4. Nowhere special**, because the
  "tower" was a dream all along (TWATF). Each world can pick.

## E. Endings after the loop

- **E1. Graduate**: fights better, lonelier, keeps a hated medal (AYNIK).
- **E2. Powerless restart**: wake at ten years old in an ordinary life
  (TWATF). A prestige that strips power but keeps bonds.
- **E3. Never ends**: the loop still runs in the last chapter (Knight).
- **E4. Farewell**: the last enemy is forgiven, not killed (AbsReg).
- **E5. Author layer**: the hero writes his story as a web novel (TWATF).

## T. Tower and floor shapes

- **T1. Floors as stages of a life** (SSS), which suits a life sim.
- **T2. Heavens judged on non-combat virtues**: music, trade, law (SSS).
- **T3. A tower as a product or a prison**: rented by a buyer (TWATF), a
  restraint on the strong (SLR), leased to gods at auction (SSS).
- **T4. Tutorials that sort and grade** (TWATF, SLR).
- **T5. Floors that build your own world** and then belong to you (SSS).

## Process note

The scout's premises were wrong for three of its top five: Dungeon Defense
(no reset known), Absolute Regression (one earned regression, no loops) and
The World After the Fall (the regression stone is fake). Verify a premise
in the text or a wiki before ranking a source by it.
