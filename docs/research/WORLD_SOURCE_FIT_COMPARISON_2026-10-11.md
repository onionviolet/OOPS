# World choice by source coverage

2026-10-11. Bounded recommendation, not an accepted world selection or implementation assignment. Checked PROJECT.md, the Harry Potter brief/source ledger, Mother of Learning brief and Skyrim brief, plus the primary pages linked below. This is not a complete reading audit or a measured count of each franchise's corpus.

## Recommendation

O1 Forgotten Realms with one frozen D&D rules edition is the strongest candidate in this shortlist for broad documented options and explicit adjudication. O2 Harry Potter is a strong candidate for a recognizable school-to-adult magical life. O3 Elder Scrolls is a strong fit for YAIRPG's skills, materials, crafting and faction progression. O4 Mother of Learning is the strongest focused novel candidate here for coherent magical study. O5 Worm is useful for unusual ability interactions and faction consequences, with a larger bespoke-power implementation burden.

These are design judgments. Source quantity alone does not establish consistency, completeness or implementation readiness. No finite source answers every possible player action.

## How to support options beyond named examples

Model actions from actual capabilities, targets, materials, knowledge, access, time and consequences. For example, a levitation ability should interact with supported movable objects and jobs through shared rules, rather than only one scripted levitation quest. Explicitly distinguish source fact, inferred application and authored divergence. An unknown source detail is unresolved, not proof of impossibility or permission.

For Harry Potter, the author supplement says potions require wandwork. This illustrates why a large fan knowledge pool cannot replace a qualified source ledger. Fanfiction can propose alternate premises but cannot silently supply book-canon facts.

A proposed initial Harry Potter boundary is the seven novels plus specifically admitted author supplements; other media remain separately selected continuities. The current local ledger is bounded companion research, not a completed seven-book audit. Implementation is not started by this comparison.

## Follow-up: near-term priority and held-book work

User selected a fresh Harry Potter audit/design pass first, including relationships/cousin starts, body/gender options, timeline variants, Chinese and English fanfiction research, and possible game inspection. D&D has a separate fresh GM for its long-term source/design foundation. These are design assignments, not authorization to implement all worlds simultaneously.

Newly available local Downloads include the seven-novel collection PDF and a 2016 Special Rehearsal Edition Cursed Child EPUB. Private read-only conversion and retrieval are owned by the source-preparation folder linked from the HP dispatch receipt. Initial absence is superseded; full semantic reading is still separate from locating/converting files.

Parallel held-book design workers cover Mother of Learning, Lord of the Mysteries and The Legendary Mechanic. Mother of Learning is the strongest alternate for a coherent magic-study opening with an existing full supplied-edition dossier. LOTM offers deep organization/economy/progression design, with documented gaps that must not be silently filled as canon. Legendary Mechanic offers a distinct workshop/craft/faction direction. Reverend Insanity and Shadow Slave remain useful held-book alternatives, but this bounded wave does not launch every candidate.

For HP, prefer selected official guides/modding/data inspection to broad executable decompilation until there is a concrete unanswered game question. The official Hogwarts Legacy FAQ confirms an original 1800s game story and PC mod support; that supports game-specific research rather than merging its mechanics into novel canon. A cousin origin is an authored genealogical premise where no sourced slot exists; changing physical appearance and choosing a starting identity are separate design dimensions. Author writing supports Polyjuice changes of age, sex and race, without establishing any arbitrary permanent body change. Fanfiction supplies alternative premises and proposed gap fills, each attributed and labelled.
