## Five connected mechanic paths

**T1: training permission → raw rank → effective mastery → function.** Ordinary learning checks the next raw rank's stat/level requirements, known category and earlier talent count. The level-up UI also checks points and cap. A Bulwark category supplies a mastery offset, applied through Birther; effective rank then combines positive raw rank, temporary rank alterations and category mastery. Shield Pummel's duration and two weapon multipliers consume effective rank, with Shield Expertise contributing to damage. The nominal high values in the scaling formulas are not hard caps. An item or escort can force-grant a talent through a different path without satisfying ordinary training permissions. See [learning](FINDINGS.json), [Pummel](/Users/weiwei/.codex/research/tales-of-maj-eyal/source/t-engine4-src-1.7.6/unpacked/tome-1.7.6/data/talents/techniques/weaponshield.lua:24) and [effective rank](/Users/weiwei/.codex/research/tales-of-maj-eyal/source/t-engine4-src-1.7.6/unpacked/te4-1.7.6/engine/interface/ActorTalents.lua:906).

**T2: legal action → payment/cooldown → attempted effect → consumer/expiry.** Pummel checks cooldown, resources, current shield and projectable target. A missing target/shield exits before its normal payment; a legal attack still completes if its second strike misses or stun is resisted. Stun first faces immunity, then a separate physical save/duration adjustment. Accepted stun halves outgoing damage, slows movement and cooldown recovery, and briefly puts eligible talents on cooldown. Expiry removes modifiers, not previous damage or spent effort. Shield Wall separately reserves stamina capacity while active, adds block/control resistance, and restores capacity/removes modifiers on release. Reservation is not the same as action cost or drain. See [stun lifecycle](/Users/weiwei/.codex/research/tales-of-maj-eyal/source/t-engine4-src-1.7.6/unpacked/tome-1.7.6/data/timed_effects/physical.lua:479) and [sustain](/Users/weiwei/.codex/research/tales-of-maj-eyal/source/t-engine4-src-1.7.6/unpacked/tome-1.7.6/data/talents/techniques/weaponshield.lua:190).

**T3: authored actor kit → legal candidates → current tactical wants → bounded fallback.** The elven elite warrior supplies a concrete shield/Pummel/tactical configuration. Source initializes the transition switch enabled, routing tactical to improved tactical under that setting. Candidate filters include equipment, cooldown, resources and reach; attack/disable weights only describe possible usefulness. Current wants, other actions and timing affect selection. Failed execution zeros that candidate and retries within a bound. If no energy was spent, the outer AI can choose maintenance or movement. Neither a weight nor the source default proves real action frequency or optimal play. See [actor](/Users/weiwei/.codex/research/tales-of-maj-eyal/source/t-engine4-src-1.7.6/unpacked/tome-1.7.6/data/general/npcs/elven-warrior.lua:97), [selector](/Users/weiwei/.codex/research/tales-of-maj-eyal/source/t-engine4-src-1.7.6/unpacked/tome-1.7.6/mod/ai/improved_tactical.lua:557) and [reviewed AI notes](AI_READER.json).

**T4: escort outcome → specific reward → separate achievement and unlock state.** A reachable portal and surviving escort produce a reward choice; failure cleans up the actor and distinguishes player-caused betrayal from other loss. Individual talent rewards force-teach the talent and may set mastery, but do not unlock the category. Category rewards record an untrained category, requiring a later category-learning step. Partial player-mode achievement progress belongs to the current actor; completed achievements and escort fate counts use separate profile outputs. One player-caused escort death can satisfy the Fallen evolution's local deed predicate before the six-event accolade, but profile unlock, subclass, stats and other evolution requirements still apply. A separately inspected end-chat path supplies the actual profile unlock. See [quest](/Users/weiwei/.codex/research/tales-of-maj-eyal/source/t-engine4-src-1.7.6/unpacked/tome-1.7.6/data/quests/escort-duty.lua:118), [reward distinctions](/Users/weiwei/.codex/research/tales-of-maj-eyal/source/t-engine4-src-1.7.6/unpacked/tome-1.7.6/mod/class/EscortRewards.lua:528) and [reviewed progression notes](PROGRESSION_READER.json).

**T5: material/eligible modifier → equipped means → actual attack/block consumer.** Material tier, generation range, stat requirement and realized attack/block values are distinct. A natural ego roll uses a level-resolved chance and stops on the first failure; later types can be skipped. Modifier magnitude can separately depend on material tier. Wear/takeoff applies and retracts item modifiers and temporary talent grants. Pummel attacks with the actual shield record; Block reads its block power, and the damage projector consumes that timed effect, rejecting self-hit or unsupported damage types. Disarming removes shield eligibility. The paired C `rng.percent` binding checks an integer threshold against a random integer; distribution/replay behavior was not tested. See [materials](/Users/weiwei/.codex/research/tales-of-maj-eyal/source/t-engine4-src-1.7.6/unpacked/tome-1.7.6/data/general/objects/shields.lua:22), [actual damage consumer](/Users/weiwei/.codex/research/tales-of-maj-eyal/source/t-engine4-src-1.7.6/unpacked/tome-1.7.6/data/damage_types.lua:409) and [reviewed equipment notes](EQUIPMENT_READER.json).
