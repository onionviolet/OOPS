## 1. Choose a harness fit that changes an actual guarded blow

**Goal and player action:** at the existing workbench, craft either a rigid reinforced harness or a flexible lashed harness, then transfer/equip that actual object on willing Lio. Compare normal native blocks and a landed pole pull. Rigid construction blocks more but costs more effort to guard; flexible construction blocks less but resists the pull's extra stamina loss. Neither prevents the ordinary damage of an unblocked hit. The original harness remains viable.

**Provisional costs and aftermath:** use counted existing kits/logs and optional resin through current recipes/jobs; spend crafting time/effort and transfer one object. Prototype rigid block6 versus current4, with guard cost+1; flexible block3 and immunity only to the hook's extra stamina drain. All numbers are proposals. Removing or losing custody/support removes the benefit; it does not erase a learned method or grant a new one. Refitting consumes and returns the same owned object variant, without duplication or touching a loan.

**Reuse and missing binding:** `items.js` already calculates component/quality shield strength. `feature_expansion_powers.js` crafts/transfers harness stock; `featureOwnedGear` enforces equipment, count and custody; `feature_expansion_battle_host.js:18-20,57-60` currently projects a single harness key to fixed defense/block. Add a small authored fit lookup in these owners, then consume it in actual guard cost and the hook aftermath branch at `feature_expansion_battle.js:335`. Keep Native Combat, actor body and equipment authoritative. No random-ego framework or parallel material inventory is needed. ToME evidence T5 ties real material/modifier fields to Block; T3 separates eligibility from useful choices.

**Persistence and exclusions:** new ordinary item IDs/variants require current key/registration/validation support and an explicit compatibility decision. Old harness keys and existing sessions retain original results. Any new encounter fit snapshot must be versioned with the session, with missing old fields retaining old semantics. Save all stock, equipment, vitals and outcome in the existing staged envelope; no new currency, grade ladder, slot or source acquisition.

**First action and later gate:** trace the live fixed-frame projection and design the two exact fit profiles, then bind crafting, equipment, preview and committed outcome. Later use generated saves on a separate origin to compare the same native guarded attack across the three fits, verify the hook resistance affects only its declared stamina consequence, remove gear and confirm loss of benefit, reload equipment/remaining stock, and inject failed persistence with no published gear/vital change. Director judges whether the tradeoff is useful. No gate ran here. Medium implementation cost.

## 2. Maintain one finite brace instead of spreading protection

**Goal and player action:** a player who already owns Brace control, has current active source/body support, mastery6 and Shared frame training can choose **Maintain one brace** on one real ally. It remains for two incoming hostile attempts instead of the current one-blow guard. Existing mastery6 protection of two targets remains the broader alternative.

**Provisional costs and aftermath:** pay2 stamina to enter, then1 stamina before each incoming attempt at the maintained guard, with maximum two attempts. Each attempt consumes one capacity even on a miss. If upkeep cannot be paid, drop protection before mitigation. Release is explicit; choosing a strike/counter releases the brace first. Expiry, helper release, unsupported body/gear or encounter settlement clears it. Activation and spent upkeep are not refunded. Actual defended outcomes can still earn the existing one next-choice defensive window; a preview or an evaded attack cannot invent one. These values and policies are original proposals, not ToME's stamina-capacity reservation rule.

**Reuse and missing binding:** `feature_expansion_powers.js` owns mastery and Shared frame; `previewFeatureBattleAction`, `commitFeatureBattleAction`, current guards/receipts and `featureNativeAction` own actual choice/cadence. Extend one guard record with finite remaining attempts and explicit maintained mode, and teach the existing native guard consumer how to pay, consume and clear it. No shared sustain engine, parallel clock or new mastery bar. ToME T2 shows held defense needs explicit cost and cleanup; T1 makes mastery improve a particular function. Native `combat_stances.js` already offers defensive stat tradeoffs, so a renamed stance alone would add nothing.

**Persistence and exclusions:** use a new explicitly admitted native session revision/schema or equivalent compatibility decision; old sluice schemas3/4 preserve their frozen guard semantics. New fields need bounded validation and cause/action receipts. Upkeep, RNG/action counter, damage, opportunity and guard state commit together through `persistExpansionPlan`; never charge from render or an independent timer. No offscreen automatic enrollment or infinite paid practice.

**First action and later gate:** read the current one-blow guard removal at `feature_expansion_battle.js:321-340` and freeze the finite guard/cleanup contract before adding UI. Later exercise one and two targets, two attempts, miss, insufficient upkeep, counter/release, helper departure and settlement; reload between attempts; rejected stale actions and failed storage must neither consume upkeep nor retain a published new guard. Compare the alternative against spreading guards in the same encounter. Director assesses pacing. No gate ran here. Medium cost with a meaningful session-compatibility decision.
