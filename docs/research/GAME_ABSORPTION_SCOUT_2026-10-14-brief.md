# Next game absorption research brief

Use web search and direct source links. Research date: October 14, 2026. Help the creative director of a browser-based, text-first idle RPG decide which games to study and selectively absorb next, based on the existing research and actual adopted mechanics below. Seek meaningful additions, deeper useful readings of known references, surprising combinations, small details, and overlooked games. Absorb means translate mechanics/design practices into our game; a recommendation is not implementation authorization.

## Accepted direction

YAIRPG combines ordinary adventure, idle practice and queued work, lived NPCs, useful professions, knowledge and discoveries, material/tool chains, distinctive powers, and many mechanically different worlds. Canon-first priorities are Harry Potter, D&D, book worlds including A Soldier's Life, Mother of Learning, LOTM/COI, Shadow Slave and Reincarnation Paradise, and anime systems including JJK, Bleach, Fate, HxH, FMA and Naruto. Original worlds and AU/parallel variants are also allowed. World rules differ; reasonable options should be reachable where fiction and means support them. Random starting points should preserve suspense; Frontier is not a required player opening. Desktop/laptop UI should use the screen effectively; mobile optimization is deferred. First milestone of one realm/one prestige sequences delivery, not a permanent scope ceiling. Existing saves and the native campaign remain intact. Hunger should offer useful food benefits while unfed ordinary play stays comfortable, with no hunger death. Skills may advance through sustained conditions or queued activity rather than demanding repeated active clicks.

## Existing adoption, with limits

Local October 14 source adoption audit classifies 597 reference-row entries across 77 game/aggregate routes: 43 landed source consumers, 328 partial, 203 untouched, 20 parked, 1 rejected and 2 not applicable. Repeated report/packet rows are not separate mechanics; these counts do not prove gameplay, human acceptance or whole-source-game parity. External research cannot verify local code; treat this summary as supplied evidence.

Existing finite consumers include ACS-inspired contextual balm, supported cooling refinement with paid inputs/failure and actual tool use, negotiated duty with kept/interrupted/refused outcomes; finite saved work plans with support rechecks, stock hysteresis and explicit pauses; Book of Hours-style guided/diagnostic study with manual, procedure, tool, bench and fatigue kept distinct; a Factorio-inspired sap/fuel retort with resin/residue capacity and useful downstream crafting; earned feats/conduct and identified/charged objects; Elin/KeeperRL/Idling-to-Rule-the-Gods-inspired resident work, real kit/control/stock/pay and phased facilities; skill mastery and preparation that change a useful function; causal NPC memories, promises, household biographies and later offers; profession study/practice/execution and teaching for coins/time/favors/free. These are selected consumers, not autonomous full societies, general institution information spread, universal medicine or a replacement engine.

Proto23 original source and xeus24 mod have been studied deeply. UI work includes staged panel/skill discovery, semantic colors, Vivid palette, item cues, craft selection/detail/receipt, adjustable desktop panels and settings. Some have bounded browser evidence; broader import, unplayed branches and human taste gates vary by owner. Please do not recommend simply adding generic skill unlocks, a second scheduler, a second inventory, generic mastery, or generic NPC memory as if absent. Instead identify the specific missing additional consumer or interaction.

Current reassessed opportunities already include tool energy/byproduct recycling; local batch alignment with a useful later cut; origin-qualified keys/access; modification of a particular existing item; functional relic carriers across transitions; institution gifts versus services; wards that persist/change after their support stops; typed capped recovery; safe away progress stopping at a meaningful choice; and seeded workshop problems with multiple reachable solutions. These are unaccepted candidates. Do not reproduce old blanket exclusions: spatial scenes, richer scripting, follower succession and AU convergence remain possible later with dependencies. Always-online server timers remain a poor match for the selected browser-local runtime; premature lookahead dialogue effects are a known rejected mechanism.
