# Recursions, laws and crossing contracts

Bounded source digest, not a complete rulebook. All source rules below are STATED or SHOWN in public publisher materials. YAIRPG contracts remain INFERRED proposals.

## Four laws change which methods make sense

The publisher's conversion article S04 includes the [S14 focus/law table](https://www.montecookgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/NU-CHAR-OPS-FOCI1.jpg). It assigns Numenera-derived foci to Strange operating laws. This is official adaptation guidance, not the complete core focus catalogue or an exhaustive set of laws.

| Law | Shown focus example / supported distinction | What is not established |
|---|---|---|
| Magic, with Ardeyn as example | Consorts With the Dead. S10's spirit-questioning route makes a similar local specialty useful for investigation | Not every magic world has the same spirits, spells or afterlife |
| Standard Physics, with Earth as example | Fights Dirty and other ordinary physical competencies | Cyphers are a cross-world exception; this label does not mean every unusual PC ability disappears |
| Mad Science, with Ruk as example | Fuses Mind and Machine; biotechnology and impossible engineering fit this classification | Not an assertion that every device needs the same fuel or every Mad Science world shares Ruk's society |
| Psionics | Separates Mind From Body and other mental/spatial expressions are assigned here | No full psionics recursion geography, fuel economy or advancement path was read |

SHOWN size signal: S14 has 5 Magic, 5 Standard Physics, 5 Mad Science and 6 Psionics focus names, plus 4 requiring special consideration. The table belongs to a Numenera-options conversion article; it is not a universal focus availability matrix. Its markers are retained in the private source image, without inventing a new rule from an unexplained glyph.

S13 printed p.10 shows a conditional mixed-law recursion: Samurai Sky is normally Standard Physics, with Magic available during particular lunar phases. Printed p.123's law-protection cypher also distinguishes targets by the law they depend on. This is operational content, not just a world theme.

## Two exceptions worth retaining

**SHOWN, S13 printed p.123:** a power-source cypher can fuel varied machinery and can restore a depleted artifact, consuming itself. Depletion therefore is not automatically permanent destruction. A later YAIRPG repair/recharge rule should follow the actual item's supported mechanism.

**SHOWN, same page:** a prison cypher produces a pocket destination and a key, using inapposite-style transfer for its captive. This distinguishes destination, access key and ongoing captivity. It does not prove every tiny dimension permits residence, ordinary jobs or a free exit. No permanent one-attempt lock should be imported into YAIRPG from an unrelated item.

## Authoring a new recursion

What is verified: fictional leakage, deliberate construction and evolved/emergent places coexist (S03/S13); a supplement advertises personal-recursion rules and a design/maintenance sheet (S06). What is unread: exact seeds, costs, size progression, ownership powers, maintenance and default laws. A proposed authoring workflow for YAIRPG can still use K1 design provenance, K2 executable law, K4 arrival, K13 useful verb, K15 crossing, K17 instance/continuity and K18/K33 persistence. That is MWTS-P06, not a recovered tabletop construction algorithm.
