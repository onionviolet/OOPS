The 2014 Strange rules and 2025 generic CSRD are separate editions/products. This pass does not infer a new Strange edition from current generic rules. No search hit from unauthorized rulebook hosts was opened, downloaded or used as rule evidence. Search results containing those hosts were incidental and rejected. No source code, engine build or digital implementation of The Strange was inspected.

Raw HTML, official PDFs, DOCX, parsed text, page maps, manifest and SHA-256 inventory stay in `~/.codex/research/mw-the-strange/extract/`. No bulk source prose enters the repo. Published text here is paraphrase and small names/field labels. No claim that an SRD license makes The Strange setting or all MCG content open.
