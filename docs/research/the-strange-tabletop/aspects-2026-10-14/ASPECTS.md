# A01-A12: public tabletop aspect pass

Scope: public publisher text, two legitimate free PDFs and targeted generic CSRD reading. All twelve aspects are PARTIAL because this is not a full commercial-rulebook or digital-build audit. A software-only facility may be not present in the inspected tabletop corpus without being absent from every licensed tool. Sources resolve through [SOURCES](../SOURCES.md).

## A01: Dialogue and conversation

**Status: PARTIAL.** Human-run scenes contain small-talk topics, NPC motives, conditional reactions to what the PCs say, and different information after the fight. Printed pp.10-12: first conversation, interrupted conversation, aftermath. No executable dialogue graph, speaker portrait data or exhaustive line inventory was inspected.

**Implementation medium:** Printed scene headings, bullet responses and parenthetical GM conditions; a human GM interprets them. Private original text is retained in the official PDF, not copied into the repo.

**Evidence:** SHOWN authored text/fields; STATED publisher explanations; YAIRPG takeaways below are INFERRED. [S10](https://assetteam.montecookgames.com/wp-content/uploads/The-Hum-MCG-Asset-Team-Adventure.pdf)
