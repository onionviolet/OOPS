The strongest design lesson is that a power should change how a character prepares, works or solves a problem while keeping its actual fuel, tool allocation, timing and aftermath. Orb also separates retained mastery from current allocation. These are useful system-level implications beyond the four immediately scoped proposals.

## Connected paths and deeper branches

**Preparation and ordinary work.** Gather Knowledge and Construct spend actual resources and feed Knowledge/Skill, casting/mastery and further progress. ManaForge’s Aura modifies Building resource gain and Workshop structure build speed/power rating; those records reach real resource and construction consumers. Haste instead creates a separate18-second-base status and returns its spell to Idle, allowing a later loadout swap while the status continues. Active ManaForge/Meditation holds casting state and prevents that swap. The player chooses a paid timing sequence, with charges and the benefit window retained. OOC-SPELL-022/025.

Quick trades more input and weight for shorter recovery; it does not directly increase cast speed. Efficient reduces both upfront and held-drain cost with a power tradeoff. Resourceful adds an **AttributesDeveloped** recovery module, so actual completed construction can contribute recharge. Recovery modules also use time or total spell casts. Inactive loadout recharge has an authored1.2% base factor, not1.2x; the matching firstpass `BigDouble.AsPercent` getter confirms the units. OOC-SPELL-008/009/021/026.

**Finite external spell dispatch and its exception.** Reverberate selects active Cantrips once in loadout order and queues delayed External records. It spends its own Spark/weight/recovery, while the targets bypass their normal charge/affordability/upfront payment/manual XP route. ChannelSpark and ChannelBlaze are eligible Cantrips: external dispatch retains their Mana-per-second drain and duration but skips the manual channel-interruption call. Concurrent source-visible channels still stop on depleted support. Delayed External records survive outgoing loadout swap; only Echo records are removed. These are unusual paid sequencing rules, not proof of a perpetual autocast loop. OOC-SPELL-023/024.

Automagic Tome actually amplifies registered Aura structures; it does not select spells or call Fire. Its contribution depends on current enabled quantity/effective support. Efficient/Meditation/Haste also feed timed tokens whose effective benefits cap and decay after supply stops. Acquisition, current allocation, temporary status and token rate remain different owners. OOC-SPELL-029/030.

**Wood, capacity, supports and improved production.** Extract Wood drains authored2200Wood/s and reserves one Mechanical capacity to support four Biotic capacity. Transmute Wood then reserves three Alchemical plus one Biotic, drains Control and produces Wood while increasing gains from other Wood sources. The first process consumes Wood; the second does not. Input starvation propagates supported work even where nominal capacity remains displayed. OOC-PROD-003/004/020; AGRO-15.

Wooden Supports consumes Skill/Wood/ForceBark, takes30seconds at its base recipe, and produces real support stock. Notes spends Supports/Paper to improve Scholar’s Book and Magic Book; their actual capacity/rate effects reach Knowledge and Mana. Woodbeams spends Supports/Wood to improve Wood Axe and Charger. These are real downstream consumers, not decorative intermediate names. OOC-PROD-021.

The material-crafting type’s actual multi-speed penalty is0, even though generic code supports parallel-count penalties. Its bulk magnitude loss/time and baseline automation-speed factor0.1 remain meaningful. The **active/passive cap distinction describes modifier categories**, not whether a human clicked: both manual and automatic ResourceGenerator rates are Passive; direct instant/final remainder Gain may use active overflow. A generic default formula cannot stand in for this build’s tuned route. OOC-PROD-002/005/006/022.

**Artifact-assisted brewing and persistent enchantment.** Brewing Rod improves actual Brewing power/speed/mastery-related rate; faster production also raises input drain per second. A Woodwork Potion brew occupies Mechanical/Biotic allocation, then pays finite consumable preparation and Toxicity for a brief Building/Parchment quality/gain effect. Quality reaches effective material spending. Philosopher’s Catalyst instead expands Alchemical capacity and changes global drain/power-group effects. Both are Tools, with one baseline type slot, so the starting choice excludes simultaneous allocation unless another earned expansion supports it. OOC-PROD-023/024.

Paid scribing creates a real scroll, then use pays preparation and Toxicity before targeting a structure. Advancement/Power/Speed enchantments reach the exact IEnchantable’s BonusLevels/Power/BuildSpeed/EffectLevel, retain across ordinary initialization and reject inferior same-kind replacements. Invalid targets can fizzle after expenditure with no refund in the reviewed branch. EquipmentSO itself does not implement IEnchantable in this assembly; do not label this artifact enchantment. The older BrewingStation schema has empty instance lists and null old recipe outputs/effects, so its structural inventory does not establish active facility play. OOC-PROD-013/025/026; OOC-SPELL-027/028.

**Agromancy changes the source itself.** The selected route consumes two empty units for one Oak, two Oaks for one Force tree, then two Force trees for one Ironwood, retaining2/4/8units of occupied land through parent reservations and growing/resting phases. Its actual action time differs from maturation: continuous fuel support, parent size/quality and speed affect work; selected transformations are serial while growing timers can run in parallel. Empty land recovery means removing a tree does not instantly make all space usable. AGRO-01–05.

Force trees can be destroyed for ongoing wood/bark, peeled into resting/regrowth, transformed into Ironwood, committed to treasure, or consumed for Enrich advancement XP. Fruit/treasure collections use completion rewards and retain occupancy/recovery; the selected Ironwood harvest produces a persistent material rate during work and destroys a tree at completion. Cancellation after output has accrued consumes the active harvest batch, while an unproductive transformation can release its parents with spent fuel retained. AGRO-06–12.

Actual harvested Ironwood feeds Extract Ironwood: continuous input funds Alchemical/Biotic/Occultic functional capacity. The modifier adds quoted nominal max and separately carries `usageDrainPenalty` support into downstream resource drains. It does not simply refund essence or shrink the displayed nominal amount in proportion to starvation. ExpertAlchemy16 is a soft use gate with its own alchemy-type penalties, which differ from spell penalties. AGRO-13–15. A treasure-acquisition/logging success-flag anomaly is retained as source-visible and runtime-unverified, not a reproduced gameplay failure. AGRO-18.

**Late restoration and retained progress.** Paid discovery offers are saved with their exact IDs/rarity/selection/RNG. Required visible items such as Surrender and Restoration can bypass the random pool. Other offers can include a useful future method with explicit missing readiness; rerolls have finite saved charges and their own exclusions. Confirmation chooses one discovery and escalates later cost. OC-P01–04/16.

Successful Surrender attaches a duration-bound passive. Its actual character-death reaction produces Divine Fragments; the active filter is broader than the descriptive reference to aberrations. Expiration removes the passive by GUID. Fragments plus Hexsteel pay Death Stone after Life Stone; Death Stone and Beacon visibility gate the Restoration upgrade/books/discovery, then its paid level70 ritual raises WorldComplete through the actual scene listener into the finale and reset manager. Research duration/chaining effects change these future paid instances and expire with their owners. OC-P18/19/22/24.

World completion plus fetched challenges enables the modern NG+ UI. Generic reset captures only qualifying saveable IPersistentReset objects, resets others and salts the global seed. Time-rune mastery/XP/seen survive, while current rune level/discovery/rarity are cleared by the callback. Retained total mastery supplies Starting Time Advancements; Advancement Persist supplies retained Advancement Boost; later Persist Persist investment improves its own future investment power. Current allocations consume the resulting budget. OC-P09–12/20/21/25.

All11 reviewed ResearchType reset flags are false, including **Enduring**. Twenty time advancement templates are persistent;28 reviewed non-time templates are not. Current-run permanent ritual effects reset. Manager-restored reset/previous-budget counters differ from generic persistent flags, and Time Played has separate save metadata. Names and displayed progress do not define reset retention. OC-P11/17/25. No Orb whole-run reset is selected for YAIRPG: its existing Field renewal and one-prestige policy remain.

## Failure, interruption and pacing

| Source path | Real aftermath retained |
| --- | --- |
| Manual held spell | Upfront payment remains; ending removes its held effects/drain and consumes the manual charge. Resource depletion can end it. |
| Loadout switch | Current cast/preparation/charging/input blocks switching; allowed switches retain spent spell objects and use attunement. Status effects and delayed records have distinct rules. |
| Alchemy/craft shortfall | Ongoing support can throttle real progress/output. Manual prepaid jobs and repeated drains have different costs/cancel rules; no universal refund is inferred. |
| Scroll fizzle | A prepared/paid finite consumable can have no valid target; the reviewed branch provides no restoration. |
| Plant transformation/harvest | Parents, growing/resting occupancy, accrued output and spent fuel determine release versus active-batch destruction. |
| Early ritual failure | Wave-scaled instant rewards occur before fractional raw-activation refund; duration/permanent/completion-event rewards are skipped. |
| Five-wave ritual death | IsFailedRun checks fewer than five waves, while full completion also checks resource fill. Source-visible Restoration End can raise WorldComplete after five-wave death with incomplete fill. Runtime unverified; YAIR must use an explicit coherent success gate. |
| Automatic buying | Busy queue/disabled/zero capacity resets its baseline timer. Actual cheap purchases still debit stock and build; it does not automate plant-chain choices or ordinary spell Fire. |
| Research pause/cancel | Pause retains invested progress; cancel clears the investment/queue. Capacity reservation and consumed fill resources remain separate. |

GameManager uses FixedUpdate and the load path restores saved state/time. No reviewed managed caller feeds elapsed save-age into catch-up simulation. This is a qualified negative source finding, not a runtime guarantee about Unity background focus or closed-app behavior. OC-P13/14. An active/idle percentage, fresh-run balance, performance and actual exception reproduction remain unmeasured.
