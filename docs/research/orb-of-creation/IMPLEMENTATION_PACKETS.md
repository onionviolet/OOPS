# Original Orb of Creation adaptation packets

Four ranked proposals after current-source reconciliation. These are research handoffs, not runtime selection or implementation. Orb inspiration does not supply YAIRPG lore, body, tools, fuel or institutional permission. Prices and timing are reversible prototypes. The wider mechanic/dependency map remains in [REPORT](REPORT.md) and [FINDINGS](FINDINGS.json).

All packets preserve the familiar opening, one realm/one prestige first milestone, private saves, earlier frozen jobs and the existing native inventory/actor/body/custody/knowledge/job/site/clock/publisher owners. Each later gameplay/save/director gate is deferred, not passed. Machine details and current file hashes are in [PACKETS](PACKETS.json).

## 1. Useful undertrained cooling with an honest price (ORB-P01)

**Player journey**

1. Actually learn Eda’s cooling source with a charged sleeve, then complete one paid supervised trial.
2. At mastery one or two, compare an early grip-cooling application against two further trials and the existing mastery-three action. Proposed early terms: one charged sleeve, four stamina and three played minutes; mastered action remains one sleeve and two stamina immediately.
3. Completion returns the empty sleeve and applies the same four-minute bounded grip field. It grants no mastery, damage, passage or free tool. Oren’s ordinary jab/guard remain usable.
4. Cancel or lose body/tool/target/source support during preparation: return explicitly reserved inputs, produce no field and retain elapsed time. Expiration follows the existing supported local clock.

**Current owners and precise gap**

The source/knowledge/three paid trials, physical sleeve charge conversion and actual hook field consumer already exist. Undertrained application is absent; this is not a new cooling family.

- A separately declared early application with mastery-one/two bounds and frozen higher costs/duration, preserving the established mastered action.
- The workshop validator currently requires completed reference_gameplay.cool_hook and its exact knowledge cause (reference_gameplay_host.js69–72). Admit a qualified new early receipt explicitly; never forge a mastered-action completion. Retain old valid fields/jobs.
- A generic per-application cooldown formula is not already present: startCommand167 reads a definition cooldown. The smallest proof uses a declared preparation job, leaving dynamic cooldown generalization unselected.

**Retained consequences and compatibility**

No teacher consent is needed after learning except where the existing action requires it. Body, actual source, owned charge, local rules, target, occupational/custody gates remain firm. Preparation refunds are explicit; learned mastery is never removed or granted by the new application.

Use existing optional workshop/condition/knowledge owners; version the new command/receipt admission while retaining existing schema1 workshop and old condition causes. Absence stays inert. Keep current/frozen jobs on original rules.

**Later observable gate**

- Generated actor earns the lesson and one trial, then the early quote shows and spends distinct actual costs.
- Early completion yields one conserved empty sleeve and the real hook condition once; missing means and stale/support-lost requests fail without field or reward.
- Mastery-three action remains cheaper and immediate. Actual hook pull is prevented only while the paid field exists; jab/guard and expiration remain distinct.
- Later save/reload/import and failed publication preserve exact receipt/charge/condition outcomes.

## 2. A tool-and-method combination that changes a real production bottleneck (ORB-P02)

**Player journey**

1. Earn the existing paired prism frame and Eda’s learned mastery-three cooling. Keep actual sap and charged sleeves.
2. At the one fitting bench choose ordinary single setting, fast paired setting, or precise controlled setting. Proposed two-resin fast batch: two sap, two charges, two stamina, four minutes. Precise batch: two sap, one charge, three stamina, six minutes plus the learned method. Ordinary single route remains unchanged.
3. Reserve the actual frame for either new batch. On completion return it, produce two existing Set repair resin, and return one empty sleeve per spent charge.
4. Use the resin with two real sound staves through the existing two-kit recipe and actual trade/preparation consumers. Charge conservation, speed, effort and bench availability produce different choices.

**Current owners and precise gap**

Vela’s loose/fitted prisms and Eda’s sleeve→resin→kit chain already have real source/native consumers. Their cross-system charge/time tradeoff is absent; no generic artifact, alchemy or stock engine is missing.

- Two new paid batch definitions binding the existing paired physical frame to the existing charged-sleeve/sap/resin items, with precise cooling eligibility only for the economical branch.
- Native-bag carrier reservation/return for the cross-module use, source/body/completion support and knowledge disclosure; the existing artifact compiler can provide held-carrier semantics but a selected integrator must join it to the cooling host.
- A read-only comparison of actual charge/time/effort and downstream native-kit output. Do not apply Orb’s abstract global bandwidth or universal tool-slot limit to every YAIR power.

**Retained consequences and compatibility**

The actual one-capacity bench and held apparatus block competing use. Cancellation or support loss returns reserved physical ingredients without finished resin; elapsed time remains. Spent charges convert to empty sleeves only on completed setting. Future recharge still consumes finite salt.

Existing stock/item keys and original job revisions remain; new optional command/receipt IDs require compatible registry admission. No abstract resource pool, quality schema, inventory, scheduler or save publisher.

**Later observable gate**

- Compare ordinary/fast/precise routes at equal two-sap funding; inspect distinct charge count, actual elapsed time, effort and same two-resin output.
- Attempt to use/sell/disassemble the reserved frame elsewhere and observe existing occupancy/custody rejection.
- Missing source blocks only precise use; missing apparatus blocks new combinations while ordinary setting still works.
- Craft the actual native kits from the resin, then later verify cancel/support loss and save/reload/failure conservation.

## 3. A retained plant can become a different finite material source (ORB-P03)

**Player journey**

1. Finish Nell’s existing crop first. Separately acquire one newly authored, finite rooted cutting from her actual stock with disclosed payment; no seed/crop is silently replenished.
2. Plant it in the actual permitted bed. Once its growth is observed, choose to clip it for a small immediate herb/fiber input or spend real material/effort on a woody transformation.
3. The transformed plant retains bed occupancy and a disclosed finite harvest allowance. Two later supported local harvests could yield actual Wet repair staves; retiring or uprooting closes the same object without a second reward.
4. Dry the actual wet staves through existing ordinary/braced/shelf routes, then use sound staves in kit/brace work. A known controlled-cooling application may shorten one declared preparation at a real charge cost, without conjuring a plant or stock.

**Current owners and precise gap**

Nell’s one-terminal crop, timed draft/cover/early/salvage routes and finite seed allocation already exist, as do finite Rill renewal rounds. A transformed persistent plant with mutually exclusive outputs and retained bed occupancy is not provided by that existing terminal crop.

- An explicitly accepted second content object and finite actual cutting stock, with stable identity, legal bed claim, branch state and remaining harvest allowance. Preserve the first crop untouched.
- Retained plant occupancy between jobs, plus supported local growth/transform/harvest consumers; site job capacity alone ends with the job and does not keep a mature plant occupying the bed.
- Exact physical wet-stave output into existing drying and kit consumers, and one optional learned preparation consumer with real body/source/charge requirements.

**Retained consequences and compatibility**

Clipping foregoes woody conversion; conversion spends stock and ties up the bed; harvests deplete the explicit allowance. Shortage pauses or rejects before a fresh commitment. Completed effort and a live transformed plant remain on interruption; uprooting grants only its disclosed salvage. No browser-closed growth or speculative global seasons.

Optional additive actor/world facts, claims and selected native item/command IDs with validated new enrollment. Do not replace current crop receipts/timers or infer old-save plants. One existing local clock and publisher.

**Later observable gate**

- Buy the same finite cutting once, plant it, and choose immediate clipping versus transformation with different actual outputs.
- Observe occupied-bed rejection, finite harvest depletion and interruption with the same plant state.
- Complete a material harvest, dry the wet stave and consume the sound stave in existing work.
- Later save/reload/failure and absence-freeze checks preserve the same plant, remaining harvest allowance, costs and stock.

## 4. Paid investigation offers saved, usable leads instead of repeated rerolling (ORB-P04)

**Player journey**

1. After the existing firsthand cutting and drying observations, pay Eda for a separate optional investigation using actual payment, participant effort and played time.
2. Completion reveals two authored leads drawn once from eligible, observed work domains, for example precise charge alignment versus woody-crop conditioning. At least one has a presently reachable action; a future-use lead shows known missing means. Essential campaign evidence stays guaranteed through its existing route.
3. Choose one lead, defer it, or keep reading the same offers. Confirmation gives attributed actor knowledge and the specific next-command discovery, while actual learning/source/tools/stock/access remain separate.
4. Exercise the linked original work consumer in P02/P03 when qualified. Reopening or later reload preserves the original offer IDs and chosen cause; it never gives a new roll for free.

**Current owners and precise gap**

Actual-observation research and fixed mutually exclusive specializations are already present. The general seeded paid-outcome and saved event/choice services exist, but this work-domain offer-set producer and confirm-to-real-consumer binding are absent.

- A small eligible pool with actor/NPC knowledge boundaries, one frozen selected offer bundle per accepted cause and explicit stage/disposition validation. A finite authored outcome table can use the existing seeded command resolver; no new RNG service is needed.
- Projection of known versus currently executable leads, explicit once-only confirmation, and next command bindings to the P02/P03 consumers. A hint is never a grant of their source, stock or access.
- Saved offer/selection validation using the existing fact/event/knowledge owners. Paid reroll, if selected at all, needs an explicit finite cost and a new receipt; no free reopen redraw.

**Retained consequences and compatibility**

Money reaches Eda’s purse; both people spend their own effort and occupied time. Interrupted reserved research refunds only its disclosed reservations and gives no offers. Completed offers persist through deference; foregone choice remains an attributed decision rather than erased history.

Explicit optional cause/offer facts or admitted event revision with absence inert; old deterministic research/specialization and frozen jobs remain unchanged. One existing RNG, native save, clock and inventory. No run reset or new prestige.

**Later observable gate**

- Generated actor with actual observation receipts accepts paid investigation, sees conserved payment/effort and one saved offer set.
- Compare ready/future leads with truthful known requirements, choose one and execute its actual later consumer.
- Reopen, reload, stale confirm and failed publication preserve one roll, one choice and one grant.
- Unknown content is excluded from searchable Notebook projections and the existing mandatory campaign route remains available.
