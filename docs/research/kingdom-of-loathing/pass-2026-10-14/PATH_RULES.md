# Every enumerated path: rules, mechanics, rewards and reuse

Scope: 57 enum entries, including 51 special challenge paths, three dietary paths, Standard, Bad Moon and no-path. These are concise distinguishing rules, not every interaction exception. No run was played.

**STATED** means the dated wiki source says it. **SHOWN** means the client models it. **INFERRED** marks a mechanism interpretation or detail still lacking an individual rule-page check. Every reuse/add split is INFERRED. The archived index supports all 51 special-path reward lists and high-level descriptions; missing individual pages leave detailed exceptions OPEN.

Reward scope: the [August 2026 index](https://web.archive.org/web/20260822021427/https://wiki.kingdomofloathing.com/Special_challenge_path) gives in-season extra Karma (50 Normal / 100 Hardcore) and an out-of-season first-path bonus (50), plus a path statuette. It also lists exceptions/extra outputs, some obtained during a run rather than as a completion payout. Grey Goo has its own stated seasonal Karma exception, so do not apply the general formula blindly. Ordinary Hardcore stainless gear is listed only where the index says so. Factoids are bestiary information, tattoos/trophies are cosmetic goals, points are a separate persistent-client field. A reward name is not proof that it can still be earned under every mode/date.

Dietary-name pages for Teetotaler and Oxygenarian in the new archive are tattoo pages, not detailed dietary rule pages. Their consumption bans/capacities are SHOWN in the client. No special seasonal reward is established for those modes here.
