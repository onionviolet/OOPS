**P3/P5/P7 (PROPOSALS):** keep discovered, visible, selected, simulated and retained states distinct. A selected panel should not decide whether paid work advances. Target 1440x900, with an affected 1366x768 check: keep useful overview, detail and activity panels visible, rather than collapsing routine work into one scroll. Long help may scroll within a reading region. Mobile optimization is deferred, and human layout acceptance stays separate from rebuilt desktop proof.
