The useful next depth is the connection between **information, means, action, elapsed time and aftermath**. A fictional toxin can be recognized without a known remedy; a remedy can be understood without medicine in hand; treatment can be possible but late; a correct report can be disbelieved. Those should become different playable states when a selected scene needs them. Extra bars or a universal perception number do not establish these interactions.

### G1: a body state should change a practical decision

Native bindings: `src/main.js/update_health`, `do_resting`, `do_sleeping`, `add_active_effect`, `update_effect_durations`, weather/exposure section of `update`; `src/active_effects.js`; `src/models/hero.js/addActiveEffectBonus`; `src/models/person.js` stat recomputation; `src/weather.js`; location types in `src/data/locations.js`. Native `src/conditions.js` is an eligibility evaluator despite its name; it is not an illness/injury store.

| Distinction | Candidate interaction and payoff | State/implementation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Damage versus injury versus exertion | Rest restores stamina; a sore ankle still blocks a delicate crossing; a supplied brace or alternate route lets the player continue | HP/stamina exist. A persistent ankle condition and its route rule are proposed. Do not infer wound healing from full HP or fatigue from HP loss |
| Exposure versus current severity | A warm meal improves cold tolerance before a rainy outing; wet clothing shortens the safe exposure window; shelter stops exposure before full recovery | Wet, tolerance and cold counters exist. Declare stage transitions, accumulation/decay and recovery separately. A timer alone should not repeatedly re-inflict an expired condition |
| Temporary versus recurring/chronic | A short-lived toxin effect can expire; a recurring bodily incompatibility may require sustained treatment, a repair or a source change | Use timed instances only where time resolves the cause. Persistent conditions need named treatment/recovery transitions, not arbitrarily huge duration values |
| Treatment versus cause removal | A medicine can relieve the effect while contaminated equipment remains hazardous; cleaning the equipment prevents recurrence without instantly restoring stamina | Record exposure source, condition instance and applied intervention separately when exercised. No real toxicology or medical simulation is required; original reagents/rules remain fictional |
| Susceptibility versus resistance versus immunity | An organic remedy works on a living body; a synthetic body needs a compatible repair process; an exceptional source may simply resist the threat | Ask for named faculties and target tags, not a universal human anatomy ladder. Immune bodies need viable content. Gene/cybernetic growth remains with the source-development owner |
| Sensing versus interpretation | An eye detects residue while smell is unavailable; a local manual identifies its meaning; an instrument supplies another route | The clue producer declares signal/modality/range/obstruction; G2 interpretation decides what can be concluded. A missing sense must not silently erase factual world state |

For the first condition, display the effect and a useful response, not six physiology bars. Preserve variants: recovery by rest, gradual treatment, a facility visit, equipment substitution, a bodily adaptation, or retreat. Pick only those used by the authored obstacle. Aftermath should change a later plan, such as needing a slower route while carrying a fragile load. A meaningful complication is more useful than a debuff that never affects an action.

Native health loss fields often intentionally store negative stat additions (`health_loss_flat: -0.5`, for example); `update` reverses that sign when computing damage. Do not report that convention as an inverted-damage bug without tracing the whole calculation. Likewise, native poison-resistance skill descriptions are not proof of a diagnosis or targeted antidote system.

Open design: thresholds/severity, treatment power, recurring causes, automatic versus prompted response and whether serious chronic conditions belong in this idle game. No unattended lethal, aging or permanent-loss policy is selected. Revisit numerical balance only after a body state changes a real choice.

### G2: distinguish knowing what from being able to do it

Native bindings: `src/items.js/BookData` and Book progress; `src/main.js/start_reading`, `do_reading`, native load reconstruction and `process_rewards`; `src/components/availability_component.js`; `src/conditions.js`; `src/rules.js`; `src/techniques.js`; `src/capabilities.js`; player facts in `src/main.js/expeditionContext`.

| Query | Example of an honest gate | Useful variant |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | “This residue matches the warning illustration” | A remembered clue, observed sample or local field guide can supply evidence; recognition can be uncertain |
| Comprehension | “You know why this treatment is appropriate here” | Translation, a mentor or a completed controlled trial; wrong/conflicting evidence is not just missing XP |
| Procedure access | “You can follow this recipe while the manual is available” | Borrowed instructions, retained memory or earned repeatability; source removal need not erase learned understanding |
| Execution competence | “You can measure/apply the formulation safely” | A narrow proficiency, supervised service or compatible instrument; no universal prerequisite tree |
| Means | “Five selected leaves and an accessible tier-1 station are required” | Purchased finished medicine, own production or commissioned work; knowledge never creates ingredients |
| Timing/access | “The remedy can arrive before the window closes” | Earlier diagnosis, a nearer service, a faster route or an authored stabilization step |
| Persuasion/evidence | “The recipient accepts your report because you showed a relevant sample” | Trust, recognized credentials or demonstrable evidence; truth alone does not update every NPC |

The first five queries can be implemented for one recipe without a belief simulator. The last query must reuse the actor owner's observer/evidence state when selected. A belief can be false; a player's discovery can be correct but stale; a canonical fact can be secret. `evaluateRule().known` indicates whether the caller supplied a usable fact, not whether the player has discovered it. A secret canonical fact passed into a UI evaluator can leak its value or reason text. Supply a projected known-fact view to display, while command validation uses authoritative legal state.

Maps should reveal supported route facts or risks, not automatically unlock travel rights or erase hazards. Appraisal can identify a material class without revealing charge/binding/defect. Diagnosis can narrow likely causes without granting a remedy. Date, location, source and confidence belong on selected evidence; do not model every trivia fact. Confidence is not an excuse for arbitrary random misinformation: author what observation can and cannot establish.

Native `language` currently selects UI translation; `Literacy` and reading progress do not implement diegetic local languages, script recognition or manual translation. Add one local script gate only if an actual document changes a plan. An imported technique can remain understood while its execution fails for body/fuel/environment reasons; retain that knowledge and explain the missing means using existing source/world contracts. Do not turn the Rat-step sequence into a compulsory sequence for every procedure or cheat.

Unresolved variants: manuals as reusable external instructions versus study unlocks; fuzzy identification versus confirmed samples; discovery retained personally versus shared in an institution. Revisit at the next contrasting knowledge handler, rather than defining a universal epistemic ontology now.

Follow-up, 2026-10-02: [practical insight in wandering zones](WANDERING_ZONES_AND_INSIGHT.md#1-insight-should-produce-usable-understanding) applies these G2 distinctions to noticed signals, interpreted monster patterns, tested counters and later automatic plans. It owns the new zone loop; this dossier continues to own the shared knowledge/means and G4 clock boundaries. Exact Insight skill roster and the wooded-outing proof remain proposals, not implemented interpretation or offline simulation.

|---|---|---|
| Recipe/skill/station access | Native recipe flags, relevant skill scaling, station tier, fixed inputs and result; “Medicine for dummies” already unlocks remedies | Stage selected native unlocks and recipe costs/result/XP before publication. A hidden recipe must also be rejected by its command, not only omitted by the UI |
| Tool ownership versus use | Native gathering checks equipped tool slots; a carried Survey rope provides the authored expedition capability | A tool in storage is not equipped or locally accessible. Preserve selected complete keys; do not consume the rare variant simply because it is listed first |
| Quality versus identity | Quality/component keys distinguish existing stack variants | Durability, charges, rolled properties, coatings and binding need stable instance/variant records plus reconstruction and ownership bindings. Adding arbitrary JSON key fields is insufficient because the decoder drops them |
| Production and custody | Shared acquisition can stage inventory owners and transfers | Selected storage operations and facility reservations need the same durable boundary. Personal supply, vendor stock, communal storage and borrowed equipment are different rights, not one unlimited pool |
| Compatibility/provenance | Existing material categories support native recipes | A cross-world material needs declared physical/energy use, origin and local substitutions when relevant. A word such as “spiritual steel” cannot prove compatibility. Powerful imported devices may work fully; fuel/repair are authored concerns |

Prototype practical research as **choose inputs → perform a small saved procedure → inspect a result → retain a discovered method → repeat production → use the output**. Every step should feed the ordinary inventory, recipe or capability system. Optional active experimentation can vary a property/yield or reveal an unknown; guided timed work can teach the same baseline method. Earned repeatability should reduce repeated decisions after discovery. Assembly can similarly produce a working tool, not a separate high-score panel.

Variants worth retaining: a fictional coating that changes one exposure rule; two ingredients with the same baseline use but different side properties; repair consuming materials and preserving object identity; a charge-limited device requiring a compatible source; a recipe discovered from a failed sample. These need separate proof after the fixed-input recipe. A system name or minigame score does not make them executable.

Wear/spoilage/upkeep are worthwhile only if they create a useful supply/repair decision. Do not add constant maintenance to every item. Author renewal deliberately: gathered consumables can renew, a unique specimen may not, a returned loan remains owned by its lender, and copying a recipe does not clone an ingredient. Native gathering tools have existing equipment roles; expanding their capabilities should respect those roles.

### G4: time and interruption belong to the commitment

Native bindings: `src/game_time.js`; `src/main.js/update_timer`, `progress_time`, `change_location`, `can_work`, activity update and effect update; native trade refresh; `src/expansion_engine.js/advanceSimulation`, pending jobs/opportunities/cancellation; scopes/transition proposals in ENGINE_EXPANSION and DIRECTION_SYNTHESIS.

| Boundary | Proposed explicit rule | Why a player cares |
|---|---|---|
| Simulation versus wall time | Give supported operations a common absolute simulated minute and a reason for advancement. Wall-time combat loops and render refresh are not calendar events | Work, rest and travel cross the same deadline consistently; changing animation speed cannot create extra opportunities |
| Boundary ordering | At one timestamp, resolve authored reservation/job completions, exposure/recovery, access expiry and newly available choices in a stated order; stable IDs break ties | A medicine completed exactly at a deadline either counts or does not, consistently. Granting access cannot retroactively authorize an already rejected action |
| Stop/resume | Propagate scheduler `remaining` and `stoppedAtChoice` to native time, location and related effects before publishing the jump | A blocking choice pauses the journey at the correct place/time instead of letting the calendar or exposure run ahead |
| Facilities/capacity | Reserve a selected workspace/tool/input at commitment; free it on declared completion/cancel/interruption; no generic unlimited helper slot | A paid station can be unavailable, another station can be viable, and cancellation cannot duplicate tools or refunds |
| Access and obligation | Save credential issuer/scope, earned state and any explicit expiry/duty separately from reputation | Knowledge can survive lost access; renewal can be another action; surrendering an office need not delete learned skill |
| Journey-end scope | A completed discovery can persist while an experiment remains paused locally, is abandoned, or returns cargo under a declared policy | Returning changes available plans without regenerating first rewards or silently carrying inaccessible local material |

Current bridging has material limits. `update_timer` first advances the native calendar, then asks expansion to advance. `advanceExpeditions` skips idle expansion clocks; it calls `persistExpansion(advanceSimulation(...))` without handling returned unspent time. Tests of expansion deadlines therefore do not prove common-clock correctness in native travel/sleep. New absolute deadlines cannot be based on the old expansion clock as though it has counted every minute of the player's life. The adapter must preserve remaining old job delays and choose an explicit saved anchor rather than retroactively firing missed events.

For the first laboratory proof, propose foreground simulated time only, no wall-time catch-up and no offline harm. An untimed active exercise freezes its lab progress while the game can continue its normal clock; it has no expiry. Timed work occupies the player using the existing assignment restriction. Voluntary cancellation forfeits only declared already committed costs; completion releases capacity and retains acquired knowledge. Interruption before commit costs nothing. Forced location change needs an explicit suspend/abort path. This narrow policy does not settle world absence, aging or future hostile unattended actions.

Later variants: bank offline time for player-controlled acceleration; safely finish previously committed risk-free jobs; stop at the first unknown choice; or simulate selected absent-world events using a disclosed bounded policy. Do not interpret `saved_at` as authorization to replay everything. A facility can persist in its local world even if the body changes; cargo, body, knowledge, rights and world history use separate existing transition domains. Cancellation, visit interruption, bodily death, true run-end, prestige and rewind remain different operations.

### G5: explanation is a runtime contract

For each surfaced action, use the same authoritative evaluation to derive **lead, disclosed requirements, exact inputs/kept tools, elapsed time, predicted effects/odds, uncertainty and next useful remedy**. Keep secret information out of that projection. Probability contributors should identify actual sources and capped/stacked behavior, not a fictional luck adjective. Unknown information should read as unknown; a known failed gate should name a plausible next action. Preview is pure, commit rechecks sequence/ownership/current access, and the saved receipt explains what happened.

Current new panels already provide real buttons, optional untimed decisions and saved responses. The Field journal's dynamic `previewText` renders duration/outcomes/contributors/rolls but does not render `preview.costs`; descriptions carry some costs, which will become brittle for changing recipes or access. Cancellation disables on `error` but not the shared `enabled` flag; command persistence guards still reject nonwriters. Native recipe/action surfaces include clickable divs with inline handlers; keyboard/touch parity is unverified. Apply accessibility work to the selected surfaces, including focus continuity through saved answers, textual ingredient/step controls and feedback announced after commit. Do not claim a whole-game accessibility audit from these source observations.

As leads grow, filter by known/available/in-progress/blocked/completed and local relevance, with a visible reason to revisit a blocked lead. Preserve prior discoveries; hiding completed content is a display choice. A report should summarize costs, output, new method and changed options, with deeper provenance on demand. Reopening or rendering cannot perform acquisitions/unlocks.

Native book/clear/skill loaders reconstruct derived unlocks via callbacks. Expansion publication rejects XP that crosses native content unlocks. Both are correct boundaries to respect, not reasons to call callbacks after a new save and hope they are harmless. Separate staged unlock flags and milestone state from reward/currency/item delivery. Hydration can restore a capability without awarding the first reward again. Keep permanent completion/ownership guards outside the capped prose history. Pruning the 100-entry history must not make a learned recipe or unique claim repeatable.

Performance remains unmeasured for the proposed expanded content. Current panels serialize broad state/context for render signatures, and new-system commits serialize the save envelope. Current actor/opportunity scans are reasonable small-cast precursors, not a demonstrated population simulator. Measure save size, commit/render duration and work per **deadline, receipt, input or meaningful state change**, using selected real jobs and generated stress fixtures. A larger fixture with arbitrary labels is not a gameplay proof. Cache/incremental invalidation should follow measured need; history pruning alone does not bound all live ledgers.
