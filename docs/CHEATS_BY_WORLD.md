# More cheats, world by world (2026-10-10)

Weibao: "more cheats and more? consider each book world accordingly."
These are 70 more cheat-like powers from our notes, beyond the 25
protagonist cheats already placed in [cheat cosmology](CHEAT_COSMOLOGY.md).
A Sonnet reader collected them on 2026-10-10 from our derived notes only;
Claude edited them. Everything is a proposal.

**Flags:** (U) means the notes leave the holder, rule or revocability
unclear. (G) means general franchise knowledge, bounded but not stated by
our notes. "Notes silent" means the notes don't say whether it can be
taken, stolen or lost, so by default it can be, by some route.

**Path prefixes:** `N` = `docs/research/novels`; `EG` =
`N/er-gen-haul-2026-10-09`; `FB` = `N/full-book`; `NW8` =
`N/novel-wave-2026-10-08`; `NW9` = `N/novel-wave-2026-10-09`; `W` =
`docs/worlds`; `D` = `docs`.

## What these add to the cosmology

- **A duping cheat with a feeding cost:** Meng Hao's copper mirror
  duplicates a tested item when fed enough spirit stones; half a stone
  fails. A ready-made model for "duping is allowed in some way".
- **Revocability shows up in every form:** a protective ring explicitly
  revocable by its patron (Renegade Immortal); a world will's loan that
  withdraws by itself (Warlock); a group power that depends on a vote
  (Omniscient Reader); vials that can't be recalled once drunk (Worm); a
  soulbound flask destroyed on the owner's death (Labyrinth); a fan whose
  spirit erodes your claim until you prove ownership (A Will Eternal).
  These match the patron-gift terms in the cosmology.
- **Clones and many selves** are common (Blood Clone, five soul clones,
  many selves, distributed avatars), each with coordination costs rather
  than free extra workers.
- **Uses and recharge** keep strong items honest: 15 uses then a year's
  recharge; six uses; a 24-hour refill.
- **Congenital physiques** (Regressor's Tale) and robot bodies (Kenshi)
  slot into the constitution and species layers of character creation.

be exceptional if made into a character's power. It does not claim the
source calls them cheats.

**Labels:** shown means recorded in inspected data/code in our research
notes, not a playthrough by this pass. Stated means a source description
or testimony. (I) means inference; all backing placements and foreign-world
predictions are proposals. (G) means general game knowledge, not locally
verified. Paths and lines cite derived notes, which retain their own
source pins and limits. No raw prose or game data is copied.

Player-layer belongs to the player, profile or session controller. Fictional
framing does not erase that boundary. Each player-layer row says whether
the notes provide such framing. Classification is an interpretation (I),
especially Auto-eat and incident direction. Revocation, theft, expiry, death
and reset are distinct; silence is recorded as unknown.

### What these add to the cosmology

- **F1 external holders:** seeds, profile unlocks and layout libraries have no proven soul carrier. Player-layer is not automatically Concept or meta.
- **F2 carrier versus eligibility:** a carried artifact, booster or Spirit Ash still needs a supported user, target, slot, fuel or place. This agrees with Section M F2.
- **F3 composite functions:** Noita's shift trigger and world conversion, and bionic skills and power supply, need separate support records. This agrees with Section M F6.
- **F4 reset boundaries:** heirs, loops, profile unlocks, retirement and save reload do not retain the same fields. Increlution clears buildings; Rogue Legacy's Architect resets selected content.
- **F5 knowledge can mislead:** seed recipes are version-bound; Disco faculties disagree. Knowledge can travel in a carrier without becoming truth or executable competence.
- **F6 partial compatibility:** a summoning item can arrive while its monument does not. Works for transport and Blocked for activation can coexist.
- **F7 clocks differ:** Bitburner estimates production, Melvor simulates ticks and Upload Labs stores acceleration. Auto-cultivation can use any selected policy, with its costs exposed.
- **F8 absorption options:** regression anchors, lineage deeds, portable design records and paid ritual automation can make player conveniences diegetic. Their source limits and original variants are in [GAMES_REPORT.md](gm/dispatch/cheat-cosmology-gaps-2026-10-10/GAMES_REPORT.md).
