## Main finding

The foundation can already commit one authored chance action, reserve supported costs and persist its selected effects. It cannot yet make native combat/crafting/shop refresh reproducible, execute arbitrary source functions, develop a bloodline or simulate an enemy's private progression. New labels or fixture sources do not supply those behaviors.

Use randomness for uncertain discovery, variable opposition and risky optional preparation. Keep understood execution reliable where mastery or preparation earns it. Give a rare faculty a new verb, operating capacity or perception channel. Give opponents actual access and finite means. Difficulty then comes from choosing and preparing a counter, with stats determining whether that counter can be sustained.

`random_range(min,max)` uses `Math.round`, so when a range contains interior integers its endpoints receive half their probability. Group sizes, drop quantities and shop counts have similar rounding. This is not uniform integer sampling. Any correction requires distribution fixtures and an intentional compatibility/balance choice. Craft-quality quantization also needs its own distribution rather than assuming uniform displayed qualities.

### Proposed probability and reliability rules

| Tool | Concrete player decision | Persistence and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Conditional weights / luck / fate | Bring a surveying faculty to shift one discovery table, or spend the slot/capacity elsewhere. Luck may bias rewards while fate changes which opportunity becomes eligible. | Existing tables support probability transfers, not a universal luck stat. Freeze contributors and eligible pool at commitment. No hidden modifier to unrelated crafting, enemy crits or lethal checks. |
| Earned reliability / pity / guarantees | Accept cheap uncertain salvage or complete a known costly route for guaranteed material; research can narrow uncertainty. | Counters keyed by actor, table/revision and opportunity family. Qualifying settled failure increments once; success resets by declared rule. Cancellation, preview and unrelated failures do not increment. Pity remains optional and unimplemented. |
| Independent seeded streams | A market refresh should not alter a combat sequence; practicing should not reroll an already found opportunity. | Proposed streams: market/vendor, encounter/instance, discovery/family, actor-development and world-event region. Allocate once from a saved root; never use `Date.now` on resume or seeds derived solely from public IDs. Retain legacy `rng` for old commitments; add validated stream state/counters without rewriting draws. |
| Information uncertainty | Diagnose a ward to learn its resistance; buy a map instead of blindly attempting each approach. | Actual hidden state is selected once; beliefs/disclosures are separate. Deterministic ignorance is not another success lottery. Show exact odds only where information justifies them, otherwise show known contributors and uncertainty. |
| Reroll / forecast / time power | Pay a charge to replace a specific unclaimed result, preview several alternatives, or replay a bounded history with declared retention. | Explicit branch/reroll command keeps the original receipt and spent charge, specifies affected scope and commits the replacement before display. No generic silent reseed. A powerful cheat can guarantee a chosen category or retain a faculty when explicitly authored; it need not be reduced to +5% luck. |

Stable commitment IDs and retained settlement state must outlive the 100-entry journal history. Preview, UI open, cancel, repeated command and import validation cannot grant a second payout. Existing cancellation preserves RNG but refund/recommit of a repeatable action can consume a new draw; that alone does not forbid result fishing. Use the same opportunity's frozen result or an explicit costly new-attempt policy. Valid import of an earlier whole save restores a historical branch; local-only saves cannot prevent intentional restoration/editing. The enforceable contract is coherent restoration with no merge of old costs and new rewards, not anti-cheat.

## 2. Enemy “systems” have three separate jobs

| Layer | Proposed data and behavior | Observable proof |
|---|---|---|
| Private faculty / progression | Source ID, owner/body binding, locally available quest/opportunity, resource budget, learned/acquired actions, rewards, growth requirement and cause. A system may grant a rare exception, expose only enemy-known information or exchange an actual resource. | An opponent spends one owned charge to record your visible move, then gains a counter only after paid study or a declared instantaneous faculty. It cannot copy unseen inventory, unavailable organs or hidden player knowledge by default. |
| Decision policy | Goals, risk threshold, accessible observations, available actions and saved policy revision. Policy asks the same validated services as the player; sensory faculty changes inputs. | Enemy conserves its last battery for escape or objective defense; misinformation can cause a bad choice. No automatic player-level scaling, omniscient inventory reads or spontaneous unaffordable upgrade. |
| Encounter mechanics | A committed projection of equipment, stance, actions, physiology, source capacity and local conditions, with windup/resolve/recovery rules. | A predicted repeated strike is countered; changing rhythm or obstructing a sensor succeeds. Running out of charges actually removes the prediction action. |

These layers can be authored independently. Disposable monsters may have encounter-only faculties; a named peer needs the durable life/claim bridge already scoped elsewhere. A technological body can have extreme strength, armor, sensors, computation, regenerating parts or devastating tools. Local and personal power scales remain independent; a magic setting does not automatically outrank technology. Its limits can be fuel, heat, information or replacement parts, not obligatory weakness.

Future variants retained: institution-issued quest faculties, adaptive biological predators, implanted tactical assistants, inherited eyes, soul archives and enemies with genuine rewind/forecast privileges. Each needs declared access/retention and a causal receipt. Their controller need not be an LLM, and “has a system” does not make every NPC the same class progression ladder.

## 3. Mechanical difficulty with understandable counters

All rules below are original proposals. Existing stances modify stats/stamina; Rat-step currently changes an expedition route. Neither currently performs the proposed interruption, control or perception effects.

| Obstacle and behavior | Martial counter | Research/craft counter | Special faculty counter and boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Braced sweep: protected windup, costly sweep, then a real exposed recovery window | Defensive stance/shield absorbs a disclosed impact cost; executable Rat-step moves outside the sweep and opens a counter. Heavy attack during recovery breaks posture; repeating it during brace does not. | A researched latch and anchored Survey rope restrain one sweep, opening recovery. A kit provides expendable safe preparation. | A sight faculty reveals a concealed windup variant early, or copies one supported step. Obscured sight prevents observation; copying the step does not copy the enemy's body or fuel. |
| Warded construct: direct energy damage resisted, control/heat channels separate | Position or shove it away from the objective; a suitable weapon attacks a disclosed seam. | Prepared tether immobilizes; a tested fictional coolant delays its channel, while sustained heat can overload a different ward. Never assume generic acid beats every ward. | Energy perception identifies the active ward; specialized disruption removes that construct, leaving physical consequences intact. |
| Adaptive opponent with one prediction charge and two observed attack records | Vary stance/rhythm or bait its counter, then punish recharge. Stats still govern endurance. | Block a sensor with authored cover or deploy a decoy; use the spent-charge window to secure a marker. | Conceal intent, feed a false observation or bypass prediction through an explicitly exceptional movement function. Counterplay targets evidence/charge, not an invisible global evasion boost. |
| Protect a worksite while a channel/pressure gauge rises | Interrupt the channel or guard a vulnerable worker; attacking another target does not save the worksite. | Repair a brace, drain a gauge or complete a preparation task, each using real materials/time. | A perception or projection power reaches a hidden switch; its range and fuel remain explicit. Success is the protected objective, not necessarily every enemy defeated. |

Begin with a small condition/action vocabulary: brace, exposed, restrained, windup, recovery and observation. Rules create costs and windows, not endless twitch prompts. Prepared policies can say “guard on sweep, Rat-step when ready, heavy during exposure, retreat below budget.” A policy checks requirements at every boundary and pauses with a readable reason. Optional untimed drills teach the cues; both manual and idle resolution use the same saved resolver, and practice is not a required repeated click tax.

## 4. Bloodline and other source development

### Identity is separate from expression and use

Proposed distinction: **ancestry/genotype** records origin or latent eligibility; **awakening** activates a faculty; **physiology/energy structure** determines actual organs, senses, fuels and capacities; **control/mastery** governs reliability and finesse; **learned techniques** retain their own knowledge/execution records. A graft or ritual can grant a faculty without rewriting ancestry. A known recipe need not imply an active organ. Biological terminology here describes fictional rules, not real inheritance science.

Current `actors[id].sources[sourceDefinitionId]` stores acquisition time, expiry, receipt, world and revision. It is one instance per definition ID; querying the current definition grants positive numeric capabilities with `sum`/`max`, conditions and provenance. It has no unique organ identity, source-level/control state, reserved energy, conflicts, functional handler or body/soul retention. Query uses the current registry's grant definition despite the stored revision; changing that definition needs an explicit revision/migration policy, not an assumption that old source functions are frozen.

| Shared narrow service | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Source instance and acquisition | Unique instance ID plus definition/revision, owner actor, body/soul/lineage identity, grant route, donor/material provenance and receipt. Inheritance, voluntary transfer, copying and grafting are distinct operations. Copy leaves the donor's instance; transfer debits custody; graft consumes a specific owned organ/material and records its new binding. |
| Eligibility, awakening and activation | Conditions checked against local world rules, body support and actual resources. Latent, active, suppressed, damaged and removed are distinct statuses; suppression keeps ownership/knowledge. Acquisition and use conditions may differ. Exceptional origins declare exactly which condition they bypass. |
| Function and capacity | Handler dispatches an actual observation/copy, transform, heat reuse, scan or control operation. Separate capacity maximum, available reserve, committed maintenance, fuel, strain and recovery deadline. Do not apply an effect twice through both capability query and native stat publication. |
| Development/evolution | Earned control, discovered functions, structural upgrades and a proposed evolution plan with visible requirements, outcomes and retention. Training can make observation continuous, unlock a different spectrum, reduce reserved capacity or add an action. Random mutation is optional, selected once with reagents/cost and saved candidate results. |
| Conflicts and continuity | Conflicts can attach to organ slots, energy conversion, active modes or a specific faculty, rather than a universal bloodline count. Body-bound structure may be lost on body change; learned memory and a soul-bound source follow explicit policies. Compatible effects stack by named rule; incompatible activation explains its blocker. |

### Three different progression paths through these services

| Original path | Acquisition and functional development | Costs, counters and retention proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Ocular archive | Inherited latent eye **or** a provenance-tracked graft. Awakening sees one visible movement cue; control sustains tracking; later pattern integration grants a specific copied technique under that technique's handler. A selected exceptional origin could internalize instantly. | Sight/target observability, compatible execution body/fuel, capacity/strain. Concealment blocks new evidence; existing learned technique can remain usable with ordinary means. Organ is body-bound; remembered patterns are separately retained. Grafting alone does not prove hereditary transmission. |
| Acquired heat-retaining transformation | Research/regimen modifies energy handling; first function stores residual heat, later control directs it to a strike or refining operation, evolution changes safe capacity or available conversion. | Storage cap, cooling/release action, environment and preparation. Cooling counters the stored resource rather than lowering all stats. Knowledge can persist while the body transformation is absent. Detailed inspiration/earlier original variant remains in [cultivation physiology](RESEARCH_CULTIVATION_PATHS.md#innate-capacity-roots-and-acquired-constitution). |
| Gene-warrior / cybernetic sensor-body | Acquired graft, local gene regimen or implant; calibration opens an environmental sense, training/control improves discrimination, structural upgrade enables actuation/repair or a new movement form. A high-tier body may be vastly powerful. | Biochemical fuel or charge, heat and material replacement; local compatible facilities and sensor counters. Hardware/body changes and remembered technique/calibration follow different retention. No forced translation to a cultivation core or ancestry purity score. Exact novel-specific gene rules remain a source gap. |

World compatibility should return an operational explanation: full function, local conversion required, externally fueled, suppressed or unavailable because an actual rule conflicts. Current home-world technique check needs replacing for selected portable functions. Carrying a source across a portal/projection/summoning/reincarnation route is not evidence it can run there. Body transfer must settle reservations and recompute supported physiology once; travel cannot refill charges by reconstructing the actor.

No breeding simulator or random creation lottery is required for the first source proof. An intentionally extraordinary origin may bypass ancestry, learning, fuel or survival rules as authored. Its strength should create different objectives and opponents, rather than being silently reduced to a small modifier.

Ordinary native defeat remains fainting/recovery. Only declared eligible hazards/zones/enemies/weapons/effects/events can request a lethal resolution, with optional conditional RNG. Resolve survival sources before bodily loss or run end, then persist consumed protection, continuation and aftermath once. Historical `total_deaths` supplies no eligibility. Concrete hazards/odds, bodily/soul retention and global reset policy remain unselected; this packet introduces none.
