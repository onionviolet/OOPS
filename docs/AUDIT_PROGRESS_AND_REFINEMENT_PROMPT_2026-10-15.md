## P2. Preserve “anything is possible, reasonably”

Apply the accepted core: each world makes its own rules; book worlds start from their source and selected premise; exceptions, outliers, cheats and other means remain possible. Latest accepted direction qualifies older wording. First-milestone sequencing does not define a permanent ceiling on the game.

Distinguish a reusable engineering/workflow convention from a law of the fictional universe. Shared save correctness does not imply shared supernatural physics. Never turn implementation convenience, a temporary slice boundary or an agent preference into a global prohibition.

For an apparent restriction, identify its scope and cause: source/local law, current circumstances, unavailable means, selected mode, missing implementation, deferred scope or actual accepted boundary. “Not available to this character now” and “not implemented” do not mean “impossible everywhere.”

Where an idea strains an existing model, explore a coherent local rule, exception, alternate premise, cheat or future capability. State the dependency and consequence. Possibility does not mean every option works in every situation, costs must always exist, every character has every option, unsupported canon is true, or everything must be built now. Preserve free or extraordinary outcomes where actual rules permit them.

Keep hidden properties and actual causes separate from what characters know. Unknown interactions do not become harmless merely because they are unknown. Preserve sealed secrets in public-facing records using IDs, routes and status.
