# Society relationships and dialogue audit

Planning-only decision summary, 2026-10-04. Runtime remains held for Weibao's review. The plan already has the right breadth: directed relationships, independent people, optional adult romance and situation-aware fresh dialogue. The next useful expansion is **specific social agreements and evidence-aware choices**, built on the current people, jobs, custody, situations and paid controllers. Adding another favor bar would contribute little.

The main foundation gap is disputed evidence ([SOC01](FINDINGS.json)). Knowledge currently keeps one value per actor/topic and replaces it at an equal or higher stage. Tamsin's original visitor scene correctly distinguishes a mistaken promise from present help, but its phase logic carries the dispute. Wider rumor/deception play needs separately attributed assertions, evidence and current beliefs, including the distinction between hearing a statement and believing its content. Begin with finite authored correction branches. A town-wide probabilistic belief network is unnecessary for that proof.

Personal commitments need concrete terms ([SOC02](FINDINGS.json)): who voluntarily promised what, to whom, with which stock/time, due clock, amendment and closure. Existing elder ties, claims and custody are useful foundations, not a generic agreement lifecycle. Returning a borrowed template, making restitution and apologizing should close different issues. Gifts, silence and accusations create no inferred agreement. Personal trust and institutional enforcement stay with separate owners.

Useful depth comes from motives that change feasible choices ([SOC03](FINDINGS.json)). Joss can value the player and still reserve his last kit for research. A craftsman can respect a refinement while declining a loan. Authored priorities and predicates already support independent paid alternatives; no new psychology engine is required. Extend this with reciprocal teaching/patronage and named care roles ([SOC06–07](FINDINGS.json)). A teacher can actually learn a student's refinement; an introduction can earn an interview; a caregiver can agree an occupied shift. None grants unsupported powers, property or affection. Household stock, institutional rights, body treatment and temporal continuity retain their existing owners.

Communication and culture need a case where they change a choice ([SOC04](FINDINGS.json)). A learned sign, an interpreter or a qualified translation faculty can clarify offered terms. Local norms should have individual exceptions and observable consequences, without random offense or a compulsory language grind. These proposed examples are original. Canonical voices, motives and customs still require exact era-relevant primary/supplied scenes before final prose.

Simplify the read model ([SOC05](FINDINGS.json)): retain stable episodes and closure receipts, then render human lines from known outcomes. A pathfinder's delivered kit currently depends on matching exact memory wording. Rewording or pruning display memories should not erase that meaning. Build one observer-context projection from actual owners, keeping private information out of player tooltips and shared context cards. Generalize after one native meeting is qualified, rather than persisting another transcript/context authority.

Retain Micro's strong adult reciprocity/withdrawal case, the Glasshouse's private → offered → heard disclosure pattern, and existing native integration blockers ([SOC08–10](FINDINGS.json)). Romance stays optional; friendship, mentorship and professional collaboration remain useful. Native rewards currently precede answer rendering, and clock-zero relationship receipt truthiness remains an already-reported risk. Neither is resolved by a new prose dossier.
