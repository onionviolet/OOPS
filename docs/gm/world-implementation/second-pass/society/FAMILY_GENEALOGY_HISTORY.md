# Family, genealogy and history extension

Direct follow-up audit, 2026-10-04. The earlier [report](REPORT.md) treated family mostly through care; that was too narrow for this request. [SOC11-SOC15](FINDINGS.json) add genealogy, upbringing, succession, causal history and living traditions. These are original proposals for review. Runtime remains held; the assigning master alone merges canonical plans. No source/library/private-save mutation or game execution occurred.

## Five additions with concrete functions

| Addition | Function that changes a decision | Contract and existing boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Kinship and ancestry**, SOC11 | Investigate parentage, recognize adopted kin, find an introduction, trace bodily or teaching provenance | Biological/soul ancestry, adoption/guardianship, current/past household, adult partnership and master-student links differ. Directed feelings remain separate. Existing spawn `bornAt` is not proof of historical birth; `createdBy` is not a parent |
| **Upbringing and household life**, SOC12 | Choose a family trade or another teacher, learn a language/sign, support a sibling, leave/rejoin a household | Care and instruction use actual people, means and occupancy. Chapter transitions declare what changed. Sources can be genuinely hereditary and powerful; learned expertise follows actual teaching |
| **Succession and legacy**, SOC13 | Accept or contest a bequest, transfer a workshop during retirement, nominate an apprentice, preserve an archive | Goods, site-use rights, office, taught methods, hereditary source and social expectations have separate carriers/eligibility. Family status does not duplicate stock or infer a debt rule |
| **Causal family history**, SOC14 | Corroborate a rumor, reunite kin, recover a tradition, repair a known grievance | Actual occurrence, surviving records, testimony, present belief and discovery time differ. A family's alleged past can cause real social pressure without becoming the descendant's author-truth guilt |
| **Living traditions and occasions**, SOC15 | Attend a reunion/memorial/ceremony, form chosen kin, mediate a school split, teach back, care for an elder | Local customs and individual motives decide reactions. A useful conversation need not pay a bonus. Long-lived people can coexist with several generations; age, body, generation and teaching rank are independent |

Additional cases within these five families include estrangement and reconciliation, migration/displacement, missing relatives, disputed records, blended households, branch families, fosterage, sworn kin, birth or adoption when supported by the selected origin policy, retirement/death/memorial, school inheritance, heirlooms and ancestral facilities. Marriage or an institutional title can supply particular recognized rights/pressure without granting affection, private knowledge or every source. The specific era/culture decides which cases exist; invented customs remain labelled original.

## A worked original family workshop

Four named adults make the distinctions observable: Ena, a retiring craftsperson; Soren, her biological son; Hale, her adopted child; Pera, an unrelated apprentice. This is a proposed Original Frontier cast, not an enrollment or rewrite of current NPCs. Their exact ages, means, names and customs await local authoring.

Ena owns one unique repair tool and holds a declared workshop right. Hale helped run the workshop; Soren trained elsewhere; Pera developed a useful refinement after real instruction. The player can investigate an old family archive, help negotiate retirement terms, arrange a funded trial, introduce a teacher or decline involvement. Ena can choose a successor for a particular role; each other person has independent goals. Soren can leave the trade without ceasing to be kin, and Hale can inherit a tool without gaining Ena's physiological ancestry.

A retirement agreement can transfer the one tool to Hale, a valid bench-use right to Soren and an actually transmissible method to Pera. Each operation uses its actual owner, terms and receipt. If the tool was loaned out, its custody must be resolved under a selected policy; no second tool appears. Ena remains an actual person who can teach or disagree. Death succession, if used later, has a separate life event and disposition policy, not an assumed consequence of this gathering.

An archive alleges an ancestor diverted a flood fund. Pera holds a contrary dated receipt; a guild contact has heard only the allegation. Investigation may change a particular observer's belief and local access. The player can disclose evidence, keep a confidence or refuse mediation. Nobody learns the whole truth from the family menu. A genuine ancestral facility/source, if selected, must provide its actual supported function; discovering the record is not automatic mastery.

Later return depends on what actually happened: the tool's holder, who learned the refinement, Ena's current life/role, the accepted workshop terms and each person's known evidence. Repeating the retirement greeting neither duplicates inheritance nor generates favor. The same four people can support craft, research, care, social conflict or a useful non-reward scene without a fully simulated village birth rate.

## Necessary graph and history contract

Use stable actual people with time-qualified typed links and provenance. Keep family truth separate from the player's discovered graph and each NPC's beliefs. Unknown dates, unidentified relatives and disputed links remain unknown; do not fill them by guessing from age or surname. Record changed household/role boundaries without erasing past kinship. Local lineage rules can support nonhuman, soul, clone or time-loop exceptions; do not force all of them into ordinary biological parentage.

A meaningful history entry needs actual context, participants, occurrence/cause, known preconditions and outcomes, relevant objects/rights/methods, and evidence/reveal paths. It can be a partial order rather than an invented exact calendar. Permanent kinship, closure and succession receipts must survive bounded display-history pruning. A source-era character uses the facts they could know then; a game divergence changes downstream possibilities rather than forcing the original future plot.

Seeded generated households, if later chosen, freeze template/revision, actual identities, relationships, history and initial means at origin commitment. Promoting a historical/background relative into a personal scene preserves that identity and transfers no stock by recreation. Detailed generations are a legitimate future scope, with their own life/origin/native/clock acceptance; a compact first family is a qualification case, not the ceiling of the design.

## Scope and later acceptance

Society owns personal kinship meanings, motives and observed reactions. Bases owns collective household/property/office rights and material support. Timelines/Origins owns birth, age, chapter transitions, historical causality and retention. Bodies/Abilities owns physiological or learned inheritance. Engine integrates canonical identity/evidence/reservation/custody and native publication; this appendix invents no API or approved schema.

Later acceptance must show stable kinship after return, unknown-parent privacy, useful upbringing/career alternatives, conserved and independently eligible succession, conflicting past evidence, independent refusal, and fresh scene reactions. Include stale/duplicate/failed publication and display-pruning cases. Time jumps and inherited sources require their own functional and compatibility decisions. No mandatory romance, player death, world enrollment or new offline advancement/loss is selected. Document checks here establish an audit, not working family gameplay.
