Do not create another inventory, clock, relationship engine or ecology RNG. Region identity references the actual world instance/era/layer and native endpoints. Biological producer identities differ from output lot identities; habitat support differs from facilities/structures. Items defines raw/refined goods and recipes. Combat determines actual contact/damage/capture/death outcomes; Regions consumes only settled eligible aftermath. Bases owns built structures and their functions; Regions consumes the resulting support.

## Harvesting breadth without seven separate engines

These are policy distinctions, not seven new skill bars or a request to implement every row.

| Family | Regional distinction | Shared output/neighbor owner |
|---|---|---|
| Gathering | Bulk, selective specimen, tap or fell; producer/reserve and method footprint | Items properties/condition; existing Herbalism/Woodcutting |
| Hunting/capture/products | Tracks and actual contact; eligible body/product/population; hunt, capture and shed collection differ | Combat settles contact/death/capture; People owns durable creatures; Items owns usable parts |
| Farming/husbandry | Permitted site, actual seed/feed/care, selected growth stage/maturity and product ownership | Bases supplies site/support; Items owns living specimen/feed/output; People owns personally represented animals |
| Mining/quarrying | Survey/accessibility versus remaining finite reserve; extraction or exposure/collapse footprint if selected | Items ore/stone/output; Combat/environment resolution only for real hazards |
| Fishing | Water access, chosen local population/product policy and seasonal/flow support; effort/gear | Items catch/bait/condition; existing Fishing; no background sound creates actual prey or a consumed bait item |
| Salvage | Finite cache/device claim, safe access, dismantle versus retain function, custody | Items actual component/device; Bases damage/support; Combat settles occupied danger |
| Environmental collection | Actual local phenomenon/flow, compatible vessel/function and stated renewal; some functions stay on site | Powers supplies true supported function; Items vessel/fuel; no universal scarcity or power conversion |

Every family can be serviced by lawful purchase, paid specialist or ordinary alternate route where actually authored. This does not invent trade access or force all resources to have substitutes. Superior source-supported methods can remove an ordinary obstacle without adding a compensation penalty.
