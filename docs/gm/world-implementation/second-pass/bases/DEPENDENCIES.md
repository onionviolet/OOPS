## Smallest proposed worksite contract

This describes semantics, not approved JSON/save fields. Bind the existing actual people, site, stock and jobs; an agreement or institution is not a second person, queue, bag or clock.

| Concern | Minimum retained distinction |
|---|---|
| Authority | Site title/possessor, jurisdiction, use/construction/booking grants; named members/decision-maker and allowed spending/output allocations. Actor agreement, orders and coercion remain actual local causes rather than a global consent flag |
| Work | Named worker/operator/helper identities, supported method/tool/site capacity, current booking and contractual milestone. Employees remain independent actors with their own stock and competing plans |
| Settlement | Actual payer/payee/currency carrier, authorized treasury balance, milestone payment, used/unspent escrow, one output owner and receipt. Existing quest grants/fees stay explicit sources/sinks |
| Continuation | Prepaid frozen or live-dependent operation; clocks, interruption/repair, utility supply and resource stop condition. Preserve absence freeze and no browser-closed loss unless a policy is separately selected |
| Consequence | Delivery acceptance, allocation to sale/care/training/research, witnessed report, damage/repair, exit/succession and current effective rights. A property dispute or raid needs actual actors/means/history and cannot be a free ownership toggle |

A selected treasurer NPC can be the existing account/inventory binding for a fixed first proof if it is explicitly named as agent for the principal. That is a prototype representation, not proof of a generic institution-owned treasury. A general collective owner needs Engine's validated choice and compatibility rules. Simple paired staged resource effects may suffice for ordinary monetary transfers; actual item-backed money must debit the same owned stock used as fuel. Avoid a finance engine until debt, credit or exchange creates a selected player case.
