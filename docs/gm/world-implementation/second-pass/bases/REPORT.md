# Bases, institutions and economy: decision return

Planning audit completed 2026-10-04. Recommend a **rented, staffed worksite with one funded order and retained aftermath** as the next base qualification after review. It exercises rights, collective choices, payments, useful production and delivery without waiting for a general town simulator. This proposes amendments to the [held plan](../../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md), not implementation approval. [FINDINGS](FINDINGS.json) holds five prioritized decisions and exact evidence; [DEPENDENCIES](DEPENDENCIES.md) gives the ownership and acceptance handoff.

The completed [micro report](../../depth/micro/REPORT.md) already identifies tenancy, structure, stock, worker occupancy and missing compiler participant forwarding. [MC05](../../depth/micro/WORKED_CASES.md#mc05-clinic-refuge-and-progression-project) connects construction, training, care, damage and absence. This pass adds operating terms and economic consequences.

1. **Define operating rights before a large base schema.** Separate title, possession, construction permission, bookings and authority to spend shared assets. Name principals, members, steward or decision rule, revision and exit terms. Households, guilds and coercive factions need distinct rules. Current site effects set an owner, while preview checks existence/capacity. Renting a bench should permit work without selling its building; losing membership should not erase personal tools. See BAS01.

2. **Distinguish fees/rewards from funded wages and output shares.** The workshop's four-coin commission debits the player but credits no worker balance. Supply delivery consumes kits/rope and grants six coins without a buyer treasury. These are legitimate authored sinks/rewards, but cannot establish independently funded employers. Declare payer, payee, budget, committed/unspent payment, output recipient and acceptance terms for the selected work order. Retain existing receipts and balances; do not retroactively invent salaries. Currency also needs its carrier: the reviewed Gu setting uses primeval stones as both payment and fuel, so two spendable balances would duplicate one stock. See BAS02.

3. **Make supply change the recipient and route.** Identify an actual use, funded order and recipient. Reservation, pickup, transit, delivery and use have different custody/time consequences. Current same-world item transfers do not establish freight journeys. A delayed Frontier consignment can leave the crew selecting another supplier; the seller still owns undelivered kits. Start with one route and one competing funded order. See BAS03.

4. **Treat continued operation as a contract.** Reserve all real workers, tools and supported capacity. Separate prepaid ordinary work from live care, utilities and defenses that depend on continued support. Declare interruption, emergency allocation, maintenance, delegated supervision and absence behavior. A roof damaged by a funded raid changes exposed stock and future bookings; physical occupation does not automatically transfer legal title. Use current freeze until another policy is selected, preserving completed work and real protection. See BAS04.

5. **Keep depth in functions, simplify management.** Reuse the existing jobs, ownership, deterministic controllers and bounded routines. Offer rental, paid services and a few operational facility modes before nested dynamic construction. Expand only when storage, training, care, research or defense changes an action. Preserve the dry rack's energy-free batch advantage and source-specific living structures/production; do not add artificial daily costs to offset superiority. Novel research can use active decisions while known work batches safely. See BAS05.

Checks establish readable, evidence-linked planning only. No game suite, build, browser, save, benchmark, source-library write or shared-plan edit ran. Runtime/token costs are unmeasured. Remaining gates are master reconciliation and Weibao's decisions, then separately authorized implementation and its deferred playable acceptance. Exact input fingerprints, drift and writer/process closeout are in [CHECKPOINT](CHECKPOINT.md).
