### What the live code does and does not establish

`src/power_models.js` checks authored function capacity, invested output, authority scope/termination, and hybrid constituent/lineage requirements. It is useful for a local power choice, not an arbitrary installed-component graph. `src/simulation_contracts.js` admits typed item, equipment, source, faculty, power, energy, site and custody operations; typed vocabulary does not supply mount identity, compatibility, an effective equipment projection or a device operating loop. `src/simulation_effects.js` stages item/resource/equipment/source/power/energy changes through shared effects. `src/field_workshop.js` already has a paid workshop assembly job and a load-bearing method that enables real alternatives to a field response; that is a positive base consumer, but its assembled facility is not a modular suit or magical device. These are source facts, not claims about uninspected modules. Existing functions should be extended at their owners, not replaced with a second inventory or power system.

## Two finite consumer paths

Numbers and names below are original proposals, not selected canon or accepted balance. Start from a technician/workshop career in one original frontier context; do not require a second realm, second prestige, or every world to use interchangeable technology.

| Stage | Technology path: repairable load-frame suit | Magical path: paired threshold ward |
|---|---|---|
| Early | **Blueprint/hypothesis:** learn a sketch for a shoulder frame and inspect a cracked lift actuator. The sketch proves the proposed geometry, not that local scrap fits. Buy or recover one real actuator; repair it at the association bench using a seal and two fasteners. **Fabrication:** paid work combines the known method and actual stock into one frame. Fit it at the Shack and lift a measured crate; the device has one output: carry/lift that load without occupying both hands. | **Blueprint/hypothesis:** copy a chalk pattern for one doorway, then ask a local keeper to identify the substrate and direction marks. The pattern is a hypothesis until tested. **Fabrication:** shape a ceramic anchor from local clay, inscribe one known line and use a measured reservoir. Paid bench test against a swinging padded weight demonstrates a single function: slow that weight at the marked doorway. |
| Developed | **Commissioned physical test:** a road crew pays for a bridge-load demonstration. With repaired actuator plus brace, the player carries the crate while keeping one hand free to set a support; the result opens an ordinary repair/escort job and a paid method lesson. Battery reserve limits repetitions; loose cable wear disables lifting until repaired. A low-draw hand winch remains a valid portable option. | **Commissioned physical test:** a storekeeper pays for overnight protection of one shutter against thrown debris. Add a second directional line only after the first ward's result is observed. The inscription chooses the protected opening; the anchor fixes it; reservoir supplies output. A correct ward stops the declared debris function. It does not silence fire, block a person entering normally, or create a universal force field. |
| Mature | **Accepted method and operating suit:** repeated load calibration validates the fit method for this local chassis. Add a control grip and protected reserve pack. Actual operating mode must fit draw, reserve, heat, joint load and operator control; running protection and lift together may exceed the installed source. A service contract supports spare seals and paid repair. The suit can genuinely outperform a hand tool on heavy loads; finesse repairs still reward trained hands. | **Accepted method and operating device:** keeper and player document the observed substrate, mark orientation, measured reservoir, supported motion and repair procedure. The two-anchor ward can protect the whole store opening while its reservoir lasts; a damaged anchor drops only its own direction. The keeper can commission inspection, replenishment and apprentice teaching. It can be plainly better than mundane shutters against the named threat while remaining bounded by what it actually protects. |

Blueprint/hypothesis, fabrication, commissioned test, accepted method and operation are distinct states. A purchased or inherited source can skip research where it really supplies validated instructions or instinctive control. Do not force a research timer on a source that grants its own mastery. The careers also have valuable noncombat consumers: heavy repair and paid hauling; workshop supply, calibration, teaching and maintenance; shutter protection, inspection and materials trade. Supporting these paths does not require the player to fight.

**Counterfeit/unsupported-source failure:** a vendor offers a “universal ghost-proof shield” blueprint, but it names a polished copper seam as a spirit anchor without any inspected evidence that this world supports that interaction. The UI can say “unverified material/target rule”; it cannot report protection as a known function. A paid inert-target test consumes the declared reagent and yields “no supported response”; no shield is fabricated, no protection bonus is granted, and the actual copper sample remains if the test did not consume it. The player can still use ordinary bracing or commission a known ward. If later source evidence establishes the copper seam really blocks that spirit, keep that successful function and its superiority; do not insert an arbitrary vulnerability, upkeep tax or rival immunity to claw it back.

## Bounded consumer contract and finite open decisions

This is a contract over current owners, not a proposed new interface or persistence layout. For one selected device, the consumer must be able to answer: what is known versus tested; which actual parts/materials are owned, held, installed or consumed; what local access/method/target conditions apply; what the chosen operating mode can do now; which support channels and simultaneous draws it uses; what clock/charge/heat/strain limit ends it; what observable output occurred; what condition needs repair; and what a paid failed trial actually consumed or taught. Previews disclose only known constraints and uncertainty. A committed test freezes its inputs/outcome; viewing it again cannot reroll. Portable functions travel with their supported body/item/source and retained supply; local workshop, authority, substrate, network or legal access stays local. Source-bound faculties remain with the source/body unless that source explicitly permits transfer.

Remaining finite decisions for the first actual consumer: (1) choose one assembly and its native equipment/output owner; (2) specify whether its parts are fixed authored variants or mutable instances; (3) name one supported target/output and one incompatible or unsupported case; (4) choose the actual support channels and repair inputs; (5) decide which operating state persists through save/reload and how legacy saves omit it safely. The shared contract already proposes the broad solution space. No full taxonomy, universal graph solver, material simulator, every-pair combination table, extra realm or universal tier ladder is needed to make this consumer concrete.
