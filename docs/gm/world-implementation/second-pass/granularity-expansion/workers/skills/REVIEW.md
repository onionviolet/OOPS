### Existing implementation, specifically

The four definitions in `src/skill_expansion_content.js` have real effects: a consensual nonlethal brace encounter; a field route that enables the existing survey procedure; native prepared-kit production with mastery-dependent yield; and funded, reserved cooperative analysis plus a bounded report of already observable project state. The route preserves the unrepaired crossing and one-survey owner. Shared inquiry reserves the craftsman's time and attention rather than reading private facts. Timber consumes native logs/coin and emits a real native item. These are meaningful different functions, not four passive bonuses.

The source also defines observe/learn/practice commands, explicit knowledge, source/faculty acquisition, native XP, and local mastery thresholds. So `PROJECT.md`'s resume text saying Ability's “module/native/build delivery is still pending” has drifted at least for the module: the current source file exists and contains the required exports/content. It does **not** prove Engine registration, native UI, bundle delivery, or working gameplay. Module present; play remains unverified.

Ability supplies `SKILL_IDS`, `SKILL_CATALOGUE`, `skillSources`, `skillDefinitions`, and pure `skillExpansionFacts(state, context)`; Engine merges the typed definitions, supplies actor-qualified facts to preview/commit, invokes native XP/item/vital/unlock publication, projects visibility, binds controls, then builds. Existing owners remain singular: native `levelable.skill_xp` owns competence XP/unlocks; `simulation.powers` owns method ownership/mastery; actor sources plus faculties own acquisition/activation; `simulation.knowledge` owns known hints/learning; native inventory owns items/materials; participant jobs and the shared simulation clock own reserved time. Do not add a skill-local XP pool, duplicate inventory/reward record, or second timer. Discovery renderers consume safe projected facts and must never grant anything.

## Two representative paths (original proposals)

These proposals show breadth, not canon or mandatory balance targets.

| Stage | Mundane craft and adventure: timber judgment | Extraordinary source: glass-thread perception (proposal) |
|---|---|---|
| Early | A player with ordinary hands and a rough log learns to read grain from a present craftsperson. Observation reveals only the offered lesson; paid instruction consumes real time/materials and records the learned method. A cautious one-kit batch is useful on a crossing survey, party rest, project supply, or sale/delivery if those consumers accept it. | A strange thread of light is noticed during a real field event. The player gets a safe clue that a phenomenon exists, not its private explanation or a control button. Aptitude may make noticing easier, but is only potential; body condition and fatigue affect whether careful fieldwork is currently possible. Neither grants the source or its power. |
| Developed | Repeated native practice advances `Woodworking` XP and the existing power mastery separately. At an earned local threshold, the same two logs and bench produce two useful kits. A wet bench, tender hands, missing logs, or no funds blocks current execution while preserving knowledge/mastery. The player chooses field preparation and reliable supplies over immediate adventure time. | A finite, authored opportunity supplies the actual source (for example, a witnessed local exposure plus a willing guide's demonstration). A committed acquisition action records origin/receipt and grants the source; a separate learn/activation step teaches its use. The function might trace one visible thread across a disturbed object, yielding a public lead for exploration. Do not let an aptitude score, body trait, or a guessed clue silently stand in for source ownership, knowledge, or active faculty. If this method needs fuel, use the existing energy owner and recovery rather than stamina/XP as an invented substitute. |
| Mature | Mastery can improve packing precision or support a distinctive profession: a trusted kit maker keeps an expedition supplied, trains another worker, or helps maintain a route. Give the mature skill a further functional choice (for example, durable field markers versus more kits from the same batch) only if both outputs feed actual owners. Failure to obtain a contract remains a livelihood outcome, not a reason to cripple the skill. | Greater control could let the player distinguish a second thread or follow a trail through a difficult environment; combine it with ordinary trail reading to plan a safer route or identify what evidence to collect. Acquisition remains rare if the selected world says so, but rarity is not a fabricated penalty. A source can be intrinsically superior for this function and stay superior; ordinary perception, records, guides, equipment, or waiting can remain viable alternatives with different costs and reach. Do not invent a counter solely to “balance” a genuinely exceptional source. |

These examples yield noncombat value: supply production, expedition planning, observation, teaching, contracts, trust, local institutions and how the player spends scarce time. Mature outcomes should change actual options or relationships and feed the selected world's career/world aftermath (a maintained route, a thriving workshop, a failed contract, a handed-off practice, or a choice to leave). A mastery label without a changed command/result is not maturity. Neither requires all careers or worlds in the first one-realm/one-prestige milestone.

## Review findings for integration

1. Keep discovery, acquisition, comprehension, current activation, means, and execution as distinct gates. A known blocked skill card may state only player-known requirements; losing a tool/source/fuel should not erase the skill or an accepted job.
2. Define early, developed, and mature by changed usable function and consequential choice, not by number of ranks. At each stage identify target/site, input/clock owner, output consumer, recovery/abstention choice, and what remains unknown.
3. Preserve routes with different costs and reach. The mundane worker must not become a deliberately weak tutorial for the extraordinary path. Conversely, extraordinary acquisition must have an explicit actual source/opportunity and player-facing cause.
4. Cross-skill interaction should use actual outputs or known evidence. It should not double-award survey completion, reveal private actor state, or award XP on preview/cancel.
5. Expand IDs from reviewed world requirements and consumer gaps. The four Frontier methods do not finish requested skill/talent/technology/magic breadth.
