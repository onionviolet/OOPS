## Two connected paths through one original field

Example setting: the Cinderbrook edge, an original wooded route adjacent to a settled frontier village. Example resources, named opportunities and costs below are proposals. Use the normal location/action interface as primary. A decorative glyph map is optional and must project saved facts rather than imply unsupported coordinates.

| Stage | Field career and stewardship | Preparation, counter and adventure |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Choose bulk gathering for common willow bark/logs, or slower selective work for an intact cutting. A local apprentice competing for the same patch reserves their own declared share. Sell or keep the yield; preserve a cutting and pay/earn access to one small tended plot. | Notice hoof-scuffed ash at a shallow crossing. Pay a short observation interval or spend a kit to inspect. The paths combine when the grower wants intact samples while the fighter needs a clear retreat lane: reserve one cutting, clear only enough brush for egress, and ask a paid guide to hold the route. This reduces immediate bulk yield yet improves a real adventure option. Long-term outcomes can be a productive tended patch, a depleted contested site, a shared route maintained by paid workers, or a closed/declined offer remembered by its actual participants. Returning to town, retiring, or ending an arc must not restore depleted unique stock or erase the people and agreements.

## Field and opportunity contract

1. **Local clock and stock.** Each authored patch has stable instance/location ID, stock capacity/current stock, producer or deposit, permitted methods, access/right, quality/residue rule, reservation order and a local recovery rule. Advance its clock only through explicitly supported played work/travel/rest or authored local events. Each step states whether stock recovers during an active outing. No browser-closed punishment or assumed wall-time catch-up. Save/reload resumes the same clock and claims; it does not redraw opportunities.
2. **Competition and conservation.** Player and collectors reserve from one stock ledger at a legal boundary. Named collectors need actual tools, time, access and funding; anonymous collectors can be bounded aggregate reservations. Resolve in stable order, expose contention before commitment where known, and never claw back settled cargo. Competing demand can consume the rare window or leave a smaller later stock, while the player retains reliable ordinary work elsewhere.
3. **Rare opportunity.** Define eligibility (location, season/local phase, route, prior clue), selection draw and seed/claim, actual occurrence versus detection, response window, physical access, costs, decline/expiry, and aftermath. Select once at an authorized tick/entry and persist the claim before display. If occupied or refused, explain the reason, retain any earned clue, and record whether it moves, expires, or becomes available later. Absence of a roll on reload and stopping automation at new opportunities prevent reroll/grind exploits. No missed-event consequence occurs merely because the app was closed.
4. **Quality and knowledge.** Quality derives from resource state, method, tool, skill and preservation; it changes a supported recipe/commission, not a generic price bonus. Record observation source, time, confidence and observer. Re-survey can update stock estimates while prior knowledge remains historical. A clue can support study, teacher instruction, field test or successful encounter; these routes grant different evidence and do not duplicate a first-discovery reward.
5. **Exact counter support.** A physical fire case might be a burning brush barrier: clear/fell the actual brush, smother it with owned soil/wet cloth, or redirect around it. The target is that barrier and those materials are consumed or damaged by the declared heat/exposure. Water does not automatically extinguish every supernatural flame. A magical flame must identify its sustaining source, reachable boundary, actual compatible suppression/ward faculty, capacity/fuel and consequence; ordinary wet cloth may char without suppressing it. A physical tripwire can interrupt only a creature whose body/path intersects it. A magical binding can catch only a target meeting its authored contact/resistance rule. Do not turn a clue into immunity or scale the target down to preserve a favored plan.

For each selected world, keep an optional concise taxonomy: resource lifecycle (renewable / recovering / finite / unique), access and rights, quality and uses, local clock, other-actor competition, opportunity policy, and counter domains. Add a bespoke family only when it changes a selected action; worlds can share useful process families while preserving their actual source rules and superior functions.
