# Six paths with changing functions and aftermath

Planning revision 1, 2026-10-05. GX1-GX6 are **original authoring probes**, not canon, registered destinations, APIs or saves. Every new person, method, range, price, material, output and clock is O/P. These paths illustrate optional local campaign selections; they do not require all worlds to have the same careers or force the existing Frontier to acquire technological/magical laws.

The current four-skill declarations already distinguish observed, learned, practiced and used states. Their source/module/native/UI/build/play statuses remain separate. The later stages here are new proposals. Cases reuse [GC1-GC8](CONSUMER_CONTRACTS.md) and existing owners; they do not reopen the completed micro transactions.

## GX1, a useful mundane field career

**Context:** proposed Cinderbrook field circuit attached to a later authored Frontier route. It is not currently registered or assumed adjacent to the Village. Native trail reading, timber judgment, Perception/Woodworking and ordinary kit consumers are reuse candidates. Good hands, tools, actual stock, known access and willing collaborators supply the means; no exceptional source is necessary.

| Stage | Actual action and function | Cost, choice and later consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Learn the existing ordinary methods through their real lessons. Inspect one local producer and choose bulk material or intact cuttings. Use a prepared kit or earned route-reading function on the supported crossing | Paid work competes with exploration time. Observation reveals a dated field estimate, not current invisible stock. Sell ordinary cargo, deliver accepted supplies, keep samples or decline the patch |
| Developed | Selective collection preserves the producer and yields intact samples accepted by a grower; clearing gives bulk now and reduces later supply. Sound-stock judgment supports two useful outputs: ordinary kits or separately authored durable markers accepted by a wet-route job | Prototype patch has ten harvest units; NPC reserves three and player four, leaving three available. Player's four yield three intact cuttings plus one unusable residue. Native item definitions/lot compatibility must be selected before this becomes gameplay. Settled cargo is never clawed back for a later collector |
| Mature | Maintain a specific coppice/tapping right, teach a willing apprentice, and select funded bulk supply, specimen care or route-restoration work. Expertise enables a low-waste work schedule and preparation of the actual retained route | No global yield bonus or automatic title. A recipient pays from a declared budget and receives actual output. Care requires one authorized local recovery interval and inputs; it cannot replenish unique sources. Delegation uses real tools/worker reservations and stop reasons |

**Career/world outcomes:** a reliable supplier has continuing demand; a restorer gains a healthier particular patch but forgoes immediate bulk; aggressive clearing leaves a depleted/contested site; declining the institution leaves ordinary gathering/trade available. Work can end or transfer to an apprentice without deleting the world or granting the apprentice all skills. Later returns show actual patch, people, rights and dated observations. Refusal does not erase competence; a stale NPC reservation changes available work before new debit. This extends progression and demand rather than repeating a generic cut/refine/base ledger.

## GX2, enormous growth of the same simple method

**Context:** Tidefold, an original hypothetical local-world rule, not Frontier or a direct book adaptation. Its ordinary residents can learn an intrinsic impulse method called Throw; the actual body/source eligibility is established at local entry or through the selected acquisition route. A high aptitude or remembered name alone does not grant it. Once acquired, the chosen rule permits growth through native competence levels alone, with ordinary repeat practice. No rare replacement, fusion, insight tax or mandatory second XP bar is added.

| Stage | Same function, changed envelope | Actual means and decision |
|---|---|---|
| Early | At prototype level one, impart controlled motion to one held object within eight meters; place a probe or throw a rope end to a visible reachable point | Actual held object, supported arm/controller and two stamina per use. The target/reach planner must support this original function; reading a description cannot execute it |
| Developed | At level three, the same declared range law reaches 24 meters. Practice can also improve chosen precision where the local policy permits it. Deliver a rescue line to a previously inaccessible ledge without entering the hazard | A rope must have real sufficient length and usable attachment; it stays one object. Choose rescue preparation, survey delivery, ordinary paid practice or an adventure. Rendering/missed throws grant no repeated completion reward |
| Mature | At level ten, range is 80 meters under the same law, with no declared final level ceiling. Supply a distant repair crew, deliver a probe across a hostile gap, or support a lifeline station | It can simply outperform ordinary throwing on distance. Object survival, destination access and attachment are separate from impulse range. No extra burnout or arbitrary rare-token cost is invented to undo that advantage |

Prototype range is `8 × native competence level`; two stamina is a chosen constant, not a sourced law or current native formula. A later world may author much larger level-only growth. These checkpoints demonstrate a real target becoming reachable, not a claim that mastery labels intrinsically alter physics everywhere. Existing method mastery can separately refine control only if that selected content uses it.

**Career/world outcomes:** become a long-range survey/rescue worker, teach the acquired local method, remain an adventurer, or leave the station to another actually trained operator. No rare loot is required to complete this path. Local application needs the world rule, actual body/source and conserved cargo; whether it travels to another world is an explicit source policy, not generic fuel conversion. A changed body may retain information without manipulation support. Source-supplied instinctive control, if selected, really supplies control without another compulsory lesson.

## GX3, technician to operating load-frame suit

**Context:** an original industrial campaign probe, Brasswake. This illustrates the requested technology/suit career rather than adding batteries or powered armor to vanilla Frontier. Existing native equipment, recipes, stock/custody, jobs and typed outputs are foundations; a modular suit/native operating object is a precise additional join.

| Stage | Actual function/access | Inputs, limits and meaningful choice |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Read an actuator sketch, inspect a broken part, repair one owned actuator and assemble a shoulder frame. A measured lift test carries one crate while leaving one hand available for bracing | One real actuator, two fasteners, one seal and paid bench time. The sketch supplies knowledge, not fitted parts. Rent a functioning frame, repair owned stock or use an ordinary hand winch |
| Developed | Fit a control grip and declared brace; a road crew's commissioned load test opens real hauling/repair work. Choose lift mode, a separately qualified protective mode, or combined operation | Prototype reserve 18 units; lift costs three per action and protection four. Two combined uses spend 14 and leave four. Output also must meet installed throughput/support, not just reserve. One battery is not independently spendable from both equipment and energy views |
| Mature | Calibrated controller and protected pack permit a rated heavy-load function and supported field repair. A service contract equips an independently funded worker or trains an operator; swapping an incompatible regulator is an actual refit choice | Prototype rated load 120 kg is an operating target, not player power rank. Real condition damage can block lift; replacing the specific damaged part restores it without replaying acquisition. No automatic wear tax is required unless the authored hardware declares one |

**Career/world outcomes:** profitable repair/hauling service, a supplied bridge crew, a failed contract retaining damaged owned equipment, rented specialist work, or a trained successor. A reliable suit may simply be better for its heavy job; ordinary repair/finesse/trade remains useful. Parts travel only with actual custody and supported installation; a local bench/employer/right does not travel. A fixed authored frame variant can prove the function first. If the selected mature choice promises individual regulator replacement, conserved installed-part identity and native refit projection are required; a renamed whole-item bonus cannot close that promise.

## GX4, artificer to a functioning anchored ward

**Context:** Kilnreach, an original artifice probe. The declared source acts on incoming nonliving motion through marked directional anchors. It is not generic immunity, spirit authority, elemental conversion or a canonical spell. A purchased/inherited validated source can legitimately skip invention and supply its own control when its rules say so.

| Stage | Actual function/access | Inputs, support and choice |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Read a pattern, obtain real substrate/anchor and operating source, then test one directional doorway mark against a measured padded weight | Pattern alone is knowledge. Paid material/time test establishes only that target/context. Unsupported copper claimed as a spirit anchor yields a limited negative result, not a shield or fabricated source |
| Developed | Two correctly oriented anchors protect the declared opening against supported incoming debris, enabling useful store/care work. Ordinary entry and unrelated targets remain unaffected | Prototype reservoir 12 units, combined upkeep two per supported local minute, lasts six minutes. This is a played authorized local interval, not protection secretly running overnight while the app is closed. Removing one anchor stops its dependent direction; actual remaining support determines the other |
| Mature | Refine the same actual reservoir under this original local rule, or legally acquire a better source, then test the larger assembly. It protects a larger store opening and supports inspection, replenishment and teaching; the keeper can fund protection of delivered medical stock | Proposed refinement consumes one owned concentrate pack and eight local work minutes, preserving source identity and remaining charge while raising its supported ceiling from 12 to 60. It is not practice XP or a free refill. After separately funded charging, the full-reserve probe of 60 at upkeep three gives 20 supported minutes. Stronger protection keeps its declared superiority; stock stays singular |

**Career/world outcomes:** a useful keeper school, protected stores and care access, a specialist service, or an abandoned damaged ward with remaining owned materials. Known recipe, owned object, active source, operating mode and site permission remain separate. Extinguishing the magical channel does not extinguish already ignited ordinary packaging; physically ruined supplies need their own remedy. Particular upkeep is an original ward rule, never an imposed universal magic penalty. Its object reservoir and source-growth command need one selected authoritative owner/native bridge; current actor-bound energy policy alone does not establish object charge, anchored operation or refinement of an already learned policy. Do not implement growth by silently relearning it or duplicating the reserve.

## GX5, inquiry to a personal technique beyond its local creator

**Context:** proposed later Frontier workshop work. Shared inquiry's current paid participant/evidence declarations and the existing workshop are reuse candidates. New wet-stock markers, jig, test and order are original later content. No generic research currency or guaranteed guild standing is needed.

| Stage | Knowledge/action/result | Actual choice and consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Read one incomplete drying/packing note and consult the willing craftsperson. Its supported subset makes cautious ordinary output; a missing seal geometry prevents the proposed wet-stock function | Keep using the known method, fund a test or decline. Possessing a fragment does not complete it. Native literacy/competence and method knowledge retain separate ownership |
| Developed | Compare real wet/dry samples with reserved attention and owned inputs. A paid physical trial of a jig creates actual sound or warped test pieces and attributed limited evidence | Player can commission a failed test without gaining the successful recipe. Successful result may publish one tested variant; actual sample consumption/output/cause persists once. Preview and report reading neither reroll nor re-award XP |
| Mature | The personal variant demonstrably makes two sound markers from two logs and one water in four local minutes, against the teacher's recorded two-water/six-minute benchmark for the same target/context. A funded wet-route order actually consumes the improved output | The reusable jig had a prior paid build and is retained; per-batch inputs do not recreate it. This exceeds that benchmark on time/water, not every creator in history. Teach with consent, keep the method private, publish it, or focus on paid production |

Prototype benchmark improvement saves one water and two minutes per valid batch. These are authoring arithmetic, not measured engine performance or tested recipe behavior. Sound/warped marker identity or condition must be bound to a real item/native recipient; a “research complete” flag cannot make them useful. A superior successful variant needs no invented drawback. A learned incomplete method remains useful within its actual supported subset.

**Career/world outcomes:** expedition supplier, respected or disputed local inventor, a voluntarily taught workshop, a failed reconstruction with retained negative notes, or livelihood using the old method. Actual teacher beliefs/permissions determine social response; useful work does not buy affection, office or global reputation. Later trial contexts can test portability, but cannot silently overwrite contradictory reports or declare an unobserved result true.

## GX6, preparation, a rare opportunity and cross-path aftermath

**Context:** one original later campaign opportunity, adaptable to a chosen local world. A keeper schedules a scarce boundary-counter lesson during eight **played local minutes** of actual supported presence. The actor has their own appointment/means; the occurrence and any eligible selection draw are saved before UI. Discovery time does not recreate or extend its occurrence. No browser-closed expiry is selected.

| Stage | Actual function/choice | Time, cost and later consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Scout three minutes, prepare two, travel two, then seek a two-minute lesson. Nine minutes misses an offer requiring lesson completion by minute eight. Alternatively use a genuinely recent qualified route report, prepare two, travel two and learn two, arriving/completing within six | The dated report must actually support skipping scouting. A known expired offer says why; an unknown one stays unknown. Being occupied with a paid job or declining has its own cause; no force-cancel or automatic refund |
| Developed | Learned counter plus actual compatible source/fuel/control and reachable boundary ends one maintained wind barrier. A rope/brush obstacle can instead counter the specific physical charging path; a safe detour remains possible | A clue or taught theory does not supply missing source. Counter costs are explicit and paid once. Ordinary burning brush after the magic ends still needs soil/wet cloth or avoidance; wrong domain/contact is an actual mismatch, not level scaling |
| Mature | Combine field evidence, sound markers/load apparatus, supported counter and a willing funded guide to deliver care stock through the hazardous route. Strong preparation changes a real rescue/delivery option without requiring every career | Source-supplied intrinsic counter control works if genuinely granted. Each helper reserves actual tools/time and each consumed kit/charge appears once. Ordinary guides, shelter and later travel remain viable when the rare lesson was missed |

**Career/world outcomes:** timely supply and a continuing route service; a failed paid crossing with retained wounds/spent stock and care offered afterward; a deliberately declined dangerous job and intact ordinary livelihood; or a missed rare lesson followed by later supported instruction at a different actual opportunity. No universal fate/luck or good/evil score is selected. A named source may instead give an exceptional shortcut if source-qualified; do not artificially weaken it to equalize ordinary routes. The rarity is optional. Reach, readiness, chance, independent willingness and clock are different gates.

## Connections and choice-to-aftermath consumers

GX1's sound cargo and dated survey can support GX3's load test or GX6's preparation; GX4's declared protection can preserve the actual trial supplies needed by GX5. These are transferable outputs/evidence only in a selected compatible campaign, not automatic cross-world fuel conversion. A paid wet-route order choosing GX5 output consumes that stock, so the same markers cannot also be sold or supplied to a simultaneous rescue.

| Accumulated choice | Later actual opportunity/outcome | What legitimate reconvergence retains |
|---|---|---|
| Supply/restoration versus clearing (GX1) | Funded steady service, intact specimen teaching, depleted patch or changed competition | Particular producer/deposit stock, existing cargo, rights and people |
| Calibrated device and practiced operation (GX3/4) | Heavy repair, protected care/storage or a supported service school | Actual source/object/parts, repairs, charge, learned information and site-local limits |
| Observed successful variant versus refused/failed test (GX5) | Improved funded order/teaching, ordinary old-method livelihood or further qualified inquiry | Paid material outcome, benchmark version, limited evidence and specific permissions |
| Prior known route and chosen preparation versus occupied/missed lesson (GX6) | Different legal crossing/rescue option, paid failure, later opportunity or ordinary work | Occurrence/response cause, source/method support, injuries/stock and actual directed relationships |

The timeline and society GMs own their broader branch graphs/ending policies; this table supplies functional prerequisites and aftermath consumers. Career maturity does not automatically resolve the whole arc. Resolving the arc does not force death, succession, departure, prestige or world destruction. Later ordinary work, teaching, repair and return remain useful when selected. Mandatory content IDs and optional cross-path combinations are frozen by the world owner, never inferred from this catalogue.
