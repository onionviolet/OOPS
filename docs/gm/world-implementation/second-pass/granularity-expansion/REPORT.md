## Five priority proposals

1. **GP1, connect growth to functions and continuing careers.** Reuse the current four IDs; add selected developed/mature applications and actual outcome consumers. Keep aptitude, competence, method mastery, source/body change and institutional access distinct. Allow a chosen local skill to grow enormously through levels alone if that is its declared rule. Creator-relative mastery compares a specific witnessed benchmark, not omniscient global rankings.
2. **GP2, add one living field and preparation circuit if selected.** A supported survey changes harvest/preparation choices; a chosen method changes that patch and the return. An ordinary funded career remains complete without exceptional loot. Resource ecology, direct counters and rare occurrences require precise selected targets and clocks, rather than a global climate or element simulator.
3. **GP3, make inquiry produce tested reusable methods.** Distinguish hypothesis, evidence, paid trial, supported conclusion, personal variant and useful commissioned output. Let a successful design surpass an old method. A failed paid trial consumes declared resources and may teach a limited result; it cannot fabricate authority, compatibility or a source.
4. **GP4, qualify one supported assembly family.** A load frame or doorway ward changes hauling, care, production or protection through real parts, modes and support. Start with fixed authored variants where enough; dynamic refitting is required only when changing the particular installed part is the player's meaningful choice. Technology, magical craft and inherited objects keep their actual local rules.
5. **GP5, connect optional opportunities and cross-path decisions to aftermath.** Preparations, inventions, ordinary jobs and independent people affect which opportunity is reachable and what remains afterward. A rare source can be genuinely superior. Missing it closes that specific advantage, not every career. Skill maturity, arc closure, institution change, retirement/departure and world/life endings remain different outcomes.

This is priority among candidate additions, not five authorized runtime packets or a replacement for the complete-world checklist. Requiredness becomes finite when a selected world's owner accepts IDs and alternatives. No world must implement all six paths, every item family, rare source, suit or ecological model. More desired breadth remains possible after reviewed additions rather than silently changing the current completion gate.
