# Item families and functional coverage

Requested follow-up, 2026-10-04: “Pills, artifacts, consumables, other stuff and more?” This expands the Items audit's explicit coverage. It is **planning only**, with runtime still held. The families below are original authoring directions unless an exact supplied-source claim is attached to a selected item. Names do not imply accepted numbers, canonical effects or completed gameplay.

## Five connected families

| Family | What the catalogue can include | Concrete player choices and actual function |
|---|---|---|
| Pills, elixirs and preparations | Restorative pills, antidotes, reserve recovery, training aids, breakthrough preparation, purification, body alteration/restoration and source-qualified lifespan or soul treatments; powders, balms, liquids and food preparations can share mechanisms | Take one dose now, save it for a trial, administer it to another supported recipient or sell it. A temporary focus effect expires; a selected permanent change modifies the actual body/source/progression owner once. A preparation can improve a real trial without granting its absent faculty. Drug resistance, toxicity, addiction, age limits and diminishing returns exist only when the particular source/original rule calls for them. IT05/IT11. |
| Artifacts, equipment and exceptional objects | Passive relics, active instruments, charged/rechargeable devices, evolving/repairable artifacts, inscriptions/enchantments, sensing/flight/protection tools, anchors and source-qualified bound, cursed or sentient objects | Equip a relic for its actual passive function; activate a sensor to detect a supported signal; feed or refine a living object under its own control/stock rule. Refinement can genuinely improve or transform a function. Ownership, attunement, custody, control and operating fuel remain distinct. A stronger artifact may simply be better. IT04/IT08/IT09/IT12. |
| Consumables and deployables | Meals/drinks, medicine, ammunition, explosives, thrown mixtures, scrolls/talismans, trap parts, emergency wards, bait, fuel, reagents, catalysts and disposable repair supplies | Choose eating, applying, throwing, firing or deploying, with actual recipient/target/contact rules. Spending a ward token can create one real persistent protective object; its expiry/removal cannot refund the already consumed token. A reusable charged device is not a one-use scroll. Bait supports the authored animal/ecology response rather than merely adding a luck label. IT03/IT05/IT12. |
| Tools, apparatus and containers | Cauldrons, distillers, furnaces, molds, calibration instruments, repair kits, workbenches and portable stations; bottles, sacks, quivers, sealed boxes, preservation vessels and source-qualified spatial storage | Choose a station/service or carry compatible apparatus; reserve the actual tool while work needs it. Fill, empty, seal, preserve or transfer a container's real contents. A bottle and potion contents differ; an empty bottle cannot provide a dose. Spatial storage needs an actual containment/boundary rule and the same conserved contents, not duplicated bag stock. IT01/IT03/IT07/IT13. |
| Resources, knowledge, access and living stock | Ores/alloys/herbs/reagents, monster materials and salvage; manuals, maps, formulas, blueprints and specimens; physical keys/seals/licenses/claim documents; seeds, eggs, living Gu, unique organs and producer artifacts | Decide between using, researching, trading or retaining scarce stock. Reading teaches its declared information; holding a key permits its particular lock only under the selected access rule. A document can evidence a claim without creating issuer permission by itself. Growing a living specimen or harvesting its supported product changes actual stock; repeated generic loot cannot reproduce a unique person's organ. IT01/IT06/IT09/IT13. |

These groups overlap deliberately: a living artifact can be equipment, a producer and a controllable source; an enchanted pill can be a dose with a source-qualified transformation. The catalogue maps functions and dependencies, not five compulsory inventory tabs or one class per noun. The list stays extensible when a new item changes a real action.

## Actual support versus new work

[Alchemy](../../../../../src/crafting_recipes.js#L1830) already crafts healing powders, juice, potions and balms from real inputs. The [medicine manual](../../../../../src/items.js#L1291) already supplies recipe/skill rewards; [Book](../../../../../src/items.js#L1089) tracks reading progress. [UsableItem](../../../../../src/items.js#L481) and the [native use handler](../../../../../src/main.js#L3399) apply timed template effects and recovered containers. [ActiveEffect](../../../../../src/active_effects.js#L14) carries duration, stats, potency/group tags and travel policy. [Artifact](../../../../../src/items.js#L720) supplies native equipment stats. Keep these positive functions.

A named ordinary restorative pill with an existing timed effect may be ordinary content after the shared transaction bridge is qualified. Its pill shape does not demand a new engine. Actual new contracts are permanent outcome carriers and source-specific dose interactions (IT11), object-bound activation/deployment and aftermath (IT12), and authoritative containment/access/information relationships (IT13). The inspected Artifact class's stat function does not establish every active artifact; the native player-use handler does not establish thrown/tactical/placed-item behavior. Source/claim/condition and job services are reusable foundations, not proof of those families.

## Shared item definition requirements

Every selected item declares its acquisition/production evidence and cause; physical quantity/instance, owner/holder/installed/contained relation; known versus actual quality/condition; available modes and recipient/target/source requirements; consumed inputs or retained apparatus; real outputs and temporary/permanent aftermath; maintenance/refinement/repair/recharge/salvage; portability/retention; and native UI/effect consumers. Only fields that change the selected function need runtime state.

Temporary effects, resource restoration, permanent body changes, learned information, source acquisition, deployed objects and institutional permission have different authoritative owners. A generic “used” flag or rarity label cannot substitute for them. A consumed item may leave a permanent change or deployed object; recovery/removal does not recreate its spent parent unless its actual rule explicitly restores one. Stack potency, repeat dosing, ingredient alternatives and side effects follow local rules. Do not invent a universal pill-poison penalty, artifact slot ceiling, binding ritual, item tier cap or compulsory maintenance tax.

Future cases can extend the already proposed bulk/instrument qualification: one focus pill with a temporary effect; one original preparation tied to an actual progression trial; an active signal relic and disposable ward; one filled preservation vessel; and a manual/key whose reading/access is distinct from possession. Select only the cases needed by the chosen campaign. Other required families stay visible in mechanic coverage rather than being declared finished by one representative item.

Items owns objects/recipes/contents and transformations. Powers owns actual body/source/execution; Combat owns tactical targeting/deployment; Regions owns ecological response; Bases owns collective facilities/production; Society owns issuer rights/disclosure. Engine binds those owners into native publication and persistence. [FINDINGS](FINDINGS.json), [dependency handoff](DEPENDENCIES.md) and [checkpoint](CHECKPOINT.md) retain exact gaps, source qualifiers and future acceptance. No new source reading, implementation or gameplay check is implied by this catalogue.
