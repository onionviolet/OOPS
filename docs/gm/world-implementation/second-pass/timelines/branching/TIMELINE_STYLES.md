# Timeline styles and ending categories

Planning recommendation, 2026-10-05. A world may combine these styles; chronology is an authoring/state contract, not a required player-facing timeline screen. Current four skill/discovery IDs are a separate authorized implementation wave. These recommendations neither select all world runtime nor change the current played-time/freeze/death/prestige policy.

## Styles and appropriate worlds

| Style | Useful choice structure / pacing | Appropriate per-world recommendation | Cost or limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Linear/chaptered | A clear beginning, linked dependencies and deliberate chapter closure. Local choices change people/resources/next eligibility even when later chapters reconverge. | Use as optional local arcs inside Frontier; academy year/crisis or institutional initiation can use it when the era/source is known. | A chapter does not mark unplayed learning/production/canon events complete. Avoid locking all ordinary work behind a single plot rail. |
| Open persistent chronicle | Jobs, adventure, discovery, research and local life run alongside milestone arcs. Endings close a particular undertaking while the world continues. | **Frontier default**, including its native opening and OF.EXPLORE/CONTROL/MASTERY. Fallout's repair/settlement careers fit this as an authoring recommendation based on its bounded campaign owner. | Finite required world IDs still define delivery. An open world need not offer infinite generated quests or simulate every distant resident. |
| Deadline/season opportunity | Disclosed appointments, caravans, expeditions, source upkeep or funded orders create timing choices, including missed opportunities while doing something else. | Overlay selectively on Frontier's actual offers; Gu caravan/market and upkeep cases need their own qualified local clock. | Show only known windows/costs; no browser-closed punishment, daily attendance tax or mandatory rare jackpot. Real Town journeys are not compressed into paperwork minutes. |
| Life/career history | Early useful action becomes practiced competence, mature vocation and a possible office handoff. Household/care/body and relationships supply causes, not just age labels. | Frontier can mature without enrolling mortal aging. Child/tree/native-origin branches use bounded phase advances only if selected. Regressor-style lives require their exact sourced carrier/era rules, not the same generic prestige. | Preview unresolved duties, learning, stock, involved people and actual body changes. No real-time childhood years, free mastered profession or automatic dynasty. |
| Loop/regression | Retained evidence enables genuinely new preparations across a supported repeated history; current nonparticipants remain nonremembering. | Mother of Learning's loop is a separate eligible/source-qualified operation beside ordinary academy/research play. RI's memory-bearing return is a differently qualified regression, not a safe checkpoint for everyone. The **original Stillwater** case works a finite two-pass research style. | Snapshot/retention/identity/export/outside-clock work is required when selected. Dilation is a separate positive mechanism, not another restart; no free physical notes or universal body reset. |
| Independent historical branch | A frozen earlier/alternative era permits a different career or intervention while preserving the present instance. | Frontier's upstream-reference counterpart/earlier branch; separately authored franchise era profiles. | New actual people/stock/history must bind. Counterparts are not the same current people; this cheaper prequel function does not satisfy live rewind. |
| Mission/extraction inside lived world | Preparation, issuer obligations, local intervention, actual return and later residence/return coexist. | Paradise-style hub: open resident chronicle plus optional mission chapters/deadlines. A Fallout or other destination can remain a home after a bounded assignment. | Issuer success, welfare, extraction initiation, arrival and connection closure are different. Do not reset the destination after mission settlement. |

**Choice:** keep Frontier open with optional local chapters and disclosed funded windows; choose temporal rules per selected world/profile. The Stillwater graph is an original contrasting case, not a replacement Frontier start or direct MoL/Paradise adaptation. The old [world-loop matrix](../../../depth/macro/WORLD_LOOP_MATRIX.md) supplies qualified comparison limits: Gu ch.1-390 case, MoL supplied 106 chapters plus epilogue, Paradise MTL U for original canon, Fallout base-game/2287. No new source claims are promoted here.

## What an ending ends

| Ending domain | What changes | What does not follow automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Arc | The particular disputed delivery, trial, rescue, crisis or agreement receives a settled outcome. | All careers/world content complete or a global reset. |
| Career maturity | A demonstrated function, reliable practice, research result or willing teaching relationship becomes useful recurring play. | Universal rank, a forced ceiling, all functions or permanent job exclusivity. |
| Local institution / household | Its rights, capacity, stock, people, charter or access change. | All regional government, global morality or the person's other relationships change. |
| Represented-world conclusion | Player resolves the selected connected local campaign path and its aftermath within declared scope. | Implementers delivered every alternative required ID; the world erased; every source publication adapted. |
| Departure / extraction | Actual body/cargo arrival at a supported destination settles. | Initiation already succeeded, all locals helped, current institution closed or return guaranteed. |
| Office retirement / succession | Specific duties/rights are relinquished; an actual willing successor owns their learned operation and accepted work. | Player life ends, source transfers, birth lineage appears or all NPC labor becomes free. |
| Life ending / death | Only the selected body's/life's declared policy settles, with actual retention and continuing people. | Rebirth reward, resurrection, world destruction or a descendant is automatically created. No new life-ending rule is selected here. |
| World destruction / erased history | A specifically supported event actually destroys/restores its declared scope and affected owners. | Any quest, closed archive, loop shutdown or missed offer implies destruction. Neither worked graph grants this mechanism. |

Final choices can settle still-open commitments, reveal information or choose among currently legal next steps. They cannot retroactively unsell a unique item, create a refused collaborator, erase injury, replace earlier knowledge or revive an unavailable source. A revealed ending preview shows known terms, not all hidden NPC motives or a deterministic future screen.

## Temporal phase advances and return policy

A chapter/time skip needs its own bounded projection: participating actor/body/age, experienced work, known learning, real production/consumption, remaining promises, office/care duties, source/condition timers and local/outside clocks. Settlement names what happens to each consequential outstanding job; missing/unrepresented days are labelled. A life-stage vignette can skip routine days without awarding years of XP or automatically remembering relationships.

A reset recipe restores only declared local domains and retains only actual supported carriers. Stillwater deliberately demonstrates a powerful retained research advantage while restoring local anchor reagents; externally spent seal/claims do not reset. Its no-cargo-before-shutdown rule is original local fiction, not a law inferred for every book. A source-qualified direct adaptation must retrieve its exact mechanism instead of inheriting this recipe.

Reentry loads the same persistent outcome and current owner state. A new offer/project may reopen access but leaves earlier refusal, sales, injury, handoff and missed opportunity records true. Unsupported absence still freezes. A successor can operate only the finite funded request under actually supported time; retirement never proves browser-closed production.
