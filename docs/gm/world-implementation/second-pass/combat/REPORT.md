# Combat encounters and AI: second-pass decision summary

Planning revision 2, 2026-10-04. **Extend the existing saved contest into a small objective-driven encounter, while keeping one shared effect/publication path.** The current Mara/Lio proof already checks real consent, body/control, owned kit, separate stamina/effort, occupancy and retained wounds. Its limits are specific: fixed participants/targets, one guard role, voluntary withdrawal and one-HP outcomes. Macro/Micro already identified the general battlefield gap. This pass refines the missing contracts and finds one concrete exhaustion risk rather than repeating that catalogue.

The prioritized [finding matrix](FINDINGS.json) distinguishes retained groundwork from additions and gives each an owner, evidence lines, original player case, cheaper alternative and future acceptance case. Five changes should shape the master's next proposal:

1. **Freeze the native fighter and action-time contracts first (CB01–CB03).** [Party contracts](../../../../../src/native_battle_contracts.js) require exactly three IDs; the [host](../../../../../src/native_battle_host.js) fixes sides/actions. Native [cadence](../../../../../src/models/combat.js) lives in private browser timers, resets on resume and normalizes all cooldowns to the fastest fighter. [Joined time](../../../../../src/main.js) pauses for the player's session. A new battlefield needs an explicit saved windup/launch/recovery policy and deterministic support/reaction ordering. Preserve the agreed contest's existing policy. Also resolve raw versus effective attack: [the getter](../../../../../src/components/levelable_component.js) already penalizes zero stamina, while [party execution](../../../../../src/party_battle.js) applies another factor. Default symbolic arithmetic implies a second half-penalty for already-empty party fighters; this is static evidence, not observed damage.

2. **Make target legality and tactical functions operational (CB04–CB05).** Separate sensing, known contact, movement, attack reach, sight, cover and effect-specific contact. A scout can detect beyond melee range. An independent launched arrow stays spent after its source disappears; a maintained effect can require continuing support. Guard must reach the actual incoming effect; an area action needs its own target phase and friendly-fire rule. [Numerical strike math](../../../../../src/combat_math.js) remains useful after legality. Its minimum-hit damage cannot substitute for source-qualified immunity, unavailable contact, control or suppression.

3. **Give fighters small credible policies (CB06).** [Existing controllers](../../../../../src/simulation.js) already choose eligible actions in deterministic order. Extend that foundation with actual objectives, observed contacts, paid means and committed plans. Keep planner knowledge separate from canonical validation facts. An escort protects cargo; a captain can recover a wounded scout; an ally can refuse pursuit. Use short priorities before considering expensive search or live generation.

4. **Connect goals, retreat and aftermath (CB07–CB08).** Current withdrawal correctly ends a voluntary contest; hostile escape requires an actual route, time, interception and pursuit handoff. Delivery or rescue can succeed while a stronger opponent survives. Retain positions, cargo, wounds, lawful stock transfers and witness-qualified consequences. One consequential injury and one salvage receipt are enough to qualify the chain; full anatomy and a new lethal policy are separate choices.

5. **Keep representation proportional and advantages real (CB09–CB10).** A few authored positions can support cover and retreat. Existing pursuit BFS means fewest legs, not necessarily fastest arrival; select the actual time/supply objective before adding weighted navigation. Replan at meaningful boundaries. Preserve the current original writ's guaranteed location despite ordinary cover, without inventing guaranteed damage, victory or a balancing weakness.

All new crossing, injury and AI examples are original proposals. No new novel/franchise rule is asserted. [Dependencies](DEPENDENCIES.md) keeps Regions, Powers, Bodies, Items, Society and Foundations ownership distinct. No runtime/token savings were measured; small graphs and bounded policies are proposed simplifications. [Checkpoint](CHECKPOINT.md) records fingerprints, document checks and closeout. Runtime remains held for director review; later playable/save/failure acceptance stays unrun.
