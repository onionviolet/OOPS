# Bodies, powers and interactions: second-pass decision return

Planning audit complete, 2026-10-04. **Retain the existing typed owners and the micro organ contract; add five source-specific execution cases before broad power enrollment.** The gap is connecting acquired means to actual functions across form, controller, anchor and support changes. A new taxonomy or another stat ladder would not establish those behaviors. The assigning master alone amends the [canonical plan](../../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md); runtime remains held for Weibao's review.

1. **POW01: bind functions to form and actual anchors.** Distinguish species, ancestry, acquired enhancement, temporary transformation, permanent structural growth and current injury. A changed body can enable flight or block manipulation while retaining learned information. MoL's transformation-shell explanation is a character account; its body-anchored enhancement actually fails after soul exit. Neither proves a universal rule for every world. Qualify one reversible form change and one lasting functional growth path. Use selected body-function profiles first, retaining simple callus/soreness records where sufficient. Full anatomy, hereditary population simulation and every possible evolutionary branch can wait for selected campaigns.

2. **POW02: separate embodiment from controller and shared means.** The existing traveler preview already guards body references, singular cargo and pooled fuel, but explicitly lacks native execution. Shadow Slave's seven bodies share one mind/soul/essence; MoL's simulacra have separate experience and agency while using the original's soul/mana. Give these different controller policies. Multiple positions do not create extra swords, souls or reserves, and a copied person's relationships are not universal shared knowledge. First qualify two bodies, one shared pool and one real weapon; defer general possession/incarnation until its separate carrier/host policy is reviewed.

3. **POW03: declare what acquisition produces.** Existing learn/copy/transfer/fusion operations are useful. Their owned/mastery/lineage records alone do not establish whether a transaction teaches a method, lends execution, moves a source, creates an organism or consumes constituents into a new source. Bind the selected route's output, donor outcome, support, revision and termination. Preserve source-supplied control: MoL's life-force integration can convey instinctive use, and traveler definitions already allow intrinsic guidance. Do not require ordinary training when the acquired source genuinely supplies it. Author concrete transactions over existing effects before proposing a universal acquisition engine.

4. **POW04: maintain support, then suppress or restore precisely.** Current energy policies separate reserve/output/control/strain; faculty suppression preserves ownership. Jobs settle frozen effects, and periodic conditions do not generically revalidate a source channel. Select a maintained channel and a body-bound anchor case with distinct startup, use, upkeep, recovery, capacity commitment, output and strain. Loss of support ends only its dependent execution; restoration does not replay acquisition or refund spent fuel. Preserve Sunny's conditionally indefinite avatar sustain. Use authorized clock boundaries and dependency changes, avoiding per-frame physiology or parallel timers.

5. **POW05: counter functions and compose actual outputs.** A dispel can end MoL levitation while leaving ignited paper burning. Repair, immunity, reflection, suppression and fusion therefore need target/domain-specific rules and retained aftermath. Keep numeric force useful and superior sources superior. Start with explicit local intercept/exception rules, not a universal element table or every-power pairing. Rule-based effects need no invented damage capacity; a finite implementation sentinel is not a sourced cosmic ceiling.
