# Native expansion and implementation feasibility

2026-10-05. **Planning complete. Extend the existing game through connected native careers; add shared adapters when a selected function needs them.** Ordinary skills, gathering, component equipment, zones, work and quests already have useful consumers. The wider vision is feasible as a sequence of finite authored families, but arbitrary combinations of bodies, powers, worlds and histories remain unresolved engineering work. ## Five connected native additions

All examples below are **original reversible proposals**, not selected names, balance or implemented campaign requirements. They can enrich familiar play without making every family compulsory in every world.

1. **N01: useful skills through increasingly hard applications.** Early: earn a taught brace, trail procedure or timber lesson. Developed: better cuts make actual kits, trail knowledge enables a supported route, and control avoids declared fatigue/injury. Mature: inspect a harder material/use case, develop a tested refinement and teach a willing learner. Existing `skills.timber.precise_pack`, `skills.trail.route` and brace responses supply first consumers; mature feats and reciprocal teaching still need content/receipts. Keep native XP, method mastery and source ownership distinct. Small-to-medium for another connected use; medium for a complete local mastery path (F01/F02/F04).
2. **N02: regional supply chains with useful equipment.** Early: ordinary wood, ore, herbs and food have local tool/season/access constraints. Developed: native recipes turn them into components, meals, consumables and fit-for-use gear for a chosen outing. Mature: a specific inspected lot or prepared equipment changes a harder job and earns repeat demand. Resource/recipe/component rows are mostly content; one material-specification result needs a narrow adapter if ordinary quality/item variants cannot represent the choice. Do not retrofit universal spoilage to make one lot matter. Small-to-medium, with medium joins for lots/continued demand (F01/F03).
3. **N03: zones with different purposes and credible threats.** Early: accessible field/cave margins offer observation, safe preparation and actual risk. Developed: forest/Town journeys bundle materials, receiving, instruction and an optional contest. Mature: the player solves a route problem through supported control, equipment, evidence, cooperation or sufficient strength. Enemy identity, motive, means and threat timeline explain the encounter; defeating its cause may avert it. Native enemies/zones/stances handle ordinary fights; a new post-acceptance encounter receipt or power/contact rule is adapter work. Medium; richer geometry is a separately selected large family (F02/F06).
4. **N04: real work and people with alternatives.** Early: existing jobs fund tools and study; a local contact offers a voluntary paid service. Developed: auditing a load, supplying a smaller order or underwriting material changes actual wages, stock and later meetings. Mature: close a finite charter as specialist, steward, willing successor or departing traveler, with ordinary work still available. Existing rewards, people, purses/jobs and relationship records carry much of this; quote/accept/amend/close and institutional rights need explicit finite contracts. Medium for a cast/charter; broad families, romance, taming and autonomous populations are larger optional cases (F04/F06).
5. **N05: a workshop that produces discoveries and return reasons.** Early: reuse earned Shack/workbench access. Developed: fund one comparison, build a fixed useful fitting and learn from its actual result, including declared failure. Mature: make a better variant, share it through willing paid teaching, or support an institution's finite project. Existing component recipes, jobs, project phases, knowledge and focus/drive provide hooks. A dynamic suit/ward assembly needs conserved installed parts, output/support and interruption only if refitting itself changes play. Medium for authored inventions; large for arbitrary assemblies (F03/F04/F05).

## One early-to-mature native career: field craft and route service

**R01, proposed route.** The entry stays the familiar rat/elder/craftsman progression. The player earns tools, Shack and actual route access, works native jobs or gathers wood, then explicitly observes and learns timber judgment/trail reading. These first methods are source-integrated and built-unverified, not a proven campaign journey.

At the developed stage, the player practices with real time/materials, produces useful native kits and learns the dry-bank procedure. A supplied ordinary approach remains viable. Earned field workshop/rack/rope, food/equipment and recovery support a combined Village → forest road → Town outskirts → Town square visit. The native base legs are **240/240/40 played minutes before modifiers**, not the held episode's shortcut 4/4/1 minutes. Town receiving, ledger/permit work and instruction should justify the trip ([Frontier rules](../../../../worlds/original-frontier/RULES_AND_STARTS.md:45)).

Then connect the held Short Measure audit/underwrite/refuse/delay alternatives to a paid inspected lot, independently funded dispatch and later report. Reserve actual employer stock/custody and respect geography. The player can use controlled protection or a separate declared native encounter to support a workday; old zone clears cannot masquerade as a new accepted contribution. These joins remain planned.

At maturity, close a finite four-order return charter through a tested material result, harder route/control use and negotiated or production-based threat settlement. A successful refinement changes a real output/operating envelope; a learner needs its own body, source, tools, consent and paid time. Specialist service, local stewardship, teaching/handoff or departure preserves different aftermath. Native gathering/trade/rest/practice remains useful when charter funds or orders end. Revisit for a newly funded need, actual reported change, known materials or mature teaching, without compulsory daily upkeep or erased refusal history. The existing field renewal is optional and supplies no second prestige.

This route illustrates N01–N05 within the existing **33-parent/15-step/seven-ending Frontier planning contract**. It neither replaces that ledger nor completes the world by implementing one ending. [WORLD_COMPLETION](../../../../worlds/original-frontier/WORLD_COMPLETION.md:3) and [E01–E09](../../../../worlds/original-frontier/IMPLEMENTATION_PACKET.md:13) retain full required alternatives, recovery, aftermath and acceptance. `src/frontier_campaign.js` is the narrower delivered rival/workshop/renewal slice; the similarly named held `original_frontier_campaign_content.js` is not the complete enrolled campaign.

## Difficulty across the full vision

Sizes are qualitative engineering/integration judgments, not hours or measured savings. **Small**: localized definitions with existing consumers. **Medium**: a finite function with coordinated adapter/UI/publication work. **Large**: several owners, campaign branches or persistent lifecycle changes. **Unknown**: unspecified generalized semantics or unmeasured scale. A complete selected family includes content and later validation; an arbitrary universal mechanism is a different task.

The original U01–U14 and later requests are explicitly mapped in JSON. Run-changing treasures, RNG/action/insight growth, hidden-strength opponents, luck/identity theft, monster taming, concurrent characters, romance/inheritance, childhood, nonhuman starts, lived hubs, rifts and source-specific superiority remain represented. None becomes mandatory in every world. Atmospheric conversation and scenery need no required payoff.

The cost multiplies at **interactions**: an ordinary taught technique in the same human body is modest; preserving that technique through a stolen organ, imported fuel, a second clock and a rewound branch requires several owners to agree. Use a common contract where two real cases already demand it: Short Measure/native regional work plus Glasshouse needs instance/clock/publication boundaries; native component gear plus a selected installed device needs explicit physical ownership. Do not build a universal interpreter from taxonomy alone ([foundations](../foundations/REPORT.md), [integration](../integration/REPORT.md), [selective absorption](../engine-absorption/REPORT.md)).
