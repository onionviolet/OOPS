# Proposed absorption packets and connected cases

Status: original proposals for later selection, 2026-10-05. These packets do not amend the canonical implementation plan or authorize gameplay edits. Engine remains the shared runtime/native/UI/save integrator; the plan coordinator and Weibao select scope. The examples below are hypothetical, not playable evidence. Names, counts, durations, fiction and mastery bands are reversible authoring choices. Both cases can live within present-continuity Original Frontier, with the familiar opening and sole Field apprenticeship prestige retained.

## Case A: a useful ordinary trade becomes remembered knowledge

Miri carries letters between Town outskirts and the village. In this proposed scene, she owns two dry resin pellets, three local coins and one lantern; the player starts the review fixture with three Rough wood logs, one Prepared survey kit and eight native coins. Miri knows pellet wick repair and the upper bridge route. The player initially knows only that her lantern sputters. Her private destination, other stock and recipe knowledge are not displayed.

The player can inspect the offered lantern, take an ordinary paid bench job, buy a demonstrated pellet, learn wick control from a willing knowledgeable craftsperson, lend a usable kit, or leave. Inspection records a directed `seen` report, not ownership or execution. A lesson reserves the actual teacher and their available attention alongside the player's time/fee; completion teaches the procedure and its supported source. Repeated careful work can unlock an insulated wick procedure that works in rain. That is a new function with a registered eligibility/result consumer, not only a larger XP rate. Existing timber judgment can already make real kits; it should remain useful rather than gain another duplicate mastery ledger.

Miri's controller has one bounded self-help action. It must find her wet-lantern condition, a usable hand, known pellet procedure, actual pellet stock and an available supported decision boundary. The accepted command consumes her pellet, clears the authored lantern condition and publishes her visible flare once. Only an actual player witness receives the pellet-use observation; arriving after the flare may reveal a spent pellet or attributed testimony instead. The selector uses an authored registered command through `previewSimulationCommand` and commitment. This is a small connecting recipe over the existing controller, not evidence of arbitrary inventory planning.

Materials and money have actual owners. A completed repair can transfer one paid pellet or kit from its maker to Miri and two of her finite local coins to the player; a player lesson reserves native money and credits the named teacher on completion. Each paired debit/credit is staged in the same plan. Reserved money remains identifiable in a pending job until completion or cancellation. Existing negative native-money effects alone are fees, not proof a teacher was paid. Miri can fund at most the available orders; depleted pellets/coins pause her next action. A kit loan uses the actual custody owner and return obligation, so lending does not also donate or consume it.

On a later visit, knownPeopleView and qualified local scene text read Miri's directed memory, the settled order claim, actual repaired lantern and route outcome. Her thanks may be ordinary texture; an actual promise or loan uses its saved owner. If the player abstains, she uses her own remaining pellet or takes the upper bridge. There is no automatic payment, lesson or unseen player discovery. Repair cancellation refunds only its declared reserved costs and teaches nothing. No timing/RNG outcome is rerolled by menu opening, reload or remembered dialogue.

**Supported seams:** `simulation.js` preview/commit/frozen jobs/controllers; `simulation_effects.js` item/transfer/resource/knowledge/relationship/condition; `capabilities.js` active source query; `skill_expansion_content.js` already functioning teaching/practice/kit/source recipes; `skill_discovery.js` observer-qualified projection; `gameplay_publication.js`, `main.persistExpansion`, `expansion_persistence.js` native durable settlement. **Missing:** original pellet/lantern content and item templates, Miri's registered means-aware action and observation producer, paired finite fee/wage recipe, insulation's actual native condition consumer and disclosure-aware entry/return panel. No generic new inventory, timer, knowledge engine or dependency is necessary for this finite case.

## Case B: the same expedition can require a different body or a different route

A later Floodline episode offers recovery of an association pump below a submerged sluice. The first case's reliable lantern supplies a real inspection advantage. Miri can now report what she actually observed, while her private route knowledge and player speculation remain distinct. The pump, repaired route and later delivery outcome each have an owner and a once-only claim. A pump catalog entry does not itself create a playable underwater area.

One proposed route is ordinary preparation: repair scaffolding, borrow a breathing apparatus under custody and consume owned spare air canisters while working. An alternative is a voluntarily acquired living reed-lung graft from a named, legitimately supplied specimen. These routes change different functions. An apparatus depends on actual gear and consumable air; a graft depends on installation, host compatibility, an active faculty, separately held reserve/strain and a learned control method. Learning the method, copying a power record or seeing another diver breathe supplies none of the organ, fuel or host support. A temporary graft-suppression condition can stop breathing while remembered procedure/mastery remains.

The graft route is a substantial later engineering packet. Its specimen needs stable physical identity, supplier/ownership/custody and consumption or installation receipts; the player's body must bind that particular installed source. Native equipment restrictions, unsupported host conditions, injury/recovery and source removal must agree before publication. Existing `body` effects can record a property, and `source`/`faculty`/`energy` effects can operate declared state, but those fields do not already conserve an installed organ or project underwater breathing into the native host. `realm_traveler_profiles.js` is explicitly detached; its `alternate_body` enum does not implement possession. This case selects neither possession, alternate bodies, death nor a new prestige.

The branch outcome is finite: recover the pump with the ordinary apparatus; recover it with the graft; negotiate a surface bypass with Miri; retreat safely; or decline and let funded residents take the longer delivery route. A broken pump can change the later route and the association's remembered decision. A source-baseline expectation that the ferry will resume is not imposed after actual conditions change. The same saved Miri returns with her actual surviving means; a copied scenario must not duplicate her stock, loan or rewards. No forced equal-strength replacement enemy follows successful early intervention.

The lightweight alternative is to implement the apparatus/bypass branch first with existing item counts, custody and typed commands, retaining the graft as an explicit unimplemented option in author-facing planning. Player-facing UI should show only discovered viable routes. Abstention closes the offer through the defined supported clock and publishes only the known aftermath. A later complete campaign may add a real organ route once the source/body consumer passes its compatibility gate. This preserves an unusual power proposal without disguising it as a damage bonus or promising arbitrary biological simulation.

**Supported seams:** current same-body native locations; frozen jobs/participants; actual stock/custody/vitals; separate procedure/source/faculty/energy; `compileSituation`, causal situations, world facts, claims and directed return memory. **Missing:** underwater work/air-consumption consumer, installed-specimen/body binding and native projection, finite branch/entry/ending definitions and a campaign aftermath UI. `world_instances.js` can validate scoped records; the current Glasshouse native binding is one compiled instance and does not automatically isolate a new campaign, clock or shop. The current present-life Frontier route can avoid another world binding.
