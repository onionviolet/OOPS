## Shared transaction and chronology rule

Each consequential action follows proposal **TX1**: inspect known evidence → quote exact inputs/revisions without mutation → recheck at commitment → reserve actual participants/site/stock → freeze permitted uncertainty once → settle declared ordered effects → persist one complete envelope → publish native/UI projections and supported observations. Existing planners supply parts, not all TX1 semantics.
The receipt binds actor/body/source/component or lot, target/endpoint, definition revision, expected sequence and relevant resource/site/relationship revisions. A unique operation ID is not a new reward manager. Duplicate commitment rejects or returns an unchanged prior receipt; it never recharges, rerolls, re-extracts or recreates output. A rejected stale quote spends/draws nothing.
Durable phases matter: failure before a new phase publishes none of that phase's debits, anatomy, RNG, knowledge, clock or output. It does not erase earlier successfully published extraction, injury, payment or use. An outcome after genuine execution can be a paid failure; this differs from validation rejection.
Clock domain is declared for each task. Local work uses authorized local elapsed, body upkeep uses its actual host clock, and external reports use their delivery clock. Preview/render advances none. At a shared boundary the future resolver freezes support changes, derives eligibility, resolves simultaneous actions under one declared priority, then publishes observations. Current jobs-before-condition ordering cannot silently substitute for this rule.

### Five independent operating channels

| Candidate O/P channel | Example / actual distinction | Required UI and counterexample |
|---|---|---|
| Continuous upkeep | Charge 1 reserve per supported active minute | Show rate and support state. Zero actions can still cost reserve; covering is not a proven off switch |
| Lower regeneration | Recovery policy 1 rather than 2 per eligible minute | Show recovery amount/context. Reserve can stay large while refilling slower; this is not continuous consumption |
| Smaller reserve | Ceiling 16 rather than 30 | Show maximum separately. Capacity loss has explicit clamp/overflow policy and no hidden refund/reacquisition |
| Startup/use cost | Activation 4 rather than 2; operation cost independently 4 | Show charges before commitment. Repeated startup differs from holding one activation for longer |
| Strain | 1 strain per declared use; recovery separately paid/timed | Full reserve may still leave control unavailable; rest need not change ancestry or specimen condition |

These are alternative hypotheses/fixtures, not five cumulative Naruto penalties. Source exceptions can remove specific constraints without receiving artificial replacement weaknesses. An actual always-active organ needs a supported exhaustion/care policy; it cannot be treated as freely toggleable because a menu would be convenient.
Worked comparison: initial reserve 18, cap 30, activation 2 plus use 4, four eligible recovery minutes at rate 2. Baseline ends at 20; added upkeep 1/min ends at 16; changing only recovery to 1/min also ends at 16, despite a different causal channel. A startup-only cost 4 ends at 18. Smaller-cap comparison ends at 16 by its explicit clamp. Equal final values do not establish equal mechanisms.

**Interruption/negative/exception:** before extraction, withdrawn consent or missing support stops before new removal; after extraction, E remains in the actual container and donor loss/care remains; after installation, attachment/recovery persists. Spoilage affects only the actual specimen. A source-qualified exceptional transfer gets its own carrier/duration/host rule and does not prove every organ follows that exception.
**Stale/duplicate/failure:** changed socket/E/body/source/consent/site/lot revision rejects before new spend. Repeated extraction receipt cannot debit a second eye. Failed persistence during installation publishes neither recipient faculty nor a second container E; already committed extraction remains. Refusal leaves attached anatomy unchanged.
**Revisit/UI:** show “Eye held in clinic” versus “Left eye installed”; separate source function, recorded method, usable execution, upkeep and recovery. Donor/Ito know witnessed phases; Mira knows only direct observation or an actually delivered report. Private origin, donor reserve and hidden stage cannot appear in a public tooltip. On a body change, retained learning does not install E into the new body; the physical eye and old host remain exactly where that transition put them.

## MC02: reedbank harvest, preservation and refinement

**Decision:** reserve a sustainable cut, accept a paid competing supplier's offer, refine the owned lot for the clinic/base, sell it, or preserve it for later research. Renewable reeds, unique organs and living acquired sources are different resource classes.

| Stage | Pre/commit | Post and clock/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Inspect | `reedbank.A` revision 7, available stock 12, legal cut rights known; supported hands/tool and route | Reveal observed growth/quality range, not a hidden exact future yield. No stock created, reserved or RNG advanced |
| Harvest | Reserve 5 node units, exact sound tool, four stamina and three local minutes; freeze one qualified yield outcome | Player output 4 raw units plus 1 residue/waste; source reservation cannot yield more than 5. A separate NPC reserves 2, leaving 5 available after both allocations |
| Preserve/store | Actual raw lot L, condition and holder; adequate container/capacity | Retain lot provenance/quality; packing delays a declared spoil condition and consumes real packing material. Poor storage changes condition, not structural grade or origin |
| Refine | Consume 4 owned raw units, 2 clean-water units, 1 heat/fuel unit, three local minutes and one bench | Produce 2 preservation wraps plus 2 processing-waste units. Trial spends stock even when its actual selected outcome fails; prior blueprint knowledge supplies no material/control |
| Use/sell | Actual wraps with allowed custody, clinic/base demand, buyer funds and eligibility | Allocate to MC01 support, MC05 roof/bench or a funded sale. Buyer pays once for real goods; no universal quality-price multiplier or automatic medicine effect |

Original bulk regrowth: after 40 authorized local minutes, a qualified living patch may add stock up to ceiling 12 under its declared growth inputs. A depleted unique eye or artifact has no regrowth row. Reopening a panel never runs regrowth. Absent frozen world time cannot advance preservation/spoilage/regrowth secretly; host-body upkeep may still follow the body's separate active clock.
Craft advancement changes function: first recognize sound reeds, then test dry preservation, then earn repeatable wrap production and larger clinic orders. Precise tool handling improves the selected cut/test, while a superior supported method may simply yield better results. A failed wet batch records its evidence without granting an untested preservation recipe. A trained competitor pays their own labor/stock and can consume the next opportunity while the player is elsewhere.
**Interruption:** prior to cutting, release only untouched reservations; after cut, keep the actual raw/residue output and paid labor. Mid-refinement, partially consumed stock remains consumed; refund untouched escrow only, never the whole original recipe plus output.
**Stale/duplicate/failure:** an NPC reservation changes node revision, invalidating the old player quote. Requote against current stock; do not reroll the committed player's outcome. Exact lot/condition/custody change rejects before conversion. Duplicate settlement adds no second raw lot/wrap. Failed publication changes neither node depletion nor native inventory, buyer funds or witnesses for that phase.
**Refusal/revisit/UI:** declining the cut preserves current goods and creates no debt; later node/competitor state follows actual authorized elapsed/history. UI shows observed stock estimate, access, material/lot selection, cut reservation, storage condition, recipe inputs and projected use. It does not display a “Harvest eye” button on a fainted patrol. Same-instance return retains depletion, finished wraps, competitor outcome and specific work recognition.
