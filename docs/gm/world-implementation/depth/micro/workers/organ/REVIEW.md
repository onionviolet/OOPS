## Source qualification

The inspected official material supports a real gift and useful acquired functions. Obito gives his left Sharingan to Kakashi after Kakashi's left eye is injured; the official manga column points to volume 27, chapters 242–244. The inspected German official episode summary says Obito asks Rin to transplant it. This supports donation and a procedure, without establishing preservation chemistry, universal surgical success or a repeatable emergency recipe. Obito's later survival and rehabilitation also mean “believed dead” cannot substitute for actual death. [Obito column](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1706), [official episode 340, German](https://naruto-official.com/de/anime/naruto2/list/01_564).

Kakashi's demonstrated copying remains a positive advantage: the official scene description identifies instant reading of Zabuza's signs and reproduction of Water Dragon Jutsu. The body still supplies execution. Lee's trained taijutsu defeats Sasuke and his Sharingan; this does not establish a universal copying ban. The acquired faculty, captured method information, learned control, executable technique and ancestry are separate outputs. Do not award Obito's entire arsenal, memories or clan identity with his eye. [Official copying description](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_2665), [Lee column, volume 5 chapter 37](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1652).

Kakashi's Mangekyo use has serious backlash, and the late-war gift enables exceptional Perfect Susano'o and Kamui use. Preserve both facts rather than inventing one universal weakness that would forbid the exception. These bounded sources do not quantify a non-Uchiha host penalty, explain every suppression mechanism or establish slower regeneration. **Non-Uchiha continuous drain, reduced regeneration, smaller maximum reserve, increased ordinary activation/use cost and inability to deactivate remain U/P here.** Original chapter-panel image requests failed; no unviewed panel text is treated as read. [Kakashi part 1, volume 31 chapters 275–276 / volume 32 chapter 282](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1903), [part 2, volume 71 chapters 688–689](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1904).

## Proposed atomic case and conservation

Pre-state: an original adult donor D has eye E attached in a particular left socket, known source revision and condition; adult recipient R has an eligible vacant socket, declared non-Uchiha lineage, compatible actual chakra support and sufficient reserve. Clinician C, clinic site, tools, preservation container and exact supply lots exist and are available. D's donation consent and R's procedure consent are specific accepted decisions. Physical contact and co-location are required. This is an original clinic case, not a claim that the canonical battlefield gift had these conditions.

Preview resolves E's identity, current body/holder, provenance chain, ownership claim, facility and participant versions. It reports eligibility and expected consequences without extracting, creating stock, revealing private origin or rolling surgery. Commitment reserves E, participants, facility and finite stock together, freezes supported duration/outcome and creates one operation receipt. Prototype duration could be ten played minutes; that value has no canonical authority.

Extraction settlement atomically removes E from D's socket and records E in the clinic container's custody. D loses only that eye's attached support; the other eye, ordinary sight where present, learned methods and remaining faculties are re-evaluated independently. D's ancestry remains D's ancestry. Injury/care follows the authored procedure. E cannot simultaneously remain attached and become bag loot. Theft uses its own authorized severe-content acquisition rule, actual removal, claimant dispute and witness evidence; changing consent text cannot turn theft into donation. Current native nonlethal victory supplies neither eligible death nor extracted anatomy.

Installation settlement atomically empties the container, attaches the same E to R's specified socket, binds its source-supported faculties and records recovery and host conditions. R gains the declared perception/copy function when installation and support permit it. R's lineage, learned techniques and chakra capacity do not change implicitly. Custodian, legal claimant, biological donor, current host and performer stay distinct. Removal reverses attachment-dependent support, not remembered methods; reinstallation references E rather than reacquiring a fresh eye.

## Burden, control and progression

Model five separate terms: upkeep consumes reserve over elapsed supported time; regeneration modifies recovery amount/rate; capacity modifies the reserve ceiling; activation/use costs charge actual actions; strain accumulates independently and needs its own recovery. Eye covering, faculty suppression, deactivation and organ removal are different states. A cover may obstruct sight without disabling the source or curing its burden; exact Naruto behavior remains U until qualified. Host training may improve particular control or efficiency only where its selected rule permits.

Proposed same-boundary order: settle component changes; derive effective capacity/support; reconcile reserve with the selected capacity-loss policy; apply and clamp recovery; settle due upkeep; check startup/use costs; apply strain and function; write evidence; publish once. Resolve simultaneous effects by declared priority and stable instance ID. Stack by actual instance with typed sum/max/exclusive rules, preventing two labels for E from doubling its benefit or burden. Insufficient upkeep reaches a defined stop/incapacitation consequence, never a negative reserve or thrown clock loop. Genuine exceptional support can change specified terms without imposing compensating handicaps.

Prototype comparison, not canon: with reserve 10, cap 20 and recovery 3 per minute, upkeep 2 leaves 11; regeneration reduced to 1 also leaves 11, but differs during non-recovering activity. Cap 12 affects full bursts; startup cost 4 leaves 6 immediately; strain can bar the next use while reserve remains high. These are alternative illustrative policies, not five cumulative mandatory penalties. Observed practice should unlock a real action, such as reading an eligible sequence while moving; copied evidence then names its demonstrator, observed segments, occurrence time and later interpretation. A clinic/storage base and harvested preservation inputs can support this loop without manufacturing another eye.

## Required failure and revisit behavior

Reject stale sequence, changed E/body/custody, withdrawn consent, missing support, occupied clinician/site or changed material lots before new spending. Duplicate operation or phase receipt returns the existing outcome, including the same RNG result, rather than removing a second eye or charging again. Recheck reserved physical prerequisites at each consequential phase; frozen outcome data alone cannot establish continued access.

Cancellation before extraction releases reservations under the declared refund policy. After extraction, interruption leaves the one E in its actual preserved custody, retains donor loss and spent supplies, and creates care/resumption requirements. It cannot refund anatomy with generic negative item costs. After installation, interruption preserves the actual attached state and recovery burden. Expiry/spoilage consumes or disables only the actual component under an explicit original rule. Save/storage failure publishes no partial donor change, recipient faculty, stock spend, witness memory, RNG progress or clock advancement for that transaction. A failure after an earlier durably published phase leaves that earlier phase intact.

Later return reads current anatomy, container/attachment state, support and authored local-clock history. Donor and clinician know witnessed procedure facts; a distant NPC needs observation or credible attributed disclosure. Reveal time does not rewrite extraction time. UI should say “Left eye installed; eligible copying available; recovery pending,” show each selected cost separately, and distinguish “Method recorded; required body/source unavailable” from “Nothing copied.” Refusal/abstention preserves the original bodies and supports ordinary study/care alternatives.
