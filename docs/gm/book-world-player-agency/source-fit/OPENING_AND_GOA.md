## Finite original player arc recommendation

Keep the source baseline ledger immutable and branch a separate player entry. Suggested authored role is a locally placed civilian/contractor with a disclosed body, ordinary means and optional hub-service agreement. Start the local clock in the same broad three-day window, but do not call the player's arrival a source event. Explain whether source-language comprehension comes from entry terms; never copy Su Xiao's three-day language notice silently.

Independent route: the player can earn food or coin by local work, trade, escort/carry duties, observation or relationship-building. Mia, unnamed residents, guard clerks and ordinary shopkeepers can have original authored names only if marked original and assigned source-bounded motives. The route produces useful local stock, trust, a practiced skill or a continuing-life contact even if the player refuses all political and protagonist-linked options. No source canon fact requires a follower, a kill, access to the palace, or plot foreknowledge.

Two distinct material interventions (all numbers/timing/results below are proposals, not source facts):

1. **Before warning, build capacity.** Buy/earn potable water, food, cloth or handcarts and spend local time recruiting willing carriers among residents. This changes the population's evacuation readiness and leaves a useful cache/route/relationship if no fire warning is learned. It does not guarantee mass rescue; source shows no sufficient public fire brigade, broad safe route or supplies. Let costs use the player's actually held stock and let refusal preserve ordinary work progression.
2. **After an earned warning, expose or divert the operation.** A player may learn a limited fire time from overheard guard/pirate talk, Hank's disclosed source channel, or direct negotiation only if physically present and credible. They can pay for/deliver proof to the affected people, bargain with a pirate crew, seek a guard gate opened, or abstain. Each option spends a separately named resource/time and changes a real actor's next choice. An early warning and a stocked route can save more residents; a failed/ignored warning still leaves consequences, fire damage and an altered later trust/account. Do not require Su Xiao to cooperate; his independent mission can proceed or be changed only by a plausible encounter and shared information.

Finite completion ledger: (1) choose explicit entry and review carried body/means/knowledge/clock; (2) establish one independent local life outcome; (3) accept or refuse source-adjacent involvement; (4) obtain or lack actionable fire information through a recorded channel; (5) resolve capacity-building intervention and fire/guard/pirate response; (6) settle the king/guard/fire local political branch as contingent, including refusal; (7) resolve player's stated exit/departure terms independently of Su Xiao's task-triggered 30-second return; (8) keep lasting rescue/death, gate, stock, trust, evidence and local work consequences; (9) allow a later return to a changed district and a continuing-life use for retained progress. A campaign may complete without the player assassinating anyone or meeting Luffy. Source evidence does not provide a player extraction guarantee, safe deadline extension, or exact return mechanism; author those separately and label the costs/protection scope.

The distinctive named-protagonist route is optional observation or contact with child Luffy and potentially the other brothers only after an actual location/knowledge lead. The source explicitly catches Su Xiao with Luffy on Mount Kolubo at a sensitive childhood stage. A player may cooperate, avoid contact, or cause divergence through a credible encounter; no route may award adult Luffy's later abilities or treat Su Xiao's personal recognition as shared.
