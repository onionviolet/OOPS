## D1 implemented behavior

Smelting level 10 unlocks the native **Springsteel ingot** recipe. Its level band is 10–20; it consumes one Iron ingot, one Steel ingot and one coal-type material and produces one springsteel ingot with guaranteed success. The explicit recipe ID registers its availability for native unlock publication and saves.

The existing Crafting → equipment → Amulet recipe accepts one springsteel ingot to make one **Springsteel brace** in the native amulet slot. The native equipment recipe reads the ingot's authored spring grade 1 profile at craft time to derive **+2 Agility and -1 Strength**, rather than a steel weapon upgrade. Native recipe quality may vary, but this grade's stats and technique trait do not scale with quality. Both the material-profile function and native craft result are tested with another grade to establish derivation; the gameplay grade remains fixed at 1.

Equipping the brace projects `equipment.springsteel.equipped` and numeric `.grade` into `simulationFacts` using live native equipment, never carried inventory. Only `field.pack_drive`, the existing forceful workshop application of `field.load_step`, reads the formula hook: **2 survey kits for 2 drive units**, instead of **1 kit for 3 drive units**. Logs, coin, duration, mastery and workshop access remain the existing requirements. Other applications, energy recovery, focus packing and technique families receive no modifier. The workshop button shows current kit output and drive cost. Unequip restores the baseline quote. An admitted job freezes the paid output/fuel; a later unequip does not change that job's yield or cancellation refund.
