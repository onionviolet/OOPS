## Player choice and result

The player may continue ordinary fitting work, invite Eda to accept the local assessor agreement, request one paid inspection, fulfill one finite approved order, or end the agreement. Nothing automatically enrolls the player or appoints Eda.

Appointment takes one played minute, with no material fee. It requires the player’s actual `feature_objects.observe_alignment` and `feature_objects.learn` completions, Eda’s retained demonstration knowledge, and her still present trained `feature_objects.grain_matching` source with an awakened active unsuppressed faculty and owned power. Eda must be willing, living and co-located, with usable hands and free attention at her existing Village fitting bench. The completed appointment sets an optional office fact on **Eda**, the existing `feature_objects.miri` actor. It grants no stat, source, power, replacement body or automatic work.

One paid inspection costs one actual native player coin, one Eda stamina and two played minutes. At admission it holds one player-owned unencumbered **Matched repair stave** and one of Eda’s own unencumbered **Fine field fitting knife** or **Dulled fine field fitting knife** guide variants. Both are removed from available stocks. It rejects an active guide loan/custody. Inspection returns the same counted stave class and unchanged guide variant at completion, credits Eda one coin, and issues one bounded one-use approval of the fungible Matched repair stave class. That approval claims neither copy identity nor provenance. A guide’s sharpness does not supply competence.

The approved order is finite and pays three existing Eda coins for one owned native Matched repair stave. The proposed handover takes one played minute. Admission holds the player’s stave and Eda’s three coins. Only successful completion transfers that one ordinary native item into Eda’s existing inventory, pays three native player coins and consumes the approval. The same approval cannot settle a second order.

An explicit ending removes future office access, while retaining Eda’s competence, the completed appointment, the inspection history and any completed delivery. Ending requires no active fitting source or available guide/material. Ending does not replenish the purse, refund settled work, erase an approval’s history or reopen the once-only appointment/order. An unused approval remains historical but cannot support delivery without the current office.
