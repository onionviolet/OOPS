# UA2: Craft 1 repeated activation and native receipt

2026-10-16. Scope: one Craft 1 double-click and repeated Enter behavior on the
current native Cooking route, using a disposable rebuilt source copy. This is a
bounded transaction check, not a UI or human-acceptance review.

## Owner reading

[UR35](../../UI_RULES.md) says inspection is free and native action controls
execute once through their existing guards. The current Craft 1 control is a
button whose click handler requests amount 1 in
[`crafting_detail.js`](../../../src/crafting_detail.js); the selected-recipe
callback in [`display.js`](../../../src/display.js) checks the native guard,
calls `window.useRecipe`, measures inventory/XP deltas and refreshes the detail.
The native commit and Work Ledger settlement live in
[`main.js`](../../../src/main.js). [MR4](../../MAKING_RULES.md) separately says
preview consumes nothing. These owners support one native transaction per click
activation delivered to Craft 1; they do not define physical double-clicks as a
new batch command.

## Current-route evidence

Built the current working source in `/tmp/ua2-current-scratch3` and served it at
`http://127.0.0.1:19319`. Relevant source hashes match the checkout. `display.js`
differs from R14 only in the readiness-row summary hunk; the callback and native
commit path are unchanged. The original R12 synthetic Cooking fixture was cloned
inside fresh browser contexts, with Rat meat chunks changed from 3 to 12 so a
second operation could spend normally. No personal save or browser state was
opened. The harness recorded browser events, native inventory and crafting
counters, XP, and the persisted Work Ledger sequence and receipts.

| Input sent to Craft 1 | Click activations received | Ingredients spent | Product made | Native attempts | XP | Work Ledger receipts |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Playwright double-click, 35 ms interval | 1 click; no second click or `dblclick` reached the control | 2 | 0 | 1 (0 success) | +2.3 | `Craft settled: no output from Roasted rat meat. 0/1 attempts succeeded.` |
| Two separate Enter presses | 2 clicks | 4 | 1 | 2 (1 success) | +11.5 | `Craft settled: no output from Roasted rat meat. 0/1 attempts succeeded.` then `Craft settled: Roasted rat meat ×1 from 1 attempt.` |
| Two consecutive Enter keydowns, second marked `repeat=true` | 2 clicks | 4 | 1 | 2 (1 success) | +11.5 | Same two receipts as separate presses |

The first attempted recipe failed and spent two chunks; the second attempt
succeeded and spent two more. In every row the Work Ledger sequence increase
equals the committed native attempt count, and the saved receipt count agrees
with those attempts. No callback count is used as transaction proof.
For all three cases, the Work Ledger in the saved `save data` envelope exactly
matched the runtime ledger after the commit.

For double-click, the first synchronous commit rerenders the detail through
`root.replaceChildren()`. The browser trace contained one pointerdown/mousedown/
mouseup/click on Craft 1, and no follow-up click or `dblclick` on that control.
So this browser route committed one operation for that gesture. This records the
observed current behavior; it does not establish a cross-browser debounce rule
or settle every physical double-click timing. Separate Enter activations, including
a second keydown marked `repeat=true`, each requested one Craft 1 operation.
