## Interpretation and next work

A filename, hash, JSON parse or list of item IDs adds no semantic reading credit.
Each completed unit must retain actual row meaning, original ID and locator,
qualified owner, consumer, evidence layer and unresolved join. A full inventory
read does not review its linked dossiers, source classes, images or private proof.

Many JSON files are evidence manifests, not another human audit. Their source,
candidate, build, served bytes, result, failure and acceptance claims need scoped
review; embedded opaque hashes are not new behavioral findings. Do not multiply
one issue by every repeated manifest occurrence. Full-source credit comes only
from explicit matching-byte semantic receipts, with historical revisions retained.

The registry pins metadata only for current registration; later changed files
keep registration drift separate from matching semantic revisions. Reuse existing
owners and exact-byte aliases, preserve stopped/negative evidence and useful
variants, and do not turn every inventory row into required implementation.

The [resume](RESUME.md), [next unit](private/NEXT_SEMANTIC_UNIT.json) and private
per-file complements keep the remaining inventory work explicit. Original lexical
candidate, supporting-document, branch and loose-artifact scopes remain open.
