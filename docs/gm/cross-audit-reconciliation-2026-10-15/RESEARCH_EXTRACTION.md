# Research-source extraction

Scope: the 24 paths in `private/BATCH_research.txt`. This is a structured extraction of their stated findings and evidence limits, not a fresh research audit. Each source hash, classification, exact inspected range, finding locator, named owner, and evidence-stage separation is in [`private/RESEARCH_EXTRACTION.json`](private/RESEARCH_EXTRACTION.json).

The batch mixes audits (16), prompts/dispatch contracts (6), one proposal/scope refinement, and one candidate index. Prompt instructions were not counted as findings. The outbound, sent, and reconciled game-research files are prompt versions for the same task. `GAME_RESEARCH_AUDIT_CHATGPT_RESPONSE` is the prompt-review response: it contains a complete replacement prompt and material-change notes, so it evidences a captured review response, but not execution of the research audit. The JoJo scope refinement authorizes and scopes later work but remains proposal/contract material. `AUDIT_CANDIDATES` is an index, not an audit. The Qud dispatch is an assignment, not completion of the requested wider audit.

## Evidence-stage accounting

Acquisition, capture/export, semantic reading, and native/runtime acceptance are separate. Several records inherit prior local exports or source archives; that supports source availability only. The listed SHA-256 values fingerprint the 24 report inputs, not their underlying books, game binaries, private corpora, web captures, or browser builds. Export counts and dataset presence do not prove reading. Source-level `SHOWN`/`READ` and synthetic `MEASURED` evidence retain their authors' stated bounds. Native/gameplay acceptance is absent or explicitly open across these records; historical browser evidence is not a current acceptance pass.

## Duplicate citations and coverage gaps

The prompt variants reuse the coverage reconciliation and reverse-engineering report as supplied context; they are not independent corroboration. `LEVEL_SCALES_AUDIT` cites both growth audits, so their shared fiction examples remain one source, not two confirmations. ER1, ER9, ER11, the offline-clock audit, and their shared ER palette overlap on clocks, transactions, rendering, and persistence; later or deeper measurements refine selected rows, but should not be added as independent evidence when they share the same code snapshot/probe. The JoJo refinement, source audit, and engine audit are successive stages: scope, bounded source facts, and YAIR source comparison. The two Qud re-audits/dispatch records are similarly distinct: finite source traces versus an unexecuted wider assignment.

Discovery labels stay distinct: “not searched” means no search was performed; “searched with limited coverage” names the search scope; “not found” means a search produced no match; “confirmed absent” means the declared bounded scope was searched exhaustively. “Unread”, “not inspected”, “payload blocked”, and “not exercised” remain separate. The candidate index's unreviewed rows, the long partial records' unread ranges, inaccessible payloads, and unrun native/runtime gates remain open. No claim is made that all findings in partial records were extracted.
