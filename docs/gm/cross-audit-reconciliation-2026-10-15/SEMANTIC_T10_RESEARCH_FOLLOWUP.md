# T10 research inventory follow-up

This packet reads the 12 assigned inventories at their pinned full-file ranges and retains 2,480 table rows individually, plus one scope record per inventory. Each row keeps its source label, count/path or local coverage wording, and named research route. Structural module, directory, namespace, type, table and declaration counts remain inventory metadata. Rows that report a selected mechanic or subsystem preserve the inventory’s stated depth, not a new source, runtime, or player-acceptance claim. No YAIR consumer or implementation is selected.

The RimWorld feature-pass and deep-pass inventories overlap but are separate dated revisions. Their counts and repeated definitions are not independent corroboration or additive systems; the earlier raw Def census and later 14,084-child/254-family census keep their own scope and caveats. Likewise, the Qud inventory is not a refresh of the distinct current depth-7 owner; its old dossier’s consumer limits stay separate. Parent/nested and duplicate/inherited/abstract rows are preserved rather than collapsed.

Specific ceilings retained include HOI4’s nested rule-family counts and native executable subsystems, Vintage Story’s acquired-directory census versus paid-build coverage, UQM’s translation units versus selected subsystem traces, Stardew’s schema declarations versus evaluator consumers, Qud’s namespace/Base XML indexes versus activation, NetHack’s published source versus installed optional content, and KoLmafia’s module/data census versus live server behavior. The full row map records each unresolved source-to-consumer join without treating inventory coverage labels as current proof.
