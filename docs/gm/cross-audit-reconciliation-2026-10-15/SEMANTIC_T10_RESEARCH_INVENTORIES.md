# T10 research inventory reconciliation

This packet records a semantic read of the 12 assigned research inventories, pinned at their manifest hashes and full-file ranges. It retains 1,406 table entries individually, plus one scope/caveat finding per inventory. The entries are source-specific catalog rows, not 1,406 unique mechanics or YAIR requirements. Their evidence remains at the inventory’s stated depth: structural indexes, counts, selected prior traces, and unread/undocumented labels do not establish current game behavior, runtime use, or player acceptance.

The rows preserve each source’s own qualification and route to its named `DEEPER_SYSTEMS.md` owner, with `CHECKPOINT.md` or `REPORT.md` linked where present. They identify candidate comparison surfaces only; no YAIR consumer or adoption is selected here. Any later design route remains subject to the existing one-realm/one-prestige milestone and the relevant live system owner. In particular, table/namespace/file counts remain source-specific; overlapping symbol families, event groups, packages, code modules, and data records are not added as if they measured the same thing.

The most consequential scope limits are preserved row by row: Zhutian’s event-group count includes handlers/formulas and is not a count of distinct systems; Siralim’s symbols and database presence do not prove active mechanics; Your Chronicle’s partial snapshot and `UNDOCUMENTED` marks do not prove absence; Melvor’s inventory is typed declarations while its current build remains unread in that lane; and HyperRogue’s module census does not imply that rendering/geometry modules belong in the next YAIR slice. These are inventory-level statements, not fresh underlying-source or runtime verification.

Detailed row mappings, source hashes, semantic ranges, and unresolved joins are in [the private JSON packet](private/SEMANTIC_T10_RESEARCH_INVENTORIES.json).
