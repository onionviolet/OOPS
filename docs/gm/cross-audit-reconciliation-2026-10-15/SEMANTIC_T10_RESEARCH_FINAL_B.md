# Research inventory continuation (T10, batch B)

This packet reviews bounded family and coverage maps from ten research inventories. Their named systems, prior/new research lanes, and explicit unread limits remain attached to their actual research owners. It makes no new game-mechanics, canon, runtime, or YAIRPG adoption claim. The [private receipt](private/SEMANTIC_T10_RESEARCH_FINAL_B.json) pins each source and separates semantic coverage rows from mechanical type/table/export indexes.

A common limit across this batch is that asset, schema, export, path, and row counts measure discoverability, not active behavior or completeness. Several inventories explicitly remain partial; their large enumerations are indexed-only in this pass. See each source’s `DEEPER_SYSTEMS.md`, cited prior lane, or checkpoint before using a specific mechanic.

The family maps preserve distinct source limits: Immortal Life application-service slices are selected methods; Discworld is a pinned source snapshot, not a live server; New Horizons is not a commercial Outer Wilds census; Hogwarts and Potionomics counts include nonactive/development/deprecated data; cultivation inventories are source-snapshot locators rather than full semantic coverage.
