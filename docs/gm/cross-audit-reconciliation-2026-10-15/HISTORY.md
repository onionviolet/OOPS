## Removal and combination boundary

Combine current routing in NEXT_STEPS, linking every original ID to its owner
and scoped evidence. Remove a stale premise from current planning only when
replacement evidence is established. Preserve its dated wording in its original
report. Old failure and later success are a lineage, not interchangeable proof.
Copied reports are aliases, not independent corroboration.

No file is moved, deleted, truncated or hidden by this archive. Physical moves
would break source locators and linked receipts without improving current routing.
Unread records, partial owners, branch variants and inaccessible private payloads
retain their evidence limits. They are not automatically queued; the [finite closure list](CLOSURE_2026-10-16.md) owns active questions. This reversible archive covers finished documentation activity,
not completed gameplay or the whole audit.
