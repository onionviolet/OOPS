# B16 dependency contracts

Read all 124 lines of the [proposed dependencies](../dispatch/body-lineage-2026-10-14/b16/DEPENDENCIES.md).
Each of 14 slice rows has a separate prerequisite, optional-reuse and unresolved
join record in the [private receipt](private/SEMANTIC_B16_DEPENDENCIES.json).
Graph, cross-dispatch and clinic/UI/timing/CV1 qualifications are separately
retained. No Mermaid render, linked full specification or native behavior was
verified by reading this file.

The important rule is already in the document: a solid edge means a contract
for its stated proposed slice. It does not choose that slice or require every
upstream engine. Authored NPCs/kin can precede general population generation;
a B6-format authored card can supply B11 without a generator; a public authored
investigation can precede genetics; CV1 can precede B6/B11/G30 and does not need
active scanner or multiple-system arbitration. The UI tools are separate clients.

Preserve actual candidates rather than silently shrinking them: B12 A1 combines
MI6, MG6 and MP3 and needs its saved day/body cause; a deterministic MI6-only
variant is a smaller B16 alternative. B11's proposed complete A3 includes RNG and
publisher reconciliation, even though some read/preparation operations are
deterministic. A1/A2 preparation is not a separately playable delivery.

Known data remains separate from generated truth. Kin edges, diagnosis, body
state, source custody, appointment/sample/body clocks and physiological
settlement need their actual owners. A dependency diagram, local timer or
source reference does not establish those consumers. Current narrow body,
mixture, P3 and durable-reveal seams remain the qualified T05/T06 evidence;
this document review does not rerun them or select a new architecture.

Only this audit packet changed. No source/shared-owner changes, tests, build,
browser, saves or gameplay were used.
