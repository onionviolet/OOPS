# F slice proposal: a household beyond your travels

Source-informed original model, not accepted game canon. RimWorld supports interval aging and crossed birthdays; Elin derives age from birth year. Neither establishes our family generation rules. See SOURCE_NOTES.

The first living-world slice is one optional original Village household, separate from native combat/trader NPCs. The player meets neighbors through the existing side log. While traveling, durable saves catch their lives up by native calendar year. Age derives from birth year. A small deterministic schedule yields a child's birth, a marriage, old-age deaths and public local rumors. No per-minute NPC work, hidden money, new quest rewards or changes to the elder/craftsman.

Retain at most 32 household people and 16 public news items. The initial slice follows one finite family, not an indefinitely regenerated population. Family expansion beyond this bound, native social/combat integration, disease/war, heirs and alternate-world populations remain outside its claim. Events use saved schedule facts, not reload-dependent randomness. Missing npc_lives is dormant, preserving old saves and vanilla defaults.

Acceptance: meet household, travel/advance generated calendar across years, see aged neighbors, births/family ties, deaths and rumors; save/reload/export/import unchanged. Tests compare one large catch-up with repeated yearly catch-up and prove no replay. Use a fresh rebuilt snapshot/origin and generated saves only.
