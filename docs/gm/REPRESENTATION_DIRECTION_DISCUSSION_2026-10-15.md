### P12–P16: proposed responses to the design questions

P12, ACCOUNT FOR NOW: separate depth of a pursuit from frequency of manual
input. Expose consequential decisions; let established routines progress under
chosen instructions. Offer closer involvement without making every harvest,
trade or craft a compulsory minigame. Summaries should explain outcomes and
permit inspecting the underlying causes. Zooming or inspecting is distinct from
issuing an action that consumes played time.

P13, ACCOUNT FOR NOW: abstract repetition before meaningful distinctions. A
named experimental plant can remain individual; ordinary plants may use plots
or batches when their differences do not affect a decision. Preserve provenance,
commitments and exceptional histories across aggregation. Changing display
detail must not reroll outcomes, duplicate stock or invent unseen knowledge.
Do not assume detailed continuous simulation of every entity is necessary.

P14, ACCOUNT FOR NOW: match representation to the current question. Plot map
for consequential placement; calendar for seasonal planning; experiment notebook
for breeding; order/material list for production; portfolio and cash-flow views
for holdings; case/relationship views for research and negotiation. Use charts,
maps or networks when their relationships matter; ordinary rows/prose suffice
otherwise. Compact and expanded views refer to the same underlying activity and
reuse existing surface/navigation contracts. A full path need not mean many
simultaneously visible panels.

P15, ACCOUNT FOR NOW: modern/superhero investing can include deposits, bonds,
funds, equities, businesses, property, technology ventures and reconstruction

settlement and intervention. An investor, founder, patron or infrastructure owner
can be a primary life path. Show cash, assets, obligations and uncertain estimates
separately. Powers affect actual relevant means: perception, foresight, production,
transport or repair can change decisions and outcomes under world rules. Do not
invent arbitrary suppression or always-profitable uses of every power. This is
fictional game design, not real-world investment guidance or verified economics.

### P17–P21: proposals, not implementation or adopted universal rules

P17, ACCOUNT FOR NOW: separate involvement preference from execution means.
Hands-on, prescribed batch, supervised routine and delegated management are
possible ways to engage with the same pursuit, not compulsory progression tiers.
A player can personally tend a rare plant while delegating staple crops.
Availability depends on actual means, rather than requiring people where a tool
or power suffices. Changing involvement need not erase acquired mastery.

P18, ACCOUNT FOR NOW: skill can alter perception, precision, diagnosis, technique,
efficiency and reproducibility; powers can alter production inputs, transformations,
environment, reach or labor. Automation executes an explicit method/policy with
actual means. An NPC apprentice, machine, construct, clone or plant-control power
can therefore support different practical outcomes. Avoid universal nuisance costs,
mandatory failure and arbitrary limits imposed only to preserve chores. Actual
costs, agency and control terms follow selected world/means. Reports expose stock,
capacity, assignments and exceptional decisions without constant interruptions.

P19, ACCOUNT FOR NOW: procedural production should compose causal properties,
not only names/rarities. Original example: material properties + preparation +
ordered transformation + tool/workplace + actor technique yield an item with
effects, delivery, potency/duration, stability and provenance as relevant.
Not every craft needs every field. A cold extraction and a boiling process can
preserve/destroy different constituents if that world supports it. Separate a
formula, actual batch, observed result and the character's confidence. Successful
compositions need real uses, substitutes/constraints and learnable regularities;
do not enumerate the entire combination space. Shared rules should preserve
domain differences between farming, metallurgy, medicines and enchantments.
Stable discoveries should survive revisiting a screen; exact deterministic versus
variable outcomes remain a design decision, not an automatic GR adoption.

P20, ACCOUNT FOR NOW: represent local spatial causes with small plots/rooms or
equipment layouts, and operational decisions with lists, queues and policies.
Only use little fields/grids where placement, adjacency, irrigation, light, access
or similar actual relationships matter. Otherwise use a compact plot card.
An alchemy workbench can show chosen ingredients and ordered process beside known
predictions and unknowns; a notebook records experiments; an automation view
shows instructions, reserves, assigned means and conditions for intervention.
Expose only applicable/discovered controls; support comparison and text/keyboard
routes. Inspection does not require running a physical experiment or spending time.
Fit the Proto23 baseline and keep the log/current work visible.

P21, LATER: first validate one connected depth chain, such as growing an ingredient,
discovering a preparation, making a useful product, applying/selling it, then
repeating via a chosen routine. Show that a skill/power changes a real decision
and a different involvement setting preserves a viable path. Add modular breadth
when it creates distinct uses, not just more sliders or generated adjectives.
This is a candidate comparison, not an authorized implementation slice.

### P22–P24: further proposals

P22: retain a material's properties/provenance across domains while letting the
actual use select the relevant properties. One original fictional herb might
serve in food, medicine, bait, dye or cultivation. Uses need actual consumers,
and preparation can change suitability. Split real stocks between uses; a known
use need not reveal all others. Preserve specific-identity/ritual recipes where
world laws require them; property substitution is not universal equivalence.

P23: powers and skills can compose through a process, such as insight revealing
a constituent, herbalism separating it and heat control enabling a preparation.
Avoid requiring bespoke pairwise combo entries for every possible combination.
Keep exceptional authored interactions when worthwhile. New recipes can emerge
through experiments, substitutions, observed outcomes, research, teachers and
documents; fixed formulas remain useful reproducible methods. Unexpected results
should have stable causes or genuine world-supported variability. Let the player
save a method with its ingredients, conditions, known effect and confidence.

P24: ingredient detail should answer known properties, known uses, substitutes,
reserved quantity and provenance. Experiments compare changed inputs/processes
and observed results. A small field or diagram serves spatial decisions, while
lists/details and notebooks serve selection and inference. Do not reveal unknown
effects in previews, searches, automation reports or graphical layouts.
