## Recommendation and source horizon

Develop the original persistent connector as the working proposal. It explains travel through discovered passages, negotiated service and independent residents without making one institution the source or owner of every ability. A method can come from its local teacher, a bodily faculty from a real lineage, a device from a workshop, and a contract from a particular issuer. The connector provides transport, introductions and useful work. It does not need universal power conversion or compulsory missions. Paradise can later be one source-bounded destination/network within that larger arrangement.

This is a proposal for the implementation master to assess, not a selection of cosmology. The familiar opening and the current one-realm/one-prestige delivery remain intact. [Multiverse structures](../worlds/MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES.md) already separates cosmology, represented scope and persistence; [lived hub](../worlds/paradise-hub/WORLD_BRIEF.md) owns residence design. A second place earns its own native binding and return proof before being called playable.

### WC01: Wet stock, a usable lesson and a useful return

**Cause:** Venn sells an explicitly partial lead to Reedbank's quay. The optional survey terms fund one tide measurement and return of an owner-approved sample, not “repair the whole district.” Essa, a local cooper, has four sound blanks and two wet blanks; Ani owns the quay scale. Essa is finishing an independently funded crate order before closing the bench. Their stock and work are frozen once, not refreshed by each visit. The player can hear the lead and decline without debt; Essa still finishes her paid order. A later report requires an actual meeting or delivered message.

**Plans:** ordinary work dries an owned wet blank for four played minutes and pays one coin for bench time; buying a sound blank pays three coins and frees time for the measurement. A consenting Essa can teach cross-grain pressure after her order, with the existing kind of paid lesson reservation. Actual practiced bracing and carried grip/body support can shorten the later assembly; a remembered description cannot. A genuinely stronger supported shaping faculty can finish the same piece without a special balancing tax. It still does not manufacture permission to use Ani's scale or a missing blank unless its actual function creates material. Choosing waiting, buying, instruction or force changes time/stock/access, not only a success label.

**Return:** one purchased blank and one approved sample move with their exact owners/holders; the loaned scale stays Ani's. No “sample received” menu creates another object. The returned lesson and observed coating failure support a paid Frontier seal experiment and a useful recipe/application, rather than an unexplained global multiplier. On reentry to the same Reedbank history, Essa remembers the purchase/help only if she saw it; spent blanks remain gone and her completed order remains completed. A superior imported method stays strong and may remove the drying step if that is genuinely within its function.

**Detail classification:** wetness and bench closing are observed clues until tested/disclosed, then real preconditions; a chipped mug is harmless flavor. Show “the blank bends under the clamp” before a diagnosed moisture label, while stating price/duration/cancellation. A bench attendant can notice demonstrated work, not the traveler's private source record.

**Runtime fit:** reuse taught-control/job/custody/cause services. Destination stock, actual moisture function, local bench schedule, native location/inventory projection and frozen-absence timing are gaps. First current-realm variant can test this cause/return lesson without advertising interworld travel.

### WC02: Successful delivery, a disputed right and unequal power

**Cause:** Iona offers four coins for one consensually supplied copy of a pump calibration record. Ani owns the original and will lend it for supervised copying; Kes offers a funded retrieval shortcut to secure his own repair commission. Private truth: a revised measurement shows Ani's district loses pressure if Kes uses the older setting. Kes has heard only the old setting. His competing plan can change only after credible evidence reaches him, not because the player learns it. Taking or refusing Iona's offer creates different contract state, not automatic alignment/karma.

**Plans:** pay Ani two coins and one played minute for a witnessed copy; earn access by repairing the scale with real stock/skill; or use a possessed, supported contact-reading faculty on the offered page. A superior remote-reading faculty can obtain the measurement sooner and keep its output. Reading does not acquire paper ownership, reproduction permission, office or every hidden source of the person holding it. If an explicitly authored conceptual faculty actually transfers a license/claim, target and issuer reach must be supported; this first case does not pretend ordinary reading does that. A powerful traveler can also force entry if a later aggression adapter supports it. Its physical result, observed witnesses and lawful custody consequences require actual causes, without retroactive power normalization.

**Return and reaction:** approved copy delivery settles Iona's stated objective once even if the player declines local repair; receipt success is not “everyone rescued.” Sharing the revised measurement may persuade Kes to refit if he has supplies/time and trusts that source. An unobserved unauthorized copy may remain unknown to Ani until a witness/report connects it. Iona knows receipt/terms and received evidence, not the private bypass. A later refusal by Ani needs her actual belief and local rights; another district's clerk cannot inherit the grievance automatically. Returning preserves exact donor object, permitted copy, spent fuel, local repair and claim settlement. A different issuer can demand another legal credential, without erasing the traveler's faculty.

**Detail classification:** Ani's stamped copy permit is a real permission; the stamp's faded edge is a clue to inspect, not universal forgery detection. Kes's hurry matters because his paid booking ends, not because every NPC needs a synthetic mood bar. A joking complaint about “another form for the forms” cannot hide price or license terms.

**Runtime fit:** source/power ownership, directed memories, claims and staged transfer can represent separate pieces. `knowledge.basis` alone cannot establish witness presence or reliable measurement. Add authored observation/permission guards and separate `prediction`, `observedRevision`, `reportedRevision`, `correctedBelief` keys where history matters. Native conceptual license theft/general aggression and cross-world legal propagation remain gaps.

### WC03: Interrupted departure, frozen absence and an earlier counterpart

**Return terms:** proposed initial policy freezes absent Reedbank local work/deadlines while the traveler experiences active home time. It is disclosed before entry and needs actual scheduler suspension/mapping; it is not current universal absence behavior. Browser-closed time remains paused. Physical passage retains the same body, wounds, memory, supported sources and remaining fuel. A local office permission stays local; a borrowed gauge requires lawful return or an explicit permitted export loan. No body replacement, rescue-success flag, free refill, forfeiture or aging occurs merely because extraction was interrupted.

**Failure and reentry:** stage exit/cargo/loan return/cost/visit status together. On storage failure, exact old save bytes and live inventory/location/claims stay unchanged; retries use the same guarded state, not another payout. Reload in “exit pending” must restore the known pending decision if that feature is built, without duplicated sample or offline damage. Completing exit ends this visit, not Reedbank's history. A second visit reads i1's damaged/repaired quay, absent teacher time and settled claim. It cannot create a fresh first sample. **Earlier alternative:** a later explicitly selected Reedbank.i0/earlier-era profile is a separate history, not rewind of i1. ## Reconcile deep-reading chunks before integration

One compact claim record can link lore, plot, timeline, people, power rules and open mysteries without copying source prose.

|---|---|---|
| 1. Pin coverage and provenance | Work/author/edition/translation status; container and chapter/member hash; ordered manifest; exact start/end locators and overlap; reader ID/model and prior-context card. Track `unread`, `assigned`, `read`, `reviewed`, `integrated` per range. | `read` requires actual range coverage; `reviewed` requires named reviewer and checks; `integrated` points to accepted claim/content IDs. Assigned ranges and catalogue labels never imply reading/completeness. Changed hashes fork a new revision; gaps remain listed. |
| 2. Reconcile entities and sequence | Stable entity ID, aliases/pseudonyms, titles/cover names, counterpart/era, organizations and object owners; separate narrative order, flashback/retelling order and actual world date/relative bounds. | An alias link has its evidence and range; similar names cannot silently merge people. | 3. Reconcile truth and knowledge | Label explicit source observation, speaker belief/report, inference and O adaptation. Proposed claim keys: `claimId`, `sourceSnapshot`, `chunkId`, `locator`, `entityIds`, `narrativeOrder`, `worldTimeBounds`, `eventId`, `prerequisites`, `evidenceKind`, `speaker`, `observers`, `knowledgeArrival`, `sourceStatus`, `revises`, `contradicts`, `openSeam`, `adaptationUse`, `reviewer`, `coverageState`.
