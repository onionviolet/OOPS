# Fate and temporal direction revision

Direct user steering in this GM chat, 2026-10-06: “Making it really damn hard to defeat protaginists or having scripted saves for them since they should be fated to survive (unless that fate is severed or something?) it can be implemented differently in each world accordingly” and the question whether eventual high power makes timeline interference futile through causal relationships.

Accepted **design direction**: evaluate and author strong setting-specific protagonist protection, including actual scripted survival and meaningful severance. Investigate future/past causality and exceptional power. Exact source-specific protections, irreversible costs, cosmology, runtime schemas and balance remain evidence-qualified proposals; this steering does not establish a universal rule that every protagonist survives or every future strong actor is already invulnerable. This role's gameplay/shared-write restrictions persist.

Superseded wording from CAUSAL_MODEL revision1:

> There is no “protagonist” damage rule. Preview uses the same body's actual reach, acquired local capabilities, defenses and risks as any admitted actor.

Current replacement: there is no mandatory cross-world protagonist flag, but a world may deliberately assign a destiny/survival law, patron intervention, restoration or future anchor. Resolution must apply that real protection and its exception **before** declaring final death. It can be stronger than ordinary damage and legitimately make ordinary killing impossible while intact. Body/source/contact mechanics still apply, and a protected actor may lose a fight, objective, possession or relationship unless those are separately protected.

Retain the earlier valid commitments: unsupported actions are not silently successful; actual death and interrupted events have real downstream consumers; no automatic inherited powers/identity; beliefs differ from truth; a saved source timeline is not by itself a metaphysical protection. Successful final death means all applicable selected protections were actually overcome, not that the engine ignores fate. Preserved prior matrix/source/audit receipts remain historical evidence, with new authoritative analytical meaning in CAUSAL_MODEL's fate/temporal sections.

No prior source dossier, PROJECT, runtime, private save, publisher or book-library record is edited here. Canonical integrator receives current direction through this role's durable file queue.
