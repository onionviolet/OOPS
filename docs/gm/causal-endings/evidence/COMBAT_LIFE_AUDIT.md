## Joins the integrator must demand

1. A named hostile command must produce a legal actual target/contact/intent/profile rather than expanding `people_ui` alone. Reuse simulation preview and one native result owner; keep agreed nonlethal contests and elder posture rules distinct.
2. A lethal/capture result needs an authored physical outcome receipt plus life/custody/body policy. The current life effect guard is useful groundwork; it does not resolve an attack, allocate witnesses or remove all downstream support.
3. Apply life/contact/support invalidation where consumers actually demand it: target commands, current session, explicitly support-dependent jobs, native availability/dialogue/quest/reward and future actor plans. Do not silently reverse completed history or ordinary frozen work.
4. Preserve separate item/power/claim/title/identity owners. A death result cannot automatically create a stock/power/role-transfer reward; each transfer needs its source-specific lawful ownership and method.
5. Keep state/version/native availability/save restoration joins with the existing `persistExpansion` owner. New named lethal/capture/revival behavior needs later durable-failure, save/reload, stale command, native visibility and playable director acceptance.
