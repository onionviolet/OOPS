# Action and outcome matrix

Status: analysis/proposed authoring, 2026-10-06. “Present” below means an inspected source consumer exists, not that current gameplay passed acceptance. No row grants arbitrary targeting. Exact source references are in [capabilities](CURRENT_CAPABILITIES.md); case-specific qualifications are in [worked cases](WORKED_CASES.md).

Revision2: strong world-specific fate/scripted survival is now a requested design option. Each row resolves after its actually applicable protection/temporal rules. Being protected from terminal death does not necessarily prevent yield, capture, robbery, objective failure or changed ascension. Survival and finality follow [CSE-16–20](CAUSAL_MODEL.md); no universal protagonist immunity or automatic fate severance is assumed.

## Attack result matrix

| Result | Record | Actual actor/dependency consequence | Known account |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unsupported reach or rejected/stale preview | No committed attack, cost, RNG or hit | Nothing physical changes. Reopen legal contact if possible | UI explains unavailable scope; actor has no automatic awareness |
| Paid failed attempt / missed hit | Attempt receipt and cost, no successful-hit fact | Target may investigate, defend or continue; hostility requires detection | Witnesses see attempt; hidden attacker stays unknown unless evidence identifies them |
| Actual source-supported defense/counter/escape | Mechanism/use/cost, attacker effect, changed target location if escape | Plans change only where support/position/time changed | Current elder nonlethal restraint is supported; imported protagonists need their exact selected rules |
| Player defeated | Disclosed faint/incapacitated/recovery result, pending obligations affected if time/support changes | NPC keeps its own gear/plans; no native total_deaths → actual local death inference | Player knows encounter; distant actors need a report |
| Opponent yields/nonlethally defeated | Yield/incapacitation receipt separate from death | Treatment, departure, renewed conflict or capture are separately represented possibilities | “Defeated” cannot be narrated as “killed” |
| Captured alive | Person-custody/availability commitment, captor/guards/space/cost | Contact/escape/rescue and suspended plans depend on real means | “Missing,” “captured” and “dead” remain different beliefs |
| Eligible real death | Explicit lethal life/form receipt with source policy | Personal future ends; selected dependant work/expectations cancel; informed survivors choose independent responses | Confirmed only by supported observation; rumor can be wrong |
| Apparent death / soul survives / copy dies / reset | Separate body/soul/controller/iteration receipts and exact return mechanism | Only the actually lost owner/form is removed. No automatic all-body kill or global reset | Actor reports may mistake a copy or temporary body for permanent death |
| Storage failure / stale result at commit | Staged plan rejected or durability fails before publication | No authoritative injury, death, loot, grievance, cancellation, ending or Ink advancement from this action | Existing pause/reload protection; later acceptance must prove recovery |

A nonlethal defeat is not automatically a capture; a capture is not automatically a death. A body is not a soul or controller, and a soul is not an institution's authority. A genuine final death can end the actor's arc with no replacement enemy. Preserve the actual costs and limits of any source survival exception.

## Added fate/temporal result distinctions

| Intervention/result | Actual change | Required downstream consumer |
|---|---|---|
| Fatal candidate intercepted by fate or scripted patron save | Actual block/redirect/rescue plus affected cost/location/support; no terminal death committed | Actor reaction to what was detected, patron/mandate status, interrupted player/NPC plans and known survival account |
| Suppress, exhaust, release, steal or sever a fate relation | Change only the declared relation/capacity/beneficiary/support, with a durable causal receipt | Update protected outcomes, forecasting/recognition/bonds only where applicable; lawful ordinary routes and learned methods remain |
| Attack an earlier self protected by a real future anchor | Physical effects and exact anchor intervention; earlier attack may become an ascension cause | Actual identity/history reach, supported alternate goal/ideology, anchor/source aftermath. A projected future level is insufficient |
| Disrupt a future milestone or isolate the time/branch connection | An admitted anchor cause/reach changes, or action is resisted under fixed/anchored rule | Invalidate affected future expectation; do not invent loss of unrelated branches or universal defeat of stronger temporal authority |
| Win a local objective against an unkillable actor | Real route/item/jurisdiction/mission victory despite survivor remaining | Objective settlement and independent later opposition; actor immortality does not cancel actual player achievement |
| Contest two incompatible destinies | Selected authority/ritual/branch/boundary policy resolves once | Exact support/cost/history/known-result receipts; no endless rerolls or universal power-tier ranking |
