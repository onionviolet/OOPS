## Recommended model

Use **authored causal situations backed by the existing simulation and one durable publisher**. Source baseline stays immutable as an author reference. Committed actions determine actual history; each actor learns through its own witnesses/reports; future events require their real participants, support and resources. A successful rescue can avert a later death, a resource diversion can stop an operation, and killing an indispensable actor can end their arc without creating an equivalent boss.

**Future strength versus future authority:** a likely future ascension does not automatically protect the present. A selected fixed history, destiny attractor or actual future anchor can. Then an attack on the younger actor may fail, become a cause of ascent, or require severing/isolating/contesting the connection. A branch can produce a separate counterpart without erasing the original future. At high power, killing a past body may become irrelevant to that identity while local people, obligations and objectives still matter. Temporal reach, protected identity, jurisdiction and causal independence are separate capabilities, not one universal power level.

Reusable mechanisms can handle life, ownership, support cancellation, knowledge, access and already-authored closure conditions. Source-specific defense, fate/severance, resurrection, loops, fortune, institutional succession, temporal authority and alternate continuations still need explicit authoring. A legitimate absolute/unlimited protection need not get an invented budget for balance; gameplay can target what it does not protect or earn a specific exception. Arbitrary unrepresented consequences are honest scope limits before commitment. Optional generated narrative may describe or propose legal actions; it cannot own state, costs, RNG, rewards or death.

Keep **protagonist ending, player ending, local society outcome and world/issuer outcome separate**. A protagonist can die while a copied warning reaches its destination; an issuer can be satisfied while residents suffer; the player can refuse the central plot and retain a useful life. Endings close particular arcs through retained evidence and once-only settlement. They need not reset the world or stop all play.
