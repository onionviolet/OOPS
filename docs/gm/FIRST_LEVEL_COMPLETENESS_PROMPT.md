# First-level completeness: early-game foundations and variety

2026-10-11. Draft prompt requested by Weibao. His clarification combines early
game, foundations, multiple timelines/starting points, cheat unlocks and possible
random cheat rolls. The exact launch roster, roll policies and gameplay criteria
below are proposals. This document does not launch implementation, accept those
policies or change PROJECT's accepted decisions.

## Proposed meaning

**First-level completeness means a coherent early-game edition with working
foundations and several genuinely different beginnings.** Each included beginning
supports a connected early arc, useful ordinary progression, discovery and a
reason to continue. Selected cheats have real acquisition and execution routes.
Selected timelines have coherent histories and durable identities. Randomness,
idle work and saves behave consistently. Later depth remains explicitly mapped.

It combines breadth of beginnings with depth of early play. It does not require
every planned world, a finished source universe or a mature campaign before
delivery. Use the existing C0–C5 levels for each declared scope; this target is
not the standard's C1, which only means specified.

### A2 Deliver beginnings, timelines and cheat acquisition

Qualify every selected start, including body, means, resources, access, actor
knowledge, relationships, obligations and first usable actions. Expanded entry
is suspenseful and random under its selected policy. Frontier is optional;
preserve native entry and old saves without forcing a Frontier tutorial.
Keep the candidate catalog and hidden identities sealed from player summaries.

Separate authored earlier/later timeline snapshots, divergent instances,
offscreen progression and actual rewind. Select the promises this edition makes
and implement only those, with explicit history/state ownership. Do not claim
full regression from an era tag. Player-caused divergence changes real relevant
events or responses. Reload retains the committed timeline; entry history must
not fabricate player achievements. Test isolation wherever independent histories
are promised. Travel/return is required only for included routes.

For cheats, preserve distinct mechanisms and source-qualified functions. Develop
the full chain: discovery or roll, acquisition, knowledge, executable ability,
actual target/effect, development and applicable retention. Include earned
unlocking and random acquisition where selected; neither should become a name,
tooltip or bonus detached from its promised function. Ordinary progression must
remain useful alongside the selected exceptional means. Do not impose universal
slots, rarity caps, drawbacks or weak starter powers without a chosen local rule.

Propose a concrete RNG contract: what is rolled, when, eligible pool, weights,
dependencies between rolls, duplicate handling, no-result outcomes, reroll rights,
and whether earned unlocks change future eligibility. Starting cheat rolls,
later earned rolls and discovering a cheat are different acquisition routes.
Decide whether rerolls, guarantees/pity or cheat-free starts belong here; do not
silently add them. Keep hidden outcomes sealed while explaining observable roll
costs and consequences. Use player-visible odds where compatible with the
selected discovery policy and exact agent-only odds for verification.

Persist committed starts and roll results with their applicable pool/rule
revision before publishing rewards. Load/import/failed publication cannot
accidentally reroll, duplicate rewards or consume another attempt. Deliberate
reroll or regression mechanics follow their authored rules. Verify both roll
distribution logic and real gameplay acquisition, not only a seeded unit test.

### A3 Complete early play and the foundations it consumes

For every included start, deliver a connected journey: establish the situation,
find usable work or another fitting pursuit, learn/develop a means, apply it to
a meaningful problem, observe a lasting consequence, and reach a clear early-game
checkpoint with available continuing play. Different starts may have different
goals, difficulty and outcomes. Avoid identical loops with renamed nouns.

Recommend a few overlapping pursuits across the edition, such as craft/study,
exploration/danger and relationships/work. Each selected path needs functional
progression and a concrete payoff; it need not be available in every start.
Connect ordinary skills, items, housing/fixtures, NPC responses and situations
where selected. Idle execution needs real inputs, stopping/cancellation rules
and truthful feedback. Do not manufacture depth through long timers alone.

Inspect and repair required consumers for inventory/equipment, skills/XP,
titles/milestones, effects, clocks, jobs, knowledge, native actions and saving.
Keep existing owners and reuse working mechanisms. Build the minimum shared
services needed by selected journeys; defer unrelated engine replacement,
arbitrary universe simulation and exhaustive audit-backlog closure.

Select explicit offline, interruption, defeat/recovery and retention policies.
Do not introduce closed-browser death or new loss rules by implication. Reconcile
the existing one-prestige goal with early-game scope: state whether this edition
delivers a playable cycle or defers it by an accepted milestone revision. Merely
adding a prestige interface or metadata does not satisfy a playable cycle.

Unrun checks stay implemented-unverified in the acceptance backlog.

## Proposed world-by-world delivery strategy

Follow-up, 2026-10-11: Weibao asked about one deep world, multiple worlds at
once, a GM per priority world and further considerations. The following is a
recommendation, not a selected roster or new dispatch authorization.

**Design priority worlds concurrently; deliver a deep early-game slice in one
world, then prove reuse in a contrasting second world.** World-local implementation
can overlap once interfaces and file ownership settle. Shared state, native
integration, migrations and combined builds retain one integrator. A parallel
planning return does not make either world playable.

| Lane | Concrete responsibility | Finish gate |
|---|---|---|
| First world GM | Full-setting blueprint plus a finite early-game edition: coherent starts, timeline snapshots, local life, ordinary progression, selected cheats and consequences | Every selected early arc and promised variant is reachable and browser/save verified |
| Second priority world GM | Source-qualified whole-world detail and contrasting early-game contract; implement independent local content when settled | Show which shared services fit and which actual local rule needs an adapter; verify its own selected journeys |
| Shared integrator | Entry/roll admission, timeline identity, native consumers, saves, cross-world contracts and combined publication | Combined behavior and compatibility evidence for the admitted roster |

Start with two priority world owners as a proposed working batch. Add another
when it has a distinct useful assignment and capacity, rather than allocating
one writer to every drafted world. Reuse existing GMs before creating duplicates.
The existing World expansion GM owns private book world 1 design; promotion to
runtime ownership needs a concrete revised assignment and exclusive paths.
Existing implementation and absorption work retain their own scopes.

Depth in the first world means connected early, middle and later possibilities
in its blueprint, with the selected early edition implemented through meaningful
consequences. Do not require its entire mature campaign before starting a second
world unless that is the chosen scope. Conversely, one town interaction cannot
stand in for the requested substantial early world.

For priority selection, weigh player interest first, source/implementation
readiness second, and a useful contrast in world rules third. The most heavily
researched world is not automatically the one Weibao wants to play. Keep sealed
world identities out of public rankings.

Resolve these edition questions through actual local contracts:

| Question | Why it changes delivery |
|---|---|
| How do starts differ? | Different bodies, circumstances and eras need different usable actions and knowledge, not just changed coordinates |
| What does a timeline promise? | An authored snapshot, independent divergent history and true rewind require different persistence boundaries |
| Which cheats belong to which worlds? | Local sources/physiology/authority and portable cheats need explicit acquisition and effect compatibility |
| When does rolling happen? | Entry rolls, earned attempts, repeat attempts and rerolls create different incentives and retention rules |
| Can early choices change later life? | Work, mastery, relationships and powers need lasting consumers and continuing goals beyond a short quest chain |

For RNG, consider whether ordinary attempts repeatedly outperform playing the
run, whether duplicates have a useful authored resolution, whether a strong
early roll has substantive play, and whether unlocks persist into later starts.
These are tuning questions, not automatic mandates for caps, pity or penalties.
Agent-only deterministic start/roll fixtures make every selected variant testable
without turning player-facing entry into a spoiler catalog.

## Which world offers the most experimentation?

2026-10-11 recommendation, not an objective ranking or accepted roster:
RI is the strongest first candidate for combining powers, material production,
trade, exploration and institutional choices. LOTM is especially promising for
investigation, unusual powers, artifacts and information-driven social play.
Naruto is especially promising for personalized techniques, bodily training,
tools/puppets and team roles. These describe adaptation opportunities; none
claims those options are currently playable.

This comparison uses the local RI institutions/life index, LOTM design owner
and Naruto module catalogue. Public premise checks used the official English
[RI synopsis](https://www.webnovel.com/book/reverend-insanity_7996858406002505),
[LOTM synopsis](https://www.webnovel.com/book/11022733006234505) and
[Naruto puppetry column](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1641).
The synopses establish broad premises, not the completeness of our proposed
sandbox or a numerical count of possible builds.

Select for independent, combinable actions that cause different outcomes,
rather than raw ability counts. A large catalogue behind one mandatory quest
is less useful for this goal than a bounded region with overlapping pursuits.
Proposed first RI proof connects an academy/clan opening, a commercial opening
and an independent field opening to shared local resources and consequences.
Exact identities, acquisition rights and starting means need source qualification.

Preserve independent player goals rather than forcing the protagonist's life
or personality. Give player-made combinations actual targets, recipients and
world responses. Start identity, era and cheats should vary independently where
coherent. Optional authored starting controls can coexist with suspenseful random
entry without disclosing the hidden pool. Random rolls should open distinct
styles of play; deliberate powerful/free options remain possible under their
selected rules. A finished local sandbox should support continued play after
its first story checkpoint, not close every activity with the plot.
