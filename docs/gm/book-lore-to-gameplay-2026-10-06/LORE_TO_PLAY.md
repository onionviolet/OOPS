# Lore that changes what people can do

Use lore as a connected set of means, expectations and commitments. A useful scene asks who can act, with whose materials/body/authority, what each person knows, what they give up, and what survives afterward. The five local packets below are a first translation of a larger map. They do not exhaust these books or establish full world parity. [Exact evidence and coverage](FINDINGS.json), [implementation instructions](IMPLEMENTATION_PACKETS.md) and [packet index](PACKETS.json) own the handoff.

**Verified in this GM:** existing analytical claim objects/notes, their source-member locators and accepted coverage rows; selected current native/exported consumers and live release/status. **Reused from the book GMs:** their supplied-edition semantic reading/review/integration, not a new full reread here. **Proposed here:** every new local scene, line of dialogue, weather rule, price, timing, method and causal outcome. Book narrator, actor testimony, suspicion and later disclosure retain separate labels. No dossier count proves authenticated publication, translation fidelity or permanent recall of every detail.

## 5. Connected choices, chronology and closure

The proposed sequence uses optional cross-links rather than five mandatory errands:

| Phase in this original branch | Useful ordinary route | Competing route and retained difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1, work/home consent | Repair personally and fund the extra Neri service; collect her produced kit only after completion. | Ask willing Orr to cover, keep resources for own work, or decline. Different performer/wage/time/property/opportunity account. |
| P2, observe/transmit/intervene | Inspect, sign a limited warning or clear the drain with a real log. | Relay privately, let Ressa decide, shelter the expressly entrusted stave, or ignore. Flood support and known accounts differ; rain itself is not erased. |
| P3, investigate with a functioning object | Pay owner-operated measurement or purchase Vela's actual prism. | Loan only after custody-safe use is bound; exact return/deposit/ownership affect later service and assembly. |
| P4, earn and transmit procedure | Learn personally and make a measured strip; ordinary log lining stays usable. | Sponsor consenting Neri with her own body/effort, or forego instruction. Knowledge/source/output belong to actual learner/performer, not payer by default. |
| P5, use or dispose of counted materials | Keep/dry offcuts for repairs; fit one cover before Nell's already warned crop deadline. | Early harvest, leave crop or obstruct notch with actual scraps. Finite crop/native food and physical drain state differ; no retroactive storm. |

The proposed 18-minute P1 window, 20-minute P2 rain window and existing six-minute crop window have different start causes. Open the crop warning through its actual existing inspection when ready, rather than beginning all deadlines at enrollment. New supported-time retention must run before scheduler expiry; generic absolute deadlines do not already freeze. Ressa's warning response/inspection/placement finishes before her exposure job occupies her; the player can still perform independent drain work. Neri's last attention can fund extra binding or a sponsored lesson, so those branches compete instead of receiving free replenishment. Numbers are unbalanced proposals; the player need not complete every optional research/lesson before rain. Native shift hours/site capacity/busy actors remain authoritative. Slower ordinary work, paid help and refusal should produce useful paths with different commitments, not one hidden perfect sequence.

Closure is scoped. Record player undertaking, independent person, local physical outcome and received known account separately. “Warning delivered, recipient refused, shelf stayed dry because player cleared drain” is coherent. So is “room repaired late, kit earned, inspection missed.” NPC departure, player return, job cancellation, bodily death, captured state, source restoration and optional prestige are separate events. The current fixed mortal/covenant/anchor endings demonstrate selected finality distinctions but are not a generic all-book fate engine.

Chronology cards for reuse must name source/member/cutoff, occurrence order, reveal order, local calendar precision, person/body/source state, private knowledge and later revisions. S11's dream catastrophe cannot be imported as real history; S13's early loop uncertainty cannot establish a reset implementation. Paradise sid26–28's post-return services cannot silently become Goa-era facts. RI's later unread-for-integration chapters cannot enter ch590 authoring just because readers reported completion. Source timelines remain immutable baselines; the game's changed future follows its actual actors and material causes.
