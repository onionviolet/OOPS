## P1: The overlapping shifts

**Player experience.** After Neri's existing one funded service has finished and its original collection is settled, she can offer one separate, explicitly negotiated commission. She wants the occupied room's shutter repaired before using its bench for another kit. Orr wants a kit for an upcoming field inspection. The player can repair the room and fund Neri, ask Orr to repair it instead of doing their own next shift, or decline and prepare their own kit. Neri can decline or postpone the extra commission based on her own existing means. A home, a wage and a request become different commitments with useful physical outcomes.

**Evidence to invention.** S01/MOL-C001-03, ch1 p27–59, distinguishes a six-month care request from Zorian's suspicion about parental motives. S03/W007-C04, Interlude1 p24–26, distinguishes settled hospital costs, promised prevention and blocked transfer. S04/Paradise sid26 p25–65 distinguishes employee/service accounts, personal concerns and institutional permissions; this testimony occurs after Goa, not at its opening. Our shutter, kit deadline, Neri/Orr negotiation and all costs are inventions. This is no universal rule that family duty grants affection, or that paying a wage grants allegiance.

**Prerequisites and entry.** Explicit new scene consent; current expansion11/auditRemainder1 and referenceGameplay1 admission; actual player `base.frontier`/supported human body and usable hands; Neri `remainder_home.neri` alive, willing, actually occupying the finished Shack room with her residence claim; her first service and collection already settled; no reserved Neri job/departure. Orr is actual `feature_society.orr`, not a new native copy. The player has acquired the normal field workshop, knows the involved people and has current permitted room/workbench access. The scene cannot begin on arrival, rendering or an absent old save. If Neri departed, close with her retained departure and offer the ordinary player route; never respawn her.

**Knowledge/time.** Neri knows her own requested work and room concern. Orr learns only the request personally delivered to them. Player sees agreed terms, current receipts and received accounts, not either actor's private controller reason or purse balance. Opening records a proposed 18 played-minute inspection window in a new situation; native calendar access still follows actual source-derived local context. Freeze unsupported absent worlds/browser-closed time. No novel calendar is converted to an exact base-world date.

**Decision and conserved means.** The extra commission is separate from `remainder_home.resident_bind`; it cannot reuse its once-only reward.
