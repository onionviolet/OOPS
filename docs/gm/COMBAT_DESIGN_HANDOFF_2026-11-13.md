## Troubles we are having

| Finding | What we observed and what is still open |
| --- | --- |
| F1 · Combat serves different jobs | Village combat already gives gear and preparation a purpose through repeatable group clears. A spar, hunt, escape, or confrontation can have a different objective and a result that matters to the next scene. Coverage depends on which encounter supplies its real equipment, resources, actors and stakes, and which world systems consume the result. [Village loop](../../src/main.js#L4839) · [Integration plan](../PROCEDURAL_CARRY_OUT.md#tie-in-considerations-seam-fun-screen-and-the-fight-system-2026-11-12) |
| F2 · Manual control can add choices or just clicks | Live Tempo stops when the player is ready, and different moves take different amounts of fight time. The resolver accepts a foe ID, but standard attack buttons let it choose the lowest-HP active foe. The live handover performs one safe turn. A headless policy driver and shared contest policies are evidence of automation in the engine. Sustained player-facing delegation, takeover and stop conditions are separate integration work. [Tempo controls](../COMBAT_AND_CONFLICT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-18.md#round-9-what-the-existing-tempo-controls-prove-2026-11-13) |
| F3 · The view can imply capabilities the fight lacks | Wang Er has positions along one axis. The user found the sprites looked misplaced and the text read as distance without a clear position. Staggering sprites for readability looked like terrain or elevation. Both views can display reach, direction, held and dropped items, and visible windups from the same state. A local route around cover requires spatial rules and a consumer. [Positioning discussion](../COMBAT_AND_CONFLICT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-18.md#round-8-make-positioning-legible-through-text-2026-11-13) |
| F4 · Plausible actions can fall outside the fight's command model | Standard shared contest moves bind self or a foe. The engine already has reach checks, costs, guarded timing, interruption and typed results. Acting on a loose wand, an exit, an ally, or an ongoing effect needs a way to address that subject and settle the result through its owner. Readiness, windup, release and arrival can change different decisions. A displayed countdown by itself grants no response. ## Chain of events

1. **Rendering and agency.** We asked whether sprites or 3D would make implementation easier. We proposed text and animated 2D as switchable views over the same fight, with manual and automatic control independent of view. Direct selection on a board can be faster while both inputs offer the same action. [Rounds 7–8](../COMBAT_AND_CONFLICT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-18.md#round-7-play-the-same-fight-through-text-or-sprites-2026-11-13)
2. **Research and correction.** Skotos and Fate suggested text with named landmarks and routes. Dwarf Fortress showed explicit aim menus, FTL showed pause and orders in 2D, and Final Fantasy XII showed configurable priorities. Checking YAIRPG revealed that live Tempo already has timed moves and body-part aim, with narrower targeting and delegation than originally described. [Round 9 and sources](../COMBAT_AND_CONFLICT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-18.md#round-9-what-the-existing-tempo-controls-prove-2026-11-13)
3. **Prototype and feedback.** A standalone Wang Er encounter used the existing seed-5 Gu fixture, a side-on board, text, animation on/off, manual moves and the fixture's default chooser. An earlier 1920×1080 headless browser run verified switching during a pending cast, manual/auto handoff, and matching result, fight time, resources and log for the same choices with motion off. The user pointed out the positional presentation. A small follow-up grouped the text by position and anchored board figures to the ground line before the user asked to stop fixing it. Final verification of that follow-up was not confirmed in the chat. [Prototype and rebuild instructions](../../prototypes/wang-er-duel/README.md)
4. **Different styles and world interaction.** Discussion moved from graphics to why fights play differently. Compare a Gu encounter about means, reach and essence with a supervised spell bout about disarming and when a referee stops it. Consider a LOTM confrontation where recognition, preparation or retreat decides the encounter. Recovery of a dropped wand during a hostile encounter became a proposed test of routes, custody, bodily means and events already in motion. [Rounds 10–12](../COMBAT_AND_CONFLICT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-18.md#round-10-a-working-fight-loop-covers-one-kind-of-play-2026-11-13)
5. **Timer representation.** Keep the current opposing attack bars for recurring strikes. For detailed fights, consider a short ordered list of visible events with action previews. Compare it with a shared timeline when overlapping deadlines matter. The example asks whether the player can duck behind cover before release, with a slower wand recovery as the comparison. [Round 13](../COMBAT_AND_CONFLICT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-18.md#round-13-from-attack-bars-to-timing-you-can-act-on-2026-11-13)
