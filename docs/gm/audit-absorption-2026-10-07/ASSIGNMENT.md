Direct human request, 2026-10-07: create a GM whose goal is reconciling and absorbing the audits, using subagents to do the work and coordinating other project chats as their results arrive. This assignment starts that role. It combines reconciliation, implementation and integration under the existing master workflow.

## Goal and scope

Reconcile the project's current audit, research, source, design and delivery owners into one evidence-backed view of what is already implemented, what remains useful to absorb, who is doing it and what actually blocks the next change. Implement the suitable remaining findings through existing or bounded new workers, integrate their real native/UI/state bindings and produce the matching rebuilt game. Continue across batches and context boundaries until the defined intake and implementation goal is complete.

The intake covers existing top-level audits and vision/design families, engine and native integration audits, world/cross-world/campaign dossiers, game/repository/fork/decompiled-source findings, DLC/deeper-source supplements, and novel/lore/causal/power/character findings. Include both completed finite deliveries and incomplete packets. The initial intake and ownership reports below are starting evidence, not proof that every source has been read or absorbed.

Make the goal finite: pin the meaningful source owners and active work lanes present at intake; absorb their relevant returned findings and the necessary connecting work. New incoming evidence can revise a row before closure. Further whole-game reading, every possible future world, or endless new game discovery is not a condition for finishing this goal. A book's presence does not select its entire world for implementation. Do not reduce the broad request to five cherry-picked rows or call all audit work done after one batch.
