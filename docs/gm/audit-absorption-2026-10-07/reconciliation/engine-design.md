## Direction that remains authoritative

[PROJECT's expansion design](../../PROJECT_LOG.md#expansion-design-proposal) remains the governing direction: deepen the inherited adventure, offer several connected progression routes, make worlds mechanically distinct, keep useful reasons to revisit, and limit the first milestone to one realm and one prestige layer. The date-stamped names, costs, reset/retention rules, and sequences in [Expansion design and inspiration audit](../../../../docs/EXPANSION_DESIGN.md) are explicitly proposed; Jade Hollow's 60-qi/12-tick sequence and remembered-breath/steady-hands choices are not accepted balance or current canonical progression. Actual selected settings and player paths remain separate in their campaign owners.

The project distinguishes the full creative horizon from finite delivery. Current ten destinations include bounded home/Paradise/Goa/Cyoria/Proto23 paths and other distinct regional campaigns; IDs do not imply complete worlds. The home/social/furnishing/visit/retort/Proto23 combined source/native/UI/build delivery is acquired. Audit root explicitly acquired its shared succession and has integrated assay, known-route and three selected ACS consumers. Matching audit rebuild e77080593d4b is delivered, implemented-unverified. [Current ledger](../RECONCILIATION.json) and [acquisition](../ACQUISITION.json) supersede prior waiting-lease observations; original scope and source-specific boundaries remain.

The 2026-10-05 vision amendments clarify the design boundary: loreful major adversaries need source-era identity, motive, means and supported counters; a branch may avert a confrontation without spawning a replacement boss. Scenic detail, incidental people, casual dialogue and unresolved texture may remain nonfunctional. Functional payoff applies to selected gameplay paths, not every fact in a source. Cumulative endings, longer timelines, parallel progression and broad campaigns are requested horizons; generic aging, death, rewind, replacement-body and offline-loss policies remain unselected unless a specific campaign owns a narrower rule.
