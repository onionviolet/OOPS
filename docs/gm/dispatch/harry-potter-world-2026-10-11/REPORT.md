## Findings and proposed direction

F1 source access changed during research. The seven-novel compilation PDF and separate2016 rehearsal-script EPUB are locally available. Dispatcher completed seven page-referenced novel TXT files, one spine-referenced script TXT and an FTS5 database. GM independently checked all text hashes, unchanged originals, database integrity and bounded retrieval. Preparation is verified; full reading, completeness and edition authentication are not. The PDF contains mixed/OCR material and visible errors.

F2 source-qualified matrices preserve16 origin/relationship families,10 era/start families and14 identity/body families. Literal cousin, added cousin, distant genealogy, sibling/twin, ward, honorary family and unrelated starts each have distinct history/property/eligibility consequences. Initial identity/body, counterpart, temporary transformation, social recognition and persistent sourced-or-authored change remain separate. No hidden branch truth is disclosed or fixed by this public report.

D1 proposed near-term opening is a child's ordinary life shortly before the familiar school intake, followed by meaningful study/craft/social choices. Unrelated classmate is the simplest baseline; cousin/ward branches are alternatives with explicit genealogy/care/history. Earlier-generation student, adult craft/teaching and game-era late entry offer materially different routes. This is a recommendation, not director acceptance or a runtime launch.

F3 selective fan survey has three Chinese and three English works, with primary work links, dates, access receipts and actual synopsis/chapter/sample limits. No full fanwork/export was acquired. Useful design questions include life before school, adult role versus competence, specialty study, cross-cultural school life and identity maintenance. No popularity/consensus or full-reading certificate.

D2 inspect official game guide/toolkit documentation and exposed schedules/quest data before executable decompilation. Current official tooling supplies useful concrete design models, but the Windows toolkit is not locally inspected and no game candidate was found in bounded filename searches. No game installed, played, downloaded or decompiled. Adaptation evidence stays a separate branch.
