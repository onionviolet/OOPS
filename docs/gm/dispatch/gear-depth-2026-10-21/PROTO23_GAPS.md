# Remaining useful Proto23 gaps for gear depth

Read-only bounded survey, 2026-10-21. Applies AD6–8, AD22, AD32–36, AD38; MR1/MR5/MR21–23/MR31; AR3/6–8/10/12 and SR5/SR9/SR21. The gear-depth lane already owns the new gear workshop source; this report names the additions that fit beside it. `PROJECT.md` and standing owners are authority; CRL rows and this ranking identify fitting proposals, not newly accepted world rules. The earlier perishable slice is source-implemented but still needs the root rebuilt browser/persistence gate.

## Ranked targets

1. **CRL36/37: processed-material identity through inventory operations (best fit).** The observation owner now stores immutable, skill-bounded carcass readings in `src/harvest.js`, but finished material outputs become ordinary aggregate stacks. Add a narrow lot identity/condition carrier only at the selected material consumer. Reuse native inventory transactions and `src/material_choice_view.js` exact selection/commit revalidation, the harvesting specimen timestamp/observation model, and `src/native_craft_transaction.js` atomic rollback. Do not add a universal perishability service. Exercise split, partial transfer, merge of unlike ages, partial processing and rollback; preserve conserved units and per-source age/condition. A remembered estimate stays attached to the observed subject/quantity; merge invalidates or qualifies its scope, while a higher skill creates a new estimate. Stale selection must re-quote/review, never spend a substituted lot. Supported observation/reopening must not reset age or grant freshness. Foreign stack-average food expiry is only a prompt; native material mechanisms and costs decide deterioration. Acceptance: rebuilt desktop fresh-origin flow through split → transfer → partial processing, reload/export/import with exact lineage and readable old/new estimates; no free freshness, duplicated stock, or hover-driven clock/knowledge change.

2. **CRL44: one conditional opportunity in existing salvage.** `src/gear_work.js` and `src/gear_workshop_domain.js` now have native dismantling and material returns, so if the forge lane needs one extra connected reward, derive one discoverable improvement from an existing salvage condition/consumer. The source Quartermaster predicate suggests an opportunity-shaped benefit, not its grant implementation or a universal title law. Reuse gear-work quote, native maker/world means, exact item key and committed transaction; expose the condition and resulting additional recovered material in quote/history. Acceptance: earn/hold the relevant means, inspect a previously familiar eligible item, quote/execute and verify only the actual eligible salvage changes; repeat, stale quote, reload and unrelated salvage yield no duplicate grant. This is lower priority until a genuine native condition and consumer are selected; absent source counterpart, not a missing base salvage feature.

3. **CRL24/25 spatial slip/landing consequence (defer past gear slice).** Existing mountain travel, rope/climb skill and physical preparation already provide a route. The missing distinction is an authored spatial position/grip/fall/landing consequence. Adapt the reference cliff toy only in an existing climbing encounter with clear location, cost, method and recoverable result; do not import wet-rock law or a generic physics system. Acceptance: prepared climb succeeds; a distinct unstable route can slip, visibly changes position/condition and offers a supported recovery; save/reload and return preserve the actual state. Existing climb alone is not an absent feature, and a staged illustration is not runtime proof.

## Owner and scope boundary

Gear workshop seam is `src/gear_workshop_domain.js` → `src/gear_work.js` → `src/gear_workshop_panel.js`, with real part stats and durability, maker/world/station checks, transactional inventory, and history. The native source supports attach/detach, heat treatment, repair, swap, honing and salvage. Reuse those connections; do not build a second gear, craft, inventory or inspection framework. NPC-heavy simulation and every illustrative foreign-world mechanic remain deferred. Current sources were checked in both the shared primary checkout and the candidate gear-depth worktree; its pending workshop files are present, so gear work is not inferred absent.
