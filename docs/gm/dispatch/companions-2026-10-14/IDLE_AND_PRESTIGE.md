They can form one playable slice; none adds a second reset layer or requires global offline simulation. A new travelling stray belongs to its own following/body owner, not a rewrite of Rill.

| Code / rank | Candidate and first-layer value | Save impact / compatibility | OPEN rebuilt browser gate at 1366x768 |

Makes the prestige decision understandable before loss. | Read existing source owners and preview; no new saved field needed. Keep current renewal semantics. If later resuming automatically, that is a separate policy change. | On a fresh test origin earn renewal with a known local companion. Preview fits desktop panels and names actual losses/retention.
