Weibao asked for "subtle micro stuff". Root palette round 6 (section DA-DS)
lists micro ideas. Your job is to ground them in real animal behaviour so
generated and authored text feels true.

Task:
1. For dogs, cats, horses, parrots and corvids, birds of prey, rabbits,
   reptiles, livestock (goat, sheep, cattle, pig, chicken), bees and ants:
   body-language signals and their meanings (stress, play, trust, fear,
   pain, illness, ageing), daily routines, learning and habit formation,
   bonding signs, grief behaviour. Cite ethology and veterinary sources
   (university extension pages, veterinary associations, peer-reviewed
   reviews). Mark contested or folk claims as such (for example "guilty
   look", dogs sensing illness, telling the bees as folklore).
2. Translate each into short text cues a life sim can show without
   numbers ("ears flat, tail low"), grouped by what the cue reveals, plus
   the hidden state it would read from (root C1-C7 tracks, needs, health).
3. Map how fantasy kinds could extend the same grammar: spirits (flicker,
   temperature), constructs (pitch, heat), dragons, swarms. Mark INFERRED.
4. Frequency and restraint: how often ambient companion lines should appear
   so presence is felt and not spammy, with idle-game examples if sourced.
