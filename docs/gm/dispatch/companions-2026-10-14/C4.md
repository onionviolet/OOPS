Task: write 14 to 18 concrete companion life stories that trace one companion
through a whole player life (or several lives and heirs), each in a different
world or world type (cultivation, wizard school, post-apocalypse, soulslike,
time loop, devil-contract city, plain medieval village, tech world, original
world). Each journey: how they met (root palette B codes), how the bond grew
and strained (C, O, P), care and idle play (E, V), a danger or loss (F, AE,
T), how it ends or carries on (D, I), which systems it touches (W), and where
it hits a per-world dial. Mark every step SHOWN in source, STATED in docs or
INFERRED. Include at least two dark journeys, two cozy ones, one army-scale
one (R), one where the companion outlives the player, one inside a time loop
and one cross-world one.

Then stress-test the palette: list every place a journey needed a rule the
palette lacks or where two palette rows conflict, as rows with fresh codes
(prefix JX), both sides kept.
