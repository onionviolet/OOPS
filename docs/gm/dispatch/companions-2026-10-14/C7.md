Lane C1 catalogues companion lore facts. You study craft: the scenes and
beats that make a reader care about a companion. Use the private full-book
library under `docs/research/novels/full-book/` (Shadow Slave, Solo Leveling,
Reverend Insanity, Worm, Mother of Learning, Lord of the Mysteries,
Legendary Mechanic, Omniscient Reader and any others with companions) and
their existing indexes and dossiers before raw chapters. Cite chapter and
file lines; quote at most a few words.

Task:
1. For 10 to 15 companion relationships across those books: first meeting,
   the moment the bond turned, the cost or strain, the payoff, how the
   companion's own wants showed, and what the author kept vague.
2. Pull out reusable beats (prefix BB): small rituals, shared hardship,
   callbacks, the companion disobeying for good reason, the quiet scene after
   a fight, the loss that lands. For each, how a text idle life sim could
   produce it from generated or authored events without feeling scripted.
3. Anti-patterns that make companions feel like gear (prefix BX).
