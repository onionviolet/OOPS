## Scope and evidence key

The [root palette](../../../COMPANIONS_FAMILIARS_AND_PETS_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md) owns A/B/C meanings. This follows [C1's six-column catalogue](../../../worlds/COMPANIONS_BY_WORLD_2026-10-14.md), extending its coverage rather than creating another actor model. [PROJECT](../../../../PROJECT.md), [user vision](../../../USER_VISION.md), the superhero row in [multiverse structures](../../../worlds/MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES.md), and [bounded comic comparisons](../../../worlds/LORE_AND_CONTINUITY.md#bounded-comic-comparisons) retain authority. Comic research does not select another world, reorder canon-first work, or reopen paused game worlds or Foundation gates.

**E1 STATED-P**: a publisher, creator or rights-holder page states the linked claim. **E2 STATED-W**: a community wiki states it; this is secondary evidence, not an official wiki or an inspected comic scene. **E3 INFERRED**: classification, proposed acquisition, bond, care, player role, start, cost and fit. **E4 UNVERIFIED**: the inspected evidence does not settle it. The source label certifies only the short claim beside its URL. Most web evidence is indexed page text, not an acquired revision; full-page exceptions and failures are recorded in the report. No SHOWN issue-content claim or unchecked MEMORY claim is used as canon here.

**K1 kinds**: A1 pet, A2 working animal, A3 mount, A4 familiar, A5 summon, A6 contracted spirit/devil, A7 shikigami/shadow beast, A8 made being, A9 undead/bound dead, A10 object spirit, A11 system/voice, A12 swarm, A13 plant, A14 place spirit, A15 hatchling. A code can describe a function without describing legal status. An alien, mutant animal, homunculus or android person can be an independent colleague. Symbiotes are living aliens; A6/A10 below are functional analogies, not a claim that they are spirits. No need to force every person into an A code.

**K2 routes**: B1 buy, B2 adopt, B3 tame, B4 raise/hatch, B5 contract, B6 ritual, B7 chooses you, B8 inherit, B9 craft, B10 rescue/steal/win, B11 cheat grant, B12 crosses with you. Team recruitment, employment and friendship sit outside acquisition codes. B7 is only an analogy when a person chooses cooperation; B5 means actual negotiated terms, not proof of magical binding.

**K3 bond**: C1 trust, C2 affection, C3 obedience, C4 understanding, C5 terms, C6 shared channel, C7 respect. Communication, affection and command rights differ. C8-C13 in the palette add wants, evolving terms, verbs, souring, shared fate and companion relationships.

All table content outside an explicit STATED clause is **INFERRED**, including proposed costs. A missing sourced price is **unknown**, not free and not permission to invent a mandatory penalty. Each fit cell uses **Default + Dial + Reasonably**. Strong starts remain possible with the actual source and history. Playing **as** any of these beings belongs to [nonhuman starts](../../../worlds/NONHUMAN_STARTS_BY_WORLD_2026-10-14.md) and [bonds and parties](../../../worlds/NONHUMAN_BONDS_AND_PARTIES_2026-10-14.md).
