## P1. Shared text grammar and rate key

Default: visible observed behavior first, plain body type, one sentence around
8-18 words and preferably under 120 characters. It may wrap to two readable
lines in a 360px log; never clip a meaningful result to force one line. Names
remain readable and identifiable without color. Numbers stay in optional Inspect
where known and useful; actual costs, elapsed work time, quantities and risks
remain explicit when they change a choice. No default affection score or +bond.

Dial: quiet / standard / chatty per browser; per-world vocabulary, species,
speech and tone. Reasonably: more talk does not grant more knowledge or benefits.
A “no numbers” care verb can have a real cost only if that cost is disclosed.
Rate codes below are shared caps, not per-family allocations to add together.

| Rate | Default proposal at 1366x768 | Dial and reasonable boundary |
|---|---|---|
| A | Eligible ambient candidate; fits global six/hour cap and eight-minute minimum spacing | Quiet 0-2/hour, standard up to 6, chatty up to 12 with four-minute spacing. No quota to fill. |
| T | Observed transition or entry into a scene; at most one companion clause on that event | Repeated travel/care transitions consume A if only flavor. A changed condition stays on card; no repeated alert while unchanged. |
| M | Notable witnessed milestone retained in history; one row per causal event identity | Default at most two automatic milestone notices/hour, folded together if more; every real outcome remains retrievable. Player-opened history is not rate-limited. No global “first-ever” ban on repeatable world events. |
| I | Explicit inspection or chosen small verb, one result in place per accepted interaction | No unsolicited feed copy for repeated petting. A changed persistent memory may be linked to history without another notification. |
| W | Meaningful warning or outcome, independent of flavor allowance | Coalesce unchanged issue by actor and cause; pin current status and destination. New severity/change can surface; no reminder storm. |
| P | Passive card / tooltip fact, no emitted line merely because a panel rerenders | Refresh from a new known fact, not random rewording on every tick. |

## P4. Ambient budget, return budget and replay restraint

**B1 Active play [INFERRED].** Standard max **6 automatic ambient lines per
60 minutes of unpaused foreground play**, shared across all companions and
pure-flavor milestone notices. Minimum eight minutes between standalone
ambient lines. Paused/hidden time earns no backlog. Use actual eligible scene
transitions, never “emit because the timer expired.” In ten fast travel events,
at most one eligible clause need appear until spacing allows another. Empty
content pool means silence. Quiet 0-2/hour; chatty up to 12/hour. Existing place
and activity background noise remains a separate source today; future combined
background policy should avoid multiplying those writers. Six is a starting
UI proposal, not an ethology-derived frequency or an observed engine rate.

**B2 Return [INFERRED].** Per supported offline-return settlement: **0-2 ambient
sentences total**, plus at most three grouped outcome rows and a separate
persistent current-issue row. Show a time basis such as “eligible queued work”
or “no companion time advanced.” Never multiply flavor by offline hours or
companions. Expand full real receipts in a reading region if more outcomes
exist; do not discard them to enforce the summary size. The return summary uses
its own two-line cap and resets active ambient spacing at return, preventing an
immediate third flavor line. Unsupported catch-up shows only a present greeting
if actually witnessed; it invents no errands, feeding, aging or absence drama.
N2/ENGINE_FIT T2 explicitly separates played idle work from browser-closed time.

**B3 Dedupe [INFERRED].** Canonical cause/actor/observer identity joins scene,
People and biography references. One notification maximum per issue version;
changing severity or resolving it can create a new meaningful notice. Projection,
opening a card, sort changes, reload and import do not emit a new event. Keep
last-shown variant/family/context or an equivalent replay-safe presentation
record under a chosen future owner; current source does not supply this companion
record. A browser-only cooldown cannot guarantee cross-browser import dedupe.
Do not backfill animal memories into old saves. Return batches get stable receipt
identity; reviewing the same batch is reading, not another return.

**B4 Variety [INFERRED].** Exclude the last three templates for that actor;
default same exact ambient wording cooling window 60 active minutes and same
family at least 20. Choose among eligible body/routine/place/social cues before
using a second of the same family. No adjacent standalone lines from one actor
when another has eligible content; a pair line uses one slot. Dials may shorten
windows in chatty worlds, but no synonymous rewording of an unchanged fact to
bypass dedupe. Keep saved names/marks/preferences fixed; select prose after facts,
not facts after prose. No eligible variety means no line.

**B5 Attention [INFERRED].** Flavor never stops work, switches tabs, announces
assertively, scrolls older reading or displaces an error. Inspect shows useful
known numbers only on request; real outcome counts remain readable. Confirmed
sickness, missing status, failed task and completed handover get W, not A, with
coalescing and a destination. Mute companion ambience independently of outcomes;
allow grief/retrospective tone off. A future implementation must reconcile screen
reader announcement policy separately from visible line rate.

**C9 link.** [C9 assignment](C9.md) owns species grounding and frequency research;
`MICRO_BEHAVIOUR.md` was not yet present at the source pass. B1-B5 are provisional
C10 interface budgets. When C9 arrives, use its observation caveats and cue pools;
do not treat six/hour as a veterinary claim or silently replace these rates.
Conflicting rate advice should be recorded with both rationales before selection.
