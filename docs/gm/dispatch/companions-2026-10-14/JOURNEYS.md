## Integration boundary

The register exhausts the extra rules assumed by these sixteen written paths at this level of detail; it does not claim to exhaust every possible branch. The stories also use ordinary palette rows, such as D1 training, without inventing a new engine rule for them. Root ownership can accept, adapt or reject each JX consideration while retaining its two sides. Canon/source validation remains a separate world-lane task. There is no implemented-unverified companion feature hidden in this delivery: it is documentation only, with all story outcomes proposed.
