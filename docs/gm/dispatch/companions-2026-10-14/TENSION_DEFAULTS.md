Use separate facts for what happened, what this mind can recall, what the player
knows, actual body/location, active channel, custody/title, permitted orders and
outstanding work. An ordinary pet's learned habits can endure without exact
verbatim lifelong recall. A non-sapient remote executor need not acquire an
invented independent mind. A sapient companion remains a person even when local
law or magic coerces it; obedience is not evidence of willing agreement.

Default clock suggestions below concern eligible simulated exposure. Closing
the browser does not create age, neglect, new infection or work receipts under
these proposals. An authored supported catch-up dial needs its own interval,
care reserve, stop boundary and disclosure. Reset manifests independently name
body/age, accessible memory, directed ties, stock, permissions and queues.

enforcement and remaining duty. A necessary care handoff is planned rather than
withdrawn as punishment for declining work. Emergency protection stays only
within an existing supported scope; it is not an excuse for a new blanket lease.
