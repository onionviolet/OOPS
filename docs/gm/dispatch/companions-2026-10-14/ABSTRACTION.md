## Seven concepts, each deliberately small

All A definitions are **INFERRED considerations**.

**A1 Actor and subject reference.** An Actor is a persistent participant that
can own decisions, knowledge, resources or work. A subject reference identifies
the thing involved when that thing is not an actor: an exact item, place,
power/card, obligation or group. Use a qualified reference to its existing
domain and instance; separate person, body, controller and player focus where
the premise needs them. Actor is not a universal base class for everything.
A sword spirit may need a person reference and a physical item/body reference;
an ordinary sword needs only its existing item binding. Being linked does not
create agency, a body, legal personhood or a second inventory.

**A2 Link.** A Link says which participants/subjects are persistently connected,
in which direction, through what kind and establishment cause. Several kinds
can coexist: friend and rival, tenant and gardener, holder and debtor. Regard,
membership, attachment and entitlement remain typed facets with their own
owners, not one universal bond score. An NPC-to-player opinion differs from
the player's opinion; a holder-to-cheat binding need not have a reverse
opinion at all. Link is principally a way to find and qualify existing records.
Only a selected consumer needing a genuinely missing relation justifies a new
validated record under the relevant owner.

**A3 Terms.** Terms describe a specific grant, duty or binding: provider,
recipient, act/domain, authority or mechanism, inputs/payer, duration/clock,
scope, settlement, withdrawal/breach and remaining obligations. They can be
negotiated, customary, magical, imposed or absent, according to the world.
An informal friendship need not acquire a contract object; a debt cannot be
settled by increasing affection. Terms distinguish offered, accepted, active,
fulfilled, revoked and disputed where those states matter. The actual executor
rechecks applicable means and authority rather than treating a text label or
high regard as executable permission.

**A4 Lens.** A Lens is the supported route by which a particular observer
learns particular fields about a subject: local senses, testimony, inspection,
appraisal, shared sight, memory access or forecast. Define domain, fidelity,
range/reach, freshness, recipients and any authored cost, resistance or error.
Precision and breadth are separate dials. A lens can be exact or truly
omniscient within an explicitly supported premise; ordinary closeness alone
does not provide that premise. The truth stays in its original owner, the
lens grants access, and remembered results go to the observer's knowledge or
receipt owner. Learning something supplies neither title nor command authority.

**A5 Clock exposure.** Clock exposure names which existing time domain advances
which state: played common minutes, local realm time, biological age,
subjective experience, deadline, loop epoch or explicitly supported wall-time
settlement. One bond may refer to several clocks without owning a timer.
Specify rate, eligibility, pause/absence and boundary conversion only where
needed. Keep age, work, terms expiry and contact growth independent when the
world makes them independent. Rewind, heir succession and world passage need
declared retained/lost slices and timestamp treatment, not an automatic reset
of every endpoint.

**A6 Custody and authority reference.** Custody identifies actual possession,
carrier and care responsibility; related authority references identify legal
title, use permission and bodily control without treating them as synonyms.
Existing item custody remains authoritative for physical stock. A living
being's carer/title and a host's control lease need their own typed semantics,
not conversion into an item loan. Competing claims can remain disputed while
the actual holder/location is known. A cheat can be held without a transferable
object; a place can have an owner without being carried. Use only applicable
facets, and bind transfers to actual conserved subjects.

**A7 Cue.** A Cue is an observer-qualified presentation of a supported fact,
act, received account or reading: a posture, a terse ledger entry, a worn mark,
a refusal, an attributed voice or a result row. It records no new private
truth simply by being rendered. Stable identity/context can select prose;
semantic events, learned facts and any required delivery/dedupe state belong
to existing evidence/presentation owners. A passive view and an emitted notice
have different replay needs. A cue may be ambiguous and a lens may clarify
it; neither prose nor a forecast performs the underlying action.

## Smallest useful application, not a new build request

**INFERRED candidate:** for the next independently selected bond operation,
write one paragraph in its existing task record answering A1-A7 with owner
references. For example, a willing companion's delivery names provider and
recipient, exact parcel custody, permitted route, funded job, applicable clock,
observed handover and replay receipt. The same checklist can describe a loan,
a rival's challenge or a reveal scan without creating a `BondManager`.

Before any new persisted facet ships, its owning lane chooses compatibility,
retention and failure handling. Before UI/gameplay claims, rebuild and exercise
the selected behavior on a fresh **1366x768 desktop** origin, with affected
save/reload/literal-file import. These are future gates for selected work,
not tests run here or requirements to implement all seven concepts first.
Mobile optimization, full offline simulation, a global relation merge and
universal multi-body/group control remain deferred. Human acceptance remains
separate from completion of this documentation lane.
