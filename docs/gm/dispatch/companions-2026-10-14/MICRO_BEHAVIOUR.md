### Ageing, separation and loss

**MB14 [INFERRED].** For generated or authored text, select only from present anatomy and an eligible recorded action: no horse feathers, cat beak grinding or visible waggle dance through an opaque closed hive. Persist individual preferences and causal history before describing them. Cosmetic narration does not update a bond, fabricate a past event or reroll personality on reload. Observer knowledge gates interpretation; an experienced keeper may read a pattern while a novice only sees the movement.

## Frequency and restraint

**FR01 [INFERRED].** Presence works through a visible place and changing habits as well as new lines. Default a quiet companion sentence in an existing scene/log plus an inspectable recent observation. Dial Off / Quiet / Lived-in. Reasonably no compulsory interaction or rewards for reading every ambient line. These rates are initial taste-test proposals, not sourced genre standards or selected mechanics.

| ID | Situation | Default | Dial + Reasonably |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR02 | Ordinary ambient text while visible | At most one eligible ambient line across the whole household every 3–5 minutes of visible real time; it is a ceiling, not guaranteed output. | Quiet 8–12 minutes; lived-in 1–2 minutes. No timers tied to simulation tick speed. Scene-linked lines share the same ambient budget. |
| FR03 | Ordinary authored scene | Start around one companion aside per 8–12 eligible ordinary scenes, subject to FR02. | World tone 5–20%; relevance and current presence gate it. Never roll for absent animals or interrupt every craft/work result. |
| FR04 | Interaction or meaningful change | Direct interaction can respond immediately. First observed new habit, injury, arrival or absence gets a causal line at an appropriate scene. | Distinct consequential feedback can bypass ambient cooldown; unrelated flavour cannot. Acknowledge an ongoing condition in place rather than generating repeated alerts. |
| FR05 | Repetition | Suppress the same cue family for about 20–30 visible minutes unless its cause materially changes; rotate companions within the global budget. | Sparse content may need longer silence. Synonym swapping does not count as a new observation. No “blink” flood from a full menagerie. |
| FR06 | Long idle/offline interval | On return, 1–3 actual summary changes, with optional detail in a journal. | No ambient backlog replay. Runtime occurrence, detection and narration remain separate; offline policy decides what can happen. A decorative recap cannot silently create neglect, death or new history. |
| FR07 | Log/UI controls | Ambient does not steal focus, reorder action buttons or require dismissal; allow mute/filter/inspect. | Desktop/laptop first; mobile optimization deferred. Critical known care feedback belongs in a readable current state surface even with ambience off. That surface is a future proposal, not implemented here. |

**FR08 [STATED + INFERRED].** Hit-Point's official *Neko Atsume 2* announcement describes placing food/playthings and waiting for visits, plus worn toys that change cat activities (pp. 1 and 3) [V52]. It supports changing visible presence and object history; it supplies no text notification rate. *Chillquarium*'s developer store description presents real-time raising and keeping fish in a customizable aquarium [V53]. It supports ambient life as an idle pleasure, not veterinary accuracy or a measured prose cadence. C9 infers the restrained text treatment from those patterns; neither example was played during this lane.

**FR09 [INFERRED].** A future desktop taste check could compare Quiet and Lived-in in a 20-minute work session with one companion, then many, and a compressed offline return. Look for remembered habits, repeated semantics, obscured work results and missed consequential changes. A player should recognize a preference without being able to infer a hidden numeric track from fixed messages. No runtime build, browser exercise or human acceptance was performed here.
