## Build

1. **Effects on the fighter (VR01).** A condition or effect the player has observed sits on that fighter's row beside its posture. A raised guard or ward counts: today it appears as a log line ("raises guard") and nowhere else, because the row's conditions list body parts (`src/tempo_fight.js:796`). Show it as a word ("Guarding", "Warded") until it drops (MT3). Its cause appears once observed or explained. Until then it stays an open observation rather than a guessed stat.
2. **Show a hit or failure where it happened (VR04).** A hit, a miss, a broken guard or a fall marks the row it happened to, briefly. The changed state then stays in that row. A hit on you marks your own row in the left panel, and the centre log line names who was hit, so the player catches it while looking at the opponents (MO4). Keep a real retreat or yield action reachable when the fight terms allow it.
3. **Two approaches, visible reactions (VR05).** For one goal (subdue the wolf, for example), two legal approaches draw different visible reactions from the opponent, and the aftermath stays readable afterwards.
4. **The result outlasts the motion (VR19).** After the flourish, the row and Reports hold the outcome. A reward does not replay on reload.
