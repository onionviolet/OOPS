## Findings that affect implementation

F1: The novel's beginner scene supplies meaningful preparation and an attributed heat-order diagnosis but no complete recipe. Proposed quantities, clock, review and effect rules are labeled authored; a future implementation cannot claim them as complete canon.

F2: Observed cues are recipe/stage/modality-specific. A reviewed appearance is not omniscient quality or proven treatment. Tool repair restores a supported vessel, never lost contents. Batch/source/observation/custody conservation must survive cancellation and reload.

F3: Native Alchemy is aggregate instantaneous craft. Timed jobs freeze accepted outcomes and have whole-cost cancellation policies; generic partial-spent recovery, nonconsuming borrowed tool reservation, batch identity/splitting, receipt-preserving assessment and stage automation need explicit integration. Current identified-object storage is finite and has its own rules, not universal HP preservation.

F4: Canon events are source expectations. Branch occurrence depends on actual actors, means, access and causes; changed prerequisites propagate only through represented dependencies. Character knowledge, temporary body state, identity, relationships and title remain separate. A local craft success does not automatically rewrite the world economy or force a canonical ending.
