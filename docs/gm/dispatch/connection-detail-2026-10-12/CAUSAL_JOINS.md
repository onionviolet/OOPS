## J01 Workshop and shared life

**Leaves/parents:** X01-B, X02-B, X04-A, X14-A/B; MM01/02/03 and MG07.
An existing adult friend or mutually interested partner states a practical tool
need during supported conversation. The need belongs to that person, not a
relationship meter. Check title and custody before selecting diagnosis/repair;
the maker obtains actual tools/material and permission where the agreement
requires it. Unauthorized work remains physically possible when means permit,
with its separate ownership consequences. Offer ordinary repair, willing free
help, commission, extraordinary repair or decline when each route exists.

**Forward:** local order/right/body constraints → supported repair of an exact
object → that object's actual user operates it → useful performance or failed
fit changes that task → user delivers a specific report → user may propose
another commission or shared activity. Consumer: native recipe/job/material or
selected equipment function, not quality text alone. Recipient: actual tool user.
Channel: co-located trial/report, or supported delivered letter. Workshop alone
learns only what it witnesses or receives.

**Back:** user's changed workload or remaining discomfort → disclosed revised
request and availability → maker can alter the fitting, schedule a visit, enlist
an assistant or pursue friendship independently of repair. Repeated gifts do
not force romance. The person can appreciate craft and decline intimacy.

Ordinary Village uses existing stock/custody and a proposed user trial. Proto23
uses counted visitor tools and an original cottage service; source romance is
not inferred. A creator can instantaneously fix function if its exact power does
so; no invented delay preserves the repair grind. That does not imply another
person agreed to joint ownership or a date.

**Order/refusal/retention:** diagnosis precedes repair, repair precedes trial,
trial precedes performance report. Cancel before material commitment returns
only reserved inputs under the selected job; after transfer the recipient retains
received evidence. Save title/custody/job/object result and actor-addressed memory
in their actual owners. Future proof CD-P2: changed task, report, independent
friendship/refusal, restoration and no duplicate reward. MD4/5 qualify the task
and quality consumer, rather than recreating all furnishing behavior.

## J02 Research and an organization

**Leaves/parents:** X04-B, X05-A/B, X15-B; MM05/09, STP-003/013/014.
A pupil observes a disagreement between a notebook expectation and a supported
local preparation. The note records method, instrument, observer and time;
negative results are findings about that tested condition. Access, admission,
attendance, teaching and credentials are separate state.

**Forward:** school/workshop offers a particular comparison with real means →
pupil performs it and keeps result → willing mentor receives that evidence via
demonstration/notebook → revised method is taught → recipient practices with

publication, adoption and executable mastery stay separate. A claim does not
make every employee capable.

**Back:** learner's failed application or different instrument → delivered report
→ teacher revises hypothesis, supplies a different tool or declines an unsupported
assertion → learner chooses replication, an ordinary substitute or a different
question. Real insight may bypass repetition where its declared faculty does so.
If a powerful method supplies understanding and compatible execution outright,
allow that outcome rather than adding mandatory training.

pinned book grants a recipe, while our visitor handling/practice is original.
Extraordinary instruments require exact compatible target semantics; abundance
removes material expense without inventing epistemic uncertainty if the power
also resolves it. Recipient: mentor and later named learner; channels: actual
contact/instruction/demo and addressed result, not world-global knowledge.

**Order/refusal/retention:** reserve experiment inputs → execute → record result
→ communicate → willing recipient practice → consumer. Cancellation stops future

knowledge, practice/function owner, stock, school offer and specific adoption
cause. CD-P3 proves teacher absence, borrowed recipe without executable means,
negative result, corrected use and restoration. MD3/5/learning gate remain local.
