## X10: ordinary life

### X10-A: expressive clothing versus equipment

**Owner/IDs/status.** MM07 with G1-009, G1-010, G6-025, G8-017; ordinary texture G3-017 and G3-020. Original Village proposal; Proto23 visitor clothing is an optional authored extension, not source canon.

**Local rule, actors/targets and means.** Player selects a owned scarf for appearance without occupying a protective equipment slot. A coat authored as weather protection instead has an actual compatible body, custody and equipment consumer. The craftsman can notice a visible scarf using sight and remember a conversation about its maker, but cannot infer hidden armor statistics. An NPC may choose their own clothes and lend a coat only if they possess it and are willing; a friendly relationship does not move their wardrobe into player inventory. Recognition is separate from protection.

**Discovery, verb and order.** Inspect garment describes known appearance and, if tested or documented, functional properties; `wear for appearance` updates a presentation choice, `equip protection` uses native equipment. Confirm compatibility and possession before changing the prior outfit. Either action leaves the other purpose intact where the authored physical layers fit. No daily fashion meter, universal warmth tax or mandatory compliment reward.

**Consumer, channel and continuation.** Native appearance consumes the scarf selection; an authored rain/travel exposure consumer alone uses the protective coat. Only co-present observers receive the visible change. A prior gift's meaning may be referenced in conversation without stat gain. Player can change style, return a borrowed coat, repair it or remain in ordinary clothing.

**Interruption/refusal, state/save and gate.** Missing possession blocks wearing; anatomical incompatibility blocks that specific fit and may open tailoring rather than banning clothes. State owners are character presentation, actual equipment and simulation custody, not a duplicated outfit inventory. Next gate: define one cosmetic selector and one local functional garment consumer, then prove reload/import retains both and returning the borrowed coat disables only its supplied function. Existing creator/equipment surfaces need inspection before implementation.

Player owns the faculty and controls placement; ordinary craftsperson holds joinery skill and its own stock/appointments. A supplier is not aware of every creation elsewhere. Infinite capacity does not require an infinite in-memory pile: finite requested batches express the capacity, and subsequent batches remain available.

**Discovery, verb and order.** Discover the held faculty through its actual acquisition; inspect an authored known capability; `create requested beams` previews output identity, placement and ownership, then atomically publishes the finite batch. **Consumer, channel and continuation.** Reserved sound timber fulfills the named workshop material requirement and may end the old gathering loop completely. The informed buyer can now commission another use, decline more supply or take free material. Shop response follows witnessed supply, offered samples or a delivered notice. No automatic regional price crash or mandatory return to scarcity.

**Interruption/refusal, state/save and gate.** Unsupported target conversion returns an adapter diagnostic with no partial inventory or fictional nerf. Player may stop generating; NPC may refuse storage/access without blocking creation at a supported owned site. Faculty owner, generated stock, provenance and order reservation remain separate save carriers. Next gate: future extraordinary-means packet proves one real order fulfilled with created material, unchanged supplier stock, repeated creation still possible and save/reload/import conservation. Current field workshop is a recipient seam, not evidence this power exists.

**Consumer, channel and continuation.** Actual tool enables one measurement/work result, regardless of legal route when physically fit. **Interruption/refusal, state/save and gate.** Rejected free loan leaves purchase/supervised alternatives open. Failed take causes no phantom tool grant; any observed attempt has its own receipt. Save: actual stock/money, title claim, custody and actor-private witnessed incident.
