## World scope and source coverage

Ordinary context: existing Frontier/Village is an optional contact/work example,
not required before a random beginning. Named Tamsin interaction is existing
source; proposed new partners are not automatic extension of Tamsin's agreement.
Source-bounded context: Proto23 visitor cottage has original rights/residents and
local costs, with limited pinned source inspiration. It is not source protagonist
inheritance, full source romance, full map parity or source timing equivalence.
Extraordinary context: Glasshouse provides inspected lease/observation seams;
rooted actor, distributed identity and free creator scenes are original proposed
comparisons, not claims that those bodies/powers already work there.

World1, HP, D&D, parallel held books, JoJo/JJK, whole source franchises and
unusual systems retain their actual source/world owners. No new canon or hidden
criteria established here. Bodies/eras outside native bindings have named adapters,
not an artificial possibility ban. Broader arbitrary histories, universal family
generation, complete romance cultures, autonomous settlements and unconstrained
intent effects are beyond this frozen planning tranche, preserved at owners.
