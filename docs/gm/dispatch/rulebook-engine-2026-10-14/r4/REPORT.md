## Ranked open decisions for Weibao

These are ranked reachable options within each question, not global either/or
choices. Rank 1 is the recommendation for a first slice. Picking a later option
for another world remains possible. Saving this report adopts none of them.

| ID | Decision | Ranked options and cost |
|---|---|---|
| R4-Q1 | First cross-world interaction | **1.** One known place-rules craft transfer with real tools and paid persistent repair; the smallest useful proof. **2.** One translated spell expression with retained home sheet; adds source/expression and reversion work. **3.** Raw spell contact; adds cross-profile defense, damage and scale adjudication. All three modes stay available per world/zone. |
| R4-Q2 | First playable combat presentation | **1.** Timed SRD turns with frozen initiative and faithful round budgets; visible bars without changing action economy. **2.** An explicitly adapted speed-based ATB world; more idle pacing freedom, different tabletop behavior. **3.** A declared aggregate battle model for appropriate large conflicts; needs separate consequence bounds and cannot claim detailed turn parity. |
| R4-Q3 | Contrast growth fidelity | **1.** Keep BRP learn-by-use in design/tests with its original sample settlement, then inspect the full owning source before native adaptation. **2.** Author an openly named original percentile-town progression slice now; accepts an invented growth rule rather than source fidelity. **3.** Use a separately sourced WFRP-style careers contrast; distinct career/institution data and reading work. No source download or purchase is required by this note. |
| R4-Q4 | Offline combat and recovery policy | **1.** Exact bounded batching under the world's already authorized offline policy, stopping at unqueued choice or unresolved danger. **2.** Explicit automated tactical/reaction plans for a selected world, including its authored consequence policy; more intent and failure cases to test. **3.** Aggregate resolution only for declared supported encounters or independent repeatable jobs; faster, with fewer detailed consequence claims. This report grants no lethal offline behavior. |
| R4-Q5 | Profile or edition updates to visited worlds | **1.** Keep old locks/readers; give new instances the new edition. **2.** Offer a separately reviewed migration at a settled boundary with exact pending-state and rest/growth mapping. **3.** Create a distinct continuity using an explicit branch policy, preserving the original history; requires actual branch/custody support, never copied unique rewards. |
