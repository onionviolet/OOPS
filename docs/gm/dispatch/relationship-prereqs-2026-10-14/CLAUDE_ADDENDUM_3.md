## 1. Bonds as a power source (BK1-BK6)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| BK1 | **Contracts with familiars and spirits.** Default: a contract is a tie with terms (what each side gives, what breaks it). Power flows by the terms, and the bond deepens with shared action. Dial: per world how binding contracts are (D&D warlock patrons, beast tamers, Shadow Slave Echoes). | Turns "summon a beast" into a relationship with leverage both ways. |
| BK2 | **Combination arts.** Default: two people who trained together unlock joint techniques or formations; strangers cannot do them. Strength scales with shared history (memory kinds), not one axis. | Gives a mechanical payoff to old friends and comrades in combat worlds. |
| BK3 | **Vows and binding promises.** Default: in worlds with them (JJK binding vows, HP Unbreakable Vow, Naruto oaths), a promise to someone can grant power or cost a life. Breaking it is a world-rule event, not just a grievance. | Obligation rows (RQ9) become power with real risk. |
| BK4 | **Protection as motive power.** Default: some paths grow faster or hit harder when protecting a bonded person (shonen "someone to protect", Naruto Will of Fire). Dial: per world, off in worlds where emotion does not feed power. | Matches genre fantasy, and it is a world rule, not a universal bonus. |
| BK5 | **Shared or lent power.** Default: lending a technique, mana, blessing or a true name to someone you trust. They can misuse it; you can take it back where the world allows. | Trust gets a stake measured in power, and betrayal can cost you abilities. |
| BK6 | **Bond loss as a wound.** Default: when a contract or soul bond breaks (death, betrayal, release), the survivor loses its power and gains a lasting mark: grief, a scar, a changed technique. | Keeps power bonds from being free. Links grief (RD15) to progression. |

## 2. Social conflict and persuasion (BK7-BK12)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| BK7 | **Negotiation as a scene.** Default: both sides have wants, a walk-away point and leverage. Knowledge of their needs (RQ6) and ties shifts the terms. No single persuasion roll. | Trade, treaties, recruitment and pleas all reuse it, and investigating someone first pays off. |
| BK8 | **Leverage and blackmail.** Default: secrets, debts and hostages are leverage. Using it gets compliance but writes fear and grievance, and the target looks for a way out. | A dark but legitimate tool with a built-in cost, fitting LOTM and court worlds. |
| BK9 | **Debate and public argument.** Default: arguing in front of an audience moves the audience's opinion more than the opponent's. Winning can cost a tie with the loser. | Separates convincing a person from winning a crowd, which matters for reputation (part 21). |
| BK10 | **Mediation.** Default: the player can step between two NPCs in a feud. Success needs trust from both sides; failure can turn both against you. | Uses NPC-to-NPC ties (RQ11) as content. |
| BK11 | **Factions inside friend groups.** Default: groups (RQ12) have internal ties; two friends who dislike each other make group events tense, and the player may be asked to choose. Reasonably: the player can refuse to choose, at a cost. | Friend circles become living things, not lists. |
| BK12 | **Boundaries and refusal.** Default: NPCs say no based on their values, mood and limits, even to close friends, and remember being pushed. The player can refuse requests too, with no hidden penalty beyond the person's reaction. | People with limits feel real; a friend who does anything you ask is a servant. |

## 3. Rituals, milestones and shared time (BK13-BK18)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| BK13 | **Personal dates.** Default: birthdays, anniversaries of meeting, a death day. Remembering one is a bond event; forgetting a close friend's can sting. Reasonably: player notes (CQ13) help. | Time gets personal markers, and the calendar gains social meaning. |
| BK14 | **Rites of passage as group events.** Default: coming of age, graduation, promotion, weddings and funerals pull ties together and write shared memories for everyone present. Forms follow world and era (CQ16, part 30). | Big moments for many ties at once, and a natural place for news and reveals. |
| BK15 | **Traditions that form.** Default: a repeated shared act becomes a tradition with a name ("Thursday dumplings"). Keeping it holds familiarity; breaking it is noticed. | The idle-friendly version of friendship: a routine that means something. |
| BK16 | **Keepsakes.** Default: gifts and found objects from people become keepsakes with the giver's memory. Losing or selling one is noticed by the giver if they find out. | Physical continuity for ties and a carrier across lives (part 29 RCL). |
| BK17 | **Shared spaces.** Default: a regular table, a secret spot, a shared workshop. A place tied to a person triggers memories when you visit after they are gone. | Places carry relationships, which makes returning home emotional. |
| BK18 | **Collective memory.** Default: a village, house or guild remembers what you did as a group story, which outlives individual members and drifts as it is retold. | Legacy at community scale, and the bridge to renown (part 21) and heirs. |

## 4. Teaching lines and lineage (BK19-BK22)

| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| BK19 | **Technique lineages.** Default: what you learn records who taught it. A teacher's style marks you; others recognize the lineage and react (respect, rivalry, old grudges). | Mentors become history, and schools of technique feel like families. |
| BK20 | **Students outgrow you.** Default: a student can surpass, reject or betray their teacher. The tie changes kind (RM12), with pride or bitterness. | A mentor arc needs an ending. |
| BK21 | **Secret teachings need trust.** Default: a master's best method opens only at deep trust and often with an oath (BK3). | Turns trust into a progression path parallel to grinding. |
| BK22 | **Your own school.** Default: teaching many students founds a lineage that persists after you, with descendants who carry your style into later lives or worlds. | Top-end social progression that fits legacy and reincarnation. |
