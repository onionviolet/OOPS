| Code | Consideration | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| CQ1 | **Life stages fit the world and era, with one fixed floor.** Default: each world and era declares its own life-stage ladder from lore: when a child starts work or school (HP at 11, an apprentice at 12, a genin as a child soldier), when someone counts as adult in law, who may sign, inherit, serve, marry, rule, and what coming of age looks like (a ceremony, an exam, a first hunt, an awakening). Arranged betrothals, dynastic contracts and wardships can exist as legal or political ties where the lore has them. One rule does not vary by world: romance and partner verbs and any romantic or sexual content need both people to be 18 or older. A lore marriage contract made in childhood stays an offscreen contract tie until then. Updated 2026-10-14 after Weibao: "make relevant to history and time period ... fit the lore". | Child and infancy starts exist. The world-fitted ladder gives eras real texture (an adult at 14 in one world, a minor at 20 in another, for work and law). The floor sits in the shared predicate layer, so no pack can bypass it. |
| CQ16 | **Era drives custom, not just tech.** Default: a world's era sets kinship (clan, household, nuclear), who arranges matches, dowry or bride price, inheritance (eldest, all children, adoption, merit), widowhood, divorce, class mixing, courtship forms (letters, chaperoned visits, balls, matchmakers). Each is a world fact, discovered like lore (part 28 RC3). | Same verb, different meaning per era. A proposal in Regency rules and in a cultivation clan are different scenes. |
| CQ17 | **Lifespan and age shape stages.** Default: stage boundaries scale with the people's lifespan and maturity (elves, cultivators, dwarves, spirits, AIs) and with the body, not the soul: a reincarnated adult in a child's body is treated as a child by locals and by CQ1's floor. | Reincarnation and long-lived races are core to the genre. Age needs body age, soul age and apparent age as separate facts. |
| CQ18 | **Life stages change verbs, ties and how people treat you.** Default: entering a stage writes a memory and shifts what elders, peers and institutions expect: chores to work, ward to heir, student to graduate, active to elder, elder to ancestor. | Gives aging and time a felt meaning (accepted 2026-10-02 direction: time should matter). |
| CQ2 | **Recognition keys.** Default: an NPC knows you by face, voice, name, habits and, where the world has it, aura or soul. Each key can change: disguise, transformation, aging, a new body, a mask. The tie stays with the person, but a greeting depends on which keys still match. | Body swaps, LOTM masks, reincarnation and stealing a source body all need "they love you but don't recognize you". Without keys, every tie follows the save id. |
| CQ3 | **Pronouns and gender as person facts.** Default: every person, the player included, carries pronouns and a gender for that world. Worlds can add customs that read it (who may court whom, inheritance rules), as world adjectives. The shared foundations audit notes a gender field alone does none of this. | Text that names people needs it. Custom-driven reactions need it as an explicit fact, not text guesses. |
| CQ4 | **Memory compaction.** Default: when the 32-memory cap fills, old ordinary memories fold into a summary line ("many shared meals, 3 years"), while pinned core memories (danger survived, a secret kept, a betrayal) never fold. | The cap exists today. Without compaction a long friendship forgets the flood that started it to make room for lunch. |
| CQ5 | **Fidelity tiers for people.** Default: three tiers. Named people have full ties. Known faces have a light row (familiarity, one memory). Crowds have only group opinion (reputation). A person moves up a tier when the player engages. | Many worlds have hundreds of people. Tiers keep save size and tick cost flat while letting anyone become a friend. |
| CQ6 | **NPCs have their own lives.** Default: friends get jobs, move, marry other people, fall ill, get promoted, on their own schedule (part 20 MF12 extends this). Dial: per world from sleepy to busy. Reasonably: big changes arrive as news or a letter, not silently. | A friend whose life never changes is furniture. This needs NPC goals and a life-event table, which is its own prerequisite next to schedules. |
| CQ7 | **Lifespan gaps.** Default: ties between beings with different lifespans (a long-lived elf, a mortal friend of an immortal player, a dog) age at each side's own rate. A long-lived player outliving friends is expected, with grief and heirs (RX7) as the bridge. | Cultivation, immortality and reincarnation make this common, and the clock prerequisite (RQ5) has to know whose clock a tie runs on. |
| CQ8 | **Power-gap shift.** Default: as the player grows strong, fear and deference axes rise in strangers and shift old friends by temperament (RD16). Dial: per world how much power awes people. | Late-game social play changes because of progression. The writer for this is power level, which no relationship writer reads today. |
| CQ9 | **Non-person relationships.** Default: ties to an institution, a house AI (TTKS estate), a god, a world's will, a spirit or an Echo use the same rows with an owner kind field. Part 28 already treats stance toward an authority as a relationship. | One store for every "who thinks what of you" keeps views and gates simple. Needs the actor id space to allow non-person owners. |
| CQ10 | **Relationships with your other selves.** Default: past lives, echoes, copies and an AU counterpart of you can hold ties toward the player (SELVES_AND_ECHOES). | Rivalry or care toward your own echo is genre-common; the store must allow actor and target to be related selves. |
| CQ11 | **Stable outcomes on reload.** Default: social reactions draw from a seeded roll fixed per cause, so reloading does not reroll a confession or a refusal. Dial: off for players who want rerolls. | Solo player, so anti-abuse is low priority, but a "no" that changes on reload makes NPCs feel fake. Uses the existing cause receipt. |
| CQ12 | **Item tags for taste.** Default: gifts read item tags (food, flower, book, weapon, handmade, rare-in-this-world). Taste (RQ6) is a list of tags, not item ids. | Fitting gifts across many worlds and packs only works if items share tags. Items need the tags before taste can mean anything. |
| CQ13 | **Player-side notes.** Default: the player can pin a person, write a short note on their card and set an opt-in reminder ("haven't seen Maia in a season"). Settings, not character knowledge (part 22 LN). | Big casts are hard to track. This is a comfort feature and never a cheat. |
| CQ14 | **Inspection and tuning tools.** Default: a dev-only panel lists ties, memories and receipts for one person and can run a year of ticks on a test save. | Drift, compaction and passive growth are hard to tune by playing. Needed before balance work, and it doubles as acceptance evidence. |
| CQ15 | **Shared language before talk.** Default: talk verbs read a shared language fact (part 28 NS3). Gestures, gifts, shared work and danger still build ties without one. | Foreign and isekai starts get slow first friendships through actions. Part 19 dialogue assumes a shared tongue today. |

Suggested order for these: CQ1 and CQ3 before any romance or custom content, CQ4 and CQ5 before large casts, CQ12 before gift content. The rest ride with the slices they serve.

## Life stages and custom follow-up (CQ19 onward, 2026-10-14)

Weibao: "more to consider? CQ1 makes sense, make relevant to history and time period and sutff and more tho, fit the lore? More to consider? Send out codex accordingly, expand on ideas and more accordingly?"

CQ1-CQ18 above are preserved verbatim. This run owns this addendum and [part 30](../../../worlds/world-depth-2026-10-13/30_LIFE_STAGES_AND_CUSTOM.md), not part 29. The current part 29 and its audit were read first. Do not merge these blindly: RFM already covers consent, attraction, care and households; RCL covers carriers; RZ covers retention; RPW covers world-specific contact. These rows add narrower stage/custom questions and refer to those owners instead of proposing another social store.

For CQ1's wording, this later instruction is exact: the fixed 18+ floor uses **both people's body ages**. Adult soul memories, species adulthood, local legal majority, appearance, a cheat and an AU cannot lower it. Unknown body age cannot establish eligibility. Childhood contracts stay offscreen legal/political ties with no romantic content. Every other boundary fits its world/era/lore. This clarification adds no variable age floor and selects no new gameplay. Live follow-up [addendum 2](CLAUDE_ADDENDUM_2.md) DV1 additionally requires adult appearance, as recorded in User Vision and part 29 RFM6. Preserve that separate restriction too; it cannot substitute for actual 18+ body age. Appearance/deception and shared ritual proposals stay with addenda 2 and 3 rather than being duplicated here.
