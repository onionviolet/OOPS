## Questions

1. **Every reward menu and choice.** Each challenge, tribulation, arena,
   event, quest, achievement and shop where Priam or anyone chooses a
   reward: the full option list, what was picked, what was given up, and
   why. Which options are overpowered, and what makes the choice hard
   anyway (exclusivity, timing, one-time, opportunity cost)?
2. **Rewinds, time travel and farming.** Every rewind, time stasis,
   dilation, simulated trial, return-to-start or time-related power. Exactly
   what each keeps and loses, its uses, cooldowns, who can detect it, and
   every rule that stops reward farming, plus any case where repeating
   something *is* allowed.
3. **How skills work in that world.** Acquisition, levels and rarity,
   high, ideal and specialist branches, skill creation, decomposition and
   merging, skills based on real knowledge, Potential and Sun points,
   Titles and merit trees, talents, Domain, Masteries, Mind Ennoblement,
   tempering, Aura, bloodlines and race tiers. How they interact with
   Concepts and stats. A clear diagram-in-words of the whole stack.
4. **Absolute and no-exception powers.** Every perk, talent, title or item
   phrased with always, never, cannot, every, or with no stated limit. For
   each: the stated rule, any exception found later, and whether the story
   ever breaks it.
5. **Enemies' rewards and powers.** What rivals and enemies gain from the
   same systems, and how the system treats them versus Priam.
6. **Twenty or more small touches** not already in `SMALL_TOUCHES.md`.

## Output

1. A short plain-language REPORT first.
2. Tables for each question, with chapter locators and a review status.
3. Five to ten ranked proposals for `docs/LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md`
   (rewards, rewinds, skills, absolute cheats). Proposals are not decisions.
4. A CHECKPOINT so a context refresh can resume.
