## What to find

Use the lens in [LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md](../../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md),
especially "One skill, many Daos, and tiers of concepts", "Every idea
somewhere, not everywhere" and "Barriers between worlds".

1. **Concepts:** what they are, how they are sensed, gained, grown, merged
   and resisted; every tier and what changes at each; whether higher
   Concepts contain lower ones; whether one skill or Concept branches into
   several; how many people can hold one.
2. **The world and its lore:** the seven primordial Concepts and their egos,
   the eighth throne, Elysium, factions, other universes, plundering of
   young civilizations, barriers between universes. Write it as a draft
   **world card** (premise, signature laws, absences, dials, names,
   scarcity, deck, doors).
3. **The daily death:** how the revival works, what dying costs, teaches or
   changes, and its limits.
4. **Other systems:** levels, skills based on scientific knowledge, titles,
   talents, Domain, Masteries, bloodlines, Mind Ennoblement, tribulations;
   how they interact with Concepts.
5. **Cheats and enemies:** every cheat, perk or blessing, including harmful
   ones (a cheat that steals luck or hurts its holder); enemy powers and how
   the story represents them; rival champions.
6. **Limits and every way past them.**
7. **Hidden information:** what the protagonist can't see, and how he
   learns it.
8. **At least twenty small touches,** each mapped to vanilla YAIR.
