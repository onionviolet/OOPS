The two proposed manor snapshots hold 60/120 household coins and 10/20 agreed allowance coins. Requesting the allowance transfers those coins from the estate, rather than creating money. A cook order consumes two actual meat chunks and a log, pays two household coins and leaves two ordinary edible Roasted rat meat items in the cook’s stock. Hospitality transfers one prepared meal; it does not grant the raw pantry. A caretaker repair uses a log/two grass and three household coins, takes 15 played minutes and restores 25 structure points. Original prototype dials can be tuned after play.

Rain wear currently applies to the authored nest, burrow, lean-to and travel shelter. Native roofs/thermal modifiers and wet bedding are actual consumers. Weatherproofing doubles the supported rain-wear interval and reduces wetting. No wind, flood, storm-event generator, regional material depletion or general mass/volume carry system is claimed. Static native cottages keep their existing protection; no arbitrary daily repair tax was added.
