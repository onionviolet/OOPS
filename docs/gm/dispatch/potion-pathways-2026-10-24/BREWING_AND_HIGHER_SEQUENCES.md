## 0. Short answers

**PZ12 (who may brew).** In the notes an ordinary person can brew a Beyonder potion: Audrey Hall mixes her first Spectator
(Seq 9) potion in a private laboratory before she is a Beyonder (SHOWN R005-C15; her non-Beyonder status is INFERRED from it
being her first potion). The method for Seq 9 to about Seq 7 is ordered measures and stirring, with no special fire,
ceremony, spell or own spirituality (STATED Old Neil `0031`, SHOWN as a witnessed preparation R004-C13), and the rare main
ingredients must not be overdosed; above Seq 7 the notes show ritual steps tied to the drink, not a brewing skill.

**Higher Sequences in one line.** Each rung up swaps skill for scarcity, then scarcity for an authored ritual, then ritual for
a world-scale or seat-scale condition (Seq 5 set piece, Seq 4 mythical form and neighbour swap, Seq 3 to 1 world acts, Seq 0
Uniqueness). Rungs below Seq 7 are where almost all of the notes' formulas, prices and institutional rules sit.
