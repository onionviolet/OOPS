# Wider gameplay remaining acceptance and breadth

The selected candidate is one connected early book-world site loop. Source identity and discoveries remain sealed. These are retained proposals or director gates, not assigned extra runtime waves.

| Code | Remaining consideration | What would make it playable |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Wider ability families: summons, transformations, mental influence, temporal and conceptual/authority functions; copying, theft, teaching and inheritance | Source-qualified acquisition, actual compatible targets and effects, limits/counters and NPC/player symmetry. Classification alone supplies no mechanics. |
| C2 | Further death continuations: heirs, inherited belongings, afterlives, resurrection or soul/body separation | Explicit local source support and actual carriers/recipient consent/custody. The delivered continuation is an independent archived new run, with no automatic inheritance. |
| C3 | Deeper dungeons and repeat visits | Authored topology, varied obstacles/occupants, encounter consequences, depletion/reset rules and changed reasons to return. Current finite site cannot refill its reward by re-entering or reloading. |
| C4 | Economy and local aftermath | Earned resources fund preparation; supplies have owners and finite stock; reports create actual follow-up work. Wider faction/NPC routines need supported clocks and knowledge, rather than universal omniscience. |
| C5 | Director fiction, balance, pacing and shared integration | Play the isolated candidate and reconcile it with the other active candidate under one writer. No public release, commit, push or shared-runtime integration occurred. |

Offline death/decay, arbitrary source/world combinations, full procedural dungeons and broader ability/death families are not delivered by this slice. Preserve these dependencies instead of advertising a universal finished engine.
