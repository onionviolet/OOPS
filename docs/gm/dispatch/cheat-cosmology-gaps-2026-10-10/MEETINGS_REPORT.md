# Section M: when cheats meet

Section M is complete as a documentation gap pass. The 33 comparisons cover all 28 distinct pairs of the eight named backings, plus five hard cases. The framework describes possible results, but it does not yet select a consistent result for a real collision. No crossover winner below is a source feat. All crossover outcomes are inferred (I), conditional proposals.

**F1:** Temporal clashes lack a shared event order and a record of what is recreated or carried. Calling two time cheats “cancel” is an example, not a resolution method.

**F2:** A backing predicts a carrier or dependency, not target eligibility, ownership or attack success. Portability, activation, contact and effect need separate checks.

**F3:** The strongest useful counterexamples are local: Fourth Wall does not fully block a foreign gaze, Gluttony fails to eat one destructive power, and soulbound does not prevent temporary lending. Meta, devour and item labels cannot decide a winner.

Everything added is a proposal. No design rule was accepted, no gameplay was changed, and no build, browser test, commit or source-library write was performed.

## Scope and method

Read AGENTS.md, PROJECT.md, the GM prompt, all of the starting CHEAT_COSMOLOGY.md and CHEATS_BY_WORLD.md, the required CHEAT_LIBRARY family/enemy sections, and the north-star sections on placement, meetings, observation and tropes. Shown means a narrated operation or failure explicitly described in our derived notes, or a fact visible in inspected game data. It does not mean this pass reread the novel or witnessed gameplay. Stated means source testimony, catalogue assertion or an untested rule report. Inferred (I) means this pass's proposal. U qualifiers remain; source certainty is not improved by appearing in this table. No new general franchise knowledge (G) was needed. No external matchup claims are used.

Home A and Home B follow the order in Pair. M27 shares one home and checks it once for both holders. “RI” is Reverend Insanity in Cicada/Gu rows, and Renegade Immortal only in the ring/bead rows. “Paradise” always names the specific work in that row; the two Paradises are not presumed connected.

**Neutral world scenario:** an original ordinary world, no imported loop template, no declared system or patron reach, no special converter. Actual bodies, held items and known carrier-held memories arrive by a valid route. This does not supply Gu essence, spirituality, aether, patron permission or target souls. An unsupported/Blocked power produces no collision. We describe that as “ignore” for table convenience, but record it as inactive, not a feat of mutual immunity. Conditional powered outcomes below apply only after the card proves activation and contact. Native availability in a home does not extend the visitor's reach. No home receives an automatic win.

**Equalization (EQ):** None uses those conditions. Reread and Hub standard relabel claims; they do not inherently grant fuel, target eligibility or authority. Treaty/Rechannel options can map a specified resource or target. Sync/Normalize may change measured magnitude or seal a named cheat if the local rule explicitly says so. Proportional/By feats/Absolute require a supplied comparison scheme; none by itself establishes precedence between resets or ownership claims. Each row names the specific extra equalization choice that could change its result. If EQ seals both cheats, the collision is absent; that is a selected world rule, not intrinsic cancel.

## Framework gaps and disputes

**F1 temporal semantics, no answer:** M04, M10, M14–15, M19, M23–25 and M33 lack event ordering, reset domain, carrier identity, template inclusion and retained state. Cicada is a will journey through a native mechanism; MoL recreates under a marker/template regime; dilation changes rate. “Two time cheats in one room” does not establish cancel. MoL's orb-in-Black-Room resource failure is a real meeting limit, not a universal time incompatibility.

**F2 eligibility and activation, no answer:** M03, M07–13, M18–20, M29 and M31–33 cannot decide contact or target type from a backing. Living avatars, shadows, corpses, divinity, robots, souls and registered items are not interchangeable nouns. Carried soul does not prove present fuel, network reach or readable samples. Gray fog has a place/anchor question that its concept-like label does not settle.

**F3 competing selectors, two answers:** the north star at `docs/LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md:2879` describes Override as higher tier/deeper origin winning, while the cosmology at original lines 24–37 treats scope relationally and tier words as vocabulary, and its intake says case by case. These are competing proposal selectors, not an accepted contradiction in PROJECT.md. M15 can get automatic Cancel from the illustrative time row, or Override from a tier reading, without either showing how the mechanisms touch. M22 could get meta Override, system Devour, or mechanism-specific Counter. M29 could be two successful harvest claims or mutual Devour; none follows from tier alone. M30 could privilege home patron or deeper origin without any grant terms. Keep the conflict visible, not resolved by an invented rank.

**F4 observation and outcome taxonomy, source conflict:** M07/M22/M28 show why Counter can be partial and axis-specific. Fourth Wall's foreign-gaze anti-feat prevents general meta-immunity reasoning. Observation, registered identity, copying, understanding and executing are separate. “Ignore” currently conflates separate effects, blocked activation and lack of contact. “Translate” conflates a label, a usable fuel converter and actual acquisition. “Merge” and “Devour” require a mechanism rather than common family names. No source supports forcing every meeting into one mutually exclusive outcome; contact may be countered on one axis while payment/trade succeeds on another.

**F5 grant/custody terms, no answer and one source conflict:** M08/M18/M30–32 separate possession, benefit, ownership, recall, return and destruction. The cosmology's generic Bound item line at original line 322 says it cannot be traded/refunded/exported. It is a Paradise shop example, not a rule for Nick's flask: the flask actually lends benefit and returns after separation. E11 does not contradict that scoped example, but would contradict applying the line to all soulbound items. Neither a donor unable to revoke a gift nor a returning flask proves third-party abstract theft impossible. Amon's row is too thin to arbitrate that contest.

**F6 composite backings and evidence versions, no answer:** M02/M31 and the outside audit need stage/function-level dependencies. The AI Chip appears as Item in the backing examples at original line 48 but Soul in its own card at line 100, where its cross-body continuity is the reason. Paradise extraction stages and passive retention differ. A single label erases the data the meeting needs. New reported evidence should update a specific scenario; no retroactive canon synthesis is implied here.

**No asserted crossovers:** there are zero documented fights between the exact foreign pairs. The observed operations constrain proposals but do not prove a crossover winner. Trade, Merge, Infect and Cascade remain reachable options when authored means exist; inventing one row for each vocabulary word would not make it source-supported. The native time-dilation failure in E07 is the nearest specific cascade example used here.
