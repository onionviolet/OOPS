## Part 1: far-field genres

Study genres far from progression fantasy and pull out mechanics a text
life sim could use. For each: core loop, two or three concrete mechanics
(with a named game as source), and how it maps onto YAIR and the north
star. At least:
- dating sims and visual novels (affection, routes, flags, calendars,
  gifts, confession timing, multiple endings; e.g. Tokimeki Memorial,
  Persona social links, Stardew Valley hearts, Monster Prom, Hades);
- farming and cozy sims (Stardew, Harvest Moon, Animal Crossing);
- management and tycoon (hospital, restaurant, theme park);
- deckbuilders and autobattlers;
- social deduction and diplomacy (Werewolf, Diplomacy, Blood on the
  Clocktower);
- detective and deduction (Return of the Obra Dinn, Shadows of Doubt);
- rhythm, puzzle and time-loop puzzle games (Outer Wilds, Majora's Mask);
- narrative roguelites (Hades, Inscryption, Slay the Princess);
- pet and creature raising (Tamagotchi, Monster Rancher, Pokemon);
- city builders and colony sims (Frostpunk, Banished);
- tabletop RPGs (Blades in the Dark clocks and factions, Apocalypse World
  moves, Ironsworn oracles), board games (Root, Arkham Horror);
- incremental and idle games not yet audited (Cookie Clicker, Antimatter
  Dimensions, NGU Idle, Kittens Game, Trimps);
- life sims (BitLife, The Sims, Princess Maker, Long Live the Queen).

## Part 2: Royal Road and web-novel tropes, with variations

From Royal Road tags and best-rated lists, NovelUpdates tags, and
r/ProgressionFantasy discussions: list the 25 to 40 most popular tropes and
premises (for example running a sect, kingdom building, dungeon core, base
building, monster evolution, reincarnated as an object, crafting, tower
climbing, system apocalypse, academy, cozy shop, healer, villainess,
regression, time loop, summoned hero, NPC who gains awareness). For each:
1. what readers love about it;
2. how it usually goes;
3. **three to five different ways to do the same trope** (subversions,
   inversions, mixing with another trope, different scale, a different
   point of view);
4. how it fits the life sim (an origin, a path, a world card, an event, a
   cheat).

## Part 3: ideas we haven't had

Twenty or more original ideas not already in the north star, each one
paragraph, each tied to a section of the north star.

## Output

1. A short plain-language REPORT first.
2. Part 1 table, Part 2 trope catalog with variations, Part 3 idea list.
3. Five to ten ranked proposals. Proposals are not decisions.
4. A CHECKPOINT.
