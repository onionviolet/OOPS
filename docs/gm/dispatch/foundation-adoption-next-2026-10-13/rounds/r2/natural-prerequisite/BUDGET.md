# R2 natural acquisition finite budget

Chosen route: native crafting of Healing balm. Starting point is the R1 literal-file earned export (95 Rough wood logs, 102 coins, field clearance, naturally acquired tools). No synthetic stock, inventory, history, RNG or clocks.

Before opening merchant stock or making new RNG attempts, limits are:

- Up to 12 naturally elapsed village stock cycles to obtain Medicine for dummies and Butchering and you, each stock rolls at 20%. Up to 7200 played woodcutting minutes to earn sale funds, while retaining three logs for the parent rough-kit route.
- Up to 120 actual combat minutes across at most 30 forest groups to unlock herbalism, with ordinary recovery and owned equipment. Stop on unmanageable combat; no stat edits.
- At most 1800 played herbalism minutes for 10 Oneberry and 5 Golmoon per planned balm attempt.
- At most 8 native search-for-boars attempts if its prerequisite becomes ordinarily accessible, and at most 15 boar groups or 120 combat minutes. Existing gated prerequisites receive bounded inspection; no new quests fabricated.
- At most 10 native Animal fat crafting attempts and at most 10 native Healing balm crafting attempts. Stop when one actual Healing balm is obtained.

Book reading uses the ordinary UI for the declared native 420/240 minutes. Playwright timer advancement drives registered native timers only; no native clock field is assigned. Material checkpoint exports are preserved. A budget ending is a route-specific blocker, not proof that all acquisition routes or the chain are externally blocked.
