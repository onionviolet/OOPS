Rain dots the village path.
Medium-sized village, built at the foot of the mountains, with rocks preventing any expansions towards north. It's surrounded by many fields, most of them infested by huge rats. There is a relatively calm, somewhat small river south of it with no bridges over it, and with some old structures on the other side, clearly not used for years if not longer. Many villagers seem to have nothing to do and are just sitting around.
