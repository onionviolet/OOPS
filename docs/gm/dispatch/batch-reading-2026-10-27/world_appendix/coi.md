World: Circle of Inevitability (English source, the Lord of the Mysteries sequel; one continuous chapter numbering, always book 1). Id prefix `coi`.

- Locators are `en b1 cN pM`: chapter N from the chapter header, paragraph M from the `pM:` line. The card's `zh 第N章 pM` form does not apply. The edition skips the number 1026; there is no gap in its text.
- The `zh` field holds the name exactly as printed in English (French-style given names and Church titles included). Titles, aliases, Sequence names and Divination or tarot names a person uses are `aliases` of that person.
- Money and value: record the unit exactly as printed; a stated count is an `amount`. Do not convert.
- Kinds: Beyonder Pathways and Sequences (a Sequence number with its name), potion formulas, abilities, rituals and Mystery Sealed Artifacts are `rules` rows (kind `ability`, `rite` or `spell`, the book's own term in `worldKind`); churches, Warlock and Beyonder societies and states are `organization`; Sealed Artifacts and Beyonder characteristics are census `item`; cities, ruins and spirit-world places are `place`; spirits and monsters use census kind `creature`.
- Pathway and Sequence are stated facts: record them as printed with the locator, never inferred from an ability shown. A Beyonder's Sequence changes over time, so each statement is its own row.
- Lead: Lumian Lee (`pov: lead`). Chapters seen through another person are not lead scenes; record their people, places and facts in census, people and texture.
- In a scene's `touched`, an ability, ritual or spell uses its rule kind (`ability`, `rite`, `spell`), never `rules`.
