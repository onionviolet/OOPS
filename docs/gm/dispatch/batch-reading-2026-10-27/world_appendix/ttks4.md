World: TTKS B4, 全人类的继承者 (ttks.tw capture, traditional script). Chinese source, one continuous chapter numbering, always book 1. Id prefix `ttks4`.

- Locators are `zh 第N章 pM`: chapter N from the shard header (renumbered from the capture's own numbering), paragraph M from the `pM:` line. The `en` form does not apply.
- The mirror's cache notice is already removed. Copy `zh` and `aliases` exactly as printed, in whichever script the chapter uses; the same person in both scripts is one id with the other form as an alias.
- Money, cultivation or system values and counts: record the unit and number as printed, never converted. A stated count is an `amount`.
- Kinds: system panels, entries, abilities, techniques, spells and rites are `rules` rows (kind `ability`, `spell` or `rite`, the book's own term in `worldKind`); sects, orders, schools and factions are `organization`; items and artifacts are census `item`; places are `place`; beasts and monsters use census kind `creature`.
- Ranks, realms and levels are stated words or numbers: record them as printed with the locator, never inferred from power shown.
- Lead: the protagonist of the opening chapters (`pov: lead`), named as the text prints it. Chapters seen through another person are not lead scenes; record their people, places and facts in census, people and texture.
- In a scene's `touched`, an ability, spell or rite uses its rule kind (`ability`, `rite`, `spell`), never `rules`.
