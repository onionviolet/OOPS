World: A Soldier's Life (English source; one continuous chapter numbering across books 1-6, always book 1). Id prefix `asl`.

- Locators are `en b1 cN pM`: chapter N from the chapter header (N runs 1-384 across the six books), paragraph M from the `pM:` line. The card's `zh 第N章 pM` form does not apply.
- The `zh` field holds the name exactly as printed in English. Ranks, titles and nicknames a person earns are `aliases` of that person.
- Money: record units as printed (copper, silver, gold; a stated count is an `amount`). Wages, fines and prices are census or texture facts with the unit, never converted.
- Kinds: essences, skills, spells, mage disciplines and the lead's dimensional ability are `rules` rows (kind `ability`, `spell` or `rite`, the book's own term in `worldKind`); essence types and gear are census `item`; legion companies, houses, duchies and guilds are `organization`; cities, estates and dungeons are `place`; monsters and beasts use census kind `creature`.
- Ranks and tiers are stated words or numbers (legionnaire ranks, mage and essence tiers): record them as printed with the locator, never inferred from power shown.
- Lead: Eryk (`pov: lead`; first person). Epilogues, "POV" chapters and interludes told through another person are not lead scenes; record their people, places and facts in census, people and texture.
- In a scene's `touched`, an ability, spell or rite uses its rule kind (`ability`, `rite`, `spell`), never `rules`.
