World: Shadow Slave (English source; one continuous chapter numbering, always book 1). Id prefix `ss`.

- Locators are `en b1 cN pM`: chapter N from the chapter header, paragraph M from the `pM:` line. The card's `zh 第N章 pM` form does not apply.
- The `zh` field holds the name exactly as printed in English. Titles and True Names a person earns are `aliases` of that person.
- Money and value: record the unit as printed in English (credits, coins, soul shards). Soul shards are also a progression resource; a stated count is an `amount`.
- Kinds: Aspects, Flaws, abilities and runes are `rules` rows (kind `ability`, the book's own term in `worldKind`); the Nightmare Spell's trials are `event`; Memories and Echoes are census `item` or `creature` rows with the book's term in the note; Nightmare Creatures use census kind `creature` with their stated rank and class in the note; clans and the Academy are `organization`; the Dream Realm and waking-world cities are `place`.
- Ranks are stated words (Sleeper, Awakened, Ascended and so on for people; rank and class for creatures): record them as printed with the locator, never inferred from power shown.
- Lead: Sunny (`pov: lead`). Chapters seen through another person are not lead scenes; record their people, places and facts in census, people and texture.
- In a scene's `touched`, an ability or rite uses its rule kind (`ability`, `rite`, `spell`), never `rules`.
