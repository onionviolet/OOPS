# Batch reading: reflection on the first live rounds (2026-10-28)

Written by the Opus coordinator before any new batch. Evidence is [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) sections 6a and 8 (shard `w3s33`, chapters 94-96, n=1), the runner in `scripts/lead_run/batch_*.mjs` and `tests/lead_run_batch.test.js`. Counts and dollars only; no source text. Facts are tagged [S] seen in the files or live results, [I] inference.

| Code | Issue found | Fix or open decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I1 | **Strict structured output has hard caps** (24 optional properties, 16 union-typed, plus an undocumented compiled-grammar size). Three live rejections at $0. [S] | Census schema compiles and runs strict. The scene schema cannot be strict even alone, so scenes and any combined call use `schemaMode: "prompt"` (schema in the system text, JSON parsed and linted in code). Decided; recorded in 6a. Cost of prompt mode: no grammar guarantee, so parse and lint failures go through the repair path (1 repair request per shard, never a third, AU13). |
| I2 | **Low effort under-writes.** Census at low: 119 rows, new-entity recall 0.63; scenes at low: 6 scenes, 0 options, 0 choices, grade C. Medium census: 24 of 27 new entities (0.89) for $0.0112. [S] | Effort is the recall lever, not the model (Sonnet at low also under-wrote: 20 rows). Census default moves to medium. Scenes A/B (PLAN 6a, round 3): medium kept 3 of 6 choices and graded C; **high** matched the agent run (6 choices, 6 of 7 options) at grade B for $0.0105. Scenes go to high. |
| I3 | **The metric was wrong.** 99 of the agent run's 126 census rows are glossary names the prompt already supplies; only 27 are new entities. Recall over all rows rewards copying the table. [S] | Gate on **new-entity recall** (reference rows not in the glossary), reader rows only, reported next to the all-row figure. `batch_compare.mjs --plan <plan>` now prints it (exact names: low 0.44, medium 0.78). The old 0.9 all-row gate stays as a secondary number. |
| I4 | **Single combined call rejected.** With scenes to write, output goes to scenes and the model drops census and option rows: 35 census rows, 0 options, grade C. [S] | Two-pass shape stands. Not retried. |
| I5 | **Script fill of known names** (`addMissingKnown`) lifts recall cheaply but (a) the rows are not reader observations, and (b) it produced 6 duplicate ids that made the medium run grade C. [S] | The fill now skips ids the reader already used (fixed before this round). Reader-only recall is reported separately so the fill cannot hide a weak reader. 2 duplicates remained offline; this round checks whether they come from the fill or from the reader and fixes the cause. |
| I6 | **Batches can overrun the workspace spend limit** (high concurrency), so the Console limit is a soft stop. [S, docs] | Keep `--max-usd` on every submit, size each batch to its cap, and submit LOTM volume 1 in waves of about 10 shards (about $0.15 a wave), not 31 at once. A per-label go from Weibao stays required. |

The authoritative cost record is `<label>_usage.csv`, not the Console. Weibao's check: Console, Usage, switch the workspace picker to the key's workspace and widen the date range. |
