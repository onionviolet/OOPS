2026-10-14. Writer: R8. Research and proposals only. This lane changes no owner, gameplay, bundle, save, commit or push. [COMMON](../COMMON.md) governs scope. [R1](../r1/REPORT.md) V01–V15 governs publication/ending status. The 15 roster entries below are exactly R1's list, with TTKS's five titles separated internally. ## E00: findings and evidence contract

**E00a. Separate office, power, and reach.** Minister, captain, headmaster, bishop, master artisan and landowner give different rights. Canon peak fighters do not automatically own the state. QE1's village → multiverse chain is an optional escalation shape, not a universal career ladder.

**E00b. Faith has three distinct meanings.** Religious office, public belief and supernatural authority may combine or conflict. A religion can be native even where worship grants no power. LOTM apotheosis is native to its rules and remains conditional; it is not automatically a cheat because it exceeds a mortal's earlier ceiling.

**E00c. Borrow light decision loops, not whole simulations.** The requested strategy games often compress economic work but retain demanding combat or emergency management. Majesty's incentives, Kingmaker's assignments and Dwarf Fortress's stock conditions are useful patterns; none proves unattended YAIRPG play.

**Evidence labels:** **Shown** = directly observed source/publication/code artifact or a scene explicitly checked by an existing source review. **Stated** = author/developer/wiki account, with attribution. **Inferred** = this report's feasibility interpretation. **Proposal** = a YAIRPG design recommendation. **U** = evidence missing. Reading a digest does not mean reading its entire source. Most public world facts here are Stated secondary accounts, not a fresh primary canon audit. Indexed wiki snippets are identified as such and not upgraded by an inaccessible page.

**Support labels:** **N** = native means/institution exists, although this player's access is conditional; **A** = an ordinary alternate history or authored continuation is needed, without necessarily a power cheat; **C** = a new exceptional power/source override is needed; **X** = the specific interpretation has no coherent fit under the selected profile; **U** = source coverage cannot certify it. X is scoped to an interpretation, never a ban on an entire personal goal. Every playable path below is **Inferred/Proposal**, even when its institution is Shown or Stated. Native support is not evidence of a canon character following that exact career or guaranteed success.

Web URLs were accessed or indexed excerpts inspected **2026-10-14**. Local source reviews carry their own 2026-10-09–14 dates. Failed direct Fandom fetches switched to indexed evidence/local reviewed records; no repeated URL variants. No source books were acquired. The original Black & White manual was downloaded to temporary storage for read-only review. No plot-outcome narration or altered sealed truth is needed. Seeker targets use route or ID only.

Path keys: **R** ruler (EGR); **W** war (EGW); **F** faith (EGF); **M** mastery (EGM); **O** founder (EGO1); **Xr** explorer (EGO2); **S** seeker (EGO3); **E** escapee (EGO4); **L** legacy (EGO5); **Q** resolver (QE4). ## E80–E89: smallest later-layer slice for each path
