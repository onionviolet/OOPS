## Mother of Learning: qualification for NP2

**Stated, secondary:** the [time-loop reference](https://mother-of-learning.fandom.com/wiki/Time_loop) describes repeated material copies generated from a base template in a bounded separate domain, with selected souls/markers carried between iterations. It explicitly distinguishes this from ordinary travel back through the original world's time. **Shown/stated in primary text:** [chapter 55, Threshold](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/21220/mother-of-learning/chapter/315711/55-threshold) describes construction from a base template, changes to that template, and resource use charged per iteration rather than simply per elapsed day. The more detailed domain explanation here is secondary-supported, not a complete primary-source audit of every exception.

**Inferred mapping:** NP2 accurately captures the player's repeated reset experience, but not a literal erasure of the original world's future. This is better treated as a world-specific reset/iteration adapter with its own topology and retained carriers. It is not NP1's indefinitely preserved, playable old branches; it is not NP4's unrelated alternate premise either. The four NP labels are a useful palette, not an exhaustive source ontology. “MoL style loops” in NP2 should be qualified as an operational analogy.

The implementation consequence is to name template, current iteration, outside domain and actual retention separately. Do not implement this explanation by overwriting a generic world record and assuming all outside objects, observers and clocks roll back. Exact exit, exceptions and power transfer belong to the source pack's own deeper verification, not this report.

## Re:Zero: evidence scope, not a resolved theory

**Stated by an in-world character in a fan translation:** [web novel Arc 4 chapter 74, part 2](https://translationchicken.com/2018/04/06/rezero-arc-4-chapter-74-the-witchs-plan-and-proposal-part-2-3/) describes the Trial's alternate presents as imagined constructs and gives competing explanations of Return by Death. It does not verify that the imagined post-death presents are real continuing worlds. This is web-novel evidence at that point, not proof about every later light-novel/anime revelation.

**Inferred design lesson:** store the character's uncertain model separately from the author-selected mechanism. A forecast, Trial or imagined aftermath is not automatically a branch save. NP2-like experience can be represented without spoiling or falsely resolving the actual world's law.
