## M000: scope and evidence

This is a content palette feeding part 22 sections 8–12 and its section 22, using [part 36's MC47 registry](../../../../worlds/world-depth-2026-10-13/36_MICRO_MOMENTS_AND_SMALL_SIGNALS.md). It adds **94 world details across R1's 15 worlds**, then **10 cross-world proposals** and **three first-build candidates**. TTKS has ten rows because it is five distinct titles; the other worlds have six each. Reincarnation Paradise's later priority addition does not silently change this assigned roster.

**Shown** means a scene or rule inspected directly in a primary source here. **Stated** means an official explanation, retained wiki revision, character statement or located private reading record reports it. A private note saying a scene was shown is still **Stated here** unless this lane inspected that primary scene. **Inferred** means a presentation inference from that basis, not another canon event. Cross-world additions and all implementation choices are **proposals**, not canon or accepted design. Each row cites its basis; [SOURCES](SOURCES.md) records route, access date and scope. Outcome-bearing scene identities and references are in [SPOILERS](SPOILERS.md), with no changed/randomized secret truth.

Q = work offered/taken; D = recording, handling or mourning death; N = a permitted return or its recognition boundary; E = ordinary aftermath. The fact in a row may support selecting one line of text, but does not prove that line exists in the source. Never invent a canonical guild, obituary form, soul detector, register or rite to fill a slot.

**First-day rule:** use an immediate aftermath scene if it exists. When only a later epilogue or continuing institution is located, the proposed first ordinary day is **Inferred**, and its exact date is **unverified**. R1's publication/continuity findings are inherited from [its report](../r1/REPORT.md), not re-audited here. An ongoing source gets an arc aftermath, not a manufactured series ending.

Every cue must read a true fact available to the current observer, fit one line or one small visual change, and obey a per-signal cooldown adjective and priority. A report arriving is not a death becoming true. NPC access to a fact is not player foreknowledge. No generic repetition of MC1–MC46 or MDQ/MDD/MDN/MDE/MDB rows is proposed below: these are source-specific instruments, permissions and practices.
