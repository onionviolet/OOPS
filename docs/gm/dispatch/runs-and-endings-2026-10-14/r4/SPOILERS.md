Costs include prerequisites, loss, dependence and failure risks, not just currency. `None stated` and `unknown` remain honest entries; never manufacture a balancing penalty.

## WN11 / WR11: Harry Potter

**Death routes:** shown/stated magical ghosts are lingering imprints of departed witches/wizards, not restored living bodies; ordinary Muggles do not become them. The beyond-death destination is acknowledged but not a mapped explorable realm. Horcruxes sustain a maimed soul after bodily loss; reconstruction of such an existing survivor is not fetching any dead person back. The Resurrection Stone gives incomplete temporary presence. A Dementor's Kiss consumes a soul while leaving a vegetative body; this does not establish where the consumed soul goes or a proven repair route. Portraits imitate/trainedly represent their subjects, not a demonstrated soul store.
