# R4: per-world death and next-life extension

2026-10-14. Docs-only research and proposals. No accepted default, engine change or gameplay proof. This is a bounded insertion packet for [SUCCESSION_BY_WORLD](../../../../worlds/SUCCESSION_BY_WORLD.md), not a second succession owner. COMMON.md reserves integration to Claude; only this lane was written. All 15 requested world groups are covered, with TTKS split into B1-B5 and LOTM/COI distinguished by era.

The later request asks about death followed by a new life later or earlier in the same world. [Continuation P4/C1-C5](../../../../worlds/CONTINUATION_CHOICE_CONSIDERATION_2026-10-13.md) already separates campaign selection from transporting a soul, memory or body. Keep that rule. The existing succession table's heirs/material-inheritance columns remain useful and should stay; extend its thin death/recognition cells with this packet rather than copy the table.
