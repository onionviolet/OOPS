# Investing, property management and more side activities


Weibao requested: "minigames like investing, property management, otehr stuff
and more>". This extends the minigame/settlement inventory above. The named
interest is user direction; the loops below are proposals, with no runtime
implementation or build selection implied. These can be ongoing pursuits,
not only short skill checks. Each world supplies its own institutions and
available means; stocks, banks, deeds and tenant law are not universal.

### MG01 Investing is a judgment loop

Start with one identifiable enterprise: what it does, who runs it, what it
needs, what the player receives and when. Separate a loan, ownership share,
purchase of future goods and outright patronage. They create different rights
and obligations, rather than four labels for the same interest timer.

The player sees quotations, accounts, rumors and inspected evidence their
character can obtain. An appraisal may reveal a broken machine or an unreliable
delivery schedule; it need not reveal a hidden outcome or perfect future price.
If the player can actually repair the machine, teach a worker or arrange a
delivery, intervention changes the enterprise's inputs. A supported foresight
power may reveal more under its real rules, rather than receiving an arbitrary
economic exception. NPC investors use their own knowledge and means too.

Returns follow the enterprise's recorded sales, costs and agreement. Do not
generate unrelated price noise solely to make a graph move. Liquid holdings,
unsold goods, promised repayments and ownership are separate. Multiple
investors divide one real distributable amount, not duplicated payouts.
Markets with tradable shares are a world-specific extension, not a prerequisite.

### MG02 A property is a place, with people and uses

Separate ownership, permission to occupy, permission to alter, and permission
to lease. Buying a building need not grant all four in every world. Its repair
state, rooms, utilities, contents and location should affect actual uses.
Tenants have budgets, preferences, relationships and choices; they do not exist
only as rent multipliers. A cheap room may house an apprentice, a converted
room may become a workshop, and personal use can be valuable without rental
income. Complaints or arrears offer fitting actions such as repair, negotiation,
extension or a change of use. Problems occur from authored or simulated causes,
not a universal punishment attached to owning something.

The scale can grow from one room to a building, estate or district, retaining
the same occupants and accounts. A rent roll is a projection of those records.
Heirs receive the surviving asset and rights under the local inheritance rules;
travel does not automatically carry a deed's authority into another world.

### One connected first-slice candidate

Proposal: a local workshop needs a repair and offers either a loan agreement
or a share of its actual sales. The player can inspect it, fund it, contribute
their own repair work if capable, or pass. Later, one neighboring room can be
used personally, leased as workspace or offered as lodging. The workshop's
output and room's occupancy connect both activities without requiring a stock
exchange or whole-city simulation. This does not make Frontier a required start;
the eventual host must support the institutions and be selected separately.

Before implementation, define the actual host, enterprise/occupant records,
quoted commitments, clock and away-time policy, settlement transactions,
delegation limits and old-save defaults. Use center actions and existing panels
first. Routine operation should have a supported idle route; consequential
choices remain within player-set instructions. Save/reload/import must preserve
ownership, liabilities, stock and due payments without rerolling outcomes or
paying twice. Browser acceptance should prove fund/pass, contribution of actual
materials, one settlement, a repair/use/tenant choice and delegation within its
budget. Those checks are proposed and not run.
