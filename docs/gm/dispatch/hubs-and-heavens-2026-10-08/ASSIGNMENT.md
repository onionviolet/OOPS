## Questions

Answer with book, locator and a status (stated, shown, inferred, unknown):

1. **Rival hubs.** Every hub-like organization (paradises, sacred lands,
   nebulae, sects that send people between worlds). How they differ in rules,
   ranks, rewards, shops and obligations. How people join, leave, switch or
   defect, and what it costs.
2. **Hub society.** Other contractors as teammates, rivals, merchants and
   enemies. Rankings, reputation, guilds, contracts, debts, quotas.
3. **Hubs competing over one world.** Races, sabotage, alliances, betrayals,
   shared or contested missions, and how a world's outcome is scored.
4. **World stance toward outsiders.** Support, neutrality, suppression,
   rejection, a leash (help that claims you), expansion. What triggers each,
   how it is felt (luck, power caps, tribulations, hunters), how it changes,
   and every way past suppression.
5. **Heavens' chosen and fate.** How a world picks favorites, how luck or
   fate is taken or stolen, and what happens to a favorite who loses it.
   Check against draft D2a in `docs/LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md`.
6. **Expansionist worlds.** Worlds that send their people to invade, plunder
   or settle, and how the home world is defended.
7. **Small touches:** at least twenty, each mapped to vanilla YAIR.
