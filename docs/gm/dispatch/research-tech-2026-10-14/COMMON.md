  either/or. Solo player: no fixed "once" anti-abuse limits; limits are

- Unbiased lens: label shown / stated / inferred, report contradictions.
- Cite sources (URL or file:line) for factual claims; mark unverified.
- Style: plain active English, no em dashes, short tables, at most five
  ranked items in any action list.

you go), topic files named in your prompt, and `REPORT.md` last: what you
