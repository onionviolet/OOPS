## Ranked suggestions

1. **Q41: one anomaly with a revisable explanation.** Extend G4 through Q10/Q13/Q33/Q34. An ordinary action records a dated observation and known conditions; one fitting follow-up can support or revise the interpretation and change one useful action. Likely fits the notebook/evidence owners, pending T1 confirmation. Proposed acceptance: actual observation and follow-up receipts, no pre-discovery answer, retained disagreement and useful consumer.
2. **Q42: one useful reread and attributed study result.** Use Q14/Q15/Q31/Q35 to distinguish a held book, completed comprehension and a repeated study output. A known text could help prepare one real workshop test or teach an eligible partner without rewarding arbitrary repeated reading. Proposed acceptance: acquisition, first reading, reread, spend/retain boundaries and one actual recipient or craft use.
3. **Q43: one expedition sample feeding a bounded lab routine.** Use Q5/Q8/Q22/Q36/Q38 for a sample → known test → report chain. The routine stops at exhausted stock or an ambiguous decision and names why; tools and time remain real. Proposed acceptance: natural acquisition, sample consumption, paid result, interruption/resume and an output consumer. No parallel background actor or new scheduler is presumed.
4. **Q44: one method transmitted to one local user.** Use Q17–Q20/Q35/Q37 to connect a written account, teaching or demonstration to a recipient's practical capability. Let the inventor choose sharing, sale or retention under that world's rules; adoption needs a real user and supplies. Proposed acceptance: recorded transmission, qualified recipient use, causal local consequence and no invented population-wide mastery.
5. **Q45: one optional repeatable refinement with an explicit return.** Use Q21/Q32/Q39 where continued process improvement fits the world. Show the supplied inputs, supported experiment size, next cost and actual yield/precision/control benefit, including any local saturation. Proposed acceptance: more than one paid completion, distinct resulting behavior and honest progress/blocker presentation. This has more balance and save work than Q41 and creates no extra prestige layer.

These are suggestions, not additional required gates. T1's code-fit report and T2's world-qualified examples should determine which can use an existing consumer economically. T4 retains historical diffusion modeling. Strong means can satisfy a missing function outright; none of these proposals requires a fixed grind, compulsory scarcity, universal decline or permanently exclusive research path.
