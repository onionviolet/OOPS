## Fit to standing direction

These are optional palettes for fitting characters, worlds and eras. No new either/or restriction, fixed once-per-life gate, forgetting rule, lethal study policy, researcher-slot cap or prestige layer is accepted here. A powerful body, teacher, archive or cheat can actually supply a missing function. Local law, cost or material limits matter only where that world supports them.

For any later interface slice, use the laptop/desktop workspace first. A proposed 1366×768 review could show the selected question, evidence source, current routine and practical result together, with known blockers visible at ordinary text size; this is an unbuilt presentation consideration. Mobile optimization stays deferred. Learning the answer to a sealed question must use its discovery route, never a source catalogue or inference panel that exposes the truth.

Food references stay separate from source-game adoption: comfortable ordinary unfed play and useful meal benefits remain the direction. Increlution's starvation/death economy and Cultist's recurring survival deadlines do not become prerequisites for research-focused YAIRPG lives. Offline research follows the active branch's existing supported clock and declared boundaries; these references do not authorize unattended loss.
