|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Kittens Game / S | Science discoveries and workshop improvements are different purchases. Source was read in memory, not copied into the repository.

**Q2 discrepancy:** the public Kittens wiki lists a compendium price of 10k science/50 manuscripts; inspected master source declares 9k/55. Paradox's forum/wiki and Satisfactory wiki routes failed; developer Steam text/API routes supplied the narrower supported claims. Exact Obra Dinn confirmation batching, Cultist lore combination, Satisfactory alternate-recipe selection math, Noita wand internals, URR full generated mystery coverage and Q27–Q30 remain open.
