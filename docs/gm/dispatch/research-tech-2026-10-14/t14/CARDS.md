# T14 revealer cards

2026-10-14. Draft considerations only. L10-L24 extend RC1-RC15 without selecting a build. Four original forms each, 60 cards total. [cards.json](cards.json) is the checked artifact; [WORLD_FIT.md](WORLD_FIT.md) qualifies source analogues and [PANELS.md](PANELS.md) retains UI decisions.

## L60: Data and authority

Each card keeps the real ten-field C0 shape. Extra authoring metadata is nested in `state`; it is not a new runtime state schema. `proposalVersion` identifies this draft only. `storeReads` explicitly marks each of IM2, IM3, IM5 and IM6 used or unused. Listed hooks are inert proposed subscriptions. `terms` is one illustrative binding, not a universal acquisition law. Sources label design inference separately from shown/stated canon analogues.

L61: IM2 receipts do not by themselves know the truth. Each exceptional comparison names its additional truth owner/read authority. IM3 graph coverage and IM5 tech context cannot stand in for market transactions, NPC projects, future incidents or speech. IM6 requires identified specimens. Missing ownership or data gives unknown, not an invented result. See technology owner IM rows at `docs/TECHNOLOGY_AND_PROGRESSION.md:1529-1538`.

L62: Stages are illustrative dormant → read → refined contracts, not compulsory XP bars or timers. Ordinary means may provide a partial version; a real exceptional faculty may supply its full permitted truth immediately. Exactness, error and interference are world-specific. No fixed charges, once-only growth or universal anti-abuse cost is added. RC14 retains one reconstruction as a first useful payoff, with later discoveries reachable.

L63: For future implementation, receipt publication must precede visible messages; no reward, skill or stock comes from revealing. Seeded truth/fragments and read history survive restore without rerolls or replay. Only an actual carrier retains evidence or the faculty across lives. Source acceptance, validator acceptance, browser behavior and human acceptance are distinct.

L64: Sealed subjects stay ID/route/status only until a supported route. A revealer is not blanket permission to read sealed truth. Desktop/laptop target is 1366x768; independent comparison panels use available space. Mobile and runtime implementation are deferred.
