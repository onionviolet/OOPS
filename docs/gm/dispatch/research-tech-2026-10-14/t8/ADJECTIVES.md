# T8: technology adjectives as local policies

2026-10-14. **Proposal only; no adjective resolver implemented.** B21-B25 extend
PW5 and PV4. Adjectives select fitting processes, not universal rank or arbitrary
anti-abuse limits. [SAVE_CONTRACT](SAVE_CONTRACT.md) freezes their resolved meaning.

## B21. Bind an adjective to a scope and named consumer

Proposed `TechPolicyBinding`: stable ID, world/instance, optional region/field/
institution scope, origin/source locator, policy revision, effective interval,
resolved parameters, affected consumer IDs, composition rule and disclosure policy.
An adjective can be a descriptive summary with no mechanics, a generation prior,
or a current transition policy. Specify which. A prosperous present is not proof
of an eternal fast-inventing world law.

Tech era labels remain descriptive aggregates over uneven fields. Draw original
world policies only within admitted laws and allowed combinations; keep canon
policies exact where source establishes them. A unknown source policy stays
unresolved instead of defaulting to hostility, universal magic or free electricity.
Basis: `docs/TECHNOLOGY_AND_PROGRESSION.md:41-59,1180-1195`;
`docs/research/procedural-materials-2026-10-13/deep/GENERATION_CONTRACTS.md:67-71`.

## B22. Four requested adjectives and one useful extension

| Adjective | Proposed named effect | What it does not grant | Reachable alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fast-inventing | More eligible native projects, shorter locally supported decision/learning intervals or stronger novelty preferences | No automatic stock, mastery, publication or universal diffusion speed | Fund shared infrastructure, import methods, pursue slow careful work |
| Guild-locked | Specific institution controls a station, instruction or license and its dissemination policy | No metaphysical ban on independent invention; no patent system where absent | Join, rent, commission, bargain, acquire another lawful means or invent elsewhere |
| Post-collapse | Starting state derived from specified lost capabilities and surviving custodians/assets | No assumption every ancient method exceeded the present or every discipline vanished | Recover, teach, trade, invent an analogue or adopt an existing local method |
| Magic-saturated | World-qualified substrates/energy/interfaces affect eligible methods and consumers | No universal magic-to-electricity conversion, arbitrary canon exception or guaranteed magical superiority | Use the compatible magical branch, a lawful ordinary branch or research an adapter |
| Open-teaching | Particular schools/publications/contacts favor actual dissemination | No instant all-world broadcast, free teacher labor or forced degraded copies | Private study, selective sharing, paid instruction or complete archives |

These are authored game implications, not source claims about any named book.
Targets retain several lawful OR methods. A policy can speed transmission but
leave adoption slow because supply is missing. Another can favor magical methods
in one field while ordinary tools remain standard elsewhere. Add only parameters
whose selected resolver consumes them.

## B23. Compose through declared dimensions

Use typed parameters such as `decisionInterval`, `transmissionDelay`,
`publicationPreference`, `stationAccessPolicy`, `evidenceSurvivalLaw` and
`eligibleMethodRules`. They have units/domains and affect specified transitions.
Never multiply five vaguely named tech bonuses together. Weight changes occur
after hard law/access qualification and before the B3 choice is frozen.

Proposed precedence: world law and source pins constrain legality; an explicitly
selected premise/AU overlay changes only its declared source scope; regional and
institution policies specialize allowed behavior; actual actor means and beliefs
decide execution. This is not permission to override canon by adding an adjective.
Two bindings on one dimension require a declared operation, such as minimum
supported interval, exact override, independent per-channel policy or composition
function. Conflicting unspecified rules fail validation without publishing draws.

For fictional `fast-inventing + guild-locked`, private guild research can move
quickly while exported teaching remains restricted. For `post-collapse +
open-teaching`, eager instruction cannot restore missing instruments by itself.
For `magic-saturated + post-collapse`, lost energy infrastructure can interrupt
some magical assemblies while ordinary tools continue. No combination silently
adds another physical law or a guaranteed catastrophe.

## B24. Adjectives can change through actual causes

An academy opening can produce a new regional teaching policy; loss of the guild's
station can remove its access monopoly; a discovery can make a magical branch
available. Save a dated new binding and its actual cause. Apply it to future
uncommitted decisions. Pending work retains its original terms/outcome unless the
operation explicitly responds to a changed condition at settlement. That response
gets its own deterministic receipt, not a rerolled initial choice.

Changing a public label alone has no mechanical effect. A reported "post-collapse"
region can repair while keeping its historical loss record. A forged claim that
an academy teaches openly does not alter actual access. When a policy change
disturbs a baseline event, re-evaluate that dependency under B4; unrelated canon
events and saved pasts continue unchanged.

Reload/content update never resamples policies, changes old parameters or invents
a transition. Imported unresolved policies remain retained for explicit compatibility.
New original worlds may choose new policy combinations at world admission.
Branch forks inherit the selected checkpoint bindings unless their authorized
premise says otherwise; fresh instances have separate identities.

## B25. Frequency, save safety and disclosure

World-specific opportunities can be rare, frequent, renewable or nearly unlimited
when their actual laws support that. An engineering generation budget is a finite
computation safeguard, not a fictional once-per-life entitlement. Duplicate replay
of one operation key is idempotent; a genuine new operation remains possible.
Do not add universal caps on inventions, publications, fragment discoveries,
teaching or revisits. Scarcity, access and costs need a fitting cause.

An adjective's true value can be hidden. Player descriptions derive from observed
institutions, demonstrations and evidence. Hidden policy/branch names cannot leak
through tooltips, map colors, list ordering, prices or available-action counts.
Exceptional means can reveal an exact binding when their discovery route supports
it. This file publishes original examples only, no sealed assignments.
