## B11. History records results, contributions and transmission separately

Proposed technology extension to a P14 historical receipt: target function and
magnitude/form, method/graph revision, result specimen/consumer ref, contributing
actor roles, test context/evidence, dependency refs, discovery class and actual
knowledge/execution/production changes. Origin is source-authored, generated
background or lived. A planned baseline project has no occurred receipt yet.

| Distinction | Retained technology meaning |
|---|---|
| New to actor | Learns an already existing method through a real source |
| New to community | Introduces an existing method to a defined receiving group |
| New locally verified design | Demonstrates a method not previously established within declared coverage |
| Independent rediscovery | Has a supported path without exposure to the prior solution |
| Refinement/recombination | References the actual prior method(s) and changed function |
| Recovery | Reconstructs or interprets retained ancient evidence, with its limits |

"First ever" needs complete enough historical coverage for that claim. Otherwise
use "earliest supported record in this region" or an attributed claimant's boast.
Absence of a surviving record does not prove absence of an earlier inventor.
Co-inventors, operators, instrument makers, translators, teachers and funders can
contribute differently. Do not collapse every role into whoever clicked Build.

## B12. Generate the invention past in dependency order

In the [common background pass](DIFFUSION_MODEL.md#b2-generate-a-coherent-starting-state-once),
each admitted historical invention must explain a prior need/curiosity, actual
means, the selected method, its test/use result and any dissemination. Draw only
eligible choices and actors who existed at the relevant time. Reserve unnamed
historical groups rather than invent exact named biographies for every event.
Preserve calendar intervals when the source or abstraction lacks exact dates.

Derive artifacts, custodians, surviving instruction and present dependencies from
these receipts. A collapse applies actual losses, and a recovery references
actual survivors. Independent parallel inventions may share upstream tools while
having no information edge between inventors. Exposure before invention permits
learning or adaptation but does not alone prove copying, theft or deceit.

Never generate attribution after looking at the player's preferred suspect.
Freeze historical roles, transfer edges and account lineage when admitted.
Lazy names or descriptive detail honor those reservations and cannot add a new
patent owner, living heir or debt. A generation conflict returns unresolved
detail/validation failure under the declared policy, not endless redraws.
See `.../p14/HISTORY.md:21-53` for this background/lazy-history discipline.

## B13. Claims do not rewrite contribution truth

Attach P14 claims/accounts to invention receipt refs with propositions such as
`designedBy`, `demonstratedBy`, `publishedBy`, `licensedBy` or `improvedBy`.
Distinguish actual contributor, credited person, claimant, sponsor and local
authority. An inaccurate textbook, a false boast and a value dispute over who
deserves recognition are different phenomena.

Each claim keeps speaker, claimed time/scope, evidence/source lineage, edition and
receipt of transmission. Deliberate deceit additionally needs the author's own
belief and intent; it is author-private until discovered. Receiving the same
story through three copied newspapers does not give three independent sources.
Correction creates a new claim/edition and reaches only actual recipients.

Patents, prizes, royalties and guild recognition use the selected jurisdiction's
real issuer, terms and backed funds. A credible-looking document cannot mint a
payment or monopolize a method in every world. A first-place prize can settle
under its contract while other inventors keep researching or reproducing the
result. No permanent global first-inventor lock or lifetime once restriction.

P14 authority: `docs/gm/dispatch/procgen-2026-10-13/p14/RETELLING.md:5-17,51-67`;
`.../p14/CONTRACT.md:25-42,63-81`. Domain outcomes remain authoritative for money,
custody and knowledge; claims never become shadow balances.

## B14. Book milestones and future divergence remain causal

Store a canon invention pin with selected continuity, source locator/evidence
mode, method/function, established holder/role and supported date/window. Unknown
date remains unknown. A source saying someone invented a device and a character
claiming priority may justify different records. Do not resolve contradictions
by quietly mixing editions or replacing the canon inventor with a generated NPC.

Undisturbed baseline projects proceed at their authorized clock boundaries.
An early player demonstration can change demand, exposure or funding. Re-evaluate
affected future dependencies, retaining a disturbance receipt. Existing historical
receipts are not rewritten; a source-supported temporal operation would need a
separate declared law and carrier contract. A failed future baseline invention
records failure if its causes no longer hold, rather than being forced to occur.

Use P14's source/gap permissions at `.../p14/HISTORY.md:45-53` and natural-course
authority `docs/TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE.md:174-198`. Mere observation or reading
a preview is not an intervention. Continued branches keep their own receipts;
fresh canon instances keep canon definitions and do not inherit invented credit
disputes or player deeds without an actual crossing policy.

## B15. One credit dispute with a useful consequence

Original fixture: a craftsperson designs a lamp bracket, another makes the gauge
that enables repeatable fit, and a merchant funds demonstration copies. A town
notice credits the merchant alone. Their actual contributions and test results
are retained; the notice is an attributed claim. A customer who reads it offers
work to the merchant. A reachable inspection/witness account can inform that
customer, who may offer a fitting commission to the craftsperson instead.

Possible choices remain open: cooperate, publish, sell the method, stay private,
challenge credit, improve the design or ignore the dispute. None grants a new
copyright/patent law by default. A public correct claim does not guarantee fame,
and accepting a customer offer still follows the contract/payment owner.

Future checks, **NOT RUN**: applied outcomes exist before their chronicles;
publication failure leaves no phantom discovery/credit/payout; facts survive
feed eviction; accounts retain editions and copied-source lineage; independent
rediscovery is distinct from exposure; corrections affect only informed audiences;
catalogue updates cannot rewrite old contributors; save/reload/import preserve
pending claims, due transmissions, exact contributor roles and next draws.
