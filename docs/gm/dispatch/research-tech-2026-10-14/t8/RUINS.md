2026-10-14. **Proposal only; no generated ruin implemented.** B6-B10 extend PW2,
TS7, TN3/TN4 and KL. [DIFFUSION_MODEL](DIFFUSION_MODEL.md) owns the shared
background pass; [SAVE_CONTRACT](SAVE_CONTRACT.md) owns stable identity/draws.

## B6. Derive a ruin from a civilization that could actually make its objects

The historical planner graph records the methods a predecessor held, their
operating means, dependencies and actual products. A later lawful transition
damages, abandons, relocates or closes an existing authored site. Derive its
survivors from that transition. Do not roll a relic tier, then retroactively
invent a civilization to justify it. Peaceful obsolescence can leave ruins too.

Proposed `RuinRecord`: instance/branch, authored place/site, historical capability
snapshot, formation/loss receipt, surviving object/record IDs, custodian/access
refs, condition law/revision, visibility policy and generation receipt. A lost
workshop, lost teacher and unreadable archive remove different capabilities.
Surviving distributed copies can keep a method in use elsewhere. Region-level
decline removes only the actually lost means or knowledge, never every ancestor
node of a damaged product.

Exact canon relics keep authored identity, function, location/access and source
limits wherever specified. Original fragments can occupy an admitted ordinary
gap but cannot become an unmentioned unique canon relic. Geometry stays authored.
Unknown chronology remains an interval. Basis:
`docs/TECHNOLOGY_AND_PROGRESSION.md:74-75,145-153,800-809,1182-1184`;
`docs/gm/dispatch/procgen-2026-10-13/p14/HISTORY.md:55-66` and
`.../p20/RESIDUE.md:7-18` distinguish trace, physical aftermath and active effects.

## B7. Fragments have coverage as well as fidelity

Proposed `Fragment`: stable ID, ruin/site and carrier/object refs, method/graph
revision, covered proposition/operation IDs, encoded TK fidelity, missing spans,
language, authenticity/provenance refs, condition and discovery policy. Its true
contents are selected once, saved, and independent of the discoverer's current
skill. Readable knowledge is a separate observer result.

| Existing fidelity | Fitting generated evidence | What it can support |
|---|---|---|
| TK1 rumor | A named purpose in an attributed account | Search direction, not a method |
| TK2 concept | An observable function or intelligible sketch | A bounded question or comparison |
| TK3 principle | An interpreted operating relation | Design investigation within actual means |
| TK4 recipe | Covered executable instructions with declared missing sections | Only the covered build attempt, not the whole graph |
| TK5 mastery | A lawful teaching/experience carrier with actual execution support | Practical learning under its carrier rules; an ordinary page does not confer tacit mastery automatically |

TK0 means no knowledge of this subject; do not label a sealed object by its hidden
subject merely to display TK0. A perfect archive/appraisal can legitimately remove
information gaps. Reading difficulty, access and condition do not impose universal
fidelity loss. For a sparse numeric TK adapter, retain fine coverage separately;
the scalar is insufficient to establish every AND requirement.

Two copies of the same section are two carriers but one informational coverage
set. Merge observations by stable proposition and source lineage. Complementary
sections can complete a method; contradictory claims remain attributed competing
beliefs. Never add TK scores until five scraps magically become mastery. A torn
diagram can reveal a working subassembly without revealing its entire factory.

observation and safe naming from

## B8. Draw correlated survivors at the historical boundary

During the common background pass, select lawful survivor bundles: a protected
archive may preserve several related sections; a flooded machine can retain
metal parts while losing its paper instructions. World-local formation and
condition laws determine which bundles are possible. Do not independently roll
condition, text coverage and fidelity into contradictory states.

Freeze fragment contents and salvage quantities at ruin admission or a reserved
lazy-materialization boundary using the pinned historical snapshot. Save unresolved
reservations with stable object slots, custody, generator revision and sufficient
constraints. A **view** of an unmaterialized optional detail shows only previously
known surroundings; it cannot call materialization. A committed investigation can
materialize under its declared policy, then use the frozen contents. Reload,
revisit, language change, inventory transfer and renaming never redraw them.

Recovery/disassembly is a different operation. It spends or preserves the exact
object under the declared custody policy and can reveal actual evidence. Save
any stochastic failure at commitment. Repeated inspection draws nothing. A real
new salvage job may consume a remaining part; retry cannot resurrect a spent
part or turn the same old scrap into a different ancient instruction.

## B9. Reverse planning gives hypotheses, not omniscient grants

Given an observed product function, enumerate lawful candidate production methods
from the planner's **author** graph internally. Filter by actual observations and
actor knowledge for presentation. A pressure-driven device does not prove its
builders used a specific fuel, institution or theory. Unknown manufacture and
known operation can coexist. T6's graph expansion is not an inverse-design solver.

Classify any promised recovery route using P6: `reconstructable`,
`currently-blocked` or `historical-only`. For reconstructable content retain a
witness acquisition/process path, initial bootstrap route and useful consumer.
Do not require the recovered product to unlock its own indispensable assay.
Blocked supply may permit trade, local substitutes or another lawful method;
a functionally similar invention is labelled an analogue, not authenticated
recovery of the original. Exact identity requirements cannot be substituted away.

P6/P8 source basis:
`docs/research/procedural-materials-2026-10-13/deep/GENERATION_CONTRACTS.md:89-95,105-111`.
No coordinates, hidden fragment subject, secret truth or true attribution enters
public reports before the actual discovery route. Secret artifacts use only their
ID, route and status outside that route.

## B10. A bounded useful recovery

Original fixture: an abandoned authored workshop retains a portable light
assembly, one mounting sketch and one maintenance section. The sketch can help
repair a lamp mount; the maintenance section supports keeping that actual lamp
working. Neither supplies a missing energy source. Full local fabrication can
follow through a teacher, retained complete instructions or independent design.
The site history references the real repair instead of granting a relic badge.

Future checks, **NOT RUN**: fragment coverage/fidelity and carrier identity survive
reload/import; duplicate copied sections add no independent evidence; learning a
language changes comprehension without changing text; salvage consumes the same
part once per actual operation; a new legal operation remains repeatable; generated
playable recovery has a valid witness; loss of current access does not rewrite
the historical certificate; canon pins, sealed presentation and old graph semantics
survive content updates. New maps, universal decay and compulsory relic destruction
are outside this proposal.
