| Local source | Shown summary / correction to pilot inference |

Speed's warp class includes teleportation, so it is not an ordinary running-speed rung. Wiki policy retains official handbook values even when stories outgrow them. ## M38: CW2 needs a cause, a recipient and an exception

“Status quo pull” can summarize observed stagnation; it should not silently undo earned progress or invent a universal anti-uplift tax. For an original world, choose a cause that someone can change. These are inferred design translations, except the cited examples' stated facts.

| Cause | Source-supported example | Playable response, original |
|---|---|---|
| Specialist process and service burden | [Worm, Drone 23.5](https://parahumans.wordpress.com/category/stories-arcs-21/arc-23-drone/23-05/) discusses tuning/maintenance and why mass production redirects specialist time. | Document ordinary interfaces, buy service, sponsor a maintainer or adopt a simpler reproducible derivative. Record what remains power-dependent. |
| Access to scarce substrate/process | Adamantium's variant cost and unique prototype in M13. | Trade, substitution, skilled processing or a cheaper variant. Access to the metal does not grant the process. |
| Institutional capture | [Alchemax, Fandom](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Alchemax_%28Earth-928%29) puts research, policing and consumer supply in one corporation. | License, join, create another supplier, leak records or build an independent service. Ownership explains unequal access even when tech is widespread. |
| Secrecy and hostile use | M18 stolen countermeasures; M26 subsidized criminal fabrication. | Publish, redact, secure, teach selected receivers, or accept rivals benefiting. Each changes actual knowledge access. |
| War and broken infrastructure | [Earth-811, Fandom](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Earth-811) concentrates advanced control technology in a persecuting regime. | Ordinary radio repair, supply evidence and distributed archives can help resistance before any miracle weapon. |
| Objectives rather than inability | [Brainiac, official DC](https://www.dc.com/characters/brainiac) values exclusive knowledge; M12 Future Foundation educates. | An inventor chooses monopoly, public service, prestige or a mixed policy. Do not presume selfishness from genius. |
| Deliberate world transformation | [Red Son/Earth-30, official DC](https://www.dc.com/characters/earth-30), [Earth-712, Fandom](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Squadron_Supreme_%28Earth-712%29) and Watchmen M61 show large social consequences. | Widespread adoption can succeed while creating surveillance, coercion, labor changes or dependence. “It spread” and “everyone benefited” remain separate. |

A diffusion receipt should name method, transmitter, recipient, received fidelity, practical means, demonstrated use and world/era. A downstream clinic using one method is genuine adoption without declaring a worldwide revolution. Conversely, a genuine mass-fabrication power can solve mass supply outright when its selected rules support it.
