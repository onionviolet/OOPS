His later-family material is outside this snapshot. Veidt’s commercial role needs a selected issue before detailed procurement claims. CW2 cannot erase the demonstrated transport uplift.

**Unique materials and processes:** Manhattan-supported materials and manufacturing may be singular bottlenecks. No ordinary route to reproduce his faculty was verified; not every civilian tool needs it.

**Gear, mantles, regulation, villain economies and multiverse:** Keene-era restrictions and government exceptions matter more than a borrowed Marvel statute. Two Nite Owl identities illustrate legacy, not automatic inherited mastery. The full criminal procurement network was not surveyed. Original Watchmen does not require DC crossover cosmology; later Manhattan multiverse feats are a separate snapshot.
