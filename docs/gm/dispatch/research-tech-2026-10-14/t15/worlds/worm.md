# M42: Worm, target T19

2026-10-14, research and original proposals. Third-priority dossier. *Worm* is a web serial, not a comic. Freeze Earth Bet/Brockton Bay before the selected disaster; *Ward* and its later technical taxonomy are not automatically part of the snapshot.

## Shown and stated basis

**Shown, author text:** [Drone 23.5](https://parahumans.wordpress.com/category/stories-arcs-21/arc-23-drone/23-05/) distinguishes quick assembly from existing components, actual acceleration/fragility limits and specialist upkeep that makes mass supply difficult. **Stated, author cast:** [Dragon](https://parahumans.wordpress.com/cast-spoiler-free/cast/) provides PRT equipment and computer systems. **Stated, Fandom:** [Tinker](https://worm.fandom.com/wiki/Tinker) describes power-assisted fabrication and limited ordinary replication; it mixes *Worm*, *Ward* and supplemental design material, so use the author passage for this dossier's maintenance claim. [Power classifications](https://worm.fandom.com/wiki/Power_Classifications) are threat-response categories, not character levels; [PRT](https://worm.fandom.com/wiki/Parahuman_Response_Team) is a paramilitary law/enforcement/emergency institution, not simply the army.

## Genius and diffusion, inferred

Armsmaster/Defiant's compact systems and Dragon's integration provide different specialist roles. A Tinker power differs from ordinary learned engineering: scanning an existing device can inform a specialist without granting an unpowered observer its fabrication faculty. Dragon's support is a clear exception to “tech never spreads.” Gear can reach ordinary operators while its most difficult maintenance remains specialist-dependent. Use actual service availability and compatible substitutions; do not force every device to crumble on a global countdown.

Unique materials can be specialist-designed products or unusually exact components rather than a world-wide named supermetal. The bounded source does not supply a comprehensive materials catalogue. Gear custody matters: an ordinary responder using issued equipment is not automatically a Tinker. Protectorate membership and a costume identity are institutional/social roles, not universal inheritable power offices.

The PRT's threat ratings select responses. They are not IQ, a power census score or promotion ranks. Employment rules, licensed activities and enforcement vary; no universal MCU Accords applies. The fan Tinker page identifies Toybox as a black-market/refuge network, illustrating resource pressure and recruiting institutions; its exact availability needs the selected story date. Ordinary villains can support technical projects through money, parts and protection without understanding their mechanisms.

Multiverse travel exists in the wider serial, but portal access must be provided by an actual faculty, actor or institution. Do not grant a civilian an industrial portal design from the existence of other Earths. A native Thinker sensing power can be an authentic revealer; an arbitrary Mother Box sibling is an original imported premise.

## Three ordinary research activities, original

| Activity | Means and evidence | Consumer / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| M42.a: emergency-kit compatibility survey | An unpowered quartermaster records issued-kit serials, ordinary connectors and published operator instructions. Ask the maintainer before assuming internal equivalence. | Match a compatible replacement for one routine communications unit; specialist internals remain outside the clerk's means. |
| M42.b: field response evidence | A volunteer combines firsthand incident accounts, camera records and the team's stated classification. Distinguish observed effect from guessed mechanism. | Update an evacuation brief or request a different specialist, without awarding a magical threat rating or automatically identifying a cape. |
| M42.c: accessibility test for one control | A willing operator tries a specialist-provided prototype's supported controls under supervision; record failures and fit. | The inventor can revise the interface so another user can operate it. Usability spread is a real result even when fabrication cannot transfer. |

Public sibling: **The Threaded Coast** has invention faculties bound to distinct hidden substrates, with cooperative service shops and competing recruiters. This is original content, not shard lore copied into the public fork. Ordinary engineering remains useful wherever the method is actually ordinary; a supported exceptional teacher or fabrication cheat can change a bottleneck.

Cheat fit: M35 maintenance assistant reads held telemetry; M32 network desk reads reports; M36 counters remain evidence-based. M29 needs an explicitly qualified detectable signature; being a parahuman does not imply every sensor can identify the power. M34 premonition can fit a locally authored Thinker faculty with stated scope. M30 a goal advisor must not silently become Contessa's actual power or an omniscient planner. M64 trace-reading needs a real local faculty, not ordinary fingerprints renamed as psychometry.

Subtle evidence: a replacement that works for one user but not another, a maintenance appointment backlog and a “confirmed by two witnesses” note.
