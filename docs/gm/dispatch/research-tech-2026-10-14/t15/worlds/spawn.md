## World-specific research palette, inferred

**Geniuses, institutions and CW2:** Research can study bargains, bodily effects and a living tool rather than build industrial science. No full native scientist/institution roster was verified. Secrecy and nonstandard substrate can limit spread in an original sibling.

**Unique materials and processes:** Necroplasm and the suit are different roles in the account; ordinary gun use is not the same resource as magical expenditure. A generic lab cannot manufacture their backing from a known label.

**Gear, mantles, regulation, villain economies and multiverse:** A bonded tool has agency; host identity and infernal office are not simple removable armor slots. Patron obligations differ from civic registration, which remains unverified. Infernal brokers are an original economy fit, not a sourced open store. Realm crossing requires an actual power/bargain; no universal civilian portal design.
