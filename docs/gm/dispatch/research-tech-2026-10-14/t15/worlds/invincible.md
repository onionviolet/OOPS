# M40: Invincible, target T1

2026-10-14, research and original proposals. First-priority dossier. Snapshot: Robert Kirkman/Cory Walker/Ryan Ottley's original comic #1-144, with a candidate entry around #37-50. TV evidence does not fill comic gaps. Sources below are Image Comics Database on Fandom, secondary issue synopses, not firsthand issue reads.

## Stated source basis

[Sinclair](https://imagecomics.fandom.com/wiki/D.A._Sinclair) locates coerced cybernetic research in #22/#31/#36 and GDA recruitment in #37; later work reaches #65 and #142. [#50 synopsis](https://imagecomics.fandom.com/wiki/Invincible_Vol_1_50) attributes the sonic counter to earlier examination, and Robot blocks its frequency. [Robot/Rudolph Conners](https://imagecomics.fandom.com/wiki/Rudolph_Conners) describes drone engineering and control. [Angstrom Levy](https://imagecomics.fandom.com/wiki/Angstrom_Levy) separates his portal faculty from a Mauler-built device aggregating variant knowledge. [Cecil](https://imagecomics.fandom.com/wiki/Cecil_Stedman) supports GDA surveillance and covert recruitment. These establish specific technical interventions against very strong bodies; the old “bodies decide nearly everything” is an interpretive emphasis, not a universal rule.

## Research, institutions and CW2, inferred from that basis

The important genius roles differ: Robot designs/control systems; Sinclair works on cybernetic bodies; the Maulers build mind-transfer/cloning-related machinery; Cecil supplies institutional money, telemetry and custody rather than being every device's inventor. Do not label all four as the same science talent.

GDA deployment is already diffusion to an institution. Secrecy, captive samples and protected facilities explain why a particular military result does not arrive as a neighborhood consumer product. That does not establish an inability to publish. Later Robot-centered political changes require their own late-era dossier; they are not an invisible status-quo reset in an early start.

Unique resources in the bounded evidence are alien biology, copied mind data and specialized cybernetic components, rather than a verified universal named supermetal. Specific alloy recipes, scourge-virus mechanisms and safe handling procedures were not verified. Keep dangerous counters abstract. A body-specific counter must identify the tested subject and circumstance; a category of 18 “weakness” pages from the TV-oriented wiki is not 18 independent reproducible comic counters.

Drone/suit custody offers gear-based agency without granting Viltrumite physiology. A team appointment, hero identity, copied memories and actual engineered body are different kinds of succession. The source supports GDA control and covert employment, not a blanket Superhuman Registration Act. Villain economies can involve contracted researchers and stolen samples; do not assume every villain purchases through an open market. Multiverse access depends on Levy's faculty and the memory machine's separate operation, not ordinary portal science automatically being common.

## Three ordinary-character research activities, original

| Activity | Means and evidence | Actual consumer and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| M40.a: test the rescue team's communicator | A borrowed standard unit, instrument access and a technician let you compare clean transmission with a recorded fault; retain device ID, test setting and observation. | Repair improves one evacuation coordination route. Testing ordinary equipment does not reveal the sealed GDA counter archive or build a harmful emitter. |
| M40.b: reconcile recovery stock | Cleanup-worker start, tagged parts and two manifests reveal whether one supported protective component is missing, damaged or mislabelled. | A mechanic can finish a rescue drone repair, or substitute an ordinary component when its interface permits. Salvage ownership and compatibility remain real gates. |
| M40.c: support a clinical follow-up | A consenting patient's ordinary mobility records, supervised measurements and a clinician help distinguish recovery from an implant malfunction. | Better fitting or a referral changes one patient's usable action; no generic cybernetic cure, involuntary test or unearned genius mastery. |

Public sibling: **The Survey City's defense office** funds rescue robotics while monopolizing some alien studies. An ordinary lab assistant can prove a communicator fault, get one drone back into service and choose whether to teach the repair to another crew. It is an original world, not named Invincible content.

## Revealer fit and subtleties

M29 detects only authored biological/energy signatures, not every strong person. M32 works through GDA or independent contacts; M35 fits drone telemetry; M36 stores observed countermeasures. M30/M33 require an explicitly original dimensional patron rather than being presumed native. A portal-borne memory report should identify its variant and whose experience it contains. A neatly repaired connector and a restricted telemetry log can tell very different stories about the same institution.

Open evidence: no complete issue read; ordinary activities are proposals; exact material list, formal civilian licensing, later global reform and the full catalog of comic counters remain unverified. No playable implementation.
