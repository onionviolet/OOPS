## World-specific research palette, inferred

**Geniuses, institutions and CW2:** Genetics, body modification and powered expertise serve military projects. Captive research and selective deployment explain blocked civilian access; no universal inability to teach is established.

**Unique materials and processes:** Celestial technology and the M’kraan Crystal are exceptional substrates/routes in the wider account. Do not replace them with generic laboratory currency or import later life/death seeds into a 1995 opening.

**Gear, mantles, regulation, villain economies and multiverse:** Engineered soldiers have acquired bodies; a laboratory rank or Horseman office is not the same grant. Regulation is caste-based capture and command. The ruler and researchers control production, with rival secret agendas. Crystal travel has its own means, not ordinary time-loop equipment.
