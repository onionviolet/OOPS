# M41: Days of Future Past, target T3

2026-10-14, research and original proposals. Second-priority dossier. Freeze *Uncanny X-Men #141-142* as the opening comparison; later *Excalibur* outcomes are a separately selected extension, not secretly included rules. Comics Earth-811, not the film where Wolverine is sent back.

## Stated source basis and time-law correction

[Official Marvel timeline guide](https://www.marvel.com/comics/discover/2519/the-x-men-s-major-alternate-timelines) identifies the assassination-driven Sentinel future and Kitty's warning. [Earth-811, Marvel Database on Fandom](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Earth-811) describes persecution of mutants and other heroes, control collars, smuggled parts and a jammer. Rachel sends Kate's consciousness; ordinary electronics alone do not perform that transfer. The same fan account cites later cross-time travel, distinguishing Earth-616 from Earth-811's literal past. [Earth-811 X-Men, Fandom](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/X-Men_%28Earth-811%29) attributes the later regime's end to reprogramming through *Excalibur* intervention. Thus “fixed bad end and repeatable send-it-back loop” is not established. A bleak natural course is a threat; repeat resets require a separate supported carrier.

## Research and diffusion, inferred

The machine regime has advanced sensing, classification, suppression and continental coordination. Technology has spread very effectively for control while prisoners lack parts and free lab access. Resistance knowledge lives in people, smuggled components and a shared escape plan. Kate's assembly contribution is ordinary technical work with an actual beneficiary, even though the larger plan uses Rachel's faculty. Do not introduce modern Reed, Forge or Trask as alive and available civilian mentors merely because counterparts exist elsewhere. Named original creators of the entire regime's machinery need further issue-specific verification.

Adamantium is a useful inherited material reference where the chosen character/source confirms it, not a free camp resource. The strongest bounded “unique primitive” here is a power-suppression device with a particular interface. Gear, cuffs and sensing infrastructure can control innate powers; owning a damaged Sentinel does not grant a recognized heroic mantle or its functioning fabrication chain.

Regulation is political classification backed by incarceration and killing, not the later Earth-616 Civil War statute. State-held technology and confiscated property can form an economy of capture; an organized commercial villain marketplace was not verified. Multiverse science separates the plan's believed destination from the destination later canon establishes. A correct time coordinate and a compatible recipient are different gates.
