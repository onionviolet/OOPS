## World-specific research palette, inferred

**Geniuses, institutions and CW2:** [Kate Corrigan, Fandom](https://hellboy.fandom.com/wiki/Katherine_Corrigan) is a human folklore scholar and consultant, giving research an ordinary expert alongside exceptional agents and von Klempt’s technical genius. Scholarship, field investigation and equipment engineering have different experts. Bureau case archives support diffusion within teams; restricted artifacts and uncertain compatibility explain limited outside spread.

**Unique materials and processes:** Occult relics and unusual biological/ectoplasmic bodies are possible substrates, but neither a catalogued relic nor a sample guarantees reproducible production.

**Gear, mantles, regulation, villain economies and multiverse:** A bureau appointment, inherited occult role and support suit are distinct. Paranormal custody is not a verified universal registration law. Secret researchers/corporate equipment provide villain-economy angles; exact procurement network unverified. Occult realms are not necessarily technologically charted parallel Earths.
