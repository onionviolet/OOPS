These are secondary summaries or official descriptions, not firsthand complete issue reads. Fandom evidence was retrieved from indexed extracts after body access failed. See [evidence rules](../GENIUS_AND_STATUS_QUO.md).

## World-specific research palette, inferred

**Geniuses, institutions and CW2:** Genius architecture, security engineering and public governance are different roles. [Bruce Wayne, Fandom](https://dc.fandom.com/wiki/Bruce_Wayne_%28Earth-22%29) describes computer/electronic engineering, robotic patrol and employees making equipment. This supports a workshop network and tailored assistive technology, not access to every Prime Earth plan.

**Unique materials and processes:** Reconstructed Kryptonian systems are a knowledge/access primitive. Named alloy supply and mass civilian replication were not verified.

**Gear, mantles, regulation, villain economies and multiverse:** Legacy identities and group leadership matter, but descendants do not automatically gain predecessor workmanship. Metahuman confinement is supported, a detailed licensing statute is not. Political manipulation differs from a surveyed villain gear marketplace. Later multiverse travel is outside the original four-issue opening.
