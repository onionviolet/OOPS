## World-specific research palette, inferred

**Geniuses, institutions and CW2:** Industrial invention exists within a changed social hierarchy. Original CW2 fit is selective opportunity and ownership, not a ban on science. Tony’s local career should not inherit every Earth-616 blueprint.

**Unique materials and processes:** Exact rare-material availability is unverified. Ordinary manufacturing inputs and local records are enough for a civilian research start.

**Gear, mantles, regulation, villain economies and multiverse:** Armored identity, employment and genetic social status are distinct. Discrimination and sovereign power need local evidence; the comic Civil War Act is not imported. Black-market access is an original option. A reality rewrite can alter records and memory together, so a carried old notebook needs a declared survival mechanism.
