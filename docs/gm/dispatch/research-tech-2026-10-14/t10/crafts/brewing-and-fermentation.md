## Sensory ladder

Four cues, three voices for the same permitted observation (U6/U30). Authoring voices, not XP thresholds.

| Cue | Novice | Practiced | Master |
|---|---|---|---|
| Activity | There are bubbles. | They appear less often than before. | The pattern has changed; bubbles alone cannot establish completion or safety. |
| Aroma | The jar smells sharp. | It differs from yesterday's recorded scent. | The change is worth noting, not tasting an uncertain batch. |
| Clarity | The drink looks cloudy. | A sediment layer has appeared. | You distinguish settling from a claim about what organisms are present. |
| Label | This jar has a smudge. | The date is partly unreadable. | You know which history is missing and refuse to invent the batch's age. |

**Beginner mistakes and tells:** Failing to label batches leaves age uncertain; treating fizz or a pleasant smell as proof of safe completion overclaims sensory evidence. Contamination stays abstract, with no corrective procedure.

**Body costs and marks:** A tired lifting arm, a damp apron and a familiar cellar smell. No intoxication bonus, pathogen recipe or tasting instruction.

**Tool care and quirks:** A container that looks alike to another needs distinct labels; a sticky seal or chipped vessel is a candidate maintenance tell. Vessel/pressure management stays abstract. Keep designated vessels/tools clean and labels legible; no pressure or preservation procedure.

**Environment, weather, season and light:** UMN supports temperature/light sensitivity for produce fermentation. Cold versus warm-season activity can be authored; no rates, thresholds, preservation or alcohol/distillation instructions.

**Small happy accident:** An unexpected pleasant aroma is recorded as a variation only after the world's independent batch qualification, never as safety proof.

**Cozy moments:** The cellar is quiet except for an occasional bubble; neighbors compare labels at a harvest gathering.

**Real-craft evidence, checked 2026-10-14:** UMN distinguishes fermentation types and requires process/safety checks beyond observation; its produce guidance is not a cider-specific recipe. [UMN fermentation](https://extension.umn.edu/es/node/172346).
