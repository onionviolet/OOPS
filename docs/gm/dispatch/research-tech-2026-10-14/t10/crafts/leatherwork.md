## Sensory ladder

Four cues, three voices for the same permitted observation (U6/U30). Authoring voices, not XP thresholds.

| Cue | Novice | Practiced | Master |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flex | The leather bends reluctantly. | The crease stays after the bend. | Flex differs across this piece; stiffness alone does not identify its treatment. |
| Edge | The edge is fuzzy. | Loose fibers roughen the cut edge. | You can see where an otherwise even edge changes character. |
| Stitch | The seam wanders. | The holes are unevenly spaced. | One section differs from the seam rhythm, making the mistake easy to locate. |
| Surface | One patch looks shiny. | The shine sits where the material rubs. | You separate an observed wear patch from guesses about who wore it. |

**Beginner mistakes and tells:** Uneven stitch spacing leaves a wavering seam; mismatched pieces meet with an awkward bulge. Do not diagnose a tanning failure from smell.

**Body costs and marks:** A sore thumb, pressure from a thimble, thread marks and a work-stained apron. Tanning and dust exposure stay abstract hazards.

**Tool care and quirks:** A burred needle can snag thread; a familiar awl handle sits comfortably in the palm. No tanning substances, recipes or treatment sequence. Store needles safely and keep handles/tools clean; do not turn care into tanning instructions.

**Environment, weather, season and light:** Dampness, dry winter rooms and changing daylight are candidate handling/inspection conditions, UNVERIFIED for the chosen leather. Season may alter available hides only through actual world stock.

**Small happy accident:** An offcut fits a small strap ornament; the accidental shape is cosmetic unless the world authors a result.

**Cozy moments:** A finished strap fits its owner; an apprentice recognizes their own neat seam on a well-used bag.
