**Tool care and quirks:** A focus stone hums one note off; a favorite rune stylus feels familiar. Care means an abstract world action, no real reagents, process, dose or lab method. A world-authored care action checks the focus and ward; no real laboratory maintenance procedure.

**Small happy accident:** A harmless ward projects a small decorative star instead of a circle, if the actual authored result permits it.

**Cozy moments:** The apprentice's first stable light rests above the desk; a familiar watches its reflection.
