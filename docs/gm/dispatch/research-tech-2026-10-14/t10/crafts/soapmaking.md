## Sensory ladder

Four cues, three voices for the same permitted observation (U6/U30). Authoring voices, not XP thresholds.

| Cue | Novice | Practiced | Master |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface | The bar looks damp. | Tiny droplets sit on its face. | Humidity is one possible explanation; droplets alone do not certify the batch. |
| Lather | The bubbles look different. | This bar makes a finer-looking foam. | You compare observed lather with a known finished bar, without judging safety by foam. |
| Scent | The bar smells floral. | A woody note sits beneath the flowers. | You distinguish the scent layers without inferring ingredients you cannot identify. |
| Shape | The cut edge is ragged. | The same corner crumbles on several bars. | The repeated edge problem calls for inspection, not a guessed chemical fix. |

**Beginner mistakes and tells:** Rushed presentation leaves ragged edges or smeared labels; calling every damp bar a failed batch confuses an appearance cue with proof. No tongue tests or caustic handling instructions.

**Body costs and marks:** A washed-out apron cuff, a tired wrist and a familiar clean scent. Chemical burns remain an abstract hazard, never a cozy moment.

**Tool care and quirks:** A chipped mold repeats an edge mark; clean designated utensils and legible labels support scene continuity. No real formula, ratio or correction procedure. Keep molds, labels and designated tools clean and organized; no caustic maintenance procedure.

**Environment, weather, season and light:** A reputable maker guide supports humid-air droplets on finished soap; seasonal humidity can supply that cue when known. Daylight changes how pale swirls read; scent persistence is a proposal.

**Small happy accident:** A harmless swirl looks like a cloud on a finished, separately-qualified bar.

**Cozy moments:** A household receives neat wrapped bars; a child notices the tiny stamped flower.

**Real-craft evidence, checked 2026-10-14:** Bramble Berry supports humidity-linked surface moisture. Only that appearance claim is used; appearance, scent and lather are not safety tests. [Soap sweating guide](https://www.brambleberry.com/how-to/why-is-my-cold-process-soap-sweating.html).
