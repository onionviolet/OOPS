## U30. Translate evidence before translating words

Use the same underlying observed fact at every skill voice. First qualify the focused observer, functioning channel, contact/range, attention, retained knowledge and actual source. Then choose a vocabulary band. A higher skill can interpret a vibration better; it cannot create a color channel. Former-human memory can explain what color means without seeing the present object's color. A native nonhuman mind gets clear adult prose, not automatic childish speech.

Touch, smell and taste are not interchangeable instruments. A slime does not gain chemical identification because its body contacts material. A bat-like fictional body does not detect internal cracks by default. Echoes can establish a permitted shape cue; the instrument's resolution decides how much. Mana perception only reads a world-authored magical property. There is no “master notices poison” shortcut and no proposed taste/contact test for hot, unknown or harmful material. PI9 still applies.

| Qualified channel | Three voices for one permitted observation | Facts it cannot silently substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Smell | “A scent hangs by the basket.” / “It resembles your known dried sample.” / “The association is familiar, though the specimen is still unconfirmed.” | Written labels, exact identity, purity, potency or safety; wind/masking and prior associations matter |

| Many eyes | “The rim leans.” / “It leans from both views.” / “The repeated silhouette supports a local shape difference.” | Duplicate objects, extra control or automatic microscopic vision; merge qualified views |
| Mana sense | “A pulse crosses the ward.” / “Its rhythm is uneven.” / “It differs from the recorded stable pattern.” | Real heat, toxic chemistry, identity or hidden origin unless this world's faculty supplies them |
| Received brood/partner report | “The tender reports a rattle.” / “They located it at the frame.” / “Your interpretation uses their earlier report; you have not checked it yourself.” | Live disconnected-member truth, their private memory, consent, or expertise the focused maker lacks |

A caption always stays readable to the human player. No-sight characters keep keyboard controls, contrast and text inspection. Quiet/effects-off output contains the same legitimate facts. If a channel is missing, another qualified channel may supply a different observation about the same work, not a fabricated translation of unavailable color into smell.

## U32. Body, scale and marks change the scene

A small maker can inspect a seam at body height while needing a fixture or partner to move the whole garment. A huge maker needs tools and a bench that fit; size need not make the craft bad. A rooted maker can sense only connected or otherwise admitted workspace; an observed distant rack is not reachable. Multiple manipulators may support a world-authored rhythm, but a many-eye view does not add hands or parallel craft slots. Inventory/custody and action eligibility remain the engine owners' work.

Translate body texture by the actual substrate: cloth lint on a furred sleeve, clay at a supported shell joint, a contact patch on a fictional slime's apron, a worn socket on an inhabited tool. Do not give a sword human calluses, a tree a tired wrist or every slime a corrosive digestive power. A selected mechanical body cost belongs to an existing body record or needs new durable state (U5), not a sensory adjective. Tool ownership and maker identity track the actual actor across body changes.

## U33. Wording and persistence boundaries

Current permitted perception and existing skill can be reprojected without new save fields. Remembering a teacher's signal, a newly learned scent association, a particular batch signature, disconnected member report or body/tool mark needs the owning knowledge/body/history contract if it is not already retained. Current channel loss removes live cue claims, while legitimate prior evidence remains labeled remembered/reported. UI redraws do not rediscover, reroll, injure, identify or reward.

At **1366x768 CSS desktop**, keep one decisive cue in existing Result details or item inspection, with optional source wording such as “from the tender's last report.” Hover and keyboard inspection must expose the same information. No species-specific sensory dashboard is proposed. Real populated fit, long labels, zoom and effects-off behavior remain unverified; mobile optimization is deferred.
