## Contradictions and open questions

**Q1, apparent historical inevitability:** the owner's TS4 description can be read as an absolute nearest-step restriction. H4 supplies a model distinction rather than changing that owner: imported expertise, an archive or a supported power can change the reachable set. No historical model justifies a universal era prohibition. Root should preserve the owner's standing “considerations” framing when folding this research.

**Q2, causes and institutions:** suppression existed, but guild behavior is debated and varied. A ban on wearing or exporting goods differs from lost ability to make them. Which local actor, stake and enforcement route fits a selected world? H20/H21 supply questions and cases, not a mandatory hostile response.

**Q3, provenance versus function:** what can the character establish about a reconstruction? H15-H18 distinguish a method that works from a method identified as the original. Greek Fire's encyclopedia entries conflict about continuity and overstate certainty when listing ingredients; the disputed details are excluded. No agent proposal may disclose a sealed fictional truth to resolve this publicly.

**Q4, time and persistence:** which clock and actual carrier preserve records, practical knowledge, institutions and biological traits in a chosen slice? Agricultural date ranges are historical evidence, not timers. No forgetting, manuscript decay, breeding delay or offline simulation is mandated by this lane.
