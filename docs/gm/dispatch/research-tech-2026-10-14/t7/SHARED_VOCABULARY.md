# T7 shared vocabulary: functions become local power questions

2026-10-14. Research and **INFERRED proposals**, not accepted mechanics or implementation. Extends PI1–PI8 and PP1–PP5 in [Technology and progression](../../../../TECHNOLOGY_AND_PROGRESSION.md#pp-the-same-planner-for-powers). All 13 world sheets use this notation and evidence policy.

## A1: A function needs a task envelope

Translate the player's request into `function + affected domain + eligible target + magnitude + form + duration + delivery condition`. These are authoring questions, not required new save fields. “Heal” alone cannot distinguish closing a cut, replacing tissue, treating infection, restoring a soul or reversing an event. “Make light” may illuminate a room without making sunlight or heat. More graph depth does not establish more output; source rules decide output (qualification to PI3/PV1).

| PI1 function | Power-effect question | Where translation fails |
|---|---|---|
| Clean, preserve | Remove which contaminant; prevent which change? | Cleaning dirt does not dispel a curse; storage need not stop time |
| Bind, contain | Restrain motion, enclose space, seal a source or enforce an agreement? | Rope, barrier, sealing and oath are different mechanisms |
| Cut, harm | Affect matter, body, soul or another named domain? | Cutting metal does not imply soul damage or conceptual severance |
| Heat, cool | Change temperature of a supported target | Light need not heat; ice appearance need not cool |
| Light | Produce visible illumination, illusion, fire or sunlight? | Different spectra/observers/counters; no automatic sunlight tag |
| Store energy, release energy | Which pool/carrier accepts input and can later pay output? | Mana, chakra, essence and souls are not interchangeable joules |
| Move, lift | Move whom/what, by force, locomotion, transport or spatial transfer? | Flight does not teleport; opening a portal does not grant its destination |
| Heal, grow | Restore which state, or develop which organism/material? | Regeneration, repair, maturation and resurrection do not share eligibility |
| Sense, measure | Which signal reaches whom; which inference is supported? | Perception is not identity, honesty, theory or perfect diagnosis |
| Send | Send signal, language, memory, command or actual object? | A warning coin is not a telephone; a receiver need not obey |
| Compute | Transform accessible information with which procedure? | Sage analysis is not omniscience; calculation does not grant authority |

A function family supports search and comparison, not substitution. Follow [Ability authoring](../../../../worlds/ABILITY_AUTHORING.md:5), especially lines 5–28 and 30–52, and [Mechanisms and means M1–M3](../../../../worlds/SKILL_MECHANISMS_AND_MEANS_2026-10-13.md#m1-separate-result-mechanism-and-means). Memories, consent, ownership, offices, names, fate and metaphysical concepts require named local operations. Do not quietly reduce them to damage or let arbitrary text execute a state change.

## A2: Nodes retain their kinds

| Node kind | What satisfies it | What does not |
|---|---|---|
| Source/body eligibility | Actual compatible faculty, organ, soul/body or source exception | A manuscript about that faculty |
| Knowledge/control | Learned method and relevant practiced execution, separately | Fuel or possession alone |
| Fuel/material | Actual locally usable reserve, component, sample or substrate | Another world's similarly named resource |
| External means | Accessible tool, workspace, teacher, collaborator or site | Their existence elsewhere or an old access flag |
| Acquisition/event | Actual refinement, grant, contract, awakening or ritual outcome | Enough generic XP or a queued assumption of success |
| Target/delivery | Eligible target, range, geometry, timing and route | A generic function match |

Source nodes can be already satisfied at a coherent powerful start. Do not add novice chores to immediate canon grants. A teacher need not be required where self-study is supported. Facts and actions differ: having mana is a fact; training control is a paid activity; becoming eligible is an event outcome. An uncertain experiment produces evidence first, rather than automatically setting a source fact true.

## A3: Feasibility is separate from actor knowledge

An authored branch has a continuity-qualified source rule and one of: **supported**, **inferred application needing a test**, **unverified source question**, or **excluded by a named rule**. Evaluate AND prerequisites only inside that branch. OR alternatives are eligible methods, sources or providers, never arbitrary ways to pay away a canon prohibition. An unverified branch is an investigation lead, not an executable completion path until the source question is resolved. An unknown rule is not a proven impossibility. A known impossible branch cannot be restored by generic ingenuity, rarity points or a vow.

A proposed new spell/extension ends in a bounded trial with an output envelope, failure result and retained evidence. Failure can narrow a hypothesis without awarding the requested power. The graph must not require the finished power to bootstrap itself unless a separately obtainable seed/source exists. No fixed anti-abuse “once” restriction is added. Source uniqueness, body limits and genuine singular events stay local.

## A4: PP3 needs a view over beliefs, not a truth spoiler

Keep author truth, actor evidence and displayed belief distinct. TK1 can expose a vague lead **only if the actor has a clue**. TK0 alternatives, their names, counts, exact requirements, hidden NPC eligibility and sealed truth stay absent. Wrong theory can remain an attributed hypothesis until a test changes it; it cannot alter the world's feasibility rules. Costs known before commitment stay visible even when the mechanism is unknown.

Example: the player asks for “a Bankai that heals everyone.” The actor sees their known care/release leads, not a menu of every soul's hidden release or a promise that their own spirit supports healing. A result query may say “no method known”; it must not assert universal impossibility from catalogue absence. A superior source can remove uncertainty when its actual rule does so. The planner supplies no knowledge itself.

[Research/starts I1](../../../../worlds/RESEARCH_TALENTS_PROGRESSION_AND_STARTS_2026-10-13.md#i1-separate-the-players-intention-from-the-characters-knowledge) already separates ambition from supplied facts. Use that rule for free text; a player-side label does not teach the character modern terminology or solution details.

## A5: Roadmap notation and honest outputs

`OR` selects a route; `AND` requires every child; `->` marks an ordered dependency. Braces name a world-qualified method, not executable code. Every world sheet supplies small and large graphs, with successful consumption/delivery as the endpoint. “Large” means wider support or greater scope, not a mandatory higher personal rank.

Small and large examples may use independent eligible starts, not an assumed single actor who owns every source. Every leaf needs an actual acquisition/activity owner before later implementation. Distinguish **personal learned method**, **acquired faculty**, **external item**, **temporary effect** and **commissioned service**. Commissioning satisfies “obtain the result,” not “personally learn its power.” If the request requires self-execution, service branches remain visibly different and cannot satisfy it. Useful partial milestones retain their own outputs and costs when a goal changes.

Repeated study/practice can be queued under its real supports; teachers, stations and attention cannot serve two incompatible jobs at once. Offline advancement is a separate supported clock policy, not granted by these graphs. Use existing M4/PM policies rather than imposing a new universal timescale.

## A6: Hybrid means a verified interface

PP4 needs a named interface: what the power supplies to the device, what the device supplies back, and what prevents one from replacing the other. Spell formula plus substrate, chakra plus puppet joints, or material transmutation plus structural engineering are testable joins. A sensor must detect the actual signal; electricity is not automatically cursed energy; a rune-shaped drawing is not automatically an enchantment.

Cross-world carriers can shorten a route only after local compatibility is qualified. A carried blueprint transfers information, not innate anatomy, a soul partner, an Aspect or a pathway characteristic. Preserve the actual source exceptions instead of forcing all sources into an equal-cost economy. Build, operation and maintenance remain distinct (PA1–PA3).

## A7: Evidence labels and canon boundaries

**SHOWN**: located event/result in primary text or an identified official adaptation. **STATED**: a named speaker, official rules text or explicitly identified secondary wiki reports it. **INFERRED**: interpretation or new design. **UNVERIFIED**: source question not resolved. SHOWN does not mean the analyst reread the whole source this turn. Local sheets identify when they rely on prior located readings or ledgers. A wiki's scene report stays STATED (wiki), even if the described event would be SHOWN in its primary source.

Default continuities are named in each sheet. Do not mix manga with filler, FMA 2003 with manga/2009, Tensura WN with LN/anime or D&D editions. Existing blocked digests stay blocked; later source ledgers may have better, separately qualified evidence. Private novel notes were consulted for locators and paraphrase only; their prose and raw book text are not reproduced. No sealed world truth was opened or disclosed.
