# A13: Naruto

INFERRED roadmap proposals, 2026-10-14. Baseline: original manga ch.1–700; Boruto scientific-tool developments and anime filler are separate profiles. [Notation](../SHARED_VOCABULARY.md).

**Rules and design boundary.** SHOWN, via bounded official manga locators: tree-climbing control (ch.18), Rasengan rotation/output/control (ch.150–154), nature-change development (ch.319, 339, 341). [N1/N2/N14/N15](../../../../../worlds/naruto/SOURCE_LEDGER.md:21). Players can train shape/control/nature applications and engineer puppets. A copied motion supplies neither chakra capacity nor a bloodline/body source. Do not infer a new kekkei genkai just by combining learned elements; exact acquisition law is UNVERIFIED beyond [N5/N12](../../../../../worlds/naruto/SOURCE_LEDGER.md:25). Clone-assisted training is an exceptional supported option with its actual costs, not every student's entitlement.

**PP2 nodes.** Chakra-bearing body, usable reserve, control, shape/nature method, teacher/notes, suitable anatomy, actual inheritance when required, seals/contracts and tool components. Shinobi rank is not a source node granting every technique.

**Small: adhere to a tree while moving.**

```text
Maintain foot contact on a vertical tree
 AND eligible chakra body + fine foot-flow control + accessible tree
 OR qualified teacher's drill / supported independent practice
 -> test balance and adhesion while stepping
 -> learned control method, not automatic medical or bloodline mastery
```

Climbing gear is an alternative ascent method, explicitly a different result if the target demands chakra adhesion.

**Large: develop a nature-enhanced Rasengan application.**

```text
Combine supported shape and nature manipulation
 AND learn/train rotation + output + containment -> usable Rasengan
 AND acquire/train the selected nature change + compatible body/reserve
 OR sequential practice
 OR clone-assisted trials, only with owned clone method and sufficient reserve
 -> controlled integration trial -> test actual delivery and bodily aftermath
```

The canon wind-development example supports this research family, not a guarantee that every nature combination yields a viable new technique. A failed integration still leaves useful shape/nature competence. Delivery safety and a ranged version need their own source evidence.

**PP4 hybrid.** Puppet body/joints plus learned chakra control and actual mechanisms, anchored by Sasori's crafting [N7](../../../../../worlds/naruto/SOURCE_LEDGER.md:27). A proposed utility puppet can manipulate cargo without inheriting a human puppet's exceptional source properties. Boruto storage tools are excluded from this baseline unless separately selected.

**PP3 hidden view.** A novice sees taught drills; unseen nature methods, clan secrets and summoning terms remain hidden. The player recognizing a jutsu name does not grant its signs or safe body. The blocked [digest](../../../../../research/power-systems/wiki-digests/DIGEST_naruto.md:7) adds no fresh verification; the official source ledger is the stronger bounded evidence. Full chapter reread, numerical costs and gameplay remain open.
