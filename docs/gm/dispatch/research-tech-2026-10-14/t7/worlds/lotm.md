# A17: Lord of the Mysteries

INFERRED roadmap proposals, 2026-10-14. Original LOTM potion-pathway baseline; Circle of Inevitability boon mechanics require a separate profile. [Notation](../SHARED_VOCABULARY.md).

**Rules and design boundary.** SHOWN in prior located original-novel readings: Seer acquisition/training (vol.1 ch.31–32); STATED (Roselle/Klein) acting and assimilation differ from merely using a power (vol.1 ch.59). STATED in prior located pathway records: Artisan mechanical/extraordinary-item work (collection M0220/M0277, vol.2 ch.60; M0631, vol.3 ch.133). Players can refine applications/occult procedures under actual pathway faculties and design items when a supported crafting source exists. They cannot write a new potion pathway by desired function, substitute generic XP for characteristics or arbitrarily mix pathways. [Digest](../../../../../research/power-systems/wiki-digests/DIGEST_lord-of-mysteries.md:7) remains a blocked lead, not fresh verification.

**PP2 nodes.** Actual pathway/sequence faculties, spirituality, authentic formula/characteristic, assimilation, applicable ritual, occult instruction, compatible object/material, item-crafting faculty and source-specific adverse effects. Acting, control, sequence and institutional access are separate.

**Small: a bounded divination inquiry.**

```text
Seek a clue about one admissible question
 OR eligible Seer performing a learned pendulum/dream method
    AND actual spirituality + method + meaningful evidence/question
 OR commission an eligible diviner
    AND disclosed question/evidence + payment/access + reported result
 -> interpret the clue -> compare with independent evidence
```

The output is a clue, not guaranteed truth. If the player lacks Seer faculties, obtaining them is its own formula/acquisition/assimilation route, not a study shortcut. High-status targets/interference require exact source qualification before a trial.

**Large: create a mechanical occult aid with a declared function.**

```text
Produce a source-qualified extraordinary device
 AND define supported effect + identify an actual usable effect source
 OR eligible Artisan's design/build project
    AND acquired crafting faculties + studied method + real compatible materials
    AND mechanics/workshop + trials + operating instructions
 OR commission a qualified Artisan
    AND specification + agreed inputs/payment + delivered tested device
 -> test function, user eligibility and source-specific consequences
```

The two examples use independent eligible starts; a Seer does not acquire Artisan faculties merely by doing this project. INFERRED application family, not a shown arbitrary “wish-to-item” recipe. If the requested function lies outside the provider's source, research a supported source or revise the target. A successful mundane mechanism can remain useful while enchantment is unresolved.

**PP4 hybrid.** Mechanical regulator plus an actual extraordinary component and eligible craftsman. Carrying a clock drawing supplies no characteristic or automatic spirituality interface. A device's custody can transfer without transferring its maker's pathway.

**PP3 hidden view.** Unknown formulas, ingredients, acting principles, higher-sequence rituals and hazardous true identities stay hidden. Attribution matters: a diviner's interpretation may be wrong. Pathway-switch rules need exact stage/group evidence, not a universal graph edge. Novel locators come from prior targeted readings; exact new device construction and costs remain UNVERIFIED. No potion quantities or procedures are supplied.
