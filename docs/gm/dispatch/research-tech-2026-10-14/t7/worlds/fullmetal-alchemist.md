# A21: Fullmetal Alchemist

INFERRED roadmap proposals, 2026-10-14. Manga/2009 Brotherhood baseline; 2003 continuity is separate. [Notation](../SHARED_VOCABULARY.md).

**Rules and design boundary.** STATED (wiki, secondary): alchemy separates comprehension, deconstruction and reconstruction; available matter, technical understanding and a valid activation method matter [DM2](../../../../../research/power-systems/wiki-digests/DIGEST_fullmetal-alchemist.md:81), [ledger:18](../../../../../worlds/fullmetal-alchemist/SOURCE_LEDGER.md:18). Players can design transformations of supported materials and study new composition/circle methods. They cannot obtain missing matter from a name, guarantee human resurrection with enough research or treat circle-free execution as ordinary drawing practice. Exceptional sources require their actual acquisition/cost. Philosophers' Stones spend a stored source rather than proving costless creation [DM1]. No taboo procedure is provided.

**PP2 nodes.** Composition/structure knowledge, actual suitable matter, comprehension, selected circle/activation method, usable local alchemical source, target contact/access, control and physical inspection. State certification grants office/access, not automatic metaphysical capacity.

**Small: repair a cracked nonliving metal fitting.**

```text
Restore a declared fitting's material continuity
 AND composition knowledge + actual fitting/material + eligible operator
 OR valid circle-based method
 OR an already-owned source-qualified circle-free method
 -> trial transformation -> inspect dimensions/continuity -> fit-test
```

The exact fitting operation is INFERRED from sourced reconstruction, not a shown named repair spell. Without compatible matter/control, a smith's repair is another legitimate result, not alchemical mastery.

**Large: rebuild a load-bearing water crossing.**

```text
Provide a usable small bridge
 AND survey load/ground/span + actual suitable matter + access/work plan
 OR qualified alchemical shaping in supported sections
    AND material knowledge + activation/source + control + structural design
 OR mundane construction with alchemy for selected compatible parts
    AND builders/tools + section transformations + assembly
 -> inspect joins/foundation -> test load envelope -> open the crossing
```

INFERRED engineering use, not a canon instant-bridge guarantee. Useful repaired supports remain before the crossing is complete. Visible shape does not certify structural safety; alchemy need not run while the finished ordinary bridge is used.

**PP4 hybrid.** Surveying, molds/jigs and load design plus real material transformation. Alkahestry sensing/healing cannot be treated as identical to Amestrian material alchemy. Chemistry knowledge helps comprehension without granting an activation source.

**PP3 hidden view.** Unknown composition, activation diagrams and source infrastructure stay hidden. A confident textbook energy explanation can remain an attributed model. [Ledger:21–23](../../../../../worlds/fullmetal-alchemist/SOURCE_LEDGER.md:21) records contradictory energy accounts and continuity-sensitive interference; this sheet leaves the disputed underlying account unresolved. Exact source, material limits and circle design need primary-continuity checks before implementation. No real dangerous procedures or gameplay claims.
