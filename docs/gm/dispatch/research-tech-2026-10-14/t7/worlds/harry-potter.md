# A15: Harry Potter

INFERRED roadmap proposals, 2026-10-14. Seven-novel baseline; official companion pages are separately labelled corroboration, not game-only spell rules. [Notation](../SHARED_VOCABULARY.md).

**Rules and design boundary.** SHOWN in novel examples: taught wand-light; Snape's authored spells (Half-Blood Prince, ch.28); linked D.A. coins using the Protean Charm (Order of the Phoenix, ch.19). Official companion [Snape overview](https://www.harrypotter.com/features/the-shrewd-skills-of-severus-snape) corroborates invention, but does not supply a universal spell compiler. STATED (Hermione, Deathly Hallows ch.15), secondarily recorded in [DH5](../../../../../research/power-systems/wiki-digests/DIGEST_harry-potter.md:33): food cannot be created from nothing, though existing food can be moved/multiplied. Players can learn charms, improve methods and investigate new spells. Invented Latin alone cannot establish a spell; existing source limits remain. Numeric mana economy and a full invention algorithm are UNVERIFIED.

**PP2 nodes.** Actual magical faculty, wand/catalyst compatibility, learned incantation/movement where applicable, understanding/control, teacher/text, real target/item and charm-specific limits. Wand wood research is not a universal source-grant recipe [H2/H3](../../../../../worlds/harry-potter/SOURCE_LEDGER.md:13).

**Small: a controllable reading light.**

```text
Illuminate a page with wand-light
 AND magical faculty + usable wand + Lumos execution
 OR teacher demonstration/practice / accessible spell instruction/practice
 -> test lighting position and extinguishing
```

A mundane lamp is a separate functional route; it does not teach wand-light or create magical capacity. Altering color/duration needs its own test rather than automatic modifier tags.

**Large: send a coded meeting alert to a known group.**

```text
Create a linked coin alert system
 AND learn the Protean Charm + suitable coins + control/source eligibility
 AND agree a code + distribute actual receivers + identify update authority
 OR qualified self-enchantment with trials
 OR commission a compatible enchanter + delivered receivers + operator lesson
 -> change the authorized coin -> verify each receiver's matching alert
```

Canon supplies a working network example, not unlimited range, voice transmission, perfect secrecy or every object's safe enchantability. Receiver acknowledgment is a proposed protocol, not a new spell granted by the charm.

**PP4 hybrid.** Charm-linked metal tokens plus stamping, a mundane codebook and distribution. A mechanical printing workflow assisted by separate eligible charms is an INFERRED target; it needs exact object/movement compatibility. Do not automatically import reliable electronics into Hogwarts's magical environment.

**PP3 hidden view.** The actor sees taught light or a witnessed alert token. Unknown charm instructions, receiver identities and undisclosed enchanted properties stay hidden. A claimed wand compatibility stays a claim until a suitable trial. Novel locators are prior/source-known examples, not a fresh full-book reading; exact spell invention steps, network limits and electronics compatibility remain unverified.
