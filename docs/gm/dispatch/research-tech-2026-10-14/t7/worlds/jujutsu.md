# A12: Jujutsu Kaisen

INFERRED roadmap proposals, 2026-10-14. Baseline: source-qualified manga rules; adaptation or AU additions require a separate label. [Notation](../SHARED_VOCABULARY.md).

**Rules and design boundary.** STATED (wiki, secondary): fuel/control, body-engraved innate techniques, learned barriers, and ritual activation differ [ledger:20–26](../../../../../worlds/jujutsu/SOURCE_LEDGER.md:20). Domains have different completeness, procedures and burnout [DJ7/DJ9](../../../../../research/power-systems/wiki-digests/DIGEST_jujutsu-kaisen.md:110). Players can train reinforcement/barriers and research applications of their **actual** technique. They cannot select an unrelated innate formula by typing an effect, buy it with arbitrary vow points or choose Heavenly Restriction as ordinary training. Extension feasibility must follow the named technique; positive-energy production does not automatically teach technique reversal or external healing.

**PP2 nodes.** Actual CE-bearing body, reserve/output/control, learned method, innate formula where relevant, compatible target, barrier geometry/predicate, ritual skill, domain embedding and technique-specific aftermath. No universal CE level or vow price table.

**Small: reduce injury from a declared physical impact.**

```text
Train a local reinforcement use
 AND eligible sorcerer body + available CE + flow control
 OR teacher-guided region reinforcement / supported self-practice
 -> timed reinforcement trial against one known impact
 -> retain method/control evidence, not a new innate technique
```

Ordinary padding is another protection result, not reinforcement mastery. A zero-CE body needs its own source-supported protection route.

**Large: enforce a nonviolent evidence hearing through Deadly Sentencing.**

```text
Conduct the source-supported domain hearing
 AND actually owned Deadly Sentencing source + CE/control + eligible subjects
 AND supported domain execution + its violence prohibition/rule explanation
 OR learn/refine execution from actual accessible source knowledge
 OR use an already-qualified source-holder start
 -> establish the domain -> present evidence/statements -> resolve its procedure
 -> apply only the source-supported verdict/sanction routing and aftermath
```

STATED (wiki) [DJ5](../../../../../research/power-systems/wiki-digests/DIGEST_jujutsu-kaisen.md:98) supplies this concrete procedural-domain example. The judge and caster have different knowledge; this is not perfect truth acquisition for the player. The eligible source-holder can refine execution/handling, not redesign a new court power from a generic barrier or vow. A visitor without that source can pursue actual acquisition evidence or commission its holder's service, where possible; neither trains the innate formula into existence. Custom extensions and exact acquisition remain UNVERIFIED.

**PP4 hybrid.** Physical site plan, screens and an instrumented barrier trial; cursed tool plus its actual bearer and trained handling. Electronics do not generate CE or acquire an innate technique. Exact tool-enchantment manufacture remains UNVERIFIED.

**PP3 hidden view.** The actor sees observed/learned reinforcement or barrier methods. Enemy domains, private innate formulae and unknown vow terms stay absent. A theory about barrier detection stays attributed until tested. Early brief shorthand about “barrierless” open domains is not authority: [live brief qualifier](../../../../../worlds/jujutsu/WORLD_BRIEF.md:34) defers to the detailed ledger. All factual rules above remain secondary evidence; primary manga verification is open.
