# A20: Frieren

INFERRED roadmap proposals, 2026-10-14. Baseline qualified to the existing manga-derived secondary snapshot, dated 2023-12-12; later manga/anime additions require evidence. [Notation](../SHARED_VOCABULARY.md).

**Rules and design boundary.** STATED (wiki revisions, not primary authentication): spells use mana and can be learned/recorded; Blue-Moon Weed research requires observing a real specimen; public Zoltraak analysis led to defensive magic/equipment [ledger:13,19,37](../../../../../worlds/frieren/SOURCE_LEDGER.md:13). Players can study, collect and research supported spells. They cannot make every desired effect true by visualization alone, obtain another character's special copy faculty from empathy lessons or bypass a source-specific observation gate with generic research XP. Exact universal spell-invention/visualization law is UNVERIFIED here.

**PP2 nodes.** Personal mana/control, accessible spell/teacher/grimoire, supported visualization/execution for the selected spell, real phenomenon/specimen, investigation time and actual compatible target. Goddess magic/curses require separate local rules, not universal mage tags.

**Small: reproduce Blue-Moon Weed flowers with the sourced magic.**

```text
Research the specimen-qualified flower effect
 AND compatible mage + personal mana/control + formulation competence
 OR locate a specimen through clue-led field search
 OR arrange legitimate observation of an already-held real specimen
 -> personally observe the specimen -> formulate/test the method
 -> test the declared flower result
```

The observation/formulation chain follows the reported research example; the arranged visit is an INFERRED acquisition alternative and must supply a real specimen. Whether grimoire/teacher transfer removes this spell's personal observation requirement is UNVERIFIED, so it is not an executable shortcut here. A different blue plant does not satisfy the target.

**Large: develop protection against a known magical attack.**

```text
Make a repeatable local counter to the observed attack
 AND identify the actual attack + evidence + intended protection envelope
 OR research/formulate supported defensive magic
    AND eligible mage + mana/control + observation + trials
 OR engineer supported protective equipment with qualified researchers
    AND material craft + compatible enchantment knowledge + trials
 -> test attack/counter matchup -> teach or distribute the tested result
```

INFERRED local project based on the reported public Zoltraak diffusion, not a guarantee against every curse, physical force or new spell. A research paper is a useful intermediate, not tested protection. Publication also lets others learn the counter.

**PP4 hybrid.** Physical protective equipment plus a source-supported enchantment; planting/survey tools plus spell research. Protective magic does not automatically replace structural durability or generate limitless passive mana.

**PP3 hidden view.** Unknown specimens, grimoire contents and concealed mana remain absent. The actor's failed search does not reveal the specimen's true location. A counter's reputation is not evidence of its present matchup. [Early digest](../../../../../research/power-systems/wiki-digests/DIGEST_frieren.md:7) was blocked; later ledger evidence is separately qualified, not a retroactive upgrade. Primary chapters, transferred observation requirements and exact equipment interfaces stay open.
