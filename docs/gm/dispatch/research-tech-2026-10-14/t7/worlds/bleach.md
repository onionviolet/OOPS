# A14: Bleach

INFERRED roadmap proposals, 2026-10-14. Original manga baseline with separately identified official anime corroboration. Ordinary Soul Reaper route here; Quincy/Arrancar/Fullbring need their own adapters. [Notation](../SHARED_VOCABULARY.md).

**Rules and design boundary.** STATED (official adaptation glossary): Shikai involves dialogue/attunement, Bankai manifestation/subjugation; spiritual demand and personal weapon binding matter [B5–B7](../../../../../worlds/bleach/SOURCE_LEDGER.md:25). SHOWN/STATED (official bios): Kido specialists and research institutions exist [B2](../../../../../worlds/bleach/SOURCE_LEDGER.md:22). Players can learn eligible Kido and develop execution of their own release; they cannot freely choose another soul's release by function or purchase its bond by buying its sword. Exact Kido invention rules and release-generation laws are UNVERIFIED, not universally prohibited.

**PP2 nodes.** Eligible spiritual body/source, spiritual capacity/control, learned Kido, activation/incantation, real target, weapon/source custody, dialogue/name/attunement and release-specific development. Military office is not automatic Bankai.

**Small: restrain a target with an eligible binding Kido.**

```text
Cast one sourced binding method
 AND eligible spiritual actor + supported binding spell + fuel/control
 OR taught full invocation / a separately verified trained shortened mode
 -> practice -> test its actual target, range and hold conditions
```

This is learning/refining a source-qualified spell, not authoring any arbitrary numbered Kido. Select the exact spell passage before implementation. Incantation omission cannot be assumed free or equally strong.

**Large: develop a personal Bankai toward a useful task.**

```text
Make one's actual release reliably serve a declared task
 AND eligible actor + actual bonded weapon + Shikai understanding
 AND source-supported manifestation/subjugation development
 OR ordinary training route
 OR exceptional training apparatus, only after its exact case is verified
 -> acquire the actual release -> test whether its functions meet the task
 -> refine a supported mode, or revise the goal without rewriting the spirit
```

A desired “protect the group” effect is a research intention, not a guarantee that this soul's Bankai is defensive. A compatible Kido specialist can provide protection as a service; that does not grant the player the specialist's release. Broken Bankai repair cannot default to ordinary sword repair [B6].

**PP4 hybrid.** Proposed spiritual-reading instrument, workshop and trained operator, or protective fittings plus supported Kido. Research institutions justify an investigation; they do not verify a universal soul scanner or factory-made Bankai recipe.

**PP3 hidden view.** Unknown names, unobserved release functions and another soul's eligibility stay hidden. An apparent incomplete name remains a belief pending actual dialogue. Ordinary repair/release restoration are separate questions. The [blocked digest](../../../../../research/power-systems/wiki-digests/DIGEST_bleach.md:7) is weaker than the later official-source ledger; exact primary manga procedures and instrument interfaces remain open.
