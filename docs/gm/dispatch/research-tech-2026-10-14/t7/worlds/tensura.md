# A19: Tensura and a nonhuman power start

INFERRED roadmap proposals, 2026-10-14. Baseline: Fuse's Japanese WN, Syosetu n6316bn; collection member IDs are not printed episode numbers. LN/anime/game abilities are separate profiles. [Notation](../SHARED_VOCABULARY.md).

**Rules and design boundary.** SHOWN in located prior WN readings: water-propulsion experiments, body damage despite pain immunity, analyzed faculties and body-dependent expression (members 2–9). STATED (Great Sage): intake, analysis, storage, mimicry and isolation have distinct functions; copies need material and analyzed information [TM1/TM2](../../../../../worlds/NONHUMAN_PROTAGONIST_MECHANICS_2026-10-14.md:19). Players can experiment with their actual form and combine abilities where an owned source supports it. An ordinary slime does not automatically own Rimuru's Predator, Great Sage or synthesis. Knowledge of an anatomy does not grant its mass, organs or expert technique. The [official portal](https://www.ten-sura.com/character/rimuru) corroborates Rimuru's exceptional grant/evolution in its own adaptation profile, not these WN pipeline details.

**PP2 nodes.** Actual nonhuman body, mass/material, magicules, senses/manipulation, owned intake/analysis faculty, acquired data/template, expressive anatomy, compatible control and explicit synthesis/evolution source where required. Skill names and grades alone do not supply missing operations.

**Small: propel a slime through water.**

```text
Move a declared short route by water propulsion
 AND actual form able to hold/expel water + water access + body integrity
 OR controlled independent trials / eligible mentor-guided trials
 -> directional control -> test stopping and collision aftermath
```

A source-qualified early Rimuru-like snapshot can use this route; it is not a grant to all slimes. A carrier/boat is an alternative transport result, not bodily propulsion mastery.

**Large: produce and apply a copied useful material.**

```text
Operate an absorption-assisted material workshop
 AND actual intake/analysis/copy faculties + accessible supported sample
 AND compatible material supply + storage/handling + operating knowledge
 OR analyze then manufacture through the owned pipeline
 OR combine the actor's supported stage with a qualified craftsperson's work
 -> prototype -> test composition/function -> repeat with actual inputs
```

Prior WN analysis/production synergy supports this family [TM4](../../../../../worlds/NONHUMAN_PROTAGONIST_MECHANICS_2026-10-14.md:61). A target such as a repaired fitting still needs its own compatible material test. No free copying of souls, offices, knowledge or ultimate skills follows. Integration requires its actual ability, not a generic “combine” verb.

**PP4 hybrid.** Analyzed material plus forge, molds, fitting tools and a craftsperson; tendrils or another compatible body can manipulate tools if actually available. Parallel Sage calculation can accelerate a supported job without revealing concealed facts or creating missing input stock.

**PP3 hidden view.** A touch-only beginning cannot expose remote sample composition or a catalogue of evolved forms. Data hidden from the actual analysis source stays hidden. The planner cannot name undiscovered synthesis branches. Early limited memory transfer and later exceptions retain their stages [TM2:35]; do not promote either to a universal rule. No new WN body reread, exact production recipe or gameplay verification.
