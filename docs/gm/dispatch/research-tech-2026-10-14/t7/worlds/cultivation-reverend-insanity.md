# A11: Cultivation through Reverend Insanity

INFERRED roadmap proposals, 2026-10-14. One specific cultivation system: Gu Zhen Ren's original novel, not a blend of qi, cores and unrelated Dao rules. [Notation](../SHARED_VOCABULARY.md).

**Rules and design boundary.** STATED (revision-backed wiki): Gu grade, host capacity, activation essence and feeding are distinct [DR1](../../../../../research/power-systems/wiki-digests/DIGEST_reverend-insanity.md:74). SHOWN/STATED in prior located novel readings: White Jade Gu refinement (ch.98–100); killer moves combine Gu (ch.299); Gu houses are structured combinations (narrator, ch.568). Players can research recipes and compose moves using real Gu and sufficient attainment; they cannot rename an unsupported function into existence, replace a missing core with generic XP or duplicate an already-existing unique Immortal Gu (ch.537). Recipes can be incomplete or fail. Exact costs are not selected.

**PP2 nodes.** Aperture/host capacity, compatible essence, controlled living Gu, core/support arrangement, refinement recipe, real materials, path attainment, dao-mark compatibility, food supply and operating skill. Refining a new Gu changes the source; a killer move coordinates existing sources. These are different outputs.

**Small: withstand a known shallow physical strike.**

```text
Provide a tested skin-defense effect
 OR acquire an existing compatible defensive Gu
    AND trade/inheritance access + bind/control + host essence + Gu care
 OR obtain White Jade Gu through its known refinement route
    AND authentic recipe + actual constituent Gu + refinement competence
    AND paid trial -> successful refinement, or retained failure evidence
 -> practice activation -> test against the declared strike envelope
```

White Jade is a shown defense source, not guaranteed invulnerability. Acquisition alone does not establish execution against every material or rank.

**Large: mobile shelter with a defensive function.**

```text
Carry and protect an expedition group
 OR design a supported Gu-house arrangement
    AND shelter/movement/defense sources + arrangement knowledge
    AND compatible activation/control + construction trials + care supply
 OR acquire/commission an existing suitable house
    AND provider + verified actual functions + price/custody + operator training
 -> test occupancy, movement, one defense case and sustained supply
```

INFERRED composite goal from narrator's ch.568 house examples, not a certified new house recipe. Missing movement Gu leaves a useful stationary shelter; substituting a cart changes the output architecture, not the Gu's power.

**PP4 hybrid.** Gu effect plus mundane chassis, cargo fittings and a care/supply organization. Tools can assist precise refinement without replacing attainment or essence. Do not turn an ordinary machine into an Immortal Gu by labeling it one.

**PP3 hidden view.** A clan manual may disclose a recipe while concealing better support substitutions. Unseen Gu, hidden houses and advanced attainment routes stay absent. A rumor of a powerful Gu does not reveal its diet or safe host requirement. Source feeding remains a local living-tool rule; comfortable player unfed play/no hunger death is unchanged. Novel evidence is inherited located reading, not a fresh full-book audit.
