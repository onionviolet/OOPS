# A16: D&D

INFERRED roadmap proposals, 2026-10-14. Revised fifth-edition profile, SRD 5.2.1 / 2024 rules, with a separately selected setting. No cross-edition spell blending. [Notation](../SHARED_VOCABULARY.md).

**Rules and design boundary.** STATED (official rules): Mending fixes a limited break but cannot restore magic; Teleportation Circle requires spell eligibility, a known same-plane destination and consumed material, and permanence takes daily casting at the same place for 365 days. [Official spell descriptions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spell-descriptions#mending), Mending and Teleportation Circle sections, inspected 2026-10-14; [source contract C06/profile](../../../../../worlds/dnd/SOURCE_CONTRACT.md:33). Players can choose eligible spells and rules-supported applications. Novel spell invention requires explicit setting/DM authoring authority; a function request grants no class feature. The free rules do not establish arbitrary custom-spell invention.

**PP2 nodes.** Rules profile/revision, real class/feature eligibility, learned/prepared spell where required, slots when required, components, casting time, target and destination knowledge. Not every spell needs concentration or a slot. Wizard, innate, divine and pact acquisition are distinct routes.

**Small: repair one tear in a pack.**

```text
Restore a nonmagical tear within Mending's size limit
 AND eligible object + actual spell access + required components + casting time
 OR personally learned/available Mending
 OR hired eligible caster, with payment and access to the object
 -> repaired pack, without restored enchantment or transferred spell knowledge
```

Ordinary stitching is another repair method; require needle/material/skill rather than pretending it casts the spell.

**Large: establish a permanent transport destination and service.**

```text
Create and operate a permanent teleportation circle
 AND valid site + legitimate access + scheduled material supply
 OR personally eligible Teleportation Circle caster
 OR contracted eligible caster/provider, with actual daily availability
 -> cast at the same site daily for 365 days -> permanent destination
 AND operating caster + known destination sequence + each departure's costs
 -> deliver travelers during the actual portal window
```

A permanent destination is not a permanently open, self-powered portal. Schedule/provider failures are explicit; no invented rule for recovering missed days is selected. Each ordinary departure is separately supported.

**PP4 hybrid.** Surveyed terminal, etched site records, scheduling and passenger flow plus the actual spell. Mundane engineering supplies access/organization, not spell slots. Spell scroll or magic-item manufacture is UNVERIFIED in this sheet and requires its own selected crafting rules.

**PP3 hidden view.** Hidden sigil sequences, private NPC spell preparation and undiscovered spells stay absent. A known circle location does not reveal its sequence. Exact casting eligibility requires the selected feature/class rules before implementation. These two spell texts were inspected; no full class, setting, custom-spell or gameplay audit was run.
