**PP2 nodes.** Personal mana, shaping precision, structured invocation, formula/mathematics knowledge, a suitable inscribed substrate, charging/control method, teacher/library access and tested operating conditions. The player can learn and research formulae, shape mana and engineer a constrained application. They cannot treat knowing a diagram as mastery, draw unlimited fuel or assume an invocation lasts indefinitely. A novel new effect beyond these examples remains INFERRED until a local trial supports it.

**Small: controlled light for reading.**

```text
Illuminate the page on demand
 OR personal cast
    AND personal mana + light method + practiced brightness control
    OR learn from a teacher / study an accessible known method
 OR use a formula lamp
    AND held compatible lamp + its fuel/charge + learned controls
 -> read a page without blocking or overlighting it
```

Learning/casting yields a personal method; borrowing the lamp yields access, not shaping mastery. Test brightness adjustment and cessation. Known structured light is a baseline, not a claim that every novice can invent it.

**Large: a workshop's keyed, adjustable lighting system.**

```text
Maintain useful lighting at several workstations
 AND inspect layout + illumination needs + operator identities
 OR design/build local formula fixtures
    AND formula study + substrate craft + keyed controls
    AND legitimate charging supply + installation + maintenance access
 OR commission a qualified formula specialist
    AND specification + fee/materials + delivered fixtures + operator lesson
 -> test each key, occlusion/placement and sustained work interval
```

INFERRED scale-up of the ch.11 cube, not a shown factory installation. Standalone lamps are a useful intermediate even if shared power distribution fails. No free perpetual charge or automatically self-learning ward.

**PP4 hybrid.** Inscribed lamp plus shutters, mounting and workshop layout; a powered formula fixture does not supply its own joinery. A mana-train-inspired drive needs its own energy/ward compatibility test, not a free transplant from a remembered engine (novel ch.1).

**PP3 hidden view.** A novice sees the taught light exercise or held lamp controls. Unknown formula channels, charging alternatives and personal-key construction stay hidden until observed/studied. “My diagram works” may remain a hypothesis until the prototype lights under its actual controls. Exact cost/range and safe capacity are UNVERIFIED here; no generic mana numbers are selected.
