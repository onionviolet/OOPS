## Findings that change the design

**V1/V12/V13:** An AND/OR display, GOAP state search, HTN task decomposition and production scheduling are related but distinct. The roadmap should preserve “make” versus “obtain,” task order, capacities and current access. No reference justifies calling a word-combination result an executable invention.

**V16/V22:** Existing native glass, potash, charcoal, Animal fat, processed wood and component recipes shorten authored roadmaps. `A Glint On The Sand` already declares glass-related recipe and sand-activity unlocks. Broad cleaning, instruments, machines, paper/printing and communications still require selected consumers. Source-defined stock/unlocks are not proof of a reachable current browser journey.

**V20/V32:** Each worked target has two world-specific paths without asserting new book canon. Reed Basin is an original cultivation example, not a selected world or revived Jade Hollow. Its qi services can collapse particular nodes, while fabrication, recipients and institutions remain real requirements where relevant. Functional equivalents are identified honestly: a cleansing fixture is not literal soap; fictional healing is not antibiotics; a qi burst is not a fission weapon.

**V34–V38:** Deterministic play follows a frozen validated artifact, its laws/consumers and saved execution receipts. A seed or temperature setting cannot replace that artifact. Runtime authoring may help interpret goals or propose methods, but never makes unsupported mechanics work or supplies stock by itself.
