# T12: optional expertise, memory and teaching texture

2026-10-14. K1-K18 are original proposals grounded in [FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md), not implemented or accepted mechanics. No new global intelligence, memory, teaching or science bar is proposed.

## Shared contract under MU4

[MU4](../../../../TECHNOLOGY_AND_PROGRESSION.md#mu-showing-micro-detail-without-clutter) controls presentation from results to detailed texture. The dial does not turn extra chores into a requirement. At “just results,” costs, usable outputs, uncertainty that matters and available choices remain clear. Richer modes add cues, recollections and dialogue; the same selected work has the same eligibility, costs and outcomes at every setting. A purely presentational setting must not silently alter simulation.

These are optional encounters and pursuits riding existing actions. Any functional refinement is separately selected and declared; none lowers baseline competence to sell its recovery. Ordinary practice remains useful. No disuse decay, missed-day penalty, compulsory recall quiz, mandatory sleep interval, fabricated failed repeat or recurring upkeep is part of K1-K18. Established methods stay usable. The texture dial can hide narrative elaboration without hiding a meaningful choice or known risk.

[Means M1-M6](../../../../worlds/SKILL_MECHANISMS_AND_MEANS_2026-10-13.md) still decides whether a real tool, body, record or collaborator is available. A perfect-memory or complete-mastery means can genuinely solve its supported gap. NPCs use the same rule; delegated work trains its performer only when that learning has a cause. World incompatibility, missing physical support and memory difficulty remain distinguishable.

UI target if a later slice is selected: **1366x768 desktop/laptop**, one inline result/tooltip and a folded low-priority feed entry in the existing work or research view. Visible outcome: what cue or explanation changed and what useful operation now works. MU1/MU3/MU5 and RD3/RD5 remain owners. Mobile optimization, new screens and a full learning simulation are deferred. No rebuilt browser evidence was produced in this docs-only lane.

## K1. Operative cue

An optional lesson begins with an already working method and an actor who can demonstrate it. A fuller description supplies one previously omitted discrimination, such as hearing which wooden joint needs refitting. Offer observing a demonstration, consulting a fuller annotation, supported practice or commissioning the result. Show the cue only through the actor's actual sensing means; another body can use an equivalent accessible channel.

The optional new operation has a real consumer, such as fitting one usable component. A learner's successful work establishes transfer for that operation, not all TK5 mastery. Existing recipes remain available. A complete supported record can suffice; every copy does not need a tutor. This extends H6 and T4's S1 suggestion without repeating their historical apparatus.

## K2. The skipped step

During selected shared work, the learner asks why the teacher changed grip or rejected a specimen. The player can explain, demonstrate again, offer a clearer note or leave the question for later. A revised explanation can support the K1 operation or become a provenance line on an existing note. No blanket probability of lesson failure attaches to high skill, and a “patient” personality is not the sole cure.

Meaningful result: the recipient can identify the relevant cue or execute the selected step. A polite nod is not proof. The relationship remembers who clarified it; low detail shows the same taught result with fewer words.

## K3. Another workable route

An authored problem has both a familiar viable solution and a known alternative that saves a real input or better suits the current means. Optional talk-through or comparison notices the difference. Retain both choices and disclose their costs. A novice may notice an unusual angle, but experience can also protect against fixation.

Do not conceal a player-known choice, force repeated failure or make all expert actions slower. RV6 can suggest changing approach when supported evidence warrants it; it cannot impose a universal plateau timer. The comparison is the interesting decision, not clearing a “fixated” meter.

## K4. Describe what it is made of

An optional inspect view describes a known item's mass, shape, material or fastening rather than only its familiar role. If the existing means genuinely support the alternative, offer one use, such as a known heavy object serving as a counterweight in a fictional workshop fixture.

Discovery opens a tested affordance or an alternative preparation route. It does not convert every item into arbitrary physics or create matter. Once learned, the use stays reachable; the player does not repeat a creativity puzzle each time. PI1/PI6 retains function vocabulary and feasibility ownership.

## K5. Competence without compulsory stages

One task's descriptions shift from general cue to relevant distinction as evidence of practice accumulates: “the joint moves” can become “the looseness is at this seat.” Select cues by the task's knowledge and means, without automatically mapping global XP to five Dreyfus stages.

A veteran may ask for a detailed explanation of unfamiliar material, and a novice may already hold exact information through a record or faculty. Optional recognition uses the demonstrated achievement. G7 titles can exist where fitting, but neither a social title nor a model label grants execution.

## K6. Diagnose, then practice through use

A known method's feedback identifies one useful difficulty. A voluntary practice order targets that operation using actual accessible tools and permitted stock, ideally producing ordinary useful output. A capable teacher, annotated exercise or genuine supported self-diagnosis can guide it. The completion report names the improvement and its tested conditions.

The result can improve that operation's consistency or expand one application only if a later selected contract supplies a real consumer. Numbers stay unset here. Ordinary production continues without these drills, failures need not grant progress, and no universal 10,000-hour requirement or talent ceiling is inferred.

## K7. Recollection and record are separate

For an authored optional conversation, the character remembers a method's purpose but is uncertain which detail belongs to an old account. An available intact note supplies that detail automatically if the player selects the related work; opening the optional recollection text is not a prerequisite.

Use an unresolved story detail or uncertain imported recollection, rather than deleting a learned recipe or lowering native skill. Previously demonstrated mastery remains earned. Notes are not immunity tokens for an imposed decay system, and this lane introduces no note damage or archival upkeep.

## K8. A later encounter reinforces a memory

Ordinary later production or a chosen related activity recalls the earlier lesson: “That was the sound your mentor pointed out.” A folded dated entry can tie the two episodes together. If optional refinements are later selected, feedback can recognize that a method worked in a second context.

The actor's routine can perform any declared repetitions with one work order. No calendar reminders, exact due dates, streaks, expiring bonuses or penalty for a long real-world absence. The spacing literature informs the episode's plausibility; it does not require rewarding elapsed time without work or resources.

## K9. Carry a cue, keep the method

A familiar annotated tool, a sketch or an existing note prompts a line of recollection away from the original workplace. Optional varied-context work can record that the actor applied the method elsewhere. This is narrative support or evidence of generalization, not a mandatory portability upgrade.

Absence of the cue never disables an already learned method. Missing precision equipment may still prevent a genuinely equipment-dependent action, with that reason stated explicitly. Do not mislabel changed material, local physics or body as context-memory failure.

## K10. Two versions, one comparison

A selected research scene contains similar known methods with different contexts. An actor can momentarily cite the earlier method while discussing the later one, representing proactive interference; or cite the new detail when discussing the old one, representing retroactive interference. Keep these directions distinct in the author's event definition.

An optional side-by-side dated note clarifies which condition changed. The actor correction occurs before any paid production, without a hidden wrong-action roll. Neither method is lost. Detecting the difference can yield a useful revised explanation or a comparison receipt instead of a blanket memory penalty.

## K11. Teach-back by doing

A willing learner demonstrates one known operation or predicts a visible result during optional shared work. Corrective feedback points to the relevant cue; a full note, repeat demonstration or capable collaborator remains available. Abstract the character's recall attempt inside the work rather than requiring the player to recite facts.

A completed example can establish one competence receipt used by K16. Repeated inspections alone confer neither XP nor output; actual repeat work can remain repeatable under its stock/time contract. No fixed “once” reward cap or recurring examination is needed. Failure leaves already earned competence intact.

## K12. Prediction beside outcome

A selected test records the actor's forecast, assumptions and actual observed result, then presents them together. Prefer language such as “one observed case” or a supported range. A mismatch offers revise, compare with a known case or continue investigating. It does not charge an extra fee to clear embarrassment.

Keep forecast and world truth distinct; seeing a wide plausible range is not necessarily worse than seeing a narrow unsupported one. Small samples cannot prove calibration. All known committed costs remain exact even if estimated work duration is uncertain. Texture off keeps the meaningful uncertainty and result available.

## K13. Revision belongs to anyone

Any actor, including a master, novice or hired specialist, can revise a particular unsupported belief after K12 feedback. A willing apprentice can provide the decisive observation. Confidence comes from that belief's evidence and context, not a universal inverse relation to skill.

Use evidence labels or remembered reactions, without displaying a “Dunning-Kruger” status, arrogance score or inevitable valley-and-peak confidence curve. Underconfidence is allowed too. No diagnosis of an NPC's intelligence is inferred from one confident line.

## K14. A lead during ordinary life

A previously encountered unresolved question can receive an authored association during an already chosen walk, conversation, meal, rest or unrelated work. The folded event says what reminded the actor of the question and offers a plausible next investigation if known. A dream version requires that world's support.

The lead may be useful, inconclusive or wrong. It does not create stock, solve unknown science or reveal sealed truth. Repeated sleep is not a demanded strategy, and RV3 adds no compulsory delay. Any later clock-based implementation must use the selected branch's actual supported clock; elapsed closed-browser time is not automatically eligible.

## K15. Guidance that can shrink or return

An optional lesson offers “show the steps” and “give the short version.” Relevant demonstrated competence can suggest a concise default, while the player can restore detail at any time. Distinguish character expertise from player familiarity with controls.

Both versions disclose the same known costs, conditions, cancellation and consequences. Neither locks hints behind a rank nor punishes experts who choose support. A hidden trade secret still follows its discovery route; the detail control never becomes an omniscient hint button.

## K16. Apprentice a useful operation

Develop H6/S1 into one optional shared job: the teacher demonstrates a cue, the learner performs an eligible operation with support, and a later supported job establishes that they can perform it for a real recipient. Relationship texture records the teacher or coworkers who helped. The worker's wage, consent, time and custody follow their existing job contract.

A learner can eventually use fuller instructions, tools, a colleague or acquired mastery as appropriate. Hiring an already competent person and commissioning the output remain reasonable alternatives. No service-years timer, compulsory manual lesson clicks or automatic player learning from the worker's output. An heir inherits the record or contact through an actual carrier, not personal mastery by surname.

## K17. The label returns

A familiar object can cue the label of something already encountered, or point the actor to a known note. The result can be a small conversation or provenance beat. A feeling of knowing alone creates no confirmed fact; if the cue is only a lead, say so.

This is restricted lexical/story texture. No tip-of-the-tongue block on action buttons, no requirement to visit an old room to remember a recipe and no shortcut into a sealed answer. SH2 can point toward a route without telling its truth.

## K18. Keep the first good piece

An optional life-record or shelf line celebrates an actual first useful output. Inspection distinguishes “this piece works” from “repeated production is reliable.” Keeping it for MO8 conversation is optional; the ordinary output remains usable or saleable.

If a later ordinary attempt differs, explain only causes the actor can establish. Do not generate a guaranteed failure after success, pretend a first attempt proves mastery, or invent a general luck buff. A declared world-specific luck faculty can act according to its own source.

Observe whose execution changed and what they can do for a real recipient. These states extend the means owner's P1 and micro/macro J2 rather than create a new universal ladder.
