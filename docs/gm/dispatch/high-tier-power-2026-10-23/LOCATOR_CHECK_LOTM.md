## Wait time: what supports each number

- **Three years** (institutions F9): 0018 p56, Dunn says Nighthawks must wait three years on the last potion and pass the matching assessment before promotion, even with big merit. 0106 p55, the captain tells Klein to "endure three years" despite enough merit and says "not everyone is Dai". [168 p8-p13] does **not** carry the wait. It supports the senior-deacon examiner only. So F9 holds, but split its locators: examiner [168 p8, p13], wait [18 p56], [106 p55].
- **About a year**: the only year figures are Dai's own pace, one year from Corpse Collector to Gravedigger and one year to Spirit Medium (0150 p52), told by Klein from Dunn. This is a person's record, and 0106 p55 treats her as the exception. It is not a church waiting rule.
- **X3 in the institutions doc** (line 163): X3 is not about waiting time. It says a local office releases the next dose after a warden's assessment, an authored divergence from the senior-deacon examiner in F9. So "(source note X3)" cannot back "roughly a year".
- If the Q1 acting count wants a year, cite 0150 p52 as "Dai's pace, the fast case". The church default is three years per step with an assessment.
