## MT5: closed-form catch-up, causal stages and budget

PC4-T1, STATED: [runtime RT7/RT8/PF1/PF2](../../../../ENGINE_RUNTIME_PERFORMANCE_AND_STACK_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md) (`:101-130`) proposes tiered simulation, yielded chunks, equality for fast-forward and synthetic benchmarks. GR11/GR17 (`generation :41,47`) requires bounded generation and game-time addresses. [P8 idle gathering](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/IDLE_GATHERING.md:15) and [HV2 MODEL](../../harvest-modules-2026-10-14/hv2/MODEL.md) defer initial closed-browser progression. [Rules from scratch](../../../../RULES_FROM_SCRATCH.md:140) presents offline behavior as defaults/dials. This lane designs a future eligible catch-up path; it does not enable it or silently change absence/aging/danger laws.

PC4-T2, INFERRED: for an isolated fixed-recipe batch stage during a constant interval `D`, let `p` be saved partial work, `tau` work per batch, `r` effective work per eligible game-time unit, `a[k]` input per batch and `b[j]` output per batch. For a stage with no inputs arriving or outputs removed inside the interval:

```text
Ntime  = floor((p + r*D) / tau)
Ninput = min_k floor(stageSpendableInput[k] / a[k])
Nspace = min_j floor(freeOutputCapacity[j] / b[j])
N      = min(Ntime, Ninput, Nspace, targetAllowance, fundedBatchLimit)
```

`stageSpendableInput` counts this stage's own unconsumed escrow plus unreserved admissible stock, excluding every other job's reservation. Existing partially transformed jobs finish through their frozen law before this repeated-batch formula applies; do not debit their already consumed inputs again. Partial work persists only under the job's actual admission rule. At the first shortage/full/target/maintenance boundary, stop or change rates and recompute; do not accrue hypothetical productive work after a stop. Joint admission reserves inputs and receiver capacity so a second maker cannot invalidate these assumptions. Empty constraint sets are unbounded, not division by zero; zero/negative rates are handled separately. Fixed-point quantities and saved remainders preserve integer units (root MT11). Batch output appears at completion, never at admission.

PC4-T3, INFERRED: for genuinely fungible continuous flows, use `stock(t+D) = stock(t) + netRate*D` only until the earliest stock floor/cap, demand threshold, arrival, power/wage/wear exhaustion, freshness, weather/law or knowledge-sensitive policy boundary. Choose a feasible flow vector honoring all input, output and transport constraints, then integrate the shared interval. Negative-net stocks determine earliest depletion; positive-net stocks determine earliest capacity hit. A graph pass is enough for a proven constant acyclic component; shared inputs need a common allocator and cycles need their own proven solution. Do not clamp each node independently afterward because that can destroy conservation or create output.

PC4-T4, INFERRED: example that catches the MT5 error. Two raw units exist. Stage A needs one raw and two game minutes per intermediate; stage B needs one intermediate and three minutes per product. With independent stations and zero initial intermediates, A completes at minutes 2 and 4; B completes at 5 and 8. At minute 6, exactly one final product exists and B has one minute of paid progress. Giving B the two end-of-window intermediates at minute 0 falsely yields two products by minute 6. Serial player labor would differ again. Stage boundaries, concurrency and saved work are part of the law, not UI assumptions.

PC4-T5, INFERRED: proposed driver returns `{settledThrough, remainingEligibleTime, delta, receipts, nextBoundary, budgetUsed}`. Determine eligibility once through the clock owner; process a pure segment, publish stock/wages/XP/work/receipts atomically, then move its durable cursor. Yield with remaining time intact. Reload resumes from the committed cursor; it never repeats the completed interval or discards unsettled time. Wall time may estimate available absence outside the core, but scheduling/draws use world/life/site/actor/method revision plus game-hour/day or stable job IDs. A random failure sequence cannot be replaced by expected-value output when individual grades or receipts matter. Use exact bounded event evaluation or a separately declared aggregate law, not an undocumented approximation.
