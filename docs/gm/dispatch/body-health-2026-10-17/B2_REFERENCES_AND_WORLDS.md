### 1.2 What to take, what to avoid

| Code | Take (default, dial, reason) | Avoid or keep optional (and why) |
|---|---|---|
| G1 DF | **Named repairs.** Default: a healing source declares which conditions it fixes (bleeding, wound, fracture, infection, nerve, soul). Dial: worlds with generic "heal" can map it to all. Reason: makes medicine a choice and a craft target. **Syndrome curve**: start/peak/end plus severity is a cheap shape for poison, illness and potion side effects | Full tissue-layer simulation. It costs a lot and an idle text UI cannot show it; keep it as a dial for one harsh world at most |
| G2 RimWorld | **Hediff list with capacity modifiers.** Default: a body carries a list of conditions, each touching named capacities (move, manipulate, see, talk, think, channel). Reason: one model covers injury, illness, implant, addiction, pregnancy, curse. **Infection race**: immunity gain vs severity, raised by treatment quality and rest. Dial per world | Doctor-skill micro and operation failure that kills. Keep failure as a worse outcome or longer recovery by default; lethal surgery is a harsh-world dial |
| G3 Zomboid | **Indicators are derived, owners are fields.** Default: the status strip (tired, in pain, feverish) reads the condition list and never stores truth itself. Reason: AR rules want one authority per fact. **Local effects**: hand pain slows hand work, leg pain slows travel, reading unaffected | The untreatable-infection death clock. It contradicts AD19 as a default; fine as a forewarned optional world rule |
| G4 Kenshi | **Limb loss plus prosthetic as a progression path**, available in worlds whose tech or magic supports it (Red Basin, Salvage Reach, cyberpunk). Reason: injury becomes a door to a new body build, not only a loss. **Unconscious and recover** matches AD19 already | Bleeding out alone in the wild while idle. Keep offline risk paused or bounded (same reason as LotM P3 below) |
| G5 Qud | **Different owners and loss rules for similar powers**: a mutation lives in the body, a cybernetic in an installed part, a granted power in an item. Reason: body change, theft and transfer stay consistent. **Disease with a folk-cure quest**: the cure is knowledge you find | Random negative mutations forced without consent. Keep as a chosen risk or a world rule with warning |
| G6 Battle Brothers | **Injury restricts one kind of work and keeps the rest useful.** Default: most injuries block a few verbs and slow others; study, crafting or teaching stay open. **Role change from lasting injury** (veteran to tutor). Reason: injury makes a story instead of a dead period | Permadeath on the default path; BB's own packet already keeps it a selected campaign rule |
| G7 Darkest Dungeon | **Mind and body as separate bars**, with a threshold that produces a named state (affliction) and a rarer good state (virtue, resolve). **Quirk accretion**: long work leaves small lasting traits, good and bad. **Treatment buildings** cost time off the roster | Stress spirals that punish the only player. Default: stress relief is reachable and visible; harsh stress is a dial |
| G8 CK3 | **Physician as a relationship**: a named doctor with skill, method and loyalty; a folk healer may help or harm. **Epidemic as world event** with court exposure. **Dated aftereffects** for stress coping | A hidden health number that the player cannot read. Default: show the condition list; hide only what the world hides (diagnosis is a skill) |
| G9 Stardew | **Pass out, wake at home, small loss** is the forgiving baseline and already matches AD19 | Nothing to avoid; it is the floor |
| G10 RF5 | **Clinic as a place with a fee and an NPC**, and ailments that each have a matching cheap cure item | A huge ailment list. Keep a few per world |
| G11 Melvor | **Auto-eat at a threshold from a player-chosen stock**. This matches HUNGER C3 (player-selected reserve policy) | Food as the only healing in worlds with medicine or magic |
| G12 Long Dark | **Risk state before the condition** ("infection risk", "sprain risk") with a time window to treat. Reason: gives the player a chance to act, which suits idle play. **Specific cure items**, often foraged | Stacking many meters on one action; HUNGER C4 already warns against it |
| G13 Pathologic | **Medicine scarcity as a moral economy**: who gets the cure, at what price. **Diagnosis as play**. Best as a world-event or district crisis, not a constant | Constant scarcity in the home world; it fights the forgiving default |
| G14 ACS | **Breakthrough failure leaves injury but keeps progress** (retry count, partial gain). Reason: risk without wipe | Silent qi deviation; show pre-signs |
| G15 ToI | **Recovery shown as calendar cost before long work** ("this injury costs about 3 months of cultivation"). Reason: lets the player plan | Nothing major |
| G16 Taiwu | **Inner vs outer injury vs poison as separate tracks** with different cures (pills, acupuncture, rest, detox). **Element compatibility** makes the wrong method harmful | Exact numeric balancing from Taiwu tables; take shapes only |
| G17 Dungeon Meshi | **Resurrection as costly healing with fuel and failure conditions**, not a reset. Reason: matches "everything is possible, reasonably" with a cost | Treating it as universal; it is a local law of one dungeon there |
