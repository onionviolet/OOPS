## Lens (the north star's newer sections)

Use [LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md](../../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md): "Followers
and factions", "Work", "Aptitude can change", "Enemy powers", "Evil
inheritances, one-way openings and the way around", "How powers meet a new
world", and the world-card format in [WORLD_CARDS.md](../../../WORLD_CARDS.md).

1. **Recruiting and keeping people:** who can be recruited and how (hire,
   rescue, persuade, buy, free from slavery), their traits and backstories,
   what makes them leave or turn.
2. **Founding a place:** outposts and towns, building, production chains,
   raids against your base, how factions react to a new town.
3. **Factions and the world state:** faction relations, what happens when a
   faction leader is killed or captured (world states), how the map changes.
4. **Bodies:** limb loss, robotic limbs, races, starvation, slavery and
   prison as states you can live in and escape; stats trained by suffering.
5. **Being a nobody:** origins, starting hardship, how a nobody becomes
   someone, and every way out of a bad situation.
6. **Dialogue and reactions:** how NPCs react to who and what you are.
7. **Twenty or more small touches** not in the first pass, mapped to vanilla
   YAIR.
8. **A draft world card** for a Kenshi-inspired harsh world (original names).

## Output

A short plain-language REPORT first, findings with data locators, five to
