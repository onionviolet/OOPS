## Questions

1. **Rewinds and farming.** Every way sources allow time to be rewound,
   looped, saved or replayed. What carries back, what doesn't, how often,
   who notices. When is repeating something to farm rewards allowed, and
   what makes it fair or self-limiting (reward pools that deplete, the
   world changing in reaction, cost per loop, diminishing returns, rewards
   that only the first pass gives)? Propose a ladder of rewind powers from
   small to overpowered, each with the circumstances where it works.
2. **Reward choices.** How sources and games make overpowered rewards a hard
   choice: exclusive picks, timing, risk-scaled picks, offers drawn from
   pools, set bonuses, debts.
3. **Cheats with no exceptions.** Powers that truly have no exception or
   price, and how the story or game stays interesting anyway. How two such
   absolutes resolve when they collide.
4. **Jumpchain lessons.** Its structure (benefactor, jump documents,
   origins, perks, items, companions, drawbacks for points, scenarios,
   ten-year stays, warehouse, body mod, supplements, gauntlets, chain
   endings) and what its community learned about balance, power creep,
   drawbacks people actually enjoy, and what keeps long chains fun. Map each
   lesson to the life sim (lives, worlds, world cards, cheat cards, hubs).
5. **Time travel as a world feature.** Worlds where time travel exists,
   paradox rules, fixed versus changeable history, and how they could be
   world cards.

## Output

1. A short plain-language REPORT first.
2. A taxonomy table per question, with sources.
3. A proposed rewind ladder and a proposed reward-choice toolkit.
4. Five to ten ranked proposals for `docs/LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md`.
   Proposals are not decisions.
