**J1, HP known-lot preparation comparison.** This is an extension proposal around the existing HP owner, not new Frontier content. Target desktop 1920×1080: the player can compare two held lots, see supported preparation and costs, execute one preparation, brew, use the output and revisit the record. Mobile optimization is deferred. No browser proof produced here.

Start with two legally obtained lots for the existing selected brew. One is prepared by the player; another carries a supplier's preparation claim. The view names the claim and observed evidence without exposing hidden truth. A known method explains the required condition and apparatus; it does not promise equivalence from a generic functional tag. If author data needed for an estimate is absent, the estimate says unsupported; if the maker lacks evidence, it says uncertain. Those are distinct states.

The player pays the existing preparation's material/time costs and the local owner changes the selected lot. One controlled comparison varies preparation while holding other conditions fixed; the existing world resolver grades the product. The use route consumes an actual dose and produces its existing world-specific consequence, rather than awarding a new generic healing property. Failure retains paid costs and the owner's declared waste/recovery behavior. The record stores committed conditions and observations without upgrading an unverified supplier claim to knowledge.

Acceptance when selected: fresh-earned route or named fixture; successful preparation/use and an underprepared/unsupported comparison; stale-lot quote rejected without spending; no new stock owner; Save/reload/Export/literal Import of an in-progress preparation and final product; rebuilt desktop showing visible costs, uncertainty and outcome.
