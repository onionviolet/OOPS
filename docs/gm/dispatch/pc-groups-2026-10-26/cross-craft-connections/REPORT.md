## More-to-consider expansion, G4 round 1

These extend PC23–PC26/A7 as proposals. They are not new standing rules or a requirement for every craft.

| Code | New angle | Default, dial, reason and useful consumer |
|---|---|---|
| G4E1 | Route changes delivery, not just effect names. | A preparation retains composition, concentration and carrier compatibility; topical contact, an oral dose, a quench and an inscribed mark each read their relevant mechanisms. Vary exposed amount, contact time, absorption or charge where modeled. A harmful oral result need not be a useful weapon coating. Prove an effect at its existing damage/body/pattern reader before naming a new family. |
| G4E2 | Separation may concentrate trouble as well as the desired property. | Split the actual parent lot into allocated outputs, waste and retained apparatus contents. Track a contaminant where one consumer reads it; do not add universal impurity meters. If an assay consumes a sample, the tested amount leaves saleable stock. A more concentrated failed oral batch might remain useful as a material, but that requires its own causal property and reader. |
| G4E3 | Transmitted knowledge needs conditions and attribution. | Keep the demonstrated steps, apparatus and observed fit alongside the source/teacher claim. A copied alias can remain uncertain until compared; a faulty note should change the attempted process rather than rewrite material truth. Demonstration and sample comparison can correct one part. Losing the teacher leaves learning; losing a leased tool/power still removes its support. Avoid a school/relationship simulation for this slice. |
| G4E4 | Match the delivery function at the promised time. | A request describes a known task, quantity and delivery condition; a sample/assay/witness supports its acceptance. Customer belief and hidden function remain separate. A shelf-stable but opened parcel can differ from its sealed predecessor; expiry travels with custody. Quote inspection cost and partial acceptance where the transaction supports it. One existing requester is enough to test this. |
| G4E5 | Off-spec is relative to a consumer. | A weak extract can fail a medical purpose yet meet a material-treatment purpose; a brittle workpiece can be tempered or dismantled under existing means. Recovery spends heat, tools, time or solvent as applicable and declares waste. Containment can precede recovery where the product mechanism warrants it. A failed batch that left no recoverable stock stays spent; recovery does not mint the ledger's remembered residue. |

### One connected proposed player journey, J1

**PROPOSED, ORIGINAL book-world material-workshop branch**, with Harry Potter as the candidate context pending fit with its live owner. This is an ordinary-material demonstration, not a claimed canon brewing rule. The visible outcome is choosing how to use one processed oil lot and seeing two different downstream consequences. UI target, if selected: populated 1920×1080 desktop, with the current 1536×864/font checks as applicable; mobile optimization and prose rewrite remain deferred.

1. Read or receive a bounded treatment note, obtain an actual plant/oil-bearing lot and vessel, and extract a counted oil amount. Preview gives known quantities/costs and uncertain cues; the product retains its law revision and parent facts. SOURCE foundation: reference access/learning and lineage. Extraction/output allocation is proposed.
2. Allocate part to hide dressing and part to quenching a practice metal component. Extend the existing treatment and gear readers with local ORIGINAL book-world laws. Dressing reads softening/retention; quenching reads its heat-transfer behavior and workpiece state. The source mechanism, not its potion name, changes each result. Both are existing consumer types; their arbitrary-oil/book-world adapters are proposed.
3. Observe the leather's later quality/lining use and the worked component's actual wear/gear contribution. Use an off-spec comparison lot: it can fail the requested treatment while still supporting the other route if the measured properties justify it. Recover a brittle workpiece through temper or salvage; discard genuinely spent contents under the recorded process. No oral-effect copying.
4. Optionally deliver one result to an existing local requester against a simple functional specification and delivery condition. Transfer the exact allocated product, pay from the requester's finite purse, and keep observed quality/certification separate from truth. This step is later than the two-reader core; it does not create a new NPC-heavy prerequisite.

J1's minimum useful slice ends at step 3. Follow-up teaches the demonstrated local method through existing reference/learning owners, tests missing apparatus separately from learning, and only then adds the optional existing-requester acceptance. Book-world source/owner readiness chooses the actual setting; no need for Weibao to select between material styles.
