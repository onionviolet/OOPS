## More to consider: connected A6 expansion

**D1. ACCOUNT FOR NOW: history can be compressed without losing cause.** Store sufficient state for the next reaction, such as extracted fraction, suspended fraction and committed constituents, plus the bounded receipts needed for learned methods. Avoid an unbounded mouse-motion log. Derive what can be derived; freeze the law revision and irreversible facts. Raw click count should not multiply power when the same accumulated work gives the same state. Operation partition equivalence should be a deliberate law decision, given the checked availability compounding.

**D2. ACCOUNT FOR NOW: intervention changes the remaining opportunity.** Stopping heat early may preserve a volatile fraction while leaving extraction incomplete. Dilution changes concentration and usable volume without recreating lost constituent. Settling may permit a cleaner decant at the cost of yield. These are proposals under declared local laws; not every failed brew is recoverable. A rescue can produce a weaker but useful output and still teach a method.

**D3. ACCOUNT FOR NOW: observation and unattended policy are part of process depth.** Skill can improve which cue is noticed, its confidence and control precision without changing material truth. A saved quick method records what it actually did and what equipment it needed. A helper follows that program with its own competence, workload, stock and stopping policy. If no available means can diagnose the cause, “cloudier than the demonstrated batch” can be useful feedback without naming the hidden reagent. Idle settlement should follow the chosen policy on the owning game clock; simulation chunk size must not create extra stirring or skipped spending.

**J1. PROPOSED connected player journey, not dispatched.** Within the selected world's existing access/menu priority, the player obtains materials and a taught reference method, reads known preparation requirements and chooses a small batch. One unresolved stage offers a meaningful choice: agitate while extracting, or leave the suspension to settle before a later addition. An ORIGINAL law reads suspension/extraction and accumulated work, while opposite directions in a symmetric test vessel remain equivalent. The player notices an ambiguous texture cue, pays for one supported correction, then collects and uses a preparation through an existing consumer. The failed comparison produces a weaker usable result or declared waste. The demonstrated successful sequence becomes a quick method, including apparatus and stopping conditions, rather than granting universal recipe knowledge.

For a HP-first version, qualify p481 before choosing its addition stage, label causal fill ORIGINAL and keep the canonical formula identity intact. For an original Frontier experiment, use its existing oral or conditioning reader rather than inventing another product catalogue. These are conditional fits, not an either/or question or authority to bypass current priorities.
