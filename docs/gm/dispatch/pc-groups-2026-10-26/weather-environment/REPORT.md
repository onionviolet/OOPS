## Connected chains and their limits

**Making:** native day-count rain → anchored field facts → paid rack/cover and held wet vessels → retained consecutive drying → dry-vessel stock → collection for existing field work. Loss of native weather history refuses support; an exposed unfinished batch rewets without deleting the actual vessels. The focused field suite checks this, including paused rain, missing history, reload and long/split equality. Generic humidity and air movement are not part of that proof.

**Bodies:** native/E1 rain → roof/equipment/lining mitigation → native rain counter/Wet; cold temperature and Wet affect native cold-status timing. Separately, opt-in P23 gust phase → eligible current human body → stamina cost/Cold resistance XP. `statusCareRainMultiplier` in [status care](../../../../../src/status_care_facts.js) and `bodyFacultyActive` in main provide existing interventions. P23 itself stages no HP damage. Its tests prove cursor/replay/remainder behavior; this report did not replay the full native Wet/cold tick loop or body replacement in a browser. The other-systems audit owns broader body-depth findings.

**Persistence/knowledge:** rack stock/progress lives in existing simulation owners/facts/resources; home condition in homes; exposure in validated optional schema; place larder/clock in registered `placeClocks`. Place projections are pure and require local presence, with numeric ratio/age requiring the sandglass route. Domain serialization tests are narrower than native Save/reload/Export/Import. No unification or migration was implemented.

### One connected proposed player journey

**J1, A13 refinement, not implemented:** select an existing book-world provisioning or workshop commission with an actual dry-material consumer. The maker acquires a counted damp lot through that world's existing supply route, inspects its local condition, and sees “This batch is still damp; covered preparation is available.” They can use an ordinary sheltered station at a known time/cost, or fit a paid rain cover that removes direct wetting while preserving the selected process's air path. A local observation gives a bounded warning rather than guaranteed future weather. They start work, pause/travel, return and find the earned progress/material state retained under the world's existing clock policy. If exposure interrupted drying, they retain the unfinished lot and recover through shelter. The finished lot becomes a real commissioned object or provision; its use consumes or installs it once.

First slice defaults to the existing rack's simple rain/cover mechanism. Add humidity only if a named second comparison needs it; a tight container versus ventilated cover is a possible later contrast. Use shared test fixtures in Frontier for parity, then prove the selected book-world route. No new NPC career, region, dynamic ecosystem or universal climate service is needed for J1.
