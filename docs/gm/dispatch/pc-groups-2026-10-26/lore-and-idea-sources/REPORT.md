## Findings and per-PC inventory

**F1. Strong foundations exist; a joined lore-to-making loop is not established.** Reference access, learned recipes, material observations, attributed knowledge, historical receipts, institutions and physical site exploration have separate owners. Their presence does not prove names, editions, contested explanations and regional methods are connected through one playable book-world route.

**F2. A knowledge summary is not a document archive.** [simulation_effects.js](../../../../../src/simulation_effects.js), `applySimulationEffects`, keeps one entry per actor/key. Equal or higher stage overwrites value, timestamp, cause and optional basis; a lower-stage observation does not replace an understood entry. The [synthetic probe](evidence/probe.json) confirms both behaviors. This is a capability boundary, not evidence that current shipped content has lost a particular manuscript. A future conflicting-account route should use distinct account identities and explicit interpretation, rather than silently reuse one key for all witnesses.

**F3. Source status and institutional authority need separate readers.** A claim can be well attributed yet false; a certificate can be socially effective yet unrelated to a material's actual function. A later source edition can revise an explanation without mutating frozen recipe/material/world truth. Existing immutable reference registration helps preserve definitions, but it does not implement this entire distinction.

## More to consider: foundations and connected journey

**ACCOUNT FOR NOW.** These are proposals applying PC54–60, not added standing codes or gates.

| Code | Useful new angle | Foundation and concrete consequence |
|---|---|---|
| G7-C1 | A name can carry a speaker's classification without identifying a lot. | Preserve stable material/lot identity and the observed word, speaker, place/language and claimed referent separately. One trade term can remain ambiguous until a useful test. Correcting a glossary must not transmute held stock or remove the old quotation. |
| G7-C2 | Two copies can be one witness. | Retain copy ancestry/translator/source when material. Ten copies of one omitted diagram are not ten independent confirmations. Distinguish damaged, intentionally redacted, mistranslated, historically superseded and simply unobserved coverage; each suggests different recovery. |
| G7-C3 | A contradiction can target conditions rather than truth. | Record tested scale, apparatus, preparation and environment with the claim. An old process may work with local water and fail elsewhere. Compare one variable where practical; several simultaneous changes establish an outcome, not which change caused it. Do not expose hidden conditions through refusal wording. |
| G7-C4 | Certification has an issuer, scope and date. | A guild can certify a batch, an operator or a method under conditions. Transfer, expiry or a revised standard can change who trusts it without changing product truth. Use one actual institution/transaction rather than a new generalized reputation track. |
| G7-C5 | A site can preserve evidence after its productive use changes. | An obsolete sluice mark or kiln repair can indicate an earlier process without proving the current water/fuel source is unchanged. Record inspection time and known modifications; revisit tests current suitability while preserving history/depletion. |

**Proposed journey J1, ORIGINAL book-world teaching/workshop branch.** Fit it to one selected book-world owner and source before runtime work. It combines A14/A15 rather than authoring seven disconnected lore menus.

A learner obtains a permitted copy of a local workshop note and one genuinely owned, ambiguously named lot. The teacher calls it by a trade term; a margin note describes a preparation that the printed diagram omits. The learner can inspect a surviving apparatus trace or ask the available qualified teacher. These routes yield distinct attributed claims, not immediate identity/competence. A small paid trial uses the existing stock/process owner and the learner's actual means. An observed result supports a scoped method revision, which then prepares a useful object for an existing repair/supply consumer. An institution may accept a witnessed test while still declining formal certification without its own examination.

Failure/recovery: an unreadable margin leaves that part unknown; absent skill/apparatus gives an honest supported blocker; a failed trial spends only committed inputs and records its outcome; changing region or water prompts reevaluation of conditions rather than forgetting the method. A later corrected copy adds evidence beside the earlier claim. Save/reload retains lot identity, copy/claim identity, test result and installation history without granting another output. A supernatural exception may supply an alternative means when its selected source supports it, with its own actual costs and discoverability.

Proposed visible outcome: “Your margin-note trial worked with this apparatus and batch. You can repeat that preparation while its known conditions hold.” Refusal: “You have the instructions, but no usable vessel here.” Exact text must read actual known facts; it must not promise unimplemented tolerance/generalization. This journey is a design proposal, not a played route.
