Group slots only when a world recipe explicitly gives the same `share` string; that group's selectors are conjunctive, quantity range is the intersection, and catalyst/consumed mode must agree. Allow integer partitions of several suitable lots into one group and of one lot across competing groups; emit input-index/material/qty allocation rows and a unique debit or retained row per physical input.
