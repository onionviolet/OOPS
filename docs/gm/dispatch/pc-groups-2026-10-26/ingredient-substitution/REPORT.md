## More-to-consider expansion: G2 round 1

These extend PC7–PC14 and A5, without starting new global PC numbering or changing shared owners. All mechanics in this section are PROPOSED. Default, dial and reason vary by selected world/activity.

| Code | New angle | Default, dial, reason and observable consequence |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Equivalence has a scope | Record “works for this role, process revision, output use and conditions.” A carrier that works for a topical paste need not be suitable for an oral dose. A teacher's endorsement and a merchant's accepted label can differ from physical equivalence without rewriting truth. Broader equivalence follows demonstrated mechanisms. |
| E2 | Functions can share matter, requirements can compete for it | One amount of oil may serve as carrier and fuel only to the extent the process actually retains or burns it. Distinguish simultaneous qualities of the same material from consumptive claims on its constituents. Reject or reduce overlapping allocations when the same quantity is already spent; do not charge a binder twice merely because it also carries an active. |
| E3 | Fit can depend on concentration rather than total | Equal active contribution at greater input volume can dilute a dose or overflow an apparatus. Use total, concentration, ratio or geometry according to the named reader. Scale cost and losses where the process requires them; leave exact symbolic identity outside this arithmetic unless its world supplies a mechanism. |
| E4 | A blend can introduce an interaction | Two individually acceptable substitutes may react, precipitate, contaminate or disrupt one another where the selected law models it. Evaluate the proposed blend through the real resolver, not an average of individual fit scores. Conversely a complementary blend can remove a limitation if that transformation has a supported route. |
| E5 | Held catalysts are not free throughput | A retained catalyst may require contact, capacity, purity or recovery work. Count concurrent use against actual held stock; change or wear it where the world models that aftermath. Do not manufacture replenished catalyst through a “retained” label. Existing catalyst retention stays valid for its authored reactions. |
| E6 | Uncertainty is actionable evidence | An observed successful blend teaches the tested range and conditions, not every ratio or every specimen. A failed high-heat trial does not show the ingredient cannot work at lower heat. Offer a known reference comparison or a small paid trial; show confidence and evidence without a precise probability invented from no model. |
| E7 | Procurement includes intended custody | A suitable ingredient can be inaccessible, borrowed, reserved or prized. Recommend from observer-known possibilities, then quote the exact chosen holdings and access costs. Revalidate at commit; a missing preferred lot returns a reason or the player's explicit substitution policy. It does not silently consume the heirloom specimen. |
| E8 | Separation changes what provenance survives | Origin/contact can attach to an entire object, a constituent, a sample or a transformed substance. A purification or split preserves/transforms that link according to the world law, not by blanket inheritance or blanket erasure. Retain only facts a reader needs; existing lineage history is a seam, not an automatic answer. |
| E9 | Directional compatibility can be conditional | Host laws may read a foreign ingredient physically while its source-world power needs a carrier, attunement or conversion. Reverse travel can have a different result. Quote known activation costs and uncertainty separately from transport ownership; absence of a supported law follows the standing inert default, not a discovered permanent impossibility. |
| E10 | Repeating a method must preserve the subject | Record actual allocations, prepared states, revision and observed conditions. Repeat can choose a permitted equivalent only under a supported policy; stale stock or changed preparation requotes. Save the demonstrated envelope rather than caching today's ranking. This extends PC12/13 without owning a scheduler or process executor. |

## One connected proposed player journey

**J1: an annotated alternative for a useful book-world preparation.** Target viewport: 1920×1080 desktop. Visible outcome: compare the known reference with owned alternatives, see the changed amount/method and its uncertainty, then make and use a real product. Mobile optimization stays deferred. This is a candidate journey for A5, not implemented gameplay or a newly selected world variant.

1. **J1a, obtain and understand.** In the selected book world, learn a preparation with an existing useful consumer. Keep its canonical identity-bearing core when required. Find one replaceable functional role from a saved source locator; if the specific alternative lacks support, label the method ORIGINAL. Acquire the reference and two candidate lots through existing sources, with exact access costs and observed facts.
2. **J1b, compare and plan.** Inspect the reference, an alternate and a blend for that role. The quote shows selected stock, essential conditions, estimated contribution, actual paid quantities and known preparation changes. One condition is independently necessary. An uncertain candidate remains an experiment; rankings do not use hidden truth. The player explicitly selects the blend rather than an automatic fallback.
3. **J1c, prepare and resolve.** Apply one useful preparation through the chosen world's owner, settle its input/yield/waste, and run the revised method. The same physical quantity may provide compatible roles without duplication. Missing essentials fail named-product recognition but still produce the world's supported experimental outcome. A failed trial leaves spent/held/recoverable material facts intact.
4. **J1d, use and repeat.** Send the real output to the existing consumer, compare its observed result with the reference, and record only the demonstrated alternative and conditions. Save/reload/Export/Import preserves allocations, method revision, evidence and aftermath. Repeat quotes the demonstrated variant; if its exact chosen stock is gone, it explains that rather than spending another specimen.

A practical implementation pilot can prove allocation against synthetic native profiles first, but its playable delivery should follow the existing book-world priority. The world/source owner chooses the functional role; this report does not invent a canon-safe alternate or make a source campaign mandatory. Source work stops once that consequential identity boundary is supported or explicitly labelled ORIGINAL.
