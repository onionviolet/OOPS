## More to consider: connected-making extension

These proposals deepen PC1–PC6 without adding new PC numbers or declaring acceptance.

| Code | Useful extension, default and dial | Foundation and observable consequence |
|---|---|---|
| CMX1: replay has a comparison contract | Default: repeat a demonstrated setup with the same known conditions. Dial: a replacement ingredient/apparatus/recipient makes it a modified attempt, with the changed facts visible. In-world reason: a notebook cannot guarantee what its maker never tested. | Extend frozen recipe/witness and existing knowledge access rather than add another stock owner. Separate “instructions available,” “made successfully” and “repeat under these conditions.” A tolerable range requires actual comparison evidence, not the authored selector's entire range. |
| CMX2: containers carry mechanism | Default: retain a cohort when age, concentration, contamination or charge changes outcome; keep equivalent units stacked otherwise. Dial: disposable phials, reusable bottles, shared pots and sealed packages differ by local mechanism and cost. | A container can hold contents without being consumed, or become waste if that craft really spends it. Track dose/contents separately from vessel ownership where the world needs it. Mixed-age merging should preserve cohorts or compute an explicitly justified physical result; transferring should not reset age. Do not retrofit a global decay clock for today's nonperishable vials. |
| CMX3: learn without drinking everything | Default: paid assay or a known test can supply observations; tasting remains a costly route when appropriate. Dial: a maker may use animals, instruments, magic or a consenting assistant where world law supports them. | Reuse HP assay and Frontier examination/knowledge separation. A locally supported measurement can make a useful original legible without requiring self-harm. A display cue is evidence of what was measured, not an automatic full identity reveal. No new NPC simulator is needed for the first version. |
| CMX4: separate process failure from recipient response | Default: compare the same preparation under a named recipient baseline. Dial: body conditioning/tolerance or environmental conditions can alter effects and should accompany the observation when known. | Existing dose/body owners already supply the causes. Avoid teaching “this herb stopped working” when the actual change is accumulated tolerance. A useful notebook preserves uncertainty and known confounders without exposing hidden ingredient truth. |
| CMX5: reject once, preserve custody | Default: an unsupported pending process reports why it cannot proceed while preserving paid/held facts for recovery. Dial: locally justified spoilage or salvage may settle it instead. | Reuse the owner's validation/transaction route. Frozen terms need only the facts consumed by that world's process, not a universal job framework. |
| CMX6: distinguish useful invention from resemblance | Default: an off-formula result may have one modest locally supported use, plus failure and side-effect possibilities. Dial: recipe-constrained magic may be much less forgiving than physical mixtures. | Extend a real consumer before adding names/grades. HP preparation/provenance rules and LOTM exact identity/allocated dose are distinct foundations. Do not imply an experimental mixture grants a canon transformation or pathway merely because it resembles the inputs. Source-informed additions remain ORIGINAL/INFERRED until a locator supports them. |

### One connected proposed player journey

**PROPOSAL, A2 refinement:** an HP maker obtains two separately identified lots through existing store/supplier/harvest routes, records what is witnessed versus claimed, and prepares them at the current cauldron. The player follows known instructions for a benchmark, then changes one preparation condition for the comparison. The notebook preserves quantities, preparation and vessel condition, showing the cost and the changed known condition without predicting hidden provenance. Existing exact/degraded/original outcomes remain meaningful.

Add one useful off-formula application only after a bounded saved-source check supports its mechanism or an explicit ORIGINAL world-consistent rule is authored. For example, a locally authored preparation might serve a modest material-treatment reader; the report does not assert this is canon or already supported. This is the unresolved content choice within the proposal, not an authorization question for this audit. The product is assayed/tested through a real reader, one dose is used, the remainder stays identified by cohort, and the player repeats with the demonstrated conditions. An interrupted brew keeps its spent inputs and named stage; native Save/reload/Export/Import preserves both the unfinished work and the observed method.

Target if selected: desktop 1920×1080, visible material custody, exact cost, timing/refusal, product use and comparison notes in the current making/menu surface; mobile deferred. Human taste review is optional and does not replace agent proof. This journey is not an assertion that the HP state machine already offers learned-method replay, a useful original reader, or volume transfer. Those are its bounded additions. Existing HP and active-wave owners must reconcile implementation ownership first.
