## LQ1. Ordinary names stay concealed on the tested surfaces

**Natural** for none and dormant: fresh Play, default no-cheat creation or selection through the actual hero-creation control, Confirm, real Save and Export. **Seeded-native** for awakened: normal load of the existing real-code-built `held_system.save.txt` fixture, whose legacy message-backed held card is discovered. The lint captures creation/play snapshots, opens Options, Authoring, the panel picker and Appearance/effects modules, inventories tooltip text and hovers exposed tooltip hosts, then clicks actual Export and reads the browser's suggested filename and downloaded payload. It collects DOM/input text, title, aria-label/description, referenced assistive labels, alt/placeholder/value/role descriptions, options, tooltips, settings lists, Appearance module lists, picker entries, document.title and localStorage **keys**. Hidden attributes/options/tooltips are conservatively inventoried too, because a menu, reference or hover can expose them. Storage values are not classified as keys; decoded save exports are inspected separately.

Names come from exported card data/nested registries in `src/cheat_cards.js`, augmented by fixture-held cards. Literal panel labels come from `definePanel`/`registerWorkspaceModule` declarations in source. There is no forbidden-name allowlist. A future exported/nested card or fixture-held card adds its ID, name, UI ID and label automatically. A missing nonquiet panel label produces BROKEN/3 instead of silently passing. Discovery currently requires an awake/message-backed held entry (including legacy C0), or explicit `known[id] === true`/`known[id].discovered === true`; possession alone is insufficient. Discovered shared panels are permitted independently of undiscovered card names. If the game's future discovery/registry contract changes, this adapter must change with it. Currently there is one registry card; the awakened state therefore has no remaining forbidden names. Its PASS does not prove future acquisition routes.
