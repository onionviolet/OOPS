## Strongest result

**F1:** the useful shared seam is parts/carriers/processes/knowledge with
per-world semantics. Ingredient sets, spell sequences, adjacency graphs,
deciphered writing and decorative glyphs are not interchangeable. Ars Nouveau
is especially useful for a program that works both manually and through a
fueled automatic executor. Noita adds ordered execution, but needs more
explanation than a row of slots.

**F2:** top 10, in order: Tinkers' Construct, Ars Nouveau, Noita, Atelier Ryza,
Monster Hunter Rise, Bannerlord, Last Epoch, Diablo II, Heaven's Vault, Chants
of Sennaar. Elden Ring follows when the immediate gap is weapon-granted actions.
These rank mechanisms by YAIRPG fit, not new campaign priorities.

**F3:** reception argues for lasting item identity, meaningful ingredient
contribution and saved routines. The same samples show costs from random part
unlocks, repeated inputs, opaque failure cues, irreversible rune ordering and
overly strong or overly helpful systems. Neither side was erased. Reception
gaps remain OPEN rather than filled with inferred complaints.
