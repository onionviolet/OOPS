## Worked original biography: The Reedward knife

**INFERRED original world O1: Reedward**, a river settlement world separate from
existing world packs. Its small magical law is that objects can hold residue from
witnessed oaths they materially helped keep or break. Local shrines can test and
bind those residues; sufficiently integrated memories can awaken a presence.
That presence is the item's own history, not a dead wielder resurrected. No
age-based magic bonus, remote identity detector or automatic inheritance of skill
is assumed. These openly authored examples are not secrets from an existing world.

Three generations: smith **Mara**, adopted heir **Iven**, and Iven's daughter
**Sela**. A knife is useful for rope work, rescue and fighting. Blade mass below
excludes the separately tracked guard, grip and sheath. Length/profile changes
would be derived from the chosen geometry law, not claimed as proportional to mass.
Every BG row is a proposed saved receipt, not played evidence.
