## OV1 and AB1-AB12: how to read each art

Each **OV1** line explicitly covers source → material → process → product →
use → wear → repair/salvage → legacy. Wear may mean depletion, fading,
obsolescence or relationship change; a world may give an object no wear law.
Legacy may retain an edition, lineage, proven method or remembered service,
without granting an heir personal mastery. Source, input and beneficiary
can be actors or information, not just divisible matter.

All cards inherit **AB1** ordered process, **AB6** observer knowledge,
**AB7** actual consumer profile, **AB8** receipts, **AB10** local law,
**AB11** real station/access and **AB12** exact references. Each card's **AB**
line adds the representations it needs: **AB4** material/batch,
**AB2** parts/interfaces, **AB3** executable pattern, **AB5** condition,
**AB9** actor-item bond. This inheritance is the explicit common mapping;
an omitted extra is unnecessary for the proposed ordinary version, not forbidden.
AB3 applies to supported executable logic, not every decorative design.
AB9 applies to a real relation, not every gift or sale.

Idle convention: a known method can queue at the worker's actual skill.
Reserve stock, worker, station and receiver; repeats select real inputs under
a visible substitution policy, stop on shortage or unmet conditions, and
settle each occurrence once. Save partial transformation, committed draws,
revision and next boundary. A preview spends nothing and reveals only its
observation grant. Unattended maturation may proceed while the actor works
elsewhere when site/world law allows it; closed-browser catch-up is a future
scheduler proof, not established by these cards. Optional hand control refines
choices and evidence; it does not become a mandatory minigame.

**Default + Dial + Reasonably:** default to one useful consumer and a short
repeatable plan. Dial process detail, upkeep, institutional access, risk,
autonomy and magical law per world. Reasonably, add a state only when a
rule, observer or story event reads it. More arts need adapters, not separate
engines or a universal crafting XP bar.

## OM1-OM16: deeper cards

### OM1. Gems and jewelry

**Inputs/process:** rough stones, metal setting, abrasives, design and lapidary
bench; inspect → orient → cut → polish → set → test. **Product/consumer:**
a fitted ring meets a recipient's taste; an optical gem focuses a supported
beam; a magical stone stores only charge its local law admits. Cut, color,
fit and capacity have separate consumers, rather than one gem score.
**Skill/knowledge:** polishing control → facet planning → inclusion diagnosis
→ calibrated optical or magical designs; learn observed hardness, defects
and tested charging behavior separately. **Idle/social:** batch known cuts
and settings; charge at a lawful source; jeweler, appraiser, wedding gift maker
or pawn broker. **OV1:** mine/trade → rough stone/metal → cut/set → ring/focus
→ wear or channel → scratches/charge loss → repolish/reset/recover metal →
heirloom and maker mark. **AB:** +AB2, AB4, AB5; +AB3 for a pattern, +AB9 only
for sourced attunement. Dial ornamental, optical and magical roles independently.

### OM2. Wands, staffs and foci

**Inputs/process:** selected wood, lawful core, handle/fittings, maker means
and candidate user; season → shape → fit core → finish → trial → calibrate.
**Product/consumer:** a casting adapter reads channel stability, handling and
actor fit; ownership alone does not teach spells. **Skill/knowledge:** straight
shaping → joint tolerances → material-channel diagnosis → fit assessment;
known core behavior differs from the user's trial history. **Idle/social:**
queue ordinary components and supported calibration drills, with recipient
presence only where the law needs it; specialist commissions and repair service.
**OV1:** gather/obtain core → seasoned stock → build/trial → focus → supported
casting → cracks or bond change → repair/refit or lawful core recovery → maker
lineage and user history. **AB:** +AB2, AB4, AB5, AB9; +AB3 when engraved.
HP allegiance is a root inspiration label, not a W6 verified general recipe.

### OM3. Leather and hide

**Inputs/process:** harvested hide with condition/provenance, curing/tanning
agents, water, thread and cutting tools; clean → cure → tan → dry → cut →
stitch → fit. **Product/consumer:** boots, waterskins, harness and protective
layers read flexibility, seal, fit or coverage. Monster traits transfer only
through declared retention rules. **Skill/knowledge:** even curing → seams
→ body/load fitting → diagnose harvest versus processing faults; learn hide
response, treatment and leak observations. **Idle/social:** racks hold actual
capacity; queue tanning and sewing with explicit unattended phases; tanner,
saddler, quartermaster and odorous-workshop neighbor. **OV1:** hunt/trade →
hide → tan/stitch → harness → hauling/riding → seam and surface wear → patch
or strip useful leather → recognizable stitch school. **AB:** +AB2, AB4, AB5.
Dial smell, exposure and weather response, rather than imposing them everywhere.

### OM4. Glass and lenses

**Inputs/process:** world-defined glass feedstock, colorants, molds, furnace,
grinding tools; melt → form → anneal → grind → polish → mount → calibrate.
**Product/consumer:** bottle seal controls storage; lens resolution changes
what a research/appraisal action can observe; telescope/scrying optics need
an appropriate detector and law. **Skill/knowledge:** vessel shaping → consistent
annealing → lens curvature → instrument calibration; learn leaks, distortion
and scope of observations. **Idle/social:** furnace batches and saved grind
plans; glassblower, lab supplier or observatory collaborator. **OV1:** quarry/
trade → feedstock → form/grind → bottle/lens → store/inspect → chip/clouding
→ remount or recover compatible cullet → optical standard and instrument history.
**AB:** +AB2, AB4, AB5; +AB3 for supported scrying. Dial optical fidelity so
a cheap lens still produces a useful, explicitly coarse reading.

### OM5. Clockwork and traps

**Inputs/process:** gear blanks, springs, frame, trigger/sensor, power and
site rights; shape → assemble → align → install trigger → wind → test.
**Product/consumer:** a clock schedules an opening; a trap consumes a real
trigger event and affects a target; a feeder dispenses actual stock.
**Skill/knowledge:** simple release → ratios/tolerances → reliable trigger
logic → fault diagnosis; know activation conditions and false-trigger evidence.
**Idle/social:** queue components and calibration, then let deployed devices
run on their own energy and site access; locksmith, watchmaker, household
helper or defensive engineer. **OV1:** salvage/mine → parts → assemble/test →
device → timing/capture → winding depletion/jam → reset/replace/salvage →
service record and design school. **AB:** +AB2, AB3, AB4, AB5. Mechanical
logic and rune logic may share typed trigger concepts without sharing fuel.

### OM6. Gunsmithing and mods

**Inputs/process:** compatible barrel/action/stock, hardware, design, bench,
proof apparatus and local permission; inspect → fabricate/select → fit →
assemble → proof → sight. **Product/consumer:** weapon mode, accuracy, fit,
reliability and ammunition compatibility change combat; receiver swaps can
change function, not merely add damage. **Skill/knowledge:** upkeep → fitting
→ precision manufacture → diagnosis and certified proof; learning a drawing
does not provide machinery. **Idle/social:** queue parts and proof routines;
armorer, militia supplier and licensed repairer where authored. **OV1:** mine/
salvage → stock/parts → fit/proof → firearm → supported firing → fouling/wear
→ clean/refit/salvage → service and ownership history. **AB:** +AB2, AB4, AB5;
+AB3 for supported electronics. Dial technology and law; no real-world
fabrication specifications are needed for these fictional operators.

### OM7. Soul and memory crafting

**Inputs/process:** exact lawful shards or memories, carrier, authorized maker
means and source-compatible design; assess → prepare carrier → assign node
roles → weave/infuse → stabilize → test. **Product/consumer:** a supported
Memory-like item supplies a specific effect with real fuel and semantic
compatibility, rather than inheriting all narrative traits. **Skill/knowledge:**
perceive structure → reproduce a supported weave → understand compatibility
→ develop a tested variant. OW2 reports Shadow Slave forging/weaving as distinct;
that is SHOWN owner evidence, not a new full-book verification. **Idle/social:**
queue permitted preparation and rehearsed work; unique moments stay saved,
not fabricated by repeat; trusted specialist or disputed relic custodian.
**OV1:** lawful acquisition → exact shard/carrier → weave → artifact → effect
→ depletion/damage → source-permitted repair/recovery → retained provenance.
**AB:** +AB2, AB3, AB4 where substrate matters, AB5, AB9 where bonded.

### OM8. Cursed tools

**Inputs/process:** exact carrier, supported curse/energy source, maker means
and any genuine vow terms; inspect → prepare → bind under local rule → test
→ document known burden. **Product/consumer:** combat/work reads both useful
function and the authored cost on the correct actor. A restriction does not
become free power because a description calls it a vow. **Skill/knowledge:**
containment → binding control → diagnose burden/trigger → supported adaptation;
knowledge may remain partial. **Idle/social:** stable methods queue where
allowed; burden settlement persists during eligible use; specialist commission,
regulated custodian or repair expert. **OV1:** acquire source/carrier → eligible
object → bind → tool → effect and cost → stability/charge change → local unbind,
repair or containment → incident history. **AB:** +AB3, AB4, AB5, AB9 when
terms relate actor and item; +AB2 only for parts. JJK is inspiration pending W1.

### OM9. Awakened weapons

**Inputs/process:** a lawful base/source, wielder relation, training context
and learned practice; meet → practice → observe → converse where supported →
understand → demonstrate. **Product/consumer:** a changed available technique
or relationship state, not a sword manufactured from a quantity of soul points.
**Skill/knowledge:** safe use → coordination → specific source comprehension
→ reliable new expression; knowing a name need not equal practical control.
**Idle/social:** supported drills and reflective practice queue; a relationship
milestone may create a saved choice for later; teacher, partner or recognized
school member. **OV1:** acquire/meet → existing source/base → train/dialogue →
changed bond/function → technique use → strain or disagreement where authored
→ rest/reconcile/repair if permitted → shared biography. **AB:** +AB9, AB5;
+AB4/AB2 only for the physical base; +AB3 only for genuine executable patterns.
Bleach's soul-bound releases are not ordinary removable mods (SHOWN OW6).

### OM10. Living crafts

**Inputs/process:** viable stock, compatible grafts/lineage, growing site,
care and supported training; select → propagate/graft → tend → assess → train
or fit → harvest/deploy. **Product/consumer:** living shelter, filter, tool or
symbiont performs one defined task; a living worker remains an entity with
its own capacity. **Skill/knowledge:** husbandry → compatibility → growth
control → stable traits; distinguish inherited, environmental and learned
traits. **Idle/social:** care schedules and event-bound growth; shortages follow
visible world policy, not a surprise universal offline death rule; gardener,
beast keeper or bio-artisan. **OV1:** acquire lineage → living stock → tend/
graft → organism/tool → supported service → age/health change → heal, regrow
or lawful biomass recovery → lineage and keeper history. **AB:** +AB4, AB5,
AB9 where relational; +AB2 for graft interfaces. AB12 references the biological
owner; AB4 does not replace genetics, body or actor records.

### OM11. Woodwork and home

**Inputs/process:** logs/boards with condition, fittings, plan, tools and
site access; season → measure → cut → join → finish → install. **Product/consumer:**
a chair supports rest; a rack adds actual drying capacity; a boat opens a route.
A cosmetic carving can remain explicitly texture. **Skill/knowledge:** sound
cuts → joins → fitted assemblies → structural diagnosis; know species response,
load tests and site fit. **Idle/social:** seasonal stock and queued cuts/joins,
installation at the actual site; carpenter, household maker, boatwright or
community builder. **OV1:** forest/trade → seasoned boards → join/install →
furniture/structure → rest/work/travel → loosening/weathering → mend/reuse
boards → household memory and maker marks. **AB:** +AB2, AB4, AB5.
Dial site scale; a furniture commission need not wait for a city simulation.

### OM12. Paper, ink and binding

**Inputs/process:** fibers, pigments/binders, water, press, text/design and
known method; pulp → sheet/dry → prepare ink → write/print → collate → bind
→ proofread. **Product/consumer:** medium holds inscriptions; a physical
manual teaches only its actual contents, with reader means and errors intact.
**Skill/knowledge:** legible sheets → durable ink → accurate copying → edition
editing and teaching; distinguish a faithful copy from true claims.
**Idle/social:** batches and saved copy plans; scribe, publisher, archivist,
guild teacher. **OV1:** gather fiber/acquire content → pulp/ink plus referenced
claims → make/copy/bind → edition/carrier → read/teach/inscribe → fading/errors
or outdated claims → restore/errata/recycle → edition lineage and attribution.
**AB:** +AB4, AB5; +AB2 for binding, +AB3 only for executable inscriptions.
Information may copy while material sheets remain conserved.

### OM13. Masks and disguises

**Inputs/process:** cloth/leather/pigment, measurements, visible identity
references and lawful or forged credentials; study → shape → finish → fit →
rehearse → test with an observer. **Product/consumer:** a disguise changes
what a particular observer can infer; papers alter access only through that
institution's recognition. It does not rewrite body or legal truth.
**Skill/knowledge:** fit → likeness → movement/voice coordination → observer
and document standards; learn where a test failed. **Idle/social:** queue
construction and rehearsals; tailor, stage artist, identity broker or infiltrator
with local consequences. **OV1:** acquire reference/material → blanks → fit/
imitate → mask/document → performance/access → wear or exposure → repair,
update or retire → performance/claim history. **AB:** +AB2, AB4, AB5; +AB3
only for an actual illusion. AB6 records observer beliefs separately.

### OM14. Instruments

**Inputs/process:** wood/metal/membrane/string, score of intended range,
acoustic tools and bench; shape → assemble → tension → tune → play-test.
**Product/consumer:** music performance reads tone, range, tuning and player

not grant performance skill. **Skill/knowledge:** basic assembly → stable tuning
→ voice selection → acoustic diagnosis; recognize defects by listening and
measuring. **Idle/social:** saved builds and tuning routines; luthier, ensemble
collaborator, festival supplier. **OV1:** gather/trade → resonant stock → build/
tune → instrument → performance → drift/cracks → retune/replace/recover parts
→ concert and maker biography. **AB:** +AB2, AB4, AB5; +AB3/AB9 only under
magical/bond law. Reuse P6 M5's instrument family, rather than a second music owner.

### OM15. Tech blueprints and fusion

**Inputs/process:** two known designs with revisions and actual evidence,
compatible functions, drafting/test means and physical prototype stock;
compare → compose dependencies → check interfaces → prototype → test → revise.
**Product/consumer:** a validated new method enables an actual output or
operation. A combined drawing alone remains a candidate, not guaranteed
invention. **Skill/knowledge:** reading → adapting → interface diagnosis →
validated innovation and teaching; practical reproduction differs from theory.
**Idle/social:** queue known tests and drafting; exploratory choices can pause
for later; inventor, patent holder where local, teacher or workshop partner.
**OV1:** obtain designs → knowledge references/stock → compose/test → design
revision plus prototype → manufacture → obsolescence/failure evidence → revise/
reuse parts → attribution and diffusion. **AB:** +AB2 for dependencies/parts,
AB4/AB5 for prototypes, AB3 only for executable control. Legendary Mechanic
blueprint fusion remains MEMORY from root, not canon-verified by W6.

**Skill/knowledge:** care → exact refinement execution → compatibility knowledge

**Idle/social:** feed standing orders and known refinements; unique specimens

### W6:OM28. Preservation, restoration and replicas

**Inputs/process:** exact old item, documented condition, compatible repair
stock, reference and conservator means; inspect → stabilize → repair selectively
→ record replacements → compare → return or display. **Product/consumer:**
an archive regains legibility, a family item regains use, or a commissioned
replica meets observable likeness. A replica has its own origin. **Skill/knowledge:**
cleaning → reversible repairs → source-aware reconstruction → distinguish actual
history from inferred additions. **Idle/social:** known treatments queue; curator,
heirloom conservator or replica maker. **OV1:** receive relic → original object/
repair stock → stabilize → restored object or separate replica → study/use →
further condition change → maintain/recover declared parts → honest biography.
**AB:** +AB2 where parts, AB4, AB5; AB8/AB12 are decisive for identity. P6 repair/
salvage owns transformations. Dial restoration versus reconstruction disclosure;
matching appearance never proves matching provenance or hidden power.
