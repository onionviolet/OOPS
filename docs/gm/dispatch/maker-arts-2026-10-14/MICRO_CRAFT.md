## Reading key and owner boundary

K1: Each row offers **F0** sensation, then **F1** a learned name/category.
F1 needs instruction, a relevant inspection or an explicit reveal grant.
Neither tier supplies exact hardness, temperature, strength or remaining life.
Property names below are proposed mappings, not existing save fields.

K2: **STATED** means the cited source supports the underlying craft detail.
**INFERRED** means a plausible adaptation, not a source-established diagnostic.
Every game sentence and property mapping is INFERRED, even on STATED rows.
There are no MEMORY-only rows. Source locators are in the ledger below.

K3: A cue needs its stated route: sight, work, fit, touch, smell or test.
Do not let an inventory tooltip report a test never performed.
Visible signs can have several causes. F1 may name the sign without its cause.
Dust, smell, sparks and ringing are observations, not full material assays.

K4: **Default:** one useful cue at a job boundary or inspection.
**Dial:** sparse/cozy texture through richer workshop/body simulation.
**Reasonably:** retain causes and knowledge scope; avoid compulsory minigames.
Known jobs can queue. Logged observations can be reviewed after idle work.
Cosmetic texture is labeled texture until an actual consumer reads it.
