# Ideas: bk-ui-directions (2026-10-30)

Proposals only (AD32). The round's findings and recommendation live in [UI directions](../../../../audits/UI_DIRECTIONS_2026-10-30.md); this file keeps the loose ideas the round did not build.

- **Notice folding.** Three "now available in navigation" notices with "Related panel" links arrive in the first minute in Tingen. One Reports line per reveal batch with a dot on the nav is enough (UR13).
- **Verb chips as a filter on Local actions.** Direction C's chips (Talk, Work, Make, Travel, Study) work as a filter on today's Local actions list without a palette; it is the cheapest piece of C.
- **Irreversible marker.** The Seer dose offer says "irreversible" only in prose. A shared marker on the option row (a glyph plus the word, effects-off safe) separates it from reversible choices in a 22-entry list.
- **Place-grouped Local actions.** After one click the Tingen list went from 7 to 22 entries, mixing the dose, spirit contracts and "Return to Tingen docks" rows. Group by place and by "available now", and fold the rest.
- **Desk skin tokens.** Direction D's parchment and brass are four tokens per world (surface, ink, rule, accent); they can ship as a world skin without any structural change (MDL6).
- **Crossing threshold as the pattern for other thresholds.** "Before you cross" (Crosses with you, Stays behind, Pauses, Waiting on the far side) is the best-structured screen seen in play; start-of-life and return screens can reuse its four headings.
- **Idle versus active line.** The job line reads "Current work & saving" whether or not work runs. A single state word (Working 1h 20m left, Waiting, Free) in the header chips answers "is anything happening".
