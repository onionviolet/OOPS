# Ideas: bk-making-ui (proposals, AD32)

Spun out of [MAKING_FORMS](../../../../representations/MAKING_FORMS.md). Short list, not required gates.

- **Swap preview on a strip:** drag one rune past another and see the ghost total change, nothing spent (MR4).
- **Failure kept as a margin note:** a failed batch writes one line in the recipe's record ("scorched at the warm step"), searchable by reagent.
- **Rune dialect:** a world where pair order is irrelevant but strip length matters; same view, different grammar declaration.
- **Bench that remembers:** worn tools and the maker's marks after many makes (CRAFTING CF8); a folded line shows the same wear as a word.
- **Apprentice folded report:** a delegate's queue reports "6 fine, 1 sludge, spent X" and a flagged oddity, not every step.
- **Hazard that teaches:** the first time a named hazard strikes, its card gains the likeliest cause, so the second attempt can plan around it.
- **Feeding calendar:** the Gu feeding board as a week strip with gaps shown, not punished; links to the shared queue.
- **Tonic taste test:** a quick assay step between hands-on and folded, so odds in the folded sheet are earned from tasting.
