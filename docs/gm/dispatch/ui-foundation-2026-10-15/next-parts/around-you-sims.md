**Inferred interaction proposal, AS19:** idle local representation is a **routine** surface at home in Around you. Selecting a known person/landmark uses its existing inspection scope. “Nearby places” opens a named **reference/inspection** center mode through the shared controller, rather than creating a second router or global map overlay. Its bottom bar says `Back to <place>`; Esc closes the top preview before returning from the map, restores selection/focus and does not travel. Area-to-destination detail is a local drilldown with a clear return to the map. Larger maps can request the existing non-log expansion path only after its owner supports the scope. The current lower-center host is not assumed to support an arbitrary upper-pane replacement. UR41–UR43 and K4 remain binding.

**Inferred display proposal, AS20:** at 1366×768, 14/21px, retain the Reports column's full width/height. Give local space roughly the current Around you allocation; do not compress You, inventory or Reports to force an atlas into it. Keep primary names in actual selected text size. Use a small visible neighborhood, a short legend and selected detail; dense maps get pan/group drilldown and an equivalent list rather than smaller text. Never require hover to read a destination or route cost. At 1920×1080, use spare map room for more spatial context before more permanent chrome. Proto23 square frames, gradient fills, quiet separators and existing token/component families surround any world-specific map skin. Glyphs require words/shape cues in addition to color. No new art pipeline is needed for the first graph.
