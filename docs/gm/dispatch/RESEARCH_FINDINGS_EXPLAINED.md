## The actual Glasshouse prototype

The Folded Glasshouse is provisional original content: an enclosed workshop and resident caretaker, Joss, connected to the Village by a paid doorway. It provides a bounded place to integrate persistent local history, finite NPC resources, training/craft/work, body and stock passage, observation and local activity time. It is not a complete novel destination.

The current built code offers a Village lead, one paid doorway opening, same-body entry/return, ordinary versus actually supported practiced repair, paid work, preparation trials, a shelf and private research that can be offered/heard, an earned NPC listener and player-made device, and a separate paid viewing slit. Joss can detect that live view without automatically identifying its viewer. Closing the doorway/view preserves the room's history. An actual acquired packing instruction/template can enable useful production back at the Shack.

## How this becomes implementation

The findings offer specific rule and scene material, not automatically registered worlds. The existing engine supplies a shared place to bind actors, resources, stock, knowledge, jobs and consequences. The combined plan has separate Engine, Ability, Original Frontier and proposed local/world responsibilities. A selected scene needs its source cutoff or original rule, actual means, distinct approaches, consequence and later behavioral gate.

For implementation discussion, keep five source-derived feature families visible: body/source transfer and compatibility; identity/authority/exit qualification; functional training and production; conditional power interactions; and world/knowledge/time divergence. Each selected case needs a traceable source claim or explicit original invention, an executable action, native binding, UI feedback and a recorded delivery state. More abstract summaries alone cannot close that implementation gate.
