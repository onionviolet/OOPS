[WORLD_CARDS.md](../../../WORLD_CARDS.md). For each series:

2. **Skills:** growth families, evolution, fusion, tiers and uniques,

4. **Bodies, genetics and bloodlines** (Genetic Ascension especially).
5. **Followers, factions, armies and work** (A Soldier's Life especially:

6. **Worlds and crossing:** what transfers, how worlds treat outsiders.
7. **Enemies and their powers.**

9. **A draft world card** per series (original names).
