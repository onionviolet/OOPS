# More generation cheats: GC15+

2026-10-14. INFERRED options, extending GC rather than renaming CL lenses.
These reuse [GA9](HOOKS.md); no new stores or global limits. Each needs a
world/card grade, cost, acquisition route and sealed projection as in
[CATALOG](CATALOG.md). They are expansion candidates, not required gates.

| Code | Cheat and useful choice | Hook and CL stack | Small proof / subtle consequence |

Subtle consequences to keep in the same owner: a future preview can disclose
social intentions even if its item names are redacted; a known counterpart
root does not share knowledge (SB19); biasing bandits can affect their
supplies and travel rather than merely reduce combat frequency; a removed
pin may remain narratively valuable as a damaged chart; offline duration
uses the world's clock, so it may expire during catch-up without punishing
absence or inventing a new offline simulator.

Builder boundary: GC18 needs the existing source/actor transfer transaction,
GC21 needs the clock/scheduler owner, GC23 needs information carriers and
GC26 needs a real rewind owner. Their read hooks can be specified now, but
no new required shared foundation follows from adding a row here. The first
playable slice remains one honest odds/why lens, as ranked in [REPORT](REPORT.md).
