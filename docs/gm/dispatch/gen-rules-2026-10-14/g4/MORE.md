## Subtle generation extensions

| Code | Failure | Concrete rule / fixture |
|---|---|---|
| SB27 | Conditional rarity mistaken for population rarity | Store cohort and eligibility denominator with the distribution. Worm's 70/25/5 powered-cohort table never labels 70% of all clerks as trigger-born. Removing an unavailable stratum reports changed eligibility; it does not quietly advertise old odds. |
| SB28 | Transliteration/order makes collisions invisible | Reserve explicit aliases and name orders under a revisioned culture map. Fullwidth/punctuation variants of a reserved codename must collide; a shared household surname alone must not. Unknown aliases need review, not invented translation. |
| SB29 | Generated paperwork becomes circular proof | Every claim keeps its origin receipt/carrier. Copying one report into two files produces one source lineage, not two independent witnesses. A nemesis or identity hypothesis cannot corroborate itself through generated fan text. |
| SB30 | Counter selection fabricates unavailable means | A witnessed weakness may suggest a counter lead, but the rival must acquire actual provider/item/knowledge and time. Test a counter candidate with no held source: keep the lead or reject that plan, do not mint equipment or reroll the hero. |
| SB31 | Imported assessment changes meaning across worlds | Carry issuer/rubric/conditions as an attributed record. A Marvel-style grid in MHA is a foreign reading, not a hero license; a PRT class in Masked City is not municipal clearance. Conversion requires a declared evaluated mapping. |
| SB32 | Overlapping tenure or duplicate claim grants double office pay | Shared/acting/plural holders require office policy and distinct scoped rights. A second claim can exist as disputed standing, but cannot independently settle the same wage reservation or claim the same rig custody. |
| SB33 | Residue document becomes duplicate property | A residue copy is another information carrier with copy/provenance receipt, not another lamp, title, signature rig or refund entitlement. Text echoes old ownership without creating an executable second custody record. |
| SB34 | Era revision rewrites installed legacy equipment | Future admission uses new eligibility; old parts keep their identity/capability revision. An era change may change available repair services through an explicit cause, but cannot automatically scrap gear or substitute a new material in an old paid job. |
| SB35 | Comic cadence secretly changes absence/death policy | Issue/phase windows key on the supported local clock and its absence policy. Equal supported elapsed time yields equal offers; closed-browser progress, aging, casualties and new consent are separate decisions, not side effects of an issue counter. |
| SB36 | Collision/filter diagnostics expose sealed identities | Public rejection reasons are neutral categories. Never print the matched hidden full name, reserved source, nemesis trigger or retcon before/after truth. A harmless unique pending-name handle must not encode a hidden trait. Review actual rendered surfaces, not only table rows. |

## Ownership and future proof

Use G3 tables, G6 pins/addresses, G5 draw hooks and existing P7/P32/P18/P15/P22
owners. These rows extend data/consumers rather than taking over those areas.
New mutable maps require the additive, opt-in compatibility decision in
[VARIANTS](VARIANTS_AND_LEGACY.md#compatibility-and-retention-decision).
Any reclassification, rename, crossing, transfer, repair or retcon is a caused
write with actual next-draw/pending/completed roundtrip evidence before gameplay
claims. Profile dials can be reweighted for calmer or stranger worlds; no route
is removed globally and no fixed once-only acquisition rule is added.
