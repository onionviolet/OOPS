## Table authoring envelope

Each profile is a manifest over G3's tables and overlays, not another picker.
Proposed required inputs: `profileId@rev`, edition/cutoff, continuity ID,
era revision, district, cohort, stable actor/root ID, power eligibility,
institution, observed receipts, local clock and dial snapshot. Every row has
`id@rev`, integer weight/reason, required/banned tags, actual output fields,
constraint, authored fallback and a named action that reads it. Use separate
addresses for origin, ability, assessment, name, look, incident and repair.
G6 owns final address syntax; E1/P1 own roots/storage. No guessed runtime API.

The origin weights below apply to **eligible powered newcomers**, not the
general census. Unpowered actors bypass that table. A selected route with
missing means produces an accessible lead or an explicit unavailable result,
not a free power or silent reroll. Birth predisposition, acquired source,
training, permission and profession are separate fields. Multiple cores can
coexist through declared compatibility; training/gear are not exclusive birth
strata. Canon/AU pins override draws without disguising the change as canon.

For one active district, prototype budgets are 24 summary actors, at most 12
promoted contacts, at most 4 promotions and 2 incident offers per local day.
Households share culture/kin inputs rather than independent surname rolls.
Summary records are addresses plus frozen admitted facts; promotion enriches
them without replacing occupation, name or prior history. P32 owns the detail
policy; P15/P18/P22 own lint, seed QA and director controls. Capacities are
profile dials, not fixed once-only reward or power limits.

Author table IDs under prefixes `worm`, `ward`, `mha`, `shared-comic` and
`masked-city`, with explicit `@1` revisions: `.origin`, `.effect`,
`.assessment`, `.codename`, `.look`, `.motive`, `.nemesis-plan`, `.contact-role`,
`.tie-in`, `.repair-job` and `.era`. Ward reuses compatible Worm rows by
overlay reference, never copied canon roster. Era is a chosen manifest/phase
input, not a daily reroll. Mantle definitions and retcon methods are authored
records in VARIANTS, not random ordinary event rewards.

| Profile | Named authored fallback when an eligible offer pool is exhausted | Consumer / zero-means behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Worm | `worm.file-audit@1` | Compare already accessible records; when no funded work exists, show an unpaid review lead, never mint wages. |
| Ward | `ward.inventory-index@1` | Index accessible reconstruction stock; no guessed provider or restored facility. |
| MHA | `mha.stock-inspection@1` | Inspect held ordinary stock; absent stock gives a supplier inquiry, no equipment output. |
| Big Two pattern | `shared-comic.claim-index@1` | Review a public claim already acquired; without a claim, offer ordinary records study. |
| Masked City | `masked-city.depot-check@1` | Inspect accessible depot equipment; missing tools gives a commission/inquiry lead. |

Fallbacks change offers, not frozen origin/power/history. Invalid capability or
assessment combinations use `unsupported-package@1` with a neutral authoring
gap, never substitute a canonical power. Proposed retry ceiling: 8 attempts per
candidate, each addressed independently. With at most 4 contact promotions
(origin/effect/assessment/name/look/role, 6 selectors each) and 2 incident offers
(motive/tie-in/repair, 3 each), the promotion/offer budget is at most 240
selector-attempt evaluations per day. This bounds that candidate work only;
G3 must declare internal selector draw costs. Repairs/succession/retcons consume
no background entity-generation allowance without a separately selected host.
