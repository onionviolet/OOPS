# Divergence pass on earlier novel waves, 2026-10-10 (common rules)

Dispatched 2026-10-10 by Claude Code at Weibao's request ("send the
notes-only reruns for the earlier waves too"). Two lanes, one per wave.
Same method as the [haul divergence pass](../divergence-pass-2026-10-10/ASSIGNMENT.md):
those waves used `LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md` itself as their lens, so their
conclusions likely echo it. Fix that from the saved notes. **Do not reread
the books** except to check a specific passage a claim depends on.

Follow AGENTS.md and the
[lens discipline](../../NOVEL_SOURCE_GM_PROMPT.md#lens-discipline-for-book-runs-weibao-2026-10-10).
Inputs are read-only: never edit the wave's folder, its north-star section
or any other shared doc. Write only in your own gitignored output folder.
No `src/`, builds, saves or commits. Subagents only for per-series passes,
at most two live, each owning its own DIVERGENCE file.

Treat per-book chunk notes as the primary record; the wave's REPORT,
COMPARISON, PROPOSALS and north-star section were written under the lens.

## Questions, per series

1. What does it do that the north star lacks? At least five points with
   chapter locators, ranked by how much they would change play.
2. Where does it contradict the north star, its own wave's conclusions or
   the other series? Contradictions are the most valuable output.
3. Re-tag every proposal the wave made from it: new, extends (cite the
   north-star section) or restates. Drop nothing.
4. What did the wave under-cover because the lens pointed elsewhere? Check
   the notes: villains' and side characters' lives, failure and death,
   economy and scarcity, humour and tone, pacing and boredom, time skips,
   permanent losses.
5. Which of the wave's north-star statements does the evidence not
   support, or support only for one author?

Count series by the same author as one source family.

## Output (your folder)

1. REPORT.md: short plain-language summary first; top ten new or
   contradicting ideas, ranked, each with its source family.
2. DIVERGENCE-<series>.md per series.
3. ECHO-AUDIT.md: the re-tagged proposal table, plus a list of north-star
   statements from that wave to qualify (cite line or heading).
4. NORTH_STAR_FINDINGS.md: a proposed subsection, paraphrase only, marked
   proposals. Do not write it into the north star yourself; the haul
   divergence pass folds it in as the single writer.
5. CHECKPOINT.md, saved as you go; write "Status: complete" only after
   hash-free validation of links and coverage of every series.
