## Five topology families and consequential questions

1. **Rift-linked miniworld:** is a rift only a route to a durable world, a fracture extending into an existing region, or the source sustaining a created space? Define endpoint identities, connection stability/duration, reopening, stranded people/cargo, flow of air/light/energy/messages, and collapse versus closure. Temporary access does not automatically reset loot or erase inhabitants.
2. **Anchored pocket/demiplane:** what binds its interior to a seed, item, person, institution or world? Separate owning/moving/breaking an anchor from controlling or destroying its interior. Define actual extent/storage/resources/maintenance, competing access, residents and useful home/research/craft progression. Bigger inside does not establish limitless supply.
3. **Overlapping subplane/layer:** material/spirit/dream/reflection layers can share places while differing in perception, body, contact and energy. Define who sees/touches/acts across, positional mapping, manifestation/projection and where a returning body fits. Detecting a hidden watcher and being able to hit it remain distinct.
4. **Historical echo or constructed realm:** a passive record, interactive recreation, durable branch and repeating trial have different causal promises. Define what can change, observer exposure, narrative versus world time, identities, inhabitants' agency, unique export/claims and supported escape/emergence. Source knowledge of a previous run is not current-world certainty.
5. **Living, internal or owned realm:** a being's inner world, collective dream, cultivated domain or realm-dependent institution can create particular relationships. Specify resident consent/agency, creator dependence, local authority, growth, permeability and what creator death/suppression changes. Do not assume every resident is the owner's property or every creator controls every function.

These families can compose and are not mandatory classes or power ranks. A small room can be exceptionally dangerous; an entire world can be ordinary. Reuse the existing MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES and world/ability authorship owners rather than inventing a separate universal engine.
