## Cross-case evidence beyond these two scenes

**CB1, information versus material capability (159 P045-P053):** Klein recalls earlier picture/voice replies, considers material transfer and proposes sacrifice-to-self followed by bestowment. These are his hypotheses and known gaps at that era, not a depiction of a working transfer. Later success does not retroactively enable it at the first gathering.

**CB2, sacrifice channel and responder (220 P017-P041/P049-P055; 221 P004-P040):** instructions distinguish target symbol, suitable language, spiritual material, experiment cost and a responding presence. The actual first material tier succeeds. Klein supplies the responding-side spirituality, the channel takes the gland, and it closes. A short channel requires scheduled attendance even though the instructions do not impose a fixed cosmological time. Proposed Beyonder-material escalation and its reimbursement are agreed contingencies, not demonstrated failure/retry or a guaranteed refund policy. Klein's 90% confidence is an estimate, not canon success odds.

**CB3, bestowment and later advancement (221 P041-P073):** notification and recipient preparation follow sacrifice; a distinct bestowment ritual puts the gland at Audrey's altar. She then examines it, brews, drinks, integrates and stabilizes. A gift of material is not a gift of rank. The prayer's request for strength does not mean this scene depicts an abstract stat buff; the actual result is the delivered object. Physical delivery is evidence of receipt here, not proof of every possible property's authentication.

**CB4, summoning and return (182 P003-P042):** a summoned spiritual state has its own perception, visibility and upkeep; ending the summon returns Klein above the fog with a corporeal charm. Returning from there to his bedroom does not bring the charm along. Proposed messenger delegation is not yet within his demonstrated means. The complete incantation/setup before this chapter remains outside the read; identity of every summoning target and universal permissions/range are not established.

**CB5, sacrificial descent contrast (1348 P021-P028/P040-P047; 1349 P035-P046):** a speaker offers supplied monsters and a price to be sought from someone else; the later rite consumes sacrificial subjects into a door. The explanation connects material roles to characteristics rather than human species labels. Do not infer the third party's consent or valid liability from the speaker's offer. Item-transfer sacrifice and destructive sacrifice-to-open-a-descent are different consumers; neither proves the other's resource/aftermath policy.

**CB6, advanced ritual contrast:** [Utopia U11/U12](UTOPIA_ENGINE_UI_AUDIT.md#u1-u12-source-process-and-its-architectural-pressure), sourced in displayed 1349-1353, separates helper terms, spirit-world counterpart, remaining efficacy, required external connection, digestion, ingredient custody, brewing and progression. Isolation useful to Neil's preparation is not a universal improvement: excessive isolation blocks Utopia's required connection. Audrey's ordinary material-delivery/brewing sequence and this advanced sustained process must both fit the architecture without forcing either onto the other's UI.

## What can be shared, and what needs different behavior

This is a comparison of selected sourced cases, not an exhaustive taxonomy. Categories can overlap within a process; a sacrificial stage can support a summoning/descent, and a bestowment can supply a later advancement. One ritual name need not select exactly one operation kind.

| Category / source | Common infrastructure worth reusing | Different engine behavior and actual consumer | Different UI/return and failure check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sacrifice**, CB2/CB5 | Identity/custody, method revision, quote, prepared context, stage receipts, clocks and responding actor. | Offering material into an accepted transfer channel differs from irreversibly consuming subjects to create a door. Bind source, target, payer, consumed support versus transferred offering and admitted recipient. | Show offering/ownership, recipient, responder availability, support and irreversible boundary. Waiting for acceptance is not completion. Test missing responder/expired channel and failed transfer without fabricating a refund or loss law. |
| **Bestowment**, CB1/CB3 | Same transfer/custody and response primitives; target/recipient evidence. | An actual giver must possess/supply the selected object/effect and route it to a recipient. Material delivery, information response and sustained faculty support have different result owners/lifetimes. Only material delivery is demonstrated in CB3. | Request/preparation -> received response -> inspect delivered object -> original brewing/task. Display actual result, not merely the invocation's words. Test successful inbound sacrifice with delayed/failed outbound delivery; no duplication or automatic advancement. |
| **Summoning**, TG1/TG2/CB4 | Session IDs, actor/body/channel, support, cost/time, admission and observer projections. | First-gathering projection, summoned spirit state, independently arriving body and a freight channel must not be conflated. CB4 has an active tether, upkeep, termination and operation-specific carriage; a meeting adds neither universal body teleportation nor transport of held inventory. | Present actual location/form, supported perception/commands, return/end and separately carried objects. Inspecting another participant grants no control. Test dismissal/expiry and the charm's different destination across two return steps. Missing setup/range/consent stays qualified. |
| **Divination**, 5 P011-P045; 7 P053-P059; 159 P009-P013 | Question/target/method, preparation, source/era, attributed evidence and cost. | Card selection, observed signal and interpretation differ. The circus's disputed provider is not proof of supernatural accuracy; Alger's theory is a statement. Klein's decision that a deity-related question is risky is not a measured universal risk law. | Show question/method, signal, interpretation/basis and useful next action. Keep unclear/no answer distinct from false belief and actual hidden truth. Test changed question/context, absent grounds and contradictory later evidence without granting omniscience. |
| **Advancement**, CB3/CB6 | Source/ingredient identity, preparation, staged transaction, body/result owner and persistence. | Possession, formula knowledge, brewing, ingestion, integration/stabilization and usable new faculty differ. Advanced rituals can require distributed history/support, not just ingredients or a prayer. Acquisition of a rank is not automatic mastery of every technique. | Progression task links ingredients/method/preparation/body to an actual attempt and next usable capability. Test a gift without ingestion, expired ritual support and successful ingestion without invented complete skill knowledge. Separate material loss, bodily risk and actual outcome. |

Share the **transaction and evidence envelope**, not one universal evaluator: actor/body/controller/world/clock; intent and target/contact; known method/means; recipient/supplier/payer; quote and current eligibility; stage result/provenance; relevant support/interrupt/closure; save/source revisions; actual next consumer and UI return. Select fields by consequence, rather than create a mandatory universal ritual schema. Local mechanisms determine response, transfer, consumption, exposure, transformation and ongoing support.

**Comparison choices:** recommended: local operation adapters over incumbent identity/transaction/evidence primitives. A single ritual wizard with one condition score is rejected as the default because it merges response, material transfer and transformation. An unusual diegetic ritual notebook can guide learned preparation and record results, but is a reference/plan, not the authority that makes a rite happen. Different world laws and later exceptions remain possible through their actual means.
