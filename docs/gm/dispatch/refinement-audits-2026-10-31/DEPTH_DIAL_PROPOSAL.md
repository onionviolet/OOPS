## What the three depths mean

| Depth | Row tiers built | Keeps | Loses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Deep** (current) | D and F rows both become intents | Every CLR6 element (choices, inputs and outputs, time and order, who was there, place, outcomes, what was noticed); quiet plants and withheld facts | Nothing the text states; costs the most per word |
| **Medium** | D rows deep; F rows merge into the neighbouring D intent or one routine-stretch intent per sub-arc, still run through the native commands (this is authored merging, not the PF13 fold command, which stays off) | All decisions, spends, dangers, new mechanisms, appointments; money, stock and time from the net columns; plants the row reader flags | Ordinary talk, travel and prep as separate playable steps. The failed fold proxy lost a withheld fact and a "who was there" in 2 of 5 F rows, so every F row with a `withheld` entry or an unknown net stays deep |
| **Light** | One reference or chronicle stage per sub-arc, plus first uses of each mechanism and the seam state | Seam state, canon order, first-shown mechanisms, a way to reach each major event | Walkable play inside the stretch. This is below the accepted reachability floor for any stretch Klein can act in, so use it only for stretches where the source gives Klein nothing to decide |

## Rates used (measured where possible)

- Deep: 152 stages cover ch1-24 P033 (about 52,000 words), so about **3.0 stages per 1,000 words**. A deep episode is about 1.8 chapters (about 3,500 words, 5-6 intents) and cost about 20 minutes serial (3 read, 12 author, 3-7 native proof; KJ3I-KJ3J).
- Medium: arc-2 rows are 57% tier D (77 of 136), so about **1.5 stages per 1,000 words** and episodes of about 6,000 words (assumption, not measured).
- Light: about **0.5 stages per 1,000 words**, one window per episode (assumption).
- Reader cost does not shrink with depth: one haiku reader per window of at most 12,000 words (AU10), because the row read feeds both the brief and the seam contract. Depth moves builder cost, not reader cost.

## Round 2 (2026-11-02; proposals under AD32, prompted by "what about the rest, more to consider; investigation isn't high priority, improving Proto23-style gameplay is")

The steer reads as: spend less of the budget on investigation text and more on the functional, information-first Proto23-style play ([USER_VISION](../../../USER_VISION.md#functional-proto23-style-ui-over-the-pictured-home-2026-11-01)). It changes two recommendations above and adds the rest of the saga.

**Changes to round 1.**
- **A3 (ch85-129, investigation-shaped): medium becomes "medium on rows with a spend, danger, new mechanism or appointment; chronicle-light on pure investigation rows"**, about 90 stages instead of 131. Reason: the accepted floor (AD47) is that every canon step stays reachable as a step, card or montage day. So "chronicle-light" here means each pure clue row is still a card or a montage day, never one stage for a whole sub-arc (that is the round-1 light and stays below the floor). It saves authored steps, not canon steps. This is a proposal that needs Weibao's yes. Condition: the row has no `withheld` entry, no unknown net and no plant a later scene reads.
- **Novelty rule for every arc: first use of a mechanism is deep, repeats are medium.** The row reads already tag `first-shown` against `repeat` (A2 has 91 first-shown). A second divination client, gathering seat or amulet sale reuses the same loop, so it needs fewer authored steps, and the saved builder time goes to the loops the player actually plays. This depends on the mechanism existing first (backlog B and R rows).
