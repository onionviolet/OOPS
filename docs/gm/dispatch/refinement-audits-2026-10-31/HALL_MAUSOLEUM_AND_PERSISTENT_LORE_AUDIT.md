### Proposed persistent-lore shape and UI

Use existing object/custody, actor knowledge/evidence, inference/intention and native job/effect owners. Proposed conceptual distinctions below describe consumers, not a mandatory new universal save schema:

`source work/entry -> physical or remembered copy + provenance/fidelity -> accessible text -> actually comprehended passage -> attributed claim -> interpretation at that time -> linked question/method/action -> later evidence and revision`.

Keep writing date, described-event interval, acquisition/reading time and later corroboration distinct where relevant. A source edition revision is not a change in world history. Stable content IDs may join copies, but not silently merge distinct entries, uncertain transcriptions, editions or sources sharing a name. Avoid a new global canon database duplicating the private research ledger in runtime.

At 1920x1080, recommended home is the existing Knows/Reference and selected document/task focus. Show acquired entries/copies, learned content and provenance, uncertain dates/words, linked interpretations/questions and useful next actions. Filters/search/timeline include admitted material; do not show unreached chapter titles, hidden counterpart identities or a count of undiscovered entries. No universal percentage-complete diary, automatic reorder into a complete true chronology, or all-pages prerequisite.

Recommended flow: task -> accessible copy -> read/copy/recall through supported means -> learned passage/claim -> compare held evidence or ask a known source -> method/objective -> original task. A deliberate revision view shows "what I thought then" and "why I now think differently" where a real changed belief exists. Reading UI can be a pure recall of admitted knowledge; it must not repeatedly invoke acquisition/harm on redraw. New exposure or a supernatural acquisition operation needs its actual producer/target/protection policy. Research knowledge about hazards does not leak to an early character.

Alternatives considered: a chronological diary scrapbook is useful as a voluntary view of acquired entries with uncertain dates retained; it is insufficient as the whole knowledge model. A detective's hypothesis board is useful when competing evidence changes a decision; it is premature for every ordinary page. An unusual voiced recollection could make selected consequential rereads vivid, but cannot replace inspectable text/provenance and is deferred. These can coexist where useful without creating independent truth owners.
