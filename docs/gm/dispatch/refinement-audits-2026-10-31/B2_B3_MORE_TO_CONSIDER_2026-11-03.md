## Per item
- **R1 Cast fights have no stat basis (highest consequence).** Pack people hold knowledge, relations, wants and tier only. The four original hostiles prove NPCs can run the same ability records and vitals as the player, so the missing piece is per-person bands, not a combat engine. Pathways differ in shape: body pathways (Sailor, Corpse Collector) read as physical bands; Seer, Spectator and Apprentice read as perception, spirit and tools, with little direct force. One band shape would misrepresent half of them. Gate: a fight window needs the person's sheet, otherwise it stays a card. Hidden strength: shown, believed and actual stay separate (WA50), so Neil's actual strength is not what Klein sees.
- **D1 Bands by tier or on demand.** Default: principals and recurring people get full sheets from the pack; role and crowd people derive from a pathway-and-sequence table (one row per pathway/sequence, reusable by every cast member and hostile). Sequence is often unstated (Klein's is never numbered, kjw6 Q13), so the table carries "open" rather than a guess.
- **D2 Exhaustion length.** 720 min is an assumed fallback. Precedent for residue is 240-480. Propose 240 and sleep as the cure (ch33 P038). Neil also says repeated practice eases the side effects (ch33 P024), a text-backed reason for the length to shrink with development (O2).
- **O1 Potion-making.** The brew envelope is product-agnostic; dose behaviour is separate per potion. Different potions differ in ingredients and price (Corpse Collector's 230-pound formula was scaled to 5 pounds), drink side effects (Seer: whispers and an urge to destroy, formula row; Corpse Collector: needs courage), and what they leave. The Seer drink is issued by Nighthawks and brewed by Neil, so the lead's consumer is the drink, not brewing. Brewing matters when the lead or an NPC makes the next potion. Existing rules to reuse: procurement attention (an observer recognises an ingredient pattern), characteristic as main material, related-pathway substitution, measured brewing below Sequence 7. Advancement needs digestion, and the probe found no digestion consumer; that is a gap to design, not to invent.
- **O2 Use-based development.** The engine already carries `faculty.<id>.development` and power `masteryBands` with `costScale`. Development can lower a use's cost and soften its residue, differently per pathway (divination accuracy, Spirit Vision stamina, a Sailor's body control). Build on those, not a new meter. Matches Weibao's steer (a use with cost, growing later).
- **R2 Side effects and failure.** B1 covers only the successful, steadied drink. Stated causes of loss of control (wrong pathway, second pathway, remnant spirituality in materials, ch33 P008) are the failure mechanisms. Canon Klein succeeds, so failure is an untaken option with a card fallback. Each pathway needs its own drink side effect and its own steadier; no blanket rule.
