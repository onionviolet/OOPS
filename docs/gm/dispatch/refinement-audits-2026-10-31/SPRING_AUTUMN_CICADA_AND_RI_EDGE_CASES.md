## Correct the inherited model

F-SAC1: RIG36's “prestige with a fuel gauge” shorthand loses the dangerous method, continuity and retained source. RIX13's example of a self-detonating Cicada misleadingly puts the Gu itself among consumed relics. Replace those interpretations with source-specific expenditure and aftermath. Keep ordinary prestige, technical save loading, historical projection and in-world rebirth separate operations.

F-SAC2: Current life resources fund the attempt; earlier-life knowledge can remain useful without restoring former cultivation or transporting every item. The Cicada's own surviving identity, weak condition and bond need their own treatment. Exact rebirth arrival-state mapping beyond these scenes needs additional source qualification.

F-SAC3: Rebirth does not make old knowledge current truth. Retain the remembered account with its prior-life provenance, then let new observations and changed causes revise its usefulness. Do not silently erase memories when an associated prediction fails, and do not promise that every large event still occurs.
