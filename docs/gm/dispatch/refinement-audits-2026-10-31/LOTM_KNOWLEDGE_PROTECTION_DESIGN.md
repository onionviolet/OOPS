# LOTM knowledge protection: recommended default and alternatives

2026-10-31. **Design proposal, not runtime implementation or an accepted world law.** Weibao first asked for leeway because the base cheat could protect against the worst external-knowledge effects, then reopened the choice: "Hoenstly, is it better for no protection? and reading things willy nilly can get me killed? more to cosider? hwat is the best way to go about this?" This keeps both requests visible rather than treating the initial suggestion as a final decision.

Applies AD5/AD6–AD8/AD22/AD38/AD44–AD46, WA54–WA60 and [LR3-L2](LORE_EXCEPTION_RESEARCH_ROUND3.md#lr3-l2-acquisition-of-information-can-itself-be-an-exposure). No new primary reading in this design pass. That source sample establishes consequential exposure, not exact safe semantic content, a complete immunity threshold or a verified universal protection supplied by the cheat. Below, the protective functions are explicit project proposals to qualify against the selected source/branch before coding.

## Recommendation

**KP1: use strong passive protection for routine information acquisition, with a deliberate deeper-exposure route and actual support conditions.** The cheat is part of the actor's means. Its normal protected route should be convenient enough that reading notes and using ordinary UI does not become a series of death-risk confirmations. Dangerous investigation remains possible through unmediated contact, a method beyond current protection, or genuine loss of protective support. Death can remain a possible consequence of an actually dangerous exposure; it is not a punishment for clicking through normal menus.

This suits the current lead-world direction: live in the world, investigate and use its advantages, while choosing how far to push. Protection need not erase uncertainty, grant access to every source, reveal sealed truths or supply correct interpretation. A protected but incomplete account can be useful without pretending that its absent detail is false. When the actual protective means can carry a fuller understanding safely, permit it under AD38 rather than imposing a permanent rank-only lock.

The rejected defaults are retained as alternatives, not forbidden choices. No difficulty selector or new gameplay mode is authorized here.

## What protection could actually do

**KP4: isolate, mediate and contain, according to the hazard.** A source-qualified external correspondence/link can be intercepted or severed by the cheat. A dangerous pattern can instead be mediated into an account that omits the operative component. Material that needs deeper support can remain contained for a later supported examination. Use the mechanisms that actually fit; isolation alone does not automatically neutralize an intrinsically harmful pattern or hostile physical carrier. “External knowledge” must not become one undifferentiated damage tag.

Start with one actual consumer rather than building all three systems. For example, a protected reading operation can use a present protective source to deliver an admitted account while keeping a dangerous contact channel closed. This is an implementation hypothesis, not a verified canon recipe. The owning source record must identify what the protection blocks, what is still learned and what remains unknown. A keyword censor or `isLead` exemption would not establish the mechanism. Another actor with equivalent means should receive the same relevant protection.

**KP5: use real costs rather than an invented tax on ordinary reading.** Passive coverage can normally be cheap while the source is available. A deeper operation may need the cheat's protected context, attention, preparation, support or stronger control where those have an in-world reason. An actual opposed source can strain or bypass protection if its mechanism supports that result. Do not add a generic fuel meter, cooldown or guaranteed backlash merely to make the feature look balanced. Exact capacity, cost and recovery remain proposed dials, not source facts.

## UI and failure that preserve the choice

At 1920×1080, actual 14/21 px, keep the selected document/message, current protective means and useful learned account together. Ordinary reading uses the protected route by default when it is genuinely supported. Avoid repetitive confirmation popups. An advanced action can say “Attempt a deeper reading” and show the known support, cost and uncertainty. It must not list unknown entities, exact fatal odds or hidden contents as a warning.

**KP6: interface inspection and diegetic exposure are distinct.** Opening an admitted journal projection does not itself invent a new reading event. Actually examining a new object, receiving a message or recalling a newly consequential pattern uses that mechanism's real event/command. A harmful physical act can still be dangerous; UI vocabulary should identify the act the player is choosing.

If protection becomes unavailable before a buffered protected acquisition releases information, the proposed default is to hold the unreleased material and refresh the available means. That requires an actual buffer/containment mechanism; it is not retroactive immunity. Once exposure has occurred, leaving the pane or restoring a ward cannot silently undo harm. Loss of support should have an admitted visible consequence where the actor can detect it, without granting omniscience about hidden failures. This separates prevention, detection, suppression and recovery.

## Future implementation contract

**KP7, ACCOUNT FOR NOW:** bind subject/channel, actual reader/body/world, protective source and its current support, exposure/operation clock, what information is released, resulting admitted knowledge and actual consequence. Distinguish protected default, chosen deeper method and genuinely unsupported/unavailable simulation. A developer gap is not a fictional death guarantee or magical incapacity.

Keep protection at the source/command/exposure seam. `observerView` is a projection; `accrueHarm` currently applies authored weights to selected committed actions, not a qualified hazardous-learning classifier. Knowledge and harm retain their own owners. Persist actual exposure receipts, protective source/revision and in-flight terms when introduced; do not auto-read later facts or apply retrospective harm during save migration. A display setting cannot change magical immunity.

Small distinguishing scenarios for the selected consumer: ordinary protected acquisition delivers the supported account without its blocked external contact; removing support before release holds the buffered acquisition rather than silently leaking it; an explicit deeper operation follows its own actual exposure result; redraw/reload does not replay the same exposure; existing harm is not cleansed by closing the page. Check equivalent means on a non-lead fixture and an observer who does not learn the hidden cause. These are proposed checks, not executed tests.

**ACT NOW, done:** recorded alternatives, the recommended default and the future source/consumer boundaries in this owning proposal, linked from the live LOTM plan. **LATER / DELEGATE, not dispatched:** when a real hazardous-reading consumer is selected and runtime work is authorized, qualify its hazard/protection mechanism and build one protected/deeper-use slice. One judgment-capable implementer, medium effort; completion uses that actual result, support-loss path and applicable AD36/AD43 persistence/browser proof. No broader lore reread or all-hazard simulator is a prerequisite.

Open: exact base-cheat capability in the chosen start/era, how safe mediation retains useful understanding, which hazards depend on contact versus intrinsic pattern, support/capacity and residual/recurring effects. No universal immunity, universal mortality or mandatory exposure mode is accepted by saving this proposal.
