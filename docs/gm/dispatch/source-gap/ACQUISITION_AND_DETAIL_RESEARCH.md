## Regression rule and boundary extraction

A regression source should yield an interaction profile, not a generic reset button. Record initiator and owner; qualifying trigger; anchor choice/control; temporal distance; world/region/layer/branch targeted; state reverted versus retained; carriers such as body, soul, memory, skill, equipment and external records; energy/payment/limits; action/effect order; eligible observers and other returners; and actual resistance/failure cases. Keep a character's understanding at that chapter separate from later narrator confirmation.

Track objective world time, subjective experienced time and lifespan/body aging separately. Knowing a future is not immunity to intervention: preserve who made the prediction, what action changed the causal chain and when the knowledge stopped applying. Training memory, bodily adaptation, fuel access, binding promises, relationships and stolen power ownership may obey different reset policies. Do not let one carryover imply all others.

For connected worlds, ask whether regression reaches absent destinations, travelers currently outside the target realm, pocket instances, observers looking from another era, and records made outside the affected history. Multiple returners need individually attributed knowledge and reset ordering. A powerful observer may notice repetition only when the source supplies a faculty or evidence. Awareness does not itself grant immunity, escape or access to every branch. These are authoring questions, not declared rules for the current runtime or automatic canon implications.

New unsupplied regression sources remain candidates; no new full-text goal or missing-text book chat is needed.
