# Systems, cheats and consequential breadth

2026-10-11. Finite expansion of [SC01–SC10](ASSIGNMENT.md), proposals only.
All worked journeys are original hypotheticals. They are neither source canon
nor browser proof. The creative director retains selection and acceptance.

A fictional **System** is an actor/tool/provider in the fiction with supported
services and authority. Shared software services implement its promises; they
are not themselves an in-world System. A passive fixed cheat need only its actual
consumers. No universal cap, cost, drawback, slot, pity rule or awakening follows.

## SC01: progression changes practical capability

Do not equate a strong body, excellent tool, learned procedure, rich workshop and
licensed office. Each can open different actions, and each has its own carrier.
A progression route freezes the thing that changes, how it changes, the actual
consumer, required support, loss/suppression rules, and known presentation.
Numbers count when they change work; stages count when they change functions.

| Leaf | Functional variants and early → middle → later horizon | Concrete player decision and observable criterion |
|---|---|---|
| SC01.V01 Mastery | Rehearsal improves an existing procedure; instruction imparts understanding; perfect comprehension may impart the whole method instantly. Early: one reliable preparation. Middle: variable conditions, combined methods or teaching. Later: original technique and workforce instruction where lawful. | Practice known work, buy teaching or experiment. Same actual task with improved control changes output/effort; copied text alone cannot stand in for ability unless the selected function supplies it. |
| SC01.V02 Body/source | Training changes actual physiology; a lineage supplies a faculty; an implant or pact supplies a distinct source. Early: acquisition/activation and one actual use. Middle: altered operating envelope or a new faculty. Later: rebuilding a body or transferring a supported source. | Train existing body, acquire support or change body. Show known method without source, then source with actual execution. A body swap preserves only declared carriers. |
| SC01.V03 Equipment/architecture | Precision, condition, material, structure and operating knowledge remain separate. Early: repair/use one actual item. Middle: upgrade a supported function or install a component. Later: an assembly, production standard or genuinely superior device. | Repair a familiar device, replace it, buy specialist service or refit it. The same item identity returns on removal; new architecture opens its advertised action. Dynamic assembly is unnecessary for a fixed-item first slice. |
| SC01.V04 Knowledge/research | Rumor, observed evidence, understanding, tested recipe and executable method differ. Early: identify a real problem. Middle: test competing explanations and make a useful design. Later: create/teach a new local method. | Follow a mentor's reliable recipe or investigate an anomaly. An unsuccessful test spends its selected inputs and produces useful evidence without falsely granting a working recipe. |
| SC01.V05 Relationships/access | Personal trust, testimony, legal credential and office authority have different effects. Early: one person permits a service. Middle: collaboration or institutional entry. Later: operate a school, office or independent network. | Earn a reference, bargain, seek another provider or refuse duties. Access opens a real activity; revoked office stops office acts without erasing personal craft. NPC recognition propagates only through an actual channel. |
| SC01.V06 Wealth/property | Liquid resources buy services; owned property supplies use/capacity; organizational assets supply rights and obligations. Early: tools/room. Middle: workshop production or hired expertise. Later: infrastructure/territory where supported. | Rent, buy, cooperate or stay mobile. Facility use changes an actual consumer; inventory wealth alone cannot operate an unstaffed process that needs expertise. |

Early delivery can select one or two dimensions and connect them. Later horizon
includes lateral specialization, conversion, consolidation and chosen decline,
without making every route mandatory or every lower method forever superior.
An extraordinary specialist may still pay an ordinary engineer, cook or advocate
because those people supply actual missing means. Where a superior means really
supersedes ordinary work, let it do so; ordinary life elsewhere remains playable.

Minimum joins: existing native skill/item owners; actual source/faculty holder;
knowledge records; actor/custody/access where selected; real task/output; native
projection; durable settlement. Body conversion, nested assemblies, new office
jurisdiction and institutional production need selected adapters, not a parallel
universal progression save. Match G2-001/010/011/023/026 and G8-006–016/021/023.

### J-FIX: fixed-function journey with growing uses

Original hypothetical: **Still Parcel** stops deterioration of a nonliving object
placed inside its carrier. It does not make the object, identify it, repair it,
transport it or improve the user's knowledge. No leveling/fuel rule is selected.
Its physical admission rule is local to this example, not a global storage cap.

Early: acquire carrier → place a prepared reagent → wait through a supported
clock interval → remove the same preserved specimen → use it in an ordinary
recipe. A matching outside specimen follows ordinary deterioration. Player may
use it, sell it or keep it. Identity/quantity/condition survive the transaction.

Middle: learn an uncommon preparation whose output otherwise spoils before the
journey completes → arrange transport and custody → deliver intact → gain a real
service/payment. Ordinary preparation and route knowledge remain necessary.
Trade can occur by selling the actual carrier or delivering removed contents,
with different custody consequences. A thief who acquires the carrier can use
its actual function without automatically learning its contents' identity.

Later: a clinic or workshop adopts preserved intermediate stock → production
cadence and actual supply change → specialists can attempt work formerly blocked
by freshness. The same cheat remains unchanged; institutions react only if they
learn the facts. The player can retain it privately, rent service, cooperate or
cede ownership. This is logistics, not a mandatory combat escalation.

Minimum proof: preserved vs ordinary samples, two actual consumers, contents
removed once, loss of holder access, preserved storage across reload/import,
and no reward duplication on failed publication. Storage plus condition/clock
consumers is missing selected binding, not proof from a numeric capability.
Match G2-016/026/029 and material/storage owners; consult SC08 for genuine pocket
worlds, whose inhabitants/time/export promises are different.

### J-BRIDGE: original lens-worker journey

Early: lens grinding → calibrated ordinary survey lens → observe a local optical
response → acquire a tested inscription method through teaching or an explicit
experiment → secure compatible material → manufacture and calibrate a **signal
lens** → use it to locate an actual concealed maintenance seam → service that
seam. The new action reads one selected signal, not all hidden truths. Each arrow
has a real input, actor knowledge, elapsed work and output. Example fiction and
mechanism are invented; no franchise law is claimed.

Middle: refine the mount for vibration resistance, collaborate with a carrier
specialist, or build ordinary lenses for customers. Better inscription alone does
not fix an incorrectly ground surface. A local perfect-manufacture cheat, if
selected, can produce a perfect physical part immediately, but does not confer
an absent signal-interpretation faculty unless that is among its actual promises.

Later: teach ordinary fabrication, publish a tested compatible design, operate a
survey service or pursue new signals. Access to one extraordinary technique does
not make every worker supernatural. A player who stops before inscription still
has an ordinary lens and a productive career. The retained ordinary skill matters
for actual fabrication and repair, rather than an invented balance tax.

First slice may adapt an existing ordinary native method and one fixed authored
item. It need not wait for random-start/world creation or a dynamic assembly
solver. Reserve its actual content, recipe/output, knowledge and native consumer
with current D1/MICRO-P3 owners before dispatch. Match G2-011, G8-011/021 and
material requirements; preserve earlier D1 S-O2/M-P2 rather than replacing them.

## SC10: a fictional System needs a host and honest authority

**software service** is engine code implementing those promises. A quest panel is
not a fictional provider; an actor resource named points is not a shop; registering
a capability is not permission to grant bodies, knowledge or jurisdiction.
Some Systems only issue quests. Some only teach or record achievements. Others
create worlds or serve a host. Do not force every row below into every variant.

### SC10.V09: player-as-System, audit and exact carriers

Original **system-entity play** variant: the player is the provider, not an avatar
with a different HUD. Early: select a fitting host, make a service offer and choose
an objective/reward the provider can actually support. The host can ask for terms,
refuse, abandon or pursue unrelated goals under their own knowledge and means.
The player observes only through its actual channels. Refusal does not silently
become control. A possession/compulsion variant is a different acquired authority
with its own actual mechanism, not an automatic System privilege.

Middle: allocate provider-owned instruction, stock or observation services among
hosts; negotiate cooperation; improve a service or change allegiance. Later:
operate several autonomous hosts, transfer the provider carrier, fork independent
providers or become a world service only when those functions are selected. No
artificial supply budget is added to a creation-backed provider. Conversely,
writing a reward offer does not create a tool when this provider only owns finite
stock. Choice: invest in one willing specialist, serve many hosts, become a peer,
supply free help, demand reciprocal work or cease service. Proof: a host declines,
one accepts and independently fulfils a feasible objective, the provider delivers
its actual output, and a competing host request resolves according to the real
supply/authority contract. No global mind-reading or world simulation follows.

**Audit and exploits:** a read-contract means reveals clauses; an observed receipt
can reveal a bad classification; an authorized inspection means can expose local
System code/rules. A lawful-in-fiction clause exploit satisfies actual criteria in
an unexpected way, such as completing a repair without buying the issuer's kit.
It differs from cheating: forged evidence, hidden manipulation of an evaluator or
an acquired authority overriding the rule. Build each as actual rule/evidence
behavior. A trusted omniscient verifier cannot be fooled merely because a UI says
"bluff"; a weak receipt-only verifier can be fooled if the selected means actually
forge its evidence. The host may challenge an error, bargain, work around it or
change providers. The provider may honor the literal clause, clarify only future
offers, dispute it through an actual process or remain deceived. No universal
punishment, price or vulnerability is introduced. Outcomes are committed fiction,
not an instruction to break the real save engine. This joins SC05's information
and cheating consumers without implying every exploit is a wager.

| Carrier/grant | On provider disconnect | On world travel | On provider/host transfer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earned ordinary mastery/understood method | Actual learned state remains. | Memory/method travels only with its declared carrier; local executability still needs support. | Changing provider does not hand the host's learning to the new host. Explicit teaching/copy may. |
| Permanent installed faculty | Remains if truly installed; explicit source destruction differs from lost service. | Body/source/authority carriers and local compatibility decide. | Moving provider alone does not move host anatomy; actual faculty transfer needs its own operation. |
| Leased live grant/remote computation | Stops at defined loss point; in-flight output follows frozen settlement policy. | Continues only with actual reach/carrier, else stops and can restore on return. | Rebind only by actual entitlement; old host loses lease without losing settled permanent goods. |
| Provider-owned storage goods | Goods keep real identity/custody; retrieval, escrow or loss policy is explicit. | Stored goods move only through actual storage carrier or linked reach. | Host transfer cannot duplicate contents or silently convert third-party goods to a gift. |
| Unclaimed earned reward | Receipt/entitlement remains with its owner under agreed terms; future availability is separate. | Claim works only through supported route/reach. | Existing entitlement remains owed to old host unless actual terms transfer it. |

Read scope: the north-star system evolution section's axis/trigger proposals and
its explicit synopsis-only research boundary. Its S0–S7 palette, survey book
claims, proposed season cadence and suggested save fields are dated proposals,
not canonical ladders, source-qualified franchise facts or implemented APIs.
This expansion uses original variants, and treats interface/mind/allegiance,
reach, integrity, users, origin and form as independently selectable changes.
A nicer voice alone can be texture; a changed mind/authority needs actual behavior
if promised. An upgrade need not force a new puzzle when increased output itself
changes real decisions or outcomes.

### J-SYS: connected quest, reward, shop and upgrade

Original hypothetical **Workshop Concord** is a device-hosted helper that can
verify its contracted host's completed recorded fabrication, grant instruction
from its local library and exchange earned vouchers for provider-owned tool stock.
Its authority includes neither universal mind reading nor creation from nothing.
Those are properties of this proposal, not restrictions on every System.

Early: host accepts a repair objective → deposits the actual customer tool into
job custody → completes native repair using available means → returns the same
repaired tool → provider sees the contracted receipt → host chooses a voucher or
one fabrication lesson → claims once. A lesson first supplies understood method;
practice/source requirements remain only if that method actually has them. Shop
purchase freezes stock, price and output and grants an actual owned gauge. The
gauge changes a real fabrication consumer. Refusal leaves ordinary repair work
available; duplicate claim/purchase cannot duplicate goods.

Middle: host chooses a collaboration service upgrade, supported by a real acquired
provider extension → a known apprentice can accept teaching → completed instruction
changes the apprentice's actual means → apprentice performs one independent job.
Alternative upgrade adds diagnosis instead; it reads only its supported phenomena.
A System level number without these functions is not an upgrade. Failed training
or an unwilling apprentice does not spend a success reward. Replay of a settled
lesson does not create another apprentice or another grant.

Later: host may run the workshop, negotiate a different provider, become the
System coordinating another host, or leave service entirely. On disconnect, the
shop and new diagnostics stop. Already owned gauge and genuinely learned repair
method remain; leased observation or storage access follows its selected terms.
Existing tool stock, pending reward entitlements and stored goods remain in their
actual custody, with a defined retrieval/escrow or loss policy. Permanent faculty
grants can survive provider loss when promised; no blanket wipe is assumed.

Proof path: bind → accept/refuse → custody/work/receipt → claim → shop → useful
output → one new service → leave/retain. Repeat claim, interruption, wrong host,
provider change mid-job and reload/import cover the settlement boundaries. A
first slice needs only one ordinary objective, one grant and one actual output;
agent autonomy and player-as-System can remain later proposals.

Minimum dependencies: provider-host relation and authority; actual objective
progress owner; event/receipt witness; one durable entitlement/payment/grant;
existing goods/knowledge/resource owner; native panels/actions; schema migration.
Do not build a universal hook bus or all reward classes before one quest service.
Match G2-012/014/020/023/027/029, G8 title/skill consumers and SC09 NPC/economy.

Every selected leaf fixes: qualified pool revision; eligibility and correlations;
weights; commitment trigger; attempt ID; cost/reservation; no-result and duplicate
policy; observation; retry/rewind rights; affected RNG owner; and export/import
meaning. Pools can depend on an authored ecology, finite merchant stock or current
NPC means rather than a timeless drop table. Distribution checks test selected
weights and boundaries; they do not demonstrate gameplay acquisition/use.

A proposed ordinary commitment sequence is validate current context → reserve or
stage actual stake/cost → calculate selected draw → stage outcome and receipt →
persist the joined plan → publish knowledge and actual effects. Failed durable
publication leaves no live payment or reward. Retry either repeats a stored
pending result or recomputes from unchanged state according to the selected
contract. Nothing guarantees commitment has occurred simply because an
animation started. A separate reserved/pending state is necessary only when the
local operation is meaningfully asynchronous.

Seed, pool revision and draw index can reconstruct a result only if algorithms,
pool ordering and eligibility remain stable. Otherwise store the committed
semantic result and provenance. Content changes cannot retroactively reroll an
already owned cache or start. Hidden weights remain sealed where needed; UI
shows observed terms, commitment and actual discovery rather than diagnostic
truth. New streams are introduced only where independence is promised; opening
a menu must not advance any gameplay stream.

Money, items, favors or enforceable obligations are different stake types.
A debt wager is possible only with a creditor/contract and actual recovery
promise; a negative coin number is not a debt system. Refusal or interrupted
play follows the local rule: return unsettled escrow, resolve a committed hand,
or record an enforceable breach. These are alternatives, not one universal
refund rule. Settlement must cover ties, voids, forfeits, unpaid opponents and
repeated callbacks. A double or nothing round is a new commitment against
owned or explicitly pledged winnings, not a redraw of an old receipt.

Original hypothetical journey GJ1: a warehouse worker earns spendable coins,
learns a visible three-choice card rule, compares a small stake with buying a
tool, and enters against a funded adult rival. The hand commits when both
accept. The player can fold under stated terms or continue using a learned
probability estimate. A win/loss changes both purses and the rival's observed
memory. The player stops at a declared budget, buys or delays the tool, and
uses it on an ordinary job. Reload preserves the settled hand and tool custody.
Variants add a supported card-reading faculty, a bluff or a publicly accepted
open-information game. None requires discovering a concealed world secret.
This is an original design journey, not executable evidence or accepted numbers.
