Every selected leaf fixes: qualified pool revision; eligibility and correlations;
weights; commitment trigger; attempt ID; cost/reservation; no-result and duplicate
policy; observation; retry/rewind rights; affected RNG owner; and export/import
meaning. Pools can depend on an authored ecology, finite merchant stock or current
NPC means rather than a timeless drop table. Distribution checks test selected
weights and boundaries; they do not demonstrate gameplay acquisition/use.

A proposed ordinary commitment sequence is validate current context → reserve or
stage actual stake/cost → calculate selected draw → stage outcome and receipt →
persist the joined plan → publish knowledge and actual effects. Failed durable
publication leaves no live payment or reward. Retry either repeats a stored
pending result or recomputes from unchanged state according to the selected
contract. Nothing guarantees commitment has occurred simply because an
animation started. A separate reserved/pending state is necessary only when the
local operation is meaningfully asynchronous.

Seed, pool revision and draw index can reconstruct a result only if algorithms,
pool ordering and eligibility remain stable. Otherwise store the committed
semantic result and provenance. Content changes cannot retroactively reroll an
already owned cache or start. Hidden weights remain sealed where needed; UI
shows observed terms, commitment and actual discovery rather than diagnostic
truth. New streams are introduced only where independence is promised; opening
a menu must not advance any gameplay stream.

## SC05: Gambling has custody, opponents and decisions

| Leaf | Early | Middle | Later | Actual obligation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SC05.01 physical games | Choose stake, game and whether to continue in an original dice/card venue. | Learn rules, read tells, change strategy or enter a fitting tournament. | Rivalries, reputation, changing opponents and institutions. | Actual opponent/house liquidity, legal moves, held knowledge, wager escrow and settlement; both wins and losses change holdings. |
| SC05.02 event wagers | Bet on a witnessed local contest or decline; distinguish odds from certainty. | Research participants and disagree with a quote based on learned evidence. | Sponsorship, bookmaking and independent actor outcomes. | Wager freezes terms before the underlying event; outcome comes from that event, not a second casino roll. |
| SC05.03 exceptional means | Use a locally supported observation/fortune/cheating function or play plainly. | Conceal, reveal or negotiate that means; opponent counters require real means. | Institutions adapt where they learn and can act. | Exact affected draw/information/physical state, observed detection and consequences. No automatic detector or punishment appears merely to balance a cheat. |
| SC05.04 repeated play | Explicit budget and stop condition; choose manual play or allowed automation. | Delegate to a capable actor, use strategy and decide whom to trust. | Venue operation, shared risk and regional liquidity. | Automation checks funds/terms/means each round, persists unique receipts and stops on its declared boundary. No infinite settlement loop from one offline update. |

Money, items, favors or enforceable obligations are different stake types.
A debt wager is possible only with a creditor/contract and actual recovery
promise; a negative coin number is not a debt system. Refusal or interrupted
play follows the local rule: return unsettled escrow, resolve a committed hand,
or record an enforceable breach. These are alternatives, not one universal
refund rule. Settlement must cover ties, voids, forfeits, unpaid opponents and
repeated callbacks. A double or nothing round is a new commitment against
owned or explicitly pledged winnings, not a redraw of an old receipt.

Original hypothetical journey GJ1: a warehouse worker earns spendable coins,
learns a visible three-choice card rule, compares a small stake with buying a
tool, and enters against a funded adult rival. The hand commits when both
accept. The player can fold under stated terms or continue using a learned
probability estimate. A win/loss changes both purses and the rival's observed
memory. The player stops at a declared budget, buys or delays the tool, and
uses it on an ordinary job. Reload preserves the settled hand and tool custody.
Variants add a supported card-reading faculty, a bluff or a publicly accepted
open-information game. None requires discovering a concealed world secret.
This is an original design journey, not executable evidence or accepted numbers.

Commit-on-creation suits physically existing contents; commit-on-purchase suits
seller provisioning; commit-on-opening suits a true generated grant. Do not make
these synonyms. Mutable contents, perishability or theft require container
custody and internal state, not a permanently cached label. Unknown/unavailable
item definitions need a preserve/hold/recovery policy rather than substituting a
new roll. Reward publication uses complete actual item keys and quantity;
opening animation, rarity title and acquisition log never replace the item.

Original hypothetical journey MJ1: an apprentice salvages a sealed repair cache,
observes its maker's mark using an ordinary trade skill, and chooses between
selling it unopened or opening it for workshop use. Opening reveals an actual
component, which can repair a damaged tool; a duplicate can be sold under a
normal sale contract. One alternative result enables a different existing
recipe. Save/reload/import retains cache identity, consumed container, owned
component, repair and sale receipts. A paid-draw variant reserves payment;
a system entitlement variant reserves the entitlement. This is proposal-only.
