# TA3 ask kinds and settlement

2026-10-14. Proposal only, covering TA-C3 and TA-L2. Build after TA1 play feedback, not a new gate on TA1. [REPORT](REPORT.md) ranks implementation. [REPEAT](REPEAT.md) owns renewal and retention; [LENSES](LENSES.md) owns knowledge and diagnostics.

## F1. Live seams and constraints

Read against [story_requests.js](../../../../../src/story_requests.js), [generated_people.js](../../../../../src/generated_people.js), [lore_history_state.js](../../../../../src/lore_history_state.js), [lore_history_view.js](../../../../../src/lore_history_view.js) and [story_aftermath.js](../../../../../src/story_aftermath.js).

P34 revision 1 accepts one ordinary item, equal to the issuer's saved goal, with quantity and needRevision both 1. Its fee leaves the person's purse when raised, enters held escrow, and releases only after acceptance and delivery. Decline refunds it. Each command clones the full envelope, checks sequence, validates and persists before native publication. Keep that transaction boundary for every kind. UI prechecks are conveniences, never settlement authority.

P4's psychological `need` (rest, belonging, purpose) is distinct from its supply `goal` and from a request's `needRef`. Do not turn resting pressure into an invented medicine diagnosis. Delivered legacy rows require the original goal fulfilled and original supplies retained. Replacing that goal would invalidate old rows. Preserve it as a legacy record.

History schema 1 permits only six action names and item/coin/skill deltas. References and known strings are at most 240 characters; applied deltas at most 64, causeRefs/historyCauses at most eight, audience at most four. New kinds, relationship changes and long canonical product IDs require explicit schema work. Current recap says “one item”; current aftermath turns every request outcome into road advice. Both need typed projections before other kinds go live.

## D1. Proposed common contract

Use the next request revision available after TA1, provisionally revision 3. Retain revision-specific readers for rev 1 and TA1 rev 2. Add optional person-owned current needs, separate from the immutable legacy goal. Each has a stable need ID, monotonically increasing revision, kind, world instance, terms revision and origin cause. Each obligation freezes issuer, need ID/revision, exact requirements, reward, escrow, knowledge requirements, cancellation terms and progress refs. One pending obligation per need revision. New generation does not rewrite a raised contract.

A settlement key includes request ID and need revision; accepted progress steps have unique step IDs. Check current sequence, write authority, current world/place, issuer availability, all inputs and progress again in the detached plan. Publish all stock, money, skill, knowledge and standing changes from that single durable result. Repeated commands are rejected or return the retained result without paying again. No partial native publication on failure.

Default item deliveries are all-or-nothing. The player selects exact owned stacks; no auto-selection of equipped, reserved, chest or vendor goods. If a later slice supports installments it must own custody, cancellation refunds and per-line balances first. Selection previews draw nothing. Consent to one request does not reserve every matching inventory item.

## D2. P2 and GX5 integration

[P2 source](../../../../../src/mixture_state.js) retains composition, process, dose and revision in canonical products. There is no cough property or disease cure in its current law. “Something for the road cough” remains authored flavor pending a real treatment adapter; do not silently map it to health regeneration. Predicate names must describe existing supported effects. A playable witness includes buying/gathering inputs, making a vial, observing a consumed dose, then making another matching vial for delivery. Price/effort must account for that experiment and repeat batch.

[GX5 projection](../../../../../src/product_appearance_view.js) recognizes only exactly observed product types, not all vials sharing a color, ingredient, recipe family or process. Delivery never allocates another appearance slot, consumes an appearance draw or teaches side effects. Pre-GX5 products remain eligible using P2 knowledge even without appearance bindings. Unknown matching products remain unknown and ineligible for an identified ask; rejection says identification is required without leaking whether hidden potency passes.

Canonical P2 IDs embed JSON and can exceed history's 240-character ref bound, including when nested inside inventory keys. Add a validated compact product-ref registry or equivalent typed reference adapter with collision checks and full owner resolution. Keep the original canonical inventory key in P2/native inventory; do not shorten physical identity or merely raise a string limit without a byte budget. Settlement uses compact delta keys resolved through the adapter. Receipt-only refs must retain product truth after physical stock is consumed or retired. No duplicate product catalogue in traveler state.

## D3. Economy, food and crafting joins

Food asks transfer goods; they do not lower player fullness, grant meal buffs or train Famine. Existing PROJECT direction is useful meal benefits, comfortable unfed ordinary play and no hunger death. NPC recurring food use needs an explicit supply-use action; psychological needs do not imply starvation. Do not switch on the optional hunger owner or make food errands a survival tax.

TA1's obtainable pool and fee rules remain the base. Trader catalog existence is not guaranteed current stock: report access prerequisites, restock behavior and current affordability separately. Recheck actual stock when buying, never refill vendors from an ask. A closed trader route pauses an offer or permits voluntary renegotiation; it does not charge the player. Higher grade asks need a reachable quality range somewhere in the world, with current station/skill shortfalls classified as stretch and undiscovered routes classified as unknown. Show an honest uncertainty/effort estimate. Never consume the shared `craft` stream to pick a need, inspect a recipe or test reachability.

Quality goods and mixtures enter a person's custody with exact variants. Legacy `possessions.supplies` accepts only the original ordinary goal item at count one, so it cannot store these new deliveries. Add a bounded, validated exact-key holder for new needs, integrated with P2 envelope reference validation. Renew only after meaningful use/transfer frees custody, or an independently real need appears. Storage refusal leaves player goods and escrow unchanged.

## D4. Amendment 1: reach bands, grades and rewards

[Amendment 1](../PLAN.md#amendment-1-2026-10-14-asks-can-be-out-of-reach) supersedes the original fresh-player route filter. Keep a data-driven item ID plus optional `grade` field in every item requirement line. An absent grade means no extra grade restriction. It does not rewrite an ordinary saved pelt into a graded specimen. A grade adapter may combine completeness and handling quality; it must follow HV1's chosen item-key or grade-suffixed-ID representation, not invent another one. Equipment numeric quality and carcass whole/damaged/scraps are different properties, not interchangeable tiers.

[Harvest PLAN](../../harvest-modules-2026-10-14/PLAN.md) is coming work, not live proof. Activate a whole/unblemished-pelt predicate only after HV1 supplies a real reachable grade producer, native identity reader and import proof. Keep pre-harvest ordinary items valid under their saved contracts. Grade asks check the selected exact variant and retain its actual grade/completeness in the receipt; recap says only the grade learned by the player. Processing and freshness rules remain harvest-owned. An ask never rerolls a carcass, changes kill drops or manufactures a missing part.

Reach band is a projection over world feasibility, player capability and player knowledge: **ready** has a known route now; **stretch** has known prerequisites such as skill, tool, money or travel still to earn; **unknown** has a world route the player has not discovered. Prefer unknown if the only feasible route is undiscovered; show known shortfalls separately. A purchased item can qualify even when its crafting route is stretch. Multi-line asks use the hardest remaining line, keeping per-line bands visible. Bands can improve as the player learns; frozen item/grade/reward terms remain unchanged. Unknown never means nonexistent. Most offers can be doable while some invite exploration; exact shares are world adjectives.

Quote integer coin fees from effort and inputs plus explicit stretch/discovery premiums. For otherwise equivalent asks, stretch and unknown pay more than ready. A fixed fee quoted at raising remains held even after the player discovers a shortcut; no repricing or loss of premium on delivery. No fee floor promises profitability against every market fluctuation. Never use secret potency, rarity truth or the exact hidden route as a premium explanation. Expose the agreed premium as compensation for difficulty/discovery.

Later reward bundles can offer a lesson, recipe hint or introduction in addition to coins. Freeze reward IDs/revisions and the player's chosen branch; reserve actual provider capacity or entitlement before accepting. Coins remain backed by a purse; a lesson needs capability and a curriculum, an introduction needs a contact/dialogue consumer, and a hint grants a knowledge claim with provenance rather than a recipe unlock or secret truth. Unsupported reward branches do not enter tables. A bounded saved entitlement may allow a lesson to be claimed later; settlement and reward claim have separate idempotent operations so interrupted lessons cannot duplicate XP. Decline returns reserved resources and grants no reward.
