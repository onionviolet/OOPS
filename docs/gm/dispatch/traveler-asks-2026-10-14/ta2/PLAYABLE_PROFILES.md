### Frontier.A7: graded hide extension, proposed

Goods, original fiction: “A whole unblemished Rat pelt for a careful repair, please.” Optional `grade` selects completeness and quality through the harvest-owner adapter; quantity 1, E2-E3/D1 for supported hunting/handling, D0 if bought from an actual qualified supplier. Stretch when tools/skill are missing; unknown only when an actual qualifying route is undiscovered. Pay actual replacement/handling cost plus funded grade and discovery premiums. Honest clue: “Perhaps a hide worker could advise on keeping a hide sound.” This does not reveal a hidden part or sealed discovery. Route: supported wolf-rat kill → HV1 whole/selective processing → qualifying lot, or actual trade/commission. **Not admitted today:** TA1 excludes non-null grades and HV1 integration is not verified here. Neither ordinary pelt loot nor a route to scraps proves obtainability of this grade. No guessed grade-suffixed item ID.
