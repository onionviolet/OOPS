## AP1.1: separate what a rank means

| Quantity | Proposed authoritative basis | Example consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Competence | Actual executable skill/body/source and tested outcomes | Can perform the supported task, even anonymously |
| Certification or office | Issuer, jurisdiction, eligibility, actual appointment | May accept a guild contract or chair a meeting within its authority |
| Public placement | A published edition under that board's rules | Gets a challenge, interview or funded offer from someone who received it |
| Fame | Audience knowledge and response | A well-known loser can attract more attention than an unknown champion |
| Tournament position | Settled matches, bracket and rules | Advances to the next eligible match; cannot be inferred from reputation |

There is no universal power score. Two incompatible boards can publish different legitimate criteria. A prestigious title does not provide magical faculty, a guild credential does not locate the player, and a defeat need not revoke an unrelated teaching license.

## AP1.4: comparison and being noticed

Proposed logical binding, not a reserved save schema: board ID, world/instance/branch/era, issuer, rule revision, eligibility predicates, member actor refs, contribution/outcome refs, adjudication status, publication cadence/clock, edition refs and audience notification refs. Reuse actors and domain receipts. A cached placement is a validated derived result; it is never a shadow competence or reputation balance.

At an eligible boundary: collect settled admissible receipts, resolve declared judge policy, compute comparison, freeze the edition, then distribute its P14 account through [AQ1](IN_WORLD_MEDIA.md). Deterministic ties can share placement or use a declared earlier-result rule; a random tie needs one saved draw frozen at adjudication. Preview sorting, reload, opening the board and different display names consume no draws. Retractions append an edition with a reason, not erase the old board.

| Notice trigger | Required cause, rather than automatic hero attention | Possible response |
|---|---|---|
| First publication or threshold crossing | Actually published/received edition, issuer eligibility | Funded commission, recruitment, friendly challenge, interview |
| Rival displaced | Rival received edition and shares an objective | Improve a supported method, rematch, dispute credit, congratulate or leave |
| Unexpected result | Judge sees a supported outcome inconsistent with their estimate | Ask for demonstration or assay, investigate, update belief |
| Sustained public service | Distinct qualifying receipts under the board law | Mentor request or institutional role offer |
| Quiet achievement discovered late | New report of an older receipt | Delayed recognition; occurrence and publication times separate |

Rank loss is not automatic humiliation. Responses depend on goals, relationship and resources. Rival improvement needs training/materials/time, not rubber-band scaling to the player's level. Unknown rivals can appear as attributed entries without showing their unseen build. Concealment, pseudonyms, regional boards, retirement and climbing several ladders remain useful routes.
