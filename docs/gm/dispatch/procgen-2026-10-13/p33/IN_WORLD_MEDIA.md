## AQ1.3: one receipt, several accounts, real recipients

Proposed flow: committed domain outcome → P14 author evidence set → frozen Account/Claims → P11 transport/delivery → observer BeliefEpisode → read-only feed row → optional P12 plan/offer. Original incident: player and maker repair a cart, a witness credits both, a merchant omits the maker, a remote reply guesses the date wrong. Correction needs the completion receipt or a reachable witness. Repetition of the same post is not independent corroboration.

Logical channel profile: world scope, medium type, access predicate, sender/issuer, audience policy, latency/clock binding, evidence filters, moderation authority, account/edition refs and delivery refs. Thread reply relationships attach to the existing account lineage. Channel membership or unread cursors can be small optional metadata; no copied claim graph or truth ledger.

Bias inputs include role, source access, audience, sponsor, interests, translation and memory. Deception requires belief/assertion mismatch and intent under P14; uncertainty alone does not identify a liar. High standing, villain affiliation or a famous handle never serves as a truth predicate. Contradictory accounts remain visible with qualified dates/scope. A report can accurately describe loss and still disagree about blame.

Reaction requires receipt/comprehension and means. Examples: a funded recipe commission after an observed useful batch; a rival asks for a rematch after receiving [AP1](RANKINGS.md); a healer requests a controlled assay after a mistaken remedy story. Chemistry remains P2/CR1/CR2; social/economic response remains CR4. An account cannot make an unobserved dose's property public unless someone actually tested or appraised it.
