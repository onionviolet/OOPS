## AS1.1: identity before the premise

| Outsider type | Proposed distinguishing data | No implicit entitlement |
|---|---|---|
| Independent transmigrator | Separate prior origin, entry event, current body/controller and source-version memories | Not the player's parallel self; no copied relationships, possessions or protagonist power |
| Regressor | Earlier attempt/cycle, witnessed memories, actual retained carrier and current scope | Memory does not supply current-body mastery, fuel, property or other people's affection |
| Returnee/cross-world traveler | Actual crossing receipt, permitted transferred objects/faculties and destination coupling | No universal language, calendar or energy compatibility |
| Parallel self/clone/avatar | P16-defined identity relationship and actual body/controller topology | Similar name does not share stock or loyalty |
| Local claiming outsider status | An attributed claim, evidence and observed behavior | May be truthful, mistaken, role-playing or lying; claim is not origin truth |

Generate origin, present means, knowledge and motive separately. Cheats can be strong, cheap, rechargeable, passive, teachable, mundane or absent under local law. No compulsory weakness attached to every powerful outsider. Distinguish an authored possible ability from an actually owned source and supported action.

## AS1.3: generating a lawful, active person

Reuse P4/P7 identities and their required native actor bridge. Proposed added refs: origin/entry receipt, current body/controller, cheat source/definition revision, owned items, P24 memory/forecast refs with source cutoff, personal goal refs, current plan stage, observed plan results, disclosure policy and known public identity. Hidden origin or cheat truth stays with the sealed owner; player reports use secret IDs and route/status only.

Generation steps: choose world-compatible origin/premise → bind permitted body/source → choose a feasible goal from actual means and unmet needs → bind partial known anchors → construct supported actions → save admitted result/revisions/draw receipts. Random choice happens at explicit enrollment, never per conversation/reload. The system must keep a witness for a promised usable cheat/goal, rather than roll a label whose actions all lack adapters.

Original possible goals: earn a workshop place, protect a remembered acquaintance, test whether a source event still applies, sell a useful imported method, build a peaceful home, return to origin, acquire a sponsor or prevent another outsider's risky experiment. Helping one may also help another; generate specific competing resources or incompatible accepted terms before calling it a rivalry.

Plans read actual stock, access, capacity, body, belief and received evidence. An NPC can test a wrong forecast, decline the hero's offer, succeed unaided, change career or reconcile. Training/improvement needs a supported job/source receipt. An observed player action changes the outsider's belief only through actual observation/report or its explicit information faculty. No private-player-build mirroring by default.

## AS1.4: conflicting foreknowledge without a global decay bar

P24 owns memory and per-facet applicability. Pin each actor's edition, cutoff, witnessed-versus-read basis and confidence. One remembers a tournament date; another remembers an adaptation with a different host. A real rescheduling invalidates the date facet after they learn of it, without invalidating an unrelated recipe. A hidden intervention cannot make their public certainty meter flicker before its information arrives.

Offer conversation, controlled tests, shared notebooks, private plans, paid forecasts, bargains, role exchange or deliberate disinformation where supported. A liar may know one useful true fact; a confident ally may be wrong. Claims of regression do not certify a successful reset. Borrowed powers, transferred knowledge and taught competence remain separate owners. AS1 adds no permanent fixed-use cap; exact cheap/strong foresight remains available where its world law supports it.

## AS1.5: concrete first journey and gates

Original AS-V1: two independently funded residents pursue one actual delivery/lesson chain. One remembers an old date, another has a supported narrow appraisal of a product. Meet either, trade known evidence, intervene in a real prerequisite, then receive a later account of the resulting plan change. The hero can cooperate with both, compete over actual workshop time, act alone, negotiate an alternate route or ignore them. Do not require literal time travel for this coexistence proof.

All gates OPEN: separate identities/custody/cheats; one NPC makes a meaningful supported action without hero input; relevant intervention changes only its dependent forecast; unseen action leaks no certainty; public claimed origin distinct from retained true origin; canonical exclusive role collision rejected or explicitly marked AU. Save/reload/literal-file Import preserve enrollment, memories, plans, owned means and next draws without respawning. Failed writes publish no actor/source. Desktop evidence shows known goals and exchange choices beside routine work; sealed origin/cheat IDs remain opaque. Actual loops, playable character switching and full autonomous NPC economy remain separately available proposals.
