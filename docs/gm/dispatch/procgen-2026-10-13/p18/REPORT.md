  audited integrated many-year story metrics runner. Existing profiles are
  workload probes. P3/P4 reports describe separate branch capabilities; their
  reported checks are not P18 execution or main integration proof.
- **F2:** Bounded feed/biography/deck receipt windows lose detail needed by
  coverage and explanation. Capture committed telemetry before pruning and pin
  required causal authority; do not reconstruct it from prose. P3's top-three
  weights cannot establish complete selection probabilities.
- **F3:** Fixed seeds preserve conditional draws, not an inevitable event
  future. Legacy decision pauses, draft offline behavior, once metadata, P4 actor
  separation and source-profile contradictions remain explicit in MORE C1-C12.
