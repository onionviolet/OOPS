# AO1: an authoring budget for N hours

2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** Extends [root AO1](../../../../PROCEDURAL_STORY_AND_CONTENT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md#ao-content-math-and-playtest-data). Uses [P18 M0-M2/M7](../p18/METRICS.md) for clock denominators, semantic novelty, repetition and reachability. This is planning arithmetic, not another metric/harness, a measured fun score, promised hours or a selected build. Recombination creates situations only when actual gameplay supports the combinations.

## AO1.1: units and model

Here **N means real session hours with game running**, including comfortable idle-running time, excluding closed/frozen time. Event cadence λ is meaningful player-seen event episodes per such hour. Measure it per speed/activity segment; do not count every background event or render. P18 separately reports played game-hours. An authored card has prerequisites/roles/choices/consequences; an expression template is original text over that semantic situation; a person has qualified stable identity and supported means. Three sentences about one receipt are not three stories.

Planning variables: C authored cards; e expected reachable/eligible share within this selected route/style; v materially different context variants per eligible card; r retained share after removing cosmetic or semantically equivalent combinations. Estimate **K ≈ C × e × v × r** accessible semantic signatures. This coarse approximation needs a reachability witness and P12 semantic grouping before acceptance. Time-varying eligibility is not actually a constant, so record the route and eligible exposure. For E = Nλ independent equally likely exposures to K signatures:

`Expected distinct U = K * (1 - (1 - 1/K)^E)`

For unequal fixed probabilities, `U = sum_i (1 - (1 - p_i)^E)`. Actual cooldowns, recurring threads, taste/pity and changing causes violate independent stationary assumptions; use P18 traces for final estimates. The equal-probability figure is an optimistic diagnostic. Around **K = 2.2E** yields about 80% first-seen signatures, a proposed planning target only. Return visits, familiar collaboration and quiet may be desired; 80% novelty is not a world law or mandatory pace. Strongly weighted cards can produce much less novelty than the uniform estimate.

Do not multiply C by every name, trait, place and response. Use only feasible coherent combinations with changed goal, constraints, real consequence or payoff. A role substitution often preserves the same semantic signature. The number of expressions influences phrasing repetition, not semantic capacity. Ongoing threads can produce meaningful callbacks without producing a new archetype every hour; report them separately.

## AO1.2: Village, worked original proposal

Assume a 20-hour session-equivalent horizon, λ=4 meaningful episodes/hour, **E=80**. Plan **48 authored cards**, **24 reusable expression templates** with two reviewed wording variants each, **18 original named people**, **12 supported role/need profiles**, and **8 authored place anchors**. These are proposed pack sizes, not counts of delivered content. E2's [Village deck](../../../../emergent/E2_STORYLETS.md) and P3/P4 branch work are reuse candidates only after mapping actual semantics, eligibility and integration. Three P4 people are not eighteen new full simulation actors.

| Budget group | Cards | Concrete use, original examples |
|---|---|---|
| Making, repair and discovery | 12 | A funded repair, inconclusive assay, apparatus fault, taught method; consume P2/P6/CR3 receipts |
| Social contacts and obligations | 12 | Thanks, joint work, invitation, changed promise; consume P19 rather than generating free affection |
| Place, supply and occasions | 12 | Staffing departure, completed fit-out, delivery delay, voluntary meal celebration; P20/P8/P11 causes |
| Threads, investigations and late returns | 12 | Evidence correction, rival collaboration, recovered item, declined job renewed by a new need; P12 closure |

Assume e=.75, v=6 and r=.85: K≈48×.75×6×.85=**183.6**, use K=184 in the illustrative occupancy equation. **U≈65.02**, about **81.27%** of 80 episodes. Six variants means a witnessed set such as three materially distinct supported constraints ×two payoff classes, not merely six names or speculative response branches. Some cards will have fewer; richer cards can compensate only through real reachable paths. The 18 people are intended for recurring attachment and available roles, not an 18-fold novelty multiplier. Templates can serve related cards but need appropriate outcomes/voice and retained phrasing variants.

If access/redundancy halves K to 92, U≈53.62 (**67.03%**). If cadence doubles with K still 184, 160 episodes yield U≈107.06 (**66.91%**). Extra event spam therefore makes a fixed pack feel repetitive sooner. Offer player-selected quiet, active or leisurely local profiles without permanently disabling content. To grow capacity, add new supported consequence families, world-local practices or returning payoffs; writing synonyms alone does not repair this gap.

## AO1.3: Harry Potter, worked canon-world proposal

Choose one source-qualified school-era instance with its source/era and AU policy pinned by P13. Existing original Village mechanics are not silently canon. J. K. Rowling's *Philosopher's Stone* ch8 supports the teaching/preparation-order example; *Chamber of Secrets* ch6 supports maturation/handling distinctions; *Half-Blood Prince* ch9 supports a revised method changing a particular brew. These are inherited **SHOWN/STATED source classifications** in [P6 K1/K3/K4](../p6/CANON_LOCATORS.md), not a fresh primary read. Their chronology spans different books; a first-year pack cannot insert the later classroom event as contemporary. A later-era/AU pack can use a qualified adaptation, or an original lesson can use the abstract method constraint without asserting the canon event occurred. Specific amounts/timers and six-way variations below are INFERRED original design, not source facts. No raw prose or private notes quoted.

For **40 session hours**, λ=3, E=120. Illustrative production budget: **104 cards**, **52 expression templates**, two reviewed expression variants each, **30 original era-compatible supporting people**, **8 qualified canon cast references** where the chosen timeline actually allows them, **12 role/need profiles** and **10 authored place anchors**. Reserved canon identities are referenced, not generated replacements/relatives. Supporting people counts are authoring assumptions, not a demographic assertion. Reuse source-authenticated profiles instead of inventing all these actors at enrollment.

| Domain | Cards | Attribution/admission boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Lessons, practice, crafting and research | 32 | Selected taught means and supported operations; exact source formulas keep exact roles |
| Friendship, clubs, help and obligations | 24 | Original incidental episodes; era-qualified roles, actual contacts and funds |
| Place use, school occasions and supply | 24 | Authored topology/institution rules; calendars/source windows fixed before variation |
| Investigation, recurring ambition and aftermath | 24 | Canon spine reserved; original seams/AU overlays explicit, clues fixed before discovery |

Use e=.50 because era, curriculum, cast and player means plausibly exclude more of the total authored pack on one route; v=6 and r=.85. **K≈265.2**, round upward to266 for a clearly approximate worked illustration: **U≈96.73, 80.61%** of120 exposures. If K halves to 133, novelty falls to **66.03%**. The apparent precision comes from arithmetic, not confidence in the assumptions. Source-compatible meaningful variants require more work than swapping spell names.

Thirty people and fifty-two templates do not mathematically guarantee K. Confirm enough present/willing/qualified roles through P19 and source windows through AN1. Where restricted canon cannot support six variants, add original seam cards or extend the chosen era with a declared route; never fabricate canon outcomes to meet the spreadsheet. Harry Potter is a worked priority-world example, not a new exclusive content choice. D&D, book, anime and original packs use their own e/v/r and means, retaining reasonable AU/exceptional options.

## AO1.4: use the estimate to commission content

For Village assumptions and K≈2.2E, approximate minimum C=`ceil(2.2*N*4/(.75*6*.85))`: **24 cards for 10 h, 47 for 20 h, 116 for 50 h**. Pack rounding may select 48 rather than 47. Scaling linearly is a first production bound; callbacks and evolving mechanics can improve longevity, while uneven eligibility/weights can worsen it. No “infinite fresh hours” claim follows from finite templates. The same legal family can recur indefinitely without every occurrence being novel.

Commission in five reviewable units: **AO1.A1** inventory existing semantic families and qualified source claims; **AO1.A2** author the missing causal cards/role requirements; **AO1.A3** give each a legal acquisition/response/closure witness; **AO1.A4** express with original templates, locators and known-only projection; **AO1.A5** calibrate against P18 and opt-in AO2 observations. No invented hours-per-card estimate: record actual author/review time and QA failure rate before promising production dates.

Future gate OPEN: versioned pack lists cards/templates/person profiles/anchors separately; P18 reports reachable witness coverage, actual player exposure, novelty/family/phrasing/cast repeat, callback and closure rates across seeds and selected styles. Examine early/middle/late horizon segments, missing-role and opt-out paths, workload/save growth and source/era contradictions. Human delight/attachment may favor recurrence; do not force the proposed 80% target after director play rejects it. [CR3 coverage](../cr3/DIRECTOR_TOOLS.md) counts material/consumer coverage, not story hours or semantic novelty; cross-link its usable crafting receipts without conflating the two.
