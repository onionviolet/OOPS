# Original-world seeds and multiverse generation

Status: PROPOSAL N15, 2026-10-14. No world, portal, seed or AU created. Applies to original fictional worlds; canon worlds keep their source snapshot and authored overlays. Use [CONTEXT](CONTEXT.md), [P9](../P9.md), [P10](../P10.md), [P5 D6](../p5/D6_SECRET_ROLL.md), [D7](../p5/D7_HOUSEHOLDS.md), [world instances](../../../../worlds/ORIGINS_AND_WORLD_TIMELINES.md) and [REPORT gates](REPORT.md#future-gates).

**Value:** related original worlds vary in law, ecology, culture, institutions, resources and opportunities while remaining internally coherent and revisitable. A world seed defines a persistent place rather than a reroll button disguised as travel.

**Cost:** high for reusable profile composition, very high for large-scale multiverse simulation. Dependencies: P1 registered world-generation scope, world-instance/source/revision validation, authored starts/maps and transitions, P7 identity reservations, P8 resources, P6 creation law and P10 original-world profiles. D6 owns sealed secret generation and discovery; D7 owns generated households/starts. This proposal does not generate geometry, another secret candidate table or unlimited catalogues.

Shown/SOURCE: `src/world_instances.js:83-89,97-110` validates endpoint, instance, continuity, era and clock. `src/world_knowledge.js:3-13` keeps world identity unknown until learned. Inferred extension: these can anchor generated original profile choices but do not establish a generative multiverse. P1's world-scoped streams are stated/branch evidence, not current main integration.

Final sibling join: [P10 ORIGINAL_WORLD_FACTORY](../p10/ORIGINAL_WORLD_FACTORY.md) owns original families, profile combinations and law composition. N15 extends admission receipts, persisted context/revisit and boundary validation for those profiles. The illustrative adjectives below are inputs to that owner, not a competing world-family catalogue. [P9 PROFILE_RULES](../p9/PROFILE_RULES.md) remains the canon qualification boundary.

## W1: generate definitions before local content

Choose an original compatible profile family and qualitative adjectives, such as lush and dispersed, mineral-rich and tightly governed, or resource-sparse with strong recycling. Store the original world seed reference privately, family/version, selected law modules, parameter units, climate/calendar mappings, culture/institution IDs, supported resource families, authored start/site bindings, causal background refs and declared exceptions. Validate a coherent profile before admitting a visit.

Law/energy/resource dimensions must agree: a world cannot use an ore recipe whose resolver is absent or claim a training institution with no accessible teacher/means. Common/rare/inexhaustible/costly qualifiers are local, not interchangeable numerical units or universal once limits. Do not take a sourced protagonist's powers as a population distribution. Regional variety can come from authored regions with profile overlays; it doesn't require generated terrain.

## W2: derivation and ownership

Root registers one world-admission draw namespace. Reserve a world instance ordinal/ID on a real admission cause and derive stable subkeys for law, environment and admitted populations. Store resolved profiles plus receipts, rather than relying on a seed and today's generator version to rebuild every visit. Schema migrations retain or explicitly translate old profiles; do not regenerate objects because a new module became available. Local domains own their later changing stock, ecology, events and relationships. The world seed does not author past player deeds.

Related worlds have explicit parent/continuity/overlay refs with declared differences. Counterparts remain distinct people and possessions in distinct instances. Travel cargo, imported knowledge, powers, biological compatibility and translation each use their actual boundary owners. Similar display names or equal seeds do not merge worlds or grant secret familiarity.

An original-world seed does not require another self or former body. Follow the latest [intro/start owner](../../../../INTRO_FLOW_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md) and USER_VISION's 2026-10-14 lean: former selves can be ordinary NPCs with little special interaction, nonexistence remains reachable, and a story world's fresh/continued branch follows its declared premise. Counterpart rules above apply only when the selected world actually includes counterparts; no special bond, rivalry or merged identity is generated by default.

## W3: first slice

Within one original realm, select between a few validated local profile combinations that share an authored arrival map, supported resource/work stations and a safe ordinary livelihood. One environment/law difference changes a P8 yield/use or P6 process choice through actual supported handlers. Revisit the same endpoint and find the same law/profile and earned state. A later explicit parallel instance can differ without rewriting the first one. First milestone remains one realm and one prestige; this slice tests a local profile seam, not opening a many-world campaign.

## W4: future gates

Reject incompatible law/calendar/start/resource combinations before draws publish; prove at least one changed action and an ordinary progression route for every admitted profile; identical saved instance survives revisit, reload and import after a generator update. Body/world boundary effects follow explicit compatibility; no duplicate cargo or person identity. Known world descriptions use learned identity only. Seeds, sealed IDs-to-truth mappings and future-world catalogue counts never render. D6 sealed review remains its separate gate. Legacy starts keep behavior until explicit enrollment/migration. Layouts, large unseen simulations and multiverse balance remain unselected future gates.

## W5: further reachable angles

World kinship and partial translation, historical migration, portals with local admission laws, resource chains across compatible realms, extinct techniques, changed magical constants, technologies that work only at a fitting scale, self-contained pocket worlds and research-created worlds. Cheap or freely accessible worlds can coexist with dangerous/rare ones. Discovery and generation are separate: a world may exist internally long before the hero can name or reach it.
