# Ruins and natural sites by world

2026-10-16. **PROPOSALS ONLY. No implementation authorized.** Requested: types
of ruins/natural sites by world, discovery, exploration, useful finds/restoration
and subtle interactions. Supporting palette for the existing
[ruin/world owner](DUNGEON_CONTENT.md#ruins-and-world-first-consideration-2026-10-16),
not a second specification. NPC-heavy systems remain deprioritized. Authored
navigation with eligible generated content remains the scope; generated layouts
stay deferred. No active implementation queue changes.

## P1–P5: highest-value distinctions

1. **P1 World law changes the action, not just the noun.** Change what can be
   observed, extracted, operated or learned, and which preparations help.
2. **P2 Discovery is a chain.** A lead, located place, entrance, surveyed route
   and usable understanding are different achievements. Finding a ruin does
   not identify its contents or solve its apparatus.
3. **P3 Function is a reward.** A site can provide a station, shelter, measuring
   instrument, cultivation conditions or access instead of portable loot.
4. **P4 Restoration has a goal.** Original operation, stabilization, preservation,
   repurposing and habitat recovery are different projects. There is no universal
   pristine state that is always best.
5. **P5 Knowledge and aftermath persist separately.** Learning does not repair
   a machine; repairing does not explain its history. Removing an object does
   not erase the observation that it was there. Returns read saved changes.

Families may overlap. A technological greenhouse can use ecology and power
constraints without enabling every WT4/WT5 feature. Start with one distinctive
operational rule and ordinary supporting affordances. Include intact, peaceful
and ordinary sites so all worlds do not become the same fallen civilization.

**Named-world application:** for Harry Potter, Mother of Learning, LOTM, Bleach
or another source world, start with a source-qualified place, era and demonstrated
law, then check permitted fill. A greenhouse, workroom, natural patch or damaged
object is a candidate kind, not a certified location or mechanic. Never invent
a canon landmark or unique relic to complete a template.
[Resource world variations](../p8/WORLD_VARIATIONS.md) retain their qualified
substance, extraction and crossing limits. A dream label alone does not qualify
a Shadow Slave site or its mechanics.

## DT: discovery and exploration sequence

| Step | Possible route | Meaningful distinction |
|---|---|---|
| DT1 Lead | Notice worked stone, follow material traces, study records, survey during travel or use supported senses | Save source, observation and date; suggestion is not confirmed site |
| DT2 Locate/approach | Follow terrain, compare records, find entrance or observe a revealing condition | Reachable location/access; no automatic room or treasure list |
| DT3 Reconnoiter | Inspect exposed structure/apparatus, test surfaces or sample accessible material | Learn conditions and likely preparations; no hidden-truth checkmarks |
| DT4 Choose/work | Prepare tools, enter by supported means, explore authored routes, gather or test | Pay actual time/resources and retain withdrawal; more than repeated search rolls |
| DT5 Interpret/use | Compare evidence, test hypothesis, operate/repair equipment or study method | Tentative interpretation, demonstrated rule and executable method differ |
| DT6 Extract/leave | Carry owned finds, record observations, finish bounded work or withdraw | Reconcile stock, installed parts, cargo and changes |
| DT7 Return | Resume surveyed route or inspect known transition | Recall known changes; new changes require observation |

Ordinary work can supply leads: harvesting exposes a fitting, quarry work reveals
old tool marks, surveying notices water flow. These are proposed integrations,
not implemented events. Minor leads can wait in the notebook; important choices
follow accepted pause/automation direction. Not every find needs an interruption.

Evidence, records and powers can reveal complementary aspects. Avoid a universal
Perception gate or promising every ability solves every mystery. A failed reading
can remain uncertain. New light, tools, conditions or techniques can justify new
evidence. Identical inspection should not be a free reroll until truth appears.
Deterministic evidence remains fixed where supported actions reveal it. Not every
stage needs chance: attempt variance and site truth remain separate, and reopening
or previewing consumes no new generation draw.

## UT: useful outcomes beyond treasure

| Category | Value | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| UT1 Material | Feedstock, unusual compatible material, sample or viable growth stock | Real stock changes; unknown properties remain unknown |
| UT2 Object | Tool, container, instrument, component or artifact | Appearance, identity, condition, operation and power source differ |
| UT3 Knowledge | Recipe record, process clue, diagram, observation or failed experiment | Possession, reading, comprehension, demonstrated method and mastery differ |
| UT4 Function | Shelter, workstation, growing bed, instrument or supported storage | Repair/access does not grant ownership, portability or unlimited output |
| UT5 Access | Entrance, restored authored connection or known route | Opening an existing edge is not generated adjacency |
| UT6 Understanding | Tested environmental relation, dated seasonal record or provenance | Guides action without a universal stat bonus |

Use transport distinctions only where supported: bulk, fragile cargo, sample
preservation or supplied charged objects. Apparatus may be more useful installed
than carried; remember its location and return route. No universal spoilage or
upkeep is required. Ordinary supplies and useful observations can reward a visit,
but history prose alone does not establish consequential discovery.

## ST: subtle interactions, where the selected site supports them

| Code | Interaction | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| ST1 Restore versus salvage | An installed part can be loot or support local function | Show known dependencies; do not duplicate the component |
| ST2 Old function versus present habitat | Draining a room improves access but changes a supported growing condition | Restoring the old state is a choice; full ecology unnecessary |
| ST3 Repair versus preservation | Clearing sediment/replacing surfaces can alter evidence | Allow recording accessible observations; destruction needs an explicit reader |
| ST4 Activate versus stabilize | Machinery/magic can change conditions | Observable warnings and assessment routes; no arbitrary lethal surprise in first slice |
| ST5 Site versus sample | Temperature, moisture or held charge may depend on surroundings | Separate intrinsic properties, condition and external support; extraction need not retain a local field |
| ST6 Entry versus escape | Temporary access can complicate return | Authored routes, readable uncertainty and real retreat; no hidden automatic survey trap |
| ST7 Opening versus containment | Work may alter supported atmosphere, barrier or product condition | Declared effects and recovery; flavor text does not establish disease simulation |
| ST8 Observation versus account | Records may be dated, wrong or describe another layer | Preserve provenance; translation does not prove an explanation |
| ST9 Repair versus ownership | A restored station need not belong to the player | Retain access/ownership; unknown is not none, but legal/heir simulation is not mandatory |
| ST10 Ordinary versus supernatural approach | Powers can bypass an obstacle or reveal another aspect | Honor body, senses, control and fuel; no universal puzzle-preserving power negation |
| ST11 Resource/route alternatives | A service changes the value of other supported routes/sources | Let alternatives coexist rather than force identical rewards |
| ST12 Time and renewal | Growth or periodic access may change while historical stock stays removed | World time and explicit causes; reload starts no new epoch |
| ST13 Cross-world use | Knowledge may travel while apparatus lacks compatible support | Preserve acquisitions; apply directional compatibility, not automatic conversion/deletion |
| ST14 Sharing knowledge | Maps convey leads without proving every interpretation | Future information routes; NPC reputation unnecessary for first site |

Do not add physics, disease, forensics and property law as cumulative prerequisites.
Unmodeled consequences cannot silently become penalties. Staged work may permit
stabilization, a small test, then a full-restoration decision. Full undo is not
assumed. Failed actions need readable aftermath and continuation/withdrawal, not
automatic loss of discoveries or a universal free reset.

## SC: contrasting small candidates

The [workshop and natural-site journeys](WORKSHOP_AND_NATURAL_SITE_JOURNEYS_2026-10-16.md)
expand SC1/SC2/SC4 into discovery, preparation, crafting, partial restoration,
return outcomes, eight world-family variants and twenty cross-system interactions.
They remain design alternatives, not selected implementation.

| Code | Original candidate | Choice | Useful result and lasting trace |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC1 Worksite | Abandoned water-driven workshop in eligible authored/original place | Recover loose fittings or prepare supported repair | Material or station; exact removed/installed parts and function persist |
| SC2 Natural site | Sheltered mineral spring with eligible deposit and ordinary growth | Observe/sample; gather or tend through supported means | Usable batch or known source with explicit renewal; no universal healing spring |
| SC3 Technological | Observatory with serviceable instrument and failed power feed | Salvage compatible part or restore power for bounded measurement | Component or useful reading; consumption, apparatus and knowledge persist |
| SC4 Ecological | Nursery with damaged shade/water control | Sample current growth or restore one supported condition | Material or growing bed; later production follows selected law |

Choose one only after identifying a real output/function consumer. Alternatives,
not four dispatched slices: SC1 continues the workshop candidate; SC2 tests
natural discovery with fewer historical assumptions; SC3 tests apparatus and
information; SC4 adds one environmental transition. Elaborateness is not proof
of a better first experience.
