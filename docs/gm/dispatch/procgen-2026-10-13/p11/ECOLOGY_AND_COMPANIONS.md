# Creatures, ecology and companions

Status: PROPOSALS, 2026-10-14. Original examples, no new canon claims. [CONTEXT](CONTEXT.md) supplies eligibility/weights/quality/exposure; [REPORT gates](REPORT.md#future-gates) apply.

## N3: creatures, ecology and monster variants

**Value:** tracking, avoidance, husbandry, gathering and combat reflect habitat and real behavior. Variants can change a useful preparation, not just inflate HP.

**Cost:** medium for one habitat/behavior package; high for populations and ecological cascades. Dependencies: [P8](../P8.md) owns habitats, harvesting, resources and logistics; [P5 X1](../p5/EXTRA_IDEAS.md) owns coherent authored enemy loadouts; P1 encounter/loot streams; existing battle/means owners. This document adds creature identity and ecological transition joins, not a second harvesting or combat formula.

Shown/SOURCE: `src/models/location.js:335-381` selects authored encounter groups and varies stats. Inferred gap: that does not establish migration, carrying capacity, learned behavior or causal species mutation. A seasonal abundance curve is a declared play model, not proven biology or canon demographics.

Final sibling join: [P8 ECOLOGY](../p8/ECOLOGY.md) owns renewal, pressure, succession and migration. N3 only binds those site/cohort refs to creature identity and supported behavior/encounter packages. [P8 CREATURE_RESOURCES](../p8/CREATURE_RESOURCES.md) owns extraction/corpse/living-source distinctions; N3 does not add another drop/harvest owner.

**Contract:** keep species archetype, individual ID when persistent, habitat role, body/life stage, temperament, learned behavior, disease/injury, faculties, available fuel and drops distinct. Context selects compatible packages. Separate population budget from encounter presentation: killing one persistent animal cannot create a new clone on reentry; a nonpersistent crowd encounter needs a declared aggregate depletion policy. Weather shifts activity/distribution, not a saved species' defining powers. If no population model is selected, expose only a saved local encounter schedule and do not imply a simulated census.

**First slice:** one original burrowing pest in one authored field has two compatible behavior packages, daylight sheltering and dusk foraging, with visible tracks. A real shelter disturbance changes the next activity state through a receipt. Hero can wait, use a supported lure, protect a crop or engage using existing combat. Tie a new encounter to saved habitat state; existing monster/loadout work supplies any functional attacks/counters.

**Gates:** prove one different successful preparation and a noncombat alternative; stable body/variant/loot under reload/import; no impossible faculty combination or unlimited drops from a persistent corpse. A disturbance affects future activity, not current frozen combat rolls. Verify migration/depletion only if that policy is actually implemented. No automatic hero-level scaling, mandatory slaughter or hunger death introduced through ecology.

**Further forms:** pack social roles, symbionts, territory, seasonal camouflage, elemental adaptation, cultivated magical creatures, anomalous habitats and deliberate engineered variants. Exceptional ecosystems can be inexhaustible or self-renewing if their world law supports it. P8 retains extraction/renewal balance, P9/P10 source profiles retain canon eligibility.

## N4: companion and pet personalities

**Value:** familiar creatures remember the hero, prefer fitting activities and contribute through actual means. Different companions remain useful without mandatory optimization.

**Cost:** medium for a small personality/relationship projection; high for party AI, care, breeding or combat. Dependencies: P4 stable identity and known traits, P3 actual adoption/meeting cause, context body/species/relationship, existing party/actor owners. [P6](../P6.md) retains creature creation/breeding; [P5 X6](../p5/EXTRA_IDEAS.md) retains heredity. A personality tag isn't an actor-controller implementation.

**Contract:** stable temperament baseline, learned preferences, observed traits, attachment by audience, memories, communication means and welfare/availability are separate fields. A civilian pet's saved personality does not grant fighter skills. Authored companions retain sourced characterization; generated backgrounds fill permitted original roles. Do not infer “cowardly” from a single sensible retreat. Personality choices should offer different context fits, with rare/unusual or initially distrustful companions still reachable.

**First slice:** P3's existing stray-dog flag is stated branch evidence, not already a full animal identity. A later authorized adapter promotes that exact adoption cause into one saved original dog record. Choose among a few compatible original temperament packages at first real adoption; meeting/training reveals them. A patient dog can aid a supported watch action, an inquisitive dog a supported search. Play remains useful without a pet and neglect does not secretly kill one while the browser is closed.

**Gates:** promotion consumes no second adoption reward and creates no duplicate animal; visible preferences match actual outcomes, only known traits render, training changes learned behavior through receipts without rerolling baseline. Cancelled work returns reserved gear/food per policy. Save/reload/import bond, memories, presence and next allowed action. Combat, care, departure, death and offline behavior need explicit world-specific gates before claims.

**Further options:** pets that disagree, nonverbal companions, constructed familiars, mounts, shared custody, task styles, sensory teamwork, jealousy or trust repair where supported. Individuality can be conveyed with one remembered response before implementing a large AI model.
