# Libraries, temples, mines, caves and maps

2026-10-16. **SITE MECHANICS ARE PROPOSALS; NO IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORIZED.**
Requested by Weibao: additional site types, differences by world, useful discoveries,
restoration, bought maps and similar interactions. This expands the
[site owner](DUNGEON_CONTENT.md) and [world palette](WORLD_SITE_PALETTE_2026-10-16.md).
The requested lasting decision principles live in
[site/exploration rules](../../../../SITE_AND_EXPLORATION_RULES.md), not in a new
universal exploration engine. All illustrative worlds, places, rewards and
mechanisms below are original proposals, not existing sealed truths or new canon
claims. NPC-heavy systems and generated layouts remain deferred.

## L: ancient library or archive

**L1 Purpose:** preserve, find and interpret knowledge. An archive may instead
hold administrative records, observations or technical work; age does not imply
accurate, complete, useful or supernatural content. Its former collection policy
and survival conditions explain what is present.

**L2 Discovery/preparation:** a cited older work, a surviving catalog fragment,
maker/document provenance, observed shelves or an eligible map can provide leads.
Locating stacks, recognizing a language and understanding a subject differ. First
visit can support a useful visible record or catalog observation without requiring
perfect literacy. Tools or supported powers may help access, light, preservation,
translation or testing, but none universally certifies the writer's truth.

**L3 Useful outcomes:** an observed method, dated survey, actual diagram, recipe
record, historical evidence or location lead. A text can identify an experiment
worth trying rather than grant the answer. Possession, reading progress, learning
and demonstrated application remain distinct. Exact work/edition/copy identity,
claims, marginalia, custody and study effects belong to
[information/library N6](INFORMATION_AND_LIBRARIES.md#n6-generated-books-knowledge-items-and-libraries)
and its creative-work owner, not another book generator.

**L4 Restoration:** stabilize a selected storage condition, recover a damaged
catalog, make a reading area usable, or repair supported retrieval equipment.
A restored index helps retrieve records it actually indexes; it does not expose
all hidden subjects. Reordering known material can be useful without creating new
truth. Repairing a book cannot recreate missing prose unless a selected copying,
reconstruction or supernatural operation supports it. A conjectural reconstruction
must remain labeled as such.

**L5 Subtleties:** record before intrusive preservation where supported; moving a
record may separate it from context; two copies can descend from one mistaken
edition. A copied shelf mark need not identify a surviving copy today. A restricted
catalog title or inaccessible file count must not leak a sealed subject. Copying
a source conveys its content/claims, not automatic understanding or repeated XP.
An archive can be worth revisiting after learning a language or research method;
do not reroll its contents to suit the player's new question.

## T: temple, shrine or ceremonial site

**T1 Purpose:** gathering, remembrance, ritual, contemplation or an actual
world-supported religious function. Physical structure, historical purpose,
present practice and supernatural effect are separate. A temple need not contain
a living deity, curse, active blessing or combat encounter.

**T2 Discovery/preparation:** public routes, inscriptions, visible construction,
records, maps or qualified perception can reveal different aspects. An inscription
records a claim or instruction. Observing a symbol does not grant affiliation or
authority, and recognizing an altar does not prove it still operates.

**T3 Useful outcomes:** a readable record, art/craft evidence, a supported practice
method, shelter, a local apparatus or a specific source-qualified ritual opportunity.
Its use needs actual capability, materials and world law. An expensive offering
or restoration does not universally purchase favor or power. Ordinary repair,
quiet study and observation can be valuable without supernatural rewards.

**T4 Restoration:** stabilize the building, preserve a surviving image or inscription,
repair a declared apparatus, or repurpose a supported space. Restoring appearance,
structural use, issuing authority and ritual operation are separate projects.
Reactivating one selected function is not turning on a generic “temple restored”
buff. Preserve existing access/custody without requiring clergy, faction politics
or worship simulation for the first site.

**T5 Subtleties:** a later addition may be as historically meaningful as the oldest
layer; scraping it away is not automatically correct restoration. A copied emblem
is not an issued credential. Physical damage and loss of a world-specific function
need not match. A rite may be enacted but ineffective, or effective without the
player knowing its full cause, only under that selected law. Such possibilities
are not permission to invent canon religion or expose sealed truths.

## M: mine or quarry

**M1 Purpose:** obtain actual material through an existing worked site. Keep
geological source, excavation access, exposed/ready material, extracted batch,
processing apparatus and ownership distinct. A mine and a natural cavern can
share a connected authored site; do not duplicate the same deposit or identity
because two labels refer to it.

**M2 Discovery/preparation:** exposed strata, worked spoil, tool marks, a survey,
an invoice or a map can provide leads. A map marking a deposit does not establish
remaining reserves, quality, safety, access rights or economic value today.
Known rock/material and tool compatibility can guide preparation where modeled.

**M3 Useful outcomes:** actual ore/stone/salt or other eligible material, samples,
recoverable equipment, process evidence or a useful authored passage. Extraction,
ore preparation, refining and product use are different operations. A promising
sample does not automatically certify the entire deposit; spoil and residue go
somewhere declared. A small useful batch may be enough for a first journey.

**M4 Restoration:** restore a work area, supported access, a local haul mechanism,
lighting or a selected drainage/airflow function. Each must affect an actual reader.
No structural physics, gas chemistry, pumping network or autonomous workforce is
required unless specifically selected. Repairing access can expose an existing
stock; it does not replenish it. Magical renewal is possible only through a real
world/site law, not a universal ore timer.

**M5 Subtleties:** some exact material cannot substitute for another just because
both are labeled ore. Material quality and extraction ease differ. Excavation may
change only supported authored access, not generate arbitrary corridors. Deposits
are not rescaled to player level. Carrying and processing constraints can matter
without requiring hauling simulations. Abandonment does not prove the mine is
free to own; honor existing access rules without making legal simulation mandatory.

## C: natural cave or cavern

**C1 Purpose:** a natural place need not have an intended function. Its formation
and present conditions can explain passages, deposits, water and eligible growth
without a lost civilization or secret shrine. Later use can leave separate layers.

**C2 Discovery/preparation:** visible entrances, accessible water/material traces,
terrain survey, dated records or a map lead to a reachable place. An entrance
location, visible chamber and surveyed passages differ. Light, body dimensions,
locomotion, carried equipment and relevant powers matter only through supported
means; one universal humanoid kit is inappropriate.

**C3 Useful outcomes:** ordinary stone/mineral samples, eligible growth, recorded
conditions, shelter, a supported path or a useful local environment. Not every
chamber needs a chest or hostile creature. An accurate observation can guide
later making or exploration without unlocking an arbitrary combat bonus.

**C4 Restoration:** usually improve access, stabilize an existing used area,
remove a supported recent obstruction or restore an earlier installation rather
than return nature to an invented pristine state. A cave need not be restored
at all. Preserve it or gather a small quantity if that is the useful choice.

**C5 Subtleties:** an opening that permits sight may not permit passage; passage
may not permit moving bulky equipment. Temporary access needs readable conditions
and a retreat policy. Do not add simulated flooding, oxygen, collapse, contamination
or offscreen deaths merely from descriptive cave atmosphere. Actual selected
hazards follow known evidence, uncertainty and the world's loss policy. Removing
an ordinary sample is not assumed to collapse the cave or regenerate it.

## MP: bought maps and other routes to knowledge

**MP1 Yes, maps can be bought.** Also found, borrowed, copied, traded, inherited,
made through survey or received through another supported route. A simple authored
seller with a fixed offer can support a future map purchase without procedural
merchant personalities, reputation or a dynamic economy. This is a proposed
consumer, not new runtime authorization.

| Map/information kind | What it may provide | What it does not automatically provide |
|---|---|---|
| MP2 Route map | Useful directions, known crossings, landmarks and dated travel observations | Current permission, guaranteed safety or every side passage |
| MP3 Site/entrance chart | Approximate or exact position and observed access | Interior survey, ownership or a usable key |
| MP4 Survey plan | Actually surveyed nodes, layers and observed fixtures | Hidden spaces or complete present condition |
| MP5 Resource chart | Reported source, sampled material and observation date | Remaining stock, full assay, exclusivity or a hidden potency certificate |
| MP6 Specialist chart | A supported energy, structural, symbolic or instrument reading | Universal interpretation or automatic operation of the depicted system |
| MP7 Notes, rubbing or catalog | A trace, index entry or location reference with provenance | A complete map, independent corroboration or proof of the account's truth |

Preserve work/edition/copy identity or the digital equivalent, authorship/source,
survey date, world/instance/era, coordinate frame, coverage, claims, corrections
and provenance where consequential. Do not create a second knowledge registry:
[information/library N6](INFORMATION_AND_LIBRARIES.md#n6-generated-books-knowledge-items-and-libraries)
already owns works/editions/copies; geography owns locations/routes; projection
owns what is understood and shown.

Buying transfers the actual copy/entitlement and price exactly once. Reading or
supported automatic interpretation establishes its permitted knowledge. A readily
understood map need not require a ceremonial study click. Directions can let the
player attempt a first journey; requiring a prior visit would defeat their purpose.
Unknown or inaccessible edges remain honest, and a drawn connection cannot create
an otherwise unsupported passage. Journal annotation records observed or believed
corrections, not an omniscient rewrite of the purchased original.

Selling a map must honor commitments it already represents: if a real survey
depicts a site, commit that site/reference and the necessary past observation
before selling the account. Detail may remain lazy only within those constraints.
A counterfeit can depict a nonexistent site as a false claim, but the generator
cannot decide whether it was real after seeing the buyer's inventory or behavior.

An authentic map can be incomplete, out of date or mistaken. Age, famous authorship
and price do not prove reliability. Multiple copies of one survey are one lineage,
not independent corroboration. A price may reflect known coverage, effort, medium
or scarcity of that information; it must not expose unknown contents through a
secret-derived premium. Not every ordinary purchase needs to be a scam: a truthful
known-route map is already useful design.

Losing or selling a copy does not automatically erase learned knowledge. A live
navigation function might still require the device or medium under its stated
law. Conversely owning an unread map does not grant all its facts. Sharing/copying
conveys the permitted claims and attribution, not the recipient's understanding.
No universal copy protection, repeated learning reward or unlimited seller purse
is implied. Confidential/secret maps remain inside their supported discovery route.

## SI: subtle joins and nearby site families

| Code | Interaction | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| SI1 Archive ↔ map ↔ field evidence | A dated survey can guide a visit and observations can correct it | Preserve both original and correction, including whether disagreement is date, location, interpretation or a false claim |
| SI2 Temple ↔ library | A collection can preserve doctrine, instructions and mundane records together | Subject classification is not truth or power certification; no new universal religion engine |
| SI3 Mine ↔ cave | Excavation can intersect an authored natural passage | One shared place/deposit graph, separate known extents, no duplicated resource under different labels |
| SI4 Restoration ↔ evidence | Clearing, sorting, draining or replacing can alter selected evidence | Record accessible observations first where supported; preservation is not a universal mandatory tax |
| SI5 Translation ↔ execution | Reading a ritual/process still may lack apparatus, body, source or safety information | Grant only actual learning effects and preserve uncertainty |
| SI6 Safety report ↔ changed conditions | A formerly surveyed route can change under an actual cause | Show dated evidence and observed updates; no random punishment merely to make maps unreliable |
| SI7 Copies ↔ discovery permanence | Knowledge and physical copies can move independently | Keep custody, personal observations and attributed claims separate through saves/imports |
| SI8 Mixed eras | A restored area can contain later repairs, older records and natural changes | Causal layers coexist; do not force every object to one randomly generated era |
| SI9 Access ↔ authority | A key, map, ritual text or emblem can support one action | None grants universal ownership, office or affiliation |
| SI10 Portable reward ↔ local service | Taking a component/text can compete with keeping a station or collection functional | Apply only supported consequences; do not pretend every book removal collapses a library |

Related families worth retaining: observatories and survey posts (measurement),
bridges/canals/waystations (access and shelter), gardens/nurseries (production),
vaults/storehouses (custody and preservation), and battlegrounds/shipwrecks
(recoverable objects and evidence). Use the same rules and actual consumers.
Do not add all of them as prerequisites or turn every site into a restoration
checklist, combat arena, mystery or guaranteed power upgrade.
