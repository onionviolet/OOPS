# Dreams, occasions, disasters and recovery

Status: PROPOSALS, 2026-10-14. Original examples. Use [CONTEXT](CONTEXT.md) and [REPORT gates](REPORT.md#future-gates). These domains do not authorize mandatory hunger, unseen offline damage or a universal director.

## N7: dreams, visions and omens

**Value:** inner life, atmosphere, uncertain leads and exceptional foresight can use the same information contract while playing differently. A dream can be meaningful without being an objective prophecy or compulsory reward.

**Cost:** low/medium for a remembered dream from known events, high for predictive branches. Dependencies: P3 delivery, D8 actual memory/account refs, body/sleep/knowledge context, optional D9 tone; [SIMULATORS](../../../../SIMULATORS_AND_CHEAT_IMPLEMENTATION.md#honesty-what-a-simulation-knows) owns foresight/fidelity and speculative engines. [P5 X5](../p5/EXTRA_IDEAS.md) retains cosmic intervention proposals.

**Contract:** `vision = {id, causeRef, kind, basisRefs, fidelityPolicy, horizonRef?, branchRef?, resolvedText, interpretationRefs, knownAudience, drawReceipt}`. Kinds include ordinary memory dream, symbolic anxiety, actual message, uncertain omen and supported foresight. Choose from known experiences for an ordinary dream. A sealed secret cannot appear because a text generator sampled a private fact. Actual revelation needs the secret's authorized discovery route; report only its ID and route status. Speculative predictions carry branch/fidelity, can diverge through real causes and never rewrite the live future on rendering.

**First slice:** after one original local deed and an ordinary sleep action, one stored dream uses only the dreamer's known participants and remembered sensation. Hero can record an interpretation or discuss it with a willing known person; the latter produces a belief/account, not a truth grant. Replay/reload renders the same dream. Later qualified foresight can be added through the simulator owner without converting all dreams into forecasts.

**Gates:** no sleeping/preview rerolls for the same committed dream occurrence; interpretation does not mint a hidden fact; no secret candidate count in UI; saved dream/meaning/history survives import and grammar update. If a vision guides an action, prove its actual knowledge or perception consumer. Ordinary no-dream play stays viable. A stronger prophetic variant needs branch-isolation, accuracy/horizon and discovery-route gates before any claim.

**Further options:** shared dreams, interrupted dreams, recurring motifs, incompatible interpretations, false omens, sensory memories, religious practices, deliberate lucid training and no-cost rare clairvoyance where the world supports it. The motif itself does not reveal a supernatural cause.

## N8: festivals and seasonal events

**Value:** a familiar place changes its people, work, music, requests and goods at fitting calendar moments. Attendance, preparation, hosting, study and ordinary work can coexist.

**Cost:** medium for one local occurrence, high for institution/region calendars. Dependencies: native saved calendar/recurring windows (`src/world_rhythm.js:5-30`, shown/SOURCE), P3 occurrences, P4 guests/organizer, N2 market demand, P6 commissions, P8 supplies and world calendar profiles from P9/P10. UI theming belongs to [the UI owner](../../../../THEMATIC_UI_AND_INFORMATION_DESIGN_2026-10-12.md), not generator logic.

**Contract:** recurring tradition definition, local date rule, actual occurrence ID/year/cycle, organizer, venue, invitations, budget/stock reservations, prepared activities, known notices and close/cancel receipts. Gregorian holidays, game-world calendars and real-device seasonal decoration are separate. A festival doesn't materialize a funded prize or open a faction's private rooms by itself. A shift of date or cancellation is a saved cause, not a failed event roll on reopening the panel.

**First slice:** an original repair fair uses one authored Village venue during a played-calendar window. Organizer offers a funded repair commission, a free observed demonstration and ordinary market access. Stock comes from the same saved owners. Hero may participate, help prepare, visit later or ignore it; a missed occurrence creates no automatic shame/debt/death.

**Gates:** repeated view loads one occurrence, next recurrence can exist under declared local frequency, complete/cancel/late entry preserve stock and receipts, calendar boundaries do not issue duplicate prizes, and new guests do not replace existing identities. Exact pending fair/import state and next schedule survive. Prove one useful changed activity and desktop readability; palette/music settings and reduced motion remain respected.

**Further options:** regional calendars, movable feasts, migration fairs, school terms, holy days, market days, funerals, jubilees, contests and player-hosted celebrations. Source-backed sacred/cultural practices need qualified claims; this packet supplies no canon ritual. Different worlds can have continuous festivals or rare commemorations through their own laws.

## N9: disasters and recoveries

**Value:** a disturbance creates finite, concrete repair, rescue, research, trade and relationship opportunities. Recovery can be as interesting as the initial threat.

**Cost:** high for destructive changes; medium for a reversible local service disruption. Dependencies: P3 actual event effects, D8 causal receipts, N1 work/aid, N2 supply, P8 ecology, existing object/site damage and repair means; optional D9 pacing. X2 weather and X5 cosmic events remain [P5](../p5/EXTRA_IDEAS.md) owners. Avoid duplicating P3's existing roof card.

**Contract:** hazard definition, actual trigger/cause, affected endpoint/object IDs, warning means, known observations, saved severity, actual deltas, reachable responses, relief capacity and staged recovery. Separate failed predictions, actual impact and sensational accounts. A crisis cannot confiscate unsupported inventories or fabricate actor death; destructive/life effects need their selected owners and explicit per-world policies. Recovery updates real objects/availability, not just a “rebuilt” line.

**First slice:** a fallen branch closes one original authored local work station for a finite repair task, leaving ordinary alternate work reachable. Warning arrives through supported local observation; help, hire, move or wait are valid routes as means permit. Actual timber/tool/work reservations restore that exact station and generate a remembered aid receipt. No map-layout regeneration or lethal roll.

**Gates:** warning uses real observation and doesn't expose hidden causes; alternate route is useful; repair restores exact object/availability; repeated settlement cannot duplicate salvage/rewards; travel and cancellation preserve owned goods. Interrupted/unsupported import doesn't silently “recover” damage by resetting. Prove causal impact and recovery before claiming a disaster system. Offline/automatic paid decisions and lethal disasters remain separate future gates, not implications of this slice.

**Further options:** flood/fire, crop disease, siege logistics, magical outages, institutional failures, relief corruption, mutual aid and long restoration histories. Risky/harsh worlds remain reachable with explicit rules and fitting means; D9 changes future selection, never erases an already saved catastrophe.
