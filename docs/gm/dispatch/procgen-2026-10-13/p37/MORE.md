# BH11–BH20: further micro texture angles

2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** Extends [root BH](../../../../PROCEDURAL_STORY_AND_CONTENT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md#bh-per-world-micro-texture), not a new stop condition or closed shortlist. All reasonable routes remain reachable through their actual means. These are additional original suggestions, not canon claims or implementation authorization. [Shape](SHARED_SHAPE.md) supplies evidence, observer, clock and phrasing fields.

| Code | New angle and concrete suggestion | Root / sibling join; important semantic distinction |
|---|---|---|
| BH11 | **Register changes within one conversation.** A known instructor speaks formally before a client, then more familiarly in private; a traveler asks a translator for a term. Store audience, relationship direction and language knowledge on each turn. | BA1/BA2, KN4, P7, P11 M7, P15. Do not create authentic slang by transliteration or infer honorifics from nationality/name. Translator knowledge is an actual service, not omniscient text. |
| BH12 | **Document wear and correction marks.** A revised invoice, duplicate order, unsigned note or receipt stamp makes information look handled. Preserve issuer, original claim, correction and actual receipt refs. | CL, TL, P6 creative works/M11, P14, P33 media. A crossed-out quote is not a refund, signature is not actual authority and formatting cannot grant ownership. |
| BH13 | **Seasonal ordinary-life variants.** Current rain changes where people stand, a festival changes an optional serving, a cold workroom changes a visible coat. Catalog actual calendar/weather applicability rather than generic world season adjectives. | RX1–3, IT1, EC4, P8, P11, P33. No fictitious moon boost, crop loss or mandatory eating from descriptive weather; real process law remains CR2. |
| BH14 | **Body-specific courtesy and sensory access.** A host moves a tool into a rooted guest's reach, supplies a suitable vessel, asks a nonspeaking guest their preferred channel, or uses a visual rather than spoken notice. | XD2–4, CO7, P26/CR1 body response and nonhuman owners. Smell/gesture channels need actual faculties and shared meaning; no compulsory human nickname/body or color-only urgency. Food stays optional. |
| BH15 | **Silence with a known mundane cause.** A usual greeting is absent because its actual speaker is busy; an empty notice board has no received message. Permit a neutral line or no line. | BB2, BF5, AU, NP, P11 M15 and P36. Absence is not a threat hint, secret count, personal punishment or automatic relationship loss. Another service route remains available. |
| BH16 | **Price talk at the right confidence.** A resident remembers a dated purchase, a shopkeeper gives today's actual quote, a neighbor repeats an attributed rumor, and an outsider asks about units. | EC1–5, MK6, SO, P29/CR4 economy. Keep negotiated, promised, paid and refunded separate. No hidden potency encoded through price adjectives or invented universal denomination conversions. |
| BH17 | **Small care rituals without mandatory upkeep.** A worker offers an optional cup, leaves an actual repair kit within reach or asks whether a guest prefers quiet. A known companion's preference can be remembered without forcing consumption. | CO6/10, SO1/2, FE6, CR1/CR4, P19 and P36 BB. A kind line is no automatic consent, cure, romance or relationship reward. Useful food benefits and comfortable unfed play coexist. |
| BH18 | **Ordinary props as durable callbacks.** A returned tool retains a known maker mark, a cup stays at its actual owner's counter, a coat's actual patch is noticed later. The prop may matter socially while carrying no magic. | MK1/2, IT2/5, PR, P6 repair/provenance, P14, P36 BC. History follows exact object/custody/repair receipts; changing text cannot merge item keys, invent provenance or export an heirloom. |
| BH19 | **Acquired local habits and optional preferences.** Repeated real orders produce a tentative usual, adopted vocabulary gets an original local form, a former trainee keeps a tool-layout preference after promotion. Keep actor-specific exceptions. | FE, KN2/4, MK1/4, AU3, NP, PR, BA4/BB1. Frequency inference is not a fixed personality fact, title/rank does not erase learned habit, and the player can still choose every available dish/route. |
| BH20 | **Contradictions made legible through sources.** Two people use different title conventions or remember different dates; a later correction updates their claims without changing actual history. Catalog whose statement each is and its jurisdiction/cutoff. | KN1/2, WL, CT1, RS, SC, P13/P14/P24/P32. No random renderer choice resolves canon conflict. Appraisal reveals only its qualified scope; certainty labels do not expose sealed truth. |

## Concrete cross-world compositions, still optional

| Composition | Small visible outcome |
|---|---|
| BH11 + BH12 + BH16 | A current commissioned repair has a formal quote, an actual correction and a familiar maker's private explanation. Buying, self-repair, learning or asking a second qualified maker remain reachable. |
| BH5 + BH12 + BH20 | A newspaper/forum report and an actual private service receipt describe the same known event differently; a correction cites evidence without making the first speaker clairvoyant. |
| BH6 + BH14 + BH17 + BH19 | A host recalls an actual optional order but asks about the guest's current body/preference. Decline, a different serving or no meal stays comfortable. |
| BH7 + BH15 + BH18 | A worker is actually busy, their known tool remains at the real station, and the return line offers a present alternative contact. No invented absentee simulation. |
| BH4 + BH13 + BH20 | A local appointment uses a verified clock while a source-disputed festival date is omitted or attributed. No invented conversion or forecast guarantee. |

These combinations read existing owners. They do not add crafting laws, special-dose rules, automation policy, NPC maker AI, calendar conversion or an independent economy. CR1–CR4 retain those mechanics. Texture can remain texture; a chosen functional interaction needs its own real consumer. More source packs, communities, eras, bodies, languages and props can extend this catalog without forcing either/or selections.
