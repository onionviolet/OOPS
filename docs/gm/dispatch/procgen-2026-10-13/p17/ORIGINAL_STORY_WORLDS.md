## O1: smallest mechanically useful original comparison

Use one authored workshop and one existing repair need. A real repair yields a P12 feat, a colleague's received P14 account and a patron reaction. An original declared role-resonance reader improves one actual follow-up repair operation, while the patron offers separately funded work. Show private repair earning resonance without public fame, and public exaggeration changing belief/access without inventing the repair. There need be no new world, grand prophecy or metaphysical universal director.

Generate situations from actual deficit, role opportunities and reachable support, then let actual choices determine the story. Queue repeated meaningful service; allow buy, make, hire, teach, negotiate, investigate, refuse and return where supported. A first fixture may implement only two routes, clearly labelled slice coverage rather than a permanent world rule. Idle and active paths read the same completion evidence.

## O2: broader original options

A culture can remember deeds through oral traditions, written records, spirits, artifacts or an omniscient rule. Those carriers have distinct survival and ownership. A world may reward an unrecorded act, let belief make fiction real, turn abandoned stories into beings, permit earned legends to be spent, or keep tales entirely decorative. Author the law and consumer for each; the text pipeline remains a renderer of actual supernatural operations.

A story resource can be stored as a capacity, consumed reserve, living source, qualification, share, bond or authority. Consumption might weaken a function, release a held memory, transfer a claimant or leave the underlying deed intact. Do not choose a single “burn story = forget history” model globally. Stronger variants can preserve the deed and reuse its power indefinitely if fitting; finite computation and durable save identity still apply.

No abuse-first nerf catalogue. Repetition, collaboration, role subversion, propaganda, generous patrons and quiet accumulation can all be legitimate solo play. Distinguish intentional fiction-supported duplication from bugged double publication. For every selected law, retain enough typed cause/ownership/assessment to import the same result; refuse a corrupted active owner instead of secretly substituting a weaker power.
