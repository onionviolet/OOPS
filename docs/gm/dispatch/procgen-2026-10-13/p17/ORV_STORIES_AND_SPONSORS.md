## D1.2: content generation with a mechanical reader

Generate an original incident from actual means and opposing goals, then let P12 identify what the player and companions accomplished. Select an eligible original Story-function template matching those receipts. Generate its title and account last through P15/P14, using only allowed knowledge. Preserve famous source Stories as authored identities; canon actors and unique authorities are reserved under P13/P7. A player can earn a distinct original deed without cloning a named Story.

First proposed journey: at one authored station, obtain a real repair/rescue request, provide supplies or organize willing help, and complete it. A reached channel shows attributed reactions; a real sponsor can donate or offer terms. Separately, one source-qualified feat reader awards an original supported protection/preparation function whose next use changes an actual obstacle. Offer silence/private assistance alongside broadcast play. Refusing sponsorship keeps coin trade, learned methods, unsponsored Stories and other source-supported routes reachable. A donation can exist without permanent patronage.

**Honesty:** freeze who saw what, actual shares, rule/source revision and supported grant. Interest comes from witnessed actual stakes/outcomes, never adjective count, fabricated suffering or how moving an LLM finds a paragraph. A sponsor can enjoy deception only after receiving and believing a real deceptive account. Private motives remain private. Use of a Story reads its acquired source and function, not the saved display name. A hidden sponsor or variant is ID-only until its discovery route.
