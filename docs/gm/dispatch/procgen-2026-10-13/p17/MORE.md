## Additional combinations without duplicate owners

**D1.M1:** audience-financed relief joins sponsor support, real logistics and a completed recovery deed. A god's intervention needs an actual supported source and aftermath under [COSMIC_EVENTS C1–C5](../../../../COSMIC_EVENTS.md), not a random run-ending effect to increase drama. **D2.M1:** teaching a source-qualified acting principle joins knowledge transfer and later independent practice, not a borrowed profession label. **D3.M1:** a library-held System offers a quest from the reader's actual question using P11 N6 collection/custody and P6 knowledge payload. **D4.M1:** remembered failed prophecies become attributed evidence; changed prerequisites alter later forecasts without claiming fate was stolen. **D5.M1:** a feast, fair or memorial changes eligible communal belief through an actual event/performance; useful food benefits remain optional and ordinary unfed play comfortable.

These are further options, not a stop nudge or a universal end state. Selection of one does not prohibit another. Common cost is multi-owner coordination and source qualification for direct canon adaptation; all examples can also be explicit original-world laws. Prototype storage/work bounds keep saves executable without becoming fixed once-per-life/per-world rules.
