## D4.2: generate opportunities with actual causes

First proposed original fixture: a real funded convoy/sect opening has several qualified candidates. A hero with an acquired scoped fortune source sees changed weights among *eligible* openings or pays a declared charge to improve one unresolved supported action. Helping a rival, sharing preparation, competing, trading, stealing under an explicitly qualified method, refusing or returning remain valid routes. The rival keeps their skill, goods and relationships after a fortune loss. An anchored route can coexist with reserve luck; neither is forced as the only model.

Preserve the candidate set, source modifier, actual draw and commitment. A resource can influence loot without influencing a court hearing, or escape without creating a new mentor. Profile declares scope and composition with the storyteller. Pacing settings are player tools, not automatically in-world destiny; theatrical UI is not probability pressure. Qualified foreknowledge reads actual saved/planned causes, not just the next RNG number: an unconsumed draw is no fixed future event when eligibility, order or actor choices can still change. Root A3 needs this qualification, not a ban on strong foresight.

## D4.3: loops, halos and contradictions

A fictional reset can legitimately create a new attempt and locally repeat an obligation. Durable settlement identities and exported goods follow the reset's retention table, including intentional duplication if the fiction explicitly provides it. File reload/import merely restores that state. Prevent accidental double reward by the same attempt; do not impose a lifetime once rule on a regressor's real later attempt.

Required scope table for selection: body, memory, skills, source reserves, story shares, audience belief, obligations, items, exported objects, NPC relationships, active jobs, history evidence, RNG and outside observers. A remembered deed can guide a plan but cannot grant current public reputation to witnesses who forgot it. Branch/counterpart identity and the player's comparison notes follow [intro timeline rounds TA/TB/TX/BR/OV](../../../../INTRO_FLOW_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md#round-10-story-world-timelines-and-branches-2026-10-14). These rounds are palettes, not a selected migration/absence model.

**R1:** Fated Villain's early warning is not a demonstrated killing prohibition; later examples and effective corrections matter. **R2:** loop memory is not evidence of innate fortune. **R3:** E5 protagonist canon anchors cannot force an event after P13's required cause is removed. **R4:** a genre-specific consumable, singular item or local reset rule cannot become a global anti-abuse cap.

Gate: one genuine source or explicitly original fortune function, positive/absent-source controls, useful action difference, future-only influence, both repeat and interrupted reset cases, outside-export accounting and exact saved draw/assessment continuation. Full historical rollback is not a prerequisite for the scoped fortune fixture. All runtime gates NOT RUN; powerful and later variants remain reachable.
