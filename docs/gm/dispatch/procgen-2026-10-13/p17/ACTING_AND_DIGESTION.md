## D2.2: generate acting opportunities, not acting points in text

Attach role-relevant situations to P11 needs and P12 arcs: an actual inquiry with uncertain evidence, a performance with real listeners, a maintained routine, an occasion needing a qualified service, or a difficult choice among fitting methods. Bind one early Sequence's principles first. The predicate must describe actual actions and feedback under that principle, not simply `job = seer` or `card has clown tag`. Missing or misunderstood principles can be investigated, taught or tested through source-qualified means.

Proposed bounded LOTM journey: obtain the source-qualified early potion by its actual existing route; encounter an original uncertain request at an authored district; investigate and make a supported role-relevant decision; receive an attributable felt digestion change; later repeat with a genuinely new request or queue meaningful eligible service. A control action with the same profession/wording but no principle-fitting behavior does not qualify. One supported reader can work before generalized story sifting, since actual action receipts suffice; P12 later composes longer patterns.

Player-visible progress can be sensations and stages where appropriate, with a technical test fixture reading exact hidden values separately. Precise percentages remain an optional world/interface capability, not an assumed canonical meter. [R15 status-window work](../../cheat-status-window-2026-10-13/GOAL.md) and [P11 interface variants](../p11/CHEAT_VARIANTS.md) distinguish baseline knowledge from a special appraisal cheat.

## D2.3: idle, integrity and further options

Qualified queued work and sustained conditions remain reachable; an actual performance/service must finish with current means and feedback. Do not require manually chosen acts or active browser input as an anti-abuse condition. Routine, unusual, broad, intense and subtle acting can have different local effects if source or explicit adaptation supports them. No fixed lifetime once limit or universal novelty requirement. Repeated rendering of one completed act is the same receipt; later real acts are new causes.

**Honesty:** consume the eligible holder's actual action, understood principle, current potion/source and assessment revision. Generated lore cannot rewrite principles or declare digestion complete. Save action and digestion delta atomically, keeping outstanding ritual/characteristic refs. Migration cannot infer years of acting from biography. Interruption, regression, body replacement and source transfer require declared retention instead of an automatic digestion reset or carryover.

**R1:** older LM4 requires in-session acts and proposes offline wages. It is not canon or standing idle policy. **R2:** a cover profession can create opportunities but cannot prove role fit. **R3:** the targeted table mixes source statements with proposed engine shapes; its simplified conservation and fixed principle counts must not become universal arithmetic. **R4:** COI's compilation has translation/numbering issues and layer/date contradictions; pin the selected source window, preserve uncertainty and do not import unrelated loop rules.

More: misunderstood roles with experiments, cooperative ritual staging, disguised acting, changing audience interpretation, competing principles, routine mastery, qualified teaching and optional anchor-building. No universal reputation requirement: private acting may work without public legend. The first gate is one qualified principle and concrete consumer, then felt change/use, meaningful repeat, interruption and exact pending/completed import. No runtime/build/native gates run here.
