## G1.1: a sparse society with causes

Generate a local social network, then let supported activities change it. A person's needs, values, current commitments, known opportunities and available means select an intended act. The recipient separately responds. Their shared event can change two directed opinions, create an agreement, leave a grievance or end an arrangement. Nobody needs to wait for the hero to enter the scene.

P4 supplies three saved travelers with traits, needs, a supply request, possessions and routines. It supplies neither backstory episodes nor generic social actors. Its `action:'talking'` is a routine label, not evidence that a particular listener heard anything. [P4 REPORT](../../../../../.claude/worktrees/proc-p4-people/docs/gm/dispatch/procgen-2026-10-13/p4/REPORT.md) states this boundary; `generated_people.js:22-25,45-60` shows it. A selected slice must bind qualified person IDs before delivering effects, as CONTRACT C1 describes.

Use three fidelity levels as scheduling choices:

| Level | Retained state and work | Promotion rule |
|---|---|---|
| Recurring cast | Individual identity, motives, sparse ties, obligations, actual stock and due plans | Player engagement, a live commitment, a consequential social event, or an authored role |
| Known background | Identity capsule, established ties, last reconciled clock, known accounts and coarse routine | A new witnessed interaction or supported plan promotes the same person |
| Anonymous crowd | Cohort/site counts and public conditions, with no fabricated individual biography | Materialize an original eligible person at a declared cause; preserve any prior individual evidence |

Distance lowers optional planning frequency, not the reality of an existing debt or spouse. A low-detail person cannot produce an exact dated confession, inheritance or birth retroactively without retained causes. Quiet background is valid. A distant named contact with an actionable commitment remains scheduled regardless of camera distance.

## G1.2: formation and change

Existing edges make candidates cheap: household contacts, coworkers, neighbors, classmates, travel companions, students and correspondents. New edges need a real contact opportunity such as overlapping time at an authored workplace or a delivered letter. Candidate discovery uses occupancy indexes and stable rotating cursors, rather than checking every pair. A busy actor's overlapping reserved job excludes them. Fear, admiration, affection, familiarity, trust and resentment can coexist; do not collapse them into one favor bar.

| Social development | Required cause and generated content | Real later consumer |
|---|---|---|
| Acquaintance or friendship | A completed shared task or conversation; each side interprets it with their own beliefs and preferences | Invitation, help willingness, future casting or conversation choice |
| Partnership | Compatible complementary needs, an accepted agreement, real tools/time/funds and explicit shares | Shared appointment, supported production or venture under P11/P21 owners |
| Disagreement or feud | A witnessed failure, disputed credit, breached terms, competing claim or attributed accusation | Mediation, avoidance, restitution, investigation, lawful complaint or locally supported retaliation |
| Repair or estrangement | Apology plus any promised restitution; forgiveness is a recipient decision, not a payment effect | Changed willingness or a continuing boundary; former relationship history remains readable |
| Courtship or marriage | Eligible adults, local relationship norms, separate wishes and accepted commitments; an institution may recognize a union separately | Household residence, ceremony, custody and care agreements; no automatic stock merge |
| Breakup or separation | Withdrawal/change of a partnership, with terms for shared property and responsibilities | New residence/schedules, divided custody or continuing cooperative care |
| Children or adoption | World-qualified family event, actual parents/guardians and supported care/residence; birth/adoption cause saved before presentation | A new distinct person, guardian duties and later child-safe learning or succession routes |

Events create content by binding actors, motive, object/site, terms, alternatives and consequences from actual state. For example, two original repairers compete for one appointment; a missed delivery yields an accusation; a third coworker offers mediation; restitution can turn the competitors into partners. Templates author the supported verbs, but the particular cast, dispute and resulting sequence are generated. P12 owns multi-step plot organization; P19 supplies these real social changes.

A group can hold a joint agreement without every member liking every other member. Shared property and guardianship are multi-party records, not symmetric opinion axes. Affection does not transfer inventory. Marriage does not prove parentage. Children are never rolled as surprise replacements for source-reserved heirs. Reproduction, magical creation, adoption and chosen family remain distinct reachable paths where the world supports them.

## G1.3: autonomy, consent and cruelty profiles

Propose per-world/per-person policies such as voluntary, negotiated, hierarchical, coercive, compassionate, vindictive, forgiving or institution-bound. These are explicitly authored adjectives with scope, actual means, costs and reaction rules. A threat can obtain compliance in a harsh world without creating trust, freely given agreement or affection. A binding supernatural oath needs its own world resolver. Cruel action can produce resistance, escape, fear, testimony or retaliation where actors have means. None of those outcomes is compulsory just because the actor has a cruel trait.

Keep agreement, willingness, legal recognition and compulsion as separate facts. The first ordinary Village proof uses voluntary adult work and nonlethal disagreement. Harsh social routes remain candidates, rather than being removed by that demonstration. Content involving romance is restricted to adult bodies and adult appearance, with no child romance or sexual content; child lives instead support family, guardianship, friendship and education. The relationship owner's proposed CQ1/DV1 age rule is a content admission boundary, distinct from its unresolved consent/cruelty tuning. [Live relationship owner, sections 12-14](../../../../worlds/world-depth-2026-10-13/29_RELATIONSHIP_PREREQUISITES.md) now contains the addenda. The [relationship audit](../../relationship-prereqs-2026-10-14/AUDIT.md) and [historical appearance addendum](../../relationship-prereqs-2026-10-14/CLAUDE_ADDENDUM_2.md) retain provenance; they do not establish newly accepted world tuning.

Repeated useful shared activity may deepen a fitting tie during idle play. A watering routine need not equal intimate trust, but a sustained reliable joint job may earn trust without extra clicks. Cadence/saturation depends on person and world. No universal daily cap, once-only friendship, mandatory upkeep, offline abandonment punishment or hunger death is added.

## G1.4: first generated local society

Proposal: opt in to a small original cohort, preserve the three P4 identities, and admit an additional eligible coworker only if the selected slice needs one. Bind two travelers to a saved joint repair appointment. They reserve their own supported stock/time; a third known person witnesses completion or learns later through an actual contact. Separate directional reactions make one relationship warmer and another merely professional. Later a funded request casts these same people differently because of that receipt.

The hero can watch, join, arrange help, mediate, refuse or pursue ordinary work. Nobody hands the hero money for an unwitnessed NPC achievement. Cooperation and disagreement can both be generated from the same compatible verb grammar; controlled fixtures can compare their causes without claiming a sampled distribution proves balance.
