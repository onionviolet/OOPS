## AB1.1: leadership is a set of supported powers and duties

Found, join, manage, advise, inherit, buy, share leadership, withdraw, split, reconcile, reform or leave a group where actual world means support it. Membership, office, ownership, loyalty, affection, competence and a supernatural bond are separate. Leading a household need not mean ruling its members; a large sect can contain several genuine schools and careers. A canon institution remains authored, with source-era offices and reservations; generation fills eligible local roles and needs rather than replacing a famous leader.

| AB1.1d household | Residence, care, shared use, individual property, schedules and agreed responsibilities | A member proposes a move, a study arrangement or temporary cover for work without surrendering their inventory or identity. |

World adjectives can be collegial, hierarchical, hereditary, elected, open, invitation-bound, oath-bound, permissive, wealthy or precarious. Declare the actual rule and its effects. A tyrannical office can command through an authored coercive law without automatically creating trust; a voluntary guild can be powerful without forced equal votes. Keep the broad route menu reachable; the first benign fixture is not a universal institution constitution.

## AB1.2: recruitment and membership arcs

Generate applicants from actual eligibility, known vacancies and contact opportunities, using P7/P4 identities. A visitor can already be someone's parent, former member or rival. Do not clone a person for each application. Invitation → terms → response → appointment → actual work → review/renewal/exit creates useful stages. An apprenticeship and paid job can coexist when time/rights allow; willingness alone cannot occupy the same person twice.

Candidate topics include desired study, pay, autonomy, status, safety, tools, religious duty, family proximity and unfinished promises. Unsupported role means stay an honest vacancy or training route. Refusal leaves a distinct person with their own plans. Joining adds a membership cause and explicit rights/duties; it does not merge personal purse with group funds or grant all secret manuals. Applicant evidence can be incomplete, wrong or forged under a supported deception route; the roster shows known evidence, not hidden allegiance.

## AB1.3: a council that gives advice from evidence

A council episode binds an actual agenda/need, convening authority, reachable advisors, each advisor's known information, recommendation, declared personal interest and available proposals. Quartermaster reports cargo shortfall; teacher proposes supervised training; treasurer supplies an actual affordable budget; a member asks to protect household time. Their disagreement can produce new funded options instead of a compulsory vote between two exclusive moral labels.

Advice is advisory until an authorized actor commits. The leader can ask for evidence, combine compatible parts, amend terms, delegate, defer, seek another expert, reject or propose another supported plan. The advisor can be mistaken without the simulation rewriting facts. [P15 V2/V4](../p15/DIALOGUE.md) supports ordinary questions plus a reviewed Ink council scene; [P15 A3/QA3](../p15/AUTHORING_PIPELINE.md) validates bounded proposal effects and affordability. Do not let dialogue variables become the group treasury.

First useful council: one actual tool shortage, one funded commission, and two eligible advisors propose repair and rental. Both remain possible as sequential or parallel operations if capacity/funds support them. Choosing a plan produces one actual new work order; a later meeting references its result. No royal-map or war engine is required.

## AB1.4: delegation, succession and defection

Delegate a scoped authority with budget, permitted actions, body/site/time reservations, reporting channel, stop reasons and review boundary. Predictable repeat work can idle within those terms. New recruits, binding oaths, dangerous new missions, inherited player debts or broadened spending require the chosen policy's authority. The player's absence does not itself reduce loyalty, seize assets or remove office. Delegation can be generous or powerful where local rules support it, with no universal quota.

Succession can follow retirement, election, dismissal, negotiated sale, illness, NPC death or source-qualified restoration. A title transfer, treasury custody, house residence and teaching rights may go to different people. P16 C2 owns successor/viewpoint choices; P27 creates office-specific vacancies/advice/claims. A willing student, colleague, family heir, collective board and external purchaser remain available where the institution law permits them. No forced heir-only rule, prestige or protagonist replacement.

Defection needs an actual reason, awareness, opportunity and means: unpaid agreed wages, witnessed mistreatment, incompatible goal, credible rival invitation, or a sourced institutional schism. Neutral resignation, transfer, temporary leave and dissent are different states. A secret plan stays sealed; public reports use its ID until discovery. Leaving returns institutional loans under actual custody rules, while personal goods remain personal. A dispute can concern specific debts, records or authority rather than spawning automatic combat. Rejoining/mediation/new arrangements remain possible through local terms.
