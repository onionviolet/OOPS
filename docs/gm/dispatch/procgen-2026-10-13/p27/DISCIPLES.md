## AB2.1: admission and teaching receipts

A learner has their own actual body/source eligibility, prior understanding, current goals, known methods, supported skill progress, available time/tools and wishes. A mentor needs a known teachable method, demonstrated competence where required, communication means and permitted access. Rank, charisma, a famous lineage or manual possession alone does not prove teaching ability. Ordinary trade apprenticeships and rare supernatural instruction both stay reachable through their actual sources.

Proposed apprenticeship joins mentor/learner IDs, world/branch/cycle, agreed purpose/terms, method and curriculum revision, lesson/job refs, actual materials/tool custody, demonstrations and assessed attempts, delivered feedback, learner's interpretation, teaching-basis receipts, current independent plan and departure/succession refs. Do not add shadow XP or relationships. A source can allow direct transmission or rapid learning; do not invent compulsory slow practice merely to prevent power.

Do not deterministically make every strict student betray, every kind student loyal, or every genius copy the protagonist's power. Disposition may moderate response; evidence and change remain visible through appropriate observation.

## AB2.3: independent arcs and lineages

Generate later hooks from real unresolved needs: a first client, an incompatible body/source, a dispute over method credit, an error requiring repair, peer rivalry, a different school, care duties, an institutional exam, a new study question or a voluntary return as collaborator. P12 owns multi-step arc structure. The mentor can coach, refer, join a project, supply a tool, reconcile, decline or learn from the student. Success and friendship can coexist with departure.

Lineage is the chain of actual teaching/recognition records, separate from biological ancestry, sect membership, ownership, title and metaphysical bond. A manual learned through a library is not personal instruction from its famous author. Copying a qualified technique does not clone its creator's source, private memories or unique artifact. A pupil's independent refinement can have distinct credit and legal/institutional permission. [P14 accounts](../p14/CONTRACT.md) preserves disputed histories without changing the actual work.

An NPC succeeding a living mentor can keep the mentor as advisor, partner or ordinary neighbor. An NPC mentor's confirmed death can leave actual manuscripts, unfinished demonstrations and different witnesses, leading into [AC1/AC2](DEATH_AND_AFTER.md). A ghost teacher, reincarnated former mentor or returning loop participant requires that world's actual identity/memory/contact law; resemblance or foreknowledge does not authenticate them.
