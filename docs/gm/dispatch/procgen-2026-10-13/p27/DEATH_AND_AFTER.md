## AC1.2: distinguish the event, identity and report

Proposed transition record references actor identity, actual body/form, current life/source law, trigger/cause, world/instance/branch/cycle, actual local time, outcome and knowledge evidence, affected jobs/loans/office refs, remains/custody, soul/mind carrier refs only where supported, continuation availability and law revision. Unknown soul state is UNKNOWN, not null interpreted as destroyed. A rumor of death is an account; missing, captured, unconscious, fake death, destroyed vessel, confirmed death, soul escape, returned life and reset are different facts.

Create social effects only when an observer learns through a valid channel. Death confirmation can trigger a vacancy or estate process under local rules without telling everyone the sealed mechanism. A later correction changes beliefs and supported claims, with prior reports retained. A funeral can be held for someone believed dead; label it that way and do not make belief kill them. Body cleanup in temporary combat cannot revive a durable named actor or settle an estate.

NPCs can remember affection, relief, anger, unfinished work, gratitude, guilt or conflicted feelings based on actual ties and what they believe happened. Mourning is not a compulsory linear sequence or a timer that must eventually reset to happy. Store a small remembered-cause/interpretation episode in P19's relationship/inner-life owner; P23 owns changes of goals and values. A later action can be care for another person, continued study, a ceremony, a resolved old dispute, a changed vocation, silence or ordinary work.

Generate callbacks from surviving concrete links: the repaired cart, taught method, known letter, paid debt, workshop appointment or annual local observance. Two people may disagree about the deceased; P14 preserves distinct accounts and disputed credit without altering the actual outcomes. Descendants can learn a memory through a real account/manual, not inheriting every ancestor's relationship automatically.

An anniversary uses the selected local calendar and retained actual event/cycle, through [P11 N8](../p11/DREAMS_AND_OCCASIONS.md#n8-festivals-and-seasonal-events) and P24's time lane. A return after several played years can acknowledge remembered ties; a real-world week away cannot fabricate several deaths or skipped grieving. An unreceived death report cannot influence a distant person's dialogue unless another actual evidence route explains it.

Extend [P15 T2/T4/V3/V5](../p15/REPORT.md) with death-confirmation basis, recipient knowledge time, remembered specific shared cause, current relationship interpretation and known continuation uncertainty. Extend P15 A3 with memorial/will/estate entries that separate claim from truth and ceremony from transfer. Reuse an existing Ink scene for an important goodbye or disputed hearing; routine accounts use typed safe frames. Private novel notes never become eulogies; all sample text is original.
