## Clock and pacing distinctions

SHOWN: `src/game_time.js:8-41,63-95` supplies saved calendar fields, season/time-of-day/week helpers. `src/world_rhythm.js:5-29` derives recurring windows from native calendar without rendering occurrences. Its current per-cycle claim belongs to particular services; BF1 does not spread that into universal attendance limits. Each new world declares nocturnal/always-open/seasonal/emergency adjectives and actual availability.

SHOWN: `src/event_feed.js:37-56,87-102` filters known events/accounts and projects only eight news rows. BF5 must use retained S-2/S-4 data with its own read watermark, not infer unacknowledged history from that lossy feed. SHOWN: `src/simulation.js:401-414` retains frozen occurrences and prunes terminal instances. BF2 needs durable pending messages and evidence references outside disposable display entries.

C1 contradiction: root BF3 says similar cards never follow each other, but P15 T4 permits deliberate familiar greetings and there may be only one eligible urgent event. Proposal: distinguish cosmetic novelty from actual consequence. Avoid adjacent cosmetic twins when another honest beat fits; preserve all actual known arrivals and necessary repeated terms. Do not forbid repeating a reasonable activity or fabricate filler danger. This reconciliation is a proposal for root, not an edit to BF3 authority.

C2 boundary: P34 explicitly says no new offline behavior and no absence punishment. An idle fit here means eligible played-time work and low attention, not wall-clock catch-up. P2 branch `src/mixture_native.js:29-33` admits one due batch then schedules from current clock, driven by UI at `src/mixture_ui.js:37`. It does not establish elapsed-time equivalence or closed-browser production. BF5 may report real saved progress, never silently simulate missed batches.
