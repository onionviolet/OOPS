## Inventory identity is a gate

SHOWN: `src/items.js:166-210` supports names/descriptions, but `:238-253` ordinary keys encode ID/components/quality. `:1217-1233` reconstructs an item from those fields through existing bindings. `src/components/inventory_component.js:21-52` merges equal keys; `:65-83` removes by complete key. Arbitrary per-copy biographies are not encoded by those ordinary stack keys. A builder needs a stable instance/custody adapter for BC1–BC3; silently putting names into template IDs would split physical identity incorrectly or overwrite every identical item. Pure batch labels can remain grouped where history is genuinely shared.

The read-only P2 branch provides generated product-reference keys and observer-dependent presentation, not distinct biographies for individual vials: `src/mixture_state.js:61-79`; branch `src/items.js:1219-1225`. Reuse projection separation, preserve its immutable revision-1 identity, and add a per-instance owner only for selected objects that need one. [REPORT P2 audit](REPORT.md#p2-read-only-audit) qualifies this seam.

STATED reference: [Bay 12 development log, 2017 artifact-holder discussion and succession helmet example](https://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/dev_2017.html) tracks physical artifact location/ownership and later handover. BC5's narrow inherited-tool trace is our INFERRED adaptation, not a promise to simulate every historical object. [MORE](MORE.md#reference-ledger) gives its limits.
