## One combined causal example

**M-P30.1, original proposal:** an extremely powerful maker establishes a small school with actual teacher/tool means (AG1.2/SC2/NP3). Their adopted child prefers music (AI1.7), a weak pupil wants work before lessons (AG2.4), and a household mount hesitates at an unfamiliar bridge (AI2.4). These are independent current aims, not a preset five-act finale.

The player can fund all supported options, solve a physical constraint directly, teach selectively, ask colleagues or continue their own art. The child practices with an actual instrument, the pupil earns an actual fitting lesson through funded work, and gradual training or another route changes the mount's travel response. A mild caused hand condition can add an optional adapted-tool/care route without inflicting a disaster. A school account records only received/witnessed outcomes. No power nerf, obligatory orphan tragedy, forced romance, hidden hunger upkeep or stronger final boss is necessary.

**M-P30.2, changed cause:** if magic provides a safe supported bridge immediately, retire the bridge-preparation problem. Offer the mount a different lawful interest, or simply record a comfortable trip. If no instrument exists, show attainable borrowing/purchase/craft paths, not pretend a song was performed. If a worker lacks funds, suspend the paid request or seek a real sponsor. Feasibility checks guard claims, not ban generous or odd worlds.
