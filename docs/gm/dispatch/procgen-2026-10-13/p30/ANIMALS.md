## AI2.1: each companion is an individual

Pet, mount and familiar are roles; one creature can fill several. Magical source, control channel, custody, trust, trained task and friendship are separate. A pet need not improve combat to be worth having. A horse can be dependable at familiar cart work but hesitant on an unfamiliar bridge. A spirit may have no stomach or ordinary age. Sapient allies keep their own person agency; calling an aranea a spider does not make it an owned pet.

Proposed personality reads: stable body/species functions, ordinary initial dispositions, learned preferences, known routines, familiar people/places, significant received experiences and current goal. Correlate fitting traits without generating an unrelated buff assortment. Personality is not a random obedience percentage or permanent moral label. Hidden origin/motives remain opaque until discovered; ordinary visible habits can be known without full appraisal.

AI2.11 own small adventures: a scheduled episode binds its actual body/location, permitted movement, finite task and safe return terms. A curious pet can explore a supported yard while the player works; a trained courier can make an authored route; a familiar can pursue a negotiated side task. Autonomous does not mean omniscient, arbitrary combat-capable or secretly teleporting cargo. New harmful departures hold for a choice; unattended wandering does not create disappearance/death.

## AI2.12: consumables and craft serve the animal

**CO7/ XD4 extension:** distinguish sensed lure, voluntarily received treat, diet-compatible feed, topical treatment, magical charge and learned association. A lure can change approach without creating a bond. Food can be useful for enjoyment, preparation or training while ordinary nonfeeding play stays comfortable; no animal hunger death or surprise permanent neglect debuff. Supported exceptional living/source needs use their own routine and reserve policy, never an attention timer.

A fictional tonic may help a selected compatible recipient through CR1's body-response adapter. Maker's species is irrelevant to patient selection. P2's `oral-village@1` and whole human-facing vial are not a pet treatment contract: **P2 worktree** `src/mixture_state.js:58–62,92–98`; `src/mixture_native.js:16–19` applies the native-player exposure and records oral observation. There is no animal recipient parameter. Reuse product truth/knowledge and add a supported target-specific dose/route; never force-dose every pet or make tonic the only taming route.

P6 tools/textiles can make actual fitted tack; repair can restore an actual harness; creative works can record observed training for another handler. P8 nonlethal collection does not grant permission to farm a pet automatically. Each distinct task uses real body/source/stock, and one creature cannot be booked simultaneously as mount, courier and workshop labor unless its authored capabilities support it.

## AI2.14: clocks, continuity and care

New episodes use [BODY_AND_AGE AH1.7](BODY_AND_AGE.md) safe pending/queued care. Ordinary care can be background supported context; no daily login, food depletion on real absence, abandoned-pet counter or missed birthday affinity loss. Played idle routines remain positive activity. Chosen time passage reviews retirement/development and any consequential transition. Species age, summon's remaining duration, construct wear/fuel and bond history are separate clocks; immortality, repair and lawful resummoning remain reachable.

Save stable creature/person/body ID, local source/law revision, custody/home/control/bond refs, trained functions and learned associations, known observations, salient experiences, pending task/cargo/reservations and eligible-progress boundary. Any personality change follows a saved received event, not save reload, renaming or inventory reassembly. Shapechange retains individual identity under its local law; pet-as-player control belongs to nonhuman owners.
