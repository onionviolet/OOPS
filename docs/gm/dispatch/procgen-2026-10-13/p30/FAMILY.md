## AI1: born, adopted, fostered and chosen family

AI1.1: Family can be blood kin, adoptive parents, foster caregivers, guardians, a communal household, a mentor who provides care, siblings, or chosen adult family. These relations can coexist and change. Parenthood, legal guardianship, co-residence, affection, heirship and teaching are distinct directed edges. Adoption does not delete earlier kin or automatically reveal unknown ancestry. A household member is neither property nor a copied reward actor.

AI1.2: A born-child route binds actual supported bodies/parentage and an explicitly chosen world law; conception, gestation/development, birth and arrival are distinct boundaries. Magical manufacture, reincarnated infant, ordinary birth and a found child use their own source/identity contracts. Preserve each future child's stable resolved identity/draw receipt at the boundary where the local law determines it; do not regenerate personality on first meeting. Pregnancy/medical complexity is optional selected content, not an automatic hardship gate. No fertility roll or prescribed parental sex arrangement is universal across bodies/worlds. Biological transmission belongs to [genetics](../../../../GENETICS_CONSTITUTIONS_AND_BROOD_PLAY.md) and [body-lineage dispatch](../../body-lineage-2026-10-14/), with sourced laws, not P30 invented inheritance.

AI1.3: An adoption/foster route binds an existing or coherently generated child, current caregivers/custody and actual willing support. Source-local law decides whether formal recognition matters. If a parent or guardian has an ongoing relevant right, preserve that rather than silently erase it; absent/unknown/deceased parents require their actual state, not a fabricated tragic origin. A simple generous village household can make adoption easy. A school placement, temporary refuge, shared care or adult mentorship remains reachable alongside it. No universal wealth threshold, orphan requirement or fixed child cap.

AI1.4: Raising children can be one life path among adventuring, making, teaching and politics. Caregiver routines allow the player to pursue other supported work. Offscreen care needs genuine willing means when it is mechanically modeled; comfortable ordinary household care may be authored as supplied context without a grocery ledger. No parent must stay online, feed daily or repeatedly pay an absence bill. Missing attention is not neglect.

## AI1.5: personality develops from received experience

Use the person/inner-life owner's stable identity and initial dispositions. Add **evidence of received experiences**, not a second child personality store. Suggested inputs: interests, sensory/body accessibility, peers and mentors, chosen activities, dependable care, cultural norms, meaningful successes/conflicts and the child's own interpretations. Parents can influence opportunities and habits; no menu guarantees an exact adult personality or power build.

| Extension | Original episode and possible routes | What actually changes |
|---|---|---|

| AI1.7 Interests versus family trade | They enjoy music while the household makes tools | Provide an instrument, compose together, seek a performer, combine crafts or continue ordinary learning. Preferences need not oppose the parent forever; no automatic rebellion story |

| AI1.9 Peer life | A real peer invites them to a club, game or lesson | Join, invite the peer home, request help, choose a different interest or keep a quiet routine. NPC-NPC friendship has its own receipt, not the parent's relationship copy |
| AI1.10 Conflict and repair | A promise or household boundary genuinely conflicts with another aim | Listen, negotiate, apologize, arrange help, revisit terms or retain disagreement. Each person's interpretation can differ; no universal parenting morality score |
| AI1.11 Cultural and mixed household | Caregivers speak/practice different fitting traditions | Learn both, translate, celebrate separately, create a shared tradition or favor an actual chosen routine. No forced either/or culture/lineage selection |
| AI1.12 Growing autonomy | A later life stage permits a new locally supported responsibility | Try a profession, remain home, travel with actual support, teach a sibling, maintain correspondence or decline an office. Leaving home does not automatically reset affection |

AI1.13 causal update proposal: committed experience → actor's scoped interpretation → optional disposition/interest/relationship adjustment → new eligible actions. Retain salient causes and current values; background routine accrual can be compacted as a duration summary. Logins/click frequency are not events. Repeated lessons can continue to help according to a learning law, without endlessly amplifying a trait from replaying the same receipt. Personality is changeable within a local law; no hidden immutable “good/bad child” seed.

AI1.14 concrete comparison: two children begin with the same curious disposition. One receives practical supervised making and learns a real simple assembly method; the other receives stories/music and learns a real performance pattern. Either can later learn the other's practice. A confident child can still hesitate in an unfamiliar situation; an anxious child can succeed with familiar means. Show an explanation from known experiences, without exposing private hidden motives.
