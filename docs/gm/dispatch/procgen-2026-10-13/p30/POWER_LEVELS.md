## AG1: life after danger becomes easy

AG1.1: A powerful character's success is real. Do not quietly raise every enemy to parity, add mandatory power loss, or sabotage a convenient capability to keep a danger meter full. Generate a question from an actual person's goals or a place's changed needs. A powerful solution can settle it quickly; the next story can follow what the solution made possible. Quiet satisfaction is a valid result.

| Extension | Trigger and original story | Actual consequence and reachable routes |
|---|---|---|
| AG1.2 Responsibility | A workshop has more qualified applicants after the hero funds it; people ask who will teach whom | Delegate, teach, fund another class, change the curriculum, keep an open workshop or decline management. Read actual teachers/tools/calendar; better capacity makes future teaching possible. SC2/AU3/NP3, not a spawned boss |
| AG1.3 Politics without personal peril | Two settlements want a genuinely available bridge, ward or medicine service to operate on different terms | Build both if means permit, negotiate shared control, appoint willing operators, provide materials, leave it private or withdraw. Physical capacity, authority and local legitimacy remain separate. Extreme power can remove physical shortage without manufacturing agreement |
| AG1.4 Mentoring | A pupil knows the procedure but lacks the inventor's bodily faculty or apparatus | Demonstrate, supply the missing means, adapt the task, sponsor another teacher or study together. Observe an independent learner using their own means. KN4/MK4; no automatic transfer of the master's power |
| AG1.5 Legacy | A learned procedure, repaired instrument or public account has surviving users and disputed attribution | Share an edition, appoint successors, preserve original copies, correct a claim, leave an attributed account or keep it private. IT5/PR1 and P14 own provenance/carryover; no new prestige layer |
| AG1.6 Ennui and renewed curiosity | The player repeatedly completes familiar work and optionally requests novelty | Offer an unfamiliar art, gentle expedition, strange but supported craft, local hobby, peer correspondence or a quiet day. Read expressed taste and actual repertoire. Boredom is an optional characterization, never a compulsory depression debuff |
| AG1.7 Outliving mortal friends | Chosen game-time progression reaches a source-supported life transition in a known relationship | Maintain a memorial, exchange stories with descendants, reconnect with a long-lived peer, extend life by a real local route or continue ordinary life. A descendant is a new person, not a replacement friendship. Never kill someone because the player closed the game |
| AG1.8 Successful prevention | A very strong healer or protector removes the initial cause | Generate useful aftermath from the actual rescue: reopened school, requested training, a celebration, changed public expectations. No automatic stronger plague or hidden balancing curse. Being able to help cheaply remains meaningful |

These are composable offers, not an exclusive class choice. Repeatable care, new students, recurring civic work and long-lived relationships follow local adjectives and actual causes. One receipt settles one occurrence; it does not prohibit future occurrences.

## AG2: weak in a strong world

AG2.1: Generate a feasible **objective**, not necessarily a feasible duel. A civilian can deliver a warning, hide a workshop, repair evacuation equipment, earn tuition, leave a dangerous route or recruit a willing protector. An overwhelmingly stronger opponent can remain overwhelmingly stronger. Failure, escape, negotiation and eventual victory stay possible through actual means; no compulsory destiny or “you are too low level” ban on participation.

| Extension | Generator reads | Original playable sequence |
|---|---|---|
| AG2.2 Routes and shelter | Known authored connections, actual detection channels, shelter capacity, travel/tool means | Observe a patrol report, choose covered travel or paid transport, reach a genuine safe receiver. Unknown intelligence stays attributed; “hide” is not a guaranteed invisibility roll |
| AG2.3 Alliances and services | Independent actor's willingness, competence, obligations, resources and knowledge | Pay, exchange useful work, ask a known ally, travel together or seek another provider. A powerful ally is allowed to solve the problem; aid is not a free duplicated power |
| AG2.4 Valuable ordinary work | Current supply/service gap, skill and work access | Repair a messenger rig or prepare known stock that a powerful team actually uses. The hero remains ordinary; their contribution has its own receipt and recognition, not fake kill credit |
| AG2.5 Information and patience | Evidence, observer horizons, opponents' actual schedules and objective | Learn a route, compare reports, wait for a supported safe boundary, warn someone or retreat. No fabricated urgent timer to punish refusal |
| AG2.6 Luck where authored | Local luck law and named saved draw committed before consequence | A fortunate meeting can open help or an escape route. It need not impose a later debt; it cannot silently reroll an already settled encounter. Ordinary preparation remains viable |
| AG2.7 Growing beyond the weak role | Real acquired capabilities, current responsibilities and chosen ambitions | Preserve old friendships/work while making new routes possible. Scale updates from the new means, rather than deleting earlier stories or returning the hero to forced helplessness |

## AG3: choose problem scale from role, power and reach

AG3.1 proposed context reads: actual body/functions and current usable power; source/fuel/counters; office and jurisdiction; known people and places; physical/channel reach; available attention, delegation and stock; expressed goals; local era/laws; observer knowledge. These are references into P11/capability/actor owners, not a new universal rank. Unknown capability stays unknown, distinct from zero.

Selection: bind a real need → find actors and causal scope → enumerate supported response paths → establish any claimed feasible route → project what the observer can know → offer/freeze the occurrence and its draws. Reevaluate when actual causes change. Power changes possible responses; role changes who asks; reach changes where an act can land. None alone determines moral worth, happiness or story importance.

| Same original problem: an apprentice cannot finish a useful brace | Reading and fitting scale |
|---|---|
| Farmhand, low combat power, local contacts | Borrow a tool, earn a fitting lesson, deliver material or ask a paid maker; a household-scale story |
| Strong wandering healer, no office | Help the apprentice's actual injury or introduce a qualified craft contact; strength does not create guild authority |
| Weak clerk with a wide correspondence network | Obtain a lawful supplier reply or reschedule an order across towns; regional reach with low combat power |
| Sect master with real staff | Change access/curriculum or fund a second bench. A school-scale decision, with room for a personal lesson |
| Immortal artisan with extreme supported making power | Make every needed brace if desired, then tell the learner's new opportunity or chosen craft story. Keep the powerful route successful |

AG3.2 scope is also a lens: household, street, institution, region, world and multiverse views may coexist when actual channels connect them. A pet's lost toy can still be offered to a cosmic being who enjoys it. AG3.3 never fabricate regional knowledge from a private conversation; recipient/channel receipts drive downstream responses. AG3.4 director explanations show known causes and unsupported dependencies, not sealed truth, internal hidden candidate counts or an omniscient rank.

Future gate: same problem/role/power/reach fixtures generate different supported routes and lasting consumers; a powerful direct solution succeeds without replacement peril; a weak noncombat route closes the objective; independent distant events remain unchanged. Saved offers/knowledge/means/next draws survive reload and literal Import. All unrun; [REPORT](REPORT.md) owns acceptance.
