## AH1: injury and recovery stories

AH1.1 proposed episode: stable cause/body/condition refs, affected functions, known symptoms, recovery law/revision, actual care plan, required stock/provider, beneficial-progress boundary, observers and lasting-change refs. Keep HP, structural injury, pain/fatigue, learned adaptation and scar separate. A scar may be cosmetic; disability need not produce a universal competence/social penalty. No model field is required unless a selected consumer reads it.

| Extension | Original story / supported choices | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| AH1.2 Work around a limitation | Recover tender hands, use adapted tools, hire a fitter, change to teaching/planning, use magic or wait | Actual work duration/access changes only for affected functions. Adaptation is a useful path, not a mandatory consolation quest |
| AH1.3 Rehabilitation as idle work | Queue a supported routine at a stable place, reserve genuine assistance if needed, check progress later | Fractional progress survives save; equal eligible game time gives equal recovery. No daily login or click quota |
| AH1.4 Remedies with different scopes | Buy a known dressing, brew an eligible topical product, commission a healer, receive supportive care or use ordinary rest | Relief, removal of causal agent, structural repair and scar removal are separate consumers. A powerful cure can perform all when its authored law actually says so |
| AH1.5 A changed craft | An artisan tests a fitted handle, teaches an adapted technique, returns to earlier work or chooses a new interest | Maker/style and real manual use, extending MK1/KN4 through P6. No mandatory loss of identity or “overcome disability” ending |
| AH1.6 Independent helpers | Another actor has available time, terms, body and competence; they can offer or refuse help | Retained service/care receipts and directed trust, not unbacked omnipresent nurses or automatic gratitude |

AH1.7 absence contract: first slice advances only eligible played/queued game time. Closing or pausing the game creates no harmful delta and no missed-treatment penalty. While a routine runs without attention, supported care continues; an unmet decision or stock boundary places this new episode in a safe pending state rather than silently deteriorating, spending substitutes or killing someone. Preserve completed care and actual expenditures. Resumption is a choice, not a catch-up bill. This applies to new P30 episodes, not a claim that unrelated live combat already provides that protection.

A later explicitly selected offline-care adapter may award funded positive recovery. It must not couple that benefit to offline contagion, age death or neglect. Define eligible care intervals, stock exhaustion boundary and current body revision; never reuse time-since-login as one universal biological clock. A safe pending illness state is an authored simulation policy, not rewinding the global calendar or claiming real medicine stops disease. Lawful dangerous active scenarios remain available through supported choices with disclosed consequences.

## AH2: community illness from actual causes

AH2.1: generate outbreak from a fixed fictional cause/law and actual contacts or shared source. Suggested minimal record references a current condition, onset/recovery intervals, susceptible receiving functions, permitted contact edges, location/route, committed exposure receipts, known evidence and a local care policy. “Susceptible” is not a moral category. Magical curse, contaminated product, mundane infection and shared environmental harm need different laws; no generic sickness spreading tag.

AH2.2 small original case: two workshop workers report similar symptoms after using the same retained fictional irritant batch. Inspection can establish batch contact; independent illness remains possible until evidence distinguishes it. Remove the actual source, change ventilation, supply scoped treatment, refer to a specialist, test another sample or continue safe ordinary work. Batch containment and patient recovery settle separately. This proves a shared exposure story before claiming contagious disease.

AH2.3 later contagious case: three known households and one supported shared meeting bind a saved contact graph. A local fictional transmission law acts only on actual susceptible contacts through eligible time. Quarantine changes contact edges; it does not cure existing infection. Reachable care, medicine logistics, supported immunity or a broad magical cure can all be useful. SC2 supply and NP3 teaching use actual CR4/P8 stock and providers; no money or hundreds of doses created by story text.

AH2.4 uncertainty: reported fever is an observation, diagnosis a scoped claim, agent removal a body-state transition, and public blame another account. Preserve contradictory tests, incomplete evidence and multiple plausible causes. Show source-neutral language until the discovery route establishes more. No hidden diagnosis in a potion name, refusal reason, contact count or “why this happened” panel.

AH2.5 care and presentation: children/animals/elders are independent recipients with support, not cheap tragedy weights. Severe outcomes require selected local fictional rules and explicit current causes; no mandatory graphic description or helpless watch timer. Choices can hold at consequential decisions while ordinary queued life remains usable. An unaffected household control proves spread has a real cause. Outbreak, mild care, highly effective cure and prevention remain reachable; no universal outbreak maximum or mandatory recurrence.

AH2.C1 contradiction: [E2 C10/C16](../../../../emergent/E2_STORYLETS.md) currently **proposes** a hard fever card, a rain-associated weight, herb-based cure and spread on ignore. That is not live epidemiology. Rain alone is not a causal infection law, a parent must be a real caregiver rather than an unrelated cast pick, and unattended dismissal cannot become an absence penalty. Reuse these as episode seeds with actual bodies/contacts/care, revise only through root. No universal herb cure is admitted.

## AH3: age without flattening people

AH3.1: distinguish chronological/local age, subjective lived time, body developmental stage, functional condition, memory evidence, legal/cultural status and source-supported longevity. Rejuvenation, reincarnation, body replacement, stasis and cultivation may change different axes. Appearance alone does not establish any other axis; cultivate lifespan only through the relevant world law. Retain the existing chronicle's age contract until an explicit compatible adapter is selected.

| Extension | Story opportunity and reachable means |
|---|---|
| AH3.2 Elders with functions | An elder can remain strong, practice art, train, run a shop, teach an actual method, lead a council or receive care. No blanket wisdom/forgetfulness/frailty roll |
| AH3.3 Intergenerational correction | A young teacher updates an old master's method; the elder retains firsthand context. Comparison changes an actual manual edition or choice, not automatic age superiority |
| AH3.4 Longevity routes | Ordinary lifespan, gentle age change, open life, cultivation extension, supported immortal body, purchased/local ritual remedy, rejuvenation or leaving bodily time behind. Preserve compatible alternatives and actual means; no forced mortal ending |
| AH3.5 Long-lived households | Align different local body clocks for visits/care/succession; descendants keep independent personalities. A powerful player can support or extend life where lawful without a required compensating loss |
| AH3.6 Chosen passage of years | Preview known consequences and pending care/child/animal milestones before a voluntary skip; unresolved consequential choices hold for review. No real-time childhood wait or unreviewed offline funeral |
