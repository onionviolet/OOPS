# P9: book-world generation profiles

2026-10-14. **DOCUMENTATION PROPOSAL COMPLETE.** P9 created a profile for every explicitly requested source family, splitting TTKS into five independent book profiles and LOTM/COI into separate era/world profiles. Other covered book leads retain concrete generation proposals with their source qualification tier. This is a design packet, not gameplay, accepted fiction or root integration. [Checkpoint](CHECKPOINT.md), [profile rules](PROFILE_RULES.md), [source map](SOURCE_MAP.md) and [backlog](BACKLOG.md) own scope, evidence limits and future gates.

Proposed play: acquire an actual world-fitting resource, choose a supported preparation or commission, meet an appropriate supplier/person, and see useful work/encounter consequences. Worlds vary in provenance, rights, source, reset, ecology and law. Those differences cannot be reduced to one rarity table. Every reasonable route remains reachable through actual means; selecting a first proof does not close other careers, AU starts or later expansions.

## Cross-world patterns

| Code | Finding / proposed pattern | Generator consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Canon is a set of authored predicates and reservations, not a flat pool of familiar nouns. | Admit original variation inside a source-qualified family; never mint a named unique artifact, protagonist role or plot truth as a rare result. Legitimate acquisition/transfer remains possible. |
| F2 | Resource forms need different operations. | Divisible matter, countable/conserved identity, assembled graph, knowledge/pattern, grant and living substrate use distinct state/effect laws. LOTM conservation does not prohibit TTKS B5 copying under its actual entitlement. |
| F3 | Generation and persistence need more than a seed. | Preserve actual resolved identities/results, content revision, cause and custody alongside stream state. No reload redraw, preview draw, content-update rewriting or failed-save publication. |
| F4 | Knowledge does not equal source, title, skill or permission. | A copied method, purchased material, borrowed tool, appointment and trained body enable different actions. Suppliers/specialists and civilian careers remain useful alternatives to gaining rare powers. |
| F5 | Temporal mechanisms differ. | Distinguish linear life, source reset, possible simulation, projected challenge, archive/submission, dream/trial layer and file restore. Per-carrier retention makes learning useful without arbitrary matter export. |
| F6 | Events need causes, actors and real rewards. | P3 composes eligible local situations from P7/P8/P11 inputs; canon event graphs constrain availability and causal aftermath rather than force endings after disruption. |
| F7 | Exact materials can coexist with flexible procurement. | Authentic purchased/prepared lots, growing, gathering, hiring, recovering and studying can satisfy the same qualifier. Unknown provenance remains uncertain; a generic property tag cannot supply canon identity. |
| F8 | More depth also means propagation. | A reagent purchase leaves a witness trail, a power bill is observable, a supplied clinic changes work, a grant leaves an imprint, a map ages, or a guest becomes a funded quest issuer. P11 owns contextual joins. |
