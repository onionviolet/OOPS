# Shared reading contract for P9 profiles

2026-10-14. **Everything in P9 is a proposal.** Evidence summaries preserve their source's status; a cited canon constraint is not an accepted adaptation or an implemented generator. P9 owns this dispatch packet only, not world design owners.

## Evidence and generation boundary

**Shown** means an observed scene/result reported by a cited reading owner. **Stated** means narrator, actor, interface or published rule says it; keep the speaker. **Inferred** means reader/wiki analysis. **Unknown** is missing evidence, not a prohibition. D&D **stated-rule** is prescriptive tabletop text, unlike a character's claim. Secondary wiki summaries retain that label even when their article describes a shown event. P9 reused analytical owners and selected source locators; it did not independently reread full books. No raw prose or private-note quotations are included.

Every generated entity must record proposed `profileRevision`, `sourceSnapshot`, `continuity`, `era`, authored parent/place, generator/table revision, cause/ordinal, durable identity and evidence basis. Store the result and custody/knowledge state as well as the seed. These are proposed fields, not existing APIs or a reserved schema. New serial chapters require reviewed deltas, never silent regeneration of existing saves.

Generate variation inside authored rules: ordinary actors, lots, commissions, eligible encounters and incidental situations. Authored canon owns geography, laws, cast, formulas, unique artifacts and event dependencies. A canon-reserved item may be **located, transferred, damaged or acquired through a qualified route**; it must never be minted as a random replacement, cloned, renamed or have its truth randomized by this generator. An explicit AU can author a separate counterpart with its own identity and reservations, rather than bypassing those protections.

Locations mean occupancy, stock, conditions and scenes attached to authored nodes. No generator may create layouts, routes, room geometry or new canon landmarks. A source with shifting space can switch among separately authored revisions once authorized; it does not authorize procedural geometry here.

## Lifecycle and option policy

Saved root plus world-instance/domain/cause keys isolate draws. Preview, inspection, reload and failed publication consume nothing. A genuine action can intentionally reroll where a qualified local rule or declared AU allows it; save restoration cannot. A loop reset is a fictional state transition, distinct from loading a file. It declares separately what returns, remains, is erased or crosses the boundary. Replaying an unchanged baseline is a useful default for a loop, not a universal ban on source-supported fresh outcomes.

Keep every reasonable route reachable: gather, grow, buy, borrow, trade, recover, commission, investigate, cooperate, fight, detour, refuse and return where each world's means allow. Conditional gates describe local circumstances, for example seasonal, licensed, hazardous, consent-bound, depleted, source-compatible or time-bound. Do not introduce global once rules. Exactly one payout for a particular fulfilled obligation prevents duplication, without forbidding later funded jobs. A canon-specific limited use belongs to that source-qualified power, not all generators.

Idle work follows sustained conditions or admitted queues, current stock/tools/staff and the common played clock. Do not require active clicking for digestion, practice or research solely as anti-abuse. Offline/absent-world advancement remains explicit and unselected here. Ordinary unfed play stays comfortable; food can give useful benefits, with no hunger death. Creature upkeep, dangerous expeditions and supernatural costs need their own local contracts rather than being silently removed or made global needs.
