## More to consider

X1: A funded ship repair can require a genuine consenting control-holder rather than only superior material. X2: Shared buffs can expose shared liabilities, so an encounter package needs exact recipients and counter-effects. X3: Crew service and practical training can build witnessed biography without forcing a new Legend. X4: A returning spirit's useful work has a deck-bound reach, distinct from an ordinary worker or universal cross-world summon.

## Contradictions and next gate

The local depth group initially looked like two unrelated titles; the inspected source report resolves this as Kairos volumeIII. It supplies no routine respec or full advancement curve; absence of a rule is Unknown, not a prohibition. Local singular rod/companion terms do not make every Legend/rank globally unique. Source appendix labels and native numerical skill/rarity axes must not be collapsed.

First proof: generate an original current-era ship/repair request → procure titled parts → secure qualified control/skill → perform useful repair or paid specialist alternative → settle and preserve component, rights, stock and biography.
