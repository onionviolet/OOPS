# Crossovers: causes arriving from somewhere else

2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** All examples below are original adaptation designs, not claims about franchise canon. [P13 canon lane](../p13/CHECKPOINT.md), [P9 profiles](../p9/PROFILE_RULES.md), [P10 profiles](../p10/REPORT.md), [E6 cross-world gossip](../../../../emergent/E6_LORE_LAYER.md#11-cross-world-gossip-and-legends), [P11 transmission](../p11/INFORMATION_AND_LIBRARIES.md) and [timeline crossings](../../../../TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE.md#t4-give-parallel-worlds-independent-state-and-selected-clocks) own their respective rules. P16 adds a cross-boundary story cause and reception consumer. It does not create another travel engine, rumor network or canon roster.

## X1: an imported account becomes a local question

Player route: meet a returning traveler, receive their dated account, then ask a local specialist, investigate a fitting object, carry a reply, ignore it or revisit later. Generate the local question from the intersection of the account's known content, receiver's actual interests and reachable means. A distant broken device might interest a repairer; it need not become a fight or urgent rescue. P12 owns any quest expansion; P14 owns retellings and P15 wording.

Proposed boundary record: origin world/instance/continuity/era, cause/account ID, sender and actual carrier, destination endpoint, sent/arrival dates with clock mappings, recipient/language/interpretation, known basis and reception receipt. A report of another world can be false. The recipient learns testimony, not the other world's complete truth, names, laws or secret catalogue. Secret references in this public packet are IDs/routes only.

**First slice:** one existing supported return route carries an original nonsecret work account. At the destination, a known craftsperson proposes a funded diagnostic inquiry using one locally obtainable sample. The same account can also be preserved or relayed without taking that inquiry. Compare delivery with no delivery: the destination inquiry has a real receipt prerequisite. Cost medium; dependencies: crossing owner, P11 N5 account/channel, P3 delivery, one actual consumer. Generated people, full world simulation and cosmic powers are optional.

## X2: visitors, migrants and rival travelers

Generate a coherent travel bundle, not independent random origin/name/power fields: origin cohort, motive, route, body/source compatibility, current carried possessions, contacts and destination options. P7 preserves names/identity; P4's separate store requires a real promotion/reference adapter before actor control. Named canon travelers remain authored, and their exclusive crossing mechanism is not a universal population trait.

A visitor can be a merchant, refugee, scholar, tourist, pilgrim, rival investigator or former acquaintance. Their goal can overlap the hero's without mandatory hostility. Rival travelers may remember a different branch, possess stale foreknowledge or have mundane information advantages. They acquire evidence through their own means and can negotiate, cooperate, compete, abandon the pursuit or settle. No automatic equalization to player strength. [Rival chronicle](../../../../RIVALS_IDENTITIES_AND_FATE.md#a-persistent-rivalry-system-for-this-game) owns adaptation and reconciliation.

**First slice:** one original returning surveyor brings a saved unfinished inquiry from a supported endpoint; compete for a real appointment, share notes or commission independent work. Retain the same person and stock on departure/return. Cost high; dependencies: real travel, stable actor/custody identity, appointments and knowledge, P12 plan continuation. A traveler label without actual travel is texture and must be described as such.

## X3: artifacts, trade and incompatible methods

An imported object keeps its provenance, carrier and exact physical identity. Destination law separately answers whether its material works, its stored energy persists, its method can execute and locals can recognize it. Outcomes may be fully compatible, inert, adaptable, translator-dependent, institutionally disputed or extraordinarily useful. These are local adjectives, not compulsory attenuation or anti-profit rules. Names and imported text do not execute powers.

**First slice:** carry one original measured repair instrument across an existing route. A locally supported calibration changes one work result; refusal preserves the instrument. Retain its original reading and revised calibration separately. Cost high; dependencies: P6 object/procedure, existing custody/travel/native item consumers, destination law adapter. No generated duplicate of a canon artifact.

## X4: crossovers changing both places

Further reachable stories include competing research schools, imported songs gaining a new audience, diplomatic marriages where world roles support them, diaspora communities, asylum, smuggling, multiverse postal services and joint emergencies. Reciprocal stories require a reply or real returning carrier. A destination's knowledge cannot silently update its origin. Crossovers can involve canon worlds through authored AU links; canon travel laws stay source-qualified.

**First slice:** a returned reply changes the original issuer's next offer using an actual delivery receipt. Cost high; dependencies: X1, bilateral endpoints, funded offer, P12 cause graph. Different rates of time can produce missed meetings, inheritances or an old reply, but clock policy must supply that age. No new geography needed.

## Contradictions and future proof

E6's three-hop expiry, five-year legend delay and reception cap are stated draft choices, not universal multiverse limits. P11 has more explicit transmission/belief records; use that owner and let worlds allow cheap, immediate or enduring channels. E6's forbidden-tag story default does not establish executable foreign physics: narrating rejection, mediation or adaptation cannot substitute for a supported resolver. P9/P10 reserve canon geography and mechanisms; procedural crossovers attach to permitted routes or explicitly authored AU links.

Future proof: same cause/carrier/person/object survives crossing, return and Import; no double stock, knowledge before arrival or secret leakage; stale destination admission does not consume a replacement draw. Failed passage preserves declared custody/recovery. Both endpoints show a changed useful consumer, with rebuilt desktop and pending/completed save proof. All OPEN; no crossing was run by P16.
