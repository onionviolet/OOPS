## I1: returning to receipts, letters and unfinished invitations

Generate a return packet from actual events since a stored read cursor: completed work, stopped jobs, known meetings, received letters, changes the character can establish and decisions still requiring input. Present brief summary, optional linked accounts and useful next actions. An unseen event may have happened without being known; a sender can be mistaken. Report occurrence date, send date and receipt date separately where they matter.

Forms: companion field reports, workshop invoices, research notebooks, postcards, harbor arrivals, diary fragments, school progress notes, oral reports and quiet no-news intervals. Select voice from the actual speaker/edition, not an omniscient narrator for every form. P14 owns assembly/chronicle grammar; P15 owns text/voice; P11 N5 carries messages. P16's additional object is the delivered bundle, its read state and unresolved-action references.

**First slice I1-A:** while the browser runs and one admitted local job completes, a supported companion or issuer sends a dated report. Returning from another panel/activity shows a brief generated bundle linking the real output and one still-open optional inquiry. Marking read or replaying the letter changes no funds/draws. Cost low/medium; dependencies: actual completion/known account, P14 or bounded receipt renderer, existing Notebook/feed, cursor. P3/P4 integration is optional if an existing authored issuer suffices.

This is played-time return evidence. Browser-closed catch-up is separate. The P3 branch report explicitly excludes wall-time advancement; E5's catch-up is a proposal. Intro round12 requests OT consideration and dispatches an audit; it does not prove that closed-browser implementation or one global cap exists. A future closed-browser variant uses the selected clock owner and compares continuous versus chunked catch-up, with no double advancement on repeated returns.

## I2: correspondence can change the next plan

A companion is not just a report printer. Generate questions from their actual findings, failures, stock and agreements. They can ask for a different tool, offer an alternative route, request a consultation or state that no further action is needed. The recipient can defer, refuse, delegate or answer where supported. No implicit spending or irreversible choice from merely reading a letter. Standing orders can be free, cheap, broad or restrictive under declared player/world settings; amounts are not a universal anti-idle quota.

**First slice:** a supported research assistant reaches an actual uncertain result and sends one evidence-bound choice of two fitting tests; a player-selected standing order may reserve the next supported test under its real budget. Cost medium/high; dependencies: I1, P6 experiment, genuine actor/controller/queue and explicit order authority. Letter count is a presentation bound, not a discarded cause or opportunity. Aggregate many receipts while retaining pending decision links.

## I3: seasonal and recurring story constellations

P11 N8 owns tradition/date/organizer/stock. P16 adds connected content across preparation, occurrence and aftermath: a maker's rehearsal creates a new commission; a delayed visitor changes who attends; a performed song creates a later request; donated supplies change what a celebration can offer. Correlation comes from participants, resources and causal receipts, not several independently rolled festival paragraphs.

**First slice:** attach one preparation inquiry and one post-occasion invitation to P11's original repair fair, sharing organizer/budget/occurrence identity. Attend, contribute remotely through a supported channel, host, ignore or return at a later recurrence. Cost medium; dependencies: actual N8 occurrence, P6/P11 work and accounts, P12 chain. Do not duplicate the fair generator. Further forms include migration seasons, commemoration of earned deeds, anniversary reunions, school terms and invented local holidays. Real-device seasonal decoration is a different settings surface.

## I4: cosmic stories have before, during and after

[Cosmic events](../../../../COSMIC_EVENTS.md) and [P5 X5](../p5/EXTRA_IDEAS.md) own omen/intervention. P16 adds generated witnesses' responses, ordinary work around the event and later causes at several scales. A rare gaze can produce a harmless critique, patronage, an abandoned pact, research, pilgrimage or reconstruction. Higher capability changes actual vulnerability and reach; it does not guarantee some penalty to maintain drama. God-like actors remain scarce and unevenly present under recorded direction.

**First slice:** one original non-destructive omen reaches two informed local people with different goals. They produce a research appointment and an optional preparation commission; resolving the real cause changes the later received account. Cost medium/high for this small form, very high for destructive intervention. Dependencies: D2 observation/channel, actual P3 occurrence, P11 requests and P12 cause; use authored actor/law. No fake save corruption, run erasure, hunger death or VFX-only proof.

## Contradictions and gates

E5's three opportunities/day anti-farm proposal conflicts with the user's no fixed anti-abuse limits. Do not adopt it. Per-world abundant/rare/cyclical/harsh/gentle profiles control generated cadence through the existing owner. E2's stored irreversible choices and P3's nonblocking safe expiry are different states; a return-bundle renderer cannot change their outcome policy. Event feed's eight news rows are a current view cap, not the entire return archive.

Future gates, all OPEN: real cause receipts; sender knows their report; complete/read/replay cursors persist independently; no duplicate settlement or RNG; pending decisions survive summary compaction; multiple small advances equal the chosen large advance; exact Save/reload/Import at unread and answered states; keyboard/focus/plain-text/reduced-motion at both desktop sizes.
