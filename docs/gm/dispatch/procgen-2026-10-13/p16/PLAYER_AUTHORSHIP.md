# Player-authored content becomes a cause

2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** [P6 creative works](../p6/CREATIVE_WORKS.md), [technique invention](../p6/TECHNIQUE_INVENTION.md), [P11 information/library](../p11/INFORMATION_AND_LIBRARIES.md), [P11 identity/culture](../p11/IDENTITY_AND_CULTURE.md), [P14 texts](../p14/CHECKPOINT.md) and [P15 authoring pipeline](../p15/CHECKPOINT.md) keep their production, simulation and rendering owners. P16 extends them with intentional authorship, editing, circulation, audience response and local pack creation. All functional outputs use supported consumers; free expression can also remain texture.

## A1: names, notes and remembered intentions

Player titles, nicknames, inscriptions, hypotheses and personal notes are meaningful authorship even when they grant no stats. Keep display alias separate from canonical object/person ID and source name. The player can describe a hope without setting world truth. A hypothesis remains their attributed belief, not evidence just because a renderer repeats it.

**First slice A1-A:** name an actual repaired tool, add an original note choosing which known repair mattered, then give/lend it through an existing supported transfer. Generate the recipient's attributed acknowledgement from observed workmanship and known relationship. Display the same title on return and in the custody record. Cost low/medium; dependencies: exact object/copy identity, known repair receipt, transfer/recipient projection. A note-only first step is texture and should be labelled so; mechanical recognition needs the actual transfer consumer.

## A2: books, songs, art and spells

Offer several composable modes: free text, structured outline, saved claim/score graph, player-selected ingredients or known methods, optional generated drafts and manual revision. Do not force free expression into a quest menu, or require writing paragraphs to operate a structured method. Preserve author, contributor, edition, intent and revised text. A generated co-draft is labelled as such. P15 owns template/LLM/tool options; P16 adds player control of the authored object.

| Work | Player input and generated continuation | Actual effect boundary / first slice |
|---|---|---|
| Book/manual | Choose known experiment claims, order chapters, add free notes; readers ask a new question from their actual gaps | P6 edition/copy/teaching contract. One reader applies one supported method; prose alone grants no fuel, mastery or secret |
| Song/poem/art | Choose motif/occasion, write or select lines, revise score; a real audience interprets it | One willing audience gives a saved fitting response, optional funded commission; no universal charisma buff |
| Spell/technique | Choose target/function/process within actual source/body means, test and name it | P6 invention owns mechanical graph and test. Generate a learned refinement request from observed failure; names cannot define executable code |
| Map/recipe | Select measured edges/observed batches and annotate uncertainty | Existing preparation/production consumer, not new topology or hidden property revelation |

**First slice A2-A:** compose one original celebration song from player-selected known deed and motif. Perform it for a willing known listener using a supported performance action; the listener retains an attributed interpretation that affects one fitting invitation or commissioned revision. Cost medium/high; dependencies: P6 score/performance, P11 occasion/audience, P15 text, real relationship/offer. Different audiences may disagree. A generated lyric is new original wording within a grammar, not reproduced canon prose.

## A3: founding religions, traditions and schools

Generate collective content from declared teachings, known acts, actual adherents, meeting places and practices. Founding a religion does not demonstrate its cosmology is true. Teachings, membership, belief, institutional office, resources and actual divine/power response have separate owners. Original liturgy is distinct from adaptation of a canon ritual.

**First slice:** create one voluntary original study tradition around a known safe method; name it, declare a practice, invite a consenting qualified participant and fund one real lesson. Generate a participant's proposed revision from their observed experience. Cost high; dependencies: P11 institutions/roles, P6 teaching/ritual procedures, actual stock/consent, P12 goals. A larger religion adds worship/institutions and only source-supported supernatural effects. Rival interpretations, syncretism, schisms, missionary travel and no-faith play remain reachable.

## A4: local mods and data packs

Player-authored packs can define original grammar tables, roles, story families, tone tags, compatible spell graphs or alternative premises through a typed content interface. Keep development tools and in-game publishing separate. Loading a local data file is not permission to fetch, post or share anything. P15 owns lint/build pipeline; P9/P10 own laws and reservations.

**First slice:** import a local original cosmetic phrase/alias pack into a disposable save through a validated preview, with author/version/license metadata, then inspect one generated description and disable future use. Cost medium/high; dependencies: pack loader, P15 validation/render boundary, saved revision/copy policy. This first slice grants no script execution, rewards or world-law alteration. Functional packs later use declared operations, validation and an explicit save compatibility decision.

Store admitted content/edition or a resolvable pinned pack as appropriate; missing pack cannot erase items, reset causes or reroll saved text. Player edits apply to a new edition/instance unless they expressly revise private metadata. Escape text as text, preserve Unicode, impose storage bounds with visible feedback and retain older editions referenced by live actions. Bounds are storage constraints, not fixed once-content rules. Canon text packs need attribution/licensing decisions; raw supplied novel prose stays outside Git and never becomes a pack here.

## First acceptance and later possibilities

All future gates OPEN: safe rendering including keyboard/screen-reader names; no text-to-effect execution; actual audience/copy/teaching consumer; exact edition/provenance and retained player words through save/import; stale edits/failed saves grant no duplicate rewards; private notes and sealed truth never interpolated. Editing an old score does not replace a committed performance receipt. Local generation can be private and offline; optional export/sharing is a distinct player action.

Reachable extensions: letters to companions, public manifestos, experimental papers, journals, spell schools, self-written challenges, ceremonial names, memorials, collaborative workshops, songs that migrate through worlds and modded original dimension families. P14 retells circulation, X1/X4 carry works, C2 passes a tradition and L1 determines which copy or memory survives a reset.
