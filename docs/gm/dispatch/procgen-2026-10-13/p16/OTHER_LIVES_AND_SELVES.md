## V1: villain-side work is a practical life

Generate obligations, allies, opposition and daily work from a role's actual leverage: estate management, a rival research school, a criminal workshop, an imperial office, a monster refuge, an antagonist's supply crew. Villain is a viewpoint/relationship label, not one universal morality stat or an automatic hidden-knowledge grant. Ordinary kindness, fear, love, administration and disagreement can occur in the same life. Canon antagonists' biographies are authored; original subordinates and permitted gaps can be generated.

**First slice:** one original disputed workshop produces a paid supply request and a conflict over access. Hero can work for it, reform terms, compete, help both parties where resources allow, undermine its project, leave or return. Generate the dispute from actual scheduling/ownership and informed participants, not a randomly assigned evil adjective. Cost medium/high; dependencies: P11 N1/M5/M10, actual permissions/funds, P12 actor goals, P7 cohort. A first supported deliver/decline journey may leave other verbs as explicit future hooks; the report must not claim them playable.

Villain-side extensions: a ruler's succession, lair staffing and logistics, ordinary families inside a feared faction, a villain who retires, a failed conqueror becoming an artisan, antagonists negotiating peace, rival protagonists whose private motives differ from public legend. These are fictional game actions. No generated message instructs agents or acts on the user's computer.

## V2: non-hero lives and ensemble stories

Generate around a teacher, parent, porter, healer, librarian, artist, rooted being or collective rather than forcing every actor into the chosen-one plot. P7/P9/P10 set world-fitting eligibility; nonhuman means need actual body/reach/support. Non-hero lives can remain ordinary or become extraordinary without a mandatory rank ladder.

**First slice:** a known support worker's own finite ambition creates a funded lesson or research appointment, with the hero assisting instead of owning its success. Generate the next contact from that worker's actual progress and knowledge. Cost medium; dependencies: P4 identity join or authored actor, P11 appointments, P6 learning, P12 plans. The worker can succeed without hero intervention where their means genuinely suffice. Give useful ordinary work during that interval.

An ensemble can show several actors affected by one deed without playable switching. Each receives a separate limited account and objective. A later multi-character mode needs controller/body/native-owner handoff; multiple viewpoint paragraphs alone are not concurrent simulated lives.

## V3: distinguish earlier people from counterparts

| Identity relationship | Generated stories | Necessary boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Same living retired person | Advice, visits, changing interests, shared projects | Actual retained actor and absence policy; no invented offscreen death |
| Heir/pupil/colleague | Carrying a duty, revising a tradition, disputed expectations | Separate person, actual inheritance/teaching/appointment |
| Legacy snapshot/retelling | Reimagining a favorite build in a new premise | Label historical snapshot versus continuously living person |
| Parallel counterpart | Cooperation, envy, changed upbringing, incompatible memories | Distinct instance/actor and owned stock; no shared affection by default |
| Earlier/future self | Warning, rescue, observed habits, competing plans | Real carrier or authored premise, cutoff knowledge and paradox law |
| Clone/avatar/collective facet | Division of labor, disagreement, reunification | Supported consciousness/body/agency/custody rules |

STATED-dossier: games research distinguishes these models and gives case labels G01 onward and patterns GR. Its linked source traces and dated descriptions retain their own limits. P16 does not recertify current game builds or repeat the case catalogue. INFERRED design fit: generating a counterpart's changed preparation from one altered life fact is more concrete than making an arbitrary perfect version with no means history.

**First slice V3-A:** a separate original branch premise contains a known alternate workshop colleague who learned a different supported method. Meet, compare evidence and arrange a real exchange with separate inventories; recognition is limited to the chosen world's public law. Cost high; dependencies: instance/start binding, actual actor promotion, method/custody/knowledge owners. Not literal time travel unless a carrier says so. Meeting the actual prior player-person needs a retained trace beyond `life_record`.

## V4: imperfect, ideal and abandoned possibilities

Future selves can be helpful, mistaken, mundane, powerful or miserable, with actual premise causes and local means. Optional fantasies include playing a once-antagonistic role, revisiting a missed friendship, keeping a retired self as a neighbor, making peace with a counterpart and preserving several versions as stories. No forced dilemma that sacrifices every other reachable self. Merging conflicting identities/claims is a distinct high-cost operation, not selecting the nicest paragraph.

**First slice:** player bookmarks a known experiment and creates a labelled noncommitting what-if account about one different preparation, without admitting any new goods/person into live history. Cost medium; dependencies: authorized isolated forecast/retelling owner and clear hypothetical status. A playable alternate self requires V3's deeper gates.
