## C1: endings generated from a state, not a victory label

Compose a closure vector from real causes: personal objective, living/body state, relationships, holdings, outstanding promises, local institutions, unresolved dangers and explicit continuation availability. An achieved goal and a damaged friendship can coexist. Retirement, comfortable ordinary life, migration, negotiated peace, overwhelming victory, failed ambition and a changed vocation all deserve closure. No mandatory final boss, death, moral score or prestige conversion.

Proposed output binds scope/cause/receipt, date/instance, actual endpoint facts, known accounts, unresolved obligations, affected actors/objects, continuation policy and rendering revision. Internal outcome and each witness's epilogue are separate. Unknown offscreen outcomes remain unknown; an attractive generated ending must not retroactively save or kill a person to fit its theme. P14 renders/retells; P15 authors voices; P12 resolves the arc.

**First slice C1-A:** after one original local commission, choose a voluntary departure or continued residence. Generate a small epilogue from actual delivery, cancellation or unfinished work, and issue one supported return offer only when its real cause exists. Staying keeps ordinary work. Departure alone does not end the native life. Cost medium; dependencies: one terminal receipt, actual local issuer/stock, P14 projection or a bounded renderer. No death engine required.

Proof: completed, declined and interrupted histories produce distinct fact-bound aftermath; returning resumes actual stock and terms. Replay of the epilogue pays nothing and changes nothing. Closure receipt remains independent of pruned display logs. Existing causal closure is bounded authored groundwork, not a universal ending generator ([audit F2](LIVE_AUDIT.md)).

## C2: succession is a change of person and custody

Generate possible successors from existing willing/eligible people, family/institution rules and actual claims. Options include heir, pupil, colleague, elected replacement, unrelated purchaser, collective stewardship or no successor. A newborn genealogical candidate needs a selected birth/household system; a text label cannot create one. Appointment, inheritance, affection, title, technique understanding and access are separate transfers. The former character can remain alive and independent.

**First slice:** a living original craftsperson delegates a supported workshop role to a consenting known apprentice while keeping personal goods. Hero can continue advising, take another role or let the institution manage its own work. Generate a transition brief from the actual unfilled tasks and retained relationships. Cost high; dependencies: role/permission owner, actor/control switch if playable, P6 teaching, P11 vacancies/appointments, exact custody. An NPC appointment can precede playable viewpoint switching, but must be labelled that narrower result.

Generate successor problems from changed responsibilities: incomplete training, rival claims, inherited reputation the successor does not share, care for retired people, divided estates and compatible co-stewardship. Preserve all fitting combinations; do not force heir versus resurrection versus new start as mutually exclusive global modes.

## C3: after endings, witnesses and worlds continue

A marked finished branch can be reopened under the intro owner's proposal. Finishing a career can leave a living person, a taught school, a repaired bridge, a rival at peace and unfinished research. Generate post-ending hooks from those surviving causes. A source's final chapter is an authored canon horizon, not a license to invent its sequel as canon.

**First slice:** a closure leaves one actual correspondence agreement; a later authorized visit receives a dated follow-up from that same actor. Cost medium/high; dependencies: C1, P11 channels, explicit branch/local clock. No offscreen death or long future montage without a supported advance policy. A hypothetical long epilogue is clearly a retelling/what-if until committed as a new premise.

## C4: endings as reusable premises

An earned aftermath can inform a separately committed later start, with chosen retained facts and a source revision. A victory's new school, a failed expedition's abandoned equipment or a voluntary exile can generate a start with fitting actions. [Timeline T1-T5](../../../../TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE.md) own snapshot and branch transformations. A biography summary cannot restore a whole world.

**First slice:** one preserved local aftermath becomes an optional role-layer start in a distinct test branch. Cost high; dependencies: actual starts/snapshot owner and identity/retention decision. Generation selects compatible roles and duties, not unsupported canon resurrection. Fresh canon, continued branch, successor, replay and carried regression remain available where supported.
