# Creature-derived resources and local rights

PROPOSAL ONLY. [Audit F8-F10](LIVE_AUDIT.md): hunting loot, authored wool/cocoon activities and useful meat/pelt/bone/fat stock exist. No general carcass partition, herd production or access/consent model is proven. P4 owns actual generated people; P7 [data shape](../p7/DATA_SHAPE.md) owns their canon-aware identities. P6 owns downstream preparation/textile processes.

D1: Author supported species/cohort templates, body part roles, loot/collection laws, eligible habitat and actual consumers. Generate size/condition, permitted specimen variation and provenance. Do not randomly grant every tissue the creature's combat ability. Hunting creates a real source outcome and, only for supported families, one partitionable carcass record. Hide, cut, bone and gland claims reserve portions against that record; existing combat drops and later processing must not pay the same part twice. Quality can reflect skilled handling, not an automatic rank-to-meat-potency formula.

D2: Husbandry offers milk, wool, eggs, shed parts or secretions only where the chosen species/law permits. Keep producer, care input, collection clock, batch and access rights separate. Paid tending and purchased prepared stock remain routes alongside ownership. Breeding is delegated to the future lineage owner, not secretly added by a wool generator. A shed shell and a killed creature's organ have different causal acquisition. An individual cannot regrow its dead body's parts absent a sourced/original explicit law; new cohorts and nonlethal cycles remain reachable.

D3: Model restriction as attributed local facts: who owns the creature/site, which authority forbids collection, which institution certifies a vendor, which participant consents, and what witnessed consequences follow. Law, ethics and physical feasibility are separate. A black-market or contested harvest remains a reachable fictional route when local mechanics support it; no global morality stat veto. Nonlethal collection, husbandry, import, salvage and a lawful substitute remain visible possibilities instead of forced hunt-or-nothing. Exact identity-bound recipes may legitimately decline a substitute, with the limitation stated by that recipe's law.

Knowledge: visible trophy/verified seller differs from true species, tissue, contamination or source identity. A forged certificate is a seller claim, not changed material truth. Creature migration can invalidate a stale hunt report; a witness cannot know hidden collection without means. Inspection may identify origin but cannot certify consent/legal ownership by chemistry alone.

Idle: queued hunts/collections need actual supported encounter means and return policy; helpers consume their own availability, labor and paid funds. Husbandry boundaries spend owned feed/care and stop on shortfall with retained producer state, not surprise global livestock deaths. Save living/carcass state, parts already allocated, provenance/custody, producer readiness and contracts. Source IDs may be anonymous cohort IDs unless individual identity has a consumer.

Book variants: HP supervised magical-creature supply has exact harvesting source gates; TTKS host/infection resources retain obligations; LOTM characteristic-bearing remains conserve identity; Shadow Slave shard/part roles differ; anime spiritual tissues are not mundane carcass loot by default. [World table](WORLD_VARIATIONS.md) gives evidence strength and restrictions still unknown.

First slice R6-A: an original small herd offers a paid shear/collection job with real saved donor capacity and useful wool/fiber; a separate supported hunting acquisition yields one carcass processed into two accounted outputs. Play both across successive cycles, not a lifetime choice. G1 no combat-drop/carcass double pay, exact fractions and repeat readiness; G2 real cost → collection → P6 textile/food use, plus permission refusal and alternate supplier; G3 producer/carcass/held job/import exact; G4 legacy animal-care/drop behavior; G5 readable rights and condition. All OPEN; this is below the first five recommendations.
