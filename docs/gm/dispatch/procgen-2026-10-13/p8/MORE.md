# More resource angles, ranked

PROPOSALS, deliberately expanded for the 2026-10-14 request. Ranking orders useful observable additions and join cost; lower ranks remain reachable options. These do not replace the first playable slice. P5 [extras](../p5/EXTRA_IDEAS.md), P6 [repair/salvage](../p6/REPAIR_SALVAGE.md), P7 people and P9/P10 world profiles own their broader domains; P11 [context](../p11/CONTEXT.md) owns shared modulation and further-domain generation.

| Rank / code | Further angle and concrete suggestion | Live seam / uncertainty | Consumer and proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 / M1 | Resource scouting as a service: buy an attributed dated assay/map instead of omniscient coordinates. Retain conflicting reports; return to check one. | Audit F4/F12 searches/survey; no general protocol/claim model in those paths. | Real paid clue → accessible sample → useful refining/brew decision. Seller knowledge cannot disclose hidden truth. |

| 16 / M16 | Research-guided exploration: a tested process requests a *functional* source property, while an exact-identity ritual needs a distinct source. | P2 method and P6 pattern logic, generation feasibility P6. | Discover actual alternate eligible supplier for one functional role; prove the exact role still declines substitution without consuming goods. |

Recommendation: keep the resource ledger capable of admitting these domains through typed operations and real consumers, then dispatch them as bounded additions. “More to consider” adds options and concrete routes, not a nudge to stop or an obligation to implement every row together.
