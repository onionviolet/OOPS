# Renewal, pressure, seasons and movement

PROPOSAL ONLY. [Audit F4/F7-F9](LIVE_AUDIT.md): played-clock searches, seasons/weather, seasonal authored work and paid replantable crops exist. They do not establish general regrowth, weather-dependent yields, migration or succession. P5 [X2 weather episode](../p5/EXTRA_IDEAS.md) and future P11 context remain the episode/context owners; P8 consumes their records rather than creates another weather clock.

D1: Each site names a renewal law and local adjectives: resilient/fragile, seasonal/everbearing, sparse/abundant, slow/fast recovery, migratory/sedentary, dormant/reactivated. Author transitions and inputs; generate starting phase, capacity and legal variation. A saved herb patch can recover harvestable leaves while roots remain; a planted annual needs seed and a new cycle; exhausted mineral stock has a different supply path. No blanket once-per-life or once-per-save eligibility switch. A particular harvest receipt settles only its actual batch.

D2: Overharvest pressure softly changes encounter effort, ready capacity or supported quality in the selected family. It recovers through resting the site, rotating routes, tending, funded restoration or recolonization. Avoid compulsory daily care and global permanent collapse as default anti-abuse policy. A harsher ecology is a named per-world/variant adjective with observable causes and alternate lawful supplies; it is not inferred from a harsh franchise atmosphere. The solo player can continue ordinary play, buy imports, cultivate stock or explore elsewhere. Food remains optional useful benefits, comfortable unfed play and no hunger death.

D3: Season and weather are distinct context. Rain may change wet condition, extraction access or leaf-ready rate under explicit laws; it does not randomize a family's active fraction. Growth consumes supported inputs only at meaningful boundaries (ready, receiver-full, feed-short, dormancy). First model one patch and one weather interaction; whole biome simulation is not needed. Succession can replace depleted pioneer patches with a saved successor cohort, retaining site history and new producer IDs. Migration moves an identified herd/cohort along authored region connections with departure/arrival/carrying capacity; it never copies the same herd into two live locations. Players can follow signs, wait, hire scouts or use trade.

Knowledge: rough “picked over” impressions differ from measured standing stock; forecast is an attributed prediction, not guaranteed truth. Evidence may conflict because observation dates, seasons or observers differ. Reveal actual outcomes and revised estimates through means, not an omniscient ecological dashboard. Sealed origin/specialness uses IDs only.

Idle/persistence: route rotation runs on played world time; next renewal/migration boundary, pressure, producer/crop cycle IDs and last evaluated minute are saved. Chunked advancement must equal smaller steps at relevant boundaries and pay no duplicate yields. Disabled owner remains absent on old saves. No closed-browser growth initially; future offline policy requires a separately explicit clock/compatibility gate. Time-loop worlds select which sites/state reset via their world owner, not a universal “reseed at midnight” rule.

Book variants: HP maturity/harvest timing, MoL loop-boundary versus continuous branch, TTKS contamination ecology versus ordinary plants, LOTM identity circulation, Shadow Slave selected era/realm. Source and unknowns in [WORLD_VARIATIONS](WORLD_VARIATIONS.md); these are adaptations, not certified universal canon ecology.

First slice R3-A: two original herb patches show declining immediate readiness after concentrated gathering; rotate or tend, see recovery and harvest again. A small local growth boundary can prove season/context influence without waiting an entire year naturally. G1 boundary/chunk equivalence and nonnegative reserves; G2 natural pressure → rotate/tend → real renewed yield; G3 pending renewal/pressure exact roundtrip; G4 preserve old gathering semantics; G5 clear recovery cues. Seeded-native season transition supplements but does not replace the natural repeat loop. All OPEN.
