## Contradictions and missing support

F1: Bleach's digest explicitly says blocked/no acquired revision; the world ledger has bounded official evidence and P10 S-B/C2 points to later secondary notes. Use the latter where qualified; none certifies primary reishi extraction. This is source-route/date drift, not a contradiction in fictional physics.

F2: FMA ledger attributes incompatible Amestrian energy explanations to Alchemy r78024 and Father r78035 for manga/2009. Dragon's Pulse records May's soul-origin theory separately. Keep the unresolved alternatives explicit and keep 2003 separate. An original implementation may choose an original law, but cannot call that choice a reconciled canon fact.

F3: JJK shorthand such as one technique per body or generic once-per-fight conflicts with DJ9 exceptional storage and source-specific conditions noted by P10 C1. Use exact body/formula law, not fixed anti-abuse rules. Resource quantities never grant an unsupported technique.

F4: TTKS member keys differ from printed headings; MoL paragraph locators are captured-edition dependent; LOTM/COI targeted dossiers and ongoing Shadow Slave snapshot are bounded. A locator is not complete-series coverage. Later direct implementation must retrieve the exact selected scene, verify SHOWN/STATED/INFERRED and record contradictions in its private source owner without quoting private prose publicly.

## Further resource profiles

Spirit stones, cursed/hollow/void materials, dream substances and soul carriers remain reachable design families even when a selected canon does not yet support a particular operation. Use an original world law or explicitly labeled AU variation to test one now; then add sourced canon adapters independently. A scarcity adjective changes supply/effort, not the definition of a substance. A local unique identity can remain singular while its acquisition, theft, recovery or lawful conversion routes remain playable. Limits follow a real law, not a global one-attempt switch.
