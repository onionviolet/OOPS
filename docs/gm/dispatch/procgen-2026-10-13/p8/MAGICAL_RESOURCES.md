# Magical and exotic resource acquisition

PROPOSAL ONLY. Live support: [audit F10-F12](LIVE_AUDIT.md) has authored creature materials and survey/means seams; P6 [audit F6/F13](../p6/LIVE_AUDIT.md) describes actual artifact parts and a finite-charge MoL lamp. These do not prove ambient-energy harvesting, generic spirit-stone generation or soul extraction. [Arcane atlas](../../../../research/procedural-materials-2026-10-13/deep/ARCANE_AND_BOOK_ATLAS.md) and [world variations](WORLD_VARIATIONS.md) own inherited canon locators; P9/P10 own full profiles.

D1: Resource form determines acquisition and operations. Divisible ambient charge, a charged physical carrier, a living substrate, a pattern, a revocable grant and an exact conserved identity are different forms. Author admissible operators, power source, perception/containment requirements, useful consumer and conservation law; generate coherent site concentration/flow, carrier condition or eligible original variant inside that grammar. Do not generate copies of named unique canon objects by making their weight tiny. A powerful source need not have a downside unless its actual law requires one.

D2: Ambient harvesting accounts for inflow, collector efficiency, retained charge, loss and site response. A mana-dense site can improve collection only under a supported local law. “Spirit stone” describes an intake candidate, not a universal refillable battery. Charged stone mass, stored energy and crystal structure remain separate. Exporting it retains origin truth; destination use asks for a directional compatibility operator with real payer, conversion and residue. A raw energy name is never a conversion factor.

Cursed/hollow/void families need a declared substrate, condition, route and consumer. “Cursed” can describe imprint, energy, obligation or an observer's belief, each separately. Void may mean absence of one field, a local erasure operation or a particular substance; do not fill all interpretations at once. Dream material names its anchor and persistence after waking. Soul stock, when the world supports it, records person/cohort identity or an explicit anonymization law, custody and what splitting/consumption means. Awakening does not duplicate a dream token into both inventories. These families are original proposal slots until a world-specific sourced adapter is selected.

D3: Knowledge is acquired through actual senses, assay, ritual, contact or experiment. A mundane observer can know visible crystal mass without perceiving charge. A qualified instrument can report measured local charge without certifying foreign use or every spiritual hazard. Hidden contamination affects truth before discovery; unknown is not safe or zero. Sealed provenance remains ID/route/status only in reports, screenshots and logs. A rejected unsupported use preserves the object and provides a neutral availability message, without exposing hidden causes.

Idle: collectors, ritual attendants and expeditions require installed apparatus/means and actual energy or access. Jobs stop at containment full, source unavailable, permission lost or capability shortfall. Keep other acquisition routes reachable: purchase, salvage, commissioned capture or supported conversion. Physically singular tokens cannot pass a bulk multiplier, helper count or repeated completion to duplicate themselves. Soft pressure and restoration apply only where the site's law permits them.

Persist law/source/continuity revisions, site charge/flow, carrier quantity/charge, exact-identity custody/contained identities, grants, condition deadlines, job escrow and next boundary. Unsupported future/imported laws reject atomically or retain inactive stock under an explicit policy. No catalogue update rewrites old charge, imprints or soul identity. No generic offline collection is assumed.
