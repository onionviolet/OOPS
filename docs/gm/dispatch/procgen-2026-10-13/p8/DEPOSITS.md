# Deposits, nodes and finding them

PROPOSAL ONLY. Live support/gaps: [audit F2-F4/F12](LIVE_AUDIT.md). Existing regional ore/herb routes and survey means supply verbs; the reviewed gathering path has no persistent deposit stock. Build on generation-contract P3 and ordinary-atlas O01/O07/O11, not a new material taxonomy.

## What is generated and authored

D1: Author a region's eligible habitats, geology classes, accessibility, survey verbs, extraction laws and useful consumers. Generate a sparse list of sites per `(save root, world instance, region, resource epoch)` with stable IDs. First admission freezes resolved site facts; visits and render never place new stock. Place nodes on existing location anchors, not generated map geometry. Sites can contain ore veins, clay lenses, timber stands, herb patches, springs and tide collections with different laws.

Generate size/reserve, locally meaningful grade, composition/impurity correlation, depth/access cost and specimen heterogeneity only where a consumer reads them. One vein can have a saved zonation profile; samples inherit zone truth rather than roll unrelated chemistry. Geological class can jointly influence extraction difficulty and gangue. Richness is separate from purity, accessibility and suitability. An impure ore may be valuable for a different declared process. Guarantee a feasible ordinary supply route for every promised first-slice consumer; rare sites need no forced guaranteed location unless the chosen world law says so.

A node reserve is count/mass/local energy under its law. Plant producer capacity is not interchangeable with mineral mass. Depleting a vein does not magically regrow metal; deeper extensions, another site, salvage or trade remain possible. A world with magical replenishment declares its energy payer and renewal adjective. A world may be abundant, fragile, slowly renewed, migrating or politically restricted without a global once rule.

## Knowledge and discovery

D2: Survey records a clue and approximate area, prospecting creates an actual sample from a particular zone, assay measures scoped fractions/recovery suitability. Surface color need not reveal reserve size or all impurities. Save observer estimates, method, scope, confidence and date separately from true site state. Contradictory assays remain attributed reports until a better sample resolves the mismatch; an old correct sample may no longer describe an exhausted surface layer. “Nothing detected” means protocol-limited evidence, not absence.

Survey without a specialized tool can offer coarse reachable clues; paid expertise/skills improve information. Lack of assay does not forbid lawful trial extraction when physical data exists. Missing model data blocks unsupported spending. A known deposit need not be accessible: tenure, body, equipment and hazards are distinct means. Any sealed site specialness appears by ID/route/status only in review artifacts, and nowhere in labels/prices/seed debug text.

## Idle and persistence

D3: Queue survey → sample → return for assay, or survey only. Search effort accrues on declared played time, with cancellation and empty-location handling. Helpers can survey only after their paid means exist. Freeze placement, reserve/zone state, generator/law revisions, admission receipt, sample batch origin, knowledge and pending action. Failed admission saves neither consumed draw nor a half-site. Returning a sample to storage does not refill its source; importing it cannot recreate the reserve.

Book variants: HP cultivation sites need lifecycle/harvest conditions, MoL crystal supply needs a supported capture law, TTKS sites can hold rule-bearing carriers rather than generic ore, LOTM identities cannot be spawned as ordinary deposits. [World-law table](WORLD_VARIATIONS.md) owns locators and unknowns.

## First playable slice R1-A

Optional Village resource ledger leads to two existing-location original herb patches. Survey one, take a paid/time-costed sample, obtain a scoped assay, gather an exact P2-compatible batch, then make and use a supported product. A different save can have different patch location/condition, with ordinary fallback trade. Start with one family and two allowed habitat branches, not all ore families.

Gates, all OPEN: G1 source placement/feasibility/zone inheritance and pure previews; G2 natural survey → sample → assay → actual yield → P2 consumer with false-negative/uncertain report; G3 exact site/knowledge/batch/job/next draw roundtrip; G4 legacy and invalid-reference preservation; G5 desktop evidence. Shared gate definitions in [REPORT](REPORT.md). Later ore slice must prove refining recovery and accounted impurities rather than only different ore names.
