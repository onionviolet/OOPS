# Regional supply, merchant sourcing and imports

PROPOSAL ONLY. [Audit F5/F6](LIVE_AUDIT.md) establishes existing trader stock/refresh/margins and regional sale saturation. Add production and delivery provenance beside that owner; do not reimplement price saturation or overwrite protected campaign stock. P11 context and market/request domain proposals own broader demand/events; P8 owns embodied raw supply.

D1: Author region connections, merchant supplier eligibility, supported currencies/valuation, delivery clocks and import laws. Generate which eligible supplier/lot is used, quantities from actually available stock or an explicitly authored external-source budget, and ordinary delays. A market region is not automatically a geographical region. Build an explicit mapping. Regional scarcity is a property of accessible sources, existing demand and transport, not a hidden universal rarity multiplier. Imported goods may be abundant at origin yet scarce at a local counter.

A trader replenishment manifest reserves a supplier lot, pays actual terms and transfers it on arrival. Template refresh remains legacy behavior outside opt-in supply-managed groups. A physical generated lot cannot also be recreated by legacy refill. If external suppliers are abstract, declare that abstraction and capacity rather than invent fully simulated villages. Preserve distinct custody for merchant, warehouse, carrier and player; same item held twice is not trade.

D2: Price response has separate inputs: existing sell saturation, available stock, declared demand, delivery cost, seller knowledge and negotiated margin. Start with one supply group and a bounded smooth response with a reachable baseline supplier. Buying out the stall can change its next quote/delivery, but it does not reveal hidden potency through price or create a universal prohibition on resale. Profit remains possible when costs and differing markets justify it. A quote freezes price/quantity, expiration and issuer means at reservation; stale quotes revalidate before spending. Never apply stock scarcity twice through both new logic and saturation without an explicit joint formula.

D3: World imports retain material identity, condition, law and provenance. A destination can recognize ordinary physical functions while refusing an unsupported energy role; another role may work through a paid adapter. Matching “mana,” “blood,” “soul” or names does not grant compatibility. An inert imported stone remains owned; lawful decoration, resale, research or return can still be useful. Customs/embargo/black-market routes follow actual jurisdiction and witnesses, not a blanket no-import flag. Every reasonable alternate source remains reachable under its own means.

Knowledge: merchant claims, invoices, certificates and actual assays are separate. Public supply notices can explain delays; undiscovered producer origins and sealed traits do not appear in pricing keys/logs. A counterfeit invoice can be believed while material truth stays unchanged. [WORLD_VARIATIONS](WORLD_VARIATIONS.md) covers characteristic custody, spiritual substrate and containment differences, with exact rules still qualified.

Idle/persistence: queue funded standing orders, with max spend/receiver/allowed substitution and stop policy. Deliveries cross played boundaries, not refresh-on-open. Save source lot references, supplier budget, manifest, quotes, paid escrow, due boundary, merchant stock and relevant price state. Failed durable settlement leaves all these plus RNG unchanged. Imports/export preserve whole reference graph; missing external packs cannot silently reroll a replacement supplier.

First slice R5-A: one original herb supplier and one merchant receive two real consignments from different source regions. Buy a sourced batch, deliberately change local supply through paid purchases/delivery, observe a declared quote response and make a P2 product. Direct gather/order remain alternatives. G1 physical-stock conservation, no double scarcity, stale quote and refresh bypass controls; G2 actual buy → supply change → arrival → revised quote/use; G3 source/manifest/vendor/batch/price/import exact; G4 legacy traders and conserved campaign keys preserved; G5 paired stock/quote/source display. All OPEN. Cross-world import is a later separate adapter test, not proved by two Frontier regions.
