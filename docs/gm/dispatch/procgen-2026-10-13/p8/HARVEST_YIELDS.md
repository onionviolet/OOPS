# Harvest technique, parts, quality and byproducts

PROPOSAL ONLY. [Audit F1-F3/F8-F10](LIVE_AUDIT.md): skill changes native authored yield/time/quality, tool presence gates many activities, and logs/sap already co-drop. No reviewed path computes a selected plant part, cutting damage or a partitioned harvested batch. Living-atlas L01-L05/L11-L16 and P6 [creation audit](../p6/LIVE_AUDIT.md) are the material/process owners.

D1: Author a small set of supported operations per family: pick leaves, lift roots, tap sap, fell timber, shear, fish, collect sheds. Generate source condition, part inventory and batch variation. Resolve each harvest using actual tool function/condition, skill, chosen technique, season/stage and selected part. Skill can improve recovery, time or damage control along declared axes; it does not turn leaf stock into root stock, reveal hidden truth automatically or replace a missing tool. A simple functional tool and manual method remain reachable where the world permits; advanced methods add options rather than replacing all others.

D2: Keep grade, useful fractions, bruising/moisture, intact geometry and workmanship distinct. “Gentle leaf picking” can yield less immediate stock and preserve producer capacity; “whole-root lift” changes that producer's remaining state and pays actual root stock. Neither locks all future root or leaf choices across the save. Tapping sap is a separate operation from felling; current co-drops need an explicit adapter before claiming that distinction. Timing can change an eligible part's condition or quantity but must not reroll species chemistry each morning.

A concrete fictional partition law can spend 10 harvest units and yield 6 selected-part units, 3 fiber/residue units and 1 recorded loss. This is a demonstration, not canon or real botanical arithmetic. Wet mass and dry active fractions require a declared basis; greater water content is not greater potency. Byproducts need an actual receiver and selected P6 consumer (cord, compost, dye or fuel), with an explicit discard policy for valueless fractions. Cutting damages intact geometry; a powder consumer can still use the retained composition. A better sickle cannot independently boost every material effect.

D3: Truth determines actual recovery and processing even when hidden. Observer reports can show tool marks, intact/bruised leaves and weighed quantity; assays reveal only their protocol's measured property. P2 receives complete material truth through its owner, while the UI receives only known observations. Preserve sampling scope: one inspected leaf cannot certify a mixed bundle or unseen gland. P5 [appearance shuffle](../p5/O3_APPEARANCE_SHUFFLE.md) owns vial appearances; P8 does not roll competing herb/potion identification maps.

Idle: saved harvest plans choose part, technique and explicit substitute policy, then admit each batch against current source/tool/receiver. Queue “collect leaves until target, return with overflow” without player clicking each plant. Long jobs freeze admitted terms or define revalidation at stage boundaries; weather changes cannot silently replace paid inputs. Persist source part depletion, reserved stock/tool, resulting fractions/condition, byproduct destinations, time and knowledge receipt. Split/restack/reload preserves every distinction with gameplay meaning.

Book variants: HP harvest timing and Mandrake maturity can matter under an exact recipe; MoL fuel crystals cannot be harvested through generic plant logic; TTKS affinity/imprint is not leaf chemistry; Shadow Slave intact anchors do not multiply when cut. See [WORLD_VARIATIONS](WORLD_VARIATIONS.md).

First slice R2-A: one original P2 ingredient supports leaf and root operations plus two methods, each with one useful distinct consumer. Harvest both sequentially through a reachable new/recovered producer; compare actual preparation result and one useful byproduct. All OPEN: G1 quantity accounting, actual tool reads and coherent inherited truth; G2 paid native harvesting/consumer plus missing-tool/refusal/cancel; G3 split/storage/reservation roundtrip; G4 old template behavior; G5 desktop selection and uncertainty. No preference is permanently forced.
