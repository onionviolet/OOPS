# Routes, helpers, farms and expeditions

PROPOSAL ONLY. [Audit F2/F4/F9/F12](LIVE_AUDIT.md): repeat gathering, idle search, paid crop stages and expeditions exist. P2's branch owns a learned-method repeat job, P4 has travelers' saved supplies/requests, and P3 supplies storylets. None of those claims alone establishes helper labor, multi-site routes or an automated farm. Use their owners via [REPORT dependencies](REPORT.md), not duplicated scheduler/population stores.

D1: Author route operators (travel, survey, gather selected part, return, store, preserve), capacity and supported absence law. Generate eligible destinations/preferences from actual discovered sites and paid means; a route cannot use a secret site just because it exists internally. Player may choose manual gathering, an authored route, commissioned supply or a learned plan. Automation can be ordinary hired labor, infrastructure or an earned capability per world; it is not automatically a universal cheat and need not require a global Frontier prerequisite.

D2: A queue is an intention, not promised inventory. Admit each stage against current ownership, target reserve, tool/body/site support and receiver; reserve exactly paid means. Save a stage receipt, frozen terms, completion boundary and cancel/return policy. Resolve a boundary atomically with actual transfers, XP and source changes. An unavailable target can pause, return or select an explicitly allowed known substitute. Report real completed stages, not the expected value of hypothetical future rolls. Receiving full stops or returns according to the chosen policy; it never discards valuable stock implicitly.

Helpers/hirelings are actual distinct participants with labor/availability/capabilities, wage contract and custody. P4 traits can influence preferences only through their actor bridge and known relationship; a displayed person cannot be copied into a new helper owner. Wages and gathered stock come from actual owners; one helper cannot simultaneously work two incompatible routes. Renewing contracts and hiring more lawful helpers stay reachable. A prototype concurrency bound is a performance or installed-capacity choice, not “once per save.”

D3: An automated farm uses an installed controller, plot tenure, real seeds/substrate, care input and receiver. Growth is still the producer law; UI automation never multiplies mass. Expeditions can gather along surveyed paths, cache/return stock and use risk/abort thresholds. Authored encounters and P1 snapshots remain combat authority. Do not invent player death while absent. Bought results and stocked farms remain alternates when a player lacks travel means. Expand scale later through real infrastructure/helpers rather than repetitive clicks.

Knowledge: route previews use known estimates and observed access; completion records what happened and new observations. NPC plans use their knowledge rather than the player's hidden truth. Private coordinates/truth never enter a generic reason string. A failed trip can return a useful clue and remaining supplies where its law supports that, without granting imagined yield.

Persistence: saved route/stage/participants, labor reservations/wages, site and inventory revisions, exact cargo/source receipts, renewal boundaries and relevant draws form one transaction. Reload cannot replay a settled stage or auto-admit historical work. First clock is played world time with closed-browser pause. A future offline option needs equivalence across weather, shortages, returns and deadlines, plus an explicit old-save absence policy. MoL loop/reset policy, HP hazardous handling, TTKS obligations and LOTM containment all remain per-world profile laws in [WORLD_VARIATIONS](WORLD_VARIATIONS.md).

First slice R9-A: queue a known two-patch herb route with target amount and return receiver. It travels, gathers against actual reserve, returns and feeds a learned P2 method; hire one funded helper for a second run once its actor bridge exists. G1 boundary/chunk equivalence, mutually exclusive labor, escrow and no hidden-site routing; G2 natural travel → real yield → return → useful product, shortage/receiver-full/cancel and later paid helper control; G3 mid-travel/mid-harvest/settled/next admission exact roundtrip; G4 existing activities/expeditions preserved; G5 route stage, cargo and interruption visible at desktop viewports. All OPEN; manual first route can precede helper integration without claiming the helper gate passed.
