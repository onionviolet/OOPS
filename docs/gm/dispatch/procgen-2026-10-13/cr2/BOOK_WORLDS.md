# CR2 book crafting laws

2026-10-14. **PROPOSALS ONLY.** Extends root [WL1-WL3/WL6](../../../../PROCEDURAL_CRAFTING_AND_CONSUMABLES_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md), rather than replacing [P9 profiles](../p9/REPORT.md). Credits and edition manifests follow [P9 source map](../p9/SOURCE_MAP.md). Summaries with locators only; no raw prose or private-note quotations. An inherited **shown** label means the cited reader reported a demonstrated result, not that CR2 reread the novel. **Stated** retains speaker/rule authority; **inferred** means interpretation or proposed adaptation; **unknown** records missing evidence. All resolver choices, new formulas, quantities, UI and gates below are inferred proposals, regardless of the underlying canon evidence.

## WL adapter vocabulary

C = composition; E = exact identity/provenance; B = abstract material budget and work; P = executable pattern/procedure. They can combine within one product. Parts/interfaces are an additional representation from [P6](../p6/REPORT.md), not an ingredient fraction. These are desired P2 adapter modes, **not four implemented P2 modes**: [source audit](REPORT.md#p2-source-audit) verifies only Village C with fixed ingredient IDs and two processes. P denotes ordered HP preparation as well as graphs, but it does not claim HP potions are rune enchantments. B never means gold can replace any exact reagent.

For each selected law, preserve world/continuity/era, source claim IDs, law revision, actual maker/body/source, typed inputs and custody, declared output/use, conserved domains and observation policy. Unknown canon quantities remain unknown. A playable original approximation gets a distinct variant ID and an explicit label. Named artifacts/formulas stay canon-fixed; their custody and use can change through qualified routes. Canon reservation is not a permanent prohibition on acquisition or a sourced duplication exception.

## WL5a Witcher-like reference and coverage boundary

WL5 names a **reference family**, not an existing `docs/worlds/` Witcher dossier. Proposed C+E/body chemistry + P known oils/decoctions, with toxicity/route specifics delegated to [CR1](../CR1.md). Toxicity cap, mutagens and monster oils in the root palette are explicitly unverified reference knowledge; no new canon assertion here. Keep an original toxicity-based world reachable under a public law; a direct Witcher adapter needs its edition and primary rules. It is not ranked ahead of accepted canon priorities.

Reincarnation Paradise belongs to the source hub, not a generic original shop world: [hub/source distinction](ORIGINAL_WORLDS.md#source-hub-and-reference-game-boundaries). All other actual world folders are covered there or in [ANIME_WORLDS](ANIME_WORLDS.md). Unknown recipe evidence is a qualification task, not a forced exclusion from future play.
