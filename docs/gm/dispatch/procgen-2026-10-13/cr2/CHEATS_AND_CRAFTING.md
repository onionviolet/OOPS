# CR2 cheats, conservation and observation

2026-10-14. **PROPOSALS ONLY.** Extends root [CT1-CT6](../../../../PROCEDURAL_CRAFTING_AND_CONSUMABLES_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md), [CHEAT_LIBRARY](../../../../CHEAT_LIBRARY.md) family cards, growth/interactions/observers, and the [world matrices](BOOK_WORLDS.md). Canon rules and cheat adjectives are independent: a foreign cheat can preserve, translate, temporarily bypass or permanently rewrite a selected law if its authored scope actually supports that. Stronger and cheaper variants remain reachable. No automatic balancing tax, global once limit or mandatory counter is introduced.

## CT7 source interoperability and override order

**CT7a proposed evaluation:** qualify world and cheat revisions → read actual input/source/recipient state → apply declared overrides with scope/precedence → resolve craft → account outputs, source-created stock and remaining obligations → project observation through permitted routes → publish together. A passive appraisal does not accidentally alter material; a perfect-craft correction does not silently rewrite recipe knowledge; a fusion does not simultaneously leave all consumed parents intact. If two law rewrites conflict, retain both choices through separately valid variants or explicit composition rules, not arbitrary hidden priority. This is a proposed contract, not an existing interpreter.

**CT7b original trial:** ordinary brew, perfect execution, and explicit duplicating variant can all be useful on the same saved stock. Observe exactly what differs: uncertainty, yield/grade, source cost or produced count. None needs an invented compensating penalty to be reachable or worthwhile. An abundant variant can shift decisions to compatibility, application, logistics or social use.

**CT7c absorption-wave boundary:** [absorb-all COMMON](../../absorb-all-2026-10-14/COMMON.md) authorizes source/fork/Proto23/Air adaptation in separate implementation worktrees, root merges/pushes; it does **not** implement CT5 biological absorption. Read its rules and [Air re-audit](../../absorb-all-2026-10-14/reports/air-reaudit.md), discovery gates and distinct attempted settlement: reuse actual capability/control/knowledge/rechecks and durable stock transactions after integration. No copied foreign code/assets where license absent. No authority from that wave expands CR2's write scope or allows a merge/build/commit here.
