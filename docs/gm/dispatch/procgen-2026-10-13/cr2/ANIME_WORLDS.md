## Crafting laws are not faction labels

**WL7k:** Each recipe tests actual means: an engineer can make a carrier, a trained caster can energize it, and a customer can own the finished product without owning either faculty. A material, a pattern, the caster and the institution can contribute together. Assisted craft, commission, found prepared stock, study, salvage and later extraordinary source acquisition remain reachable. No compulsory faction choice or fixed once rule follows from these source gaps.

**WL4b:** For any exceptional manufacture that creates matter, record its explicit source domain and observation limits. FMA cannot pay exchange with equal shop price; JJK Construction cannot be declared mass-conserving by borrowing FMA; HxH Conjuration cannot be made permanent by borrowing JJK. [Cheat matrix](CHEATS_AND_CRAFTING.md) specifies what an intentional override must declare.

No primary manga frames or anime footage were inspected here. Official prose pages are stated-official evidence, even when they point to shown scenes. Future canon proof binds the selected scene, not the entire franchise. Numeric original experiments remain labeled original, and public franchise twists are separate from sealed changed truths.
