2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** Extend [root AV1](../../../../PROCEDURAL_STORY_AND_CONTENT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md#av-environmental-storytelling). [P20 RESIDUE](../p20/RESIDUE.md) owns formation and clearing laws, [P14 CONTRACT](../p14/CONTRACT.md) owns retained outcomes and claims, and [P24 INFORMATION](../p24/INFORMATION.md) owns evidence questions and checks. P35 adds a readable scene projection and clue-to-evidence navigation. It creates neither topology nor a second physical aftermath resolver.

## AV1.1: receipt to scene, then observation to interpretation

Proposed flow: actual committed outcome → domain-owned surviving object/condition → world-qualified residue transition → observer's accessible clues → original scene description → optional evidence/action links. A receipt records what happened; the current domain owner determines what still remains. A burned field cannot still be visibly burning after its fire has ended merely because a past receipt says it burned.

Logical bindings, not a selected schema or exported API:

```text
SceneBinding
  world/instance/branch/place refs, producer outcome refs,
  actual survivor refs, residue law/revision, state transition refs,
  authored scene grammar/revision, safe observation protocol refs,
  actor-specific knowledge/evidence refs, coverage interval

ClueObservation
  observer, survivor, observedAt, observed condition/signature,
  protocol/means, basis, uncertainty, safe view handle,
  related known claims, optional supported investigation/action refs
```

Generate physical aftermath only as part of an authorized domain transaction. Render chooses wording and composition from that already settled state; it never damages a cart, consumes stock, creates a corpse, teaches a fact or advances a random stream. Decorative variations can use a stable presentation binding, isolated from gameplay draws. Materially relevant choices are committed and saved with their producer. A scene that lacks enough retained cause data stays a present observation with an unknown origin, rather than receiving an invented earlier battle.

## AV1.2: five concrete scene grammars

All examples are original fixtures and proposed rules. SHOWN means something the observer can perceive; STATED means an attributed account; INFERRED means an interpretation with its supporting observations. These are independent of author-level true/false assessment.

| Scene | Required real past and surviving state | Readable clues and uncertainty | Reachable uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abandoned camp | Actual occupation and departure receipts at an authored site; retained ash, tent marks or left object under its local law | SHOWN cold ashes and flattened grass; STATED traveler's dated departure account; INFERRED camp occupied recently only if the declared weather/aging protocol supports that range | Inspect, compare a known visit, ask a witness, document, clean or simply pass. Ashes do not prove flight, starvation, death or hostile intent. |
| Broken cart | Actual damage/incident outcome and a still-damaged axle/cart instance with custody | SHOWN split axle and uneven track; STATED driver's explanation; INFERRED overload or attack only through a fitting diagnostic rule | Repair, salvage with actual rights/amounts, hire help, examine or use an existing alternate route. Scenery grants no free cargo. |
| Burned field | Actual fire outcome, affected plot and current burned/recovering state | SHOWN charred rows; STATED farmer's account; INFERRED deliberate burning remains a hypothesis unless evidence establishes it | Study cause, help recovery, harvest only supported remaining stock, commission aid or leave. Food remains optional; this cannot create a hunger-death gate. |
| Grave or memorial | Qualified death/burial or explicit memorial-construction outcome, actual marker and known attribution | SHOWN marker and inscription; STATED inscription's claim; INFERRED remains/identity may be unverified. Missing body, fainting and departure are distinct | Read, commemorate, ask a known mourner, restore the marker or continue. Never create a dead person or ghost from battle defeat or the marker's wording. |
| Repaired wall | Actual damaged-state and joint repair receipts; current repaired structure; retained materials/join style when observable | SHOWN newer stones and visible joint; STATED repairer's credit; INFERRED technique or maker signature needs real expertise and supporting evidence | Inspect history, compare credit, maintain or use the restored access/work function. Reopening the scene does not pay the repair again. |

A clue can be quiet and useful without becoming a quest. Offer independent physical inspection, testimony, dated records, expert assistance or exceptional perception when local means support them. Inspection can cost time/sample/tool use where that protocol says so; ordinary reading can remain free. Do not force a single skill, predetermined suspect, dialogue sequence or either/or route. Repairing does not require solving the historical dispute.

## AV1.4: history survives cleanup without freezing the present

Clearing ash, repairing a cart or restoring a field writes a new domain outcome. Preserve prior receipt and dated observations; update current scene through the new state. Different visitors can know different causes. An inaccessible trace is unavailable now, not evidence it never existed. Missing retention coverage is unknown. Pin the evidence needed by ongoing investigation or commitment before P14 compaction; routine aggregates cannot answer an exact axle question whose record was discarded.

P8 owns sampling, recoverable quantities and custody. P6 owns repair/salvage and buildings. [CR4 maker histories](../cr4/ITEMS_OVER_TIME.md) owns provenance-sensitive object changes; [CR1 body response](../cr1/BODY_AND_SPECIES.md) owns actual exposure. P35 cannot silently convert a visual spill into an oral potion, allergy, toxic workplace or NPC behavior. Extend root **RX4/RS3** only as an aftermath view over an implemented contamination owner, and **MK1/MK2/IT4** only through known maker/lasting-property evidence.
