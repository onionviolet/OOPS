## AW1.1: an entry begins at actual contact

Create a known-context projection after encounter, sighting, received claim, qualified study, supported appraisal or actual experiment. A purchased book can provide attributed assertions; it cannot certify them or grant unsupported mastery. A person entry can start with a known alias while actual identity remains unresolved. Two identical names never establish the same actor. Companions, factions, places, creatures, techniques, objects and methods use the owning qualified IDs internally.

Proposed entry binding: subject ref, scope/world/branch/life, observer lens, known safe labels, encountered/learned times, visible claim/observation refs, source lineage, coverage, supported current-action refs and player annotation refs. It indexes P14/P11 evidence instead of copying a second belief store. Known names and summaries can be cached by knowledge revision; invalidating a cache cannot discover hidden records.

Compile in order: filter by disclosure → bind known subject labels → collect that observer's evidence → distinguish observation/account/inference → compute compatible scopes and contradictions → render original summary → calculate visible search/facets/counts. An internal truth assessment is not a player-facing verdict. A secret's route may grant a precise disclosure; merely making an entry or opening a tab does not.

## AW1.2: readable sources and contradictions

| Entry section | Proposed visible content | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| Known so far | Concise summary with a basis on each factual claim | Only the observer's supported scope, not all author truth |
| SHOWN | Dated direct observation, experiment or demonstrated action; protocol/body/context | The recorded result under that protocol; one test does not identify an entire family |
| STATED | Attributed witness, edition, teacher, inscription or official source; original lineage | That the observer received this statement, not that it is true |
| INFERRED | Player/character hypothesis or source-qualified inference; supporting evidence and scope | A proposed explanation; confidence does not turn it into observation |
| Disputed / revised | Incompatible propositions with time, edition, scope and visible evidence; later correction | A conflict or dated change. Absence of evidence is not disproof |
| Current use | Real inspect/ask/study/test/craft/travel controls from their owners | Actual present availability, not a generated power or stock grant |

Contradiction relation references predicate, subjects, claimed time/interval, scope and incompatible values. A repaired bridge and an earlier broken bridge are a timeline, not contradictory claims. Two witnesses crediting different people can conflict about sole credit while agreeing on completion. A book about another body/era can be valid there and untested here. Preserve original editions and copied source chains; ten copies are not independent corroboration. Player annotations cannot rewrite material truth or grant NPC knowledge.

Worked original entry: a place has SHOWN newer wall joints, STATED two different accounts of who repaired it, and a dated joint-repair receipt acquired through a legitimate record route. The entry can state who the receipt credits while preserving each older account. It cannot infer deceit, motive, friendship or universal trust. Corrections reach NPCs only through P11's actual channels.
