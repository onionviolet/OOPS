# P23 moral texture: R1–R3 extensions

2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** Extends [root R1–R3](../../../../PROCEDURAL_STORY_AND_CONTENT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md#r-moral-texture). [P21](../p21/) owns conflict/economic causes, [P19](../p19/) social propagation, [P14](../p14/) accounts and [P12](../p12/) arcs. P23 adds motivation, interpretation and change over time. Everything below is original proposal unless separately labeled SOURCE.

## R1a: dilemmas come from real interests and constraints

A dilemma generator first binds actual affected actors, incompatible present commitments or uncertain consequences, and several feasible actions. It then offers original text from what the deciding actor knows. There need not be a globally best option, but a clean solution may exist if the player has sufficient means. Do not remove that solution to preserve drama.

Separate known immediate costs, uncertain future effects, obligations actually accepted and parties who might hear about the deed. A delayed consequence requires an existing causal mechanism: a missed delivery, a retained debt, a report carried to an issuer, an injured person's unmet care need. A narrator's judgment cannot substitute for it. Delays follow played/local time and supported channels; leave real-world absence effects unselected.

Original case: a workshop owns one compatible beam. It has an accepted paid customer booking, and a nearby group requests aid for a known damaged shelter. Reasonable approaches include fulfilling the booking; seeking the customer's consent to defer; buying compatible stock; borrowing from a willing owner; arranging another repair method; sharing labor; or declining involvement. Taking the beam without permission can remain a supported illicit route with actual custody/enforcement consequences. No invented guilt penalty follows from refusing an unaccepted request.

If the player can conjure a compatible beam through an actual source, both repairs may succeed. If a delay prevents real harm, record that success. Do not replace the rescued victim or invent fresh scarcity to make generosity costly. [COSMIC_EVENTS](../../../../COSMIC_EVENTS.md#consequences-relative-to-capability) supports capability-relative consequences; it does not require an omnipotent punishment for every act.

## R1b: retained cause to delayed response

```text
deed (domain owner): actors, actual transfer/action, time, effects, cause
intention: player-declared or actor-known motive, otherwise unknown
account (P14): speaker, recipients, observed basis, source-chain, learnedAt
judgment: assessor + norm/source/version + known evidence + interpretation
response (P19/P21/P12): actual willing actor + means + lawful/supported action
```

Store judgment separately from truth. A faction may praise a false story if it actually believes it; the underlying rescue remains unperformed. A corrected account may revise future recognition without undoing genuine paid work. Confidential acts produce no public reputation until a real witness, report or power supplies reach. Delayed consequences can be positive, mixed, absent, averted or unresolved. Queue only responses with actual causes and means.

## R3a: same deed, different readings

| Assessor | Possible reading of the beam case | Distinct actual consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | The promise was fulfilled, renegotiated, or broken | Pays, accepts revised terms, withholds under agreed terms or seeks actual remedy. |
| Shelter residents | Useful aid, inadequate aid or no involvement | Remember delivery, offer collaboration or still need another supplier. Gratitude is not automatic lifelong loyalty. |
| Workshop members | Sound management, generosity, favoritism or unlawful use | Negotiate scheduling/control using their real authority. Membership alone grants no confiscation power. |
| Local institution | Permitted repair, contract breach or property dispute | Investigation/enforcement only through a source-qualified jurisdiction, evidence and available actors. Written law and enforcement differ. |
| Player-character | A chosen value expressed, compromised or revised | Optional reflection under M1; no universal guilt or virtue score. |

Each group can disagree internally. Culture, religion, faction and wealth are not deterministic personality labels. Personal gratitude, legal status, popularity, fear, competence and supernatural corruption remain different variables. A private good intention does not automatically exonerate visible damage; visible damage does not establish cruelty. Intent may be stated by the actor, inferred by a witness or unknown. Preserve those evidence labels.

Faction norms may be contested, opportunistic or mistaken. Call a response an attributed judgment rather than a moral fact. Original no-karma worlds and explicitly cosmically graded worlds can coexist; a supernatural grader needs its own source-qualified assessor/reach and grant law through [P17](../p17/CONTRACT.md). It is not a default hidden karma bar.

## R2a: redemption and corruption are patterns with choices

P12 sifts repeated actual causes; P23 proposes interpretive labels and possible next opportunities. A redemption interpretation might require acknowledged specific harm, an attempt at restitution, changed conduct under a later relevant opportunity and an affected person's response. None automatically guarantees forgiveness. Reparation may be refused; another actor may forgive without payment; a personal decision to change can be meaningful before public acceptance.

A corruption interpretation might involve repeated chosen coercion, concealment, instrumental use of others or accepted harmful bargains. Keep each act's context and opposition. Do not equate power gain, forbidden study, anger, a feared body or faction disapproval with moral decline. **Moral corruption** and **supernatural contamination** can exist independently. An NPC can see a villain where the player sees a reformer, without the generator declaring either account final.

Evidence pattern, not a prescribed integer meter:

```text
harm/commitment receipt → actor's acknowledged or disputed interpretation
→ feasible chance to respond → actual repair/repetition/restraint choice
→ affected actor's known response → optional sifted arc assessment
```

A repeated paid donation is a genuine action; do not dismiss it as farming without a world law. It also cannot erase an unrelated assault merely because a total score rose. A new harm does not delete earlier repair. Copying the same receipt or reloading cannot issue another settlement; later distinct acts can qualify again. Rule revisions preserve assessment/grant lineage rather than paying for the same deed again under a new version.

## R2b: reachable directions beyond a binary alignment

Keep reform, defiance, deception, reconciliation, restitution, escape, institutional change, villain play, indifference and quiet retirement reachable through fitting means. An arc can reverse, recur or remain unfinished. The player need not accept a label to choose a different next act. An NPC's change requires actual opportunity, ability and decision; a heartfelt generated speech alone cannot restore destroyed property or command another actor's forgiveness.

Idle delegation records `explicit choice`, `selected policy`, `NPC initiative` or `safe expiry`. A missed card is not evidence of a deliberate refusal unless its accepted terms establish that meaning. Automated conduct should not secretly shift the player's moral identity. [E2 §7](../../../../emergent/E2_STORYLETS.md#7-urgency-tiers-standing-orders-and-the-character-acts) is an earlier proposal; current [identity K8/K10](../../../../worlds/PLAYER_CHARACTER_IDENTITY_AND_CONTROL_2026-10-13.md) governs control. Harsh or coercive settings can have real local consequences, but require the selected premise and actual enforcement, not automatic thought policing.

Future gates: identical deeds known to different audiences produce distinct attributed readings; no omniscient reaction; absent witness does not imply guilt; late correction changes a supported response; strong success may resolve the dilemma cleanly; no fake moral XP from text/timeout/reload. Persist cause, accepted liability, audience reach and response commitments across Save/reload/Export/literal-file Import. All OPEN. [REPORT](REPORT.md) retains the implementation boundary.
