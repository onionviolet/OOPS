## N1a: a board of intentions, not compulsory quests

A goal can come from a player premise, an authored background, a known need, an enjoyed activity, a promise actually accepted, or an NPC's own motives. Keep origin and owner visible where known. A generated suggestion can remain unpinned, pinned, active, paused, revised, satisfied or retired. Retiring a personal aspiration is not failure to honor an external contract. Accepted contracts keep their actual terms until separately renegotiated/cancelled.

Offer a board card with the desired state, why it was suggested, current known means, one useful next step and alternative routes. Let the player add a broad goal or a precise endpoint, edit its meaning and pin several overlapping goals. A personal text note can remain a note; map it to an executable predicate only after a supported interpretation is selected. Do not pretend arbitrary text is a solvable plan.

```text
AmbitionView@proposal:
  goalRef, ownerActorRef, origin, motivationCauseRefs, playerPriority
  desiredPredicateRef, scope/world/instance/life, knownAssessment
  threadRefs (P12), acceptedCommitmentRefs (domain owners)
  routeCandidates, selectedRoute?, delegationPolicyRef?, status
  revision, previousGoalRef?, completionCauseRefs
```

This is a semantic join, not a new persistent copy of quest progress, stock, skills or faction offices. The ambition may name an outcome beyond present means. Distinguish attainable now, a known missing prerequisite, uncertain route and unsupported current model. Unknown is not impossible. A preview does not spawn a shop, reserve an apprentice seat or draw an event.

## N1b: generating steps from actual causes and means

P12 owns operator composition. Proposed ambition admission sequence:

1. Resolve the selected goal's world, era, instance and actual target predicate. Read only the context fields it needs through [P11](../p11/CONTEXT.md).
2. Enumerate source-legal operators and real opportunities. Bind actual actors, stock, instruction, sites, time and willingness. Distinguish a plan believed feasible from an executable step.
3. Offer useful routes and their known terms. A named draw may select a concrete opportunity among eligible ones; freeze it only when the opportunity is admitted. Player priority is not random.
4. Admit one chosen step through its domain owner, using actual reservations and publication. Completion receipts update the predicate; failure or changed causes re-evaluate the plan rather than manufacture success.
5. Preserve achieved subgoals and route history when replanning, changing ambition or succeeding early. Do not add extra obstacles to force a three-act arc.

Partial-order steps allow learning, funding and procurement in different orders. Shared subgoals satisfy several ambitions using the same real receipt where appropriate; they do not duplicate the reward. A supported powerful action can finish a goal immediately. New goals can follow success without automatic escalation or compulsory prestige.

## N2a: NPC ambitions collide because means overlap

[P19](../p19/) owns relationships, contact and social response. P12 owns the NPC plan. P23 supplies motivation and priority changes from that actor's experience. NPCs read their own knowledge, values and means; their private goal is not automatically shown on the board. Known statements can be sincere, incomplete or deceptive. A reported ambition needs speaker/basis/time, not an omniscient badge.

Original collision: two admitted apprentices want the same actual morning bench appointment. Both may be conscientious. They can alternate shifts, cooperate on a joint task, seek another instructor, compete under known rules, buy additional capacity or abandon the appointment. Reserve the real time slot when a booking commits, not when an ambition is suggested. No quest holds an NPC's entire life. Opposition can arise without declaring either person villainous.

Generated escalation reads changes in actual means: missed supply, a new patron, remembered unfair treatment or a revised aspiration. It does not spawn a rival on every player level-up. A powerful player can add capacity and resolve the conflict for both. Other friction may persist only if there is another real cause. NPCs may form coalitions, exchange favors, pursue compatible goals or remain indifferent.

An NPC's ambition persists independently of player acceptance. The main source's authored priority controllers can demonstrate one simple plan; P4 travelers need a real identity/controller bridge before they can execute arbitrary plans. Do not interpret hourly browsing on P4's branch as a complete ambition solver.

## N1d–N2b: progress, delegation and closure

Player-selected standing policy may queue registered safe work under stated money/item/time limits, methods and stopping conditions. A pinned ambition alone never authorizes a vow, sacrifice, purchase or attack. A suggested next step is advisory. Offscreen advancement follows the selected local clock; this packet adds no closed-browser history. A world can offer generous, effortless or exceptional means through an actual law without a universal cost rule.

Renegotiation can replace the desired state while retaining completed work. A lost career can lead to teaching, reform or retirement; it need not be treated as a corrupted identity. Nonhuman actors can want a compatible wielder, territory, contact or a repaired vessel through body-specific operators; [P24](../p24/) and the nonhuman-start owners qualify those means. Cross-life goals survive only through [P16](../p16/) carrier policy, not shared display names.
