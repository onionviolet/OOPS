## M1a: experience proposes an interpretation, the player chooses a commitment

Separate five things: what happened, what this actor perceived, their attributed interpretation, a preference they endorse, and an instruction that actually authorizes action. Betrayal can suggest caution toward a particular partner; it does not establish that all strangers are untrustworthy. Receiving kindness can suggest gratitude, relief, obligation or skepticism. None is the uniquely correct response.

Per-world controls can compose: texture with no mechanical drift; visible suggested drift requiring endorsement; bounded automatic drift within player-selected topics; fixed player-authored values; and an explicit host/controller disagreement premise. Direct player input wins under ordinary control. Disabling drift stops future updates; it does not silently erase earned skills, past choices or other actors' memories. Adoption, revision, postponement and dismissal are practical controls, not mandatory dialogue every time something happens. No hidden habit counter may turn a default timeout into an ideology.

Proposed issue-scoped record, not an existing API or mandatory save schema:

```text
interpretation: actor + current life/carrier + world/instance + issue/target
  propositionRef, stance, confidence, importance, state
  observedCauseRefs, supportingAccountRefs, counterevidenceRefs
  origin: background | playerAuthored | suggested | delegatedHabit | exceptionalInfluence
  occurredAt, learnedAt, proposedAt, endorsedAt?, ruleRevision, projectionPolicy
```

Confidence concerns an assessment, not moral worth. Importance concerns how much the person cares. Endorsement is not consent to disclose the belief, spend money or surrender control. Opposed values may coexist across contexts: loyal to a friend, distrustful of that friend's employer. A lie spoken for a job is not automatically a sincerely held belief. Unknown motives stay unknown.

Concrete generator: a completed outcome admits eligible interpretation families through [P11 context](../p11/CONTEXT.md). Bind the people and facts before choosing original wording. Prefer the most specific supported interpretation; include counterevidence where known. Freeze any selected suggestion and its draw receipt on admission. Viewing, dismissing, Save/reload or correcting a factual account consumes no replacement draw. New experience can legitimately create another suggestion. Player-written interpretation uses a supported topic and optional personal text; the text itself cannot write world facts or grant a power.

First example: a known repairer keeps a paid appointment after an earlier late delivery. Suggest revising the assessment of **this repairer's punctuality**, show both dated causes, and offer a future appointment reminder if endorsed. Trust never supplies the repairer's private stock. A public statement of trust can influence their response through an actual conversation; private endorsement alone cannot.

## M2a: wants, fears and reasons that generate playable content

| Extension | Generate from | Useful opportunity and control |
|---|---|---|
| M2a Practical want | Actual unfinished task, skill gap, promise or newly encountered affordance | Suggest learning a needed knot, buying qualified help or adapting the plan. Pinning creates an [ambition](AMBITIONS.md), not an automatic purchase. |
| M2b Curiosity and delight | Enjoyed craft, discovery, companionship or chosen taste | A small experiment, visit or performance can matter without a fear or penalty attached. |
| M2c Specific fear | A selected fear or a known adverse experience with its interpretation | Offer preparation, accompanied practice, avoidance, investigation or ordinary postponement. Do not invent a phobia from one failed roll. |
| M2d Mixed wants | Two real commitments or desired outcomes | Generate a planning opportunity: combine jobs, share a resource, postpone, negotiate or pursue both over time. Do not force one aspiration to be deleted. |
| M2e Resolved or obsolete want | Achieved predicate, changed circumstances or explicit player revision | Mark satisfied, reconsidered or dormant. Keep the reason in history and stop suggesting stale work. |

For a suggested want retain target, supporting causes, known attainable routes, expected effort class and status. Rank relevance using attachment and current means, with repetition memory; never increase anxiety solely because the player ignores the panel. A visible three-card presentation window can be a UI choice; it is not a cap on ambitions. Queueing requires the separate budget/method/stop contract from identity K8. Acceptance authorizes only the chosen goal terms, not every means of pursuing it.

Ordinary idle play stays comfortable without completing wants, performing emotional maintenance or eating. Scarce, stubborn, tranquil, intense, self-directed, forgiving and inexhaustibly curious profiles belong to particular worlds/people. They are optional adjectives, not a mandatory stress tax. [P11 dreams N7](../p11/DREAMS_AND_OCCASIONS.md#n7-dreams-visions-and-omens) can render remembered motifs; P23 chooses meaning and interest without granting prophecy.

## M3a: dramatic irony without thought policing

Use the identity owner's distinction between choosing an action and establishing a fact. Player knowledge can guide an inspection or a guess without a character knowledge flag. A successful guess still needs a represented result; telling an NPC a claim does not make it proved. Exact techniques can require actual mastery even when the human knows their name. Also support the reverse gap: contextual reminders expose the character's established expertise when the human lacks it.

[P24](../p24/) owns knowledge records, foreknowledge confidence, cutaways and time structures. Offer it these interfaces rather than a second knowledge store:

| Boundary | P23 reader | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Human remembers another run | Player sets an ambition or preparation | No forced lore excuse. Retained character memory requires the actual [P16](../p16/) carrier. |
| Player sees an allowed cutaway | Suggestion may be player-directed | Do not silently copy the scene into character knowledge or NPC testimony. |
| Character suspects something | Inner interpretation and optional hypothesis | Known observation/account basis, with uncertainty and alternatives. |
| Character knows a local method | Competence reminder and routine execution | Authored background or earned instruction; no invented childhood story. |
| Actor learns a hidden fact | Revision can become eligible | Authorized discovery receipt only. IDs/routes in public design; no truth, secret count or answer-bearing label. |

Dramatic irony can make a character's sincere confidence interesting. It must not make an ordinary action secretly fail because the human is too informed. Optional stricter discovery profiles can filter suggestions; they cannot erase the human's memories. Foreknowledge follows its source/continuity and real divergence, not an author who rerandomizes familiar facts to punish familiarity. ## M4a: growth, scars and healing with actual consumers

[E8E adaptation](../../../../emergent/E8E_ADAPTATION.md) supplies exposure/signature/response ideas; [P5 D3](../p5/D3_TALENT_ROLL.md) owns earned talent selection and settlement. P23 adds **how a person interprets and develops through the exposure**, not another talent lottery or adaptation ledger.

Distinguish a physical mark, learned practical control, transient distress, a persistent feared association, and another actor's interpretation of it. Current source already separates a palm mark from recovered soreness and conditioned grip. Do not give every scar a penalty, every loss a power, or every survivor the same reaction. Growth can also follow safe teaching, patience, joyful work and a changed understanding.

Proposed transition: known experience → optional interpretation → supported practice/support/reflection → completed transition receipt → registered trait or revised association → real consumer. A fear may remain while preparation improves. Healing may remove a local avoidance bias while preserving a visible mark and mastery. Hardening may improve focus without requiring emotional numbness. Exceptional instant healing or painless learning stays reachable through a world-supported source.

Original candidate: interrupted workshop work leaves a known hesitation about that specific task. Player can adopt no trait, record the concern, arrange a supervised attempt, or use another method. Completed supervised work can retain a **checked setup** technique with a useful next-job preparation consumer. A texture-only mark is valid too, labeled as such. No player identity change is inferred from simply paying for the safe route.

Future gate: equal adverse outcomes with different selected interpretations must not force the same identity; recovery preserves history and useful learned control; a mechanical trait changes an actual action. [MORAL_TEXTURE](MORAL_TEXTURE.md) owns moral judgments; [STORY_PROGRESSION](STORY_PROGRESSION.md) owns sourced supernatural transitions.
