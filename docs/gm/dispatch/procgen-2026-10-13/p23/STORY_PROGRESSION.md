## S1a: a breakthrough needs a real moment and a real rule

An event can supply a question, an insight opportunity, evidence of understanding, or a mechanical transition. These are different outcomes. A sunrise can be a meaningful moment without granting a realm. A technically ready cultivator may recognize a personal reason while teaching; a practical craftsperson may finally understand load distribution after a successful repair. Both can be valuable, but only the selected world's law connects understanding to power.

Proposed reader sequence: resolve current body/source/method → collect relevant retained experience and endorsed meaning → qualify a source-specific insight predicate → offer reflection or an executable transition → commit supported costs/grant atomically → demonstrate a useful next action. The narrative explanation is generated after qualification; eloquent text cannot make an ineligible actor advance. Materials, reserve, structure, insight, institutional recognition and ritual remain separate gates. A powerful insight need not produce a penalty unless the world says so.

Allow several source-fitting triggers: successful application, repeated ordinary practice, teaching, observing nature, surviving a real danger, chosen reflection, learning from a failed experiment or a supported exceptional gift. Dangerous loss is not compulsory. An unrelated misfortune cannot be inserted to farm a breakthrough. Idle queues and sustained supported conditions can produce genuine experience; clicking a journal repeatedly is not experience.

Proposed assessment retains rule/source revision, eligible source/body refs, relevant outcome refs, interpretation/endorsement refs where needed, actual qualification result, grant ref and next-consumer ref. An unknown required law suspends the supernatural reader, leaving ordinary work available. Qualification with no grant is reported as insight or readiness, not a completed rank transition.

## S2e: generate a personal trial from this life's actual history

An original **history-shaped trial** can reuse the [trials owner](../../../../TRIALS_AND_TRIBULATIONS.md) while P23 contributes motif selection and meaning. Distinguish a remembered dream, a voluntary rehearsal, a supernatural trial, contaminating influence and literal control loss. Similar presentation does not make them the same operation.

Proposed admission contract:

1. Choose a qualified world law and trigger: voluntary reflection, source-required advancement trial, actual exposure warning or an explicit outside examiner. Resolve world/instance/current life and retained memory carrier.
2. Read retained typed causes that this actor knows or the examiner can legitimately reach. Use endorsed fears, disputed promises, attachment, pride, desire, joy and unresolved questions. Never infer a regret from missing log text or fabricate a traumatic childhood.
3. Bind a motif to a particular cause. A recreated figure is a simulation/projection unless the actual person is present. Distinguish what happened from how it is remembered and what the trial alters. Reveal sealed facts only through a separately authorized discovery route; ordinary trial selection exposes neither truth nor secret existence.
4. Offer several source-legal approaches with known terms: prepare, face, question, seek help, reject the premise, postpone, bypass with a supported power or disengage where the local law permits. Actual physical trials keep their real damage/life rules. Pure rehearsals cannot kill a participant by text alone.
5. Freeze trigger, cast/projection refs, motif, transformations, allowed methods, failure/withdrawal terms, assessor/grant rule revisions and draws at admission. Settlement updates the owning source/body/progression state with a real cause. Reload/import preserves the same pending trial.

Original example: an accepted shelter delivery really failed because the route became blocked. The actor **chooses** to record fear of making another promise. A voluntary rehearsal reconstructs the known obstruction and asks them to plan with a willing helper, a different route or a limited promise. It can yield a usable planning method if completed; it does not rewrite the failed delivery, compel guilt or bring the real recipient into a dream. An actor who did not adopt that interpretation gets a different eligible motif or no trial. Successful prior delivery can instead inspire a challenge about teaching or trusting others.

Source-specific supernatural readers may legitimately involve involuntary pressure, but authority changes need explicit state: current controller/host, source, reach, affected faculties, known warning, duration, exits and transition receipts. A risk setting cannot silently hand control to an NPC policy. A consented dangerous action can have a real irreversible outcome through the selected world's qualified rules; this docs lane selects none. No closed-browser forced sacrifice or historical control theft is implied.

## S2f: meaningful recurrence and recovery

The same concern may recur under new circumstances; a settled memory can be revisited voluntarily. Repetition can improve preparation, expose a different meaning or simply remain a ritual. Generous, cumulative, inexhaustible, harsh, forgiving, contextual and identity-bound profiles are world/adapter adjectives. Avoid universal “one epiphany,” “one trial,” “three strikes,” novelty taxes or a fixed reward cap. Duplicate settlement of one occurrence is transaction integrity, not gameplay scarcity.

Healing removes only the specified association/condition or source effect. Keep actual experience, acquired mastery, repaired property and others' knowledge distinct. A successful psychological rehearsal cannot clean a contaminated characteristic without a supported method. A physical cure need not settle a social grievance. Powerful assisted recovery can be valid; no compulsory quest-length suffering.

## S3a: titles earned from actual feats, with a source-specific effect

Use P12's sifted feat, P14's account and P17's assessment. Distinguish self-description, nickname, public title, institutional office, system recognition and a power-bearing Name. A title string cannot confer all of them. A real issuer must hear adequate evidence and have authority; an explicitly supernatural assessor uses its own reach. Store assessor, scope, qualified feat refs, grant/recognition receipt and world law, rather than regenerate titles on rendering.

Original useful slice: a workshop recognizes a completed supported teaching deed and grants an instructor appointment under its real schedule, enabling one paid lesson with actual learners and stock. An independent magical title may grant a registered technique where a selected world permits it. Two audiences can call the same deed different things; translation, renaming or exporting the deed changes no power. A false legend can feed belief power only through a real eligible audience under that world's law.

Live titles are already records (`simulation_effects.js:358–360`); live progression settles authored advancements (`simulation.js:340–351`). Neither demonstrates a generated feat-to-title consumer. A future builder must show acquisition → recognition → actual changed action, not just a badge. Recurring offices, evolving titles, revoked recognition and several compatible identities remain reachable. Actual exclusive world roles are scoped constraints, not a global either/or personality rule.
