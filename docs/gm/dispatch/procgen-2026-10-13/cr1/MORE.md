## Original worked combination

XD2.f: A player makes one known medicinal soup, allocates an ordinary serving for themselves and a diet-compatible serving for a beast, and retains the rest. A constituent common to the soup and the player's tonic shares one tolerance mechanism. The beast has an independent receiving body and a distinct response. The player may instead use a tonic, an equivalent supported salve, a paid helper or ordinary unfed work. Each method has its actual route/amount and can remain available.

A compatible purification service removes a named residue later, without resetting permanent-gain history, clearing unrelated poison or teaching every ingredient property. The beast's care receipt can affect its bond if supported; its medicine does not buff the player merely through ownership. No generated secret truth, canon formula or medical instruction is included.

Future evidence needs paid preparation and use, independent target-control outcomes, exact leftovers, shared-family tolerance, saved/pending/final receipt import and comfortable unfed play on rebuilt laptop/desktop origins. All are specified-not-run here.
