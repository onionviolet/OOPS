# Frieren generation profile

2026-10-14. PROPOSAL ONLY, supplemental source. *Frieren*, Kanehito Yamada/Tsukasa Abe. [Evidence S-R](SOURCE_NOTES.md). Selected Frieren r14471 and Qual r14595 notes are STATED-secondary, snapshot 2023-12-12. No new SHOWN-primary claim; all gameplay is INFERRED.

| Code | Domain | Fitting proposal and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| FR1 | Resources / harvesting | Original ordinary herbs, ore, paper, apparatus and region-compatible reagent lots support commissions and practice. Treat personal mana, observed signature, stored magical medium and method knowledge separately. Ambient mana harvesting/storage is UNKNOWN, not established by a spell's existence. [P8](../p8/) owns yields/handling. |
| FR2 | Materials / creation | Generate material tools and original spell-study experiments from qualified functional primitives through [P6](../p6/TECHNIQUE_INVENTION.md). A grimoire/record is knowledge with provenance, not automatic execution or an infinitely copied fuel source. Frieren r14471's flower-field spell is a specific taught method, not proof all imagined flora become harvestable permanent matter. |
| FR3 | People / names | Original regional households, couriers, artisans, researchers and eligible students using [P7 W3](../p7/OTHER_WORLDS.md). Reserve named Hero Party, legendary teachers/demons, unique family ties and signature feats. A German-looking name table is not a world-wide culture rule. |
| FR4 | Encounters / events / loot | Coherent authored demon/beast/mage packages, local motives, mana observations, supply jobs and disputed records. Rewards: paid work, learned account, real tool, useful ordinary spell or a researched counter. Finding a text does not grant perfect insight into a hidden opponent. |
| FR5 | Locations | Fill an authored village/road/archive or chosen Northern Lands site. Continental Magic Association r6639 locates a branch at Äußerst; its note does not establish exam procedures or an accessible training economy. No generated continent topology. |
| FR6 | Talents / aptitude | Freeze compatible mana/control/sensing or learning predisposition separately from species/lifespan, reserve and actual repertoire; use [D3](../p5/D3_TALENT_ROLL.md). No canonical odds or automatic ancient mastery. Ordinary crafts, patient study, teachers and later rare source routes remain reachable. |
| FR7 | Economy | Real buyers for useful ordinary magic, manuscripts, repairs and escorted supplies. Long-lived actors have dated knowledge, actual funds and obligations rather than unlimited free tutorship. No automatic infinite gold from a cosmetic flower spell. |

**FR8 chronology/AU:** choose a cutoff and years relative to the hero journey; long-lived and short-lived people have different biographies. Qual r14595, Zoltraak system impact, reports that research after sealing made a terrifying attack and defense ordinary. An older snapshot therefore cannot use later counter knowledge automatically. Alternative mentor/relationship, delayed research, different local school or survival can compose as explicit AU causes, without claiming later serial truth.

**FR9 secrets:** `P10.FR.S1`, proposed/unassigned, R1 supported comparison/experiment and R2 independent record/contact. Truths unauthored; [D6](../p5/D6_SECRET_ROLL.md) principles. Detection does not reveal all hidden laws or names.

**FR10 idle:** long study, controlled casting practice, research and funded utility service fit queued play. Save actual fractional progress and known method/evidence; do not require human years to represent an elf's learning. A source-qualified powerful method can simply be better. No new closed-browser advancement, offline aging/loss or mandatory feeding.
