Canon-first priorities remain intact. Original Frontier is optional, never a required opening; one realm/one prestige remains the milestone. These profiles accept no new world, balance, start, ontology or fiction.

## Cross-world patterns and findings

| Code | Finding / design pattern | Concrete consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Energy words hide different objects | Treat matter, personal fuel, ambient field, charged carrier, evidence, knowledge, contract right and living identity separately. JJK residue is not automatic fuel, reishi is not reiatsu, a Nen category is not an ability, and a contract is not a potion. [P8 magical resources](../p8/MAGICAL_RESOURCES.md) owns acquisition forms. |
| F2 | A grammar needs functional limits and consumers | Generate coherent eligible lots, people, incidents and tested ability packages. Author source laws, interactions, geography and exceptional identities. “Generate a curse” must resolve targeting and a real counter, not only its name/HP. |
| F3 | Expertise, institutions and sources differ | Aptitude, body, lineage, taught method, reserve, permission, custody, rank and recognized identity remain independent. A paid observer/service is a real alternative to gaining a rare innate faculty. |
| F4 | Truth and observation have different lifetimes | Save resolved truth, evidence basis and actor knowledge separately. Unknown truth operates; unsupported model data blocks or uses a declared fallback. Neither sorting, price, predicted output, tooltip nor NPC behavior can leak sealed truth. Use [P11 context](../p11/CONTEXT.md), especially actual versus KnownContext. |
| F5 | Canon is causal, not a forced ending | Pin continuity/era/source cutoff and actor state. Generate local circumstances; after a real intervention re-evaluate downstream causes. Relationship/start/mentor/source AUs can compose and retain unchanged facts. Never merge Fate routes, FMA continuities or later biographies merely to broaden a pool. |
| F6 | Original worlds allow broader generation without arbitrary mechanics | Entire original resource families, households, histories and aptitude distributions can vary. Author a rule grammar and compatibility matrix first; per-save hidden laws/truths use sealed IDs, routes and retained revisions. [D6](../p5/D6_SECRET_ROLL.md) is the bounded precedent, not a universal factory implementation. |
| F7 | More depth can be ordinary life | Sampling, repair, paid reports, teaching, provenance disputes, infrastructure and delivered goods can change later work. Not every world needs a fight, rare power or whole new subsystem to show its signature rule. |
| F8 | Source coverage changes which claims are ready | CSM/FMA have qualified local syntheses; other SYNTH dispatch files are instructions. Naruto/Bleach official anchors are bounded; later wiki notes do not become primary authentication. [SOURCE_NOTES C1–C8](SOURCE_NOTES.md) preserves the material contradictions and missing laws. |

The proposed logical profile carries world/instance/continuity, source snapshot, era, overlays, rule/grammar revisions, authored location/role reservations, generator scope, actual resolved records, cause and draw receipts, means references, projection and clock/compatibility policies. These are conceptual joins to existing owners, **not a new universal persistent owner, numerical schema or claimed API**. Use only the subset a selected consumer needs.
