## Public premise and procedural freedom

Borrowed mechanisms can work differently here, but the author must define what actually happens. A fear-born threat, bonded weapon, personal aura discipline, material transformation and negotiated power can coexist when their scopes, sources, costs, targets and interaction order are authored. Franchise names do not carry laws automatically. Local rules can also simply make a route superior; no compulsory compensating drawback or forced either/or.

| Code | Domain | What can be fully procedural within authored constraints | Stable authored boundary |

[P8](../p8/) owns harvesting. | Declare which is matter, fuel, evidence, living agent or legal claim. Never average chakra/aura/mana/reishi into one universal substance. |

## Per-save laws and truths

**OW9:** [D6](../p5/D6_SECRET_ROLL.md) supplies immutable selection plus separate knowledge stages, independent discovery routes, preserved revisions and fair useful payoffs. Proposed unassigned opaque slots `P10.O.S1` and `P10.O.S2`; routes R1/R2; no candidate laws, assignments, answers or clue bindings are authored in this public file. Public start premise and genuinely hidden specialness are separate. A public choice may select an advertised law profile; a sealed draw reveals nothing until its actual route. Neither may redraw on preview, entry, return, save or Import.

A future sealed authoring record should validate combination compatibility, ordinary-life reachability, two attainable evidence routes, at least one useful consumer per selected rule, and a lawful resolution for overlapping effects. Freeze resolved selection IDs/revisions at creation; keep existing semantics after catalogue changes. Broader world-law selection is an extension beyond D6's one original secret, not already implemented by linking it.

## Timeline, variants and idle fit

**OW10 timeline/AUs:** generate original past events and roles only within a causal grammar, freeze the resulting facts, then derive attributed accounts through [D8](../p5/D8_LORE_GRAMMAR.md). A new variant can alter public institutions, relationships or starting era while composing with a sealed profile. Branches have distinct people/custody after the fork. No automatic reset, export duplication or retroactive replacement of an old world.

**OW11 idle:** world adjectives specify applicable progression clock, renewal, recovery, automation, risk and body needs. Queue known supported procedures with actual means and explicit stop conditions. Comfortable ordinary unfed play remains supported; no hunger death. Strong late automation may remove upkeep when its real source does so; do not reinstate scarcity merely to balance it. Closed-browser behavior needs a selected host adapter and cannot be inferred from the adjective alone.
