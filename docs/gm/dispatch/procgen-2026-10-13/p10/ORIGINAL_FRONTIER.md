## Authored core, procedural surroundings

STATED-project: the owner keeps native people/body/money/skills/quests, fixed Short Measure discrepancy, actual corridor travel, four careers and five arcs. Old saves do not enroll or acquire debt/discovery on load. No random origin is required for this campaign. Expanded random entry has no mandatory Frontier prerequisite. INFERRED: new background lots, customers and local incidents can make repeated ordinary life richer without rerolling those facts.

## Timeline, variants, secrets and idle fit

**OF9 timeline:** existing persistent chronicle and causal milestones stay authored. Generate new admissions at current played time; never insert fake past deeds or households into an old save. Logs reflect last known observation. Consequences can coexist across careers; a chosen conclusion does not erase independent work/history. No new prestige, forced restart or required world playthrough.

**OF10 AU/branch:** alternate fresh household/start, changed local institution, optional original branch, carried source or future region can compose in separate instances or reviewed explicit enrollment. A new random law must not retroactively change a paid old operation. Frontier echoes need real carriers or declared original-world outlier rules, not silent JJK law import.

**OF11 secrets:** existing [D6 S1](../p5/D6_SECRET_ROLL.md), R1/R2, status proposed; candidates remain sealed, not read for P10. Reuse that owner instead of inventing a second Frontier S1. Its per-save optional-branch policy has priority over a generic world-factory roll. No candidate truth or payoff identity is exposed here.

**OF12 idle:** retain actual native/common-clock semantics and finite supported queued routines. Bind next batch resources/wage/site; stop on shortage, access change, target, unknown hazard or new consent. A character can keep ordinary gathering/training/jobs/rest while a client lacks funds. Closed-browser production remains unsupported under the current Frontier owner; P10 does not enable it. Meals help; comfortable unfed play and no hunger death remain standing direction.
