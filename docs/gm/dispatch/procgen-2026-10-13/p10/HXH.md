2026-10-14. PROPOSAL ONLY. Yoshihiro Togashi's *Hunter × Hunter*. [Evidence S-H/C4](SOURCE_NOTES.md). All systems below are INFERRED/proposal. Nen r421055 and its retained note are STATED-secondary; no new SHOWN-primary claim.

## Source law and generation boundary

[Nen r421055](https://hunterxhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Nen?oldid=421055), Principles / Water Divination / Aura Types / Conditions / Risk, supports distinct control methods, six categories, uneven learning and personal conditions. A copied method may retain its creator's target semantics (DX1). Activation failure is not a vow breach (DX2); continuous requirements and permitted substitutes are explicit (DX3). Personality typing is an unreliable character claim, not a diagnostic generator.

## Timeline, AUs, secrets and idle play

**H9 timeline:** choose source cutoff and arc before admitting named participants. Record exam year, travel, alive/present actors and institutional knowledge. Ongoing-source future events stay unasserted. A generated helper's actions may divert a shipment or rescue, after which dependent encounters recheck actual history.

**H10 AU:** different exam cohort, teacher, home region, acquired source, relationship or institutional response can compose. Keep ordinary training, difficult category work, alternate teachers and rare source-changing routes reachable. Do not force a disability/death vow to compensate for talent or require every unusual method to have a niche.

**H11 secrets:** `P10.H.S1`, proposed/unassigned; R1 supported observation/testing, R2 records plus witnessed comparison. Truths unauthored; [D6](../p5/D6_SECRET_ROLL.md) principles apply to changed original mysteries, not replacement of named canon abilities in baseline.

**H12 idle:** queue safe Ten/Ren/control drills with declared reserve, fatigue and recovery, plus study and funded fieldwork. Equal eligible played time gives equal advancement independent of tick frequency. High-risk forced awakening, new vow, exorcism or irreversible sacrifice requires a separate supported commitment. No automatic “personality quiz unlocks Specialist” or offline lethal training.
