## Evidence vocabulary

| Code | Meaning in these profiles |
|---|---|
| E1 SHOWN | A directly inspected primary scene demonstrates it. P10 inspected no primary manga/anime scenes, so makes no new SHOWN claim. An older ledger's primary-source status is attributed to that ledger. |
| E2 STATED | A named source asserts it. A wiki assertion is STATED-secondary even when describing a scene; an official synopsis is STATED-official, with its limited scope. Speaker beliefs stay attributed. |
| E3 INFERRED | A design reading or plausible extension, not verified source law. Every proposed generator, rate, job, resource loop and AU here is INFERRED/proposal. |
| E4 UNKNOWN | Relevant evidence is missing, disputed or insufficient. Unknown is neither forbidden nor a granted ability. |

Source classification is separate from mode. These files use dated local snapshots. They make no claim about the latest serial, live wiki, or completion of ongoing reader lanes. No private novel notes/prose were read or quoted. Selected paraphrased wiki notes were read; no raw payload prose was copied.
