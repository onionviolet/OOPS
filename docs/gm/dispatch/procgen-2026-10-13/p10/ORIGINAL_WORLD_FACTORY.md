## What is free to generate

A world can generate original resource families, people/cultures/names, compatible aptitude distributions, local history, encounter packages, institutions, market demand, ordinary sites inside authored geography and hidden selections. “Fully procedural” means all concrete records in a selected grammar can vary. It does not mean code, metaphysics, cultures or meaningful choices arise without authored constraints and consumers.

## Laws, mysteries and per-save persistence

**G11 laws:** a future profile binds world/instance/continuity, source/original status, public premise, compatible rule IDs/revisions, allowed generation families, authored anchors, aptitude distribution revision, admitted people, material definitions, history facts and selection receipts. This is conceptual data, not current schema. Independent law modules require explicit precedence/composition rules, units, body/source compatibility and supported consumers. An impossible combination fails validation before admission, rather than quietly weakening a rule or substituting a different seed result.

**G12 secrets:** [P5 D6](../p5/D6_SECRET_ROLL.md) is the bounded existing proposal for S1/R1/R2; generalized world creation is additional work. Future family slots `P10.G.S1` and `P10.G.S2` are unassigned/unauthored. R1/R2 only; no candidate values, causal answers or per-save assignments in this public document. Every selected secret requires independent reachable routes, a useful optional payoff, neutral pre-discovery projection and retained revision. Law truth can operate before known, but does not grant NPCs clairvoyance or auto-block ordinary play. Original generated facts and claims are stored separately.

**G13 lifecycle:** draw at the declared new-instance transaction, save actual resolved definitions and IDs, then admit actual people/goods from finite budgets. Preview, reload, revisit and Import restore instead of draw. Explicit new instance/reroll/branch is a separate action with declared retained domains, rather than a disguised save repair. Preserve legacy absence and current saves' semantics. Source/algorithm updates do not retcon existing truth or history; review migration or retain resolver revisions.

## Novel candidates and cosmology routes

**G14 source transfer:** the candidate scout's MoL, Perfect Run, Cradle, Traveler's Gate and Wandering Inn author descriptions motivate loop, checkpoint, valley progression, realm access and profession/residence **questions**, not their exact laws. [P9 task](../P9.md) owns book-world profiles. Direct settings need source-specific profiles; do not proceduralize a named protagonist's exclusive ability or infer inter-world laws from an author synopsis. This factory offers original counterparts with distinct authored mechanics and attribution to any structural inspiration actually used.

**G15 cosmology:** any family may sit in a shared universe, independent network, branch, pocket, layer, expedition route or constructed realm. Combine only with explicit passage, body/cargo, clock, connection and persistence contracts. A small represented region can belong to a large persistent world. Reset/cycle policies require conservation/identity rules; they do not automatically create another prestige or duplicate exported goods. Realm layers are not parallel timelines by default.

## Idle fit and distinctive further possibilities

| Code | Angle | Concrete proposal |
|---|---|---|
| GM1 | Local abundance is a rule, not an exploit to suppress | Let an abundant world support mass production and shift useful decisions to actual transport, application or relationships. Do not add a fixed once limit to restore scarcity. |
| GM2 | Institutions derive from resources | A real scarce workshop service creates a customer/teacher network; freeze actual budgets and rights before generated jobs can pay. |
| GM3 | Background history is not simulated omniscience | Generate coherent original historical facts once; admit a background figure as an active actor with frozen prior commitments only when needed. D8 writes attributed accounts of those facts. |
| GM4 | Time can differ without idle punishment | Define played/absence/production clocks before applying local renewal. Boundary equivalence and fractional progress matter; closed-browser support is a separate implementation gate. |
| GM5 | Every body deserves ordinary life | A tree, construct or nonhuman can have suitable work, relationships and optional preparation. No invented hunger/death tax to normalize it to humans. |
