## Timeline, AUs, secrets and idle play

**N9 timeline:** original manga 1–700 baseline with exact era/team ages, alive/present cast, organ state and affiliations. Early and late versions are not interchangeable. N10/NU7's suspect Rin captor attribution and NU8's excluded one-shot teaching are quarantined. A rescue, changed seal or moved source changes downstream canon; no destiny correction to erase it.

Ordinary training, clan teaching, acquisition and outlier routes coexist. Separate body/source inherited status from social recognition and known family claims.

**N11 secrets:** `P10.N.S1`, proposed/unassigned; R1 supported field/sensory examination and R2 records/testimony comparison. Truths unauthored; [D6](../p5/D6_SECRET_ROLL.md) principles apply only to changed/original mysteries.

**N12 idle:** queued control practice, toolmaking, supply work and permitted patrol advance on supported played time with stamina/source, recovery and real stock. Shadow-clone accelerated training is an actor/method-specific case (N2), requiring actual bodies/partition/memory/fatigue rules before automation. No magical infinite labor from cloning; no generic cap to “balance” a sourced superior method. Stop at danger/new consent; no new offline injury or loss.
