This is a local ritual policy, not a global once rule or restriction on later sourced acquisition. [Magic Circuit r395092](https://typemoon.fandom.com/wiki/Magic_Circuit?oldid=395092), Mechanisms, distinguishes circuit quality/quantity/composition, energy conversion and Foundations; cross-work exceptions require filtering.

## Timeline, AUs, secrets and idle play

**F9 timeline:** choose one work/route, start date, ritual variant, local era and dated roster. Before a divergence, causal events follow that baseline; after it, recalculate alive/present Masters, catalysts, contracts and public evidence. A heroic record's source history is separate from one summon instance's memories. Unknown inter-instance memory rules are not filled from another game.

**F10 AU:** alternate participant, relationship/household, catalyst provenance, ritual implementation, age-of-magic setting or mentor can be composable overlays. Preserve unchanged dependencies and author the changed seats, fuel, recognition and consequences. Fuyuki authority cannot silently acquire Grand Order regeneration, roster access or leveling. All reasonable routes remain candidates where supported means exist.

**F11 secrets:** `P10.F.S1`, proposed/unassigned, with R1 material/instrument study and R2 independently corroborated archive/contact route. Truths unauthored; [D6](../p5/D6_SECRET_ROLL.md) is the selection pattern, never a randomized rewrite of a baseline heroic identity.

**F12 idle:** queue study, mundane income, safe preparation or agreed upkeep with explicit funds and consent. Servants are agents with their own choices, not idle tools. Known stock shortage can stop a queued workshop operation; no unattended Command Spell, new bargain or irreversible life-source sacrifice. Closed-browser progression is not introduced.
