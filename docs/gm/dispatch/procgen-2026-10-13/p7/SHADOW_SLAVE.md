## D1: powers and institutions

Separate ordinary civilian, source-qualified Nightmare entrant, rank, Aspect/Flaw, Aspect grade, core count/class, mastery, attributes, Memories/Echoes and institution recognition. Do not turn every person into an awakened user or every awakened person into the divine protagonist's multi-core path. A True Name, legacy, rare Aspect or high-rank authority requires its actual eligibility/event. No numerical awakening/rank prevalence is established here.

Work/medical/logistics roles require finite funds, equipment and reachable people; civilian aid remains useful. Government training/service, evacuation/recovery support and legacy/clan affiliations are proposed institutional cohorts to qualify by selected chapter snapshot. Badge, rank and combat performance are separate observations. Buying a Memory or hearing an account does not transfer its owner's innate faculty or all source knowledge.
