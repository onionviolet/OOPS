## P1: plausible start population

Pin an early billet/Legion support snapshot: generated recruit with issued kit, supply helper with owned stock, courier with a funded task. Weights 4:3:2 within that cohort, not Empire caste/mage frequencies. Define whether the visitor is enlisted, attached or civilian before duties. Existing Castile/Delmar/Damian appearances and compressed co-location are authored adaptation decisions, not random roles to fill again.

Later Hound, Guild or Caliphate populations have different authority/knowledge and require new era cohorts; do not use a later rank/relationship at the early billet. Source people at a later endpoint cannot be pulled backward with their acquired knowledge.

## D1: powers and institutions

Affinity, aether capacity, imprinted spell, trained execution, institutional citizen/college status, military rank and equipment are separate. A porter role does not generate dimensional space; rare source profiles require actual eligibility/acquisition. A generated ordinary soldier should not receive the protagonist's collector or unique exceptional access just because the original slice demonstrates storage.

Lion Legion supplies recruit tests, orders, issue/return custody and patrol/watch. Hounds have specialist tasking/training; Mage College/First Citizens are political and magical access structures, not generic damage tiers. Guild identity/contracts differ from company authority. Caliphate institutions reflect specific local testimony and era, not an omniscient universal orc culture. No quantitative affinity census is established here.

## R1: reservations and AU boundary
