Use the existing proposed rebuilt-town working-life snapshot: local carrier, ordinary repair worker, lodging assistant. Template weights 4:3:2, conditional on the local work cohort. ## D1: powers and institutions

D&D is a rules family with many settings, editions and continuities. Save rulesProfileId separately from setting/era. A job does not automatically confer a player class, level, tool proficiency, feat, spell slot or patron contract. NPC statblocks and player-class packages differ. Skill, ancestry, background, deity affiliation and earned training have distinct evidence. Magical prevalence is settlement/edition-dependent and unknown here; do not turn a three-adventurer proposal into a town census.

Bind town trades, shrine, lodging and caravan access to actual resources and jurisdiction. Use authored named NPCs where the source assigns an office; new contacts fill vacant ordinary roles. A shrine worker is not automatically a spellcasting Cleric. Learning about Mending does not mean knowing or being able to cast it.

## R1: reservations and AU boundary

Reserve selected-setting characters, aliases, unique divine/artifact identities, named office-holders and plot-specific adventure roles. Do not generate an existing god as a random priest, a legendary hero under an alias, or the actual proprietor of a canon shop under a new name. Forgotten Realms, Eberron and other settings need independent packs. A tabletop campaign or novel/game parallel is an explicit continuity, not implicit permission to merge all versions.
