## P1: plausible start population

Proposal: adult household/nursery supply court, three generated adults: nursery helper with actual tools, delivery worker with a funded request, household customer with limited funds. Conditional template weights 4:3:2; these tune encounter variety, not wizard/Muggle population proportions. Keep household ties among original people. If a child start is selected later, authored guardian/age/supervision constraints compose with D7; do not retroactively give the adult visitor parents.

School-year cohorts need their own admission/age/year/house snapshots and vacant background slots. Shop streets, ordinary nonmagical neighborhoods and elite school classes must use different demographics. A student seat grants neither expertise nor every known spell.

## D1: powers and institutions

Magical eligibility, family descent, learned spell/potion craft, wand/tool compatibility and institutional access are separate. No numeric wizard prevalence is qualified here. Muggle-born magical children and nonmagical members of magical families prevent a surname-to-power shortcut; H2 is supplemental evidence, not a universal seven-novel genetics formula. No generated aura-level ladder or quantified core default. Nursery work can be useful without a casting source.

Bind Hogwarts/Ministry/shop/household roles to the selected era and actual admission/employment rules. House is an institutional affiliation, not a deterministic temperament. Occupation does not grant access to a unique wand-maker's methods or canon property. A generated contact may know a canon name publicly; actual meeting, tutoring, friendship and kinship require authored edges and source-era availability.

## R1: reservations and explicit AU boundary

Reserve canonical full names/aliases, distinctive mononyms, established families where lineage would be implied, named school staff/shop owners and plot-specific unnamed roles. Do not generate a chosen child, unique prophecy subject, canon family heir, secret relative or singular artifact owner. Shared ordinary given names can be allowed under G2; signature identities remain blocked. A relationship AU or role counterpart is authored first, not a randomized Potter/Malfoy child with an inherited kit.
