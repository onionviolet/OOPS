## P1: plausible start population

Use an original Rush Valley-adjacent workshop: parts runner, workshop helper and customer. Weights 4:3:2 in this work cohort. Civil engineers can make useful parts without alchemy. East City military, Xingese traveling practice and Ishvalan relief need their own cultures/era aftermath. Manga/Brotherhood and 2003/its sequel are distinct branches with different identities and causes; do not reuse a mixed character roster.

## D1: powers and institutions

Material knowledge, alchemical/alkahestric method, arrays/tools, actual matter/energy, execution and specialization are distinct. No elemental birth classes or randomized metaphysical rank. A learned alchemist can be uncertified; State status grants institutional rights/duties, not a preset damage tier. Automail engineering requires its own skills/tools and does not grant alchemy. No quantitative alchemist/State-certificate/alkahestry demographic odds are established here.

Household/workshop ownership, military certification, civilian supply, religious/community relations and royal clan access have issuer/jurisdiction/era. A gifted researcher needs acquired knowledge and actual materials. Circle-free access, soul binding, stored-soul source and special bodies need source-specific history; civilian generation does not hand them out. An original ordinary alchemical specialization can be authored after source review without duplicating a canon signature method.

## R1: reservations and AU boundary

Reserve canon people/codenames, named military and royal offices, unique carriers, established soul-bound identities and plot-specific subjects. Do not generate a new canon Homunculus identity, protagonist Gate history, royal heir, sibling or owner of a singular source. Shared office classes can have background members only after roster eligibility is reviewed; unique seats cannot. Relationship/start AUs specify their altered edges/branch, not a universal exemption from material/body consequences.
