## P1: plausible start population

Start outside combat: civilian supplier, records worker, qualified support contact for a funded evacuation/supply task. Weights 5:3:1 in that local cohort. School cohorts must use fixed canon seats/staff plus explicitly vacant background/support roles; never grow the named class roster by pretending every generated person was always there. Ordinary pre-crisis, Shibuya-era and Culling Game populations have different access, danger and rules. Pin one era, not the union.

## D1: powers and institutions

Cursed-energy presence, perception, reserve/output/control, innate technique, inherited eligibility, learned reinforcement/barrier work, equipment and grade are distinct. Most mundane cohort members need no executable supernatural kit. A qualified support worker need not have a technique; a sorcerer may have one without a domain. No quantitative sorcerer/clan/grade census is certified here. Grades are institutional assessment, not a randomized energy tier.

Named clans and shared inherited methods need explicit lineage and source-qualified availability. Do not invent another Great Clan or a unique eye/technique combination through routine rolls. Birth restrictions are imposed body/source profiles, not consent-based vows. Schools, auxiliary supervision, medical/support work and higher authority each need role rights and era availability. A canon teacher does not teach every generated recruit or reveal private plans by default.

## R1: reservations and AU boundary

Reserve canon cast/aliases, body-host relationships, fixed named students/staff, unique vessel/eye combinations, named shikigami/artifacts and singular plot roles. Shared clan techniques may exist only through a sourced ordinary eligibility template; canon-exclusive powers require an explicit AU rule. No protagonist/vessel/ancient reincarnation by routine background enrollment. Canon relationships are authored edges. A relationship AU is specific, not automatic family access or compatible power inheritance.
