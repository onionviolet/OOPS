## D1: powers and institutions

Distinguish ordinary people, informed nonpowered support, acquired potion pathway/Sequence, characteristic ownership, digestion, ritual eligibility, current control/resources and institutional recognition. Power is not granted by surname, employment or reading a formula. High sequences/unique seats and source-exclusive authorities are reserved; generator weights cannot invent an extra occupied divine position.

COI also requires separately sourced boon/pact/bestower and burden fields. A boon's recipient is not automatically an ordinary potion-pathway member at a same-number rank. A sequence number never implies all formula knowledge, mastery, sane control or resources. No numerical pathway demographics are qualified here. Church affiliation, official team access, licensed work, gang membership and secret-order ties have separate witnesses/permissions. Public clerk status is not blanket occult knowledge.
