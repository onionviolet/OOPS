Perception, physical targeting, domain passage, spiritual reserves, learned techniques, body/soul form, weapon bond and releases differ. Most first-cohort roles use ordinary means. A report buyer gains testimony, not spiritual senses. Soul Reaper/Quincy/Fullbring/Arrancar eligibility is source/body/history-specific; don't roll a generic class with all four possibilities. Captain office, Shikai/Bankai access and combat mastery are separate; noncaptain release cases disprove a rank-to-release lookup.

Bind living services, academy, division offices, care corps, noble houses and research support to the selected source/era. An unseated actor can perform ordinary support without being a new canon officer. No whole-world spirit/perception/Bankai frequency is certified. Exceptional heritage and damaged-source recovery need exact scene rules.
