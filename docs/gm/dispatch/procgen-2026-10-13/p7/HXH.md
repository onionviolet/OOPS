## P1: plausible start population

Use one ordinary travel/port district: worker, exam-bound traveler, lodging assistant. Weights 4:2:3 in the conditional cohort. An exam applicant, licensed Hunter, professional fighter, mafia support worker and ordinary resident have different availability/means. Arena, island, expedition and succession-era populations need independent manifests, not increasing random level bands around the player.

## D1: powers and institutions

Civil life, Hunter licence/occupation, aura awareness/control, trained fundamentals, category affinity, actual personal ability and current reserves are separate. Not all exam travelers know Nen; a licence does not specify mastery. Forced initiation and rare talents need authored causes and consequences. No population frequency or fixed six-category percentage is source-qualified here; use no supernatural roll in the first mundane cohort.

Personal abilities come from aptitude, practice, commitments and source-defined acquisition. A broad category is not a complete copied ability. Specialist eligibility and exceptional trait/heritage require qualified templates. Conditions, continuing predicates and breakable vows differ. Hunter Association, exam staff, port work and other institutions have actual jurisdiction and membership; generated local workers need no privileged connection to the chairman or named examiner.

## R1: reservations and AU boundary

A sourced ability-transfer system requires an explicit acquisition receipt and original semantic predicates; name generation never performs it. AU lineage/roster changes remain authored.
