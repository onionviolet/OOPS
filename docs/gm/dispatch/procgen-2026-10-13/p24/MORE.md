# Further O, P and Q angles

2026-10-14. **PROPOSALS ONLY.** Deliberate expansion of root O1–O3/P1–P3/Q1–Q4 with new angles and concrete consumers. Codes below extend those letters; they are not edits to the root palette or selected decisions. [REPORT](REPORT.md) links owners and evidence. Costs describe relative join complexity, not time estimates. All examples are original; reasonable cheap, strong, repeatable and unusual variants remain reachable under per-world adjectives.

## More knowledge structures

| Code | New angle | Concrete suggestion / consumer | Cost and specific future gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| O4 | **Knowing how to find out** differs from knowing the answer | Save a known witness/protocol/library lead even when its old answer is stale. A regressor remembers whom to ask and can rebuild present evidence | Medium; P11/P14/P24 O1. Known lead survives divergence without granting the witness's current answer |
| O5 | **Evidence expiration and coverage** | A recent road survey replaces only the conditions it actually sampled. A dated official notice can remain legally operative until a real revocation | Medium; P20 place/P11 jurisdiction/channel. Staleness is domain-specific, not global decay of all knowledge |
| O6 | **Common knowledge and coordination** | A notice delivered to everyone enables a meeting; one private warning does not. Generate acknowledgement and joint preparation requests | High; P19/P11 messages. Prove each recipient's arrival and acknowledgement; no omniscient “everyone knows everyone knows” flag |
| O7 | **Adversarial foreknowledge** | Two eligible returners remember different scopes and react to each other's actual interventions. New events emerge from changed plans | High; P13/P19/P21. Other returner has real means and memory carrier; no free revelation of hidden rival status |
| O8 | **Forecasts change their own causes** | A public flood estimate causes real preparation, so damage differs. Record predicted conditions, who learned it and who acted | Medium/high; P13/P11/P12. Do not label an averted conditional prediction “wrong” or make prophecy compel an outcome |
| O9 | **Knowledge loss, privacy and recovery** | A stolen notebook removes a physical access route while understood methods remain. A source-qualified memory erasure changes declared belief episodes only | High for erasure; P6/P14/temporal owner. Preserve exact scope and recoverability; never erase private saves or infer a universal memory price |
| O10 | **Tacit, sensory and procedural knowledge** | A beast recognizes a smell without a written name; a craftsperson demonstrates a motion a book cannot fully teach | Medium; senses/P6 teaching. A verbal description grants neither sense nor execution mastery; assisted routes remain |
| O11 | **Unknown world law as research** | Repeated actual observations can reveal one supported operating rule of an original world, with counterexamples and scope | High; P9/P10/P11 M2. Law exists before testing except explicitly qualified K3 worlds; no new cosmology rolled as a convenient answer |
| O12 | **Information services and recommendations** | A qualified contact sells a dated route assessment or second opinion, with funded terms and disclosed coverage | Medium; P11 N1/N2/M1. Quote does not leak truth through hidden value; buyer receives a useful report without acquiring the assessor's faculty |

O4–O12 consume the existing claims/context/means model. P14 owns changing belief history, P19 transport through social contact and P22 director inspection. A provenance graph can explain why an answer is weak without requiring a universal numerical Bayesian model. A source-authored omniscient faculty is a valid exceptional route once its actual scope is implemented.

## More time forms

| Code | New angle | Concrete suggestion / consumer | Cost and specific future gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q5 | **Many rhythms, one publication authority** | Personal appointment, school term, regional shipment and source arc each contributes a due boundary; composition creates a fitting enquiry | Medium/high; P11/P12/P20. Explicit clock mappings and causal order, no extra timers or double advancement |
| Q6 | **A window is not an appointment** | A recruitment season offers several meetings; a narrow ferry departure has its own funded terms | Medium; P11 schedules/P12. Exact, interval and event-relative dates stay distinct; missed window has declared useful aftermath |
| Q7 | **Waiting produces new evidence** | Repeat a sample over a real supported cycle, wait for an arrived letter, or watch a declared flowering condition | Medium; P11 M2/P8. Negative observation needs coverage; waiting never draws hidden truth again to guarantee progress |
| Q8 | **Memory age versus body age** | Generate reconstruction lessons for an old mind in a new body without falsely calling it an inexperienced character | Medium/high; nonhuman/P16/P23. Knowledge, practice and body/source remain distinct; no automatic free mastery |
| Q9 | **Retrospective mysteries** | A dated discovered letter opens a question about an already happened event; its clue graph is built from retained receipts | Medium; P14/O3. Occurrence and reveal order remain separate; a discovered flashback does not run old effects again |
| Q10 | **Intervals with deliberate time skips** | Summarize several supported quiet seasons while pinning pending work and player-selected interrupts | High; P14/P16/clock owner. Exact supported advancement and committed outcomes, no implied offline world simulation or skipped terminal gate |
| Q11 | **Continuing traditions after divergence** | A canceled war leaves a memorial plan changed into a reunion or a different civic occasion, when real organizers choose it | Medium; Q1/P13/P11 N8. No commemoration of an event that never occurred; known memories from another branch labelled and carried |
| Q12 | **Objects age on their own domains** | A manuscript, spirit bond and temporary preparation use their actual local exposure/time law after a crossing | High; P6/P8/temporal owner. No auto-expiry from the wrong realm's clock; measure retained exposure and crossing scope |
| Q13 | **Long-lived social cohorts** | A cultivator, ghost and ordinary household share a project while generations change around them | High; P19/Q3. Succession, learned relationships and actual life policy, no invented deaths from narrated centuries |

Each new option can compose with others. For example, O4 + P8 + Q9 yields a library enquiry: remembered cataloguing method, four distinct holders' perspectives and a dated discovered loan receipt. O8 + P4 + Q11 yields a warning, several witnessed responses and a tradition changed by actual prevention. P12 owns the arc structure; P24 supplies knowledge/time constraints.
