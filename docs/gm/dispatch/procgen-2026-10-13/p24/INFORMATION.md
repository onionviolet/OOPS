## O2.1: separate why a source is wrong

| Error class | Generate by a fitting cause | Useful player response |
|---|---|---|
| Outdated | A measured bridge route changed after the map edition was made | Survey, ask a current traveler, buy a newer edition, repair the route or use an existing detour |
| Incomplete | An author measured only the main road or omitted a method's operating condition | Fill a known gap, seek a specialist, test a partial method, or proceed with uncertainty |
| Mistaken | A real observation was misread or a transcription changed a unit | Compare independent measurements or reproduce the supported operation |
| Biased | A sponsor preferred one route or credited one participant | Compare perspectives, inspect evidence, negotiate, annotate or publish a reply |
| Deliberately forged | A capable actor altered a particular label, page or custody claim | Authenticate marks, examine the copy, interview a witness or recover a prior edition |
| Context mismatch | Correct instructions belong to another era, body, fuel, language or continuity | Translate, adapt with real testing, commission help or obtain the fitting method |

Keep content/work, edition, physical copy and annotation identities from P6. A stolen copy can still be accurate; a beautiful expensive book can still be wrong. Translation and copying preserve a source chain, so ten copies are not ten independent witnesses. Intent is not visible merely because the error is detectable.

Maps describe authored topology. Their uncertainty can concern access, condition, ownership, travel time or omitted known edges. A wrong map never creates a new canon road or procedurally generated layout. An exploratory route must resolve through the actual location/travel owner. Source-reserved books retain their sourced content and identity; generate original supplements, damaged copies or explicit AU editions only inside approved scope.

## O2.2: claims and corrections remain attributed

Reuse P14 Claim and BeliefEpisode, P11 message arrival and P6 edition/copy records. Add only error consumers' references: claimed subject/time, scope of validity, coverage, source lineage, contradictory claim refs, test/protocol refs, known correction and current status. Publisher reputation is an input to belief, not truth. A hearsay claim can be faithfully recorded without being accepted.

Shown/SOURCE: [knowledge write](../../../../../src/simulation_effects.js:281) stores stage/value/time/cause/basis and replaces a same-or-higher-stage record at the same key. That does not retain general contradictory testimony or a sequence of corrections. Proposal: keep distinct claims/belief episodes in P14 and publish a selected summary through the existing knowledge adapter. Do not downgrade an unrelated understood method to express one uncertain fact.

A correction is a new claim with evidence and recipient scope; it does not edit old books or retroactively change what the NPC said. Losing a physical copy need not erase learned understanding. A changed bridge can make an old map obsolete while it remains a correct account of its original date. Display “dated report” before implying fraud. Sealed IDs/routes/status only in reports; ordinary metadata must not reveal a hidden answer, secret existence or undiscovered alternate edition.

## O3.1: questions from actual unknowns

Select a question only where a real actor lacks relevant evidence and there is a supported action or explicitly labelled unresolved lead. Read P11's ActualContext and KnownContext separately. Four useful forms: determine a current condition, reconstruct a past event, test a functional hypothesis, and assess a forecast. These can become P12 quests/arcs; P24 supplies the question and evidence relation.

Logical operation:

```text
questionRef, scopeRef, askerRef, subjectRefs, propositionRef
knownEvidenceRefs, actualDomainOwnerRef, coverageStatus
candidateProtocolRefs, reachableEvidenceChannelRefs
resultRefs, hypothesesRefs, contradictionRefs, continuationPolicy
```

The actual owner already determines the matter/event in E7 K1 cases. Generate the question and fitting enquiries from it, never roll an answer after the player chooses a suspect. E7 K2 can require repeated samples of a real distribution; one sample is not a universal law. K3 first-observation binding needs an explicit world law, a saved commitment and consistency with prior interactions. A lazy backstory cannot overwrite an object's already functioning hidden property.

P11 M2's controlled experiments need a real resolver, actual sample/tool costs, known operating conditions, a declared comparison and recorded negative/inconclusive outcomes. For noisy processes, compare repeated supported trials without promising certainty from a tiny sample. Causal receipts can constrain a hypothesis; mere correlation is not proof. Unsupported simulation data yields “not established by these means,” rather than an invented answer.

## O3.2: evidence graphs without compulsory routes

Represent each hypothesis's supported predictions and each channel's reach, time, language/body requirements, costs and failure conditions. Preserve distinct witness, physical trace, ledger, reproduction, expert, exceptional perception and cooperation routes wherever the world permits them. Several may coexist and give different detail. No forced single clue, skill or correct dialogue order.

A selected solvable mystery must have at least one reachable adequate evidence route from the declared start and a useful alternate where the premise supports one. If actual causes destroy the only trace, allow a disclosed unresolved case or a new supported lead. Never move a clue into the player's pocket to rescue a planned ending. A difficult world may deliberately leave some questions unanswered; it must not advertise them as guaranteed solvable.

Use chronology carefully: “I saw nothing” is negative evidence only if the witness had actual presence, time coverage and detecting means. Three witnesses repeating one letter are one source chain. Two accounts can conflict honestly because they cover different dates or facets. A clue name, recommendation order, reward premium or unavailable button must not encode hidden correctness.

## O3.3: generated content, beyond picking a card

Original delivery dispute: the real ledger contains dispatch, transfer and arrival receipts, and an actual local need creates an enquiry. Assemble two accounts from each witness's real contact window, choose evidence protocols fitting the hero's means, and create an attributed resolution from the evidence acquired. A later funded request uses the established custody/arrival state. The culprit, failure or benign explanation is not invented by the resolution text.

Original method inquiry: an old field manual claims a tool works in all rain. Real work records show it only worked while sheltered. Offer a sheltered/exposed comparison through the supported tool/work resolver, or ask its maker for qualified instruction. The resulting annotated edition helps the next preparation without granting a power source. Do not silently enable an unimplemented weather damage rule to make this example work.
