## O1.1: distinguish the kinds of future knowledge

| Basis | What can be generated from it | What it cannot silently establish |
|---|---|---|
| Transmigrator remembers a source | A forecast tied to the remembered edition, cutoff and specific scene claims | Perfect memory, the right adaptation, or that this branch must follow it |
| Regressor remembers an attempt | A witnessed prior-cycle outcome, with its former conditions and actual memory carrier | Current affection, retained possessions, current-body mastery or unchanged NPC choices |
| Scholar predicts | An estimate from known plans, weather or records | Undisclosed future truth or omniscient prerequisites |
| Simulator explores | A labelled conditional result from an isolated supported model | An actual event, payout, or a promise of fidelity outside model coverage |
| Genuine foresight | The specific scope and accuracy supplied by a sourced faculty | Universal vision from a saved RNG state alone |

Root A3's saved-draw observation is useful, but the inference that the actual future follows “for free” is too broad. A saved draw determines a random choice only once its candidates, causes, future actions and world rules are specified. Future actors can act differently; source-authored events may use no draw. Offer scoped forecasts or isolated simulations with stated assumptions. [P11 N7](../p11/DREAMS_AND_OCCASIONS.md#n7-dreams-visions-and-omens) owns dreams/visions; [P16 loops](../p16/LOOPS_AND_RESETS.md) owns resets, and the simulator owner owns execution fidelity.

## O1.2: per-anchor, per-facet assessment

Logical fields, **not an API or reserved save schema**:

```text
forecastRef, knowerRef, carrierRef, originScopeRef, currentScopeRef
anchorRef, eventRef, sourceVersionRef, sourceCutoff, basisClaimRefs
rememberedFacets: actor | place | timeWindow | means | result
dependencyRefsByFacet, baselineConfidenceClass, assessmentRevision
appliedDivergenceReceiptRefs, internalApplicability, internalAssessment
beliefEpisodeRefs, disclosedAssessmentRefs, lastKnownEvidenceAt
```

A remembered appointment can retain a useful place and contact after its date changes. Confidence in a date is distinct from confidence in the person or the outcome. Source evidence mode, source quality, memory fidelity, internal forecast applicability and the character's confidence are separate axes. SHOWN does not mean inevitable; confident does not mean true.

Proposed evaluation: a committed P13 receipt changes a watched prerequisite. Reevaluate only affected facets and downstream declared edges in that branch. Dedupe by originating cause and changed prerequisite revision, including cascades, so a single intervention is not charged repeatedly through several paths. Independent anchors remain unchanged. Attempts with no applied change do not decay confidence. Changes by NPCs or other regressors count when they actually settle.

For soft dependencies, a prototype can use `c = c0 × product(1 - relevanceImpact)` over distinct active relevant changes, with impact weights authored per world and facet. This is a qualitative scoring aid, not a calibrated probability. Prefer wording unless a world grants numeric appraisal. Hard missing causes set applicability to `inapplicable` for that exact scene; missing model data sets `unresolved`. Neither is just a smaller chance roll. Surviving independent routes can leave the broader pressure intact.

Keep the original memory immutable. A later repair receipt can support a new assessment and restore some confidence if the required causes really return; it does not erase the intervention or pretend the old expected date remained correct. Confirming a current outcome produces current evidence, not validation of every other remembered scene. No automatic erosion solely from elapsed time, chapter count, unrelated shopping or total branch-change count. An enabled memory-loss law is a separate causal consumer.

## O1.3: confidence must not leak unseen changes

The internal P13 assessment may react immediately to a hidden settled change. Ordinary displayed confidence changes only after the knower receives relevant observation, testimony or an authorized divergence-sense receipt. Until then the character can be confidently wrong. A meter that silently drops when an unseen antagonist acts would be an unintended information power.

For each visible assessment, retain its evidence and arrival time. A known uncertainty can say “the route may have changed”; it cannot expose hidden alternative actors, sealed candidate counts or exact internal invalidation reasons. A genuine divergence sense can reveal a rough changed band according to its supported scope; identifying the cause or answer needs its own route. Player spoiler preference is separate from character knowledge and from sealed discovery.

| Player-only knowledge use | Allowed as player direction, tagged as external intuition, or a character action requiring a means; root/P23 owns the selected policy |

Strong, cheap, renewable and even exact foresight remain reachable where supported. No universal cooldown, fixed use count, uncertainty penalty or compulsory cost. A prophecy that enforces an event needs an actual enforcing actor/source, reach and counter, rather than a confidence formula that overrides missing causes.
