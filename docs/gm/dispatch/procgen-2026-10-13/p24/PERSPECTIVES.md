## P1.1: body changes the verbs and evidence

Cast by actual body/controller, senses, mobility, communication, social recognition, possession interface and usable means. Do not attach a generic human card to a different species name. Keep shared P11 context, P12 thread composition, P19 social edges and P3 commitment semantics; supply body-specific scene and consequence bindings.

|---|---|---|---|
| Beast | Familiar scent route, territory, kin/pack, safe shelter, actual food opportunity | Track a changed scent, mark or share shelter, approach/avoid a willing handler; learned route changes later travel | Diet, senses and social ties come from this body. All examples are INFERRED original proposals. Source-faithful nonhuman starts need the canon lane's selected source/version/era and exact body rules. Human starts, hybrids, changing forms, disembodied minds and other reasonable kinds remain reachable. An extraordinary sense can outperform ordinary sight without a forced drawback; its real scope supplies the gate.

## P1.2: thread anatomy by body

A thread uses `need/cause → sensed signal → reachable agent/means → supported act → applied result → known account`. Several body-specific readings can refer to the same cause. A repaired culvert gives a human a work receipt, a beast a restored crossing and a core a visitor-access change where those consumers exist. Shared IDs connect the stories; they do not copy rewards or knowledge to everyone.

Keep existential risk, fuel, nourishment, comfort and opportunity separate. Do not make a missing human shop or spoken dialogue a universal nonhuman penalty. A rooted or carried protagonist needs a consequential starting verb, optional queues, feedback and useful ordinary activity. Transformation preserves the person's actual prior story but rechecks present means, sense access and time profile. A sword's remembered route does not confer legs; becoming human does not erase its holder history.

Shown/SOURCE limitation: [causal protection](../../../../../src/causal_story_core.js:339) checks a living controlled human acting body and local contact. [Native bindings](../../../../../src/causal_story_native.js:34) have controlled-body validation. These are bounded original cases, not generic body adapters. Reuse receipt/observer patterns, not their human admission unchanged. Body-start authors need engine support before a P24 generator is called playable.

## P2.1: meanwhile has four information scopes

| Cutaway mode | What the player may see | What the character gains |
|---|---|---|
| Received account | What a real witness reports through an arrived channel | Attributed claim/belief, with time and basis |
| Established perception | What the controlled actor or linked sensor actually detects | Only that sense's supported scope |
| Dramatic irony | Opt-in player-only scene admitted by world/spoiler/disclosure policy | No automatic knowledge, relationship or targeting fact |
| Retrospective reveal | Earlier occurrence rendered after an authorized discovery | Current evidence with separate occurred-at and learned-at dates |

“Could plausibly learn later” is not sufficient permission to reveal now. Use a received account, perception route or an explicit player-only dramatic-irony policy. Even player-only scenes obey sealed discovery and spoiler policy; a dramatic-irony flag cannot unseal a changed truth. Narrator labels, scene selection, named locations and cast silhouettes can leak as much as dialogue.

Each scene binds occurrence/outcome refs, viewpoint actor, observed evidence, occurred-at/window, composed-at, delivery channel/arrival if any, audience scope, spoiler level, safe cast aliases and frozen text revision. P14 owns the accounts and P15 the rendering. Viewpoint thoughts need that actor's own established knowledge or an explicitly admitted narrative view; do not reveal author truth via a fake internal monologue. P23 owns inner-life generation. A partial witness can be sincerely mistaken.

## P2.2: offscreen does not grant a new clock

A cutaway projects an already committed event on its endpoint's supported clock. If the world is frozen while absent, a “meanwhile” cannot advance it just to have something to show. No live event exists? Show dated recollection or supported texture, or allow quiet. An isolated counterfactual scene is labelled hypothetical and creates no actual deed.

Shown/SOURCE: [feed](../../../../../src/event_feed.js:37) explicitly withholds global simulation logs; [received scene projection](../../../../../src/causal_story_scenes.js:63) uses admitted accounts/work/deadline; [host presentation](../../../../../src/causal_story_host.js:459) reads player knowledge. Preserve those information boundaries. The existing scene module is neither NPC viewpoint simulation nor a new content generator.

Player action from dramatic irony follows the selected M3 policy. External intuition may guide an ordinary choice where allowed, but cannot unlock a knowledge-required command or identify an unseen target by accident. If a world allows metaknowledge as a real cheat, record its carrier and grant through that owner. Player and character views can coexist without forcing one universal mode.
