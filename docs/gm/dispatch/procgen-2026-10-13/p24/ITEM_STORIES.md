# P3: items that carry histories and create holder events

2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** Extends **root P3 (items with stories)**, not dispatch P3 (the storylet deck). [E7 object lore](../../../../emergent/E7_OBSERVATION_AND_MYSTERY.md#lore-behind-objects-depth-on-demand), [P11 M9/M16](../p11/MORE.md), [P6 creative works](../p6/CREATIVE_WORKS.md), [P14 receipts/accounts](../p14/CONTRACT.md) and [P13 reservations](../p13/RULES.md#c5-reservations-secrecy-and-attribution) keep their existing owners. P24 adds history-dependent event consumers and wielder perspective.

## P3.1: four things an object can carry

| Layer | Authority and proposed use |
|---|---|
| Physical object identity and present custody | Existing item/stock/custody owners. Who can wield, lend, repair or sell it now |
| Actual biography | P14 receipts referring to exact maker, holder, repair, transfer and use outcomes. Past events stay true after sale |
| Claimed provenance | Attributed labels, testimony, catalogue entries, counterfeit marks and disputed family stories. They influence belief and appraisal, not the object's past |
| Active memory/agency | A qualified sentient/psychometric faculty, with scope and observer route. Object history alone grants neither a mind nor supernatural power |

An heirloom can be meaningful without a stat buff, and a plain tool can create useful social events. A named history is not itself a power source. When a world does make deeds empower an artifact, [P17](../p17/) owns that resolver and its sourced law; P24 supplies actual deed refs. Chronology, fame, magical condition, resale value and owner attachment can disagree.

## P3.2: event generation follows exact causes

| Trigger | Original generated story | Useful consequence and means |
|---|---|---|
| New actual holder | Former maker offers fitting care advice; a recorded claimant asks about a real loan | Paid repair, clarification, return, retain or negotiate through custody/right owners |
| Actual repair or replacement | A maker's known mark survives or is newly added; another repairer disputes a label | Better inspection, a provenance correction or a real service quotation |
| Recorded use with a witness | A rescued person recognizes the tool used; a wielder remembers its failure | Supported gratitude, changed preparation or another funded commission |
| Inheritance/transfer | A recipient learns part of the previous holder's known account | Shared study, family enquiry, remembrance or a disclosed obligation transfer |
| Lost/recovered object | A real transfer/travel trail supports a recovery lead | Trace, buy back, ask, commission recovery or abandon the enquiry |
| Qualified bond/awakening | A supported artifact actor forms a relationship with a willing wielder | Actual dialogue/influence and bond effects under the nonhuman/power owner |

Generate a fitting actor request and original account from retained cause, not a fresh legendary deed. A thief's possession, lawful ownership, loan holder, soul binding and controlling mind remain distinct. A hostile or mistaken claimant can make a claim without acquiring a right. Settlement moves only existing owned objects/funds or an explicit world-supported output; no extra “legendary copy” appears as a reward.

Wielder events read that holder's current goals, knowledge, compatible body/source and actual possession. The item does not select a new owner through text alone. A supported sentient refusal changes only the aid its law permits; it cannot drain unrelated stock, hijack the player or invent a universal moral alignment. Several holders may create different stories around the same object while the actual custody remains singular where the law requires it.

## P3.3: logical joins and identity pressure

Use refs to `objectInstanceOrLot`, product/definition revision, custody transaction, history receipt, claim/account, known evidence and supported bond actor. A generated background record carries origin `generatedBackground`, creation context and law revision; a lived record carries a settled outcome. A legend receives audience and source lineage. Do not add another inventory, history owner or artifact registry from this list.

Before implementation, inspect the actual native item identity/stacking contract. Class/inventory key is not automatically an exact instance identity. An ordinary homogeneous material lot can carry quantity-scoped provenance. Two distinguishable heirlooms need distinct persistent identity or a deliberate nonstacking adapter before their stories can be claimed exact.

| Operation | Proposed identity rule |
|---|---|
| Split lot | Allocate actual quantities from one parent lot; history links the parent, rather than granting every child the whole heroic deed |
| Merge | Preserve constituent provenance where queried; do not erase materially different functional/known histories to fit a stack |
| Repair | Record exact changed component/condition and repairer; preserve the prior damaged state as history |
| Reforge/consume | Old object ends or transforms under a declared rule; output has lineage refs, not automatic identity or transferred agency |
| Copy work | Share the work/edition; each real copy has actual count/custody. Copying its story does not copy a singular physical artifact |
| Fictional duplication | Allowed only through the selected world's actual mechanism and resulting identities/custody; preserve its authored scope |

Past offscreen ownership requires actual supported events or separately admitted background, never timer-generated biography for every lost item. An inaccessible object need not be fully simulated. If history is compacted, pin minimum values required by an active enquiry, bond, anniversary or claim. [Visit compaction](../../../../../src/world_visit_history.js:21) and [lossy biography](../../../../../src/life_record.js:1) show why arbitrary display entries cannot be the sole authority.

## P3.4: canon-reserved artifacts remain reachable

[P9 profile rules](../p9/PROFILE_RULES.md#evidence-and-generation-boundary) reserve source identity, laws and actual custody. Locate, transfer, steal, damage, recover, inherit, study, destroy or acquire a named artifact through a qualified route; reservation is not immortality or a permanent player exclusion. Reproducible ordinary source objects need their own manufacture/acquisition law. Explicit AU counterparts need authored IDs and differences.

Never randomly mint, clone, rename into a generated item, assign an unknown past, or shuffle the truth of a singular canon artifact. A vacated loot slot is not a licence to create a replacement. A source update pins its version; existing played provenance is not regenerated.

Public implementation fixtures remain original; direct recreation data belongs to the private-pack route with attribution. Sealed IDs/routes/status only in reports, no hidden identity through price, icon, title or claimed provenance.

## P3.5: first journey and future gate

Original repaired work tool: observe its actual damaged condition, complete a real fitting repair, lend the exact tool to a willing known worker, then hear their limited account of a supported work event. A later enquiry or funded commission reads the tool/holder/repair receipts. A forged label gives a different belief without changing the tool's actual repair or custody. A free decline remains available where the local terms permit it.

Gate: one retained history changes an actual later offer/work preparation; distinction between past holder and present holder; split/merge/repair policy; no duplicated unique item or reward; lost item and lost copy do not erase all knowledge. Exact pending loan, completed repair, item ID, known accounts and next draw survive Save/reload/Export/literal-file Import. Unsupported old item keys need explicit compatibility, not retroactive invented pasts. Sentient-item body/control proof is separate from ordinary provenance. All implementation gates **OPEN / NOT RUN**.
