# GX2 observation and intervention contract

2026-10-14. All schemas, names, costs and implementation paths below are proposals, not new authority or existing exports. Domain state keeps its owner. Reuse the [Cheat library OV/II model](../../../../CHEAT_LIBRARY.md#more-to-consider-cheats-that-show-things-the-overarching-model-and-implementation-2026-10-14), [kit CLR rules](../../../../worlds/WORLD_CORE_KIT_2026-10-14.md#cheats-that-show-the-kit-lens-cheats-cl), [P11 context](../p11/CONTEXT.md) and [story BI/BO](../../../../PROCEDURAL_STORY_AND_CONTENT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md#bi-too-many-contracts). This packet specifies only the generation adapters.

## C1: three operations with different effects

**Inspect** reads a detached snapshot and yields a transient projection. It consumes no draw, grants no knowledge and charges nothing. It may inspect internally retained truth, but player-visible projection is restricted by the observer and route. Viewing already learned information remains free unless the world explicitly requires a fresh measurement.

**Observe** is the actual lens action. It stages an authorized reveal, real costs and optional tell; saves knowledge and its receipt; then publishes. It changes what the player knows, not the subject's truth. A forecast is knowledge of a conditional model, not a fact that the event happened.

**Intervene** stages a named decision override, bias, transfer, branch or concealment policy. It saves intent, original/replacement receipt, actual cost and domain consequences together. A read-only lens cannot do it; a card may separately possess both capabilities. Appraisal never makes a better potion, People sight never changes a trait, Vein sense never creates stock. Powerful truth edits stay possible as separately declared actions with validated downstream repair.

## C2: semantic adapter, not a new universal store

Proposed minimum adapter: stable subject/decision ref, world-instance ref, owner/content revision, readSnapshot, projectForObserver, validateObservation, and staged commit hook. An intervention-capable owner additionally provides declared decisions, eligibility, apply/rollback and retention policy. Bind only the two selected consumers first, following library II12. GX4 owns wider GA/GI registry implications; no GX2 runtime reservation.

Observation semantics: observer, subject, property/protocol, basis (`shown|stated|inferred`), value or range, grade (`presence|category|range|exact|future`), observedAt, source/evidence IDs, world-law/subject revision, conditional assumptions, freshness/status and disclosure route. Store the value if later reconstruction would change it. Keep conflicting observations side by side with their basis; stronger grade does not erase testimony. Seal-linked knowledge holds IDs/evidence/stage, resolving authorized text through the sealed owner rather than duplicating truth prose.

Intervention semantics: action/cause ID, actor/card ref, target world-instance, stream/deck/decision key, expected owner revision, original receipt ref, requested rule/category, world adjective profile revision, real cost refs, staged replacement receipt, status (`pending|applied|cancelled|stale`) and downstream settled receipt refs. Pins/rerolls retain both original and new results. Save pending pins as intent receipts; save application receipts on consumption. Never put durable causal references only in P1's 32/256 or P3's 20-entry rings. Reuse P38/P34 receipt retention after actual integration, or extend the owning domain's existing record narrowly.

Revealed knowledge belongs to the player observer and feeds other player surfaces. It is not the NPC's knowledge, a source faculty, a taught method or a world fact. Underlying P2/people owners may keep compatibility projections (`observed`, `knownTraits`) while their authoritative observation metadata is bound to the selected knowledge owner. Decide that binding once with root/P34/GX4, not one per cheat. P2's `observed` flag denotes experimental dose today; GL1 must not set it merely to bypass an unknown tooltip.

## C3: one durable transaction

1. Resolve exact instance, ownership/means, supported revision and route; copy required owners, draws and native resources. Read context once and validate eligibility before any weighting.
2. Stage reveal or edit, charge and receipt on the copy. Forecast uncertainty/decoy appearance uses a separate saved reveal key, never the target draw. A pure exact projection needs no extra RNG. Keep original truth until an explicitly supported intervention applies.
3. Validate complete staged save, including native inventory/purse, knowledge, pending decisions, ring retention and next cursor. Write durably before UI/messages/rewards. Failure leaves originals intact. Concurrent action with changed revisions rejects or requotes.
4. Publish from saved state. Render, reload, load and Import validate and project; they do not enroll, reroll, charge or manufacture evidence. Repeating the same cause returns the prior receipt; a new action remains available by its world terms.

These are future implementation checks, not a claim all audited hosts already implement this sequence. Inspect validators privately without printing a private save or sealed truth.

## C4: cost and reach adjectives

A versioned world profile maps adjectives to local parameters at admission: free or taxing; effortless or ritual; near, room-bound, local or world-spanning; vague or exact; common or exceptional; private or conspicuous; repeatable, renewable or depleting; mundane or source-bound. A pure/no-cost cheat stays pure. Do not invent fatigue, cosmic debt, attention or daily charges for symmetry. Strong/unbounded access can remain available in a fitting world or explicit sandbox.

Operational budgets are different: forecast depth/CPU is measured and resumable; ring retention is storage; one particular outcome cannot settle twice; a held vessel is occupied. None becomes a fixed once gameplay cap. Multi-step/far/strong options stay reachable as expansions or service/ritual alternatives. World source restrictions are declared capabilities, not blanket bans on reasonable acquisition.

## C5: anti-softlock and secrecy

Ordinary work, acquisition, decline, cancel, safe exit and save/recovery remain usable without a lens. Missing optional owner means unavailable information, not false zero or a generated fallback. Costs cannot exceed owned/reserved resources; reject safely and show only publicly knowable reasons. Failed pin/reroll does not delete an existing opportunity. If target terms become stale before apply, cancel/refund or requote according to saved terms.

Impossible candidates remain vetoed before Bias/Pin. Powerful reality editing may add a valid category only through a different declared owner action with actual source/consumer. All-rarity or all-quality bands remain possible where eligible. Existing once-authored cards remain source facts; a future repeatable instance needs a new supported occurrence with a cause, not resetting settlement receipts.

Pre-route projections hide truths, selected candidate IDs, hidden counts, aliases, raw seeds/keys that derive a secret, causal chains, grades, rarity cues and eligibility/price/sort/error differences that would reveal it. Secret S1 can be represented in reports by ID/status, but even its existence needs the route's permission in play. Lens stacking intersects disclosure permissions; two individually weak signals cannot combine into an unauthorized truth oracle. Sandbox kit depth can report a missing capability without dumping sealed records; explicit unsealing is separate.

## C6: desktop surface and future gate

At 1366x768 use the existing domain's selected record and action beside a readable evidence comparison. Keep cost, grade, freshness, basis and ordinary actions visible; use left/right support regions for history and pins while one foreground transaction is active. Long evidence scrolls inside its reading region. Do not funnel every lens into another status window or hide useful panels behind collapsed headers. Spread, Shelf and Merged system remain options per UI CS2; overlays are optional and must also have text/focus access. Color is redundant with labels/glyphs. Respect Effects off and reduced motion.

Every catalogue slice requires own rebuilt fresh-origin journey, native changed-positive consumer, Save/reload pending and completed, Export and literal-file Import into a second fresh origin, comparison of truth/knowledge/resources/receipts/next draw, failed-write and old-save-absence checks, rebuilt 1366x768 and 1600x1000 evidence. Natural acquisition and seeded-native rare/long-window cases are reported separately. Human taste/balance, canon fidelity and independent sealed fairness remain distinct. GX2 runs none of these runtime gates.
