## W1: qualify the historical mechanism

Every selected world needs continuity, era, source horizon and locators for the **particular historical/textual mechanism** it will implement. A craft passage, author synopsis or wiki description is not evidence for that world's newspaper distribution, afterlife testimony or memory law. Below, **STATED-inherited** and **SHOWN-inherited** describe what the named analytical owner reports; P14 has not independently inspected those original scenes. **INFERRED/proposal** marks the suggested gameplay. **UNKNOWN** names an unresolved mechanism, never a ban.

Credit original works and creators/rightsholders in their selected packs; retain YAIRPG's attribution and LICENSE. Direct recreations belong in private packs outside git/public bundle under PROJECT's Book worlds direction. Public proposal analysis can name sources and give analytical locators; generated shipped examples use original content. A source's institutions are not automatically reliable narrators. Different adaptations and fan works remain distinct. Authored gaps, defined in [HISTORY H4](HISTORY.md#h4-canon-gaps-and-authored-facts), are permissions, not blank checks.

## W5: originals and unusual histories

[Original Frontier](../p10/ORIGINAL_FRONTIER.md) keeps authored campaign, existing geography and upstream uncertainty. Generate a small admitted background work record or extend D8's actual deed first. It is an optional low-join proof, never a prerequisite opening for canon play. [Original-world families](../p10/ORIGINAL_WORLD_FACTORY.md) can generate more extensive eras, institutions and public traditions under authored laws. [Odd worlds](../p10/ODD_WORLDS.md) can support new mechanisms with original sources/names. [Proving Ground](../p10/PROVING_GROUND.md) uses synthetic history and separate fixture retention.

Adjectives govern historical texture and options: oral, bureaucratic, abundant, censored, forgetful, long-lived, mutable, sacred, skeptical, interoperable or isolated. An archivist, paid witness, magical reader, purchased copy, private research, public performance and chosen silence can coexist where means allow. No fixed one-book, one-life, one-legend or once-per-world rule is imposed.
