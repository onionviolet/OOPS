## M22: larger reachable forms

**Competing histories of one original era** can let the player reconstruct a narrow supported sequence while interpretation stays open. **Historical reenactment** can stage a different ending as performance without changing the past. **Uchronia within the world** can be fiction or a qualified simulated future, separately labelled. **Post-ending histories** can summarize continued ordinary life without implying a new mandatory antagonist. **Cross-world translations** can change reception through actual carriers and compatible language, while both source and recipient worlds retain their own clocks and identities.

**Memory magic and deliberate erasure** are legitimate selected mechanics with explicit targets and carriers. Erasing an NPC's memory need not erase a debt document or physical trace. **Belief-shaped worlds** can make a public legend cause new consequences where authored laws permit it. **Historical revision through time travel** can change actual state through the timeline owner's selected operation; the previous branch/player record and external observers follow that operation's declared retention policy. P14 supplies the before/after evidence, not a general rewind engine.

No harder capability has to receive an artificial drawback or rarity restriction. Local costs, repeatability and record survival come from world adjectives and actual means. Implementation still selects a finite observable consumer, saves its causal data and verifies it before claiming it works.
