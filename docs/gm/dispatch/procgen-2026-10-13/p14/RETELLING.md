# P14: claims, retellings and growing legends

2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** Extends [P5 D8](../p5/D8_LORE_GRAMMAR.md) and [X7](../p5/EXTRA_IDEAS.md), with [P11 N5/N6](../p11/INFORMATION_AND_LIBRARIES.md) and [P6 creative works](../p6/CREATIVE_WORKS.md). Those owners keep fact storage, transmission, circulation, copies and supported learning/performance effects. P14 adds the content transformation and its epistemic audit.

## R1: truth, belief, assertion and reception

An actual event, a speaker's belief, their assertion and an audience's belief are four different things. Deliberate deception requires a mismatch between the speaker's believed state and their assertion plus intent; an inaccurate witness is not automatically lying. An author-level evaluator may know a claim is false. The player sees “unverified” until their own evidence establishes more. Confidence, prestige, repetition, literacy and institutional authority do not establish truth.

Proposed account compilation:

1. Select a reachable author/speaker and only their known evidence as of composition. Their purpose, role, relationships, literacy and available medium constrain the possible work. A commissioned account still needs actual access to its subjects or sources.
2. Select claims and a declared transformation: faithful summary, selective omission, framing, exaggeration, conflation, misattribution, mistaken date, mistranslation, satire, invention or intentional lie. Decide this content at commitment, not when the player checks it.
3. Bind credited author, actual author if different, sponsor, intended audience, source lineage, time, language, genre and frozen claim expressions. Keep author intent and hidden provenance private unless discovered.
4. Render through the P15 pipeline from the account's permitted claim set. Save the resolved passage/summary and edition; reject unbound names, facts, effects or future knowledge. Fiction may explicitly invent content, but that content stays tagged as fictional assertion.
5. Publish through P6 production/performance and P11 distribution. Audience beliefs update only after actual receipt/comprehension; supported social actions still need means and local policy.

False statements can be defined without inventing a new true event. A claim might assert that actor B performed a recorded repair by A, or that a never-recorded additional payment occurred. Internal verification stores its comparison/basis separately. Public account payloads do not include a hidden `isFalse` marker, truth ID mapping, deception roll or sealed candidate count.

## R2: bias has dimensions

| Axis | Possible concrete change | What must remain separate |
|---|---|---|
| Access | Only heard the ending; absent during preparation | Unknown details versus deliberate omission |
| Loyalty / grievance | Praise a patron, minimize a rival's contribution | Their motives versus the objective work receipt |
| Audience / genre | A tavern song compresses names; a school text standardizes dates | Entertainment, pedagogy and factual precision |
| Incentive / sponsor | Sell copies, attract pupils, justify a budget, recruit followers | Sponsor's claim versus issuer's actual legal authority |
| Memory / translation | Reverse sequence, confuse two holders, lose a qualifier | Original source and each later mutation |
| Censorship / danger | Suppress a name, circulate anonymously, use allegory | Missing public detail versus proof of a particular hidden cause |
| Interpretation | Disagree about intent, importance, justice or sacred meaning | Values and disputed explanations versus checkable events |

Do not make every institution dishonest or every ordinary witness reliable. A propagandist can state true facts selectively; a devout scholar can correct a false chronology; an adversary can offer accurate testimony. Source labels stay SHOWN, STATED, INFERRED or UNKNOWN even when the author is powerful or admired. Histories can contain contradictory claims without a universal truth detector or mandatory midpoint compromise.

## R3: medium-specific generation

The suggested contents below are original designs. Exact franchise formats and supernatural text behavior need source qualification in [WORLD_FIT](WORLD_FIT.md). Each medium can be texture-only, explicitly labelled, or use its named supported consumer. No universal reputation/skill buff is implied.

| Medium | Generated content from receipts/accounts | Bias/error opportunity | Concrete optional consumer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gossip / rumor | Short attributed claim about an event/person, or an explicitly ungrounded assertion | Missing qualifier, misheard identity, intentional insinuation | Ask a witness, compare a record, commission an inquiry; P11 N5 relays it |
| News / gazette | Headline, dated incident summary, credited sources, known correction column | New information presented as new event; sensational scale | Decide useful preparation or approach a real current job |
| Song / ballad | Refrain, brief original verses or performance synopsis, named subjects | Compress many helpers into one hero; rhyme-driven date/name distortion | P6 performer presents it to a reachable audience; listeners' recognition can change |
| Book / memoir | Title, short chapters/synopsis, claim/citation graph and author standpoint | Self-credit, forgotten contributors, disputed edition | P6 work/edition; P11 library access, copying, annotation or study |
| Play / pageant | Cast roles, scene sequence, dialogue intents and staged ending | Multiple people conflated; satire mistaken for testimony | Actual rehearsal/performance/commission with supported audience response |
| Propaganda | Sponsor's selected claims, slogans, omissions and distribution purpose | True success recast as sole institutional achievement | Funded recruitment/petition or reputation action only for informed audiences |
| Scripture / commentary | Tradition, attributed revelation, historical references, interpretation lineage | Metaphorical versus literal dispute; interpolated commentary | Qualified ritual/study/community action; text alone does not make a deity or miracle real |
| School curriculum | Edition's lesson order, agreed chronology, source extracts as original summaries | Canonized error, omitted labor, outdated map or method | P6 teaching protocol and actual learning prerequisites; offer an evidence-backed revision |
| Wanted poster | Issuer, jurisdiction, allegation, description, status/date and funded reward terms | Mistaken identity, stale charge, forged issuer | Verify, contest, evade, negotiate or fulfill an actual bounty under its owner |

A wanted allegation does not make a crime true, and the printed bounty does not mint payment. A historical description is not a live tracker. A textbook may teach an inaccurate belief; that does not change physical law. An enchanted or reality-altering text needs its own source, target, function, fuel/authority and disclosure route. Acquiring a book or chanting a ballad does not silently gain that power.

## R4: player legends grow through retelling

Start from an actual deed, witness account or deliberate invented story about the player. Save a **legend lineage** linking editions and parent claims. Growth is driven by actual retellings, performances, sponsors, collected testimony or local interest; fame is not an automatic all-world broadcast. A formidable but unknown hero may have no public legend. A quiet helper may become locally beloved. An invented celebrity can be famous without the alleged deeds.

Proposed mutation operations can combine: simplify collaborators, move location to a familiar place, magnify scale, attach a title, conflate two lives, attribute an ordinary achievement to a legendary predecessor, conceal a living contributor, or expose a newly verified detail. Each mutation creates a new claim/edition with parent links. Repetition with the same ancestry is not independent corroboration. Bound mutation depth/fan-out for storage and pacing, not a fixed three-hop law for every world. A printing network, oral culture or supported instantaneous channel has its own adjectives.

**Original example:** actual records retain the hero supplying timber, a craftsperson making a joint repair and a neighbor organizing access. A local song credits “the traveler who fixed the storehouse.” A later patron's pageant omits the neighbor and claims the whole settlement was saved. The ledger keeps the joint repair; the player can enjoy the song, commission an accurate version, challenge the patron, stay anonymous, or ignore it. No forced either/or between fame and quiet life, and no automatic demand to correct every harmless distortion.

If a legend changes behavior, name the audience, what they heard, their interpretation and actual action. A collector might fund an offer for the provenance-bearing tool; a rival might ask for a contest; someone might seek an introduction. Neither hearing nor believing grants them the player's hidden build or location. [Rival owner](../../../../RIVALS_IDENTITIES_AND_FATE.md#a-persistent-rivalry-system-for-this-game) and P12 control responses/arcs, rather than a new reputation system here.

## R5: contradiction, correction and investigation

Keep mutually incompatible claims side by side. A contradiction record identifies claim refs, incompatible fields, scope/time and known evidence, not a narrative decree that one side is evil. Two accounts can both be partially correct. Distinguish “no supporting record,” “contradicted by observed evidence,” “intentionally forged,” and “source continuity unresolved.” Lack of a surviving record is not disproof.

A reachable check can use a present witness, dated custody receipt, physical trace, independent record, expert method, translation or supported psychometry. Its reliability and costs follow local laws. New evidence appends a correction/retraction/challenge. The original edition remains readable as a historical artifact. P11 carries corrections to actual recipients; they do not teleport to everyone who heard the rumor. Recipients can acknowledge evidence while retaining a value judgment or private grievance.

Sealed truths remain opaque IDs in public artifacts. A selected discovery may authorize a specific revelation; a rumor grammar is not that discovery by itself. A wrong account must not systematically reveal the true answer by negation or by predictable candidate elimination. Lint and browser proof must inspect titles, summaries, list ordering, item prices, accessibility labels, debug output and action availability, not just paragraphs.

## R6: endings, unusual worlds and more

Posthumous fame may differ from what a living player knew. A branch's private player chronicle and its in-world obituary need different audiences. A cosmic entity reciting a life uses only its declared observation powers or received evidence; scale does not grant universal omniscience. [COSMIC_EVENTS](../../../../COSMIC_EVENTS.md#engine-shape) owns intervention and presentation; P14 supplies evidence-backed content.

Further reachable cases include pseudonymous editions, erased credits, oral genealogies, forged decrees, annotated translations, competing calendars, player-authored testimony, a play that changes a law, and a world where belief genuinely changes reality. For that last case, belief creates a **new causally supported world effect** under the world's law. It does not rewrite the prior receipt silently. P16 owns meta/crossover/loop behavior; P15 owns wording and generation technology.

Future proof: one actual event supports two conflicting accounts and a changed later edition; show one mistaken believer, one deliberate liar and one accurate source with distinct internal states; a reachable check corrects only informed recipients; reload/import and grammar updates preserve all editions and their lineage. No live system is claimed by this document.
