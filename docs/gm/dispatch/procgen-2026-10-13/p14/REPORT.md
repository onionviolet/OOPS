# P14: generated history, chronicle, legends and in-world texts

2026-10-14. **DOCS COMPLETE / ALL PROPOSAL.** P14 changes no runtime, world owner, source library, build or save. No commit was made. Root owns integration and commits. [CHECKPOINT](CHECKPOINT.md) is the resume surface; [BACKLOG](BACKLOG.md) names the remaining future acceptance gates.

The proposed result is a world with a past worth investigating and a player life worth rereading: generated eras leave real survivors, actual deeds become an inspectable chronicle, and people turn limited accounts into gossip, songs, books and legends. Those stories can be wrong and still affect people who actually hear them. They never become event truth merely through repetition or polished prose.
