## HP-G: generated attachment and canon pull

Gap stories: study partners, greenhouse work, supplier disputes and school support. Offscreen: a licensed shop, ordinary household or other sourced school cohort. Before canon: an adult supplier career with no later childhood cast imported. After canon: repair, education, family and livelihoods with appropriate surviving actors. Concrete generator suggestion: a supply worker's known shortage plus actual stock produces a commission, a funded lesson and a later changed preparation opportunity through P11/P12.

INFERRED design: Harry resists through learned means, allies and source-qualified protections, not a hidden general protagonist flag. Institutional terms and independent antagonist motives create pull. Helping a classmate changes that relationship; it affects a future investigation only if access, testimony or timing is actually dependent. A delivery intervention can change whether an actor encounters a dangerous item. Recompute relevant exposure events and offer compatible investigation/repair rather than a replacement victim.

Inapplicable scenes include an encounter with an actor absent from that anchor, an artifact-mediated trip whose actual object was removed, and a lesson with a unavailable teacher. Distinguish delayed report from canceled event. Actor ignorance can persist after the engine knows why the scene changed.
