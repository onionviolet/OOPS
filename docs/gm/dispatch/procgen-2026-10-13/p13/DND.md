## DD-A: spine must be selected per campaign

STATED-rule source contract distinguishes revised core/SRD, legacy rules and the Phandalin lead. Selected adventure bodies are not yet qualified here. The following is an **INFERRED/proposal adventure-class spine**, deliberately not asserted as a sourced Lost Mine of Phandelver plot. This gives concrete structure while preserving its missing source gate.

| Candidate anchor / partial order | Participants / prerequisites | Fixed versus mutable |
|---|---|---|
| DD-A1 town before a chosen published adventure | Authored setting era, actual named inhabitants/office holders, lawful party/body/gear and local access | Edition rules and entry history fixed; original clients, downtime work and party composition vary. Bind adventure title/version/page before calling its event canon. |
| DD-A2 active objective → clue delivery | Published named role/objective if selected; original witness/courier only in a declared vacant role; clue custody, route and independent faction motive | An authored clue's prior existence is fixed; its arrival, interpretation or loss can change. No duplicate clue artifact to repair a missed route. |
| DD-A3 supported confrontation after access prerequisites | Actual participants, keys/paths, spell/monster edition, motivation and resources | A missing key/actor/access invalidates that exact scene; negotiation, later pursuit or a different real route may remain. No arbitrary replacement named villain. |
| DD-A4 outcome → downtime/next era | Actual defeat, settlement, institution and stock deltas | Earned outcome stays. Further contracts, civic work and later threats can be newly generated; a module's expected finale does not guarantee a world's ending. |

To populate actual canon: choose setting/DR-era independently from rules edition, then one adventure or novel continuity. Extract named hinges, independent faction plans and date windows from that source. Table play is its own committed continuity; a published module's possible outcomes are not all simultaneous historical facts.

## DD-G: content and resistance

Generate repair/service contracts, eligible downtime, regional encounters and ordinary residents on authored geography. Use a named table only after its body, die expression, edition and exclusions are read; P9's DMG contents alone do not establish its result weights. Original homebrew content is labelled and remains separate from canonical spell/item definitions.

INFERRED design: party choice is a major disturbance source. Named NPCs resist using actual stat blocks, allies, goals and logistics. Divine/fate pressure needs setting evidence; the DM/generator is not automatically an omnipotent diegetic actor. Changing a faction's supplies can delay a siege without preventing an unrelated festival. Saving a witness changes available testimony, not everybody's knowledge. If the expected dungeon goal is removed, generate a supported aftermath or new client need rather than unowned replacement treasure.

Per-world switches: edition/errata, setting, adventure/novel precedence, calendar, party/cohort, lawful generation method, d20 uncertainty policy, deity/patron reach, module outcome graph, rest/concentration/crafting rules, homebrew overlays and ongoing table continuity. Repeated livelihoods stay reachable under their actual costs, demand and renewal.

Reserve adventure-essential named people, exact mysteries, unique artifacts/deity identities, named hoard custody and occupied offices. Ordinary generic SRD creatures/items may instantiate legally; a stat block's existence does not authorize a new named deity, artifact clone or role vacancy. P7/P9 retain species/class/background versus actual capability.

Fan hooks: civilian town life, alternate party, SI with fallible module knowledge, villain-side faction worker, fix-it protecting a source-qualified witness, relationship AU or changed era. Limits: SRD cannot source adventure chronology; publication year is not DR year; revised and legacy crafting progress differ. Gate a selected hinge with source pages plus intervention/abstention/independent-event proof before claiming canon divergence.
