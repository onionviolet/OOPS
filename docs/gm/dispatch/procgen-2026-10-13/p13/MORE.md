## Connected combinations to keep reachable

M1+M17 makes a saved prevention become a disputed public play with a new repair commission, using the same receipts. M3+M7 makes two qualified pupils negotiate shared instruction, rather than forcing one to be permanently locked out. M6+M15 lets a distant festival proceed while a regional crisis changes its supply, separating local joy from headline events. M8+M20 lets two imperfect readers revise different plans from one actual intervention. M13+M18 lets a source-ending branch continue with ordinary care while later sources remain separately pinned.

[Cosmic events](../../../../COSMIC_EVENTS.md) and [Rivals](../../../../RIVALS_IDENTITIES_AND_FATE.md) keep actual actor reach, scarce attention and personal motives. A cosmic intervention is not P13's generic correction tool. [Starts/endings palette](../../../../STARTS_ENDINGS_AND_RANDOMNESS_PALETTE.md) preserves wider bodies/eras/closures, while selected actions need actual supported consumers.

## Qualification examples

Canon-plausible means “fits the selected laws and known constraints,” not “the source explicitly depicted this new event.” A world owner can qualify an original service scene as INFERRED/proposal without pretending its wages/dialogue are canon. A canon-critical replacement, unique power, named historical family edge or extra ritual seat needs its own authored source/AU rule.

Causal closure, viewpoint, actor moral judgment, outcome quality and player's preference are independent. Do not filter villain-side or successful powerful interventions out simply because they depart from a familiar hero story. Likewise, do not recast coercion or an unfulfilled forecast as freely chosen cooperation or accomplished destiny. Keep source contradictions and independent people visible through the unbiased lens.
