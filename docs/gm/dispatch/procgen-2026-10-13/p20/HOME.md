## H5.2: home episodes have an actual trigger and a useful response

| Code | Cause and generated episode | Reachable actions and real consequence |
|---|---|---|
| H5.2a Visitor | Known person receives an invitation or has an actual reason/channel/access to visit | Meet now, arrange a later window, use a public place, lend a supported tool or decline. Calendar/visit and custody change only on commitment. No forced affection or invitation from omniscient history. |
| H5.2b Renovation | Actual plan, material or condition makes a fitting improvement possible | Build, repair, commission, borrow a station, study or keep ordinary work. Installed capability changes a specific job; memories can mention who helped. |
| H5.2c Neighbors | A witnessed noise/use/access conflict or mutual-help episode | Agree hours, mediate, compensate, move supported work or disagree. A negotiated schedule writes its owning service rule, not a prose-only peace flag. |
| H5.2d Pets/dependents | Actual admitted being, willing custodian and local bodily/environmental needs | Play, train, request care, seek a fitting habitat or supported relocation. P11 ecology/companions owns life/upkeep; no implied closed-browser death or universal mandatory feeding. |
| H5.2e Security/loss | A selected actual actor has motive, means, access and a qualified hazard/encounter cause | Observe, conceal, secure, negotiate, recover or accept a loss under declared local rules. Theft moves exact existing goods, with known evidence and reachable recovery; it is not a nightly random inventory tax. |
| H5.2f Return/legacy | Actual absence, ownership succession or supported carrier transition | Compare dated last-known condition, greet people who know this life, recover an owned object or continue a kept practice. A new life does not inherit recognition/title/custody merely from player memory. |

These episodes can overlap without requiring permanent either/or roles. A home can be workshop, sanctuary, salon, rental, garden or sect hall under qualified local permissions. Hosting and solitude are both viable. Multiple homes can share a known correspondence channel while retaining distinct endpoints, stock and rights. Hospitality or display can be texture without a reward; distinguish descriptive value from a supported mechanical service.

## H5.3: smallest connected story

S20-3 original fixture: after an actual supported fixture installation, an introduced neighbor who learns of it asks for one appointment to inspect or use it. Freeze site, participant, quoted terms and time. A public demonstration or private visit can coexist as supported choices. Reserve the actual shared station and each actor's own tools/materials; queue the work on eligible played time. Result uses the existing real craft/service consumer; a home episode links installation → received invitation → actual visit → completed work → later account. No visitor can acquire the owner's notebook contents, concealed goods or private source by simply entering.

Do not replay Neri's existing permanent invitation choice. A later repeatable appointment/visit is a separate new cause and owner-approved adapter, preserving legacy residence claims. New ordinary visitors can return under local adjectives, without a global once cap. A housing claim and a fixture reservation are different resources; visiting neither transfers title nor displaces a resident. If interrupted or the fixture is removed, follow frozen wait/refund/cancel terms without a new roll or unsupported output.

Cost medium, with higher risk for theft/succession. Dependencies: H1 place links, P6 actual installation/service, P11 M1 appointment/N1 funding and context, P3 event delivery if integrated; P4 optional original guest through a verified identity bridge. An authored willing contact is enough for the first proof. Any canon home starts at its qualified era/body/title snapshot; Frontier home acquisition is not mandatory.

Proposed HomeStoryBinding references authored place, actual title/access/occupancy owners, fixture/job refs, invited actor refs and knowledge/message/history refs. Those references do not mirror furniture, rooms, stock, relationships or calendars. Branch/cycle policy preserves or resets each owner explicitly.
