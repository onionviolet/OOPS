## H4.1: ask what survives before calling it haunted

| Class | Proposed retained carrier | Real consumer required before a mechanical claim |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence trace | Site/sample signature and dated observation; not necessarily stored energy | Compare observations, establish a route or improve preparation through a supported sensor. |
| Physical aftermath | Actual damaged/contaminated object or environmental condition | Real repair, cleaning, access, material exposure or ecology effect. |
| Persistent field/blessing | Source-qualified site binding, support/fuel and effect law | A fitting target/operation receives a real declared effect while support remains. |
| Continuing agent | Actual identity/body/source, goal, knowledge and reach | A source-qualified spirit/ghost can act, communicate or receive care. Text alone never creates its consent or memories. |
| Recurring imprint/echo | A world-supported replay phenomenon, distinct from a person | Observation or timed interaction changes one supported outcome; replay does not imply resurrection. |
| Collective account | People/accounts saying a place is haunted | Rumor/investigation/social reaction only, unless that world separately has an actual belief-to-reality resolver. |

Intensity of emotion, number of deaths or storyteller tension is not a universal conversion formula. A harmless workshop can carry a moving human story with no supernatural field. An intentional ritual can create a lasting effect without a death if its selected world law supports that. Powerful, cheap, renewable or persistent sources stay possible through local rules. No generic price or lifetime use cap is added.

Proposed effect record references: originating applied outcome, authored place/site, carrier or agent identity, local law/source snapshot and revision, formation predicates, retained actual inputs, target/reach, current support, exposure clock, decay/renewal/clearing transitions, observer evidence and mechanical consumer. These are logical fields mapped to owning source/effect domains, not a universal mana store or appended fields in `world_instances.sites`.

## H4.3: contradiction register and lifecycle

R1: E7's suggestion that things near deaths drift toward haunted is an option, not source-independent law. R2: P10 energy vocabulary is not fungible; don't upgrade trace evidence into fuel because its name contains energy. R3: COI layer/loop-date and Shadow Slave source-coverage disagreements remain their owners' unresolved issues. R4: source-imprint and contamination/removal accounts have different evidence modes and scopes; avoid blending them. R5: HxH condition/activation and Fate cross-work conflicts remain [P10 C3/C4](../p10/SOURCE_NOTES.md), not universal vow rules.

Formation is an actual committed effect from qualified causes. Store carrier/support and resolver revision. Decay, renewal, relocation, cleansing, exorcism, reconciliation, containment and studying follow separate world laws and exposure clocks. Clearing a current effect records a new receipt; it does not erase its history, witnesses or surviving agent. A destroyed carrier can end an effect without removing learned knowledge. Never claim cleansing solves an unresolved identity or grants the cleaner a copied source.

S20-5 candidate: source-qualified JJK trace survey at one authored site, or explicitly labelled original trace fixture while primary law is pending. Obtain dated observation through real means, preserve/compare evidence, choose a preparation with a real changed consumer. That proves trace mechanics only, not ghosts, blessings, post-mortem agency or all book worlds. All future gates OPEN: unsupported observer sees no private signature; source/decay/handling exact; no generic fuel reward; changed/repeated causes produce valid new traces without reload rerolls; effect removal and old account remain distinct; pending survey/knowledge/next draw roundtrip; sealed ID routes independently reviewed if selected; rebuilt desktop evidence and human acceptance remain separate.
