## PAU1.a: standing orders with hysteresis and honest stops

Order record: stable order ID/revision; learned method/revision; eligible output predicate; exact ingredient-selection policy; receiver; lower trigger and upper target; per-input protected reserve; acceptable forecast/assay policy; priority; permitted station/worker; game-time applicability; repeat policy; pause/stop reason and last admission/settlement refs. A threshold counts actual usable stock held in the chosen receiver plus eligible owned in-flight output, excluding sold/promised/quarantined/other-party stock.

Original proposal: keep ten observed draughts, trigger below five, preserve three herb packs. With four eligible vials and no held output, admit one batch. Before admission, check that available stock after debit still meets the reserve. A held batch counts once toward the target; completed output replaces that in-flight count. Repeat admission continues toward ten, rechecking terms each time. Near the lower threshold, hysteresis prevents continual pause/restart chatter. Numbers are editable world-local proposals, not defaults required everywhere.

No silent substitution. Default learned P2 method uses its exact original packs. Optional explicit policies may select oldest known-compatible lot, player-ranked alternatives or supervised unknown trials if a supported law exists. Pin each admitted batch's exact key/amount and resolved terms. Editing an order changes future admissions; the held job keeps its original revision. Shared stock uses one atomic allocator with explicit priorities and a fair tie rule. Protected reserves apply across overlapping orders; they cannot each count the same three packs as spare stock.

Stop reasons distinguish input shortage, protected reserve, station/worker occupied, inaccessible site, unsupported revision, receiver full, changed known forecast and saving unavailable. Display the specific public/known reason plus remedy. A secret predicate cannot announce its hidden truth as a shortage explanation. On shortage: stop safely and retain the order. Offer Retry now and an explicit Resume when restocked policy; no polling that repeatedly spends admission resources or draws. Fixed once caps are absent; world adjectives can make work cheap, limited, generous or inexhaustible through actual laws.

At 1366x768, order list and current stock remain beside a selected-order editor; show trigger, target, input floors, next admission costs and stop reason together. Keep Pause/Resume/Cancel current batch distinct. At 1600x1000 add a compact recent-settlements pane. Selection, scroll and numeric input survive updates.

## PAU2.a: production chains are linked real jobs

Represent a plan as references to existing stage methods, stations, intermediates, transport manifests and receivers. P8 owns harvest/cargo; P6 owns drying/extraction/bottling/other transformations. CR3 composes their supported stages. The illustrative grow → dry → extract → brew → bottle → deliver chain is not provided by P2 today. First candidate chain can use two actual stages once one extra adapter exists.

Admission binds only the next payable stage, rather than conjuring all future outputs or locking scarce stock forever. Reserve actual shared tool/station/worker and output capacity. A completed intermediate enters its real holder, then the next stage admits against current stock/access. Backpressure pauses upstream admissions if downstream receivers are full. A stage can produce several accounted outputs with distinct destinations. Transport time/custody comes from P8, not instantaneous transfers between remote stations.

Acyclic plans are easiest initially. Reachable recycling is also reasonable: represent it as explicit repeated stages with actual costs, new occurrence IDs and a progress boundary, never recursive instant resolution. Report a detected deadlock with known occupied resources; allow reprioritizing, partial collection or canceling untransformed work. Pausing a plan does not freeze a world-law aging process unless that law permits it. CR4 owns [NPC workers and their compensation](../cr4/NPC_CRAFTERS.md); a worker requires skill, willingness, wages/terms and exclusive labor capacity, not a generated name alone. Reuse world-scoped material truth with actor-scoped methods/observations; a worker's assay reaches the hero through a real report/teaching route, rather than setting the hero's discovery flag directly.

## PAU3.a / PAU4.a: worker execution and quality routing

PAU3 is the integration surface for CR4's worker contract. Each admitted batch freezes the actual maker's supported skill/style and available means. A helper can operate while the player is elsewhere only after a real autonomous-labor/time adapter exists. P2 currently requires the player free in Village for settlement.

Quality is a vector tied to a receiver: known recovery interval, observed side effect, purity measurement, strength, consistency or container suitability. There is no universal additive quality score. A player-visible route evaluates the operator's known measurements, certification or an explicitly purchased assay. Raw unseen truth cannot sort a perfect high-grade bin. World-supported automated instruments can measure a property through their own observation route and then route the real batch.

Original rule set: certified target grade → reserve receiver; lower measured grade → practice receiver; unmeasured result → quarantine pending test. Unknown and failed are different. Offer player-directed manual use/assay, storage, lawful trade or supported reprocessing. No automatic drink, sale or discard as an unstated fallback. Automatic destinations require the player's saved policy and actual receiver capacity/terms. Classify once by durable receipt; moving bins does not repeatedly grant discovery or commission credit. Lower-grade routing cannot pretend an output is fertilizer until its actual consumer supports that use.

If a forecast meets target but actual execution misses it, store both forecast basis and observation. Output remains owned; choose Stop after first miss or Continue and route under policy. The forecast never suppresses actual side effects. CR1 owns [body/use law](../cr1/DOSE_AND_TOLERANCE.md), CR2 [world-law compatibility](../cr2/ORIGINAL_WORLDS.md), CR4 [economy](../cr4/ECONOMY.md). CR1's RS2 observation-backed safe-work threshold can stop an opted-in routine before its next exposure boundary; a constitution with a supported immunity/no-hazard law can instead permit continuous work.

## PAU5.a: batch planner over known recipes

Planner inputs: chosen learned methods, current exact free stock, protected reserves, station/time horizon, receiver demand, known quality constraints and allocation objective. Offer Make most doses, Minimize known cost, Meet a selected request and Leave rare stock untouched as selectable objectives. Show alternatives, not an imposed either/or. Initial planner can be a transparent greedy allocator; label it heuristic and show the concrete plan. Claiming optimality requires a solver and proof for that bounded objective.

For one fixed method with per-batch quantities q_i and free stock a_i after reserves, count bound is min floor(a_i/q_i). This is only an ingredient bound: station, receiver, eligible time and current jobs can lower actual admission. Multiple methods compete for the same a_i, so do not sum independent maxima. Show allocations by exact keys and projected leftovers, with a reason for each bottleneck. Current P2 has whole packs and one vial per resolution; no promise of fractional input or multiple-serving output.

Preview is read-only and does not enroll the mixture owner, allocate IDs, spend stock, settle work or draw. The plan records stock/context revisions. Run plan revalidates and stages before writing; if stale, give a new quote instead of silently selecting different rare stock. Known forecasts and uncertain constraints remain explicit. Experiments use a separate opt-in trial plan, not automatic identification of every makeable product.

## SC1.a–SC3.a: scale is a law and capacity choice

Distinguish repeating N normal vials from one N-times-larger process. P2's uniform quantity multiplication preserves its concentration-normalized dose, changes physical mass/product identity and still yields one whole vial. Its 0–99 pack controls are technical admission bounds for revision1, not a world rule about maximum batch size. Preserve that revision's behavior; add new scale semantics explicitly.

## Scheduler and cancellation contract

Do not promote P2's UI poll into a factory clock. Its one-due-batch settlement followed by `due = currentClock + 3` is shown in source; a large clock jump does not prove catch-up equivalence. A new scheduler must traverse eligible boundaries independent of panel visibility, snapshot the conditions that each stage law reads, and commit each occurrence exactly once. Catch-up work can be computationally chunked while retaining its next boundary; do not discard overdue material outcomes. Existing P2 jobs need an explicit compatibility decision before changing their timing.

Initial new automation can remain played-time only, with declared free-player versus staffed/unattended applicability. Travel, sleep, other work, world switching and closed-browser progress are separate policies. No offline claim without selected semantics and elapsed/chunk equivalence tests. Cancellation returns exact untransformed escrow. After an irreversible stage, publish actual intermediate/residue and refund only remaining held inputs. Canceling order intent differs from canceling a paid batch. Completed output is never refunded as original inputs after a render failure.

Persist order/plan/method revisions, exact held input keys, capacity ownership, stage/progress/eligible clock/next boundary, receiver, observation and settlement refs, stopped state and committed draws. Unsupported meaningful state rejects preflight before replacing a save, or uses an explicitly chosen inactive-retention policy. Old owner absent remains absent. Future gates: shared-stock races, threshold hysteresis, shortage/restart, full receiver, quality uncertainty, pending/final Save/reload/literal Import, stale quote/write failure, cancellation on both sides of transformation and scheduling equivalence. All NOT RUN.
