# CR3 collection and learned principles

Status: PROPOSAL ONLY, 2026-10-14. Extends root CL1–CL2 and PG2. The [root palette](../../../../PROCEDURAL_CRAFTING_AND_CONSUMABLES_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md) retains their definitions. Use P2's retained products/observations and P8's [site/batch observations](../p8/REPORT.md); do not invent a competing material, site or knowledge registry.

## CL1.a: potion codex that groups without erasing variants

Open from the crafting workspace or notebook. At 1366x768 show a family list, discovered variants/comparison table and selected product's observed use plus reproducible method. Include owned/free/held count, observed route, tested body/protocol, method revision and last observation. At 1600x1000 a second pinned product makes dose/process comparisons easier. Histories scroll inside their reading panes; mobile optimization deferred.

P2 retains exact product records after consumption, so an observed consumed vial can remain an entry. An unobserved product in current inventory gets a neutral made/seen record. Broader seen-in-shop entries require an actual sighting receipt, not a scan of raw product truth. One observed P2 product cannot identify every ratio or family member. Labels remain Made, Seen, Tested under this protocol, Method saved and Principle supported. A product can have several of these states independently.

Group by known carrier/preparation, visibly observed form or supported family evidence. Unknown members cannot be grouped by hidden healing/sedating potency. Inside a group select exact variants by safe local handles, shown inputs and observations. Similar known response may group different canonical keys visually; it must not merge stock, change saved identity or equate unknown provenance. Store player names as notes, never chemistry.

Do not expose P2's serialized canonical key as the visible entry ID, URL, copy text or accessibility label. It embeds internal composition data. Use a neutral view handle mapping to the exact internal reference. Player comparisons and exports of *journal notes* contain only observer-authorized evidence; a journal export is not a full save and must never become an authority that rewrites physical truth.

## CL1.b: honest completion in a combinatorial world

There is no universal finite denominator for all ratios, scales, routes and future packs. Present discoverable authored milestones and coverage of an explicitly known catalogue/protocol. A player can choose a bounded challenge, such as comparing both known preparations for each of their learned recipes. State its scope, eligible known entries and revision. Finishing it does not mean all world products are known.

Do not show hidden recipe counts, sealed silhouettes or a changing percentage that reveals a secret's presence. Only give a world percentage if that world's public rule explicitly provides a finite disclosed set. An open world can show “12 methods tested; 3 questions unresolved” instead of a false 12/100. Missing dormant pack entries stay retained and marked unavailable from current known access, without exposing new hidden names. New ordinary pack content creates a new coverage scope, not retroactive failure of an earlier completed challenge.

Technical distinctions: product identity count, measured-response grouping and recipe count are separate. P2 uniform scale changes identity even with equal normalized dose. A collector can retain both entries while the table groups equal observed response. No reward for endlessly making scale-only duplicates unless a world explicitly values that production; milestone evidence must demonstrate the named principle.

## PG2.a: field guide and herbarium

Guide entry references observed specimen/batch, known location or site, harvest part/handling, visible form, player sketch/description, acquired sample custody and dated observations. Species/family hypotheses are separate from confirmed identity. A dried sample records its changed state; it is not the fresh plant's entire truth. A physical specimen lives in an actual holder, while notes can remain after it is spent. Never clone a specimen into a guide inventory on render.

P8 supplies acquisition/site and P6 [agriculture](../p6/AGRICULTURE.md) supplies living lineage. CR3 adds comparisons and follow-up questions, not a second generator. P2's current four fixed ingredient IDs and generated profiles are not a regional flora census. First guide can record paid or actually gathered P2 ingredients and the exact product trials they contributed to, with no unearned botanical appraisal.

Offer Compare fresh/dried, Compare region and Compare harvest part only when those actual batches and supported laws exist. Unknown trait values remain blank/questioned. Origin can be stated by a trader without being shown by harvest; retain that attribution. Route maps use known authored sites, not undiscovered placement truth. Custom sketches/notes are optional original content; no raw novel text is copied into the guide.

## CL2.a: milestones unlock a method of reasoning

Unlock criteria reference evidence diversity and demonstrated distinctions, rather than a flat brew count. Original candidate: complete one matched pair that isolates preparation and another independent pair that supports the same inference; then learn a “compare preparation” protocol which aligns recorded trials and suggests a controlled next test. The exact count/threshold is an editable original law, not canon or a universal cap.

A protocol improves predictions only within its validated family/route/condition scope and measurement resolution. It does not reveal every hidden fraction, manufacture a skill bonus or certify a false theory. New contradictory observations can narrow the model; keep the acquired protocol and prior evidence. Broader principles may come through experimenting, study, a teacher or an authorized appraisal cheat, all via actual acquisition. CR4 owns teachers and [recipe transmission](../cr4/MAKERS.md); CR2 owns [world/cheat observation law](../cr2/CHEATS_AND_CRAFTING.md).

A failed experiment can contribute a useful distinction; identical repeated tests can improve an authored uncertainty estimate if the measurement model supports it, but do not automatically earn endless discovery credit. Negative/inconclusive evidence is not silently counted as a positive example. No imposed either/or specialization or permanent first-choice lock.
