2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** “More to consider” is deliberate expansion. These extend the menu with distinct uses; rank describes a local first proof, not a reason to remove later options. [STRUCTURES](STRUCTURES.md) covers base forms; [CONTRACT](CONTRACT.md) covers common lifecycle.

## M1–M15: angles beyond a linear quest chain

| ID | New angle / concrete suggestion | Actual consumer and extra gate |
|---|---|---|
| M1 **Obligation webs** | One repair promise connects borrower, owner, helper and issuer; offer partial restitution or a consenting novation to another performer | N1 custody/terms plus actual new consent. Transferring an obligation is distinct from handing over an item; old claimant/reward cannot remain live twice |
| M2 **Research arcs with negative results** | A failed test narrows a question, enables a better-controlled experiment and creates a useful instruction request | P11 M2 and P6 method/creative-work owner. A negative result preserves spent stock and actual observation; do not draw a desired answer after the experiment |
| M3 **Institutional plots** | A workshop needs to fill a vacancy while two qualified people pursue it through training, references or sponsorship | P11 M5 owns role capacity/issuer. Appointment changes real authority/access, while losing applicant can pursue another role or private practice |
| M4 **Collective success** | Several small contributions restore a common station; reward/access follows each accepted contribution and actual commissioning terms | Share one project and reservations across arcs. The player's final action cannot take sole credit for NPC work or issue duplicate full payments |
| M5 **Counterplans and decoys** | A rival announces one goal but funds another; surveillance can reveal preparation without proving intent | Actor beliefs, actual payments, access and witnesses. Decoy is an admitted deliberate act, not text generated to explain an inconsistency after the fact |
| M6 **Changing identities in an arc** | A disguise, new office or supported body change affects access while the same person remains responsible for a promise | Identity owner and rival contract; reference actual controller/person/custody, keep aliases observer-scoped. Name equality cannot merge actors or transfer their debts |
| M7 **Quiet middle chapters** | After a repair, two people keep working together before a later invitation or disagreement becomes fitting | Real appointments/shared projects and relationship observations. Quiet is viable progress; do not make every calm window spawn a disaster |
| M8 **Asymmetric interpretation** | The issuer records a favor, helper records a paid job, bystander thinks it was manipulation | D8 fact/account plus P11 rumor transport. Sifter can identify differing interpretations, without choosing one as truth or universal friendship |
| M9 **Opportunity interference** | Two NPC plans compete for a teacher's appointment; funding a second session resolves competition when means exist | Existing schedule/consent/fees, not a forced winner/loser menu. A scarce world can retain real contention; a generous world can support both |
| M10 **Failure to notice** | An ignored clue remains a trace until its actual decay; later expert assistance gives a new investigation route | Evidence owner records trace age/coverage and supported detection. Absence of a remembered clue is not absence of the incident; no UI leak of unseen clue count |
| M11 **Reframing an old deed** | Newly discovered evidence changes a person's view of an earlier delivery, opening restitution or renewed trust | Add a new attributed account and relationship response. P14 owns retelling; P12 only coordinates a new goal from the new belief, without rewriting the deed |
| M12 **Persistent unfinished ambition** | A character's local commission ends but their desire to travel, learn or establish a business survives | Goal remains separate from one arc; new request needs new means/cause. Dormancy releases reservations and carries no indefinite player pause |
| M13 **Player-declared goal** | Player chooses “help this person settle” and game identifies supported approaches from that person's willing goal and local housing/work means | [P16 authorship](../p16/PLAYER_AUTHORSHIP.md) owns player-authored/meta content; P12 uses the same action graph. A typed intention is not consent from the person or a new house deed |
| M14 **Negotiated endings** | A feud closes through restitution while a shared career begins; a romance becomes friendship or a practical partnership | Each participant and obligation closes separately. P16 owns larger ending/continuation policy. No single good/bad ending score erases mixed outcomes |
| M15 **Crossover aftermath** | A transferred actual tool enables a foreign-world job, but title/qualification/language may not transfer | P16 and travel/power owners own carriers and compatibility. P12 attaches only after legal identity/custody/means resolve; familiar tropes are not a conversion law |

## M16: structural variety without a giant content matrix

Use a small operator grammar with separate world-qualified method sets and observer-safe realization. Record coverage by meaningful differences: goal, dependency topology, cast continuity, available routes, real stakes, knowledge, expiry and aftermath. Multiple wordings of the same mechanical graph are text variants. One generic “collect 3 X” graph with ten nouns remains one structure; an assisted, negotiated or investigated solution is another structure only if its actual actions/causes differ.

A compiler/lint proposal can check role existence, graph dependencies, declared fact readers/writers, compatible means, safe free expiry, every terminal's reservation disposition, secret projection and source locators. P15 owns content-authoring tools and text QA; P12 supplies arc-level invariants rather than a competing pipeline. Include difficult cases in content review: player already solved the problem, issuer mistaken, actor leaves, nobody willing, helper later becomes recipient, event resolved out of expected order, and a very strong player bypasses several preparations.

## M17: preferences and playful modes remain composable

A peaceful life, a romance, villain play, research career, rivalry and communal project can coexist, constrained by actual local compatibility and available time. Offer priority/autonomy dials as proposals: notification interest, explicit paid delegation, quick summaries, longer conversations or risky expeditions. None should turn into a compulsory one-path class or permanently delete unchosen reasonable goals. Ordinary useful UI stays available; framing a feature as a cheat belongs to its existing owner.

A world with prophetic or fate-based structure can have authored exceptions, including generous/no-cost outcomes. It must declare who can cause them and how they become known. D9 does not secretly punish successful play or invent a stronger villain to restore pacing. [E5/D9](../p5/D9_STORYTELLER.md) retains local pace; [E7](../../../../emergent/E7_OBSERVATION_AND_MYSTERY.md) retains epistemic variants.

## M18: evaluation worth collecting later

Measure natural time to first meaningful action, unresolved commitments, alternate route use, repeated cast recognition, distinct causal graphs and save growth. Keep causal validity, playability and human interest separate: a valid planner can produce dull stories; vivid dialogue can describe invalid events. Pair an actual intervention trajectory with an unchanged control, use seeded-native traces for isolation and ask the creative director about fiction/pacing only after a playable result exists. Counts of generated titles or text tokens are weak evidence of interesting arcs.

Offline stories, population-wide intrigue, free-form LLM action invention, broad romance/social practice coverage and general narrative planners remain possible future scopes. Their first proof should name a real useful consumer and its extra owner joins. They are not required to finish the bounded Village recommendation in [REPORT](REPORT.md).
