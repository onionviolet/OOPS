## M16: structural variety without a giant content matrix

Use a small operator grammar with separate world-qualified method sets and observer-safe realization. Record coverage by meaningful differences: goal, dependency topology, cast continuity, available routes, real stakes, knowledge, expiry and aftermath. Multiple wordings of the same mechanical graph are text variants. One generic “collect 3 X” graph with ten nouns remains one structure; an assisted, negotiated or investigated solution is another structure only if its actual actions/causes differ.

A compiler/lint proposal can check role existence, graph dependencies, declared fact readers/writers, compatible means, safe free expiry, every terminal's reservation disposition, secret projection and source locators. P15 owns content-authoring tools and text QA; P12 supplies arc-level invariants rather than a competing pipeline. Include difficult cases in content review: player already solved the problem, issuer mistaken, actor leaves, nobody willing, helper later becomes recipient, event resolved out of expected order, and a very strong player bypasses several preparations.

## M17: preferences and playful modes remain composable

A peaceful life, a romance, villain play, research career, rivalry and communal project can coexist, constrained by actual local compatibility and available time. Offer priority/autonomy dials as proposals: notification interest, explicit paid delegation, quick summaries, longer conversations or risky expeditions. None should turn into a compulsory one-path class or permanently delete unchosen reasonable goals. Ordinary useful UI stays available; framing a feature as a cheat belongs to its existing owner.

A world with prophetic or fate-based structure can have authored exceptions, including generous/no-cost outcomes. It must declare who can cause them and how they become known. D9 does not secretly punish successful play or invent a stronger villain to restore pacing. [E5/D9](../p5/D9_STORYTELLER.md) retains local pace; [E7](../../../../emergent/E7_OBSERVATION_AND_MYSTERY.md) retains epistemic variants.

## M18: evaluation worth collecting later

Measure natural time to first meaningful action, unresolved commitments, alternate route use, repeated cast recognition, distinct causal graphs and save growth. Keep causal validity, playability and human interest separate: a valid planner can produce dull stories; vivid dialogue can describe invalid events. Pair an actual intervention trajectory with an unchanged control, use seeded-native traces for isolation and ask the creative director about fiction/pacing only after a playable result exists. Counts of generated titles or text tokens are weak evidence of interesting arcs.

Offline stories, population-wide intrigue, free-form LLM action invention, broad romance/social practice coverage and general narrative planners remain possible future scopes. Their first proof should name a real useful consumer and its extra owner joins. They are not required to finish the bounded Village recommendation in [REPORT](REPORT.md).

|---|---|---|
| M19 **Personal undertakings without a quest giver** | Master a craft, establish a refuge, map a route, assemble a meaningful collection or help a willing person reach their goal. A long undertaking has useful intermediate achievements and can change direction. | Extends M12/M13 and S1b. Goal tracking reads actual learning, access, objects and people; it does not create a reward purse or consent. A milestone still matters if the player retires the larger goal. |
| M20 **Cross-world requests with directional settlement** | A home-world patron funds a journey to obtain a method, material, witness or specialist, followed by adaptation and delivery. Outward travel and useful return both matter. | Extends M15/S3a and N1. Bind issuer, actual cargo/person, applicable law, terms and receiving-world acceptance. An item can arrive intact yet fail a promised function; truthful disclosure and renegotiation follow the contract rather than automatic quest success. |
| M21 **Several contributors, several terms** | One expedition supports a scholar's sample, a merchant's delivery and a healer's treatment research. Different participants can benefit without sharing every goal. | Extends M1/M4/M8 and S3j. Shared capacity and custody are real; credit and rewards settle each agreement. One scarce object promised twice creates a conflict or renegotiation, not duplicated delivery. Splitting a sample works when its material law and terms permit it. |
| M22 **Recurring service becomes a relationship** | Repeated ordinary work can earn familiarity, a particular invitation, mentoring, partnership or a disagreement. A building-complete flag does not automatically supply teachers, students, medicines or public support. |
| M24 **Multiple approaches that meet the real goal** | A protection job can use escort, route design, transport, negotiation or a working barrier. Research, craft, commerce and force contribute through different means. | Extends S2/S3a and AD38. Completion checks the demanded outcome and actual terms, not a profession badge. A payer may care about proof or refuse a changed contract; a successful physical solution need not automatically meet every agreement. |
| M25 **Aging opportunities and a legitimate dormant goal** | Some offers expire, some ambitions wait, and some subjects change independently. Long play needs all three rather than treating every untracked hook as urgent. | Extends M10/M12 and CONTRACT expiry. | M26 **Closure, maintenance and aftermath** | Finishing a local conflict or commission gives a real conclusion; living with the repaired place, changed bond or surviving consequence can continue afterward. | Extends M14 and S4/S5. A later event must not silently reopen a paid commission, resurrect defeated threats or charge the completed work again. |
| M27 **Player as issuer, sponsor or teacher** | Publish a funded commission, hire qualified help, support a student's project or organize a common effort. | M28 **Information, custody and proof as different objectives** | Discovering a treatment, producing a vial, bringing its maker and gaining permission to teach it are distinct quests that can intersect. | Extends M2/M6/M8/M15 and S3e/S3i. Preserve knowledge, objects, people, access and IP/secrecy where local institutions actually recognize it. A convincing report need not be true; an authentic object need not prove who made it. |
| M29 **World-specific quest authority** | A promise, job, civic duty, bounty, religious charge or supernatural System quest can use different incentives and enforcement. | Extends procedural-story D3 and S1. Ordinary funding/agents enforce ordinary terms; a world entity grants effects through its stated mechanism. A quest label does not establish cosmic punishment, universal objective truth or permanent pursuit tracking. |
| M30 **Power, refusal and opposing purposes** | A powerful character can solve a former obstacle cheaply, refuse work, outbid an opponent, join a scheme or pursue a selfish goal where the mechanisms support it. | Extends S3c/S3d/S5/S6 and M17. No mandatory stronger replacement enemy or alternative chore restores a desired arc length. Actor responses need actual knowledge, motive and means; an ignored plot can proceed, fail or stall according to its own support rather than the player's quest log. |

**ACCOUNT FOR NOW:** the quest graph is a coordination/recognition layer over actual causes, not a second world-state authority. Where appropriate, expose known terms, relevant evidence, progress, next supported approaches and a useful record of closure. Optional tracking and reminders are presentation; accepting terms, spending, disclosure and changing a plan are commands. A journal should distinguish a personal idea, a received offer, an agreement, work performed and a report someone approved. The campaign can be long without a global checklist requiring every quest.
