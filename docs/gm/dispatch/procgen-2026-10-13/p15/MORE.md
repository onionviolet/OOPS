## M3: grounded extensions to world voices

P9's per-world profiles retain different evidentiary limits. Its [Soldier profile](../p9/SOLDIERS_LIFE.md) records an earlier stated belief contradicted by a later shown exception; a dialogue pipeline can preserve both with dates instead of rewriting the old speaker. [COI](../p9/CIRCLE_OF_INEVITABILITY.md) retains conflicting date/ontology evidence; the writer must not smooth it into a confident narrator explanation. [Shadow Slave](../p9/SHADOW_SLAVE.md) retains a mismatched digest item row; a fluent description cannot repair that source error. These are summaries of the profiles' contradiction records, not new primary canon verification.

For synopsis-only worlds such as [Traveler's Gate](../p9/TRAVELERS_GATE.md), author a clearly original prototype voice or obtain a selected primary speech scene later. Do not call genre imitation canon dialogue. For [TTKS](../p9/TTKS.md), five worlds get separate vocabularies, source windows and institutions. For [P10](../p10/REPORT.md), adaptations/continuities stay separate and school rank, office and actual ability remain distinct. No public source secret or changed hidden truth is needed to specify this pipeline.

## M4: combinations worth making concrete

An ordinary request can join X1/X7: a partially understood notice reaches a translator, whose delayed reply creates an attributed clarification while the funded work remains unchanged. A friendship conversation can join X5/X6/X15: the same admitted person uses an earned nickname and harmless shared joke, with a reviewed voice corpus. An in-world handbook can join X3/X9/X12: concise explanation and fuller narrative share one P6/P14 claim graph, and a revision diff exposes changed instructions. A source-aware negotiation can join X4/X13/X18: dated terms and a changed law lead to a visible requote, not retroactive alteration.

Each combination should start with one supported action and one visible changed response or understanding. Keep the other options available; local adjectives such as familiar, formal, multilingual, deceptive, telepathic, inexhaustible or freely repeatable decide what a particular world can support. Save integrity protects one occurrence from duplicate settlement without limiting future conversations, requests or authored works globally.
