## One original expanding thread

X1: A new fitted tool changes real work. A known customer commissions another from actual funds. An apprentice learns the tested edition and pays for their own materials, adapting one step to a different supported station. Their result is useful but has a recognizable different finish. A competitor buys a sample and independently tests a cheaper substitute. These are receipt-linked changes, not four unrelated story rolls.

X2: A festival commissions several presentation variants while a repair cooperative shares real apparatus and transport. One reseller claims the apprentice's batch came from the original maker. A retained reference sample and custody account allow investigation; two messengers citing the reseller are one source root. The goods remain usable under their actual law even if attribution is disputed.

X3: The maker can license, publish, specialize, partner, change presentation, compete on price, teach the cooperative, sell the workshop or pursue another life. Retirement can transfer a tool and edition while the audience separately decides whether the successor deserves the same trust. A later surviving old-life object follows its actual clock and custody. Each branch adds a useful consumer and preserves the other reasonable routes.

These are hypothetical connected fixtures, not source events or selected gameplay. They require the owner adapters named above; no prose-generated event manufactures tools, money, knowledge, bodies or powers.
