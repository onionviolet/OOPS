## SO1: gifts read preferences and circumstances

SO1.1: Give a recipient a scoped preference model: liked function/taste/appearance, body compatibility, cultural meanings, known history and current need. Some facts are public statements, some observed reactions, some uncertain inference. A stated sweet preference and a visible allergy warning do not imply omniscient detection of every constituent. A player can ask, remember an earlier gift, consult a friend through actual contact or try an uncertain gift.

SO1.2: A gift's actual properties determine supported bodily/use outcomes. Received claims, presentation and recognized maker/history determine perceived meaning. These can disagree: a beautiful bitter tonic may disappoint despite working; a modest fitted tool can be treasured; a false branded gift can initially impress and later be disputed. Mechanical compatibility and social satisfaction do not collapse into one rarity score.

SO1.3: Stage offer → acceptance/decline → actual custody transfer → optional use → observed reaction as separate steps. Accepting a bottle does not automatically drink it. A recipient may save, share, display, sell, return or refuse the object. Giving a unique heirloom transfers it rather than duplicating its biography. Consumption uses CR1's recipient adapter and does not apply effects to the hero by default.

SO1.4: Repeat gifts remain legitimate. A collector can appreciate repeated matching pieces; a fulfilled need may make another identical dose less useful; an abundance of gifts can affect expectations under a local relationship law. Count new real episodes, prevent replay of one transfer, and use generous, formal, suspicious or reciprocal world adjectives rather than a universal once-per-person or daily quota. Affection, indebtedness, obligation and willingness remain distinct.

First slice can use known process, presentation and dose ranges already supported by a selected adapter. Sweetness, allergies and taste require real product/body fields; P2 alone has none. Unsupported preferences can be labeled texture but cannot decide physical acceptance as though implemented.

## SO2: the healer role is practice, not a title buff

SO2.1: Cases derive from an actual supported condition, reported symptom or request. Diagnosis records uncertainty and protocol. Treatment requires eligible recipient, route, dose and effect consumer. P2's health regeneration is a precursor, not proof of a general illness/cure model. A mistaken report can be investigated without changing body truth.

SO2.2: Becoming a healer changes who contacts the maker, who trusts them and who offers help through observed treatments and accounts. Reputation can be mixed: effective in one specialty, costly, kind, experimental or unavailable. A title/credential depends on an actual issuer; informal village reliance can exist without certification. Spec recipes, purchased medicine, referrals, hired experts, teaching and charitable work coexist.

SO2.3: An appointment reserves the actual staff/station/time, dose and declared funds. The player chooses paid work, gifts, barter, standing outreach or ordinary adventuring. Gratitude can change later access, information or assistance through supported episodes; it does not create a payable debt unless terms and world authority establish one. No unattended global patient emergencies or forced feeding/death routine.

SO2.4: Record intended treatment, administered lot/dose, immediate observation and later outcome separately. A recovery receipt can support future trust without granting the hero knowledge of hidden causes. Treatment failures, partial relief and adverse effects reflect real chemistry/body/law; an extra ignorance danger roll is unnecessary. Disclosure, consent, coercion and investigation are authored local acts and consequences rather than inferred universal prohibitions.

## SO3: contests evaluate actual work and stated standards

SO3.1: A contest/exam has admitted participants, available means, task/spec, evidence protocol, timed stages, judges, funded stakes and distinct rules for entry, measurement and awards. Objective targets (actual dose, fit, yield) can coexist with presentation preferences and a judge's bias. A fraudulent or mistaken judge changes the award claim, not the product truth.

SO3.2: Support open-recipe, blind-sample, constrained-stock, team, timed or exhibition events. These are per-event formats; winning one is not the sole route to mastery or guild access. Several repeat contests and practice opportunities remain possible. A beginner can observe, assist, buy a sample, enter another bracket or ignore the event.

SO3.3: Submitted product enters exclusive judging custody. Measurement consumes a sample only if declared; remaining goods have an explicit return/use policy. Reopening results cannot reroll score or pay stakes again. A masterwork roll follows [MK3](MAKERS.md); award ties/subjective weights have their own committed cause/draw where needed. Judge knowledge cannot reveal sealed truth through rankings, refusal text or hidden score fields.

## SO4: plots use the same products and limited knowledge

SO4.1: A plan binds actual actor, motive/belief, acquired recipe/stock, manufactured product, delivery route, target contact, exposure and observed aftermath. Rumors of poisoning are accounts; author truth about a dose stays separate. NPCs use [the same resolver](NPC_CRAFTERS.md), not a story-only magic debuff.

SO4.2: Read route and circumstances. Blade coating requires a real coated object/contact; sleeping tea requires ingestion and body compatibility; a scent needs supported spread/exposure; a truth draught needs the local mind/communication law. Social effects do not silently grant control of another actor. Strong mind powers, resistance, consent, deceit and nonconsensual plots remain selectable world-supported mechanics with fitting consequences, without a universal ban or automatic romance result.

SO4.3: Fix the relevant truth at its actual committed boundary, before detection. Evidence can come from receipt/custody, witnessed handling, sample assay, victim observation, confession, seal comparison or a supported appraisal power. Preserve alternatives: intervene, substitute, reveal, bargain, cooperate, investigate, prepare a countermeasure or decline involvement. Detection does not force one accusation or final moral choice.

SO4.4: First original plot candidate is a disputed delivery with a known label and retained contents, then later a supported sedative or antidote case once CR1 admits that consumer. It can turn out to be mishandling, mistaken instructions, deliberate adulteration or a false accusation under saved original causes. Reports preserve contradictory accounts; public output never states a sealed culprit/truth. No raw source scenes are needed to invent this fixture.

Later observed use can add a different reaction. A third uninformed neighbor retains their prior opinion until a P11/P19 message arrives.
