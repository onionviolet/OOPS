## Two connected original showcases, hypothetical only

X1: **The remembered carrier.** Paid guard in a practice contest → participant
knows who intercepted the attempt → willing merchant offers a delivery → cargo
arrives under real terms → a receiving-capacity problem produces a funded repair
request → next partnership uses improved packaging. Same cast, independently
supported causes. No battle win required to obtain ordinary work.

X2: **The road after the campaign.** Real route restriction → held supply →
finite local shortage → survey or negotiation → reopened route → excess arrivals
→ clearance goods → restoration project uses the surplus. Declining every offer
still leaves ordinary play. Treaty news can be disputed until delivery/witness
evidence arrives. No war engine, forced crisis or offscreen destruction is claimed
by this imagined sequence.

## Boundary cases worth retaining

Evidence absence cannot mean “nothing happened” or identify a secret. Repeated
rumors from one source are not independent proof. A partner's death can leave an
estate obligation without making their successor the same person. Currency reform
requires an issuer/time/conversion rule, not silent purse rewriting. Infinite debt,
free conjuration and other exceptional means need their real scope and cost
model; an ordinary finite balance formula must not silently erase the exception.
Free refusal, parallel pursuits and later return remain fitting possibilities.

Source constraints versus invention, faction authority versus reputation,
capital versus profit, promise versus realized result, participant experience
versus public legend, and recorded history versus playable branch restoration
must stay separate in every chosen slice. [P18](../P18.md) owns QA metrics and
[P22](../P22.md) director tooling; P21 supplies domain fixtures and observable
failure cases rather than another harness/editor design.
