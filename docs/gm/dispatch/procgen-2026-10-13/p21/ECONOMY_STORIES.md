## J1.1: generated economic episodes read actual balances

Proposed original episode: a delivery is interrupted for a supported cause →
available unreserved repair stock falls below funded current orders → merchant
quotes a finite buy order → player/importer supplies it → shortage closes →
later excess deliveries can soften future demand and produce a clearance offer.
Every transition names source lots, orders and applied transfers. A coin flip
cannot independently label the village prosperous or ruined.

Distinguish on-hand, reserved, in transit, offered for sale and believed available.
Net accessible stock can read current unreserved supply plus reachable arrivals
minus committed demand, only inside the chosen time horizon. Forecast supply is
not already owned stock. A boom also needs actual paying demand and productive
capacity; a price increase alone is not prosperity. Busts can follow completed
orders, overcapacity, lost access or withdrawn funding, with supported causal
receipts. Missing broad economic measures stay unavailable.

| Extension | Concrete generation | Useful return |
|---|---|---|
| J1.2 Local shortage | Compose a dated buyer request from actual uncovered need | Deliver, commission, import, negotiate quantity or decline; payment comes from that buyer |
| J1.3 Player-made glut | Player's real sale joins existing saturation and supply accounting | Lower next offer with an explanation; seek a different buyer/use, process goods or wait for supported demand recovery |
| J1.4 Capacity boom | Funded repeat orders meet a real bottleneck | Venture in tools/training/transport; later exact throughput or delivery availability changes |
| J1.5 Bust and reuse | Orders end but stock/tools/actors remain | Refitting, ordinary work, sale, partnership or research; no forced bankruptcy solely for declining new jobs |
| J1.6 Expectations | Actor receives a dated forecast or rumor and changes a future order | Profit/loss follows actual purchases and events; false news does not rewrite physical supply |

## J1.7: one price calculation and clear terms

Keep the existing price/margin/saturation owner. For one opt-in supply-managed
group, jointly declare how supply, demand, delivery, law and recognized customer
terms feed that calculation. Retire legacy refill for that group only, preserving
protected campaign stock. Do not count the same sold lot as both saturation and
new scarcity without an explicit reconciled formula. Start with P11's separate
finite buy order while this integration remains unresolved.

Every quote carries currency/rounding, quantity, payer/issuer, revisions, validity,
binding reservation status and known reasons. A changed preview is requoted;
accepted reserved terms do not mutate. Fear, respect, pity, suspicion and actual
jurisdiction can each raise, lower or close future offers if their consumer exists,
following the [user's price direction](../../../../USER_VISION.md#world-depth-and-canon-first-world-priority-2026-10-13).
Do not impose one universal fear discount or invent canon wages from it.

No hidden potency premium unless this seller has evidence to appraise it. Price,
filters, delivery time, bargain wording and displayed alternatives must not reveal
sealed origin or truth. A fraud can change beliefs; physical goods stay fixed.
Supernatural creation/import or a strong trading power can make profitable resale
possible under its law. Actual repeated profit is not grounds for a global ban.

## J2.1: recurring merchants have businesses and relationships

Use the same admitted P4/P7 person, P19 relationships and trader stock identity,
with an explicit cross-store mapping after integration. No name-based join.
Separate enterprise funds, personal possessions, consigned goods, credit rights
and the player's stake. Rivalry reads actor knowledge and objectives, never the
hero's hidden inventory or build by default.

| Extension | Actual decision basis | Generated scene / mechanical consequence |
|---|---|---|
| J2.2 Competitive order | Two merchants can fund the same reachable supply need | Competing bids or differentiated quality/delivery terms; outstanding reservations avoid purchasing the same lot twice |
| J2.3 Cooperative sourcing | Complementary stock, routes, expertise and consent | Shared consignment/commission uses real contributions and an agreed revenue split |
| J2.4 Reputation earned in trade | Known completed deal, delay, refusal or disputed quality | Later willingness, collateral, credit or help changes; refused offers do not automatically create grudges |
| J2.5 Rival changes strategy | Actual lost sale or received market evidence, budget and time | New product, relocated counter, alliance, exit or a supported counteroffer; no infinite undercutting funded from nowhere |
| J2.6 Betrayal / reconciliation | Real agreement and breach evidence | Audit, mediation, restitution, boycott or renewed partnership. A rumor remains an accusation, not a debt writer. |

Contracts can be mixed across different lots/ventures. Partnering with one person
does not permanently forbid trading with another. Exclusive terms, when fitting,
apply to that funded occurrence/territory/time and are disclosed before acceptance;
per-world permissive, mercantile, coercive or ceremonial adjectives define them.
Aid, barter, gifts, ordinary employment and opting out remain useful options.
