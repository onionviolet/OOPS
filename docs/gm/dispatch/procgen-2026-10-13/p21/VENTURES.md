## J3.2: project facts and promised returns differ

Logical proposed record, not a new universal owner/schema:

```text
venture identity, world/instance/branch and retained definition/law revisions,
operator/enterprise, objective and cause, contributor roles and agreement refs,
real inputs/escrow/custody, supported stages/jobs and due boundaries,
buyer/demand commitments, disclosed risk/knowledge basis,
standing spend/substitution/stop policies, accepted variation/draw refs,
outcome/contribution receipts, unsettled proceeds or loss and settlement refs
```

Reuse management/simulation job, custody, stock and durable publisher owners.
The venture groups those commitments and policy decisions; it cannot maintain a
shadow bank. Commercial forecasts, expected returns, guaranteed reserved payouts
and realized net results are separate. A seed does not promise success or supply
the money owed to an investor.

| Extension | Concrete venture | Actual return and potential story |
|---|---|---|
| J3.3 Caravan / ship | Fund or supply a consignment on authored route edges with operator/cargo capacity | Delivered goods and paid sale; named delay, negotiated detour or recovered cargo can alter next terms |
| J3.4 Workshop | Buy a supported tool/process or labor commitment against actual demand | Real throughput/quality/service change plus contributor credit; no automatic exponential yield |
| J3.5 Expedition | Commission a supported survey/recovery at an authored site | Fixed-before-discovery evidence, original reachable finds and actual returned stock; uncertain result can still teach a useful limit |
| J3.6 Research | Fund an answerable test with qualified investigator, apparatus and inputs | Method/negative evidence with a real consumer; paperwork alone supplies no power/body/fuel |
| J3.7 Reconstruction / public venture | Fund a damaged object/site repair with consenting operators | Useful service and access, possibly no monetary profit; history remembers who supplied and who worked |
| J3.8 Credit / insurance / mutual aid | Source-qualified terms, lender/insurer capital and actual covered obligation | Deferred payment, covered loss, gift or assistance; enforcement is jurisdiction-specific, never automatic universal soul punishment |

Player may be financier, worker, supplier, operator, investigator, customer or
several roles. Several ventures can coexist if their actual capacity and
reservations allow; no fixed lifetime cap. A one-ship world has one ship until a
real construction/lease route exists; an abundant fleet world can offer many.

## J3.9: a story return must be about the committed project

Use the P14 receipt/account distinction. Report cast is the actual operator,
crew, buyers and known witnesses. A storylet reads real stage history rather than
rolling “pirates attacked” at collection. If risk was generated at admission,
retain its commitment privately and reveal only supported observations; if a
later causal boundary decides a hazard, record that boundary and available causes.
Never redraw the outcome when returning, reloading or opening the report.

An original sample: a carrier discovers the receiving workshop cannot handle the
agreed crate size. This requires actual declared receiving capacity and a
disclosed stop/substitution policy. The carrier holds cargo, sends a dated request,
and can arrange a paid repacking job from reserved means. Return includes exact
remaining cargo, fee, delivery receipt and the carrier's account. A later venture
can pay for better packaging because the player learned from this one.

Outcomes can be profit, break-even, partial delivery, cancelled/refunded work,
consensual loss, a useful discovery or social access. “Failed” is too coarse.
Losses occur only through selected disclosed project/world rules and actual
causes, not inactivity. Unspent reserve belongs to its owner; incomplete reports
cannot settle proceeds. Contribution credit and money need not share one formula.

## J3.10: absence, queues and choices

First slice follows current played-clock jobs, not wall-clock dividends. Browser
closure freezes the project. Autonomous work uses actual staff/tools/stock and
funded policies; a missing input pauses that project with retained escrow.
Policies can approve a known substitute, max additional spend, ordinary wait or
refund. No paid emergency response is silently inferred from “help neighbors.”
Unknown critical terms await input at a safe boundary without blocking other
ordinary work. No forgotten venture creates late fees, hunger death, lost homes
or forced combat merely because the player was away.

[Intro OT/BR](../../../../INTRO_FLOW_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md#ot-offline-time-across-branches)
proposes active-branch/offline policies but does not implement them. A later
qualified offline or time-skip route must advance one clock authority, preserve
pending choices and prevent duplicated funding across branch projections.
[P16](../P16.md) owns loop/return retention: a remembered profitable method does
not imply that reset cargo, funds or partners remember it too.
