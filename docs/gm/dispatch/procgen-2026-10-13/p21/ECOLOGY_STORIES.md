## I4.1: a cause graph, not a random invasion label

Proposed original graph: a counted prey cohort moves along an authored edge after
an actual habitat change → a predator cohort can follow under its movement rule →
observed tracks support a dated report → a producer's accessible work changes →
that real deficit generates a funded P11 N1 request. Not every link must fire.
Predators may stay, follow another source or retreat. Unknown prey is not zero;
unsupported habitat or movement law cannot produce a canon-labelled migration.

P8 owns cohorts, producer phases, renewal and harvest receipts. P21 reads a typed
change (old/new phase, actual cause, scope, cohort/site refs, time and observation
evidence) and creates a fitting request/account through P3/P11/P14. It does not
roll a second ecosystem or regenerate creature identities in text.

## I4.8: actual species laws differ

Ecological roles are hypotheses/author rules, not universal biology inferred from
an enemy name. A supernatural being may follow fear, spiritual substrate,
corruption, institutional activity or an explicit source rule rather than eating
prey. JJK traces, Bleach substrate, Shadow Slave creature classification and RI
living powers therefore cannot inherit an original wolf model unchanged. See
[MORE world bindings](MORE.md) and P9/P10 for exact qualifications.

If a world supports divine creation, cloning, respawn or inexhaustible production,
record its source and cost or declared no-cost scope. Such a source can change
capacity and markets; an ecological “balance” rule must not nullify it. Authored
rare beings remain reserved, while lawful acquisition, alliance and AU versions
stay reachable. Mortal death, soul persistence and a replacement cohort are
different events.

## I4.9: knowledge and comfortable idle play

Public display can say “fewer tracks in the surveyed field, observed yesterday,”
not expose an exact hidden population, sealed origin or candidate count. Use
[E7](../../../../emergent/E7_OBSERVATION_AND_MYSTERY.md) for observation means and
[P11 information](../p11/INFORMATION_AND_LIBRARIES.md) for reports. A merchant's
price must not reveal an undiscovered species/property. A plausible rumor cannot
change actual prey merely by being rendered, unless a selected world has that
specific belief-to-reality law.

No hunger death or compulsory animal-care login. First opt-in uses played time,
queued surveys/restoration and safe work alternatives. Declared ecological
progress during played time may create new opportunities while the player does
other work; it cannot be an idle-targeting disaster escalator. Closing the browser
does not grow a nest, empty stock or destroy crops in this slice. If offline
progress is selected later, routine benefits and important boundaries need the
timeline owner, with no absence punishment.

## First proof and gates

S4: on two authored original gathering sites, concentrate harvesting, observe
supported pressure, rotate/tend and receive a funded survey/restoration request.
Renewal changes actual next yield or work, and the later account names the correct
site and action. This layers over P8 R3-A; it does not pretend that herb recovery
proves predation, births or a full monster ecosystem. Add one authored prey/predator
movement law only in a separately bounded follow-up.
