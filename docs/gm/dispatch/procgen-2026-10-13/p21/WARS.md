# Wars, sieges and campaigns that reach local life

2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** Extends root **I3/B1**, using
[REPORT gates](REPORT.md). [P12](../P12.md) owns arcs/plans, [P13](../P13.md) owns
canon/divergence, [P19](../P19.md) the social network and [P20](../P20.md) the
settlement. P21 adds the conflict-to-local-consequence interface.

## I3.1: a war requires opposing goals and means

Generate an original conflict only after two admitted factions have incompatible
current objectives, evidence/beliefs, decision authority and feasible means.
Territorial control, membership, public reputation and legal recognition differ.
One merchant dispute does not imply a war; failed negotiation may produce a
boycott, raid, strike, court case or nothing. Canon wars use authored source
events and dependents; never roll a replacement conflict to enforce an ending.

Proposed conflict record references faction goals, contested place/right/object,
authorizing actor, grievance/claim basis, declared scale, objective predicates,
mobilized forces and supply reservations, clocks, supported actions, ceasefire
terms and consequence receipts. It owns conflict decisions only. P8 retains goods
and routes, P20 buildings/population, P19 people and P14 remembered history.
No copied warehouse, parallel battlefield purse or second clock.

## I3.2: campaign operations on authored connections

| Phase | Actual input | Generated opportunity / necessary result |
|---|---|---|
| Mobilize | Recruitment authority, willing/compelled participants under selected law, money and equipment | Quartermaster commission, medical support, opposition petition or civilian work. A title alone provides no army. |
| Move / sustain | Existing authored routes, destination access, transport capacity and actual supplies | Escort, scouting, trade or blockade-running; no procedural geography required. |
| Contest a front | Forces with supported reach/detection/defense, objective and knowledge | Battle, negotiation, withdrawal, misinformation or an alternate front. Numerical army abstract is labelled abstraction, not individual combat proof. |
| Siege | Real access/control changes, defender reserves, engineering means, relief routes | Repair fortification, paid relief delivery, evacuation or talks. Ordinary local work remains possible. |
| Settle | Satisfied/abandoned objectives, enforceable treaty and remaining means | Demobilization, reconstruction, restitution, prisoner exchange, contested occupation or a continuing ceasefire. Peace creates content too. |

Food in a campaign can fund meal benefits or a declared logistics reserve.
Shortfall can defer mobilization, reduce supported readiness or trigger resupply;
it never creates player hunger death. Do not introduce unsourced NPC starvation
as a shortcut. Broad life/destruction effects require their own world-qualified
consumer. Magical supply may be cheap, instantaneous or inexhaustible where its
law supports it; transport and control must respond to that fact.

## I3.3: the village receives consequences through real edges

Concrete original chain: an admitted faction commissions bridge access for a
campaign. A committed blockade closes one supply edge. An existing shipment is
held under its terms, causing a counted merchant deficit. A funded buyer requests
an alternate delivery. Arriving displaced people retain identity, carried goods,
skills, needs and known testimony; they are not a generic misery multiplier.
The player may deliver, hire transport, negotiate, shelter visitors, scout, fight,
support either side, help both civilians or continue ordinary work.

News names its occurrence date and received date via
[P11 N5](../p11/INFORMATION_AND_LIBRARIES.md). Reaching a village is an authored
route/arrival, not teleporting every refugee there. Shelter capacity and consent
belong to P20/P19; useful guest/repair work can use P11 N1. Public claims of a
victory may conflict with actual route access. The player can investigate without
an omniscient front map or pre-route disclosure of sealed causes.

## I3.4: causal scale and extraordinary actors

Preserve [timeline T3–T5](../../../../TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE.md): changes affect
dependent events; unaffected history persists. A commander's death can stop one
plan while an institution with real succession continues another. A substitute
needs that motive, role and means; it cannot appear only to restore canon.
[Intro MT/SP/OV/CV](../../../../INTRO_FLOW_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md#mt-motives-behind-canon-events)
are proposals, including stronger canon-pressure ladders, not authority to ignore
those causal requirements.

Use [cosmic relative-consequence rules](../../../../COSMIC_EVENTS.md#consequences-are-relative-and-god-likes-are-few-weibao-2026-10-10).
A source-supported overwhelming actor may remove a fortification or make a
conventional siege pointless. Compare actual reach, fuel, defenses, authority and
knowledge, not a shared world rank. Alternate responses can seek protection,
relocate, negotiate or change objectives. Harsh/powerful worlds remain available;
an unwinnable authored conflict is different from manufacturing an unavoidable
crisis as punishment for being idle.

## I3.5: idle policy and campaign memory

First slice uses played time and local opt-in. No closed-browser war ticks,
absence-triggered requisition, missed-duty debt or destruction on return.
Funded standing policies can handle routine transport or repair within max spend,
known substitute, labor and stop conditions. An unresolved strategic choice uses
a declared safe pause/defer at the affected commitment; it should not stall all
ordinary play. A campaign need not ask for every tactical step.

Chronicle links operations to the same goal/front and contribution receipts.
Record objective results and actual costs, not “victory” from a boast. Armistice,
occupation, demobilization and reconstruction keep independent causes. A faction
can change leadership or split with actual institution state, without the history
generator inventing a new war faction. Local frequency/severity adjectives replace
fixed war quotas or lifetime once restrictions.

## First proof and gates

S5 is a finite local **campaign-supply episode**, not a general war engine: two
original admitted goals, one authored route restriction, one existing shipment,
one alternate useful delivery and a negotiated reopening. Later selected book
eras can use the same joins after exact military/control/supply laws are bound.
Village is an optional reusable host, never a mandatory canon-world opening.

OPEN: faction authority and real mobilization accounting; no doubled cargo or
world time; unrelated-event control; source-dependent substitutes; truthful news
delay; refuge/custody/consent; safe idle continuation; pending blockade/manifest
and negotiated terms roundtrip; missing pack/old-save policy; rebuilt desktop and
human acceptance. Full combat fronts, forced conscription, permanent occupation
or destructive siege outcomes remain reachable proposals with separate gates.
