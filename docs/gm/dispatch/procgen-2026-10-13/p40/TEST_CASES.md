## Runner shape and gates

G1 SOURCE: For each relevant test run original same-seed replay, JSON/export/import midway, failed write, no preview mutation and exact next cursor. Include stream cells, legacy deck RNG, generated owner seeds/revisions, due values, sequence, inventory/exposure and retained references. Compare authoritative canonical subsets, not raw UI signatures. Fixture traces carry IDs/status; full cause diagnostics remain private if the pack is sealed.

G2 TIMING: Follow P18's returned `remaining`, `stoppedAtChoice` and `budgetExhausted`, returning every changed owner into the next call. Sample policy actions at the same declared boundaries. Never use `state.clock = target` to make parity pass; AP2's direct-clock context is a source probe of P2's adapter contract, not evidence of a legal native jump journey.

G3 PRESENTATION: Rebuilt fresh origins, 1366x768 plus 1600x1000, useful controls/full names and filtered observations visible. Save/reload and UI Export/literal-file Import compare exact pending and settled output. No build/browser gate ran in P40. No mobile acceptance substitute.

G4 DIRECTOR: Fiction, pacing, fairness and comfort are separate human judgments. Statistical distribution is a tuning report, not a mandatory anti-winning cap. Food remains useful and optional, ordinary unfed play comfortable, no hunger death. Gameplay repeatability follows local laws; idempotence prevents replay of one settlement, not new opportunities.
