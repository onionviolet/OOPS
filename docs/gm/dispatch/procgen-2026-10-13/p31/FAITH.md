# AL1 and AL2: faith as practice, attention and institutions

2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** Extends [root AL1-AL2](../../../../PROCEDURAL_STORY_AND_CONTENT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md#al-faith-in-practice), [B4 myths/religions, B6 law/trials](../../../../PROCEDURAL_STORY_AND_CONTENT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md#b-more-content-to-generate) and [R3 different readings](../../../../PROCEDURAL_STORY_AND_CONTENT_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md#r-moral-texture). A prayer event, received response, believer's interpretation, temple service and binding oath have separate causes. No universal piety bar, hunger law or daily attendance tax is selected.

## AL1.a: a world declares what prayer can actually do

Proposed `FaithLaw`: ID/revision, world/continuity/era, eligible practices, possible receivers, real reception channels, attention/response policy, source/payment rules, evidence filters, supported grant operators and coupling to local clocks. A world can have silent gods, reachable gods, contested testimony, many active patrons, impersonal ritual laws, belief-shaped beings or no demonstrated divine mechanism. These possibilities compose by place/patron, rather than forcing one cosmology or one religion.

Support a practice record with actor/carrier, intention/topic, actual rite/offerings, location/time, audience, addressed receiver claim, terms if any, committed cause and observation policy. Addressing a name is a claim about a receiver, not proof that such an entity exists or heard it. Keep actual reception private until a supported route reveals it. A saved invocation receipt cannot reroll when the player reopens a shrine.

## AL1.c: prayer, offering, attention, response

| Extension | State transition | Concrete suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| AL1.c1 Prayer | Performed supported practice → possible reception event → observed result or no observed response | Let the hero ask for guidance, thank, lament, petition for another person or pray without requesting a mechanical benefit. Texture is valid when named as texture. |
| AL1.c2 Offering | Allocate actual item/time/service → recipient/custody or declared consumed/transformed destination | Select known gift, labor or crafted object. A temple's collector receiving it and a deity noticing it are separate receipts. |
| AL1.c3 Attention | Actual receiver with supported channel evaluates received request under its law | Save relevance, actual notice and response schedule. Attention need not be currency or guarantee favor. A powerful patron can be freely generous if authored that way. |
| AL1.c4 Silence | No response observed within the known window | Keep response state distinct from receipt/reception truth. Silence can coexist with later help; do not make it announce a hidden deity or secret test. |
| AL1.c5 Sign | Real observed event with actor interpretation | Record what was seen and what believers infer. Multiple plausible accounts remain attributed unless a route establishes origin. |
| AL1.c6 Miracle | Actual source/capability changes a supported body/site/work result | Source, target, range, cost and grant timing must reach the real consumer. A glowing paragraph is no healing or divine proof. |
| AL1.c7 Demand | Delivered terms from an actual receiver or an attributed clergy claim | Player may accept, negotiate, postpone or refuse. Acceptance forms an AJ1/AJ2 obligation only through its explicit formation policy. |

Offerings are actual transfers/consumption. Incense, oil, candles and talismans reuse [P6 M1/M7](../p6/MORE.md); generated crafting truth remains with P2/P6 and local ritual consumption with CR2. P2's oral regeneration is not consecration. Burning a carrier does not by itself buy divine favor. If a shrine accepts a maker's skillful object, its preference reads supported properties plus actual evidence of workmanship, not hidden global quality.

Donations can fund a clergy service or communal project. A purported miracle may instead be skilled medicine, staged deception, coincidence or supernatural intervention, depending on retained causes. NPCs can disagree without the report choosing an unsupported answer. A fraudulent priest can sincerely believe their doctrine; an honest priest can be mistaken. Certainty, honesty, authority and efficacy are separate axes.

## AL2.a: temples and clergy have actual lives

Proposed temple instance references site/access, institution/office, actual members, doctrine editions/claim refs, funds/stock, supported services, calendars, authority/jurisdiction and received accounts. Reuse [P11 institutions M5/M10/M14](../p11/MORE.md), [P6 buildings](../p6/BUILDINGS.md), [P19 social episodes](../p19/CONTRACT.md) and [CR4 maker economy](../cr4/CHECKPOINT.md). No temple-specific duplicate people/cash store.

Clergy may teach a ritual, tend a garden, offer qualified care, arbitrate, preserve records, administer charity, preach, recruit or negotiate donations. Only actual means support a service. Appointment/office authority differs from faith, personal powers and doctrine knowledge. A lay craftsperson can make a votive or maintain a shrine without joining clergy; a priest can hold a ceremonial office without possessing a miracle faculty. Attendance, service, independent study, skepticism and ordinary life coexist as routes.

AL2.b: Doctrine disputes use dated attributed propositions: permitted practices, meaning of a sign, distribution of aid, rightful leadership or interpretation of an oath. Keep each faction's doctrine, enforcement policy and received evidence distinct from world truth. A local reform can change a service schedule or distribution rule through actual office/member/resource receipts; it cannot rewrite a magical law by debate unless that world's law explicitly permits belief to do so.

AL2.c: Pilgrimage binds real route/site/checkpoint and chosen purpose. Travel/cargo/reservations come from [P8](../p8/REPORT.md), journey episodes from [P27 story lane](../p27/CHECKPOINT.md). A roadside shrine, assisted trip, paid transport, repeated visits or a world-supported remote communion remain reachable where laws permit. Arriving is a receipt; spiritual meaning is interpreted; a blessing has a separate source. The same journey can fulfill a promise and a personal ambition only when both objectives actually match.

AL2.d: Heresy trial begins with an allegation and a jurisdiction, not an objective universe-level guilt flag. Record notice, doctrine edition, alleged act, received evidence, actual witnesses, hearing, verdict, appeal and enforcement. The act may satisfy one village's welfare goal, violate a lord's law and offend a temple's doctrine (root R3). Reputation reacts per faction after actual notice. No global good/evil value collapses these readings.

Reachable responses include contesting evidence, challenging jurisdiction, explaining intent, securing an advocate, seeking another institution, bargaining, recanting, reforming, escaping or using a supported extraordinary power. Harsh fictional institutions may impose severe consequences where sourced/authored; quiet worlds may resolve disagreement socially. Do not fabricate universal heresy courts for named franchises without their source/era law.

## Idle fit and first original faith journey

Player can queue a known maintenance or pilgrimage stage with actual materials/time and stop terms. Optional regular practice uses a chosen budget; no default auto-donation, new vow, fasting or secret disclosure. Missed attendance is not breach without accepted terms. Ordinary unfed play remains comfortable and there is no hunger death; temple meals can offer optional benefits, food donations cannot create mandatory player hunger, and authored ascetic practices need their own supported law rather than a global starvation model.

Original proposal: at a known shrine, learn two attributed interpretations of a request for help. Donate a crafted candle or perform equivalent agreed maintenance; observe actual transfer and a disclosed supported light/service effect. Prayer can produce a saved no-observed-response result without stopping normal play. A later local spirit response, if enabled by a separate original law, changes one actual work capability through its real source. Two clergy react differently to the same public deed; an uninformed third person retains their prior view. Investigate, accept, negotiate or decline further requests.

At 1366x768 show selected rite/offering/cost beside observed responses and temple services; at 1600x1000 add attributed doctrine/accounts. Keep ordinary work controls reachable. Mobile deferred. First slice excludes global divine population simulation, complete theology and offline pilgrim travel; these remain expandable possibilities, not required gates.
