## Joined scenario, several reachable routes

Original proposed setup: a craftsperson promises a useful brace; a buyer promises a lesson after receipt; a shrine offers a workspace; a broker has a scoped claim about a method. Each role has actual time, stock and knowledge. No secret truth or verdict is specified here.

The hero can craft with an observed method, buy a fitting product, hire qualified help, negotiate part delivery, ask for a waiver or arrange a new deadline. Obtaining a method can involve paid information, independent experiment, a favor-backed lesson, public instruction or permitted spying. A craft result meets the actual spec regardless of which allowed route supplied it. Success records delivery and the reciprocal lesson remains a separate debt until performed. An offering is optional; it can fund the shrine's service without proving divine attention.

If a claim is disputed, inspect custody/evidence, ask a witness, use supported appraisal, publish a scoped account or protect it. A threatened disclosure does not choose the recipient's response in advance. A temple can call a transaction improper while a buyer approves and a guild treats it as competitive business, with delivered accounts and local jurisdiction controlling reaction. The player can pursue multiple routes over time; scarce capacity changes scheduling, not permanent option reachability.
