## AJ2.a: classify the mechanism before pricing it

Social promises, legal contracts, magical bilateral contracts, self-restrictions, divinely witnessed vows, innate restrictions and technique activation costs differ. Preserve E8D's later correction register rather than its earlier universal-economy shorthand. Benefit can be learned knowledge, access, authorized service or a changed power operation. Some oaths grant no benefit at all. Not every restriction buys more strength; no universal exchange score generates arbitrary powers.

Proposed `BindingLaw` references world/continuity/era/revision, mechanism, eligible parties/carriers, formation event, actual enforcer/source, allowed predicates, evidence channels, interpretation, benefit operators, payment domain, breach mode, coercion, release/renegotiation, death/transfer/loop policy and source-evidence status. Bind one law and its version to the promise instance. Add per-party grant/payment refs, enforcer bindings and event subscriptions. The authored interpreter supplies executable semantics; free-form wording remains display/input for validated templates.

## AJ2.c: enforcement and evidence

| Mode | Required event sequence | What the player can investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Prevention | Attempt receipt → local denial → no prohibited act committed | Actual blocking condition/channel; failed activation need not be breach. |
| Automatic penalty | Committed act → typed breach predicate → one consequence receipt | Enforcer only reads its declared evidence domain. A fact-reading oath differs from sincere-belief oath. |
| Judgment | Allegation → notice → evidence/hearing → attributed verdict → actual execution | Court jurisdiction, admissible evidence, defense, bias, appeal and actual enforcement means. Verdict can disagree with historical truth. |

Contract events distinguish attempt, paid use, committed act, culpable breach and verdict. One event can trigger several bindings, but each binding settles its own cause once. Coercion, accidental acts, proxies, disguises, dreams and involuntary transformation require local interpretation, not automatic absolution or automatic guilt. An enforcer may know what the public does not; its capacity is not a universal NPC telepathy grant.

Bindings can collide. Admission shows conflicts the actor can know; preserve unknown ones without leaking their existence. Support waivers, delegation, changed circumstances, temporary suspension, lawful release, loopholes, amends and explicit breach where local laws allow. A lawfully impossible conflict stays impossible until actual means alter it. Priority cannot silently save the hero by rewriting accepted terms. Powerful cheats can override a law through an actual directional capability/source event; do not ban them as anti-abuse or grant them by name alone.

## AJ2.d: promise and power are separate settlements

Formation checks actual parties, carrier, ritual equipment, source, knowledge and access. Freeze terms/interpretation and any supported uncertainty policy, then reserve payment and persist the binding before granting benefit. Nonconsumable witnesses are participants, not stock fractions. Paid sacrifice, collateral escrow and renewable per-use resource are different ledger domains. Destroying a parchment only removes the bond if the world law actually binds enforcement to that parchment.

Breach may remove a granted advantage, spend a held stake or apply a separate sanction. Removing the advantage cannot restore years/body/stock already sacrificed. Death, world transition, office succession and prestige bind to the selected carrier rule. A promise's social memory can remain after magical release; paying a legal debt need not erase distrust. Restore retained law versions and active subscriptions without regranting a benefit or replaying a penalty.

Idle policy can block a known forbidden automatic action while keeping alternatives available. It cannot secretly swear extra restrictions to optimize power. Routine ritual stages may queue with actual materials and participants. Per-world fluent, harsh, forgiving, literal or costly profiles tune behavior; a quiet original world can support purely social/legal contracts. [Faith](FAITH.md) handles patron attention without converting every prayer into an oath.

## AJ2.e: first binding demonstration

Original proposal: an actual supported tool-use technique accepts a narrow self-restriction and changes one real operation; show the known terms and benefit, perform an allowed action, deliberately perform a warned breach under player choice, then observe the declared advantage removed. Independently form a two-party deposit contract with a funded reward and qualified witness. A social contract and a self-vow need separate proof, not one magical catch-all.

Future gates, NOT RUN: primary locator/edition check for whichever canon law is selected; activation condition versus vow; law-scoped unknown penalty; one truthful and one mistaken account; enforced event versus attempted act; coercion/proxy; paid sacrifice versus refund; contradictory bindings; released advantage withdrawal; actor/carrier/office transition; pending/final Save/reload/Export/literal Import. Human fidelity and fiction acceptance remain separate from source tests and browser proof.
