## W2.3: selection and subversion

Proposed preference: base eligible weight × bounded world/era/cohort factor × requested genre/taste factor × recent-structure variety factor. Hard world laws, cast, costs and disclosure filter first. Only the existing saved owner draws. D9 can pace admissions; it cannot change a settled result. No novelty penalty removes the last legitimate route or a player-requested familiar payoff. Preview and re-render consume nothing.

Attach realization choice `straight | inversion | consequence | reconstruction` separately. A public challenge can reward conspicuous strength, expose missing evidence, open a negotiation or become a friendly rematch. Do not punish every familiar reward with betrayal. A strong power remains strong. Weighting should vary **who needs what and which operation changes the situation**, not merely how often someone insults the hero.

Future gate: fixed-context structural coverage, low-frequency reachability witness, source-qualified exceptions, saved next-draw parity and a human comparison of two coherent world packs. No claim of measured probabilities or checks run here.
