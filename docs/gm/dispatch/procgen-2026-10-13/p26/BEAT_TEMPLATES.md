## W1.1: a beat is a change in a supported situation

A template describes a reusable causal shape, not a copied scene, a compulsory canon event or a prose prompt that can award its own result. Create content by binding eligible roles, actual needs, materials, clues, local laws and available actions, then compose supported transitions. This can produce a new situation and aftermath, beyond choosing among fixed written outcomes. P12 owns the thread/arc lifecycle. P3 owns admitted occurrences; P13 owns source continuity and divergence. An optional quiet encounter can have no mechanical reward and still matter as a remembered shared occasion.

Extend P15 rather than rebuild it: [T2 semantic frames / T4 saved realization](../p15/RUNTIME_TEXT.md), [DIALOGUE](../p15/DIALOGUE.md) for the existing Ink seam and conversation lifecycle, [A3 envelopes / QA1–QA14 / B1–B5](../p15/AUTHORING_PIPELINE.md) for admission. P26 adds `beatSpec`, `tropeProfile`, `genreOverlay` and `noveltyPolicy` as **logical authoring metadata**, not new runtime APIs. No new writer, compiler, scheduler or LLM is required by this document.

## W1.2: shared shape

| Field | Proposed content and validation |
|---|---|
| Identity and scope | Stable template ID/revision; work/creator/edition; world/continuity/era; allowed attachment from P13; branch/world-instance refs; source horizon. A template revision is not an occurrence ID. |
| Evidence | Per claim: SHOWN, STATED with speaker/interface, INFERRED analyst abstraction, or UNKNOWN; primary versus supplied-edition/secondary quality; chapter/member/paragraph/claim locators; contradiction IDs. Every generated shape and weight is INFERRED/ORIGINAL PROPOSAL even when its basis is SHOWN. |
| Roles | Typed actor/object/site roles, distinctness rules, knowledge/permission/body/source/stock requirements. An institution is not interchangeable with its officer. Bind introduced labels through P7/P11/P15, never a secret alias or random canon replacement. |
| Preconditions and law reads | Actual cause/need, authored node, available means, local clock/window, dependencies, outstanding promises and disclosure scope. `unknown` differs from false. Read a law reference; do not treat a trope weight as a law. |
| Turning point | A declared observation, failed operation, actual arrival, negotiation, threshold or intervention that changes available approaches. It requires a real writer/receipt; an RNG choice cannot fabricate a witness or completed work. |
| Outcomes | Several supported options with costs/effects, useful small success, withdrawal/refusal, partial/unresolved state and later return where plausible. Enumerated examples are extensible, never an exhaustive global menu. |
| Recurrence and tone | World-local adjectives such as seasonal, ceremonial, recurring, hard-to-find, repairable, contested or legendary. Same-occurrence settlement is idempotent; new causes remain possible. Genre, gag and plain presentation can coexist. |
| Memory and joins | Freeze cast, terms, selected bindings/revision, draw receipt and P15 realization. Retain consequential receipts with their actual owners; project known result to P14/P25. Do not read a lossy biography to recover obligations. |

A useful structural fingerprint is `(cause family, role relation, means constraint, turning-point operation, outcome deltas)`. Different words alone do not make different beats. Shared shapes can recur with purpose; a paid repair, lesson and farewell should not all become identical fetch jobs.

## W1.3: assembly, with all reasonable routes retained

1. **A1 Qualify.** Select source/era, actual anchor, allowed attachment, world laws and disclosure. Resolve essential contradictions; leave unrelated mysteries unknown.
2. **A2 Bind.** P11 supplies actual and observer-known context separately. P7 reservations and P13 occupancy checks choose actors, stock and sites. No eligible cast means no admission, with no draw consumed.
3. **A3 Compose.** P12 connects a supported opening, turning point and possible aftermath. Genre overlays prefer operations without creating facts. Trope weights rank eligible shapes; a manual request can still select another plausible one.
4. **A4 Commit.** Existing durable owners publish changes and frozen occurrence/turn terms atomically. A failed publication, stale quote or preview creates no payout, witness, gag step or novelty milestone.
5. **A5 Express and remember.** P15 realizes safe frames or uses Ink for authored anchors. P14 records received accounts; actual action owners retain consequential truth. Reevaluate downstream causes after intervention, rather than repairing canon to preserve the template.
