# X1–X3: genre packs over one thread engine

2026-10-14. **PROPOSAL ONLY.** P12 owns thread/arc structure, P13 world law/continuity, P15 expression/Ink/authoring and D9 pacing. Genre is an overlay on those owners, not eleven new quest engines. Link [beat shape](BEAT_TEMPLATES.md), [tropes](TROPES.md) and [P15 QA1–QA14](../p15/AUTHORING_PIPELINE.md).

## X1.1: shared engine, different promises

A genre pack proposes roles, preferred operations, rhythm, disclosure and evaluation of an actual outcome. It cannot create a clue, romance, frightened reaction, tournament win or supply shortage just to meet an outline. Packs can mix by thread, scene or act: a school-life term can contain a heist followed by cooking and quiet recovery. Changing preference affects future optional admission; already paid jobs, clues and promises remain intact. No either/or world lock or global minimum danger.

| Pack / ID | Typical progression | Reads / meaningful outcomes | Special guard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mystery X1.MY | anomaly → several evidence routes → competing explanations → test → revised belief | Actual clue carriers, witness access, known claims, causal hypothesis; solve, partial finding, expose error, leave open | Fix interacting truth before clues commit; do not choose culprit after the player's guess. Contradictory testimony remains attributed. P24 owns perspectives. |
| Romance X1.RO | introduction → shared time → disclosed interest → reciprocation/decline → continuing relationship | Independent wants, known boundaries, meetings and remembered gestures; friendship, affection, uncertainty, decline, later reconnection | No automatic attraction/consent from an affection number, heroic rescue or trope role. Supports multiple relationship arrangements where people agree. P19/P23 own relationships/interiority. |
| Horror X1.HO | unsettling evidence → interpretation → mounting exposure → protection/escape/confrontation → aftermath | Actual hazard and protection means; dread can remain aesthetic, survival need not require combat | Tone/intensity known before commitment; no fake save corruption, misleading system failure or hidden global hunger death. Sealed threats use owner discovery routes. |
| Heist X1.HE | scope → recon → crew or solo means → plan → attempt → evidence-led complication → exit/aftermath | Real target/custody, route, capabilities, knowledge and responding agents; recover, partial retrieve, negotiate, abort | No automatic betrayal/alarm or universal magic lock. See X2. |
| Tournament X1.TO | eligibility → preparation → pairing → declared contest → adjudication → aftermath | Source-qualified rules, participants, equipment, injury policy and evidence; win, lose, demonstrate, dispute, withdraw | Narrow victory condition need not mean power parity. Bracket RNG never changes settled entrants. |
| School life X1.SC | schedule/obligation → lesson/peer scene → practice/test → feedback → next occasion | Staff, course knowledge, access, materials and friendships; learn, struggle, help, appeal, take another route | Do not assume canon classmates are vacant generated roles; no copied exam or global school enrollment. |
| Cooking X1.CO | audience/purpose → source ingredients → prepare → cook → share/sell → response | Real edible lots, method/tools, tastes and actual benefit; successful dish, safe imperfect dish, rework, commission | P6 owns process/product. No arbitrary poisoning because a comedy pack is active. Ordinary unfed play stays comfortable. |
| Survival X1.SU | assess environment → prepare shelter/resources → test → adapt → sustain/leave | Authored habitat, body, weather, supplies and supported dangers | Source costs are local; no new default hunger death or unseen absent-world harm. A safe retreat is useful content. |
| Intrigue X1.IN | competing aims → access/info → bargain → intervention → public/private aftermath | Jurisdiction, known leverage, documents, actual independent choices; coalition, stalemate, reform, departure | Social success cannot mind-control an actor or make all witnesses informed. P21/P19 retain conflict/social state. |
| Military X1.MI | orders/intelligence → supply/roles → deployment → operation → report/recovery | Command authority, bodies, morale evidence, actual logistics and force asymmetry; defend, evacuate, retreat, negotiate | Group leadership/death stay with P27; a command does not guarantee obedience or casualty policy. |
| Slice-of-life X1.SL | invitation/routine → shared activity → small surprise or no surprise → response → remembered ordinary time | Actual availability, tastes, tools, stock and shared experience; warmth, craft, conversation, rest | No danger, twist, XP payout or relationship change is required. See X3. |

## X2.1: worked heist, original fixture

This is an abstract original fixture for an authored archive site, not a canon scene or a runtime implementation. Premise: a deposited repair ledger is due to be destroyed under a disputed order. Its owner asks for recovery. The same thread can support authorized retrieval, a covert theft, a negotiated loan, copying with permission, institutional appeal or abandonment. Those are distinct acts with different custody and reputational outcomes, not interchangeable labels. P13 checks fit if installed in a book world.

| Phase | Actual inputs / supported decisions | Receipt and consequence |
|---|---|---|
| H1 Scope and recon | Identify the particular ledger/custodian and order; inspect only accessible rooms; ask clerk, inspect public schedule, pay an observer or risk direct surveillance | Known schedule is dated testimony or observation. No map beyond authored nodes, guaranteed secret door or omniscient guard plan. |
| H2 Means and plan | Solo, recruit a willing clerk/scout, hire a locksmith or use a qualified spell; reserve fee/tools/time and select entry/exit contingencies | Crew members retain actual location, task consent and independent availability. A learned spell without its source/fuel cannot open a lock. |
| H3 Attempt | Choose permitted route; admission freezes known terms, not a future success. Native operation checks real lock, credential, access and custody state | Spend actual effort/fuel for attempted operation. Failure can leave a changed lock, elapsed time or observed trace only under declared local rules. |
| H4 Complication and improvisation | The custodian actually moved the ledger, a played shift changed, or a tool failed under its real condition. Re-scout, negotiate, switch supported approach, wait or abort | Complication requires an actual cause, not a compulsory dramatic die. A previously moved ledger is not duplicated at the planned target. No forced betrayal. |
| H5 Exit and aftermath | Return original, retain it under contested title, make an authorized copy, surrender, compensate, or leave empty-handed | Transfer exact custody; deliver only if owner accepts the actual object. Record witness-specific evidence, fees, damage and unfinished obligations. An undetected act creates no universal wanted flag. |

Useful branches: correct permit + real access gives a clean retrieval; stale recon makes the target absent and opens a later search; missing tool allows hired help; detection can lead to appeal, pursuit, safe surrender or escape according to actual means. Strong teleportation or divination can legitimately simplify a heist when sourced; do not invent an immunity simply to preserve its length.

Future proof: acquire target knowledge naturally, execute one plan and a materially different alternate, demonstrate a valid abort, preserve who saw what and exact custody through pending/completed Save/reload/Export/literal-file Import. A seeded moved-target fixture is separate from natural acquisition. P18 can check no unfunded target duplication; P15 QA5 reports UNKNOWN for unsupported stealth/lock adapters. No fake implementation by printing success.

## X3.1: worked slice-of-life, original fixture

A familiar neighbor has an open afternoon and agrees to share a simple meal at an authored home. No looming crisis, mandatory argument or concealed disaster is added. A quiet scene is a valid completed thread.

| Phase | Choice / actual basis | Small consequence or none |
|---|---|---|
| L1 Invite | Ask a known person; propose now/later, solo cooking, a picnic or a paid café; they accept, decline or suggest another time from actual availability | Retain the agreed time and known preference. Declining does not become betrayal or permanent relationship loss. |
| L2 Prepare | Use owned ingredients/tools, buy a dish, share costs, let a willing friend cook, or simply have tea | Actual stock/time/payment are charged only for chosen acts. No nutrition penalty for choosing conversation instead. |
| L3 Share | Choose a known favorite, discuss a witnessed event, ask about a hobby, teach a small skill, play a game or sit quietly | P15 supplies safe voice frames/Ink authored turns. A person can enjoy the occasion without disclosing secrets or granting a power. |
| L4 Small variation | Weather changes picnic comfort through the real weather owner; one guest brings a titled ingredient; a harmless mistake permits rework | These are optional eligible variations. No surprise is required. Humor reads actual events and can be muted independently. |
| L5 Close / return | Clean together, keep leftovers, reschedule, share a lawful recipe copy, or leave without another objective | P6 keeps actual dish/copy identity. P19 may record shared time; P14 can remember the occasion. No automatic affection/XP farming reward is necessary. Another genuinely available meal remains possible. |

In HP this could be an ordinary student tea break with sourced access and ingredients, in Soldier an off-duty meal within orders, in LOTM a neighborhood supper at the selected era. These are proposals, not extracted canon scenes. Use each file's laws and qualified actors rather than swapping names onto the same impossible activity.

Future proof: naturally arrange and complete a quiet occasion with optional refusal/rescheduling, show both desktop views, leave other ordinary work usable, and roundtrip its actual terms, goods and memory. Human acceptance is whether the scene feels pleasant and people remain themselves; a test cannot certify warmth.

## X1.2: mixing and review

Track genre promise, actual transition and player reading separately. A failed heist can become a negotiation or a recovery thread without forcing a new genre selection; a horror mystery can finish with an ordinary safe explanation if that is the pinned truth. If the source has no relevant adapter, retain a proposal or a labelled texture scene instead of pretending it changed world state. [P22](../p22/CHECKPOINT.md) owns director requests, [P28](../p28/CHECKPOINT.md) preference learning/onboarding and [P25](../p25/CHECKPOINT.md) presentation/export. Genre packs do not silently infer taste from one chosen response.
