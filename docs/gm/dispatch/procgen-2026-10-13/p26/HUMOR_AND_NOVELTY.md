## Z1.1: humor has a cause and an audience

Humor can come from incongruity, dramatic irony within known information, understatement, over-preparation, a reversal of expectations, character contrast, literal procedure or recognition of an earlier harmless mishap. A joke should fit the speaker, listener, setting and moment. A serious world can contain comic relief without turning all threats into parody. Comedy need not be Western sarcasm or modern memes; no random catchphrase borrowed from canon.

P15 T2 supplies a known semantic frame and its voice/register. Add proposed metadata: comic mechanism, setup receipt, audience recognition, local tone, optional escalation predicate, cost/harm scope, chosen response, recurrence adjective and resolution state. These are authoring joins, not a new actor-state schema. A narrator can be funny; an NPC does not therefore know the narrator's joke. Translation/register review can preserve a shared comic action when a pun fails.

## Z1.2: running-gag lifecycle

Proposed states: `introduced → recognized-return → variation → payoff → resting`, with optional renewal from a genuine new cause. They describe narrative roles, not a fixed number of occurrences. A gag can remain gentle, recur seasonally, change ownership, end early or return after a long absence. Progression is conditional; no compulsory escalating disaster.

| Record / operation | Contract |
|---|---|
| Identity | Bind one gag family plus actual recurring people/object/site, world/branch and setup receipt. Two visually similar animals are not automatically the same animal. Canon cast/objects remain reserved. |
| Recognition | Per audience, retain whether the setup was actually heard/witnessed and which variant they encountered. Use P14/P19's appropriate retained references; no omniscient social memory. |
| Escalation | Prefer changed context, competent adaptation, role reversal or a larger harmless audience before greater harm. Admission requires the real new cause and means. Callback doesn't revive a repaired failure. |
| Player response | Laugh, help, ignore, join, stop the routine, ask for seriousness, or simply continue. Muting comic presentation need not delete actual world consequences. No required choice or explanation to prove dislike. |
| Durable result | Save occurrence and gag variant with P15 frozen text/revision and consumed draw in existing owner. Failed publication adds no recognition or escalation. The same receipt never pays or advances twice. |

## Z2.1: legendary is a world-local adjective

A legendary occurrence can be uncommon, periodic, deliberately summoned, hidden, invitation-bound, fate-linked, witness-rare, travel-dependent, impossible for one entrant yet reachable through another supported route, or common at a high-power location. Rarity describes **the event in its world and sampling cohort**. It does not impose a global timer, one-per-save rule, several-hundred-hour wait, compulsory pity or anti-abuse budget. Root's hundreds-of-hours language is one possible discovery experience, not required access latency.

A rare event has actual cause/means, supported effect, disclosure route, impact/recovery policy and stable instance/revision. It may give spectacle, a contact, opportunity, unique source-qualified transfer, new method, communal memory or quiet recognition. “Legendary” need not mean more damage or a catastrophe. Relative capability changes an event's impact, following [COSMIC_EVENTS](../../../../COSMIC_EVENTS.md), not a global random punishment.

Pure texture rarity can be cheap. Function-changing cosmic or narrative power uses its existing real authority owner and separate selected gate; P17 handles story-as-power. An event can have both a chance discovery and deliberate research/summon route where justified. Rarity weights never reveal a sealed answer through changed totals, labels, unlocks, tooltips or debug output. Public results use secret ID/route/status only.

Future Z2 proof combines a valid eligible-state witness, a seeded rare branch through real admission, ordinary-route controls, preview purity and saved next-draw parity. A long sample with zero occurrences does not prove unreachable; a forced fixture does not prove natural encounter rate. Show actual effect on the selected player route and human memorability separately.

## Z3.1: story firsts read receipts, not narrative adjectives

First is scoped: this body, life, branch, world instance, companion pair, household or selected lineage. A known first differs from first actual occurrence and first recorded occurrence. An old save with no evidence starts at “first recorded since tracking” rather than acquiring invented history. A first-ever world claim needs complete relevant evidence or remains unknown.

| Candidate | Authoritative detection | What may be remembered / caveat |
|---|---|---|
| First learned/practically used method | actual acquisition and successful execution receipts, separately | A lesson offered is not learned; a method description is not use. |
| First completed commission / useful craft | funded settlement, product/custody and real function | No duplicate achievement on import or on rereading its account. |
| First shared meal / chosen home / return | actual occasion agreement/completion, occupancy or visit receipt | Friendship or happiness is not inferred from co-location. Retain consequential references without pinning every texture visit. |
| First love / betrayal / reconciliation | explicit authored relationship milestone, actor/player interpretation or recorded disclosure | These words are subjective. Label whose account; no machine infers love from a score or betrayal from a missed message. |
| First kill / loss / extraordinary victory | selected battle/death/outcome owner, target identity and scope | Native defeat is unconscious recovery; no true-death milestone invented. A boast or rumor is not a combat receipt. P27 owns death treatment. |

Optional achievements are a projection of these receipts. Rewards, if selected, belong in their real action owner, not a biography callback. Allow hiding labels, keeping a private memory, defining another milestone or revisiting its route. A first records history; it never forbids a second relationship, repeated craft or another life. Forks preserve inherited firsts as parent history and distinguish newly earned branch firsts without merging counterparts' lives.
