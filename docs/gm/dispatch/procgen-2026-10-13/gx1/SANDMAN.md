| Domain | Profile constraints and concrete suggestion |
|---|---|
| Generated vs canon-reserved | Original mortal visitors, dream accounts, custodians and minor petitioners where source permits. Reserve Endless identities/offices, named inhabitants, iconic artifacts and arc outcomes. Original metaphor does not become canon cosmology. |
| Resources / harvesting | Mundane paper/repair stock, copies of known accounts and source-qualified dream carriers. An experienced dream is information, not automatically a material token or power currency. Exact dream extraction/ownership is unqualified. |
| Creation | A physical original tale/edition can have a real audience or teaching consumer. Supernatural nightmare/dream manufacture needs authority, source, carrier and effects; no arbitrary stat potion from a pretty metaphor. |
| People / names | Mortal regional names separate from original nonhuman naming conventions and role titles. Claimed identity, nature, office and known name differ. Canon attendants cannot be randomly replaced. |
| CG3 encounters | Original petition package: unmet request, actual petitioner means, attributed memory, two supported ways to assist and a refusal route. A dream threat needs a qualified body/interaction/counter law; ordinary research can first establish useful information. |
| CG2 legacy mantles | Original archive/custodial role can transfer access, records and obligations. An Endless office or source-specific succession is authored and scene-qualified; title inheritance alone cannot confer cosmic authority. |
| CG5 power origins | Dream visitor, trained mortal, admitted supernatural role and object holder are distinct eligibility packages. A dream-themed presentation does not grant dream travel or reality editing. Exceptional source/AU acquisition remains possible after qualification. |
| CG4 challenge cards | A petition or source-qualified dream trial on an authored node with issuer, objective, memory/carrier retention and exit. Changing dream scenery is description unless a separately authored spatial revision is selected; no layouts. |
| CG8 issue / arc cadence | Anthology-like personal stories group actual receipts. A recurring motif can connect different known accounts without forcing a climax or revealing concealed cosmological truth. Narrative closure is not automatic death or a completed cosmic office. |
| CG7 retcon / reboot | A new framing/description reprojects saved accounts. A true story/history alteration requires explicit authority and a retained-memory manifest. Different tellings remain attributed claims; no overwriting one into objective truth. |
| CG6 secret-identity pressure | Pressure concerns concealed nature, roles or who authored an account. Recognising a symbol/voice requires evidence; identity knowledge does not imply access to all memories or powers. Sealed variants use IDs only. |
| Idle fit | Reading, writing, restoration, rest and qualified dream practice can progress under actual conditions. Sleep is not unconditional XP or forced dream travel. Pending supernatural bargains require their own consent/standing-intent policy. |
