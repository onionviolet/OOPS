| CG4 challenge cards | Authored house/archive/site with complaint, entry rights, evidence channels, local deadline where supported and exit. A case chain is not a dungeon map; no layouts. |
| CG8 issue / arc cadence | Receipt-backed case beats finish with useful archive/relationship consequences. Historical interludes project recorded events; they do not issue fresh items. |
| CG7 retcon / reboot | Earlier-published or newly revealed historical cases are reviewed deltas. Preserve resolved case truth/chronology; explicit AU reconciliation handles later contradiction. Never regenerate witnesses. |

| Idle fit | Research, preservation, equipment preparation and qualified field queues with real staff/access. Investigation choices wait; a closed app does not generate a destructive haunting or missed ritual. |
