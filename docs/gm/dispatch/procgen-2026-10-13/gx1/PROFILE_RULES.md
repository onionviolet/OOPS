The later GX1 instruction overrides COMMON's old worktree/build/commit procedure: main checkout, only `gx1/`, docs only, root commits. ## P1: source and profile boundary

Use **SHOWN/SOURCE** for inspected code, **SHOWN/PRIMARY** only for an actually inspected scene, **STATED/PUBLISHER** for official synopsis/catalogue claims, **STATED/SECONDARY** for a research owner's wiki summaries, and **INFERRED/PROPOSAL** for generator design. A proposed issue/episode locator is a future reading target, not evidence that its contents were inspected. [SOURCE_NOTES](SOURCE_NOTES.md) records the actual boundary and contradictions. All generator-domain rows in the family profiles are INFERRED/PROPOSAL unless explicitly attributed otherwise.

Select work, medium, edition/language, continuity, source cutoff, entry era and authored region before eligibility. Publication year, fictional date and played clock differ. An editorial era (CG1) may affect tone and candidate weights; it does not itself change saved bodies, institutions, laws or memories. An adaptation and its novel are separate profiles even when a shared law is later verified.

Generate original gap fillers inside qualified authored laws. Reserve named heroes/villains, aliases, plot-essential unnamed roles, unique powers/artifacts and causally important events. Reservation prevents accidental generation or cloning; source-qualified learning, succession, rescue, theft, recovery, transfer and explicit AU counterparts remain reachable. A vanished holder does not make its power or mantle a vacant random slot. Direct runtime recreation data stays in a private pack outside git/bundle per [PROJECT](../../../../../PROJECT.md); these docs contain attributed analysis, not cast catalogues, comic art or raw prose.

People and names defer to [P7 guidelines](../p7/GUIDELINES.md) and [data shape](../p7/DATA_SHAPE.md). Each family links them. Use curated culture/era cohorts and reviewed original names; store spellings, aliases and identity independently of title, source, rank, licence and knowledge. No familiar surname grants a canon relative, power or office. Collision handling is internal and cannot disclose a secret alias mapping.

X1: maintain a canon-density mask by authored node/era, with reserved event predicates. Suggest background commissions that intersect a canonical event only through real supply/rescue consequences. X2: track who paid for and witnessed a heroic deed; generate contradictory press accounts from that transmission, not from omniscient truth. Local adjectives can be abundant, concealed, licensed, renewable, inherited, fragile or inexhaustible. Replaying a receipt cannot duplicate its settlement; new causes can produce new work. No fixed player-wide "once" rule, forced either/or, hunger death or new offline-loss policy is proposed.

## P5: common future acceptance gate

All runtime gates are **PROPOSED / NOT RUN**. Before canonical implementation, qualify the exact selected operation from a primary scene/rule or retain an original labelled fixture. Bind actual live owners and an explicit old-save absence/migration policy. Meaningful focused tests cover eligibility, coherent package/counters, pure previews, staged draw accounting, failed writes, invalid import and legacy compatibility.

Then use an own rebuilt fresh origin for a native journey: actually acquire means, admit work/encounter, reach a useful changed consumer, and exercise an alternative or refusal. Save/reload at pending and changed-positive states; UI Export and **literal-file Import** into a second fresh origin compare IDs, truth, revisions, funds/custody, persona evidence, progress/choices, receipt settlement and next draws. Native natural, seeded-native and source evidence are separate. If UI changes, rebuilt **1366x768** and **1600x1000** prove readable choices, controls and known results. Private saves remain untouched. Human fiction/balance/taste acceptance is separate. Writing this packet closes none of these gates.
