## Worked original route: the relay-thread workshop

O-CM7a, proposal: a paid cartridge process makes fictional adhesive thread; a two-part spool/nozzle device delivers it to secure a survey rig. This extends P6 COATINGS/TOOLS and CM7 with a consumable/device interface, rather than adding a second potion resolver. The player may buy cartridges, make a learned formula, hire a maker or research a new one under available world laws.

The cartridge definition carries composition/process/revision. Its instance carries remaining charge; the device instance carries installed nozzle, pressure class and condition. A design revision records compatible cartridge families and a real supported repair/work consumer. The ordinary scaffold task supplies the initial target. A field test reveals adhesion on that surface and the delivered portion, not every chemical truth or another person's maker identity. A replacement nozzle can change actual delivery without changing the already-made cartridge's truth.

Idle route: queue known cartridges on supported played time; hold only actually admitted stock. Feed a threshold request through CR3 rather than invent a workshop queue. NPC maker has their own means/knowledge through CR4. Prestige/crossing carries actual objects and catalogue closure through existing owners. Ports and gates are in [REPORT](REPORT.md), with no build selected.

For a private Spider-Man version, exact sourced device/fluid, character, continuity and acquiring means would replace these original laws only after qualification. This note neither creates that pack nor copies a canon formula. For a private Locke & Key or manhua workshop, use its selected identity/source rules instead of forcing cartridge arithmetic onto symbolic or living power.
