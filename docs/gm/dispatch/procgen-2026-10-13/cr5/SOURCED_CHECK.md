## Evidence and continuity boundary

**SHOWN** means CR5 inspected a page, source code or an official presentation artifact. It does not mean CR5 read the original comic scene. **STATED-primary** means an official creator/publisher explanation; **STATED-wiki** means a fan summary with the cited page/section or its issue/chapter lead. **INFERRED** means a design consequence or proposed analogy. **U** means unresolved, conflicting or unsupported in the inspected boundary. These coexist: a page is shown, while its account of a scene is stated. Under [LORE_AND_CONTINUITY](../../../../worlds/LORE_AND_CONTINUITY.md), official explanations map to C, official adaptation facts to A, proposals to O, inference to I and unresolved claims to U. No whole-series or issue-level reading is claimed.

Review route: web indexed official/wiki text, and successful direct official-page opens. Marvel direct opens returned 403; switched to indexed text. Marvel/MHA Fandom domain-restricted search reported robots blocks; indexed text remained available via ordinary search. No repeated blocked fetches, account login, purchases or novel-text intake. Wiki revision IDs are unavailable on this route. The following are **named author claim snapshots**, revision r1, not claims that the live wiki was archived immutably. A chosen private pack must pin exact source revisions/editions and execution scenes before implementing a canon rule. Inspection date is not an in-world date or latest-publication cutoff.

## C1-C5: consequences of the corrections

| Code | Proposal for root reconciliation, without editing root owners |
|---|---|
| C1 | Replace exclusive-source language with exact **recipe, origin, identity, authority or body** predicates. Scarce know-how is not automatically AB5 uniqueness. |
| C2 | Separate character rating, hunter licence/rank, item rarity and measured performance. Neither wiki category counts nor a printed grade establishes world probabilities or exact output strength. |
| C3 | Pin a claimset/era before manufacturing canon: comic model versus MCU Mark; New Earth versus Prime Earth; original manga versus anime; main webtoon versus Ragnarok; album versus film. |
| C4 | Every extraordinary production rule needs its actual source, actor, carrier and consumer. This permits powerful cheats, duplication and superior methods when the selected law supports them. It does not impose balancing drawbacks. |
| C5 | Mark all CX cheats and SU10 styling as original proposals unless a selected scene proves the specific capability. Existing analogues remain useful even when they are narrower. Source silence is unresolved, not prohibited. |
