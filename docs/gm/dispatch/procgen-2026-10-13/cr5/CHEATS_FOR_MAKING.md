## Proposed lens record and action boundary

Semantic fields, not reserved save schema: `observerRef`, `lensRef/revision`, `subjectRef/revision`, `worldInstanceRef`, allowed property/claim scope, `basis` (measured, stated, modelled, supernatural), value or supported range, confidence/error semantics, evidence/occurrence refs, observed clock, and attribution/contradiction refs. Body recipient, route, dosage and current station context belong in the subject when they affect the observation. Know-which-continuity is separate from know-the-formula.

A **prediction** is a saved claim only if the player intentionally records it. A **measurement** follows an actual valid protocol. A **supernatural reveal** changes knowledge under the lens's declared access law. An **override** changes the world under an explicit action and stages those changed owners too. Do not make a recipe ghost silently alter the recipe it reads, or a blueprint reader manufacture parts for free.

Proposed admission: read existing target/context and observer knowledge without registration → check lens/body/source/access and current revisions → compute scoped view → if observation is requested, stage its allowed knowledge/cost/draw changes → validate the full envelope → durable write → publish. Stronger lenses may directly reveal truth where authorized by world law and discovery route. An ordinary adviser operates only on its known model. A failed save changes neither lens uses, RNG, stock, claims nor product truth.

**Sealed route contract:** if an eligible lens discovers a sealed secret, ordinary UI may display its truth only within that discovery route. CR5 reports/checkpoints continue to show ID, route and status only. No value leaks through warning intensity, price, colour, empty-slot counts, priority, sort order, recommendation text or filtered-out candidate totals. Developer tools use synthetic fixtures unless that secret was specifically unsealed. A disclosure filter is not an invented anti-cheat weakness.

## First original reveal comparison

O-CX17a: acquire two relay-thread nozzles and their two legitimate design sheets. Ordinary inspection sees wear and port size. A measured trial reveals delivered portion on the surveyed surface. Version-diff sight reveals the known changed part; recipient-fit sight supplies only the supported operator/route compatibility. The player chooses a supported nozzle, delivers an actual cartridge portion and secures a real rig. Expert hire, ordinary trial and a powerful direct lens are reachable alternatives.

Specified controls: same recipe with changed label; copied maker mark; identical grade but different relevant interface; hidden truth versus missing model; another observer without shared knowledge; incompatible off-world decoder; negative/inconclusive assay; write failure; mid-dose and changed-positive Save/reload/Export/literal-file Import. These are **NOT RUN**. A single boolean-observed test cannot verify the richer lens contract.
