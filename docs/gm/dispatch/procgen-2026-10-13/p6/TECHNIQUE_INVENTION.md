## Shared invention axes

| Axis | Proposed contract |
|---|---|
| Logic | L3 executable operation/condition graph and information pattern; L4 required source/body/lineage/binding identity; L2 gesture/carrier/array/body arrangements where causal; L1 refined pills or physical intermediates. |
| Inputs | Known primitive operations, actual source and target means, experimental material/fuel, practice site and selected objective. A borrowed teacher's capability is explicitly the teacher's contribution, not the student's new faculty. |
| Process | Set goal → compose supported operators/conditions → predict → experiment → observe → revise/version → validate → rehearse → author manual. A failed theory remains a theory. Publishing a manual does not certify its claims. |
| Skill | Discipline competence, execution precision, research and teaching are separate. Invention learns supported connections, not every power with matching flavor. Continuous queued drills can progress through actual eligible effort/conditions. |
| Knowledge | Hypothesis, received formula, demonstrated mechanism, own mastery and teachable explanation remain separate. Tests reveal only their scope. Player free text is metadata; mechanical selectors come from typed controls. |

Generated: supported operator graph, precise conditions/costs, learned definition revision, experimental receipt and manual content graph. Authored: finite operators with world-qualified units, available means, validator, source exceptions and native target consumers. The output must change actual work/casting/combat/teaching. Mere naming, randomly selecting a prewritten power or adding a numerical buff is not the procedural-creation goal.

## Idle, persistence and identity

Queue experimentation, study and rehearsal with exact effort/fuel/material and target reservations. Stop on unsupported body/source or shortage; preserve partial fractional mastery and observation receipts. First slice played time only. Equal eligible elapsed time gives equal progress, independent of clicks/logins; a design preview consumes no draw.

Save graph/condition semantic versions, validated world-law/source prerequisites, research receipt, actual knowledge/ownership/mastery and execution counters, teaching edition and mutable vow state separately. Formula revisions create new immutable definitions; existing readers/products retain their revision. Vow obligations need due trigger and settlement state, not a static modifier. Import validates definitions before references and never grants a missing faculty to repair an unknown operator. Known lawful source exceptions remain possible through their explicit route.

## First playable slice S9-A: invent a fitting procedure

After actually learning one mundane fitting method, compose two supported stage orders and one handle/effort choice at the Village bench. Spend material/time to test each; record the real result; save a working procedure; run it as a queued method producing a different usable tool/kit outcome. This makes a learned method rather than a new innate power. Author a manual and demonstrate a qualified collaborator following it without inheriting the inventor's mastery. S11's reusable manual adapter is a dependency for that final teaching gate.
