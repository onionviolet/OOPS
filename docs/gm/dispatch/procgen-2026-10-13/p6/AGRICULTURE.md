## Product law and axes

| Axis | Proposed contract |
|---|---|
| Logic | L4 producer/lineage identity; L2 graft/host/body relationships; L1 derived food/reagent material; L3 only for a locally supported magical cultivation pattern. A seed bundle is not a homogeneous potion. |
| Inputs | Actual viable propagule or compatible parent identities, site rights/capacity, feedstock/water/soil and tools. Creature stock has real body/life state and space/feed means; its biological lineage is not copied from NPC family generation. |
| Process | Sow/inoculate/graft, tend, mature, harvest; crossing makes an offspring under an authored inheritance law. Grafting preserves scion/rootstock roles and does not average their genomes. A harvest can end or retain a producer according to its family. |
| Skill | Cultivation/husbandry affects supported care, selection and yield reliability; does not grant nonexistent viability, speed or every parent's trait. Skill is distinct from site quality and local body/energy compatibility. |
| Knowledge | Observed stages, recorded parentage and measured harvest responses; genotype and future yield stay limited to learned evidence. A visual observation need not assay potency. |

Generated: correlated supported cultivar/strain traits, growth response, offspring/harvest records and actual derived batch. Authored: ecology, viability and compatibility, finite inheritance operators, care boundaries and harvest law. The real consumer is a meal/reagent/feed/tool material already supported by a generated-product adapter. A beautiful plant with no use may be texture, but is not proof of procedural ingredient manufacture. Useful traits might improve yield under one environment or change a cooking response; never roll an independent buffet of unrelated buffs.

## Idle and persistence

Enroll one producer per actual capacity, pay supported care once per chosen routine, then grow through eligible game time. Queue tending/harvest and reserve next seed only at its admission; stop on shortage/receiver full. Leave current MG06 closed-browser rule unchanged. Offline growth, seasons and autonomous workers are separate later applicability decisions, not requirements for an idle slice. No hidden starvation/upkeep of existing creatures is introduced.

Save producer/body/lineage identity, generation/law revisions, environment/care interval, developmental stage, next boundary, fractional progress, custody and harvest settlement. Offspring draw is retained at committed conception/propagation and not rerolled on harvest/reload. Actual seeds remain heterogenous sublots where traits matter. Save resolved offspring truth, not only parent seed IDs; content edits cannot rewrite old lineages.
