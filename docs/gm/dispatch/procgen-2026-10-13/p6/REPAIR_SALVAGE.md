## Product law and axes

| Axis | Proposed contract |
|---|---|
| Logic | L2 replaces/refits parts; L1 recovers or spends material; L3 repairs a pattern only through its own supported law; L4 preserves unique identity and custody. |
| Inputs | One actual owned object/instance, assessed damage, compatible offcut/replacement/binder, tools, means and energy. A knowledge record is not the object. |
| Process | Diagnose → choose repair/refit/disassemble → recover declared parts or scrap → test. Remelt deliberately destroys prior structure. Upgrade installs a real new part or process; repair alone restores declared function and does not increase tier. |
| Skill | Diagnosis, fitting and salvage control affect supported losses/repair quality. Irreparable damage stays irreparable unless the world has a sourced restoration route. |
| Knowledge | Observed fault versus inferred cause; actual replaced part truth governs performance. Diagnosis may reveal limited damage without revealing sealed construction truths. |

Generated: repair revision and actual changed condition, recoverable quantities, installed replacement graph and resulting response. Authored: repairable fault classes, recovery tables/operators and upgrade laws. Real consumer: restore sharp fitting-knife work or refit a generated brace/tool whose native work function had changed. No new universal durability tax; use explicitly worn/dulled items already in a chosen route or opt-in supported condition.

## Idle and persistence

Queue assessed repair with exact object and replacement escrow. Object cannot be equipped, sold or separately salvaged while held. Cancel before irreversible operation returns actual escrow; after cutting/remelting, publish declared partial output/waste rather than recreate the original. Repeat repair plan applies to independently eligible objects, not the same settled object twice. Played time initially; future offline equivalence needs its own test.

Persist stable object ID and repair history separately from immutable product-type truth; selected mutable condition cannot alias a stack of differently worn objects. Record custody, installed parts, planned irreversible boundary, residue and completed receipt. No duplicate object references between inventory and installed slots. Preserve legacy optional durability settings and condition keys.

## Book laws

- K5, HP Order of the Phoenix ch15/CCF15: restoring a bowl does not restore the spilled essence. Container repair cannot refund lost contents.
- K6, MoL ch43/ch46/ch48: frame repair, glyph/control repair and replenishing fuel are different operations. Repairing metal does not recharge or grant casting through a relay.
- K10, Shadow Slave ch695-701: incompatible or failed weave construction can damage anchors; remaking a pattern needs actual maker means, not merely enough scrap mass.

[Source limits](CANON_LOCATORS.md) apply; no unsourced universal recoverability percentage.
