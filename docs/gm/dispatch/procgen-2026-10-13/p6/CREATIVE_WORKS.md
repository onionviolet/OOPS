## Product law and axes

| Axis | Proposed contract |
|---|---|
| Logic | L3 information/score/procedure graph; L2 physical binding/surface or instrument; L1 paper/ink material; L4 attribution/author/edition and exact object provenance only where queried. Information copying differs from duplicating a unique physical item. |
| Inputs | Actual observations, known methods, author skill, paper/ink or performance means, target audience and purpose. Drawn map depends on measured route evidence; imagined art may be fictional without asserting world facts. |
| Process | Outline/select → compose/annotate → draft → revise/test or rehearse → make edition/copy/performance → distribute. Original generated text uses finite grammar over actual chosen claims; no novel prose ingestion or raw-book publication. |
| Skill | Research accuracy, literacy, explanatory teaching and artistic performance are distinct supported skills. Fine binding does not make false instructions correct; expertise need not make a good teacher. |
| Knowledge | Facts/hypotheses/testimony retain basis, audience and uncertainty. Manuals can be incomplete/wrong without changing physics. A learner acquires knowledge through study, competence through actual practice, and source/body means separately. |

Generated: content graph, supported quality dimensions, edition, audience interpretation and neutral prose/score/map representation. Authored: topic/claim vocabulary, explanation/render grammar, practice prerequisites and consumer rules. Player titles/notes are metadata and cannot rewrite mechanical requirements or sealed truth.

| Work | Actual proposed consumer |
|---|---|
| Recipe/manual | A learner queues a supported method, paying their own materials/source; incomplete instructions change supported study/test outcomes. |
| Map | Route planner/expedition uses known measured edges and uncertainty, changing actual preparation/navigation. No new dungeon topology required. |
| Book/account | Literacy/study or a qualified reader's knowledge; D8 can supply attributed deed facts but public prose never becomes authoritative truth. |
| Music | A real performance or recorded composition changes a supported practice/recovery/audience response; score copying alone is not performance mastery. |
| Art | A named patron's taste/commission or displayed object's appearance/reputation consumer; no universal automatic stat buff. |

Selling/reputation require an actual buyer/audience, budget/demand and received work. Repeating an edition is legitimate production, but cannot repeatedly settle the same delivery or mint unbacked payment. Pure texture is allowed; identify when it has no mechanical consumer.

## Idle and persistence

Queue writing/copying/rehearsal/study through eligible played time and actual tools. Bind the current claim/protocol edition at admission. Stop on paper/ink shortage or unavailable audience. No required constant active input; a manual can support repeated work. Save immutable content/claim graph, edition/grammar/law revisions, author/basis/audience, physical-copy inventory, score/map and teaching progress plus queue escrow. Copies share a content ID while actual objects have correct counts/custody. Learned knowledge survives losing the book under its local rule; losing a book does not supply fuel or grant mastery. Old notebook route loads unchanged.

## First playable slice S11-A: write a repair manual another maker uses

After observing a successful actual fitting procedure, pay paper/ink/time to write a manual with input requirements, stage order and known result. Transfer a real copy to an eligible maker, arrange one supported teaching/study interval, then see that maker use their own stock/tool/effort to produce a usable repair. A funded commission can pay for the actual delivered copy; reputation records that receipt once. P4 generated maker is optional; an existing supported maker is enough.

Acceptance, all OPEN: SOURCE validates content versus copy identity, honest claim basis, limited teaching, stock/payment accounting and publication rollback. NATIVE proves known experiment → written object → transfer/study → maker's actual output and one real funded delivery; empty or unsupported instructions grant no source. Save across drafting, transfer and learner work; reload/Export/literal-file Import preserve editions, knowledge, copies, payment receipt and progress. Common gates apply. Maps/music/art/books each need their named native consumer, not accepted by a manual alone.
