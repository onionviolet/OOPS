## Idle and persistence

Known blueprints queue inscription with actual carrier/fuel/tool reservations. Research may progress through selected drills; universal passive mastery is not assumed. Persistent arrays spend from one real reservoir at meaningful time boundaries, pause on exhaustion and resume only after lawful supply. Shared modes cannot each claim a full charge. First slice uses played time, no new offline energy farm.

Save graph/operator versions, port bindings, carrier/anchor custody, coverage, energy ledger, operator source and installed/current mode. Save generated definitions before validating instances. Copying information may cost writing/study while installation spends material; do not multiply conserved tokens. Cancellation law explicitly accounts damaged carrier/anchors. Legacy authored artifact keys and lamp procedures stay unchanged.
