## A1: Coordinator-owned acceptance gate

1. **G1 direct/counter/workaround:** Both policies show attack delivery, allowed or
   blocked aid, supported suppression, suppressor removal and persistent aftermath
   through the selected actual consumer. No duplicate activation cost or duration
   reset on restoration; removal at/after expiry cannot revive a barrier.
2. **G2 timing/support:** Change required tool/body/location or source during an
   accepted action. Admission-only versus sustained support follows the selected
   policy. Exact-expiry delivery, source loss, suppressor expiry and two simultaneous
   suppressors resolve in declared order. Invalid target/contact leaves input state
   unchanged; a legitimate blocked attempt follows its disclosed cost policy.
3. **G3 actor swap:** Exchange attacker, protected actor and suppressor user without
   player-specific privilege. An uninformed NPC cannot choose a counter from hidden
   execution facts; it needs acquired means, observed basis and opportunity. Test
   encounter-response and timed-completion entry points if selected, not only commands.
4. **G4 transactions/persistence:** Failed durable write, stale quote/target, duplicate
   commitment, save/reload/import while suppressed and old-content loading preserve
   IDs, costs, expiry and observer evidence. Preview does not change RNG, clock,
   knowledge, event history or teach an NPC the player's intended action.
5. **G5 UI/native proof:** Known explanation separates present/suppressed/removed
   and quote/attempt/observed result. No hidden causes in disabled reasons/tooltips.
   Coordinator rebuilds and runs the selected sequence from a fresh disposable
   origin; source tests alone do not close gameplay.
