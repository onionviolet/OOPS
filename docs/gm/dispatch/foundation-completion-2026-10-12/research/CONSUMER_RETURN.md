## Selected consumer gates

G1: actor A learns a report while B remains uninformed; returning Ink scene changes only after actual information delivery. Unknown/private content must not appear in reasons, quote text or UI merely because it is stored.

G2: a learned procedure changes one supported action, while absent tool/body/faculty/fuel blocks execution. Copy/transfer/practice retain their declared distinctions and current actor; do not grant donor mastery by default.

G3: an invitation is independently answered; refusal changes no funds/materials and no forced affection. Accepted work reserves actual participants and pays exactly once. A durable continuing promise survives more than32 unrelated memory deliveries, interrupted contact and generated save/reload/import.

G4: owner/holder/loan/access are observed before and after actual transfer/return and failed publication; inaccessible or equipped/borrowed goods cannot silently become ordinary stock. Recipient uses output under their own support and policy.

G5: definition-only versus enrolled controller fixtures differ observably; scheduler action invokes the selected actor with their own body/inventory/support/knowledge and its remembered consequence. No personal saves, inspector provision or pure catalog count substitutes for natural native proof.
