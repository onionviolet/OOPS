## PW2. Standing, frequency and legality answer different questions
Extend `docs/CHEAT_LIBRARY.md`, WR1 and WR5, by allowing simultaneous descriptions.
Its five standing words remain useful summaries. They need not be an exclusive ladder.
A common power can be forbidden. A legal rare gift can still use ordinary world rules.
A world-law effect may exist everywhere while only a few people can invoke it.
“Cheat” can describe exceptional access to rules rather than literal violation of physics.

| Description | Meaning to consider | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World law | The setting supplies this rule within its declared domain | Every body has equal access |
| Common | Many people in the named comparison population have access | Universal availability, legality or cheapness |
| Rare gift | Unusual access that fits the world's recognized possibilities | Stronger than every ordinary method |
| Cheat | Access beyond the ordinary baseline or an exceptional rule exception | Unlimited power or automatic foreign-world operation |
| Forbidden | A named authority prohibits possession, use, teaching or trade | Metaphysical impossibility or universal moral agreement |

## RY2. Keep chance quantities distinct
Consider stating base rate, eligible acquisition chance, encounter chance and start-selection weight separately.
For illustration only: a district has 10,000 adults and 10 active lens holders.
Its active prevalence is 0.1 percent in that district, at that recorded time.
A guild can employ eight holders without making eight in every ten townspeople holders.
A scenario selecting from guild staff can encounter one often without altering the district rate.
A research route can yield a lens reliably after its real requirements without changing birth incidence.
Do not multiply arbitrary world, class and family weights as though they were independent probabilities.
A weighted draw gives the selected weight divided by the eligible weight total, not the raw weight as a percentage.
For dependent starts, consider choosing a coherent package before selecting compatible power variants.
Stated basis: library RS1 and RS5; `FIRST_CHEATS_AND_DEATH_REWARDS_2026-10-13.md`, D1 and D5.

## RY3. Social distribution deserves causes
A hereditary gift can cluster among landowners because inheritance preserves it.
A taught gift can cluster among rich families because tuition and safe practice are costly.
A dangerous industrial exposure can concentrate powers among workers while employers own the equipment.
A public school or mutual-aid network can spread access without giving everyone equal mastery.
A wandering patron can favor outsiders, producing holders with little money or legal protection.
A monopoly can purchase most artifacts while poor residents rent their services.
These are original possibilities, not claims that wealth intrinsically causes better souls.
Consider independent inheritance of faculty, carrier, training, property and legal title.
A child may inherit the device and debts but lack the body to use it.
A lineage can lose its monopoly when a reproducible method spreads.

## RY7. Pity belongs to a declared process

Extend UP5 by identifying which qualifying attempts advance pity and what the guarantee grants.
A transparent search mode might increase the chance of a useful lead after completed expeditions.
A research route might guarantee a usable partial version after enough real evidence.
A patron might promise an offer after a term of service.
A mystery mode can conceal the fictional mechanism while respecting the selected display policy.
A guarantee can deliver an opportunity, a choice, a fragment or the power itself.
Distinguish those rewards before calling an unlucky run protected.

Illustrative fixed independent odds: ten 10 percent attempts yield about a 65.1 percent chance of at least one success.
Ten attempts do not imply a guarantee. A pity schedule needs its own changing conditional odds.
Failed previews, reloads and canceled unpaid actions need not count as attempts.
Scope could be per source, life, lineage or player profile, with known reset behavior.
A world can choose no pity, generous pity, repeated guarantees or direct selection.
None requires a global “only once” limit or a permanent loss of earned discovery.

## RY9. Rarity should shape progression without replacing it

A common family can have deep mastery. A rare family can begin as a tiny convenience.
Choose acquisition frequency, initial usefulness, growth rate, ceiling and exposure independently.
For a quiet life, consider a weak broadly useful gift with cheap upkeep.
For a dramatic outlier life, consider immediate strong power and consequences that fit its actual acts.
A powerful start need not be secretly weakened until it feels ordinary.
A normal-strength world need not inflate every enemy because the player found something rare.
Keep routine work useful through other goals, people, mastery, research and choices.
Where a power truly removes a bottleneck, let it remove that bottleneck and expose the next fitting decision.
Do not add arbitrary taxes merely to restore the old inconvenience.

Extend library “A good progression loop for cheats” with acquisition pacing separate from power growth.
Waiting for a lucky drop is not a substitute for a satisfying first use.
A rare discovery can open a reliable development path once obtained.
A reliable research path can preserve a rare advanced variant for later without making every attempt fruitless.
