# Multiversal cheats: considerations, not specs

2026-10-13. Docs-only research for the cheat-considerations dispatch.
Status: proposal palette for coordinator review. No mechanics selected or implemented.
Scope: powers, institutions and lives whose meaningful reach crosses worlds.
Only this output file is authored. Existing owners remain authoritative.

Multiversal can mean many places reached, many histories remembered, or many laws touched.
Those are different promises. A traveler can cross worlds without overpowering a town.
A world-destroying faculty can remain unable to cross its own border.
Consider each option for a fitting character, world and time in their development.
Keep reasonable exceptional versions reachable, including free carry and overwhelming power.
Rarity, costs, repeatability and ceilings are local dials. None becomes a fixed “once” rule.

## Crossing worlds without collapsing their rules

Inferred proposals. Extend the cosmology's backing table and Verse equalization section.
Use only the questions needed for a selected crossing. This is not a mandatory expanded schema.

| Code | Crossing question | Useful options and distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XW1 | What actually crosses? | Body, soul, item, memory, projection, message or source information; each has its own carrier |
| XW2 | Where can the source reach? | Named worlds, a treaty network, line-of-contact relays, recognized clients, or genuinely broad reach |
| XW3 | What reaches the destination first? | A remote message can arrive before cargo; local services can work before the patron's power channel |
| XW4 | What is the local baseline? | World law, common faculty, rare gift or exceptional advantage; compare the actual function |
| XW5 | How is it treated? | Accepted, regulated, feared, ignored or forbidden; legal treatment need not equal rarity or strength |
| XW6 | What can activate here? | Check carrier, user, fuel, contact, eligible target and actual support independently |
| XW7 | What does the converter do? | Relabel, supply fuel, map targets, alter output or grant authority; do not silently bundle these |
| XW8 | What happens to unsupported parts? | Blocked action, weakened effect, frozen service, retained record, transformed function or declared loss |
| XW9 | What happens on return? | Resume retained state, reconnect to changed terms, repair a severed link or discover that support has ended |
| XW10 | What did the character learn? | Actual readings, observed failures, testimony or hypotheses; a hidden border rule remains hidden |

Standing, strength and operation are separate readings.
A common hub license may permit travel while providing little force in a destination.
A native world-law panel may remain visible through an imported cheat after its local supplier stops.
A forbidden power can be weak. A celebrated power can be devastating.
Inferred: extend the library's standing section with a separate local legal/social annotation.
This avoids reading “forbidden” as a higher power grade or as proof of detection.

## Multiversal economy: what is being exchanged?

Inferred proposals. Extend cosmology Treaty rates, Layer economies and Exchange-rate markets.
The library already offers bought, bargained, found and researched cheats.
The addition here is the journey from advertised offer to usable benefit.

| Code | Exchange | Important distinction | Interesting work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MV20 | Goods for goods | Price differs from usefulness; a foreign tonic can retain stored energy without being locally reproducible | Test batches, source ingredients, deliver safely |
| MV21 | Fuel for fuel | Quantity, purity, storage loss and conversion method all matter | Research converters, maintain batteries, rent a refinery |
| MV22 | Power for credentials | Rank can open contracts without becoming spendable combat energy | Prove a feat, dispute an outdated grade, buy an assessment |
| MV23 | Service for reach | Transit, extraction, remote lessons and protection can be bought independently | Compare routes, subcontract, negotiate return guarantees |

| MV25 | Attention, fate or memory | Different currencies can change relationships or what the holder remembers | Find a broker, contest a valuation, decline a personal price |
| MV26 | Information about rates | A quote is knowledge with age, provenance and uncertainty | Survey markets, publish findings, exploit a mismatch |

A market quote could say: accepted input, delivered output, quantity, fee and known delivery conditions.
An operational adapter separately says whether the recipient can use that output here.
A hub rank-to-price table is not automatically a mana-to-qi converter.
Knowledge of a skill, capacity to execute it and a license to teach it can have different prices.
Remote shop access can sell a claim whose goods wait at a counter rather than teleporting into inventory.
Physical delivery, direct implantation and local service are distinct offers.

Consider directional rates and uncertain quotes instead of one universal power coin.
An Absolute-scale cosmology can still choose a common currency where that fits.
Scarcity can move to compatible recipients, delivery routes, maintenance, trust or time.
Low-friction exchange can also be the fantasy. Not every trade needs five fees and a customs quest.

Inferred: arbitrage can be enjoyable in-fiction content rather than something automatically forbidden.
A profitable circuit can deplete stock, change prices, attract brokers or finance a new route.
A duplication cheat can explicitly create new supply where its premise permits it.
Absent such a mechanism, accidental duplicate settlement remains a correctness defect.
This follows `docs/TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE.md`, Treat time cheats as actual state transformations.
Repeat rewards can be supported deliberately. Saving twice should not accidentally pay twice.

## Worked examples

All EX entries are inferred original proposals, not franchise claims or selected secret premises.
Each gives a small playable promise and possible growth. None fixes rarity or repeat limits globally.

### EX1. The contractor whose gift outlives the contract

An ordinary traveler accepts a hub's survey work. The hub lends translation and extraction.
It also teaches a navigation method as a permanent gift under stated terms.
In the destination, translation works through a relay. A damaged relay freezes new instructions.
The learned method still helps identify the return marker; it does not summon transport.
After resignation, the hub can end extraction service but cannot recall the taught gift under those terms.
First useful play: compare finishing the survey, repairing contact or arranging a local return.
Growth: buy independent passage, mature another loan or remain a supported contractor.
Extends MV2, MV24, XW2 and XW9; basis is cosmology Patron gifts.

### EX2. A myth at home, a shop clerk at the hub

A holder has a small portable shop token. Their village treats foreign stock as miraculous.
At a junction hub, similar tokens are common tools used by modest traders.
The holder gains peers and repair services. Their carried stock and personal skill remain intact.
A remote purchase reserves goods at a counter; delivery requires a paid route or a friend carrying cargo.
First useful play: import a useful tool or sell a local ingredient without receiving universal combat power.
Growth: research direct delivery, open a branch counter or specialize in rate information.
Extends MV3, MV15-MV17 and MV20-MV26; rarity and delivery terms can vary in other worlds.

### EX3. The window that has two suppliers

A traveler brings a known informational system into a world with ordinary native status windows.
The native supplier reads local class facts. The imported supplier keeps a private travel journal.
The same panel labels those known sources. Neither gives automatic access to other people's windows.
Beyond the imported system's relay reach, journal updates stop; native class information still works.
On leaving the world, the journal reconnects while local class readings become explicitly last seen.
First useful play: compare a new local reading with a retained personal observation.
Growth: learn a converter or acquire actual appraisal. Do not reward a mere skin change with power.
Extends XW34-XW37; basis is library standing and panel-kit discovery/eligibility separation.

### EX4. The battery and a profitable exchange circuit

A traveler carries a battery that accepts one hub fuel and supplies a supported local workshop.
An invented quote exchanges ten input units for six output units after conversion and fee.
The battery's storage loss and the workshop's consumption are separate known costs.
An old reverse-market quote seems profitable. Surveying it reveals actual current stock and price.
First useful play: power a useful repair, then decide whether a trade circuit is worth maintaining.
Growth: improve the converter, buy transport capacity or intentionally exploit a real mismatch.
An explicit duplication faculty can change supply; reconnecting the shop alone creates no duplicate order.
Extends MV21, MV26 and XW13; numbers illustrate one quote, not a universal exchange rate.

### EX5. A room anchor and an external debt

A traveler anchors a supported workshop room before an experiment.
They borrow fuel from a hub account outside the anchor's reset domain.
The room's failed experiment can rewind while the hub's paid delivery and debt remain.
If fuel export is forbidden, that is a known term; if deliberate duplication is supported, name that premise.
First useful play: choose a smaller experiment, negotiate a different loan or test the anchor's retention.
Growth: expand the anchor's domain, move its endpoint or obtain a patron who can reach both sides.
The example does not choose a universal conservation rule or automatic rewind priority.
Extends MV7 and XW30; basis is timeline time-transformations and cosmology time collisions.

### EX6. The heir gets a route, not the traveler's whole life

A traveler dies abroad after arranging a will, teaching one student and storing an item at a hub.
The home heir receives a delivered route book. The student keeps the method they actually learned.
The hub item remains in custody until a recognized claimant retrieves it under its terms.
A soul-bound faculty follows its own continuation mechanism rather than the estate's address.
First useful play: the heir deciphers a known route and contacts the custodian.
Growth: acquire travel, dispute a debt or continue an unfinished workshop project.
Another authored version can grant full inheritance or free delivery. This modest version is one option.
Extends MV11 and XW39-XW42; basis is origin composition and death reward recipients.

### EX7. A narrow concept meets a capable border inspector

A traveler can find an exit from inhabited places. They arrive inside a sealed transit station.
The faculty reveals a service hatch, not permission to use it or immunity to its hazards.
An inspector notices the opened hatch through a real alarm, without reading the traveler's secret memories.
First useful play: negotiate passage, repair the alarm, seek another exit or use the faculty openly.
Growth: learn whether uninhabited ruins, dream spaces or contracts count as places for this version.
An exceptionless variant can find exits from those too where deliberately authored.
Extends MV9, MV32 and XW32; “concept” alone settles neither breadth nor the inspector's authority.

### EX8. The quiet multiversal resident

A known companion travels in a declared interface carrier and comments on visited places.
