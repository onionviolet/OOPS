## Scope and evidence

**F1: The main pressure is concurrent questions, not the number of systems.**
A tree, sword or brood can require action, executor, partner and received evidence
at once. Folding all of that into one interchangeable context view loses useful
information; expanding every named panel loses the laptop height budget.

Target: **1366x768 and 1920x1080**, comfortable desktop text. Visible outcome
of this lane: a traceable surface census and explicit fit/conflict proposals.
Mobile, runtime work, builds, browsers, tests and save access are deferred or
prohibited by `COMMON.md`. No actual viewport fit or gameplay behavior is claimed.

**SHOWN** here means the local design text was inspected, with file/section/line
locators below. It proves what a plan says, not the underlying code or a cited
book/game fact. All placement, gates, promotion choices, scenario counts and
fit budgets below are **INFERRED** U5 proposals. No external research claims,
unchecked game references or MEMORY evidence are used. Source-stage claims are
not independently verified. Accepted direction still comes from USER_VISION,
PROJECT and the relevant owner, above these palettes.

A surface family is one distinct question/readout/interaction, not necessarily
a panel. World skins, each recipe, each anatomy part, each minigame and each
cheat instance are not additional families. Repeated proposals with the same
question use shared homes: the roster, inventory, history and policy editor are
reused, even where multiple rows name distinct contributions. This bounds
'every implied surface' to the 15 supplied documents, including their directly
named families, not recursively every dossier they link. Backend QA, schema,
simulation and source-reading tasks do not become player panels merely because
they appear in a plan. Developer-only surfaces are listed separately in D.

## O2: Contextual-slot competition and arbitration

Arbitration ranks the **pending decision**, not a permanent system priority.
Foreground ownership is the explicit transaction being handled. Background jobs
never capture X simply because they tick or finish. Critical interruptions use
SC5 attention; if execution truly changes context, switch atomically and preserve
the old draft's inputs, selection, focus and back route. Revalidate its quote on
resume against stock, body, time, location, counterparty and authority. Never
auto-commit a suspended purchase, cast, bid, body change or policy. No fictional
pause guarantee is invented: world clocks follow their actual selected policy.
