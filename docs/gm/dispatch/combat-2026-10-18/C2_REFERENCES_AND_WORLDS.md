# C2: combat and conflict, references and world canon

Read-only research, 2026-10-17. No repo file edited. Proposals only, not accepted decisions.

Labels: **STATED** = from a repo audit/report (path given) or the game's published design. **MEMORY** = my recall, unverified. **SHOWN** = from a repo world doc. Repo world K10 rows are themselves marked INFERRED by their author; I flag that where it matters.

## Existing repo authority that any combat design must respect

- **AD19 / OD12:** recovery is forgiving by default; native defeat is unconsciousness and recovery; danger and recovery strictness are separate dials; harsher play is optional or forewarned. (`docs/RULES_INDEX.md`, `docs/DIRECTION_SYNTHESIS.md#od12...`)
- **AD20:** a locally unwinnable situation may require going elsewhere or restarting. **AD21:** difficulty changes are explained and affect future play.
- **RC1:** match the cause; do not label a lethal outcome "unconscious" without a suitable premise. (DIRECTION_SYNTHESIS OD12)
- **BHC21-BHC25:** ordinary defeat leaves temporary Recovering, lasting injury is an explained dial; harm records its cause; severity scales per world; scars are identity cues; one condition contract over body/item/soul/place carriers. (`docs/BODY_AND_HEALTH_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-17.md`)
- **Runtime today:** `src/enemies.js` has `takeDamage({can_faint})` returning `fainted`; several world briefs preserve "native fainting, nonlethal duel and field-renewal terms" (elden-ring, fallout, skyrim WORLD_BRIEF). `docs/ENCOUNTERS_AND_PURSUIT.md` separates trigger, locator, access, contact and persistence, and lists wounds, grudges, debts, captured property and recurring opponents as unbuilt persistence.
- Opponents keep source-authored strength; no auto-scaling to the player (ENCOUNTERS_AND_PURSUIT, chainsaw-man, original-frontier briefs).

## Part A: game references

|---|---|---|
| Battle Brothers | Mood keeps a short reason history; missed pay/food affects individuals differently; severe mood can trigger desertion; wounds can redirect a fighter into another role; departure and death have separate record paths; combat, economy and ironman harshness are separate settings (STATED, `docs/research/battle-brothers/REPORT.md` F1, F4). Morale states (wavering, breaking, fleeing) per unit in battle and gear durability repaired with tools (MEMORY). | Retreat as a real order with morale cost; injury that changes role rather than only lowering numbers; separate harshness dials; wear consumes repair supplies so combat feeds the craft economy. |
| Dwarf Fortress | Body topology, tissue layers, arteries, nerves and infection are explicit combat and care inputs; attacks need grasp/stance support, so a lost faculty can invalidate an attack; healing names distinct repairs (bleeding, nerves, infection) rather than restoring a number; weapon contact/penetration vs material yield/fracture (STATED, `docs/research/dwarf-fortress/REPORT.md` DF-B01, B02, B04, M01). Sparring in barracks trains skill; combat reports are long blow-by-blow logs (MEMORY). | Wounds as facts on body parts that gate verbs (no grip, no sword); care as several repair kinds; sparring as low-risk training activity; a terse summary with an expandable blow log. |

| Mount & Blade | Battles check morale; routed troops flee; lords can be captured and ransomed; prisoners can be sold or recruited; surrender is offered when outmatched (MEMORY). | Surrender and ransom as normal ends of a fight; prisoners as an economic and political asset; an offer to yield before contact when strength is lopsided. |

Pawns are downed by pain or blood loss before death; downed enemies can be captured, treated and recruited; missing parts take bionics; pain and consciousness are body capacities (MEMORY). | A "downed" state between fighting and dead; capture-treat-recruit as a relationship route; capacities (moving, manipulation, sight) as the interface between wounds and work. |

| Crusader Kings 3 | Stress relief leaves a coping trait or a timed health/skill penalty; death memories are typed facts with participants and dates that later scenes query (STATED, `docs/research/crusader-kings-3/REPORT.md` F2, F5). Wars run on war score from battles, sieges and occupation; peace by white peace or surrender; single combat duels and prisoners for ransom or execution, with opinion consequences (MEMORY). | Conflict as politics: who wins a war is a negotiated settlement, not only a body count; memories of a fight shape later relationships; killing has social cost. |

| Slay the Spire | Deck of attacks, blocks and powers; visible enemy intent each turn; relics change rules (MEMORY). | Visible intent as legibility: show what an opponent is about to do so policy choices make sense; preparation (loadout) mattering more than reflexes fits idle play. |

## Part C: combat design angles (default, dial, in-world reason)

| Code | Angle | Default, dial and reason |
|---|---|---|
| CA1 | **Defeat is a state, not an ending.** | Default: defeat lands in a downed or fainted state and resolves to rescue, retreat, robbery or capture (AD19). Dial: a world or mode where defeat can kill, forewarned (SS, JJ, lethal D&D profile). Reason: most real fights end when someone can no longer fight, and the world decides what happens next. |
| CA2 | **Yield before contact.** | Default: a lopsided encounter offers surrender, payment or retreat first. Dial: honor cultures or monsters that do not accept yielding (demons, Nightmare creatures). Reason: Mount & Blade and D&D parley show most actors prefer not to fight to the death. |
| CA3 | **Idle fights run on policies funded by real stock.** | Default: auto-combat with eat, withdraw and flee thresholds that consume owned food and supplies (Melvor auto-eat). Dial: active control pays a small bonus; offline combat is opt-in. Reason: idling a fight should cost what the fight actually burns. |
| CA4 | **Injury carries into the life-sim, scaled by setting.** | Default: temporary Recovering that slows work and travel (BHC21). Dial: lasting named injuries with care routes, or Wildermyth-style chosen price of survival. Reason: a fight that changes nothing afterward is not felt; a forgiving floor stays. |
| CA5 | **Wounds gate verbs, not just numbers.** | Default: a wound names its part and blocks or slows specific verbs (grip, run, cast with hands). Dial: depth per world; a cultivation body may ignore what cripples a low-magic one (BHC23). Reason: DF and RimWorld capacities make injury legible and push care and craft. |
| CA6 | **Gear wears from use.** | Default: weapons and armor lose condition from actual blows and need repair supplies (tools IP2 wear-from-work). Dial: wear rate by material and world. Reason: links combat to the making economy and gives smiths ongoing demand (Battle Brothers, Demon Slayer blades). |
| CA7 | **Morale and strain as a second track.** | Default: fear and stress can make a companion or the player flee, freeze or refuse before HP runs out. Dial: off for fearless constructs; strong in horror worlds (LM, Chainsaw Man fear). Reason: Darkest Dungeon and Battle Brothers show breaking is usually what ends fights. |
| CA8 | **Captives and prisoners are people.** | Default: captured actors keep identity, can be ransomed, released, recruited or kept, each with social cost. Dial: legality by world (MHA arrest, Kenshi slavery, CK3 ransom). Reason: Kenshi and RimWorld show capture creates the richest follow-up stories. |
| CA9 | **Killing has law and memory.** | Default: a killing creates witnessed facts, grudges and legal response (CK3 memories, ENCOUNTERS persistence). Dial: frontier zones with thin law; licensed killers (hunters, exorcists). Reason: one authority per fact; a body count is a social event, not a free reward. |
| CA10 | **Conflict has nonviolent winning routes.** | Default: most conflicts can be settled through negotiation, payment, evidence, evacuation or reputation (Griftlands, Qud water ritual, W2 K10 "outs"). Dial: some threats only yield to force or a specific counter (Devils, demons). Reason: a life-sim player may be a smith or clerk; Weibao wants careers that mature without combat. |
| CA11 | **Fights are information.** | Default: an encounter reveals opponent intent, ability and weakness to the degree observed, saved as knowledge, not truth (Slay the Spire intent, HxH hidden conditions, Frieren concealment). Dial: deception-heavy worlds can feed false reads. Reason: knowledge and truth stay separate (MR, SR claims vs truth). |
| CA12 | **Training by sparring and by losing.** | Default: sparring and drills grow skill at low risk; losing real fights grows toughness slowly. Dial: by world teaching (dojo, Corps, school club). Reason: DF sparring and Kenshi toughness give non-lethal progression for a mostly idle player. |
| CA13 | **Strength is source-authored, not scaled.** | Default: opponents keep their canon strength; places and eras set danger (W2 "default X is safer than Y; dial by era"). Dial: event pressure rising with a war or crisis window. Reason: already standing in ENCOUNTERS and world briefs; keeps world identity. |
| CA14 | **War is politics at a bigger scale.** | Default: wars resolve by war score, occupation and settlement terms, and the player can contribute through logistics, rescue or supply (Skyrim recovery office, Black Clover border defense). Dial: direct battle roles where the world supports them. Reason: CK3 and Warsim show war outcomes are negotiated, and a life-sim player is often behind the line. |
| CA15 | **Cross-world power meets local law.** | Default: an imported power works by its own mechanism but draws local attention, enforcement or a convert/suppress adapter (W2 HP-L5, K2 import policies). Dial: per world absorption rule. Reason: a strong outsider in MHA or HP still faces licensing and courts; being able to win a fight is not permission to fight. |
