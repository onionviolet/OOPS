## Sources

- **None of these books are in the local library** except Reverend Insanity
  (`docs/research/novels/full-book/reverend-insanity/`, full notes). Use
  public sources: NovelUpdates pages and reviews, fan wikis and their
  cultivation-realm pages, translator glossaries, r/noveltranslations and
  r/ProgressionFantasy discussions. Mark every claim as from wiki, review or
  discussion, since nothing is a full read.
- **Er Gen:** Renegade Immortal, Beseech the Devil, I Shall Seal the
  Heavens, A Will Eternal, Pursuit of the Truth, A Thought Through Eternity,
  and his shared universe across books.
- **Gu Zhen Ren:** Reverend Insanity from local notes; any other works.
- **Other major authors** (pick the best documented): I Eat Tomatoes
  (Coiling Dragon, Desolate Era, Swallowed Star), Cuttlefish That Loves
  Diving (Lord of the Mysteries is local; Circle of Inevitability notes
  exist), Mad Snail (Battle Through the Heavens), Tang Jia San Shao (Soul
  Land), Chen Dong (Perfect World, Shrouded Divine Arcade), Zhttty
  (Reincarnation Paradise-adjacent Infinite Terror), Ancient Godly Monarch
  or others with distinctive systems.

## Questions

1. **The progression ladder** of each novel: realms and their names, what
   changes at each realm, how breakthroughs work, what fails.
2. **Distinctive mechanics** per author: Er Gen's Dao comprehension,
   "dao seeds", karma and life-and-death insights, the shared Er Gen
   multiverse; Gu Zhen Ren's paths, Gu and killer moves; heavenly
   tribulations; soul and body cultivation splits; clones; reincarnation.
3. **How each goes from local to multiversal,** and how the endings handle
   transcendence.
4. **Recurring tropes and how each master varies them:** sects, inheritances,
   ancient beings, cheats, arrogant young masters, tribulations, multiple
   worlds.
5. **What makes these authors' progressions feel earned** compared with
   weaker cultivation novels.
6. **Twenty or more small touches** mapped to the life sim.
