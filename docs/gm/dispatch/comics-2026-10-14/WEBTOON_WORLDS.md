## Scope and evidence

This is a reference palette under [COMMON](COMMON.md), not a world roster,
private franchise pack or implementation specification. It extends the
[earlier targets](../../../research/comics/PLAYTHROUGH_TARGETS.md) rather
than repeating the Marvel/DC pilot. [User vision](../../../USER_VISION.md),
[PROJECT](../../../../PROJECT.md) and
[bounded continuity](../../../worlds/LORE_AND_CONTINUITY.md#bounded-comic-comparisons)
keep authority. Comic research does not move comics ahead of the accepted
canon-first order. Direct adaptations belong in the private pack.

**STATED** below means wiki article text or indexed excerpts inspected on
2026-10-14, with its page and relevant section named. Fandom is secondary,
often mixes novel/comic/anime/sequel facts, and may contain speculation.
Search indexes are cached, so this is not a current-revision API audit.
**SHOWN** is a directly observed official comic panel or local document.
**INFERRED** is a proposed game translation, negative bounded conclusion or
counterfactual. No unverified MEMORY claim is promoted to canon.

Natural course means sourced pressures and the canon trajectory if the player
does not disturb them. It does not establish what would happen if the lead
never existed. Entry eras and non-lead roles are INFERRED authoring options;
their underlying places/events are sourced. No calendar timer, offline death,
regression entitlement or exact acquisition recipe is selected here.

The three added comparisons are **The Gamer**, **Tales of Demons and Gods**
and **Battle Through the Heavens**. The last two supply CW2's manhua contrast.
TBATE is treated as a Tapas webcomic comparison, not evidence that every work
in CW1 is a Korean hunter/system manhwa. Its publisher credits the comic
adaptation separately from the novel. [Tapas series details](https://tapas.io/series/tbate-comic/info).

## W. Ten world references

### W1 Solo Leveling

**Boundary:** original manhwa trajectory; Ragnarok, games and anime-only
events are excluded unless named. Wiki pages mix those branches.

**STATED, power and rank:** mana awakening produces hunters; ordinary
awakened output is largely fixed, with rare reawakening exceptions. The
ladder is E, D, C, B, A, S. Guilds and the Association turn power into work
and public standing. [Hunters, Overview](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Hunters).
S is a measurement ceiling, with wide internal differences; international
achievement rankings and National Level status are additional measures.
[S-Rank, Overview](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/S-Rank).

**STATED, window and return:** the original System gives Jinwoo quests,
stats, allocation, inventory and a shop. Other people cannot see his
interface. [System, Characteristics](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/System).
The Cup rewinds ten years, has exhaustible power, and preserves the Rulers'
and Monarchs' memories/power. This is an artifact operation, not automatic
hunter death regression. [Cup, Abilities](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Cup_of_Reincarnation).

**STATED, patrons and course:** supernatural beings grant power to selected
vessels, without an ORV-style broadcast economy. [Rulers, History](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Rulers).
Uncleared dungeons can break into civilian space; the wiki gives a seven-day
clearance deadline. [Dungeons, Overview](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeons).
Jeju, the Japan crisis and the Monarch conflict form distinct escalation
eras. [Story Arcs](https://solo-leveling.fandom.com/wiki/Story_Arcs).

**INFERRED, starts and roles:** enter during the established gate economy,
before Jeju, or during the later invasion. A support hunter, raid logistics
worker, Association investigator or civilian evacuation planner can matter
without acquiring Jinwoo's system. **Default:** public certification and a
private exceptional window. **Dial:** concealed output and reassessment
access. **Reasonably:** better fieldcraft can improve survival without
silently raising a fixed awakening grade.

### W2 Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint (ORV)

**Boundary:** distinguish the webnovel's full rules from scenes already
adapted in a selected webtoon arc; no full webtoon coverage claimed.

**STATED, power and ladder:** incarnations buy stats with coins, gain skills
and collect Stories. [Stats, Overview](https://omniscient-readers-viewpoint.fandom.com/wiki/Stats).
Constellation grades are Historical, Narrative/Fable and Myth; these are not
an E-to-S hunter ladder. Stories ground their power and Status.
[Constellation, Ranking](https://omniscient-readers-viewpoint.fandom.com/wiki/Constellation).

**STATED, window:** scenario participants normally open their own Attributes
Window mentally. Reading somebody else's requires specific authority or a
skill. Dokja's Fourth Wall blocks even his self-access in the normal case.
[Attributes Window, Description and Trivia](https://omniscient-readers-viewpoint.fandom.com/wiki/Attributes_Window).
Some chapter summaries describe temporary access; do not treat that block
as an eternal absolute. [Chapter 180](https://omniscient-readers-viewpoint.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_180),
[Chapter 181](https://omniscient-readers-viewpoint.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_181).

**STATED, return and audience:** Joonghyuk's regression carries consciousness
and memories to the scenario beginning, with bodily/ability recovery
handled separately by Transmission. His ability page mentions near-death
and voluntary activation; the patron page narrows activation to severe
injury plus consent. These descriptions need scene reconciliation.
[Joonghyuk, Stigmas](https://omniscient-readers-viewpoint.fandom.com/wiki/Yoo_Joonghyuk/Abilities),
[Oldest Dream, Stigmas](https://omniscient-readers-viewpoint.fandom.com/wiki/Oldest_Dream).
Dokkaebis run channels; constellations watch and donate. Patron contracts can
grant stigmas, while a stream contract has negotiated terms.
[Dokkaebi, Channel owners](https://omniscient-readers-viewpoint.fandom.com/wiki/Dokkaebi),
[Incarnation, Other abilities](https://omniscient-readers-viewpoint.fandom.com/wiki/Incarnation).

**STATED, course:** the paid service imposes lethal scenarios on ordinary
commuters. [Dokja, opening synopsis](https://omniscient-readers-viewpoint.fandom.com/wiki/Kim_Dokja/Synopsis).
**INFERRED, starts and roles:** opening train, early station shelter, or a
later named scenario. Supply runner, healer, coalition member and channel
negotiator all have useful roles. **Default:** shared self-window under
scenario law. **Dial:** appraisal access, sponsor terms and broadcast reach.
**Reasonably:** being entertaining can earn a donation, but does not itself
equal a power increase or grant regression.

### W3 Tower of God

**Boundary:** original webtoon and explicitly marked SIU commentary; games,
anime additions and obsolete stat cards are separate evidence.

**STATED, power:** shinsu supports life and powers techniques. Direct use
usually depends on floor authority; items can carry contracts, and
Irregulars have exceptional permissions. [Shinsu](https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Shinsu),
[Shinsu Manipulation, Contract](https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Shinsu_Manipulation).

**STATED, ladders:** Regular rank follows climbing: F, E after floor 20,
D after 30, then C/B/A. C's exact floor wording differs between wiki pages;
upper thresholds are not all settled. This is distinct from raw skill.
[Regular, Ranking](https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Regular),
[Ranked Regulars](https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Ranked_Regulars).
Reaching the empire's floor 134 grants Ranker eligibility; Advanced and High
Ranker describe the upper 10% and 1% in translated commentary.
[Ranker commentary](https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog%3AMgf60/Update_of_older_translation_by_GoDai).
Item and skill grades are another scale. The wiki warns that an older
people-grade extrapolation is wrong. [Ranking and Grading](https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Ranking_and_Grading).

**STATED, interface and power society:** Pockets translate, communicate,
hold points and display contract confirmation; visible and invisible modes
exist. This is an artifact interface, not a private XP allocator for every
person. [Pocket, Functions](https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Pocket).
Administrators govern tests and floor permissions, while appointed Rulers
exercise political authority. [Tower's Rules](https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Tower%27s_Rules),
[Ruler](https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Ruler).

**INFERRED, return and audience:** no ordinary death-reset rule or automatic
view-count power is established by these sources. Patronage should be tied
to a named faction, teacher or contract. **STATED, course anchors:** tests,
the Workshop Battle, Hell Train and later conflict occupy different parts.
[Contents](https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Contents).
**INFERRED, starts and roles:** floor-two team support, a mid-climb artisan,
or a Hell Train preparation era. A Light Bearer, scout, merchant or former
climber can have a local life; giving up climbing can lead to a new job in
the Middle Area, according to [Regular, Giving up](https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Regular).
**Default:** permission and climb records. **Dial:** test participation,
faction backing and available floor. **Reasonably:** an outstanding low-floor
fighter can exceed expectations without receiving a higher climb credential.

### W4 The Beginning After the End (TBATE)

**Boundary:** Tapas comic era selected separately from the later novel.

**STATED, power and ladder:** mana cores purify from black through red,
orange, yellow, silver and white. Red/orange/yellow use dark, solid and light
stages; silver uses initial/mid/high. The wiki also lists Integration and
Arthur's separate aether-core layers, which should remain later-novel
references until their adaptation scope is checked. Augmentation,
conjuration, deviations and artifacts create different routes.
[Magic and Objects, Mana Core Cultivation and Artifacts](https://tbate.fandom.com/wiki/Magic_and_Objects).

**STATED, return:** Grey reincarnates as Arthur in a different world with
memories. This is not a repeatable retry of his childhood. The character
page mixes an early assassination belief with later soul-transfer detail.
[Arthur, introduction and Personality](https://tbate.fandom.com/wiki/Arthur_Leywin).
**INFERRED, window and sponsors:** the checked mana explanation establishes
no universal RPG window. Royal/asuran backing and teachers are not a
constellation audience. A displayed core gauge would translate perception,
tests and knowledge, not prove that natives see a menu.

**STATED, course anchors:** early childhood, academy and preparations for
continental war are separate timeline periods.
[Timeline, Early Years and Preparing for War](https://tbate.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline).
**INFERRED, starts and roles:** a village mage apprentice, academy peer or
wartime quartermaster can act without Arthur's exceptional aether route.
Other characters have their own training and civic roles, such as
[Kathyln's student/soldier profile](https://tbate.fandom.com/wiki/Kathyln_Glayder).
**Default:** local core cultivation. **Dial:** era, schooling, lineage and
artifact access. **Reasonably:** a later-novel ability does not appear in an
early comic start merely because the wiki puts both on one page.

### W5 Return of the Mount Hua Sect / Return of the Blossoming Blade

**Boundary:** the manhwa's chosen chapter range; novel arc names are routing
aids and not proof those chapters are adapted.

**STATED, power:** internal qi, bodily foundation and sword methods matter.
Six Elements breathing prioritizes purification over immediate qi gains;
the foundational sword form differs from the practical replacement taught
during the sect's shortage. [Six Elements Breathing](https://return-of-the-blossoming-blade.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Elements_Breathing_Technique),
[True Six Elements Sword](https://return-of-the-blossoming-blade.fandom.com/wiki/True_Six_Elements_Sword_Technique).

**STATED, ranks and return:** second-/third-grade disciples identify
generations and standing, as illustrated by Baek Cheon and Yunjong, not a
numeric cultivation ladder. [Baek Cheon](https://return-of-the-blossoming-blade.fandom.com/wiki/Baek_Cheon),
[Yunjong](https://return-of-the-blossoming-blade.fandom.com/wiki/Yunjong).
Cheongmyeong dies after the war and returns in a young beggar's body a
century later. [Manhwa chapter 1, Summary](https://return-of-the-blossoming-blade.fandom.com/wiki/Manhwa/1).
**INFERRED:** a new person is not entitled to that return; no fixed E-to-S
ladder, system window or recurring regression is established here.

**STATED, course and institutions:** the sect has lost status and inherited
techniques. One signature sword art requires direct teaching, rather than
just possessing a manual. [Twenty-Four Plum Blossoms](https://return-of-the-blossoming-blade.fandom.com/wiki/Twenty-Four_Plum_Blossoms_Sword_Technique).
Merchant ties and rival teaching philosophies shape its recovery.
[Zhongnan Sect, Teachings and Background](https://return-of-the-blossoming-blade.fandom.com/wiki/Zhongnan_Sect).
**INFERRED, audience:** reputation can affect recruitment and trade; it is
not automatic supernatural income from viewers.

**INFERRED, starts and roles:** ruined-sect arrival, first rivalry contest,
or a later alliance era. Novice, merchant liaison, cook, medicine assistant
and manual conservator offer work without being the returned master.
**Default:** training and sect recovery. **Dial:** generation, office,
teacher access and restored curriculum. **Reasonably:** treat a seniority
label and demonstrated sword competence as separate facts.

### W6 Second Life Ranker

**Boundary:** Obelisk climbing era. The wiki's later Earth-system aftermath
is excluded from an early Tower start.

**STATED, power, ranks and window:** Players clear trials, train and gain
skills; the Tower processes their accomplishments into skills and a
self-window with stats, traits and descriptions. Passing the floor-50 trial
and rising beyond it qualifies a Ranker; the wiki distinguishes low, mid
and upper High Ranker power. Apostle, Supernaturalist and Lord are overlapping paths,
not sequential rungs. [Player, Definition, Classifications and Player Window](https://second-life-ranker.fandom.com/wiki/Player).
**INFERRED:** this is world law for eligible Tower participants. Common
self-access does not establish open access to everyone else's true name.

**STATED, return:** Yeon-woo inherits his dead brother's memories through a
pocket watch and follows them into the Tower. Dragon succession is also
limited by his vessel's development. This is inherited information and
legacy, not the premise of rewinding Yeon-woo's own life.
[Volume 1, Pocket Watch and Tutorial](https://second-life-ranker.fandom.com/wiki/Volume_1).

**STATED, sponsors and course:** gods use apostles to act on lower floors,
with power and soul obligations. [Gods, Apostles](https://second-life-ranker.fandom.com/wiki/Gods).
Gods/demons observe impressive climbers and offer contracts or gifts; mortal
clans and craft workers are part of the same history.
[Yeon-woo, Henova and Gods and Devils](https://second-life-ranker.fandom.com/wiki/Cha_Yeon-woo/Relationships).
The Obelisk has floor trials and constraints on divine intervention.
[Obelisk](https://second-life-ranker.fandom.com/wiki/Obelisk).

**INFERRED, starts and roles:** tutorial cohort, lower-floor town, or a later
clan-conflict snapshot. Artisan, merchant, ordinary climber and a patron's
agent can pursue their own aims. Without player intervention, the selected
trials and clan struggles continue; the lead's revenge stays an NPC thread,
not the player's compulsory quest. **Default:** shared Tower system.
**Dial:** patron terms, inherited knowledge and clan exposure.
**Reasonably:** attracting a patron is an opportunity with obligations,
not a universal likes-to-stats conversion.

### W7 The Greatest Estate Developer

**Boundary:** selected TGED webtoon episodes; sequel works and the complete
novel ending are not silently imported.

**STATED, power and ranks:** manaheart and Asrahan cores support combat;
Lloyd's progression table has trainee and low/intermediate/high Sword
Expert entries with comic chapter locators.
[Lloyd, Cultivation Progress](https://dragonschef.fandom.com/wiki/Lloyd_Frontera/Skills).
Sword Master uses aura, while a broader wiki ladder extends to Grand Master.
The broader page says it has no official parallel mage ladder.
[Cultivation level](https://dragonschef.fandom.com/wiki/Cultivation_level).

**STATED, system and audience:** episode 2 introduces Relationship Points
earned from improved relationships with key people. Episode 12 spends RP
on skill growth. [Episode 2](https://dragonschef.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_2_%28TGED%29),
[Episode 12](https://dragonschef.fandom.com/wiki/Episode_12_%28TGED%29).
Surveying is described as a system skill exclusive to Lloyd, derived from
Suho's knowledge. [Surveying](https://dragonschef.fandom.com/wiki/Surveying).
**INFERRED, visibility:** these sources support a protagonist-specific
interface; they do not prove every detail of who could observe its panels.
Private-by-default is a proposal pending scene verification. RP is
relationship change, not a count of anonymous spectators.

**STATED, return and course:** Suho wakes in Lloyd's body after reading an
embedded novel. That novel predicts the estate family's collapse and a
different course for Javier. [Knight of Blood and Iron](https://dragonschef.fandom.com/wiki/Knight_of_Blood_and_Iron).
A later wiki account describes pressure toward original destiny.
[Aotearoa, Conception](https://dragonschef.fandom.com/wiki/Aotearoa).
**INFERRED:** transmigration is not ordinary death regression; destiny
pressure needs its own selected arc, not an assumed law in every start.

**INFERRED, starts and roles:** debt-ridden estate before its first building
contract, expanding public works, or a named later fate crisis. Survey
assistant, builder, supplier, tenant representative and knight can matter
through projects. **Default:** useful construction and local trust.
**Dial:** RP eligibility, prior technical knowledge and fate pressure.
**Reasonably:** helping one resident can change that person's trust and a
specific reward without raising world-wide popularity.

### W8 The Gamer

**Boundary:** original webtoon; use a named season/episode range for a future
pack. The wiki's summary and homepage are visibly older than full coverage.

**STATED, power and interface:** Gaia gives Jihan a Natural Ability that
turns activities into skills and experience, includes voice-command
status/inventory/skill windows, and can extend benefits through party or
contract routes. It is exceptional among ability users, rather than the
default interface for the public. [The Gamer ability, Usage and Windows](https://the-gamer.fandom.com/wiki/The_Gamer_%28Ability%29).
**INFERRED, visibility:** entitlement belongs to the holder and permitted
extensions. The inspected page does not settle every panel's physical
visibility or every party member's UI permissions.

**STATED, ladders:** personal levels, skill levels and Abyss rank coexist.
Jihan's social-status table lists F through S, then Heavenly Thousand,
Strong Hundred and Divine Ten. It maps them to multi-spell progression.
[Jihan, Social Status](https://the-gamer.fandom.com/wiki/Han_Jihan/Social_Status).
Another profile assigns a different letter to Heavenly Thousand; this
needs reconciliation rather than a universal conversion table.
[Lolikiano profile](https://the-gamer.fandom.com/wiki/Lolikiano_Mistrim).

**STATED, course and backing:** Seoul's supernatural society has factions,
trained and innate ability users, and repercussions for affecting mundane
life. Gaia is backing and law enforcement, not a spectator platform.
[The Gamer, Terminology and Setting](https://the-gamer.fandom.com/wiki/The_Gamer).
**INFERRED, return:** HP-style physiology and recovery do not establish a
death-to-past reset or automatic resurrection.

**INFERRED, starts and roles:** initial school/dojo discovery, a named faction
conflict, or a later dungeon economy. Trained martial artist, broker,
enchanter and dungeon worker can grow without being Jihan.
**Default:** secret power society plus one unusual interface.
**Dial:** party sharing, compatible teaching and public exposure.
**Reasonably:** the system can package real practice into skills, but no
source here makes all learned theories instantly executable.

### W9 Tales of Demons and Gods

**Boundary:** manhua snapshots distinct from novel/donghua; wiki tables
often supply separate columns. Do not copy the novel's latest cultivation
into an early manhua start.

**STATED, power:** soul force, cultivation methods and integrated demon
spirits are distinct from ordinary bodily fighters.
[Demon Spiritualist, Description](https://tales-of-demons-and-gods.fandom.com/wiki/Demon_Spiritualist).
Soul crystals reveal qualities and force. Glory City's popular belief in
fixed potential by soul-realm color conflicts with the account that a
suitable technique can train other colors to high rank.
[Soul Realm](https://tales-of-demons-and-gods.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Realm).

**STATED, ladder:** unranked/White, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Black Gold and
Legend; the five main tiers have five subdivisions. Later references add
Heavenly Fate, Heavenly Star, Heavenly Axis, Dao of Dragon and Martial
Ancestor, with still higher named tiers. Those later references require
their own era boundary. [Demon Spiritualist, Ranks](https://tales-of-demons-and-gods.fandom.com/wiki/Demon_Spiritualist),
[Ranks](https://tales-of-demons-and-gods.fandom.com/wiki/Ranks).

**STATED, return and course:** Nie Li's remembered life includes Glory City's
destruction by a horde. [Nie Li, Alternate Life](https://tales-of-demons-and-gods.fandom.com/wiki/Nie_Li/Plot).
He returns to age thirteen; the Book page presents its causal link as his
suspected explanation, not a reproducible reset procedure.
[Temporal Demon Spirit Book](https://tales-of-demons-and-gods.fandom.com/wiki/Temporal_Demon_Spirit_Book).
Later Fate Souls can support local revival at a Soul Hall after a death;
that differs from returning to childhood.
[Timeline, chapters 337-338 entries](https://tales-of-demons-and-gods.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline).

**INFERRED, window and audience:** crystals and trained perception support
appraisal, not a shared RPG window. Family/sect backing is institutional,
not an ORV broadcast economy. **INFERRED, starts and roles:** Glory City
school before a horde, defense preparation, or a Draconic Ruins institution.
Alchemist, inscription worker, defender, spirit trader and ordinary student
can contribute. **Default:** local rank and evidence-based assessment.
**Dial:** technique access, hidden talent and return mechanism by era.
**Reasonably:** a mistaken school appraisal can change after better testing
without making every rank reading false.

### W10 Battle Through the Heavens

**Boundary:** CW2's manhua comparison, with explicit novel-only rules.
The wiki's Cultivation page warns that it follows novel terminology rather
than manhua or donghua. Its ladder is a reference to cross-check, not a
claim that every comic scene was inspected.

**STATED, power and ladder:** Dou Zhi Qi, Dou Zhe, Dou Shi, Da Dou Shi, Dou
Ling, Dou Wang, Dou Huang, Dou Zong, Dou Zun, Dou Sheng, Dou Di, with bridging
states around the late tiers. Training methods and combat techniques have
a separate Huang/Xuan/Di/Tian grade. Physical milestones, flight and spatial
abilities give ranks functional anchors. [Cultivation, Dou Realms and Technique Ranking](https://battle-through-the-heavens.fandom.com/wiki/Cultivation).
Inner View is a learned capacity from Dou Zhe, not a general RPG menu.
[Dou Zhe, Techniques](https://battle-through-the-heavens.fandom.com/wiki/Dou_Zhe).
**INFERRED, window:** a text body/qi panel would represent that capacity;
universal numeric appraisal would be an extra grant.

**STATED, return and patrons:** the wiki identifies Xiao Yan as a soul from
Earth. [Xiao Yan](https://battle-through-the-heavens.fandom.com/wiki/Xiao_Yan).
Yao Lao's ring mentorship and the initial lost-talent premise anchor his
progression. [Wiki introduction](https://battle-through-the-heavens.fandom.com/wiki/Battle_Through_the_Heavens_Wiki).
**INFERRED:** neither establishes a repeatable death regression or an
audience that pays power.

**STATED, course:** clan conflict escalates toward the Hall of Souls and
Hun Tiandi's catastrophic sacrifice plan in novel history.
[Hun Tiandi, Plot](https://battle-through-the-heavens.fandom.com/wiki/Hun_Tiandi).
Manhua progression columns provide narrower locators than novel chronology.
[Xiao Yan, Cultivation](https://battle-through-the-heavens.fandom.com/wiki/Xiao_Yan/Cultivation).
**INFERRED, starts and roles:** early clan assessment/duel preparation,
academy era, or a late crisis whose manhua coverage is first checked.
Apothecary, mercenary, ingredient supplier and technique researcher offer
non-lead routes. **Default:** cultivation with distinct technique and soul
scales. **Dial:** clan resources, tests, mentor access and regional threat.
**Reasonably:** biological cultivation, alchemical certification and social
authority should not collapse into one level number.

## A. Abstraction considerations for CA3 and CA6

### A1 CA3: a rank is a typed claim with a local issuer

**INFERRED:** CA3 should carry a local ladder key, label/substage, what it
measures, issuer or method, known functional anchor, test date, confidence,
access rights and any known measurement ceiling. The holder's real capacity
and an institution's last certificate are different records. A rank can be
correct and still insufficient to predict a fight.

| Example | Claim | Concrete difference it makes |
|---|---|---|
| W1 hunter S | Meter cannot resolve the upper value | Display unresolved output and the issued grade; do not invent SSS from overflow. |
| W3 Regular E | Floor-20 credential | Travel/test eligibility changes; combat competence stays separately observed. |
| W5 second-grade | Sect generation | Seniority and curriculum access can change without a bodily upgrade. |
| W9 soul color | Property plus an institution's interpretation | Better technique or testing can change the judgment without changing the original measured color. |
| W10 Dou Zhe | Cultivation milestone | Inner View and method use become possible, with source/era checks. |

**Default:** one local meaning per displayed label. **Dial:** exact numbers,
bands, test precision, public certificates and hidden readings.
**Reasonably:** show the reason an estimate is uncertain, rather than an
omniscient number followed by a cosmetic question mark. Partial ordering and
multiple simultaneous ladders are enough; no universal strength score is
implied. A promotion can create access, recognition or a new function, not
only multiply damage.

### A2 CA6: shared audience record, distinct causal paths

**INFERRED:** reuse the shape of an observer/beneficiary record, but retain
actor, relationship, known channel/reach, terms, actual transfer, obligation
and disclosure state. Do not make fame, trust, coins, contract privileges
and story power freely interchangeable.

| Code | Backing mode | Conversion that needs a real cause |

**Default:** CK14 is a true extra only where the chosen world lacks that
conversion. In W2, receiving ordinary audience support is part of local law;
the cheat could instead improve discovery, concealment or bargaining.
**Dial:** who can watch, what they learn, transfer costs and compatible
contracts. **Reasonably:** one patron can value an act another hates, and
quiet work can earn local trust without becoming broadcast entertainment.
A common interface makes theft, coercion and privacy disputes possible; it
does not make every use socially harmless.

### A3 Return is distinct from UI and rank

**INFERRED:** keep trigger, authority, destination era/body, memories,
skills, carried objects, affected population and timeline identity separate.
The UI can report a permitted return; it cannot provide one just because the
world has status panels. A regression, later reincarnation, cross-world
transmigration, memory inheritance and local revival need different records.
**Default:** actual source rules for a selected snapshot. **Dial:** AU
retention and branch composition. **Reasonably:** mark an authored variant
as such; never convert a source's depleted artifact into an arbitrary global
return-count limit or silently make reload reroll a return.
