### F2. M&M's useful distinction, with a source limit

SHOWN in the open rules: attack bonus + effect rank cannot exceed twice PL;
Dodge + Toughness, Parry + Toughness and Fortitude + Will have that paired
limit too. A resisted effect without an attack roll is capped at PL. Thus a
PL10 example might have attack 8/effect 12 or attack 12/effect 8. More build
points can buy breadth without raising those caps. NPC effective PL is
estimated from traits and need not match their point budget. [S04][s04]

The source is an open rules mirror, not Green Ronin's official site, and
renames proprietary terms. Exact branded-book correspondence remains open.
The core design distinction is corroborated, not a Fourth Edition audit.
Green Ronin now lists Fourth Edition Match-Ups alongside the Third Edition
quickstart; this lane intentionally retains the requested familiar 3e model.
[S16][s16]

## Map to the root abstractions

### A3. CA3 rank ladder: name what is being ranked

INFERRED: keep **physical capability**, **trained competence**, **institutional
standing** and **current narrative leverage** distinguishable. M&M's budget
and combat cap are different; Masks' social Labels are different again.
Marvel Multiverse's Rank combines training/power for its specific game;
Snap's Power is a board score. None licenses a universal conversion table.

**Default:** named local bands with practical anchors and a known evidence
source. **Dial:** visible/hidden numbers, domain, continuity/edition, reading
confidence, training breadth and whether a temporary source changes capacity.
**Reasonably:** a trained unpowered investigator can beat a stronger foe in
an investigation without becoming physically stronger. A high office can
grant access without adding damage. A bluff can change observers' readings
without changing the body. Superior acquired power can remain superior.

Illustrative original bands could read ordinary, trained, exceptional, city
scale and wider scale. They are proposals, not source ranks, balanced tiers
or mandatory ceilings. Test any selected ladder against a real task anchor
rather than assuming adjacent names imply equal gaps.

### A4. CA4 office / mantle: powers, trust and title can part ways

INFERRED: extend the pilot's transfer idea with **expectations from living
holders, retired holders, mentors and institutions**, as Masks' Legacy
suggests. Title claim, recognition, gear custody, powers and obligations
are separable. A successor might receive the name and enemies while still
learning the craft; a predecessor might retain capability after retirement.

**Default:** preserve the office record and evaluate an actual transfer route.
**Dial:** appointment/trial/lineage/item choice, contested recognition,
training duty, former-holder authority and portable versus local rights.
**Reasonably:** a mantle claimant inherits an unfinished promise and a rogue's
interest, not automatically every predecessor's friendships or techniques.
This sharpens CA4/CL7; CA4 was a requested map even though it is not a K4 root
row assignment. No shared owner was edited.

### A7. CA7 identity pair: add who knows and who expects what

INFERRED: a pair of names alone does not produce a double life. Use the
existing identity owner to relate the same actor to alias-specific knowledge,
obligations, reputation and recognition by each observer. Keep that distinct
from inner self-image. Masks supplies the distinction between Janus' two
lives and socially shifting Labels; Atom Eve supplies choice-driven family
and school friction; Midnight Suns supplies meaningful life with teammates.

**Default:** exposure depends on observed clues and communications, not
global suspicion for all NPCs. **Dial:** secrecy norm, legal identity rules,
costume/body recognition, obligation conflict, information spread and ease
of living publicly. **Reasonably:** missing work affects the civilian
employer, while a trusted teammate may know both names; exposure can be
local before it becomes public. An identity pair need not prevent more
aliases or transformed bodies where the world supports them. Quiet routine
can protect a cover during idle without forcing emergency log-ins.

### A8. CA8 recurring actor: encounter reuse is not a return cause

INFERRED: distinguish the actor's durable state from an encounter's card,
mission or spawn. Freedom Force's discovery file is a useful information
record; Arkham's cases organize a personal foe route; neither inspected
source proves autonomous escape and learning. The life sim needs custody,
allies/resources, injuries, knowledge, intent and opportunities to matter.

**Default:** a surviving foe can act through established means; an imprisoned
foe stays constrained until an escape/rescue/corruption cause exists.
**Dial:** security quality, outside help, willingness to retreat, available
recovery, personal fixation, preparation rate and world tone. **Reasonably:**
a foe who saw a repeated tactic can prepare a counter; an uninformed foe
cannot learn a private power merely because another villain fought it.
Repeat missions can reuse templates while preserving different actors.
Any future Doom-like budget limits available intervention; it does not
conjure means or require every return to have higher raw damage.

## Mortality and CK19

**F4. Keep four meanings separate.** SHOWN: Freedom Force uses knockout;
HERO's preview names knockout, injury and death separately. STATED: MSF
has battle elimination/revive, while Midnight Suns has a specific story
resurrection. [S18][s18], [S09][s09], [S33][s33], [S30][s30] MEMORY:
Arkham's retry is a checkpoint restore. These do not measure one common
death/return rate.

**P1. CK19 finite survival leverage** is INFERRED, not confirmed as a shared
rule in these games. Hero/Plot/Determination resources are related references,
but improving a roll, mitigating harm, surviving defeat, returning from death
and restoring a save are different powers. Exact survival uses in the
unchecked books need checking before claiming a direct precedent.

**Default:** visible charges/spend rules when the world or cheat makes them
knowable, with cause-linked replenishment. **Dial:** luck/effort/allies/medical
support/story protection, covered hazards, refill condition, injury and debt
left behind. **Reasonably:** spend protects a particular eligible outcome
and leaves a receipt; reloading does not replenish it or reroll the outcome.
An arc-end refill is an authoring choice, not sourced necessity. Power can
stop at injury, retreat or rescue instead of guaranteeing survival against
every force. Costed revival, when available, keeps intervening consequences.
