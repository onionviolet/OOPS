## K5-F2. Sliding time is a chronology policy, not a slow clock

STATED: Marvel Database's
[Sliding Timescale](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Glossary:Sliding_Timescale),
Definition, History, Inconsistencies, and In-Universe References, describes a
moving present that compresses modern hero history. Its cited 2025 estimate is
roughly 15 years, not an eternal fixed number or a universal four-to-one ratio.
It distinguishes topical references from historical anchors: WWII stories stay
in the 1940s, while a modern origin's war or technology may change. The Siancong
War supplies a movable conflict; *Ultimates* (2015) #5 supplies an in-world
account of important events moving toward the present. School years, holidays
and ages do not fit a perfect conversion.

INFERRED: keep publication order, causal order, local elapsed time and displayed
calendar dates distinct. Default: freeze the selected entry revision and let a
lived instance accumulate actual time. Dial: fixed calendar, elastic background
dates, compressed eras, or explicit time manipulation. Reasonably: sliding a
background origin should not reduce completed work time, undo a child born in
play, refund consumed goods or change an already settled appointment. Updating
dates in a new source snapshot differs from rewriting an active history.

## K5-A2. CI4 / CK10: status-quo pressure with actual causes

INFERRED: homeostasis is a useful scheduler candidate for tagged pressures.
The sources do not prove that comics run a hidden periodic baseline reset.
City reconstruction, renewed recruitment, public forgetting and resurrection
have different means and costs. A villain escaping custody preserves the loss
of their old equipment; a clone is a new claimant; a repaired shop can retain
new ownership. Similar visible state does not mean restored history.

Default: reuse the owner's durable clock/publication path and schedule an
eligible causal opportunity, then resolve its actual effects. Dial: strength,
delay, domains, responsible actors, funding, discoverability, interruption,
absence policy and target baseline revision. Reasonably: evaluate disturbed
preconditions before committing, retain committed random outcomes across
reload/import, and prevent duplicate progress across visits. Seed alone does
not preserve arbitrary earned history or settle a changed content revision.

CK10 default: automatic protection of earned divergence from background
baseline normalization. Dial: an explicit anchor can broaden protection,
hold otherwise transient effects, preserve memories through a selected rewrite
or resist a particular restoration authority. Reasonably: specify its actual
domain, source, maintenance and attack route. A purchased anchor should not
become a new tax for keeping every ordinary earned improvement. Its loss can
restore eligibility for a disclosed pressure, not silently delete the past.

## K5-A3. CA9 / CA10: counterparts, migrants and shared events

INFERRED: CA9 benefits from a typed relation: counterpart, predecessor-era
version, actual migrant, substitute, clone, successor mantle holder, or
explicitly merged identity. Default: similarity shares no inventory, office,
memory or debt. Dial: selected sources can share memory or consciousness.
STATED exception: [Marvel's Maker profile article](https://www.marvel.com/articles/comics/the-maker-explained-mister-fantastic-doppelganger-ultimate-universe)
describes reconstructed Makers sharing consciousness. That exception is
source-backed, not permission to merge all variants. Miles migrating to 616
also differs from meeting a 616-native counterpart.

INFERRED: CA10 coordinates named participating histories and their local
effects. Default: a bounded participation set, one event identity and declared
effect order. Dial: shared threat, incursion, temporary overlap, salvage,
permanent convergence or reconstructed successor histories. Reasonably: keep
participant-specific memory/custody/clock outcomes and a recoverable publication
record. A possible later implementation can prepare affected records before
publishing; unsupported participants stay unresolved rather than receiving a
fictional successful migration. Shared branding does not advance every world
or resolve every local plot. Participation, evacuation and intervention are
different: declining a tie-in task need not stop an environmental crisis.

## K5-A4. CO5: when may the pull touch an earned change?

INFERRED extension consistent with the live owners. Default: earned divergence
stays durable; normal risks continue from actual causes. “Warning” alone does
not justify resurrecting a saved enemy's replacement victim or secretly forcing
the source ending. Use the world's law and the specific affected domain.

| Code | Proposed circumstance | Useful player promise |
|---|---|---|
| K5-P1 | Ordinary causal pressure: a rival sabotages a workshop, a term expires, weather damages repairs | The exposure, actor means and consequence follow the world's known rules. A new adverse event can change earned state while the earlier achievement remains true. |
| K5-P2 | A chosen pact, regression, restoration or cycle with declared losses/retention | Before commitment, explain the affected earned domains and available response. Preserve the prior history at the archive/resume scope the world actually promises. |
| K5-P3 | A declared crisis threatens an earned site/person/project | The risk follows a selected cause and scope; provide the represented defense, evacuation, anchor, alternative branch or acceptance route. A warning is not a substitute for those mechanics. |
| K5-P4 | An anchor fails or is attacked through its declared weakness | Resolve the attack/upkeep outcome first; expose the next pressure and its timing. A missed maintenance check need not imply instant retroactive erasure. |
| K5-P5 | Another continuity or a new source snapshot is selected | Explain this is another history; the old earned branch keeps its own status. Changing the default template does not normalize a currently played instance. |
