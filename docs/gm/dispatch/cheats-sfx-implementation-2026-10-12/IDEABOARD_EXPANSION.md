## D1: Finite first cycle recommendation

Use P02, an original **work-ledger System**, joined to one actual native crafting
consumer. The ledger is an in-fiction provider with an identified host and
specific authority to record a commission, mint its points, teach a method and
supply leased assistance. A quest UI and software registry alone do not supply
that authority. Original fiction is explicitly proposed; source-grounded worlds
and private packs remain with their owners.

The connected journey is: encounter ledger → refuse or bind → accept actual
craft commission → obtain existing materials → use native recipe → submit a
qualifying output → claim points once → buy a permanent taught preparation →
use it in native production → acquire an expanded leased assistance module →
make and use a second useful output → disconnect → retain taught preparation,
lose future assistance → continue ordinary craft. The exact recipe and output
must be frozen by the root after live consumer qualification. Prefer an already
reachable recipe and item over an unrelated new workshop system. Source inspection
confirmed recipe success/quality already depends on native skill and station in
`src/crafting_recipes.js`; this design does not certify any chosen modifier hook.

P02 needs an outcome in the existing item owner that can be consumed, sold or
installed. Clicking a fake craft inside the System screen, incrementing a local
quest count, or adding a descriptor-only buff fails the chain. A temporary
preparation can alter one declared native recipe parameter; a permanent method
can supply a new preparation verb. Show its exact effect in the native consumer.
Do not silently reinterpret permanent teaching as temporary remote access.

A small example is **measured preparation**: the ledger teaches a preparation
that the player applies to a selected ordinary batch, improving the declared
success or quality calculation. An upgrade offers **guided calibration** for a
second selected craft application through leased provider contact. The second
output has a different practical consumer, not merely a larger reward number.
These are adjacent original variants, not accepted skill names. Where existing
craft support makes another connection more natural, root should substitute it
and record the exact selected semantics without renaming inherited SC IDs.

Finite proof must include an available opening, both uses, permanent-versus-leased
loss, one saved lifecycle and readable workspace presentation. It does not depend
on random starts, a new realm, generated quests, NPC society or every cheat family.
The root owns frozen execution selection and may reduce unsupported variant
coverage explicitly, but a useful grant, upgrade and actual consumer must remain.

## D4: Visual effects are a substantive lane

The human clarified that VFX is part of this task. The first cycle should have
an actual visual power signature, not only a receipt or sound setting. Select
**guided calibration** as a real supported application: its settled native craft
result supplies the displayed before/after or calibrated parameter. A small
monospace span can bring uneven marks into alignment (`| . |  .|` → `|·|·|`),
with one brief convergence stroke on the ledger content surface. This indicates
actual assistance performed, not arbitrary reward rarity. Keep the buttons,
mandatory decisions and saving/read-only indicators on a stable unaffected
layer. For reduced motion, show the final aligned marks and the same
actor-perceived plain sentence. A host with a supported precise measurement can
see exact calibrated values; an unqualified witness sees steady workmanship.

For module expansion, a restrained visual frame unfolds around the newly
available service content, then rests. Provider loss breaks only that service's
connection stroke and leaves permanent teaching and ordinary craft visible.
These provide motion, line geometry and panel-surface transformation tied to
three real mechanics. They do not claim a barrier, combat adaptation or damage
power exists. Compare converging marks versus one crisp calibration stamp on the
same real event during stage three; compare brief unfold versus immediate static
frame on the same real upgrade. Root may choose equivalent visual geometry after
native qualification, preserving actual causes and text equivalence.

## G1–G5: Exact first-cycle acceptance recommendations

- **G1 Connected power:** ordinary inputs through native crafting → actual accepted output → one claim → actual purchase → demonstrable native effect → useful upgraded second application. Refusal, unavailable material/service, insufficient points, stale quote and repeated claim reject without false effects or grants.
- **G2 Authority and observation:** own measured receipt is available to host; unauthorized observer sees only supported impression; provider loss removes leased execution while taught method and settled goods remain. Event text, aria text, logs, previews and exports match projection.
- **G3 Settlement and durability:** regenerated fresh-origin save/reload/import retains terms, receipts, method, module and disconnected state. Compatible old save has no invented backfilled entitlement. Failed publication/persistence follows the actual owner transaction policy with no debit-only or grant-only outcome.
- **G4 Workspace and effects:** rebuilt effect follows the real host at 1920 and responsive widths, relocated and resized; required choices/read-only/saving status remain legible. Reduced motion/static/effects-off give equivalent perceived facts. Cancel and quiet repeats alter no gameplay clock, command, payout or RNG.
- **G5 Taste prototype:** compare quiet stamped receipt and brief kinetic receipt after the same qualified gameplay cause; compare restrained frame expansion and plain service-line change for real upgrade. Human preference remains open, independent of functional/browser/persistence checks.
