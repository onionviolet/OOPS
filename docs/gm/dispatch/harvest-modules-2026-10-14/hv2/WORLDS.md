## W1. Local law interface

Bind `(world instance, continuity/edition, era, place, source template revision, actor/body, operation)` to a frozen law revision. The [world core kit](../../../../worlds/WORLD_CORE_KIT_2026-10-14.md) supplies K2 laws, K7 institutions, K8 economy, K9 materials, K11 discovery, K15 crossings and K18 proof. HV2 does not require completing that entire palette before another rat-family slice.

| Law dimension | What the adapter must answer |
|---|---|
| Physical feasibility | Which parts exist, what survives death, eligible technique/body/tool, and whether tissue, charge, identity or information moves |
| Conservation / renewal | Units and payer, source depletion, loss, regrowth/recharge/replant or no renewal, exact-token custody and permitted exceptional duplication |
| Access / social consequences | Owner, collector permission, participant consent, issuer restrictions, witnesses/detection, sanctions and reachable illicit/contested routes where supported |
| Knowledge | Who can perceive anatomy/charge/contamination, assay scope, discovery route, truthful unknown display and sealed ID projection |
| Handling / crossings | Freshness clock, storage/preservation, carry and directional destination-use rules; temporary suppression retains dormant material under an explicit policy |

Physical means, institutional permission, legality and ethical preferences remain separate facts. An institution may certify a collector or seller without certifying every tissue. A stolen resource can remain physically useful and provoke an actually witnessed response. No global morality veto, guaranteed witness or implicit “licensed = safe.” Where consent/control has a native consumer, collection reads it; do not fake it as a positive flavor tag.

## W3. Economy consequences and smallest safe joins

Full rat processing makes parts more reliable than independent rare drops. Inspect HV1's actual yields before setting prices: estimate parts per kill and played minute, subtract tool/time/preservation costs, then compare native sale returns, tanning inputs and traveler fees. Do not compensate automatically by cutting every price. Tail scarcity can fall while intact hides retain a separate demand, and scraps need a useful sink.

Extend [P8 trade/supply](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/TRADE_SUPPLY.md) and the existing regional saturation owner rather than adding a second market. Whole sources, raw lots, scraps and prepared products need explicit legal sale destinations and custody transitions. A trader buying a carcass receives its remaining claims; player cannot process it afterward. Merchant template restock must not mint a singular token sold to them. Grade price premiums read observed/certified properties, not sealed truth. Ask escrow and harvest sale are different payments for different transferred goods; the same goods cannot be handed over twice.

Preservation can make a distant route viable while charging actual fuel/storage capacity. Helpers create labor supply and costs, not free outputs. Rare-variant demand can support commissions/trophies without making common parts worthless. Broad dynamic scarcity, transport markets and ecological simulation are deferred; first balance gate is one useful source-to-consumer loop and its sale/ask controls.
