## C2. Bypass declarations, not hidden exceptions

An operation records which requirements the cheat bypasses: knowledge, tools, time, damage, receiver capacity, access or conservation. Anything unlisted follows the ordinary resolver. A harvest-everything cheat can ignore authorization under a local law and still provoke an actual witnessed institutional response; an undetectable version needs its own detection law. A powerful clean-kill can preserve all anatomy without inventing an offsetting curse.

Ordinary whole processing and cheat extraction remain different options. Cheat results retain acquisition route and actual transformation provenance. If a cheat duplicates matter intentionally, the law names a duplication source/output receipt, energy/cost if any, and whether exact identities can copy. Never implement duplication by replaying an old harvest receipt or forgetting depleted claims. Prices and consumers can react to disclosed cheat-made stock under their declared laws; do not silently penalize it as counterfeit.

C2a: Previews show known costs and bypass scope without consuming stream draws. A commit changes only the selected future operation/draw; save result, cheat law revision, actor/payer, source delta and receipt. Reload/import never runs the cheat again. Turning a cheat off removes future bypass, not previously acquired legal stock. Crossing to an incompatible world retains dormant stock/ability under an explicit adapter policy rather than silently deleting it.

## C3. Player lenses versus developer inspection

Player display reads observer knowledge and supported cheat disclosure. Appraisal reports “unknown purity” when it cannot measure it, not a safe-looking default. Anatomy sight unlocks knowledge through the actual discovery route. Grade drivers show only known inputs; a price premium or disabled part label cannot leak sealed specialness.

Developer inspection can expose nonsealed source IDs, decisions/receipts, source revision, claim balances, grade inputs, eligible clock, reservation state and refused predicates. The persisted source truth may contain sealed implementation data, but user-facing reports, screenshots and indexes project only secret ID/route/status. Agents may inspect sealed implementation for tests privately. No generic inspector is an authorization to unseal.
