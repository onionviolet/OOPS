# C3: Growth and levels per YAIRPG world

Read `docs/gm/dispatch/skill-growth-2026-10-10/COMMON.md` first and follow it.

**Goal:** "a lot of stuff depends on the world." For every world YAIRPG
has a card or folder for, audit how growth and levels work in its source
(or design, for original worlds) and propose a growth profile: native
growth models (G), what a level means (L), scale shape (S), the local
ceiling and ways past it, population curve, gap rule, how outside powers
and levels read there, and what's distinctive at the bottom and the top.

**Worlds:** the nine cards in `docs/WORLD_CARDS.md` (extend its "How you
grow in each world" section's ideas, don't repeat them) and each folder
in `docs/worlds/` (bleach, conceptual, elden-ring, fallout, harry-potter,
jujutsu, mother-of-learning, naruto, novel-worlds, original-frontier,
paradise-hub, proto23, proving-ground, skyrim). Also see
`docs/worlds/PROGRESSION_AND_ASSEMBLY.md` and `docs/CHEATS_BY_WORLD.md`.

**Sources:** the world's own docs, its research dossiers and wiki
digests under `docs/research/`, then genre knowledge labeled MEMORY.
Fandom wikis may be checked politely through their API per
`docs/research/SOURCE_DEEP_DIVE_PLAYBOOK.md`. No Royal Road (C1 owns it).

**Output:** `docs/research/skill-growth-2026-10-10/WORLD_GROWTH_AUDIT.md`:
one section per world with a compact growth profile table, source notes
and labels; then a cross-world comparison table; a list of worlds that
would show each G, L and S code best; gaps where a world has no source
evidence yet (and which new audit would fill it); and the checkpoint.
Do not edit WORLD_CARDS.md or world folders; Claude will fold accepted
profiles in later.
