## Findings that affect implementation

**F1:** A source grant is not a working aptitude. Inspected `capabilities.js`
aggregates active sources as numeric max/sum contributions. A learning-speed
promise needs an actual native XP consumer and provenance policy; body preparation
already has a more direct source-to-task example. Prototype numbers remain local.

**F2:** Body preparation offers a reusable join, but no broad constitution proof.
`body_progression.js` already distinguishes inherited natural balance, trained
grip and a texture-only palm mark. Its grip description promises shorter packing
and marker work. Extending a similar process in the selected world is more
concrete than assigning a new talent label. These are source observations only.

**F3:** Some older origin prose prices inherited powers or rejects combinations
unless unlocked. Prior G1 correctly supersedes that with world-qualified full,
free, extreme and exceptional variants. The STP cards add no compulsory penalty,
rarity ceiling, ancestry purity, human developmental schedule or moral alignment.
