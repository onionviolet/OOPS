# Center-menu clutter considerations

2026-10-11. Proposal only, based on the two director screenshots and targeted current source inspection. No runtime changes or director acceptance.

F1: The patrol card repeats its payout and next progression, includes long NPC dialogue and future watchcraft instruction, and presents an unavailable promotion as a full control. These compete with immediate local actions.

F2: The same scrolling host contains optional-world/beginning controls and local clerk services. Source modules independently append/prepend into location_actions_div; a shared ordering/visibility owner is a useful selected implementation seam. This observation does not establish every panel's behavior.

D1: Keep current activity, urgent commitments and directly usable local actions at the top. Group other known local interactions under shallow People, Work and Projects views; place supported travel/beginning controls with navigation. Preserve the existing native center menu and reachable alternatives.

D2: Use compact summaries per pursuit. Patrol example: Apprentice; full round 50 coins; next rank 0/2 shifts, 0/2 kit returns. One next action and one concise blocker. Put dialogue, mechanics, history and distant requirements behind an accessible Details control. Known actionable costs and irreversible consequences remain visible before commitment.

D3: Present state-dependent next steps rather than every prerequisite simultaneously. Explain watchcraft when its learning route is actually relevant; hide unlearned/unknown future content according to character knowledge. Discoverability for already-known alternatives must survive grouping.

D4: Keep current timers, cancellation, kit return/debt and other unresolved commitments visible across category changes. Retain expanded state, scroll and keyboard focus; background timer updates must not reorder cards.

D5: Let one center-menu coordinator own panel order, category, visibility and attention. Existing feature modules retain gameplay and content ownership. Avoid a second gameplay writer or save migration solely for layout.

Observable prototype gate, if implementation is selected: find/start ordinary patrol without scrolling through several unrelated cards; find kit return and current work immediately; reach the clerk and a supported navigation route in one category switch; see known costs/blockers; preserve focus/scroll across ticks. Check actual narrow and normal desktop widths. Exact categories and copy remain proposals.
