# Learning and continuing life details

2026-10-11. Additive original proposals, not new accepted world rules. Existing
owners remain authoritative. These close MM09/MM10 of the [manifest](MANIFEST.json).
No fictional source text or secret criteria are reproduced.

## MM09: A useful method can become somebody else's capability

Provenance: G3-010, G3-018, G2-011, G6-005, G6-022, G8-008/G8-011/G8-012;
I11/I18/I24/I36/I38. Owners: docs/MEANS_AND_APPRAISAL.md,
docs/TECHNOLOGY_AND_PROGRESSION.md, docs/worlds/proto23/WORLD_BRIEF.md and
Frontier WORLD_BRIEF. The latter briefs were read; their historical status labels
are not treated as current runtime proof. Sections S04/S06/S07/S09/S10/S11/S13/S14.

The new consequence is a teaching chain with a real recipient. A capable player
can make a service independent of their presence without pretending that a
written recipe grants its reader the author's body, tools or authority. Start
with a receiving worker's existing unmet task. Ask what they have tried; show a
sample; lend a suitable tool if willing; demonstrate; let them attempt; examine
the result; revise the instruction; commission a later independent application.
Each verb changes a different thing. Owning the notes allows consultation;
understanding permits choosing a method; a supported successful application
changes the actual task. A milestone title may commemorate that history, but
is neither the operation nor the employer's recognition.

Local variants stay different. Village Watchcraft describes scanning and reporting
with loaned kit, following its existing career/study owner. Frontier material
instruction uses the particular stock and workshop role, not patrol credit.
The Proto23 visitor brief supplies its bounded learned hand-tool procedure and
practice/material consumer: new teaching is an authored visitor extension,
not a claim about the source game's pupil or skill rewards. In Glasshouse,
a report may demonstrate what was heard without teaching the listening means.
A source-world student or supernatural transmission needs a qualified local
source card before its actual faculty can be promised. A complete power that
grants understanding and execution should grant both where its scope says so;
ordinary apprenticeship is not imposed as its artificial delay.

Admission uses the recipient's consent or an explicitly selected coercive route,
actual common language or interpretation means, contact, material/tool access
and supported body. Teaching can be paid, reciprocal, a favor, free, or a remote
record where the local actor makes that possible. There is no universal tuition
or student slot cap. The learner may seek a cheaper teacher or want a different
method. A player's misunderstanding of their needs can produce a useful
conversation rather than a failed-stat roll.

Order: inspect current task and knowledge; agree the scope; reserve only real
inputs; accept time on the existing supported played clock; recheck support at
completion; resolve an actual attempt; return unused custody; publish outcome;
then create observer-specific feedback. The proposed first proof is one student,
one method, one task and one later repeat. Its duration is a world-owner design
dependency, not an invented engine default. Interrupted demonstration retains
only what actually happened. Failed application can leave an observed defect
and reusable notes if those were produced, but grants no fabricated success or
regional standard. An old read receipt does not substitute for this attempt.

Growth means increasing independence and applicability: assisted repair becomes
a solo repeat; the learner diagnoses a different batch; they teach a willing
second person when qualified. Ordinary expertise still matters when the player
can create goods perfectly: it locates demand, chooses a fitting specification,
communicates use and organizes distribution. A power supplying all of those
functions may bypass them too; its actual declared reach must be preserved.
An institution changes only after someone purchases, adopts or authorizes the
method. The player's silent discovery cannot rewrite every workshop's practice.

Retain the exact learner/method/task/outcome, notes' custody and reports actually
received. Lost tools suspend tool-dependent use; learned understanding remains
unless a selected memory transformation changes it. Save restoration must restore
that boundary together, without handing out the same pupil reward again.
Player UI shows known progress and the next concrete requirement. NPC skill
state stays private unless held assessment means reveal it.

Dependency MM-D09: a world owner must freeze recipient, actual task and evidence
of independent operation. MM-P2 can supply a single funded material consumer;
its inspector must bind the selected native skill/method rather than invent
teaching XP. Future proof: complete one lesson, leave, obtain a scoped actual
worker result through a real report, return to the changed stock, reload/import
and repeat the service without repeating the lesson reward. Counterexample:
a learner with only a copied name and unsupported body does not execute the
method, while a genuine complete transmission is not reduced to that name.

## MM10: Finishing a pursuit leaves a life to keep living

Provenance: G3-016/G3-020/G3-021, G4-03/G4-06/G4-09/G4-10/G4-11,
G6-027, G8-002/G8-005; I02/I03/I05/I14/I21/I22/I24/I39. Owners:
Frontier WORLD_BRIEF and TIMELINE_AND_ENDINGS, docs/TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE.md,
docs/LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md. The ending brief is a route, not a freshly verified
implementation. Sections S01/S02/S04/S05/S10/S11/S12/S14.

An ending closes a particular commitment, not the entire person's ability to
cook, rest, practice or speak to someone. The proposed micro scene is a handover:
review unfinished orders; show the candidate successor what is owned, borrowed
or promised; choose what to transfer; agree what the player will still do;
settle or explicitly retain each obligation; leave contact instructions; stay
for ordinary life or depart through an actual route. Refusing succession leaves
an alternate lawful private life or unresolved service with its stated causes.
Do not silently label refusal a run failure.

Frontier's existing finite brief allows specialist, charter and departure results
with repeatable life afterward. The additive detail here is a small independent
service transferred by an actual willing actor, not replacement town government.
Proto23 guest tenancy can end while learned notes and legitimately movable
possessions remain; source protagonist property never becomes the visitor's.
A Glasshouse work/contact relationship cannot automatically grant ownership of
the room or its passage. In a selected lineage or source-world role, office,
faculty, kinship and legal possessions are separate transfers. An heir may use
what was actually left; a new unrelated life does not inherit a stranger's purse
merely because the player remembers it. Resurrection, immortal return and true
historical rollback stay separate powerful possibilities, each with its own
restored/retained domains.

A successor requires willingness or a chosen actual control mechanism, contact,
the role's real means and transfer rights. Being powerful can accomplish physical
transfer without persuading a population that an office is legitimate. Conversely,
actual authority-changing power must change the authority it promises. NPCs can
ask to change the job, decline it, finish their own term or nominate someone else.
A title that remembers the player's achievement can remain while current office
and access change, using the existing title/progression owner rather than a new
all-purpose prestige meter.

Order: settle completed outcomes first; quote exact remaining stock/obligations;
record accepted transfer and carrier; publish the one handover; remove only
transferred control; begin the recipient's supported continuation; project local
knowledge. Reopening the conversation cannot transfer the same property twice.
An interruption before commitment transfers nothing; interruption after durable
commit restores that committed result. A discovered stolen or missing tool
changes the feasible handover, not unrelated completed history. Retain the reasons
for closure and the chronological result rather than replacing every prior choice
with a single ending flag.

The macro response follows causal support. A functioning independent service
can let its customers resume another activity. One successor quitting creates
an actual local service gap; it does not collapse a realm without downstream
reliance. Absent-player continuation uses only the selected supported clock
scope. Closing a browser causes no invented years, death or debt interest.
Return reveals what can now be observed, or what a real person communicates;
the player does not receive an omniscient regional catch-up screen.

Dependency MM-D10: world owner freezes one pursuit's closure scope, transferrable
assets/claims, successor agreement and supported continuation. MM-P1 supplies
custody; MM-P4 supplies exact absence scope. Broader rebirth/heir initializer,
true death and arbitrary rollback remain their original G1/G4 dependency routes.
Future proof: close the selected pursuit, retain home/rest/work actions, transfer
one actual operation, leave and return without rerolling its history, restore
save/import, and attempt duplicate handover. Counterexample: an unwilling heir
never gains the job by kinship alone; a faculty-transfer power changes its
supported faculty without falsely transferring unrelated office or debts.
