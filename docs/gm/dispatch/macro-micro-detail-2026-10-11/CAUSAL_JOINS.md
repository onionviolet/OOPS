# Two-way causal joins for MM01–MM10

2026-10-11. All new sequences are proposals. Each arrow needs the named actual state/actor consumer before implementation can claim the downstream result. [Detail cards](DETAIL_CARDS.md) retain local variants, original G IDs and unresolved leaves. CJ08 joins MM09 learning and MM10 continuing life to the eight primary cards. These joins are finite reviewed design cases, not tests run or claims of engine availability. They preserve all eight card dependencies; selecting a local packet need not implement the full chain.

## CJ01: Work custody to household support and back

**Forward.** A Frontier buyer has a funded need for usable packing pieces → offers one inspected order with an authorized tool → worker completes and returns that exact tool under MM01 → buyer receives usable pieces and pays from the quoted purse → worker chooses to buy or make MM02's support fixture → recipient actually uses the accommodation → a caregiver now has a free interval and can accept another funded task. MM03's tested quality determines whether the pieces work, rather than a “good craftsmanship” badge granting household capacity.

**Feedback.** A caregiver's later chosen job can replenish household stock; a recipient choosing independent seated work can supply another actual order. Their capacities alter the next quote or need. If their household has enough support they can pursue leisure instead, reducing labor supply without creating a failure state. This is a possibility, not compulsory growth into a regional firm.

**Counterexample CJ01-C1.** The worker finishes a patrol and earns money but keeps Tessa's loaned kit. Career credit, earned pay and return obligation follow the existing local contract independently. Buying a chair does not return the kit or free it for Iona. Do not clear both because the worker did something socially useful.

**Exceptional CJ01-X1.** A real creation faculty supplies the requested pieces and accommodation without work or a purchase. The resulting capacities change as promised; no mandatory wages or ordinary craft prerequisite survives. The lender still needs an exact loan resolution only if the holder actually borrowed something. A selected authority/history-editing power may resolve that differently, but ordinary material duplication does not do so implicitly.

**Observable later proof / stop.** Show actual recipient use and caregiver action availability before claiming freed time; show a finite receiving contract before claiming its extra income. Stop at MM02-D1 if there is no support-to-action consumer. Origins: G3-005/007/009/011/019, G6-008/011/012, G2-017.

## CJ02: Repair to public reliability and back to specifications

**Forward.** One exact tool yields an out-of-tolerance fitting → MM03 measurement establishes a bounded defect → owner repairs or replaces it → a real trial yields a usable fitting → MM01 delivery passes its specified receiver → MM05 office can issue its actual guarantee if authorized → buyers with a received guarantee may commission that service.

**Feedback.** A named new buyer needs a tighter function, different substrate or larger volume → office changes its actual request → maker studies, experiments or chooses a superior process. More benches increase parallel capacity only if downstream assembly, worker attention and demand permit. A precise tool and extra benches solve different constraints.

**Counterexample CJ02-C1.** A native item's high numeric crafting quality does not establish that its connector fits an unrelated device. A famous smith's title is not a measurement. A calibration of an old specimen is not evidence that a replacement retains that calibration. The receiving operation must read a real matching property/variant.

**Exceptional CJ02-X1.** Perfect crafting that guarantees the requested function skips an ordinary failure/repair loop. If it also guarantees valid compatible authority, its guarantees may be genuine; if it creates only excellent matter, an institution can still require its actual issuing process. No generic licensing barrier is invented in places that do not use one.

**Observable later proof / stop.** Compare actual old/repaired/replacement results at the same task; then compare authorized versus private service. Stop at MM03-D1 or MM05-D1 rather than asserting “regional reliability.” Origins: G6-003/008/009/022, G3-013/014, G2-015/023.

## CJ03: Site ecology to access, distribution and downstream intervention

**Forward.** One enrolled exposed process meets a selected rain boundary → unsheltered stock changes → MM04 cover installed before a later boundary protects the next lot → MM03 sound output survives processing → actual cart/foot/door route delivers goods → the recipient can operate its real service. Geography matters: Frontier's long native route is a trip; Glasshouse's manual door is a passage; viewing is information only.

**Feedback.** A service's changed use may cause a new funded route need, actual extraction demand or a request to divert runoff. A downstream owner receiving runoff can ask for another design. One workshop's solution can shift exposure elsewhere only through a represented water/site consumer; no global ecology reaction is inferred.

**Counterexample CJ03-C1.** A rain event before shelter installation cannot be undone by later shelter. A finished drain flag cannot protect a distant field with no modeled connection. An observation image of dry stock neither transfers that stock nor reveals the unviewed route. Different-world weather cannot affect Frontier drying merely because both clocks advanced.

**Exceptional CJ03-X1.** Weather control, instant drying, flood removal, flight, portal delivery or creation at the destination can each remove their actual constraint. If a power reverses past damage, it changes that past only under the declared history scope. If it creates stock inside the receiving building, intermediate roads and warehouses are genuinely unnecessary.

**Observable later proof / stop.** Show exposure window, actual protected result and held receiving cargo. For broader water consequences stop at MM04-D1's downstream consumer; for strong bypass stop at MM06-D1's exact function. Origins: G6-019/020/021, G4-03/05, G2-016/017/021.

## CJ04: Institutional service to belief and new institutional action

**Forward.** MM05 office has a competent worker, useful space, inputs and funds → performs a named inspection or lesson → sends a bounded report by an actual channel → recipient believes or disputes that report → changes a specific contract/access decision → institution can issue a new funded service or review. A listener can hold an erroneous belief and still act consistently with their evidence.

**Feedback.** Evidence of a failed guarantee reaches the responsible issuer → issuer can inspect, compensate, reform a process, withdraw recognition or refuse under authored rules → the next service actually changes. A private correction can remain private; wider action requires another communication step. Ordinary institutions may sincerely adopt a useful extraordinary method.

**Counterexample CJ04-C1.** A charter with no instructor does not teach. A promotion with no actual policing means does not enforce a border. A single frightened witness cannot make a distant faction know the holder's precise source, inventory and intentions. If a messenger cannot leave, escalation cannot simply materialize elsewhere.

**Exceptional CJ04-X1.** A holder with real universal communication, direct mind-reading or recognized absolute authority can bypass the ordinary report chain to its actual scope. A mere blinding show of power cannot be quietly promoted to those functions. The design preserves genuinely extreme means rather than demanding ordinary bureaucracy anyway.

**Observable later proof / stop.** Observe the issuing actor's actual knowledge and the service/decision it enables; compare disconnected and connected report routes. Stop at authority, evidence or channel adapter gaps rather than writing a faction-wide reputation number. Origins: G3-004/013/014/018, G4-14/15, G2-023/027.

## CJ05: Abundance to continuing livelihoods and new demand

**Forward.** MM06 holder creates functional goods → goods belong to actual people/storage and reach their consumers → relieved need reduces the next ordinary order → households can consume, share or redirect funds → suppliers make authored choices based on actual observed change → a new service, craft specialization, gift network or quiet abundant life becomes reachable.

**Feedback.** Recipients may need compatible handling, a different function, transport or no further supply at all → their choices give the holder new requests. If a perfect function already handles all those needs, the chain stops in real abundance and play moves to optional relationships, exploration or projects. Regional change occurs only over the consumers represented, not every person in an implied nation.

**Counterexample CJ05-C1.** Duplicating ten loaves cannot automatically double native money, refill a buyer's purse, grant accepted deliveries, confer career ranks or produce worldwide inflation. Those need actual ownership, transactions, contract acceptance and economic consumers. Goods created without notification may have no institutional response yet.

**Exceptional CJ05-X1.** A world-wide abundance power can really relieve world-wide scarcity if selected and backed by a represented aggregate consumer. It is not capped to one household for balance. A power explicitly creating money or authentic credentials may do those too; material-only creation does not silently imply them. When the function fulfills a recipient's desire directly, distribution should not remain an artificial puzzle.

**Observable later proof / stop.** Show created goods, actual use and changed next demand; separately show one supplier's informed choice. Stop at MM06-D1 for general aggregate adoption. Origins: G2-017/025/027, G6-012/020/021, G3-012/013/019/020.

## CJ06: Body change to labor, equipment and social recognition

**Forward.** MM07 transformation changes actual anatomy/faculty/control → action re-evaluates fit, ability and held tool → a now-compatible task becomes reachable or a prior task pauses → successful result changes a real order/service → observers can recognize competence or a changed identity through their means → access or new work changes through MM05's procedure.

**Feedback.** A new job or chosen body creates demand for an adapted handle, workplace or care arrangement → MM03 repair/fitting and MM02 accommodation produce actual support → the holder can practice a new method or perform independent work. A superior transformed body may require no adaptation; do not insist on one as a tax.

**Counterexample CJ06-C1.** Looking like the office holder does not necessarily copy their legal authority, mind, skill or keys. Losing a hand does not erase all learned knowledge. A new body cannot use the previous body’s equipped object merely because a saved “prepared” flag remains. Equally, a perfect transformation that explicitly retains all those functions cannot be weakened to ordinary disguise.

**Exceptional CJ06-X1.** Perfect transformation, telekinetic manipulation or a distributed body can solve the interface/work constraint, according to its declared scope. A source-holder body swap moves powers only if that mechanism promises it. A local institution can recognize identity through testimony or a valid extraordinary identity means; no universal appearance lock is imposed.

**Observable later proof / stop.** Compare the same actual task and fitted equipment before/during/after one chosen change, then observe one person’s recognition process. Stop at MM07-D1 for dynamic anatomy/control or traveler occupancy. Origins: G1-009/010/018/020, G2-018/026, G6-007/025, G3-007/014.

## CJ07: Absence to supported activity, evidence and changed return choices

**Forward.** MM08 task has a consenting worker with transferred means → body visit or supported contact advances the declared local clock → actual work completes or pauses at a named boundary → report reaches a recipient through an available channel → returning holder inspects the same history → renews, changes, collects, repairs or leaves. Payment and goods follow the same settled cause; a greeting alone cannot manufacture a completed job.

**Feedback.** Return reveals a real stock shortage, expired support or completed project → holder chooses fresh supplies, a new communication means or no further work → next absence can have different supported activity. An informed worker can propose amended terms. A reliable self-running institution may continue without the player if its actual clock/actors/inputs permit, without becoming compulsory attendance.

**Counterexample CJ07-C1.** Glasshouse recorded replay is not renewed viewing support. Iona's paused-outside-Village shift cannot finish simply because an unrelated realm clock moved. A missing letter is not proof the recipient refused; a report received at noon is not exact midnight telemetry. A return cannot reset a finite export claim or replay a stipend.

**Exceptional CJ07-X1.** A true remote channel, avatar, time acceleration or global work support can advance and report its real scope. Joining multiple support intervals advances time once, not once per watching body. Full rollback must restore affected work, goods, payment and knowledge domains promised by that power; a local scenario restore cannot impersonate it.

**Observable later proof / stop.** Record interval ownership, expiry/completion order and recipient evidence; compare absent unsupported, supported contact and bodily present. Stop at MM08-D1 for cross-instance/offline history, or selected rollback-domain dependencies. Origins: G3-021, G4-05/06/07/08/09/10/14, G2-004/006/024.

## CJ08: Retirement and loss back into ordinary continuing life

**Forward.** A mature maker/worker teaches one willing successor and transfers an actual tool/right separately → successor has a body and expertise for the selected service → the service continues or changes with actual funding/inputs → household/community receives that result → donor retires, travels, takes other work or remains a friend. A life can end a career without ending play or forcing prestige.

**Feedback.** Successor discovers a different method or refuses an old obligation → actual service and family choices change → donor may advise, help, reclaim only rights that still permit it, or stay retired. The new arrangement can produce different demand and shared history. Surviving carriers, not past fame alone, preserve the useful legacy.

**Counterexample CJ08-C1.** A deed cannot teach mastery; a taught lesson cannot transfer property; kinship cannot recreate a sold tool. An unrelated apprentice can become the best worker without inheriting a biological trait. Furniture loss removes its service without deleting the family. An old chronicle cannot reconstruct exact missing inventory for a claimed lossless resume.

**Exceptional CJ08-X1.** True perfect inheritance, resurrection or artifact consciousness may preserve capacities beyond ordinary teaching and estates. Those promises must be included exactly, not narrowed to a small heir bonus. A person duplicating themselves produces separate agency/history only if that mechanism specifies it; a copied photograph is not a successor.

**Observable later proof / stop.** Show separate transfers/learned means and one successor task, then show the living donor's remaining choices. Stop at actual control/transition, estate law, retained source or archive-fidelity dependencies. Origins: G3-016/020, G1-023/025, G6-027, G2-005/020/026, G4-08/10/12.

## Interaction readback and bounded performance

Across CJ01–CJ08, a cause is the actual accepted operation/event, not prose or a screen visit. Operations need current ownership, selected body, real resources, participant/site occupation, route and knowledge. Inspecting or rendering cannot mint results. A failed durable write cannot publish half a wage, transfer, promotion, repair or report; exact save staging belongs to the implementation integrator. Restoration keeps settled causes and identity. Each selected feature must state whether it is old-save absent, additively migrated or explicitly incompatible.

Implement the actual finite chain first. A workshop with three funded receivers can be authored as three real contracts; it need not simulate every household in a nation. Recurring larger change needs an explicit renewed demand/funding cause and bounded processing of supported event intervals. Unspent time must remain accounted for at scheduler limits; large time jumps cannot silently skip a decision boundary. Actor-private evidence prevents global polling from revealing hidden motives or sealed truths. A simple summary can display “two deliveries settled; one waits for materials,” but it needs the actual outcomes and current stop reason, not a second state writer.

The held dependencies do not block unrelated ready work. MM03's fixed owned-tool repair needs no full-body selector; MM02's local accommodation needs no national market; MM01's existing entry needs no new random-origin initializer; MM08's frozen return needs no offline year simulator. Strong-power and source-world variants remain live design routes with their exact missing consumer named, rather than being discarded because the first implementation packet is smaller.
