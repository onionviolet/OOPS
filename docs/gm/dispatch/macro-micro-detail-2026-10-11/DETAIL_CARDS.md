## MM01: The useful job includes what happens to its tools

**Local fiction and variants.** In Village, preserve Tessa's existing patrol contract, kit custody and career meanings. Proposed extension: a worker can ask for a specific delayed return or arrange a substitute before lending the kit onward. Tessa cares about the next patrol's actual readiness, not an abstract obedience score. In Frontier's optional cooperative, a funded receiving order names a sample, specification, buyer and acceptance route; a smaller amended lot can solve the buyer's need without pretending it is a full delivery. In Glasshouse, an ordinary loan may be usable inside the room but cannot travel through the door under current fail-closed cargo terms. Viewing a tool supplies no physical handling. This makes a workshop commission, observation report and exported object different kinds of work.

**Source-qualified possibility.** Proto23's inspected guarding duty stops on leaving and pays at its local completion boundary. A visitor commission with a loaned hand tool is an original branch extension; it must not copy the source protagonist's job history or entitlement. Public first-book-world detail stays generic supply duty; source identities and local terms must be qualified by its private owner.

**Discovery, verbs and means.** Hear the offer, read or have someone explain its terms, inspect the available kit, quote, accept, borrow, perform, pause, request amendment, deliver, return, settle or refuse. Literacy is one means of understanding a written specification; a willing interpreter can be another. Performing requires the body, current access and exact useful tool. Knowing Watchcraft does not create its kit. A borrower can reserve a permitted tool for a job; neither custody nor a reservation changes legal ownership.

**Timing and order.** Reveal known terms before acceptance. Freeze the agreed job revision, payer and exact return obligation on acceptance. Reserve only the current authorized operation. At completion evaluate actual work, goods, recipient capacity and agreed terms, then stage transfer, payment, memory and closure in one durable result. Amendment changes future obligations only after both sides assent; it cannot erase a previously settled wage. A return can be recorded independently of career credit. Proposed negotiation occupies a short conversation; native job durations remain with their owners rather than being shortened here.

**Responses and continuing use.** Tessa can accept a reliable substitute, prefer the original kit or refuse an unsafe arrangement according to authored readiness and her held evidence. A buyer can accept a reduced specification for a reduced actual need. A worker's early competence opens inspection, teaching or supervision routes, while mature useful work still produces patrols or received goods. A patron can pay, trade tuition, owe a favor or teach freely where fitting. Refusing one job leaves ordinary pursuits available.

**Interruption and retention.** Leaving early preserves the local contract's actual partial-work and cancellation rules; it does not grant a completed shift. A borrowed tool sold, broken or taken elsewhere cannot be returned through a stale boolean. Late restitution and apology can settle different material/social matters. Save keeps exact custody, agreed revision, partial work and settled cause. The buyer knows inspected goods and reports received, not the worker's undisclosed cheat. Replay, render and return visits cannot pay a closed contract again.

**Unresolved leaf MM01-D1.** Local lender amendment/substitute semantics and UI are absent from this design's verified runtime scope. Select one lender, one substitute identity and one route; join actual custody/occupation and the existing root publisher. Borrowed cross-realm passage additionally needs the existing consent planner to acquire a real native UI/obligation consumer. MM01 can be implemented locally without that cross-world feature.

## MM02: Care changes someone's day rather than becoming a generic meter

**Local fiction and variants.** Frontier proposal: a roof patch and accessible work surface let a recovering resident sort small goods while seated. They choose paid work, reading, visitors or rest; care is not a ticket to extracting labor. Village proposal: a neighbor prepares a meal or accompanies a person along a known safe route; food affects only the selected native or enrolled local need. Default-off hunger remains off unless chosen. Glasshouse proposal: a co-located visitor arranges a dry resting corner and gives a requested practical aid; an image through the slit can report a visible difficulty but cannot perform physical care. No new resident illness, hidden physiology or need is asserted.

**Source-qualified possibility.** Proto23's inspected bed/hearth/fuel functions permit an attributed home-support analogy. A guest cottage accommodation is original, not the protagonist's ancestral home or a canon diagnosis. Existing Mother-of-Learning brief describes a bounded paid care/recovery route; broader domestic care, long illness and source characters' preferences remain private/source-qualified authoring, not facts inferred here.

**Discovery, verbs and means.** Ask what help is wanted, notice an observable difficulty, offer a seat, prepare compatible support, bring actual supplies, accompany, delegate, teach self-use, check back, decline or stop. An ordinary helper sees a limp or hears a request; they do not see an exact diagnosis without a relevant means. A physician, construct technician and plant tender can have different useful domains. A harmless chair needs handling and recipient access; medicine needs its selected target/treatment scope. A robot can benefit from compatible repair without being forced into biological healing.

**Timing and order.** Establish the recipient's expressed choice or the authored emergency authority, then quote shared space, supplies and occupied attention. Install or provide the actual aid before applying its service effect. A support task can reduce the amount of direct assistance needed only if the recipient's actual action consumer recognizes that support. Release caregiver time after the service changes, then offer that person's next available activity. No income or friendship is minted simply by installing furniture. Durations depend on the task; this card proposes no universal care tick or mandatory daily upkeep.

**Responses and continuing use.** A proud resident may accept an adapted tool while declining accompaniment. Another may want company even when physically capable. The caregiver may welcome shared shifts, ask payment, offer help freely, prefer another pursuit or refuse. Growing skill unlocks specific accommodation design, better observations or safe teaching, not a universal cure. An exceptional healer that truly cures the selected cause removes that care need; ordinary expertise remains useful for consent, fitting, residual needs and unrelated people, only where those actually remain.

**Interruption and retention.** Interrupted accompaniment ends at the actual route point, not safe arrival. A half-installed rail is not usable support. Removing a fixture removes its held function but does not erase friendship or learned self-care. Save keeps recipient identity, supplied aid, observed support result and any specific accepted promise. A record of past help stays historical; it does not grant an absent helper live medical telemetry. The selected absence policy determines future support, with no invented closed-browser injury.

**Unresolved leaf MM02-D1.** Select one consenting original recipient, one actual limitation, one accommodation and one action whose support changes. Existing furniture bonuses are not evidence of a general care dependency model. The local service/body consumer and caregiver occupation join must be specified before any claim of freed time or changed employment. Diagnosis, age and chronic-need simulation are separate optional dependencies.

## MM03: Quality has a purpose and repair has an object

**Local fiction and variants.** Frontier proposal: a packing brace can hold coarse cargo while a measured fitting enables an accurate receiver seal. Its load capacity and its precision answer different questions. Village proposal: maintain an owned tool or use the existing loaned patrol kit as custody, without inventing combat stats for that kit. Glasshouse proposal: compare a crafted viewing device against a known calibration task. Better optical alignment improves the selected observation operation only if its channel consumer supports it; it does not create a wider identification faculty or turn a recorded scene into live truth.

**Source-qualified possibility.** Proto23's existing visitor hand-tool/stave route offers a concrete original sharp-to-dull lifecycle around a source-inspired work setting. A proposed repair can preserve the exact native adapted tool; no whole-source durability system is claimed. Book-world material properties, special alloys or magical repairs need their exact source-local evidence and function definition.

**Discovery, verbs and means.** Handle, inspect, measure, compare, diagnose, sharpen, replace an authored part, recalibrate, trial, install, remove, lend, salvage or keep the old tool. Identify the property the task needs, then use a measurement means that actually reveals it. A scratch can be observable without giving remaining lifetime. A specialist can demonstrate seal leakage; perfect appraisal may read it directly where supported. Material quality, damage, fit, skill and quantity remain independent.

**Timing and order.** Check exact object ownership, installed/loaned/reserved status and permission; select repair versus replacement versus salvage. Reserve declared inputs and work space. Complete the operation on that exact identity, then use the changed object in an actual receiving task. Salvage consumes the object and produces declared stock once; a repair restores or changes only its stated properties. Recalibration costs time or material only if the selected local operation requires it. The opt-in native wear/coin-repair prototype is not imposed on unrelated fixtures.

**Responses and continuing use.** A joiner may buy a coarse brace for a coarse load despite rejecting it for precision. A customer can ask for a guaranteed tested fitting or accept a cheaper uncertain sample. A keeper may value an heirloom's retained mark separately from its function. Mature craft can reduce work time, improve relevant tolerance, recover valuable stock or teach an alternate process. A truly superior tool may outperform an old one in every relevant respect; no artificial old-tier niche is required.

**Interruption and retention.** A cut-but-unfitted part retains only completed work. Selling the target or losing the workshop invalidates later quoted steps. Concurrent repair/salvage of the same object is rejected or explicitly serialized. Failed durable settlement leaves no charged inputs or phantom repaired state. Save preserves the same item identity and selected condition/function state. A remembered calibration result cites its sample/revision and date; later replacement does not automatically inherit that evidence. Skill knowledge can survive object loss; execution still needs current means.

**Unresolved leaf MM03-D1.** Pick one object property-to-function curve and receiving operation. Native numeric crafting quality is incumbent; arbitrary precision and interchangeable components are not proven by it. Fixed repair variants can proceed without a universal assembly graph. Identity-preserving object repair/salvage and device-specific functional-quality binding remain exact adapter dependencies if not supplied by the selected object owner.

## MM04: A route is infrastructure, exposure and someone's access

**Local fiction and variants.** Frontier proposal: shelter an exposed drying lot, keep runoff away from one enrolled garden and deliver sound packing pieces through the actual long Town route. Shorter NPC draft travel is not evidence of instant geography. Village proposal: repair a small footpath to improve a declared accompaniment or cart route; only that route changes. Glasshouse's physical doorway and observation slit are already different means. A sheltered room can support one protected process, while a view does not carry cargo and an open physical door is not a regional transport network.

**Source-qualified possibility.** Proto23 has inspected gate/forest place labels, but the visitor branch has not implemented those forest routes. A proposed material errand beyond its current three places therefore needs a selected source/local route extension. No map connection or local season is invented as canon. Private book-world geography remains with its exact era/region owner.

**Discovery, verbs and means.** Scout, sample, cover, divert, test passage, negotiate access, carry, arrange delivery, harvest, repair or avoid. Weather readings describe the current sampled location; a map or local guide may add route knowledge. A covered drying rack requires actual protection and stock. A vehicle route requires fit, load capacity, driver and permission, while flight or teleport may bypass the ordinary path exactly as their held function permits.

**Timing and order.** Select one environmental event/window and its enrolled consumer. Apply exposure only while the process is actually exposed under a supported clock. Complete protection before the harmful boundary to avoid that future effect; late intervention can stop further exposure but cannot rewrite damage already suffered unless a declared restorative means does so. Deliver only after actual travel or supported transport. Received stock then changes a named downstream operation. A returned harvest claim cannot replay. Regrowth, depletion and habitat recovery need selected rules; none is a universal assumption.

**Responses and continuing use.** A householder welcomes less runoff; a downstream user may object if diversion moves the problem onto their land. A route keeper may grant free access, ask work, charge, or have no relevant claim. A merchant may change a quote after observing better delivery reliability. Mature ecology work can create useful monitoring, alternate sources and stable infrastructure. It need not turn into conquest or mandatory environmental management.

**Interruption and retention.** Flooded access changes reachable actions, not every unrelated inventory item. A dismantled cover exposes only the selected process. Interrupted travel preserves the actual location/cargo and selected settlement policy. Save retains site state, exposure interval, consumed stock and route change in the same history. NPCs learn an improvement through inspection, use or communication; a repaired path does not instantly advertise itself worldwide. Unsupported offscreen ecology freezes rather than pretending it was simulated.

**Unresolved leaf MM04-D1.** Choose one site/event/crop or processing consumer and one route whose actual availability/time/capacity changes. Wider ecology, downstream water transfer and regional price changes need their own receiving state owner. A local sheltered-processing packet can finish without those broader systems; the larger claim must stay open until its recipient exists.

## MM05: A title, a staffed service and a legitimate right are three things

**Local fiction and variants.** Frontier proposal: a cooperative receiving office can provide inspection if someone competent is present with a bench and funded attention. A charter determines who may issue its guarantees; a personal expert can still inspect privately. Village preserves Tessa's named promotion conditions and Iona's supported patrol, which do not automatically form a new guard government. Glasshouse cooperation requires the actor's current local means and co-location; a public claim about an artisan can influence willingness only through evidence the listener actually receives. Neither viewed competence nor office ownership transfers Joss's faculty.

**Source-qualified possibility.** Proto23's inspected Dojo/place/reading facts support a source-qualified training setting, but visitor accreditation, staffing and institutional succession are original branch proposals. JJK's world brief explicitly marks its manga claims memory-derived and requiring SOURCE_LEDGER qualification. Its schools/clans/grades may provide a contrasting authority context only after selected era/source verification; this card claims no canon procedure.

**Discovery, verbs and means.** Ask for service, inspect the issuer, apply, show papers, introduce a sponsor, demonstrate competence, contest a decision, fund, recruit, delegate, resign, reform or operate privately. A registry entry establishes the right recognized by that registry. Personal mastery establishes an executable function. An observer's belief may misidentify either; the service responds to its actual procedure rather than a global rank comparison.

**Timing and order.** Quote current service eligibility, worker capacity, required input and payer. Accept a particular service, not every future institutional duty. Reserve the named worker/site; perform the inspection or lesson; deliver the bounded result and consume its actual funding. Issue recognized paperwork only through the held issuing authority. A reform changes future procurement or eligibility after its actual adoption; it does not retroactively confer a title or erase competing claims.

**Responses and continuing use.** A client can value practical results over seals. A rival may dispute the guarantee, copy a process, buy expertise, propose cooperation or ignore the office. Escalation needs an actual report, messenger and decision-maker. Mature leadership opens alternate service organization, teaching succession or broader trusted distribution. Retirement can retain personal mastery while ending office access. No compulsory universal bureaucracy or automatic hostility to power is added.

**Interruption and retention.** An empty purse stops funded work under quoted terms, not every free service. Losing a teacher closes that teaching route unless a real replacement exists. Removing a license can change recognized access without removing skill. Save separately retains role/right, worker means, completed service, funding and issuer provenance. A copied certificate may look convincing, but only the selected copying/recognition mechanism decides whether it is genuinely authoritative. Correcting one official's belief does not update an entire faction without communication.

**Unresolved leaf MM05-D1.** Select one institution, one actual service, one jurisdiction and a dissent/recognition route. General recurring staffing and authority succession are not demonstrated by the Village rank ladder. A finite funded receiving service can proceed using a real local contract; broad registry, political reform and rumor diffusion remain separate consumer dependencies.

## MM06: Creation can end scarcity, and play can move with the result

**Local fiction and variants.** Frontier proposal: a holder duplicates sound packing fittings or creates compatible food for an actual household/receiving service. If it truly has no cost, the game charges none. The buyer can need fewer ordinary orders because the goods genuinely exist. Village proposal: duplicated ordinary lanterns can equip additional people but do not themselves earn shift credit or settle the original kit debt. Glasshouse proposal: a duplicated tool may operate inside if the held ability promises functional copies. Passage still evaluates the copies' actual ownership/carrier and permitted connection; the observation slit is not cargo transport.

**Source-qualified possibility.** Proto23 visitor branch can receive an original extraordinary imported maker only through a declared carrier/function, without claiming that the inspected source grants it. Any source-world perfect creation/copy power needs exact source, premise and scope qualification: matter-only, fully functional object, bonded artifact and authority duplication are different promises. No unsupported canon ceiling is supplied here.

**Discovery, verbs and means.** Acquire the real faculty by a qualified local/start/carrier route; inspect its known scope, choose a target, create or duplicate, trial, own, gift, distribute, sell, install, teach handling, conceal or leave. A physical replica, operational replica and source-authority replica are distinct selected variants. Perfect production can bypass an ordinary workshop if its function says so. A limitation is imposed only by that chosen function, never to rescue the previous economy.

**Timing and order.** Freeze the actual target/result specification and supported copied properties. Resolve the ability's real operation, mint fresh result identities/provenance, then assign actual custody/ownership and usable functions. Functional duplicates both work when promised. They do not inherit a past wage's settled transaction cause because an object/function copy is not replaying a payment. A stronger declared history-editing power may have a different consequence and must name that scope explicitly. Subsequent distribution uses actual receiving routes, unless the power also performs delivery or direct relief.

**Responses and continuing use.** A household may consume or share the food; a supplier can change trade, specialize, collaborate or stop supplying the relieved need. A powerful producer can choose private abundance, public service, paid sale, gift or delegation where possible. Regional institutions need only respond if aware and affected. Mature play can become selection of recipients, durable self-service design, new experiments, long-term promises or quiet life. The world need not invent a scarcity tax to keep ordinary gathering relevant.

**Interruption and retention.** Distinguish incomplete output, completed creation and uncollected goods. Suppression after completed production does not delete independent goods unless the selected ability ties their existence to ongoing support. Save preserves created counts, distinct identities, real functions, bonds/authority where promised and recipient transfers. Reload does not call creation again. Known descriptions show witnessed outcomes and learned scope, never hidden truth solely to explain a rejected attempt. An NPC holder uses the same actual functional gate.

**Unresolved leaf MM06-D1.** Select one explicit power variant and one output's actual consumer. Item/provenance cloning, unique/bond/credential duplication and general economic adoption are adapter/design dependencies, not established support. A finite ordinary-object creation and household-use packet can avoid credential/identity-copy questions; the fully authoritative version remains preserved pending its exact mechanism.

## MM07: A changed body changes the means, not the whole biography

**Local fiction and variants.** Frontier proposal: a newly enlarged or multi-limbed body can lift a heavy load but may need an adapted handle for fine fitting. The actual capability can be superior in both tasks if the transformation promises that. Village proposal: changed appearance can affect Tessa's recognition while learned Watchcraft and kit custody survive in their own owners; current patrol execution still checks the held body/means. Glasshouse current selected door carries one native body with actual cargo. Alternate bodies, companions or a remote-controlled shell need their own carrier/occupancy qualification; a view alone supplies neither.

**Source-qualified possibility.** Proto23 visitor adaptation preserves the existing visiting body, not the source pupil's age or inherited property. A body-adapted guest workstation is original. Source-world innate technique/physiology, possession or perfect transformation cannot be inferred from a generic shape change. JJK brief's body/technique proposals require source ledger qualification before reliance; no canon location or transfer law is asserted here.

**Discovery, verbs and means.** Check a known task, try the new grip, refit clothing, resize a supported attachment, arrange help, demonstrate identity, switch form, return, transfer control or decline. Separate consciousness, controlled body, actual anatomy, carried objects, executable source, learned method and recognized identity. A large hand can hold a small tool only if that interface actually works; a levitated or conjured precision hand can be another held means.

**Timing and order.** At a real transformation/control event determine exactly which body/faculties, belongings and occupancy move. Re-evaluate active work and equipment against that resulting state before the next effect. A removed or incompatible fitting stops its actual contribution; it does not destroy the item or forget the lesson. Offer disclosed stop/refund/refit choices where the selected contract permits. Recognition is a subsequent observer event through actual senses, testimony, marks or records, not a automatic rewrite of relationships.

**Responses and continuing use.** A friend can recognize voice, a registrar may need a sponsor, and an expert can hold a stronger identifying means. Fear, interest and indifference are authored responses to their evidence, not inevitable hostility to nonhuman bodies. Mature use can open underwater repair, remote work, collaborative handling or an entirely changed livelihood. A body-specific method can remain dormant until support returns, or be retained perfectly if the mechanism promises it. Neither is a universal rule.

**Interruption and retention.** A mid-job body change can make continuation impossible, easier or unchanged; settlement follows actual performed work and agreed terms. A swapped host retains its independent possessions/agency according to the selected mechanism. Save keeps controller/body/source identities, real loadout, dormant functions and recognition records. Reload must not repeat a transformation or award another first-use feat. Stored appearance does not by itself establish consciousness continuity or valid ownership.

**Unresolved leaf MM07-D1.** Select one reversible form change and one actual action/equipment consumer. Arbitrary consciousness swapping and dynamic body-adapted assemblies remain separate dependencies. A fixed fitting alternative can demonstrate this leaf without implementing every anatomy; cross-realm alternate bodies additionally require the traveler/occupancy adapter and explicit native compatibility decision.

## MM08: Returning brings evidence of a continuing history

**Local fiction and variants.** Village Iona's existing supported solo round advances on the common played clock while the player remains in Village and pauses elsewhere. Preserve that scope; a delegated expedition is new design. Frontier's optional contracts can continue only under their selected actual clock/worker means, not a fictional short courier timetable. Glasshouse visits and live supported viewing union local time; closing/expiry removes support for new sampling, while the selected already-paid job semantics stay with the owner. Recorded replay neither samples nor advances. These differences make leaving, watching and bodily visiting distinct decisions.

**Source-qualified possibility.** Proto23 visitor cottage freezes unsupported absence and retains used stock/furniture/knowledge; that is this branch's chosen handling, not a claim of whole-source offline equivalence. Book-world loops, historical eras and canonical offscreen events require a selected source/premise and retained domain scope. This card promises no full-world year simulation.

**Discovery, verbs and means.** Inspect known unfinished work, agree a bounded task, hand over exact supplies, arrange a channel, leave, send a message, view through a supported means, return, ask for a report, inspect the actual result, dispute or renew. Delegation uses the worker's own body, training, custody, route and attention. A player bag is not a remote common resource. Asking a person later can reveal a completed event even without a live channel; a live instrument can provide different current evidence.

**Timing and order.** Before departure select the applicable existing absence policy and outstanding obligations. Transfer the task's means explicitly. Advance supported intervals once through the selected clock; pause/freeze or bounded catch-up are distinct authored policies. Respect expiry, completion and decision boundaries. On return materialize the same instance/history, obtain evidence through an observer/channel, then settle claims only if the task's actual completion rule permits. Return itself does not refill budgets, reroll people, heal or pay a job again.

**Responses and continuing use.** A worker may have completed the task, waited for materials, chosen a permitted fallback or refused a new duty. They may care that the player returned to help, brought supplies or ignored a received message. A mature household/institution can arrange real delegated service and independent lives without compulsory attendance. An exceptional communication or distributed-body means can improve support and information exactly as promised; it need not imply unlimited reach, unless that is its actual scope.

**Interruption and retention.** Lost contact stops the specific supported observation/work that relied on it. It does not erase an already received message or all local activity. A chapter jump can be offered only with declared retained stock, people, commitments and archive fidelity. Frozen absence is honestly unchanged state, not invented intervening dramas. Save keeps instance identity, clock intervals, task settlement, actual supplies and timestamped recipient evidence. Missing private content preserves dormant progress through its existing compatibility owner rather than reconstructing a new past.

**Unresolved leaf MM08-D1.** Select one actor/task, one clock policy and one reporting channel with exact completion/expiry semantics. General offscreen history, cross-instance expedition, arbitrary archive resume and yearly social evolution are not proven by Glasshouse leases or Village patrol. A local paused task plus return/report packet can finish without those capabilities; a broader active history remains gated by its declared state/clock/consumer adapters.
