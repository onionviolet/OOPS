## Goal and inputs

Flesh out the game's macro structures and micro details together. Expand and
improve existing ideas, showing how local actions change larger systems and how
those systems shape ordinary choices. Preserve the full world horizon while
making the next work concrete. Do not return only a checklist or idea names.

Recover AGENTS.md, PROJECT.md, docs/GAME_MASTER_PROMPT.md,
docs/IDEA_INDEX.md, docs/USER_VISION.md, docs/WORLD_COMPLETION_STANDARD.md and
docs/gm/WORLD_ACTIONABILITY_GM_PROMPT.md. Read the completed
docs/gm/dispatch/idea-expansion-2026-10-11/ return and its checkpoint before
repeating prior work. Consult relevant existing world/system owners and current
dispatch checkpoints. Inspect source under docs/ENGINE_AUTHORING.md wherever
claiming a usable engine contract. Treat all live dirty work as others' work.

Macro coverage: distinct world rules, geography/ecology, history and natural
course, institutions/factions, resource and economic chains, technology,
progression paths, population/NPC activity, travel/continuity, endings and
continuing life. Micro coverage: actual verbs, routines, skills and milestones,
objects/materials/quality, bodies and powers, people/services/dialogue, knowledge
and discovery, facilities, timing, interruption, failure, removal, repeat use,
feedback and durable consequences. These are review families, not universal
rules every world must adopt.

## Work and acceptance

Account for the relevant existing idea families and S00–S14 sections in a stable
detail ledger. Each receives a disposition, original owner/ID, existing depth,
needed expansion and next route. Reuse earlier IDs. Record remaining source or
world coverage explicitly instead of declaring every world complete.

Deepen the important gaps with concrete detail cards and two-way causal chains:
world rule or institution → local opportunity/constraint → player/NPC action →
lasting local change → wider response → new choices. Include quiet everyday
play and exceptional/powerful interactions. Use several contrasting current
world contexts so the design does not collapse into a generic settlement.
Choose a finite first tranche from the actual gaps, publish its manifest, then
complete every selected leaf or identify its exact unresolved dependency.

Each functional card gives provenance and proposal status, local variants,
acquisition/discovery, actual verbs, prerequisites and held means, timing/effect
order, costs only where justified, other actors' response, growth and continuing
use, interruption/failure, retention and knowledge boundaries. Include texture
and character detail where useful without forcing every sentence into a mechanic.

Review cross-section joins, strong ability/cheat use, changed bodies/equipment,
lost access, absence/return, repeat rewards, save restoration, causality,
performance and usability where relevant. Preserve possibilities rather than
inventing arbitrary caps, weakness or universal balancing rules.

Translate selected details into bounded implementation packets or precise source,
design or adapter dependencies. Inspect real callers/state owners before making
support claims. Packets specify target behavior, files/interfaces, positive and
counterexample cases, persistence decision and future observable proof. No
runtime tests are required to prove a planning deliverable.

Close after coverage reconciliation, substantial expansion of the declared
tranche, interaction review and actionable dependency-ordered packets. Read back
the outputs and check links/IDs/provenance. Record what remains proposed, unread,
unimplemented or unverified. Director review remains separate.
