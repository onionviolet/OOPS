# B17 original public-fork body proposals

2026-10-14. All rows are INFERRED original designs, not accepted content,
implemented gameplay or renamed franchise characters. [Comic CO6](../../../../COMIC_WORLDS_AND_MEDIUM_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-14.md#co-overarching-considerations)
keeps direct versions in private packs. These use body mechanisms as inspiration;
they do not reproduce costumes, signature materials, family plots, named
institutions or superhero aliases. No art or source prose was copied.

Possible setting fit: a canal-and-rooftop district within the pilot's original
[Masked City proposal](../../../../research/comics/REPORT.md#draft-world-card-the-masked-city-original-proposal).
This is an optional local fit, not a registered world or a revision of that card.
Civilian work gives each body an immediate use. Powerful versions and mature
starts are allowed; the defaults below are illustrative, not obligatory ceilings.

## Body knowledge and small lived effects

Use the existing G70/G76 and means/appraisal owners. SB1's relay log can tell a
clinician that an interface responded; it cannot reveal hidden parentage. SB2's
known charge record can forecast a work shift without asserting a remaining
lifespan. SB3's two occupants may report different details truthfully. SB10's
assay can return unknown outside usable signal conditions. A more capable cheat
can know more if its mechanism supports it and follows the authored discovery
route. Do not invent universal scanner mistakes.

Keep G77 texture connected to actual causes: a sleeve tailor recognises SB7's
new joint diameter; SB5 leaves different traces in two forms; SB4's returning
offshoot knows a route the household has not heard yet. No extra upkeep bars
or constant idle interruptions are implied.

If a later slice is selected, define acquisition, development, alternate means,
loss, restoration, offspring/template transmission and cross-world retention
before implementation. Verify its real action, observer knowledge and applicable
save/reload/import on a rebuilt fresh origin. Desktop is primary at 1366x768;
mobile is deferred. None of those gameplay gates was run for these proposals.
