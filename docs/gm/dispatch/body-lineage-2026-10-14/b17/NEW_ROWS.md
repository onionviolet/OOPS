## Integration boundaries

CB16-CB23 are local B17 code reservations for the root to reconcile before
integration. CB22 and CB23 are especially narrow comparisons, not newly verified
whole-world systems. Worm and Godclads retain their prose-source identity.

No new CB row for symbiote breeding, universal resurrection, hybrid probability,
body swapping or a trauma-prediction organ was added: those either refine existing
owners or lack qualified source detail here. Such routes remain reachable as
original designs with authored mechanisms. [STAND_INS](STAND_INS.md) supplies
examples without upgrading these gaps to canon facts.
