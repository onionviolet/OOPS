genetics owner stays untouched. Evidence IDs resolve in the four SRC files.

## G76: complete CC1-CC6 audit

All six are INFERRED cheat proposals. Their analogies do not prove a universal
canonical device. Keep their cards in the existing cheat owner; do not create
a rival rank, identity, fact or mantle subsystem.

| Row | Correction and evidence | Default + Dial, in-world reason and cost |
|---|---|---|
| CC1 | Split handbook descriptors from institutional threat estimates. STATED M12; inherited western worm.C2. Neither establishes a true scalar body rating or ceiling. | Default: dated sourced record with its dimension and context. Dial: live appraisal if the held means supports it. Cost: access, scan time or fuel; a perfect reader may be exact within its actual scope. Join comic CA3/K4. |
| CC2 | Detection is of a specified marker or signal, not universal latent ancestry. STATED M13 supports a detection aid but not every CB1/CB2 carrier. | Default: assay or local signal detection, with unknown outside its scope. Dial: range, markers, masked signals and active/latent sensitivity. Cost: sample, equipment, contact or fuel per mechanism. Do not treat no signal as no lineage. |
| CC3 | “Any body” and “retcons change the answer” overpromise scope. No origin-recall mechanism was established; M02/D03 only supply changed accounts. | Default: retrieves records or traces it can access. Dial: causal sight or true history for an authored route. Cost: archival access, focus or causal reach. Retcon memory follows K5's law; do not silently overwrite remembered facts or leak a sealed origin. |
| CC4 | Seeing a face differs from knowing the civilian identity, lineage, possessor or shapeshifter beneath it. CB15 is an application, not proof of an infallible device. | Default: resolves a covered face through its real sensory means. Dial: disguises, bodies, remote records, mental/soul signatures. Cost: range, time or detectable probing. Use comic CA7; observer suspicion stays distinct from confirmed identity. |
| CC5 | Trigger proximity is UNVERIFIED as a canon capability. W02 explicitly leaves forced-trigger theories uncertain; stress is not a reliable power countdown. | Default: warning about observed distress with uncertainty. Dial: genuine source/causal foresight in an original world, with an authored discovery route. Cost: access, focus or forecast horizon. No distress score becomes a guaranteed trigger, and safety can be chosen without a forced event. |
| CC6 | A record can show past holders and eligibility claims; it cannot nominate a body's heir merely by resemblance. Existing pilot F7 and comic CA4 own the analogy. | Default: known succession history, qualification and claimant records. Dial: appointment authority if granted separately. Cost: archive access, testimony or institutional standing. The current holder's body, equipment and skills transfer by their own routes. |

Exact source truth need not be wrong or weak by default. Where a cheat really
knows a fact, report it accurately. Missing knowledge, stale evidence and biased
grading are specific possible mechanisms, not mandatory nerfs.
