## Contradictions and remaining qualification

M01 corrects the puberty absolute in CB1. M03 explicitly contradicts fixed
touch and harm requirements across all Rogue eras. M02 preserves both heritage
labels; it does not support a blanket mutant-to-nonmutant replacement rule.
M12 is evidence that the digest's acquisition failure is route-specific, not
proof that no Marvel evidence exists anywhere in this project.

CB4's franchise-wide noninheritance claim is unsupported. M05/M07/M10 show
different acquisition and replication routes but supply no general offspring
odds. CB8's pain, agelessness and scar erasure claims remain unqualified as
universal traits. Direct Wolverine profile fetch failed; switched to publisher
articles, without a retry. No claim uses the anomalous Hyperstorm search result.

No SHOWN comic-scene evidence, comprehensive X-gene model, fertility law,
whole issue/era reading, or full-dump coverage is claimed. [CORRECTIONS](CORRECTIONS.md)
owns the proposed CB/CC changes; K5 owns continuity and mantle mechanics.
