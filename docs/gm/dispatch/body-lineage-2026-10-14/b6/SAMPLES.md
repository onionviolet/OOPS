The QA coverage deck samples each world without replacement; this is a coverage tool, not a normal-body distribution or a fixed rarity budget. All tuples have equal fixture weights, unrelated to proposed real cohort rarity.

**Fixture prerequisites:** each emitted card belongs to a distinct admitted synthetic body; eligible original lineage/parent records exist for inherited rows and actual issuer/recipient/rite records exist for C06/W06. A card describes potential separately from the future acquisition/use of fuel, instruction or tools. Those means must be obtained and checked before use. No actual family or item is generated in the project.

Common lifecycle: possession may precede diagnosis; awakening requires its listed test/instruction only where specified; repeated paid use can improve control by a selected handler. Growth never invents mastery from parentage. Loss of the relevant tissue/source disables its function while history/learning remain. Source-compatible stronger variants are allowed; larger range, more precise evidence or better storage need a real mechanism. Observer recognition requires the listed discovery. Mundane and supernatural rows do not all share one growth ladder.
