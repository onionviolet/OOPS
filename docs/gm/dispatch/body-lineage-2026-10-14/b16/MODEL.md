## M3 Causal loop and defaults

Start from a fitting character with actual means. Their body can change how they perform an ordinary action. That action can leave an observable trace. A witness or test can form a bounded belief. A teacher, household, service or institution can act on that belief through its own means. A later transmission leaves only what its real carrier can carry. The resulting successor still has their own body, knowledge and motives. This joins micro detail to lineage without requiring a population simulator or a colony endpoint.

Default: quiet contextual effects, no invented universal drawback, no new compulsory realism meters, no automatic closed-browser decay. Dial: a selected source/body/world can make function, cost, timing or social consequences decisive, absent or cleanly beneficial. Reason/cost: name the actual organ, method, provider, material, practice, connection or custody that makes it happen. The body's identity does not imply biological eligibility, allegiance, consent or knowledge.

G1-G74, the prior [integration](../../body-lineage-expansion-2026-10-11/INTEGRATION.md) and the person/gimmick sections in [LIFE_SIM](../../../../LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md) remain detail owners. This synthesis neither replaces them nor turns their examples into selections.

## M6 Sourcing and time precision

B10 makes sourcing a typed source-use relation: exact holder, carrier/part, method, recipient, consumer, law revision and claim versus fact. Quality, freshness, purity, compatibility and provenance need independent causes. A market, corpse, resource manifest and containing token cannot each supply the same physical unit; a copied pattern or revocable grant follows a different operation from consumed tissue. B14 confirms that subtle content can start with a witnessed work cue or quiet recovery line. Confidence is not competence; a diagnosis is not required to fit a useful tool. Appointment expiry, actual body settlement, sample time and message arrival are separate events. These are useful refinements of OV1/OV3/OV5/OV6, not new global penalties.

## M7 CV1 and observer-known invalidation

B15 supplies the final concrete knowledge join: a bounded typed value inside B2 observation records, qualified body/person/world/clock/source references and a pure shared bodyKnowledgeView adapter. This is a proposed validated join, not proof the present simulation string-value schema supports it. Two conflicting readings retain separate identities/source lineage; a repeated copy is not independent evidence. Snapshot readings always retain their sampled date. A stale qualifier requires observer-known invalidation, so identical admitted evidence yields identical UI even when hidden body truth differs.

CV1 composes a versioned bodyLens extension on the existing known system card, enabled by one free durable review action. It grants no faculty and stores no body copy. A held independent body card and multiple-system arbitration remain palette. B15 reports schema validation for 22 cards; that does not implement their hooks or sensors. These limits make OV1/OV6 operational without turning the projection adapter into a universal knowledge framework.
