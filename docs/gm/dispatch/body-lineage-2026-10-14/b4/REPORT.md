# B4 body and lineage canon catalog

2026-10-14. Documentation-only return within `b4/` on
`codex/gameplay-foundations`. No shared owner, runtime, build, save, staging or
commit was changed. [COMMON](../COMMON.md) and [PLAN](../PLAN.md) retain lane
authority. This catalog supports the existing genetics considerations palette;
it does not select a mechanic or reorder worlds.

Delivered **63 source cards**: 62 current-digest subjects and the world-linked
Circle of Inevitability sequel. **29 cards have some bounded rule, event or
reference-summary evidence; 34 retain body-related leads without claiming canon
qualification.** All **119 current top-level digests** are accounted for;
**57 are skipped** for lack of a usable body/lineage finding in the consulted
material. A skip does not mean the complete source lacks relevant lore.

Read [PATTERNS](PATTERNS.md) for recurring and rare mechanisms and
[CORRECTIONS](CORRECTIONS.md) for the genetics reference audit. The most concrete
wording repair is **F1**, the era and survivor scope of the Uchiha example.
**F2–F3** qualify Dragonblood awakening and Squib inheritance; other rows
separate unverified precision questions from demonstrated errors.

Evidence meanings:

| Label | What it establishes |
|---|---|
| shown | The consulted source describes an actual story event or operation. A wiki report remains secondary; B4 did not thereby witness or authenticate the primary scene. |
| stated | A source rule, author/editorial description, character explanation or analytical-source summary. The row identifies the channel; testimony is not automatically world truth. |
| inferred | B4's comparison or deduction. Code mappings always belong to this layer even when the underlying fact is shown. |
| inferred / unverified lead | A blocked digest's retained mapping, metadata hook or remembered claim. It is a retrieval target, not a newly verified canon rule. |
| unknown / not established | The consulted evidence does not answer the question. It does not establish impossibility. |

Cards map to BD, CL, BP, MX, CN, SG, AG and BM without restating G1–G42.
Where no numbered row fits, a family-level mapping names the gap. Explicit
analogies do not equate different causes: BM2 comparisons outside cultivation
do not imply qi, BD6 occupancy does not imply an ancestor, and an MX fusion
comparison does not imply reproduction. Constitution faculties without a
CN1–CN8 match stay family-level. No new global code allocation competes with
the other lanes.

Coverage includes bloodline versus learned family techniques, constitutions,
acquired bodies, sex/gender and institution rules, aging and power heredity.
Each source card says which parts remain unverified. Bodily sex, identity,
presentation, reproductive role and observed/registered status are not
interchangeable. SG11 dimorphism has particularly little qualified evidence;
the catalog does not invent sex-based statistics to fill that gap.

The cards preserve contradictions: HP book/film school composition and later
Parseltongue continuity; JJK Kukuru membership; FMA manga/2003 origin and soul
differences; Ubuyashiki illness versus curse accounts; old Frieren elf wording;
CSM reader certainty about aging; old Shadow Slave status and source identity;
FEV testimony versus population biology; Elden rebirth attribution and quest
conflation; Snow Elf historical accounts; and Release That Witch's general pain
claim versus Anna's painless transition. They are not silently reconciled into
new world laws.

This catalog does not reopen deferred game-world work. |

Open source gates are exact mechanism/era checks for selected leads,
recovery of unavailable raw dump inputs, primary confirmation of indexed wiki
extracts, and the specific contradictions retained on cards. Full genetic
probabilities, reproduction after sex/body changes, most lifespan ceilings and
dimorphism distributions were not verified. No sealed truth was copied into
these public artifacts; franchise facts remain public. No human gameplay or
balance acceptance is claimed.
