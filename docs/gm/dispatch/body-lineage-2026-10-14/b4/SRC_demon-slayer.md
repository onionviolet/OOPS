Coverage and contradictions: Do not turn a blood-borne conversion into Mendelian reproduction. The hereditary-disorder and supernatural surname/marriage explanations are an unresolved mechanism tension, with databook scope distinct from manga scenes. Breathing schools are taught, body-fitted methods; this evidence gives no generic hereditary-element rule. Mark-related age limits and sex-changing disguise remain unverified here.

Unmentioned BD/CL/BP/MX/CN/SG/AG/BM subcodes are **not established by this review**, rather than ruled out by the setting. In particular bodily sex, identity, presentation, reproductive function and legal/social eligibility must not be inferred from one another.
