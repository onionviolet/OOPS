Coverage and contradictions: Base game 2022 is the local boundary. Keep Nightreign’s Grafted Monarch and later DLC separate. The rebirth note contains an apparent Ranni/Rennala attribution problem and conflates some quest unlocks; no lore mechanism relies on those disputed statements. It does not establish a sex-changing procedure, graft transmission to children or universal divine immortality. Existing world work remains deprioritized.

Unmentioned BD/CL/BP/MX/CN/SG/AG/BM subcodes are **not established by this review**, rather than ruled out by the setting. In particular bodily sex, identity, presentation, reproductive function and legal/social eligibility must not be inferred from one another.
