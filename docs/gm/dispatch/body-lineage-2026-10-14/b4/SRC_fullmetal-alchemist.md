Coverage and contradictions: Manga/Brotherhood Stone-born Homunculi and 2003 failed-resurrection Homunculi have different origins, souls and vulnerabilities. Homunculus infertility is marked uncertain in the note; do not assert it as a settled universal. Neither alchemical skill nor Armstrong-style family instruction is proved genetic by this evidence. No sex-change or ordinary species-lifespan table is established.

Unmentioned BD/CL/BP/MX/CN/SG/AG/BM subcodes are **not established by this review**, rather than ruled out by the setting. In particular bodily sex, identity, presentation, reproductive function and legal/social eligibility must not be inferred from one another.
