# Canon patterns for body and lineage authoring

2026-10-14. Research comparison and **proposals**, not accepted mechanics.
Read [REPORT](REPORT.md) for evidence meanings and limits. Recurrence below
means multiple consulted cases, not a statistical survey of all fiction.
Each source card retains its own continuity, claim and uncertainty.

| P4 | Innate eligibility and practiced expression coexist. A family method can depend on anatomy without encoding its lesson. | P6 | Aging, remaining lifespan, appearance, regeneration and survival are distinct. [Frieren](SRC_frieren.md), [RI](SRC_reverend-insanity.md), [Demon Slayer](SRC_demon-slayer.md), [Black Clover](SRC_black-clover.md), [D&D](SRC_dungeons-dragons-2024-revised-fifth-edition.md). | AG1, AG2, AG4, AG6 | Default: preserve the setting's actual life curve and specific expenditures. | P7 | Social pedigree can outlive a faculty, and a political purity claim can exceed its biological evidence. Dial: a setting can have a real concentration mechanism. Reason: records, marriage strategy and physical inheritance use different evidence. Investigation, concealment and recognition have costs through real actors and institutions. |
| P8 | Bodies become economic targets through specific usable parts or procedures. [HxH](SRC_hunter-x-hunter.md), [Elden Ring](SRC_elden-ring.md), [FMA](SRC_fullmetal-alchemist.md). | CL8, CN4, BM4 | Default: name what the buyer can actually use and how they acquire it. Dial: an intact organ, ingredient, blood sample or cosmetic trophy can have different markets. Reason: possession is not integration. Prices, extraction and compatibility come from the particular world, without making every rare person universally hunted. |

Mapping gaps: ordinary transmission, a congenital magical organ, engineered
line founding and non-ancestral vessel replacement do not always fit a numbered
BD/BP/CN/BM row. Cards use a **family-level mapping** with that gap named,
rather than repurposing BD1, CN8 or BP9 inaccurately. A later integrating owner
could add specific codes after reconciling B1–B5. B4 assigns no competing numbers.

There is little qualified dimorphism evidence in this corpus. Do not fabricate
SG11 stat differences from clothing, sexed language, an all-girls film
delegation or a single strong individual. The existing world/species/body dial
remains available; absence of source proof is not a design prohibition.
