# G30 extension: bloodline as one prestige carrier

2026-10-14. **Unselected proposal.** PROJECT.md owns whether this becomes the
first milestone's one prestige layer. [AUDIT](AUDIT.md) records current support;
[ALTERNATIVES](ALTERNATIVES.md) compares other carriers and references. These
G30 subcodes extend the genetics palette without replacing its existing codes.

## G30.01 The candidate loop and its limits

Develop a faculty during a life, establish what can really transmit, leave
usable records and material support, then continue as an eligible successor.
The heir begins with some useful inherited means and a different personal
learning problem. The predecessor can end mid-story and still leave what was
actually earned. Finishing a book's climax is not required to transmit a faculty.

**Default:** one selected realm, one local lineage faculty with two applications,
a BP3 fragment and a funded BP8 trial as a finite comparison slice. These are
proof coverage suggestions, not slot limits. No general breeding simulator,
afterlife hub, branch atlas, multi-world progression or extra currency is needed.
The existing estate remains an independent source of inherited things.

**Dial:** several faculties, full inherited power, randomized expression,
adoption rite, school succession, deliberate retirement, same-soul return or no
heir. **Reason and cost:** local means decide what exists, who receives it and
which work/stock/access makes it possible. A world can grant a powerful birth
faculty outright; no compulsory curse or invented balancing tax is added.

Succession chooses the next person and settles carriers. Prestige is the
selected reset/retention policy for the life-scale loop. They may coincide in
this candidate, but ordinary estate succession can occur without prestige,
and prestige can choose another supported carrier. Travel keeps a living
person; ascension changes their progression/world layer. Neither creates heirs.
Existing field/structured renewal must be reconciled explicitly if G30 is
selected, so it does not silently become a second milestone layer.

## G30.02 What survives, how much, and how it is earned

For categorical effects such as a blood key, “partial” does not mean half a
door opens. The heir either satisfies that key's requirements or needs another
route. For numeric functions, choose an authored inherited baseline plus the
heir's own development. Do not apply a single 50% multiplier to unrelated
traits, curses, memories, titles and gear.

## G30.03 Transmission happens before or at a declared event

**Default:** birth-related faculties snapshot at the authored conception/birth
event. Later parental training is somatic/personal until a real transmission
route updates the heir. An adult heir already has a body, practice and history;
selecting them does not rebuild it from the dying parent's final sheet.

**Dial:** gestational updates (GN4), a later BD5 kinship rite, direct blood
transfer, a teaching interval, a transmitted ancestral will, germline editing
before later children, or a source whose lineage updates all members. **Reason
and cost:** record the actual operation, affected recipients and timing. A
father/mother's C40 reached after a child's birth does not retroactively set
that child's C32 unless the local mechanism permits and performs an update.

Do not generate a matching descendant out of nowhere at death. A supported
start may author an existing family background, but labels its history as
authored rather than player-earned. Candidate heirs, relationships, expression
and generated facts freeze at their real creation event. If a later child or
rite is allowed, it creates a new event with its own means and outcome. Reload,
preview and import do not reroll the old event. Infertility, disinterest in
family play or no surviving child leaves school/adoption/soul/fresh-life routes.

## G30.04 Earning legacy without rewarding a death button

**Default:** productive practice, research, teaching, service and preserving a
working transmission each change a named asset. They do not also mint an
unexplained lifetime score. Continuing a good life can improve the next life by
leaving more usable means; dying immediately changes none of those means.

**Dial:** a genuine sacrificial awakening, death-bound pact or prestige currency
if a selected cosmology supplies it. Its benefit and cost must have that cause.
Repeat value can be steady, rising or diminishing by local law. Deduplicate an
occurrence receipt, not all future occurrences of that action.

The C40 to C32 example with no further development would yield C25 on the next
equivalent transmission, not automatic exponential growth. A child who develops
their transmissible C32 to C50 before a later qualifying event could transmit
C40. Neither sequence promises balance: a renewable ritual or immediate
sacrifice loop can change the result and needs its own disclosed accounting.
There is no reward simply for growing the family tree or counting deaths.

## G30.05 What the heir sees at start

Target for a later UI implementation: **1366×768 desktop/laptop**, independent
useful panels and comfortable text. Mobile optimization is deferred. This is
a proposed visible outcome, with no browser evidence claimed.

| Start surface | Known content | Useful opening action |
|---|---|---|
| Identity and daily means | Own name, body, age stage, location, own supplies, available inherited function and observed limits | Use resonance to inspect a familiar instrument; ordinary repair/service also remains available. |
| What reached you | Delivered possessions and papers; who taught what; recognized rights; known unresolved obligations; where trial stock waits | Study the inherited tuning note, visit the registrar, or work for supplies. |
| Family/past-life account | An attributed account of predecessor and known BP3 impressions; uncertain claims clearly marked | Ask an actual witness or test the fragment in safe practice. |
| Next worthwhile work | A short funded practice queue and the known BP8 lead, with requirements/costs and pause conditions | Queue regulation/study or inspect trial eligibility. |

The start should have a useful inherited difference, not a screen of locked
icons. A dormant faculty can instead give a reachable awakening route plus
other immediately useful inherited assets. Current code's placeholder “The
heir” and simple kin/apprentice label are not enough for a true existing-person
start; selected content must bind that identity to its actual history.

Predecessor preview and successor receipt distinguish “delivered”, “awaiting
claim”, “known unresolved” and “not carried”. Unknown truth, hidden parentage,
sealed curse causes and trial answers stay in protected content. UI uses known
evidence and discovered IDs, not the proposed author's complete save record.
The player may remember more than the heir, but that does not populate actor
knowledge or grant an executable technique.

## G30.07 Idle pacing

**Default:** routines use supported played-time scheduling: regulation, practice,
copying notes, teaching and preparing trial stock can be queued with real inputs.
Pause at missing supplies, meaningful new discoveries or required commitments.
No repeated click drill, reproductive micromanagement or mandatory wait through
childhood. An already adult heir is valid; a narrated upbringing must state
which schooling/resources/history were authored or actually funded.

An initial tuning comparison could take two ten-minute practice jobs plus a
five-minute safe demonstration. These are illustrative **played minutes**, not
real calendar years or measured balance targets. Birth/development, personal
age, local calendar, training duration and wall-time stay distinct. Any time
compression must advance/reconcile affected people and commitments coherently;
speeding training does not silently speed gestation or replenish the estate.

**Dial:** longer life curricula, childhood play, safe time skip, genuine offline
practice, background school work or a harsh lived-time world. **Reason and cost:**
actual teacher attention, materials, fuel and local clock policy. Existing
narrow offline packing does not authorize offline aging, conception, death,
trial completion or automatic succession. Absent-world freeze/no aging while
closed direction stays intact unless a selected supported policy changes it.

Before a chosen succession boundary, finish/cancel/settle jobs and borrowed
means under their real owners. Do not duplicate paid work, inherit reserved
stock as free estate goods, or let a deceased teacher continue a queued lesson.
Lethal trials wait for a disclosed commitment by default; safe practice can
continue under a prepared policy. Worlds may support different risk policies.

## G30.09 Save shape, sole owners and compatibility

owner `lineage_legacy` for transmission evidence and succession plans. Active
body state belongs to the selected body owner (coordinate B2), active faculties
to existing source/faculty owners, items to inventory/custody, and living world
state to its actual owners. The sidecar links them and owns hereditary bindings;
it does not keep second writable copies of inventories or mastery.

selected authoritative owners, linked by additional carrier receipts in the
offer. Neutral references above are illustrative; exact native actor mappings
and cross-validation must be selected before implementation. Current reusable
`player` is not a durable life ID, so historical actors need stable identity
even when only one protagonist is controlled.

This is a save proposal, not proof of migration, crash recovery, retained-world
