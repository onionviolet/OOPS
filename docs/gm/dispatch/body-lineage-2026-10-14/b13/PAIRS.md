# B13 pairs as investigations

2026-10-14. **Original proposals, docs only.** Extends [G57](../../../../GENETICS_CONSTITUTIONS_AND_BROOD_PLAY.md#g57-subtle-look-alike-pairs), without reserving new G codes or selecting a build. [E7](../../../../emergent/E7_OBSERVATION_AND_MYSTERY.md) owns observation kinds; [means](../../../../MEANS_AND_APPRAISAL.md) owns who can read. Cross-links use LA/BC IDs as public palette codes, not live secret answers.

Default: a small encounter produces an attributed observation, an optional comparison and a useful decision before certainty. Dial: background texture, an exact instrument, a long investigation or a central world mystery. Reason and cost come from the actual protocol, person and world: time, access, equipment, a favor, payment, exposure or none. Costs below are possible consequences of choices, not compulsory penalties for possessing a trait. These game protocols are authoring sketches, not real clinical guidance.

An observation establishes what happened under its recorded conditions. A diagnosis proposes a cause. A useful action can be justified with less evidence than ancestry, mechanism or criminal intent. Keep mixed explanations reachable: strength plus technique, disease plus poison, hereditary faculty plus taught gesture. An inadequate test returns inconclusive, not the opposite answer. Actual cause belongs to the world/body owner before testing in E7 K1 cases.

## LA1: strength and leverage/technique

- **Evidence:** an impressive lift or throw; the witness records load, handle, footing and motion, not “innate strength.” BC4 tool wear can suggest a practiced grip.
- **Separating test:** a master or load-measuring apparatus compares supported tasks with different grips/angles and the same declared load. Broad performance supports general capacity; a large angle-specific advantage supports technique. Body reach, injury, equipment and practice remain competing causes. Alternative: ask the maker to explain and demonstrate the familiar tool's geometry.
- **Wrong read and cost:** a recruiter calls the subject strong from one favorable throw and assigns an awkward carrying job. The real job takes longer or needs assistance; the buyer loses time, not an invented permanent strength stat. Dismissing a strong technician wastes suitable training too.
- **Play:** keep the technique, commission a fitting handle, or take broader training. Default one local work comparison; Dial a martial exam or precise biomechanical scan. ES2/ES3; DT2/DT4.

## LA2: endurance and pain tolerance

- **Evidence:** the person reports little pain and continues work. Log completed duration and visible performance separately from their report; neither reads internal tissue state.
- **Separating test:** a doctor observes a bounded existing work/recovery session and uses whatever injury-reading means they hold. “Feels fine” beside worsening supported condition separates warning response from capacity. Prior work and recovery records offer a second route. Do not stage an injury to manufacture a clue. Both high endurance and high tolerance can be present.
- **Wrong read and cost:** a foreman believes silence means readiness and extends a shift. If the existing injury resolver supports deterioration, a condition worsens; otherwise the consequence is delayed care, unfinished work or a replacement-worker expense. Calling every quiet worker injured can exclude a capable person.
- **Play:** rest, use a fitting work plan or seek care without settling the entire constitution. Default monitoring; Dial a harsh expedition or exact body reading. ES2/ES5; DT1/DT4.

## LA3: talent and early practice

- **Evidence:** the newcomer performs well at a first public lesson, but it is only their first witnessed lesson. Childhood tools, a teacher's account and dated exercises can reveal prior practice.
- **Separating test:** compare the same supported learning tasks over recorded instruction and starting mastery, or authenticate earlier lessons. Convergence after comparable practice weakens a broad innate-talent claim; it does not prove no aptitude exists. Changed teachers, access, motivation and fatigue limit comparison. A specialist may directly measure one faculty if that world's means supports it.
- **Wrong read and cost:** a school gives a rare scholarship on a mistaken label or withholds ordinary instruction because “talent will carry them.” Poor method fit wastes tuition and training time. Accusing a practiced learner of fraud can damage trust without disproving their competence.
- **Play:** recognize the real mastery and choose better instruction; records can revise the label without removing skill. Default a teaching conversation; Dial multi-season cohort study. ES3/ES4; DT2/DT6.

## LA4: bloodline trait and learned family mannerism

- **Evidence:** a gesture, accent or stance appears among household members. An adopted child may share it; an estranged biological relative may not. That pattern concerns learning, not a verdict on belonging.
- **Separating test:** ask an actual teacher to teach the behavior to a willing outsider, and inspect household/lesson records. Test the claimed faculty independently through an authenticated bloodline reader or supported functional trial. A taught gesture may accompany a real faculty; adoption rites can create real kinship under BD5, distinct from birth ancestry.
- **Wrong read and cost:** a gatekeeper accepts mimicry as authority and grants access, or excludes a genuine heir who never learned the gesture. Correcting the gesture claim does not settle legal succession or take back a completed transfer automatically.
- **Play:** learn the family method, establish ancestry by GN8, or pursue ritual/legal recognition. Default a mistaken greeting; Dial a clan archive dispute. ES3/ES4/ES7; DT2/DT6/DT4.

## LA5: constitution and habit/diet

- **Evidence:** repeated warmth, recovery or productive sessions coincide with a routine, meal or substance. BC1 and BC7 open an enquiry, not a congenital label.
- **Separating test:** compare dated observations across a naturally interrupted routine or a willing, reversible supported change. Record exposure, time and other treatments. Fading favors a maintained effect; persistence may be an enduring acquired constitution, stored fuel, long-lived substance or congenital faculty. A specialist who can read the carrier provides another route. CN5/CN6 can genuinely build a constitution, so this is not a binary “real versus fake” test.
- **Wrong read and cost:** a buyer pays for a hereditary cure when the useful effect depends on daily preparation, or stops a working regimen after calling it mere habit. The cost is misplaced payment or lost benefit where the mechanism supports it.
- **Play:** preserve a useful routine while commissioning a narrower reading. Default compare a condition log; Dial controlled long-term study. ES2/ES3/ES5; DT1/DT2/DT4.

## LA6: illness and poison

- **Evidence:** several people feel unwell after a shared occasion. Record who ate, handled or shared what, onset windows and who remained well. Neither a shared meal nor a lone victim identifies an assailant.
- **Separating test:** a doctor compares exposure and contact histories; a qualified herbalist/alchemist assays a retained sample with custody and a control. A disease-reading means can test a supported infection cause. A negative assay only excludes substances within that assay's scope at that time. Spoiled food, sensitivity, mixed causes and missed exposure remain possible.
- **Wrong read and cost:** “contagious” can close a clinic or job unnecessarily; “poisoned” can provoke an accusation, wasted antidote purchase or missed disease response. Deliberate poisoning needs evidence of action and intent beyond identifying a substance.
- **Play:** arrange care, preserve evidence, compare accounts, hire a tester or withhold accusation. Default one recoverable local meal enquiry; Dial a crime arc with world-specific severe stakes. ES2/ES3/ES4/ES5/ES6; DT1/DT3/DT5.

## LA7: injury limp and fatigue/old habit

- **Evidence:** an uneven gait occurs after a shift or on one surface. A single observation lacks baseline, load and footwear context.
- **Separating test:** a doctor compares supported movement before/after rest and across fitting surfaces, with the subject's old history. A stable pattern alone proves neither injury nor habit; healed injuries can leave learned motion and fatigue can aggravate an old condition. Use an actual structural reading if available. A familiar companion's dated account is an alternate baseline.
- **Wrong read and cost:** a clerk marks permanent incapacity from a temporary tired walk, reducing a work offer; or a coach dismisses a real injury as a habit and assigns unsuitable drills. Costs follow that employer or supported condition, not a universal disability penalty.
- **Play:** change the task, rest, fit an aid or seek care; cure is an option, not required for agency. Default a return-to-work encounter; Dial rehabilitation or disguise. ES2/ES3/ES5; DT1/DT2/DT7.

## LA8: calm and numbness/suppression

- **Evidence:** a steady voice and little visible reaction. The subject may report calm, absence of feeling, deliberate control or decline to explain. Their report remains attributed.
- **Separating test:** a willing conversation across ordinary situations, known treatment history or a scoped mind/energy reading can distinguish some mechanisms. A real trigger may expose a difference when encountered naturally; do not automatically provoke it as an “optimal test.” One reaction does not settle a person's whole inner life. Suppression can be an effective learned means and coexist with composure.
- **Wrong read and cost:** a leader assigns emotional strain assuming effortless calm, or calls a controlled person dishonest. Lost trust, an interrupted task or an existing trigger response can follow. No automatic trauma, lie or hostile intent tag.
- **Play:** negotiate task boundaries, seek a fitting adviser, retain useful composure or investigate privately. Default conversation; Dial supported mind-reading with notice/counterplay. ES4/ES5; DT1/DT2/DT3.

## LA9: luck and a hidden constitution

- **Evidence:** striking favorable outcomes. Record all eligible trials, failures, opportunities and environmental causes, rather than collecting successes alone.
- **Separating test:** a scholar follows repeated supported comparable trials with a declared baseline and stopping plan. Persistent excess supports “unusual outcomes under these conditions,” not proof of a particular constitution. Skill, selection, a protective actor or deceptive reports may explain it. A scoped faculty reader or qualified discovery route can establish mechanism. A null series may miss a conditional faculty; it does not prove absence.
- **Wrong read and cost:** a merchant lends on assumed invulnerability and loses actual stock when protection does not apply; a skeptic ignores a supported advantage and misses useful preparation. Testing spends real time/material where necessary, without endlessly minting rewards.
- **Play:** take a cautious useful opportunity, hire a reader or keep a trial journal. Default a few recorded incidents with uncertainty; Dial long research or exact fate sight. ES3/ES4/ES7; DT3/DT4/DT6.

## LA10: aptitude and interest

- **Evidence:** someone practices often and improves quickly at a favored subject. Attendance, current mastery and enthusiasm are different observations.
- **Separating test:** a teacher compares supported learning with prior practice, method fit, access and chosen effort recorded. Ask the subject what they enjoy rather than deriving joy from performance. A matched unfamiliar task can help; plateau or boredom alone identifies no innate ceiling. Direct aptitude tests read only their authored faculty/schema.
- **Wrong read and cost:** a master assigns a career the person dislikes, or calls disinterest incapacity and denies instruction. Wasted tuition, slower volunteered practice or a refused job follows actual behavior; no automatic personality rewrite.
- **Play:** sample another teacher, pursue the interest, use a fitting method or retain competence for practical work. Default a lesson choice; Dial a school placement dispute. ES3/ES4/ES5; DT2/DT4.

## LA11: beauty and charisma/presence

- **Evidence:** people respond favorably in one meeting. Record audience, spoken delivery, appearance, familiarity and any sensed effect separately.
- **Separating test:** compare supported interactions by letter, voice or dim light, using willing recipients and actual communication. Continued influence without visual appearance supports a nonvisual component; voice, reputation, writing skill and a supernatural aura remain distinct. A presence reader can test a supported channel. Changed audience is a confound, not a clean comparison.
- **Wrong read and cost:** a patron pays for cosmetics when they need communication practice, or a guard strips a disguise expecting influence to vanish. Failed persuasion, wasted payment or changed suspicion follows that encounter. Appearance and presence can both matter.
- **Play:** choose clothing, rhetoric, a letter or a supported aura counter. Default a meeting/letter comparison; Dial a masked diplomatic event. ES3/ES4/ES6; DT2/DT3/DT7.

## LA12: family resemblance and coincidence/another family

- **Evidence:** matching face, voice, mark or mannerism; a family story names a possible relation. BC4/BC5 and MH features provide leads, not paternity or inheritance.
- **Separating test:** authenticate independent GN8 records, actual sample custody or a world-qualified kin reader with reference coverage. A genome assay cannot diagnose a soul pact merely from its name; kin sense may read ritual kinship rather than reproductive descent. Compare the instrument's specified relation, not “family” in general. A reliable record route can work without a scanner.
- **Wrong read and cost:** a claimant discloses private records to the wrong family, a court misassigns an estate, or a stranger expects trust never earned. A corrected diagnosis changes a claim; recovering property needs legal/social means. Disproving birth ancestry does not erase adopted belonging.
- **Play:** ask privately, obtain a scoped certificate, challenge a claim or decline the enquiry. Default resemblance conversation; Dial contested succession. ES4/ES6/ES7; DT4/DT6/DT7.

Every pair's test must produce a recorded result and a declared next action. [EVENT_SHAPES](EVENT_SHAPES.md) supplies shared card families; [DIAGNOSTICIANS](DIAGNOSTICIANS.md) supplies means. A selected builder needs actual resolvers for tests and consequences, not just diagnostic prose.
