# B13 breadcrumbs before diagnosis

2026-10-14. **Original proposals, docs only.** Extends [G58](../../../../GENETICS_CONSTITUTIONS_AND_BROOD_PLAY.md#g58-small-tells-and-breadcrumbs). BC codes name public content families. They do not label a live secret's answer or guarantee a secret exists.

Default: show a tell only when a present observer's sense or an arrived report detects it. Dial: quiet texture, recurring observation, an exact scan or a substantial investigation. Reason/cost: opportunity to witness, time, comparison access and chosen disclosure. A familiar observer can notice change more easily without gaining every body fact. A copied rumor is one source chain, however many people repeat it.

## BC1: warmth and few remembered illnesses

**When:** touch during care, a fitting thermal sense, or a parent's dated account over actual contact windows. “Never sick” is a witness claim with finite coverage, not an omniscient lifetime fact. **Who:** parent/carer, doctor, old master; a stranger can notice warmth now.

**Can prove:** observed warmth in those conditions, or that the witness recalls few illnesses. **Cannot prove:** congenital constitution, immunity to all disease, a faculty's name or future safety. Room temperature, clothing, routine and a temporary effect compete. Compare baseline and supported conditions through LA5, or commission a scoped reading. Default one care moment; Dial a longitudinal awakening route. Cost: chosen study/service, not forced awakening. ES1/ES2/ES7.

## BC2: animals calm or wary

**When:** a real animal reacts on approach, with its handler, distance, wind, food, familiarity and recent fright recorded. **Who:** stable hands, hunters, pets with their own sensing means. A pet's reaction reaches the player through observed behavior or a supported bond, not automatically translated thoughts.

**Can prove:** this animal's response to this encounter. **Cannot prove:** universal animal affinity, blood ancestry, evil intent or species. Odor, training, confidence, feeding and aura can coexist. A handler compares willing familiar/unfamiliar contacts while a fitting reader tests the proposed faculty separately. Default a mount-handling choice; Dial line-bond enquiry. Cost: handler time, access or none. LA11/LA12; ES1/ES3/ES6.

## BC3: plants lean or wilt nearby

**When:** a gardener notices an actual change during tending; save its onset and exposure instead of retroactively inventing weeks of damage. **Who:** gardener, herbalist, a plant actor with a supported sense and communication route.

**Can prove:** a recorded plant response and, with a suitable comparison, association with the sampled exposure. **Cannot prove:** curse, affinity, culpable intention or every species' response. Water, light, soil, carried substances and delayed effects remain alternatives. Compare supported pots/plots with matching care, or use a qualified environmental reader. Default a tending lead; Dial curse/affinity discovery. Cost: time/materials for a real test; no fake crop simulator. LA5/LA6; ES1/ES3/ES7.

## BC4: ink, calluses and tan lines

**When:** hands or uncovered skin are visible at work, a fitting touch inspection, or an attributed account. **Who:** observant people, practiced player, craftsperson; the sense's range and light matter.

**Can prove:** visible stain, wear or exposure pattern. **Cannot prove:** a profession, mastery, current employment, identity or a genetic trait. Borrowed tools, disguise, former work and recreation can explain it. Ask about routines or observe the supported work; match wear to actual tool use rather than a profession label. Default a conversation opening; Dial impersonation or trade-history enquiry. Cost: time/access, with suspicion only if that observer notices intrusive inspection. LA1/LA3/LA4; ES1/ES4/ES6.

## BC5: ring mark on a bare finger

**When:** a hand is exposed after recent removal, or a witness reports seeing the mark. **Who:** spouse, investigator, attentive companion or player.

**Can prove:** a band-shaped mark, perhaps prior wear under a qualified physical reading. **Cannot prove:** marriage, fidelity, oath, rank, current spouse or deception. Jewelry, work gear and old commitments provide alternatives. Ask the wearer or authenticate relevant vow/registry records with their actual access; a hidden oath needs its own means. Default private conversation; Dial legal or oath enquiry. Cost: records/service or disclosure risk, not an automatic relationship penalty. LA12; ES1/ES4/ES6.

## BC6: herb, oil or smoke scent

**When:** a usable scent trace persists and the observer is within their actual range; wind, rain, washing, masking and contamination are part of coverage. **Who:** beastkin with a real smell faculty, hound, herbalist, nearby neighbor. A species label alone does not supply diagnostic resolution.

**Can prove:** detected odor class at the sampled time/place; calibrated reference comparison can narrow it. **Cannot prove:** workplace, precise route, substance dose, ownership, biological kinship or guilt. Shared clothes, ambient smoke or transferred oil can carry it. Compare retained references and actual custody/contact, then ask or follow a supported trace. Default an activity lead; Dial forensic tracking. Cost: reference/access/time or none. LA6/LA12; ES1/ES3/ES6.

## BC7: unexpectedly fast healing

**When:** a medic or familiar observer compares a real wound's dated state before and after care. A rival can notice a visible change but cannot read private treatment automatically. **Who:** medic, companion, rival, the subject observing their own body.

**Can prove:** observed recovery within that interval and treatment context. **Cannot prove:** autonomous regeneration, a hereditary carrier, unlimited regrowth or no future injury risk. Earlier injury estimate, hidden treatment, temporary faculty and altered body all compete. Compare treatment records or commission a carrier-specific read; do not injure someone to force a trial. Default a follow-up care note; Dial a regeneration enquiry. Cost: chosen service or observation time. LA2/LA5; ES2/ES4/ES7.

## BC8: dreams of unfamiliar places

**When:** the dreamer recalls a dream; whether a dream really has a source belongs to its world/temporal owner. Freeze remembered details before comparing them to a later account. **Who:** dreamer first; a seer, confidant or memory-reader learns only through actual disclosure/perception.

**Can prove:** the dreamer's recorded recollection and subsequent matches within a stated scope. **Cannot prove:** ancestry, prior life, prophecy, exact geography or another person's identity. Forgotten stories, suggestion, coincidence and deceptive channels remain alternatives. A sealed route may verify a limited independently recorded detail without showing its truth in this packet. Default optional journal/conversation; Dial supported dream sight. Cost: travel, archive access or a seer's service; an exact free faculty is allowed. ES4/ES7/ES8.

## BC9: déjà vu at a site

**When:** the character experiences familiarity at an actually reached site, or recalls a dated prior visit; the event does not establish a hidden teleport or history. **Who:** experiencer; seer only after being told or through a held reading means.

**Can prove:** reported familiarity, and any separately verified matches or supported local response. **Cannot prove:** a soul/bloodline connection, an earlier visit, site ownership or access to an inheritance trial. Similar architecture, suggestion, forgotten travel and a real faculty compete. Compare travel records and independently retained observations; a qualified site/kin/soul channel can establish its own narrow link. Default a revisit question; Dial a world-qualified discovery route. Cost: travel/test/access, no forced pilgrimage. ES4/ES7/ES8.

## Delivery and evidence limits

The feed says “you noticed” only for direct observation. A delivered report says who reported it and when; a hound handler reports an interpretation, not the hound's private certainty. NPC-only sightings stay in that observer's knowledge until a real channel arrives. The same BC can lead to several questions or ordinary explanations; do not raise a player's clue counter merely because an author knows a secret exists.

A missed tell may recur when another real exposure happens. A discarded sample or departed witness can leave an unresolved case; a replacement route needs actual world support. Default a known reminder or later follow-up; Dial long-lived memory/archives. Pin evidence needed by an active enquiry before feed compaction. BC8/BC9 use secret IDs/routes/status only in agent reports, with no reserved live IDs or authored hidden answers here.
