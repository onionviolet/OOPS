# B13 diagnosticians and the means they hold

2026-10-14. **Original proposals, docs only.** [Means/appraisal](../../../../MEANS_AND_APPRAISAL.md) owns MN/AP/LS/AA; [G15/G24](../../../../GENETICS_CONSTITUTIONS_AND_BROOD_PLAY.md) separates body truth from diagnoses and recognition. This packet assigns no new power to an existing named NPC. Doctors, masters, seers, scanners and beastkin are role candidates, not mandatory ranks or an implemented cast.

## Means palette

Default: a specialist can answer one fitting question. Dial: novices, narrow experts, cross-channel teams, self-diagnosis, cheap renewable services or exact exceptional sight. Reason/cost: actual study, instruments, contacts, consent/access, time, fuel or a service arrangement. A title does not grant the means; a capable individual may perform a facility's function without owning a building.

AP levels are illustrative and domain-specific. DT1 can read a condition at AP6 while knowing no combat techniques; DT5 can have extraordinary scent resolution without AP3 level numbers. Precision, scope, coverage and confidence are separate. A successful exact means gives the answer it can truly provide; do not manufacture a universal failure chance or compulsory drawback.

## Diagnosis is a sequence of acts

| ID | Act and outcome | Player and NPC means symmetry |
|---|---|---|
| DX1 | Form a question from known evidence: what happened, what function is present, what carrier explains it, or what recognition is warranted | An NPC who only sees a limp can suspect; they cannot know a private diagnosis because an author needs a pursuer |
| DX2 | Quote a scoped service with known costs, time, access and disclosure; ask, examine, borrow a tool or seek another specialist | A rival needs the same actual sample, operator, reach and contact. An expert's willingness is separate from their capacity |
| DX3 | Run the supported protocol and record sampled context, means, coverage, control and result | Refusal, unavailable sample, blocked read, schema mismatch, out-of-range, negative and inconclusive are distinct outcomes |
| DX4 | Interpret or challenge: compare independent channels and retain conflicting claims | Disagreement can be honest, stale, biased or forged. Detecting an error alone does not establish motive |
| DX5 | Choose care, instruction, concealment, disclosure, recognition or no intervention; later observation can revise the reading | Knowledge, communication and intervention produce separate receipts. A certificate may persuade a court even when anatomy remains unknown |

A free decline should leave ordinary play available unless a selected world's real situation imposes a constraint. Default nonblocking enquiries; Dial an authored emergency with explicit time/commitment policy. No universal “test once,” mandatory invasive examination or hidden autospending. Repeats sample a new supported occurrence or improve coverage; retrying one receipt does not create another discovery, fee or reward.

## What the observer knows versus what the player sees

Use four separately scoped concepts: actual body/world state; raw observations; attributed interpretations; current observer belief. Legal/social recognition is a fifth consumer, not proof of body state. Bind subject and observer to actual world/instance/continuity and occurrence. A familiar name alone cannot join two counterparts.

SHOWN/SOURCE: [simulationFacts](../../../../../src/simulation_effects.js:131) exposes internal target body/condition/knowledge to execution predicates. Its [observed person facts](../../../../../src/simulation_effects.js:157) are separately projected. This is not a license to display internal target facts as diagnosis text. Render only the viewer's admitted evidence. A specialist's interpretation can be recorded as testimony without the viewer acquiring the specialist's faculty.

SHOWN/SOURCE: [knowledge writes](../../../../../src/simulation_effects.js:281) store only `stage`, `value`, `at`, `cause` and optional `basis`. The stages are `seen`, `interpreted`, `understood`; equal/higher stage replaces the same key. These are learning depth, not certainty or evidence validity. A confident mistaken expert can still produce an `interpreted` account. A correction must not downgrade an unrelated understood technique.

Current [basis validation](../../../../../src/causal_situations.js:52) supports `recollection`, `observation`, `instruction`, `inference` and requires an attributed person and actual situation. It has no arbitrary `confidence`, `protocol` or `evidenceRefs` fields contract. Proposed richer records reuse [P24 information / P14 claims](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p24/INFORMATION.md), not a new B13 truth registry. Small slice alternative: separate stable keys for distinct observations/readings/corrections and a current summary, with structured meaning in the owning adapter. State that bounded choice in the builder's compatibility record.

## People and reporting

SHOWN/SOURCE: [knownPeopleView](../../../../../src/people_knowledge.js:6) requires witnessed introductions, then [creates rows](../../../../../src/people_knowledge.js:12) with details and memories. [Craftsman feedback](../../../../../src/people_knowledge.js:29) is a narrow body-feedback precedent; it does not diagnose every target. [Provider merge](../../../../../src/people_knowledge.js:81) merges by person ID, and [book contact rows](../../../../../src/people_knowledge.js:97) use attributed received knowledge. A B13 adapter must explicitly project introduced subjects and received diagnoses; inserting a knowledge key alone does not create a generic People row.

Proposed row: known name/alias; last met/reported place and date; observation; “DT1's report: possible cause”; scope/coverage; correction beside old reading; known test/contact actions. Stranger detail stays a described impression until introduction. Subject refuses a reading? Show the refusal only if witnessed, without a private reason. A rival's knowledge appears through witnessed changed behavior or an arrived account, unless the player holds a means to inspect their belief.

At desktop 1366x768, preserve the existing People/Notebook and feed surfaces: investigation responses in the content panel, known people evidence alongside it, recent notices in the feed. Detailed dated comparisons can scroll in their reading region. This is a presentation proposal, unrendered and unverified. Mobile optimization, a new standalone diagnostic panel and broad NPC body simulation are deferred.
