## Ownership and control

The proposed [P23 issue-scoped interpretation](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p23/INNER_LIFE.md#m1a-experience-proposes-an-interpretation-the-player-chooses-a-commitment)
holds stance, confidence, importance, known causes and endorsement. [P23
ambitions](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p23/AMBITIONS.md) holds chosen aims as refs to
real goal predicates; [P23 story progression](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p23/STORY_PROGRESSION.md#s1a-a-breakthrough-needs-a-real-moment-and-a-real-rule)
requires an actual source rule before insight grants power. Neither duplicates
skills, sources or world truth. [B2 physical/person](../b2/SPEC.md#g24-b22-persisted-shape)
and [B7 truth/observer](../b7/CONSISTENCY.md#g48-b7c5-observer-claims-remain-outside-generation-truth)
supply known changes and person/body identity, not automatic feelings.
[P19 contact and directed social episodes](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p19/CONTRACT.md#c1-identity-and-ownership-before-social-writes)
holds public response and support. [P24 knowledge](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p24/INFORMATION.md#o22-claims-and-corrections-remain-attributed)
and [time](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p24/TIME_STRUCTURES.md#q0-use-the-actual-clock-domain)
hold received evidence and clock bindings.

Default is texture or an optional suggested interpretation. The player can
endorse, revise, dismiss or postpone without a hidden penalty. Endorsement alone
does not buy treatment, adopt a vow, disclose a belief or change control. An
NPC can hold its own private interpretation; player-facing text needs disclosure
or a supported sense. A selected automatic-drift/controller-conflict premise
uses the live identity owner's policy, not a B14 timeout trick.
