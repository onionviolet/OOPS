## Causal admission and retention

```text
body operation receipt
  -> actual changed capacity / local input / exposure
  -> affected actor or site through its real means
  -> supported job outcome or delivered account
  -> separately attributed social interpretation
  -> funded action / rule ratification / optional reflection
```

For each arrow retain writer, target, scope, time, mechanism and actual result.
A storylet can organize those references but cannot substitute for a missing
resolver. No-affected-neighbor and no-delivery controls must remain unchanged.
Correcting a rumor changes beliefs or future responses, not already spent stock.
A power that removes the real cause can stop its downstream consequence.
Repeated distinct meaningful actions remain possible; replay of one cause is
idempotent. Reserve closure state before committing dependencies; coalesce
routine text, not outstanding obligations, sampled inputs or custody.

For SC3, freeze parent/template inputs at the real creation epoch under B7.
A parent's later change does not edit existing children. Guardianship/adoption,
biological descent, office and property are different links. Family succession
uses existing heir/estate authority, not copied affection or invented debt.
No new prestige layer or complete population simulator is required by B14.
