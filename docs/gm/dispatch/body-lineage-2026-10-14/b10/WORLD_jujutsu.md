## Five sourcing variables

These are independent proposed facts/observations, not five compulsory meters. Unmeasured values stay unknown; changing custody or reopening storage does not reset condition.

| Variable | This world’s relevant distinction |
|---|---|
| Quality | Actual object/formula/source and intact bodily carrier; grade alone does not establish effect. |
| Freshness | Carrier integrity/containment history; ordinary tissue decay and persistent object law need separate qualification. |
| Purity | Imprint, energy, obligation and biological contamination differ; purification does not automatically erase a technique. |
| Compatibility | Recipient body/source/target and supported healing; birth restriction is not a chosen installable vow. |
| Provenance | Object holder, creator, occurrence, technique source and selected continuity; no anatomy from a clan surname. |

## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Source a qualified object or supported treatment and keep formula, fuel and access distinct.

**Dial:** Containment courier, paid healer, clan warehouse access or explicitly original collector/body AU.

**In-world reason and cost:** Real means, object custody, appropriate healer/source and exact technique constraints. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Clan authority, hostage risk and warehouse permission differ from body eligibility. Imposed birth exchanges have no retroactive consent; contracts and criminal consequences follow exact parties and local rules.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.

**Resource joins:** [P8 creature sources](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/CREATURE_RESOURCES.md), [harvest](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/HARVEST_YIELDS.md), [logistics](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/LOGISTICS.md), [magical source forms](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/MAGICAL_RESOURCES.md), [trade/import](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/TRADE_SUPPLY.md) and [world boundaries](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/WORLD_VARIATIONS.md). Apply only operators compatible with this world. These P8 proposals are not already implemented genetics.

## Open source/use gate

Ancient-object extraction, source retention after death, technique copying/grafting and generic ambient rates remain unverified. Later Modulo/Simurian sources are not blended into main-series era.

First selected proof must obtain an actual source by its stated route, inspect only what the observer can know, pay the real process cost, and produce a useful changed action or treatment. Compare a compatible/unsupported use without deleting retained stock or exposing hidden truth.
