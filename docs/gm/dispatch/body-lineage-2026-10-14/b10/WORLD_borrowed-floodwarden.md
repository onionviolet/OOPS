## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Commission a compatible replacement vessel with a declared single-subject transfer.

**Dial:** Keep restored body, return to dormant original, remain abroad or prepare an equivalent resonance body.

**In-world reason and cost:** Workshop materials, actual organs, passage/refuge apparatus and reconstruction work. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Restored operating keys and harbor title are separate claims. Empty prepared vessels differ from occupied donor bodies; local courts/keepers retain their own authority and relationships.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
