## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Source field evidence and compatible care, keeping bodily eligibility separate.

**Dial:** Guided field study, paid healing, a specific costly teacher transfer or explicit body-modification AU.

**In-world reason and cost:** Travel, actual observations, caster means and the selected transfer cost. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Association access, village hospitality and individual teacher choices differ. Learning from an observation is distinct from consent to blood donation or a spell transfer.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
