## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Source physical specimens and a scoped diagnosis during a supported window.

**Dial:** Care-supported research, market specimen, source-qualified gene operation or explicit freely-crossing-genes AU.

**In-world reason and cost:** Actual tissue, care time, apparatus and window-related body risk. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Civic work and gene ambitions are separate lives. Caretakers, hunters and diagnostic authorities have their own rights; no generic gene caste or compulsory predation is inferred.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
