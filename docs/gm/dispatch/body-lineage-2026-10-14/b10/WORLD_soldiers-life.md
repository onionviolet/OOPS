| Variable | This world’s relevant distinction |
|---|---|

## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Source ordinary care and useful monster/plant material through a titled expedition.

**Dial:** Paid procurement, local preservation, native nonhuman start or a separately qualified modification.

**In-world reason and cost:** Expedition time, supplies, protection, storage access and actual processor. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Legion duties, independent civilian suppliers and contested creature collection can differ. Cruelty and consent follow selected polity/actors; all are proposals, not a universal military prohibition.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
