## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Procure one useful sample and obtain a scoped diagnosis before any change.

**Dial:** Academy procurement, paid specimen broker, ordinary medicine, source-qualified beast bloodline or explicit original scan AU.

**In-world reason and cost:** Tuition/grant funds, apparatus, sample stock, time and actual procedure specialist. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Specimen legality varies by region in the public owner. Volunteers, prisoner suppliers and corpse lots can produce different institutional/revenge consequences; none proves physical compatibility.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
