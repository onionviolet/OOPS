## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Acquire blood or a named contract source for an eligible use.

**Dial:** Voluntary donor, bought stock, corpse possession, source-qualified heart fusion or explicit recovery AU.

**In-world reason and cost:** Real blood/vitality, exact contract price, eligible participants and source-specific trigger. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Japan’s hunter-only contract/gun access is a snapshot wiki assertion, not real-world legal advice. Donor consent, legal sacrifice eligibility and NPC reaction are distinct; cruelty follows participants and consequences.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
