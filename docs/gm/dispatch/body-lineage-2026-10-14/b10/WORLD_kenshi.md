## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Source a real replacement limb and retrain its useful movement.

**Dial:** Merchant, specialist maker, salvage, paid care or a declared imported working aid.

**In-world reason and cost:** Real parts, fitting, specialist effort and actual local supply. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Faction attitudes, capture/labor markets and clinical consent vary by chosen locality. Do not infer universal status from one religious faction or transplant original Red Basin laws into source canon.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
