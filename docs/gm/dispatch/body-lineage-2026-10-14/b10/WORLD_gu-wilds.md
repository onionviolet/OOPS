## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Acquire a compatible living means and feed it for a declared body use.

**Dial:** Tended Gu, market stock, clan allocation, inherited recipe or explicitly supported aptitude change.

**In-world reason and cost:** Food, essence, skilled recipe execution and actual custody. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Clan assessment, coercive ownership and independent trade are local facts. A removal/refinement method can offer a route around an imposed bodily obstacle at its own cost.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
