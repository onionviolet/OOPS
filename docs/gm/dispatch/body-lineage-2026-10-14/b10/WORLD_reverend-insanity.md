## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Obtain an eligible living Gu and keep operation/food/host separate.

**Dial:** Trade, supported wild collection, refinement, implanted vow removal or source-qualified bodily alteration.

**In-world reason and cost:** Food, activation essence, actual recipe/access and removal means. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Clan trade, coercive implants and voluntary contracts have different actor histories. An implanted enforcer can be removed by the selected counter-method; unclear inner-intent semantics stay unresolved.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
