These are independent proposed facts/observations, not five compulsory meters. Unmeasured values stay unknown; changing custody or reopening storage does not reset condition.

|---|---|
| Quality | Part fit, repairable assembly and clean care input; prestige does not raise a part’s performance. |

## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Source a fitted replacement and actual rehabilitation.

**Dial:** Local maker, salvage, imported working aid, voluntary donation or an explicit new tech route.

**In-world reason and cost:** Water, titled parts, mechanic/medic time, fit and local transport. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Fabrication licences, well-house debts and religious suspicion depend on who knows what. Cruel extraction, voluntary care and smuggled parts have distinct witnessed local consequences.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.

**Resource joins:** [P8 creature sources](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/CREATURE_RESOURCES.md), [harvest](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/HARVEST_YIELDS.md), [logistics](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/LOGISTICS.md), [magical source forms](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/MAGICAL_RESOURCES.md), [trade/import](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/TRADE_SUPPLY.md) and [world boundaries](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/WORLD_VARIATIONS.md).
