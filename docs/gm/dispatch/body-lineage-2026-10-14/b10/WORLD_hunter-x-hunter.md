## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Source real food for the particular queen and generate only a source-compatible original offspring palette.

**Dial:** Independent soldier, cooperative nonhuman-food colony AU, commissioned specimen or separately sourced eye-market route.

**In-world reason and cost:** Real food, care, time and nest; rates/mix rules remain proposed until scenes are qualified. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Predation, coexistence and donor consent belong to the chosen colony/local society. A cooperative colony without human predation is an explicit AU, not retrospective canon.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.

**Resource joins:** [P8 creature sources](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/CREATURE_RESOURCES.md), [harvest](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/HARVEST_YIELDS.md), [logistics](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/LOGISTICS.md), [magical source forms](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/MAGICAL_RESOURCES.md), [trade/import](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/TRADE_SUPPLY.md) and [world boundaries](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/WORLD_VARIATIONS.md).
