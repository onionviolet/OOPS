## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Acquire fitted prosthetic means or source-qualified care with a real specialist.

**Dial:** Biological fusion, soul-bearing source, original non-soul-consuming construct AU or paid mechanical aid.

**In-world reason and cost:** Materials, actual surgeon/maker, recovery and coordination; stored-soul use spends its own source budget. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Military control, independent mechanics and exploited research subjects differ. Willing souls have a specific reported source effect; universal consent mechanics and local legal rules remain separate.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
