## Five sourcing variables

These are independent proposed facts/observations, not five compulsory meters. Unmeasured values stay unknown; changing custody or reopening storage does not reset condition.

| Variable | This world’s relevant distinction |
|---|---|
| Quality | Connector geometry, intact actuator and usable template; item grade does not authenticate a person. |
| Freshness | Living tissue viability, battery charge and mechanical wear differ; parked backups need a declared support law. |
| Purity | Alloy/material contamination and firmware/template tampering; neither is ancestral purity. |
| Compatibility | Body/interface, actual power and control; a new limb still needs coordination unless installed method truly supplies it. |
| Provenance | Wreck/module serial, maker, prior owner, firmware/template revision and licensed or contested custody. |

## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Acquire a titled part and commission a compatible fitted implant.

**Dial:** Salvage, corporate clinic, self-build, licensed/pirated module or actual paid backup.

**In-world reason and cost:** Blueprint, actual construction, power, fitting, maintenance and backup support. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Corporate recall rights, clinic agreements and black-market repairs differ. Paid body ownership is not permission to delete an occupant; empty manufactured vessels and independent AIs have separate states.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
