## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Acquire source-qualified tissue or fitted puppet means and develop actual control.

**Dial:** Voluntary gift, acquired organ, medical service, ordinary puppet construction or explicit AU procedure.

**In-world reason and cost:** Actual organ/material custody, surgeon, energy/strain and practiced coordination. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Village clinic, clan custody and illicit experiments can differ in law, consent and retaliation. A donated organ does not transfer clan membership; no generic refusal of harsh-world routes is imposed.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.

**Resource joins:** [P8 creature sources](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/CREATURE_RESOURCES.md), [harvest](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/HARVEST_YIELDS.md), [logistics](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/LOGISTICS.md), [magical source forms](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/MAGICAL_RESOURCES.md), [trade/import](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/TRADE_SUPPLY.md) and [world boundaries](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/WORLD_VARIATIONS.md).
