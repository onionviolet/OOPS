## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Source a manufactured vessel or an eligible ritual manifestation through actual means.

**Dial:** Artificial-worker life, summoned perspective, separately sourced catalyst procurement or explicit vessel AU.

**In-world reason and cost:** Maker capability, apparatus, energy and actual ritual/authority terms. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Creator purpose is not proof of absent will. Artificial workers, Masters and independent Servants have different local obligations; consent and compulsion keep exact party-specific rules.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
