## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Commission finite care and preserve a scoped sample for analysis.

**Dial:** Original ingredient-growing project or separately source-qualified mage/body route in another branch.

**In-world reason and cost:** Stock, crown payment, specialist time, paper and actual analysis means. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Household privacy and Kael’s family time matter here; clinical access is negotiated. No consent from a named student is generated automatically.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
