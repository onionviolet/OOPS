## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Qualify the actual eligible carrier and a real legacy reward.

**Dial:** Estate caretaker procurement, new-body practice, qualified reserve vessel or explicit alternative trial gate.

**In-world reason and cost:** Travel/run terms, current-body practice and real source/estate support. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Estate managers, trial custodians and local relatives have separate interests. A held bloodline does not make all natives obedient or waive a rival’s claim.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
