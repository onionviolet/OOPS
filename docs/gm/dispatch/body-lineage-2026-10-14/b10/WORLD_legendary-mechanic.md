## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Qualify an actual implant/component supply and fit operation.

**Dial:** Ordinary engineering, native supplier, supported player seat or explicit backup-body AU.

**In-world reason and cost:** Blueprint/material access, assembly, power and fitting procedure. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Native rights, player status, corporate stock and employment differ. Local service/recall terms remain authored until exact source scenes are qualified.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
