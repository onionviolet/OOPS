## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Investigate an actual elixir lot and its claimed body effect.

**Dial:** Licensed supplier, black-market purchase, original assay service or separately qualified alteration route.

**In-world reason and cost:** Purchase, independent examination, preparation and actual source risks. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Corporate/control claims and black-market relationships follow the chosen actors. Consent is relevant to the specific buyer/subject, not an assumed universal law governing every elixir.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
