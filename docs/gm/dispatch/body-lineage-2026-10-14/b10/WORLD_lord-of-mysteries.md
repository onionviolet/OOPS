## Five sourcing variables

These are independent proposed facts/observations, not five compulsory meters. Unmeasured values stay unknown; changing custody or reopening storage does not reset condition.

| Variable | This world’s relevant distinction |
|---|---|
| Quality | Pathway/sequence content and usable preparation; rarity, quantity and digestion are different. |
| Freshness | Physical reagent age and containment history; characteristic content has no generic herb-decay law. |
| Purity | Former-owner imprint, external corruption, auxiliaries and contained sequence content are separate. |
| Compatibility | Exact main input/formula, recipient pathway and advancement prerequisites; an equal tag sum is insufficient. |
| Provenance | Actual source entity, conserved identity, current holder and artifact containment; seller claim remains a claim. |

## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Acquire a titled main source and auxiliaries, then assess before a qualified preparation.

**Dial:** Creature-source, deceased-holder transfer, purchase, church allocation or original auxiliary farming.

**In-world reason and cost:** Actual formula, specialist means, custody transfer and supported containment; no invented universal cleanse. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Church restriction, occult-market practice, corpse treatment and donor consent vary by actor/locality. A corpse-harvesting route has witnesses and claimants; chemistry does not settle ownership.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.

**Resource joins:** [P8 creature sources](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/CREATURE_RESOURCES.md), [harvest](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/HARVEST_YIELDS.md), [logistics](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/LOGISTICS.md), [magical source forms](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/MAGICAL_RESOURCES.md), [trade/import](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/TRADE_SUPPLY.md) and [world boundaries](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/WORLD_VARIATIONS.md). Apply only operators compatible with this world. These P8 proposals are not already implemented genetics.

Exact extraction/cleanse quantities and hereditary transfer remain open/U; do not add a generic purity-to-mastership or soul-copy rule. Sealed S1/S2 stay ID/route/status only.

Compare a compatible/unsupported use without deleting retained stock or exposing hidden truth.
