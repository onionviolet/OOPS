## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Procure responsive tissue and apply a known compatible graft with both occupants’ actual permissions.

**Dial:** Plant structure, donor tissue, manufactured empty vessel, independent craft contract or voluntary separation.

**In-world reason and cost:** Workshop, viable input, growth support, actual procedure and shared-fund agreement. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** O2 gives standing consent for ordinary work but requires both subjects’ agreement for major bodily/shared-fund changes. Coercive variants require an explicit AU and causal NPC reactions, not a hidden default.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
