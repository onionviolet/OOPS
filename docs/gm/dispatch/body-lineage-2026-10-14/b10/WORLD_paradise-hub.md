## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Obtain an actual typed item, assess it and commit an actor-specific change.

**Dial:** Blood item, alchemical refit, living conversion, companion care or explicit compatibility AU.

**In-world reason and cost:** Purchase/material expense, procedure time, specialist means and source-specified outcome risks. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Contractor, paid worker, companion and independent resident have different standing. Voluntary conversion is a specific occurrence; no global contract consent, universal safety service or cruelty tax is inferred.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
