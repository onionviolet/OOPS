## Five sourcing variables

These are independent proposed facts/observations, not five compulsory meters. Unmeasured values stay unknown; changing custody or reopening storage does not reset condition.

| Variable | This world’s relevant distinction |
|---|---|
| Quality | Intact useful gland, conditioned herb and actual prepared dose; rank does not confer potency. |
| Freshness | Selected delicate batches only; ordinary old herbs/loot do not acquire global decay. |
| Purity | Composition, contaminants and preserved geometry; no clan-purity model. |
| Compatibility | Declared treatment or original organ function and this body; sample does not grant the monster’s skill. |
| Provenance | Creature/patch, actual holder, harvest method and processing record, with claim versus assay separate. |

## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Gather a useful care ingredient and commission a real treatment or fitted aid.

**Dial:** Original one-organ gene-thief prototype, nonlethal secretion husbandry, donor service or imported-compatible aid.

**In-world reason and cost:** Tools, owned stock, actual processor, care and practiced changed movement. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Original grower permissions, donor agreements and witnessed theft/revenge vary per local actor. Cruelty is a chosen world/route adjective, not a compulsory penalty or hunt-only progression.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.

**Resource joins:** [P8 creature sources](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/CREATURE_RESOURCES.md), [harvest](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/HARVEST_YIELDS.md), [logistics](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/LOGISTICS.md), [magical source forms](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/MAGICAL_RESOURCES.md), [trade/import](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/TRADE_SUPPLY.md) and [world boundaries](../../procgen-2026-10-13/p8/WORLD_VARIATIONS.md). Apply only operators compatible with this world. These P8 proposals are not already implemented genetics.

## Open source/use gate

All new source procedures here are proposals. Existing native animal care, loot and herbs do not prove carcass partitioning, generated chemistry, gene editing, inherited faculties or native persistence.

First selected proof must obtain an actual source by its stated route, inspect only what the observer can know, pay the real process cost, and produce a useful changed action or treatment. Compare a compatible/unsupported use without deleting retained stock or exposing hidden truth. For an implemented stateful slice: rebuild, fresh-origin desktop 1366x768, Save/reload and literal-file Import with an explicit compatibility decision. None was run by B10.
