## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Obtain evidence and a compatible interface through a named custodian.

**Dial:** Original civic diagnostic clinic, inherited equipment or explicit newly authored body modification.

**In-world reason and cost:** Test access, tools, time and separately granted machine rights. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** The chosen legacy institution may control assessment and use rights; coercive testing, voluntary testing and forged recognition produce different local histories.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
