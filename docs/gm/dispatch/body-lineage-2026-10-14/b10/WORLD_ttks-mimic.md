## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Source a scoped trace and test an identity claim.

**Dial:** Authored diagnostic service or separately qualified mimic body route.

**In-world reason and cost:** Observation tools, access and current-body practice; material retention must be established. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Impersonation may matter to an institution or victim through witnessed evidence. No universal law/consent rule is inferred from the word mimic.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
