## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Source a district-compatible vessel and actual operating atlas.

**Dial:** Survey shell, sensory-specialized replacement, instrument repair or earned person-carrying door.

**In-world reason and cost:** Maker/support, compatible design, physical retrieval and apparatus. Collection gives owned information/potential or a specific input. Actual body use needs its operator, recipient, supported law and cause; transmission to an heir is a separate later event.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Sponsors, councils and survey guilds have different permissions. Instrument bodies have no independent occupant under O3; making an independently minded one is a declared changed premise.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
