## Default, dial and local response

**Default:** Source an actual supported instrument/body service and separately develop personal faculties.

**Dial:** Original support laboratory, artificial shell, sourced extraordinary power gift or selected Asauchi test.

**Consent/cruelty/local authority:** Research-facility access and personal bonds differ; named experiments and gifts have their own agency/consent. No universal soul-resource market or mandatory spiritual-cruelty score is created.

**Idle proposal:** queue only already admitted procurement, preservation, preparation or recovery with actual stock/means, a receiver and a stop reason. Revalidate source access/conditions at relevant boundaries. No fixed lifetime collection cap, no default offline biological crisis, and no blanket hostile reaction.
