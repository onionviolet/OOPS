# B10 source-to-use causal chains

2026-10-14. Nine proposed chains across distinct worlds, micro through household/workshop to institutions, ecology or cross-world supply. B/P/L/U have the [matrix](MATRIX.md) meanings. None was played or implemented. Source rules and eligibility stay with the linked world notes; plausible later outcomes require the named intermediate actors and actual demand. A strong source does not need an invented downside. No chain supplies sealed truth.

## C1 Harry Potter: a hair changes a body, a sample changes trust

[World note](WORLD_harry-potter.md), SO5/SO7/SO10/SO12; S1 K2, Chamber of Secrets ch10. The target input/preparation is inherited SHOWN/STATED; the supplier, disputed custody and later investigation are P.

A collector receives a particular person's hair → keeps target identity separate from ordinary ingredients → obtains the named preparation ingredients and follows their selected harvest/preparation requirements → uses the eligible temporary-form preparation → undertakes an actual disguise task → a witness or investigator acquires evidence by their own means → the target or supplier changes a later offer because of the actual use. At workshop scale, a reliable source-and-use ledger can support a legitimate impersonation/care service; a fraudulent ledger can support contested access and a real investigation. Neither service installs wizard heredity.

Quality: correct part/preparation. Freshness: specific harvest conditions, not generic daily decay. Purity: ingredient/contaminant facts, not pure-blood status. Compatibility: intended-person role and actual recipient. Provenance: target-linked sample/custody. Failure/counter: wrong target material or counterfeit claim changes the supported result/evidence; recipe/response needs exact qualification, not an invented random curse. Alternative: negotiate another real source or buy qualified prepared stock. Verification proposal: compare authentic target input and incompatible/incorrect input, scoped observations, actual temporary effect and exact retained stock/condition on reload.

Population expansion requires actual demand, stock, trained people and production time; it is not a brood-conquest reward.

Quality: intact gland versus crushed ingredient. Freshness: selected tissue viability. Purity: relevant contents/contaminants. Compatibility: declared body/interface and operator. Provenance: producer, part allocation, processor and recipient. Failure/counter: a destroyed gland may remain a usable fictional ingredient without being transplantable; another intact source remains reachable. Alternative: buy resin or commission external production while keeping the current body. Verification proposal: source → paid integration → useful secretion → real craft use; compare direct stock purchase, no combat-drop/carcass double pay, actual coordination and downstream container effect. Inheritance requires a separately demonstrated route.

Alternative: source-qualified main-material substitution or another held source where the exact formula permits, with actual expense. Verification proposal: transfer once → inspect → useful qualified consumer, conserve contained IDs across death/market/failed publication; no recomputation into duplicate lower-sequence tokens.

## Cross-chain boundaries

Every chain has its own acquisition and consumption law. Samples, models, organs, sources, learned procedures, grants and people stay distinct. Household/guild/ecological growth requires actual intermediates, supported production, demand and time; it is not an automatic world-conquest multiplier. Heritable change needs a separate G26/G30/G53 route, not an assumption because a body was altered.

These are specified acceptance ideas only. B11 and actual implementation owners choose one authorized slice, its native consumers and compatibility strategy. Existing source/testing evidence is not refreshed here. No runtime edit, build, browser, personal-save or human acceptance result is claimed.
