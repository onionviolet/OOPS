# B9 modification patterns

2026-10-14. Proposals distilled from [22 bounded source cards](REPORT.md#source-inventory). These extend G53's use, not its authority or code vocabulary. Every GM/GD mapping is analyst inference. No setting inherits another setting's costs, limits or biology automatically. Recurring means multiple catalogued sources, not a measured survey of all fiction.

## Recurring mechanics worth using

| ID | Mechanic and evidence | GM/GD mapping | Default | Dial | In-world reason and actual cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Track what really changes: genome, tissue, organ, soul, device or development. [FMA FMA1](SRC_fullmetal-alchemist.md), [JJK JJK1](SRC_jujutsu-kaisen.md), [Deus Ex DX1](SRC_deus-ex.md), [Brave New World BNW1–2](SRC_brave-new-world.md). | GM2–GM8; GD1, GD2, GD5 | A method names its substrate and target. | A world can connect substrates through an explicit adapter. | A soul rewrite changes the body in JJK; a medbot installs a device in Deus Ex. Knowledge, facilities and source material pay for the actual operation. |
| P2 | Separate source custody, diagnosis, procedure and expression. [Naruto NAR1–2](SRC_naruto.md), [RimWorld RIM1](SRC_rimworld-biotech.md), [Gattaca GAT2–3](SRC_gattaca.md). | GM4, GM5, GM8; GD2, GD6, GD7 | A sample enables investigation or production; it is not instant power. | An exceptional body can perform several stages itself. | The eye, the scan and the installation have different carriers. Costs are material, expertise and elapsed work, with no mandatory laboratory when the faculty supplies its function. |
| P3 | Give inheritance its own route. [HxH HXA1](SRC_hunter-x-hunter.md), [RimWorld RIM2](SRC_rimworld-biotech.md), [40k WH2](SRC_warhammer-40k.md), [MHA MHA1](SRC_my-hero-academia.md). | GM1, GM4, GM8; GD1 | Record somatic change, reproductive transmission and manufactured succession independently. | A ritual can deliberately connect them if its world supports it. | A Queen's offspring, a child's germline and a harvested progenoid are different successors. Costs can be reproductive time, care, growth or surgery. |
| P4 | Split operator capacity, recipient support and active loadout. [RimWorld RIM3–4](SRC_rimworld-biotech.md), [BioShock BIO2](SRC_bioshock.md), [MHA MHA2](SRC_my-hero-academia.md), [Deus Ex DX2–4](SRC_deus-ex.md). | GM3–GM8; GD3, GD4 | Name the actor and process actually constrained. | Raise or remove a local limit through a real constitution, facility or power. | Assembly complexity needs equipment; metabolism needs support; equipped options may need slots. Cost follows the relevant bottleneck, not an invented universal mutation count. |
| P5 | Treat reversal as several operations. [Fallout FO3](SRC_fallout.md), [HP HP1–2](SRC_harry-potter.md), [BioShock BIO3](SRC_bioshock.md), [JJK JJK3](SRC_jujutsu-kaisen.md). | GM2–GM8; GD4, GD5 | Distinguish prevention, suppression, expiry, repair, replacement and restoration. | Clean restoration or permanent effects can be local rules. | A strain-specific cure, Wolfsbane and a Gene Bank solve different problems. Cost may be a known baseline sample, provider or material; irreversible procedures must reveal known consequences before commitment. |
| P6 | Preserve personhood separately from morphology and function. [FMA FMA2](SRC_fullmetal-alchemist.md), [HxH HXA3](SRC_hunter-x-hunter.md), [Orphan Black OB1–3](SRC_orphan-black.md), [High Evolutionary HE2](SRC_high-evolutionary.md). | GM3–GM9; GD4, GD10 | Keep memory, self-identification, control and social recognition distinct. | A particular method can truly affect any of them. | Chimera memories and a clone's different life are not cosmetic details. Learning new coordination, renegotiating relationships or coping with actual cognitive change are possible costs; no automatic loss-of-humanity tax. |
| P7 | Let access controls have mechanisms. [40k WH3–4](SRC_warhammer-40k.md), [Orphan Black OB1–2](SRC_orphan-black.md), [Deus Ex DX5](SRC_deus-ex.md), [Gattaca GAT3](SRC_gattaca.md). | GM5–GM8; GD6, GD7, GD8 | Separate sample custody, contract, claimed ownership, observation and biological enforcement. | A genuine kill switch or gene lock is possible when explicitly sourced or authored. | A vault, patent claim and communication implant constrain different acts. Cost comes from clinics, stock, service terms or evasion, not omniscient remote control. |
| P8 | Make ecology and institutions respond through real carriers. [Resident Evil RE1/RE4](SRC_resident-evil.md), [HxH HXA5](SRC_hunter-x-hunter.md), [Jurassic Park JP2](SRC_jurassic-park.md), [Upgrade UPG2–3](SRC_upgrade.md). | GM1, GM2, GM6, GM9–GM11; GD1, GD8, GD9 | Specify who reproduces, what spreads, who observes and who can intervene. | Self-limiting, persistent or geographically bounded spread follows the selected method. | Quarantine, breeding and exposure create different timelines. Habitat, carrier movement and institutional action are actual costs and causes; a dose does not silently change an entire world. |

## Rare or especially distinctive mechanics

| ID | Mechanic and evidence | GM/GD mapping | Default | Dial | In-world reason and actual cost |

Time, recovery, preservation and custody matter; generation is not ordinary reproduction or free duplication. |

[HxH HXA1–3](SRC_hunter-x-hunter.md). | GM1/GM4 analogies; GD1, GD4, GD10 | Give source-derived traits to offspring through the Queen's actual method. | An original route may instead remodel the eater. | Food-derived heredity is this setting's unusual mechanism. | P11 | The mark in your genome is evidence of a claim. [Orphan Black OB1](SRC_orphan-black.md), compared with [Gattaca GAT3](SRC_gattaca.md). | GM5 analogy; GD2, GD7, GD8, GD10 | Store marker, test, claimant and response separately. | A marker can be forged, removed or linked to a real lock by a separately authored mechanism. | An identifier must be discovered and interpreted. | P12 | A template can be made, copied and raised into a society. [High Evolutionary HE1–2](SRC_high-evolutionary.md), [Spore SPO4](SRC_spore.md). | GM9; GD6, GD8, GD10 | Sapience creates actors with their own learning and goals. | A connection can provide teaching or actual control if defined. | Education and institutions differ from changed anatomy. | P13 | Restoration creates a new ecological participant. | P14 | Mutation can be an environmental information problem. [Annihilation ANN1–4](SRC_annihilation.md). | GM2/GM4 analogies; GD2, GD4, GD5, GD10 | Show observations and attribute the explanation to its speaker. | An original world can make the theory correct, partial or false. | Exposure continues while people investigate. Cost is actual exposure and uncertainty, not an undisclosed author-imposed penalty. |
