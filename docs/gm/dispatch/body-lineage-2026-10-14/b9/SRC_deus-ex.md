# Deus Ex: modification evidence

2026-10-14. B9 docs-only research. Labels follow [REPORT](REPORT.md#evidence-labels). GM/GD mappings are analyst comparisons.

Boundary: original Deus Ex (2000) manual. Human Revolution/Mankind Divided mechanical augmentation, Neuropozyne and biochip events are not folded into this nano-augmentation model.

Source, accessed 2026-10-14: [official game manual via Steam](https://cdn.akamai.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/6910/manuals/manual.pdf?t=1700763294), printed pp.8–9 (PDF pages 5–6), Augs, Choosing Augmentations, Upgrades and Bio-Energy. PDF text inspected.

| ID | Label | Codes | Bounded finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| DX1 | stated, manual | GM7; GD6 | New nano-augmentations need the relevant canister and medbot. Upgrades use another canister without the same installation requirement. Procedure access and upgrade access differ. |
| DX2 | stated, manual | GD2, GD3 | Canisters reveal options for particular body slots; the player chooses between paired capabilities. These are authored game constraints, not evidence that all nanotechnology has the same slot ceiling. |
| DX3 | stated, manual | GD5 | Installed choices and upgrades are permanent in the described system. Turning an augmentation off is not uninstalling it. |
| DX4 | stated, manual | GD3, GD4 | Most active augmentations drain bio-energy; some built-ins remain on. Resource consumption differs from rejection, genetic instability or loss of humanity. |
| DX5 | stated then inferred | GD7, GD10 | Infolink permits headquarters monitoring and communication. Embedded access can affect autonomy without proving an inheritable genetic lock. Infolink entry; autonomy is analyst inference. |

Proposal: Default to an actual installed device, power consumer and service provider. Dial reconfigurable or permanent packages by world; cost can be energy and access to installation. DNA rewriting, reproductive inheritance, dependence on immunosuppressants, kill switches and legal ownership are not established by this manual. Hardware access and genome ownership remain separate questions.
