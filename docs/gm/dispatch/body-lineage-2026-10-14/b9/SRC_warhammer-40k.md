# Warhammer 40,000: gene-seed: modification evidence

2026-10-14. B9 docs-only research. Labels follow [REPORT](REPORT.md#evidence-labels). GM/GD mappings are analyst comparisons.

Boundary: Space Marine organ lineage. The 2016 article describes the older 19-organ package; it is not an all-era organ-count rule. Horus Heresy history is labeled separately.

Sources, accessed 2026-10-14: [official Anatomy of Power Armour](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/Du2wvip7/the-anatomy-of-power-armour/), More than human; [official Gene-seed Crisis extract](https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_19-03_thehorusheresy_black_book_extract_emperors_children-oywc6iqhan-hwpwpigftx.pdf), section The Gene-seed Crisis (indexed passage only, PDF open failed); [secondary gene-seed overview](https://warhammer40k.wiki.gg/wiki/Gene-seed), conservation and Chapter ownership, indexed extract. These sources do not cover every Chapter or Primaris difference.

| ID | Label | Codes | Bounded finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| WH1 | stated, official | GM4, GM8; GD6 | Astartes creation involves multiple invasive implants with differentiated functions. Gene-seed is a lineage/source system for the package, not one organ that grows every other organ automatically. Anatomy, More than human. |
| WH2 | stated, official historical account | GD1, GD9 | Recovered progenoid glands allow new gene-seed organs to be grown for other recruits. This is surgical/material succession, not evidence of ordinary parent-to-child inheritance. Crisis extract. |
| WH3 | stated, official historical account | GD4, GD7 | Recoverability and reserve vaults limit replacement; a blight destroys stored material. Biological supply-chain loss differs from a personal capacity bar or a software lock. Crisis extract. |
| WH4 | stated, secondary | GD7, GD8 | Chapters conserve and claim their gene-seed stock. This supports institutional custody, not universal remote control over a recipient. Secondary overview. |
| WH5 | inferred | GD3, GD5, GD10 | A functioning implant package can change bodily identity without proving reversible recruitment, ordinary fertility or shared memories. Those questions need selected-era evidence. |

Proposal: Default to grown, implanted and later harvested carriers, each with custody. Dial original organ packages or qualified Chapter-specific differences; cost can be cultivation time, surgery and recoverable stock. Hereditary flaws, rejection rates, Primaris totals, reversal and reproductive status remain unverified here. No arbitrary gene theft is imported from Genestealer branding.
