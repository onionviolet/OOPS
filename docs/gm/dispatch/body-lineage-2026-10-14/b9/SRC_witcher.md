# The Witcher: modification evidence

2026-10-14. B9 docs-only research. Labels follow [REPORT](REPORT.md#evidence-labels). GM/GD mappings are analyst comparisons.

Boundary: Netflix adaptation only for verified details below. Sapkowski novels and CDPR games are separate continuities requiring their own qualification; no cross-version recipe or success rate is adopted.

Sources, accessed 2026-10-14: [Netflix guide](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/witcher-bible-guide-continent-creatures), Trial of the Grasses / Elixirs / Witchers; [Netflix prototype explainer](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/witcher-blood-origin-who-is-the-first-witcher?inapp=true%2F), Fjall's transformation. Official adaptation explainers, not novel verification.

| ID | Label | Codes | Bounded finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| WIT1 | stated, official | GM2, GM3; GD4, GD6 | Magical mutagens and a dangerous trial create witchers; training remains another requirement. The process needs specialist knowledge and has lethal failures. Guide, Trial of the Grasses. |
| WIT2 | stated, official | GD3, GD4, GD5 | Temporary elixirs improve selected functions and can be toxic even to altered bodies. Potion dosing is a different limit from enduring mutation. Guide, Elixirs. |
| WIT3 | shown, official recap | GM4 analogy; GD4, GD10 | The prototype incorporates a monster-heart connection and becomes unstable, losing discrimination between allies and enemies. This early procedure does not define every later witcher. Prototype explainer. |
| WIT4 | stated, official | GD1, GD6, GD8 | Lost knowledge constrains new production; the claimed Elder Blood ingredient belongs to this adaptation. Prejudice about missing emotion is contradicted by Geralt's behavior. Guide, Witcher Mutagen / Witchers. |

Proposal: Default to a lasting procedure followed by learned control, with temporary preparation handled separately. Dial stable versus experimental recipes; cost follows lore, rather than requiring every magical enhancement to be messy. Fertility, genetic transmission and a reliable reversal are not established by these bounded sources. In particular, do not turn Netflix's Elder Blood ingredient into a novel-wide law.
