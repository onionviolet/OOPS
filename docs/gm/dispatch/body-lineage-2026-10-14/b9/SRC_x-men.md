# X-Men: modification evidence

2026-10-14. B9 docs-only research. Labels follow [REPORT](REPORT.md#evidence-labels). GM/GD mappings are analyst comparisons.

Boundary: Marvel comics examples, not Fox-film cures, MCU mutants or every publication era merged together. Repo [Marvel digest](../../../../research/power-systems/wiki-digests/DIGEST_marvel-comics.md) has no acquired revision and remains a lead.

Sources, accessed 2026-10-14: [Marvel Wolverine reading guide](https://share.marvel.com/comics/discover/121/wolverine-from-animal-to-x-man), introduction; [Marvel Elixir biography](https://www.marvel.com/characters/Elixir), indexed biography passage about a vaccine and cure serum (page open failed); [secondary mutant overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutant_(Marvel_Comics)), opening / Mutants and mutates; [mutant-gene compilation](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Mutant_Genes), X-Gene location and parentage contradictions. No listed issue was read in full.

| ID | Label | Codes | Bounded finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| XM1 | stated, secondary | GD1; GM2 boundary | Innate mutant faculties are distinguished from acquired mutate origins. Radiation-origin heroes cannot all be filed as the same X-gene inheritance route. Mutant overview. |
| XM2 | stated, official | GM4 analogy; GD4, GD10 | Wolverine combines genetic mutation with imposed experimentation. The guide does not equate an installed enhancement with the original mutant faculty. Wolverine introduction. Exact Weapon X procedures remain unverified here. |
| XM3 | stated, official indexed summary | GM5 analogy; GD5 | The Elixir account names an intervention affecting future X-gene expression and a cure serum. This supplies a specific intervention lead, not proof that every depowered mutant can be restored by any healer. Elixir biography passage. |
| XM4 | stated, secondary compilation | GD1, GD2 | Statements about X-gene location and maternal/paternal transmission vary between stories. Do not derive a universal sex-linked inheritance formula from the name X-gene. Mutant Genes, On Chromosome 23 / Carried by female / Carried by male. |
| XM5 | inferred | GD8, GD10 | Mutant social identity can persist through changed expression. A power's suppression, community membership and individual selfhood require separate records. Exact registration law needs a chosen comic arc. |

Proposal: Default to qualifying the individual faculty and publication arc. Dial mutations, experimental upgrades and reversible suppression separately; cost follows the selected intervention. No universal capacity budget, cure permanence, gene ownership statute or inheritance probability is verified.
