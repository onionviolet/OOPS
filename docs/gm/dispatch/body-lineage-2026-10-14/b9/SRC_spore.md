# Spore: modification evidence

2026-10-14. B9 docs-only research. Labels follow [REPORT](REPORT.md#evidence-labels). GM/GD mappings are analyst comparisons.

Boundary: base Spore (2008) and Creature Creator. A game editor's abstractions are not a literal inheritance model.

Sources, accessed 2026-10-14: [EA series description](https://www.ea.com/games/spore?isLocalized=true), customized organisms through sentient space travel; [EA Creator announcement](https://ir.ea.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2008/Spore-Creature-Creator-Available-Worldwide-June-17/default.aspx), design/import species; [secondary DNA Points](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/DNA_Points), opening / Other Sources; [secondary Monolith](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Monolith), Usage. No gameplay exercised.

| ID | Label | Codes | Bounded finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPO1 | stated, official | GM5 analogy; GD6 | The player designs morphology and imports created species. This is authoring control rather than a verified in-world sequencer or precise genetic operation. EA descriptions. |
| SPO2 | stated, secondary | GD3 | DNA points buy body parts. This is an earned editor currency, not measured genome complexity or a metabolic safety budget. DNA Points, opening. |
| SPO3 | stated, secondary | GD5 | Selling or exchanging parts refunds points in the creator. Editing reversibility does not establish restoration of every population history or dead individual. DNA Points, Other Sources. |

This creates another polity rather than a guaranteed obedient pet. Monolith, Usage; obedience boundary is inference. |

Proposal: Default to a usable body-preview editor with clear local costs. Dial reproductive change versus direct redesign and separately authored uplift; cost follows DNA points, tools or time in that world. Exact sexual heredity, biological rejection, creator ownership and irreversible ecological outcomes remain unverified. The tool's word evolution does not make player-selected body assembly natural selection.
