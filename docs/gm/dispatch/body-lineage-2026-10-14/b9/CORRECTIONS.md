## What this does not correct

G53 already permits clean changes, reversal and world-specific exceptions. Its GD1 somatic/heritable split and GD5 reversal palette are useful and should stay. No new mandatory flaws, forced purity ladder, universal cap, universal infertility or irreversible-change policy is recommended. GD2 read-before-write is a design default, not a claim that every canon practitioner sequences a genome before acting.

GM7 groups two broad families for authoring convenience; nanotechnology and synthetic biology do not prove one another's presence. GM9 describes uplift by outcome, which need not involve DNA. GM10 can mean species reconstruction or restoring a lost supernatural faculty, which need different evidence. These are authoring distinctions, not demands to remove the palette entries.

The six named public reference families were checked at bounded depth: Fallout, Witcher, FMA, 40k, RimWorld and Gattaca. Full primary works, implementation numbers and exact scientific mechanisms were not certified. The integrating owner can adopt the precision notes without reopening excluded runtime or world work.
