# BioShock: modification evidence

2026-10-14. B9 docs-only research. Labels follow [REPORT](REPORT.md#evidence-labels). GM/GD mappings are analyst comparisons.

Boundary: first game's plasmid/tonic model. BioShock 2 and Infinite are not merged into its equipment rules.

Sources, accessed 2026-10-14: [2K official PS3 manual](https://assets.2k.com/1a6ngf98576c/44QfFXIkkvaGAIgYlRSARd/bccf98e53286dfe35bf5baceff9da93d/BIOSHOCK_PS3_MANUAL_ENG.pdf), ADAM / Plasmids / Gene Tonics / Machines; [manual transcription](https://manuals.plus/m/b3a4e435ea58912a4415b6d8403078dd61aa7e26b13993e24bf211f7e0a51fae), Gene Banks and Gatherer's Gardens; [ADAM wiki](https://bioshock.fandom.com/wiki/ADAM), opening and Attributes; [Plasmid wiki](https://bioshock.fandom.com/wiki/Plasmid), opening. Manual evidence was recovered through indexed/transcribed material after the BioShock 2 PDF fetch failed; no game was played.

| ID | Label | Codes | Bounded finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| BIO1 | stated, secondary | GM2, GM5 analogy; GD6 | Processed ADAM permits genetic alteration through plasmids/tonics. A purchasable designed ability does not establish base-by-base editing by its user. ADAM / Plasmid openings. |
| BIO2 | stated, manual | GD3 | Slots constrain equipped abilities and can be expanded through ADAM purchases. This is a gameplay loadout constraint; it is not proof of a universal physiological gene limit. Manual, Plasmids and Machines. |
| BIO3 | stated, manual | GD5 | Gene Banks store and exchange unequipped plasmids/tonics. Swapping an active loadout does not prove removal of all biological damage from splicing. Manual, Gene Banks. |
| BIO4 | stated, secondary | GD4, GD10 | Excessive splicing is associated with bodily/mental instability and dependence. The page's broad limitless-potential language is not a verified guarantee. Plasmid opening / ADAM Attributes. |
| BIO5 | stated then inferred | GD9; GD7 | Sea-slug-derived ADAM supplies a biological production economy. Control of supply can create dependence on institutions; that does not itself establish gene DRM. ADAM opening; economic inference. |

Proposal: Default to separate acquired library, equipped expression and lasting condition. Dial clean bank swaps versus persistent damage by method; cost can be actual ADAM stock and EVE use. Heritability, exact safe dosage, legal ownership and a complete cure remain open.
