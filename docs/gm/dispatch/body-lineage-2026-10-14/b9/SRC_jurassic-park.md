# Jurassic Park / Jurassic World: modification evidence

2026-10-14. B9 docs-only research. Labels follow [REPORT](REPORT.md#evidence-labels). GM/GD mappings are analyst comparisons.

Boundary: original 1993 film reconstruction premise, with later official franchise material kept separately labeled. Added for GM10 and GD9; no real de-extinction capability is asserted.

Sources, accessed 2026-10-14: [1993 film synopsis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_Park), Plot, visitor-center explanation and breeding discovery; [licensed exhibition Creation Lab](https://jurassicworldexhibition.com/au/experience), reconstructed dinosaurs; [Universal Rebirth synopsis](https://www.universalpictures.com/movies/jurassic-world-rebirth/videos), genetic-material mission and proposed medicine. Secondary film account plus official later-franchise summaries; no rewatch.

| ID | Label | Codes | Bounded finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| JP1 | stated, secondary | GM5 analogy, GM10; GD2, GD6 | The park reconstructs extinct animals from recovered sequence material and gap-filling. Sample, completed template and living population are distinct products. 1993 Plot, visitor-center explanation. |
| JP2 | shown, secondary | GD1, GD4, GD7 | The intended single-sex containment fails when the animals breed; the film attributes the escape from that control to incorporated amphibian traits. This is fictional causation, not biological advice. 1993 Plot, breeding discovery. |
| JP3 | stated, official later-film premise | GD9, GD7 | Rebirth frames dinosaur genetic material as a potential medicine source and the object of a contracted expedition. Production value can motivate extraction without granting ownership of every living carrier. Universal synopsis. |
| JP4 | inferred | GD5, GD10 | Reconstructed, gap-filled organisms need not be exact restorations of an extinct individual's identity. Erasing the new population would not undo its ecological history. |

Proposal: Default to partial source information, a declared reconstruction method and an actual habitat. Dial faithful restoration or openly composite animals, with facility, care and ecological costs. Exact sequence fidelity, production protocols, lawful ownership and reversible population control remain unverified.
