Open: precise Nomu procedures, transferred-factor capacity, reversal protocols, law, hereditary probabilities and manufacturing organisms. B4's OFA compatibility lead is retained as a lead, not used to assign costs to every Quirk. No GM5 precise editing protocol is certified here.

Proposal: Default to separate output, tolerance and training. Dial strong synergy or clean inheritance where supported; cost can be time, care and an uncertain breeding outcome rather than a universal mutation penalty.
