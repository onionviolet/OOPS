Proposal: Default to tracking template, individual life, identifier and claimant separately. Dial conspicuous marks, hidden provenance or contested ownership; cost can be sample access, care and institutional conflict. Do not present the show's patent rhetoric as current real-world law. Precise editing, reversal, fertility and inherited pathology are unverified here.
