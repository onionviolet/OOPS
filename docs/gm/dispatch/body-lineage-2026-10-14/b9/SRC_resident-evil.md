# Resident Evil: modification evidence

2026-10-14. B9 docs-only research. Labels follow [REPORT](REPORT.md#evidence-labels). GM/GD mappings are analyst comparisons.

Boundary: The Umbrella Chronicles and its official guide's earlier-game recaps. Films, later viruses and parasite systems are excluded from these bounded facts.

Source, accessed 2026-10-14: [Capcom-hosted Prima official guide preview](https://static.capcom.com/residentevil/files/REUC_PPP.pdf), printed pp.26 and 30 (PDF pages 7 and 8), The Infected; printed p.39 (PDF page 11), Train Derailment 1. PDF text inspected. This is an official licensed guide, not a fresh in-game file audit.

| ID | Label | Codes | Bounded finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| RE1 | stated, guide | GM2, GM6; GD6 | Cerberus are deliberately produced by administering t-virus to military-trained dogs; some snakes and bats are secondarily infected. Designed weapons and spillover cases differ. Printed p.26. |
| RE2 | stated, guide | GD3, GD4 | Host compatibility matters: the Infected Bat exemplifies pronounced growth in a compatible mammal. The source gives no mutation-slot number or general dose model. Printed p.30. |
| RE3 | stated, guide | GD4, GD10 | Zombies lose reasoning while undergoing decay. This outcome is specific to the described infected humans, not every subject of every franchise agent. Printed p.30. |
| RE4 | shown, guide recap | GM6; GD8 analogy | Infected leeches carry an outbreak onto a train. The transmission event is concrete; its complete contagion model and legal response remain unverified. Printed p.39. |
| RE5 | stated then inferred | GD7 | A corporate project produces weapons and re-engineered bodyguards. Control of manufacture does not establish continued obedience or encoded ownership in every infected subject. Ivan entry, printed p.30; inference about ownership boundary. |

Proposal: Default to distinguishing intended product, infected host, escaped carrier and exposed bystander. Dial clinical or uncontrolled exposure through the chosen agent; cost follows tissue/cognitive effects. Prevention, infection clearance and anatomical reversal need separate evidence. Heritability, GM9 uplift, exact antidote windows and universal t-virus science are not established.
