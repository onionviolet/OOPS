Dial permissive or gene-stratified institutions with different interests; cost can be testing, documentation and concealment. Do not make every genetic prediction infallible or transplant this society's discrimination into all tech worlds. Exact editing protocols, mutation toxicity and manufacturing organisms are outside this review.
