# B5 borrowing palette

2026-10-14. Proposals mapped to
[the existing G1-G42 owner](../../../../GENETICS_CONSTITUTIONS_AND_BROOD_PLAY.md),
not a replacement specification or new accepted G-code sequence. B5-M IDs stay
local to this lane. Game evidence and access dates are in [GAMES](GAMES.md).
Root owns integration; B2 owns body/schema proposals and B3 owns prestige proposals.

The incumbent palette already has layered bodies, hidden diagnoses, real carriers,
functional injury, quirks and earned succession. The added value here is the
choice of resolution model, readable operation and cost. Each Default is a
candidate for a fitting world/character, not a rule imposed on every world.
Each Dial may expand, simplify or disable it. A true world absolute can follow
its lore. Superior physiques need no fabricated compensating weakness.

All cost estimates and proposed representations below are **inferred**. L is a
small definition plus an existing consumer; M adds persistent operations or
several consumers; H needs interacting actor/anatomy/population systems. Content,
UI and save compatibility can dominate computation. “Cheap” never means tested.
Candidate acceptance cases are unrun, not implementation gates already selected.

## B5-M01 Possession, expression and transmission

**Maps:** G1/G4/G15/G18/G24, GN2/GN9/GN11, BD1, MX1.
**References:** B5-G01 CK3, B5-G03 Niche, B5-G06 RimWorld. **Disposition:** adapt first
if an inheritance slice is selected.

**Default:** a faculty instance records origin, carrier, expression and an explicit
transmission rule. A dormant inherited instance, a suppressing graft and a known
diagnosis are separate. **Dial:** allele pairs, tiered expression, quantitative
polygenic scores or magical succession rules, including fully expressed advantages.
Use the setting's causal model rather than assuming RimWorld suppression is DNA.

**In-world reason/cost:** childhood development, organ support, a suppressing
implant or an awakening changes function; a physician/test service costs means
and time. Birth itself has the selected world's care/resource policy.

**Browser-idle cost:** M. Resolve transmission at creation/birth, expression at
meaningful support changes. Keep method/seed versions so later content changes
cannot silently reroll a saved child. **Candidate proof:** a carrier with no
usable function produces a fitting descendant; an acquired-only graft works in
the parent but does not transmit; suppression removal restores the original
supported function. Save/reload/import preserve all three distinctions and known
versus unknown information.

## B5-M02 Visible resemblance without a portrait engine

**Maps:** G17/G18/G19/G41, PH11, GN1/GN6/GN8/GN9, SG4/SG6, BSR3/BSR5.
**References:** B5-G02 Sims by edition, B5-G01 CK3. **Disposition:** small trial.

**Default:** a few appearance descriptors inherit separately from identity,
reproductive role and mechanical aptitude. A witness recognizes a resemblance
through sight/familiarity. **Dial:** more quantitative features, recognizable
family markers, identical-body twins or morphing portraits where art warrants it.
Similarity is evidence of resemblance, not certain parentage or a shared self.

**In-world reason/cost:** actual visual traits, disguises, examination and family
records; different observers have different access. **Browser-idle cost:** L/M
for descriptors, H for portrait morphs and age art. Reuse recorded observations
rather than revealing latent family information to everyone.

**Candidate proof:** two twins retain distinct upbringing/history; disguise
changes a witness's available evidence without changing ancestry; a family claim
and test result can disagree. Root/B1 must supply world rules for such disagreement.
Sims 2 and Sims 4 are alternative appearance models, not one combined algorithm.

## B5-M03 Relatedness as a contextual rule

**Maps:** G8/G18/G27/G29/G40, GN7, CL3/CL6/CL9, MX6, RP2/RP4.
**References:** B5-G01 CK3, B5-G03 Niche. **Disposition:** park beyond a finite family
unless a selected world needs it.

**Default:** parent edges support a contextual relatedness query at pairing or
birth, with a plain uncertainty statement. **Dial:** close-kin risks, no effect,
local blood-concentration benefits, hybrid benefits or a richer pedigree model.
A Niche-style matching-immunity check and a shared-ancestor query are distinct
rules; one is not evidence of the other.

**In-world reason/cost:** the specific inherited faculty, adverse allele or magical
law determines benefit/risk. Marriage also spends political/relationship opportunity
and care time, independent of genetics. No universal purity ranking.

**Browser-idle cost:** M for a known small pedigree, H for large graphs, missing
parents and retained historical ancestors. Query on events; cache only with an
ancestry revision. **Candidate proof:** unknown ancestry reports uncertainty;
a distant relationship and identical immunity alleles can lead to different
results; legal adoption changes household/succession without altering biological
relatedness unless an actual ritual changes the selected rule.

## B5-M04 Breeding beyond the player

**Maps:** G3/G7/G8/G18/G29/G40, GN5/GN10, MX5/MX6, RP1/RP5.
**References:** B5-G03 Niche, B5-G18 DragonVale, B5-G23 Critter Mound; contrast B5-G17 IdleOn.
**Disposition:** trial only in an owned animal/plant/familiar slice.

**Default:** choose actual parent organisms and an intended use; a nursery resolves
a saved offspring outcome with variation. **Dial:** alleles, quantitative parental
ranges, local compatibility rules, budding or magical creation. Timer completion,
genetic expression, maturation and learning are distinct events.

**In-world reason/cost:** real parents, care, habitat, food or a setting-supported
substitute. Keeper instruction can matter independently of inherited structure.
Do not import the Critter Mound fork's irrevocable jobs or fixed breeding limits
as universal rules.

**Browser-idle cost:** M. Persist parent/template references and committed outcome;
preview only information the keeper can diagnose. Timed work pauses or continues
under an explicit care policy. **Candidate proof:** a specialist offspring changes
one useful job; a weaker child remains usable in another fitting role; absent
care changes scheduling truthfully. Catch-up plus reload/import never duplicate
births, spend resources twice or replace a chosen parent with a newer template.

## B5-M05 Body support rather than universal injury penalties

**Maps:** G6/G17/G22/G35/G37/G39, PH1/PH4/PH8/PH9/PH10, TR6, WR1/WR3,
BM1/BM4.
**References:** B5-G07 DF, B5-G08 Kenshi, B5-G06 RimWorld, B5-G09 Qud.
**Disposition:** adapt within an owning combat/body slice.

**Default:** selected parts supply named functions such as grip, gait, breathing
or voice. A located injury changes its actual supported actions. **Dial:** simple
injury cards, part trees, fine tissue layers, redundant/distributed organs or
regeneration that avoids the injury altogether. Keep the body generic enough for
nonhuman means; an arm is not required for every form of manipulation.

**In-world reason/cost:** lost or impaired support and actual treatment/downtime.
A hurt hand need not reduce unrelated memory or walking. **Browser-idle cost:** M
for a selected arm and tool consumer; H for tissue propagation, all equipment,
NPCs and combat. Derived capacities should invalidate when support changes,
not require a full body recalculation per frame.

**Candidate proof:** grip-dependent work adapts/pauses with a reason while
unrelated work continues; knowledge survives loss of its executor. Desktop target
1366x768 shows current function and recovery in an optional body view. No UI
behavior claim is made here; rebuilt browser proof would be required later.

## B5-M06 Repair, aid, replacement and specialty

**Maps:** G6/G10/G22/G37/G39, WR5/WR6, BM1/BM4/BM5/BM6.
**References:** B5-G08 Kenshi B1-B5, B5-G06 RimWorld, B5-G09 Qud, B5-G12 Wildermyth.
**Disposition:** adapt alongside M05; existing Kenshi proposal remains owner.

**Default:** a modest aid restores a useful everyday function; further parts can
specialize. **Dial:** a graft, regrowth, external assistive device, magical
substitute or perfect restoration. Cure is an option, not compulsory progression.
A stronger replacement can simply be stronger when its mechanism allows it.

**In-world reason/cost:** fitting, stock, a provider, repair material, learned
coordination or a constitution that handles those needs outright. Organic healing
and mechanical repair have different means. No automatic rejection tax.

**Browser-idle cost:** M for part custody, fitting and job resumption; H for arbitrary
body/equipment compatibility. **Candidate proof:** cheap repair restores the
chosen work; an advanced alternative opens a new application; removed part
custody, capacity and job status restore exactly. Define which practice belongs
to the person and which to a particular part. An acquired implant does not
become an heir's birth trait without a transmission mechanism (M01).

## B5-M07 Care and complications with interpretable timing

**Maps:** G7/G31/G36/G37, HI2/HI4, SB1/SB2/SB3/SB5/SB6, WR2.
**References:** B5-G06 RimWorld, B5-G07 DF, B5-G10 DDA, B5-G21 Battle Brothers.
**Disposition:** conditional trial; epidemics are a separate larger candidate.

**Default:** selected conditions have a cause, stage, usable treatment and a
reason for the next change. **Dial:** no complications, chronic support,
body-specific drug response or severe disease. Drug exposure is somatic state;
family resistance needs its own transmission rule. Avoid turning every known
substance into universal toxicity/dependence.

**In-world reason/cost:** actual medicine, provider competence, cleanliness,
recovery time or a supported constitution. Present treatment prerequisites,
expected improvement and uncertainty. **Browser-idle cost:** M for scheduled
condition transitions; H for spatial epidemic/contact networks and many drugs.
Do not simulate disease spread by checking every actor pair each tick.

**Candidate proof:** care advances only with real inputs; blocked care names the
missing means; the same substance can have different supported body effects.
A chosen offline policy stops at a meaningful complication or continues through
pre-authorized treatment. Current no-hunger-death direction remains in force;
foreign survival models do not override it.

## B5-M08 Acquiring a mutation is a causal operation

**Maps:** G4/G6/G10/G18/G23/G36/G39, GN5/GN11, CN6/CN7, SB5/SB6,
BM4/BM6.
**References:** B5-G09 Qud, B5-G10 DDA, B5-G06 RimWorld.
**Disposition:** adapt in a chosen transformation slice.

**Default:** one collected source supports a specified operation; sampling,
understanding, body installation and expression have separate receipts.
**Dial:** random or directed mutation, temporary expression, distributed mosaic
parts or engineered hereditary change. A technique can change a whole body if
its means support that; no compulsory instability.

**In-world reason/cost:** sample custody, local reagent/energy, a procedure and
development time. A DDA-style reservoir is one model, not required vitamin math.
**Browser-idle cost:** M for one operation; H for large conflict/replacement graphs.
Show supported outcomes, missing prerequisites and lasting effects before
irreversible settlement. Record each operation's method/version/provenance.

**Candidate proof:** collecting a sample does not immediately grant its faculty;
installation changes a real consumer; interruption and removal follow their
declared resource/retention policy. A variant grown while offline appears as the
already committed result, not a new roll on reopening.

## B5-M09 Cultivation changes a process or a working part

**Maps:** G11/G12/G14/G15/G17/G23/G35/G36/G39, PH2/PH3/PH4,
CN1/CN5/CN6, TR1/TR2/TR3/TR5/TR6/TR7, SB1, BM2.
**References:** B5-G14 Tale of Immortal, B5-G15 ACS. **Disposition:** trial a narrow
constitution; park full remolding simulation.

**Default:** a physique changes a real process, such as uptake, storage or work;
a method develops its use. **Dial:** named aptitude modifier, a trained organ,
ACS-like part-label requirements or an entire reconstructed body. Training
benefits and recovery depend on that method; ACS/Tale evidence does not establish
real-world overtraining or universal pill residue.

**In-world reason/cost:** regimen, actual essences/materials, mentor and time;
a breakthrough can change the mechanism. No automatic requirement that every
path trade power for a flaw. **Browser-idle cost:** L/M for one process modifier
and consumer; H for many body labels, plans and scarce essences.

**Candidate proof:** same resource behaves differently under two supported
constitutions; part investment unlocks a usable operation; learning a method
without its executor remains knowledge. Requirements preview distinguishes
passive body effects from an equipped combination. Source qualification of ACS
physical remolding remains open, separate from its existing C01-C10 trace.

## B5-M10 Quirks at the action that uses them

**Maps:** G20/G32/G35/G41, LQ1/LQ2/LQ3/LQ5/LQ6/LQ7/LQ8/LQ11,
MS1/MS4, TR5, BSR1/BSR2.
**References:** B5-G13 Darkest Dungeon, B5-G06 RimWorld, B5-G11 Rogue Legacy.
**Disposition:** strongest small mechanic trial.

**Default:** a quirk has one relevant trigger, useful or inconvenient consequence,
a discoverable origin and a supported way to manage/change it. **Dial:** purely
flavorful, strong, learned, temporary, fixed by lore or inherited through a named
mechanism. A fear learned in one life is not genetic by label alone.

**In-world reason/cost:** practice, accommodation, medicine, counseling, equipment
or a relevant event. Treatment availability can cost time/means; retaining a
quirk can fit the person. **Browser-idle cost:** L per bounded action consumer,
M for autonomous behavior/treatment. Avoid attention drains such as constant
fire babysitting unless that harshness is the selected experience.

**Candidate proof:** a quirk changes the relevant action and leaves unrelated
actions alone; a settled routine handles it quietly; player intervention has a
clear cause. Saves retain origin and status. No five-slot limit, compulsory
negative set or “harder body earns more money” rule imported globally.

## B5-M11 Age, development and teaching

**Maps:** G7/G15/G18/G21/G30/G35/G40, GN12, AG1/AG2/AG3/AG4/AG5/AG6,
TR5, RP5.
**References:** B5-G12 Wildermyth, B5-G02 Sims, B5-G19 Immortality Idle.
**Disposition:** extend existing age/mentor owners, not another age engine.

**Default:** species-specific development and meaningful life events can change
available means; an elder can teach a named successor. **Dial:** childhood play,
time-skip, different peaks, agelessness or rejuvenation. Recruited legacy heroes
and an actual child retain different identity/age policies.

**In-world reason/cost:** maturation, taught practice and caregiver/mentor time.
Inherited capacity does not supply the lesson automatically. **Browser-idle cost:**
M for stage/events and recipient bookkeeping; H for full family life courses.
Queue meaningful transitions rather than changing every physical field each day.

**Candidate proof:** a child receives a supported faculty but still needs
instruction; an adopted student receives a lesson without biological inheritance;
an ageless body does not use ordinary decline. Offline aging and interruption
policy need selection by the owning life-clock workstream, not by this research.

## B5-M12 An honest heir and carry receipt

**Maps:** G8/G10/G15/G27/G28/G29/G30, CL2/CL6/CL10, BP3/BP8, MX5.
**References:** B5-G01 CK3, B5-G11 Rogue Legacy, B5-G12 Wildermyth, B5-G19 Immortality
Idle, B5-G22 BitLife. **Disposition:** adapt inside B3 if prestige is selected.

**Default:** preview each successor's age, real relationships and inheritance:
body faculty, taught method, artifact custody, estate, office and reputation use
separate carriers. **Dial:** optional heir, adopted successor, reincarnation,
legacy hero, randomly offered new person or another supported continuation.
These are different modes; none requires another prestige layer.

**In-world reason/cost:** founder acquisition and preservation, teaching,
arrangements and applicable institutions. No free carry just because a stat
existed at death. **Browser-idle cost:** M for one lineage/event receipt; H for
contested estate branches and full dynasty politics.

**Candidate proof:** a biological child without legal title and an adopted legal
heir receive different supported things; a faculty's executor can be lost while
its method remains. Heirless continuation still has a route. Exact retained
records survive export/import. Ancestral memories/trials need authored carriers;
these games do not by themselves prove BP3/BP8 lore.

## B5-M13 Cultural inheritance and earned family methods

**Maps:** G7/G8/G11/G15/G27/G28/G30/G35, CL10, BP3, TR3/TR5/TR6.
**References:** B5-G12 Wildermyth retirement, B5-G19 Immortality Idle specialty/
techniques; contrast B5-G01 congenital traits. **Disposition:** adapt without a
new genetic model.

**Default:** preserve a named taught method, archive or trade relationship with
an actual custodian/learner. **Dial:** family reputation, independently learned
tradition, technique variants fitted to a changed body or genuine supernatural
memory with a separate source. “Family technique” is not automatically DNA.

**In-world reason/cost:** practice, instruction, records and access. An unrelated
student can learn where the method permits. **Browser-idle cost:** L/M for one
lesson/history consumer; H for generated combinatorial techniques and archives.
Use existing skill/provenance/teaching seams, qualified by the owning implementation
lane rather than claimed newly available here.

**Candidate proof:** a taught successor executes one useful method with their
own means; a blood relative with no access has not learned it. Losing the school
changes access without erasing every person's knowledge. Save custody and dated
instruction, not a generic dynasty XP pool.

## B5-M14 Scale from persons to cohorts deliberately

**Maps:** G3/G7/G8/G9/G10/G18/G40, GN10, RP1/RP5.
**References:** B5-G05 Stellaris, B5-G16 Trimps, B5-G17 IdleOn, B5-G20 Evolve, B5-G23 Critter Mound.
**Disposition:** park population scale until a chosen brood demands it.

**Default:** named family members remain persons; repetitive ecological supply
can use cohorts with explicit counts and local means. **Dial:** fully individual
organisms, aggregated reproduction or a distributed playable network, according
to perspective and lore. Aggregation is an implementation choice, not shared mind.

**In-world reason/cost:** habitat/support, providers, assignments and time. A cohort
cannot create a surgeon or archive just by reaching a population number.
**Browser-idle cost:** M for homogeneous cohorts, H for migration, institutions
and autonomous lineages. Persist referenced individuals and aggregate unreferenced
routine outcomes through the selected history policy.

**Candidate proof:** aggregate birth/accounting conserves resources; promoting
one member to a named actor creates coherent provenance without granting the
player unseen knowledge. Isolation leaves supported local routines intact while
contact-dependent commands pause. No all-world population simulator selected.

## B5-M15 Readable comparison and safe queued continuity

**Maps:** G4/G9/G10/G24/G30/G37/G40, GN9, WR1/WR5, RP5.
**References:** B5-G03 Niche comparison complaints, B5-G05 Stellaris template
integration, B5-G15 ACS requirements, B5-G18 DragonVale timers, B5-G23 Critter Mound sorting.
**Disposition:** accompany a selected mechanism, not a standalone UI build.

**Default:** compare present function, known transmission, current work and
required care side by side; queue settled reversible operations under an explicit
policy. **Dial:** per-part detail, nursery overview, caretaker templates,
confirmation stops or authored harsh autonomous outcomes.

**In-world reason/cost:** known examinations, actual caregiver/lab capacity and
resources. A chosen template applies through a real operation; selecting it
cannot silently edit ancestry or every existing member.

**Browser-idle cost:** M. At 1366x768 keep person/body and operation/result visible
as independent panels; optional detail scrolls locally. Mobile deferred. Event
boundaries and persisted RNG are cheaper/more truthful than rerolling a population
on load. **Candidate proof:** pause on missing support; complete a paid operation
once; show why another remains blocked. Fresh-origin rebuilt browser and
save/reload/literal import must prove results. No offline death/aging/default
policy is inferred from another game's timer.
