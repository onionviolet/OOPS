# B1 world × body-row family matrix

2026-10-14. Docs-only selective proposals; no product decision or implementation.

**D1 coverage:** all actual world folders with briefs, D&D’s foundation, named worlds in the entry/depth/scouting briefs, Fate/HxH nonhuman briefs, TTKS’s five separately named books, four Er Gen titles, the named original cards, three literal-self originals and separately represented home-network destinations. A source world and its original inspired card get separate notes. Goa has its finite mission boundary, not whole One Piece coverage. No digest-only title is silently promoted to a world.

**D2 vocabulary:** `owned` = selected subrows already have bounded source or design ownership; `fit` = inferred suitable addition; `skip` = no selected subrow in this family. A family with owned and fit subrows displays `owned`; each world note lists the exact disjoint subrows. Every other numbered subrow is skipped for this pass. A skip may mean unsupported evidence, outside this start, or poor use of this slice; it is not a canon ban. Every world note supplies its reason. Existing proposals remain proposals; an owned label proves neither primary authenticity nor runtime support.

**D3 scope exclusions:** `templates`, `conceptual`, `world-depth`, `cheat-directions`, `deeper-interactions-audit` and `self-as-player-expansion` are support/work folders. Their named world briefs are covered; folder names are not new worlds. Unnamed genre seeds in MULTIVERSE_STRUCTURES stay unselected. Identity laws’ 171 coverage entries and the 119 digest subjects include motifs/reference works without world briefs; they are source indices, not a required world roster. Fate and HxH now have W10/W11 nonhuman start briefs and are included. Other digest-only franchises require a local world/start brief. Earlier listed source limits are reconciled with newer source owners, not silently treated as current.

**D4 defaults:** separate SG identity/address from body and reproduction where selected. Do not turn family name, manual, registry or office into anatomy. Ordinary food/rest remains comfortable under accepted food direction; no added hunger death. Idle bodily growth needs a supported practice/condition. No generic pill toxicity, compulsory lineage conflict, purity hierarchy, repeated-dose cap or universal clone discount is chosen. G22 supports accommodations; G24 remains schema proposal; G30 belongs to the separate prestige lane.

**D5 source use:** world notes link locators in briefs, ledgers/syntheses, identity/succession and applicable digests. SHOWN/STATED/INFERRED inherit their owner's limits; wiki is dated secondary evidence, MTL keeps U, old general-knowledge rows are leads. This pass does not reread raw novels or fetch live wikis. Public franchise facts are allowed; changed/randomized world truths stay sealed. No sealed file was opened or truth copied.
