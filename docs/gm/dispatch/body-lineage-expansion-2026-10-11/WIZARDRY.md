## Distinct things a wizard can possess

| Thing | What is actually held | What it does not imply by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Hereditary faculty | A transmitted operation, interface, perception or other defined inheritance | Complete training, noble status, loyalty or ancestral memories |
| Constitution | Enduring arrangement of body, mind, soul, core or their relationship | Heredity; eligibility for every school; a mandatory flaw |
| Learned procedure | Understood and practiced instructions carried in a person or record | Its fuel, organ, target information, legal authority or external support |
| Ritual role | A participant's actual contribution to an arranged operation | Ownership of the entire ritual or knowledge held by other participants |
| Pact relation | Access supplied or authorized by a declared party and connection | That descendants or replacement bodies inherit the relationship |
| Artifice | A functioning device, graft or facility with provenance and custody | The user's mastery, the maker's knowledge or unrestricted replication |
| School membership | Access, instruction, responsibilities and social recognition | An inherited faculty, every teacher's knowledge or automatic execution |

A person can hold several at once. A single effect can depend on several of
them without becoming several independently awarded powers. Inborn magic may
be immediately executable; training can improve it instead of withholding its
basic function behind an invented school gate.

## Three causal journeys

**BW-J1: a misunderstood student changes the teaching system.**
An apprentice hears a faulty enchanted joint but fails a visual placement exam
(BW02). A tutor obtains a recorded demonstration, designs an embodied lesson
(BW01) and teaches inspection rather than awarding generic spell mastery. A
maker turns the narrow tested operation into an instrument other workers can
use (BW08, BW04). Repairs improve actual buildings and create demand for
trained inspectors (BW10). The school revises its admission procedure only
after its decision-makers receive evidence. An heir inherits the instruments
and revised curriculum, not the founder's hearing faculty (BW09). The feedback
returns to the individual: more usable tools and better teaching become
available. Scope stops at one joint, one lesson, one instrument and one school
response for a first proof. Each transition is proposed, not executed.

**BW-J2: an inherited interface becomes a durable institution.**
A descendant can operate an ancestral survey lens while an adopted heir owns
the observatory (BW03). Their agreement enables a specific survey, aided by a
familiar whose report actually reaches the lens operator (BW07). A laboratory
reproduces one useful measurement in a public instrument (BW08), allowing more
surveys without copying the whole bloodline. Recorded methods, retained staff
and installed equipment continue after either founder leaves (BW09, BW10).
The regional consequence is an improved route map and new navigation work,
only where the observations support it. Families can retain other advantages;
no forced loss of power pays for broader access. First proof: a lens operation,
two distinct inheritance claims, one received report and one usable map result.

**BW-J3: a changed body and a traveling method make a new workshop.**
A pact-bound wizard changes hosts (BW06), retaining the relation only if its
actual identity carrier survives. One remembered spell requires an adapted
execution procedure (BW01). After crossing, a carried device retains its known
operation but requires the destination's supported supply or a real converter
(BW11). The wizard establishes a workshop with executable instructions and
actual tools (BW04); devices need only the support their mechanism declares
(BW10). A local institution can learn and spread this function through records
and trained people (BW09), altering everyday work. The origin world receives
no automatic news or new technology. First proof: declared retained/lost means,
one destination use and one taught operation, with no invented cross-world rank.

## Retention, knowledge and implementation boundaries

Keep true faculty, diagnosis, recorded evidence and social claim distinct.
Taking a sample can reveal information; it need not transplant a faculty.
Transplanting an organ changes bodily means; it need not transplant allegiance
or memory. Copying a ritual document transfers instructions, not participant
authority. Inheriting a school can convey custody without personal skill.
Teaching transfers the supported learned result; a mentor can teach what their
student can execute through different means. A local exception can transmit
stored procedures or memories when its actual mechanism says so.

For selected implementation, author acquisition, body/source change, actual
consumer, observer access, interruption, repetition, duration, departure,
succession and save restoration where those boundaries affect that detail.
Do not make twelve new compulsory meters or force every wizard into a school,
family, pact or profession. Unusual power can remove a problem outright.

The three journeys are candidate design proofs, not three simultaneous runtime
assignments. None establishes current UI or engine support. Exact-canon
qualification, runtime owner reservation, rebuild, fresh-origin browser use
and applicable save/reload/import remain future gates after a slice is selected.
