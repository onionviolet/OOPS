## What this adds

G1–G15 already distinguish body, constitution, bloodline, method, knowledge,
transmission and institutions. This return deepens those connections.
**New detail** means a concrete operation absent from that palette, rather than
a claim that the topic has never appeared elsewhere in project planning.

BC01, BC03, BC04, BC07, BC08 and BC10 supply new details: measured energy
conversion, body-relative method translation, independently commissioned
breakthrough components, autobiographical body states, dependency-aware
training schedules and physical cultivation infrastructure. BC02, BC05, BC06,
BC09, BC11 and BC12 deepen existing G6/G12/G15 connections.

## Three causal journeys

**BC-J1 The overlooked refiner.** A test measures reservoir capacity and reports
accurate but limited results (BC11). The character instead demonstrates useful
conversion in a workshop (BC01), earns a refining commission, and helps a
teacher document the required bodily procedure (BC03). The school may add a
processing assessment; local craft supply grows if reliable production follows
(BC10). Better tools and commissioned feedstock then help the character refine
their own method. The expansion requires witnessed evidence and real output at
every link, rather than declaring that the whole sect now recognizes a genius.

**BC-J2 A constitution that becomes a teaching tradition.** A founder develops
an actual gripping and channel configuration (BC02/BC05), records a successful
body-relative drill (BC03), and trains several pupils with different supported
anatomies. Their workshop builds compatible training equipment and schedules
shared use (BC08). A mountain settlement becomes reachable through the pupils'
actual traversal means, affecting trade and migration. On succession (BC12),
the appointed teacher receives records and apparatus; descendants inherit only
what the founder's real transmission mechanism carries. Those different heirs
can cooperate or pursue separate ambitions without automatic faction conflict.

**BC-J3 Long-lived public work, separate personal breakthrough.** A mature
character already has a heat-producing constitution, a known refining method
and coherent prior relationships at random entry. They heat a workshop directly,
then create a self-sustaining installation through an available making means
(BC10). New artisans use the reliable service; the institution funds a required
body reconstruction (BC04). The character leaves for advancement, while the
installation continues and its owners keep their own history. Later rejuvenation
(BC07) retains only the defined body changes. Returning does not reclaim the
workshop automatically, reset its workers or award the original commission again.

## Retention and knowledge distinctions

| State | Actual holder / carrier | Avoid conflating |
|---|---|---|
| Root or faculty | Declared body, soul, mark, pact or other mechanism | Affinity label with complete instruction or universal eligibility |
| Current cultivated structure | Specific living or spiritual structure | Appearance, ancestral claim or social title |
| Learned procedure | Person's memory/practice, written record or other supported store | Read manual with practiced execution; inherited capacity with mastery |
| Energy preparation | Current reservoir, vessel or external supply | Last measurement with current charge; changed fuel with compatible fuel |
| Institutional knowledge | Particular examiner, teacher, record keeper and their received evidence | One discovery with faction-wide or region-wide knowledge |
| Service / installation | The sustaining actor or genuinely persistent constructed object | Personal departure with universal shutdown; construction with guaranteed access |
| Inheritance | Actual recipient and carrier of each left-behind thing | Biological descendant with automatic memories, office, property or loyalty |
