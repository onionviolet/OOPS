## Four contrasting original premises

These public illustrative premises are invented here. They are not selected
worlds, hidden discoveries, franchise lore or a shared multiverse law.

| Premise | What a locally named “furnace constitution” actually is | What a locally named “ember bloodline” transmits | Why the decisions differ |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-A bodily furnace | An organ converts ordinary food into usable heat and a particular refining energy | Heritable developmental instructions for growing that organ | Food preparation, organ growth and learned regulation matter; losing local spell infrastructure need not stop the organ |
| P-B resonant furnace | A channel geometry couples to an external field and heats a held workpiece | A transmissible channel arrangement, developed through training | The field and its reachable anchors matter; a complete body can remain intact where the effect cannot couple |
| P-C covenant furnace | An embodied mark authorizes a remote furnace service under an actual covenant | A succession claim whose valid appointment can include an adopted heir | Custody of authority, recognition by the service and communication matter; DNA alone cannot grant the service |
| P-D fabricated furnace | A manufactured core supplies energy and an actuator shapes its output | A family craft tradition passes blueprints and manufacturing skill, while cores are separately owned | Repair, fabrication and teaching matter; a recipient can use an owned core without becoming a genealogical descendant |

No premise is automatically weaker, hostile, corrupting or temporary. A powerful
core or sufficient external connection can solve supply outright. Additional
limits require an actual selected mechanism. The shared names can be a historian's
translation or a traveler's rough analogy, with no claim of identical operation.

## Three causal journeys

**BL-J1 mistaken reading to a wider teaching change (BL01, BL03, BL08).**
A newcomer with a P-A organ passes no signal through a city's P-B admission
instrument. Its reading is accurate about coupling and incomplete about usable
power. The newcomer demonstrates a precise metal-working action to a teacher.
That teacher can witness output, offer measurement lessons and arrange a fitting
examination; the whole city acquires no automatic knowledge. A workshop uses the
newcomer's output, then trains apprentices on available actuators. Over time,
that school adds an evidence route to admission and spreads better temperature
practice. Returning benefit: more students and commissions support the player's
chosen projects. The observer's belief, the test's scope, admission decisions
and true body function remain separate persisted questions.

**BL-J2 a historical industry restored through actual means (BL04, BL05, BL09).**
A later-era character enters a former refining town. Its mills and homes remain,
but an authored field change ended an old channel-dependent step. The character
uses ordinary craft to maintain tools and learns preserved theory from an
accessible archive. A compatible carried source can supply the missing step if
its real coverage is sufficient, or a supported independent core can perform a
replacement process. Craftspeople restore selected work, new supply changes
commissions, and the town develops along a changed course. Return to the
individual: locally repaired tools and collaborators support further practice.
Historical titles and old family claims do not automatically return, and the
new service can genuinely solve the production problem without a forced curse.

**BL-J3 succession and a diaspora become different continuities (BL06, BL07, BL12).**
A founder leaves a workshop, a hereditary organ line, a covenant appointment and
written technique notes. A biological descendant grows the organ; an adopted
successor can operate the covenant; a taught apprentice inherits neither source
but retains their real skill. They need not be enemies and can jointly run the
workshop. One descendant crosses carrying their body and notes, while service
contact has its own reach. A distant branch teaches locally executable parts,
builds a new practice and sends records home only through an available carrier.
Macro result: one ancestry supports several institutions and histories. Return
benefit: the founder's home workshop can later obtain new methods or materials
through real trade. Relatedness, appointments, contacts and shared knowledge do
not collapse into one inherited membership flag.

## Retention, knowledge and continuity distinctions

| Event | Retained or transferred state to declare | Knowledge / recognition boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Body replacement | Organ, channel geometry, soul mark and learned regulation have different carriers | A familiar-looking body proves neither retained powers nor remembered skill |
| Birth, adoption or designated succession | Biological faculty, legal custody, covenant authority and instruction transfer separately | Family belief and an official record may differ from function and valid service access |
| Crossing or returning | Carried source, stored supply, control knowledge and remote connection are resolved independently | Prior-world diagnoses can remain remembered claims; destination means establish current operation |
| Field loss or restoration | Preserved structure, lost support and newly supplied support differ from destroyed anatomy | A failed output can reveal a condition without revealing the full world rule |
| Death of a rare provider | Actual records, objects, surviving users and valid appointments remain; unsupported consciousness does not | Witnesses can remember outputs without knowing how to reproduce them |
| Historical or absent-world progression | Era snapshot and authored continuity policy determine actual changes | News and explanatory history require a record, witness or other accessible channel |

## Source limits and selected-world gates

No primary-source uncertainty was introduced, so no new web pass or named-book
research was performed. These premises claim no Warhammer, cultivation-novel or
wizard-franchise canon. Exact adaptations require their existing source owner,
precise covered passages, selected era and explicit divergence labels. Genre
words such as “physique,” “root,” “bloodline” and “wizard” supply no transmission
law by themselves. No private source library or sealed truth was read or exposed.

These twelve cards are a reusable proposal vocabulary, not twelve compulsory
systems. Before selecting one world slice, qualify its actual local mechanism,
starting snapshot and historical causes, then bind actions, consumers,
observer-specific presentation and retained state. Maps to completion sections:
S01/S02/S04/S05/S07/S09/S11/S12, with S13/S14 for presentation and saves.
Rebuild, fresh-origin browser journeys and relevant save/reload/import checks
remain future gameplay gates. The current task checks only this artifact's
structure, link targets and scope labels.
