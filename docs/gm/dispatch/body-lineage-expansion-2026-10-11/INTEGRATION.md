## What world-based expansion now asks

A selected setting must say what the faculty is, what carries it, what sustains
it, how it develops, what a witness can establish, and how history responds.
These are author questions, not compulsory player meters. Similar words can
describe a bodily furnace, an externally coupled geometry, a covenant interface
or a manufactured core. Those original premises create different daily work,
institutional relations, succession and crossing outcomes.

Three useful proof candidates preserve distinct ambitions:

- A misunderstood body demonstrates a useful function and changes one
  teacher's actual instruction or placement decision (BC-J1/BW-J1/BL-J1).
- A founder leaves distinct faculties, records, tools and appointments, which
  continue through different successors (BC-J2/BW-J2/BB-J3/BL-J3).
- A place's lost supporting condition is supplied or replaced, making one
  surviving process work again without restoring every old title or power
  (BL-J2, with BC10/BW10/BB11 support distinctions).

These are candidates, not selected implementation assignments. They do not
require a weak start or a colony endpoint. G16 in the shared palette routes
this integrated return without copying the entire card inventory.
