# Brood, ecology and biological civilization expansion

2026-10-11. Lane BB, under [the assignment](ASSIGNMENT.md). Exclusive writer:
`/root/body_brood`. All cards and cases below are original proposals, not
accepted mechanics, source-world canon, implemented behavior or executed tests.
No sealed world truths are included. Root owns integration into the
[shared palette](../../../GENETICS_CONSTITUTIONS_AND_BROOD_PLAY.md).

The useful expansion is a chain of actual functions: a body changes a task,
people adapt a practice, and that practice changes a place. The place then
changes what individuals can do. More population, biomass or network nodes do
not by themselves establish knowledge, skill, leadership or production. A
sufficiently strong faculty may perform the entire function directly; facilities
and research steps are not mandatory taxes on exceptional means.

## Three causal journeys

### BB-J1: sample becomes a service, not automatic gene loot

A scavenger acquires a useful fictional filtration specimen (BB01/BB03).
Observation shows a functioning output with a supported companion organism;
this reveals that specific dependency, not every property of either organism.
The scavenger obtains the companion through an actual route and uses the
assembly on one water container (BB10). A clinician learns this application
through records and practice, or a world-qualified direct transfer (BB04).
A household commissions a larger functioning assembly and records custody.
Builders use it in one connected public supply (BB12), changing local water
access and viable settlement options.

Return to micro: reliable supplies let a resident undertake a previously
unsupported long workshop operation. More settlement residents do not
magically improve the original organ. If the route instead has complete
support-free copying, use that actual power and skip invented dependency work.
Acceptance chain: specimen custody → correctly scoped expression → treated
container → real recipient supply → changed work opportunity. All proposed.

### BB-J2: a split network builds a research tradition

A member surveys a crossing and shares its earlier condition (BB05/BB06).
A separate local crew later sees a change while the message link is unavailable.
Both groups continue feasible local duties under their actual agreements
(BB07). Reconnection delivers reports with distinguishable observation context;
it does not force the last arriving message to become truth. An archive stores
both observations and their provenance (BB03), and a reachable specialist uses
them to author a safer inspection method (BB04).

At institutional scale, several cells agree to retain and exchange reports,
with each recipient's access explicit. This becomes a survey tradition rather
than instant collective expertise. Return to micro: the next scout has a useful
inspection checklist and still needs the actual means to execute it. A fully
shared consciousness can be a different valid world mechanism; do not create
these delay rules where it has genuinely simultaneous shared evidence.
Acceptance chain: independent observations → local continuation → coherent
reconciliation → supported teaching → improved actual inspection. All proposed.

### BB-J3: a founder leaves a place that has its own future

An organism constructs a fictional sheltering habitat (BB10) and a living
conduit with an explicit repair method (BB12). A household moves in, adapts
instruments and teaches daily use (BB02/BB09). The founder establishes what
is maintained by its body, by resident workers or by the process itself
(BB11). Local organisms occupy the changed niche through their authored routes,
not spontaneous population rewards.

When the founder departs or dies, surviving residents, tools, learned methods
and any actual self-sustaining habitat continue. A biological heir can inherit
a faculty without owning the conduit; an adopted custodian can maintain it
without that faculty, if its actual means suffice. A regional construction
tradition can then spread by migrants, teachers, plans or functioning samples.
Return to micro: later residents have suitable housing and accessible tools,
so an unusual body becomes a viable ordinary life rather than a forced combat
specialization. Acceptance chain: operating habitat → real occupancy → explicit
maintenance/succession → continuing local life → a carried construction method.
All proposed; neither abandonment nor death automatically causes collapse.

## Retention and knowledge distinctions

The selected implementation should declare the following separately, reusing
its existing state owners rather than adding a second simulation ledger:

| Boundary | What can persist | What does not follow automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Sample acquisition | The sample, actual custody, obtained observations and supported archive copies | Expression, competence, memory, soul authority or complete ancestry knowledge |
| Body alteration | Unrelated knowledge, identity and separately carried faculties; transformed tissue and its actual outputs | Keeping every old body function, losing every old skill, or transmitting a somatic change to children |
| Network interruption | Local bodies, agreed tasks, local evidence and locally held means | New remote observations, orders, refreshed facts or remote resource access without a functioning carrier |
| Separation or changed allegiance | Personal practice, actual memories, property and any independent faculties | Retained shared-channel access, erased ancestry, copied collective knowledge or unconditional loyalty |
| Founder death or succession | Actual surviving people, inherited/transferred assets, records and explicitly surviving connections | Automatic hive collapse, a cloned founder consciousness, full expertise for every heir or universal political recognition |
| World crossing | Whatever body, partner, sample, record, person or faculty the actual crossing carries | A whole institution, shared mental link or all support conditions just because one member arrives |
| Save restoration | Actual state and actor-specific evidence restored coherently under the selected save contract | Promoting diagnoses into truth, duplicating consumed samples or filling unobserved offline history |

Original gene theft can be predatory, scavenged, traded, donated, commissioned,
engineered or granted. Keep actual acquisition and influence truthful; no moral
reward ladder or forced hunting campaign is proposed. Shared access is not
ownership. Identical DNA or copied memories alone does not settle personal
identity; a world can supply an explicit continuity mechanism. Visible evidence,
claimed lineage, actual mechanism and legal recognition remain separate.
