## What to find

1. **Out-of-pocket cheats and systems:** the strangest, most specific
   mechanics (a system that pays you for being slapped, a cheat that only
   works while lying, a skill shop priced in embarrassment). At least 60
   distinct entries across at least 25 titles.
2. **Abilities as gal-game heroines:** affection, routes, jealousy,
   endings, confession-as-breakthrough. Every variant you can find.
3. **Abilities as monsters/Pokémon:** catching, training, branch evolution,
   fusion, types, trading, storage, shiny/unique variants; include 御兽
   (beast-taming) and Pokémon fan fiction.
4. **Premise swaps** fan fiction and web novels use: cheat to the villain,
   system to a side character, genre swap, world with inverted rules.
5. For each entry: title (original and English), platform/link, the
   mechanic in two plain sentences of paraphrase, what makes it out of
   pocket, and one way it maps onto YAIR's life sim (a cheat, a world rule
   or a small touch). Mark how far you read (synopsis, opening, deep).
