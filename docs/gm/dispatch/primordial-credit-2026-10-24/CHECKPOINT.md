## Seam chosen and why
Fallback, not the standing route (the decision rule's default was available on paper because `src/economy_standing.js` is unmodified). Reasons, each in current code: (1) `creditView()` and `view().factions` in `economy_standing_domain.js` map every key of `FACTIONS`, so a new lender shows in every player's Standing panel, and that domain and `economy_standing_ui.js` are modified by another session; (2) `takeLoan` sets `due = now + 28 days` and `tickStanding -> settleDue` ratchets and drops standing after 31 days, which cannot hold a ten-year flat schedule; (3) `borrow()` passes no body value, pays out through `addCoins` (coins, not CP) and the UI offers Borrow 10 and 25 only. So: a new domain `primordial_credit` with its own optional slice, using the obligations ledger shape, `defineGate` and the carry ledger. The standing UI line is absent.
