# Power-system surveys, 2026-10-10 (common rules)

Dispatched by Claude Code at Weibao's request: "maybe make codex lanes to
look across the types of possibilities like chainsaw man and other series
and more?" Three lanes (A anime and manga, B Chinese and Korean web fiction,
C Western books, comics, games and myth) share these rules.

## Read first

`docs/EMERGENT_SYSTEMS.md`, `docs/RULES_FROM_SCRATCH.md`, and in
`docs/emergent/`: `E8_POWER_ASPECTS.md` (ten axes), `E8B_POWER_CATALOG.md`
(families, sources, delivery, vow gaming), `E8C_POWER_PATHS_AND_ANIME.md`
(path parts and shapes), `E8D_VOW_SYSTEM.md` (vow slots, JJK section).
Check existing research to avoid repeats: `docs/research/` and
`docs/LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR.md` (search series names).

## Sources and limits

- Mechanics only, in your own words. No copied prose, panels or long
  quotes; at most a few short terms. Public build uses original names;
  franchise names are fine in research notes.
- Use public references: official sites, Wikipedia, fan wikis, author
  statements, and local copies listed in
  `docs/research/novels/LOCAL_LIBRARY.md`. Fetch politely per
  `docs/research/novels/SUBAGENT_RUN_RULES.md` ("Be a polite client"):
  serial per host, 4 to 8 s gaps, robots honored, stop on challenge, no
  logins or purchases. No pirated full text.
- Mark each claim's source; separate verified from remembered.
- No YAIR `src/` edits, builds or commits.

## What to produce per series

A row set mapping the series' power system onto our model:
- **Path** (E8C parts): currency, steps, gate, risk, shape, branching,
  grader, ceiling.
- **Families** used (E8B) and **sources** of power.
- **Axes** (E8) where the series makes subtle variants.
- **Vows, contracts and costs** (E8D slots): what is traded, how breaking
  is punished, interpretation tricks, collective rules.
- **Unique mechanics** worth absorbing, and **gag or rule-abuse** moments
  the series is known for (paraphrased).
- One-line "what we take" per mechanic, marked proposal.

## Output

`docs/research/power-systems/<lane>/`: `SERIES.json` (structured rows),
`REPORT.md` (plain-language summary, top 15 mechanics ranked by how much
they add, a cross-series table of vow and contract designs, and proposals
for E8 to E8D), `SOURCES.md`, `CHECKPOINT.md`. Plain English, short words,
no em dashes. Don't edit the E8 files; Claude folds results in.
