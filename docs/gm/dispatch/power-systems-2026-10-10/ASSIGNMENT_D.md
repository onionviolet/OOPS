# Lane D: broad scout for power-type ideas, with source requests

Follow `ASSIGNMENT_COMMON.md` in this folder. Output to
`docs/research/power-systems/broad-scout/`. Weibao: "send out broad
research, but then make a point to make me find source material if need
be".

## Goal

Find books and series whose **central idea** is an unusual power type or
rule, across Chinese, Korean and Japanese web fiction, light novels and
Western web serials, using public metadata only (official synopses, tags,
rankings, reviews, wiki summaries). Lanes A to C (done, in
`docs/research/power-systems/`) covered famous series; read their REPORTs
and skip repeats. Focus on lesser-known titles with sharp hooks, like
Weibao's picks: a mimic hiding among wizards, drawing one trait a year, a
JJK fan world, inheriting all of humanity, a wasteland border inspector.

Lenses: kinds of observation, future sight, immortality and revival,
vows and contracts (including joke or gamed vows), shards and inheritance,
power paths with odd currencies, rule-abuse and gag physics, simulators and
draws, mimics and hidden identities, inspectors and rule enforcers,
systems with strange prices.

## Output

- `CANDIDATES.json` and `REPORT.md`: 40 or more candidates, each with
  title, author, platform, a one-paragraph mechanic summary in your words,
  which lens it serves, and a 1 to 5 score for how new the mechanic is.
- For the top 10 that need full text, **append rows to
  `docs/research/SOURCE_REQUESTS.md`** (IDs S6 onward) with the best URL
  Weibao can open in his browser (ttks, 69shu, official site) and why.
  Don't edit other rows.
- Polite fetching only; stop a host on its first challenge. Never pass bot
  checks or log in.
