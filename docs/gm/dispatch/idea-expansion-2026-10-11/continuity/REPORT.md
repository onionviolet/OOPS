## Contract defects to resolve rather than inherit

| Code | Earlier wording or assumption | Required treatment |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | A branch can always be rebuilt from canon and seed, and hundreds fit under rough estimates | Full played actor/custody/knowledge/commitment/RNG history can exceed that model. Qualify delta completeness and measure storage/performance for declared scope; lossy summaries do not promise exact resume. |
| R2 | Same-ID branches can merge by union, or near-equal worlds can collapse | IDs alone do not resolve conflicting revisions, debt, actors, unique goods or causal events. Preserve conflict lineage and adjudicate ownership atomically. |
| R3 | Knowledge stages imply permanent certainty or a single overwritten account | Actual readings can be mistaken, contradicted or stale. Preserve dated evidence and confidence separately from current interpreted truth; generic rank-monotonic knowledge cannot silently solve this. |
| R4 | Appraisal always has a price/counter/rank ceiling; selves always teach rather than grant | Those are illustrative source variants. Explicit free, inexhaustible, omniscient, direct-transfer or materialization functions retain their actual mechanisms. G2 qualifies their function; G4 handles information and identity consequences. |
| R5 | Known instances imply general branches, or a rewind checkpoint implies full time travel | Existing finite native bindings are useful foundations, but each widened owner set needs exact integration and compatibility proof. Do not duplicate an existing mechanism merely because an old audit labels it missing. |

Director-facing criteria remain generic. A selected sealed reviewer can inspect
hidden truth and route fairness, but reports only opaque IDs, route and evidence
stage. Player actions can be valuable before certainty without revealing the
answer through a disabled-action hint, clock display, atlas label or error.
