## F1 Actual consumer boundary

Native recipes already separate skill, station tier, component tier and numeric
quality. `Recipe.get_success_chance` reads relevant skill/station and the equipped
accessory adapter. `get_quality_range` reads skill, component quality and tier.
These are useful foundations, but they are not arbitrary material-property or
part-interface checks. Do not replace native quality with a new rarity scheme.

The furnishing definitions have physically supplied rack/bench phases, exact
stock drying, notebook adaptation, fit-stop stave work and sharp-to-dull kit
production. The notebook is an actual held/returned carrier. Current furniture
projection adds owned, accessible passive functions; native rest/sleep and XP
consumers in `main.js` read it. These are source-inspected claims. Their wave's
browser and director gates remain with its existing owner.

The retort has real input/fuel/receiver reservations and separate collection and
residue disposal. It refuses a new batch when its actual receivers have no
space. It uses a finite work plan, not an arbitrary factory network. Existing
site projects compile finite prerequisite phases into shared jobs/claims.

The material/crop lane binds a measured strip to an actual observed sample and
joins one opted-in crop/drain boundary. The native weather owner exposes
location/time-dependent temperature, rain and light. Neither establishes a
general ecology, expiry, hunger, durability or climate-damage system. Keep these
local choices rather than inferring defaults from absorption prototypes.

`previewPowerModel` supplies selected scalar functional capacity and costs;
`resolveEffectAmount` uses bounded numeric formulas. A dynamic component graph,
multichannel industrial solver, stable arbitrary specimen installation/removal,
regional adoption and general experimental-evidence model remain missing.
