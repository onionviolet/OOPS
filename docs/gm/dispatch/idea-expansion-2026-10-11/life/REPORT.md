## What was missing and what is retained

**F1: Social foundations already exist.** Simulation people, directed relationships, observer knowledge, custody, claims, sites, finite purses, timed paid work and registered controllers are source foundations. Society's Hessa/Orr/Vale and Frontier's finite orders/Jun operation show several relevant actual consumers. Village teaching terms are another bounded source example. They are retained rather than declared absent or rebuilt generically. None establishes every NPC's autonomous lifetime, a full market, an entire institution or cross-world contact.

**F2: More meters would not fix the main gap.** E3 offers useful goal/planner ideas but its rest, belonging, status and purpose numbers are proposed world choices. A real person needs a reason and means to act, not compulsory universal hunger or affection. Target selection, generated actor validation, evidence/recognition, general agreement lifecycle and local actor/native integration remain precise contracts. A ghost need not require belief, nor a deity worship, merely because a palette table lists that form.

**F3: The middle and maturity of lives deserve their own completion leaves.** Existing family and branching audits already distinguish ancestry, care, retirement, inheritance, rights, methods, loyalty and ongoing livelihoods. The Royal Road report independently locates shallow aging, parenting, long marriage, careers, domestic nonhuman lives and money/housing/illness progression. G3 turns these into functioning detail cards without treating synopsis ideas as verified novel canon. A cozy life can remain cozy; an exceptional person can choose ordinary work without their capability being weakened.

**F4: State and knowledge need different owners.** A witnessed event, rumor, belief, trust, promise and title differ. Current knowledge replaces one value for a topic at equal or higher stage; richer competing evidence is not established. Relationship effects use `if (!s.causes[receipt])`, so a clock-zero cause remains a named deduplication risk. No repair is made here. Permanent commitments cannot depend on wording or bounded display memories. G4 should own evidence and clocks; G5 should join sole effect writers and projection. This lane owns social meanings and outcomes.

**F5: Scale must follow actual causal chains.** A household project may improve a service, free a worker's time, change supply and then alter a region. Each step needs an actual consumer. It cannot stop at a successful quest flag or institution name. Powerful cheats can remove real scarcity or labor; consequences come from remaining distribution, interests and local means, rather than artificial replacement costs.

## Functional journeys and causal chains

### J1 ordinary workshop and continuing home life

Start in a coherent low-income household with an actual language, body, shared shelter, tools and reachable work supplied by G1. Meeting the joiner reveals a funded order, including who owns its sample and who can teach. Choose wages, apprenticeship or a different available pursuit. Knowledge of the method remains separate from practice and executable body/tool support. A real completed repair returns its loaned tool and pays an actual worker from the payer's purse.

The repaired roof changes the household's actual support or usable space only if the selected environment consumer supports that function. The free space can permit a care shift or a funded lesson. A resident who receives that lesson can perform their own supported task and retain their own wage. A useful refinement reduces real work time/material loss when its method consumer allows it. Saved receipts support different later greetings. The ordinary end is stable specialist work, shared home, teaching or retirement, not mandatory mayorhood.

Micro-to-macro chain: tested joint → usable repair → changed household capacity → funded resident work or study → supplied neighboring service → broader workshop contract. Each arrow is its own observable leaf; if the service cannot consume the repair output, the chain stops at the named missing consumer rather than a claim of regional change.

Variants include no hunger, well-fed bonuses, actual household stock or harsh ration policies. Poor support is allowed and must preserve realistic reachable agency, not guarantee free help. Applicable source rules can make a person refuse, demand payment, teach for nothing or act coercively. No generic consent bar is pasted onto the world.

### J2 exceptional power meets a lived economy

An exceptionally strong resident or traveler really performs an otherwise difficult operation: moving a load, repairing many supports, creating food, healing or enabling transport. G2 defines the effect; G6 identifies actual outputs/capacity. If the function has no cost, no new cost is invented. The world does not pretend the method is slow or weak to maintain an earlier wage loop.

A receiver knows only the demonstrated result. The recipient still needs distribution, storage, access or qualified handling only when these remain real causes. A newly supported clinic can treat more people through actual staffing and compatible supplies; competitors may adapt, collaborate, seek a monopoly, threaten or ignore it according to their means. The player can donate, charge, delegate, hide the method or leave where those routes are possible. Capacity, ownership, public recognition and private relationships can diverge.

Micro-to-macro chain: actual power execution → conserved outputs or actual relief → recipient operation changes → buyer/client alternatives change → institution's funded service changes → political/social response through actual observers. The acceptance checks must compare ordinary and exceptional actors against the same fixed local task, and show superior function succeeding where applicable.

### J3 mature family, career and successor

A retiring teacher has an actual unique tool, a workshop-use right, teachable expertise and several relatives/apprentices with separate goals. No fixed family cast is installed by this report. The world GM authors the specific source-local people. Preparing succession during life can preserve a record, train a learner, fund a reserve or transfer a tool. The donor can retire and remain a person with ongoing pursuits.

At the selected transition, resolve surviving assets, custody, valid rights, actual learned/transmissible methods and local debt law separately. An adopted successor can inherit the tool without physiology; an unrelated apprentice can hold the best refinement without owning the workshop; kin can leave the trade or refuse obligations. Nothing is recreated just because the prior life once owned it. Continuing play follows the heir, the retiree or an unrelated life according to the selected control/chronicle policy, which belongs to G1/G4/G5.

Micro-to-macro chain: kept lesson/loan → qualified successor capability → valid role or asset transfer → actual service continues or changes → household/community remembers supported events → later generation inherits only remaining carriers. Missing life-control/succession consumers remain explicit, and the Village public yearly list does not stand in for these operations.

### J4 communication and disputed local history

A familiar person says a freight worker broke a promise. The receiver can ask for terms, inspect an actual receipt, ask an available witness, disclose evidence, keep a confidence or decline mediation. Hearing the allegation and believing it are separate. The world GM supplies real source-local voice and context before final dialogue. A translator can help read the terms if it has that scope, without revealing the speaker's motives.

Micro-to-macro chain: received claim → witness-specific belief → bounded chosen disclosure → specific access or contract response → local institutional procedure → wider transmission if an actual channel exists. A corrected private belief does not globally rewrite reputation, and an unseen world secret stays sealed.
