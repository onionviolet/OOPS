## Earned, research, resources and roguelike structure

P2, proposed meaning: earned describes a concrete cause in play, not a compulsory
grind or universal experience shop. Studying an anchor can reveal a method;
experimentation can validate it; practice, teaching, crafting, a found component,
a bargain or a breakthrough can provide or apply it. A compatible gift or
special start can bypass that path. Not every improvement requires all steps.

The wording "researces" may mean research or resources; account for both without
requiring a clarification. Research can be chosen study/experimentation using
known clues, texts, observations, suitable tools and eligible routine time.
Resources can come from actual work, exploration, trade, crafting, gifts or
the relevant cheat. Avoid a generic research-point currency unless its source
and useful gameplay justify it. A timer alone does not explain a discovery.
Memory of a method does not automatically supply a new body's tools/materials.

P3: roguelike/roguelite structure and cheat improvement are compatible. A life
can vary its circumstances, opportunities, body and build, while the continuing
self learns and the cheat gains durable functions. Not every run needs random
loot or a run-ending death. Meaningful activity can cause growth before return;
return settles and displays the result rather than necessarily minting points.
Death-based rewards remain possible for a specifically authored power, not an
automatic reward for repeatedly ending the same opening.

P4: applied improvements to the continuing base cheat should normally persist;
a local laboratory, artifact, patron connection or physical anchor follows its
actual backing. Thus remembering how to build a checkpoint differs from carrying
one. No universal attachment schema or retention rule is selected yet.

Anchor improvement examples, not commitments: place a later checkpoint; improve
date/arrival control; retain a trained faculty or a compatible item; strengthen
reliability; extend reach or number of usable anchors where meaningful. Prefer
one visible new capability over an unexplained tier number. Charges, sacrifice,
counterplay, prestige resets and fixed cap ladders are not mandatory.

## Research and the first improvement: focused proposals

P8, research access: studying the base cheat should have an approachable starting
lead when the character can investigate it, rather than requiring an unrelated
advanced cheat first. Reflection on known effects, a non-destructive observation
or a teacher/text can start the route. A mature ordinary village start could
study and practice placing a return point. An infant or nonhuman start follows
its available faculties or supplied means; no identical action menu is assumed.

P9, research loop: observe a specific limitation, form a usable idea, investigate
through an appropriate routine/experiment, then apply and verify a concrete
improvement. This is a design example, not four mandatory stages for every route.
Routine study may progress passively under accepted routine rules; decisions
pause when needed. No mandatory suicide experiment, death count, new currency or
repeat-the-tutorial gate is proposed. A compatible discovery or gift can supply
the improvement directly. Costs and safe tests must come from the chosen means.

Knowledge and applied improvement may persist differently: learning a recipe
can survive without its workshop; modifying the continuing base cheat can remain
part of it. How unfinished research resumes depends on whether it consists of
remembered insight, external notes, samples or an active apparatus. Keep useful
recorded understanding rather than blindly resetting one generic research bar.

P10, early carryover candidates: a compatible seed of energy that helps rebuild
capacity; a bound faculty; or an acquired cheat whose actual carrier transfers.
The seed must have a useful consumer, such as enabling an otherwise unavailable
first exercise, rather than being only a decorative number. Define regeneration,
accumulation and conversion before using repeated returns as a power source.
An authored compounding cheat remains possible; neither accidental duplication
nor a universal ban on compounding is a design decision.

## Continuity outcomes: do not collapse them into one death menu rule

P19: temporal return restores an earlier state; revival restores or supplies a
living embodiment at the current date; transfer moves the continuing self to a
vessel; sealing can preserve existence while restricting agency. A technique
can combine them, but survival alone does not guarantee freedom or recovery.

A backup may be a prepared empty body, an independently living clone or another
kind of vessel. Transfer, replacement, shared control and merging need different
identity/control rules. Do not silently overwrite an independent clone or grant
control of every clone merely because it resembles the player. A technique's
concrete acts, requirements and consequences matter more than an "evil" tag;
none require a universal morality meter or mandatory corruption mechanic.

A ring can be a player-created refuge, an emergency carrier, an enemy prison or
a replacement form. Its senses, thought, communication, mobility, available
powers and release/return routes follow its actual means. An enemy seal does
not automatically strip the accepted base cheat; blocking it needs an actual
supported mechanism and suitable known warning, with discovery limits respected.
Nor does base-cheat persistence imply it can overcome every specific suppression.
Exact starter fallback and suppression rules remain open. Locally trapped play
can require another route or return under accepted recovery direction.
