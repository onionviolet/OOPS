## Separate the state that changes

| Distinction | Actual meaning | What does not follow automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Detect observation | Evidence that a qualified channel is sampling this subject now | Observer name, motives, body, location or reach |
| Identify observer | Supported attribution to a person, endpoint, era or institution | Ability to contact, harm or compel that observer |
| Reach observer | A source can send an eligible signal/action through this specific channel | Arbitrary access to everything the observer owns |
| Change observation | Alter the channel, sampling, outgoing representation or allowed record | Change the historical event or the observer's memory |
| Recognize construction | A resident has evidence or a tested model of a constructed place | Knowledge of its maker, universal falsity of residents or escape |
| Escape a boundary | Actual body/source/identity continuity crosses a declared boundary | A fresh duplicate, access to every layer or immunity afterward |
| Acquire persistence | One represented continuation obtains an enduring anchor/identity support | Restored donor life, all predecessor powers or universal resurrection |

Detection is not identification. A mistaken identification remains an actor's inference with evidence and confidence; it does not replace author truth. Knowledge of a method is distinct from ownership of its source, physical support and usable execution. Superior sources can legitimately outperform weak variants in every relevant dimension. Do not invent equal fuel taxes, high-level immunity or a mandatory ordinary counter.

### Proposed references, not installed APIs

`ActorContinuationRef = {actorId, identityRevision, lifeRef, bodyRef, originRef}`. Appearance, represented role and remembered biography are separate records. Changing a name or wearing a body does not silently change this identity.

`ObservationRef = {observationId, revision, sourceOwner, sourceInstance, subjectRef, observerRef, endpointRef, channelRuleId, openedBy, openedAt, sourceTimeRef, observerTimeRef, activeSupportRefs, disclosurePolicy, status}`.

`EvidenceRef = {evidenceId, recipientId, causeRef, sampledRef, sampledRevision, observedAt, channelId, content, attribution, confidence, staleRule}`. Detection and false attribution can both be saved. A caller-supplied actor ID is not evidence that the subject knows their name.

`BoundaryRef = {boundaryId, revision, containingContext, containedContext, makerRef, admittedDomains, exitRuleId, continuationPolicy}`. A maker need not have absolute control over every resident/source. An authored construction can contain real stock, independent goals and durable history.

`HistoricalLinkRef = {linkId, sourceEraRef, observerEraRef, continuityId, revision, direction, permittedTransforms, chronologyPolicy, causeRef}`. Archive footage, a model, a fixed self-consistent link and a new historical branch are different modes.

`PersistenceAnchorRef = {anchorId, revision, ownerRef, holderRef, supportedContinuation, exclusiveClaimRef, actualSupport, transferRuleId, consumptionOrDisplacement, termination, acquisitionReceipt}`. It distinguishes an anchor's ownership from who is physically holding it.

These are requirements for registered adapters, not current JSON effect types. The tiny proof uses one finite authored relation via existing facts/situation receipts; broader claims need validated new records. No free-form concept strings, eval or second state writer.

### Shared causal order

1. Preview derives known legality/costs from current sources, body, target, custody and the observer's supported evidence. It creates no link, disclosure, draw or receipt.
2. Commitment rechecks the exact request and sequence, freezes allowed targets/mode/revisions, pays/reserves actual inputs and creates a cause. Opening a channel is an action; rendering its panel is not.
3. Resolution applies the selected rule order: support/contact, signal/detection, identity disclosure, permitted response, transformation, termination. A source can skip a step only through an explicit function that supplies it.
4. Observation projects only qualified information to each recipient. Record what was sensed versus inferred, with channel/time and revision. Changing outgoing content does not erase already received evidence.
5. Persist changed owners, inputs, partial effects, identity/custody and receipts before native/UI publication. Interruption retains completed consequences. Restoration returns only declared domains, not spent fuel, lost goods or other people's memories.

The existing shared scheduler has its own order: due jobs, conditions/life/events, pursuit, sorted controllers, then programs. It is not a general simultaneous-action resolver. Each future relationship declares its ordering domain instead of assuming whichever order makes the scene work.

## Family 1: the subject answers the lens

### Worked situation: The Returned Gaze

At an optional Village demonstration, the player opens a public viewing instrument. It shows a visitor in a screened yard. The visitor, Ilex, taps the inside terminal and a line appears beside the image: “There is a listener at the outer glass.” An ordinary visual observer could not see through the screen. The active lens itself supplies the return path.

Author truth: `observation.ilex` is one persistent simulation actor in `base.frontier`. The instrument has exactly one paired inner/outer terminal, one current viewing channel and one local shutter. Its initial kit belongs to Ilex. Opening creates the actual channel; no knowledge is awarded merely for meeting an eligibility condition or opening Notebook.

Ilex owns an awakened, active Return Gaze source and a learned return-notice mode. His living perceptual support is present. This original variant needs no fuel payment or cooldown; its power comes from the acquired faculty and this supported channel. It detects sampling and can modify the image at the inner terminal. It cannot identify an anonymous external person from an internal actor reference, leave the yard through an information channel, read their biography or steal their body. A stronger source variant could identify/reach them if it truly supplies those functions; it would be a different rule, not unfairly weakened to match this fixture.

| Operation | Means and evidence | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Detect | Active source, living support, currently open paired channel | Ilex knows a listener is present; he does not know their name |
| Identify | Player deliberately sends an introduction through the terminal | Ilex knows the claimed present identity and agreed exchange, not verified past lives |
| Reach | Channel admits text/image return signals | Ilex addresses the endpoint; this supplies no bodily travel or HP attack |
| Change | Inner terminal admits a registered overlay/cut operation | Ilex can place the message or terminate this stream; old observations persist |

Opening's single transaction stages the actor/source/power/channel and an explicit eligible NPC detection. The implementation must execute the NPC mode through its actual power command, not write “noticed” merely because a player chose a label. No attempt cadence or random roll is needed for this genuinely reliable faculty.

### Choices and remembered aftermath

**A, mechanical closure:** install a wooden shutter at the outer terminal using one actual owned Rough wood log and two native stamina. Known native strength/support is required. The action consumes the log into an installed fixture and closes the actual sampling channel. Ilex records a terminated anonymous link; he cannot attribute it to a named player. The player keeps evidence that ordinary physical closure worked and receives no kit. Later inspection sees the same installed shutter, without another debit. This succeeds because it stops the channel, not because low-level wood defeats a high-level mind.

**B, cooperative disclosure:** choose to introduce yourself and permit one bounded calibration exchange. Pay one native wooden coin; Ilex gives one real Prepared survey kit from his own stock and explains how current samples differ from archived impressions. Staged currency debit/credit and item transfer must conserve the actual owners. This teaches understood provenance checking, not Return Gaze ownership or execution. The link closes after the exchange. Later Ilex can recognize that introduction, remember the exchange and discuss the checked record; he has spent his kit and retains payment. B buys useful supplies and instruction while giving Ilex identity information that A preserved.

**Abstain:** leave or keep silent. Three played minutes after opening, the inner shutter Ilex preset closes the stream. He keeps his kit and receives no payment. A later return to Village discovers the dark lens and his posted closure note. The preset cutoff does not depend on Return Gaze remaining active. No silent promise, debt, hostility, fake reward or offscreen body attack is created. The player's knowledge does not update remotely just because the instrument changed. Never opening the instrument enrolls no situation at all.

**Unsupported attempt:** a cloth disguise changes visible appearance but leaves the sampling link active, so it does not defeat this faculty. Replaying stored footage with the live channel closed causes no new detection: the subject is not being sampled. Looking again at the same evidence cannot farm kit, learning rewards or relationships. If the actual source is suppressed before a fresh activation, no return-notice occurs; do not substitute author omniscience.

For the tiny proof, player routes are immediate saved transactions. This avoids claiming general contact rechecks for delayed jobs. Shutdown wins only if committed before the source-cut boundary; at/after the deadline the automatic cut resolves first. Withdrawal/closing the UI retains the opened channel until a legal closure or source cut. A failed save publishes no opening, source activation, message, kit transfer, knowledge or cost.

### Useful follow-through

Evidence gives practical choices: identify a live versus stored report before relying on it, ask permission before inspecting a person, close an actual instrument rather than hide from its image, or bring sufficient body/force to break a supported terminal. This small scene can later support archive work, source calibration, privacy disputes and stronger return channels without claiming those families are delivered now.

## Family 2: a historical subject detects a later reader

### Worked situation: The Message Under the Second Seal

An old flood ledger contains an authored observation seal. In the present, the player can activate it to watch archivist Vey during a historical evacuation. Vey has Epoch Sense, which detects this later sampling channel and its direction. The ordinary written ledger alone has no such link.

Source/support: the present seal is an actual charged owned fixture; one activation consumes one seal charge. Vey's period body and acquired faculty can detect and answer the qualified channel at no added fuel cost in this variant. The temporal boundary admits a short signal, not cargo or a body. Hearing Vey does not grant the faculty, a temporal body or rewind authority. An advanced unrelated source that actually admits objects or bodies remains superior and is not arbitrarily reduced to a text message.

**Select a chronology mode before writing runtime.** This worked case uses a fixed-history, self-consistent message. Narrative order is present activation → witnessed historical encounter → present reading of its retained mark. Chronological order is Vey receiving the later observer's committed message in the old era → evacuation/ledger sealing → present activation that fulfills the link. Neither order advances the live save into the past.

| Information or change | Direction and owner | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Historical visual sample | Old scene → present reader | Reveals only the seal's qualified scene window |
| Detection of later gaze | Link → Vey's historical knowledge | Identifies a later endpoint, not every future person/event |
| Chosen short message | Present commitment → Vey | One authorized signal; its content is a retained cause |
| Ledger mark | Vey → old ledger → present reader | Makes the committed message recoverable in the same history |
| Evacuation outcome | Existing historical causal record | No mutation in this selected mode |

**A, investigate without contact:** spend ordinary study time reading intact records and corroborate the seal's date. Learn the safe present sluice route from qualified records without opening a temporal link, identifying yourself or paying a charge. You miss Vey's private oral explanation and retain ordinary uncertainty.

**B, open and reply:** consume the seal charge, observe Vey's reply, then choose an allowed message such as your agreed alias or “leave the second seal intact.” Vey writes that exact committed content in an earlier unread compartment. On return, inspecting the compartment yields the matching message and provenance. That correspondence is useful evidence of a real link. It grants no generic world reset or guaranteed arbitrary paradox. A mutually exclusive message spends the same charge and produces a different disclosure, even if the evacuation remains fixed.

**Abstain:** leave the sealed ledger untouched. Present repairs and other records remain usable; Vey acquires no knowledge from this activation, and no abandoned temporal debt is imposed. A previously activated link interrupted before sending retains the actual visual evidence and consumed activation charge, with no unsent message arriving in history.

Causality gate: seal the observer's selected message into an immutable `HistoricalLinkRef` before projecting its historical receipt. A present compartment is explicitly unread until that cause exists; it cannot simultaneously hold a contradictory pre-discovered message. Invalid contradictory histories reject rather than retcon remembered evidence. If arbitrary historical intervention is later wanted, use a new branch with separately owned causal state and native bindings. Show which origin record remains, which branch changes, and what knowledge travels; do not overwrite the old ledger in place.

Vey might spoof his identity or misinterpret the epoch. A signal establishes an endpoint and correspondence, not omniscience. Failed storage sends no message and consumes no charge. Replayed activation cannot create a second contradictory receipt. Stale branch/era/seal revisions reject. Suppressing Vey's faculty can stop detection without changing what an ordinary record contains. A broken seal closes further signals, while completed disclosure and already written marks remain.

Current-engine fit: paid commands, attributed knowledge and an original fixed scene can present this case as an authored archive illustration. General cross-time signaling, self-consistency, historical record revelation and mutable branches require O3 below. A current clock number or `situation.context.eraId` is not that implementation.

## Family 3: a resident tests and challenges a constructed place

### Worked situation: The Room That Repeats Its Rain

The player enters a research chamber containing an enduring modeled quarter. Resident engineer Sen notices that a broken rain gauge resets while her remembered measurement does not. She asks the player to perform a test. This is evidence of a local construction with a selective reset, not proof that the whole campaign or player is unreal.

Author truth: the chamber's construction boundary resets the weather fixture, not resident identity, memory, borrowed stock or every goal. Sen is one durable resident with her own inventory and knowledge. The reset marker is an authored chamber operation, not browser reload. Construction can be mistaken for looping weather, repaired equipment or deception until a controlled experiment supports the inference.

**A, ordinary test and accommodation:** lend a marked brass weight under exact custody, measure before/after one chamber rain cycle and compare a saved external reading. Sen retains the loan and experiment receipt until its lawful return. This confirms which fixture resets and supports moving her workshop away from the reset zone. Sen stays, works and benefits; awareness need not make escape desirable or possible.

**B, actual boundary crossing:** if the player has an acquired Doorwright source with body interface, maintained gate support and scope that admits Sen's actual continuation, open the supported exit. Sen chooses whether to cross. The operation transfers her body/owned cargo/obligations and retained identity once into the containing context. Her workshop stays inside unless separately admitted. A sufficiently superior source that can cut this boundary may cross without the research path, when its real function supports it; do not add an “NPCs cannot leave” immunity merely to preserve the puzzle.

**Abstain:** stop experimenting. Sen continues her own work from what she actually knows and may seek another collaborator. She does not automatically solve the construction or receive the player's future information. A later visit retains her stock, loan and partial evidence. A resident who wrongly infers a construction records that belief; the engine does not immediately grant a true boundary.

Targets/reach: Doorwright addresses this boundary and admitted actor/cargo, not the real host, every containing layer or the maker's memory. Acquired knowledge of the source's procedure cannot replace its owned faculty/fuel/eligible body. Breaking the construction is distinct from escaping it; a destructive mode needs its own affected domains, interruption and selected casualty/life policy. This packet selects neither destruction nor lethal/offline consequences.

Interruption: stop before crossing and retain spent gate startup, experiment evidence and actual loan; no actor teleport. Stop after Sen crosses and retain her external identity/body and remaining interior workshop. Do not roll the move back merely because the UI closes. Two actors cannot simultaneously occupy the same unique body in different contexts; a projected appearance is separately typed.

Current-engine fit: a persistent resident, facts, paid experiments, custody and directed knowledge are feasible. Separate construction domains, real reset projection, actor-specific boundary relocation, cargo/publication and independent native worlds require O2/O4. The current transition planner's world metadata does not establish them. Repeated rain text alone cannot pass this case's gate.

## Family 4: a represented person obtains an enduring anchor

### Worked situation: The Empty Berth

An archive performs a reconstruction of cartographer Arin, whose remembered life ended long ago. Its current resident has developed new measurements and remembers their own conversations. The archive normally retires this resident when the performance ends. Arin learns that an unused return-anchor outside the reconstruction can support one continuation and attempts to acquire it.

This is an original mechanism, not a Shadow Slave claim. Distinguish historical Arin, the archive's reference model, and the one current represented Arin. Continuing the represented resident does not establish that the deceased historical person has been restored, nor that every representation contains a soul. New experiences belong to this actual resident. Copying the reference model would create a different continuation and would not consume this one's anchor receipt.

Source/body/support: the represented Arin possesses a specific Claim-Taking faculty and a supported information-to-custody aperture. The target is this unique compatible external anchor. The faculty can assign anchor ownership through a qualified pledge or exploit its unguarded claim edge. It supplies no body by itself. The anchor supplies one original prepared vessel, autonomy after archive closure, and a retained life/identity revision. “Stealing existence” is implemented only by the actual transfer and displaced donor support.

| Before/after domain | Before | After a valid acquisition |
|---|---|---|
| Continuing actor | One resident `arin.reconstruction.run7` | Same continuation, new supported body/context |
| Unique anchor | Owned by archive trustee, held at exterior berth | Owned by Arin under recorded voluntary transfer or theft |
| Historical reference | Ended biography and reference model | Remains a reference; no free resurrection receipt |
| Trustee support | One unused compatible return berth | No longer available to the trustee or another resident |
| Resident memory | Own experienced conversations/measurements | Retained with attribution; not all historical Arin knowledge |
| Power execution | Faculty supported by represented body/aperture | Only functions actually supported by the new vessel survive |

**A, consensual emergence:** verify that the berth is unused, negotiate with its actual owner, and supply the declared vessel preparation. Transfer the exclusive claim and anchor, then move Arin's continuation. The trustee loses that berth and records a permitted transfer; Arin retains gratitude and a concrete obligation only if one was agreed. This route preserves both parties' stated intent and can gain a new collaborator.

**B, opportunistic theft:** leave the claim edge unguarded or deliberately supply the reachable custody token. Arin can use Claim-Taking to seize the actual anchor before closure. The trustee really loses ownership/use, Arin acquires enduring support, and witnesses learn only what they see or later verify. A supporter may gain the cartographer's loyalty but inherit a dispute; assisting is not guaranteed to make Arin friendly. The source can genuinely overpower ordinary custody where its registered theft rule admits it, but stronger protection wins only for an actual matching function.

**Abstain/protect:** keep the external token/claim out of the aperture or decline the transfer. The archive performance can end under its declared finite support; Arin's prior contributions and the player's evidence remain. An archived record is not called permanent life. Protecting this anchor does not guarantee that Arin cannot find another real anchor later. No default eternal imprisonment, hostile save deletion or forced reset is selected.

Ordering is anchor check → exact donor/custody debit → exclusive claim/continuation binding → prepared-body activation → boundary move → observed reports. Persist the whole transformation together. A vanished vessel after a startup payment rejects/interrupts before final transfer. A successful transfer followed by later vessel damage does not recreate the donor anchor. Recovery requires an actual supported repair, replacement or resurrection rule.

Do not implement this by `spawn` plus `life:alive`: spawn creates a new actor and life state provides no continuity/anchor conservation. Likewise `power:transfer` moves a definition's owned flag, not body, soul, fate or an exact source instance. Two concurrent contenders cannot acquire the same anchor; preview does not reserve it, first successful commit wins, and the other request becomes stale. A donor already without support cannot lose it twice.

Current-engine fit: claims/transfer/custody, durable people, scalar body fields and life policies are useful ingredients. General represented-domain identity, anchor/vessel support, atomic donor loss, conserved body/source-instance movement and return from an ended support domain require O2/O4/O6. This case remains a later implementation, not an unearned resurrection grant hidden in a knowledge effect.

## Interaction and failure review

A later reader might supply an anchor through an actually object-permitting temporal aperture; a text-only seal cannot. A resident might detect a spectator without detecting its maker. These are new registered transformations, not deductions from labels.

| Gate | Required evidence/retained outcome |
|---|---|
| Missing/inactive source or body | No unsupported detector activation, escape or emergence; preserve memory/ownership unless declared loss applies |
| Wrong endpoint/era/context/target | Reject before spend; no private cause disclosure in rejection |
| False or stale evidence | Preserve attributed report with sampled time/revision; new truth does not silently overwrite earlier independent evidence |
| Closed live link versus replay | Closed source gives no new sensing; replay reads retained record without new NPC knowledge or resource reroll |
| Deliberate ordinary concealment | Match it against actual signal, not actor level; a superior detector still detects when its mechanism bypasses concealment |
| Interruption before/after impact | Partial paid information/custody/body changes persist; unsent messages/uncrossed actors do not happen |
| Simultaneous contenders/order | One declared sequence resolves exact owners; no duplicated unique anchor/identity/kit |
| Stale/repeated request | Expected sequence and meaningful scene/link/anchor revision reject; unique claim survives pruned display logs |
| Failed storage | No live source/message/history/identity/cost/stock publication; retry same durable predecessor once |
| Save/reload/import | Generated old/new/mid-open/terminal records validate; no remote revelation, doubled activation, new body, fresh charge or extra kit |
| Forgotten display text | Permanent cause/identity/claim evidence remains; bounded logs are not authority for unique acquisition |
| Suppression/restoration | Ongoing function stops/returns under real support; learned methods and completed knowledge remain, no retrospective refund |

### Source-informed original variant: steal the active return qualification

The Empty Berth's unused-anchor route is deliberately distinct. A harsher original variant makes the target a visitor's already-active return qualification, rather than an unused berth. An emergent resident can overpower the permitted claim edge and take that unique qualification just before a pocket closes. Its actual capability may be zero-fuel and overwhelming; ordinary strength cannot negate it without a matching counter. The visitor loses that return route while retaining body, memory, learned methods and faculty ownership. A name/key-dependent return command becomes unavailable because its eligible key is gone, not because all powers were erased.

The recipient gets only the qualification and persistence support the rule transfers. Donor and recipient must not both exit on one unique carrier claim. An independently issued spare, a genuine compatible personal exit source or a negotiated anchor can provide a different result; these are original proposed alternatives, not canon counters. Abstention/protection denies this aperture when the resident has no other supported route, but does not force every superior source to fail.

This severe variant requires an explicit donor fate/return fallback and observation policy before runtime: stranded residency, rescue or another supported exit are distinct decisions, and this packet selects no death, personal-save reset or automatic recovery. ## Boundary situation A: the dry room behind a short door

CF01, original proposal. In the same Frontier realm, Nera maintains a small Cedar Fold workroom behind a four-played-minute doorway. The interior has its own saved site/stock/resident identity, but is not a new cultivation realm or prestige. The public seed-key opens access; it does not sustain the room. Nera owns her drying lamp and finite fuel. The player needs dry material for ordinary survey work.

**A, ordinary exterior work:** use two real logs and two stamina to build a reusable weather cover in the Village workshop, then perform a four-minute drying job using its actual capacity. The player stays outside, learns the local craft, keeps the cover and uses the resulting actual dry material. No pocket entry, resident introduction or interior knowledge is granted. Subsequent jobs consume real stock; the cover is not free production.

**B, pocket cooperation:** pay one coin for Nera to open the four-minute route and reserve one of her actual lamp charges. Transfer the player's eligible damp material to her interior work stock, enter with a compatible body/cargo and agree on a three-minute dry/handover job. Nera uses her lamp/fuel; the player brings material instead of building the cover. Both participants can see the marked material actually cross. The dried material returns to its owner once; Nera retains payment, spends her fuel and remembers the introduction. The useful choice is reusable independence versus cheaper access to someone else's finite facility and acquaintance.

**Abstain:** keep the damp material or pursue other work. Nera continues her own paid jobs from her existing stock; no contract/payment/loan is invented from silence. A later visit reveals only work actually performed, with supply depletion and her prior memories intact. She is a resident/collaborator, not an animated crafting button.

Known boundary: while the door is open, this route admits visible light, speech, ordinary air and explicitly transferred body/cargo; it admits no bulk-water stream, free energy or remote source command. Shut it and those flows cease. The interior/lamp/stock persist. Reopen with the same seed/interior reference and pay a new actual access cost; this creates a new visit/lease, not a new Nera, kit reward, material lot or lamp charge. Another person with actual authorized means/knowledge can open another route under its own policy; owning this key is not absolute control over the interior.

Interrupted return: offer the declared warning/exit choice before this prototype door ends. If the player remains, their compatible body/cargo stays inside and they can ask Nera for a separately paid reopening, wait for an arranged external operator or use an actually owned compatible exit source. None is guaranteed if its means are missing. Interrupted handover leaves the material with its actual holder and claim; it does not simultaneously appear in the native bag. Closing the route is not “collect all loot” or “destroy pocket.” This prototype selects no suffocation, forced death, automatic extraction, offline punishment or silent cargo loss.

Visible evidence: marked material, lamp fuel, known route expiry, a stable door/interior identity and a changed later stock report. Do not reveal Nera's private stock/work merely from key ownership. Failure/save gates include borrowed consumable rejection, full job site, incompatible body/oversized cargo, blocked return, stale seed/route revision, duplicate handover, closed-route contact and failed storage at entry/exit. CF01 needs real interior/actor/cargo/return binding; a metadata travel animation cannot pass it.

## Boundary situation B: the watcher at the closed seam

CF02 connects to that same saved Cedar Fold. Nera holds an old compass that belongs to Oras, a seam traveler. Its legitimate anchor trace gives him evidence of its location. The player may watch the workroom through a separate viewing slit. Oras, already present in an overlapping layer, detects that sampling and addresses the slit. Ordinary material sight cannot find him; an actual layer-view instrument can show him without granting contact.

Oras owns two distinct original functions: **Seam Hearing** detects the active sampling slit but does not identify its anonymous observer, and **Seam Passage** admits his compatible body through this compass-linked pocket boundary even when the ordinary door is shut. Both have living support and a true acquired faculty. This variant pays no per-use fuel or cooldown. The compass trace supplies locator/access scope; his presence is not generated from an unknown player's global position. His door bypass really works. Closing the observation slit stops that sampling, while closing only the door does not stop his independent passage. These are stronger means than Ilex's text-only lens function, not a silent upgrade of it.

**A, return property/negotiation:** meet through an admitted signal, verify Nera's attributed compass loan and return the actual compass through a valid cargo path. Oras's selected original policy wants his compass, so he withdraws his approach after a confirmed return; Nera remembers who helped settle it and can keep working. Speech alone, a counterfeit, unrelated payment or previewing the compass does not settle custody. The player learns a legitimate reason for the contact without learning Oras's entire build. Negotiation changes his goal/action policy, not his ability's strength.

**B, actually reach and repel:** a player with an acquired Layer Grip or a compatible collaborator can make real contact in Oras's layer. In this optional nonlethal proof, his fixed bracing output is six; the player's supported grip output at least eight and four real stamina can displace him beyond this compass-trace's approach radius. Stronger earned force works when it has actual reach. An ordinary punch with no layer contact fails regardless of visible target; an insufficient grip fails for actual output. Oras keeps compass ownership, Nera remains its holder and the dispute persists. A later meeting recalls the displacement, not gratitude or a returned compass. A sufficiently stronger true passage source could ignore this limited trace-radius defense; do not generalize it into high-level immunity.

**Abstain/leave:** stop watching and leave. Oras uses his independent passage to collect the compass if Nera permits the physical return, under their existing loan; player silence gives no consent/debt on Nera's behalf. A later local visit reveals the missing compass and Nera's attributed account. If she refuses, follow their actual conflict policy, not an automatic peaceful ending. The prototype's selected Nera policy returns the verified owner's compass; other worlds may permit a genuine confrontation.

Body/position/flow gate: a silhouette, a projected voice, layer residence and bodily manifestation are separately saved. Neither viewing nor an internal actor ID authorizes impact. After any layered contest/return, place the actual body at a currently compatible free position; a blocked exit requires its declared alternative, not teleporting into occupied native space. Do not import D&D's exact damage/exit rules into this original nonlethal fixture. Suppressing Oras's passage blocks future bodily entry but does not revoke his compass ownership or erase heard words. Suppressing detection does not automatically disable already qualified tracking or passage.

CF02's outcomes preserve compass owner/holder, spent stamina, disclosure, Nera's work/stock and each witness's actual knowledge. Stale/failed stored return or displacement publishes no partial compass, second body, source activation or relationship. Already returned compass cannot be returned again after reopening. Resetting an illustrative echo of this encounter cannot export a second compass from the durable pocket. CF01/CF02 use no forced world collapse or new lethal policy.
