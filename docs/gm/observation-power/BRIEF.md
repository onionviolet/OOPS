## Four worked families

1. Observation awareness: an entity notices a real in-world observer/link. Distinguish detecting a trace, identifying who/when, reaching it, and changing the observation. Privacy/false attribution and information available to each person matter.
2. Temporal observation: a historical subject detects a later observer under an authored source rule. Specify chronological/narrative order, information direction, whether a message or action can cross, and which causal record changes. Looking at history does not automatically mutate it.
3. Constructed-world awareness: a resident infers/tests construction, then uses an actual supported capability to challenge its constraints. Awareness alone need not enable escape; a superior source that genuinely crosses the boundary can do so. The construction's residents can have enduring agency and consequences.
4. Emergence/return: a representation gains actual persistence through a concrete anchor, identity/fate/property acquisition or other verified/original rule. Specify which represented actor continues, what is transferred or displaced, who owns it, and which body/source/life state supports the result. One property or unique identity cannot silently be awarded twice.
