RNG, gambling, lootboxes, ordinary-to-extraordinary development and creating book
worlds. This is a proposed refinement of completion criteria, not selection of

## Proposed meaning

First-level breadth delivers several genuinely different ways to begin, develop
and change one's life inside a finite early-game edition. Each selected route
changes actual available actions or outcomes, joins the world's ordinary life,
and leads beyond its first reward. A cheat needs its promised function and,
where selected, meaningful development. Chance systems need committed outcomes
and useful player decisions. The whole-world blueprint can reach far beyond
this edition without making every future idea a delivery blocker.

## B1 Progression has several dimensions

Distinguish mastery, body/source development, equipment, knowledge, relationships,
institutional access, wealth/property and exceptional powers. Worlds select
their own dimensions and rules; these are review lenses, not universal meters.

For each selected path, specify acquisition, practice or investment, the first
meaningful improvement, new executable capability, a practical application and
continuing development. Increasing a number can count where it changes a real
outcome; a qualitative stage must actually change functions or scope. A higher
rank label alone does neither. Avoid turning every path into combat XP.

Early-edition proof: trace a reachable start through improvement into a changed
decision/outcome and continuing play. Use natural resources and time as well as
accelerated boundary fixtures. Record the later horizon without requiring its
entire mature implementation for this edition.

## B2 Cheats develop through their own mechanisms

Separate growth of the holder, mastery of an existing function, acquisition of
additional functions, changes to the cheat itself, and combinations with other
means. A stronger holder does not automatically upgrade the cheat; a powerful
cheat need not begin weak or have an invented ceiling, payment or drawback.
Some cheats are fixed functions whose expanding applications provide depth.

Each selected card identifies real acquisition, actor knowledge, executable
verbs, targets/effects, relevant development, integrations, loss/transfer and
retention. Qualify source rules and variant premises separately. If evolution
is promised, its first selected change needs actual before/after play evidence.
If no evolution is promised, prove substantive use rather than inventing levels.

Review interactions such as analysis supporting research, storage enabling
logistics or companions supporting instruction as original candidates, not
facts about any named source. Match shared-state and effect ordering to the
actual selected functions. Neither unlimited compatibility nor forced balancing
penalties follow from putting two cards together.

## B3 Ordinary to extraordinary is a causal bridge

Potential bridges include acquired knowledge, apprenticeship, invention,
exceptional materials, bodily change, institutional access, discovery, bargains
or chance acquisition. Select world-grounded routes instead of giving every
ordinary skill an automatic supernatural endpoint.

Original example: ordinary craft supports better tools; a learned local method
and suitable material then enable a genuinely new device; that device changes
what the player can do. Every arrow needs a working action and output. Ordinary
expertise may also support, profit from or coexist with extraordinary power
without itself becoming magical. A player can remain an ordinary specialist.

If this bridge is included in the edition, prove a complete early transition
and a useful role for retained ordinary means afterward. Do not force all starts
through the same awakening, protagonist identity or compulsory career.

## B4 RNG is a set of distinct contracts

| Chance domain | Proposed review questions |
|---|---|
| Entry/body/era | Which axes are selected together or independently? Are all admitted combinations coherent and usable? |
| Cheat/talent acquisition | Start roll, earned attempt, discovery or transfer? What is eligible and what does each outcome actually enable? |
| Loot/quality | What changes: quantity, identity, quality, properties or condition? Does rarity mean scarcity, rather than assumed strength? |
| Encounters/events | What is rolled once, what responds to conduct, and what can preparation alter? |
| Research/crafting | Does uncertainty concern knowledge, execution, output or discovery? How do mastery and better means change it? |
| NPC/world history | Which facts are generated, which develop through actors' means, and which remain source-authored? |

Every selected domain records pool and revision, weights, prerequisites,
commitment point, costs, repeat/duplicate/no-result handling, reroll rights,
information exposure and save compatibility. Guarantees, pity and caps are local
choices, not automatic requirements. Persist committed outcomes and costs before
reward publication. Preview, reload, import or unrelated UI work cannot silently
grant another attempt. Separate unrelated streams where the promised behavior
requires it; declare actual rewind/reroll powers separately.

Verify distribution logic and actual acquisition/use. Exact hidden pools and
odds stay agent-only where discovery requires it. Tell the player what their
means can establish and the observable commitment they are making.

## B5 Gambling is an optional player pursuit

Gambling means voluntarily staking something on an uncertain result. Propose
world-grounded dice/cards, wagers on events, contests or institutional games
where fitting. Give them rules, opponents/house means, actual stake custody,
settlement and useful social/economic consequences. They need not all exist or
be required for progression. This proposal concerns game resources only.

Define what information and preparation can change, including bluffing,
appraisal, cheating, detection or fortune manipulation when selected. Opponents
must act on their held means. Show wins, losses, abstention and relevant
interruption/refusal cases. Specify whether automation is allowed and how it
respects budgets and stopping conditions. A repeated coin flip with no relevant
decisions is a chance transaction, not proof of a developed gambling pursuit.

## B6 Lootboxes are a different acquisition format

A lootbox is a container or paid draw revealing randomized contents; gambling
can exist without one, and a found cache need not be a wager. Candidates include
found caches, purchased mystery packs, earned draws and system lotteries.
Exact fiction and eligibility follow the host world's rules.

Specify whether contents commit on creation, purchase or opening, who owns the
container, whether it can be appraised/traded, and what duplicate or unusable
results do under selected policy. Guarantee/pity, fragments, exchanges and
targeted alternatives are options, not mandatory defaults. Outcomes need actual
uses and custody; legendary labels do not establish functional value.

Proof covers payment/opening, each selected result class, duplicate handling,
failed publication and reload/import without duplicated rewards. Reconcile
deliberate rollback powers with the same contract. These are in-game proposals;
no real-money purchase or monetization feature is selected.

## B7 Creating book worlds as authored content

Separate reusable services from the local source's laws and the chosen adaptation
premise. Freeze represented edition/range, era, region, eligible roles/bodies,
ordinary livelihoods, exceptional routes, people/institutions, continuity,
discovery and first finish line. Qualify selected functions against source and
retain canon, inference and invention labels. Preserve the entire setting's
actionability blueprint alongside the finite early edition.

Changing a world identifier, importing an ability catalog or retelling the main
character's quest does not produce a complete playable world. It needs coherent
independent player goals, actual inhabitants and economy, consequences, natural
history and usable continuation. A source's rare/protagonist means are not
automatically available to every start.

Follow PROJECT's private-pack/public-content boundary, attribution and source
library protections. Keep unknown identity and modified truths sealed. No new
world roster, acquisition, parallel dispatch or source-library edit is authorized
by this proposal. Multiple starts/timelines may fit one realm; a wider launch
roster still needs reconciliation with the accepted milestone.

## B8 Creating worlds as a player power

Separately consider authored powers for pocket realms, simulated worlds, dream
domains, territories or genuinely created inhabited worlds. These differ in
reality, autonomy, fidelity, time, inhabitants and what can leave them. A
territory interface or bounded model must not claim a real independent universe.

If selected, specify the creator's actual authority: what can be created or
changed, what operates independently, supported entry/exit, matter/information/
power carriers, clocks, resources where applicable, retention and lifecycle.
Costs or limits exist only where the chosen mechanism supplies them. A strong
power can be strong; unsupported scope receives an implementation gap rather
than an invented lore restriction.

Prove one bounded but meaningful create → inhabit/use → alter → leave/return →
save/reload journey with relevant exports and autonomy if promised. Preserve a
larger horizon separately. No player-world-creation runtime is assigned here.

## B9 Other joins that change the result

| Area | Completion question |
|---|---|
| Pacing and goals | Does development change decisions? Is there useful play after the early checkpoint or an overwhelming acquisition? |
| NPC progression | Can inhabitants learn, compete, use exceptional means and respond with their actual resources and knowledge? |
| Economy and power | Do production, duplication, prophecy or storage change their real consumers and institutions where promised? |
| Idle/offline | Are inputs, concurrency, cancellation, budget and stopping conditions truthful, with explicit absence/loss policy? |
| Failure and redirection | Can refusal, lost access or an unsuccessful attempt lead to coherent next actions under the local rules? |
| Knowledge | Do discoveries and observations reveal only what each actor's means supports, including mistaken readings? |
| Travel and legacy | What actually carries, who holds it, and what survives departure/death/prestige under the selected policy? |
| Combination and scale | Which declared joins work, in which order, and with what performance boundary? Avoid claiming all combinations. |

## B10 Finite delivery gate

For the selected edition, each included family needs its declared reachable
loop and required variants, actual improvement or useful sustained application,
lasting consequence, native UI consumer, relevant cross-family interactions and
persistence evidence. Applicable areas receive detail IDs and observable cases;
an excluded optional family stays outside scope rather than being called done.

Use existing C0–C5 levels. C4 is delivery of this frozen early edition with every
required leaf/join accounted for and no required placeholder or test-only caller.
C5 remains Weibao's actual acceptance. Natural early play, rebuilt fresh-origin
journeys, consequential failures and save/reload/import/compatibility where
changed are required by existing project verification rules. Report required,
verified and open counts for this revision, not a generic breadth percentage.

Recommended contract emphasis: different development routes with useful ordinary
life; functional selected cheats and their applicable growth; a causal ordinary-
to-extraordinary bridge where selected; explicit independent chance contracts;
and source-grounded world life. Gambling, lootboxes and player-created worlds
are optional selected families, not release blockers merely because they were
considered. Exact counts, roster and numerical policies remain unselected.
