## First playable slice

**S1, outcome:** a deliberate original early-Tingen adult records probationer can
decline an allocated Seer dose, or take it under supported supervision, establish
basic stability, separately learn control, use limited material perception on a
real sample, sleep to recover and reuse the same method on another actual sample.
This is a playable local branch, not Klein's body/memories or an automatic new-game
default. Acquisition -> control -> repeatable execution is the progression delivered.
Full digestion/Sequence advancement remains separate.

**S2, entry and minimum authored world:** use a labelled disposable prototype start
that deliberately instantiates the local adult, one institutional supervisor who
can supply/teach the selected operation, a local preparation/teaching place and an
available sleeping place. No retained Frontier visitor substitution. The original
supervisor and local branch are authored, not claims about a new canon character.
The profile has no earlier conflicting source/innate corresponding trait; actual
body/history is still validated at execution. Do not change the default world or
load, overwrite or migrate personal saves to enter this prototype.

**S3, stock and real consumer:** opening native state contains one prepared-dose lot
owned by the supplier, one ordinary comparator and two distinct supported
supernatural sample lots, one inspectable unit each, under actual custody. Preparation
is authored prehistory with a pinned qualified recipe/provenance receipt, not an
interactive general crafting system. Do not also pretend its consumed preparation
inputs remain available stock, or debit them again at ingestion. Allocation transfers
no faculty; one valid ingestion consumes the single dose once. The same observation
method supports both supernatural lots and the ordinary comparator without per-target
quest grants. Findings have a real downstream handling use: player can return a
screened sample through an authorized custody transfer with its limited finding,
so an actor/stock owner actually receives the material and attributed information.
This does not certify safety or manufacture an item; grant no automatic reward.

**S4, explicit interaction contract:** preview/decline spends nothing; ingestion and
initial guided attention publish atomically; formal teaching remains interruptible.
Then perform bounded examination, end sight, optionally return the sample with its
finding, recover through supported native sleep and examine the second sample.
D1–D5 below own cancellation, resource, recovery and uncertainty semantics. Source
ownership survives canceled teaching; opening/rendering/closing a view never consumes,
refunds, cures, restocks or advances a method. No sustained acute-control branch,
forced overdraw or unsupported hazardous invocation is exposed.

**S5, ready to code, not proven:** no further consequential player preference is
pending for this scope. Source qualification and reversible authoring choices are
resolved below. Runtime authorization and actual write/build assignment remain
required; IG2–IG4 are concrete integration work/checks for the authorized executor,
not a new interview or a requirement to prove gameplay before writing code.

### Straightforward authoring decisions

**D1, first supported control boundary:** preview/decline -> atomic supervised
consumption and initial guided attention -> source acquired/basic stabilization,
formal method unlearned -> interruptible instruction/toggle practice -> bounded
material use -> actual sleep recovery -> use on a second target. The original
post-dose unfinished-lesson state remains, now explicitly after initial stabilization.
No UI close/cancel is interpreted as refusing the already performed attention.
Source ownership and consumption persist even when formal teaching is abandoned.
This does not assert every canon initiation resolves successfully; it authors one
qualified success route without an unsupported simulator for all alternatives.

**D2, bounded perception:** a finished observation includes entering, examining and
ending sight, charged once. A held lesson may spend work in declared performed
segments and exposes its learned/presently taught stop route. Navigation is never
the stop command. All actual use duration is costed; pauses do not secretly tick
active sight while the world is paused. Do not add a perpetual active mode merely
to support two examinations. No forced overdraw command in this slice.

**D3, one resource truth:** reuse the inspected integer energy policy for reserve
and strain, with a LOTM-specific definition. Prototype values: capacity 6, output 1,
control 1, efficiency 1:1, strain limit 6, strain per performed use 1. Each bounded
examination costs one unit; formal practice has one five-minute performed segment costing one unit; a bounded
examination costs one unit over one played minute. These durations are prototype
authoring. Cost for performed work commits at that segment’s start and is not
refunded by cancellation; formal learning/refinement commits only on completed
qualified work, with an idempotent receipt. Practice stops at completion/cancel. Acquisition provisions the original profile's initial reserve once,
never on render, lesson restart or reload. These are author values, not novel
measurements. Keep overflow symptoms, exertional strain and any later corruption
separate; a second mirrored stamina/sanity ledger is unnecessary.

**D4, recovery contract:** an explicitly supported sleep job of 120 played minutes
restores up to six reserve and cools up to six exertional strain on completion,
clamped to capacity; duration/amount are prototype tuning. It requires the actual
living body, a supported available rest place and normal native sleep eligibility.
Canceling before completion gives no completion recovery; native elapsed time and
other actual costs stay spent. Loading a completed receipt or reopening a menu cannot award recovery again.
Only the shared clock’s supported job completion can award it; no alternate clock
writer or direct elapsed-time setter is introduced. Sleep never removes
a source, grants method/digestion, cleanses corruption or reverses death. This
bounded implementation uses completion-based recovery, not a universal biological
claim that partial sleep has no value. The native integrator must reconcile existing
sleep effects once so the same sleep does not recover the same pool twice.

**D5, real consumer:** one qualified ordinary comparator and two distinct actual
source-qualified supernatural sample lots share the same observation method and
custody checks. Observing a signal records only its supported limited finding.
The informed player may then choose the S3 return-with-finding operation or
other actual authorized handling;
perception does not itself move items, identify the whole recipe or certify safety.
A moved/replaced sample invalidates the old preview and basis. Negative sight is
not proof there is no supernatural hazard, hidden observer or poisoned material.

The defaults above are reversible design choices authorized for planning, not
new user quotations or implemented behavior. Numeric tuning is no longer an open
preference question. The resource and sleep/native joins need implementation proof.

### Subtle requirements when an exchange becomes playable

**MX1, find it before shopping:** a lead, introduction, membership or known route
reveals only its supported venue/event. Attending needs actual location, time and
entry terms. A venue can exist without the player knowing it. Failed admission
need not destroy the lead. Missed events do not secretly advance paused worlds;
apply the existing world clock/absence rule. No automatic refresh on entering UI.

**MX2, people and offers are finite:** each offer has a real actor, possessed stock
or a clearly labeled promise/order, asking terms, location and revision. A wanted
listing is demand, not a fulfilled supply. No rare item spawned from a contact's
name, faction reputation or the buyer's level. Sellers may be mistaken, selective,
legitimate or deceptive; do not make every secretive seller a scammer.

**MX3, evaluate a claim without omniscience:** separate actual item/lot from seller
description, buyer observation, authenticity evidence and buyer interpretation.
Appraisal is a paid/performed bounded method where means allow it. A supernatural
signal is not exact identity, safe consumption or a complete formula certificate.
A copied formula, translated text and ability to execute it are different outputs.
Testing may consume/damage a sample or reveal interest, only when authored causes
support that consequence. Failed/partial appraisal preserves attributed findings.

**MX4, settle the actual bargain:** freeze seller/buyer identities or authenticated
aliases, specific item/text/service, quantity, condition, price, fee payer, currency,
delivery place/time/channel and known remedies. Transfer only actual rights/custody
and actual payment in one supported settlement. Holding institutional property is
not authorization to sell it. A broker's quote, protected meeting or third-party
appraisal does not implicitly create escrow, insurance or truthful testimony.
Bespoke barter can exchange a limited disclosed fact for a material; knowledge is
not an ordinary stack that disappears from its former holder. Copying/resale and
confidentiality follow actual world/contract terms, not universal restrictions.

**MX5, delivery and obligations survive menus:** an advance payment creates an
actual receivable/delivery duty, not immediate possession. A promised service needs
the provider's means/time and a completion condition. Loan principal, due basis,
interest, collateral and consequence must be explicit if credit is exposed. No
punitive debt across a paused life, a new body or a rewind without actual accepted
world/continuation semantics. Full debt/default/escrow systems remain later work;
a first market feature can use direct finite cash settlement.

**MX6, secrecy belongs to observers:** masks, aliases, private rooms, messengers and
brokers protect different channels. A buyer's interests can reveal a likely path,
wealth or intention without anyone learning their whole identity. Track who saw
which listing, exchange or testimony; no global reputation/heat value that makes
all NPCs share a private event. Institutions may monitor or intervene for actual
causes. Neil's tolerance account is not a guarantee of legal immunity everywhere.

**MX7, after the sale:** depleted lots stay depleted, sold samples move custody,
purchased texts preserve their actual revision/provenance, and local trust updates
only for witnesses or later supported reports. Refresh/reload/import cannot reroll
inventory, erase debt, change agreed terms or pay the seller twice. Cancellation
before acceptance differs from breach after a real commitment. A character can
walk away from a listing without being forced into a job or plot.
