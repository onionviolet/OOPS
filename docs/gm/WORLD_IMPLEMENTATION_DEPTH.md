## WD01: Exchange preparation and Commonwealth water work

Boundary: [Fallout brief](../worlds/fallout/WORLD_BRIEF.md), [campaign](../worlds/fallout/CAMPAIGN.md) and [ledger](../worlds/fallout/SOURCE_LEDGER.md) F1–F5. Base-game location/features are bounded evidence; intake mechanics, local cast, budgets and Exchange commission are O. No canonical fusion-core formula/radiation cure/faction ending is selected.

**Intended journey:** earn a reliable livelihood in the connector, take a limited water inspection, then decide whether to become a visiting specialist or a resident workshop partner. Success for Iona's inspection receipt need not resolve the local water-rights conflict.

| Domain | Actual proposed rule/means |
|---|---|
| Body/source/fuel | Retain the same body/wounds and learned Frontier `field.load_step`; mastery2 supports output3, real `field.drive` use spends3reserve/1strain with its member execution prerequisite. Destination binding/recovery is required; a device battery is not drive/chakra/FP. |
| Cargo | Manifest only actual carried test stock, tools and spare seals; identify owner/holder/loan. A deposited chest item is not carried cargo. A loaned calibration fixture must return or receive an explicit settlement. |
| Knowledge | Inspect readings before diagnosing; uncertain supply rumor and measured result use distinct records. Bracing moves material but does not certify water or reveal sabotage. |
| Institution | Iona controls the declared contract; Mina controls a bay, Imani finite testing/medicine, Orren transport/claim rights. A connector receipt does not grant local access or faction rank. |

**Prepare/reside:** ordinary paid kit production in Frontier funds service or spare stock; a connector bench supports later calibrated production. Iona has one review desk; Venn sells dated partial route reports; Kes competes with his actual cargo/transport budget. Their names, leases and jobs remain O. Residency grants paid work/site access, not a globally mandatory daily mission.

**Ordinary route:** inspect the inlet; fund Mina's ordinary fitting and Imani's actual diagnostic charge; negotiate a funded transport reservation with displayed local duty. O proof values can use one seal, one test charge and a four-minute bay job, plus the chosen service fee. Retain tested knowledge and cooperative access, spending supplies/contract means rather than extraordinary fuel.

**Power/tool route:** use legitimately supported Step/drive to hold the inspected beam, or an owned compatible maintained lifting rig with actual charge. Replace the paid hoist service; still spend the local seal/test charge and honor sample custody. A genuinely stronger supported lift may bypass this bottleneck completely. It still cannot fabricate clean flow evidence. Rig charge/refit/condition beyond a fixed authored variant needs a real adapter, not cosmetic tiers.

Mina cannot fit parts while working another reserved job; Imani may refuse an unfunded sample. Orren learns action from a witness/crew report, not the player's private source list. Direct force can overcome an appropriate fixed unit when the actual combat/bodies support it; it may damage infrastructure and leave known hostility. This is a future outcome, not current native Fallout combat.

**Abstention:** no accepted inspection debt. Orren's funded carrier takes available stock and Mina offers a costlier later restoration. Actual ownership/route change occurs independently; the player learns it only through a supported report. Accepted default differs from refusal and keeps disclosed spent inputs/obligations.

**Return/revisit:** settle Iona's specific receipt once; carry one legitimately owned portable fixture or leave a loan locally. Exporting the only fixture reduces Imani's testing capacity. An actual calibrated fixture can unlock paid Frontier fault diagnosis once its native item/recipe adapter exists. Reentry preserves pump state, people, duties and unique claim. It does not pay the same inspection again.

**Future growth:** short certification/repair income funds a shared bay; medium harbor parts and farm irrigation connect new stock/rights; long public utility versus exclusive corridor changes partners and regional conflicts. The same region remains valuable after high-power advancement through collaborators, rare parts, evidence and unfinished projects.

**Positive/counterexample:** supported Step plus3drive opens physical preparation; remembered Step with zero fuel or incompatible body cannot. The latter can buy ordinary work. A powerful unsupported “magic” label does not stop radiation; a genuinely applicable protection function should.

## WD02: Exchange residence and Bleach living-district records

Boundary: [Bleach brief](../worlds/bleach/WORLD_BRIEF.md), [campaign](../worlds/bleach/CAMPAIGN.md), [ledger](../worlds/bleach/SOURCE_LEDGER.md). Original manga vols1–74, labelled adaptation corroboration only; exact spiritual interfaces/releases remain source-gated. The living-district office, job, local people and study terms are O; no automatic Soul Society passage or protagonist heritage.

**Intended journey:** support a neighborhood through ordinary records/repair, or use a genuinely eligible perception source, then keep a useful residence while developing a personal or institutional career. Equipment research and personal soul development remain different investments.

| Domain | Actual proposed rule/means |
|---|---|
| Body/source/fuel | Retain actual living body; perception, spiritual contact and cross-domain passage are separate. A valid already acquired detector may work for its real reason; no arrival grants a Zanpakuto, Quincy origin or Fullbring bond. |
| Cargo | Real paper/repair stock can move under physical custody. Soul-bound releases are not portable cartridges. Material-to-spiritual conversion, soul projection and alternate-body equipment need separate declared interfaces. |
| Knowledge | Jun's ordinary witnesses know street movement; Nao's qualified observations know only their supported scope. Buying her report does not give the player spiritual sight or private mechanisms. |
| Institution | Emi owns the bench, Jun actual resident records, Nao her trained service/finite reserve. Their consent/testimony is not manufactured by copying handwriting or showing Iona's receipt. |

**Prepare/reside:** translate actually known records, pack dry copies or rent a paid repair slot at the connector. The office remains a local lived facility, with neighbors, garments/tools, relief orders and apprentices. A physically limited resident can keep records or hire carriers; no compulsory combat test makes those careers invalid.

**Ordinary route:** compare disclosed address records, pay witnesses and repair a mundane detour with actual timber/cloth/time. Spend more material/work, preserve Nao's scarce reserve, deliver the lawful shelter parcel and retain an ordinary repair method. Unknown spiritual causes remain unknown; a safe route can solve the local objective without identifying every spirit.

**Power/tool route:** pay/reserve Nao's qualified finite survey, or use an actually eligible owned perception tool/source under its inspected support. An O fixture may spend2actual local reserve units for a bounded route observation, then shorten the lawful handoff. It yields better evidence within that method's envelope; it does not manufacture a personal release or make a forged record authentic. A superior real faculty can simply perform better without a contrived weakness.

Freeze what the survey observes and when: an immediate paid observation with later report transport is different from a continuously sustained detection job. Existing frozen jobs cannot promise fresh source/contact checks at every future moment. Nao leaving/busy/dead or lacking consent before commitment prevents her service; a delivered report remains dated evidence rather than live tracking.

**Abstention:** Jun funds a longer transfer if means permit; an unprotected delivery or witness opportunity can be lost. No promise/reward appears from inspection alone. Later conversation references the actual detour, dated report or missed parcel, without exposing hidden souls or creating universal grievance.

**Return/revisit:** preserve the delivered manifest once, actual parcel/copy custody and which people heard which account. Take an authorized ordinary copy or housing technique back for paid archival/packing work; spiritual sources retain their own support. Local office and witness consent remain local. Reentering the same district resumes its relief service and relationships, without renewed first-study/source rewards.

**Future growth:** short repair/report jobs fund a dry record store; medium specialist instrument tests or genuinely eligible personal training create different functions; long public testimony office versus private relief network changes information and collaborators. A later cross-domain archive is content/passage expansion in the same continuity after body/cargo support, not a free fresh soul.

**Positive/counterexample:** qualified survey produces a limited attributed report; remembered lore plus an ordinary camera cannot silently see/strike spirits. A genuinely compatible imported detector/attack works if it actually supports that target. Strong physical power still repairs debris without granting spiritual contact.
