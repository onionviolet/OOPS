## A. Kinds of companion

One shared idea: a companion is an actor with its own body and wants, plus a
link to the player. The kinds differ in what the link is made of.

| Code | Kind | Link made of | Feels like |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Pet** | Care and habit | A cat that ignores you until dinner; a dog that waits at the door |
| A2 | **Working animal** | Training and use | Sheepdog, hunting hawk, plough ox, messenger bird, truffle pig |
| A3 | **Mount** | Training, trust, tack | Horse, wolf, wyvern, giant beetle; changes travel itself (section G) |
| A4 | **Familiar** | A magical bond, often shared senses or power | Witch's cat, wizard's owl that sees for you |
| A5 | **Summon** | A call answered for a while | Spirit Ashes, toad contracts, a demon for one job |
| A6 | **Contracted spirit or devil** | Terms, a price and an end clause | Devil contracts, spirit contracts, a genie with rules |
| A7 | **Shikigami or shadow beast** | Technique; it lives in your power | Creatures stored in your shadow, paper servants |
| A8 | **Made companion** | You built it | Golem, homunculus, clockwork bird, simulacrum, animated broom |
| A9 | **Undead or bound dead** | Necromancy or a debt | A skeleton servant, a ghost dog that refuses to leave |
| A10 | **Weapon or object spirit** | Wielding and resonance | A sword with a name and moods, a ring grandpa |
| A11 | **System or voice companion** | The cheat itself | A sarcastic system window, an AI, a fairy navigator |
| A12 | **Swarm or colony** | Control range and feeding | Bees, insects, gu, a mushroom colony |
| A13 | **Plant companion** | Tending and place | A mandrake, a guardian tree, a carnivorous houseplant |
| A14 | **House or place spirit** | Living in the place | Brownie, kitchen god, a house that likes you |
| A15 | **Egg or hatchling** | You were there at hatching | Dragon egg, beast-taming novel eggs; imprinting |

## B. How you get one

| Code | Route | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Buy | Markets, breeders, pet shops, slave-beast auctions in darker worlds |
| B2 | Adopt a stray | It follows you after you feed it twice. Cheapest, warmest start |
| B3 | Tame | Patience, food, beating it, saving it, or matching its nature; per-species method |
| B4 | Hatch or raise | Imprinting gives a deep bond; raising costs years of care |
| B5 | Contract | Negotiate terms; mis-read terms are a story (section C) |
| B6 | Summon ritual | Unknown result, or a chosen target with a price |
| B7 | It chooses you | Wands-and-owls energy; a spirit picks a host for its own reasons |
| B8 | Inherit | A dead master's familiar, a grandparent's horse, the last life's pet now old |
| B9 | Craft | Golem parts, homunculus flasks, paper shikigami; links crafting and alchemy |
| B10 | Rescue, steal or win | From a cruel owner, an arena prize, a lab escapee |
| B11 | Cheat-granted | A starter pet, a gacha pull, a taming cheat (section J) |
| B12 | Followed you across | Your Earth cat reincarnated beside you; a companion that crossed worlds with you |

## C. The bond

**Default:** the link is several separate tracks, not one loyalty bar. **Dial:**
a world can collapse them (a summon is pure obedience) or add more. **Reasonably:**
a strong bond comes from shared history, not just time spent.

| Code | Track | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Trust | Will it follow you into danger, let you treat its wounds |
| C2 | Affection | Does it seek you out; grief when parted |
| C3 | Obedience | Will it do what you say when it disagrees |
| C4 | Understanding | Can you read it, and can it read you; telepathy is the top of this |
| C5 | Contract terms | Written or magical rules that bind either side, with end clauses |
| C6 | Shared channel | Senses, mana, health, lifespan or pain flowing between you |
| C7 | Respect | Proud beasts serve the strong; a dragon may love you and still not obey |

More bond angles:

- **C8 Wants of its own.** Food, play, territory, a mate, freedom, revenge.
  A companion that disobeys for a good reason (it smells the trap) is a story,
  not a bug.
- **C9 Contracts read over time.** Clauses surface as you learn the language or
  the spirit trusts you. Returning a name releases (North Star's Natsume row).
- **C10 Bond verbs unlock.** Higher tracks open combo moves, shared sight,
  riding, sending it alone on errands, merging forms.
- **C11 Bond can sour.** Neglect, cruelty, broken promises. A soured familiar
  may leave, sulk, sabotage, or report to someone else.
- **C12 Shared fate.** Some links share death (a bonded dragon dies with its
  rider) and some share life (your familiar's survival keeps you alive). Per
  world and per contract.
- **C13 Multiple companions have opinions about each other.** Jealousy, pack
  hierarchy, the cat and the dog arrangement, a familiar that hates your mount.

## E. Care and daily life

- **E1 Food.** Feeding ties to the [hunger palette](HUNGER_AND_BODILY_NEEDS_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md).
  A dragon's appetite is a household budget event.
- **E2 Housing.** Kennel, stable, aviary, a spirit realm, your shadow, a pocket
  dimension, a ranch. Where it lives decides who sees it.
- **E3 Health.** Injuries, disease, healers who treat beasts, beast medicine as
  a trade.
- **E4 Law and society.** Beast registries, licences, dangerous-creature laws,
  a dragon in a market square, familiars banned from exam halls.
- **E5 Reputation.** "The one with the giant wolf." Companions shape how
  strangers read you, and NPC pets are a way into NPC friendships.
- **E6 Upkeep as idle.** Daily care is a gentle queue, not a chore: a short
  check-in that is quick when things are fine and becomes a story when they
  are not.

- **G6 Crossing worlds with a companion.** Default: it follows the same carry

  (your wolf arrives as a puppy, a spirit has no anchor there).

## I. Viewpoint and continuity

- **I1 Switch viewpoint.** Play a scene as the companion when the bond allows
  shared senses; full play as the companion goes to the nonhuman owners.
- **I2 Companion carries on.** After your death it seeks a new holder (North
  Star succession row); it can recognise your heir or your next life.
- **I3 Heirs inherit companions** with their own feelings about the heir.
- **I4 A companion holds memories** you lost: a familiar remembers a past life,
  a sword spirit remembers earlier wielders.
- **I5 Hidden depths.** A companion can be more than it looks. Any such truth
  in a specific world is a sealed secret, tracked by ID only.

### O. Minds and personhood

| Code | Idea |
|---|---|
| O1 | **A mind spectrum.** Animal, clever animal, near-person, full person. Default: the species and the world decide; Dial: evolution, a contract or a cheat can move one along it |
| O2 | **A sapient companion is a person.** Its consent, pay and freedom matter the way an NPC's do. Darker worlds treat bound spirits as property; the player can follow, exploit or fight that custom |
| O3 | **Freeing a companion** is a real choice: it may leave, stay as a friend, or come back years later |
| O4 | **Its own goals and quests.** A familiar hunting its old master's killer, a horse that wants to return to its herd |
| O5 | **Its own social life.** Friends, rivals and mates among NPC animals and spirits; the town's cats have a hierarchy your cat climbs |
| O6 | **Its own reputation.** The wolf is known separately from you; a feared companion can open or close doors you can't |
| O7 | **Unreliable companion.** Its reports can be wrong, biased or self-serving, like any observer |

### P. Talking with it

- **P1 Ladder of understanding:** body language, learned signals, a few words,
  full speech, telepathy. Each step widens what you can ask of it.
- **P2 Misreadings** are small stories: you thought it wanted food; it was
  warning you about the well.
- **P3 Translators:** a druid, a beast-speech cheat, a child who understands
  animals, a spirit medium.
- **P4 Its voice in the journal:** short lines from its point of view once it
  can speak, or as the player imagines them before then.

### Q. Merging and sharing a body

| Code | Form |
|---|---|
| Q1 | Fuse for a fight (union forms, combined transformations) with a cost to separate |
| Q2 | Wear it: a living armour, a cloak spirit, a symbiote |
| Q3 | It lives inside you: a sealed beast, a spirit in your shadow, a parasite partner |
| Q4 | It is part of your power: a guardian that only exists through you |
| Q5 | Swap bodies or senses for a while |
| Q6 | Gradual blending: years of bond make you a little more like it, and it like you |

### R. Scale: one friend to an army

- **R1 Named few, nameless many.** A handful of companions carry full
  personality; herds, swarms and shadow armies run as groups with a leader.
- **R2 Command overhead.** More followers cost attention, food and control
  range. Default: a soft limit from the player's means; Dial: a cheat or
  hierarchy (lieutenants) raises it. No fixed cap.
- **R3 Promotion.** A nameless soldier earns a name through deeds and becomes
  a real companion.
- **R4 Logistics.** Armies of beasts need land, feed and law, and neighbours
  notice.

### T. Darker turns

- **T1** Cruelty has consequences: fear, sabotage, the companion's escape, a
  bad name among animals and spirits.
- **T2** Abandoned companions go feral and may return as enemies or as a
  wild pack's leader.
- **T3** Bonds that feed on you: a familiar that takes years, blood or memory
  as its price.
- **T4** Cursed or haunted pets, and companions tied to a debt someone else
  will collect.
- **T5** A companion with a hidden owner or purpose. Any per-world truth of
  this kind is a sealed secret, tracked by ID only.
- **T6** Grief done badly: a player who resurrects a pet wrong. Revival stays
  reachable with a fitting cost.

## Round 3 (2026-10-14)

Same request again. Two more lanes join C1 to C3: C4 writes whole-life
companion journeys and stress-tests this palette; C5 covers generated

### AA. Where companions come from

- **AA1 Ecology.** Wild populations, food chains, habitats and seasons decide
  what can be found where and when.
- **AA2 Rarity with reasons.** A species is rare because it is hunted, slow to
  breed, or only hatches in a war year, not because a table says so.
- **AA3 Extinction and revival.** Overhunting can end a species in one world;
  an egg, a fossil or another world can bring it back.
- **AA4 Invasive companions.** A creature carried across worlds can wreck a
  local ecology or become the start of a new one.

### AB. Companion as elder

| Code | Idea |
|---|---|
| AB1 | **Mentor familiar.** An old spirit teaches a young mage, and judges them |
| AB2 | **Raised by beasts.** A start where the companion is the parent figure |
| AB3 | **Nanny beast.** Your companion helps raise your children and the heir grows up with it |
| AB4 | **Keeper of the house's past.** The family hound or house spirit has known five generations |

### AC. The roles reversed

- **AC1** Someone stronger keeps *you*: a dragon's favoured human, a god's pet
  mortal, a witch's apprentice-familiar. Full play from that side belongs to
  the nonhuman and identity owners.
- **AC2** Your companion acquires a companion of its own.
- **AC3** The companion becomes the master: it outgrows you and you become its
  advisor (D6 taken further).

### AE. Ways to lose one besides death

| Code | Loss | Way back |
|---|---|---|
| AE1 | Lost or ran away | Search, posters, a tracker, it finds its way home |
| AE2 | Stolen or confiscated by law | Rescue, court, ransom, a trade |
| AE3 | Sold or given away by you or an heir | Buy back, or meet it later with a new family |
| AE4 | Transformed or corrupted | Cure, accept the new form, or let it go |
| AE5 | Forgets you (curse, memory loss, rebirth) | Rebuild the bond; the body may remember before the mind |
| AE6 | Contract taken over by another | Contest it, renegotiate, or the companion chooses |
| AE7 | Left behind in another world or timeline | Return trips, summons, letters through a thin wall |

### AF. Memory and recognition

- **AF1** It remembers how you treated it, in detail, for its whole life.
- **AF2** Soul recognition: a companion knows you in a new body or a new life.
- **AF3** It recognises disguised people, swapped bodies and impostors, which
  can expose the player too. Links [identity laws](worlds/IDENTITY_LAWS_BY_WORLD_2026-10-14.md).
- **AF4** Time loops and branches: Default: an ordinary pet resets with the
  loop; Dial: a bonded or spirit companion can keep some memory, or be the
  one thing that does. Links [timelines](TIMELINES_AND_DIVERGENCE.md).
- **AF5** A companion from the future: a regressor's familiar, or a pet that
