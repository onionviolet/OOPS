# Encounters, pursuit and scripted events

Updated 2026-10-03. This owner distinguishes the implemented encounter/pursuit increment from future authored enemies and event families. PROJECT.md owns creative direction; ENGINE_AUTHORING.md owns public runtime interfaces; PLAYTEST.md owns executed evidence.

## Independent choices for authors

A trigger says **when and why an encounter becomes due**. A locator says **how its actor can find a target**. A scene says **what happens at contact**. These are separate policies. An ordinary rival can have a fixed story deadline; a heavenly enemy can arrive after an earned achievement; a random discovery can begin a deterministic pursuit. None requires one universal enemy controller.

| Dimension | Existing or newly exercised policy | Further proposed content |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Direct action, saved played-time delay, caused event checks, completed-project predicate, existing contextual chance/guarantee and recurring calendar windows | Story chapters, witnessing an offense, killing an ally, using a forbidden technique, theft, debt, invitations, ritual dates, faction discovery, world crises |
| Finding | Witnessed local start/departure; bounded active ink-signature detection; declared same-realm heavenly authority | Reports, tracks, scent, public reputation, noisy energy use, shared dreams, bloodline resonance, soul marks, contract jurisdiction, global omen or location-independent trial |
| Access | Paid ordinary route movement/search, declared private shelter, signature masking, authority manifestation | Borders, sect sanctuary, wards, licensed entry, bribery, protective allies, bounded pursuit across selected worlds |
| Contact | Persistent request; idle acceptance; shared participant reservation; existing frozen nonlethal scene executor | Ambush, warning, trade, recruitment, challenge, duel, arrest, raid, rescue, possession, judgment, negotiation, cooperative expedition |
| Persistence | Frozen policy/lead/phase/request, own supplied budgets, one session and settlement, directed observation, source/relationship consequences | Wounds, grudges, debts, faction reports, captured property, recurring opponents, lasting alliances or binding judgments |

A locator has declared means. Ordinary concealment works against the present witness/ink tests. Heavenly authority can explicitly ignore it. The current heavenly writ's guarantee covers this realm, ordinary cover and the Shack; its granted authority manifests without paying another route cost and its response request has no deadline. It stops if its actual authority or participant is removed. Future enemies may have broader reach, different exceptions or no supported counter to locating you. Those choices must be authored, rather than inferred from every enemy being able to read player state.

A guaranteed arrival can offer resistance, negotiation, a protected representative, sacrifice, a trial or a lasting aftermath. Its arrival guarantee, strength, lethal eligibility, response deadline and outcome guarantee need separate declarations. A strong opponent need not auto-scale to the player. A future severe scripted consequence needs its own warning, interruption, defeat/recovery and settlement rules. The current proofs are voluntary and nonlethal; they select none of those irreversible policies.

## More original event families to author

These remain proposals, not additional delivered campaigns. Their ingredients can use the same trigger, knowledge, paid actor, response and durable-effect services:

| Family | Cause, arrival and useful interaction |
|---|---|
| Ordinary pursuit | A witnessed offense, contested claim or public bounty leads to reports and a paid local search. Settlement, shelter or a changed relationship can close it. |
| Marked nemesis | A bloodline, cursed item, soul seal or stolen identity permits declared tracking. Purification, disguise, replacing the item or changing jurisdiction can alter later meetings. |
| Heavenly chosen or rival | An independent advancement attracts a rival's attention. Their own means, allies, failed searches and later breakthroughs change encounters. |
| Heavenly judgment | A forbidden act or threshold produces an omen, a deadline and a guaranteed envoy or tribulation. Preparation changes responses and aftermath under the declared guarantee. |
| Scheduled visitor | Teachers, caravans, assessors or recurring tournaments have real arrival/access windows, cancellations and later reports. Attendance can unlock methods or agreements. |
| Consequence chain | Theft, insult, rescue, defeat, refusal or keeping a promise causes later reports, reprisals, favors or settlement offers. A chain retains who learned what and why. |
| Contextual ambush | A committed road segment, visible wealth, injury, night or noise creates one eligible draw. Detection, a scout, diversion or protection changes the resulting encounter. |
| Opportunity race | A ruin, resource, inheritance or breakthrough draws independently funded contenders. Cooperation, bidding, contest, concession and aftermath share the actual claim. |
| Faction operation | A patrol, arrest, sect invitation, recruitment or raid follows information and jurisdiction. Standing, guarantees, allies and custody matter at actual settlement. |
| Environmental crisis | Weather, migration, disease, supply failure or a spirit disturbance evolves independently. Observation, assistance and deliberate abstention retain their distinct costs. |
| Personal intrusion | Dreams, possession, memory challenges, inner demons or a source backlash have specific access to body/mind/source state. They need their own recovery and agency policies. |
| Multi-stage world event | Omen, gathering, arrival, conflict and reconstruction develop on declared boundaries. Earlier intervention changes later public/local opportunities and consequences. |

The next authored enemy can select a few of these dimensions. A universal bestiary, narrative generator, lethal world simulation, cross-world pursuit, full native party battle, automatic prestige and unrestricted history reversal are unfinished. First milestone remains one realm and one prestige layer, with vanilla continuity preserved.
