# World entry rules

Weibao, 2026-10-26: "save accordingly, and save rules so these will be taken
into considerations for other book worlds where things like these matters too?"

Getting into a world decides what the player keeps, who they are there, what
they owe and what the world thinks of them. The 2026-10-26 LOTM review found an
entrance that put two worlds in one universe, a mechanism read from a book's
opening that its later volumes overturn, and starts that never said what
crosses. These rules make every world's entry answer the same questions, so the
next book world gets them right first.

Status: standing consideration rules from Weibao's request, 2026-10-26. Every
world pass, start record and entry UI must answer them. The defaults named
inside a rule are dials, not mandates (AD5): a world may choose otherwise with
an in-world reason. Nothing here makes an unaccepted design default accepted
unless it cites an AD line. Source rounds: [intro rounds 25–28](INTRO_FLOW_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md#round-25-base-gameplay-and-getting-into-a-book-world-2026-10-26)
(BGP1–BGP26, XM1–XM13, ERA1–ERA3, YS1–YS6, PW1–PW6).

## Rules

| Code | Rule | Default, dial and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WER1 | **Worlds are separate unless a premise joins them.** No notice, ticket, road, purse or posting may link two worlds by implication. | Default: entry is a crossing with a named carrier (AD4, AD11, Rulebook 5). Dial: a world whose canon has travel between realms, or a declared remix premise, may join them with a cost and record. Reason: the Tingen notice made the Village and LOTM one universe by accident. |
| WER2 | **Every start declares its crossing method.** | Pick from the shared methods (XM1 soul into the recently dead, XM2 release by a world power, XM3 whole-body transport, XM4 rebirth as an infant, XM5 shared host, XM6 dream visit, XM7 summoned by locals, XM8 native-born with later memories, XM9 time return, XM10 carried by the cheat) or define a new one with the same answers. Reason: the method decides what the player loses. |
| WER3 | **Each method answers nine questions.** Body; memories; base cheat; items and money; identity and papers; language; who notices (WA14); the former life's state (AD12); obligations. | The start confirm step shows them in plain words before commitment (UR43, UIV1). Wording must match the mechanics; where they differ, record the mismatch as open. Reason: players should choose knowingly, and text must not promise what code does not do. |
| WER4 | **Find the source's real entry mechanism, including late reveals.** | A world pass reads past the opening: later volumes, sequels and the wiki cross-check (WPR rules) before modelling how outsiders arrive. Reason: LOTM's opening looks like plain transmigration; the books later show an engineered cocoon release with lasting strings attached. |
| WER5 | **Canon's mechanism is a premise, not a mandate.** | Offer the canon method where it fits, alongside a neutral crossing, and give the canon route its real consequences (pulls, claims, hazards). Reason: AD1 lets premises vary; one book's mechanism should not force every life into its plot. |
| WER6 | **Era is part of the start.** | A start names its date or era, and checks canon state at that date: who has arrived, what has been released, which institutions exist, which protagonists are where. A route that is ordinary in one era can be a divergence in another (AD2). Reason: a fourth LOTM cocoon is a divergence in 1349 and ordinary after 1352. |
| WER7 | **Origin must fit the method.** | If canon fixes who can arrive by a method (LOTM cocoons held Modern-Era people), a premise using it implies that origin. A continuing self from another world uses a method that fits it (AD10). Reason: the method and the past life must agree. |
| WER8 | **The host or body decides a lot, and is shown.** | Age, sex, health, species and circumstances come with the start (AD13) and appear before confirming. Dial: guided random may roll an unusual host; a specific one is a means (AD23). |
| WER9 | **What carries is stated per crossing.** | Memories and the base cheat carry by default (AD11); everything else needs a carrier the start names. Money from another world does not cross by default. Reason: AD11 and economy carryover (ECR27). |
| WER10 | **Age sets access, not a block.** | A young body does what it plausibly can; adult-only doors say why (GTR rules, UIV1). Young starts come in tiers: young host, born-there with a summarized childhood, then playable childhood later (YS1–YS3). Reason: AD38, and cost should set build order, not taste. |
| WER11 | **Live beside protagonists, never as them.** | A book world's player life is a fan premise next to canon figures. Check sequels and spin-offs for later protagonists and eras (LOTM's second book follows Lumian Lee from 1358). Reason: AD1 and the existing private-pack rule for direct recreations. |
| WER12 | **How the world reads an outsider is a dial.** | Default: a quiet crossing. Worlds whose canon reacts to outsiders (divination, fate, a system, a god's claim) may notice through their own mechanisms. Reason: drama comes from the world's laws (WA14). |
| WER13 | **One start list, no lobby world.** | Launch and the in-game Beginning view read the same start records, tagged new life, visit or move. No world is a required waiting room for another. Reason: random-entry direction (2026-10-11) and one grammar for one act (BGP8). |
| WER14 | **Entry is a world-pass coverage item.** | Every world audit or expansion (WPR rules) records WER2–WER12 answers for each start, or names the gap. Reason: these questions get skipped unless a pass must answer them. |
| WER15 | **Credit and licence for every source.** | Each absorbed world, game or fork records its source, credit and licence status. Freely available is not licensed; without a licence, take mechanics and shape, write our own code, assets and text, and credit the inspiration (PW3). Reason: attribution is owed, and permission is a separate fact. |
| WER16 | **Entry changes to saved worlds need a compatibility decision.** | Changing a start record, pack identity or carry rule that saves hold needs a migration or an explicit compatibility decision (AGENTS safety). Reason: the Soldier's Life pack rejects changed identity. |

## Current worlds against these rules (2026-10-26)

Start records today carry location, body and effects; none declares method,
era, origin or carry (WER2–WER9). The answers below are what each world pass
should settle; canon claims are to be checked against saved sources (WER4).

| World | Current start(s) | Entry questions to settle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lord of the Mysteries | Village crossing to four early-Tingen starts (1349) | Method per era (WER6); cocoon premise and its origin (WER7); host life instead of carried purse (WER9); launch entry (WER13) |
| Harry Potter | School first-year at eleven; adult glass trade | Born-there (XM8/XM4) fits the school start; how an adult outsider arrives; what a Muggle-born's family knows |
| Shadow Slave | Divergent Awakened artisan | The world's own carrier for awakening (WER4); era relative to the protagonist's story (WER6, WER11) |
| Reverend Insanity | Adult clan-sponsored Gu novice | Native-born or crossing (WER2); time-return premises are canon-shaped there (XM9) and need their own era check |
| Shadowbound company | Original fan-law life | Original premise: declare method and carry like any other (WER2, WER9) |
| Mother of Learning | Cyoria visitor arrival | Whether arrival is local travel inside the world or a crossing (WER1) |
| A Soldier's Life | Round-trip visit from the Village, four premises incl. a Village-veteran remix | Visit as XM6 or a declared remix premise (WER1); reword only with a pack version and compatibility check (WER16) |
| Village (YAIR) and originals | Default opening | Native-born (XM8); credit and licence recorded (WER15) |
