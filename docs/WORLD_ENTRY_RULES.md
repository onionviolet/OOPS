# World entry rules

Weibao, 2026-10-26: "save accordingly, and save rules so these will be taken
into considerations for other book worlds where things like these matters too?"

Getting into a world decides what the player keeps, who they are there, what
they owe and what the world thinks of them. The 2026-10-26 LOTM review found an
entrance that put two worlds in one universe, a mechanism read from a book's
opening that its later volumes overturn, and starts that never said what
crosses. These rules make every world's entry answer the same questions, so the
next book world gets them right first.

Status: standing consideration rules from Weibao's request, 2026-10-26. Every
world pass, start record and entry UI must answer them. The defaults named
inside a rule are dials, not mandates (AD5): a world may choose otherwise with
an in-world reason. Nothing here makes an unaccepted design default accepted
unless it cites an AD line. Source rounds: [intro rounds 25–28](INTRO_FLOW_CONSIDERATIONS_2026-10-13.md#round-25-base-gameplay-and-getting-into-a-book-world-2026-10-26)
(BGP1–BGP26, XM1–XM13, ERA1–ERA3, YS1–YS6, PW1–PW6).

## Rules

| Code | Rule | Default, dial and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WER1 | **Worlds are separate unless a premise joins them.** No notice, ticket, road, purse or posting may link two worlds by implication. | Default: entry is a crossing with a named carrier (AD4, AD11, Rulebook 5). Dial: a world whose canon has travel between realms, or a declared remix premise, may join them with a cost and record. Reason: the Tingen notice made the Village and LOTM one universe by accident. |
| WER2 | **Every start declares its crossing method.** | Pick from the shared methods (XM1 soul into the recently dead, XM2 release by a world power, XM3 whole-body transport, XM4 rebirth as an infant, XM5 shared host, XM6 dream visit, XM7 summoned by locals, XM8 native-born with later memories, XM9 time return, XM10 carried by the cheat) or define a new one with the same answers. Reason: the method decides what the player loses. |
| WER3 | **Each method answers nine questions.** Body; memories; base cheat; items and money; identity and papers; language; who notices (WA14); the former life's state (AD12); obligations. | The start confirm step shows them in plain words before commitment (UR43, UIV1). Wording must match the mechanics; where they differ, record the mismatch as open. Reason: players should choose knowingly, and text must not promise what code does not do. |
| WER4 | **Find the source's real entry mechanism, including late reveals.** | A world pass reads past the opening: later volumes, sequels and the wiki cross-check (WPR rules) before modelling how outsiders arrive. Reason: LOTM's opening looks like plain transmigration; the books later show an engineered cocoon release with lasting strings attached. |
| WER5 | **Canon's mechanism is a premise, not a mandate.** | Offer the canon method where it fits, alongside a neutral crossing, and give the canon route its real consequences (pulls, claims, hazards). Reason: AD1 lets premises vary; one book's mechanism should not force every life into its plot. |
| WER6 | **Era is part of the start.** | A start names its date or era, and checks canon state at that date: who has arrived, what has been released, which institutions exist, which protagonists are where. A route that is ordinary in one era can be a divergence in another (AD2). Reason: a fourth LOTM cocoon is a divergence in 1349 and ordinary after 1352. |
| WER7 | **Origin must fit the method.** | If canon fixes who can arrive by a method (LOTM cocoons held Modern-Era people), a premise using it implies that origin. A continuing self from another world uses a method that fits it (AD10). Reason: the method and the past life must agree. |
| WER8 | **The host or body decides a lot, and is shown.** | Age, sex, health, species and circumstances come with the start (AD13) and appear before confirming. Dial: guided random may roll an unusual host; a specific one is a means (AD23). |
| WER9 | **What carries is stated per crossing.** | Memories and the base cheat carry by default (AD11); everything else needs a carrier the start names. Money from another world does not cross by default. Reason: AD11 and economy carryover (ECR27). |
| WER10 | **Age sets access, not a block.** | A young body does what it plausibly can; adult-only doors say why (GTR rules, UIV1). Young starts come in tiers: young host, born-there with a summarized childhood, then playable childhood later (YS1–YS3). Reason: AD38, and cost should set build order, not taste. |
| WER11 | **Default: live beside protagonists.** Dial: a life may follow, replace a bystander or diverge from them; being the protagonist is available in the private direct-adaptation pack, where the whole interface takes the lead's perspective, and in worlds whose protagonist is the player's own avatar (SG8, AD1). | A book world's ordinary player life is a fan premise next to canon figures. Check sequels and spin-offs for later protagonists and eras (LOTM's second book follows Lumian Lee from 1358). Reason: public attribution, canon fate rules built around that character, and a start that declares its mode like any other. History: until 2026-10-28 this row read "Live beside protagonists, never as them"; Weibao accepted the PX18 rewording with the be-seat default on 2026-10-28 ([record](USER_VISION.md#cast-lives-the-be-the-lead-seat-from-the-leads-own-perspective-2026-10-28)). |
| WER12 | **How the world reads an outsider is a dial.** | Default: a quiet crossing. Worlds whose canon reacts to outsiders (divination, fate, a system, a god's claim) may notice through their own mechanisms. Reason: drama comes from the world's laws (WA14). |
| WER13 | **One start list; a lobby is a premise, not an accident.** Launch and the in-game Beginning view read the same start records, tagged new life, visit, move or hub (WL3, WL4). | Default: no world becomes a required waiting room by accident, as the Village did for LOTM. Dial: hub worlds exist where a premise, world or cheat makes one (WER17), and may be the start. Reason: random-entry direction (2026-10-11), one grammar for one act (BGP8), and hub premises such as Reincarnation Paradise that Weibao already selected. Revised 2026-10-26 after Weibao asked about lobby worlds; the first wording ("No world is a required waiting room") was a blanket rule against the project's own default-dial-reason framing. |
| WER14 | **Entry is a world-pass coverage item.** | Every world audit or expansion (WPR rules) records WER2–WER12 answers for each start, or names the gap. Reason: these questions get skipped unless a pass must answer them. |
| WER15 | **Credit and licence for every source.** | Each absorbed world, game or fork records its source, credit and licence status. Freely available is not licensed; without a licence, take mechanics and shape, write our own code, assets and text, and credit the inspiration (PW3). Reason: attribution is owed, and permission is a separate fact. |
| WER16 | **Entry changes to saved worlds need a compatibility decision.** | Changing a start record, pack identity or carry rule that saves hold needs a migration or an explicit compatibility decision (AGENTS safety). Reason: the Soldier's Life pack rejects changed identity. |
| WER17 | **A hub world declares its own entry answers.** A hub is a world others are reached through: a mission hub (Reincarnation Paradise, a Main God Space), a multiverse crossroads (D&D's Sigil), a cheat or system space, a between-lives realm (GHS28), or a private realm or home pocket. | Each hub states: who runs it and why; how you arrive and whether you can leave or refuse missions; what crosses through it and how (hub points, contracts, a storage space; often the hub is the carrier WER9 asks for); its time rate against the worlds it sends you to; failure and death stakes; whether visited worlds keep their own histories (AD4) or are copies; and its nested starts, each with its own WER answers (WL4). Dial: a hub can be optional, mandatory, hidden or found later (LIFE_SIM_NORTH_STAR route notes: a hub may accept only assigned drops until a back door is found). Reason: hubs are a real premise family; they need rules, not a ban. |

## Random local start and employment clarification (2026-11-01)

[Weibao’s clarification](USER_VISION.md#random-local-lives-and-earned-employment-2026-11-01) applies WER8 and existing AD13/AD23. A random local life is a valid alternative to a named protagonist or an already-employed start. Default: start from the sampled person’s actual household, cash, knowledge, qualifications, ties and obligations; discover vacancies and seek work through the institution’s actual hiring route. Dials: existing employees retain their jobs, inheritance/transmigration may retain status where the mechanism supports it, and referrals, informal work or exceptional entry can bypass ordinary applications with a reason. Employment, membership, clearance and supernatural advancement are separate consequences; hiring is not a pre-granted potion.

Current LOTM probationer starts begin employed. They do not establish an application/interview/payroll loop. Klein’s canon life retains its own source-defined route. ACCOUNT FOR NOW: start profile and run policy must not be equated with permanent class, institutional membership or ability grants. LATER: implement a bounded random-local housing → vacancy → application → hiring/refusal → first paid work route when that seat is selected; test starting circumstances, failed hiring, wage settlement, save/reload and a viable alternate livelihood. This does not change the current Klein-first build order.

## Inherited dead-host and advanced-start palette (2026-11-01)

[Weibao’s examples](USER_VISION.md#inherited-dead-host-circumstances-and-advanced-starts-2026-11-01) extend the same WER/AD13/AD23 entry framework. They are desired possibilities; the mechanism and source qualification for each world remain to be designed. No new universal death law is asserted.

**ST4 ACCOUNT FOR NOW, cause versus arrival:** entry answers separately identify former occupant, body, new controller/occupant, death event, arrival time and surviving causes. Ordinary dials include replacement, revival, coexistence or reconstruction where the world supports them, with an in-world means/cost. Restoring life does not imply full healing, cleansing, memory recovery or instant safety. A body that cannot support life needs a specified restoration mechanism or a different viable arrival premise. The immediate survival problem is part of the start, not a silent reset to full health.

| Case / pressure | What can persist or transfer, subject to source mechanism | Distinguishing scenario before implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ST5 Wilderness death | Actual location, weather/time, injuries, exposure, poison, gear/ammunition, carried supplies, lost companions, shelter distance and ongoing threats. Skills and route knowledge may have gaps. | Arrival does not teleport to town or refill stock. Survive through available shelter, care, escape or help; interruption/save/reload preserves the threat. Rescue or powerful aid may be a supported premise, not a forced balance rule. |
| ST6 Social/institutional inheritance | Body recognition, documents, possessions and actual property custody; household duties, debts, employment, reputation, promises, enemies and missing-person/death reports. These differ from the entrant’s own remembered identity. | A known face may be recognized without the new occupant possessing every password, skill or relationship. Legal/organizational claims and other people’s consent are separate from physical possession; witnesses may update beliefs through actual evidence. |
| ST7 Lore-danger death | Distinguish harmful information, memory fragments, active contamination, an attached mark/claim, an external observer and the artifact/environment that delivered it. Which survive occupant replacement is world-specific, not an assumed cleanse. | Newly admitted memory or rereading may remain hazardous; deleting a remembered report need not undo an independent mark or recipient. Keep unknown truths out of start titles, previews, counts and debug information exposed to players. A neutral premise such as waking at an abandoned camp can preserve discovery; the exact danger is source-qualified privately. |
| ST8 Advanced host | Anatomical condition, world-specific cultivation/potion/body resources, gear, learned techniques, authorities/contracts and hostile attention have separate owners and transfer conditions. Rank is not a universal level package. | Losing a former controller’s technique while retaining a changed body, or inheriting costly power and obligations, must be representable. Reopen with one source-backed advanced start, actual usable ability/cost/support and a failure/aftermath case. |

**ST9 ACCOUNT FOR NOW, seeded entry and disclosure:** sample the host/circumstance/hidden-cause tuple under the selected premise, era and content/generator version, commit it once per new life, and let previews show only known facts. Reopening a preview or reloading does not create another death cause, stock or roll. Explicitly starting a new life may choose a new seed; this is save integrity and reproducibility, not anti-cheat policy. Time between death and arrival matters when decay, pursuit, travel or continuing contamination has a consumer. Player-facing risk disclosures describe what is known and what the chosen entry method actually guarantees. Unknown cause is kept unknown, without inventing a guarantee of safety.

**ST10 ACCOUNT FOR NOW, disclosure and first response:** distinguish the player's selected premise from the character's knowledge. Default low-spoiler cards can describe a wilderness awakening or a troubled inheritance; an explicit advanced-start option may disclose a broad danger category without revealing the sampled cause or future plot. The disclosure dial follows the chosen experience and sealed-secret rules. Give a hazardous start a meaningful first response through the ordinary arrival/decision clock boundary, rather than silently curing the threat or advancing past the player's chance to act. A deliberately harsher premise can narrow that opportunity with clear known terms. This is no invulnerability grant.

**LATER / DELEGATE:** qualify and deliver these as separate bounded starts when selected: an ordinary dead-host local life, a wilderness survival case, then an advanced/lore-danger case. Suggested capability: substantive source synthesis and implementation, medium effort. Done for each: one actual entry transaction, explicit persistence/transfer terms, known-only preview, viable first decisions, real ongoing cause/cost, interruption and save/reload proof. No bulk world generation, new reader dispatch or current KJ2 expansion follows from this palette.

**ST11 ACCOUNT FOR NOW, inherited work and knowledge:** inherited contracts may bind a face, body, named identity, soul, item or institution under different world laws. An unfinished job/ritual revalidates its actual controller, payer, materials and support on arrival; a new occupant is not automatically the former signatory or practitioner. Memory may be fragmentary or unreliable, with attribution/comprehension separate from a physical carrier. Read-only start previews do not themselves commit harmful learning, recovery, stock or random draws; any hazardous memory transfer happens through the actual entry operation. Reopen these cases with the first inherited contract, active task or hazardous-memory consumer, not a speculative universal inheritance engine.

## Developer build-through and coverage beyond the lead (2026-11-03)

[Weibao's question](USER_VISION.md#protagonist-playthroughs-as-the-developer-build-through-2026-11-03) confirms the developer-route reading above for every protagonist life, not only Klein's: the lead's life builds and verifies everything it touches, and ordinary and alternative lives reuse those systems through the same commands (CLR8). The lines below are proposals (AD32), ranked by how much they change a decision.

**ST12 ACT NOW (done in this record):** the developer-route classification applies to each lead-life world. [HP_LEAD_RUN_START](gm/dispatch/refinement-audits-2026-10-31/HP_LEAD_RUN_START_2026-11-03.md) is Harry's developer route under ST1, not a player start; ST2-ST3 govern Hogwarts-era player entry when selected.

**ST13 ACCOUNT FOR NOW, the lead's path is not the world.** An ordinary start reaches jobs, households, districts, organisations, ability paths and dates the lead never touched; a dead-host start (ST5-ST8) lands where and when the lead was absent. Default: each arc's gap loop (LSD472) tags a miss as on-path (build work now) or off-path (recorded in the arc's plan, built when a selected start or a second case needs it, CLR7). Dial: a world whose book is ensemble-heavy has a wider on-path ring. Reason: building every implied place now prebuilds unchosen content (section G of the lead-life rules).

**ST14 ACCOUNT FOR NOW, canon inputs only.** In the forced canon run, every mechanism has run only on canon state, and pinned results or canon pull can make a beat land without the mechanism producing it. Default: before an arc's systems count as ready for other lives, run one beat with an off-canon input (a different actor, purse, timing or choice) and read whether the result follows by mechanism (AD38). Pinned results that hide strain (LSD472 kind 4) count as debt against that readiness. Reason: the WER developer-tooling row above already says the harness does not prove open play; this names the cheapest check that does.

**ST15 ACCOUNT FOR NOW, locked options are the alternative-life frontier.** Each "Not how it happened" option shown in the canon run is an alternative-life promise. Default: the arc's gap list records each locked option as resolves-by-mechanism, needs-build or out-of-world. Reason: the developer route then yields the open-run backlog with no separate survey.

**ST16 ACCOUNT FOR NOW, depth follows the life, not the book.** People get depth by tier from how much the book shows them near the lead (LSD126-LSD137, proposal). A life that makes a once-met person central needs that tier raised by contact, from the same records, with fills labelled (CLR5). Knowledge comes from the selected person's state, never the lead's reports (ST2, sealed-secret rule).

**ST17 ACCOUNT FOR NOW, two kinds of done.** "Lead replay done" (LSD156: events in order inside their windows) and "world ready for an ordinary life" (ST3's done check) are separate measures. A progress report names which one it gives.

**LATER / DELEGATE:** a coverage view that lists, per world, which systems a non-lead seat has exercised on a natural route. Suggested capability: sonnet-level implementation, small-medium effort. Done when one non-lead life's systems appear beside the lead's on the current build.


## Content reconciliation B340: full owner scope and evidence limits

All134 original lines were reviewed: WER1–WER17, the dated world table, random-local employment, developer/ordinary entry, ST1–ST17 and the inherited-host/advanced-start palette. WER11 already preserves the old never-as wording and its October28 replacement; no second rewrite or new acceptance is needed. The developer build-through classification and current Klein/Harry selection are supported by the later direction owners. ST13–ST17's exact extra checks, field choices, coverage UI and raised NPC tiers remain proposals where labelled, rather than being adopted merely by this review.

The October26 matrix is historical. Its no-method/era/carry statement does not establish current absence across all starts, and subsequent KJ2 route/menu claims retain their actual receipt scope. The named developer route's browser verification is not a fresh test here, proof of every arc or source fidelity, random-start generation, natural hiring/payroll or whole-world ordinary coverage. A selected low-spoiler player life uses its own knowledge, household, papers, support and obligations; starting employed is a valid preset rather than proof that an application loop exists. Current path and date/era claims need their actual record when changed.

Preserve nine crossing answers without turning them into nine omniscient disclosures. Known terms, visible body/circumstances and the method's actual guarantee can be stated before commitment, while hidden causes, claims and future events stay behind actual discovery. A later source revelation can invalidate an opening-only mechanism; mark unknown source boundaries rather than inventing a complete late-book read. A wiki cross-check does not replace the selected source. Canon arrival, neutral crossing, AU/remix and an imported continuing self can have different carriers and origins; the ordinary canon method's origin is not a global prohibition on exceptions.

The dead-host palette usefully separates cause, body, controller, arrival and surviving threats. Revival is not automatic full healing, and inherited recognition does not grant passwords, competence, consent or soul-bound authority. Work in progress may preserve real elapsed effects and partial stock; revalidating a new controller must not blindly restart it or erase an obligation tied to a different actual identity. Harmful information, contamination, a mark, an observer and a dangerous artifact are different dependencies. Previewing a start is distinct from the entry operation that actually transfers memory or material.

ST9's committed sampled tuple and WER16 require actual compatibility rather than trusting a seed/fingerprint alone. Identical seeds need the same generator/content and relevant state; new-version behavior is not proven unchanged by default missing fields. Genuine uncertainty remains uncertain. Dangerous source starts need actual meaningful initial response under their chosen premise, without a blanket invulnerability or hidden safety guarantee. A harsher method can narrow that response through its known terms and real law.

WER15 separates attribution, licence and available source. This reconciliation did not verify any current licence or grant publication rights; credit does not establish permission. Hub examples and nested starts do not make an unselected hub compulsory, nor do quiet crossings imply no observer can ever detect them. AD51 supports a first useful shared seam, and AD52 compresses repeated work while preserving actual entry/clock/custody/knowledge consequences. No WER/ST rule code, new start, source qualification, entry transaction, runtime, UI or save was changed or freshly verified here.
