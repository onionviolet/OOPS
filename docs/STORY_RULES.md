## Canon and divergence

| Code | Rule | Default, dial, reason | Builds on |

| STY1 | **Canon follows its course unless disturbed enough** (restates AD2). Divergence comes from causes. | Default: canon events list their preconditions; averting a cause can avert the event (WA15). Dial: how much push a fact needs, per fact (fragile or robust, LSD243). | STY7 | **Forward anchors bind generation, not the player** (restates WA24). | Default: player-caused divergence may change an anchor and is labelled; generated fill may not. Dial: none. Reason: WA24. | WA24 |
| STY33 | **A canon result fixes where a scene lands; the route there can vary.** The anchor is the outcome band (who wins, what gets made, who trusts whom by when); the way there can differ by method, order, helper, timing inside the window, price paid and which of the lead's means carried it. Each route runs through real mechanisms and pays its own costs. | Default: a canon-run beat offers the canon route and any other route the state reasonably supports (AD38); canon pull (STY3) acts on the result, never on the route; a route that misses the band is divergence (STY6, level 3 of STY5). ## Levels of fate

Added 2026-11-10 at Weibao's question ("levels of fate?", with the Reverend Insanity theory that Heaven's Will pushed the world toward the destruction of Fate Gu to give the non-humans a chance). Revised the same day from the [fate research handoff](gm/causal-endings/FATE_REDDIT_CLAUDE_HANDOFF_2026-11-10.md), which surveyed the Reddit threads (R1–R4) and checked chapters in the held edition (C1–C5). The ladder and rows are agent proposals. The book's fate war and its unfinished ending are public plot.

What the sources support: Heaven's Will is shown shaping openings and pushing against concentrations of power (C1, C5). The theory that it set the whole fate war in motion for the variant humans is plausible but unproven; the threads and chapters support several competing aims, including a narrower effort against Spectral Soul (C1, R1–R4). Several plans can converge without their authors agreeing on the aftermath (R4, C4).

The ladder describes how fate feels at a given fact, place and era. F0–F4 are levels; F5 and F6 are modifiers that can sit at any level, not stronger pressure.

| Level | Name | What holds the course | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F0 | None | Ordinary causes and chance | A world with no course force |
| F1 | Causal mass | Several independent, live routes lead to the same outcome (LSD243) | Every world, by default (STY2) |
| F2 | Soft pull | An influence biases an eligible draw or creates a cause through a real channel, leaving traces (RLZ1–RLZ3) | Ripples that revert while Fate Gu is whole (ch1754, ch1777) |
| F3 | Actor with an agenda | Something with goals acts through what it can detect and reach (LSD239) | Heaven's Will helping some rebirths and later opposing (ch1022, ch1362) |
| F4 | Instrument | A tool imposes a rule or gate within its reach | Fate Gu; its damage opened the luck path (ch1093) |
| F5 | Exemption (modifier) | A target is outside one mechanism's reach, for a reason it carries | Fang Yuan's complete otherworldly status against the Fate attack (ch1960) |
| F6 | Contention (modifier) | Several plans act on the same facts; each resolves by custody, timing and method | Heaven's Will, Star Constellation, Heavenly Court, Spectral Soul and Red Lotus (C2, C4) |

## Building a story slice

1. Name the canon beat or caused event, its preconditions and its key (STY1, STY8).
2. Pick the run mode and pull (STY3, STY4); keep untaken options as stubs (WA28).
3. Route effects through existing verbs or place acts (STY15); news through the knowledge rules (STY9).
4. Write the Reports line with RPT rules and a voice sample (PRS7).
5. Tests: the beat and pull tests beside `beat_records.js` and `canon_pull.js`; browser proof at the desktop viewport (AD43).
