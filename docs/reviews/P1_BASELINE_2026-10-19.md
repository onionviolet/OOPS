## What this revises in the scale plan

- **F1. Tick cost is far lower than the plan says.** Plan 3.1 and 3.2 item 4 cite 30 ms per game minute fresh and 90-130 ms developed (a 2026-10-16 busy-host figure). The gate's Node `advanceSimulation` is 0.13 to 5.2 ms per minute, about 20 to 200 times lower. The two measure different things: the gate times only the expansion simulation step in Node, while the old figure likely included the whole browser tick (render, save, UI). So the "43 s to 3 min of catch-up per day away" arithmetic and the claim that active-actor tick cost binds is **unconfirmed**. It now needs a whole-tick measurement before P5 leans on it. Simulation-step cost for a 68-actor crowd is small.
- **F2. Browser idle cost, not simulation, is the visible load.** Crowded idles at 52 % task busy with 15 long tasks (max 343 ms), and 31k-59k DOM nodes (noisy) against the plan's 16-27k. That is the UI-list pressure the plan rated "not binding" (3.2 item 6); in the crowded fixture it is the largest number here.
- **F3. Storage write volume exceeds the 2026-10-14 budgets.** Crowded writes 34,103 KB/min (budget 25,500) at 60 writes/min, i.e. about 570 KB per write. The stored save is 279 KB, so the volume is repeated whole-save writes, not save size. Save size itself stays far under the 4 MiB guard.
- **F4. The standing gate reports FAIL, from budget drift.** 5 of 26 checks: fresh task busy 13.4 > 13; mid long tasks 15 > 14; mid storage 8,564 > 7,700; crowded long tasks 15 > 12; crowded storage 34,103 > 25,500. Determinism and page errors pass everywhere. Budgets are a 2026-10-14 PROPOSAL set on a busier host and an older head. Not changed here: re-setting budgets would hide F3 rather than answer it.
