## Proposed defaults and rule clarifications

These are recommendations for the existing owners, not newly accepted UR codes. Prefer a compact selected-recipe summary inside the current list/detail layout. A tooltip alone loses task stability; expanding every ingredient/formula loses scanning speed. A small input→output strip could help longer production chains later, but this simple balm does not need a diagram.

| Code | Proposed owner clarification, default / dial / reason | Existing coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | **UR42/71: Keep the known facts needed for the current commitment together and visible.** For a consumable recipe this normally includes output purpose, material quantities, meaningful effect magnitudes/duration, relevant risk and the action. Put derivations, long lore and exceptional stock allocation behind labelled details. Dial by task, familiarity, world knowledge and space. Reason: a short display that forces repeated inspection has not simplified the decision. | Existing rules support it; make the application explicit rather than add another rulebook. |
| P2 | **UR46/64: Density means useful comparison, not number of rows or minimum text.** Consolidate repeated ingredients and omit irrelevant generic rows; retain explicit unknown states when the missing knowledge affects the choice. Dial by domain and stakes. Reason: “not applicable,” “not assessed” and “not supplied by this UI” mean different things. | Quality uncertainty still matters for graded gear; do not remove unknowns wholesale or silently fix the projection in presentation code. |
| P3 | **UR71/74: Reuse the authoritative product-effect projection across recipe, inventory and effect inspection.** Different surfaces may choose different detail levels; supported bonuses, units and conditions must agree. Dial by observer knowledge and target. Reason: the current recipe summary receives less useful data than its full result renderer. | No second effect formula, universal appraisal or hardcoded reference numbers. |
| P4 | **UR57: Check whether the player can choose between two relevant recipes with the necessary facts visible before crafting.** Record extra inspections, retained list selection and blocked/changed-stock handling. Dial evidence to changed behavior under AD36. Reason: passing geometry or a collapsed-detail test can miss poor decision support. | Add this to a future touched crafting journey, not a new universal all-world suite or present completion claim. |

Suggested visible order: product name and short practical description; meaningful known use effects and duration; aligned inputs with usable/needed counts; readiness, conditional odds and applicable time; Craft amount/action. Failure spending remains visible. Show relevant XP when training is the task; keep routine formula/bonus breakdown on demand. Value is useful for a trade comparison, not a substitute for a supported sale quote. Existing stable action placement and list/Reports context remain.

The same approach applies to cooking, smithing, equipment comparison, services and other quoted actions, with each task's actual decision facts. Experimental mixtures keep unknown outcomes unknown; this reference does not authorize revealing effects before observation. See `src/crafting_home.js` for that distinct consumer.

No rule, runtime, balance, save or accepted direction was changed. No commit or push. Runtime tests are unnecessary for this assessment-only change; the disposable build/browser are the bounded comparison evidence, not gameplay completion.

### F7: Separate four meanings of “layer”

| Layer meaning | Distinction and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Task/disclosure | Browse → inspect/compare → configure/review → commit → running work/settlement, with method/history reference alongside. These are roles, not compulsory pages or a five-click wizard. A routine craft can combine several in one pane. |
| Knowledge | Actual properties, observer knowledge and permitted forecasts differ. Opening a deeper panel does not authorize more truth. Observed success with one formulation does not automatically identify every ingredient or guarantee another maker's response. MR1/4/10/31 apply at every display depth. |
| Activity depth | Quick, guided and deep are MR18's ways of executing an activity, not collapsed/expanded UI. More explanation can be opened while quick crafting remains selected. A deep-mode choice changes supported actions, not the underlying laws or an arbitrary quality multiplier. |
| Software/presentation | Domain resolution/quote → observer projection → surface renderer → skin/world styling. Improve an incomplete projection before decorating its renderer. Share facts and interaction conventions while retaining separate composition, parts, pattern and identity logics under MR29. |

### F12: Important interactions across the layers

Selecting a better part can alter output, stock, quote and comparison at once. Preserve exact selection and inspectable subject while revalidating at commit; do not silently substitute another copy after sorting or consuming a stack. A shared physical input serving several roles must not be charged once per role: `craft_ingredient_selection.js` already consumes canonical physical debit/retained rows and rejects duplicate exact keys. Reuse that boundary rather than infer cost from displayed functional slots.

Book-world consumers also exist now, rather than only future stage designs. Selected source inspection of `hp_brewing_panel.js` shows separate obtaining, lot preparation/stewing, sample and brewing sections, observer-limited lot descriptions, method/repeat selection state and experiment comparison imports. `hp_brewing_domain.js` binds this to exact native material holdings, the played clock, a domain state owner and durable transaction; its comment explicitly distinguishes menu selection order from actual addition stages. `book_world_lotm_bench.js` separates formula knowledge, physical auxiliaries, start, collection and later use. These are source-level examples of why a single balm card is insufficient; this round does not certify their present full journeys, fidelity or installation. Book-world source rows containing unbound quantities or owner-needed effects are research records, not executable recipes merely because a UI could display them.

Running and cancelled work need the mechanism's actual spending/return semantics. The mixture panel's cancel returns held inputs; the herbal timed-heat panel says materials and charcoal are already spent and abandon produces waste. A shared “Cancel and refund” label would be false across those two existing consumers. Closing inspection is neither cancellation nor an instruction to rerun work on reload. Receipts belong after durable settlement, distinct from a forecast or a started acknowledgement.

Known producers/uses can connect browsing, inventory, storage and crafting without a new chain planner: `recipe_connections.js` and its current `display.js` consumers already provide those links and local returns. Preserve them and existing stock/output context before adding another navigation surface. A material's known substitute role does not erase its identity, price, quality, custody or any distinction the actual process consumes.
