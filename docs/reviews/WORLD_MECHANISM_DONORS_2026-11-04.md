## Two concrete donor studies

### Naruto: inheritance is broader than a pedigree bonus

The strongest proposal is **capability origin and bodily carrier**, with genetics as one local route. Track separately: familial origin, current bodily structure, whether/when the faculty expresses, the learned method, control, supply and the evidence observers possess. No universal DNA locus or dominance rule follows from a clan name. Clan membership, biological descent, taught secrets and institutional permission can disagree.

The [official Naruto coverage](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_2624) confirms the narrow label of Sharingan as Uchiha kekkei genkai. The local ledger supports separate control, teaching, eye provenance, puppetry and sealing anchors. The later Narutopedia material in AB12 is **secondary**, not a qualified complete inheritance algorithm. Its example of one survivor among ten subjects should remain an observed sample until stronger evidence establishes a success probability; it should not silently become a canonical independent 10% roll.

**Proposed distinguishing case:** two actors know the same taught method; one has a compatible inherited/acquired faculty and one does not. A procedure can change the actual carrier where the source supports it, without granting every taught method, clan membership or social permission. Later, a qualified test can reveal something the character previously did not know. Exact Naruto transplant cost and copying boundary remain scene questions, not invented balancing numbers.

Useful recipients include local original genetics, cultivation constitutions, species/ancestry, implants, magical organs and transferred sources. HP family blood status remains a social/ancestral fact with its own source-specific effects; it is not substituted for spell power. LOTM progression is not reduced to Mendelian inheritance. This donor is a good bounded research task now, with gameplay integration at the next selected bodily/source consumer.

### Witch Hat Atelier: a physical design can be executable means

The [official introduction](https://tongari-anime.com/en/) establishes drawing Casting Seals with special tools and the social concealment of that knowledge. That supports the premise of a construction-based magic system. It does not specify a complete diagram parser. Neither the local linear rune grammar nor a fan simulator supplies canon geometry.

**Proposed first study:** one source-qualified useful spell, its drawn structure, physical tools/surface, activation, output and a changed/damaged/incomplete design where the source shows one. Questions to answer: which marks determine operation and parameters; whether orientation, placement or completeness matters; what the ink/tool contributes; what copying preserves; what is reusable or consumed; how a person discovers the defect. These are research questions until actual panels qualify them.

Default implementation candidate: editable structured diagram parts with a readable preview and a physical workpiece, using local grammar rules. Later freehand drawing could be added if its recognition/artwork cost serves play. Component placement and freehand input can coexist. Avoid turning real-world mouse precision into compulsory magical skill. The character's practiced drawing and available tools can matter mechanically while the player chooses a design.

Useful recipients: FMA arrays, wards, inscriptions, talismans, circuits and engineered devices at the level of **constructed artifacts with evaluated behavior**. Their grammars and transformation laws remain distinct. Do not equate an FMA array, a Naruto seal and a Witch Hat Casting Seal because each is drawn.
