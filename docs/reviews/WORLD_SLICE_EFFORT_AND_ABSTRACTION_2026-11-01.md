## S1: starts cost according to the life they support

Cheap variation changes initial funds, housing, contacts or learned skill while consuming the same supported loop. A distinct livelihood needs its own admission, earning, progression and aftermath. A different body, controller, era or power law can need new mechanisms and persistence. Adding names to a menu does not deliver those lives.

JJK candidates: a trainee with supervised assignments; a support/investigation worker with a genuinely noncombat livelihood; an independent practitioner with a qualified means of training, work and recovery. Curse/incarnated-host or advanced inherited-technique starts belong to a higher-cost group. These are candidates requiring source/era qualification, not canon eligibility claims.

LOTM candidates use ST2–ST11: local worker/applicant, employed institutional novice and later wilderness/inherited-host/advanced premises. Existing employed presets do not prove applying for a job, becoming qualified or surviving each new entry. Klein remains the development route; ordinary starts retain known-only previews and their own practical first decisions.

Marginal planning estimates after the shared loop exists: roughly 2–8 active hours for a source-qualified data variant plus entry/persistence proof; 1–3 workdays for a distinct livelihood using proven mechanisms; several days to weeks for a new body/power/era mechanism. These are low-confidence bands and overlap E3's included starts; do not add them twice.

## A1: abstract routine work, preserve consequential choices

Recommended working proposal: a shared activity envelope with separate world/case logic, following MR18/MR29 and the [scope ladders](../SUBSYSTEM_SCOPE_LADDERS_2026-10-16.md). Default to summarized work where internal detail does not change a player decision; offer selected methods, delegation or closer play when decisions matter. This can substantially reduce authoring/click burden without flattening the world. The same-day [difficult-concept palette](../gm/dispatch/refinement-audits-2026-10-31/DIFFICULT_CONCEPT_ABSORPTION_PALETTE.md) owns exceptional mechanism contracts; this report addresses activity resolution and cost. Summarizing player work does not replace that palette's concrete source distinctions with vague generic powers.

Example: **Search the records, 30 minutes.** Choose archive, witness or supernatural method where actually available. Admission checks access, language, skill, tools and resources. Work consumes time and committed costs. Settlement produces an attributed lead, a supported negative finding, a failed attempt or exposure, according to the case and method. The notebook/next action consumes that result. A paid negative result still tells the player what was attempted and its limits.

Use tables/templates for routine inquiries and practice; reserve authored choices for interpreting conflicting evidence, trusting someone, taking a dangerous method, revealing information or committing to action. An unusual alternative is policy-based delegation: select budget, method and stopping conditions, then receive a report from a qualified NPC. That requires its actual worker/support and cannot substitute for the player's unknown knowledge.

The distinguishing invariant is causality, not animation or click count: repeated search of an exhausted record set does not manufacture a new true clue; a changed source/method/skill can reveal something new. Rumors and mistaken conclusions remain attributed. A legitimate supernatural method can bypass an ordinary investigation step according to that world's law. A timer does not itself authorize truthful knowledge, cancel danger or supply missing materials.

For a selected implementation, trace command → committed job/costs → case/knowledge state owner → notebook/access/next-action consumer. Test one failure: lose archive access or ritual support partway through. Settled knowledge matches work actually completed, costs follow the selected policy, and reload cannot duplicate the result. Keep hints separate from solved truth and observer knowledge separate from hidden causes. These are applications of existing boundaries, not a new universal simulator or extra gate.

**Would it be better?** Yes for repeated investigation, study, routine rituals, travel and ordinary work, where the current alternative spends effort on steps with little choice. Use a hybrid for signature discoveries, consequential bargains and tactical combat. Full manual simulation and a single universal success bar are rejected as defaults; choose detail per actual decision and world.
