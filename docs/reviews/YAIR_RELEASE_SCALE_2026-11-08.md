# YAIR release scale and the first OOPS release

The [upstream game changelog](https://miktaew.github.io/yet-another-idle-rpg/changelog.html) documents feature updates and their follow-up fixes. The comparison below uses the lead version in each update family. OOPS inherits a YAIR development baseline, so the comparison credits changes OOPS adds to what the player could already do.

| YAIR update | What changed in play |
| --- | --- |
| `v0.3.5` and `v0.4.0` | The economy rework made loot prices respond to sales and recover over time. The stances update changed how players fight and added encounters, skills and training. These are focused updates with effects that recur throughout play. |
| `v0.4.3` / `v0.4.4` | Gathering and crafting connected materials to usable gear, alongside a new interface and combat areas. |
| `v0.4.6` | Timed location actions opened routes and rewards. Caves and mountains supplied new progression, with better tools and higher-tier materials and gear. Storage and fast travel supported the larger map. |
| `v0.5.0` and `v0.5.1` | The former added quests and linked weather, clothing and travel time to exploration, with regional markets and more encounters. The latter added fishing, Perception and new regions with enemies, materials and training. |
| `v0.5.5` | Equipment construction and scaling changed across the game. More Village content, recipes, a challenge zone and a discovery log gave players more to do with those changes. |

**Working reference.** Use `v0.4.3` / `v0.4.4` or `v0.4.6` for a typical major update and `v0.5.0` for an especially broad one. Judge scope by how much a release changes what players decide and do, how much playable content exercises those changes, and whether the outcomes feed later choices. A deep campaign arc or a system that changes play across several activities can meet the same bar. The [release check](../../README.md#versions-and-changelog) applies to the exact candidate and its included assets.

**OOPS today.** Village watch and trade is a proven loop at its [historical verification boundary](../gm/dispatch/village-loop-2026-10-27/VILLAGE_LOOP_CHECKPOINT.md#verification-vl1). It is smaller on its own than the major-update reference. The strongest scope candidate is a coherent early Klein arc that connects paid work and relationships, Seer advancement and repeatable powers, and decisions about home or supplies. The [campaign receipts](../gm/dispatch/refinement-audits-2026-10-31/LOTM_KLEIN_FULL_JOURNEY_PLAN.md) and [cross-world engine walks](../gm/dispatch/cross-world-systems-2026-11-07/CHECKPOINT.md#current-result-and-next-window) cover enough distinct systems to make that a credible comparison. Some proofs begin at continuation fixtures, span different builds, or use [private story packs](../gm/dispatch/cross-world-systems-2026-11-07/WAVE3_INPUTS.json). They establish the individual routes and leave release-scale proof open.

**Open release check.** Choose the content and assets the intended build can carry. Rebuild that candidate and walk the headline arc from the normal start to a meaningful payoff and a follow-on choice, including save and reload. A finished arc within a longer campaign can qualify, following the [existing world order](../../PROJECT.md#current-focus).
