# Where OOPS should diverge from Proto23, YAIR and Arkivia

2026-11-24 · Design review and proposals. [Project direction](../../PROJECT.md) continues to own build order.

**Use the protolikes for what one minute feels like. Use Caves of Qud for what a week can become.** OOPS should keep the satisfaction of learning by doing, a legible clock and useful numbers. Its own payoff is that the same practice changes your work, what someone trusts you to do, and what you can bring to a different world. The starting Village should sustain its own long campaign and give a traveler reasons to come home.

## First experiment

**A1 · One ordinary week in Tingen.** My pick for the first integration target when the foundation order permits. Give Klein a choice between taking a shift and spending the time on a personal lead. Let one shift produce an ordinary observation he can investigate himself or follow up through a colleague. On a later visit, show a changed offer or response from someone who knows what happened. Use the current clock, inquiry and relationship paths. Salary stays on its existing terms in this example. Compare single actions with a saved routine and check that the time spent, the lead and the later response agree. Use the existing player UI so the reason is visible where the decision is made. Finish by walking it in a rebuilt browser through the normal route, then reload and revisit.

**A2 · Prove reuse and then a crossing.** Try the same structure with a different input and consequence, such as Harry's preparation affecting what he can do in a practical lesson, or Fang Yuan's patrol report changing the next assignment. Follow with the Village–Tingen round trip from the existing plan. Link repetition to future use, and let each world's institutions, schedule, materials and powers supply the differences.

**A3 · Keep a bounded career as a later design test.** A regiment makes a good unusual case. The player can train as a recruit, finish a tour, then take another seat if that life ends. The successor can face the reputation, people and obligations the regiment actually accumulated. A same-person time loop can carry what its mechanism preserves. This tests the proposed run shapes with the same continuity work and keeps the ongoing-life style available.

Evidence scope for this review is the shared checkout on 2026-11-24, selected source files, dated browser receipts from the owning lanes, and primary references. The examples and order above are design judgments. This review did not replay the leads or check a release candidate.

### Walk one week three ways

**OD6 · One observation, three plausible choices.** Illustrative Tingen scenario, outside a required book scene. After a shift, Klein finds an ordinary discrepancy in two public delivery records. A personal inquiry can start from the same records if he comes across them elsewhere. The discrepancy is a proposed test situation with fictional contents.

| Play it this way | Cost and consequence to observe |
| --- | --- |
| Follow up yourself | Spend the available evening on the inquiry. An experienced reader can narrow it to one entry. The evidence is still incomplete until a second source confirms it. Klein keeps control over who sees it. |
| Ask a colleague | Tell a particular person and give them enough context to help. They have their own availability and can follow up later. They now have a reason to remember the request. Klein can spend the remaining time on a different pursuit. |
| Let it sit | Spend the time on ordinary work or another goal. If a supplier independently checks the same entry, they might act on it. If nobody follows up, it can remain unresolved. The quarter still has work, people and other avenues to pursue. |

Look for one consequence after a return or reload. A colleague offers a relevant introduction after being told and finding something, or a supplier changes a delivery option when their own check warrants it. Show the explanation where that new option appears. Keep deeper facts in the notebook or Reports as encountered information. If the colleague never had time or never heard the claim, there should be no response attributed to them. Use one result and one downstream decision before adding complications.

**OD7 · Reuse the shape under different rules.** Harry reads and practices for a lesson. A practical result goes to a particular teacher who can offer a next exercise. Fang Yuan submits a patrol report or acts on it privately. Clan authority and the wolf tide produce different pressures from a school timetable. The shared portion is the path from a completed act to a result to an informed decision. The subject, means, opportunities and reward can differ. Validate a second real consumer before adding a detector, event layer or stored relationship score to serve hypothetical cases.

**OD8 · Give the Village a long arc in its own right.** Work, study and personal power can eventually support a repair shop, a route or another long project that local people use. At the start you can make a living doing some of the work. Later you can choose whom to teach, which work to take, and whether to fund or delegate it. A shop is a future example, with a progression route through practicing, serving a customer and supplying or employing someone. It should have uses before travel, reasons to return after travel, and outcomes worth pursuing even if a character stays there. See the [existing vision and playable priorities](../../PROJECT.md#vision).

## Round 3 · First adaptations and feasibility

Effort here describes a single playable slice, including normal-route proof. A small change can reuse the existing action and saved state. A medium change connects existing owners and needs content or cross-system checks. A large change introduces several persistent consumers or different routes with equivalent outcomes. The donor research chat owns the wider survey. These are starting proposals, with the conjectured player actions spelled out so they can be checked.

| Idea | Adaptation and existing seam | Feasibility and player proof |
| --- | --- | --- |

OOPS already has native skill discovery, learned methods and Known uses in `recipe_connections.js`. On one early Village material, reveal or surface a method through a fitting acquisition, take the player to its real station, then make and use the result. | **Small** if the unlock and output use exist and the work is surfacing them. **Medium** if a new consumer is needed. **Other aspects to judge on that same loop.** Can a player tell the difference between an undiscovered method, a known method with missing means and a task already in progress? Do clicks, small animations and changes in the scene show progress without burying the decisive result in Reports? Can a player recover when a tool is unavailable, a shop is closed or an ingredient runs out? A repair option, another shop or changing the method can make the underlying rules more visible. For late play, give a built routine or an ordinary skill a second useful consumer before adding another tier of its XP curve.

**Practical order.** Use OD13 to prove one minute of play and exercise OD14 through its existing quote. Take OD15 or OD16 as the first integration test based on which already has a reachable result and consumer. Use OD17 after the selected action works step by step. Broader cross-world markets, successors and off-screen societies gain more from those receipts than from widening their framework first.

Evidence in this round includes a current source read and 20 passing focused tests across journey quotes, Village careers and craft workings. The scope is those tests and the cited earlier play receipts. The proposed adaptations are still design work.

## Round 4 · Why come back tomorrow?

**Next design question. What makes the player say “I know what I want to do tomorrow” after an ordinary evening?** OD13–OD17 can make an action understandable and satisfying. The next test is whether several actions make a life the player wants to continue. Use the [master plan's existing fresh-start and three-evening targets](../MASTER_PLAYABILITY_PLAN_2026-10-25.md#horizon) as the frame. The implementation chat is still working on the five adaptations. The ideas here propose a later playtest.

| Idea | In one Tingen week | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OD18 · Offer different good evenings | Work the desk and pursue its lead. Spend the evening on private study to prepare a technique. Or visit someone and follow up on a promise. | Each route should pay in a different useful way, such as means, knowledge or help. Give one pair roughly comparable returns. If the same schedule wins no matter what the player wants, look at actual opportunity costs and what each result enables. |
| OD19 · Let one pursuit support another | Study gives Klein a better way to examine evidence. A trusted colleague can then offer access to something he could also pursue through a different means. | The player can explain what the earlier choice changed and find its next use. The paths can overlap without making each evening a prerequisite for the next. |
| OD20 · Let a missed opportunity change the route | The office closes before Klein gets there. Some work can wait. A time-sensitive lead might be taken up by another person, leaving an alternate route or a discoverable consequence. | Try choosing something else deliberately. See whether tomorrow offers a plausible next move and whether the player understands what they gave up. Same-hour appointments and scarce stock can matter without turning the week into a checklist. |
| OD21 · Let mastery change your role | A practiced watch worker can mentor a recruit. A capable investigator could later delegate a check or choose which inquiry is worth attention. | Compare what a novice and a competent character decide during the same activity. The experienced character should have a different decision or useful freedom to redirect their time. |
| OD22 · Make the next step discoverable in play | At the end of the evening, show a completed result and a nearby relevant opportunity, and keep optional context in the notebook or Reports. | Give a player the screen with a goal but no walkthrough. Can they find an action now, name something worth doing tomorrow, and resume the idea after reload? |

**First playtest after the absorption wave.** Take one ordinary start and play three genuinely different evenings. Pick one result to carry into the following day, replay with an alternate choice, and return after a reload. Record what made the player want another session, what sent them hunting through menus, and whether one route crowded out the others. The most revealing counterfactual is to skip the obvious job on purpose and see whether a good evening still exists. The Village provides a practical comparison using Watchcraft. Tingen is the proposed book-world test once its foundation order allows the tie-in.

For later consideration, try a season in a place people use differently because of the player's project. A workshop the player supplied, a route they helped keep open, or a colleague they trained can all leave a reason to return. One persistent relationship or place with that quality will tell us where a wider calendar, economy or succession system would actually add play.
