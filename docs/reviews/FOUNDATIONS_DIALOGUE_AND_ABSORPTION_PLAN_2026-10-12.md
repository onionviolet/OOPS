## One loop across three scales

Original synthetic proposal: a person requests help with an owned tool.

Micro: establish contact/recognition, discuss symptoms, inspect nondestructively,
quote a supported repair, use actual means, repair or refuse. One explicit
suppressor/counter fixture can change operation eligibility.

Meso: the recipient independently answers, retains custody/rights, uses the
result in one supported task, and remembers the outcome. A later appointment
reads a durable obligation rather than a prunable text history.

Macro: one represented institution receives a report and changes one funded
order or access decision. Broader economic change waits for actual consumers;
a synthetic institutional recipient is enough to prove this join.

CW01/02/10/12/13/14/25/27/28/29/36 are relevant. XWC1/XWC5 provide comparison
cases; XWC3 adds recognition; XWC6 adds surviving custody/records. XWC4 remains a
dedicated temporal dependency, not something generic timers already implement.

Record the causal cut: repair does not send a message, force agreement, restore
a missed meeting or inform every institution. Record what changes, what remains
unchanged and what is unmodeled. Strong supported means can solve multiple links
without an invented tax. Manufacture success and recipient compatibility differ.
Interruption before acceptance differs from interruption after costs/work commit.

### One durable goal, bounded work units

Use the existing audit/implementation owner and ledgers as the chain's memory.
Keep the full selected foundation horizon visible, including deferred details,
while closing small verified units. A successor inherits unfinished authorized
work, not an invitation to redefine the goal or invent more mechanics. "All
foundations" needs a frozen initial manifest and explicit later additions; the
U/K families are coverage lenses rather than a promise to simulate everything.

| Chain segment | Outcome | Gate before the next segment |
|---|---|---|
| R: research qualification | Reconcile existing source findings, versions, contradictions and actual current engine seams for one selected behavior | Mechanism/consumer/owner known; material unknown resolved or represented as a clearly limited original fixture policy |
| D: executable design join | One action sequence, actual state owners, local policy inputs, failure/counterexample and compatibility | Execution packet names exact adapters/reservation and observable outcomes, not just feature labels |
| I: implementation | Reuse current host/scheduler/save; create the actual consumers and development interaction | Changes compile/build as required and focused checks pass; unplayed behavior stays unverified |
| V: connected proof | Rebuilt fresh-origin browser action and persistent consequence, with affected save/reload/import/failure cases | Evidence matches current code/content and declared scope; human fiction/pacing acceptance stays separate |
| H: review and continuation | Review result, close proved leaves, retain remaining gaps and select next connected unit | Receiver can resume from live checkpoint and exclusive write transfer; no rerun of unchanged completed work |

These are work stages, not five mandatory chats. Prefer one implementer retaining
implementation and verification. Fresh research or GM successors are useful at
meaningful delivery/context boundaries; routine substeps need no new conversation.
Disjoint source research can advance alongside implementation of already qualified
work. Reconciliation at the integration seam remains the coordinator's job.

### Handoff content

Use the task's existing CHECKPOINT as a short index, not a copied transcript:

1. Human-authorized goal, selected unit, exclusions and still-pending decisions.
2. Checkout, actual dirty inputs/content versions, exact file writers and current
   worker/process identities; pending operations and uncertain mutation outcomes.
3. Completed artifacts and evidence, source coverage, failing/unrun gates and
   acceptance scope. Include only relevant fingerprints, never private saves.
4. Remaining ranked gaps with disposition/owner and the precise next command or
   investigation. Link detailed reports and source locators instead of repeating them.
5. Predecessor/successor IDs, effective ownership transfer and readback state.

Before dispatch, check whether a successor already exists. Confirm creation and
initial progress through the app's bounded wait. After confirmed dispatch the
predecessor relinquishes that writer area. The receiver checks live instructions,
inputs and pending processes, then resumes the recorded next action. A stale
checkpoint never overrides a current owner or dirty edit. An unclear transfer
needs reconciliation, not two simultaneous writers.

### Keep research finite and implementation honest

Research each unresolved mechanism only to the point where its selected consumer
can be implemented and tested. Reuse source findings already qualified; resolve
conflicting versions rather than re-auditing everything. Each finding ends with
reuse/adapt/trial/defer/skip plus an owner and next gate. A source gap can defer
one mechanism without blocking independent supported work.

Prototype progress is measured by usable actions and retained consequences.
Documentation counts, installed tools, declared capabilities and passing pure
tests are not the implementation goal. Final integration must test the actual
combined relevant journey; individually passing candidates do not prove their
joined state/clock/knowledge boundaries. Repeat checks only when affected inputs
or a concrete failure justify it.

Stop creating successors when the selected technical manifest and combined
acceptance gates are satisfied, or no remaining authorized unit can progress.
Do not restart unchanged external blockers in fresh chats. Remaining human-only
fiction/pacing review stays visible without keeping autonomous chains running.
Existing no-commit/push/install/publication limits travel with every handoff.
The active workflow audit remains audit-only; implementing and auto-dispatching
this broader foundation chain require their own selected execution scope.
