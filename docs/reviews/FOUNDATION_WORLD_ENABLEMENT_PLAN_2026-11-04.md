## Complexity: what reuse does and does not pay for

Plan each selected world/slice using separate dimensions. No numeric difficulty is assigned from title, chapter count, module count or genre.

| ID | Cost dimension | What the task record should expose |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Source and law | Exact version/era, qualified mechanism, exceptions, numerical anchors and unresolved evidence. Full known trajectory informs early contracts, with unread gaps explicit. |
| C2 | Distinct operations | New behavior versus new rows. Separate detection, interpretation, permission, execution, payment and consequence when they are different operations. |
| C3 | Composition | Interacting clocks, effects, support, stock, observers and institutions. Name one meaningful awkward boundary, not just isolated happy paths. |
| C4 | Content and ordinary life | Locations, cast, lessons, jobs, duties, services and their receiving uses. Shared school machinery does not author a curriculum or a livable economy. |
| C5 | Upper-range scale | Full-trajectory capabilities, concurrent actors/support, large quantities, entity counts and simulation cost. Early chapter progress cannot certify this headroom. |
| C6 | Lifecycle and compatibility | Failure, interruption, expiry, support withdrawal, body change, death, reset, travel, reload, content revision and missing packs as relevant. |
| C7 | Presentation and proof | World-specific vocabulary, explanations, discovery-safe readouts, desktop UI, useful continuation and delivery state on an identified bundle. |

The work ledger can use **reuse/adaptation + new mechanisms + local content + interaction repair + delivery proof + named uncertainty** as qualitative cost buckets. This is not a numerical estimator. Reuse can reduce implementation work while adding integration and regression work. A title with fewer powers can still have costly institutions, politics, survival or branching lives.
