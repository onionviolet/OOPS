## Original continuation proposals

| ID | Idea | Concrete distinction and possible play |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | Infinite debt | Infinite principal, endlessly accruing finite debt, and a recursive obligation where repayment creates another claim are different powers. Collection could draw energy, restrict one action or reclaim a borrowed effect according to the actual technique. Debt may be transferable, inherited, disputed, forgiven or uncollectible where local means support it. An infinite balance alone does not prove infinite collection speed, reach or authority; a power can explicitly have those too. |
| O2 | Adjectives as operative properties | Compare transferable debt, inherited debt, forgotten debt and retroactive debt. Each selected word needs a concrete result and applicable local rule. In a literal word-magic world, changing wording may itself cause the result; elsewhere the word describes what a method actually does. |
| O3 | Materials, form and recognition | Polyjuice-like temporary impersonation connects prepared stock, an individualized dose, actual changed body functions, clothing/tools and separate observer beliefs. Original variants might be delayed, selective, contagious or persistent, but those words do not establish HP canon. Noita-like material powers connect amount, constituent identity, coating/swallowing/impact and body/world effects. |
| O4 | Composition across these families | An original impact-triggered debt mark, a coating that carries a curse, or a disguise that becomes stronger when a witness believes it connects delivery to a real effect. Separate carrier, trigger, effect and resulting history. No generic cross-world interaction or canon mechanic is implied. |
| O5 | Infinite depth retained separately | A pocket, shadow or wound can contain endless layers. Depth, access, traversal and contact remain separate properties unless its power explicitly joins them. This is an original possibility, not the corrected meaning of the user's infinite-debt request. |

One possible connected demonstration, still unselected: obtain a finite liquid,
inspect it, apply it through two different routes, see distinct actual results,
then modify one supported property and repeat. A body/disguise demonstration
would additionally need restoration and observer-specific recognition. An
infinite-debt demonstration would need an actual claim, its collection rule and
one meaningful player response. These are alternative proofs, not a new backlog
of authorized implementation tasks.
