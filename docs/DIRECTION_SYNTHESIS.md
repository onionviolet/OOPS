## Recommendation

Build a character-centered idle RPG where story discoveries open capabilities, preparation determines available solutions, and selected chapters can later be revisited with retained knowledge. Ordinary adventure is the common foundation. Martial mastery, learned magic, cultivation, artifacts and institutional support become different ways to interact with that adventure.

The central promise: **learn how the world works, develop a way to act on that knowledge, and sometimes return to change an earlier outcome.** Numbers measure growing strength; mechanics determine what that strength can accomplish; story records what the player chose to do with it.

This is a recommended synthesis, not a vote to implement every researched system. Keep the current one-realm, one-prestige-layer milestone. Jade Hollow is now an archived reference prototype; cultivation placement is still provisional.

## Story structure: a spine with branches, returns and changed outcomes

### World campaigns and book settings

Follow-up, 2026-10-01: the larger structure is a persistent character moving through interconnected world campaigns. Each world can contain several chapters, institutions, power paths and local outcomes. Later discoveries elsewhere can open unresolved routes on return. Worlds may be peers or higher tiers. Shared state carries character history, knowledge and explicitly transferable mastery/possessions; local state owns events, relationships, resources and permissions. Compatibility determines which powers work, need an adapter or remain unavailable.

Worlds may be themed original, mechanically book-inspired original, or direct book settings when explicitly selected. A direct setting needs an entry era/location, player role, verified relevant rules and a canon policy: compatible side story, divergent history or bounded reconstruction. Prefer proposing a role alongside the canonical plot so choices need not reenact a protagonist. Reading-list presence does not select a setting. Temporal rules can differ by world; universal rewind is not required. These are proposals, not accepted book selections.

### Journey flow, portable powers and interrupted visits, saved 2026-10-02

Weibao explicitly requested saving these ideas: make the expansion flow from base YAIRPG, use powers across worlds with meaningful balance, draw inspiration from multi-world books, and consider interrupted journeys. This section owns the creative proposal. PROJECT.md records the requested scope; ENGINE_EXPANSION.md owns state/transaction requirements. Names, opening events, exact rules, timing and source roster remain reversible proposals.

Proposed base-first flow: an ordinary Village problem leads to a discovery; native study, training, supplies or crafting lets the player investigate; the discovery opens a first outside chapter; local play grants useful knowledge, a method or a possession; return creates another solution or consequence in the base adventure. An incomplete visit can instead open recovery, investigation or an alternative destination. The current crossing, guard practice, native manuals, forest materials and old structures across the river are possible connections, not a newly selected plot. The traveler should have a clear local reason to care before the larger cosmology is explained. Reveal the cost, expected duration, relevant power rules and return options before departure.

Introduce the selected new rule through a useful base-world interaction before requiring a long outside chapter. The current Rat-step enemy-learning loop demonstrates this kind of integration, though it is original local content and does not establish travel. Keep the native opening available; later authored starts may begin elsewhere. Completing every base quest or profession should not become a universal prerequisite for travel. Each new world should supply one distinct decision, a useful return interaction and room for further local depth. World entry, ascension and prestige remain different transitions.

Treat power portability as a declared property of the particular power, its source and the destination, rather than one blanket rule. A bloodline can travel with a retained body; learned procedures can outlast the faculty that taught them; a device can carry fuel; a patron's permission can have geographic scope. Capacity and conversion limits can grow through progression, and an exceptional origin may intentionally bypass an ordinary restriction.

| Proposed power category | Portability question | Meaningful preparation or growth |
|---|---|---|
| Learned method or factual knowledge | Does the traveler retain the procedure, language and proficiency, and possess a way to execute it locally? | Practice with a local tool, translate instruction, research an equivalent input, or commission assistance |
| Bodily/bloodline faculty | Is the same body retained, is the faculty awakened/active, and what provides its fuel? | Develop the source itself, improve control, store fuel or acquire an authored adaptation; a new body has its own retention policy |
| Ambient or world-law power | Does the destination supply the environmental condition on which it operates? | Create a bounded compatible field, use stored energy or discover a different application; imported power can remain fully effective when its conditions hold |
| Equipment, carrier or companion | Does the object/actor travel, with its identity, charge, binding, needs and controller intact? | Repair/refuel locally or develop a compatible supply chain; movement preserves ownership and unique claims |
| External blessing, office or contract | Does the external source recognize the traveler here, and which rights or obligations follow? | Negotiate scope, carry recognized evidence or establish a new connection; personal reputation and institutional authority stay distinct |

The UI should explain why a method is available, what it needs and which discovery could change that. A power learned in another world can deliberately remain overwhelming in an earlier region. Avoid automatically matching every old enemy to the traveler or inventing a new immunity for every successful build. Later interest can shift to discovery, precision, protecting another person, simultaneous objectives, local authority, infrastructure or the consequence of changing history. A powerful solution can remove an old bottleneck and create room for a new ambition.

Balance across worlds uses authored effects and encounter strength bands, not a universal mapping between fictional ranks. Measure preparation time, time to a useful action, resource use, reliability, available solutions and required manual repetition as well as combat strength. A copied movement pattern, flight and remote manipulation need different targets/limits; a single damage multiplier cannot express all three. Explicit trigger ordering and stacking prevent accidental recursive processing without forbidding intentionally strong combinations. New shortcuts can be valid rewards. Copying, resource multiplication, time powers and trans-world trade need declared renewal/ownership rules so their intended economic impact survives without accidental duplicate rewards. Permanent failure, lifespan, unattended loss and exhaustive upkeep remain open design choices.

#### When the journey is cut short

Track which scope ended: an activity, expedition, world visit, life or timeline. The native `src/main.js/kill_player` currently treats combat defeat as unconsciousness, relocation and a recovery effect; it does not end the character's life. The future travel engine must preserve that ordinary meaning unless selected content explicitly changes it.

| Proposed interruption | Immediate outcome | Continuing history |
|---|---|---|
| Retreat or missed objective | Return or abandon the current undertaking with its declared costs and carried possessions | Keep actually acquired knowledge and completed discoveries; the unresolved problem or missed opening can change |
| Defeat, capture or exile | Enter an authored recovery/escape/access problem; the world visit may continue or be suspended | Preserve the cause, selected actor conditions and local response rather than repeatedly granting first-entry rewards |
| Forced extraction, closed route or world disaster | Declare the departure destination, left-behind people/items and route status | Local history persists; reopening, another entrance or permanent separation is an authored outcome |
| Bodily death or true death | Resolve the declared continuation or run-end plan | [Defeat and run policy](#defeat-true-death-and-run-progression-clarified-2026-10-02) separates loss of a body from the end of the run; retention is explicit |
| Retirement or chosen journey ending | Close selected obligations and establish the character's ending/legacy | Ending can preserve a completed history; optional prestige uses the one declared layer, with previewed losses/retention |

Partial journeys should matter through real acquired progress and saved consequences. Award only declared completed milestones; unresolved goals, unique claims and rewards cannot pay again on reload or repeated extraction. Someone saved before departure can remember it; an abandoned ally or institution can react according to what it knows. Foreknowledge, a new entrance, better preparation or an origin's explicit recovery power can later reopen a route. Rebirth does not silently rewind the old world's inhabitants; historical reversal requires its own timeline policy. A departure can be a narrative ending without becoming a prestige reset.

### Scale, starting worlds and technological/magical development, saved 2026-10-02

The user requested further research on Nemesis-style progression, unequal world power, a small soldier-like opening that reveals a larger worldview, tech/magic settings, alternative starting worlds and engine readiness. The proposal here owns creative progression and scaling; [recurring-rival refinement](RIVALS_IDENTITIES_AND_FATE.md#nemesis-style-progression-refinement-2026-10-02) owns personal arcs, and [source readiness](ENGINE_EXPANSION.md#engine-readiness-audit-2026-10-02) owns implementation status. These directions do not select a military campaign, a particular book or a universal power ladder.

Start locally and expand what the character knows through events with practical stakes. Proposed base-first path: Village work/training and an optional recruit or artisan role; a small assignment and recurring peer; a regional problem exposing an institution or unfamiliar power; a first world lead; then a bounded outside chapter and useful return. Native guard practice, supplies, books, crafting and the crossing can support the opening. Ordinary obligations give people and locations significance before cosmology becomes important. A small opening need not mean a weak origin: a latent or already exceptional faculty can have useful early applications without revealing every world immediately. Expose a real choice before a long training wait; reciting cosmology or completing all professions is not a travel gate.

Use a world danger profile with overlapping ranges and typed capabilities. Ordinary inhabitants, trained specialists, rare apex actors and deployed institutional assets can have very different strength. Personal power and technological development are independent axes: gene warriors, cybernetic or synthetic bodies, engineered organisms and other local systems can make technological individuals peers of or stronger than magical practitioners. Either kind of world may also contain ordinary civilians and exceptional institutional assets. A stronger traveler can legitimately overpower a lower-band threat. Preserve that achievement, while an unfamiliar rule or differently scoped objective can still matter. A world label should not grant every inhabitant the same strength multiplier. The earlier narrower illustration is preserved in PROTOTYPE_HISTORY.md.

| Proposed scaling axis | What to compare | Example decision it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Personal force and resilience | Authored damage/control, survivability, recovery and compatible energy/body | Whether a direct confrontation is practical and whether an old threat has become trivial |
| Range, mobility and information | Reach, travel, concealment/detection, reaction opportunities and what the actor knows | A close-range specialist, distant weapon or concealed caster can require a different approach |
| Local rules and interaction | Environment, fuel, effect type, wards/counters and portability | A self-fueled power travels differently from an ambient-dependent one; a researched adapter can enable another use |
| Infrastructure and collective power | Laboratories, supply chains, communications, prepared sites and organized support | A technologically capable region can offer valuable production or access even when individual inhabitants are weaker |
| Objective and authority | What must be protected/learned/created, credentials, evidence and local relationships | Combat dominance can coexist with a research bottleneck, a rescue objective or a contested institutional claim |

Native numeric effects and declared interaction rules are the implementation measure; fictional ranks supply source context and local progression, not automatic cross-book equivalence. A first calibration should compare two routes through the same authored threat, then the same power in two contexts. Show known danger/requirements before travel; appraisal, scouting and remembered evidence can improve estimates. Later bands can increase scope as well as numbers, such as individual action, area control or support of an institution. Keep math and trigger processing bounded; representing grander fiction does not require simulating every distant actor or multiplying stats indefinitely.

Tech and magic are world-local systems that may coexist. A tech route can develop biological augmentation, implants, synthetic bodies, tools, materials, sensors, medicine, power supply and automation through research/production. A magic route can develop control, affinities, casting, wards and resource conversion through its declared local methods. Crossovers can create prepared devices, improved manufacture or another use of a native material when their conditions are authored. Technology does not automatically lose because a world is magical, and magic does not automatically bypass every machine. Body-derived and equipment-derived capabilities have separate sources and retention rules. Avoid requiring every world to repeat one profession tree with different names.

Keep starting world and origin separate. Base-first is the recommended first delivered journey because it exercises the retained game and gives a familiar return destination. Later, a validated starting profile can begin as a local recruit, an amnesiac, an artisan or a traveler in another world. It must initialize the right body, knowledge/unknown history, skills, possessions, local permissions, timeline and viable leads without replaying inappropriate Village unlocks. Authored and random starts can be separate modes. Support selected coherent combinations before expanding the full origin-by-world matrix; existing saves retain their actual starts and do not gain new retroactive rewards.

New grounded-opening references, checked 2026-10-02: [Second Life as a Soldier](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/126560/second-life-as-a-soldier), Soldier_of_Avalon, has an author synopsis about an ordinary entrant joining the army, learning under limited resources, and a stated later kingdom-building direction. Its proposed lesson is expanding responsibilities and relationships before expanding campaign scope; the advertised non-OP tone is not a mandatory rule for our origins. [A Soldier's Life](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/58243/a-soldiers-life), Alwaysrollsaone, is another candidate: its author-hosted page labels the synopsis reader-written and describes entry into a Legion and developing abilities. That provides a bounded discovery lead, not a verified later plot or proof that it is the title Weibao meant. No full-book audit or reading/purchase commitment is claimed.

Proposed delivery remains small but extensible: a playable combat technique, a remembered local peer/rival, one meaningful timed research/work outcome, then the second-world return/interruption proof and an alternative start using established bindings. A rival should be able to settle, become an ally, retire or be surpassed; their rank and growth follow their own cause/resources/time. This sequencing lets the player gradually discover the larger worldview while implementation adds one verified interaction at a time. Keep the one-realm/one-prestige first milestone; broader worlds and strong combinations remain the long-term direction.

### Technological power and world access, clarified 2026-10-02

Weibao clarified that people in technological worlds can themselves be powerful, including gene warriors, and asked for access routes beyond reincarnation. This records the direction and original proposals; no particular source setting, upgrade tree or access mechanism is selected. Technology, magic, personal strength and societal capability are separate axes, with authored interactions rather than a default technology-versus-magic hierarchy.

Gene-warrior development can concern actual body functions, adaptation, recovery, senses, movement or energy handling. Cybernetics can add installed modules and interfaces; a synthetic body can change the actor's physical operating rules; piloted or worn equipment can supply another layer. These are different progression routes even when two yield similar strength. Acquisition, integration, control, maintenance/recovery and advancement should create useful actions; do not reduce every improvement to an interchangeable stat percentage or assume all technology is disposable gear. A biological or installed faculty travels with its declared body; a remembered blueprint may survive loss of that body and enable reconstruction where materials/tools permit. Hybrid development can combine compatible scientific and magical methods through authored recipes/effects.

| Proposed access route | What changes or travels | Distinct gameplay/retention question |
|---|---|---|
| Physical passage: portal, rift, artifact or dimensional vehicle | The same actor/body and declared cargo cross a route | How is the route discovered, opened, maintained and revisited; what happens to time and access behind the traveler? |
| Projection: dream, avatar or remote embodiment | Control/experience reaches another context while the original body may remain | Which knowledge or rewards return, who owns the visited body, and can the original body or connection be affected? A simulation and an actual visited world need different claims |
| Reincarnation or transmigration | The traveler receives or inhabits a new body with a declared origin/history | Which memories, mastery and soul-bound sources continue; which organs, bloodlines, implants and possessions belong to the new body? It need not require a prior true-death outcome |
| Summoning or a contract-mediated invitation | Another actor/institution provides access under declared terms | Is the passage physical or projected, which permissions/obligations follow, and what ends or renews the connection? |
| Ascension or a higher-plane transition | An earned change in capability or access opens another layer | Does the current body transform or cross intact, which local rules change, and what permits return? This is separate from an optional prestige reset |

Routes can be discovered, researched, built, inherited, negotiated or granted by an exceptional power. A tech route can construct access, a ritual can establish it, and a finding can bypass ordinary requirements. Do not require every traveler to die or climb one universal realm ladder. Entry, return, duration, cargo/body policy, clocks and failure outcomes belong to the selected route. Several entrances can reach the same persistent world, with different costs and relationships; re-entry must not regenerate its unique rewards or erase its history.

Progression can deepen the current life, broaden knowledge and methods, change the body/equipment, build local access/relationships, and eventually change available worlds. Skill mastery can improve an old technique or reveal a new application. Research produces knowledge; production creates owned objects; bodily development changes execution; an institutional achievement opens opportunities. These do not all need separate always-visible XP bars. Show the next useful milestone for the selected path, and let other worlds supply a new application or compatible input. A strong origin can remove an early restriction; new decisions can move toward discovery, synthesis or commitments.

### Defeat, true death and run progression, clarified 2026-10-02

The user asked about death meaning fainting versus true death ending a run. Proposed vocabulary below distinguishes the outcome, continuation and retention. Exact lethal difficulty, revival powers, end conditions and new-run inheritance remain unselected. Native `kill_player` currently logs unconsciousness, moves the character to recovery and adds Recovering; its historical `total_deaths` increment is not evidence of bodily or true death. Future UI/counters should distinguish defeats, supported lethal outcomes and run endings while preserving existing save fields.

Requested default, 2026-10-02: ordinary player defeats usually mean fainting/recovery, while specific zones, enemies, weapons, effects or authored events can permit a run-ending outcome. A chance-based lethal result is a proposed option within an explicitly eligible threat, not a hidden universal roll after every ordinary defeat. Exact sources, odds and loss policies remain unselected. Expose the relevant danger category, available protections and a meaningful preparation/avoidance route; information may be uncertain where the character lacks knowledge. Persist any selected lethal result and aftermath once, with no reload reroll. Survival mechanisms resolve their actual eligibility before a true-death run ending. The player's default recovery rule does not automatically make every monster kill or named NPC outcome nonlethal. This is requested design scope, not implemented lethal gameplay.

| Proposed outcome | Active run | Declared continuation |
|---|---|---|
| Downed, fainted or ordinary defeat | Continues | Recover, retreat or enter an authored capture/injury problem; no automatic body replacement or prestige reward |
| Bodily death with a valid survival mechanism | Can continue | A committed revival, regeneration, backup body, surviving soul or other selected power follows its real conditions, resource/claim policy and aftermath |
| True death with no valid continuation | Ends | Close this run once and preserve its chronicle/declared world aftermath; a later new run has its own initialization and optional meta-retention policy |
| Voluntary reincarnation, body transfer or chosen ending | Depends on the selected route/mode | A new life can continue the same traveler/run through a supported source, or begin a separately declared run; optional prestige remains its one distinct layer |

An eligible survival power can prevent the final run-ending outcome; a faint does not trigger an on-death theft, revive charge or new-body reward. A real bodily death can be consequential even if a supported source preserves the traveler. Record actor, body, world/timeline, cause, outcome and the chosen continuation before any result is published. Avoid automatically treating every defeat as a lethal harvest or every new body as a free full restore. An intentionally renewable survival power can exist through an authored renewal policy rather than an accidental double payout.

For a continuing run, list retained knowledge/proficiency, current-body sources, equipped/cached possessions, claims, obligations and local relationships separately. A copied procedure can remain known while a replacement body lacks the eye, implant or fuel needed to use it; another origin may deliberately retain or reconstruct those faculties. Reincarnation does not automatically rewind NPC lives or grant the new body the old person's social recognition. Unfinished projects can pause, be left behind or pass to another actor under a declared ownership policy. If true death ends the run, show its final record and the next allowed continuation/new-run action; reloading cannot reopen the same unique rewards or silently resume an ended actor.

Persistent discoveries or inherited legacies may make a later run different if the selected mode permits them. They need not provide another global multiplier, and true death is not automatically the voluntary prestige operation. Keep the first milestone to one realm and one prestige layer. State the category of danger and available protections before a supported lethal commitment; world clocks, spontaneous events and unattended loss still need the policies in the journey proposal. None of these outcome/route records currently exists as an implemented run-ending system.

### Vague awakening opening, proposed 2026-10-02

Weibao proposed keeping the opening vague, such as "you mysteriously wake up...". This is an available presentation direction, not a selected backstory or implemented opening change. Start with what the character can observe: an unfamiliar place, their condition, nearby people and an immediate need. Total amnesia is optional; useful instincts or remembered skills can coexist with uncertainty about arrival and identity.

Original sample: "You wake beneath an unfamiliar roof. Someone has bandaged your wounds. You remember how to hold a blade, but not how you came here. Outside, the village is already stirring." This offers a connection to the base adventure without promising an anchor, patron, simulation, prophecy or multiverse explanation. A recurring symbol, an inconsistent account or recognition of something unfamiliar can provide later clues. NPC interpretations may differ; established facts and gameplay consequences should remain coherent.

The cause may be revealed gradually, differently by origin, or remain unresolved. Do not promise a mandatory final cosmology reveal. The anchor/refuge below remains one optional explanation and route, not the default truth implied by the opening. Survival and travel can initially be described through observable events while their source stays mysterious. Operational defeat, continuation, retained progress and true-death rules still need clear outcomes when those mechanics are introduced. Mystery concerns explanation, not arbitrary changes to what a committed action does.

### Survival lore candidate, proposed 2026-10-02

Weibao asked what lore could explain survival across the proposed journeys. One optional original candidate is that the traveler's continuity has an anchor outside any one body/world. This is a proposal, not accepted cosmology, an adaptation of a named book, or implemented survival gameplay. The [vague awakening opening](#vague-awakening-opening-proposed-2026-10-02) can leave the actual cause open. The [defeat/run policy](#defeat-true-death-and-run-progression-clarified-2026-10-02) owns outcome terminology; this section owns the possible fictional explanation.

The person physically lives in the current world, while an external anchor preserves their continuing identity and declared remembered knowledge. The anchor may have formed through a world-crossing accident, an encountered relic, an experiment or a pact; it need not make the character a predestined chosen one. An amnesiac start can mean the connection or its memory is damaged. A local soldier/artisan can discover it through ordinary adventure. Lineage, profession and later power acquisitions remain separate from the survival premise.

Ordinary base-game defeat still means unconsciousness/recovery. A genuinely lethal event can instead leave the body dead while a supported anchor retrieves the surviving identity and starts its declared recovery or embodiment route. Reconstruction, reincarnation, projection and return through a passage are different mechanisms; the anchor need not grant all of them at the beginning. Preserve actual learned experience according to its policy, while organs, bloodlines, implants, equipment and local possessions follow body/ownership rules. An advanced source may deliberately preserve or rebuild more. No world history is automatically rewound and other people still experience the aftermath.

Survival progression can improve anchor stability, information retained, reach, access to a refuge, compatible replacement bodies or another independently owned protection. Technological research may establish a consciousness archive or engineered body, while magical development may strengthen the soul or a sanctuary binding. These are candidate applications of the original crossover premise, not claims about every reference world's metaphysics. Their actual acquisition and limits follow selected content. Lifespan, aging, environmental survival and immunity remain separate body/power rules; an anchor alone does not supply a suitable atmosphere or physical protection.

True death closes the run when the current embodiment is lost and no valid continuity/recovery source remains. A deliberately established threat might damage the anchor; this should be a meaningful known category of danger, not a new universal enemy counter whenever the player gains power. Avoid repeatedly destroying or draining the premise solely to maintain ordinary grind. Recovery can cost time, a prepared body or resources where the authored route calls for it, and can be very reliable for a deliberately exceptional origin. Fainting consumes no revival entitlement and grants no on-death acquisition.

Alternative explanations remain open: a patron retrieves the traveler under a contract, a discovered relic binds continuity, a technological archive transfers a mind, or a mortal start survives ordinary defeats and earns exceptional survival later. An anchor is one possible common connection between these mechanisms, not a compulsory origin restriction for every future mode. Distinguish technology-assisted transfer, surviving original consciousness and a newly instantiated copy through the selected identity policy when that distinction affects play.

Original opening hook: the character recognizes a symbol, phrase or location from a dream; investigating an ordinary Village lead reveals a partial anchor/refuge; a later encounter demonstrates one concrete survival or passage rule. Who created the connection, why it is damaged and which other travelers share it can be gradual mysteries. A powerful origin can reveal or bypass parts of this process. First playable lore proof would explain existing recovery and add only one supported discovery/continuation mechanic before expanding the cosmology. Save the proposed rules before relying on them for true death or cross-world rewards.

### Refinement-first starting proposal

Current steering retires Jade Hollow access during this pass while preserving its save data dormant. The existing prototype demonstrates preparation routes but does not establish campaign entry, branching narrative or mechanical enemy trials. If entry later becomes earned, migrate valid existing progress explicitly. Improve explanation of current rules before introducing further currencies or skills.

For each future world, write a compact brief: source/adaptation policy, defining conflict, two operating constraints, alternative solutions, temporal/retention rules and one cross-world interaction. Detail its first playable chapter; use the result to guide later depth. The initial chapter establishes shared machinery, not a limit on the intended world campaigns. No new gameplay is implemented by this follow-up.

Use a chapter graph with a small common spine. Branches change preparation, allies, discoveries and later solutions, then meet at shared milestones that retain the consequences. Rejoining the same chapter need not erase the choice: one player arrives with a surviving mentor, another with a restored manual and a different faction relationship.

Forward movement opens the next chapter or power opportunity. Sideways movement explores a faction, region or alternative method at comparable strength. Returning to an older location preserves ordinary progress. Rewinding a chapter restores a declared earlier scenario state. A setback changes the current problem or recovery route rather than mechanically forcing a full restart.

Do not model all of this as a single story level. Track current chapter, active branch, known leads and world consequences separately. The journal can show the current objective, available leads, resolved outcomes and replay anchors without exposing undiscovered plot.

Begin with one authored regional conflict and a small cast. Example premise: a failed watchpost expedition left a damaged record and several conflicting accounts. The player can retrieve the record, rescue a witness or secure supplies. The later discovery of a memory anchor makes an earlier failure revisitable. The cause, factions and anchor are original story candidates, not fixed lore.

Choices should change at least one observable thing: an available solution, relationship, resource cost, retained discovery or later event. A choice that changes only a line of flavor should not be presented as a major branch. Keep long-range plot surprises while showing immediate mechanical costs and the category of risk.

## Time travel: start with one bounded chapter anchor

Recommended later prototype: replay one self-contained expedition from an explicit anchor. This tests your idea without requiring the whole economy, calendar, inventory and every NPC to be rewound. In fiction it may be a real local reversal, an echo or a reconstruction; decide the explanation before writing its consequences. A simulation cannot honestly promise that it changes the real past.

| State | Proposed replay rule |
|---|---|
| Journal and observed clues | Remembered across attempts; a clue must actually have been observed |
| Learned technique familiarity | Retained; repeated identical attempts do not generate uncapped mastery or permanent stats |
| Scenario events, temporary kit and local trust | Reset to the anchor; knowing someone is not the same as having earned their trust in this timeline |
| Ordinary external character, inventory and reputation | Stay outside the first replay's reset domain; enter with a bounded scenario kit derived from declared capabilities |
| Outcome rewards | Preview one selected outcome to commit when leaving; replacing an outcome removes its scenario-owned reward entitlement rather than stacking mutually exclusive payouts |

For the first test, keep committed rewards as a route/knowledge entitlement until replacement accounting is safe. Do not award spendable money or movable items for every replay. Temporary items cannot escape the scenario. Compare attempts in the journal, but do not accumulate several incompatible NPC outcomes in the active world.

Initially allow outcome replacement only before advancing into dependent chapters; show that boundary before the player leaves. Replaying a chapter after later events depend on it needs either a separate timeline slot or explicit downstream invalidation. Neither is solved by flipping an old quest flag. A comparison-only reconstruction can remain available later without claiming to rewrite the active history.

Rewinding should expose a new decision: use a learned patrol schedule, warn a witness, bring the correct tool or take a newly understood route. Skip familiar exposition and routine solved steps. Limit one attempt by simulated action opportunities, such as enough time to secure two of three objectives; never make the player log in at a real-world hour. Avoid a scarce rewind currency in the first prototype, since it adds grind before the replay is proven fun.

Keep replay distinct from optional prestige. The earlier cultivation rebirth is archived; the current first milestone permits one prestige layer with its own explicit policy. Campaign-wide regression, imported future gear and a fully rewritten historical world remain separate, larger designs.

Alternative if replay proves tedious: let accumulated knowledge unlock an earlier chapter's unresolved side route without resetting events. This supports moving around the story graph with far less state complexity, though it does not let the player undo a past outcome.

## Progression and power paths

Shared foundations: ordinary skills, recipes, equipment, discoveries and character relationships. A technique's visible lifecycle is found, understood, practiced and applied; not every stage needs its own resource bar. A credential or title should open a service, instruction or action tied to what earned it.

Power paths specialize applications. Martial methods emphasize stamina, interruption resistance and stances; learned magic emphasizes procedure, fuel and counters; craft/artifacts borrow capabilities at a supply or slot cost; cultivation changes bodily/energy capabilities after earned initiation. These are candidate identities, not four simultaneous skill trees. Research and craft characters need useful field actions, not merely lower prices for fighter builds.

Knowledge, preparation and executable power use the selected technique/origin policy. Reuse existing stance tradeoffs where appropriate; a prepared loadout is one option, while an exceptional copying faculty can have a different or growing capacity. Techniques can complement each other through authored interactions and explicit stacking. [Portable-power policy](#journey-flow-portable-powers-and-interrupted-visits-saved-2026-10-02) owns the cross-world questions and balance proposal. Later worlds earn their place by changing decisions and offering useful returns.

Ancestry, constitution and affinity can change efficiency, available specializations and environmental fit. Keep a baseline campaign route and acquired alternatives. Do not commit to breeding, genetic percentages or irreversible character-creation rolls from the present research.

## Difficulty: combine numbers with readable mechanics

Use fixed authored strength bands for the main campaign. Training and gear should make an older threat visibly easier. Avoid secretly scaling every enemy to the player's current stats, which would obscure the payoff of idle growth. Later optional challenge variants can add disclosed modifiers without rewriting the baseline encounter.

Numbers govern endurance, damage, accuracy, fuel use and preparation efficiency. Mechanics govern armor, interruption, hazard exposure, objective protection and route eligibility. Knowledge reveals counters and alternatives. A stronger character may brute-force some ordinary obstacles at higher supply cost; strength should not substitute for every informational or environmental requirement.

Example: an armored sentry threatens a fragile ledger. A heavy stance improves armor penetration but spends stamina; a crafted brace protects the ledger while a weaker character fights more slowly; a learned exterior route bypasses the sentry and forgoes its combat loot. The campaign milestone is shared, but the route, expense and reward differ. Author another obstacle favoring quick or defensive play so heavy stance does not become a universal answer.

Escalate one dimension at a time: introduce a rule, let the player apply it, then combine it with a familiar rule. Ordinary encounters can run automatically using a prepared loadout. First bosses or trials can pause at phase decisions; no per-second reactions are necessary. New difficulty should ask for a different plan before demanding a much longer wait.

Use deterministic foundational access and adequate preparations. RNG can vary optional loot or quality after a guaranteed viable route exists. Failure should explain its cause, charge only disclosed costs and offer a recoverable next lead. A forced bad ending is not a substitute for a useful failure mechanic.

Measure balance through time to the next decision, viable approaches, consumed supplies, recovery cost and repeated manual clicks. Initial target: compare martial and craft/exploration builds at similar total preparation time, not identical damage stats. Difficulty presets, if added later, can change disclosed safety margins and attrition rather than require separate plot writing or merely slower XP.

## Events, conditional consequences and journey endings

Requested exploration, 2026-10-01: scripted events with different outcomes based on stats, knowledge and other circumstances; scheduled and conditional random events; peer survival; journey endings within the repeated-play loop; and potentially long, grind-heavy progression. All rules below are original proposals pending playable review.

Scripted events follow authored story conditions. Scheduled events follow simulated world time. Conditional random events draw from eligible situations, such as an injured traveler on a traveled road or unusual growth after rain. These can interact: a scripted siege changes scheduled supplies and eligible encounters. Use cooldowns, repetition limits and deterministic foundational routes; rare opportunities should vary or accelerate progression rather than be the only campaign entrance.

Specify what advances time during training, travel, rest and eventually offline play. Consequential decisions initially pause for the player. Events must declare whether they wait, expire in simulated time or resolve through a previously chosen policy. Do not quietly expire a life-saving choice because the browser closed. Safe routine work and first-time story decisions need different automation rules. Offline simulation remains a separate unimplemented contract.

For granular events distinguish recognition, capability, means, opportunity and relationship. Specific poison knowledge identifies a treatment; medical skill supports its execution; inventory supplies it; timing makes it possible; trust determines whether a warning is believed. High Literacy alone should not identify every poison. Track details only when they change an intervention or aftermath, rather than introducing a stat for every narrative adjective.

Example peer injury: treatment consumes scarce medicine; carrying the peer out abandons another objective; faction rescue creates an obligation; foreknowledge enables prevention. No adequate intervention may lead to death, but stabilization or a recovery branch can exist where plausible. Saving someone may leave impairment, disagreement or changed trust. Competence, affection and confidence in motives need not move together; begin with a few authored relationship facts instead of a universal friendship score.

Journey outcomes can include success, retirement, departure, defeat and sacrifice. Ending a chapter/world need not reset the character. A new life must separately preview lost body/local state and retained knowledge or legacy. Initially connect an ending to the existing one-prestige-layer choice rather than adding a second global reset. Workshop, school contact or route knowledge are candidate legacies, not implemented rewards. Define repeat rewards, first-time discoveries and shortened solved stages explicitly.

Long grind is permissible when visible milestones, specialization and better reliability lead to decisions. Buying, commissioning and faction help can substitute for self-training every profession. Track time to useful decisions and repeated manual input. A longer XP wait is not itself a new difficulty mechanic. First event proof: one scheduled opportunity, one conditional encounter and one consequence reflected in a later scene, integrated into the ordinary expedition rather than a separate event simulator.

## Probability: eligibility, reliability and degrees of consequence

Requested exploration, 2026-10-01: uncertain rescues and other percentage-based outcomes. Recommendation: reliable access to essential content, probabilistic performance where appropriate, and several possible consequence grades. Do not turn every story action into a random roll.

Separate prerequisites from odds. Known procedure, minimum capability and essential tools establish eligibility; skill, severity, preparation, assistance and circumstances influence reliability. Improvised alternatives may have different outcome distributions. Better medicine can reduce complications; mastery can shorten treatment; assistance can reduce catastrophe. Avoid collapsing every improvement into the same global success bonus.

Illustrative treatment distribution only, not selected balance: 60% survival without lasting impairment, 30% survival with a complication and recovery lead, 10% failed treatment with evacuation still possible. These categories are exclusive and sum to 100%; treatment failure is not automatically death. Actual distributions must follow the selected event's fiction and tested balance.

Show estimates consistent with knowledge: exact game probabilities for diagnosed conditions, a disclosed uncertainty range or qualitative assessment where diagnosis is incomplete. Distinguish predicted probability from the outcome actually resolved. Missing knowledge can conceal a cause or possible intervention, but visible odds must not falsely exclude hidden modifiers. Explain important contributors and immediate committed costs.

Prefer one outcome roll per meaningful commitment. Five independent 80% checks require about 33% probability to pass all five; multi-stage rolls need real intervention opportunities rather than accidental compounded punishment. Define critical success/failure, caps, diminishing returns, correlated hazards and whether skill can guarantee a safe outcome before introducing them. Routine repeated work can resolve chances automatically with summaries; irreversible story consequences pause for deliberate commitment.

Persist the event instance, committed inputs, applicable probability/rules version and resolved outcome atomically. Reloading cannot reroll or repeat costs/rewards. Temporal replay needs an explicit randomness policy: initially reuse the underlying event draw for an unchanged attempt, while changed preparation alters its outcome thresholds. New events can receive new draws; repeated identical rewinds should not be the optimal rescue strategy. This is proposed policy, not implemented RNG or a claim that foreknowledge reveals the draw. A selected treasure could explicitly override this policy, with its own limits.

For frequent repeated activities, consider streak protection or guaranteed fallback where prolonged bad luck would block progression. For authored consequential events, improve preparation, reveal another intervention or offer an aftermath route rather than promising that eventual repeated rolling will save everyone. Show conditional versus overall probabilities honestly. Skill can sometimes make safety certain at the expense of speed, resources or another objective.

## Starting treasures, opportunities and exceptional advantages

The follow-up [Run-defining advantages comparison](RUN_DEFINING_ADVANTAGES.md) examines trope families and novel evidence where an origin changes the entire run: retention, production, acquisition, allocation or distributed agency. It distinguishes bounded modifiers from new campaign rules and records the source gap for a precise RMJI bottle adaptation. The starting roster and earlier proposed comparison pair remain provisional.

Requested exploration, 2026-10-01: different starting advantages such as a time-travel ring, a guiding system or a vessel that improves resources, inspired by book-associated golden fingers/cheats. The candidate artifacts below are original designs. No particular book artifact, starting roster or direct adaptation is selected.

An advantage should reshape information, preparation, time or access, give a recognizable journey identity and grow through discoveries. It can be deliberately powerful. Balance need not mean identical combat strength; compare campaign viability, convenience, risk, speed and unique possibilities. Preserve obstacles or costs that keep the surrounding world relevant. Avoid making every origin the same all-stat multiplier or forcing everyone to obtain every cheat.

| Starting opportunity | Different play it enables | Proposed limit or cost | Growth opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time-anchor ring | Remember an expedition and revisit an authored checkpoint to change a plan or outcome | Bounded reset domain; no duplicated exported items/rewards; unchanged attempts do not obtain free fresh rolls | Recover new anchors or learn to preserve one additional kind of scenario progress |
| Guiding system | Diagnose requirements, reveal leads and choose an authored contract with a useful reward | Information has a declared scope; it does not automatically know every world or choose commitments for the player | Learn local rule models; unlock a specialization or optional task planner |
| Resource vessel | Improve or mature a selected compatible herb/material, changing supply economics | Limited batch/capacity; needs a viable input and simulated processing; cannot multiply money, quest keys or every material | Discover compatible substrates, quality transformations and competing uses for output |
| Inherited archive or mentor presence | Interpret a method earlier or obtain contextual advice and practice guidance | Partial/domain-specific knowledge; comprehension still needs executable capability and supplies | Reconstruct missing teachings or extend the mentor's usable expertise |
| Adaptive relic | Borrow a situational utility that covers a build weakness | Limited prepared mode, fuel or environment compatibility; do not assume permanent unrestricted copying | Learn a new bounded mode through an observed field application |

Start by prototyping two contrasting origins against the same expedition, rather than five bespoke opening campaigns. Permit deliberate selection for testing; random origin assignment can later be an optional mode with a viable baseline, reroll policy and clear stakes. A found opportunity can be an alternative to a birth gift. An ordinary start can be supported if wanted, without treating it as a compulsory challenge mode.

The advantages must interact with the same event coherently. A ring lets the player act on a learned warning; a system supplies diagnostic guidance; a vessel helps produce treatment stock; an archive teaches the relevant method. None guarantees rescue without the remaining requirements. Compare both unique solutions and the prices of shared solutions.

Decide ownership, discoverability, upgrade conditions, theft/destruction policy, whether an origin can be changed, world compatibility and retention on journey ending separately. Do not impose permanent origin locks or passive upkeep loss in the first test. Switching origin after a committed event cannot retroactively grant its rewards. Stacking may be explored later through explicit budgets and compatibility; one primary advantage initially keeps comparisons readable.

For book inspiration, record title, exact source, canonical function and exceptions, then the invented adaptation and gameplay purpose. Existing dossiers are pointers with bounded coverage, not a license to assert unverified artifact rules. Verify new exact lore when selecting it for design. A directly recreated protagonist cheat and an original mechanic inspired by its narrative role are separate choices. Keep private ebook text out of repository documents.

Recommended addition to the refinement sequence: after the base expedition works, compare a ring origin and a resource-vessel origin with one conditional rescue event. Verify changed decisions, preparation costs, probability previews, aftermath, save/reload and reset boundaries. Their exact rules and balance remain provisional. No new gameplay, randomness system or origin selection UI is implemented by this document.

## Further considerations

| Question | Recommended initial answer |
|---|---|
| How much branching content can be sustained? | Short local branches that converge with remembered consequences; reserve divergent chapter endings for a small number of meaningful commitments |
| What makes a replay emotionally matter? | One person, discovery or relationship has a different outcome; do not give every ending an identical multiplier |
| Does knowledge remove all challenge? | It reveals options; the character still needs the capability, kit and opportunity to act on them |
| Can players idle without surrendering choices? | Routine known preparation repeats to a target; stop at a new discovery, risk limit, missing supplies or authored decision |
| Can players leave without penalties? | Simulated time for scenario pressure; no offline faction decay, pet starvation or unattended irreversible choices |
| How do old chapters remain relevant? | Specific recipes, contacts, routes and new applications; no compulsory upkeep tour of every world |
| How will players understand dense systems? | Journal with leads and requirements, visible cost/risk previews, encounter explanations, readable summaries and keyboard/touch access |
| What is the scope boundary? | One chapter first; no dynamic morality engine, fully simulated economy, procedural infinite campaign or complete multi-book power catalog |
