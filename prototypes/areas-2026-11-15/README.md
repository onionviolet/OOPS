# Areas inside a place, 2026-11-15 (round 17, PS172-PS178 and PS184; proposals, AD32)

Weibao's idea: areas inside a place that you move between, so each area shows its own people, stations and verbs, and entering a building swaps in its own set. This mock draws it in round 14's frame at 1920x1080, as of slice 2, where the Here line counts open their verbs. It uses the [round 14 mock](../yair-columns-2026-11-15/)'s styles and the [UI kit](../ui-kit-2026-11-15/)'s forms. A gold dashed outline marks what is new. Nothing is built until he looks.

Open `index.html?a=a1` to `a4` (keys 1 to 4).

Across the four screens every PS178 gate kind appears: locked, permission, hours, danger, hidden, guarded, means, world law and social cost. Moving is free now (PS177); a world can give a link walking time later. Area names, gates and timings are examples. Hogwarts places are canon.
