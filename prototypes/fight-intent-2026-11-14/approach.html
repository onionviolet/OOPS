One approach strip, two fight views

Mock, not a build. One approach strip sets how you fight and what you intend, ahead of time. The original YAIR auto-combat (left) and the new fight card (right) both read it. With "Finish" set, the original combat plays exactly as it does today: a foe at zero is finished and drops loot.

Stance · how you fight

YAIR's own stances: Crushing Force, Defensive Measures, Flowing Water and the rest. Each changes the numbers and costs stamina. "Pulled Blows" joins them as a stance. Today's Hold back toggle becomes this stance, and it has a skill that cuts the chance of a slip as it levels.

If they go down · your terms

Finish / Spare / Take alive / Ask me. This setting changes the outcome, not the numbers. Its default comes from the world and the foe; set it once per kind of foe. "Take alive" appears where the world has a means: a net, a capture Gu, a creature trainer.

Stakes · what the fight is

Hunt, duel, brawl, robbery. The situation and the other side set the stakes; the player does not pick them. A chip shows them, with the reasons on hover. They change through moves, like a knife drawn or a plea, and the chip flips when they do.
